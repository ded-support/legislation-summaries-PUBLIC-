Summary

LB42 explicitly defines “Department” as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). It authorizes DHHS to prescribe nurse aide training curricula, adopt and promulgate related rules and regulations, and approve training courses (to be consistent with federal requirements). DHHS is also the agency to which a person listed on the Nurse Aide Registry may petition to remove a conviction finding after one year. The bill contains no provisions specifying changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition clarifying that "Service provider" is a person certified by the Department of Health and Human Services to provide developmental disabilities services, and that this applies to providers under the Medicaid Comprehensive Developmental Disabilities Waiver (as defined in §71-6501). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
