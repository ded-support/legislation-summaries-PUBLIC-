Summary

LB580 reappropriates any remaining funds in the Lead Service Line Cash Fund to the Nebraska Department of Environment and Energy for FY2025‑26 to be used for lead service line replacements pursuant to Neb. Rev. Stat. §71‑5328. Remaining funds not allocated for qualified labor training organizations are reappropriated to the Department specifically to provide grants to metropolitan utilities districts for lead service line replacements under §71‑5328. The bill contains an emergency clause and takes effect upon enactment.
