Summary

LB463 expands school safety duties and grant authorities primarily for the State Department of Education (SDE) and the state school security director. Key agency actions and funding changes:

- Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (Dept. of Health/Medicaid oversight) may now fund grants for costs associated with school cardiac emergency response plans. Investment earnings credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024.

- The state school security director (SDE) must develop a cardiac emergency response plan template and collect district safety plans; SDE also to provide training for cardiac emergency response teams.

- SDE may administer grants (districts and educational service units; ESUs may apply on behalf of nonpublic schools) to purchase AEDs and implement plans; the Commissioner prescribes application forms.

- Annual, non‑identifying reporting to the Legislature required (due Dec. 1).

- Intent to appropriate $10 million from the School Safety and Security Fund to SDE to run the grant program. The State Investment Officer invests fund balances.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that the legislature establish a cardiac emergency response plan template for schools and define related terms. Agencies/entities mentioned: "the department" (charged with providing training for cardiac emergency response team members serving public and nonpublic schools), school boards (required to annually review/evaluate each district plan), and local emergency medical services providers (required coordination). The template mandates AED placement and routine maintenance, designation and training of staff (coaches, nurses, athletic trainers), annual drills, and team activation protocols. The bill authorizes grants for costs associated with implementing the plan pursuant to section 7, but contains no specific appropriation amounts.
