Summary

LB211 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-3403 (Property Tax Growth Limitation Act). It specifies that a political subdivision’s preliminary property tax request authority equals the amount of property taxes levied by the county board of equalization in the prior year, less exceptions used under §13-3404. It permits additional increases based on (a) the subdivision’s growth percentage and (b) an inflation component that varies by public-safety intensity: if public safety services (per §13-320) comprise ≥20% of the subdivision’s property-tax-funded budget, the inflation adjustment is the greater of zero or the inflation percentage; if <20%, the inflation adjustment is the greater of 2% or the inflation percentage. The bill repeals the original §13-3403 and takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause. No appropriations or agency funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula allowing a political subdivision to increase its property tax request authority in two components, both applied to prior-year property taxes net of exceptions (per §13-3404(1)–(2)): (1) a growth-percentage increase; and (2) an inflation-based increase. If public safety services (per §13-320) are ≥20% of the property-tax–funded budget, the inflation component is the greater of zero or the inflation percentage. If public safety is <20%, the inflation component is the greater of 2% or the inflation percentage. No specific agencies receive direct appropriations; this changes allowable tax request authority.
