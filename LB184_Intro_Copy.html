Summary

This bill adds prohibitions and penalties related to nitrous oxide and assigns enforcement roles to existing agencies. It bars tobacco specialty stores and licensed tobacco retailers from selling or distributing nitrous oxide and prohibits sale of devices specifically designed for recreational inhalation. Violations carry misdemeanor penalties, license revocation/forfeiture by the courts, and seizure and destruction of contraband at the violator’s expense.

Agencies mentioned/affected:

- Cigarette Tax Division of the Nebraska Tax Commissioner: may revoke or suspend licenses for related violations and treat contraband cigarettes as subject to seizure and destruction.

- Courts: authorized to order revocation/forfeiture of tobacco-related licenses.

- U.S. Food and Drug Administration and federal foreign-trade-zone provisions are referenced for exclusions.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions and penalties on sale, distribution, and transport of objects containing nitrous oxide:

- Applies to holders of licenses under sections 28‑1420 to 28‑1429 (tobacco specialty stores): selling or allowing removal of nitrous oxide items is a Class II misdemeanor for a first offense, Class I for subsequent offenses, and courts may revoke and forfeit the license per section 28‑1429.

- Delivery sales by licensed sellers are a Class I misdemeanor; common carriers who knowingly transport such items for violators are also guilty of a Class I misdemeanor.

- Selling devices designed for recreational inhalation: Class III misdemeanor first offense, Class II thereafter.

- Violations are deemed deceptive trade practices under the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. All offending items are subject to seizure, forfeiture, destruction, with costs borne by the person from whom seized.

- Exemptions: shipments to foreign trade zones per 19 U.S.C. §81a et seq., and government employees acting in official duties.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
