LB350 items referencing the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and the Department of Correctional Services:

- DHHS: Judges may commit defendants to DHHS for competency restoration (state hospital, other state facility, or contract provider). If DHHS proposes treatment by a contract facility/provider it must file a report with the court. If a committed defendant remains in a county jail, DHHS shall reimburse the county for lodging (daily lodging, food, medical services, transportation, other necessary costs) at $100/day for each day after the first 30 days, with annual July 1 CPI-based adjustments. DHHS also provides the emergency protective custody certificate form used by law enforcement.

- Department of Correctional Services: Persons taken into emergency protective custody who have prior sex-offense convictions must be admitted to a jail or a Department of Correctional Services facility unless a medical/psychiatric emergency requires a medical facility.
