Summary

LB467 amends the Utilization Review Act (sections 44-5416 to 44-5431) to require health carriers and utilization review agents to implement and maintain a prior-authorization application programming interface (API) by January 1, 2028, consistent with federal regulation 45 C.F.R. 156.223(b) as it existed January 1, 2025. The API requirement does not apply to prior-authorization requests for drugs (drug defined by 45 C.F.R. 156.221(b)(1)(v) as of January 1, 2025). The bill updates the statutory citation to include this new section and repeals the original section 44-5416. No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
