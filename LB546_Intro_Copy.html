Summary

LB546 amends §81‑829.40 to restate and clarify the Governor’s authority and duties in disasters, emergencies, and civil defense emergencies. It specifies the Governor’s general direction and control of emergency management and the Nebraska Emergency Management Agency (NEMA); requires proclamations to describe affected areas and be filed with NEMA, the Secretary of State, and local government clerks; and confirms activation of state, city, village, county, and interjurisdictional emergency management organizations and plans. The Governor is affirmed as commander in chief of the militia and may suspend regulatory statutes and employ all available state and local resources. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
