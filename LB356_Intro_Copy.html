Summary

LB356 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §82-309 to specify that the Nebraska Arts Council consists of fifteen members appointed by the Governor from Nebraska citizens known for professional competence and experience in the arts. The statute directs that, in making appointments, consideration be given to recommendations from representative civic, educational, and professional associations and groups concerned with or engaged in the production or presentation of the arts. The bill also repeals the original §82-309. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
