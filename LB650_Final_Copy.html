Summary

The bill assigns roles and limits across agencies:

- Department of Economic Development (DED): administers the Community Development Assistance Act (certifying programs, adopting rules, approving proposals) and reviews Urban Redevelopment Act applications (fee $500; approvals capped at $8 million expected incentives), transmitting approved materials to the Department of Revenue (DOR).

- Department of Revenue / Tax Commissioner: prescribes forms/rules, certifies and approves multiple tax credits (including food-donation credits—up to $500,000 in FY2025‑26—and reverse‑osmosis/other credits), caps Community Development Assistance Act credits at $350,000/year, publishes county lists of property subject to tax sale, and gains expanded enforcement, audit, confidentiality, and disclosure authorities (including permitted disclosures to municipalities, certified municipal representatives, and the Auditor). The Tax Commissioner may disclose taxpayer data to the Department of Labor when necessary.

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): collects vehicle sales tax at registration, may retain a collection fee (deposited to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund), must remit collections to the Tax Commissioner by the 15th of the following month (fee forfeited if collection rules are violated), and remits specified penalties set at $5.

- County/large-county provisions: county treasurers (in counties ≥150,000 pop.) must remit $1 of their collection fee for each of the first 5,000 registrations to the State Treasurer for credit to the DOR Enforcement Fund.

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): budget division directs timing/amounts for a $750 million General Fund transfer to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund (FY2024‑25); DAS employees are covered by the misdemeanor prohibition on disclosing taxpayer information.
