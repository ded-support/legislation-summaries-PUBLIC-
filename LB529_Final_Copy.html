Summary

LB529 amends the State Procurement Act (section 73-803) to revise definitions used for procurement. It clarifies that "division" refers to the materiel division of the Department of Administrative Services and redefines "state agency" to exclude the University of Nebraska, the Nebraska state colleges, the Nebraska Investment Council, the courts, the Legislature, and any officer or agency established by the Nebraska Constitution. The change is applied to actions on or after July 19, 2024. The bill repeals the prior version of section 73-803 and declares an emergency, making the act effective upon passage. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
