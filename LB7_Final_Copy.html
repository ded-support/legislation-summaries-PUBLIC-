Summary

This bill assigns the Department of Agriculture specific enforcement and reporting roles under the Foreign‑owned Real Estate National Security Act:

- Any restricted entity (or agent/trustee/fiduciary) that meets certain CFIUS conditions must annually certify compliance to the Department of Agriculture by January 15, 2026, and each January 15 thereafter.

- Any person may notify the Department of Agriculture of a violation or potential violation.

- The Department of Agriculture is charged with investigating alleged violations; the Director of Agriculture must refer suspected violations to the Attorney General or retained outside counsel for enforcement.

- The Director of Agriculture, together with the Attorney General and retained counsel, is responsible for determining whether a person is subject to or in violation of the act.

No provisions in the bill change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual certification duty to the Department of Agriculture: any "restricted entity" (or its agent, trustee, or fiduciary) that has either (1) a CFIUS determination of no unresolved national‑security concerns or (2) an active national security agreement with CFIUS must certify that status to the Department of Agriculture on or before January 15, 2026 and each January 15 thereafter. The change also clarifies exemption language for certain foreign owners under the Foreign‑owned Real Estate National Security Act but preserves exceptions for restricted entities. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Agriculture are specified.
