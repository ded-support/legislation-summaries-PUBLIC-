Summary

LB334 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32-607 regarding candidate filing forms and filing officers. It specifies the Secretary of State as the filing officer for statewide and multi-county offices (including judges desiring retention) and designates county election commissioners or county clerks for county, city, village, and certain school offices. Candidate forms must list specified contact and qualification information (email optional) and note any civil penalties under the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act. Critically, the bill requires that the residence address, mailing address, and telephone number on filing forms for judges desiring retention be kept confidential by the filing officer. The original section is repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
