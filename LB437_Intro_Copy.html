Summary

LB437 repeals the Nebraska Health Care Certificate of Need Act and numerous related statutes. It amends sections 71-20,103 and 71-20,105 to harmonize review procedures under the Nonprofit Hospital Sale Act, specifying that the department shall review applications and issue decisions within set timeframes. The Attorney General retains a role to elect review within twenty days and, if elected, complete review within sixty days. The bill eliminates obsolete provisions and repeals a long list of statutory sections. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are addressed.
