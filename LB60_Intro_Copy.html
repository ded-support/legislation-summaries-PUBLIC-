Summary

LB60 (2025) amends section 9-829 of the State Lottery Act to permit sale of lottery tickets via vending or dispensing devices by authorized lottery game retailers. The bill retains prohibitions on ticket sales by telephone or mail and on extending credit for ticket purchases. The original version of section 9-829 is repealed. Agencies/departments: changes apply to the State Lottery framework and to licensed lottery game retailers. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
