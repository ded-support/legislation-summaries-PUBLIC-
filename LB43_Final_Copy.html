Summary

LB43 amends Nebraska electric-siting statutes to add definitions (including “foreign adversary” per 15 C.F.R. 791.4) and narrows “military installation” to specified USAF silos and bases (31 C.F.R. citations). It requires owners of generation or >700‑volt transmission projects within ten miles of such installations to certify to the board (and its executive director) that no electronic-related equipment/components are manufactured by a foreign adversary, with a NERC compliance exemption for suppliers meeting North American Electric Reliability Corporation standards. Privately developed renewable facilities remain exempt from board review if they notify the board 30 days before construction, submit decommissioning plans and security, consult the Game and Parks Commission, and execute joint transmission development agreements with transmission owners or RTO processes. The board may preapprove limited use of foreign-adversary components and may sue the State for decommissioning noncompliance. No appropriations or budget changes are included. The act is an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for electric suppliers and facility owners near military installations to notify or certify to "the board" that their generation/transmission facilities contain no electronic equipment or components manufactured by a defined "foreign adversary" (per 15 C.F.R. 791.4 as of Feb 7, 2025). Identifies covered military sites by reference to 31 C.F.R. sections (as of Jan 1, 2025), including U.S. Air Force ballistic missile silos and bases. Owners of facilities tied to the transmission grid at ≥100 kV and meeting nameplate/injection thresholds must alternatively certify compliance with North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) critical infrastructure protection standards. The board may preapprove use of foreign‑adversary equipment only if no reasonable alternative exists and avoiding it would cause greater harm. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
