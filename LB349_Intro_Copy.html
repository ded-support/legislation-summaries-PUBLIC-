Summary

LB349 primarily amends provisions administered by the Nebraska Power Review Board (the board) and its executive director. Key changes:

- Expands defined terms to expressly include "electric energy storage resource" and revises "electric supplier," "private electric supplier," and "privately developed renewable energy generation facility."

- Requires board application/approval before constructing or acquiring electric generation facilities, electric energy storage resources, or transmission facilities over 700 volts, while preserving specified exemptions. Privately developed renewable facilities remain exempt if they comply with section 70‑1014.02.

- Establishes enforcement procedures administered by the board and its executive director for private electric suppliers who commence construction less than 30 days before required notice: a $500 fine, a 20‑day cure/pay window, mandatory cessation if not cured, and an administrative hearing process (hearing set within 60 days; board decision within 60 days). Hearing costs may be charged to the supplier if found in violation.

- Repeals the original versions of the amended sections.

No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statutory definition of "electric energy storage resource": a resource that can receive and store electric energy for later injection to the grid (6)(a), and expressly excludes devices solely for reactive power (capacitors, synchronous condensers) or equipment solely to power electric vehicles (6)(b). Other inserted phrases appear to be minor textual references to "electric energy storage resources." No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
