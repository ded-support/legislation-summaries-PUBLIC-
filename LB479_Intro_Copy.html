Summary

LB479 amends section 77-27,132 to direct that, for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023, all proceeds of sales and use taxes imposed under section 77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (per section 3-101) be credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. The bill takes operative effect October 1, 2025, and repeals the original version of section 77-27,132.
