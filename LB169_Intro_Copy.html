Summary

LB169 amends Nebraska tax law and reporting requirements. Key agency provisions:

- Department of Revenue: expands the Tax Expenditure Reporting Act to require reporting of revenue loss from failing to tax services purchased for nonbusiness use and to list specified sales-and-use tax expenditures by category.

- Nebraska Investment Finance Authority, State Racing and Gaming Commission, and Nebraska State Fair Board are explicitly listed among entities referenced in the sales-and-use tax expenditure categories.

- Political subdivisions and electric cooperatives are addressed regarding exclusions for certain utility-related services.

- Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act: clarifies the state occupation tax is in addition to state sales and use taxes on mechanical amusement device gross receipts and prohibits political subdivisions from imposing additional mechanical-device taxes other than local option sales and use taxes.

Operative date: October 1, 2025. No direct appropriations or new funding allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text enumerates a broad set of service receipts (chartered flights; clothing cleaning except amounts exempt under §77‑2704.14; dating; hair care except cutting; interior design; lobbying; chartered local passenger transportation; marketing/telemarketing; massage; nail care; dance/golf/tennis instruction; ground sightseeing; skin care; pool cleaning/maintenance; tattoo/body modification; telefloral delivery; travel agency; weight‑loss services) and expressly includes state sales and use tax on gross receipts from operation of mechanical amusement devices (excluding local option sales/use taxes). No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
