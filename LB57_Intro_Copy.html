Summary

LB57 states the Legislature's intent to appropriate funds to the Department of Health and Human Services for Medicaid assisted‑living facility rates (Program No. 348). It specifies $7,926,576 for FY2025‑26 ( $3,345,808 General Funds; $4,580,768 Federal Funds) and $8,243,639 for FY2026‑27 ($3,479,640 General Funds; $4,763,999 Federal Funds). The amounts are designated to increase Program 348 Medicaid assisted‑living facility rates under Nebraska’s aged and disabled home and community‑based 1915(c) waiver to a daily rate of $88.24 for FY2025‑26 and $91.78 for FY2026‑27 for both rural and urban assisted‑living waiver days.

Intent to appropriate to the Department of Health and Human Services:

- FY2025-26: $7,926,576 total ( $3,345,808 General Fund; $4,580,768 Federal Funds).

Funds are designated for Program 348 to raise Medicaid assisted‑living facility rates under Nebraska’s aged and disabled home-and-community‑based 1915(c) waiver to a daily rate of $88.24 in FY2025‑26 and $91.78 in FY2026‑27 for both rural and urban assisted‑living waiver days.
