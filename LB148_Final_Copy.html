Summary

LB148 amends section 38-131 to add subsection (6) specifying that, for purposes of interpretation by the FBI, the term "department" in that section means the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. No provisions in the bill explicitly allocate funds, appropriate monies, or change budgetary amounts for the Department of Health and Human Services.

For the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- The Compact requires DHHS to share licensure, disciplinary, and investigative information and to comply with Commission rules and reporting duties.
