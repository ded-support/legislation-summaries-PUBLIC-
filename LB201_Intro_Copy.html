Summary

LB201 amends the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act (section 8-2701) to require licensees or authorized delegates to collect a fee for each money transmission sent outside the United States equal to 10% of the transaction value, capped at $10,000. Fees are remitted quarterly to "the department" on forms prescribed by the department with the assistance of the Nebraska State Patrol, due by the 15th day following each calendar quarter. The act becomes operative January 1, 2026, and repeals the original section. No specific appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that licensees or authorized delegates collect a fee on each money transmission to persons outside the U.S.: 10% of the transaction value or $10,000, whichever is less. Fees must be remitted quarterly to "the department" using a form prescribed by that department, prepared with assistance from the Nebraska State Patrol. All fees and forms are due by the 15th day of the month following each calendar quarter. No explicit appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
