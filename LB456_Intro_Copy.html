Summary

LB456 amends §48‑146.02 of the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act. It authorizes three‑judge panels of the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court to seek suspension or revocation of a workers' compensation insurer's authorization from the Director of Insurance, to suspend/revoke a risk management pool's group self‑insurance authority under the Intergovernmental Risk Management Act, or to revoke a self‑insurer's approval under §48‑145, without affecting preexisting liabilities. The Attorney General, at the compensation court administrator's request, may file show‑cause motions, be treated as a party, present evidence, and appear before the panel; the presiding judge appoints panel judges and does not serve. The bill enumerates insurer/pool/self‑insurer practices that trigger action, permits the compensation court to examine records of insurers, pools, self‑insurers, adjusters, third‑party administrators, and agents (subject to work‑product and attorney‑client privilege), and allows the court to adopt implementing rules. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
