Summary

LB385 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §87-302 (Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act), expanding and clarifying categories of deceptive trade practices — including false privacy-policy statements; restrictions on installing or activating file‑sharing programs on others’ computers; treating each misbranded individually packaged substance as a separate violation; prohibiting publication/monetization of sexually explicit, obscene, or harmful-to-minors material when participants are minors, trafficking victims, or nonconsenting (with a telecommunications/broadband exception); and barring certain right-to-list home sale agreements. The bill cites federal 47 U.S.C. §254 and several Nebraska criminal statutes for definitions, repealing the original section. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
