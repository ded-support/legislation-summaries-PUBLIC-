Summary

LB21 adopts the Uniform Unlawful Restrictions in Land Records Act. It authorizes property owners to record an amendment removing an unlawful restriction; the amendment must identify the owner, the affected real property, and the original document and include a mandated conspicuous statement. Amendments must be executed and acknowledged according to the requirements for recordation and recorded in the land records of each county where the original document is recorded (i.e., county land records/registries). The amendment does not affect restrictions that are lawful, nor does it constitute republication that would extend a restriction’s statutory expiration. No state agencies, departments, appropriations, or budget changes are specified.
