Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Agriculture and "Director" as the Director of Agriculture (or designated representative). Creates the Nebraska Apiary Advisory Board, composed of the Director and additional members appointed by the Director (including at least one commercial beekeeper, one hobbyist/sideliner, and one beekeeper active in a beekeeping organization); the Director serves as chair and fills vacancies. The Director may invite (but not require) a University of Nebraska entomology/apiculture representative to join. Board members serve without compensation but are eligible for expense reimbursement under sections 81-1174 to 81-1177. No explicit changes to Department funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Nebraska Apiary Advisory Board within the Department of Agriculture. The Department director appoints and chairs the board, selecting at least one commercial beekeeper, one beekeeper active in a club, and one hobby/sideliner beekeeper; may invite (nonrequired) a UN entomology/apiculture representative. Appointees serve without pay but may receive expense reimbursement under state travel statutes. The board will identify research gaps, recommend prevention/recovery actions for bee losses, assess impacts on Nebraska food systems, develop public education strategies, and work with the Legislature. Separately, the Board of Regents must create a full-time University of Nebraska–Lincoln extension educator with apiculture expertise. No explicit appropriations or budget allocations are included.
