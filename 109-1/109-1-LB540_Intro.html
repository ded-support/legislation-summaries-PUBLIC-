Summary

LB540 explicitly defines "Department" as the Department of Agriculture and "Director" as the Director of Agriculture (or designated representative). It creates the Nebraska Apiary Advisory Board, to be chaired by the Director of Agriculture, with board members appointed by the Director and the Director responsible for filling vacancies. The board may include an invited University of Nebraska entomology representative. Members serve without compensation but are eligible for expense reimbursement pursuant to existing statutes (sections 81-1174 to 81-1177). The bill contains no new appropriations or other funding changes directed to the Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Apiary Advisory Board under the director (Department of Agriculture). The director appoints and chairs the board, fills vacancies, and may invite (but not require) one University of Nebraska entomology/apiculture representative. Membership must include at least one commercial beekeeper, one beekeeper active in a club, and one hobbyist/sideliner beekeeper. Members serve without compensation but may receive expense reimbursement under sections 81-1174 to 81-1177. The board will identify research gaps, prioritize needs to improve managed and wild bee health/habitat, recommend actionable protection and recovery steps, review related statutes/policies, and guide education/outreach. The Board of Regents must create a full-time apiculture educator in the UNL extension service. No new appropriations or specific funding/budget allocations are provided.
