Summary

LB712 amends section 77-4008 of the Tobacco Products Tax Act to set the tax on electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) at 40% of (i) the purchase price paid by the first owner or (ii) the price at which the first owner who made or manufactured the ENDS sells it. The tax remains imposed on the first owner; for retail dealers, tax liability arises when ENDS are brought into Nebraska, manufactured in Nebraska, or sold to Nebraska consumers. When both buyer and seller are licensed under section 77-4009, the seller is liable for the tax. Amounts collected are to be used and distributed pursuant to section 77-4025. The bill repeals the original section. No specific agency appropriation or new funding allocation is named.
