Summary

LB712 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-4008 (Tobacco Products Tax Act). It sets the tax on electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) at 40% of (i) the purchase price paid by the first owner or (ii) the price at which the first owner who made/manufactured/fabricated the ENDS sells it to others. It retains snuff at $0.44 per ounce and other tobacco products at 20% of purchase/wholesale price. For retailers, the ENDS tax is triggered when products are brought into the state, manufactured in-state for sale, or sold to consumers. Collections are to be distributed pursuant to §77-4025. No specific state agencies or appropriations are named. The original section is repealed.
