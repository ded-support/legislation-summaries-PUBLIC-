Summary

LB36 defines "department" as the Department of Transportation and designates the Nebraska DOT as authority over the state highway system and federal‑aid primary road designations (subject to U.S. Secretary of Transportation approval). The DOT may redesignate, relocate, or recreate routes with State Highway Commission advice and the Governor’s consent, considering traffic, costs, safety, and economic factors. The bill authorizes the DOT to acquire land, interests and easements (including by eminent domain) for highway purposes; to establish and operate mitigation banks or in‑lieu‑fee programs for compensatory mitigation; to enter cooperative agreements and adopt related rules; and requires payment in lieu of ad valorem taxes for mitigation property acquired after July 1, 2026. No explicit appropriations or budget allocations to the DOT are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the Department of Transportation to establish, use, and operate a mitigation bank or in‑lieu‑fee program to provide compensatory mitigation for transportation projects it administers (impacts to threatened/endangered species or habitat, wetland dredge/fill, and habitat restoration/creation/enhancement/preservation). State regulatory agencies must give priority to the department's mitigation bank when reviewing mitigation plans. The department may enter cooperative agreements with public or private parties and may acquire real property for the program—including by purchase, gift, or eminent domain. Owners of mitigation bank property acquired after July 1, 2026 must pay a sum in lieu of lost ad valorem taxes to the county. The department may adopt rules. No explicit new appropriations or line‑item funding changes are specified.
