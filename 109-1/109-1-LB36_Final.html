Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Nebraska Department of Transportation (NDOT) and assigns NDOT primary authority over the state highway system and designation of federal‑aid primary roads. NDOT may establish, operate, and use mitigation banks or in‑lieu‑fee programs (including wetlands banks) for compensatory mitigation, and state regulatory agencies are directed to give priority to NDOT programs. The department may enter cooperative agreements, promulgate rules, acquire real property (purchase, bequest, donation, or by eminent domain), and redesignate, relocate, or recreate the state highway system with State Highway Commission advice and gubernatorial consent. No explicit appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes for NDOT are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates authority for the Department of Transportation to establish, use, and operate mitigation banks and in-lieu-fee programs to provide compensatory mitigation for DOT-administered projects (impacts to threatened/endangered species/habitats, wetland dredge/fill, and habitat restoration/creation/enhancement/preservation). State agencies are directed to give priority to using these DOT programs when reviewing mitigation plans. The department may enter cooperative agreements with public or private parties, acquire real property (purchase, bequest, donation, or eminent domain) for mitigation purposes, and adopt implementing rules. Property acquired for mitigation after July 1, 2026 must incur payment in lieu of lost ad valorem taxes to the county. No explicit changes to appropriations, dedicated funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Transportation are specified.
