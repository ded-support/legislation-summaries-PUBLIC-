Summary

The bill consolidates and clarifies authority in the Nebraska Department of Transportation (defined as “Department”), vesting the Director‑State Engineer and the Department as the direct custodians and primary authority over the state highway system and federal‑aid primary roads. It affirms DOT powers to plan, construct, maintain, acquire, operate, protect, redesignate/relocate, and enter agreements (including with the U.S. and adjoining states) regarding interstate bridges and highways, and to acquire or condemn land for highway purposes. The Department may establish and operate mitigation banks or in‑lieu‑fee programs for compensatory mitigation, adopt rules, enter cooperative agreements, and acquire real property (including by eminent domain) for mitigation; state regulatory agencies are directed to prioritize DOT mitigation banks. No explicit changes to DOT funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates authority for the Department of Transportation to establish, operate, and use a mitigation bank or in‑lieu‑fee program to provide compensatory mitigation for impacts of DOT-administered transportation projects (threatened/endangered species/habitat, wetland dredge/fill, restoration/creation/enhancement/preservation). State regulatory agencies are to give priority to using the department's program. The department may enter cooperative agreements with public or private entities, acquire real property (purchase, bequest, donation, or eminent domain) for mitigation, and adopt rules. Property acquired after July 1, 2026 must pay a sum in lieu of lost ad valorem taxes to the county. No explicit appropriations or new budget allocations for the department are included.
