Summary

- Requires the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) to pay committed offenders wages at least equal to the Wage and Hour Act, open deposit accounts for wage-earning offenders, and return/control earned funds at release (minimum one-third paid at release; full payment within six months).

- Directs the DCS director to promulgate rules on hours, labor conditions, compensation, due process, and restitution collection; authorizes withholding/transfers to satisfy restitution and requires an annual legislative report (number of inmates with restitution judgments, average payments, total collected).

- Requires DCS to act in accordance with the federal Occupational Safety and Health Act.

- Creates/clarifies the Reentry Cash Fund administered by DCS (funded by remittances under §33-157 and donations) to pay tuition, fees, and other reentry program costs for offenders in work camps.

- §33-157 directs initial transfers from the Nebraska Crime Victim Fund to the DCS Facility Cash Fund to cover LB510 implementation costs as certified by DCS.

- Defines occupational classification using the U.S. Department of Labor’s Dictionary of Occupational Titles.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Labor (Wage and Hour Act): The bill extends Wage and Hour Act wage protections to inmates and committed offenders—requiring wages "equal to or greater than" statutory levels—and explicitly includes the State and its political subdivisions (including inmates employed by the Department of Correctional Services or jails) within the employer definition.

- Department of Correctional Services: The department must open deposit accounts for committed offenders earning wages (per section 83-183.01), give inmates control of those accounts, ensure inmate wages meet Wage and Hour Act standards, and comply with federal Occupational Safety and Health Act requirements for inmate employment. The Jail Standards Board will adopt rules on labor conditions, restitution transfers, and annual restitution reporting; administrators must provide data to support those reports.

No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
