Summary

LB443 amends the Nebraska Criminal Code (section 28-101) to create the offense of unlawful squatting and assigns duties to law enforcement. Law enforcement officers with probable cause must issue a warning citation (probable-cause examples listed include personal property indicating abandonment or nonpermanent structures). The citation requires the recipient to appear within three business days before a designee of the officer’s law enforcement agency and produce documentation authorizing residence (deed, title, rental agreement, proof of payments; photos/electronic copies acceptable). Failure to appear or produce authentic authorization exposes the person to arrest; conviction is a Class I misdemeanor. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included. The prior version of section 28-101 is repealed.
