Summary

LB375 creates the Grocer Reinvestment Option Fund, to be administered by the department (agency name not specified). The department may use up to 15% of fund money for administrative costs, adopt rules, and must report annually to the Legislature (first report due October 31, 2025) on projects funded, geographic distribution, applicant identities, and resulting new investment. The fund will consist of legislative transfers and private gifts/grants; monies available for investment are to be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The bill expresses legislative intent to appropriate an unspecified amount for FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27. Grants must be spent within three years; unspent grant funds are recouped and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund.
