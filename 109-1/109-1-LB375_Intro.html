Summary

Creates the Grocer Reinvestment Option Act and establishes the Grocer Reinvestment Option Fund, to be administered by "the department" (agency unspecified in bill text). The department may use up to 15% of the fund for administrative costs. Fund sources: legislative transfers and gifts/grants/private contributions. Available funds are to be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Legislature intends to appropriate an unspecified amount for FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27. The department must report annually (by Oct. 31) on funded projects, locations, applicants, and new investment. Grants must be spent within three years; unspent disbursed grant funds are recouped, remitted to the State Treasurer, and credited to the General Fund. The department may adopt rules and regulations.
