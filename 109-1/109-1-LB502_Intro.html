Summary

LB502 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-1714 (Farm Labor Contractors Act) to specify criminal penalties: violations of §48-1711 or §48-1712 are Class II misdemeanors; intentionally defacing/altering or misusing a farm labor contractor license, permitting such use, or acting without a license are Class II misdemeanors; and seed corn producers or farm labor contractors who violate §48-1715 are guilty of a Class IV misdemeanor. The bill repeals the original §48-1714 and states it harmonizes provisions. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
