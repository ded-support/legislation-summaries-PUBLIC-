Summary

LB399 amends the Mutual Finance Assistance Act. It authorizes creation of mutual finance organizations under the Interlocal Cooperation Act or Joint Public Agency Act with three-year agreements that (1) set an agreed maximum property tax rate, (2) require each member to levy that rate within its boundaries for one of the three tax years (not necessarily the same year for all members), and (3) cap levies at that rate in the other years; levies for bonded indebtedness and lease-purchase contracts in existence on or after July 1, 1998, are excluded. It lowers the eligibility threshold for distributions from the Mutual Finance Assistance Fund to rural or suburban fire protection districts and mutual finance organizations that contain either an assumed population of 30,000+ or at least 60% of the assumed county population residing outside first/primary/metropolitan class cities. Uses U.S. Bureau of the Census estimates and certifications under section 77-3,119. Operative October 1, 2025. No new appropriations specified.
