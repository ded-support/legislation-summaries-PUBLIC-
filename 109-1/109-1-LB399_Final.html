Summary

LB399 amends the Mutual Finance Assistance Act (secs. 35-1204–35-1205). Key agency/entity effects:

- Authorizes mutual finance organizations formed under the Interlocal Cooperation Act or Joint Public Agency Act; agreements must run three years, require each member to levy the same agreed property tax rate within their boundaries for one of the three tax years (not necessarily the same year), and limit levies to no more than that rate in the other years. Levies exclude bonded indebtedness and lease-purchase contracts in existence on or after July 1, 1998.

- Revises qualification for distributions from the Mutual Finance Assistance Fund to rural/suburban fire protection districts and mutual finance organizations by setting the outside‑city assumed‑population threshold at 60% of a county’s assumed population and detailing the Census‑based assumed population calculation (referencing section 77‑3,119).

- Operative date: October 1, 2025.

No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are changed; the bill alters eligibility and levy rules affecting fund distributions.
