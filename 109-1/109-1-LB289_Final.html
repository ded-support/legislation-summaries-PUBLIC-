Summary

LB289 amends statutes governing villages, elections, and municipal economic development. Key agency/departments and procedural changes:

- County board: must appoint initial village trustees at incorporation consistent with petitioner’s choice of a three- or five-member board; cannot incorporate territory within five miles of another municipality.

- Village board of trustees / city councils: villages may choose three or five trustees; procedures for changing board size via voter petition or board resolution; canvass by county canvassing board; vacancies handled per existing statutory procedures. City or village governing bodies may serve as a board of adjustment.

- Election commissioner or county clerk and village clerk: administer and verify petitions, file resolution, and place the question on the statewide general election ballot.

- Local Option Municipal Economic Development Act: expands “qualifying business” to expressly include retail trade and clarifies certain categories (housing construction/rehab, early childhood care/education, advanced telecommunications).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a procedure allowing village electors to decide at a statewide general election whether a village board of trustees will have three or five members. Key agencies/departments: village clerk (to provide petition forms), election commissioner or county clerk (to assist with petitions, receive filings at least 70 days before the election, place the question on the ballot, and canvass/count results), and the county canvassing board (to complete the canvass). Petitions require signatures from at least 5% of registered village electors and are verified per statute; a village board may instead adopt a submitting resolution. References procedures in sections 32-568, 32-569, and 17-202 for filling vacancies. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budgets.
