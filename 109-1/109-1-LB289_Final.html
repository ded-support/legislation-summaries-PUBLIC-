Summary

LB289 modifies statutes governing villages and municipal economic development. Key agency references and operational changes:

- County board: must accept incorporation petitions that now designate whether a new village board will have three or five trustees and will appoint three or five initial trustees at incorporation.

- County canvassing board: completes canvass of village incorporation and later trustee-number elections.

- Election commissioner or county clerk: receives petitions/resolutions to submit trustee-number questions (petitions require signatures from at least 5% of registered village electors, filed ≥70 days before a general election); conducts election and canvass.

- Village clerk: provides petition forms and assists with petition procurement.

- Village board of trustees and city councils (first and second class): may, by ordinance, serve as the board of adjustment (decisions require a two-thirds vote of the body).

- Local Option Municipal Economic Development Act: redefines “qualifying business” to expressly include retail trade (with a statutory definition).

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures allowing village voters to choose whether their board of trustees has three or five members via petition or board resolution submitted for a statewide general election. Key agencies: village clerk (issues petitions), election commissioner or county clerk (assists with petitions, accepts filings at least 70 days before the election, places the question on the ballot, and gives notice), and the county canvassing board (conducts the canvass and finalizes results). Petitions must equal at least 5% of registered village electors from the prior statewide general election and be verified per section 32-631. Vote outcomes trigger statutory procedures: increasing to five invokes sections 32-568/32-569 (vacancies), decreasing to three invokes section 17-202. Specifies initial term lengths (two four-year and one two-year) by vote ranking. No funding or appropriation changes.
