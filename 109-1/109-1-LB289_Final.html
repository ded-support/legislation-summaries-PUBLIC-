Summary

LB289 amends village governance, zoning, and municipal economic development statutes. Key agency/deparment actions:

- County board: may incorporate villages that specify whether the initial village board will have three or five trustees; appoints initial trustees accordingly.

- Election commissioner or county clerk and county canvassing board: handle petitions or board resolutions to change trustee number (petitions require signatures from at least 5% of registered village electors, procured via the village clerk with election commissioner/county clerk assistance, filed ≥70 days before the general election, and canvassed post‑election).

- Village clerk: provides petition forms.

- City councils (first/second class) and village boards of trustees: may, by ordinance, serve as a board of adjustment; decisions require a two‑thirds concurring vote.

The bill expands “qualifying business” under the Local Option Municipal Economic Development Act (adds advanced telecommunications, tourism, film production, housing construction/rehab, early childhood care/education, retail trade, and a relaxed standard for cities ≤5,000). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures allowing village electors to choose at a statewide general election whether their village board of trustees has three or five members. Agencies/departments referenced and responsibilities:

- Village board of trustees: may adopt resolution to submit the question (majority vote) and will follow statutory procedures if size changes.

- Registered voters: may file petitions (>=5% of prior statewide-election registrants) to trigger the question.

- Village clerk: must provide petition forms.

- Election commissioner or county clerk: assists with petitions, receives petitions/resolutions (filed >=70 days before the general election), places the question on the ballot, and conducts/counts the vote.

- County canvassing board: completes the canvass and decides the proposition.

Statutory follow-up: if increased to five, vacancies handled per sections 32-568/32-569; if decreased to three, follow section 17-202. Initial term lengths for trustees after incorporation or change are specified. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
