Summary

LB619 directs the Department of Transportation to award joint grants to cities of the primary class and their counties to construct motor-vehicle infrastructure connecting a highway (per §39‑101) to the National System of Interstate and Defense Highways. Recipients must report to the Department annually until grant funds are expended. Unused grant funds must be repaid to the Department of Transportation; repaid amounts are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The Legislature states intent to appropriate $15 million from the General Fund to the Department of Transportation for FY2025‑26 for these grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Transportation is directed to administer joint grants to cities of the primary class and their counties to construct motor vehicle infrastructure that connects a defined state highway (per §39‑101) to the National System of Interstate and Defense Highways. Grantees must report to DOT annually until grant funds are expended; any funds not used for the intended purpose must be repaid to DOT and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The Legislature intends a $15 million General Fund appropriation to DOT in FY 2025–26 for this program.
