Summary

LB86 appropriates $500,000 from the General Fund for FY2025‑26 to the Nebraska Department of Natural Resources (Program 334). The funds are designated as state aid to be used solely to issue a grant to a natural resources district that has more than 250 watershed structures. Grants may fund operation, maintenance, or repair projects for those structures; a project is eligible only if its total cost exceeds $30,000 per structure. The bill includes an emergency clause, making the appropriation effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates $500,000 General Fund for FY2025–26 to the Department of Natural Resources (Program 334) to issue a grant to any natural resources district that owns more than 250 watershed structures. Funds are for projects involving operation, maintenance, or repair of those structures; a project is eligible only if its total cost exceeds $30,000 per structure. The appropriation is designated as $500,000 General Funds for state aid and may be used solely for this purpose.
