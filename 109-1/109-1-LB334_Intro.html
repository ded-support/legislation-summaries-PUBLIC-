Summary

LB334 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32‑607 regarding candidate filing forms and filing officers. It confirms required form fields (name, addresses, phone, office sought, party for partisan offices, civil-penalty/surety-bond disclosure under the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act) and lists filing officers: the Secretary of State for statewide/multicounty offices (including directors of various public districts and judges desiring retention) and county election commissioners or county clerks for county, city, village, and certain school-district offices. The bill requires the filing officer to keep a judge‑desiring‑retention’s residence address, mailing address, and telephone number confidential. The original section is repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
