Summary

LB601 (DeBoer) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29‑2101 to clarify and enumerate the statutory grounds for granting a new trial after a conviction, explicitly listing: procedural irregularity or abuse of discretion; misconduct by the jury, prosecutor, or state witnesses; accident or unavoidable surprise; verdicts unsupported by sufficient evidence or contrary to law; newly discovered evidence; newly discovered exculpatory DNA or similar forensic testing evidence obtained under the DNA Testing Act; and errors of law. The bill was referred to the Judiciary Committee and repeals the original section. No executive agencies or departments are created or altered, and the bill contains no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations.
