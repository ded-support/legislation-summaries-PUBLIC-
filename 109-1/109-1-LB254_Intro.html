Summary

LB254 directs the Legislature’s intent to transfer state General Fund appropriations into the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund: $25 million for fiscal year 2025‑26 and $25 million for fiscal year 2026‑27. Funds are designated to provide workforce housing grants under the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Act. The bill includes an emergency clause making the act effective upon enactment. No specific executive agencies or departments are named.
