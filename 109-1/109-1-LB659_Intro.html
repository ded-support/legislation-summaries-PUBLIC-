Summary

LB659 amends Nebraska’s Election Act to impose procedural and reporting requirements on election commissioners and county clerks using vote‑counting devices. They must run at least three independent pre‑count tests (by the commissioner/clerk, the chief deputy or a registered voter of a different party, and the device installer/operator). Each political party may appoint one watcher via the county central committee; additional observers may be authorized. Commissioners/clerks must certify test completion to the Secretary of State (who will post the certification) and provide the Secretary with an anticipated test completion date. Revised centralized‑counting rules require sealed transport and storage of ballots and device media, procedures for overvoted/damaged and write‑in ballots, security measures, drop‑box handling, and a written election‑day plan submitted to the Secretary of State 25 days before the election. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires election commissioners or county clerks to run at least three independent pre-count tests of vote‑counting devices (by the commissioner/clerk; the chief deputy or a registered voter of a different party; and the device’s installer or operator). County central committees provide one watcher per political party; watchers/observers must comply with section 32‑1525 and may only be excluded if an unobstructed CCTV (or similar) view is provided. The official must certify the test completion date to the Secretary of State, who will post it on the Secretary of State’s website, and must provide the Secretary of State an anticipated completion date. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
