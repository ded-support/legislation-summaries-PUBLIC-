Summary

LB304 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to administer Nebraska’s social services program and to participate in the federal Child Care Subsidy/Child Care and Development Block Grant programs, extending child care assistance eligibility and transitional assistance rules (including a 10% earned-income disregard and provider accommodation provisions). DHHS must promulgate rules (subdivisions operative July 1, 2025), adopt an annual fixed-rate schedule for subsidy payments (effective Oct. 1 each year), and collaborate with a private nonprofit for an independent evaluation of prior income-eligibility changes if privately funded (evaluation due July 1, 2024). Funding: CCDBG funds shall cover costs from the eligibility changes; if insufficient, TANF funds may be used; no General Funds (except administration) shall be used. DHHS must also develop policy guidelines for additional cash assistance in extreme hardship cases.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language simply sets an implementation/start date of "Beginning 2025." It contains no Department of Health and Human Services–specific program provisions, nor does it change or appropriate any DHHS funding, budget allocations, or appropriations.
