Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) responsibility for administering the state's social services program, including authority to contract with other social agencies at prevailing rates. DHHS must participate in the federal Child Care Subsidy/Child Care and Development Block Grant program, enforce criminal-history checks for participating providers, allow specified transitional child care assistance, and promulgate rules (certain subdivisions operative July 1, 2025). DHHS must adopt and annually revise a fixed-rate schedule for child-care payments effective October 1 each year. Funding: federal CCDBG funds shall be used to cover costs from the 2021 income-eligibility changes; if insufficient, TANF funds may be used; no General Funds may be used except for administration. DHHS shall collaborate with a private nonprofit on an independent evaluation (if privately funded) and shall develop policy guidelines to permit cash-assistance extensions for extreme hardship.
