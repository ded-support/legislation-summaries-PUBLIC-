Summary

LB465 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§75-305 and 75-328 affecting fee collection and fund routing for transportation network companies (TNCs) and other regulated motor carriers. The commission will continue to set and collect annual fees; fees are due Jan 1 (delinquent Mar 1) and remitted to the State Treasurer within 30 days. TNCs may pay either an annual flat fee (not to exceed $25,000) or an annual per-vehicle fee (not to exceed $80 per personal vehicle). Fees collected under the TNC subsection are to be credited by the State Treasurer to the Transportation Network Company Regulation Cash Fund for enforcement. Fees from other regulated motor carriers are credited to the General Fund. The bill also requires personal vehicles used under the TNC provisions to comply with the Motor Vehicle Registration Act.
