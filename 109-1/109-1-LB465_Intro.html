Summary

LB465 amends statutes governing transportation network companies (TNCs). It directs the commission (which administers motor carrier regulation) to allow TNCs to choose either an annual flat fee up to $25,000 or a per‑vehicle fee up to $80, with the commission setting fee amounts so total collections do not exceed costs of administration and enforcement. Fees are due Jan 1 (delinquent Mar 1); initial permits issued on or after July 1 pay 50%. Collections are remitted to the State Treasurer within 30 days. The State Treasurer must credit TNC fees to the Transportation Network Company Regulation Cash Fund for enforcement; fees for other regulated motor carriers remain credited to the General Fund. The bill also requires personal vehicles used under the cited provisions to comply with the Motor Vehicle Registration Act and repeals the original sections.
