Summary

LB465 amends provisions governing fees and vehicle use for transportation network companies (TNCs). It authorizes the commission to set annual fees so collections do not exceed amounts necessary to sustain administration and enforcement. TNCs may elect either a flat annual fee (cap $25,000) or a per‑vehicle annual fee (cap $80 per personal vehicle). Fees are due January 1, delinquent March 1, with initial permits issued on/after July 1 charged 50% of the annual fee. The commission collects fees and must remit them to the State Treasurer within 30 days. TNC fee revenue is credited to the Transportation Network Company Regulation Cash Fund for enforcement; non‑TNC carrier fees are credited to the General Fund. Section 75-328 is amended to require personal vehicles used under sections 75-323 to 75-343 to comply with the Motor Vehicle Registration Act.
