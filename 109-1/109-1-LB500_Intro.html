Summary

Creates the 21‑member School Financing Review Commission. Statutory members include the Commissioner of Education (or designee), the Property Tax Administrator (or designee), a gubernatorial appointee, three nonvoting legislative members (including members of the Education and Revenue Committees), a postsecondary education representative, a member of the Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council, multiple school board members and administrators from Class III–V districts, a certificated teacher, and six at‑large members. The commission must deliver a final report to the Governor, the State Board of Education, and the Legislature by December 1, 2025; thereafter it will review state education mission goals, the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act implementation, and issue adequacy-of-funding reports biennially (every even‑numbered year July 1, 2028–2034). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Creates a 21‑member School Financing Review Commission including: the Commissioner of Education (or designee), a Governor appointee, three nonvoting legislative members (including members of the Education and Revenue Committees), the Property Tax Administrator (or designee), a postsecondary finance expert, an Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council member, specified school board members and administrators from Class III–V districts, a certificated teacher, six at‑large members, and others. Duties include reviewing State Department of Education goals and implementation of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act.
