Summary

Creates the School Financing Review Commission (21 members) with specified membership including the Commissioner of Education (or designee), a gubernatorial appointee, three nonvoting legislative members (including members of the Education and Revenue Committees), the Property Tax Administrator (or designee), a postsecondary expert in school finance, a member of the Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council, regional school board members and administrators, a certificated teacher, and six at-large members. The commission must report initially to the Governor, State Board of Education, and Legislature by December 1, 2025; review the State Department of Education’s mission/goals; evaluate implementation of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act; solicit school input; and file biennial adequacy-of-funding reports to the Governor, State Board, and Legislature on July 1 of even years 2028–2034. Emergency clause makes it effective on enactment. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a School Financing Review Commission (21 members) including: the Commissioner of Education (or designee); a Governor appointee; nonvoting legislative members; the Property Tax Administrator (or designee); a postsecondary rep with school‑finance expertise; an Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council member; multiple school board members and administrators from Class III–V districts; a certificated teacher; and six at‑large members. The commission must: deliver a final report (by Dec. 1, 2025); review/state goals set by the Legislature and the State Department of Education; review implementation of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act and prior recommendations; solicit input and develop best practices; and biennially (by July 1 of even years 2028–2034) report to the Governor, State Board of Education, and Legislature on adequacy of school funding sources. No specific appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included.
