Summary

Creates a 21-member School Financing Review Commission with members drawn from state agencies and bodies: Commissioner of Education (or designee), a Governor appointee, three nonvoting legislative members (including members of the Education and Revenue Committees), the Property Tax Administrator (or designee), a postsecondary education representative, an Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council member, multiple school board members and administrators representing Class III–V districts, a certificated teacher, and six at-large members. The commission must submit a final report to the Governor, State Board of Education, and Legislature by December 1, 2025; thereafter it will review state education missions/goals, solicit stakeholder input, assess implementation of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act, and file biennial adequacy reports by July 1 of even years 2028–2034. Bill takes effect on enactment. No budgetary appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a 21‑member School Financing Review Commission. Members include the Commissioner of Education (or designee), the Property Tax Administrator (or designee), one Governor appointee, three nonvoting legislators, a postsecondary school‑finance expert, an Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council member, multiple school board members and administrators from Class III–V districts, a certificated teacher, and six at‑large members (two per congressional district). The commission must deliver a final report to the Governor, the State Board of Education, and the Legislature by December 1, 2025, and then report biennially on school funding adequacy (by July 1 of even years 2028–2034). It will review the State Department of Education’s mission, recommend goals/best practices, and review implementation of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
