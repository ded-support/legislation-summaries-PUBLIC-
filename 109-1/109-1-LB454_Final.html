Summary

LB454 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§71‑809 and 71‑812, changing oversight and funding rules administered by the Department (and its division) and regional behavioral health authorities (RBHAs). It authorizes behavioral health providers, with department approval, to initiate new services or expand capacity if they meet department network enrollment standards, are enrolled with the division and an RBHA, and are contracted with an RBHA. It expands the Behavioral Health Services Fund’s housing-related assistance (fund administered by the division) to include very low‑income adults with substance use disorder in addition to serious mental illness. If housing obligations are met, the division may redirect up to 20% of such money to RBHAs for acquisition/rehabilitation of housing. The fund remains investable by the state investment officer and transferable to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds subsection (5) allowing a behavioral health services provider, with approval by "the department," to start new behavioral health services or expand existing capacity. Conditions: the provider must (a) meet the department's network enrollment standards, (b) be enrolled as a network provider with the department and a regional behavioral health authority, and (c) be contracted with a regional behavioral health authority. No agencies beyond "the department" and regional behavioral health authorities are referenced, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
