Summary

LB454 amends duties and funding rules for regional behavioral health authorities (RBHAs) and the Division of Behavioral Health (the division) within the Department. RBHAs must adopt and submit uniform financial-eligibility policies, fee/copay schedules, annual budgets and reports to the division; maintain separate budgets/accounts; comply with department rules on conflicts and procedures; and generally administer, plan, contract for, and audit publicly funded behavioral health services. RBHAs may provide services only after competitive bidding (with limited exceptions) or with director authorization. Providers may expand or add services with department approval if enrolled/contracted.

The bill redefines administration of the Behavioral Health Services Fund by the division, allows legislative-directed transfers to the General Fund, and specifies fund uses (grants, loans, provider reimbursement). Money from section 76-903 must be used for housing-related assistance for very low-income adults with serious mental illness or substance abuse disorder; if obligations are met, up to 20% may be distributed to RBHAs for housing acquisition/rehabilitation. The state investment officer may invest fund balances.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection authorizing behavioral health services providers, with approval by "the department," to create new behavioral health services or expand existing capacity. Eligibility requires: (a) meeting the department’s network enrollment standards, (b) being enrolled as a network provider with the department and a regional behavioral health authority, and (c) having a contract with a regional behavioral health authority. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
