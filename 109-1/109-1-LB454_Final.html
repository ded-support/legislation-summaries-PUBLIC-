Summary

LB454 amends duties and funding provisions for the Department (and its Division of Behavioral Health) and regional behavioral health authorities (RBHAs). RBHAs retain planning, budgeting, contracting, reporting, audit coordination, and uniform fee-policy duties; they may only provide division-funded services after competitive bidding (or if no qualified providers) with director authorization and a division contract. The bill permits a behavioral health services provider, with department approval, to add new services or expand capacity if it meets department network enrollment standards, is enrolled with the department and RBHA, and is contracted with the RBHA.

The Behavioral Health Services Fund remains administered by the division, containing legislative appropriations and other receipts (transfer to the General Fund permitted by Legislature). Money from section 76-903 is designated for housing-related assistance for very low-income adults with serious mental illness or substance abuse disorder; if obligations are met, up to 20% may be allocated to RBHAs for housing acquisition/rehabilitation. The division will allocate funds by formula, contract with RBHAs, and investments are handled by the state investment officer.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a behavioral health services provider may, with approval by "the department," begin new behavioral health services or expand existing capacity only if the provider: (a) meets the department's network enrollment standards, (b) is enrolled as a network provider with both the department and a regional behavioral health authority, and (c) holds a contract with a regional behavioral health authority. Agencies mentioned: the department and regional behavioral health authorities. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
