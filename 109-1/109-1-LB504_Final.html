Summary

This bill creates the Age-Appropriate Online Design Code Act. State enforcement: the Nebraska Attorney General may pursue civil penalties (up to $50,000 per violation) beginning July 1, 2026; collected penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. Federal and state references: the bill relies on Federal Trade Commission guidance to define “dark patterns,” uses Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI data to adjust revenue thresholds biennially, and exempts data subject to federal regimes (GLBA Title V, HITECH, HIPAA section 264 regulations) and certain clinical-trial protections (including FDA requirements). Government entities (federal, state, tribal, local) are excluded from the Act. The Act is operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds references to federal and state agencies: Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics (CPI used to adjust the $25 million revenue threshold); Federal Trade Commission (FTC) — its determinations of "dark patterns" as of Jan 1, 2024 apply and COPPA/FTC rules are incorporated; federal statutes/regulators (GLBA Title V, HITECH, HIPAA regulations) exempt certain data; Food and Drug Administration and International Council for Harmonisation standards govern clinical trial data exemptions. State enforcement: Nebraska Attorney General may bring actions (but not seek civil penalties under the act until July 1, 2026). Civil penalties up to $50,000 per violation are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Nebraska's Constitution.
