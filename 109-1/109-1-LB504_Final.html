Summary

LB504 (Age-Appropriate Online Design Code Act), introduced at the Governor’s request, references several government entities and enforcement mechanisms. It adopts Federal Trade Commission determinations of "dark patterns" (as of Jan 1, 2024) and uses the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics to adjust the $25 million revenue threshold. Data subject to Title V of the Gramm‑Leach‑Bliley Act, the HITECH Act, or HIPAA regulations (section 264) and clinical-trial data meeting FDA human-subject protections are exempt. Federal, state, tribal, and local government entities are excluded. The Nebraska Attorney General enforces the act under deceptive trade practice law (may not seek civil penalties until July 1, 2026); penalties up to $50,000 per violation are remitted to the State Treasurer. Operative date: January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds references to federal agencies and programs throughout the Age-Appropriate Online Design Code Act: revenue thresholds are indexed every odd-numbered year to the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics; "dark patterns" include practices identified by the Federal Trade Commission as of Jan. 1, 2024; and "parent" is defined by the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and FTC rules. The act exempts federal, state, tribal, or local government entities and data subject to federal statutes/regulations (GLBA Title V, HITECH, HIPAA section 264) and clinical trials meeting ICH or FDA human-subject protections. Civil penalties (up to $50,000 per violation beginning July 1, 2026) are payable to the State Treasurer and distributed per Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution; the Attorney General may not seek such penalties before July 1, 2026.
