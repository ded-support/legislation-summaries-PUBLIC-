Summary

LB482 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §44‑359 to clarify attorney‑fee rules in private insurance litigation. Trial courts must award a reasonable attorney's fee to a plaintiff who prevails on an insurance policy or fraternal benefit certificate claim (excluding workers' compensation), taxed as costs; appellate courts may award fees for appellate proceedings. A plaintiff cannot recover fees if the judgment does not exceed a prior offer under §25‑901. The statute expressly excludes claims based on post‑loss assignments by property owners for insured property damage. The change primarily affects Nebraska trial and appellate courts (judiciary). No state agencies are allocated funding, and the bill contains no appropriations or budgetary changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an exception to subsection (1): subsection (1) will not apply when a plaintiff's right to sue is based on a post‑loss assignment of rights or benefits stemming from a property owner's claim for damage to insured property. No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
