Summary

LB482 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §44-359 to clarify attorney-fee awards in insurance litigation. Under the bill, Nebraska trial courts must allow a reasonable attorney’s fee to a prevailing plaintiff in actions on insurance policies (excluding workers’ compensation), to be taxed as costs; appellate courts must likewise allow fees for appellate proceedings. The plaintiff cannot recover fees if the judgment does not exceed an opposing offer under §25-901. The statute expressly excludes actions based on post-loss assignments of property-damage claims by property owners. The original section is repealed. No state agencies beyond the courts are referenced, and there are no changes to appropriations or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an exception clarifying that subsection (1) does not apply when a plaintiff’s right to sue is based on a post‑loss assignment of rights or benefits arising from a property owner’s claim for damage to insured property. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
