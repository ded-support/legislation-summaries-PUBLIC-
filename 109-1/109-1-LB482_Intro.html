Summary

LB482 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §44-359 (insurance). It preserves the trial and appellate courts’ authority to award a reasonable attorney fee to a plaintiff who obtains judgment on most insurance-policy or fraternal benefit society claims (fees taxed as costs), but clarifies language regarding the fee being awarded in addition to the plaintiff’s recovery. It adds an express exclusion: the fee rule does not apply when the plaintiff’s claim arises from a post-loss assignment of rights or benefits in a property-damage claim by a property owner. The bill contains no changes to state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
