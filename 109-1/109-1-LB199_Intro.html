Summary

LB199 makes several administrative changes affecting state actors:

- Secretary of State: retains authority to issue, renew, deny, suspend, or revoke certificates of registration for civil litigation funding companies; may discipline registrants for specified violations including knowing failure to disclose funding contracts. Registrants must annually submit prescribed data (number/amount of fundings, repayment metrics, fees charged, shortfalls). Secretary must prepare and electronically submit an aggregate annual report to the Clerk of the Legislature and the Legislature’s Judiciary Committee. Administrative Procedure Act procedures apply to enforcement actions; temporary certificates may be issued while applications are pending.

- Courts, agencies, and tribunals: recipients of mandatory disclosure of nonrecourse civil litigation funding contracts.

- Revisor of Statutes: to assign the new statute of limitations section to Chapter 25, article 2.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are addressed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a disclosure duty for nonrecourse civil litigation funding: consumers or their attorneys must promptly provide copies of such contracts to each opposing party or their attorney, "any court, agency, or tribunal" where the claim is pending, and any known indemnitor/insurer. The duty applies even before a lawsuit or administrative proceeding is filed, is ongoing, and requires disclosure of new or amended contracts within 30 days. The existence of the contract and all participants are discoverable. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
