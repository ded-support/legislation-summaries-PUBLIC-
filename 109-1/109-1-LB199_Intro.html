Summary

LB199 amends Nebraska law governing nonrecourse civil litigation funding and personal-injury statutes. Key agency actions and duties:

- Secretary of State: issues, renews, temporarily issues, may refuse, suspend, revoke, or refuse renewal of registration for civil litigation funding companies based on character/fitness, statutory violations, or knowing failure to comply with new disclosure rules; Administrative Procedure Act procedures and hearings apply. Registered companies must annually submit prescribed data (volume, amounts, repayment occurrences, fees, shortfalls).

- Reporting: Secretary of State must annually submit an electronic aggregate report to the Clerk of the Legislature and the Judiciary Committee on in-state funding activity.

- Revisor of Statutes: assigned Section 1 to Chapter 25, article 2.

- Courts/agencies/tribunals and insurers are explicit recipients of mandated disclosures of funding contracts; administrative proceedings are included under the Act.

No appropriations or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a mandatory disclosure regime for nonrecourse civil litigation funding. Consumers or their attorneys must, without awaiting discovery, deliver copies of such funding contracts to: (a) other parties/their attorneys; (b) any court, agency, or tribunal where the claim is pending (expressly covering administrative proceedings); and (c) any known person — including insurers with preexisting indemnity/defense obligations (the insurer disclosure duty applies even before a case or administrative proceeding is filed). The duty is continuing: new or amended contracts must be disclosed within 30 days. Litigation funding contracts and all participants are expressly discoverable. No changes to government funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
