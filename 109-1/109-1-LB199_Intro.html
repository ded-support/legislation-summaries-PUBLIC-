Summary

LB199 amends Nebraska’s Nonrecourse Civil Litigation Act and related procedure. Key agency actions:

- Secretary of State: issues, renews, may refuse/suspend/revoke registration for civil litigation funding companies (including temporary certificates); may act for concerns about character/fitness/financial responsibility or violations (including new disclosure duty). Actions require notice and hearing; the Administrative Procedure Act applies.

- Regulatory reporting: registered funders must annually submit prescribed data to the Secretary of State (number/amount of fundings; repayment counts; fees including annual percentage and itemized fees; cases where realization was less than contracted).

- The Secretary of State must prepare and electronically submit an annual aggregate report to the Clerk of the Legislature and the Judiciary Committee.

- Revisor of Statutes: assign the new personal-injury statute section to Chapter 25, article 2.

No appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds mandatory disclosure rules for nonrecourse civil litigation funding contracts: consumers or their attorneys must, without awaiting discovery, provide copies to each party/their attorney, "any court, agency, or tribunal" where the claim is pending, and any known person (including insurers) with indemnity/defense obligations. The duty applies even before litigation or an administrative proceeding is filed, is ongoing, and requires disclosure of new or amended contracts within 30 days. Such funding arrangements and all participants are discoverable in related claims. No specific government agencies or departments are named beyond the generic "court, agency, or tribunal," and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
