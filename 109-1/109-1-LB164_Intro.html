Summary

The bill designates "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it program administration duties: receive and review grant applications (in order received) for development or rehabilitation of affordable commercial space in economically distressed areas; award multiple targeted grant types with specified per-category annual caps (up to $20M; $10M; $5M; $2.5M; $2M; $4M; $3M) and a maximum of $3.5M per project; and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act. The Department must electronically submit an annual report to the Governor and Legislature detailing total grants awarded, project types, and jobs created. The Legislature intends to appropriate $46,500,000 annually from the General Fund to the Department to carry out the Urban Development Incentive Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions designate the Department of Economic Development as administrator of the Urban Development Incentive Act, requiring developers to apply on department forms and the department to review applications in order received and adopt rules. The department may award multiple grant types for developing/rehabilitating affordable commercial space in economically distressed areas, with per-category annual caps: $20M (up to 20% of project costs); $10M (up to 10% to secure private financing); $5M (up to 15% of down payment, max $200K each); $2.5M (up to $50K for job training); $2M (up to $25K for public consultation, +$10K bonus); $4M (sustainability grants: up to $50K renewable, $75K green certification, $30K wellness); $3M (technical assistance: up to $20K consultants, $5K workshops, $15K studies). Max per project is $3.5M. The department must file an annual report to the Governor and Legislature. The Legislature intends to appropriate $46.5 million annually from the General Fund to the department.
