Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it primary administration of the Urban Development Incentive Act. The department must prescribe application forms, review grant applications in receipt order, and may award grants (with per-category annual caps: $20M, $10M, $5M, $2.5M, $2M, $4M, and $3M) and a per-project maximum of $3.5M. It must electronically submit an annual report to the Governor and Legislature including total grants awarded, project types/counts, and jobs created. The department is authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act. The Legislature intends to appropriate $46,500,000 annually from the General Fund to the department to carry out the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text designates the "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and charges it with administering the Urban Development Incentive Act. The department will accept prescribed applications, review them in order received, adopt rules, and must file an annual electronic report to the Governor and Legislature (grant totals/amounts, project types/counts, and jobs created). It may award multiple targeted grants for affordable commercial development in economically distressed areas with per-award limits and annual category caps (examples: up to 20% of project costs, annual cap $20M; up to 10% for financing assistance, cap $10M; down-payment, job training, community engagement, sustainability, and technical-assistance grants with specified per-award and annual caps). Single-project maximum is $3.5M. The Legislature intends to appropriate $46.5 million annually from the General Fund to the department to implement the act.
