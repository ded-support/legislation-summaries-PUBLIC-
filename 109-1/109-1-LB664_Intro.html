Summary

LB664 amends Nebraska’s Administrative Procedure Act to change how state agencies handle rulemaking and legal challenges. Agencies must solicit public comment before notice publication and must allow any person to submit supportive or opposing comments or materials electronically or by mail without requiring in-person travel. In rule challenges, the petition for declaratory judgment must be filed in district court (county of petitioner’s residence, principal business, Lancaster County, or the county of the agency’s headquarters), and the agency is required to be a party to the proceeding. Courts may invalidate rules that are unconstitutional, exceed an agency’s statutory authority, or were adopted without required statutory procedures (excluding the Negotiated Rulemaking Act). Agencies are prohibited from restricting venue. The bill takes effect immediately; no funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural protections for "agency" rulemaking: agencies must allow any person to submit comments for or against proposed rules, accept submissions electronically or by mail, and may not require in-person travel. Judicial petitions challenging agency action may be filed in district court in the petitioner’s county of residence, the petitioner’s principal place of business, Lancaster County, or the county where the agency has its headquarters. Agencies are prohibited from restricting venue contrary to this section. No agencies are newly created and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
