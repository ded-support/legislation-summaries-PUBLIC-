Summary

LB664 amends the Administrative Procedure Act (Reissue Revised Statutes §§84-906.02, 84-911). Agencies are required to permit any person to submit comments, written materials, or documentation for proposed or adopted rules or regulations, allow submissions electronically or by mail, and may not require in-person travel; agencies are also encouraged to solicit public comment via newspaper notice. The bill clarifies venue for declaratory-judgment challenges to rules: petitions may be filed in the district court of the county of the petitioner’s residence, the petitioner’s principal place of business, Lancaster County, or the county where the agency has its headquarters; the agency must be a party and may not restrict venue. Statutory procedures exclude Negotiated Rulemaking Act procedures. Original sections are repealed; the act takes effect immediately. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for state agencies and the district courts in administrative rule challenges: agencies must allow anyone to submit comments or supporting/opposing materials electronically or by mail and may not require in-person travel to submit them. Challenges to agency action must be filed in the district court in a county with proper venue — specifically the petitioner’s residence county, the petitioner’s principal place of business, Lancaster County, or the county where the agency has its headquarters. Agencies are prohibited from taking actions that would restrict venue inconsistent with this section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are affected.
