Summary

LB664 amends Nebraska’s Administrative Procedure Act. It requires state agencies considering rules to solicit public comment (including by newspaper notice), permit any person to submit supportive or opposing comments or materials by mail or electronically, and not force in‑person submissions. The bill revises judicial review of agency rules: declaratory‑judgment petitions may be filed in district court in the petitioner’s residence county, the petitioner’s principal business county, Lancaster County, or the county of the agency’s headquarters; the agency must be made a party. Courts may invalidate rules that violate the constitution, exceed statutory authority, or were adopted without required statutory procedures (excluding Negotiated Rulemaking Act procedures). Agencies are barred from restricting venue. Original sections repealed; emergency effective date. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires any Nebraska state agency, when proposing or adopting rules, to accept public comments or materials in support or opposition electronically or by mail and not to require in‑person travel to submit them. Establishes that petitions challenging agency action must be filed in district court in a county with proper venue: the petitioner’s county of residence, the county of the petitioner’s principal place of business, Lancaster County, or the county where the agency has its headquarters. Prohibits agencies from imposing restrictions that would contravene these venue rules. No specific departments are named beyond generic "agency" and Lancaster County; there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
