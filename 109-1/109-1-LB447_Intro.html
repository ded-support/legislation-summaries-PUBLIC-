Summary

LB447 directs the Department of Economic Development to update annually the workforce-housing construction cost caps (owner-occupied $275,000; rental $200,000 per unit) based on the most recent change in the Producer Price Index for All Commodities published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. The bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for the Department of Economic Development or any Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that redevelopers and their contractors/subcontractors use and comply with registered apprenticeship programs for construction work on redevelopment contracts, with an exception if the apprenticeship program does not respond within 15 business days. Defines: "labor hours" (excludes foremen, superintendents, owners, and bona fide executive/administrative/professional staff per 29 C.F.R. §541); "qualified apprentice" (represented by a 501(c)(5) organization and employed in a program whose training facility is in-state); and adopts the federal definition of "registered apprenticeship program" (26 U.S.C. 3131(e)(3)(B)). No explicit references to the Department of Economic Development or the Department of Labor, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
