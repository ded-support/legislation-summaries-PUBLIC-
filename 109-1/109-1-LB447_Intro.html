Summary

LB447 amends the Community Development Law to (1) require the Department of Economic Development to annually update workforce-housing construction cost limits based on changes in the Producer Price Index for all commodities published by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics, and (2) add wage and registered-apprenticeship requirements for certain redevelopment contracts that reference federal registered apprenticeship definitions. The bill cites the U.S. Department of Labor (BLS) data for the index and uses the federal definition of registered apprenticeship. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Economic Development or any Department of Labor entity are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds contractor/developer obligations to employ laborers, mechanics and apprentices under redevelopment contracts and to comply with registered apprenticeship program standards (or if a program fails to respond within 15 business days). Defines "labor hours" (excludes foremen, superintendents, owners, bona fide exec/admin/professional per 29 C.F.R. part 541), "qualified apprentice" (represented by a §501(c)(5) organization and trained in‑state), and ties "registered apprenticeship program" to the federal definition at 26 U.S.C. 3131(e)(3)(B). No explicit references to the Department of Economic Development or the Department of Labor, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. (The federal apprenticeship definition/CFR citations suggest potential Dept. of Labor relevance for oversight, but no agency duties or funding are specified.)
