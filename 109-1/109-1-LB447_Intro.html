Summary

LB447 amends the Community Development Law to define "workforce housing" and requires the Department of Economic Development to annually update the construction cost caps for owner-occupied and rental units based on the most recent Producer Price Index for All Commodities published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language requires redevelopers (and their contractors/subcontractors) on specified redevelopment construction projects to employ laborers, mechanics, and apprentices through registered apprenticeship programs and to comply with those programs’ standards — or if a registered apprenticeship program does not respond to a request within 15 business days. It defines “labor hours” (excluding foremen, superintendents, owners, and bona fide executive/administrative/professional employees per 29 C.F.R. part 541); “qualified apprentice” (represented by a 501(c)(5) organization and employed in a program whose training facility is in this state); and ties “registered apprenticeship program” to the federal definition in 26 U.S.C. 3131(e)(3)(B). No changes to Department of Economic Development or Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
