Summary

LB466 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43-245 (Nebraska Juvenile Code) to revise definitional terms. Key agency references and changes:

- Office of Dispute Resolution: "Approved center" must be approved by the Director of the Office of Dispute Resolution.

- Department of Correctional Services: "Staff secure juvenile facility" expressly excludes institutions operated by the Department of Correctional Services.

- Referral/approval authority: Restorative justice facilitators must be approved by the referring county attorney, city attorney, or juvenile or county court judge.

- Other cross-references remain to juvenile, county, and district courts and to mental health treatment facilities (per §71-914).

The bill also clarifies that "alternative to detention" does not include placements using physical construction or hardware to restrain movement (except manually controlled delayed egress ≤30 seconds) and redefines "young adult" as older than 18 but younger than 21. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
