Summary

LB354 amends section 77-27,139.03 to (1) require the Auditor of Public Accounts to provide the Department of Revenue, by February 1 each year, a list of bond and nonbond tax request amounts from the most recent municipal budgets for use in calculating bond and nonbond tax levies for state aid purposes; (2) bar cities of the first class from receiving state aid from the Municipal Equalization Fund; and (3) retain funding rules that prorate Municipal Equalization Fund distributions if insufficient and credit any excess fund balance to the General Fund. The bill repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added phrase simply extends or clarifies the provision to apply when the municipality "is a city of the first class." There is no language affecting the Department of Revenue, nor any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department. No fiscal impact on DOR is indicated.
