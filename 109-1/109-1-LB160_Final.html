Summary

LB160 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-216.01 (barber licensure) to revise examination and training requirements administered by the board that oversees barber examinations. Applicants may retake the exam up to two additional times without further coursework; after failing a third time, an applicant must complete a 500-hour barbering course (max ten hours/day) before further examination. After 250 hours, the board may allow an applicant to demonstrate proficiency to waive the remaining hours; if the applicant then fails the exam, they must complete the remaining 250 hours before a fifth exam attempt. The bill repeals the original section. Committee of reference: Health and Human Services. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that the licensing board may allow an applicant who fails the barber exam to retake it up to two more times (total three attempts) without additional coursework. If the applicant fails a third time, they must complete a 500‑hour barbering course (max 10 hours/day) before further examination. After completing 250 hours, an applicant may petition the board to demonstrate proficiency to waive the remaining hours; if they fail the exam after that opportunity, they must finish the remaining 250 hours before being allowed a fifth exam attempt. No state agencies beyond the licensing "board" are named and no funding or appropriations changes are included.
