Summary

LB160 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-216.01 governing barber licensure examination procedures administered by the state licensing board (the "board"). Under the bill, an applicant who fails the barber exam may retake it up to two additional times without further schooling; a third failure triggers a required additional 500‑hour course of study in a barbering school (maximum ten hours per working day). After completing 250 hours, an applicant may seek board approval to demonstrate proficiency and waive the remaining hours; if the applicant then fails, they must finish the remaining 250 hours before a fifth exam attempt. The bill was handled by the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee. No funding, appropriation, or other budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for applicants to the barbering licensing board: applicants may retake the board exam two additional times without further coursework; after a third failure they must complete 500 additional barbering school hours (maximum 10 hours per day) before reexam. After 250 hours, the applicant may request board-approved proficiency testing to waive the remaining 250 hours; if they fail the exam after that waiver opportunity, they must finish the remaining 250 hours before a fifth exam attempt.
