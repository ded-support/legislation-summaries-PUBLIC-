Summary

LB438 requires authorized gaming operators, beginning on an implementation date set by the Tax Commissioner, to check a collection system for outstanding state tax liabilities before paying sports-wagering winnings; operators must deduct any identified debts and remit the deducted amounts to the Department of Revenue to be credited against those liabilities under section 9-1306. The bill also amends tax distributions: gross gaming revenue generated by sports wagering is remitted to the State Treasurer as follows — 2.5% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 5% to the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund, 27.5% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund, 20% to the General Fund, and 45% to the Education Future Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language authorizes and defines online sports wagering and platform providers, requires operators/vendors to obtain commission approval of platform controls, and limits operations to persons located in Nebraska and to vendors contracted with an authorized gaming operator. For the Department of Revenue: it must collect and remit the tax under section 9-1203 on gross gaming revenue from sports wagering to the State Treasurer for credit as follows — 2.5% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund; 5% to the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund; 27.5% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund; 20% to the General Fund; and 45% to the Education Future Fund. No separate appropriations or direct budget allocations to the Department of Revenue are specified.
