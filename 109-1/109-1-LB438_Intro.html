Summary

LB438 requires authorized gaming operators, prior to paying sports-wagering winnings, to check the state collection system for debts or outstanding state tax liabilities under the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act. If a winner is flagged, the operator must deduct the identified amount and remit the deduction to the Department of Revenue to be credited against the debt or tax liability pursuant to section 9-1306.

The bill also revises tax distribution for racetrack gaming: non-sports gross gaming revenue—75% to the State Treasurer (2.5% Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund; 2.5% General Fund; 70% Property Tax Credit Cash Fund) and 25% to local governments. Sports-wagering gross revenue—remitted to State Treasurer as: 2.5% Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund; 5% Racing and Gaming Commission Racetrack Gaming Fund; 27.5% Property Tax Credit Cash Fund; 20% General Fund; 45% Education Future Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill requires the Department of Revenue to collect and remit the tax imposed by section 9-1203 on gross gaming revenue from sports wagering to the State Treasurer. Those receipts are to be credited as follows: 2.5% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 5% to the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund, 27.5% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund, 20% to the General Fund, and 45% to the Education Future Fund. No direct appropriations to the Department of Revenue are added; the change specifies distribution of sports-wagering tax revenue among state funds.
