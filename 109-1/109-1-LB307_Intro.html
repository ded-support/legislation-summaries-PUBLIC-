Summary

LB307 directs two-year General Fund appropriations (amounts shown as placeholders) for FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 to fund tuition waivers. It allocates appropriations to:

- the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges (Program 48), and

- the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska (Program 781).

Each appropriation includes designated General Funds to be used only for tuition waivers provided pursuant to the First Responders Recruitment and Retention Act, the In the Line of Duty Dependent Education Act, and Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 80‑411 and 80‑901. The bill contains an emergency clause making it effective upon passage.
