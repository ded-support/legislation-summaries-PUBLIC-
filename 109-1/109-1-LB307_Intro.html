Summary

Bill LB307 appropriates General Fund monies for tuition waivers to two state higher-education governing bodies: the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges (Program 48) and the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska (Program 781). For each body the bill authorizes unspecified FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 General Fund appropriations ($XXX) and explicitly earmarks portions of those appropriations for tuition waivers under the First Responders Recruitment and Retention Act, the In the Line of Duty Dependent Education Act, and Neb. Rev. Stat. §§80-411 and 80-901. Funds are restricted to that purpose. The act contains an emergency clause making it effective upon passage.
