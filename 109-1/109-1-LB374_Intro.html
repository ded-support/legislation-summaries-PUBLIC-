Summary

LB374 clarifies that, for the criminal background‑check provision in section 38‑131, the term "department" means the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. Applicants' FBI national criminal history record information checks and fingerprinting reports are to be released to that Division. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services appear in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Health and Human Services focus):

The added Licensed Midwives Practice Act requires the Department of Health and Human Services to license professional midwives who meet specified qualifications (CPM credential, approved education or bridge pathway, high school diploma) and to recognize equivalent out‑of‑state licensure as determined by the board. The department will administer licensure and collect initial and renewal fees as prescribed in sections 38‑151 to 38‑157. Regulatory details and practice standards are to be adopted by the Board of Licensed Midwives under section 38‑126. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the department are included in the added text.
