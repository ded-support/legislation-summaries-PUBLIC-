Summary

The bill expressly defines, for FBI interpretation related to criminal background checks, that the term "department" in section 38-131 means the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. This is in the context of fingerprinting and national criminal history record checks for initial licensure applicants. No appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes for the Department of Health and Human Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Licensed Midwives Practice Act administered by the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) and a five‑member Board of Licensed Midwives. The department is responsible for licensing and determining applicant qualifications (including CPM credentialing and education standards), issuing/renewing licenses, and enforcing the Act per board rules. The board will adopt practice standards, testing and medication authorizations (e.g., oxytocin), consent form and newborn screening requirements. The department shall establish and collect initial licensure and renewal fees as provided in sections 38‑151 to 38‑157. Licensed midwives may practice without supervision or collaborative agreements. No new appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes are included in the added text.
