Summary

The bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services, in consultation with the State Department of Education, to develop model anaphylaxis policies for use by school districts and licensed child care programs. DHHS must solicit input from relevant medical professionals, parents, school staff, and others; include emergency response protocols, individualized health-care plans, communication strategies, and exposure-reduction measures; review and update the models at least every three years; and post them on the applicable department's website. The model policies may be used as guides for required local policies. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) must, in consultation with the State Department of Education, develop and publish on its website model anaphylaxis policies for school districts and licensed child care programs. Policies are to be created with input from medical and educational stakeholders and must include emergency treatment protocols, individualized health care plan guidance, communication strategies, exposure-reduction strategies, and child/parent communication guidance. DHHS must reexamine and update the models at least every three years. The bill imposes no new appropriations, funding, or budget allocations to DHHS in the added text.
