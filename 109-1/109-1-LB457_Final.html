Summary

The bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services, in consultation with the State Department of Education, to develop model anaphylaxis policies for school districts and licensed child care programs. DHHS may solicit input from allergy specialists, pediatricians, school nurses, parents, administrators, food service directors and others. Required policy elements include emergency response procedures, individualized health care plan guidelines, communication plans, exposure-reduction strategies, and age‑appropriate communication with children and families. DHHS must review and update the models at least every three years and make them available on the applicable department’s website. Licensed child care programs and school districts may use the DHHS model as a guide when adopting local policies. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for DHHS are specified in the bill.
