Summary

LB638 assigns administration of the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Act to the Nebraska Department of Natural Resources (DNR), authorizing collaboration with Natural Resources Districts. It (re)creates the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund under DNR control, permits transfers/gifts/grants into the fund, and requires DNR to apply (before the FY2025‑26 deadline) for a $25 million Nebraska Environmental Trust grant payable in five $5 million annual installments. If awarded, the State Treasurer will credit proceeds to the cash fund and DNR must maintain a subaccount for the Trust grant; monthly interest earnings to that subaccount trigger an equal transfer from the cash fund back to the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund. Awarded grants are credited to the cash fund. The state investment officer will invest fund balances under existing investment statutes. The Act now terminates December 31, 2030, and takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions require the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) to apply, before the FY2025‑26 application deadline, for a $25 million grant from the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund (NETF), payable in five $5 million annual installments. If awarded, the State Treasurer will credit the grant proceeds to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund to support the project purposes consistent with that fund. DNR must create a subaccount within the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund for the NETF grant receipts, calculate monthly interest earned on that subaccount, and notify the State Treasurer, who will transfer an equal amount from the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund back to the NETF.
