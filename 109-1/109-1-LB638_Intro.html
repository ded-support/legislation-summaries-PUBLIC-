Summary

This bill amends the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Act and tasks the Nebraska Department of Natural Resources (DNR) with administering the program, with authority to collaborate with natural resources districts. DNR must (1) determine per‑acre incentive rates (not less than $10/acre) and review targets biennially, and (2) apply for a $25 million grant from the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund payable as five $5 million annual installments; if awarded, proceeds are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund. DNR must establish a subaccount for Trust funds, report monthly interest to the State Treasurer (who transfers a like amount back to the Trust), may apply for other grants (credited to the cash fund), and funds are invested by the state investment officer. The Act now terminates December 31, 2030, and takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a requirement that the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) apply, before the FY2025‑26 application deadline, for a $25 million grant from the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund, payable in five $5 million annual installments. If awarded, the grant proceeds are to be remitted to the State Treasurer and credited to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund to support projects consistent with that fund's purposes. DNR must establish a subaccount in the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund to track grant receipts. Monthly, DNR will calculate interest earned on that subaccount and notify the State Treasurer, who will transfer an equal amount from the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund back to the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund. No other appropriations are specified.
