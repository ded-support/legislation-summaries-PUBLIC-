Summary

Department of Natural Resources (DNR) administration: LB638 designates the DNR as administrator of the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive program and allows collaboration with local natural resources districts.

Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund: The bill continues DNR administration of the Cash Fund, authorizes legislative transfers and gifts/grants as fund sources, and requires the DNR to apply (prior to the FY2025-26 application deadline) for a $25 million Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund grant payable in five $5 million annual installments. If awarded, proceeds are to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Cash Fund. DNR must establish a subaccount for those grant proceeds, calculate monthly interest earnings, and notify the State Treasurer to transfer a like amount from the Cash Fund to the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund. DNR may pursue other applicable grants; awarded grants are credited to the Cash Fund. Fund investments are handled by the state investment officer under existing statutes.

Other provisions: The Act’s termination date is set to December 31, 2030, and it takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Natural Resources must apply (by the FY2025-26 deadline) for a $25 million grant from the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund, payable in five annual $5 million installments, with grant purposes aligned to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund. If awarded, the State Treasurer will credit the grant proceeds to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund to support the grant projects. DNR must create a subaccount within that cash fund to track the Trust grant dollars. At month-end DNR will compute interest earned on the subaccount and notify the State Treasurer, who will transfer an equal amount from the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund back to the Nebraska Environmental Trust Fund.
