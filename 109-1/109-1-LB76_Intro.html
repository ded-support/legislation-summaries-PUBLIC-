Summary

LB76 references the United States Department of Labor twice. It requires the retirement board to use the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI‑W) — using June 30 factors — to calculate purchasing‑power and cost‑of‑living adjustments for Nebraska State Patrol retirees. If that CPI is discontinued or replaced, the board must select a reasonable substitute index published by the U.S. Department of Labor. The bill contains no explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.
