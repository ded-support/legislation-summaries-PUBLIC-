Summary

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns it primary administrative responsibilities under the Opportunity Scholarships Act.

- Department certifies and may revoke certification of scholarship-granting organizations; organizations must certify with the department before awarding scholarships.

- Taxpayers and scholarship organizations must notify the department of intended tax-credit amounts; the department must determine within 30 days if an intended credit exceeds available limits and notify the organization (and indirectly taxpayers) of available amounts.

- The department processes notifications in receipt order and enforces annual tax-credit caps: $25 million for calendar years 2025–2027, with potential year-to-year increases (up to a $100 million cap) tied to prior-year demand.

- No direct appropriations or other funding or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions assign the Department of Revenue (department) primary administration of the new Opportunity Scholarships tax-credit program. The department must:

- Certify and (if necessary) revoke scholarship-granting organizations based on specified 501(c)(3), program, priority, and scholarship-size requirements.

- Receive notifications from organizations of taxpayers' intended credit amounts, determine availability within 30 days, and notify organizations/taxpayers.

- Process notifications in receipt order and track contributions (taxpayers must remit within 31–60 days or the department excludes the amount).

- Enforce an annual statewide tax-credit cap: $25 million for 2025–2027; thereafter adjusted yearly (up to 125% growth if prior demand exceeded 90% of the cap) until a $100 million ceiling.

No explicit new appropriations or direct Department of Revenue budget allocations are specified in the added text.
