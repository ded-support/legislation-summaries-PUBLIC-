Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue.

- The Department of Revenue is authorized to certify and revoke certification of scholarship-granting organizations under the Opportunity Scholarships Act and to receive required notifications from those organizations.

- Scholarship-granting organizations must notify the Department of intended tax-credit amounts; the Department must determine within 30 days whether the intended amount exceeds the annual credit limit and inform the organization (and thus the donor) of availability. If a contribution is not received in the required window, the Department will remove that amount from its calculations.

- The Department will process notifications in receipt order against annual tax-credit limits (initially $25 million for 2025–2027, adjustable thereafter up to $100 million under specified rules).

- The Department of Revenue may recapture a specified housing purchase tax credit if eligibility conditions are not met.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Opportunity Scholarships Act and designates the "Department" as the Department of Revenue. The Department of Revenue must certify and may revoke certification of scholarship‑granting organizations, process organizations' notifications of intended donor tax credits, notify organizations within 30 days if intended credits would exceed availability, and track/remove notified amounts when contributions are not received. The Act establishes nonrefundable Nebraska income tax credits (individuals, pass‑throughs, corporations, estates/trusts) with per‑taxpayer caps (generally the lesser of contribution, 50% of tax liability, or $100,000; estates/trusts $1,000,000) and five‑year carryforward. It sets an aggregate annual credit cap: $25 million (2025–27) with formulaic growth to a $100 million max. No explicit appropriations or new Department budget allocations are provided.
