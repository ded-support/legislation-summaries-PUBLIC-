Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns it key administrative duties for the Opportunity Scholarships Act. The Department must certify (and may revoke certification of) scholarship‑granting organizations that meet statutory criteria. Scholarship organizations must notify the Department of each taxpayer's intended tax‑credit amount; the Department has 30 days to determine availability and must process notifications in receipt order. The Department also tracks whether contributions are received within required timeframes and excludes untimely amounts when enforcing the program's annual tax‑credit cap. The annual cap is $25 million for 2025–2027 and thereafter adjusts annually (up to $100 million) per the bill. No direct appropriations or budget allocations are specified. The act is operative for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2025.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue duties and fiscal effects:

- The department is defined as the Department of Revenue and must certify and may revoke certification of scholarship-granting organizations (SGOs) meeting 501(c)(3) and program requirements (priority rules, scholarship limits, average award cap of 75% of statewide per‑student spending).

- Administrative role: SGOs notify the department of taxpayers' intended credit amounts; the department must determine within 30 days if amounts exceed the annual cap and notify the SGO. Notifications are processed in receipt order.

- Fiscal/credit rules the department administers: nonrefundable income tax credits (carryforward 5 years) with taxpayer caps (generally lesser of contribution, 50% of tax liability, or $100,000; estates/trusts $1,000,000). Taxpayers may not direct scholarships to specific students.

- Annual statewide credit cap: $25 million for 2025–2027, then may grow (125% if prior year usage >90%, otherwise unchanged) up to a $100 million ceiling. No direct appropriations to the department are specified; credits will reduce state revenue within those caps.
