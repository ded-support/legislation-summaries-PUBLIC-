Summary

Bill LB513 raises statutory salaries for the Nebraska Supreme Court's Chief Justice and associate justices. It sets salaries at $225,055.35 (effective July 1, 2024), $228,431.18 (effective July 1, 2025) and $231,857.65 (effective July 1, 2026). Salaries are payable in equal monthly installments. The bill reiterates that the Chief Justice and Supreme Court judges may not hold any other compensated public office or accept compensated federal appointments during their terms. The bill repeals the prior version of section 24-201.01, designates July 1, 2025 as the operative date, and includes an emergency clause making the act effective upon passage and approval. No separate appropriation language is provided.
