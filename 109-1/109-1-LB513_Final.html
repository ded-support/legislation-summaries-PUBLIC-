Summary

LB513 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §24-201.01 to increase compensation for the Nebraska Supreme Court: effective July 1, 2025 the Chief Justice and associate justices' salary is set at $228,431.18, and on July 1, 2026 it rises to $231,857.65 (the statute also notes the July 1, 2024 figure of $225,055.35). Salaries are payable in equal monthly installments. The bill reiterates that the Chief Justice and Supreme Court judges may not hold other paid public office or compensated federal employment. The original section is repealed; the act becomes operative July 1, 2025, with an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Sets new salaries for the state Supreme Court: effective July 1, 2025, the Chief Justice and justices' annual pay is $228,431.18; effective July 1, 2026, it increases to $231,857.65. No separate appropriations or funding allocations are specified in the added text.
