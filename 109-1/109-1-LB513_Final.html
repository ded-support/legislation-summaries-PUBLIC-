Summary

LB513 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §24-201.01 to adjust compensation for the Chief Justice and justices of the Nebraska Supreme Court. Salaries are set at $228,431.18 effective July 1, 2025, and $231,857.65 effective July 1, 2026 (prior salary on July 1, 2024 was $225,055.35). Salaries remain payable in equal monthly installments and judges are prohibited from holding other paid public offices. The bill repeals the original section, states an operative date of July 1, 2025, and includes an emergency clause so it takes effect upon passage and approval. No separate appropriations or explicit budget allocations are included in the text; these increases will raise state compensation expenditures for the Nebraska Supreme Court.
