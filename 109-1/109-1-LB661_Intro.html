Summary

This bill assigns specific duties to state entities regarding retirement-system investments. Key agency actions:

- Nebraska Investment Council (appointed members): deemed fiduciaries for statewide retirement-system assets; must follow prudent-investor standards, avoid investments whose sole/primary objective is economic or social development, and must not engage with designated "restricted entities." Upon publication of a restricted-entity list, the council must identify and divest related holdings as soon as practicable.

- State investment officer: subject to the same restricted-entity prohibition for Public Employees Retirement Board assets; must follow Nebraska Investment Council directions; file monthly operational reports (within 15 days) to the Governor, State Treasurer, Auditor of Public Accounts, Department of Administrative Services, and council; and provide semiannual electronic reports to the Legislative Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee and Clerk detailing restricted-entity holdings (first due Jan 15, 2026).

- State Treasurer: must publish a list of restricted entities by Oct 1, 2025, and update it at least every six months.

No appropriations or funding changes are included. The act takes effect upon approval (emergency declared).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added duties and restrictions for retirement-system investing: appointed members of the council and the state investment officer (for systems administered by the Public Employees Retirement Board) are prohibited from investing in "restricted entities" and must identify and divest any existing holdings "as soon as practicable" after the State Treasurer publishes the restricted-entity list. The State Treasurer (or designee) must create and publish that list by October 1, 2025, and update it at least semiannually. The definition references multiple federal lists and agencies (Office of Foreign Assets Control/Treasury, Department of Defense, Commerce (Entity/Denied/Unverified/Military End User lists), FCC, Department of Homeland Security, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, SEC, and federal presidential executive orders). The state investment officer must file semiannual electronic reports to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee of the Legislature and the Clerk of the Legislature (first due Jan 15, 2026). No funding or appropriation changes are included.
