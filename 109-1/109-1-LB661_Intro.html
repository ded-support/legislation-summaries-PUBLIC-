Summary

LB661 assigns specific investment and reporting duties to state financial entities. The Nebraska Investment Council (appointed members) and the state investment officer are designated fiduciaries for investments of retirement systems administered by the Public Employees Retirement Board, the Nebraska educational savings plan trust, the ABLE program, and state funds; they must avoid investments whose primary objective is economic development or social purposes and must not invest with listed "restricted entities." The state investment officer must identify and divest retirement-system holdings in restricted entities and provide monthly pooled-accounts reports to the Governor, State Treasurer, Auditor of Public Accounts, Department of Administrative Services, and the council. Semiannual reports on retirement-system investments in restricted entities go to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee and the Clerk of the Legislature (first due Jan 15, 2026). The State Treasurer must publish a restricted-entity list by Oct 1, 2025 and update it at least semiannually. The bill becomes effective upon enactment. No appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions and divestment duties for Nebraska retirement-system investors. Appointed members of the investment council and the state investment officer (for systems administered by the Public Employees Retirement Board) may not invest in “restricted entities” and must identify and divest holdings “as soon as practicable” after the State Treasurer publishes a restricted-entity list. The State Treasurer must create and publish that list by October 1, 2025, and update it at least every six months. The state investment officer must file semiannual electronic reports to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee of the Legislature and the Clerk, naming entities, asset class/sector, and amounts; first report due Jan 15, 2026. Sources for the restricted-entity definition include lists maintained by OFAC (U.S. Dept. of the Treasury), DoD, FCC, DHS, CBP, and the SEC. No funding or appropriation changes.
