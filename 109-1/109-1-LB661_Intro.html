Summary

LB661 assigns and clarifies duties for the Nebraska Investment Council, the state investment officer, and the State Treasurer concerning public retirement and investment funds. Key provisions:

- Council appointed members and the state investment officer are fiduciaries for Public Employees Retirement Board systems, the Nebraska educational savings plan, the ABLE program, and state funds, and must not invest in "restricted entities"; they must identify and divest related holdings as soon as practicable.

- The state investment officer must continue monthly operational reports to the Governor, State Treasurer, Auditor of Public Accounts, Department of Administrative Services, and the Council.

- Semiannual electronic reports to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee and Clerk of the Legislature must list each restricted entity holding, asset allocation class/sector, and amounts invested (first due Jan 15, 2026).

- The State Treasurer must publish a restricted-entity list by Oct 1, 2025, and update it at least every six months.

- Investments made by Class V school boards remain outside Council/state officer fiduciary liability.

No appropriations or funding changes are made. The bill repeals prior sections and takes immediate effect under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a prohibition and divestment regime for Nebraska public retirement investments tied to a State Treasurer–published "restricted entities" list. Key state actors and duties:

- State Treasurer: must create and publish the restricted-entity list by Oct 1, 2025, and update it at least every six months.

- Appointed members of the retirement council and the state investment officer: prohibited from investing with listed entities and must identify and divest related holdings "as soon as practicable" after publication.

- State investment officer: must file semiannual electronic reports to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee of the Legislature and the Clerk of the Legislature (first due Jan 15, 2026), detailing named entities, asset class/sector, and amounts invested.

Federal agencies/lists cited as bases for restricted-entity designation: U.S. Dept. of the Treasury/OFAC, Dept. of Defense, FCC, Dept. of Homeland Security, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, and the SEC. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
