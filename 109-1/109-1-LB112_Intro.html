Summary

LB112 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§60-171 and 60-173 concerning salvage-branded certificates of title. Key agency references and changes:

- Department (and its director): required to receive insurer notifications when owners retain salvage vehicles, immediately enter a salvage brand on the department’s computerized record, and accept insurer electronic reporting via the department’s electronic reporting system once the director sets an implementation date under §60-1508.

- County treasurer: must receive properly endorsed certificates and issue salvage or previously salvaged branded certificates (previously salvaged if repaired and inspected under §60-146).

- Insurers: must obtain and surrender titles or notify the department if owners retain vehicles, report electronically when required, and may apply to the department for a salvage brand without an endorsed title after 30 days if they submit a prescribed affidavit documenting attempts to obtain the title.

No appropriations, funding, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and deeming rules: "consumer care" is defined by reference to section 60-1401.10. A vehicle may be deemed "structurally totaled" by a facility performing consumer care or by the insurer that covered the vehicle when damaged. "Structurally totaled" is specified to mean totaled because of a kink/crease in a frame rail, unibody, or structural component (including engine cradle or rear differential). Exclusions: total loss due solely to hail/cosmetic damage, or where repair cost does not exceed 75% of the vehicle's actual cash value. No government agencies, appropriations, or budget changes are referenced.
