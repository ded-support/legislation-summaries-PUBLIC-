Summary

Bill LB112 amends §§60-171 and 60-173 (Reissue Revised Statutes) governing salvage-branded motor vehicle titles. Key provisions affecting government entities:

- Department (state motor vehicle agency): must immediately enter a salvage brand on the vehicle’s computerized record when notified an owner retains a salvage vehicle; will accept electronic reporting from insurers beginning on an implementation date set by the director under §60-1508.

- County treasurer: upon receipt of an endorsed title, issues salvage- or previously-salvaged-branded certificates depending on whether the vehicle has been repaired and inspected under §60-146.

- Director: sets the electronic reporting implementation date.

- Insurers: required to notify the department and may apply to the department for a salvage-branded title without an owner-signed title after 30 days if they provide an affidavit documenting attempts to obtain the title.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions: ties "consumer care" to the meaning in section 60-1401.10; defines "structurally totaled vehicle" as one totaled due to a kink/crease in a frame rail, unibody, or structural component (including engine cradle or rear differential); specifies it is not a total loss for hail or cosmetic damage; and excludes vehicles where repair costs do not exceed 75% of actual cash value. Also provides that a vehicle can be deemed structurally totaled by a facility performing "consumer care" and by the insurance company that insured the vehicle at the time of damage. No specific government agencies or departments are named beyond the statutory reference (section 60-1401.10), and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.
