Summary

The bill (1) creates a Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund to receive specified receipts; the Department of Revenue will administer the fund to defray publication and administrative costs. Fund balances are investable by the State Investment Officer and may be transferred to the General Fund only by legislative direction. (2) It directs the State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator of the Department of Administrative Services to designate appropriate fund types for the Nebraska educational savings plan trust and requires the Treasurer to report annually to DAS’ budget division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst on transfers from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund to the State Investment Officer's Cash Fund. (3) It establishes three trust funds (Program, Expense, Administrative) operated without General Fund appropriations; the Administrative Fund may receive transfers from the Expense Fund only by legislative appropriation, and the fee‑supported Expense Fund must remit a pro rata share to the State Investment Officer's Cash Fund for investment management costs. (4) The Department of Health and Human Services must annually (by March 1) transmit birth/parental data to the State Treasurer to identify Meadowlark Program eligibles; the data‑transfer cost is payable from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund.
