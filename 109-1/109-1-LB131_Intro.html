Summary

The bill establishes administrative and reporting requirements affecting the Department of Revenue, Department of Administrative Services, and Department of Health and Human Services. It creates a Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund (for receipts under specified tax sections) to defray publication and administrative costs; the fund may be invested by the state investment officer and transfers to the General Fund may occur at the Legislature's direction. The State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator of the Department of Administrative Services must designate required state fund types for the Nebraska educational savings plan trust; the trust operates without General Fund appropriations, its Administrative Fund expenditures require legislative appropriation, and the fee-funded Expense Fund must transfer a pro rata share to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund with annual reporting to DAS’s budget division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst. The Department of Health and Human Services must annually transmit child/guardian identifying information to the State Treasurer for Meadowlark Program administration; related costs are payable from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund.

Key funding/appropriation changes:

- New DOR Miscellaneous Receipts Fund with investable balances and possible Legislative-directed transfers to the General Fund.

- Educational savings plan trust: no General Fund support; Administrative Fund expenditures subject to appropriation; Expense Fund required to transfer investment-management shares and cover DHHS data-transfer costs.
