Summary

The bill (1) creates a Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund, administered by the Department of Revenue to defray publication/listing production and departmental administrative costs; balances may be invested by the State Investment Officer and transfers to the General Fund may occur at the Legislature’s direction. (2) The State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator of the Department of Administrative Services must determine required state fund types for the Nebraska educational savings plan trust to meet §529 standards, and the State Treasurer must annually report to DAS’s budget division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst amounts transferred from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund. (3) DHHS must annually (by March 1) transmit Meadowlark Program enrollee data to the State Treasurer; transfer costs are paid from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund. The trust shall operate with no General Fund appropriations; administrative expenditures require legislative appropriation, and the Expense Fund (fee‑supported) funds trust costs and pro rata investment‑management transfers.
