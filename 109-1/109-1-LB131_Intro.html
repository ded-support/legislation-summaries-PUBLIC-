Summary

LB131 directs agency responsibilities and funding for the Nebraska educational savings plan trust and the Meadowlark Program. The State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator (Department of Administrative Services) must classify trust state fund types to ensure IRC §529 and state policy compliance. Transfers from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund (equal to the Nebraska Investment Council pro rata budget share) require annual reporting to the DAS budget division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst. Transfers into the Education Savings Plan Administrative Fund are permitted only by legislative appropriation; the trust is to operate without General Fund appropriations. The Department of Health and Human Services must annually (by March 1) transmit Meadowlark beneficiary data to the State Treasurer; DHHS bears no direct appropriation and transmission costs are paid from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund. The Department of Revenue will administer a new Miscellaneous Receipts Fund for specified receipts to defray publication/administrative costs; its balance may be transferred to the General Fund and is investable by the state investment officer.
