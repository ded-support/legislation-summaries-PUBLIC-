Summary

LB153 requires the Department of Health and Human Services to: seek federal approval to extend Medicaid postpartum coverage from 60 days to at least six months (subsection 6); provide electric personal-use breast pumps and at least ten lactation consultation visits by Jan 1, 2025 (subsection 8); provide coverage and reimburse providers for necessary translation and interpretation services by Jan 1, 2024 (subsection 9); submit, by Oct 1, 2025, a state plan amendment or waiver to the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services to implement at least six months of postpartum coverage through the Children's Health Insurance Program (subsection 10). The bill designates the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to finance the CHIP postpartum coverage and explicitly adds CHIP postpartum coverage to the fund’s allowable uses; investment earnings in the fund are credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services must, by October 1, 2025, seek CMS approval via a CHIP state plan amendment to obtain federal matching funds to provide at least six months of postpartum coverage for mothers whose child is enrolled under the unborn child option. Covered services must match the comprehensive postpartum services provided under Medicaid; the department may instead pursue a 12‑month coverage SPA. The Legislature expresses intent to fund these services from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (see §68‑996). No specific dollar amounts or new appropriations are specified.
