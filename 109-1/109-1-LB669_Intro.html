Summary

The bill references the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) in several specific duties: it defines "hospital" as an institution licensed by DHHS under the Health Care Facility Licensure Act; requires that DHHS publish printed informational materials (including alternatives to abortion and instructions for finding immediate medical assistance after taking mifepristone) which physicians must notify patients about; directs DHHS to compile and maintain a geographically arranged list of providers/facilities offering ultrasounds (noting those offering free services) for physicians to furnish on request; and makes civil liability for failure to inform about those printed materials contingent on DHHS having published and made them available. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that licensed health personnel must privately ask and evaluate pregnant women about coercion to have an abortion, interpersonal/domestic violence, and human trafficking; post the national domestic violence and human trafficking hotline numbers in a manner that ensures privacy; and, if a woman discloses coercion or victimization, provide those numbers and offer a confidential telephone call. Staff must document that the questions were asked and that hotline information and call opportunity were offered. For the Department of Health and Human Services, this creates new compliance, licensing, training, and oversight duties to enforce these requirements. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
