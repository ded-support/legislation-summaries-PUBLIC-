Summary

LB669 references the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) in several operational duties. It defines "hospital" as facilities licensed by DHHS under the Health Care Facility Licensure Act. The bill requires DHHS to publish printed materials that describe the unborn child, list agencies offering alternatives to abortion, and include information (also posted on DHHS’s website) about finding immediate medical assistance if a patient wishes to continue a pregnancy after taking mifepristone. DHHS must compile and make available a geographically arranged list of providers, facilities, and clinics that offer ultrasounds by qualified personnel. Civil liability for a provider’s failure to inform patients about those materials does not attach unless DHHS has published and made the materials available. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that licensed health-care providers must (1) ask and evaluate pregnant women, in a private manner, whether they are being pressured/coerced into abortion or are victims of interpersonal/domestic violence or human trafficking; (2) if a woman discloses coercion, abuse, or trafficking, provide the national domestic violence and national human trafficking hotline numbers and offer a confidential telephone call. These are procedural obligations the Department of Health and Human Services would oversee through its licensing/ regulatory authority. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are specified in the added text.
