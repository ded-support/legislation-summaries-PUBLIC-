Summary

- Defines "hospital" as institutions licensed by the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) under the Health Care Facility Licensure Act.

- Requires physicians or their agents to inform patients that DHHS's website provides information on finding immediate medical assistance if they change their mind after taking mifepristone.

- Requires physicians to notify patients that printed materials provided by DHHS describe the unborn child, list agencies offering alternatives to abortion, and include the mifepristone assistance information; DHHS must publish and make those materials available for physicians to be subject to civil liability for failing to offer them.

- Mandates that, if requested, physicians provide a comprehensive, geographically arranged list compiled by DHHS of providers, facilities, and clinics that offer ultrasounds (including those offering free ultrasounds), with contact details.

- The bill contains no explicit changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that licensed providers must, in a private manner, ask and evaluate pregnant women whether they are being coerced into abortion, have experienced interpersonal/domestic violence, or have been victims of human trafficking. If a woman discloses coercion, domestic violence, or trafficking, the provider must give the national domestic violence and national human trafficking hotline numbers and offer the woman an opportunity to make a confidential telephone call. The provision also requires asking these screening questions and providing the phone numbers and call opportunity when applicable. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included; the Department of Health and Human Services would likely have oversight/implementation and potential training or administrative duties, but no new budget authority is provided.
