Summary

This bill assigns several specific duties to the Department of Agriculture:

- Restricted entities (or their agents/trustees/fiduciaries) that rely on a CFIUS determination or national security agreement must annually certify compliance to the Department of Agriculture by January 15, 2026, and each January 15 thereafter (sec. 76-3709(2)(b)).

- Any person may notify the Department of Agriculture (or the Attorney General) of suspected violations; the Department is charged with investigating alleged violations (sec. 76-3712).

- If the Director of Agriculture has reasonable suspicion of a violation, the director must refer the matter to the Attorney General or outside counsel for enforcement (sec. 76-3712).

- The Director of Agriculture, Attorney General, and retained outside counsel are responsible for determining whether a person is subject to or in violation of the act (sec. 76-3715).

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that restricted foreign owners (nonresident aliens, foreign corporations, foreign governments, etc.) who either (A) have a CFIUS determination of “no unresolved national security concerns” (with no covered control transaction since) or (B) have and maintain a CFIUS national security agreement must annually certify that status to the Department of Agriculture on or before January 15, 2026 and each January 15 thereafter. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Agriculture are included.
