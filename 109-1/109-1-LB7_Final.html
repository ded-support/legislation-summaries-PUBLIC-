Summary

Key Department of Agriculture provisions in LB7:

- Restricted foreign entities that qualify under CFIUS conditions must annually certify compliance to the Department of Agriculture by January 15, 2026, and each January 15 thereafter (sec. 76-3709(2)(b)).

- Any person may notify the Department of Agriculture of alleged violations; the Department is charged with investigating violations (sec. 76-3712(1)-(2)).

- If the Director has reasonable suspicion, the Department refers matters to the Attorney General or outside counsel retained by the Department for enforcement (sec. 76-3712(2)).

- The Director of Agriculture, Attorney General, and retained outside counsel share responsibility for determining whether a person is subject to or violating the act (sec. 76-3715).

No direct appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes for the Department of Agriculture are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds exemptions for persons/entities from an “excepted real estate foreign state” as determined by CFIUS (31 C.F.R. citations), while excluding “restricted entities.” Restricted entities are barred from acquiring/holding/operating leases for oil, gas, or other hydrocarbons under the referenced subsection. A restricted entity may qualify only if (a)(i) CFIUS has determined there are no unresolved national‑security concerns and no covered‑control change since that determination, or (ii) it holds a valid national‑security agreement with CFIUS. Such entities must annually certify compliance to the Department of Agriculture on or before January 15, 2026, and each January 15 thereafter. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Agriculture are specified.
