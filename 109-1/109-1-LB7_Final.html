Summary

LB7 assigns several enforcement and reporting roles to the Department of Agriculture. Restricted entities that meet CFIUS conditions must annually certify compliance to the Department on or before January 15, 2026, and each January 15 thereafter. Any person may notify the Department (or the Attorney General) of violations or suspected violations. The Department is tasked with investigating alleged violations; if the Director has reasonable suspicion, the Director must refer the matter to the Attorney General or outside counsel retained by the Department for enforcement. The Director of Agriculture, together with the Attorney General and retained outside counsel, is charged with determining whether a person is subject to or in violation of the act. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a compliance/filing obligation for the Department of Agriculture: restricted foreign persons (nonresident aliens, foreign corporations, foreign governments, or their agents/trustees/fiduciaries) who have either a CFIUS determination that no unresolved national‑security concerns exist or an approved national security agreement must annually certify that status to the Department of Agriculture. First certification is due on or before January 15, 2026, and then by January 15 each year thereafter. No language in the added text changes funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Agriculture.
