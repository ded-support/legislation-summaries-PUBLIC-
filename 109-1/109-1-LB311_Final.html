Summary

- The bill requires the Public Service Commission (PSC) to consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (or designee) of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) when evaluating applications for permits, certificates, or designations to provide medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) services contracted with DHHS, Medicaid-managed care organizations, or agents of the department.

- A designation authorizing a regulated motor carrier to provide NEMT must be found necessary for the present or future convenience and necessity of medicaid clients; applicants bear the burden to demonstrate responsiveness to public need, and protestants must show existing carriers meet the need or that the applicant would impair existing operations.

- Transportation network companies and participating drivers are prohibited from providing transportation under contract with DHHS or its contractors without specific PSC authorization; the PSC must grant authorization unless protesters meet the statutory standard.

- The bill contains no DHHS appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that when the commission considers authorizing providers to offer Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) under contract with (i) the Department of Health and Human Services, (ii) a Medicaid-managed care organization contracting with the department, or (iii) another agent acting for the department under section 75-303.01, the commission must consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (or designee) to assess consistency with the public interest. No changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
