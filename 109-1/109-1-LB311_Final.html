Summary

- The bill requires the Public Service Commission to consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care, Department of Health and Human Services) when determining public‑interest consistency for permits, certificates, or designations authorizing medicaid nonemergency medical transportation services provided under contract with the Department, a Medicaid‑managed care organization under contract with the Department, or an agent working on the Department’s behalf (see amendments to section 75-311).

- The bill also requires transportation network companies and participating drivers to obtain specific authorization from the Commission before providing transportation for any person under contract with the Department of Health and Human Services or its contractors; the Commission must grant authorization unless protested on specified public‑interest grounds (see amendment to section 75-342).

- No provisions in the bill change Department of Health and Human Services appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that when the commission considers authorizing providers to deliver Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation under contract with the Department of Health and Human Services, a Medicaid-managed care organization under contract with DHHS, or another DHHS agent (per section 75-303.01), the commission must consult the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (or designee) of DHHS’s Division of Medicaid and Long-Term Care. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are included.
