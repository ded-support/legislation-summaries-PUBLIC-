Summary

LB311 references the Department of Health and Human Services in two contexts:

- Motor carrier permits and designations for medicaid nonemergency medical transportation: applications for authority to provide such services under contracts with the Department (or its managed‑care contractors or agents) must involve consultation with the Director of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care (or designee) when the Public Service Commission assesses public convenience and necessity.

- Transportation network companies/participating drivers: they may not provide transportation under contract with the Department or its contractors without specific PSC authorization; the commission must grant authorization unless protestants demonstrate existing carriers meet the need.

No changes to Department of Health and Human Services funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that when the commission considers authorizing providers to deliver Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation under a contract with (i) the Department of Health and Human Services, (ii) a Medicaid-managed care organization contracted with the department, or (iii) another agent acting for the department under §75-303.01, the commission must consult with the Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care’s Director of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care (or designee). No changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
