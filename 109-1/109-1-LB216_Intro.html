Summary

LB216 centralizes district‑court clerical functions under the Nebraska Supreme Court. Beginning January 7, 2027, elected clerks of the district court and their employees convert to state employment under the Supreme Court; the State (with funds appropriated to the Supreme Court) will pay their salaries, benefits, and expenses. The State Court Administrator receives court books/files (county‑owned equipment remains with counties). Counties must transfer and partially reimburse the State for accrued sick and vacation leave per specified formulas. Retirement treatment is addressed (transition to State Employees Retirement System or preservation of county retirement benefits with proportional cost‑sharing). The Supreme Court will fund certain district and separate juvenile court IT and communications costs. Federal reimbursements for child‑support administration will be credited to the State Treasurer for Agency 5 – Supreme Court. Oaths filed with the Secretary of State; emergency effective date.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that, beginning Jan. 7, 2027, elected clerks of district courts and their employees become state employees under the Nebraska Supreme Court. The State (with funds appropriated to the Supreme Court/Agency 5) will directly pay salaries, benefits, and expenses; the Supreme Court controls new clerk positions and non‑county‑consistent pay/benefit changes. Counties must provide accrual value data to the Supreme Court. Asset/records rules: county keeps furniture/equipment; books/files/records transfer to the State Court Administrator. Leave and retirement funding: counties reimburse the State for portions of transferred sick leave (25% up to 1,440 hours) and reimburse 100% of transferred vacation leave value (up to 280 hours); excess leave reimbursements go to employees per provisions. Retirement transfer costs are shared proportionately between county and state. Transferred staff may join Nebraska State Insurance Program (no waiting period). Federal reimbursements for child‑support processing are credited to the State Treasurer for Agency 5 – Supreme Court. Oaths filed with Secretary of State.
