Summary

LB216 restructures district court clerk functions and shifts fiscal responsibility to the judicial branch. Key agencies impacted:

- Nebraska Supreme Court / State Court Administrator: assumes employment of elected district court clerks and their staff beginning January 7, 2027; becomes responsible for salaries, benefits, expenses (funded by appropriations to the Supreme Court), personnel rules, and receipt of transferred books/files. State Court Administrator will negotiate child‑support cost‑reimbursement agreements (effective Jan 1, 2026) and federal reimbursements are credited to Agency 5 – Supreme Court via the State Treasurer.

- Counties / County boards: must provide transfer data, transfer accrued sick leave (up to 1,440 hours) and vacation (up to 280 hours), and reimburse the state (25% of sick‑leave value, 100% of vacation‑leave value transferred). Counties retain ownership of furniture/equipment; retirement cost sharing between county and state is required; metropolitan/primary class counties have alternate retirement provisions.

- Department of Health and Human Services: will receive statistical reporting coordinated by the State Court Administrator.

The bill eliminates the elected clerk of district court office and creates consolidated clerk offices; effective dates and transitional provisions are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective Jan. 7, 2027, elected clerks of the district courts and their employees become state employees under the Nebraska Supreme Court. The State of Nebraska, using funds appropriated to the Supreme Court, will pay all salaries, benefits, and expenses of transferred staff; no loss of income is allowed. Counties must provide accrual info for sick/vacation leave. Counties retain furniture/equipment but must transfer books/files to the State Court Administrator. Leave-transfer rules: up to 1,440 sick hours and 280 vacation hours transferable with county reimbursements (sick: county pays 25% of transferred value; vacation: county pays 100% of transferred value); various reimbursements for granted leave and excess balances are specified. Retirement treatment references the Retirement System for Nebraska Counties and the State Employees Retirement System; costs are to be shared proportionately. Transferred employees and dependents may join the Nebraska State Insurance Program without a waiting period. Federal reimbursements for direct child-support costs are to be transferred to the State Treasurer for credit to Agency 5 – Supreme Court.
