Summary

The bill makes these agency-specific changes:

- Department of Motor Vehicles: reduces the late sales/use tax filing penalty to $5 when collected by a county treasurer or the DMV.

- Department of Administrative Services: added to the list of officials whose unauthorized disclosure of taxpayers’ returns or investigatory information is a Class I misdemeanor.

- Department of Revenue (defined as “Department”): authorized to implement and participate in the Streamlined Sales and Use Tax Agreement, adopt rules, jointly procure with other member states, provide retailer notice via its website, maintain local-boundary, tax-rate, and ZIP-to-jurisdiction databases (including 9‑ and 5‑digit rules), securely store audit work papers, and share confidential returns with auditors and certified municipal designees. It will administer multiple tax-credit programs, set application processes, prorate awards when limits are exceeded, and apply these approval limits: food-donation credits—total cap $500,000 annually beginning FY2025‑26; section 77‑7012 credits—$1,000,000 in FY2024‑25 and $1,500,000 thereafter; child‑care credits—until annual total reaches $15 million. The bill also references the U.S. Department of Agriculture for food-bank eligibility.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue

- Model 1 sellers will not receive collection fees when a certified service provider (CSP) performs the seller's sales/use tax functions and is compensated under the Streamlined Sales and Use Tax Agreement.

- Adds a refundable credit per section 77-7203 for taxpayers qualifying under the Child Care Tax Credit Act for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan 1, 2024.

- Sales tax rate lookup: use nine‑digit ZIPs and, where a five‑digit area spans multiple rates, apply the highest combined tax rate.

- Child care credits are subject to an annual cap; once reached, additional credits are disallowed and requests received the day the cap is exceeded are prorated.
