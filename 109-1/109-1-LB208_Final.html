Summary

LB208 affects the Department of Revenue (DOR), Department of Administrative Services (DAS), and Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV); the Department of Agriculture is named but no specific duties are detailed in the summaries provided. DOR: ratifies Streamlined Sales & Use Tax Agreement amendments through Dec. 31, 2024; may act jointly with other states, adopt rules, procure services, maintain sales-tax rate/ZIP-code databases, notify retailers via its website, and share confidential returns with auditors under specified confidentiality and data‑retention/model‑1 seller privacy rules. DOR will administer tax-credit programs, including a new nonrefundable food‑donation credit (50% of value, up to $2,500) for grocery stores, restaurants, and agricultural producers (tax years beginning Jan. 1, 2025) with a $500,000 annual approval cap (prorated if exceeded). Under section 77‑7012 DOR may approve $1,000,000 in credits in FY2024‑25 and $1,500,000 annually thereafter; refundable child‑care credits will be approved until $15 million total approvals. DAS personnel are explicitly prohibited from disclosing confidential tax information (Class I misdemeanor). DMV: certain collection penalties are set at $5.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: no changes indicated.

Department of Motor Vehicles: no changes indicated.

Department of Revenue: multiple tax law adjustments. Model 1 sellers whose sales/use tax functions are performed by a certified service provider (and the provider is compensated under the Streamlined Sales and Use Tax Agreement) will not receive collection fees. Establishes a refundable Child Care Tax Credit per section 77-7203 for taxpayers for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on/after Jan 1, 2024. Clarifies rate sourcing: use nine‑digit ZIPs and, if a five‑digit area contains multiple tax rates, apply the highest combined tax rate. Annual credit caps are enforced with proration among requests received on the day the limit is exceeded. Adds a residency specification (Nebraska resident).
