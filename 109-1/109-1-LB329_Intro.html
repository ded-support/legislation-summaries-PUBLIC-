Summary

LB329 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. § 28-316.01 (repealing the original section) to revise definitions and criminal provisions for sexual abuse by a school employee. It specifies the State Department of Education as the approving/accrediting authority for applicable public, private, denominational, or parochial schools. The bill defines "school contract worker" (persons 19+ who work at such schools but are not employed by them, e.g., school resource officers, construction workers, consultants) and expands "school employee" to include either school employees or school contract workers. "Student" is defined as persons aged 16–19 enrolled/attending such schools or within 90 days of enrollment. It classifies offenses (first-, second-, and third-degree sexual abuse by a school employee) and assigns felony classes. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "school contract worker" and inserts that term into subsection (d). The definition covers persons 19+ who, as part of their employment, work at a public, private, denominational, or parochial school "approved or accredited by the State Department of Education" but are not employed by the school (examples: school resource officers, construction workers, consultants, independent contractors). No agencies other than the State Department of Education are mentioned, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
