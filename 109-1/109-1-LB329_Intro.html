Summary

LB329 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-316.01 to expand and clarify definitions used in the statute. It defines "school contract worker" (person 19 or older who works at a school but is not employed by it) and explicitly includes such contract workers within the definition of "school employee." "Student" is defined as a person aged 16–19 enrolled in (or who attended within 90 days) a school approved or accredited by the State Department of Education. The bill retains criminal classifications and penalties for sexual abuse by a school employee (first-, second-, and third-degree offenses as Class IIA, IIIA, and IV felonies, respectively). The original section is repealed. No agencies beyond the State Department of Education are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of “school contract worker”: a person 19+ who, as part of their employment, works at a public, private, denominational, or parochial school approved or accredited by the State Department of Education but is not employed by the school (examples: school resource officer, construction worker, consultant, independent contractor). Also amends subsection (d) to include a school contract worker in the (i)/(ii) enumerated categories. No agencies aside from the State Department of Education are mentioned and there are no funding or appropriation changes.
