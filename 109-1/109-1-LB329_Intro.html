Summary

LB329 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-316.01 (R.S. Cumulative Supp., 2024) to revise definitions and criminalize sexual abuse by school employees. It (1) defines “school contract worker” (persons 19+ who work at State Department of Education–approved or -accredited public, private, denominational, or parochial schools but are not employed by the school) and expressly includes such contract workers within the definition of “school employee”; (2) defines “student” as persons aged 16–19 enrolled or recently attending such schools; and (3) specifies offenses and penalties (first-degree for sexual penetration — Class IIA felony; second-degree for sexual contact — Class IIIA felony; third-degree for a pattern/scheme — Class IV felony) and bars consent as a defense. The bill repeals the original section. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "school contract worker" as a person aged 19+ who, while employed elsewhere, works at public, private, denominational, or parochial schools that are approved or accredited by the State Department of Education but is not employed by the school (examples: school resource officer, construction worker, consultant, independent contractor). Also amends subsection (d) to include a school contract worker in the specified clauses. No agencies beyond the State Department of Education are referenced and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
