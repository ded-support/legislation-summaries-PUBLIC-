Summary

- Department of Transportation: authorizes the Department of Transportation to enter into agreements with approved destination district applicants to construct roads, bridges, and other infrastructure within a destination district, subject to applicable federal and state laws and codes.

- Department of Economic Development: requires annual updating of workforce-housing construction cost thresholds by the Department of Economic Development based on the Producer Price Index for all commodities.

- Department of Labor: the bill cites the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics as the source of the Producer Price Index used for those updates.

No direct appropriations, agency budget increases, or other funding allocations to these departments are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development

- Will accept destination district applications through Dec 31, 2025, review/confidentiality rules, and approve up to two statewide districts (40‑year term, ≤5,000 acres) if projects exceed $3 billion and expect ≥10 million annual visitors.

- May set a new state occupation tax (in addition to sales/use tax) for transactions on property owned by the applicant; rate set after notice and hearing.

- May designate a trustee bank to hold occupation tax proceeds and use those funds in agreements with the district applicant.

- May form and staff a Community Redevelopment Authority for the district (appoint/remove members; one must be a county board member).

Department of Transportation

- May enter into agreements with approved district applicants to construct roads, bridges, and other infrastructure within the district (must meet applicable laws/codes).
