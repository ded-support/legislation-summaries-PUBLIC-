Summary

LB66 adopts the Uniform Health‑Care Decisions Act, establishing statewide rules for advance health‑care directives, powers of attorney for health care, default surrogates, capacity determinations, and guardianship interactions. Government entities affected include Nebraska courts (timelines and standards for hearings, capacity findings, and guardian authority), health‑care institutions and their medical records administrators (duty to document directives and transfer patients when refusing care), and responsible health‑care professionals (notification duties, ability to require surrogate declarations, and permitted refusals). Nursing homes and assisted‑living facilities are referenced by statutory definition (including the Social Security Act nursing‑facility definition and section 71‑5903), with owner/operator/employee status affecting surrogate eligibility. Emergency medical responders receive a limited liability carve‑out. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and procedures for advance health-care directives, powers of attorney, default surrogates, capacity determinations, and rights/remedies. Government entities referenced: the federal Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. §1396r(a)(1)) for the nursing home definition; state courts/judicial branch for findings of capacity, guardianship actions, and related hearings; and state licensing/certification authorities implicitly referenced for “health‑care institutions” and assisted‑living facilities (see cross‑reference to section 71‑5903). Also references emergency medical responders and the role of guardians appointed by courts. No specific changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
