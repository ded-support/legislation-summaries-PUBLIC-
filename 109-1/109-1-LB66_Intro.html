Summary

LB66 adopts the Uniform Health‑Care Decisions Act, creating rules for advance directives, powers of attorney for health care, default surrogates, and advance mental‑health directives. Government actors referenced or affected include: state courts (timelines and standards for capacity determinations and guardianship authority); guardianship proceedings (court authorization to revoke directives or order noncompliance); health‑care institutions and their medical‑records administrators (duty to document directives and manage transfers when refusing care); responsible health‑care professionals and emergency medical responders (documentation, disclosure, liability limits); and regulated long‑term care settings such as nursing homes and assisted‑living facilities (citations to statutory definitions, disqualification rules). The bill creates civil remedies and immunity provisions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Uniform Health‑Care Decisions Act establishing definitions and rules for advance health‑care directives, powers of attorney, default surrogates, and advance mental‑health directives. Key government actors referenced: state courts (for capacity determinations and guardianship orders), guardians/guardianship courts, and emergency medical responders. Health‑care institutions and professionals, nursing homes (defined by reference to the Social Security Act, 42 U.S.C. 1396r), and assisted‑living facilities are regulated in their roles and conscience‑based refusal/transfer duties. No new appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included. (Civil remedies and statutory damages are specified, but no fiscal transfers or agency budget changes are proposed.)
