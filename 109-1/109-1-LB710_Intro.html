Summary

LB710 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77‑2715.07. It explicitly authorizes the Department of Revenue to recapture the $5,000 nonrefundable residence‑purchase credit (subsection (7)) if the claimant sells or transfers the residence or ceases to use it as their primary residence within five years after the taxable year the credit was claimed. No other provisions explicitly reference the Department of Revenue, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds subsection setting the state credit rate: 10 for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin before Jan. 1, 2025, and 20% of the federal credit under IRC §32 for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2025. The Department of Revenue would apply these rates when administering the credit (see cross‑reference to subdivision (2)(e)(i)). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
