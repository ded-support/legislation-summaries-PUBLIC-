Summary

LB710 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2715.07. It specifies that the $5,000 nonrefundable credit for individuals who purchase a primary residence in an area declared extremely blighted (subsection (7)) is subject to recapture by the Department of Revenue if the claimant sells/transfers the residence or ceases to use it as a primary residence within five years of the taxable year the credit was claimed. No departmental appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language sets the state credit at 10% for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin before January 1, 2025, and at 20% of the federal credit under IRC §32 (the federal EITC) for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2025. This is a rate/eligibility change the Department of Revenue would apply when administering the credit. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the provided text.
