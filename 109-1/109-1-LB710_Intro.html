Summary

LB710 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2715.07. It authorizes the Department of Revenue to recapture the $5,000 nonrefundable home-purchase credit (for residences in designated extremely blighted areas) if the claimant sells or transfers the residence or stops using it as their primary residence within five years of the taxable year the credit was claimed (see subsection 7(d)). The bill contains no explicit changes to Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a two-tier formula for the refundable tax credit administered by the Department of Revenue: for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) before January 1, 2025 the credit is set at "Ten" (presumably ten percent), and for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2025 the credit equals twenty percent of the federal credit under IRC §32. The provision appears to require the Department to apply the new percentages when calculating Colorado credits. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included in the added text.
