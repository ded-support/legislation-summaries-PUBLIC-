Summary

LB78 (Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Assistance Act) identifies the Department as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and directs DHHS to administer housing stability programs for victims, including grants, loans, reimbursements, formula-based distribution, contracting with qualified third parties, and—if obligations are met—redistribution of up to 20% excess to certified organizations for housing acquisition/rehabilitation. The Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund is created, administered by DHHS, funded by legislative transfers and public/private sources, and invested by the state investment officer. The documentary stamp tax allocation is amended so that, from each $2.32 collected, $0.07 is credited to the new Survivor Housing Assistance Fund (other allocations specified). The Department of Revenue retains rulemaking/declaratory-ruling authority and may not overturn a county assessor’s sale qualification without its own review.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Assistance Act and names the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) as lead. DHHS must promote housing stability for domestic violence and sex‑trafficking survivors by providing grants, loans, rental/utility/security deposit assistance, and provider reimbursements; it may contract with qualified 501(c)(3) nonprofits or tribes. DHHS will allocate assistance by a formula developed with stakeholder consultation and may redirect up to 20% of excess funds toward acquisition/rehabilitation housing for survivors. Establishes the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund, administered by DHHS, to receive legislative transfers and public/private contributions; funds are investable under state law. No specific appropriation amounts or direct changes to the Department of Revenue’s duties or budget are specified in the added text.
