Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): "Department" is defined as DHHS. DHHS must encourage and facilitate housing stability for domestic violence and sex‑trafficking survivors by providing grants, loans, housing‑related assistance, and reimbursements to service providers; it may contract with qualified third‑party nonprofits and may allocate up to 20% of excess assistance funds for acquisition/rehabilitation of housing. DHHS will administer the newly created Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund, which consists of legislative transfers and public or private contributions and is subject to state investment rules.

- Department of Revenue: The Tax Commissioner designs documentary stamps and may issue declaratory rulings. Registers collect documentary stamp tax and remit proceeds to the State Treasurer, who credits (per $2.32 tax) $0.95 to Affordable Housing Trust Fund, $0.25 to Site and Building Development Fund, $0.25 to Homeless Shelter Assistance Trust Fund, $0.30 to Behavioral Health Services Fund, and $0.07 to the new Survivor Housing Assistance Fund. The Department of Revenue will not overturn county assessor sale‑qualification determinations absent a departmental review showing error.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and requires DHHS to promote housing stability for domestic violence and sex‑trafficking survivors by providing grants, loans, rental/utility/security deposit assistance, and reimbursements to service providers. DHHS may distribute housing assistance (with up to 20% of any excess used for certified organizations' housing acquisition/rehabilitation), must allocate assistance by a formula developed in consultation with relevant organizations, and may contract with qualified third‑party nonprofits or tribes. Establishes the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund, administered by DHHS, funded by legislative transfers and public/private sources and investable under state investment law.
