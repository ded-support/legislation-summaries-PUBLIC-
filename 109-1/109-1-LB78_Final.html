Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): defined as "Department." DHHS must encourage and facilitate housing stability for domestic violence and sex‑trafficking survivors by providing grants, loans, housing‑related assistance, and reimbursements; may contract with qualified third parties; may allocate up to 20% of excess assistance costs to certified organizations for housing acquisition/rehabilitation. DHHS will administer the newly created Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund, funded by legislative transfers and public or private receipts; funds may be invested by the state investment officer.

- Department of Revenue: taxpayers may seek a declaratory ruling from the Department of Revenue on documentary stamp tax disputes. The Department of Revenue may not overturn a county assessor's sale‑qualification determination unless its review finds it incorrect.

- Funding change: documentary stamp tax allocations revised to credit 7 cents per $2.32 to the new Survivor Housing Assistance Fund (alongside specified allocations to other funds).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Assistance Act and directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to promote housing stability for victims by providing grants, loans, rental/utility/security deposit and related assistance, reimbursements to providers, and by contracting with qualified third‑party nonprofits or tribes. DHHS must manage/distribute assistance using a department formula developed with domestic‑violence/trafficking organizations, may allocate up to 20% of any excess assistance for acquisition/rehabilitation housing, and may contract for service delivery. It creates the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund, administered by DHHS, funded by legislative transfers and public/private sources and investable under state law. The added text does not assign operational duties to the Department of Revenue, but includes a fragmented provision directing specific cents of an identified amount to be transferred to the new fund.
