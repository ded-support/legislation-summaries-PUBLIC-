Summary

LB426 amends education statutes to (1) authorize a one‑hour American flag education program (grades 5–8) that may be developed with local veterans organizations and whose costs may be paid from lottery education funds or private donations; and (2) revise distribution of State Lottery Operation Trust Fund money used for education. For FY2024‑25 through FY2028‑29, statute prescribes specific percentage transfers: 8% Behavioral Training Cash Fund; 2% College Pathway Program Cash Fund; 7% Community College Gap Assistance; 10% State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund; 3% for distance education incentives and flag education programs; 1% Door to College Scholarship; 8% Excellence in Teaching; 1.5% Expanded Learning Opportunity Grant; 1.5% Mental Health Training; and 58% Nebraska Opportunity Grant. Through FY2023‑24 funds went to the Nebraska Education Improvement Fund under prior allocations. Agencies (school boards, State Board of Education, superintendents, State Department of Education) have implementation duties; recipients must report annually to the Auditor of Public Accounts, who compiles a legislative report. The Education Committee must recommend allocations for FY2029‑30 by Dec. 31, 2027.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an optional one-hour American flag education program for students from 5th through 8th grade, to be offered by public, private, denominational, and parochial schools as part of social studies. The program may be developed in consultation with a local recognized veterans organization (see §80‑401.01). Each participating student shall receive a 3'×5' American flag and a flag-etiquette booklet. Program costs are to be paid from lottery funds pursuant to §79‑3501 or from private donations. The amendment also cross-references section 79‑724. No specific appropriation amounts or new budget line items are specified.
