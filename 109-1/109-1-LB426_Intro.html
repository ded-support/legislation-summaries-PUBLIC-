Summary

LB426 amends school statutes to authorize a voluntary American flag education program (grades 5–8) administered by school districts (school boards, superintendents, and committees on American civics) and permits funding via lottery education funds or private donations. It revises distribution of State Lottery Operation Trust Fund moneys used for education: for FY2024-25 through FY2028-29 transfers are prescribed by percentage to specific state funds (e.g., 8% Behavioral Training Cash Fund; 2% College Pathway; 7% Community College Gap Assistance; 10% State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund; 3% for distance-education incentives and flag education programs; 58% to Nebraska Opportunity Grant Fund, et al.). Through FY2023-24 funds remained in the Nebraska Education Improvement Fund with specified FY2023-24 allocations and a transfer of its balance (less a small reserve) to the Behavioral Training Cash Fund. Auditor of Public Accounts reporting and a Dec. 31, 2027 Education Committee recommendation on post‑2029 allocations are required. Funds may be invested by the state investment officer; agencies may retain portions for administrative costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates an optional one‑hour American flag education program for students between 5th and 8th grade as part of social studies, covering the U.S. Flag Code, the thirteen folds, flag etiquette/presentation, and flag history. Programs may be developed with local recognized veterans organizations (see §80‑401.01). Each student receives a 3'×5' American flag and a flag etiquette booklet. Program costs are to be paid with lottery funds pursuant to §79‑3501 or private donations. Text also references flag education programs under subdivision (4) of §79‑724.
