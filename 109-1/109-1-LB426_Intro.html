Summary

Bill amends education statutes to (1) authorize an optional one-hour American flag education program for grades 5–8 (schools may partner with local recognized veterans organizations); each participating student receives a 3' x 5' flag and booklet, with program costs payable from lottery proceeds under section 79-3501 or private donations; and (2) revise distribution of State Lottery education funds for FY2024-25 through FY2028-29. Allocations direct percentages to specific state funds/agencies: Behavioral Training Cash Fund (8%), College Pathway Program Cash Fund (2%), Community College Gap Assistance Program Fund (7%), State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund (10%), a 3% bucket for distance-education incentives and flag education programs, Door to College Scholarship Fund (1%), Excellence in Teaching Cash Fund (8%), Expanded Learning Opportunity Grant Fund (1.5%), Mental Health Training Cash Fund (1.5%), and Nebraska Opportunity Grant Fund (58%). Auditor of Public Accounts and the Education Committee retain annual reporting and review responsibilities; transfers after FY2029-30 are left to legislative direction.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an optional one‑hour American flag education program for students between 5th and 8th grade in public, private, denominational, and parochial schools covering the U.S. Flag Code, flag folds, etiquette, presentation conduct, and history. Schools may develop/present the program in consultation with a "local recognized veterans organization" (see section 80‑401.01). Each student would receive a 3'×5' American flag and an etiquette booklet. Program costs are to be funded from state lottery funds pursuant to section 79‑3501 or from private donations. The amendment references section 79‑724 but does not create a new appropriation beyond directing use of lottery funds or donations.
