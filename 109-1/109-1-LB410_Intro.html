Summary

LB410 (2025), introduced by Sen. J. Cavanaugh and referred to the Legislature’s Banking, Commerce and Insurance Committee, requires health insurance coverage for prosthetics and orthotics as prescribed. The bill defines "orthotic" as a custom‑fitted or custom‑fabricated medical device applied to correct deformity, improve function, or relieve disease symptoms, and "prosthetic" as artificial limbs and associated components, including replacements when required by changes in a patient's condition. The text contains no references to state agencies or departments and includes no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
