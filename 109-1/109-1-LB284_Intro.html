Summary

LB284 appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), Program 514: $500,000 from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund for FY2025‑26 and $500,000 from the same fund for FY2026‑27 (total $1,000,000) as Cash Funds designated as state aid. The aid is limited to a single nonprofit (501(c)(3)) providing health screenings, sports competitions, educational opportunities, and leadership training for persons with developmental or intellectual disabilities. The appropriation specifically prohibits using these funds for state employee permanent/temporary salaries or per diems. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Appropriates $500,000 in FY2025-26 and $500,000 in FY2026-27 from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program 514.
