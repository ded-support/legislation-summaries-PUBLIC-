Summary

LB360 (2025) limits homeowner association regulation of political signs by specifying categories of signs that associations may prohibit or remove: signs larger than 4'×6', signs with language or graphics judged offensive to an ordinary person, signs accompanied by music/sounds or streamers or otherwise distracting to motorists, and signs that violate any law. Associations may remove signs that violate restrictive covenants not preempted by the bill. No state agencies or departments are referenced, and no funding or appropriations changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds four prohibitions on signs: larger than 4×6 feet; containing language/graphics offensive to an ordinary person; accompanied by music/sounds, streamers, or otherwise distracting to motorists; or that violate any law. Also explicitly permits a homeowner association to remove signs that violate restrictive covenants not barred by this section. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are affected.
