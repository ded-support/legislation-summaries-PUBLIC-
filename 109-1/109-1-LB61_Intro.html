Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services to take multiple actions and pursue federal approvals, including deadlines: submit a HCBS waiver amendment to add memory‑care rates for the aged and disabled (application due by Sept. 30, 2025); submit a Medicaid waiver/state plan by Oct. 1, 2025, to designate two medical‑respite facilities and report annually to the Legislature; seek federal approval to extend postpartum coverage; provide coverage for continuous glucose monitors (per prior statutory deadlines); provide electric personal‑use breast pumps and up to ten lactation consultation visits; and cover translation/interpretation services and maximize federal matching funds. Funding: intent language proposes General Fund and federal appropriations (placeholders) for memory‑care daily rates for FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27; lactation consultations funded at a 145% increase over current departmental rates; and multiple services funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to apply to CMS by September 30, 2025 to amend the aged-and-disabled home-and-community‑based services (HCBS) waiver to add a memory‑care rate for assisted‑living facilities. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate unspecified amounts (placeholders) from the General Fund and matching federal funds for FY 2025‑26 and FY 2026‑27 to pay those memory‑care rates. The appropriated funds are to be used by the department to pay a specified daily memory‑care rate for assisted‑living facilities under the HCBS waiver (rate and dollar amounts are not provided in the text).
