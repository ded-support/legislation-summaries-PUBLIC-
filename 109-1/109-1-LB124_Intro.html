Summary

LB124 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-394 (Homicide of the Unborn Child Act) to modify penalties for motor vehicle homicide of an unborn child. It (1) retains a default Class I misdemeanor for unintentional vehicular deaths of an unborn child but (2) elevates offenses to felonies when committed in violation of specified motor-vehicle statutes: violations of §60-6,213 or §60-6,214 become Class IIIA felonies; violations of §60-6,196 or §60-6,197.06 become Class IIA felonies, and become Class II felonies if the defendant has a prior §60-6,196-related conviction. As part of conviction, the court must impose a driving prohibition and revoke the operator’s license for 60 days up to 15 years (revocation not concurrent with any jail term). The offense is treated as separate from other related offenses. No funding or appropriations are included.
