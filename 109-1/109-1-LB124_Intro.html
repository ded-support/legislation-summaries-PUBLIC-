Summary

LB124 (Holdcroft), referred to the Judiciary Committee, amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-394 (Homicide of the Unborn Child Act) to revise penalties for motor vehicle homicide of an unborn child. The bill: makes the offense a Class I misdemeanor generally; raises it to a Class IIIA felony if caused by violation of §§60-6,213 or 60-6,214; raises it to a Class IIA felony for violations of §§60-6,196 or 60-6,197.06 (and to a Class II felony if the defendant has a prior §60-6,196 conviction). As part of conviction, the court must impose a driving prohibition and revoke the operator’s license for 60 days to 15 years (revocation not concurrent with any jail term). The offense remains a separate and distinct crime. No appropriations or funding changes are included.
