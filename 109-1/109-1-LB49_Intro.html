Summary

LB49 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-405 to subject school districts' general authority to restrict access to school property and activities to new limits in the bill. It defines "ban and bar action" (prohibiting individuals from campus, events, or communication) and makes violations enforceable under criminal trespass statutes (sections 28-520 to 28-522). The State Board of Education must develop and distribute a model ban-and-bar policy by January 1, 2026, specifying that such actions may be used only for immediate, significant threats or to enforce court orders and must be limited in scope. The bill references school districts and school boards; no funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
