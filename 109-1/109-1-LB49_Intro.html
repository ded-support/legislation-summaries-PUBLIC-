Summary

LB49 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-405 to clarify limits on school districts’ authority to restrict access to school property and activities. It defines “ban and bar action” and provides that such actions may be enforced as criminal trespass under §§28-520 to 28-522. The State Board of Education is directed to develop and distribute a model ban-and-bar policy by January 1, 2026. The policy must require that bans and bars be used only for immediate, significant threats to safety or to enforce court orders and that actions be limited in scope. The bill affects school districts and school boards; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
