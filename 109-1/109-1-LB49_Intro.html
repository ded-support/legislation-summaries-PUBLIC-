Summary

LB49 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-405 to condition a school district’s authority to restrict access to school property and activities on limitations set forth in the bill. It defines "ban and bar action" and requires the State Board of Education to develop and distribute a model ban-and-bar policy to school districts by January 1, 2026. The model must limit use of bans and bars to responses to immediate, significant threats or to facilitate enforcement of court orders and allows enforcement as criminal trespass under §§28-520–28-522. The bill references school boards and school districts; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the State Board of Education to develop and distribute a model "ban and bar" policy by January 1, 2026. Adds definitions for "ban and bar action," "Board" (State Board of Education), "school board," and "school district" (cross‑referencing section 79‑101). The model policy must limit ban-and-bar use to immediate, significant threats to persons or to enforce court orders and restrict its scope. Enforcement may be as criminal trespass under sections 28‑520 to 28‑522. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
