Summary

LB165 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§28-441 and 28-442 to authorize syringe services programs and to create narrow exceptions to drug‑paraphernalia penalties under the Uniform Controlled Substances Act. The exceptions cover program operators, staff, volunteers, and participants engaged in retrieval or disposal of syringes, needles, or related equipment at or immediately adjacent to program sites. The bill retains a Class II misdemeanor penalty for violations of the amended section. No state agency or department is named in the provided text, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation provisions. The bill also repeals the original versions of the amended sections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a program participant is covered (i.e., treated as part of the program) when the participant is retrieving or disposing of syringes, needles, or related equipment at or immediately around the program site.
