Summary

- Department of Correctional Services (DOCS)

- Courts may commit an offender to DOCS for a complete institutional study to inform sentencing (sec. 29-2204.05/29-2204 discussion).

- The bill clarifies that a "staff secure juvenile facility" expressly excludes any institution operated by DOCS (sec. 43-245 definition).

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

- Peace officers may deliver juveniles taken into temporary custody to DHHS, which must place the juvenile in the least restrictive temporary setting, supervise placement, and may consent to emergency medical, psychological, or psychiatric treatment; DHHS's temporary custody ends if no court order is entered within 48 hours (sec. 43-250 subsections).

- For juveniles needing mental-health evaluation, officers may deliver them to DHHS (with required certification and notification to county attorney and family) (sec. 43-250).

- DHHS, jointly with the Office of Probation Administration, shall use, oversee, and may reimburse day-reporting and evening-reporting centers for juveniles and may adopt related rules (sec. 12).

No specific appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Correctional Services: Adds a sentencing cap for young adults—any person convicted of a Class IB felony when under 22 now faces a maximum of 60 years and a minimum of 20 years imprisonment—potentially reducing exposure to longer incarceration terms. No agency-specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Department of Health and Human Services: Requires DHHS (with the Office of Probation Administration) to use day‑reporting and evening‑reporting centers for juveniles as appropriate, to adopt rules for selecting/overseeing those centers, and to reimburse them (rulemaking and reimbursement authority granted). Prohibits transferring adjudicated juveniles out of state except for emergency medical or mental‑health treatment and reinforces confidentiality obligations for juvenile information. No explicit funding amounts or new appropriations are specified.
