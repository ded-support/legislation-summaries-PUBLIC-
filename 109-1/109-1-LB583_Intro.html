Summary

LB583 assigns responsibilities and funding flows among named agencies:

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): will administer the Child Care Grant Fund and award grants (up to $15,000; one award per recipient every two years) to licensed child‑care providers, community organizations, or schools; DHHS may contract with a statewide organization to manage applications, review, and technical assistance. DHHS also holds the Medicaid Fraud Settlement Fund from which 25% of the state portion (and separately 5% per the documentary stamp tax) is directed to the Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund for distribution to federally qualified health centers.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: will administer the Military Installation Development and Support Fund (funded in part via section 76-903 revenues), with at least $100,000/year to the department for a statewide SkillBridge strategy and at least $100,000/year to veteran mental‑health community providers; project expenditures require a public/private match ≥50%.

- Department of Economic Development: will administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund (receives funds from section 76-903) and may prioritize Economic Recovery Contingency Fund projects per the 2023 coordination plan.

- Department of Revenue: the Tax Commissioner (Dept. of Revenue) designs documentary stamps and may issue declaratory rulings; the department may not overturn county assessor sale‑qualification decisions without its own review.

- Military Department: included in the statutory definition of "military installation."

- Documentary stamp tax allocations (per $3.30 collected): $1.05 Affordable Housing; $0.25 Site & Building; $0.25 Homeless Shelter; $0.40 Behavioral Health; $0.05 Economic Recovery Contingency (for a grant‑coordination office); $0.05 Military Installation Development and Support; $0.05 Innovation Hub Cash Fund; $0.05 Health Care Homes; $0.40 Child Care Grant Fund; county retention $0.75.

- Economic Recovery Contingency Fund transfers: specified transfers to the Museum Construction and Maintenance Fund ($750,000 in 2024; $15,000,000 in 2025; $7,000,000 in early 2026) and a $30,000,000 transfer to the Inland Port Authority Fund (and transfer of unobligated balances thereon July 31, 2026).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Economic Development: New allocation (5 cents per $1.05 transferred under section 76-903) funds an Economic Recovery Contingency Fund to establish/operate a statewide office to pursue and coordinate grant funding; Innovation Hub Cash Fund receives 5 cents per $1.05 for operational support of innovation hubs.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Child Care Grant Fund expanded to fund licensed child-care providers for staff retention/recruitment incentives and capital expenses; grants awarded by need, administered via a contracted statewide organization providing application processing and technical assistance. Behavioral Health Services Fund receives 40 cents per $1.05; Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund also receives transfers.

- Department of Revenue: No direct program changes noted; role limited to transfers under section 76-903.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: Receives at least $100,000/year to contract with community organizations to create a statewide SkillBridge strategy/program to attract veterans.

- Military Department: Military Installation Development and Support Fund receives 5 cents per $1.05 to support community-based veteran services (per subsection 55-901).
