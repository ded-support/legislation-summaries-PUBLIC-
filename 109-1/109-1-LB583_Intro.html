Summary

This bill directs specific administrative roles and funding flows for the listed agencies:

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): administers the Child Care Grant Fund (money from section 76-903 and 43-2623); grants up to $15,000, once per two-year period, with DHHS contracting a statewide organization for application review and technical assistance. DHHS’s Medicaid Fraud Settlement Fund must transfer funds to the Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund (25% of the state portion, plus an additional 5% for services under 330(h)/(i)); that fund also receives money from section 76-903.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: administers the Military Installation Development and Support Fund (funded in part via section 76-903); at least $100,000/year to the Department for a statewide skillbridge program and $100,000/year to veteran mental-health providers; expenditures require a public/private match ≥50%. "Military installation" includes facilities under the Nebraska Military Department.

- Department of Economic Development: administers the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and the Innovation Hub Cash Fund (both receive monies under section 76-903); Affordable Housing allocations specify $0.05 to emerging developers and $0.20 to special-population/innovation programs per $1.05 transferred. The department may prioritize projects from the Economic Recovery Contingency Fund.

- Department of Revenue/Tax Commissioner: Tax Commissioner designs documentary stamp tax stamps; register of deeds collects tax; the Department of Revenue may issue declaratory rulings and shall not overturn county assessor sale determinations absent department review.

- Documentary stamp tax allocations: from each $3.30 collected (register keeps $0.75), the remainder is credited: $1.05 to Affordable Housing Trust Fund; $0.25 Site & Building Development; $0.25 Homeless Shelter Assistance; $0.40 Behavioral Health Services; $0.05 Economic Recovery Contingency; $0.05 Military Installation Development and Support; $0.05 Innovation Hub Cash Fund; $0.05 Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved; $0.40 Child Care Grant Fund.

- Economic Recovery Contingency Fund transfers: State Treasurer directed to transfer $30,000,000 to the Inland Port Authority Fund (no later than five business days after April 17, 2024); $750,000 (by Dec 31, 2024), $15,000,000 (by Dec 31, 2025), and $7,000,000 (Jan–Jun 2026) to the Museum Construction and Maintenance Fund; any unobligated fund balance on July 31, 2026, transfers to the Inland Port Authority Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Child Care Grant Fund receives a large allocation (40 cents per $1.05 transferred under §76‑903) to fund licensed child‑care providers for staffing retention/recruitment incentives and capital expenses; grants sized by demonstrated need and administered as (two‑year) awards. DHHS must contract with a statewide organization to run applications, review awards, and provide technical assistance. Behavioral Health Services Fund also receives 40 cents and the Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund receives 5 cents per $1.05.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA): At least $100,000/year is appropriated to DVA to contract with community organizations to create a statewide SkillBridge strategy/program to attract veterans; another $100,000/year must go to community providers of veterans' mental‑health services.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): Innovation Hub Cash Fund receives 5 cents per $1.05 for operations; 5 cents per $1.05 is directed to an Economic Recovery Contingency Fund to establish/operate an office to pursue/coordinate grant funding (administration implied).

- Military Department: Military Installation Development and Support Fund receives 5 cents per $1.05 to support community‑based veteran services as described in §55‑901.

- Department of Revenue: No explicit programmatic or appropriation changes to the Department of Revenue are specified; funds are transferred under §76‑903.
