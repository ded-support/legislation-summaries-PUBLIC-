Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): DHHS will administer the Child Care Grant Fund (funded in part by section 76-903) and award grants (up to $15,000) to licensed child care providers, community organizations, or schools; DHHS also houses the Medicaid Fraud Settlement Fund and 25% (and a 5% category) of the state portion is transferred to the Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund, which the bill also makes eligible to receive documentary-stamp-tax receipts under section 76-903.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) / Military Department: DVA will administer the Military Installation Development and Support Fund (receiving section 76-903 receipts), must require at least a 50% match for projects, and ensure at least $100,000/year to DVA for a statewide skillbridge strategy and $100,000/year to community providers of veteran mental-health services. "Military installation" explicitly includes facilities under the Nebraska Military Department.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): DED will administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund (receiving section 76-903 receipts) and may prioritize projects funded from the Economic Recovery Contingency Fund.

- Department of Revenue: Tax Commissioner/Department of Revenue will design documentary-stamp tax stamps and may issue declaratory rulings; the department may not overturn a county assessor's sale qualification without review confirming an error.

- Funding allocations from the $3.30 documentary-stamp tax (per section 76-903): $1.05 to Affordable Housing Trust Fund; $0.25 to Site and Building Development Fund; $0.25 to Homeless Shelter Assistance Trust Fund; $0.40 to Behavioral Health Services Fund; $0.05 to Economic Recovery Contingency Fund; $0.05 to Military Installation Development and Support Fund; $0.05 to Innovation Hub Cash Fund; $0.05 to Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund; $0.40 to Child Care Grant Fund.

- Economic Recovery Contingency Fund transfers (timed): $30 million to Inland Port Authority Fund (by five business days after April 17, 2024); $750,000 to Museum Construction and Maintenance Fund (by Dec 31, 2024); $15 million (by Dec 31, 2025); $7 million (Jan–Jun 2026); remaining unobligated funds on July 31, 2026, to Inland Port Authority Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: Child Care Grant Fund expanded to fund licensed child-care providers for staff retention/recruitment incentives and capital expenses; DHHS must contract with a statewide organization to run application, review and technical assistance. Behavioral Health Services Fund and Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund also receive dedicated transfers.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: receives at least $100,000/year to contract with community organizations to create a statewide SkillBridge/attraction program for veterans; an additional ≥$100,000/year is directed to veteran mental‑health services via community organizations.

- Military Department / Military Installation Development and Support Fund: receives a dedicated allocation (0.05 per dollar) to support community organizations serving veterans per subsection (2) of §55‑901.

- Department of Revenue: identified as the source of transfers (sec. 76‑903 and §43‑2623) that allocate specified per‑dollar shares among multiple funds.

- Department of Economic Development: a 0.05 per‑dollar allocation is directed to an Economic Recovery Contingency Fund to establish/operate an office to pursue and coordinate grant funding on behalf of the state (funding allocation noted; administering agency not specified).
