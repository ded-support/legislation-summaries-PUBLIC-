Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Administers the Child Care Grant Fund (funded via section 76-903) and will award grants (up to $15,000) to licensed child care providers, community‑based organizations, or schools; DHHS also receives and distributes transfers from the Medicaid Fraud Settlement Fund—25% (and a separate 5% allocation for services under 330(h)/(i))—to the Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund, which also receives revenue from section 76-903.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs and Military Department: The Military Installation Development and Support Fund is administered by the Department of Veterans' Affairs (funded via section 76-903); it requires matching funds (≥50%), may fund installation-support projects, and defines "military installation" to include activities under the Nebraska Military Department. At least $100,000/year allocations for a statewide SkillBridge program and $100,000/year for veteran mental‑health community providers are specified.

- Department of Economic Development: Administers the Affordable Housing Trust Fund (receives section 76-903 revenue) and may prioritize Economic Recovery Contingency Fund projects; the Affordable Housing Trust Fund must allocate, from each $1.05 received, at least $0.05 to emerging developers and $0.20 to special population/innovation housing.

- Department of Revenue: Tax Commissioner/stamps administration retained; Department may issue declaratory rulings. The documentary stamp tax ($3.30 per $1,000) allocations from section 76-903 are specified: $1.05 to Affordable Housing, $0.25 Site & Building, $0.25 Homeless Shelter, $0.40 Behavioral Health, $0.05 Economic Recovery, $0.05 Military Installation, $0.05 Innovation Hub, $0.05 Health Care Homes, and $0.40 Child Care Grant Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions (by agency):

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Establishes a Child Care Grant Fund (receives 40¢ per $1 from sec. 76‑903) to award need‑based grants to licensed child‑care centers and at‑home providers for staff retention/recruitment incentives and capital expenses. DHHS must contract with a statewide organization to manage applications, review, and provide technical assistance. Behavioral Health Services Fund also receives 40¢ per $1; Health Care Homes for the Medically Underserved Fund receives 5¢ per $1.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA): Receives at least $100,000/year to contract with community organizations to create a statewide SkillBridge/veteran attraction program; at least $100,000/year is directed to community providers of veteran mental‑health services.

- Military Department: Military Installation Development and Support Fund receives 5¢ per $1 to support community‑based veteran services (per sec. 55‑901).

- Department of Economic Development: Innovation Hub Cash Fund receives 5¢ per $1 for operations; 5¢ per $1 to an Economic Recovery Contingency Fund to establish/operate an office to pursue and coordinate grant funding statewide.

- Department of Revenue: Implements transfers under sec. 76‑903 according to the new per‑dollar distribution schedule; from each $1.05 transferred at least 5¢ must go to emerging developers and at least 20¢ to special‑population/innovation housing programs.
