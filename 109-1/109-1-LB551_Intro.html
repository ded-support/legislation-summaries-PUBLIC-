Summary

LB551 amends section 85-1511 to require community college boards to file two annual audits with the Auditor of Public Accounts, the Coordinating Commission, and the Department of Administrative Services: (1) a comprehensive fiscal-year audit of books, accounts, all revenue sources, and the county-certified property valuation — filed by November 15; and (2) a comprehensive audit of full-time-equivalent student enrollment and reimbursable educational units (including three most-recent years’ verified totals used for aid calculations) — filed by August 15. The bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text bars the Board of Regents, the Nebraska State Colleges Board of Trustees, and community college boards from creating or authorizing new academic tenure for anyone not tenured before the act’s effective date. Each board must adopt written policies on employee agreements; acceptable dismissal grounds (including just cause, program discontinuance, financial exigency); annual faculty evaluations; minimum standards of practice; standards for review/discipline; and dismissal procedures. The Department of Administrative Services is not referenced and there are no appropriations, funding, or budget changes affecting DAS in the added language.
