Summary

LB551 requires community college areas to file two annual audits with the Department of Administrative Services (in addition to the Auditor of Public Accounts and the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education): (1) a comprehensive audit of books, accounts, records and affairs covering the most recently completed fiscal year, including all revenue sources used for operations and capital improvements and the county‑certified property valuation—filed by November 15; and (2) a comprehensive audit of full‑time equivalent student enrollment and reimbursable educational unit totals for the most recently completed fiscal year, including a three‑year verified report used for aid calculations—filed by August 15. The bill contains no explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions prohibit the Board of Regents, the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges, and community college boards from creating or authorizing any new academic tenure system for employees who are not already tenured as of the act’s effective date. Each board must adopt written policies covering employee agreements; acceptable grounds for dismissal (explicitly including just cause, program discontinuance, and financial exigency); annual faculty performance evaluations; minimum standards of faculty practice; standards for review and discipline; and procedures for dismissal for just cause, program discontinuance, and financial exigency. There is no mention of the Department of Administrative Services, nor are there any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
