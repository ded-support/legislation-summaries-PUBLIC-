Summary

LB85 amends procedures for transferring certificates of title involving decedents’ estates. It requires persons seeking transfer of a title for a motor vehicle, motorboat, all‑terrain vehicle, utility‑type vehicle, or minibike to furnish the Department of Motor Vehicles an affidavit demonstrating applicability of the estate‑affidavit process and compliance with its requirements to obtain a new certificate of title. After ten years from estate closing, the DMV must waive the requirement to show that no personal‑representative application is pending (subdivision (a)(5)) if the applicant provides evidence the estate closed and a certified authenticated death certificate. No funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language allowing an "abstract of death" (as defined in §71-601.01) to be used when a certified death certificate is delayed. For the Department of Motor Vehicles, this means the DMV may accept an abstract of death as alternative proof of death for title, registration, ownership transfers, or other death‑related transactions. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
