Summary

LB85 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §30-24,125 to address title transfers handled by the Department of Motor Vehicles. It requires persons seeking transfer of a certificate of title for a motor vehicle, motorboat, all-terrain vehicle, utility-type vehicle, or minibike to furnish the DMV an affidavit showing applicability and compliance with the statute to obtain a new certificate of title. If ten years have elapsed since an estate closed, the DMV must waive the §30-24,125(a)(5) requirement (showing no pending or granted personal‑representative appointment) when presented with evidence the estate closed and a certified, authenticated copy of the decedent’s death certificate. No funding, appropriation, or budget provisions are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language allowing, when a certified death certificate is delayed, the use of an "abstract of death" (as defined in §71‑601.01). For the Department of Motor Vehicles, this means an abstract of death can serve as interim proof of death for DMV transactions that require death documentation (e.g., title or registration matters). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
