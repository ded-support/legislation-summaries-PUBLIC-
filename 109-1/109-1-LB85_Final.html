Summary

LB85 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §30-24,125 to add DMV-specific requirements for title transfers after a decedent's death. Persons seeking transfer of a certificate of title for a motor vehicle, motorboat, all-terrain vehicle, utility-type vehicle, or minibike must furnish the Department of Motor Vehicles an affidavit demonstrating applicability and compliance with the section to authorize issuance of a new certificate of title. Ten years after an estate has closed, the DMV must waive the subsection (a)(5) requirement (that no application or petition for appointment of a personal representative is pending) if the applicant provides evidence the estate closed and a certified authenticated copy of the decedent’s death certificate. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a clause allowing, when a certified death certificate is delayed, the use of an "abstract of death" (per section 71-601.01). For the Department of Motor Vehicles this permits accepting the abstract in lieu of a certified death certificate for routine DMV actions (e.g., updating or cancelling registrations, titles, or licenses tied to a decedent). No language in the added text creates or changes any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the DMV.
