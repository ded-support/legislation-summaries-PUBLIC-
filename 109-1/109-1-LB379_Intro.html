Summary

LB379 amends section 68-1724 (Welfare Reform Act). It specifies cash assistance for recipient families with children is limited to a total of twenty-four months, with the time limit extended if the state fails to meet terms of the self‑sufficiency contract. The bill requires the department to develop policy guidelines to permit additional cash assistance for families facing extreme hardship and to revise self‑sufficiency contracts and extend assistance when no job is available. It establishes cost‑shared, work‑related child care assistance up to 200% of the federal poverty level prior to October 1, 2026, and 185% thereafter, with family contributions up to 20% of gross income. No specific appropriations, new funding amounts, or budget allocations are included.
