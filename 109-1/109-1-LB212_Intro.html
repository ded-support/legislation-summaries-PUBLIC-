Summary

LB212 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-4008 (Tobacco Products Tax Act). It sets tax liability on the first owner (and sellers when both parties are licensed under §77-4009) and prescribes rates: cigars/cheroots/stogies — 20% of purchase or manufacturer price, capped at $0.50 each; snuff — $0.44 per ounce; electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) ≤3 mL — $0.05 per mL; ENDS >3 mL — 10% of purchase or manufacturer price; all other tobacco products — 20% of purchase or manufacturer price. For retail dealers, tax is triggered when ENDS are brought into the state, manufactured in-state for sale, or sold to consumers. Collections are to be used and distributed pursuant to §77-4025. No specific agency is named, and the bill contains no new appropriations or explicit budget allocations. Operative October 1, 2025; original §77-4008 repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a cigar tax provision: cigars, cheroots, and stogies are taxed at 20% of (a) the purchase price paid by the first owner or (b) the price at which a first owner who made/manufactured/fabricated them sells them, with a maximum tax of $0.50 per cigar. No state agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced or changed.
