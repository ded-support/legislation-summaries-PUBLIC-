Summary

LB212 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-4008 (Tobacco Products Tax Act). Key changes: sets the tax on cigars, cheroots, and stogies at 20% of the first‑owner purchase or sale price with a $0.50 maximum per cigar; retains snuff at $0.44 per ounce; taxes electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) ≤3 mL at $0.05 per mL and ENDS >3 mL at 10% of the first‑owner purchase or sale price; taxes other tobacco products at 20% of purchase or sale price. For retail dealers, tax timing for untaxed ENDS is clarified. When a licensed purchaser buys from another licensed seller (per §77‑4009), the seller is liable for the tax. Revenues continue to be distributed pursuant to §77‑4025. Act operative October 1, 2025; original section repealed. No direct appropriations or agency budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a 20% excise tax on cigars, cheroots, and stogies, calculated on either (i) the purchase price paid by the first owner or (ii) the price at which a first owner who made/manufactured/fabricated them sells them, with a per-item cap of $0.50. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no appropriations, funding, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
