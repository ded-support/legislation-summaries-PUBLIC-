Summary

LB150 makes extensive changes affecting multiple state agencies:

- Department of Correctional Services (DOCS): abolishes the Division of Parole Supervision and the Director of Supervision and Services, vests parole powers in DOCS and the Director of Correctional Services, expands DOCS duties (facility administration, parole supervision, risk‑and‑needs assessments, performance metrics, lifetime community supervision, pilot residential program), requires DOCS collaboration with probation and the Board of Parole, mandates twice‑annual reviews and reporting, grants electronic record access to Public Counsel/Inspector General (medical/mental‑health records subject to consent), creates a Parole Program Cash Fund funded by a $25 monthly parole programming fee, and directs DOCS to allow private providers to bid on community work release/reentry centers (Legislature intends a $1,000,000 General Fund appropriation).

- Department of Banking and Finance: requires financial institutions to file garnishment service‑of‑process designations with the Department, which posts and updates a public list; Department granted immunity for acts/omissions under this duty.

- Department of Health and Human Services: prescribed forms and access for emergency evaluations, authority over certain paternity/medical testing and limited exemptions for overdose‑review records; mental‑health board must notify DHHS and State Patrol when firearm‑related disabilities are removed.

- Department of Revenue: employees under section 77‑366 excluded from the statute's "law enforcement officer" definition.

- Department of Transportation: directional sign eligibility tied to assurance of compliance with anti‑discrimination laws.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Creates the Regional Mental Health Expansion Pilot Program administered by the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice to fund a single county law-enforcement agency to add mental‑health beds regionally; the commission will set grant eligibility/audit rules, identify grant funding, and seek federal funding (no specific appropriations or amounts included). It may permit temporary admission to a jail or Department of Correctional Services facility with an available mental‑health bed until a medical facility is available and explicitly allows mental‑health board hearings by videoconference.

- Department of Banking and Finance: adds/clarifies corporate‑entity definitions and procedures for service of garnishment summons on corporate entities (service methods and copy‑sending rules); proof of compliance not required to continue garnishment.

- Department of Health and Human Services: affected functionally by pilot expansion of mental‑health bed capacity but no direct statutory duties or budget appropriations added.

- Departments of Revenue and Transportation: no changes, funding, or duties added.
