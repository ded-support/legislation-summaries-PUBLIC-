Summary

LB150 centralizes and expands correctional and reentry authority in the Department of Correctional Services (DCS): it abolishes the Division of Parole Supervision, transfers parole powers to DCS/the Director, requires DCS to administer parole, lifetime community supervision, electronic offender records, individualized reentry plans, validated risk‑and‑needs assessments, performance metrics, and a pilot residential program for technical parole violations, and to collaborate on community correctional facilities and the Community Work Release and Reentry Centers Act (including contracting with private providers). Fiscal changes: parolees pay a $25 monthly parole programming fee credited to a new Parole Program Cash Fund for DCS; the Legislature intends a $1,000,000 General Fund appropriation to implement the reentry centers act. Department of Banking and Finance: financial institutions must file service‑of‑process/designation info for web publication; department/agents granted immunity. DHHS: authority over genetic testing/audit reporting in paternity cases; prescribe emergency protective‑custody certificate; maintain/update firearm‑disability/treatment commitment data (including NICS updates); confidentiality limits in overdose fatality reviews. Department of Transportation: tourist‑oriented sign applicants must assure nondiscrimination; violations bar eligibility. Department of Revenue: employees excluded from the law‑enforcement‑officer definition.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Banking and Finance: Adds/clarifies "corporate entity" definition for garnishment service, excludes certain financial institutions, and sets procedures for serving garnishment summons on corporate entities (service per §25-509.01 or by mutual agreement; requirement to send a copy to the registered agent if service is elsewhere; proof not required to continue garnishment).

Department of Correctional Services: Allows use of jails or Department of Correctional Services facilities as temporary placement under the Regional Mental Health Expansion Pilot Program and references DOC facilities in mental-health hearing and admission language; permits videoconferenced mental-health board hearings involving such facilities. No budget changes specified.

Department of Health and Human Services: Pilot expands regional mental-health bed capacity administered via the Commission; mental-health board procedural changes (videoconferencing); amendments to child welfare sections apply to pending and new cases. No direct appropriations.

Department of Revenue: No substantive programmatic or funding changes; garnishment service procedural edits may affect wage-garnishment processes.

Department of Transportation: No changes or funding provisions.
