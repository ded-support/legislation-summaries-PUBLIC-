Summary

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Multiple appropriations and authorizations for FY2023-24 and FY2024-25, including Medical Assistance (Program 348) totals of $3,564,427,540 (FY23-24: GF $1,000,583,595; CF $133,894,141; FF $2,429,949,804) and $3,170,728,603 (FY24-25: GF $1,059,760,230; CF $72,117,244; FF $2,038,851,129). LB1087A funds for Program 348 and authority to certify/unexpended-balance transfers to Developmental Disability Aid are retained; DHHS must provide specified reports.

Department of Revenue: Homestead Exemption (Program 108) appropriation of $2,000,000 General Fund for FY2024-25 (state aid).

Department of Administrative Services (DAS): $100,000 Cash Fund for State Insurance (FY24-25); DAS must draw warrants and monitor Program 348, the budget administrator may administratively increase salary/per diem limits and execute transfers between DHHS programs as certified.
