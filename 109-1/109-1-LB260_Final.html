Summary

This bill gives multiple provisions affecting the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and Department of Revenue:

- DAS: authority to monitor Program 348 appropriations; Director of Administrative Services to draw warrants; budget division administrator may increase salary‑limit for accrued leave payouts and administratively transfer DHHS‑certified unexpended Medical Assistance (Program 348) appropriations to Developmental Disability Aid (Program 424). DAS budget division directs a State Treasurer transfer schedule. DAS Program 594 (State Insurance) receives $100,000 Cash Funds (FY2024‑25).

- DHHS: Medical Assistance (Program 348) totals FY2023‑24 — $1,000,583,595 GF; $133,894,141 Cash; $2,429,949,804 FF (total $3,564,427,540). FY2024‑25 — $1,059,760,230 GF; $72,117,244 Cash; $2,038,851,129 FF (total $3,170,728,603). FY2024‑25 additions: pharmacy dispensing fees and dental rate increases (specified GF/FF amounts), $450,000 Cash/year for smoking cessation, mandated nursing facility rate totals, reporting requirements. Other DHHS appropriations: Children's Health Insurance Program No.344 — $6M GF/$3M Cash/$14.3M FF (FY2024‑25); Child Welfare Aid No.354 — $15M GF (FY2024‑25); Mental Health Operations No.365 — $4.5M GF (FY2024‑25). Under LB1087A, Program 348 also receives large HQA and federal allocations (e.g., FY2024‑25: $617.5M HQA Fund, $15M Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund, $1.45B federal).

- Department of Revenue: Homestead Exemption (Program 108) — $2,000,000 General Fund (FY2024‑25) for state aid.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services (DAS)

- Authorizes the budget administrator to administratively increase limits on permanent/temporary salary and per diem expenditures to cover certified payments (e.g., accumulated vacation/sick leave) upon agency director request.

- Affirms the Director of Administrative Services draws warrants against funds up to appropriations.

- Appropriates $100,000 Cash Fund for FY2024‑25 to Program No. 594 (State Insurance).

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

- FY2024‑25 appropriations: Children's Health Insurance — $6,000,000 GF, $3,000,000 Cash, $14,300,000 Federal (total $23,300,000) for state aid only.

- Child Welfare Aid — $15,000,000 GF for state aid only (FY2024‑25).

- Juvenile Services Operations — salary limit set at $500,000 (FY2024‑25).

- Mental Health Operations — $4,500,000 GF appropriation and a $6,300,000 salary limit (FY2024‑25).

Department of Revenue

- Homestead Exemption (Program No. 108) receives $2,000,000 General Fund in FY2024‑25 for state aid only.
