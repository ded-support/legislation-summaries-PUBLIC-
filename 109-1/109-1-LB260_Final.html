Summary

LB260 contains multiple provisions referencing the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and Department of Revenue (DOR):

- Department of Revenue: Program 108 (Homestead Exemption) — $2,000,000 General Fund state aid for FY2024‑25.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Program 348 (Medical Assistance) — FY2023‑24: GF $1,000,583,595; CF $133,894,141; FF est. $2,429,949,804 (total $3,564,427,540). FY2024‑25: GF $1,059,760,230; CF $72,117,244; FF est. $2,038,851,129 (total $3,170,728,603). Additional DHHS appropriations include Children's Health Insurance (FY24‑25: GF $6,000,000; CF $3,000,000; FF $14,300,000), Child Welfare Aid (GF $15,000,000), Mental Health Operations (GF $4,500,000), Public Assistance (FY24‑25 GF $61,018,454; CF $5,360,000; FF $152,207,325) and TANF $10,000,000 for food-distribution grants. LB1087A directs hundreds of millions (approx. $617.5–$632.5M CF) plus $1.45B federal funds to DHHS Program 348.

- Department of Administrative Services: DAS shall monitor DHHS Program 348 appropriations/expenditures; the budget administrator may administratively increase salary expenditure limits in defined circumstances and effect transfers certified by DHHS (Medical Assistance → Developmental Disability Aid). DAS Director draws warrants; Program 594 (State Insurance) receives $100,000 Cash Fund for FY2024‑25.
