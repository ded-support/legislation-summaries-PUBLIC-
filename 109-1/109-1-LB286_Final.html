Summary

LB286 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§81-12,110 and 81-12,208 and repeals the originals. It requires the department (and its director) to accept iHub applications under the Nebraska Innovation Hub Act only through December 31, 2025, and imposes a $1,000 iHub application fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Innovation Hub Cash Fund. The bill also bars new applications under the Nebraska Rural Projects Act after December 31, 2025, while allowing consideration and agreements for complete applications filed on or before that date; a $1,000 Rural Projects application fee is remitted to the Nebraska Rural Projects Fund. The department must adopt evaluation/scoring guidelines, set approval procedures, require annual iHub reporting to the director and Urban Affairs Committee, and post reports online.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Makes the director responsible for continuing to accept applications under this section through December 31, 2025. No specific state agency or department is named beyond "the director," and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
