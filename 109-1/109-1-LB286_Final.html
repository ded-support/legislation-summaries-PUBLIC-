Summary

LB286 amends two statutes to impose application deadlines and clarifies reporting/fee handling. It directs the department (and its director) to accept Nebraska Innovation Hub (iHub) applications only through December 31, 2025, and requires the director to determine iHub designations and enter MOUs with approved applicants. It also bars new applications under the Nebraska Rural Projects Act after December 31, 2025, while allowing consideration and execution of any complete applications filed on or before that date. Application fees ($1,000 each) are collected by the director and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Innovation Hub Cash Fund and the Nebraska Rural Projects Fund. Annual iHub reports are filed with the director, the chairperson of the Urban Affairs Committee, and posted with notice to the Governor and Legislature. Original sections are repealed.
