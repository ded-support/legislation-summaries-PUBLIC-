Summary

LB419 amends veterans law and affects the Department of Veterans’ Affairs and related officers. It expands eligibility for Nebraska veterans homes to include Nebraska National Guard service, explicitly including active duty for training, and clarifies surviving spouses/parents must meet two‑year Nebraska residency and be unable to earn a livelihood. The Director of Veterans’ Affairs (or deputy/designee) retains authority to receive, approve or disapprove Nebraska Veterans’ Aid Fund applications originating from local post service officers, county veterans service officers, state service officers, or recognized veterans organizations, with a one‑claim/30‑day limit (funeral exception). The bill reiterates that staffing and state service office expenses require specific legislative appropriation and may not come from the $12 million trust or its interest; trust principal reverts to the State Treasury on completion.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds eligibility language covering veterans (including those on active duty for training) who need long-term care at a Nebraska veterans home. Requires applications be filed through a service officer or a state service officer. Limits applicants to one claim per 30-day period, with an exception allowing an additional claim for funeral expenses. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
