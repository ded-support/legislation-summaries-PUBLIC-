Summary

LB419 amends statutes governing Nebraska veterans services. Key agencies and offices affected:

- Department (Veterans' homes): continues to provide long‑term care at Nebraska veterans homes and clarifies eligible service (including Nebraska National Guard active duty and training).

- Office of the Director of Veterans' Affairs: retains responsibility for administering income from the Nebraska Veterans' Aid Fund and for approving or denying aid applications.

- County veterans service officers, state service officers, recognized veterans organizations and the state service office are explicitly identified as authorized originators/representatives for aid applications.

- State Treasurer and Legislature: existing fiscal controls remain—expenditures for assistants, clerical staff, and the state service office require specific legislative appropriation; interest from the Aid Fund cannot be used to organize/maintain veterans organizations; the $12 million trust and its income remain protected, with principal reverting to the state treasury upon trust completion.

No new appropriations or dollar allocations are established in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds eligibility language tying long-term care to availability at one of the Nebraska veterans homes and explicitly includes veterans who performed active duty for training. Requires applicants to work with a "service officer" or a "state service officer" when filing. Limits claims to one per 30-day period, with an exception allowing an additional application for funeral expenses. No agencies beyond the Nebraska veterans homes and service/state service officers are named, and no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are altered.
