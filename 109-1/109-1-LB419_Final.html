Summary

LB419 amends two veterans statutes. It expands Nebraska veterans homes eligibility (Dept. of Veterans’ Affairs) to include persons who served on active duty in the Nebraska National Guard, including active duty for training, and retains residency and care-need requirements; it also specifies surviving spouses/parents qualify if Nebraska residents for two years and unable to earn a livelihood.

It clarifies administration of the Nebraska Veterans' Aid Fund: the Director of Veterans' Affairs (or deputy/designee) approves or denies aid applications that may originate with local post service officers of recognized veterans organizations, county veterans service officers, or state service officers. The bill authorizes expenditures only as specifically appropriated by the Legislature for staff and a state service office, prohibits using interest accumulations for veterans organizations, and bars payments from the referenced $12 million trust principal.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds eligibility and application procedures referencing Nebraska veterans homes and service officers. Specifically: eligibility now covers those (including active duty for training) who need long‑term care available at one of the Nebraska veterans homes. Applicants must submit claims through a service officer or a state service officer. Claim frequency is limited to one per 30‑day period, with an exception allowing an additional application for funeral expenses. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
