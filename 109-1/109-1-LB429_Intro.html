Summary

LB429 regulates how school boards may interact with professional employees' organizations. It defines "professional employees' organization" (groups for professional development or liability protection) and "school employee" (administrators, teachers, paraprofessionals, student teachers). The bill requires school boards to provide equal access to any professional employees' organization when granting use of employees' physical or electronic mailboxes, permission to attend or display materials at employee meetings or orientations, and posting signage or materials in schools. It also prohibits school boards from naming or referring to school days or breaks using the name of any such organization. No changes to funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for school boards to provide equal access to all "professional employees' organizations" (defined as organizations for professional development or liability protection) regarding: use of employees' physical/electronic mailboxes; participation or representation at employee meetings/events (including orientations); creation/posting of signage or materials (boards, posters, pamphlets); and prohibits naming any school day or break after a professional employees' organization. “School employee” is defined to include administrators, teachers, paraprofessionals, student teachers, and similar staff. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
