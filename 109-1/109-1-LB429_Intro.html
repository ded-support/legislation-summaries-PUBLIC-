Summary

LB429 restricts how school boards may interact with professional employees' organizations. It defines "professional employees' organization" (groups for professional development or liability protection) and "school employee" (administrators, teachers, paraprofessionals, student teachers). School boards must provide equal access to any organization's physical or electronic mailboxes, meetings/events (including orientations), and posting/display of signage or materials whenever access is granted to one organization. The bill also prohibits naming a school day or break after any professional employees' organization. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "professional employees' organization" (groups for professional development or liability protection) and "school employee" (administrative staff, student teachers, paraprofessionals, teachers, administrators). Requires school boards to provide equal access: no exclusive use of employees' physical/electronic mailboxes; equal permission for organizations to attend or display materials at employee meetings/events (including orientations); and equal posting rights for signage/materials. Prohibits school boards from naming school days or breaks after any such organization. No other government agencies are referenced and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
