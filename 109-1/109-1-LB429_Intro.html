Summary

LB429 restricts school board interactions with professional employees' organizations. It defines a professional employees' organization (focused on professional development or liability protection) and school employee (administrators, teachers, student teachers, paraprofessionals). If a school board allows one such organization access to employees' physical or electronic mailboxes, recruitment at employee meetings/events (including orientations), display/posting of signage or materials, or participation by name on school calendar days/breaks, the board must grant equal access or permissions to any other requesting professional employees' organization. The bill also prohibits designating calendar days/breaks using an organization's name. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "professional employees' organization" and "school employee" and requires school boards to provide equal access to all such organizations. Specifically, school boards cannot grant one organization exclusive use of employees' physical or electronic mailboxes; if one organization may attend or display at employee meetings or orientations, others requesting permission must be allowed; the same parity must apply to posting signage or materials in schools; and school boards may not name school days or breaks after any professional employees' organization. No state agencies beyond school boards (or schools) are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
