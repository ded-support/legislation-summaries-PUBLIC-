Summary

LB429 restricts how school boards interact with professional employees' organizations (defined as groups for professional development or liability protection). It requires school boards to provide equal access to any professional employees' organization that requests use of school employees' physical or electronic mailboxes; equal permission to display materials or send representatives to employee meetings or orientation events; and equal ability to post signage or materials (e.g., bulletin boards, posters, pamphlets). The bill also prohibits a school board from naming or referring to a school day or break using any organization's name. No state agencies beyond school boards are referenced and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "professional employees' organization" (an organization for professional development or liability protection) and "school employee" (administrators, student teachers, paraprofessionals, teachers, administrators employed by a school board). Requires school boards to provide equal access to any professional employees' organization for physical/electronic mailboxes, attendance or representation at employee meetings/orientations, and posting signage/materials (bulletin boards, posters, pamphlets). Prohibits school boards from naming any school day or break after a professional employees' organization. No state agencies or departments beyond school boards are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
