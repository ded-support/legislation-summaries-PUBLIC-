Summary

LB29 amends the Administrative Procedure Act to require state agencies to review all existing and pending rules beginning January 1, 2026 and every three years thereafter. Each agency head must designate an oversight individual and submit an electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature by June 30 of the review year addressing: public health/safety/welfare necessity; cost‑benefit; effectiveness measurement; consideration of less‑restrictive alternatives; and whether the rule stemmed from state statute, federal mandate, or court order. The Legislature’s Reference Committee will refer reports to relevant standing committees, which must report to the Clerk by December 15 with recommended legislation or clarifications. Agency rulemaking is suspended during the review except for rules affecting health/safety, time‑sensitive matters, or those subject to statutory deadlines. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Starting Jan 1, 2026, all state agencies must review all existing and pending rules every three years. Agency heads must appoint a review supervisor. Agencies must file an electronic, detailed report to the Clerk of the Legislature by June 30 of the review year addressing: public health/safety/welfare necessity; cost‑benefit; whether effectiveness is measured; consideration of less‑restrictive alternatives; and whether the rule arose from state statute, federal mandate, or a court decision. The Legislature’s Reference Committee will assign each agency report to the appropriate standing committee, which must file its recommendations by Dec 15 and may propose clarifying legislation. Rulemaking under the Administrative Procedure Act is suspended during the review, except for rules that affect health/safety/welfare, are time‑sensitive, or meet statutory deadlines. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
