Summary

Creates the Research Excellence Cash Fund, to be administered by the University of Nebraska. The fund may receive gifts, grants, bequests, and transfers authorized by the Legislature. Authorized uses are research-based investments in data, data collection, and ongoing research important to Nebraska’s economy (explicitly including the Nebraska Mesonet). Available monies shall be invested by the state investment officer in accordance with the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are set. Emergency clause makes the act effective upon passage and approval.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Research Excellence Cash Fund," to be administered by the University of Nebraska. The fund may receive gifts, grants, bequests, and transfers authorized by the Legislature and may be used for research investments in data, data collection, and ongoing research that supports Nebraska’s economy (explicitly including the Nebraska Mesonet). Funds available for investment will be managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. No specific dollar appropriations are included; legislative transfers are authorized as a potential funding source.
