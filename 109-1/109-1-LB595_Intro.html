Summary

Creates the Research Excellence Cash Fund to be administered by the University of Nebraska. The fund will consist of gifts, grants, bequests, and transfers authorized by the Legislature and may be used to support research-based investments in data, data collection, and ongoing research important to the Nebraska economy (explicitly including the Nebraska Mesonet). Any fund balances available for investment are to be managed by the state investment officer pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. No specific dollar appropriations are included. The bill takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
