Summary

LB585 amends the Community‑based Juvenile Services Aid Program administered by the commission, specifying budget set‑asides and programmatic partners. Two ten‑percent allocations of the annual General Fund appropriation (excluding administrative funds) are required: one ten percent for development/maintenance of a common data set and evaluation (with the University of Nebraska at Omaha, Juvenile Justice Institute, assisting in development, evaluation, logic models, and reporting) and a second ten percent for grants to community‑based organizations located in qualified census tracts with annual budgets ≤ $1 million focused on preventing juvenile crime prior to system involvement. Remaining funds are apportioned by commission rule via a formula based on residents ages 12–18 who come into contact with the juvenile justice system; the commission may require up to a 5% local match. The commission must consult with the Office of Probation Administration, juvenile diversion programs, courts, law enforcement, and the Nebraska Association of County Officials when adopting rules and reporting annually to the Governor and Legislature.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a 10% set‑aside of the annual General Fund appropriation to the Community‑based Juvenile Services Aid Program (excluding administrative budget funds) for grants to community‑based organizations located in qualified census tracts (per 26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I), as of Jan 1, 2025) with budgets ≤ $1 million and focused on preventing juvenile crime before justice‑system contact. Distribution criteria must consider staff with lived juvenile‑justice experience, organization budget size, tract location, and served population demographics. The University of Nebraska at Omaha Juvenile Justice Institute will assist grantees in creating logic models and evaluation measures.
