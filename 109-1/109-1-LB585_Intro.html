Summary

LB585 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43-2404.02 to adjust Community-based Juvenile Services Aid Program administration and funding. It preserves a 10% General Fund set‑aside (excluding administrative funds) for a common data set and evaluation—allocated between the commission and the University of Nebraska at Omaha, Juvenile Justice Institute (UNO JJI) per historical percentages—and adds a second 10% set‑aside for grants to community-based organizations located in qualified census tracts with annual budgets ≤ $1 million that prevent juvenile crime prior to justice-system involvement. The remainder is apportioned by commission rule using a county/tribal youth population formula; the commission may require up to a 5% local match. The commission maintains the data set and provides it to UNO JJI for evaluation. Rulemaking and reporting obligations involve the commission, UNO JJI, Office of Probation Administration, directors of relevant juvenile programs, and the Nebraska Association of County Officials.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Allocates 10% of the annual General Fund appropriation to the Community‑based Juvenile Services Aid Program (excluding administrative funds) to grant-making for community‑based organizations located in IRS-defined qualified census tracts (26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I) as of Jan 1, 2025) with annual budgets ≤ $1 million, focused on preventing juvenile crime before system involvement.

- Establishes distribution criteria to prioritize organizations with staff who have lived juvenile‑justice experience, smaller budgets, location in qualified census tracts, and the demographics of populations served.

- Directs the University of Nebraska at Omaha, Juvenile Justice Institute, to assist grant recipients in developing logic models and evaluation measures to assess program impact.
