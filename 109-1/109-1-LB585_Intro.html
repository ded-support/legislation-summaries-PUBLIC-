Summary

LB585 amends Section 43-2404.02 governing the Community-based Juvenile Services Aid Program. It preserves a 10% annual General Fund set‑aside (excluding administrative funds) for development/maintenance of a common data set and evaluation—allocations to the commission and the University of Nebraska at Omaha, Juvenile Justice Institute (UNO JJI) are specified—and adds a separate 10% set‑aside for grants to community‑based organizations located in qualified census tracts with annual budgets ≤ $1 million that provide pre‑contact juvenile crime prevention. The remainder of program General Funds is apportioned by a commission formula based on county/tribal youth populations and juvenile justice contact; the commission may require up to a 5% local match. The commission must consult and coordinate with the Office of Probation Administration, juvenile diversion programs, law enforcement, courts, Nebraska Association of County Officials, and UNO JJI, and report annually to the Governor and Legislature. Original section repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a set‑aside and distribution rules for Community‑based Juvenile Services Aid Program funding. Specifically:

- Directs that 10% of the Program’s annual General Fund appropriation (excluding administrative budget funds) be reserved for grants to community‑based organizations located in qualified census tracts (per 26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I) as of Jan 1, 2025) with annual budgets ≤ $1 million, for pre‑contact juvenile crime prevention.

- Requires grant distribution to consider: staff with lived juvenile‑justice experience, organization budget size, qualified‑census‑tract location, and served population demographics.

- Assigns the University of Nebraska at Omaha, Juvenile Justice Institute, to help grantees develop logic models and evaluation measures.

No additional appropriation amount is specified beyond the 10% set‑aside.
