Summary

LB296 requires the State Department of Education, the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), the Office of Probation Administration, and the State Court Administrator to enter a memorandum of understanding to share data on students under juvenile‑court jurisdiction. The MOU contemplates SDE contracting a consultant; DHHS is included in the collaborative development of policies and will receive the consultant’s draft report. SDE must create and maintain a centralized education records system and employ registrars; the cost of that system and the registrars is to be paid from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund. That fund is comprised of lottery transfers, grant repayments, and interest, may be transferred to the Education Future Fund, and is administered by SDE. No direct appropriations to DHHS are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No changes affect the Department of Health and Human Services. The added provisions require the State Department of Education to create and maintain (beginning July 1, 2026) a centralized education records system for students under juvenile court jurisdiction, employ registrars to ensure uninterrupted services, timely record transfer, and award credit for completed academic work. Schools must immediately share records on request. Registrars will analyze high school records and the Commissioner may award credit and, in some cases, a state diploma (beginning 2026-27). Costs for the system and registrars are to be paid from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund; no DHHS appropriations or budget allocations are changed.
