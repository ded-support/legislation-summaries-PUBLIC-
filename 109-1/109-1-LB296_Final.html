Summary

The bill requires the State Department of Education, the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), the Office of Probation Administration, and the State Court Administrator to enter an interagency memorandum of understanding (by October 1, 2023) to share data on students under juvenile-court jurisdiction to improve educational and service coordination. DHHS is explicitly listed as a participating agency in the collaborative policy development and will receive the consultant’s draft recommendations. The measure also funds the State Department of Education’s centralized education records system and the employment of registrars (to support these students) from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund, and amends that fund’s authorized uses to include the centralized records system and registrars.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text assign duties, funding, or responsibilities to the Department of Health and Human Services. The amendments require the State Department of Education to create and maintain (from July 1, 2026) a centralized education records system for students under juvenile court jurisdiction, employ registrars to manage records and award earned high‑school credit, and allow diploma issuance beginning 2026–27. Costs for the system and registrars are charged to the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund; no DHHS appropriations or budget changes are included.
