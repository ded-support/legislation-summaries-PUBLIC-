Summary

The bill assigns several administrative and fiscal duties to the Department of Revenue, the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Department of Revenue must adopt rules and forms for the Recreational Trail Easement Property Tax Exemption Act, the new Adoption Tax Credit (refundable Nebraska credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit, effective for taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2026), net operating loss computations, and tax‑exemption applications; it gains recapture authority for the residence purchase tax credit. The bill creates a Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund to defray departmental costs, with possible transfers to the General Fund and investment by the state investment officer. DAS (Treasurer and Accounting Administrator) will set required fund types for the Nebraska educational savings plan trust; transfers from the Expense Fund to the Education Savings Plan Administrative Fund require legislative appropriation, and Expense Fund transfers to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund require annual reporting to DAS’s budget division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst. DHHS must annually transmit Meadowlark Program birth/guardian data to the State Treasurer; transfer costs are paid from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund. The bill also exempts certain publicly traded partnership reporting from specified withholding/remittance provisions.
