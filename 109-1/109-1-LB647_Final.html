Summary

The bill directs the Department of Revenue to adopt rules for the Recreational Trail Easement Property Tax Exemption Act and creates the Adoption Tax Credit Act (effective for taxable years beginning/ deemed to begin on/after Jan. 1, 2026), establishing a refundable Nebraska credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit. It creates a Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund (receiving monies under §§77‑3,109 and 77‑3,118) to defray production and administrative costs; the Legislature may transfer monies to the General Fund and balances may be invested under state investment statutes. Publicly traded partnerships (IRC §7704(b)) filing annual returns must report unit‑holder data for Nebraska income > $500 and are exempt from certain remittance requirements. The State Treasurer and DAS Accounting Administrator will set fund types for the Nebraska Educational Savings Plan Trust (Program, Expense, Administrative); the trust operates with no General Fund appropriations, Expense Fund transfers to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund must be reported annually to the DAS budget division and LFA, and Administrative Fund spending requires legislative appropriation. DHHS must annually (by March 1) transmit birth/parent/guardian data to the State Treasurer for Meadowlark Program eligibility; transfer costs are paid from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue (DOR): New rulemaking duties. DOR must adopt rules to implement the Recreational Trail Easement Property Tax Exemption Act (guidance for nonprofit easement holders, public‑benefit/cross‑connectivity certification, and compliance methods). DOR also may adopt rules to implement the Adoption Tax Credit Act, which creates a refundable state income tax credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan 1, 2026.
