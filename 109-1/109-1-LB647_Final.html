Summary

The bill directs actions by three agencies:

- Department of Revenue (DOR): authorizes rulemaking to implement the Recreational Trail Easement Property Tax Exemption Act and the Adoption Tax Credit Act (a refundable Nebraska credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit for tax years beginning 1/1/2026). Creates the Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund (receipts under sections 77‑3,109 and 77‑3,118) to defray publication/administrative costs; balances may be invested and the Legislature may transfer amounts to the General Fund. Grants DOR authority to recapture a residential purchase tax credit if the home ceases to be the claimant’s primary residence within five years. Amends Tax Commissioner procedures for entity-level elections, remittance/refundable-credit rules for electing partnerships/S‑corps, nonresident partner/shareholder procedures (with exceptions), extended refund/credit claim deadlines to Jan. 31, 2026, and enhanced notice-of-deficiency requirements.

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): directs the State Treasurer and DAS Accounting Administrator to determine state fund types for the Nebraska Educational Savings Plan Trust; the trust shall operate with no General Fund appropriations. Education Savings Plan Administrative Fund transfers from the Expense Fund require legislative appropriation. The State Treasurer must report annual Expense Fund transfers to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund to DAS’s budget division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): must annually (by March 1) transmit birth and parent/guardian data needed for the State Treasurer to identify Meadowlark Program “qualified individuals”; DHHS’s data-transfer costs are payable from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: required to adopt rules for the new Recreational Trail Easement Property Tax Exemption (exemption remains while a perpetual, recorded easement provides public access/connection and is held by eligible holders — nonprofits meeting mission, conservation, and management capacity). Rules must define nonprofit eligibility, certify public benefit/connectivity, and ensure compliance.

- Department of Revenue: may adopt rules for the Adoption Tax Credit Act (effective taxable years beginning/after Jan 1, 2026). Creates a refundable Nebraska income tax credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit (26 U.S.C. §23).

- Department of Administrative Services and Department of Health and Human Services: no added provisions, funding changes, or appropriations referenced in the underlined text.
