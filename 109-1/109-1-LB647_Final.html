Summary

LB647 directs agency rulemaking, reporting, and fund changes. Department of Revenue must adopt rules for the Recreational Trail Easement property‑tax exemption (nonprofit eligibility, public‑benefit/trail‑connectivity certification, perpetual‑easement compliance) and may implement a refundable Adoption Tax Credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit beginning in 2026. The Tax Commissioner will prescribe exemption/reaffirmation forms and require publicly traded partnerships to file unit‑holder data. The bill creates a Department of Revenue Miscellaneous Receipts Fund (specified receipts) to defray administrative costs; legislative transfers to the General Fund and investment by the state investment officer are authorized. DAS and the State Treasurer must set fund types for the 529 trust and report pro rata expense transfers to the Investment Council budget. DHHS must annually (by March 1) transmit newborn data to the State Treasurer; transfer costs are paid from the Education Savings Plan Expense Fund. Meadowlark caps: up to $250,000/year each for employer incentives and low‑income matching; endowment may accept private and legislative transfers but no General Funds.

Department of Revenue

- Must promulgate rules for the new Recreational Trail Easement Property Tax Exemption: define nonprofit holder eligibility, certify public‑benefit/connectivity of easements, and set compliance procedures.
