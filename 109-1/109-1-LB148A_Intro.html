Summary

LB148A appropriates funds to the Nebraska State Patrol (Program 100) to implement Legislative Bill 148. For FY2025-26 it provides $29,629 from the General Fund and $28,236 from the Nebraska State Patrol Cash Fund. For FY2026-27 it provides $30,921 from the General Fund and $28,236 from the Nebraska State Patrol Cash Fund. Expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from these appropriations are capped at $33,856 for FY2025-26 and $34,872 for FY2026-27.
