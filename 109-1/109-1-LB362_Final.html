Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: directs DHHS to oversee Nebraska’s 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline—including contracting for counselors, call‑center operators, and providers—and to coordinate with the Public Service Commission to ensure 988 connectivity and interoperability with the 911 system.

- Department of Revenue: requires the Department of Revenue to set the prepaid wireless surcharge annually (effective Jan. 1) based on prior July 1 charge levels, post at least 90 days’ advance notice of changes online, and provide the Public Service Commission surcharge calculation and collection data on request.

- Funding: designates the prepaid wireless surcharge as the sole funding obligation for 911 service, telecommunications relay service, and universal service for prepaid wireless; creates a 911 Service System Fund but includes no direct appropriations or budgetary allocations to DHHS or the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds standards and goals for a statewide next‑generation 911 system: interoperability, security, common standards, capacity to receive/process/analyze all 911 request types, integrate additional data, and share info among PSAPs. Establishes staggered three‑year terms for committee members beginning in 2025. Exempts providers from collecting the wireless surcharge for customers served under the Nebraska Telephone Assistance Program who do not receive a monthly billing statement. No direct appropriations, budget allocations, or new funding lines are specified. Potential impact for the Department of Revenue: reduced wireless surcharge collections from exempt NTAP customers (not quantified).
