Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services oversight responsibility for Nebraska’s 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, including authority to contract for counselors, call‑center operators and related services, and to coordinate with the Public Service Commission to ensure 988 connectivity with the 911 system. The Department of Revenue must set the annual prepaid wireless surcharge (effective January 1), post at least 90 days’ advance notice of changes on its website, and provide surcharge calculation and collection data to the Public Service Commission on request. The bill designates the prepaid wireless surcharge as the sole funding obligation for prepaid wireless services related to 911, telecommunications relay service, and universal service. No specific appropriations or new budget allocations to either department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Establishes next‑generation 911 requirements: interoperability, security, common standards, capability to receive/process/analyze all 911 request types, integrate additional data, and enable information‑sharing among PSAPs and emergency service providers.

- Governance change: beginning in 2025 current committee membership will transition to staggered three‑year terms (no more than one‑third expiring at once).

- Department of Revenue impact: carriers are not required to collect the wireless 911 surcharge for customers receiving services under the Nebraska Telephone Assistance Program who do not receive a monthly billing statement.

- No explicit appropriations, budget allocations, or additional funding amounts for the Department of Health and Human Services or Department of Revenue are included in the added text.
