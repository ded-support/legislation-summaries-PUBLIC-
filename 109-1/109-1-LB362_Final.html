Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) responsibility for oversight of Nebraska’s 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, including contracting for counselors, call‑center operators and other service providers, and coordinating with the Public Service Commission (PSC) to ensure 988 can connect to and receive communications from the 911 system. The Department of Revenue must determine the annual prepaid wireless surcharge (effective January 1), post at least 90 days' advance notice of any change on its website, provide the PSC with prepaid surcharge calculation and collection data on request, and the bill makes the prepaid wireless surcharge the sole funding obligation for prepaid wireless 911, relay, and universal service. No direct appropriations or other budget allocations to either agency are included; otherwise the bill revises statewide 911 statutes without additional agency funding changes.
