Summary

The bill assigns operational duties to two agencies without changing appropriations. The Department of Health and Human Services must oversee Nebraska’s 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline—contracting for counselors, call‑center operators and other service providers—and coordinate with the Public Service Commission to ensure 988 can connect with and receive communications from the 911 system. The Department of Revenue must annually set the prepaid wireless surcharge (effective Jan. 1) using prior‑year charges per a specified methodology, post at least 90 days’ advance notice of changes on its website, and provide the PSC with surcharge calculation and collection data on request. No direct funding or budget allocations for either agency are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for a next‑generation 911 system: interoperability, security, commonly accepted standards, capability for emergency centers to receive/process/analyze all 911 request types, integrate additional useful information, and share data among PSAPs and providers. Begins staggered three‑year terms for committee members starting in 2025 (no more than one‑third expire at once). Exempts collection of the wireless surcharge for any customers in the Nebraska Telephone Assistance Program who do not receive a monthly billing statement. No explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services or the Department of Revenue are specified; the surcharge collection exemption could reduce future surcharge receipts but no direct revenue or appropriation changes are stated.
