Summary

LB413 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-655 to expand and clarify rate-setting authority for boards of public power districts, public irrigation districts, and public power and irrigation districts. Key agency-related points:

- Boards may set and differentiate rates for electrical energy, water service/storage, and commodities including ethanol and hydrogen, using objective criteria (e.g., load size, load factor, firm/nonfirm service).

- Boards may negotiate different rates for commercial/industrial customers (and for electric cooperatives, nonprofits under the Electric Cooperative Corporation Act or Nebraska Nonprofit Corporation Act, agencies under the Municipal Cooperative Financing Act, and municipalities). Such negotiated rates are limited to five years and, when tied to a state or political subdivision economic development agreement with >500 kW and ≥60% annual load factor, must include a production component at least equal to incremental production cost.

- Following mergers/consolidations, merged district boards may apply differing predecessor-area rates for up to five years if supported by cost-of-service or rate studies.

- Original §70-655 is repealed. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
