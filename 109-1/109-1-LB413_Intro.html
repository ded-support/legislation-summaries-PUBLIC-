Summary

LB413 amends section 70-655 (Chapter 70, art. 6) governing public power districts, public irrigation districts, and public power and irrigation districts. It explicitly authorizes district boards to set rates for electricity, water, storage and other commodities (including ethanol and hydrogen) and permits rate differentiation by objective criteria (e.g., load size, firm/nonfirm service, technology risk, length of commitment). Boards may negotiate special commercial/industrial rates (effective ≤5 years); where a customer has a state or political‑subdivision economic development agreement with >500 kW and ≥60% annual load factor, negotiated rates must include a production component at least equal to incremental production cost. Following mergers, districts may maintain different predecessor rates for up to five years if supported by cost‑of‑service or rate studies. No appropriations or budget changes are included.
