Summary

LB145 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §2-958.02 to require the Director of Agriculture to administer a grant program from the Noxious Weed and Invasive Plant Species Assistance Fund, receiving, prioritizing (with the advisory committee), and awarding grants to local control authorities and weed management entities. Beginning FY2025-26, the Legislature intends to appropriate $6 million annually for vegetation management within natural stream banks/floodplains; those funds are limited to activities and equipment primarily aimed at improving streamflow conveyance and are to be disbursed only to weed management entities, local weed control authorities, and natural resources districts (priority to basins under interstate compacts/decrees). The Department of Agriculture may adopt rules to implement the section, and the Director may apply annually to the NRCS (USDA) for conservation funding.
