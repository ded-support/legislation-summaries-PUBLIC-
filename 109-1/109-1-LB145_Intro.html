Summary

The bill requires the Director of Agriculture to administer a grant program from the Noxious Weed and Invasive Plant Species Assistance Fund to assist local control authorities and other weed management entities. The director must receive applications, award and prioritize grants (in consultation with the advisory committee), may adopt rules and regulations to implement the program, and may annually apply to the Natural Resources Conservation Service of the U.S. Department of Agriculture for conservation funding. Beginning in FY2025-26, the Legislature intends to appropriate $6,000,000 annually for vegetation management within natural stream banks/floodplains; those funds are limited to activities and equipment for programs improving streamflow conveyance and will be disbursed to qualified weed management entities, local weed control authorities, and applicable natural resources districts.
