Summary

LB190 amends §50‑1208 of the Legislative Performance Audit Act, revising duties of the Legislative Performance Audit Committee and procedures for the Legislative Auditor’s office. Key agency-related changes: requires a five‑year rotating audit schedule for all state agencies; permits the committee (majority vote) to adopt additional performance‑audit requests; mandates written pre‑audit notice to the agency director, program director (when relevant), and the Governor; requires an entrance conference and written agency instruction to cooperate; directs the office to draft a scope statement and audit plan (including methodologies and projected completion date) subject to committee majority approval; obligates political subdivisions to provide requested information; and requires committee approval for scope/plan revisions with written notice to the agency. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that "the committee" must review and approve a rotating audit schedule covering all state agencies (or portions thereof), ensuring each is audited at least once every five years. No specific agencies or departments are named beyond "state agencies," and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
