Summary

LB190 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §50-1208 (Legislative Performance Audit Act) to clarify duties and procedures for the Legislative Performance Audit Committee and the Office of the Legislative Auditor. Key provisions require the committee to approve a rotating five-year audit schedule for all state agencies; adopt additional audit requests and scope statements by majority vote; and approve audit plans (methodology and projected deadlines) by majority vote. The bill requires the Legislative Auditor to notify agency directors, relevant program directors, and the Governor before audits, hold an entrance conference, and instruct agencies to cooperate. Political subdivisions must provide requested information. The Legislative Auditor may seek committee approval for scope or plan revisions. The original section is repealed. No funding or appropriations changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the committee to review and approve a rotating audit schedule covering all state agencies (or portions thereof), so each is audited at least once every five years. No specific agencies or departments are named, and the addition does not include any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
