Summary

LB190 amends section 50-1208 of the Legislative Performance Audit Act to clarify committee and audit-office duties. Key provisions require the Legislative Performance Audit Committee to approve a rotating audit schedule so each state agency (or portion) is audited every five years and to adopt additional audit requests by majority vote. The Legislative Auditor's office must notify the agency director, relevant program director, and the Governor in writing before beginning an audit, hold an entrance conference, and require agency staff cooperation. The office drafts a scope statement and, after committee majority approval, develops an audit plan with methodologies and projected deadlines; political subdivisions must provide requested information. The Legislative Auditor may seek committee approval for scope or plan revisions by majority vote, and agencies must be notified of approvals or revisions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
