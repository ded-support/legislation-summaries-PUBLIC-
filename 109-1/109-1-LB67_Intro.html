Summary

LB67 creates the Sexual Assault Emergency Care Act and assigns enforcement and oversight functions to "the department" (and its director), with appeal rights under the Administrative Procedure Act. Hospitals providing emergency care must offer and, unless declined, dispense emergency contraception, provide written/oral information, train personnel, and adopt policies to address staff moral or religious objections. Complaints may be filed with the department, which may investigate, conduct informal conferences (department or contracted peer review representative), hold hearings, and issue written decisions. Sanctions: a first substantiated complaint results in a written warning and corrective requirements; second and subsequent substantiated complaints carry fines of $1,000 per affected survivor or $1,000 per month until required training is provided. No appropriations or other budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a complaints/enforcement process centered on "the department" (the agency charged with enforcing the Sexual Assault Emergency Care Act) and its director, and permits use of contracted peer review organizations. Key points:

- Any complaint about compliance may be filed with the department; the department decides whether to investigate using listed factors.

- Procedural timelines: hospitals have 15 days to respond to notices; informal conferences must be held within 30 days; reports, decisions, and appeal-request windows are specified (20 working days, 10 working days, 5 working days, 30 days, respectively).

- The director assigns an investigator or peer-review representative (not the original investigator) for informal conferences; hearings follow department rules and Administrative Procedure Act appeal rights apply. Witnesses may be subpoenaed and paid per rule.

- Sanctions: first substantiated complaint = written warning and required corrective action. Second and subsequent = $1,000 fines either per survivor denied emergency contraception information/offering or per month until required training is provided.
