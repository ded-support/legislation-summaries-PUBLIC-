Summary

LB34 conditions Nebraska’s adoption of year‑round daylight saving time by setting state standard time to UTC−5 (central) and UTC−6 (mountain) once authorized by federal law or order and after three adjacent states do likewise. Federal entities referenced: the Uniform Time Act of 1966, the U.S. Secretary of Commerce, the U.S. Secretary of the Navy, and the U.S. Secretary of Transportation. The bill also prescribes election poll opening/closing times and ballot deadlines by time zone. For state personnel administration, it standardizes sick‑ and vacation‑leave accounting to 11:59 p.m. central time, directs the Director of Personnel to adopt rules, preserves Governor (and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council for legislative employees) discretion to extend leave, and clarifies payment treatment under the Public Employees Retirement Board for forfeited leave. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state time-standard provision for Nebraska (Central zone = UTC−5; Mountain zone = UTC−6). It references the General Conference on Weights and Measures as the maintainer of Coordinated Universal Time and names the U.S. Secretary of Commerce (in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy) as the federal official who interprets/modifies UTC for the United States. Operative date is the first Sunday in November after federal authorization or absence of conflict with U.S. law or any order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation and after three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time. The provision becomes void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
