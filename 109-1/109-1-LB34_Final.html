Summary

LB34 amends Nebraska time statutes to enable a transition to year‑round daylight saving time and to harmonize time references across state law. It: (1) establishes future operative standard times as UTC‑5 (central) and UTC‑6 (mountain) in 49‑1301, contingent on federal authorization (including any order by the U.S. Secretary of Transportation) and adoption by three adjacent states; (2) renders current daylight‑saving provisions null when that subsection becomes operative (49‑1302); (3) adjusts statutory election poll and ballot‑receipt hours by time zone (32‑908); and (4) standardizes sick and vacation leave accounting to 11:59 p.m. central time and directs the Director of Personnel to adopt implementing rules (81‑1323, 81‑1328). Agencies mentioned include employing state agencies, the Director of Personnel, the Governor, the Legislative Council/Executive Board, and the Public Employees Retirement Board; federal offices referenced include the U.S. Secretaries of Transportation, Commerce, and the Navy. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language sets Nebraska's standard time as UTC−5 in the central zone and UTC−6 in the mountain zone. It defines Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) as maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and interpreted or modified for the United States by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The provision becomes operative on the first Sunday in November after (1) it is authorized by U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation or is no longer in conflict with such federal law/order, and (2) three adjacent states adopt laws establishing a single year‑round standard time. The section will be nullified if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No agencies are assigned funding and no appropriations or budget changes are included.
