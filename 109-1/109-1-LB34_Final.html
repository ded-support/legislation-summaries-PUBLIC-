Summary

LB34 amends Nebraska time statutes and related state personnel provisions. Key agency references and effects:

- Elections: Section 32-908 specifies poll hours and ballot-deadline times by time zone (mountain: 7 a.m.–7 p.m.; central: 8 a.m.–8 p.m.).

- Time policy: Section 49-1301 sets Nebraska’s standard time (Central UTC−5; Mountain UTC−6) to become operative only after applicable federal authorization and after three adjacent states adopt single year‑round standard time. Section 49-1302 (current daylight‑saving schedule) becomes null once that occurs. The statutes reference the U.S. Secretary of Commerce and Secretary of the Navy (definition of Coordinated Universal Time) and the U.S. Secretary of Transportation (federal authorization).

- State personnel: Sections 81-1323 and 81-1328 fix sick‑ and vacation‑leave accounting to 11:59 p.m. central time and retain roles for the Governor, Legislative Executive Board, Director of Personnel, employing agencies, the Public Employees Retirement Board, and the State Personnel System.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state standard-time provision for Nebraska: central zone = UTC‑5, mountain zone = UTC‑6. Defines Coordinated Universal Time as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and “interpreted or modified for the United States” by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The provision becomes operative on the first Sunday in November after (1) it is authorized by U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation (or no longer conflicts with them) and (2) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time law. It becomes null if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
