Summary

LB34 revises Nebraska time statutes and related state employment leave provisions. It: (1) authorizes adoption of year‑round daylight saving/standard time (central = UTC−5; mountain = UTC−6) once authorized by federal law or order and after three adjacent states adopt identical measures; (2) makes current daylight‑saving dates null when that change becomes operative; (3) preserves election poll hours by time zone; and (4) standardizes sick‑ and vacation‑leave accounting to 11:59 p.m. central time and retains administrative provisions assigning rulemaking to the Director of Personnel. The bill names the Governor (for leave extensions), the Executive Board of the Legislative Council (for legislative employees), employing agencies, and the Public Employees Retirement Board (retirement treatment of leave cashouts). No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state standard-time provision: Nebraska would set standard time at UTC‑5 in the central zone and UTC‑6 in the mountain zone. It defines “Coordinated Universal Time” as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and interpreted/modified for the U.S. by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The subsection becomes operative only after (1) authorization by U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation (or when no longer in conflict with such federal law/order) and (2) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time. The provision also becomes null and void if subsection (2) of section 49‑1301 becomes operative. No agencies are allocated funding and no appropriations or budget changes are made.
