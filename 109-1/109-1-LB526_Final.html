Summary

LB526 authorizes public power suppliers—defined as public power districts, municipal electric utilities, or other government entities providing electric service—to impose reasonable, non‑discriminatory requirements on cryptocurrency mining operations (facilities ≥1 MW) to cover infrastructure upgrade costs. Permissible measures include requiring direct payment or a letter of credit and imposing terms or conditions, but only after the supplier performs a load study to determine costs and impacts. Prospective miners must notify and meet interconnection requirements of the local supplier. Suppliers must publish on their websites the number of mining operations under their jurisdiction and each operation’s annual energy usage. Suppliers may interrupt service per existing rate schedules and policies. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a regulatory framework for "cryptocurrency mining operations" (≥1 MW) handled by "public power suppliers" — defined as public power districts, municipal electric utilities, or other government entities that provide electric service. Public power suppliers may require miners to pay for or secure (e.g., letter of credit) infrastructure upgrade costs and may impose terms and conditions, provided requirements are fair, reasonable, and not unduly discriminatory. Suppliers must conduct a load study before imposing requirements. Prospective operators must notify the local supplier and meet its interconnection rules. Each supplier must publish on its website the number of mining operations it oversees and each operation’s annual energy usage. Suppliers may interrupt service to miners per established rate schedules/policies. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
