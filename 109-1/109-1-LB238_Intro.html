Summary

LB238 amends the Foster Care Review Act. It designates local foster care review boards whose members are selected by the executive director of the Office (of foster care review), sets board size (4–10), requires demographic representation, and prohibits appointment of employees of the Office, the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), residential child‑caring or child‑placing agencies, or courts. Boards must send member lists to DHHS and the Office of Probation Administration. Boards are explicitly exempted from the Open Meetings Act. Duties clarified: semiannual foster‑care file audit case reviews, submission of findings/recommendations to the court within 30 days, authority to request court review hearings, and permanency planning guidance. The bill repeals original §§43‑1304 and 43‑1308 and outrightly repeals §43‑1306. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that "local boards" are not considered "public bodies" under the Open Meetings Act. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
