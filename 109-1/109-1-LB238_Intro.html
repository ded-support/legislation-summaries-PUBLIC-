Summary

LB238 amends the Foster Care Review Act (sections 43‑1304 and 43‑1308). Key agency-related provisions:

- The executive director of "the office" selects local foster care review board members (boards sized 4–10) and must send member lists to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and the Office of Probation Administration. Employees of the office, DHHS, residential child‑caring or child‑placing agencies, or a court are ineligible for appointment.

- Local boards are expressly exempted from the Open Meetings Act (no longer public bodies).

- Designated local boards conduct semiannual foster‑care file audit case reviews, submit findings and recommendations to the court within 30 days, and may request court review hearings.

- The bill repeals original sections 43‑1304 and 43‑1308 and outright repeals section 43‑1306 (R.S. Cumulative Supp., 2024).

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
