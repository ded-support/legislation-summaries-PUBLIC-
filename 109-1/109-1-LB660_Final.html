Summary

Highlights regarding specified agencies:

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS)/State Building Division: DAS oversight is expanded—authorized agencies must obtain approval from the Director of Administrative Services before executing design‑build or construction manager‑general contractor contracts; the state building division must adopt procurement guidelines and will adjust statutory dollar thresholds (currently $100,000) for construction every four years using a construction‑cost index. The division retains facilities planning, construction, and administration duties, remits rental proceeds to the State Building Revolving Fund and State Building Renewal Assessment Fund, and administers the State Emergency Capital Construction Contingency Fund.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): The Division of Aeronautics (DOT) must create and maintain a List of Secure Drones Authorized for Purchase by Jan 1, 2026, and, effective Jan 1, 2027, only drones cleared by that list may be purchased; state funds are prohibited for purchases of drones excluded by the list.

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): Buildings and grounds owned, leased, or operated by DCS are exempt from certain DAS facilities‑administration provisions (maintenance, custodial, security, parking, and related duties).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions affecting specified agencies:

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Authorized agencies must obtain DAS (or designee) approval before executing design‑build or construction manager–general contractor contracts; DAS’s State Building Division will adopt procurement guidelines and DAS must adjust specified dollar‑amount thresholds for inflation every four years using a construction‑cost index. DAS is listed as a consulting party for the Secure Drone Purchasing Act and may be asked to provide reports to legislative committees.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): The Division of Aeronautics must create and maintain a "List of Secure Drones Authorized for Purchase" by Jan 1, 2026, and will clear devices under the Secure Drone Purchasing Act. Effective Jan 1, 2027, state agencies and, practicably, political subdivisions may only acquire drones cleared by the division; state funds are prohibited for purchases of unapproved drones.

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): Not specifically named; it would be subject as an "authorized agency" to DAS approval requirements for alternative contracting and to the drone purchase prohibitions.

- Funding/appropriations: No new appropriations. Key fiscal changes: DAS indexing of dollar thresholds and an explicit ban on using state funds after Jan 1, 2027 to buy unapproved drones.
