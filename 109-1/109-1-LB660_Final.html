Summary

Key agency provisions in LB660:

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): The State Building Construction Alternatives Act places design‑build and construction manager–general contractor procurement under the state building division of DAS. Authorized agencies must obtain Director of Administrative Services approval before using such contracts. DAS/state building division shall adopt guidelines and will adjust dollar thresholds for contract/letting every four years beginning Jan 1, 2026. The division remits rental proceeds to the State Building Revolving Fund and the State Building Renewal Assessment Fund and must provide periodic project cost and appropriation reports.

- Department of Correctional Services: Buildings and grounds owned, leased, or operated by the Department of Correctional Services are exempted from certain DAS facilities administration duties.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): The Division of Aeronautics (DOT) will maintain a List of Secure Drones in consultation with DAS. Effective Jan 1, 2027, state agencies may purchase only DOT‑cleared drones; state funds are prohibited for purchases barred by the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): New approval requirement — agencies must get the DAS director's sign‑off before executing design‑build or construction manager–general contractor contracts, submitting written justifications (cost, schedule, complexity, emergency, competition safeguards). DAS’s State Building Division must adopt RFQ/RFP guidelines and publication rules. Agencies must hire licensed architects/engineers under the Consultants' Competitive Negotiation Act (those hires cannot later bid). DAS will adjust specified dollar amounts every four years starting Jan 1, 2026 using a construction cost/construction‑cost index to account for inflation. DAS is also a consultant on the Secure Drone list.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): The Division of Aeronautics must create a List of Secure Drones by Jan 1, 2026; effective Jan 1, 2027 state agencies may only purchase drones on that list. State funds may not be used to buy prohibited drones; violating contracts are void. Security criteria (DoD Blue UAS, NDAA FY2024 compliance, US‑hosted data, AES‑256/TLS, segmented networks, MFA/role‑based access) are specified.

- Department of Correctional Services: No additions or funding/appropriation changes affecting DCS in the underlined text. No new appropriations specified.
