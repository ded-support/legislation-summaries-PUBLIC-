Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DoAS): The state building division (DoAS) is given authority to implement the new State Building Construction Alternatives Act, adopt guidelines for design‑build and construction manager–general contractor procurements, and must approve agency requests to use those contracting methods. DoAS will adjust the statutory dollar threshold for contracts (currently $100,000) every four years beginning Jan 1, 2026, using a construction cost index. The division retains statewide facilities planning, construction, and administration duties, will remit rental proceeds to the State Building Revolving Fund and State Building Renewal Assessment Fund, and is consulted in creation of the Secure Drone list.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): The Division of Aeronautics (DOT) will produce and maintain a List of Secure Drones Authorized for Purchase (by Jan 1, 2026) and clear devices; effective Jan 1, 2027, state agencies and state funds may only acquire drones cleared by that division.

- Department of Correctional Services: Buildings and grounds owned, leased, or operated by the Department of Correctional Services are exempt from the state building division’s maintenance/administration provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Gains explicit oversight of design‑build and construction manager–general contractor (CMGC) procurements — agencies must get DAS (or designee) approval before contracting, submitting a written justification and specified evaluation criteria. State Building Division (under DAS) must adopt procurement guidelines (RFQ/RFP, short‑listing, evaluation weights; cost must carry ≥50% weight). CMGC use limited to projects over $30 million. DAS must adjust dollar thresholds for inflation every four years starting Jan 1, 2026 (using a chosen construction cost index). DAS is also a consulting party for the Secure Drone Purchasing Act list.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): The Division of Aeronautics administers a Secure Drone Purchasing Act: by Jan 1, 2026 produce a List of Secure Drones (in consultation with DAS, others); effective Jan 1, 2027 state agencies may only acquire drones cleared on that list; state funds prohibited for non‑cleared drones; contracts in violation are void. Cybersecurity/data‑localization and encryption standards are specified.

- Department of Correctional Services: No substantive additions, funding, or appropriation changes affecting DOC appear in the added text.
