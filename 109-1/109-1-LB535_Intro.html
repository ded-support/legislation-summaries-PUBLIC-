Summary

LB535 expressly references the agencies as follows:

- Public safety officer definition added: includes employees of the Department of Correctional Services; includes employees of the Department of Health and Human Services only when the offender is committed as a dangerous sex offender under the Sex Offender Commitment Act (sec. 3).

- Section 28-934 expands covered locations to include secure youth confinement facilities operated by the Department of Correctional Services when the offender is committed to such a facility.

- Section 29-2221 continues mandatory imprisonment in a Department of Correctional Services adult correctional facility for habitual offenders and adds assault under section 4 of this act to the offenses that can trigger enhanced habitual criminal mandatory minimums.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Summary (Dept. of Correctional Services & Dept. of Health and Human Services)

- The added language explicitly classifies employees of the Department of Correctional Services as "public safety officers," extending the bill’s enhanced assault provisions to DOC staff.

- Employees of the Department of Health and Human Services are included as "public safety officers" only when the offender is committed under the Sex Offender Commitment Act as a dangerous sex offender.

- The bill broadly raises protections for covered workers (on-duty) against serious injury, use of dangerous instruments, and assaults involving bodily fluids.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either department are included.
