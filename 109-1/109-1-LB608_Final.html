Summary

Department of Correctional Services

- The bill adds “correctional officer” defined to include individuals employed full-time by a jail or by the Department of Correctional Services.

Department of Revenue

- Applicants and recipients must file documentation with the Department of Revenue proving employment, residential status, or eligible-disabled status when applying for tuition waivers and annually while receiving benefits; tuition‑waiver agreements require annual tax returns filed with the Department to verify Nebraska residency for five years post‑award.

- The Department of Revenue is designated to administer and enforce the First Responder Recruitment and Retention Act and may adopt rules.

- Institutions must provide the Department a yearly list of qualifying children recipients; the Department will maintain records and, by August 1 each year, report recipients who failed to file required tax returns.

Funding/Appropriations

- Beginning July 1, 2028, subject to available appropriations, the Coordinating Commission shall reimburse state universities for 50% of specified tuition waivers for correctional and youth‑detention officers, certain eligible disabled persons, and their qualifying children; reimbursements are prorated if appropriations are insufficient.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Correctional Services: The bill expressly defines "correctional officer" to include full‑time employees of a jail or the Department of Correctional Services responsible for daily supervision and security. Correctional officers (and qualifying children) may access tuition waivers, but waivers for former correctional officers or youth detention officers and their qualifying children are limited to attendance at state universities and only available beginning July 1, 2027. Eligible disabled persons who were correctional officers must have maintained satisfactory performance at time of disability.

- Department of Revenue: Applicants (first responders, eligible disabled persons, and qualifying children) must file documentation with the Department of Revenue annually proving employment/status and Nebraska residence to receive the tuition waiver.

- Funding/appropriations: Beginning July 1, 2028, subject to available appropriations, the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education will reimburse state universities for 50% of those specified tuition waivers from the prior year; claims are documented, and reimbursements are prorated if appropriations are insufficient.
