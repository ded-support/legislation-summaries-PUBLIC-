Summary

- The bill defines “correctional officer” to include individuals employed full‑time by a jail or by the Department of Correctional Services responsible for supervision and security of incarcerated persons.

- The Department of Revenue is designated to administer and enforce the First Responder Recruitment and Retention Act (rulemaking authority), to receive annual documentation from applicants proving employment/status and Nebraska residency, and to maintain records of qualifying children who receive tuition waivers. Institutions must provide lists to the Department by December 31; the Department must report by August 1 each year which waiver recipients failed to file a tax return documenting Nebraska residency.

- Beginning July 1, 2028, subject to available appropriations, the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education shall reimburse state universities for 50% of tuition waivers granted the prior year for correctional officers, youth detention officers, certain formerly employed disabled persons, and their qualifying children. Reimbursement claims require documentation and will be prorated if appropriations are insufficient.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Correctional Services: The bill adds a definition of "correctional officer" as a full‑time employee of a jail or the Department of Correctional Services responsible for adult facility supervision. Correctional officers (and their qualifying children) are included in tuition‑waiver eligibility; waivers for correctional‑officer‑related eligible disabled persons and their children are limited to state‑university attendance beginning July 1, 2027. Disabled former correctional officers must have had satisfactory performance at time of disability. No direct appropriations or new operating funds are directed to the Department of Correctional Services.

- Department of Revenue: The bill requires applicants (first responders, eligible disabled persons, and qualifying children) to file annual documentation with the Department of Revenue proving employment/status and Nebraska residence to receive waivers.

- Funding: Beginning July 1, 2028, the Coordinating Commission will reimburse state universities 50% of these specific waivers, subject to available appropriations and prorating if funds are insufficient.
