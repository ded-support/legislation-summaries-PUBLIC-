Summary

- Department of Labor: The Office of Labor Market Information (Dept. of Labor) will calculate the "Nebraska statewide average hourly wage" using Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages data by October 1 prior to application; hourly wage = reported average annual weekly wage ÷ 40.

- Department of Revenue: The Department of Revenue must file an annual electronic, fiscal‑year/accrual report by October 31 (GASB basis), appear at a joint Appropriations and Revenue Committee hearing by December 15 to present it, and provide supplemental information within 30 days if requested by three or more committee members. The report will list signed and active agreements, taxpayer identities, and biennial, agreement‑specific totals of credits used (with first‑year reporting rules); confidentiality protections apply.

- Funding/allocations: wage credit = 5% of qualified payroll, capped at $4 million per year and $40 million total statewide; $5,000 nonrefundable application fee paid to State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor

- The Office of Labor Market Information (Dept. of Labor) will calculate the "Nebraska statewide average hourly wage" using Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages data by October 1 each year; that wage benchmark determines eligibility and required pay levels for the program.

Department of Revenue

- The Tax Commissioner (Dept. of Revenue) administers applications, approves agreements, executes contracts, verifies compliance, recaptures disallowed credits, and may adopt rules.

- Reporting: Dept. of Revenue must submit an annual GASB‑basis report to the Legislature by Oct 31 and present at a joint Appropriations/Revenue hearing by Dec 15; biennial, aggregated disclosure of credits used is required, with confidentiality protections.

- Funding/allocations: $5,000 nonrefundable application fee (to State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund); wage‑credit program caps: $4 million per year and $40 million total. Credits offset employer withholding/payor tax liability and are treated as nonstate/public funds.
