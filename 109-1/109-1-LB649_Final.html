Summary

- Department of Labor: The bill directs the Office of Labor Market Information of the Department of Labor to calculate the Nebraska statewide average hourly wage (using Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages data) by October 1 of the year prior to application; hourly wages are computed by dividing the reported average annual weekly wage by 40.

- Department of Revenue: The Department of Revenue must submit an electronic annual report (fiscal-year, accrual basis meeting GASB) by October 31 and appear before the Appropriations and Revenue Committees by December 15; supplemental information requested by three or more committee members must be provided within 30 days. The report must list signed and active agreements, identify taxpayer parties, and disclose agreement-specific aggregated credit use biennially (with confidentiality protections).

- Fiscal provisions: a $5,000 nonrefundable application fee is credited to the Nebraska Incentives Fund; wage-credit caps are $4 million per year and $40 million total.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor

- Office of Labor Market Information must calculate the "Nebraska statewide average hourly wage" using Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages data and provide it by October 1 preceding an application.

Department of Revenue

- The Tax Commissioner (DOR) administers the program: accepts $5,000 nonrefundable applications (remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund), approves applicants, executes agreements, monitors compliance, and enforces recapture of credits for noncompliance.

- Wage-credit limits: 5% of qualified employees' compensation, capped at $4 million per year and $40 million total statewide.

- DOR must file an annual GASB‑basis electronic report to the Legislature by Oct 31, appear at a joint Appropriations/Revenue hearing by Dec 15, provide certain agreement and credit disclosures (with confidentiality protections), and supply requested supplemental information within 30 days.

- No new appropriations to either agency are specified.
