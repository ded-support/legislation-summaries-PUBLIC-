Summary

- Department of Labor: The Office of Labor Market Information of the Department of Labor will calculate the "Nebraska statewide average hourly wage" using annual Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages data by October 1 prior to application; hourly wages are computed by dividing reported average annual weekly wage by 40.

- Department of Revenue: The Department of Revenue must submit an annual electronic, fiscal-year (accrual) report to the Legislature by October 31 and appear at a joint Appropriations/Revenue Committee hearing by December 15. The report must list signed and active agreements, identify taxpayer parties, provide agreement-specific aggregated credit-use data every two years (with first-year reporting rules), comply with GASB standards, and protect confidential information. Supplemental information requested by three or more committee members must be provided within 30 days.

- Funding/allocations: wage credit = 5% of compensation to qualified employees; annual statewide cap $4,000,000; total program cap $40,000,000; $5,000 nonrefundable application fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor

- Office of Labor Market Information must calculate the "Nebraska statewide average hourly wage" annually (using QCEW data) by Oct. 1 for program eligibility determinations.

Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner)

- Administers applications, approvals, written agreements, verification, recapture/waiver, disclosure to acquiring taxpayers, and rulemaking authority for the wage-credit program.

- Must submit an annual, GASB‑compliant electronic report by Oct. 31 and appear Dec. 15 before the Appropriations and Revenue Committees; supplemental data requested by ≥3 members due in 30 days. Reports list signed/active agreements, taxpayer identities, and biennial aggregated credit‑use figures (subject to confidentiality limits).

Fiscal impacts/allocations

- $5,000 nonrefundable application fee remitted to State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund.

- Wage‑credit limits: $4 million maximum per year and $40 million aggregate cap; credits reduce employer withholding and are treated as nonstate/public funds.
