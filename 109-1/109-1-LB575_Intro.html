Summary

LB575 amends the Property Tax Request Act (77-1633) and property tax levy limits (77-3442). Key administrative changes: county clerks (or designees) must organize joint public hearings (scheduled July 14–24, after 6 p.m.); county clerks must notify county assessors and participating subdivisions by July 4; participating political subdivisions must electronically send specified budget/tax information to the county assessor by July 4; the county assessor arranges postcard printing. Fiscal provisions: initial postcard printing is paid from the county general fund; ongoing creation and mailing costs (including staff time, materials, postage) are charged proportionately to participating political subdivisions based on parcel counts. The levy-limit section is clarified to cap a subdivision’s levy at the amount needed to raise the same dollar total as the prior year when valuation increases, with a two‑thirds governing‑body vote required to exceed it. The act takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a levy cap for political subdivisions: if total taxable valuation rises year-over-year, the subdivision’s levy is limited to the amount needed to collect exactly the same total property tax revenue as the prior year. A political subdivision may exceed that limit only by an affirmative two‑thirds vote of its governing body. Notes an exception per section 77‑3444. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no new funding, appropriation, or budget allocation provisions.
