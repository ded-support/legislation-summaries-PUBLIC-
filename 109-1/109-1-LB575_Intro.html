Summary

LB575 amends two revenue statutes. Under §77-1633 (Property Tax Request Act) counties, cities, school districts, and community colleges seeking to increase property tax requests beyond the allowable growth percentage must participate in a county-organized joint public hearing (held between July 14–24 after 6 p.m.), with designated representatives, and submit prescribed information electronically to the county assessor by July 4. The county clerk organizes the hearing; initial postcard printing costs are paid from the county general fund and then charged proportionately to participating political subdivisions. The statute prescribes detailed postcard and notice content and reporting duties of the county clerk.

Amendments to §77-3442 (levy limits) add a statewide cap: when a political subdivision’s taxable valuation rises, its levy may be no higher than needed to raise the same dollar amount as the prior year, except if two-thirds of its governing body vote to exceed that limit. The act is effective immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a levy cap for political subdivisions: if a jurisdiction’s total taxable valuation rises from the prior year, its property tax levy is limited to the dollar amount necessary to raise exactly the same total property tax revenue as the prior year (i.e., no increase in dollars despite higher valuation). A political subdivision can exceed that limit only by an affirmative two‑thirds vote of its governing body. Applies except as provided in section 77‑3444. No specific state agencies, new appropriations, or budget allocations are created or changed.
