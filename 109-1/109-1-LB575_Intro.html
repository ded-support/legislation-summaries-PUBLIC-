Summary

LB575 amends the Property Tax Request Act (77-1633) and property tax levy limits (77-3442). Key agency actions and fiscal effects:

- County clerk: must organize joint public hearings (one per county) between July 14–24 after 6 p.m.; notify county assessor and participating political subdivisions by July 4.

- County assessor: receive electronic submission of required information by July 4 and forward postcard data to a printing service designated by the county board.

- County general fund: pays initial cost to print postcards; subsequent creation and mailing costs (including staff time, materials, postage) are charged proportionately to participating political subdivisions based on parcel counts.

- Levy change: adds a cap requiring a political subdivision’s levy, when total taxable valuation rises, be limited so it raises no more property tax revenue than the prior year unless two-thirds of the governing body vote to exceed it.

- Dept. of Natural Resources and DOT’s Division of Aeronautics remain referenced for existing levy exceptions.

- Emergency clause: effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new levy restriction (subsection (12)) on political subdivisions: if a subdivision’s total taxable valuation rises year-to-year, its property tax levy is capped at the amount required to raise the identical dollar amount of property tax revenue it raised the prior year. A subdivision may exceed that cap only by an affirmative two‑thirds vote of its governing body. References an exception in section 77‑3444. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and no direct appropriations, funding lines, or budget allocations are created—this is a constraint on local property tax revenue growth.
