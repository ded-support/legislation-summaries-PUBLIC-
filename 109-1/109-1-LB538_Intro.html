Summary

LB538 requires school boards and governing boards of postsecondary institutions to adopt written discrimination and harassment policies that explicitly address antisemitism. It establishes Title VI coordinator roles in state education entities: the bill creates/assigns a Title VI coordinator within the State Department of Education and directs the Coordinating Commission on Postsecondary Education to designate/employ a Title VI coordinator. The commission’s statutory staffing authority is amended to include that designation. The commission’s coordinator must monitor discrimination and harassment (including antisemitic conduct), investigate complaints under the new policies, and file an annual report. The bill does not include any express changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Coordinating Commission on Postsecondary Education must designate a Title VI coordinator. That coordinator will monitor discrimination and harassment in public institutions, including antisemitic discrimination as defined elsewhere in the act. Duties include investigating all complaints of discrimination or harassment under the policy required by section 3 and filing an annual report. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
