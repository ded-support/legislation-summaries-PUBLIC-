Summary

The bill amends Nebraska's treatment-and-corrections statutes to (1) define "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and "Director" as the Director of Correctional Services and to tie "committed offender" and "facility" to department-operated facilities; (2) define "restrictive housing" as out-of-cell time of less than ten hours per day; (3) require the department to adopt rules establishing levels of restrictive housing, criteria and procedures for placement, and individualized transition plans developed with the committed offender; (4) limit restrictive-housing placements to no more than fifteen consecutive days and bar placement of members of vulnerable populations except for immediate segregation pending classification; and (5) authorize the department to develop secure mental-health housing that provides meaningful social interaction and programming. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

For the Department of Correctional Services: the added provisions (1) limit restrictive housing—no inmate may be held more than 15 consecutive days and no more than one inmate may be placed in a single-occupancy cell unless all inmates voluntarily agree in writing; (2) appear to require "ten hours per day" (context implies a minimum daily period, likely out-of-cell time); and (3) add/clarify definitions for populations relevant to custody and treatment, including developmental disability, serious mental illness (defined as biologically based conditions that substantially limit life activities, with examples such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, major depression, OCD, etc.), and traumatic brain injury.
