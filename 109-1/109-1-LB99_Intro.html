Summary

LB99 amends provisions of the Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act to (1) expressly define "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and "Director" as the Director of Correctional Services (plus related committed-offender and facility definitions) and (2) require the Department to adopt and promulgate Administrative Procedure Act rules establishing levels of restrictive housing, individualized transition plans, and criteria/procedures for placement. The bill limits restrictive housing to 15 consecutive days, defines restrictive housing as under ten hours out-of-cell per day, prohibits placement of vulnerable populations in restrictive housing except for temporary segregation pending classification, and directs the Department to develop secure mental-health housing with meaningful access to social interaction, exercise, stimulation, and therapeutic programming. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions limit use of restrictive housing: inmates may not be held in restrictive housing for more than 15 consecutive days and (subject to a referenced ten-hours-per-day provision) no more than one inmate may be placed in a single-occupancy cell unless all affected inmates voluntarily agree in writing. The amendment also adds statutory definitions relevant to placement decisions: “developmental disability” (per section 71-1107), “serious mental illness” (a biologically based brain disorder that substantially limits life activities, listing examples such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, major depression, OCD, etc.), and references traumatic brain injury. No direct changes to Department of Correctional Services’ funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
