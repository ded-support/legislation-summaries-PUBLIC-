Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and "Director" as the Director of Correctional Services. Expands statutory definitions to specify restrictive housing as confinement with out‑of‑cell time under ten hours per day. Requires that no inmate be held in restrictive housing except pursuant to rules and regulations adopted and promulgated by the department under the Administrative Procedure Act, and limits restrictive housing to no more than fifteen consecutive days. Directs the department to promulgate rules establishing levels of restrictive housing, behavioral/mental‑health criteria, individualized transition plans developed with the committed offender, and regulations addressing risks for vulnerable populations. Authorizes the department to develop secure mental‑health housing that provides meaningful social interaction, exercise, environmental stimulation, and therapeutic programming. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Correctional Services: the added provisions limit restrictive housing to a maximum of 15 consecutive days and prohibit placing more than one inmate in a single-occupancy restrictive housing cell unless all occupants voluntarily agree in writing. The bill also adds definitions relevant to custody and placement decisions: "developmental disability" (by reference to §71-1107), and a broad definition of "serious mental illness" (biological brain disorder substantially limiting life activities, listing examples such as schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, delusional disorder, bipolar disorder, major depression, and OCD). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
