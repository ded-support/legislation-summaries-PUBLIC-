Summary

LB663 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §23-114.01 governing county planning commissions and county boards. Key agency provisions:

- Requires each county planning commission member and each county board member to complete two hours of role-specific education per term, developed and presented by the respective county attorney.

- Clarifies conditional-use/special-exception procedures for county planning commissions and county boards: mandatory written factual findings with decisions; timelines for completeness determinations and final action (commission: 30/10/30/90-day sequence; board: 30/10/30/90-day sequence); presumption that applicants will comply with local, state, and federal requirements (burden shifts to challenger); and prohibition on conditioning approvals on obtaining other agency permits.

- Retains existing compensation/per diem provisions and appellate routes (county board or district court as applicable).

No new appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Requires each planning commission member and county board member to complete two hours of role-specific education per term; the respective county attorney must develop and present that training.

- States that conditional use permits/special exceptions must be decided based solely on county zoning regulations; commissions/boards cannot condition approval on applicants obtaining other federal, state, or local agency permits, nor are applicants relieved of any separate agency permit requirements.

- Establishes a presumption that applicants will comply with all local, state, and federal requirements; challengers bear the burden of proof, and absent contrary evidence the commission/board must approve.

- Sets procedural deadlines: commission—30 days to deem an application complete (10-day notice of deficiencies, 30 days after supplemental info, 90 days to act or recommend); board—mirror 30/10/30/90 timelines to determine completeness and decide.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
