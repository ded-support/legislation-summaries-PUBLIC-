Summary

Creates the Give to Enable Support Act and Give to Enable Support Cash Fund, administered by the State Treasurer. The fund accepts private contributions only (no General Fund transfers) and is invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment law. The State Treasurer implements the Give to Enable Support Program beginning January 1, 2026, accepts applications January 1–June 1 annually, may approve applications subject to available fund balance, establishes accounts by April 1 following approval, and may adopt rules. The bill amends Neb. §77-2716 (income tax adjustments) to allow a subtraction for contributions to the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund — treated like 529/ABLE contributions — up to existing limits ($5,000 married filing separate; $10,000 other returns), provided not deducted for federal tax. No new General Fund appropriation is created.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund and the Give to Enable Support Program, both administered and implemented by the State Treasurer. The Treasurer will accept private contributions (no transfers from the General Fund) and credit them to the cash fund; funds available for investment will be managed by the State Investment Officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The program starts Jan 1, 2026; the Treasurer will accept applications annually Jan 1–June 1 and may approve as many applicants as fund resources permit. Approved accounts must be established by April 1 following approval and funded at least to the minimum required to open an account. The Treasurer may adopt rules; accounts operate under sections 77-1401 to 77-1409. No new appropriations or General Fund allocations are authorized.
