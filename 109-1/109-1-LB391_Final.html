Summary

Creates the Give to Enable Support Act establishing the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund and the Give to Enable Support Program. The State Treasurer will administer the fund and program (program begins Jan. 1, 2026), accept private contributions only (no transfers from the General Fund), approve participant applications annually (Jan. 1–June 1) as funding allows, establish accounts under existing sections 77-1401 to 77-1409, and adopt rules. The State Investment Officer will invest fund balances pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to permit a Nebraska income tax reduction for contributions to the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund (subject to existing contribution limits: $5,000 married filing separately; $10,000 other returns). The original §77-2716 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund and the Give to Enable Support Program, both administered by the State Treasurer. The fund will accept private contributions only (expressly prohibiting transfers from the General Fund) and any investable balances will be managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The program begins January 1, 2026; the State Treasurer will accept applications each year Jan 1–June 1, approve as many applicants as available fund resources permit, establish accounts by April 1 following approval, fund them at least to the minimum required to open, set higher amounts at their discretion, and adopt implementing rules and regulations. No new appropriations or General Fund allocations are authorized.
