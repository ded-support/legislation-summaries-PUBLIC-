Summary

Creates the Give to Enable Support Act and a Give to Enable Support Cash Fund administered by the State Treasurer, who will accept private contributions (no General Fund transfers), invest fund balances via the state investment officer under existing state investment law, and implement the Give to Enable Support Program beginning January 1, 2026. The Treasurer will accept annual applications (Jan 1–June 1), establish accounts for qualifying individuals using fund distributions, and adopt rules. The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to permit a state income tax subtraction for contributions to the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund (not deductible federally), subject to existing $5,000 married filing separate / $10,000 per return limits, thereby creating a state tax expenditure. No specific appropriations or General Fund allocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund and the Give to Enable Support Program, both administered and implemented by the State Treasurer beginning January 1, 2026. The Treasurer will accept only private contributions into the cash fund (explicitly prohibiting transfers from General Funds) and the state investment officer will invest any available fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Treasurer will accept annual applications (Jan 1–June 1), approve as many as funding allows, establish accounts by April 1 following approval, fund each account at least at the minimum required to open, and may adopt implementing rules. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are listed.
