Summary

- The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) shall assess market rates and costs and adjust child care reimbursement every odd-numbered year to at least the 75th percentile; nationally accredited providers and programs rated step two or higher under Step Up to Quality may receive higher rates based on quality ratings (section 43-536).

- DHHS shall administer the state's social services program, may contract for services, and must participate in the federal Child Care Subsidy program (section 68-1206). DHHS will promulgate rules implementing accommodations and enrollment provisions effective July 1, 2025.

- Funding: for July 1, 2021–Sept 30, 2026, Child Care and Development Block Grant (CCDBG) funds must pay costs from prior income-eligibility changes; if insufficient, Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) funds may be used. No General Funds (except administration) shall be used for those costs. DHHS shall collaborate on a privately funded independent evaluation.
