Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to administer Nebraska’s social services and to participate in the federal Child Care Assistance/Subsidy programs. DHHS must assess market rates using an approved 45 C.F.R. methodology and adjust child-care reimbursement rates every odd-numbered year to at least the 75th percentile; nationally accredited providers and programs rated step two or higher under Step Up to Quality may receive higher, quality‑based rates. DHHS shall implement fixed-rate schedules (annual Oct. 1 effective date) and may adopt tiered rates tied to Step Up to Quality ratings. DHHS will apply specified income‑eligibility and transitional assistance rules (185% FPL before Oct. 1, 2026; 130% on/after) including a 10% earned‑income disregard after 12 months and must promulgate related rules. CCDBG funds (7/1/2021–9/30/2026) cover costs from prior eligibility changes; if insufficient, TANF may be used and General Funds (except administration) may not. DHHS shall collaborate on a privately funded independent evaluation.
