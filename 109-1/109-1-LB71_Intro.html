Summary

The bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to assess market rates using an approved 45 C.F.R. 98.45 methodology and to adjust child-care reimbursement rates every odd-numbered year at no less than the 75th percentile. DHHS may reimburse nationally accredited providers and programs rated step two or higher under the Step Up to Quality Act at higher, quality-tiered rates. DHHS shall administer the state's social services program, participate in federal child care assistance and Child Care Subsidy programs (with specified income eligibility thresholds: 185% of FPL prior to Oct. 1, 2026; 130% on/after that date), promulgate rules for new accommodations (operative July 1, 2025), and annually adopt a fixed-rate schedule (effective Oct. 1). The statute directs CCDBG funds (7/1/2021–9/30/2026) to cover costs from prior eligibility changes; if insufficient, TANF may be used and no General Funds (except administration) shall be used.
