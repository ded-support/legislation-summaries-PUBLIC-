Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (department) these duties: assess market rates using an approved 45 C.F.R. 98.45 methodology and adjust child-care reimbursement every odd-numbered year at no less than the 75th percentile; reimburse nationally accredited providers and programs rated Step Up to Quality step two or higher at higher rates; administer state social services and participate in the federal child care assistance/Subsidy programs (with specified income eligibility thresholds and a 10% earned-income disregard after 12 months); promulgate rules for provisions becoming operative July 1, 2025; and adopt an annual fixed-rate schedule (effective Oct 1) for provider reimbursement, allowing tiered rates for higher-quality ratings. Funding: Child Care and Development Block Grant (CCDBG) funds shall cover costs of prior income-eligibility changes through Sept. 30, 2026; if insufficient, TANF funds may be used; no General Funds (except administration) for those costs. The department shall collaborate on an independently funded evaluation if private funds are available.
