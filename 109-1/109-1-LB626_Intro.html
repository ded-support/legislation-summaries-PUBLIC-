Summary

LB626 amends the Community Development Law definitions for workforce housing and directs the Department of Economic Development to annually update statutory housing-unit cost caps (owner-occupied $275,000; rental $200,000) using the most recent change in the Producer Price Index published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. The bill cites the federal Department of Labor only as the source of the index. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Economic Development or the Department of Labor are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of “affordable housing” as: (a) workforce housing; (b) housing targeted to households earning <60% of the county median income; or (c) housing under IRC §42 (LIHTC). Also inserts a criterion that an area may be characterized by having “less than twenty percent of the housing in the area” be affordable. The additions do not reference the Department of Economic Development or the Department of Labor and include no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency.
