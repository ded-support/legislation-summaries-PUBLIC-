Summary

LB436 amends Nebraska’s Regulation of Health Professions Act, clarifying roles and procedures for the Department’s director, the division, the Health and Human Services board, and regulatory entities under the Uniform Credentialing Act. Key provisions:

- Director, with board advice, appoints technical committees; for unregulated professions committees consist of six appointed members plus a board member chair; for scope-change reviews the existing regulatory entity serves as the technical committee. Composition limits and impartiality requirements specified.

- Division directed to adopt rules establishing standards for application of review criteria.

- Committees must use scientific evidence, hold public fact‑finding hearings, comply with the Open Meetings Act, and file written findings and recommendations (including least‑restrictive regulation when applicable).

No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural and substantive oversight requirements tied to the Uniform Credentialing Act. For regulated health professions, the profession’s regulatory entity must serve as the “technical committee” for scope‑of‑practice change applications, consult with applicant groups and opponents, assess risks, and recommend solutions. For unregulated professions or those without a regulatory entity, a separate review process is indicated. The additions require adoption/promulgation of rules, evaluation of education/training and competency safeguards, and assurance that the state has means to verify qualifications and discipline licensees. No changes to funding, appropriations, or specific state departments/agencies or budget allocations are included.
