Summary

LB436 amends the Nebraska Regulation of Health Professions Act (secs. 71-6221, 71-6223, 71-6224, 71-6226) to clarify procedures for credentialing unregulated health professions and for changes in scope of practice. Key agency actions:

- The director (with advice of the board and the division) reviews letters of intent and final applications, notifying applicant groups of eligibility/acceptance within 15 days and setting review order when needed.

- The director appoints technical committees; the board designates a chair. For unregulated professions, committees comprise six appointed members plus a board member chair; for scope changes, the profession’s regulatory entity under the Uniform Credentialing Act serves as the committee.

- The division must adopt rules setting criteria standards. Committees must consider scientific evidence, hold public factfinding hearings, comply with the Open Meetings Act, make written findings, and recommend the least restrictive regulation.

- Applicants bear the burden of proof.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
