Summary

LB436 amends the Nebraska Regulation of Health Professions Act to refine review and oversight procedures. Key state actors and duties:

- Director and division: require letters of intent and final applications; director must notify eligibility and acceptance within 15 days and may set review order; division must adopt rules establishing standards for applying statutory criteria.

- Board: advises the director and designates a board member to chair technical committees.

- Technical committees: for unregulated professions, six appointed members plus a board-designated chair (not from the applicant or directly affected professions); for scope changes to regulated professions, the profession’s regulatory entity serves as the committee. Committees must use scientific evidence, hold public factfinding hearings, comply with the Open Meetings Act, and file written findings and recommendations.

- Regulatory entities under the Uniform Credentialing Act: serve as technical committees when scope changes are proposed.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds substantive criteria and procedures for scope‑of‑practice and regulation changes. For professions without a regulatory body, applications involving unregulated health professions are addressed under the Uniform Credentialing Act; for regulated professions the profession’s regulatory entity must serve as the technical committee. That entity must consult applicants and opponents, assess public health/safety/welfare risks, recommend solutions, and ensure adequate education/training, rules, oversight, and means to verify qualifications and take disciplinary action. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
