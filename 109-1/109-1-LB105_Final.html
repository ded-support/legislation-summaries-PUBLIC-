Summary

LB105 amends provisions governing public power and public power and irrigation districts. Key agencies mentioned:

- Nebraska Power Review Board: retains approval authority over subdivision maps; may allow subdivisions to follow whole or divided voting precincts or county lines without regard to population for certain multi-county or irrigation-operating districts; may authorize added county representation outside chartered territory.

- Secretary of State: districts must transmit precinct-division or retail-customer certification information with the election certification to the Secretary of State; the bill removes a prior requirement for Secretary of State approval of precinct divisions.

- County election officials (election commissioner or county clerk): precinct lines after each decennial census are to be followed when feasible.

- Counties: districts are responsible for additional election costs caused by precinct divisions or certifications and must pay county statements within 30 days.

No changes to appropriations or state funding. The act takes effect immediately (emergency clause).
