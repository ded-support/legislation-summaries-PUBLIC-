Summary

LB464 creates a criminal offense for organized retail crime, amends and replaces section 28-101, and (1) defines terms and penalties, (2) allows aggregation of retail market value across victims for charging and classification (but not aggregated into more than one offense), (3) makes aggregate retail market value an element the state must prove beyond a reasonable doubt, (4) permits consideration of conduct or convictions within ten years, and (5) eliminates as a defense that merchandise was not actually stolen if it was explicitly represented as stolen. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions allow aggregating the retail market value taken pursuant to a single organized retail crime scheme from multiple victims in one indictment/complaint (but not aggregating the same amounts into more than one offense). Aggregate retail market value is made an essential element that must be proved beyond a reasonable doubt. Also, it is not a defense that merchandise was not actually stolen if it was explicitly represented as stolen/embezzled/converted. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations changes are mentioned.
