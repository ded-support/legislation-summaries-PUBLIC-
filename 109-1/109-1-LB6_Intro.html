Summary

LB6 amends section 28-416 of the Uniform Controlled Substances Act to require that any person convicted of a violation of that section who is sentenced to the Department of Correctional Services shall attend appropriate drug abuse treatment and counseling. The bill contains no provisions changing funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.
