Summary

LB103 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §27-412 (Nebraska Evidence Rules) to tighten limits on admitting evidence of a victim’s past sexual behavior or sexual predisposition in civil and criminal sexual-misconduct proceedings. It preserves narrow exceptions: evidence to show another source of physical evidence, evidence of prior sexual behavior with the accused (if similar and showing a pattern relevant to consent), and evidence required by constitutional rights; and in civil cases only if probative value substantially outweighs harm. The bill requires a written pretrial motion (generally 15 days before trial), service on parties and the victim/guardian, and an in camera hearing outside the jury. It bars questioning victims in pretrial proceedings about matters rendered inadmissible by the section. No state agencies or departments are referenced; no funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a clause extending protected status to persons "or has been the victim of any other sexual assault" and a subsection (5) prohibiting questioning a victim during any pretrial hearing or proceeding—including depositions—about matters that are inadmissible under this section. No government agencies, departments, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
