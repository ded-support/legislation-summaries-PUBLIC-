Summary

LB103 amends Neb. Evid. Rule §27-412. It clarifies that evidence that a sexual-misconduct victim engaged in other sexual behavior or "has been the victim of any other sexual assault," and evidence of sexual predisposition, is inadmissible except under narrow exceptions (specified for criminal and civil cases). Procedural requirements: a party seeking such evidence must file a written motion at least 15 days before trial (unless excused), serve all parties and notify the victim or guardian, and the court must hold an in camera hearing outside the jury. The bill expressly prohibits questioning a victim during any pretrial hearing or proceeding, including depositions, on matters inadmissible under this section. Named governmental entities are the Nebraska Legislature (bill sponsors/committee) and the courts; no agencies/departments beyond the judicial system are specified and there are no funding or appropriation changes.
