Summary

LB103 amends Nebraska Evidence Rule §27-412. It clarifies that evidence that a victim engaged in other sexual behavior, was the victim of another sexual assault, or that shows sexual predisposition is generally inadmissible in civil or criminal sexual-misconduct cases, with limited exceptions: (1) evidence to show another person was the source of physical evidence; (2) evidence of specific prior sexual behavior with the accused, if similar and probative of a pattern bearing on consent; and (3) evidence required by the accused’s constitutional rights. In civil cases, such evidence is admissible only if probative value substantially outweighs harm. The bill requires a written pretrial motion (generally 15 days before trial), service on parties and the victim, an in camera hearing, and prohibits questioning victims in pretrial proceedings about matters inadmissible under the section. No state agencies or departments are named and no funding or appropriations are affected.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision that a victim may not be questioned during any pretrial hearing or proceeding — including depositions — about matters that are inadmissible under this section.
