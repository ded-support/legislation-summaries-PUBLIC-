Summary

Requires every public and private two- and four-year college or university in Nebraska to file quarterly reports with the Attorney General and electronically to the Legislature detailing "reportable funding" (foreign contracts, gifts, grants, donations, payments, etc., over $50) received from defined foreign sources. Reports must include amount, type and purpose, foreign source identity/address, and copies of related contracts; the Attorney General prescribes the reporting format and must post reports on the AG's website. The Attorney General is charged with enforcement and may impose civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation and order forfeiture of unreported funds. Payments by a natural person foreign payer for an individual student’s tuition or related fees are exempt. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds quarterly reporting duties for Nebraska public and private two- and four‑year colleges to submit to the Attorney General—and electronically to the Legislature—details and contracts for any “reportable funding” (foreign-source payments over $50). The Attorney General is authorized to prescribe the reporting format, post reports on the AG website, and enforce compliance, including civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation and forfeiture of unreported funds. Tuition paid by a foreign individual for their own enrollment is exempt. No changes to appropriations or agency budgets are included.
