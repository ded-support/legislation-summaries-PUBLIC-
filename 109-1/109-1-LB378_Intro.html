Summary

LB378 requires every public or private two‑ or four‑year college or university in Nebraska ("covered institution") to file quarterly reports, due the first day of each calendar quarter, to the Attorney General and electronically to the Legislature listing all foreign‑source funding exceeding $50 over the projected funding relationship. Reportable funding includes contracts, gifts, grants, endowments, payments, pledges, and related agreements. Reports must state amounts, purposes, whether ongoing, detailed foreign‑source identification (name/address), and attach related contracts/agreements. Payments by a natural person otherwise qualifying as a foreign source for an individual student’s tuition/fees are exempt. The Attorney General prescribes the reporting format, posts reports on the AG website, enforces the requirement, may assess civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation, and order forfeiture of unreported reportable funding. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a quarterly reporting requirement for Nebraska public and private two‑ and four‑year colleges to submit to the Attorney General (and electronically to the Legislature) detailed disclosures of all “reportable funding” from defined foreign sources, including amounts, purpose, source identity, and related contracts. The Attorney General prescribes the report format, posts reports publicly on the AG website, and enforces compliance, with civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation and forfeiture of unreported funds. Payments by private individuals who otherwise qualify as foreign sources for a student’s tuition/fees are exempt. No changes to appropriations, funding allocations, or budgetary provisions are included.
