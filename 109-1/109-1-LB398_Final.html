Summary

The bill expands agency duties, fee structures, and fund flows for DMV, DHHS, DOR, DOT, and the Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA). DMV: broadens issuance/mainta­­nance of organizational, military, veteran, disability, specialty and Space Force plates (Space Force plates effective 1/1/2026); may mail plates, adopt rules, verify veteran eligibility via the DVA registry, set wrecker/salvage reporting fees, and implement an updated operator‑license system (by 7/1/2032). Key fee/allocation changes: organizational plate fee cut from $70 to $40 (effective 10/1/2025) with revenue split 60% DMV Cash Fund / 40% Highway Trust Fund; $40 personalized-plate fee split 25% DMV Cash Fund / 75% Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund; $5 alphanumeric to that cemetery fund; $3 transfer/postage to DMV Cash Fund; driver‑record and electronic record access fees allocated to specified replacement, license plate, and records funds (bulk rates change 7/1/2026; $520 programming cap). DVA: maintains veteran registry and establishes Veterans Employment and Pets for Vets funds (fundable by section‑35 credits and appropriations). DHHS: administers Organ & Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund; Choose Life and Donate Life plate fees credited to DHHS/HHS funds to supplement TANF and donor programs. DOR: may receive SSNs for specified uses; Wildlife Conservation Fund may reimburse DOR for income‑tax designation administration; Treasurer transfers equal designated contributions. DOT: empowered to investigate/order railroad crossings; landowners share special‑crossing costs above $1,500.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: major additions—new and revised specialty plates (disabled veteran, Purple Heart, Space Force, many alternate plates), registration categories and fees (e.g., $1 commercial fertilizer trailer, RV fee tiers). New issuance rules (trusts may apply, certification/eligibility verification, CDL medical certification/CVIS/CLIS record update timelines). Personalized-plate fees: $40 initial/renewal for several types; portions remitted to funds as shown below.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: affected via fund receipts and eligibility—VA disability rating (≥10% service‑connected) required for certain disabled‑veteran plates; 75% of $40 personalized disabled‑veteran/Purple Heart fee credited to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund (benefiting veteran cemeteries).

- Department of Health and Human Services: receives alternate "Choose Life" plate proceeds to its Cash Fund to supplement federal TANF for DHHS programs.

- Department of Transportation: designated recipient — Highway Trust Fund receives proceeds from Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit plates (40% of $70 fee).

- Department of Revenue: no substantive program changes; bill directs remittance/crediting of plate and record fees to the State Treasurer with specified allocations among DMV Cash Fund, License Plate Cash Fund, Records Management Cash Fund, and other named funds.

- Other funding: Home of Arbor Day Plate Cash Fund created (administered by UN Board of Regents) to grant money to Nebraska Statewide Arboretum.
