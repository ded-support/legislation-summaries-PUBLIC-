Summary

The bill makes multiple agency-specific changes:

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): Revises organizational plate fees from $70 to $40 (effective 10/1/2025) with 60% to the DMV Cash Fund and 40% to the Highway Trust Fund; authorizes postage/handling and electronic reporting fees (credited to the DMV Cash Fund); expands veteran/special plate designs and requires DMV verification via the Department of Veterans’ Affairs (DVA) registry. Establishes new record‑access and bulk fees, reallocates driver‑record revenue (including $4 per record credited to a new Operator's License Services System Replacement and Maintenance Fund to build/maintain a replacement system by 7/1/2032), and sets record fee distributions (41 2/3% DMV Cash Fund, 25% General Fund, 33 1/3% Records Management Cash Fund). Requires fingerprint checks for issuance staff; allows SSN sharing with Department of Revenue.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA): Maintains veteran plate registry; creates Veterans Employment Program Fund and Pets for Vets Cash Fund funded by amounts credited under section 35 and possible legislative appropriations; funds may be invested.

- Department of Revenue (DOR): Authorizes use of the Wildlife Conservation Fund to pay DOR administration costs for the income‑tax designation program; that fund includes amounts credited under section 35 and a General Fund transfer equal to certain designated contributions.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Designated recipient of Choose Life plate proceeds (credited to DHHS Cash Fund to supplement TANF) and administrator of the Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): Implements complaint/investigation/hearing authority over railroad crossings; references federal motor‑carrier regulations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Authorizes many new alternate plates (including Space Force, disabled veteran, Purple Heart, Cornhusker spirit, Arbor Day, charity/awareness plates), sets effective dates (many Jan 1, 2026), new plate types/rules, RV and commercial fertilizer trailer registration fees ($18/$30/$42 and $1 respectively), and CDL medical-certification and CDLIS timing changes (medical certificate requirement and "not-certified" updates by implementation date no later than June 23, 2025). Fee splits: personalized veteran/Purple Heart plate applications $40; DMV receives 25% of that; Cornhusker plates $70 with 60% to DMV Cash Fund. Record-search and plate-fee allocations to License Plate Cash Fund and Records Management Cash Fund specified.

- Department of Health and Human Services: "Choose Life" plate fees credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement TANF.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: 75% of the $40 personalized veteran/Purple Heart plate fee is credited to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund (supporting veteran cemetery operations).

- Department of Transportation: Highway Trust Fund designated as recipient of 40% of Cornhusker plate fee revenue.

- Department of Revenue: No changes identified.
