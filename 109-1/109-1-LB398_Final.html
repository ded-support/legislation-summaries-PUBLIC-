Summary

The bill expands agency duties and creates/redirects several fees and funds:

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): broad authority to design, issue, deliver and administer many specialty plates (including military/veteran, Purple Heart, Cornhusker); accept prepaid applications; issue temporary stickers; collect NMVTIS electronic reporting fees from wreckers/salvage dealers; charge postage/handling and a $3 transfer fee remitted to the DMV Cash Fund. Organizational plate fee reduced from $70 to $40 effective Oct. 1, 2025; plate revenues split (commonly 60% DMV Cash Fund / 40% Highway Trust Fund); certain veteran fees ($5 alphanumeric; $40 personalized-message) are allocated to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund per stated splits. DMV CDL/records expansions include posting medical/self‑cert data, verification duties, portal access fees (per‑record and bulk rates), and creation of an Operator's License Services System Replacement and Maintenance Fund (implementation by 7/1/2032); investment earnings credited to the General Fund beginning 10/1/2024.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA): maintains veteran eligibility registry used by DMV; may receive veteran-designation display; creates Veterans Employment Program Fund and Pets for Vets Cash Fund (funded by credited monies/appropriations) and will administer those programs; funds may be invested.

- Department of Revenue: may receive CDL applicant SSNs under §77‑362.02; Wildlife Conservation Fund may cover DOR administrative costs for the income tax designation program, with State Treasurer transfers equal to designated contributions.

- Department of Transportation: authority to investigate/hear complaints and order changes to railroad crossings, with cost‑sharing rules for special crossings over $1,500.

- Department of Health and Human Services: designated recipient of Choose Life plate proceeds (Health and Human Services Cash Fund) and administrator of the Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund to support outreach and supplement TANF.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: Choose Life specialty plate fees will be credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement federal TANF funds for DHHS.

Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): Major additions—new and revised specialty plates (disabled veteran, Purple Heart, Space Force, Cornhusker, Arbor Day, etc.), eligibility rules (trust ownership allowed), issuance dates, plate types (alphanumeric and personalized), and enforcement of CDL medical self‑certification with deadlines and faster CLIS record updates. Fee changes: personalized-message disabled veteran and Purple Heart plates carry a $40 fee (25% to DMV Cash Fund; 75% to Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund); Cornhusker plates $70 (60% DMV Cash Fund; 40% to Highway Trust Fund); commercial fertilizer trailer registration $1 (effective Jan 1, 2026); RV registration schedules set; record-request fees allocated between License Plate Cash Fund and Records Management Cash Fund. DMV to remit fees to State Treasurer per allocations.

Department of Transportation: Highway Trust Fund is the designated recipient for Cornhusker plate fees (receives 40% of $70).

Department of Revenue: No substantive changes or budgetary allocations affecting the department in the added text.

Department of Veterans' Affairs: No direct funding or appropriation changes; federal VA certifications referenced for eligibility only.

Additional: Home of Arbor Day Plate Cash Fund created, administered by UN Board of Regents to award grants to the Nebraska Statewide Arboretum.
