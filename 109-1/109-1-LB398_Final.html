Summary

The bill makes multiple agency-specific changes:

- DMV: reduces organizational license‑plate fee from $70 to $40 (effective Oct 1, 2025); 60% of initial/renewal fees go to the DMV Cash Fund, 40% to the Highway Trust Fund. Allows postage/handling and $3 transfer fees (to DMV Cash Fund); authorizes electronic‑reporting fees for wrecker/salvage dealers. Creates new veteran/military/Purple Heart/disabled‑veteran/alternate plates (eligibility verified via the DVA registry). Sets plate fees ($5 alphanumeric to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund; $40 personalized with 25% to DMV Cash Fund and 75% to that cemetery fund). Revises driver‑record revenue splits (41 2/3% DMV Cash Fund; 25% General Fund; 33 1/3% Records Management Cash Fund), directs $4 per record to the Operator’s License Services System Replacement and Maintenance Fund, and creates that fund (investment earnings credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024). Expands DMV duties, system buildout, fee schedules, and staff training/background checks.

- Dept. of Veterans' Affairs: provides its registry for plate eligibility; establishes Veterans Employment Program Fund and Pets for Vets Cash Fund (funded by section 35 and appropriations; investable).

- Dept. of Transportation: may investigate railroad‑crossing complaints, hold hearings, order crossings/relocations; landowners pay half of special crossing costs exceeding $1,500.

- Dept. of Health and Human Services: Choose Life plate fees go to the HHS Cash Fund to supplement TANF; DHHS will administer an Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund funded by specified plate proceeds.

- Dept. of Revenue: may receive SSNs for specified purposes; the Wildlife Conservation Fund may cover administration costs for the income‑tax designation program and will receive a General Fund transfer equal to designated contributions (amount set by the Tax Commissioner).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions affecting named agencies:

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): New and revised specialty/alternate plates (Space Force, disabled veteran, Purple Heart, Cornhusker spirit, Arbor Day, Choose Life, Donate Life, etc.), new fee structures (e.g., $40 personalized disabled‑veteran/Purple Heart fee; $70 Cornhusker fee), allocations of collected fees to the DMV Cash Fund and designated recipients, a $1 registration fee for commercial fertilizer trailers (effective Jan 1, 2026), RV registration fee tiers, and CDL/medical‑certification compliance rules requiring DMV to update CDLIS records (including marking drivers “not‑certified,” downgrades and cancellations) with deadlines by June 23, 2025.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Choose Life plate proceeds are credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement TANF federal funds.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit plate fees direct part of proceeds to the Highway Trust Fund.

- Department of Veterans’ Affairs: Plate eligibility references VA service‑connected disability determinations; fee revenue (from certain personalized veteran/Purple Heart plate fees) directs 75% to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund to support veterans’ cemetery operations.

- Department of Revenue: No direct changes or appropriations affecting the Department of Revenue are included.
