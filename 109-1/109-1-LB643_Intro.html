Summary

LB643 allows a taxpayer to appeal to the Department of Revenue asserting that their single‑family residential property was offered for sale at fair market value for at least 90 days with no good‑faith offers. If the Department of Revenue sustains the appeal, the taxpayer may claim Nebraska income tax deductions for interest, taxes paid on, or maintenance of that property. The bill contains no provisions altering Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds exceptions and an appeal process administered by the Department of Revenue: individuals generally cannot claim Nebraska income tax deductions for interest, taxes, or maintenance on single-family residential property except for (a) their principal residence, (b) property owned by qualified nonprofits, or (c) owners who sold at least 10% of their single-family holdings during the year to owner-occupants or 5% to first-time homebuyers. It allows a taxpayer to appeal to the Department of Revenue showing a property was offered at fair market rate for 90 days with no good-faith offers; a successful appeal permits those deductions. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
