Summary

LB555 (2025) amends the Funeral Directing and Embalming Practice Act to create an "assistant funeral director" credential, define its supervised scope (funeral services, interments, family arrangements, and management of licensed funeral establishments), and require each assistant to have a written collaborative agreement with a licensed funeral director. The assistant is expressly prohibited from performing embalming; violation mandates immediate termination of the agreement and employment. Licensed funeral directors must supervise assistants and retain collaborative-agreement records. Disciplinary enforcement is to proceed under existing credentialing statutes (e.g., sections 38-178, 38-179, 38-196) and applies to department rules or other regulatory bodies. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are specified. The bill contains an emergency clause making it effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definition and limits for "assistant funeral director": they may perform principal funeral-directing functions (conduct services, arrange interments, work with families, manage operations) only under supervision and control of a licensed funeral director. Assistants are expressly prohibited from embalming; violation requires immediate termination of the collaborative agreement and the assistant’s employment in Nebraska. Employing funeral directors must enter into and retain collaborative agreements, supervise assistants, and keep records of duties. Also adds noncompliance with section 8 of the act as a listed failure. No government agencies or departments are referenced and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
