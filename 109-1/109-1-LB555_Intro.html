Summary

LB555 amends the Funeral Directing and Embalming Practice Act to create a credentialed assistant funeral director role. Assistants may perform principal funeral-directing functions (management, services, burial arrangements, family coordination) only under the supervision and control of a licensed funeral director; embalming is expressly prohibited and violation requires immediate termination of the collaborative agreement and employment. Licensed funeral directors must execute, supervise, and retain records of a collaborative agreement with each assistant. The bill amends disciplinary authority (section 38-1424) to include failure to comply with the collaborative-agreement requirement and references enforcement under existing statutory procedures (section 38-196) and rules of "the department or other body having regulatory powers." The act repeals original sections and takes effect upon enactment. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates an “assistant funeral director” role authorized to perform principal funeral-directing functions (e.g., conducting services, arranging interments, working with families, daily management of a licensed funeral establishment) under the supervision and control of a licensed funeral director. Employers must enter written collaborative agreements, supervise assistants, and retain records of agreements and assigned functions. Assistants are expressly prohibited from embalming; violation triggers immediate termination of the collaborative agreement and employment in Nebraska. Also references enforcement via noncompliance with section 8. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes in the added text.
