Summary

LB555 amends the Funeral Directing and Embalming Practice Act to create an assistant funeral director credential, requiring a collaborative agreement and supervision by a licensed funeral director, recordkeeping, and prohibition on embalming (violation results in immediate termination of the agreement and employment). The bill adds failure to comply with the collaborative agreement as a grounds for disciplinary action under section 38-1424 and subjects credentials to discipline pursuant to section 38-196 and applicable department or other regulatory body rules. It repeals the prior versions of amended sections and includes an emergency clause. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an assistant funeral director classification under the Funeral Directing and Embalming Practice Act: assistants may perform principal funeral-directing functions (services, interments, family arrangements, daily management) but may not perform embalming. A licensed funeral director must enter into and supervise a written collaborative agreement with each assistant and retain records of the agreement and the assistant’s functions. Violation of the embalming prohibition or failure to comply with section 8 results in immediate termination of the collaborative agreement and employment in Nebraska. No state agency or department is named, and no funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
