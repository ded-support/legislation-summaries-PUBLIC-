Summary

LB633 amends §79-239 and creates the Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program. Key state entities: State Board of Education (board), State Department of Education, and the Commissioner of Education. Changes require rejecting school districts to send written notices specifying reasons, appeal procedures to the State Board, and the option to decline appeal and request a tuition account. Parents may appeal to the State Board (hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act; district bears burden on capacity defenses) or request an account, which the board must issue upon timely request. Districts must annually report rejected-application data to the State Department; the Commissioner compiles and electronically reports to the Legislature each September. The board may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or contracting and may adopt rules. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program and assigns administrative duties to the State Board of Education. If a parent/guardian submits a request plus a copy of a public school district rejection notice to the Board within 30 days of notification, the Board must issue an option enrollment tuition account. Defines terms (Board, eligible student, parent, program). An eligible student meets compulsory attendance requirements if program conditions are met. Clarifies the State of Nebraska gains no additional authority over public, private, or denominational schools via the program. The Board may adopt rules and may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or to contract with a third party.
