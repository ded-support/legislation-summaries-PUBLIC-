Summary

LB633 amends section 79-239 and creates the Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program. Key agency actions and requirements:

- School districts must annually submit prescribed data on enrollment-option application rejections to the State Department of Education (number rejected, reasons, disability status, provision of capacity information).

- The Commissioner of Education compiles those submissions and reports electronically to the Legislature annually (beginning Sept. 1, 2024).

- Parents may appeal rejections to the State Board of Education (hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act; districts bear burden to prove capacity limits) or decline appeal and request a State Board‑issued option enrollment tuition account for the student.

- The State Board may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or contracting and may adopt rules.

- The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program administration by the State Board of Education. Parents (or guardians) have 30 days after receiving a district rejection to send a request and the rejection notice to the State Board, which must then issue an option enrollment tuition account. Defines key terms (Board = State Board of Education; eligible student; parent; program). An eligible student remains subject to compulsory attendance rules if complying with program requirements. Clarifies the State cannot use program participation to expand control over public, private, or denominational schools or require their admission/continued enrollment. Budget: the State Board may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or contract with a third party. The Board may adopt rules and regulations to implement the program.
