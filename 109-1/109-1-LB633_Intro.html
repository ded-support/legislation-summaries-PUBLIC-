Summary

LB633 creates the Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program and assigns administration and oversight to the State Board of Education. School districts rejecting option-enrollment applications must notify parents of appeal rights and the option to request a tuition account. Parents may (a) appeal rejections to the State Board of Education (hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act; districts bear burden to justify capacity limits) or (b) request an option enrollment tuition account, which the Board must issue. Each district must annually report rejected-application data to the State Department of Education; the Commissioner of Education will compile and electronically report those data to the Legislature each September. The State Board may adopt rules, use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration (or contract third parties), and the act takes effect immediately as an emergency.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures making the State Board of Education responsible for receiving a parent's 30-day request (with the school rejection notice) to decline an appeal and issue a Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account. Defines terms (Board = State Board of Education; Eligible student = applicant rejected by a public district; Parent; Program name). Clarifies eligible students meet compulsory attendance if they and their parent comply with program rules. States the program does not grant the State of Nebraska authority over private/denominational schools or require their admission/continued enrollment. Fiscal change: the State Board may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or to contract with a third party. The Board may promulgate rules.
