Summary

LB633 amends section 79-239 and creates the "Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program," assigning primary roles to the State Board of Education and the State Department of Education. Rejecting school districts must provide certified written notice to parents explaining reasons for rejection and advising parents of (1) the right to appeal to the State Board under the Administrative Procedure Act or (2) the right to decline appeal and request a tuition/scholarship account. Parents have 30 days to file either action; upon receipt the State Board must issue an option enrollment tuition account. Beginning July 1, 2024, school districts must annually submit prescribed data on rejected applications to the Commissioner of Education; the Commissioner compiles and reports to the Legislature annually. The State Board may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or contracting and may adopt rules. The act is effective on passage (emergency clause).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a process letting parents who decline to appeal a rejected enrollment option request an “option enrollment tuition” (scholarship) account from the State Board of Education. The request plus a copy of the rejection notice must be received by the State Board within 30 days of the parent's notification; upon receipt the Board must issue the account. Defines terms (Board = State Board of Education; eligible student = student whose application was rejected; parent; Program = Nebraska Option Enrollment Tuition Account Program). An eligible student satisfies compulsory attendance if parent and student meet program requirements. Clarifies the State has no new authority over participating schools. Budget: the State Board may use up to 5% of scholarship account funds for program administration or contracting. The Board may adopt rules to implement the program.
