Summary

LB645A appropriates funds to the Public Employees Retirement Board (Program 41) to implement Legislative Bill 645. For FY2025‑26 the bill directs $40,000 from the School Expense Fund and $26,000 from the State Patrol Expense Fund. No funds are appropriated for FY2026‑27. Of the FY2025‑26 appropriation, total expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $20,000 (and $0 for FY2026‑27). The act contains an emergency clause and takes effect upon passage and approval.
