Summary

References to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) in LB598:

- Transfers to Education Future Fund: The State Treasurer is directed to transfer $1,000,000,000 (FY2023-24) and $250,000,000 (FY2024-25) from the General Fund to the Education Future Fund "on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services." The bill expresses intent for $250,000,000 annual transfers thereafter.

- Payment certification and warrants: The Department of Education must certify state aid amounts to the Director of Administrative Services. For special education reimbursements, the State Treasurer will notify the Director of Administrative Services of available funds in the General Fund and Education Future Fund, and the Director shall draw warrants against such appropriated funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (focus: Department of Administrative Services)

The added provisions require the State Department of Education to accept LEP funding applications by Jan 15 and pay eligible school districts from the Education Future Fund by Jan 31. Eligibility/calculation: take half of the prior-year LEP expenditures, multiply by 107%, and pay the difference between current-year LEP expenditures and that amount. If total awards exceed the Fund balance, payments are reduced proportionally. Beginning 2025‑26, the department must also reimburse districts/cooperatives for additional costs under Section 504 at the same percentage used for special education reimbursement; these payments are supplemental and the department will set reporting rules. No changes, appropriations, or budgetary responsibilities are assigned to the Department of Administrative Services in the added text.
