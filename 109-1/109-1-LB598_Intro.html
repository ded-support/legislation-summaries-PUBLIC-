Summary

LB598 assigns several specific roles to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). The State Department of Education must certify state aid amounts to the Director of Administrative Services on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 annually. The State Treasurer is directed to transfer $1.0 billion (FY2023‑24) and $250 million (FY2024‑25) from the General Fund to the Education Future Fund on dates and in amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of DAS; the bill expresses intent for $250 million annually thereafter. For special education reimbursements, the State Treasurer notifies the Director of Administrative Services of available funds in the General and Education Future Funds, and the Director draws warrants against those appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text change duties, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Administrative Services.

Substantive additions affect the Department of Education: school districts may apply (by Jan 15) for Education Future Fund payments to cover extraordinary limited-English-proficiency (LEP) costs, with a formula-based maximum payment and pro rata reduction if fund balance is insufficient; payments issued by Jan 31. Beginning FY 2025‑26, the department must also reimburse districts/cooperatives for additional costs of students served under Section 504 at the same percentage used for special education reimbursement, establish reporting/documentation procedures, and treat this as additional (not replacement) funding.
