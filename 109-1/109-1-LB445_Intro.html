Summary

- Establishes the State Building Construction Alternatives Act to be administered by the state building division of the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), including procurement processes (design‑build, construction manager‑GC) and discretionary payment of stipends to unsuccessful design‑build proposers.

- DAS must adjust the dollar threshold for projects subject to bid every four years beginning Jan 1, 2026, using a construction cost index.

- The DAS division will remit rental proceeds to the State Building Revolving Fund and the State Building Renewal Assessment Fund and administer the State Emergency Capital Construction Contingency Fund.

- Disbursements from the Nebraska Arts Council Cash Fund are to be made on warrants drawn by the Director of Administrative Services.

- Buildings/grounds owned, leased, or operated by the Department of Correctional Services and nonoffice leased facilities for the Department of Transportation are exempt from certain DAS facilities‑administration duties.

- The bill contains no new explicit appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Adds/clarifies DAS authority and procedures under the State Building Construction Alternatives Act to use design‑build and construction manager–general contractor procurements. Requires short‑listing, minimum two proposers when possible, detailed RFP content, confidentiality of alternative technical concepts, stipend payments to unsuccessful responsive proposers (with stipend conferring IP to the state), mandatory cost weighting of at least 50% in evaluations, staged negotiations with ranked proposers, and authority to cancel or re‑solicit. DAS must adjust specified dollar thresholds every four years beginning Jan 1, 2026, using a chosen construction‑cost index. Also adds reporting obligations to provide a report upon request to the Appropriations Committee, Building Maintenance Committee, or any legislator.

- Department of Correctional Services: No changes apparent.

- Department of Transportation: No changes apparent.
