Summary

The bill directs the Department of Labor, in collaboration with the Department of Economic Development, to establish: (1) a grant program (max $20 million/year) for local educational institutions and nonprofits to develop and operate digital skills training centers in qualified census tracts; and (2) a grant program (max $20 million/year) to cover tuition, materials, transportation, equipment, and child care for low-income individuals attending such centers. The Department of Labor, with Economic Development and industry input, must develop standardized, industry-aligned curricula and run employer/industry partnership and apprenticeship supports. The Legislature intends to appropriate $50 million annually from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the Department of Labor beginning FY2025-26 for these grants, and the fund statute is amended to expressly authorize such grants.

- Defines "qualified census tract" by reference to 26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I) as of Jan 1, 2025.

- Department of Labor, with the Department of Economic Development and tech‑industry input, must: create two grant programs—(a) grants to local educational institutions/nonprofits to develop and operate digital skills training centers located in qualified census tracts (cap: $20 million per fiscal year); (b) grants to cover tuition, materials, transportation, equipment, and child care for low‑income individuals to attend such centers (cap: $20 million per fiscal year).

- DOL/DED to develop standardized, industry‑aligned curricula (core skills, specialty tracks, bootcamps). DOL will partner with employers and grantees to support apprenticeships, job placement, and career advancement.

- Legislative intent to appropriate $50 million annually from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to DOL beginning FY2025‑26 for these grants.
