Summary

The bill directs the Department of Labor, in collaboration with the Department of Economic Development, to: establish two grant programs—(1) up to $20 million per fiscal year for local educational institutions/nonprofits to develop digital skills training centers in qualified census tracts; and (2) up to $20 million per fiscal year to cover tuition, materials, transportation, equipment, and child care for low-income individuals attending such centers. The Department of Labor, with Economic Development and industry input, must develop standardized, industry‑aligned curricula (core skills, specialty tracks, and bootcamps) and shall partner with technology employers to support apprenticeships, job placement, advancement, and outreach. The Legislature intends to appropriate $50 million annually from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the Department of Labor beginning FY2025‑26 for grants under the Digital Skills Empowerment Act. Section 81‑407 is amended to authorize the Department of Labor to administer the Fund for these grants.

Summary (focus: Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor)

- The Department of Labor (DOL), working with the Department of Economic Development (DED) and technology industry input, must create two grant programs: (1) up to $20 million/year for local educational institutions/nonprofits to develop and operate digital skills training centers in qualified census tracts; (2) up to $20 million/year to cover tuition, materials, transportation, equipment, and child care for low‑income individuals attending those centers.

- DOL will set application requirements, award priorities if funds are insufficient, develop industry‑aligned standardized curricula (core skills, special tracks, bootcamps), and partner with tech employers to support apprenticeships, job placement, and career progression.

- Intent to appropriate $50 million annually from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to DOL starting FY2025‑26 for grants under the Digital Skills Empowerment Act. DED’s role is collaborative; no direct DED appropriation is specified.
