Summary

LB701 amends section 68-901 (public assistance law) to (1) state legislative intent and provide for reimbursement of doula services as prescribed, (2) establish a work group to develop an implementation plan (including determinations of appropriate training, certification, or experience for doulas), and (3) define "doula" as a nonclinical professional who provides prenatal, labor/delivery, and postpartum emotional, physical, and informational support and who shall not perform clinical tasks or diagnose/treat. The bill repeals the original section 68-901. No specific state agencies or departments are named in the provided text (the bill was referred to the Health and Human Services Committee), and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "doula" as a trained professional providing emotional, physical, and informational support before, during, and after labor (including prenatal visit attendance, labor support, and postpartum resources). Training, certification, or experience requirements will be set by an implementation plan produced by the work group referenced in subdivision (3)(a). Doulas are explicitly barred from performing clinical/medical tasks or diagnosing or treating. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
