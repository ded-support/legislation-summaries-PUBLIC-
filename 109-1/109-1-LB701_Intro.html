Summary

LB701 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68‑901 (public assistance) to state legislative intent to reimburse doula services, require a work group to develop an implementation plan, and add a statutory definition of “doula” (including training/certification criteria and a prohibition on performing clinical/medical tasks). The bill was referred to the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee and repeals the original §68‑901. No specific state agencies are named in the excerpt and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "doula" as a trained professional providing emotional, physical, and informational support before, during, and after labor (including prenatal visit attendance, delivery support, and postpartum resources). Requires doulas to have training, certification, or experience as set by an implementation plan developed by the work group in subdivision (3)(a). Prohibits doulas from performing clinical/medical tasks or diagnosing/treating. No specific state agencies are named and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
