Summary

LB701 amends section 68-901 (public assistance) to express legislative intent to reimburse doula services, establishes a work group (see subdivision (3)(a)) to develop an implementation plan, and defines "doula" as a trained support person who may attend prenatal visits, labor, and postpartum care. The bill requires appropriate training, certification, or experience as set by the work group’s implementation plan and prohibits doulas from performing clinical/medical tasks or diagnosing/treating. It repeals the original section 68-901. The text contains no specific agency names, nor does it specify new appropriations, funding amounts, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "doula" as a trained professional providing emotional, physical, and informational support before, during, and after labor — including attending prenatal visits, supporting delivery, and offering postpartum resources. Requires doulas to have training, certification, or experience as specified by an implementation plan developed by the work group referenced in subdivision (3)(a). Explicitly prohibits doulas from performing clinical or medical tasks or diagnosing/treating. No specific state agencies, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
