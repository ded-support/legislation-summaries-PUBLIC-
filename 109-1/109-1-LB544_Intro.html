Summary

LB544 amends §48-601 of the Employment Security Law to disqualify claimants from unemployment benefits for any week in which they (1) fail to respond to a job offer within two business days or (2) fail to appear for a scheduled job interview without notifying the prospective employer. The bill requires "the department" charged with administering the law to establish an employer reporting system (website portal, email address, and telephone hotline) and to verify employer-submitted reports before denying benefits for the cited weeks. The original §48-601 is repealed and replaced. No specific appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds three requirements: (1) individuals are disqualified from benefits for any week they fail to respond to a job offer within two business days or fail to show for a scheduled interview without notifying the prospective employer; (2) "the department" must create a web portal, email address, and telephone hotline for employers to report such failures; and (3) before denying benefits under this section the department must verify any employer-submitted information. No specific agency beyond "the department" is named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
