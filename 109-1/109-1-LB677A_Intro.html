Summary

Appropriates $1 from the Medical Cannabis Control Fund to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (Program 96) for FY2025-26 and $1 for FY2026-27 to aid implementation of Legislative Bill 677. Expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from these appropriated funds are capped at $1 for each fiscal year. The act includes an emergency clause, making it effective upon passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates $1 in FY2025‑26 and $1 in FY2026‑27 from the Medical Cannabis Control Fund to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (Program 96) to support implementation of Legislative Bill 677 (109th Leg., 1st Sess., 2025). Specifies that total expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from these appropriated amounts shall not exceed $1 in each fiscal year.
