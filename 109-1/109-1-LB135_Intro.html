Summary

LB135 standardizes election procedures for school‑related fiscal measures by requiring bond, levy, budget‑override, lease‑purchase, teacherage, and special‑tax questions involving school districts to be submitted in compliance with subsection (3) of §32‑559. It shifts operational responsibility to election commissioners and county clerks (certification, ballot preparation, early‑voting distribution, counting) for those school elections, authorizes election commissioners/county clerks to bill school districts directly for special‑election costs (§32‑1203), and requires 50‑day notice for joint‑entity bond elections that include school districts or ESUs (§13‑809, §13‑2507). The bill amends deadlines and notice/filing duties tied to excess levy and budget votes (§77‑3444, §13‑519) and references reporting to the Property Tax Administrator, Secretary of State, county boards, and the Auditor of Public Accounts. Several prior statutes are repealed. No new appropriations are created.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural requirements making school-district ballot measures subject to subsection (3) of §32-559 and assigns duties to school boards, election commissioners, and county clerks. Key agency actions:

- School boards must file/certify questions by set deadlines (Mar. 1 for primary, Sept. 1 for general, eighth Friday before a local general election).

- Election commissioners or county clerks must give first-class mail notice ≥30 days before the vote, publish the sample ballot 7–15 days prior, furnish early-voting ballots ≥21 days before the election, and count ballots with two disinterested persons.

- County clerks/election commissioners must coordinate across counties (certify registration books, forward early-vote requests); school boards must certify out‑of‑county voter lists ≥5 days before the election.

- Returns are canvassed by the school board.

Budget: the two disinterested counters must be paid at no less than the minimum wage set in §48-1203. No other appropriations or funding changes.
