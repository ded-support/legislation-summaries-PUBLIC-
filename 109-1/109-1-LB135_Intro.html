Summary

LB135 standardizes election procedures for school-related bond, levy, budget and tax questions by requiring those votes to be held "in compliance with subsection (3) of section 32-559." Key agency roles and changes:

- Election commissioners and county clerks: conduct school elections, prepare/count ballots, receive certifications, and may bill school districts directly for special-election costs under section 32-559/32-1203.

- Secretary of State: receives timely cancellations/withdrawals of ballot issues.

- Property Tax Administrator: must be notified of allocated property-tax levy authority to joint public agencies.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: receives records when residents approve budget exceedances at meetings.

- County boards: authorize levies approved by voters; joint public agencies must hold elections before pledging allocated levy authority for bonds.

The bill imposes no new appropriations but shifts election procedure, notice, timing, and cost-allocation responsibilities among these agencies and repeals several superseded statutory sections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions repeatedly require school‑district elections to comply with subsection (3) of §32‑559 and assign roles/responsibilities to these government actors: school boards (must file/certify ballot questions by March 1 for statewide primary, September 1 for statewide general, and the eighth Friday before a subdivision general election; must certify out‑of‑county registered‑voter lists ≥5 days before the election and receive returns for canvass); election commissioners and county clerks (conduct elections, furnish/count ballots, appoint two disinterested counters, receive ballots from receiving boards, prepare early‑voting ballots ≥21 days prior, and coordinate certification/ballot distribution when a district spans counties); receiving boards. Notice rules: first‑class mail ≥30 days and single newspaper sample‑ballot publication 7–15 days before. Fiscal item: the two appointed counting persons must be paid at no less than the hourly minimum set in §48‑1203. No other appropriations specified.
