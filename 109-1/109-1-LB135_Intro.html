Summary

This bill standardizes election procedures for school-related bond, levy, budget and tax questions by requiring such elections to be held in compliance with subsection (3) of §32‑559. Key agency impacts and changes:

- Election commissioners and county clerks: designated to conduct school bond/levy elections (prepare ballots, early voting, ballot counting with two disinterested persons), receive clerk certifications by statutory deadlines, and may bill school districts directly for special-election costs under §32‑1203. Political subdivisions remain liable for costs if they cancel (§32‑559).

- School boards: must file orders/certifications and follow §32‑559 notice/timing requirements; may be billed for election costs.

- Joint entities / joint public agencies and joint public agencies including school districts: prohibited from issuing bonds or pledging levy authority without elector approval under §32‑559; levy allocations must be reported to the Property Tax Administrator (§13‑2507).

- Secretary of State: receives timely cancellation/withdrawal notices; Auditor of Public Accounts: receives records when voters approve exceeding growth at meetings (§13‑519).

- County boards: retain role authorizing excess levies after voter approval (§77‑3444).

Several existing sections are repealed; no direct appropriations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for school-district ballot measures and clarifies roles for school boards, election commissioners, county clerks, receiving boards and multi‑county election coordination. Key points: school bond/levy questions must comply with §32‑559; school boards must file ballot orders by specified deadlines (March 1 for primaries, Sept. 1 for general, eighth Friday before a political‑subdivision general). Notice requirements: first‑class mail at least 30 days and publish a sample ballot 7–15 days before the election (not required to be given by the election commissioner/county clerk). Early‑voting ballots must be ready 21 days prior; ballots are counted by the election commissioner or county clerk with two disinterested appointees; multi‑county certification/ballot distribution rules and a five‑day voter list certification are added. The two disinterested counters must be paid at no less than the minimum hourly rate in §48‑1203. No other appropriations stated.
