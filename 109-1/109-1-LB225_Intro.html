Summary

LB225 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-168 governing duplicate vehicle certificates of title. It continues to authorize the Department (state motor vehicle department) or any county treasurer to issue duplicates on a department-prescribed form upon payment of the fee set in §60-156, and requires the application to be signed by the applicant or an authorized signer under §60-151. County treasurers may issue duplicates with department approval or when title records have been destroyed under §60-152 upon sufficient proof; applicants lacking proof may apply directly to the Department, which may authorize a county treasurer to issue a duplicate. Duplicates will show only unreleased liens; procedures for new purchasers and indemnity/cancellation remain. The bill repeals the original section. No changes to appropriations or funding are included.
