Summary

LB89 (Stand With Women Act) requires public schools, public postsecondary educational institutions, private schools, private postsecondary institutions subject to the Act, and athletic associations that sponsor interscholastic athletics to maintain and implement policies providing separate athletic teams based on sex. The bill defines "female," "male," and related terms and directs the governing body of each covered school/institution to adopt an implementing policy (including provisions on visitor/public conduct). Postsecondary institutions are defined by membership in accrediting bodies recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. Individuals born with differences in sex development retain protections under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act. The bill contains a severability clause. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds findings supporting sex-separated athletics and defines key terms (athletic association; boy, girl, man, male, female defined by reproductive biology; postsecondary educational institution as Nebraska colleges/universities/community colleges that are members of an accreditor recognized by the U.S. Department of Education; private and public schools). Requires governing bodies of affected public and private K‑12 and Nebraska postsecondary institutions to adopt policies implementing the Stand With Women Act, including visitor/public conduct provisions. Specifies individuals born with a disorder or difference in sex development receive protections/accommodations under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
