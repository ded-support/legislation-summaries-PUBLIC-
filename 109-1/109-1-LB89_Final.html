Summary

LB89 (Stand With Women Act) requires public school and public postsecondary institution governing bodies — and private schools/postsecondary institutions subject to the Act — to adopt policies implementing sex‑segregated athletic teams and related visitor/public conduct rules. The bill defines "postsecondary educational institution" as an institution in Nebraska that is a member of an accrediting body recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. It also states that individuals born with differences in sex development retain protections and accommodations under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act. The bill contains a severability clause. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions (athletic association; boy/girl/man/woman/male/female; postsecondary educational institution; private/public school) and requires governing bodies of Nebraska public and private K–12 and postsecondary institutions subject to the "Stand With Women Act" to adopt implementing policies (including visitor/public conduct) to maintain separate athletic teams by sex. References postsecondary institutions as those accredited by a body recognized by the United States Department of Education. States individuals born with a disorder or difference in sex development receive protections and accommodations under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
