Summary

LB89 ("Stand With Women Act") requires the governing body of each public school and public postsecondary educational institution in Nebraska, and applicable private schools and postsecondary institutions, to adopt policies implementing the Act (including visitor/public conduct provisions) that maintain separate interscholastic athletic teams based on the sex definitions set forth. The bill references accrediting bodies recognized by the U.S. Department of Education for postsecondary institutions and affirms that individuals with disorders or differences of sex development retain protections/accommodations under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions (athletic association; boy/girl/man/male/female with reproductive-based definitions; postsecondary educational institution defined as Nebraska colleges/universities/community colleges that are members of an accrediting body recognized by the U.S. Department of Education; private and public school). Requires the governing body of each public school, public postsecondary institution, and each private school and private postsecondary institution subject to the Stand With Women Act to adopt a policy implementing the Act (including visitor/public conduct provisions). States individuals born with a disorder/difference in sex development receive protections and accommodations under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
