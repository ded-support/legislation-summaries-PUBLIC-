Summary

LB156 amends section 81-8,219 to carve out that, in adoption or placement cases, the State Tort Claims Act applies to claims alleging misrepresentation or deceit by the Department of Health and Human Services. Specifically, DHHS may be liable for failing to warn, notify, or inform regarding a ward's mental and behavioral health history, educational history, and medical history, including any history as a victim or perpetrator of sexual abuse. The bill contains no appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language expands covered incidents to include sexual assaults of a child that occur: on school grounds; in vehicles owned, leased, or contracted by a school and used for school purposes; in vehicles driven for a school purpose by a school employee or designated person; or at school‑sponsored activities or athletic events. There are no provisions in the added text that alter Department of Health and Human Services authorities, programs, appropriations, or budget allocations. No DHHS funding or appropriation changes are specified.
