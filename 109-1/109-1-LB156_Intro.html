Summary

LB156 amends the State Tort Claims Act (section 81-8,219) to create a specific exception concerning the Department of Health and Human Services. While misrepresentation or deceit claims generally remain excluded, the bill provides that, in adoption or placement cases, the State Tort Claims Act does apply to claims against DHHS for misrepresentation or deceit in failing to warn, notify, or inform about a ward’s mental and behavioral health history, educational history, and medical history, including any history as a victim or perpetrator of sexual abuse. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language defines covered offenses as those arising from a sexual assault of a child that occurred: (i) on school grounds; (ii) in a vehicle owned, leased, or contracted by a school and used for a school purpose; (iii) in a vehicle being driven for a school purpose by a school employee or designee; or (iv) at a school‑sponsored activity or athletic event. There is no mention of the Department of Health and Human Services, nor are there any funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes affecting DHHS.
