Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services to implement, beginning FY2025‑26, an increase to reimbursement rates paid to providers of developmental disability services equal to 150% of the state minimum wage. The Legislature states intent to appropriate federal funds (amount shown as $XXX) beginning FY2025‑26 to the department for these reimbursement rate increases; those funds are to be increased annually by the change in the core Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers and may be used only for this purpose. The act takes effect immediately upon passage.
