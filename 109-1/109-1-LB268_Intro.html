Summary

LB268 directs the Department of Health and Human Services to implement, beginning FY2025-26, an increase to Medicaid reimbursement rates for providers of developmental disability services equal to 150% of the state minimum wage. The bill states the Legislature's intent to appropriate an unspecified amount ("$XXX") of federal funds beginning in FY2025-26 to the department to fund those increased reimbursement rates; those appropriations are to be adjusted annually by the change in the core Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers and may be used only for this purpose. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Health and Human Services: beginning in FY 2025–26 DHHS must raise reimbursement rates for developmental disability service providers to an amount equal to 150% of the state minimum wage. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $XXX in federal funds starting FY 2025–26 to DHHS to cover these reimbursement increases. That appropriation is to be adjusted annually by the change in the core Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI‑W), and the funds are restricted for use solely to pay these provider reimbursement increases.
