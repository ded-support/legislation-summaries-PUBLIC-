Summary

LB250 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §8-126 to set residency expectations for a majority of bank board members—primary residence in Nebraska, within 25 miles of the bank’s main office, in the county of the main office, or in counties where the bank has branches. It requires that prospective bank directors obtain approval from the Department of Banking and Finance before serving. The Director of Banking and Finance may, after notice and hearing, revoke such approval if a director is found to be operating unsafely or unauthorized, and may adopt rules and prescribe forms to implement the section. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
