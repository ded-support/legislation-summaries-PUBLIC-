Summary

LB250 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §8-126 to revise qualifications and oversight for bank directors. It requires reasonable efforts that a majority of a bank’s board have primary residences: in Nebraska, within 25 miles of the bank’s main office, in the main-office county, or in counties where branches are located. Directors must be of good moral character, with integrity and business experience. No person may serve as a director until the bank obtains approval from the Department of Banking and Finance. The Director of Banking and Finance may revoke approval after notice and hearing if a director acts unsafely or unauthorizedly, and may adopt rules and forms to implement the section. The bill repeals the original section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
