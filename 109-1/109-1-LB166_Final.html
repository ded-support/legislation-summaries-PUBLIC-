Summary

LB166 amends public-records and candidate-filing rules and names the government offices affected. County assessors, registers of deeds, and now county treasurers must withhold residential addresses of law‑enforcement officers, Nebraska National Guard members (when activated), and judges upon a prescribed application; the county assessor must notify the register of deeds and county treasurer and withholding lasts five years (renewable). The Secretary of State must treat judicial retention requests as nonpublic records but publish an electronic list of names and districts of those filing for retention. Candidate filing rules specify filing officers: the Secretary of State for multi‑county/state offices; election commissioners or county clerks for county, school-district, and city/village offices. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that any written request filed with the Secretary of State (including those involving county treasurer and judicial-office matters) is exempt from public-record disclosure under section 84-712.01 and related public-record statutes. The only disclosure requirement is that the Secretary of State must publish, electronically, a list identifying judicial officeholders who filed for retention in that calendar year, including each name and the district or area served. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are specified.
