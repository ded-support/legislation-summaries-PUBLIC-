Summary

LB166 amends public‑records and candidate‑filing statutes and affects several county and state offices:

- County assessor, register of deeds, and now county treasurer must withhold residential addresses of law‑enforcement officers, Nebraska National Guard members, and judges who apply. Applications (including ID/proof and parcel number) trigger a five‑year confidentiality period, renewable every five years; the assessor must notify the register and treasurer.

- Judicial officeholders’ written retention requests filed with the Secretary of State are excluded from public‑record disclosure, but the Secretary of State must publish an electronic list of names and districts of those filing for retention.

- Candidate filing forms must include residence address (email optional). Forms for multi‑county offices are filed with the Secretary of State; county/city filings go to election commissioners or county clerks.

No appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that any written request filed with the Secretary of State is not a public record under section 84-712.01 and is exempt from disclosure under sections 84-712 to 84-712.09 or other law. The sole exception requires the Secretary of State to electronically publish a list of judicial officeholders who filed for retention that calendar year, showing each name and the district/area served. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
