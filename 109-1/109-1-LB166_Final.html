Summary

Bill LB166 amends public‑records and candidate‑filing statutes. It requires the county treasurer (in addition to county assessors and registers of deeds) to withhold residential addresses of judges, law‑enforcement officers, and certain Nebraska National Guard members who apply, with five‑year renewals. It makes written retention requests by judicial officeholders filed with the Secretary of State nonpublic under section 84‑712.01 and otherwise exempt from disclosure, while requiring the Secretary of State to electronically publish a list of names and respective districts/areas seeking retention. It clarifies candidate filing form contents and confirms filing officers: Secretary of State for multi‑county/state offices and election commissioners or county clerks for county, city, and most school‑district filings. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Secretary of State to treat any written request filed with the office as non‑public under Neb. Rev. Stat. §84‑712.01 and exempt from disclosure under §§84‑712 to 84‑712.09 and other law. The sole exception is that the Secretary of State must electronically publish an annual list of judicial officeholders who filed for retention that calendar year, showing each name and the district or area served. No agencies beyond the Secretary of State (and the referenced judicial officeholders) are tasked with duties, and no funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
