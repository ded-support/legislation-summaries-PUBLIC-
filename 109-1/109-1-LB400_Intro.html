Summary

LB400 amends the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act (section 48-1,110, Reissue Revised Statutes) to establish rebuttable presumptions that certain cancers in firefighters arose out of and in the course of employment. Key administrative points: the presumptions apply to currently employed firefighters (diagnosed during employment); to retired professional firefighters with diagnosis timing tied to years of service (different rules for retirements before vs. on/after Jan. 1, 2026); and to volunteer firefighters regardless of retirement date. Purchased service credit in a retirement system may not be used to calculate years of service for these presumptions. Claims filed under the post-2026 retiree rule are limited to medical benefits only. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds specific carcinogen findings (hydrocarbons linked to urinary tract/ureteral cancer; benzene and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons linked to uterine cancer) and creates rebuttable presumptions that firefighter cancers are work‑related: any cancer diagnosed during employment; for retirees (professionals retiring before Jan 1, 2026, and all volunteers) diagnosed within up to 60 months after last work, using a period = 3 months × full years worked; for professionals retiring on/after Jan 1, 2026, diagnosed within a post‑separation period equal to years worked if <20 years, or at any time for ≥20 years. Purchased retirement service credit cannot be used to calculate years. Claims under subsection (7) after retirement are limited under the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act to medical benefits only. No funding, appropriation, or specific agency budgets are changed.
