Summary

Bill LB400 amends the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act (section 48-1,110) to create rebuttable presumptions that certain cancers in firefighters arose out of and in the course of employment. It references known carcinogens (including agents classified by the International Agency for Research on Cancer). Key provisions: presumptions apply if cancer is diagnosed during employment; distinct presumptive windows for retirees (a 60‑month cap with a formula of three months per year for certain retirees; revised rules effective Jan 1, 2026, tying presumptions to years of service with lifetime coverage for those with 20+ years); purchased retirement service credit cannot be used to calculate years of service; claims under the post‑2026 retiree rule are limited to medical benefits only. Agencies/departments: the Nebraska Workers' Compensation system and unspecified retirement systems are implicated; IARC is referenced. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds statutory rebuttable presumptions that certain cancers (including those linked to benzene and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons) are work‑related for firefighters. Key provisions:

- Immediate presumption if cancer is diagnosed during active employment.

- For retirees: a rebuttable presumption within a period up to 60 months after last work, multiplied (3 months × full years worked); applies to professional firefighters retiring before Jan 1, 2026, and all volunteer retirees.

- For professional firefighters retiring on/after Jan 1, 2026: if <20 years’ service, presumption lasts a period equal to years worked; if ≥20 years, presumption is lifetime.

- Purchased retirement service credit cannot be used to calculate years for these rules.

- Claimants under the post‑2026 rule are limited to medical benefits under the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Act.

No agencies are newly created and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
