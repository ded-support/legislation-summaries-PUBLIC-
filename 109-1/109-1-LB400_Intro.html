Summary

LB400 amends the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act (section 48-1,110) to establish rebuttable presumptions that certain cancers in firefighters are work‑related. Key provisions:

- Presumption if cancer is diagnosed during employment.

- For retirees: a presumption applies for cancers diagnosed within a post‑employment period tied to years of service (3 months per full year, capped at 60 months). This rule applies to professional firefighters retiring before Jan 1, 2026, and to volunteer firefighters regardless of retirement date.

- For professional firefighters retiring on/after Jan 1, 2026: if <20 years of service, presumption lasts a period equal to years worked; if ≥20 years (including investigators/instructors), presumption is lifetime.

- Purchased retirement service credit is excluded from service‑year calculations.

- Claims under the post‑2026 provisions are limited to medical benefits (no wage compensation).

No state agencies or budget/appropriation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds specified carcinogens (benzene and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons) associated with urinary, ureteral, and uterine cancers and makes the list non‑exhaustive. Establishes rebuttable presumptions that cancer in firefighters arose out of employment: for active firefighters diagnosed during employment; for retirees diagnosed within a period (capped at 60 months) beginning at last work date and extended by three months per full year of service (applies to professional retirees before Jan 1, 2026, and all volunteer retirees); and for professional retirees on/after Jan 1, 2026 (if <20 years’ service, presumption lasts after separation equal to years worked; if ≥20 years, anytime in life). Purchased service credit is excluded from service‑year calculations. Claims under the post‑2026 rule are limited under workers’ compensation to medical benefits only. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are referenced.
