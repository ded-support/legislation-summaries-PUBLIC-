Summary

LB486 directs the Department of Health and Human Services to implement a standard reimbursement rate for service providers under the Aged and Disabled Personal Care Services waiver. The rate must align with the CBIZ Optumas rate study dated December 7, 2022, and be rebased at least once every two years. The bill does not specify any appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must establish and implement a standard reimbursement rate for providers under the Aged and Disabled Personal Care Services waiver. The rate must align with the CBIZ Optumas rate study dated December 7, 2022, and must be rebased at least every two years. The provision assigns implementation responsibility to the Department; it does not specify any new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
