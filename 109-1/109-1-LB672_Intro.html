Summary

LB672 (Raybould) amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act (sec. 49-1401) to cap contributions to a candidate committee at $1,000 per donor during an election period (defined as the calendar year of the election). Candidate committees must refund within ten days any contribution that causes a donor to exceed $1,000 and must disclose on subsequent campaign statements the contributor's name and address, amount received, date received, and date returned. A candidate's personal funds are exempt. The bill was referred to the Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee and repeals the original section 49-1401. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or referenced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a $1,000 per-person contribution limit to a candidate committee for an election period (defined as the calendar year of the election). Candidate committees must refund any contribution that causes a person’s total to exceed $1,000 within 10 days and disclose the refund on subsequent campaign statements, including contributor name and address, amount received, date received, and date returned. Candidate's personal funds are exempt. No government agencies, departments, appropriations, or budget changes are referenced.
