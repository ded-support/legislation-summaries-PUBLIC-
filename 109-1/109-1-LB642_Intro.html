Summary

LB642 establishes the Artificial Intelligence Consumer Protection Act and assigns enforcement and oversight roles principally to the Nebraska Attorney General. The Attorney General may, in connection with an ongoing investigation, require deployers or contracted third parties to produce risk‑management policies, impact assessments, and related records within 90 days, evaluate them for compliance, and treat disclosures as nonpublic (with proprietary/trade‑secret protections). Banks, credit unions, their affiliates/subsidiaries, and service providers are exempt if subject to state or federal prudential regulator guidance that the Attorney General finds substantially equivalent; such guidance must, at minimum, require regular audits for antidiscrimination compliance and mitigation. Insurers and certain fraternal benefit societies governed by the Unfair Insurance Trade Practices Act are exempt. The bill cites federal research and safety rules (45 C.F.R. part 46; FDA) for exemptions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
