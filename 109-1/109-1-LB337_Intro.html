Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and directs the DMV to design, procure, and furnish special‑interest license plate decals, charge postage/handling fees (remitted to the DMV Cash Fund), and set a $50 special‑interest plate issuance/renewal fee. Of that $50, $25 is credited to the DMV Cash Fund and $25 to the Highway Trust Fund; if manufacturing costs exceed the statutory plate charge, amounts otherwise payable to the DMV Cash Fund are first credited to the Highway Trust Fund up to the excess, with any remainder to the DMV Cash Fund. DMV staff or county treasurers must collect the fee and surcharge from successful school and LPE‑learner's permit applicants, and the DMV must accept/record approved motorcycle safety/course proof on driver records. Separately, all sales and use tax proceeds from aircraft sales/leases are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund effective July 1, 2023.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds ATVs and utility‑type vehicles (UTVs) into the Motor Vehicle Registration Act. The Department of Motor Vehicles must title and register these vehicles (certificate language includes "manufactured for off‑road use"), issue ATV/UTV plates under §60‑3,100, and collect registration fees (text specifies $3 for ATVs and $3 for UTVs). It also lists vehicle/plate categories related to registration. Utility‑type vehicles are defined by size (≤74" wide, ≤180" long, four+ nonhighway tires) and expressly exclude certain vehicle types.

For the Department of Transportation, the bill treats registered ATVs/UTVs as motor vehicles when operated on highways per §60‑6,356 and prohibits their operation on interstates, state highways, expressways, freeways, and where local ordinance forbids. Safety provisions address helmets and occupant‑protection/seatbelt exceptions.

No appropriations, new funding, or budget allocations are included.
