Summary

LB158 requires health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers (as defined in Neb. Rev. Stat. §44-1303 and §44-4603) to count amounts paid by a plan enrollee or paid on the enrollee’s behalf toward the enrollee’s overall contribution to any out-of-pocket maximum or other cost‑sharing requirement for health benefit plans issued, amended, extended, or renewed on or after January 1, 2026. An exception preserves health savings account (HSA) eligibility under federal tax law: the rule applies only after the enrollee meets the IRC §223 minimum deductible, except that preventive care per IRC §223(c)(2)(C) counts immediately. No appropriation, funding change, or budget allocation is included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and requires that for health plans entered, amended, extended, or renewed on/after Jan. 1, 2026, health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers must count any amount an enrollee (or someone paying on their behalf) pays toward the enrollee’s out‑of‑pocket maximums and other cost‑sharing. It preserves HSA/HDHP rules: this counting won’t apply if it would make an HSA ineligible under IRC §223 until the plan deductible minimum is met, except preventive services under IRC §223(c)(2)(C) are counted regardless. References state code definitions (§44‑1303, §44‑4603) and the Internal Revenue Code; no agencies are newly created and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
