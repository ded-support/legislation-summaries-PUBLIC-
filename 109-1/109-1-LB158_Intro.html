Summary

LB158 (2025) requires health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers to include amounts paid by, or on behalf of, a health benefit plan enrollee when calculating that enrollee’s contribution toward any out‑of‑pocket maximum or other cost‑sharing for plans entered into, amended, extended, or renewed on or after January 1, 2026. "Health carrier" and "pharmacy benefit manager" are referenced to Neb. Rev. Stat. §§44‑1303 and 44‑4603. To preserve health savings account (HSA) eligibility under IRC §223, the counting requirement for HSA‑qualified plans applies only after the enrollee meets the IRC §223 minimum deductible, except that preventive care under IRC §223(c)(2)(C) is counted regardless. The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and requires health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers (per state statutory definitions at sections 44‑1303 and 44‑4603) to count any amounts an enrollee—or someone paying on their behalf—pays toward the enrollee’s out‑of‑pocket maximums or other cost‑sharing for plans entered, amended, extended, or renewed on/after Jan 1, 2026. Includes an exception to avoid making Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) under IRC §223 ineligible: the counting requirement for HSA‑compatible plans applies only after the IRC §223 minimum deductible is met, except that preventive services under IRC §223(c)(2)(C) are counted regardless. References the Internal Revenue Code (federal tax law); no agencies are newly created and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
