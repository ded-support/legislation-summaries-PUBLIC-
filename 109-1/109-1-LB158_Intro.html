Summary

LB158 (2025) requires health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers (terms referenced to Neb. Rev. Stat. §§44‑1303 and 44‑4603) to include amounts paid by a health benefit plan enrollee—or paid on the enrollee’s behalf by another person—when calculating the enrollee’s contribution toward any out‑of‑pocket maximum or other cost‑sharing requirement. The rule applies to plans entered into, amended, extended, or renewed on or after January 1, 2026. An exception preserves Health Savings Account (HSA) eligibility under Internal Revenue Code §223: the counting requirement for HSA‑compatible plans applies only after the IRC §223 minimum deductible is satisfied, except that preventive care counts regardless. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and requires health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers, for plans entered/renewed/amended on or after Jan 1, 2026, to count any amounts paid by an enrollee (or paid on their behalf) toward the enrollee's out‑of‑pocket maximum and other cost‑sharing requirements. References state code definitions for "health carrier" (section 44‑1303) and "pharmacy benefit manager" (section 44‑4603). Provides an exception to avoid making health savings accounts (HSA) ineligible under Internal Revenue Code §223: the counting requirement applies only after the IRC §223 minimum deductible is met, except that preventive services under IRC §223(c)(2)(C) must be counted regardless. No government agency appropriations or budget allocations are included.
