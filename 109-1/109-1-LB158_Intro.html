Summary

LB158 requires health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers to include amounts paid by a health benefit plan enrollee (or paid on the enrollee’s behalf) when calculating the enrollee’s contribution toward any out‑of‑pocket maximum or other cost‑sharing requirement for plans entered, amended, extended, or renewed on or after January 1, 2026. The bill references Nebraska statutory definitions for "health carrier" (see §44‑1303) and "pharmacy benefit manager" (see §44‑4603) and defers to federal tax law: the rule is limited where application would render a health savings account ineligible under Internal Revenue Code §223, in which case counting applies only after the IRC §223 minimum deductible is met (preventive care is counted regardless). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement (effective for plans entered, amended, extended, or renewed on/after Jan. 1, 2026) that health carriers and pharmacy benefit managers must count amounts paid by an enrollee or paid on their behalf toward the enrollee’s out‑of‑pocket maximums and other cost‑sharing limits. It preserves Health Savings Account (HSA) eligibility by delaying this counting until the enrollee meets the IRC §223 minimum deductible, except that preventive services (IRC §223(c)(2)(C)) are counted immediately. “Health carrier” and “pharmacy benefit manager” are referenced to state statute sections 44‑1303 and 44‑4603; “person” explicitly includes government entities. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are changed.
