Summary

LB404 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29-2263 (probation). Key provisions:

- Courts must specify probation terms (≤5 years for felony/second-offense misdemeanor; ≤2 years for first-offense misdemeanor) and provide notice at sentencing that the offender may be eligible to have the conviction set aside; the State Court Administrator is directed to develop standardized advisement language and any necessary forms.

- The court may discharge a probationer on application of a probation officer, the probationer, or on its own motion.

- New: the court may extend the term of probation on joint application of the probation officer and the probationer, within existing statutory limits.

- Clarifies that probation officers may impose administrative sanctions with the probationer’s full knowledge and consent as authorized by existing statutes.

No agencies other than the State Court Administrator, courts, and probation officers are referenced. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for a court, upon joint application by the probation officer and the probationer, to extend the probation term subject to the limits already set in subdivision (1)(a). Affects the court and probation personnel; no specific state agencies or departments are newly created or referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
