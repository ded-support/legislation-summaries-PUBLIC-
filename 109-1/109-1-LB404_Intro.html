Summary

LB404 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29-2263. It (1) expressly authorizes a court to extend a probation term on joint application of the probation officer and the probationer, within existing statutory limits; (2) preserves the court’s authority to discharge or modify probation conditions and notes that probation officers may impose administrative sanctions under §§29-2266.01 and 29-2266.02; and (3) directs the State Court Administrator to develop standardized advisement language and any necessary forms to notify offenders at sentencing and upon satisfactory completion or discharge about eligibility and procedures for having a conviction set aside. The bill repeals the original section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
