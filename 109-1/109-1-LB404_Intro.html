Summary

LB404 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29‑2263 to (1) permit a court to extend a probation term within statutory limits on the joint application of the probation officer and the probationer; (2) reconfirm that courts may discharge or modify probation and specify post‑release supervision terms per §28‑105; and (3) clarify that probation officers may impose administrative sanctions with the probationer’s consent. The bill additionally directs the State Court Administrator to develop standardized advisement language and any necessary forms for (a) sentencing advisements about setting aside convictions and (b) notices following satisfactory completion of probation. The original section is repealed. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Allows the court, upon a joint application from the probation officer and the probationer, to extend the probation term within the limits of subdivision (1)(a). Agencies affected: the judiciary (the court) and probation services (probation officer/probation department). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
