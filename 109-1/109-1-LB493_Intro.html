Summary: LB493 adopts the Uniform Public Expression Protection Act (an anti‑SLAPP framework). It permits a defendant to file a special motion for expedited dismissal within 60 days of service, stays related proceedings and limits discovery while the motion is pending, requires courts to hear and rule on motions within specified 60‑day deadlines, and grants an appeal as of right from denials. The bill mandates awards of court costs, reasonable attorneys’ fees, and litigation expenses to prevailing moving parties, provides severability, declares an emergency, and outright repeals sections 25‑21,241 through 25‑21,246, R.R.S. Neb.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural protections under the Uniform Public Expression Protection Act: filing a special motion triggers a stay of proceedings (including discovery and hearings) with limited discovery exceptions and carve-outs for unrelated motions and emergency public‑health/safety injunctions. Courts must hear the motion within 60 days (or later for good cause), and rulings can be appealed as of right within 21 days. The court must dismiss with prejudice if the moving party shows the Act applies and the respondent fails to rebut or to establish a prima facie case; prevailing movants are entitled to court costs, reasonable attorney fees, and litigation expenses.
