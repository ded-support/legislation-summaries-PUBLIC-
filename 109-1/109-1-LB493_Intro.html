Summary

LB493 adopts the Uniform Public Expression Protection Act, repeals sections 25-21,241 through 25-21,246 (R.R.S.), and replaces Nebraska’s prior public petition/participation provisions. It creates an expedited special motion to dismiss (generally within 60 days of service), imposes automatic stays of proceedings while motions are pending, allows limited discovery for specific needs, requires timely hearings and rulings, permits appeals as of right, and mandates fee-shifting (court costs, reasonable attorney fees, litigation expenses) to a prevailing moving party. No state agencies or departments are referenced, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations. An emergency clause is included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds expedited special-motion procedures under the Uniform Public Expression Protection Act governing courts. Key provisions: a defendant may file an expedited dismissal motion within 60 days of service; filing stays all proceedings (including discovery and hearings) unless limited discovery is authorized to obtain necessary information, or the court allows hearing of unrelated motions or emergency injunctions (e.g., imminent public health/safety threats). Courts must hear the motion within 60 days (subject to narrow exceptions), rule within 60 days after hearing, and appeals must be filed within 21 days; an appeal generally stays proceedings until resolved. If the motion prevails, the court must award costs, reasonable attorney fees, and litigation expenses. No executive agencies or departments are referenced and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
