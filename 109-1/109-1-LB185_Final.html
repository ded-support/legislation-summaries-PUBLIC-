Summary

LB185 amends the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (section 76-1410) to define "qualified third party" as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit or a federally recognized Indian tribe that has an affiliation agreement with the Department of Health and Human Services to provide services to victims of domestic violence and sexual assault under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act. No provisions in the bill change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations involving the Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The underlined additions expand the definition of “writing/written” to expressly allow electronic delivery, storage, and presentation of landlord-tenant notices (including email and posted notices) if they meet Uniform Electronic Transactions Act standards; set detailed consent, hardware/software notice, verification/acknowledgment, withdrawal, and fallback-to-mail rules; and preserve federal E‑SIGN protections. There are no provisions in the added text that reference the Department of Health and Human Services, nor do they create or modify any DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
