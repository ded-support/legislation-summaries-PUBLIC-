Summary

LB185 adds a definition of "qualified third party" that explicitly requires such organizations to have an affiliation agreement with the Department of Health and Human Services to provide services to victims of domestic violence and sexual assault under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act (amending section 76-1410). No provisions in the bill amend DHHS’s funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
