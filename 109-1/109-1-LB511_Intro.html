Summary

The bill imposes agency-specific duties and procedural changes. Department of Correctional Services must deliver, have signed, retain, and distribute Sex Offender Registration Act written notices to persons committed for registrable offenses prior to release (or before discharge, parole, or work release if immediately incarcerated) and forward a copy to the Nebraska State Patrol. Department of Health and Human Services must conduct education/outreach on healthy families, pornography, and prostitution; require affiliation agreements with qualified third‑party domestic violence/sexual‑assault providers under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act; and assaults on DHHS employees are enumerated as racketeering offenses under the Public Protection Act. Department of Motor Vehicles must include the duty‑to‑register notice on operator and commercial license applications and its records must be checked when seized property is a Nebraska‑titled motor vehicle in forfeiture proceedings. The bill creates a law‑enforcement grant program but includes no specific appropriations to these three agencies.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Correctional Services: New duty requiring DCS (or city/county jails) to have persons subject to sex-offender registration read and sign a written notification explaining the duty to register, retain a signed copy, and provide copies to the registrant and the Sex Offender Registration and Community Notification Division of the Nebraska State Patrol. No dedicated funding or appropriation to DCS is included.
