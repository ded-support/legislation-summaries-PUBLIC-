Summary

The bill directs DHHS to lead education, awareness, and outreach campaigns addressing healthy families, pornography, and prostitution, and explicitly enumerates DHHS employees among protected persons in expanded racketeering-related assault offenses. DHHS must also execute affiliation agreements with "qualified third parties" that provide services to domestic violence and sexual assault victims under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act. The Department of Correctional Services must provide persons committed for registrable offenses written Sex Offender Registration Act notice before release, obtain a signed copy, retain it, and forward a copy to the registrant and the Nebraska State Patrol registration division. The DMV must (1) be queried for title and lien information when vehicles subject to forfeiture are seized and (2) include written Sex Offender Registration Act notice on operator and commercial license applications. No specific appropriations or budget allocations for these agencies are identified.
