Summary

LB693 amends section 87-302 of the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act to add a prohibition (subdivision (a)(25)) on receiving compensation, directly or indirectly, for preparing, presenting, prosecuting, advising, consulting, assisting, or referring individuals for assistance in veterans benefits matters before the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, the United States Department of Defense, or the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs, except as permitted by state or federal law. "Veterans benefits matter" and "compensation" are defined. The bill preserves lawful attorney fee divisions and does not apply to county veterans service officers. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions affecting representation, assistance, and referrals related to veterans benefits before the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, U.S. Department of Defense, and the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs. Except as allowed by state or federal law, persons may not receive compensation (money or other things of value) for preparing, presenting, prosecuting, advising, consulting, assisting, or referring someone for any "veterans benefits matter" as defined. Allows attorney fee-splitting that complies with Nebraska law and ethical rules. County veterans service officers are exempt. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
