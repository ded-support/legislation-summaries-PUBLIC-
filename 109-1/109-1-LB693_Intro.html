Summary

LB693 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §87-302 (Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act) to make it a deceptive trade practice to receive compensation, directly or indirectly, for preparing, presenting, prosecuting, advising, consulting, assisting, or referring any individual in connection with veterans benefits matters before the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, the United States Department of Defense, or the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs. The bill defines "veterans benefits matter" (claims under laws/regulations administered by those agencies affecting veterans, dependents, survivors, or other eligible individuals) and defines "compensation" as money, things of value, or financial benefit. Exceptions: fee-splitting between attorneys allowed under Nebraska law/rules and county veterans service officers are exempt. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions on receiving compensation for preparing, presenting, prosecuting, advising, consulting, assisting, or contracting such services on veterans benefits matters before the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, U.S. Department of Defense, or the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs, and likewise prohibits paid referrals for such services, except as allowed by state or federal law. Defines "veterans benefits matter" and "compensation." Preserves lawful attorney fee‑splitting and explicitly exempts county veterans service officers. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Veterans' Affairs are included.
