Summary

LB188 expresses legislative intent concerning appropriations to the Department of Health and Human Services for Medicaid nursing facility rates (Program No. 348). It specifies total appropriations of $462,480,546 for FY2025‑26 and $476,717,026 for FY2026‑27, to be used in the Program No. 348 rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor. The totals are intended to include rate enhancements and other Medicaid nursing facility–related purposes. The department must file an electronic report by August 1, 2025, explaining how the FY2025‑26 inflation factor was calculated, and another electronic report between December 15–31, 2025, identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations (including encumbrances and retroactive payments). The act takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires that the Department of Health and Human Services apply the total appropriations for Medicaid nursing facility rates (Program No. 348) — $462,480,546 for FY2025‑26 and $476,717,026 for FY2026‑27 — in the rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor. States those totals include funds for rate enhancements and any other Medicaid nursing facility purposes. Directs DHHS to electronically report to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and Clerk: (1) by August 1, 2025, how the FY2025‑26 inflation factor was calculated; and (2) between December 15–31, 2025, the amount of any remaining unobligated prior appropriations for Medicaid nursing facility payments, identifying encumbrances and retroactive payments.
