Summary

LB188 expresses legislative intent regarding appropriations to the Department of Health and Human Services for Medicaid nursing facility rates (Program No. 348). It specifies total appropriations of $462,480,546 for FY2025‑26 and $476,717,026 for FY2026‑27 (General/Federal splits not listed) and directs that these totals be used in the nursing facility rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor, and may include rate enhancements and other Medicaid nursing facility purposes. DHHS must file: (1) by August 1, 2025, an electronic report to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and Clerk explaining how the FY2025‑26 inflation factor was calculated; and (2) between December 15–31, 2025, an electronic report identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations for nursing facility payments, including encumbrances and retroactive payments. The act takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.
