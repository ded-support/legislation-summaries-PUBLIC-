Summary

- Department of Revenue: LB316 imposes a 10% excise tax on retail sales of cannabidiol products effective January 1, 2026. Retail sellers must keep electronic sales records as prescribed by the Department of Revenue, provide them on request, file monthly returns and remit tax by the 20th day of the following month. The department may require electronic filing and electronic funds transfers. The Department of Revenue will collect the tax and remit receipts at least monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.

- Department of Health and Human Services: The bill defines "Department" in the Uniform Controlled Substances Act as the Department of Health and Human Services.

- Department of Agriculture / USDA: The bill defines USDA as the United States Department of Agriculture and recognizes USDA-licensed hemp producers (per 7 C.F.R. part 990, subpart C) including their authorized employees or agents.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary
