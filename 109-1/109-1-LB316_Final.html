Summary

- Department of Revenue: Levies a 10% excise tax on retail cannabidiol product sales effective Jan 1, 2026. Retailers must keep electronic sales records as prescribed by the Department of Revenue, file monthly returns by the 20th, and remit tax (department may require electronic filing and EFT). The Department of Revenue will collect the tax and remit receipts at least monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.

- Department of Agriculture (USDA): Bill references the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA), USDA licensing and USDA regs under the Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 for hemp producer licensing and transport/registration provisions.

- Department of Health and Human Services: The Uniform Controlled Substances Act definition designates “Department” as the Department of Health and Human Services. No appropriations included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "cannabidiol product" as a finished hemp consumer product containing hemp-derived CBD, meeting specified THC limits and excluding synthetically created cannabinoids.

Creates a new excise tax effective Jan 1, 2026: a 10% tax on retail sales of cannabidiol products. Department of Revenue duties: prescribe recordkeeping and return forms, require electronic records/filing and EFTs as needed, collect the tax, and remit collections at least monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. The tax is additional to other state/local taxes and sellers must file monthly returns (due by the 20th) reporting sales and remit payment.

No new duties, funding, or appropriations are assigned to the Department of Agriculture or the Department of Health and Human Services in the added text.
