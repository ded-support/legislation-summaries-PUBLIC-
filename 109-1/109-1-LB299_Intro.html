Summary

LB299: Department-specific items only

- Department of Administrative Services: Section 4-111 requires applicants attesting lawful presence or eligible-alien status for certain public benefits (including professional/commercial license applicants) to use an attestation format prescribed by the Department of Administrative Services; agencies may permit electronic attestations substantially similar to that format.

- Department of Labor: Section 84-1307(5)(b) provides that if a specified contributory retirement plan or contract is terminated, Department of Labor employees who were active participants on the termination date shall be granted vesting credit for years of participation.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: The bill requires applicants for the enumerated public benefits to "attest in a format prescribed by the Department of Administrative Services" that they are an "eligible alien" (defined by 8 C.F.R. 274a.12 as of Jan 1, 2025). DAS must therefore create and prescribe the attestation format/procedure.

- Department of Labor: The bill explicitly includes "benefits under the Employment Security Law" among benefits to which eligible aliens authorized for employment are entitled, meaning unemployment/employment security programs administered by the Department of Labor must be made available to such eligible aliens (and related family/dependent benefits when tied to employment).
