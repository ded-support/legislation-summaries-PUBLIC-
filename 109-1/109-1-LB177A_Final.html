Summary

LB177A appropriates funds to the Nebraska State Patrol (Program 100) to implement Legislative Bill 177. For FY2025‑26 it provides $233,030 from the General Fund and $135,750 from the Nebraska State Patrol Cash Fund. No funds are appropriated for FY2026‑27. Expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from the appropriated funds are capped at $179,591 for FY2025‑26 and $0 for FY2026‑27.
