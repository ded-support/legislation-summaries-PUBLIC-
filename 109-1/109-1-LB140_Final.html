Summary

LB140 requires each school board of a public school district to develop and adopt a written policy governing student use of electronic communication devices. Permitted exceptions include instructional use, safety or emergency response, investigatory needs, monitoring or managing a student's health care, and other uses the school board or an appropriate school employee approves. The bill prohibits schools from monitoring, collecting, or accessing information on devices not owned by or provided for academic use by the district. It contains an emergency clause, making the act effective upon passage. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that schools may monitor student devices when necessary to manage a student's health care or when the school board or an appropriate school employee deems it appropriate. Also clarifies the section does not authorize monitoring, collecting, or accessing information on electronic devices not owned by or provided for academic use by the school district. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are referenced.
