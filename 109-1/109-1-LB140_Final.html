Summary

LB140 requires each school board of a Nebraska public school district to develop and adopt a policy governing student use of electronic communication devices. The policy must specify permitted and prohibited uses, and may allow device use for instructional activities, to monitor or manage a student’s health care, or when otherwise approved by the school board or an appropriate school employee. The bill prohibits schools from monitoring, collecting, or accessing information on devices not owned by or provided for academic use by the district. It was referred to the Education Committee and was introduced at the Governor’s request. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The act takes effect immediately upon passage due to an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two justifications for school-run monitoring: (iv) to monitor or manage a student's health care, and (v) when the school board or an appropriate school employee determines it appropriate. Also clarifies the section does not authorize monitoring, collecting, or accessing data on electronic devices that are not owned by, or provided for academic use by, the school district. No state agencies beyond local school boards/districts are referenced and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
