Summary

LB453 amends Nebraska guardianship and conservatorship law. Key agencies: Nebraska State Patrol must forward nominated guardians’ fingerprints to the FBI and issue a criminal-history report to the State Court Administrator; the report must be filed with the court at least ten days before the appointment hearing unless waived or expedited. Applicants (except financial institutions, their officers/employees, or trust companies) pay the actual fingerprint/background-check cost. Temporary guardians and temporary conservators are exempt from the fingerprint/FBI check. The Public Guardian may serve as temporary guardian; if unable because of caseload limits (per §30-4115), the court shall appoint a limited temporary guardian subject to availability of funds appropriated under §81-3141. Counties pay court-fixed expenses/fees for such guardians and may seek reimbursement under §81-3141. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Authorizes the Nebraska State Patrol to submit applicants' fingerprints to the Federal Bureau of Investigation and to forward those prints for a national criminal history record check. The State Patrol must issue a report to the State Court Administrator containing the applicant's criminal history record information. The applicant is required to pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the criminal background check. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
