Summary

LB453 amends guardianship and conservatorship procedures to:

- Require guardian/conservator nominees (except financial institutions and trust companies) to authorize the Nebraska State Patrol to submit fingerprints to the FBI and to issue a criminal-history report to the State Court Administrator; the patrol must file that report with the court at least ten days before the appointment hearing unless waived or expedited. Applicants pay fingerprinting/background-check costs. Courts cannot sign orders until the report is filed and reviewed. Emergency temporary appointees are exempt.

- Specify temporary guardians and conservators are not required to provide fingerprints or the national criminal-history report.

- Authorize courts in "covered counties" to appoint limited temporary guardians to obtain/validate benefits and access relevant personal and financial records; third parties (including financial institutions) must provide access.

- Allow appointment of individual limited temporary guardians when the Public Guardian is over capacity, subject to availability of funds appropriated under section 81-3141; counties pay appointed guardians' fees and may apply to section 81-3141 for reimbursement.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Authorizes the Nebraska State Patrol to collect and submit applicants' fingerprints to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for a national criminal history check and to prepare and send a report containing the applicant's criminal history to the State Court Administrator. Specifies the Patrol must forward fingerprints to the FBI for the check and that the applicant is required to pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the criminal background check. No changes to agency appropriations or budget allocations are provided.
