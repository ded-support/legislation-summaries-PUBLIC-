Summary

LB453 amends Nebraska guardianship and conservatorship statutes. Key agency provisions:

- Nebraska State Patrol: required to submit applicants' fingerprints to the FBI and provide criminal history reports to the State Court Administrator; reports must be filed with the court at least 10 days before appointment unless waived or expedited. Applicants pay fingerprint/background-check costs. Financial institutions and trust companies are exempt.

- State Court Administrator and courts: receive and review the federal criminal-history reports before signing appointment orders.

- Public Guardian/Public Guardianship Act: may serve as temporary guardian/conservator; if the Public Guardian has exceeded caseload ratios (per section 30-4115), courts must appoint individual limited temporary guardians subject to availability of funds appropriated under section 81-3141. Appointments stop when those funds are exhausted.

- Counties/county boards: must pay approved expenses/fees for appointed limited temporary guardians and may apply under section 81-3141 for reimbursement. Temporary guardians/conservators are not required to submit fingerprints.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Authorizes the Nebraska State Patrol to submit applicants’ fingerprints to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for a national criminal history check and to provide the resulting criminal history report to the State Court Administrator. Requires the State Patrol to forward fingerprints to the FBI and to issue the report to the State Court Administrator. Costs: the applicant must pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the criminal background check. No state funding or appropriation changes specified.
