Summary

LB348 directs the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services to receive appropriations of $3,000,000 from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund for FY2025‑26 and $3,000,000 from the same fund for FY2026‑27. Funds are allocated to Program 354 for domestic violence services. The bill contains an emergency clause, making it effective upon passage and approval.
