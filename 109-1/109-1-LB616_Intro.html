Summary

LB616 authorizes limited use of automatic license plate reader (ALPR) systems and modifies enforcement of red‑light violations. Key agencies and duties:

- Department (Department of Motor Vehicles): approves driver's safety training curriculum and fees, adopts rules, and maintains attendee records.

- Local authorities: may enforce red‑light violations via ALPR if they offer violators a choice to pay a fee or attend an approved driver's safety training program (fee waived if the violator participates), assess no points, and do not issue warrants for nonpayment/nonparticipation.

- County treasurer: may require unpaid fees be paid as a precondition to vehicle registration renewal and must allocate fee proceeds to each county, local school system, school district, city, and village in the vehicle's tax district.

- Law enforcement, parking enforcement entities, and weighing stations: authorized ALPR uses are enumerated. Evidence from ALPRs is restricted to proceedings enforcing this section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for local authorities to use automatic license plate reader (ALPR) systems to enforce red‑light violations, subject to conditions: violators may pay a fee or enter a driver's safety training pretrial diversion (per sections 29‑3605 to 29‑3609); the fee is waived if they complete training; no motor‑vehicle points (per §60‑4,182) or warrants for nonpayment/nonparticipation. If neither completed nor paid, the person must pay the fee to the county treasurer before vehicle registration renewal (§60‑392). The county treasurer must allocate fee proceeds to each county, local school system, school district, city, and village in the vehicle’s tax district. Evidence from ALPRs is limited to proceedings enforcing this section. Agencies/departments implicated: local authorities (law enforcement), county treasurer, and local education and municipal entities; no statewide appropriation is created, but revenue allocation to local jurisdictions is required.
