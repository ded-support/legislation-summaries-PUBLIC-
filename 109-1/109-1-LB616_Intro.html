Summary

LB616 authorizes limited use of automatic license plate reader (ALPR) systems and adjusts agency roles and fee handling.

- Department (DMV) responsibilities: approve driver's safety training curriculum and fees; adopt rules/regulations; allow jurisdictions to use training fees to operate programs and other safety/education efforts.

- Local authorities may enforce red‑light violations using ALPRs if they offer violators a choice to pay a fee or attend an approved driver's safety training (pretrial diversion). If the violator attends, the local authority must waive the program fee; no points assessed and no warrant for nonpayment/nonparticipation.

- County treasurers collect unpaid fees as a condition of registration renewal and allocate proceeds to the county, local school system, school district, city, and village in the vehicle’s tax district.

- Law enforcement ALPR use is otherwise restricted but permitted for specified enforcement purposes (including red‑light violations); ALPR evidence is limited to proceedings under this enforcement authority.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for local authorities to enforce red‑light violations using automatic license plate reader (ALPR) systems (as defined in §60‑3202) for steady red indications (§60‑6,123) if specified conditions are met: offenders may pay a fee or enter a driver's safety training program via a pretrial diversion plan (see §§29‑3605 to 29‑3609); the program fee is waived if chosen; no motor‑vehicle points (§60‑4,182) or warrants for nonpayment are allowed. Noncompliance requires payment of the fee to the county treasurer before vehicle registration renewal (§60‑392). The county treasurer must allocate fee proceeds to the county, local school systems/districts, cities, and villages in the vehicle’s tax district. ALPR‑derived data is admissible only in proceedings enforcing this section.
