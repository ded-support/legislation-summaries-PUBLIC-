Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-903 to (1) require the Department of Revenue to determine the prepaid wireless surcharge annually (effective January 1) based on the charges described in subsection (2) as of the preceding July 1 and (2) require the Department to post at least 90 days' advance notice of any surcharge change on its website. It also requires the Department to provide prepaid wireless surcharge calculation and collection data to the Public Service Commission upon request. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an appropriation of one dollar, under subsection (1), to maximize operational support for all public safety answering points (PSAPs) statewide. No other Department of Revenue funding changes, allocations, or budget details are specified.
