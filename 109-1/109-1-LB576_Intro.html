Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Revenue responsibility to determine the annual prepaid wireless surcharge, effective January 1, using the charge components in subsection (2) as of the preceding July 1. The department must post at least 90 days' advance notice of any surcharge change on its website. The prepaid rate is computed as the sum of three percentage components (the wireless E‑911 surcharge divided by 50; the Telecommunications Relay System Fund surcharge divided by 50; and the Universal Service Fund surcharge adjusted by the FCC safe‑harbor), rounded up to the nearest tenth of one percent. The Department must provide prepaid surcharge calculation and collection data to the Public Service Commission upon request. No appropriations or funding changes for the Department are included.
