Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-903 to assign the Department of Revenue these duties regarding the prepaid wireless surcharge: (1) determine the annual prepaid wireless surcharge effective each January 1, based on the charges in subsection (2) as of the preceding July 1; (2) post at least 90 days' advance notice on the department website of any surcharge change; and (3) provide the Public Service Commission, upon request, with prepaid wireless surcharge calculation and collection data. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are specified.
