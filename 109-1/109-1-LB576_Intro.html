Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-903 to (1) require the Department of Revenue to determine the annual prepaid wireless surcharge effective January 1, using the charge components in subsection (2) as they existed on the preceding July 1; (2) require the Department to post at least 90 days' advance notice of any surcharge change on its website; and (3) require the Department to provide prepaid wireless surcharge calculation and collection data to the Public Service Commission upon request. No changes to appropriations, funding levels, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue or other agencies are included.
