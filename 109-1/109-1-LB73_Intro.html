Summary

Bill LB73 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1414.07 to set annual continuing-education requirements for law enforcement officers (excluding noncertified conditional officers): the greater of 32 hours for officers serving in counties with 40,000+ residents or 20 hours for counties under 40,000 (population determined by the U.S. Bureau of the Census). It retains exemptions for the year of initial full certification and for retirement in good standing, and treats non-employed officers as serving in their county of residence. Required topics include de-escalation, mental health, substance abuse, at least two hours of anti-/implicit-bias training, firearms, officer wellness, legal updates (including First and Fourth Amendment issues), and vehicular pursuit policy review. Training formats may include seminars, college courses, agency instruction, or online delivery. Operative date: January 1, 2026. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a population-based distinction for a law enforcement officer’s required hours: counties are classified using the most recent federal decennial census or revised certified count by the United States Bureau of the Census; officers in counties under 40,000 require twenty hours (larger-county requirement referenced but not restated). Also adds that an unemployed officer seeking to maintain professional status is treated as serving in the county of their residence for purposes of subdivision (1)(b). No state agencies beyond county governments and the U.S. Bureau of the Census are newly created or referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
