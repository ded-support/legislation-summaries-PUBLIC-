Summary

LB73 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1414.07 to set annual continuing-education requirements for certified law enforcement officers administered by law enforcement agencies. Annual hours are now the greater of: 32 hours for officers serving in counties with 40,000+ residents (per the U.S. Bureau of the Census) or 20 hours for counties under 40,000. Required topics include de-escalation, mental health and substance-abuse refreshers; at least two hours of anti‑bias/implicit-bias training; firearms; officer wellness; legal updates (including legislative, First and Fourth Amendment issues); vehicular pursuit policy review; and agency‑determined training. Unemployed officers maintain status based on county of residence; new certificants and retirees are exempt in their certification/retirement year. Operative date: January 1, 2026. No appropriations or funding changes included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a population-based trigger using the United States Bureau of the Census (most recent decennial census or revised certified count) to determine which hour requirement applies to law enforcement officers: counties under 40,000 inhabitants are assigned a twenty‑hour standard; counties at or above 40,000 are subject to the alternate (greater) standard. Also adds that a law enforcement officer who is not employed but seeks to maintain professional status is treated as serving in the county of the officer’s residence for purposes of subdivision (1)(b). No state or agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
