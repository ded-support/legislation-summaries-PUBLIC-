Summary

LB73 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1414.07 to revise mandatory annual continuing-education requirements for certified law enforcement officers (excludes noncertified conditional officers). Training hours are set as the greater of: 32 hours for officers serving in counties with population ≥40,000 or 20 hours for officers in counties <40,000 (population by latest federal decennial census or revised certified count). Required topics include de-escalation, mental health, substance abuse, a minimum of two hours of anti-bias/implicit-bias training, firearms, officer wellness, legal updates (including legislative, First and Fourth Amendment issues), vehicular-pursuit policy review, and agency-determined training. Officers first certified in a year and those retiring in good standing that year are exempt. Continuing education may be delivered by law enforcement agencies, colleges, conferences, seminars, or online. The act becomes operative January 1, 2026; no funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a population-based distinction for required hours: county population is measured by the most recent federal decennial census or the most recent revised certified count from the United States Bureau of the Census. For officers in counties under 40,000 the requirement is twenty hours; officers in counties of 40,000 or more are subject to the alternate (greater) standard referenced. Also adds that a law enforcement officer who is not employed but seeks to maintain professional status is treated as serving in the officer’s county of residence for purposes of subdivision (1)(b). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
