Summary

- Department of Transportation (DOT) bid procedures: for contracts estimated over $250,000 DOT must publish notice once weekly for three weeks in the county newspaper and on its official web page at least 20 days before bid receipt; the web posting alone for 20 days satisfies the notice requirement even if newspaper publication is defective. For contracts of $250,000 or less, DOT may either follow the above procedures or solicit bids from at least three potential bidders and may award if it receives at least one responsive bid.

- Adjustment of thresholds: DOT may annually (Oct. 1) adjust the dollar amounts in those bid thresholds by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics (twelve‑month period ending Aug. 31), rounded up to the next $1,000.

- DOT authority and legislative consent: DOT may procure materials, equipment, services, and buildings; it may not construct any building with an estimated cost greater than the adjusted dollar amount in section 81‑1114.01 without legislative consent.

- No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds electronic-notice requirements for departmental bid solicitations: agencies must publish notice and post it on their official web page at least 20 days before the bid deadline; temporary web outages do not defeat compliance. Newspaper notices must identify the department's official web page. If the solicitation has been posted on the designated web page for 20 days prior to bid opening, that posting alone will satisfy notice requirements even if the newspaper notice is defective. The text also bars the department from pursuing certain purchases or leases with estimated costs exceeding the adjusted dollar amount for capital construction projects referenced in section 81-1114.01. No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor or Department of Transportation are included.
