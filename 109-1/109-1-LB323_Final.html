Summary

LB323 amends statutes governing the Department of Transportation (DOT). For DOT construction contracts over $250,000 (as determined by the department), bid solicitations must be published on the department’s official web page at least 20 days before bid opening (newspaper publication remains required but web posting satisfies notice even if newspaper notice is defective). For contracts ≤ $250,000, DOT may either follow the above procedure or request bids from at least three potential bidders and may award on one responsive bid. DOT may annually adjust the dollar thresholds (Oct. 1) by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, rounded to the next $1,000. DOT may not construct buildings exceeding the adjusted capital-construction dollar amount in section 81-1114.01 without legislative consent. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procurement-notice requirements for state agencies (affecting the Department of Labor and Department of Transportation): notices must be published in a newspaper and on the agency’s official web page at least 20 days before bid closing; the web posting satisfies the 20‑day requirement even if the site is temporarily unavailable. Newspaper notices must identify the agency’s official bid webpage; if the webpage has been posted for 20 days, a defective or shortened newspaper notice will not invalidate the solicitation. Also inserts purchase/lease language and bars the department from awards with an estimated cost exceeding the adjusted dollar threshold for capital construction projects in section 81-1114.01(3). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
