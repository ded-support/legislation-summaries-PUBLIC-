Summary

LB187 amends Nebraska real estate statutes and affects actions by the Nebraska Real Estate Commission, Nebraska State Patrol, and the Federal Bureau of Investigation, with a reference to the State Department of Education for school-exemption status. Key agency provisions: the Commission retains rulemaking, course approval and monitoring authority, may suspend/withdraw course or instructor approvals, and may investigate, discipline, fine, suspend/revoke licenses, and set licensing requirements. Fingerprinting and national criminal-history checks are required for original license applicants; fingerprints are submitted by the Nebraska State Patrol to the FBI and applicants pay actual fingerprinting costs. Operative dates: most changes effective July 1, 2025 (some provisions effective Jan 1, 2026); an emergency clause makes the act take effect immediately. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements around real estate agency and licensing: buyers of residential property (1–4 units) must enter a written agency agreement; designated brokers acting as single agents for other property types are constrained when performing acts under section 81-885.01. The bill authorizes "the commission" to adopt and promulgate rules specifying mandatory course subject matter and sets that satisfactory evidence of course completion is valid for four years for license applications. It also inserts language about referral-fee compensation and references nonresident/citizen status. No specific state agencies beyond "the commission" are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
