Summary

LB187 amends provisions of the Nebraska Real Estate License Act and primarily affects the regulatory roles of the Real Estate Commission, the Nebraska State Patrol, and the Federal Bureau of Investigation, with a reference to the State Department of Education for school-exemption recognition. Key agency actions established or clarified:

- The Real Estate Commission (the commission) retains authority to approve real-estate education (including distance education), set mandatory course content, monitor and suspend/withdraw school or instructor approvals, adopt rules, investigate complaints, discipline licensees, and place licenses inactive for training noncompliance.

- The Nebraska State Patrol (or a commission-selected fingerprint service) will collect fingerprints and submit them to the FBI for national criminal history checks; applicants pay actual fingerprinting costs and authorize release to the commission.

- The State Department of Education is referenced regarding exempt schools/home-school programs.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Operative dates: parts effective July 1, 2025 and January 1, 2026; the bill declares an emergency.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements and regulatory authority related to real estate transactions and licensing. Key points:

- Buyers of residential property with one to four dwelling units must enter a written agency agreement with the party they will be represented by.

- Designated brokers acting as single agents for other property types are referenced (language fragmented).

- The (real estate) commission is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules specifying mandatory subject matter for required courses.

- Proof of course completion is valid for four years for license applications.

- Clarifies that certain compensation may be treated as a referral fee and includes language about nonresidents/citizens.

No specific state agencies beyond the commission are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
