Summary

LB437 repeals the Nebraska Health Care Certificate of Need Act, amends sections 71-20,103 and 71-20,105, and outright repeals numerous related statutory sections. It assigns responsibility to "the department" to receive, review, and approve or disapprove applications under the Nonprofit Hospital Sale Act and to issue the department's decision following that review. The Attorney General is authorized to notify applicants within 20 days whether the Attorney General will review an application; if so, the Attorney General must complete review and approve or disapprove within 60 days. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the bill.
