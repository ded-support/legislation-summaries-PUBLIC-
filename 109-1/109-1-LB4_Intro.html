Summary

This bill amends PSC jurisdiction (adding the Telecommunications Exchange Deregulation Act) and authorizes the Nebraska Public Service Commission to administer exchange deregulation petitions. Only an electing local exchange carrier may petition the PSC; the PSC must decide within 90 days using population and competitor-presence criteria (≥100,000 inhabitants, or <100,000 with at least two other carriers statewide—or alternative 75% square-mile test). The PSC may collect confidential carrier information and adopt rules. Deregulated carriers are exempt from carrier-of-last-resort, service-quality/reporting, and most pricing obligations (except pricing conditions tied to PSC-administered grants). Transitioning carriers lose eligibility for Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund support for deregulated exchanges and the PSC must reduce fund allocations in its formula accordingly. The bill also makes conforming changes to section 86-124 and repeals prior sections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key agency actions and funding changes:

- The commission (authority to implement/enforce the act) may require carrier disclosures, keep proprietary information confidential under section 84-712.05, adopt rules, and adjudicate deregulation petitions within 90 days using most recent U.S. Bureau of the Census data.

- The commission will classify/reclassify exchanges and carriers (deregulated, transitioning) and may reregulate.

- Funding: transitioning carriers lose eligibility for Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund support for deregulated exchanges; the commission must, to the extent feasible, reduce those carriers’ fund allocations in the funding formula to account for deregulated exchanges.

- Deregulated carriers are exempt from carrier-of-last-resort duties, service-quality/reporting standards, and most pricing requirements unless tied to commission‑administered grants.
