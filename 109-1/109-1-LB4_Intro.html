Summary

LB4 amends state telecom law to add and clarify the Public Service Commission's (PSC) jurisdiction over the new Telecommunications Exchange Deregulation Act. The PSC may require carrier disclosures, protect claimed confidential proprietary data, adopt rules, and must decide petitions to deregulate an exchange within 90 days. Only an electing local exchange carrier may petition; deregulation criteria use U.S. Census population data (U.S. Bureau of the Census) or presence/coverage of competing voice-service carriers. Deregulated carriers are relieved of carrier-of-last-resort, quality-reporting, and most pricing obligations. Transitioning carriers lose eligibility for Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund support for deregulated exchanges, and the PSC must, where feasible, reduce their fund allocations accordingly. The bill also repeals and replaces the prior sections it amends.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary: The bill empowers the state "commission" (the telecommunications regulator) to require carrier disclosures, protect proprietary information under section 84-712.05, adopt rules, and decide exchange deregulation (final order within 90 days). It directs the commission to use the most recent U.S. Bureau of the Census data for population determinations. Financially, transitioning carriers lose eligibility for Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund support for deregulated exchanges, and the commission must, insofar as the funding formula permits, reduce those carriers' fund allocations to account for deregulated exchanges. The commission also may reclassify carriers and oversee discontinuance and related proceedings.
