Summary

LB4 (2025) amends PSC jurisdiction and adopts the Telecommunications Exchange Deregulation Act. It explicitly places the Act under the Nebraska Public Service Commission's authority, authorizing the PSC to require carrier disclosures, protect claimed proprietary information per section 84-712.05, and adopt rules to administer, deregulate, reregulate, or review exchanges. Only an electing local exchange carrier may petition to deregulate an exchange; the PSC must decide within 90 days using census-based population or multi-carrier presence tests (including an alternative 75% coverage test). Deregulated carriers are relieved of carrier-of-last-resort, service-quality reporting, and most pricing obligations. Transitioning carriers lose eligibility for Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund support for deregulated exchanges, and the PSC must reduce fund allocations accordingly. The bill revises related statutory cross-references and repeals the original sections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key agency actions and funding changes:

- "The commission" (the state telecommunications regulator) may require carrier disclosures, protect proprietary submissions under section 84-712.05, adopt rules, and must decide deregulation petitions within 90 days.

- The commission will use U.S. Bureau of the Census decennial or revised counts to determine exchange populations and must reclassify carriers following deregulation.

- Deregulated carriers are relieved of carrier-of-last-resort, service-quality reporting, and most pricing obligations (except pricing conditions tied to grants administered by the commission).

- Transitioning carriers lose eligibility for the Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund for deregulated exchanges; the commission shall, to the extent feasible in the funding formula, reduce their fund allocations.

- Discontinuance procedures for service in deregulated exchanges are narrowed to subsection (1)(b) of section 86-134.
