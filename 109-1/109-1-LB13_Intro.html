Summary

- Designates the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to administer the state's social services program and to participate in the federal Child Care Assistance/Subsidy program, including compliance with Child Care Licensing Act criminal history checks.

- DHHS may contract with other agencies and must adopt fixed-rate schedules for provider reimbursement (annual effective date Oct. 1).

- DHHS must file a state plan amendment to pay providers based on a child's enrollment or authorized hours rather than attendance and shall promulgate rules implementing statutory subdivisions that become operative July 1, 2025.

- DHHS is directed to collaborate with a private nonprofit on an independent evaluation (if privately funded) and to receive the report.

- Funding: Child Care and Development Block Grant funds (July 1, 2021–Sept. 30, 2026) shall cover costs from prior income-eligibility changes; if insufficient, TANF funds may be used. No General Funds (other than administration) may be used for those costs in that period.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) must file a state plan amendment to change provider payment methodology so payments are based on a child's enrollment or authorized hours rather than actual attendance. The provision directs a procedural change to reimbursement rules; no specific funding, appropriation, or budget allocation is included.
