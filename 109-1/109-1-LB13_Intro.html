Summary

The bill amends duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Key provisions: DHHS shall administer the state social services program and may contract with other agencies. DHHS must participate in the federal Child Care Assistance/Subsidy program and provide child-care assistance to families up to 185% of FPL prior to Oct. 1, 2026, and 130% on and after that date, with specified transitional assistance rules. DHHS must file a state plan amendment to pay providers based on a child's enrollment or authorized hours (not attendance), adopt an annual fixed-rate schedule, and promulgate related rules (some provisions operative July 1, 2025). Funding: costs from the 2021 income-eligibility changes (July 1, 2021–Sept. 30, 2026) shall be paid with CCDBG funds, then TANF if needed; General Funds (except administration) are prohibited for those costs. DHHS may collaborate on a privately funded independent evaluation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to file a Medicaid state plan amendment to change provider payment for child services from an attendance-based model to one based on a child's enrollment or authorized hours. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are specified.
