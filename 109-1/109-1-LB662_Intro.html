Summary

LB662 requires each state agency to prepare and maintain a federal funding inventory. For each federal receipt the inventory must identify positions obligated by the agreement; other resources or obligations incurred by the agency; the applicable federal law, regulation, or grant citation; an operating plan if federal receipts decline by 10% or more from the prior fiscal year; the actions or results promised to obtain the funds; the state statutory objective served; and a list of receipts with foreseeable end dates and schedules. The bill does not make appropriations or change funding allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds reporting requirements for any state agency that accepts federal receipts. Agencies must report positions obligated by the agreement; other resources or obligations incurred; the federal law/regulation/grant citation; an operating plan if federal receipts fall by 10%+ from the prior fiscal year; the actions/results promised to receive the funds; the state statutory objective met by accepting the funds; and a list of federal receipts with foreseeable end dates and the expected receipt schedule. No specific agencies are named and no direct appropriation or budget allocation changes are included.
