Summary

LB662 requires each state agency to produce a federal funding inventory detailing federal receipts and related obligations. Required elements include: amounts and sources (grant/program names); purposes of the receipts; positions and other resources obligated by each award; citations to applicable federal law, regulation, or grant provisions; an operating plan if federal receipts fall by 10% or more from the prior fiscal year; the specific actions or results the agency promised to receive funds; the state statutory objective served; and a schedule of receipts with foreseeable end dates. The bill applies to every state agency; it does not itself create or change appropriations or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds reporting requirements for state agencies receiving federal funds. Agencies must identify positions and other resources/obligations tied to each federal award and cite the governing federal law/regulation or grant provision. They must also provide: (e) an operating plan if federal receipts drop 10%+ from the prior year; (f) a detailed description of actions/results promised to obtain the federal receipts; (g) the state statutory objective served by accepting the funds; and (h) a list of federal receipts with foreseeable end dates and the expected schedule of receipts. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
