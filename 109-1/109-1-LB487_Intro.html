Summary

LB487 creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force (seven voting members: two from the Commission on African American Affairs and four geographically apportioned), appointed by the Governor with legislative committee confirmation. The Attorney General must provide administrative, technical, and legal assistance; all state agencies and the State Treasurer must cooperate and may be required to provide information or funds. A Restoration Study Fund is established, to receive transfers from the Legislature (including from the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund) and donations, and to be expended by the task force. Tax proceeds under section 77-4303 are redirected to the Restoration Study Fund until Nov. 1, 2028. After that date, 5% of such proceeds go to the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund, 50% of remaining proceeds to county Drug Law Enforcement and Education Funds, and the balance to the Nebraska State Patrol Drug Control and Education Cash Fund. The state investment officer will invest fund balances. The task force terminates Nov. 1, 2028.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force with gubernatorial appointments (subject to legislative committee confirmation) and chair deadlines (appointments by Oct 1, 2025; first meeting by Nov 1, 2025). Agencies/departments involved: Commission on African American Affairs (members), the Governor, the Legislature, state agencies (must cooperate and provide requested information, even if confidential, with confidentiality protections), the Attorney General (to provide administrative, technical, and legal assistance), the State Treasurer (to receive and credit funds), and the state investment officer (to invest fund assets under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and State Funds Investment Act). Establishes the Restoration Study Fund to be credited by legislative transfers (including from the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund) and gifts; fund used by the task force. Act terminates Nov 1, 2028.
