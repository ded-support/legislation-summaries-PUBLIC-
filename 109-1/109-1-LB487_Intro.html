Summary

LB487 creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force (7 voting members: 2 appointed from the Commission on African American Affairs and 5 geographically distributed), appointed by the Governor with legislative committee confirmation. The task force receives administrative, technical, and legal assistance from the Attorney General, may hire staff, compel testimony and obtain state agency information, and must report annually; it terminates November 1, 2028.

A new Restoration Study Fund is established; the State Treasurer will credit transfers, donations, and designated tax proceeds to it. Proceeds of the tax in section 77-4303 (marijuana/controlled substances) are credited to the Restoration Study Fund through November 1, 2028. Beginning November 1, 2028, 5% of those proceeds go to the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund (administered by the Tax Commissioner), 50% of the remainder to counties for their County Drug Law Enforcement and Education Funds, and the balance to the Nebraska State Patrol Drug Control and Education Cash Fund. The state investment officer may invest fund balances.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force with members appointed by the Governor (subject to legislative standing committee confirmation) including two members of the Commission on African American Affairs. The Attorney General must provide administrative, technical, and legal assistance; state agencies and district courts must cooperate and may be compelled to provide information. Members serve without compensation but may receive expense reimbursements per sections 81-1174 to 81-1177.

Establishes the Restoration Study Fund. The State Treasurer will credit transfers from the Legislature (explicitly permitting transfers from the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund) and gifts; the task force may expend the fund, hire staff, contract for research, and the state investment officer will invest available funds under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The task force terminates November 1, 2028, with annual reports due each November 1.
