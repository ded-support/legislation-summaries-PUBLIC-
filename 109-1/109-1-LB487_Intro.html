Summary

Creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force (seven members: two appointed from the Commission on African American Affairs and others by congressional district) — Governor appointments subject to confirmation by the Legislature's relevant standing committee. The Attorney General must provide administrative, technical, and legal assistance; state agencies and the State Treasurer must cooperate and may furnish confidential information. Task force may hire staff, contract for research, and is entitled to state travel reimbursement. The task force and its Restoration Study Fund terminate November 1, 2028.

Fiscal provisions: proceeds of the tax under §77-4303 are redirected to the Restoration Study Fund from enactment until Nov. 1, 2028 and expended by the task force. The Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund may transfer monies to the Restoration Study Fund until Nov. 1, 2028. After Nov. 1, 2028, tax proceeds distribution resumes: 5% to the administration cash fund; 50% of the remainder to counties (County Drug Law Enforcement and Education Funds); remaining funds to the Nebraska State Patrol Drug Control and Education Cash Fund. Funds are invested by the state investment officer; Tax Commissioner administers the tax.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force with seven gubernatorial appointees (subject to legislative standing‑committee confirmation) and requires cooperation from state agencies. Agencies/departments specified: Commission on African American Affairs (two members), the Office of the Governor, the Legislature (standing committee), district courts (to compel testimony/subpoenas), all state agencies (must provide information), the Attorney General (administrative/technical/legal assistance), the State Treasurer (custodian of a new Restoration Study Fund), and the State Investment Officer (invests fund assets under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act). Members serve without pay but may receive expense reimbursement per state travel statutes. Funding: establishes the Restoration Study Fund to be credited by legislative transfers, gifts/bequests, and expressly permits transfers from the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund; the task force may expend the fund for its work. Task force terminates November 1, 2028.
