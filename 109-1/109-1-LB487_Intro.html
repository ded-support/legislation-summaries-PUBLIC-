Summary

LB487 creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force (seven voting members: two appointed from the Commission on African American Affairs and five regionally apportioned by congressional district). Members are gubernatorial appointees requiring confirmation by the Legislative standing committee; the Attorney General provides legal, administrative, and technical assistance. The State Treasurer must maintain a newly created Restoration Study Fund (funded by legislative transfers, donations, and transfers from the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund) and the state investment officer invests fund balances. The task force may hire staff, subpoena documents, and must terminate November 1, 2028.

Fiscal changes: all proceeds of the marijuana/controlled substances tax are credited to the Restoration Study Fund through Nov. 1, 2028. Beginning Nov. 1, 2028, 5% is credited to the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund; of the remainder, 50% is remitted to counties for County Drug Law Enforcement and Education Funds and the balance to the Nebraska State Patrol Drug Control and Education Cash Fund. The Legislature may transfer from the marijuana administration fund to the Restoration Study Fund until Nov. 1, 2028.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Restitution and Redress for Redlining Task Force (seven voting members: two appointed from the Commission on African American Affairs and four geographically apportioned by congressional district); members appointed by the Governor (subject to legislative standing committee confirmation), serve without pay but receive expense reimbursement, with first appointments due Oct 1, 2025 and first meeting by Nov 1, 2025. The task force may subpoena witnesses, compel documents (seek district court orders), contract with public/private entities, and receive administrative, technical, and legal assistance from the Attorney General. All state agencies must provide requested information and maintain confidentiality where required. Establishes the Restoration Study Fund, to be credited by Legislative transfers (including from the Marijuana and Controlled Substances Tax Administration Cash Fund) and gifts; the fund is expended by the task force and invested by the state investment officer under state investment law. The act terminates Nov 1, 2028.
