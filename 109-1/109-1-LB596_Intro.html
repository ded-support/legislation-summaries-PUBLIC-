Summary

LB596 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §25-2228 to recognize publication of legally required notices on websites or applications of digital news publications that do not have a print edition and to treat such online publication for the required number of days/weeks as legally sufficient. It retains that notices may continue to be published in newspapers and reiterates the existing requirement (effective Oct. 1, 2022) that newspapers or digital news publications post notices on a statewide repository website established and maintained by a majority of Nebraska newspapers; failure to post there does not invalidate a notice. The bill repeals the original section. No state agencies or departments are named, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language allowing required notices or publications to be met by posting on a digital news publication’s website or application for the prescribed number of days or weeks (including when no print edition exists). No government agencies, departments, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced.
