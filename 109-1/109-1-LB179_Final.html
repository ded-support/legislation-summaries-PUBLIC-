Summary

LB179 amends statutes governing municipal police and firefighters.

- Cities of the first class / Police Officers Retirement System: raises required police officer employee contributions from 7% to 9% of salary effective October 1, 2025. Cities continue to "pick up" those contributions and treat them as employer contributions for federal tax purposes; contributions are credited to employee accounts and the Police Officers Retirement System Fund.

- Cities / Firefighters Retirement Act: requires the retirement system (and its retirement committee) to permit optional benefit forms allowing retiring firefighters (for retirements on or after Jan 1, 1997) to elect a single lump-sum payment or one or more partial payments. The retirement committee and city council retain roles in selecting/prescribing annuity comparisons and lump-sum determinations.

- Funding: if a firefighter's retirement value cannot purchase the statutory minimum pension, the city must use funds from the retirement system's unallocated employer account to provide the required benefit.

- Operative date: firefighter option and repeal provisions effective Oct 1, 2025.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two provisions: (1) effective October 1, 2025, each police officer must contribute 9% of salary to the retirement system; and (2) permits retiring firefighters to elect to receive their retirement value as a single lump sum or as one or more partial payments in amounts and frequency they choose. No specific state agency beyond the retirement system is named and no new funding or appropriations are included.
