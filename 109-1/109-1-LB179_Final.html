Summary

LB179 amends Nebraska retirement statutes affecting cities of the first class and their public safety retirement systems. Key agency/financial provisions:

- Police: Section 16-1005 raises the employee contribution rate for police officers from 7% to 9% of salary effective October 1, 2025. Cities of the first class continue to "pick up" these contributions (treated as employer contributions for federal tax purposes under IRC §414(h)) and must pay them from the same municipal payroll funding sources; employees may not opt out. Voluntary contributions and interest-crediting rules remain.

- Firefighters: Section 16-1027 directs the retirement committee and funding medium to offer optional benefit forms permitting single lump-sum or one-or-more partial payments of a firefighter’s retirement value (applicable to retirees on/after Jan 1, 1997). If a minimum statutory pension cannot be provided from a firefighter’s retirement value, the city must use funds from the retirement system’s unallocated employer account to satisfy the benefit.

- Operative date: Benefit-form changes (Sections 2 and 4) become effective October 1, 2025.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two provisions: (1) Beginning October 1, 2025, each police officer must contribute 9% of salary to the (public) retirement system. (2) Grants firefighters, upon retirement, the option to receive their retirement value as a single lump sum or as one or more partial payments in amounts and at frequencies they elect. No specific state agency is named beyond the retirement system, and no explicit appropriations, budget allocations, or funding transfers are specified.
