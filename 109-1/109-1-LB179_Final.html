Summary

LB179 amends police and firefighters' retirement law for cities of the first class. It raises police officer employee contribution rates to the Police Officers Retirement System from 7% to 9% effective October 1, 2025, and continues the requirement that cities of the first class “pick up” those contributions (treated as employer contributions for federal tax purposes) with payment from the same municipal salary funding source. For the Cities of the First Class Firefighters Retirement Act, the retirement committee/city funding medium must permit optional benefit forms allowing single lump‑sum or partial distributions of a firefighter's retirement value (applicable for retirees on/after Jan 1, 1997); shortfalls to meet minimum pensions may be funded from the retirement system’s unallocated employer account. Operative date: Sections on optional distributions and repeal provisions take effect October 1, 2025.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two provisions affecting public safety retirement benefits: starting October 1, 2025, each police officer must contribute 9% of salary to the retirement system. Separately, firefighters are permitted, upon retirement, to elect receipt of their retirement value as a single lump-sum or as one or more partial payments in amounts and timing they choose. No specific appropriations, agency budget allocations, or employer contribution changes are specified.
