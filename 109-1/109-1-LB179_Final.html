Summary

LB179 amends two retirement statutes affecting cities of the first class.

- Dept./agencies referenced: Police Officers Retirement System (employee accounts and Police Officers Retirement System Fund), retirement committee, city council, cities of the first class, Internal Revenue Service, and the retirement system’s unallocated employer account.

- Police officers (sec. 16-1005): employee contribution rate increases from 7% to 9% of salary effective October 1, 2025. Cities of the first class “pick up” these contributions and treat them as employer contributions for federal tax purposes; withholding continues until IRS/federal rulings. Voluntary additional contributions remain allowed.

- Firefighters (sec. 16-1027): optional benefit forms must include single lump-sum or one-or-more partial lump-sum distributions for retirees with retirement dates on or after Jan 1, 1997; retirement committee and city council retain roles in funding/annuity choices. If a firefighter’s retirement value cannot provide a required minimum pension, the city must use funds from the retirement system’s unallocated employer account.

- Operative dates: firefighter changes and repeal of original 16-1027 effective Oct 1, 2025; other sections effective on their statutory effective dates.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that beginning October 1, 2025 police officers must contribute 9% of salary to the (public) retirement system. Also authorizes firefighters, upon retirement, to elect to receive their retirement value as a single lump sum or as one or more partial payments at chosen amounts/frequencies. No specific state agencies or budget appropriations are named and no direct funding or appropriation changes are included.
