Summary

LB224 requires the State of Nebraska to provide paid maternity leave for state employees. Key provisions: full‑time state employees who give birth and assume a parental role are entitled to 12 weeks paid leave (part‑time leave prorated). Leave may not begin before birth, must be used within six months, is generally non‑intermittent (unless by mutual written agreement or a health‑care provider certifies medical necessity), and runs concurrently with federal Family and Medical Leave Act protections. Leave is not charged to sick, vacation, compensatory, or other leave. Employees are protected from defined retaliatory actions and are guaranteed return to their prior or an equivalent position. No appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a state-paid maternity leave program for state employees: full‑time state employees receive 12 weeks paid leave (part‑time prorated) to bond with a newborn. Leave may not start before birth, must be used within six months, is limited to 12 weeks per 12‑month period, generally cannot be charged against other leave, runs concurrently with federal FMLA, and may be intermittent only by written agreement or medical certification. Defines “state employee,” “maternity leave eligible employee,” and “retaliatory action,” and guarantees return to the same or equivalent position. Mentions health care provider and FMLA (29 U.S.C. 2601 et seq.). No agencies, appropriations, or new funding/budget allocations are specified.
