Summary

Bill amends the Cities of the First Class Firefighters Retirement Act (sections 16-1024, 16-1025). Affected governmental entities: cities of the first class, the Firefighters Retirement System Fund, and the Internal Revenue Service (referenced for federal tax treatment under IRC §414(h)). Key changes to contribution rates and offsets:

- Employee contributions: scheduled increases to 12.7% by Oct 1, 2026 remain, but beginning March 1, 2025 firefighters employed by a city with >60,000 population in a county >100,000 and covered by an absolute coverage group will instead contribute 6.5%.

- Employer (city) contributions: scheduled increases to 15% by Oct 1, 2026 remain, but the same large-city cohort pays 13% beginning March 1, 2025.

- Offsets: beginning July 20, 2024 a 6.2% offset to contributions applies for firefighters/cities in absolute coverage groups, excluding the large-city cohort above.

Cities continue to pick up employee contributions and to fund any additional amounts into the Firefighters Retirement System Fund. The bill takes effect immediately (emergency clause).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds contribution requirements effective March 1, 2025: firefighters employed by a city with >60,000 population located in a county with >100,000 population who participate in the retirement system and are covered by an "absolute coverage group" must contribute 6.5% of salary to the retirement system. The city of the first class meeting those same population conditions must contribute 13% of the periodic salary for each such participating firefighter. References to "retirement system," cities (including "city of the first class") and counties are the only government entities affected. No separate appropriations, new agencies, or broader budget allocations are included.
