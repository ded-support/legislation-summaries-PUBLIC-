Summary

Bill amends the Cities of the First Class Firefighters Retirement Act to change contribution rates and offsets administered through the Firefighters Retirement System/Fund. Employee contributions rise on a schedule to 12.7% (effective Oct. 1, 2026) unless the firefighter is employed by a city over 60,000 population in a county over 100,000 and covered by an absolute coverage group—those remain at 6.5% beginning March 1, 2025. Employer (city) contributions rise to 15% by Oct. 1, 2026, with the same large-city/county exception keeping contributions at 13% for affected firefighters starting March 1, 2025. Beginning July 20, 2024, a 6.2% offset to contributions applies for firefighters and cities covered by absolute coverage groups, except for the large-city/county combination. Cities continue to pick up employee contributions (IRC 414(h) treatment) and must fund additional amounts into the unallocated employer account as needed. IRS and Internal Revenue Code provisions referenced. Emergency clause: effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds mandatory contributions to the firefighters’ retirement system, effective March 1, 2025. Firefighters employed by a “city of the first class” (city >60,000 population in a county >100,000), who participate in the retirement system and are in an “absolute coverage group,” must contribute 6.5% of salary to the retirement system. Those cities must contribute 13% of each such participating firefighter’s periodic salary to the retirement system. No other state agencies or specific appropriations/budget line items are referenced; the change is limited to employee and municipal contribution rates to the retirement system.
