Summary

LB108 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§16-1024 and 16-1025 (Cities of the First Class Firefighters Retirement Act). Key agency/financial effects:

- Firefighters Retirement System Fund/retirement system: employee contribution schedule retained (6.5% → 8.7% → 10.7% → 12.7% by 10/1/26), but beginning March 1, 2025 firefighters employed by a city of the first class with >60,000 population in a county >100,000 and covered by an absolute coverage group will instead contribute 6.5%. Payroll deductions credited to employee accounts; accounts earn regular interest.

- Cities of the first class (municipal employers): general employer rates remain 13% → 14% → 15%, but beginning March 1, 2025 applicable large cities (same population criteria) will contribute 13% for affected firefighters. Cities must fund additional amounts into the unallocated employer account as needed.

- Offsets: beginning July 20, 2024 a 6.2% offset applies to contributions for firefighters and smaller cities covered by absolute coverage groups, but not to the large-city employers/firefighters noted above.

- IRS/ federal tax treatment (section 414(h)/IRS) language retained. Emergency clause: effective on passage; original sections repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an effective date (March 1, 2025) and new contribution requirements to the retirement system: firefighters employed by a city with >60,000 population in a county with >100,000 who participate in the retirement system and are in an “absolute coverage group” must contribute 6.5% of salary. The employing “city of the first class” meeting the same population criteria must contribute 13% of each such firefighter’s periodic salary to the retirement system. No other agencies, new appropriations, or budget line items are specified.
