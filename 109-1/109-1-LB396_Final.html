Summary

LB396 amends procedures for public power entities and related agencies. It requires public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, rural power districts, and agencies created under sections 18-2426 to 18-2434 to prepare annual written proposed budgets showing prior two years’ revenues and expenditures, current-year estimates, and a fiscal-policy summary. Proposed budgets must be posted at the entity’s principal headquarters at least seven days before the board adoption meeting; a newspaper notice (at least seven days prior) must state availability and location. Any interim changes must be added at headquarters before the meeting, the public must be allowed to testify, a written record kept, and post-adoption change summaries made available. LB396 also requires districts’ CPA audits to be filed with the Auditor of Public Accounts within 180 days after year-end. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
