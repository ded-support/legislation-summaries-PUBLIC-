Summary

LB396 amends procedures for certain Nebraska electric districts and related agencies (public power districts, public power and irrigation districts under Chapter 70, art. 6; rural power districts under Chapter 70, art. 8; and agencies created under §§18-2426 to 18-2434) and audit filing requirements.

Key provisions:

- Budget requirements (§13-516): these districts/agencies must prepare written proposed budgets showing prior two years' revenues/expenditures, current-year estimates, and a fiscal policy summary. Proposed budgets must be available at the entity’s principal headquarters at least seven days before the board meeting adopting the budget. A newspaper notice (at least seven days prior) must state the meeting time/place and where the proposed budget is available. Changes made before the meeting must be posted; the public may testify and a written record is required; adopted-budget changes must be summarized and available for inspection.

- Audit filing (§70-623): annual CPA audits (per §84-304.01) must be kept at the district’s principal place of business and filed with the Auditor of Public Accounts within 180 days after fiscal year end.

No appropriations or funding allocations are included.
