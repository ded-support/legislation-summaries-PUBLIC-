Summary

LB90 amends statutes governing improvement districts in cities of the first class, focusing on actions by the mayor and city council and the city clerk. Key agency-related provisions:

- City clerk must publish creation notice once weekly for not less than 20 days. Owners representing >50% of front footage may file objections within 20 days to require repeal of the ordinance.

- Mayor and city council must create districts on petition (3/4 front footage) and may deny formation if water, sewer, or street grading are absent, providing a written explanation to interested parties.

- Bidding: council must solicit materials suggested by a petition from owners (25%); bid openings are postponed 10 days to allow owner selection.

- Funding: costs assessed by special assessments (installments up to 20 years) or paid in full within 50 days; council may set interest on installments up to the statutory rate (section 45-104.01). Special assessments constitute the sinking fund for city-issued street improvement bonds payable in up to 20 years.

Original sections 16-617 through 16-624 are repealed and replaced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the mayor and city council (notably of a city of the first class) to create improvement districts that may combine two or more types of work (sidewalks, public ways, public spaces, intersecting improvements) in a single ordinance. Specifies the mayor and city council must follow notice and bid-advertising steps (see §§16-619, 16-624) when forming districts and contracting work. Funding remains by levy of special assessments on specially benefited property; owners may pay the entire assessed cost within 50 days of levy to be exempt from liens. References issuance and terms of street improvement bonds as authorized by ordinance and §§16-617 and 16-622. No direct new appropriations or departmental budget allocations are created.
