Summary

LB90 amends statutes governing improvement districts in cities of the first class. Key governmental actors: the mayor and city council (authority to create/repeal districts, determine assessments, select materials, postpone/award bids, reject bids, declare delinquency and issue bonds) and the city clerk (publishes creation notices, accepts owner petitions/objections). Funding and budget provisions: costs are recovered by special assessments apportioned to benefited lots, payable in up to 20 annual installments with interest (rate set by council not to exceed the rate in section 45-104.01); owners may prepay within 50 days to avoid liens. The council may issue city bonds (maturity ≤20 years) with special taxes/assessments as the sinking fund. Property-owner petition thresholds and objection/notice procedures are revised; council may deny district formation if utilities/grading are lacking and must provide written grounds. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures giving the mayor and city council (particularly in cities of the first class) authority to create improvement districts for sidewalks, public ways and mixed-type improvements, following notice under §16‑619 and bid advertising per §16‑624. Funding is by levying special assessments on property “especially benefited” in proportion to benefit; property owners may prepay the entire assessed cost within 50 days of levy to remove liens. It also references financing via street improvement bonds issued pursuant to §16‑622 and the bond‑ordinance provisions in §16‑617. No new departmental appropriations or direct budget allocations are specified.
