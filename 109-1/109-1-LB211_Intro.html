Summary

LB211 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-3403 (Property Tax Growth Limitation Act). For fiscal years beginning July 1, 2025, a political subdivision’s preliminary property tax request authority is set at the amount levied by the county board of equalization in the prior year, less exceptions used under §13-3404. It permits additional year-to-year increases based on (a) the subdivision’s growth percentage and (b) an inflation-based percentage. If public safety (per §13-320) comprises ≥20% of a subdivision’s property-tax-funded budget, the inflation component uses the greater of zero or the inflation percentage; if <20%, the inflation floor is the greater of 2% or the inflation percentage. No direct appropriations or new agency budgets are created. The bill takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula altering local governments' (political subdivisions') property tax request authority based on the share of their property-tax-funded budget spent on "public safety services" (as defined in §13-320). The allowable increase equals two components, each computed on prior-year property taxes minus exceptions used under §13-3404(1) and (2): (1) the base growth percentage; and (2) an inflation component. If public safety ≥20% of the property-tax-funded budget, the inflation component is the greater of zero or inflation. If public safety <20%, the inflation component is the greater of 2% or inflation. No direct appropriations or dollar amounts are specified.
