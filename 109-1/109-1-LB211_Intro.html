Summary

LB211 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-3403 (Property Tax Growth Limitation Act) for fiscal years beginning July 1, 2025. It retains the preliminary property tax request authority for political subdivisions as the amount levied by the county board of equalization in the prior year less exceptions under §13-3404, then modifies allowable increases. If public safety (per §13-320) is ≥20% of a subdivision’s property-tax-funded budget, increases equal growth percentage plus the greater of zero or the inflation percentage. If public safety is <20%, the inflation component is floored at 2% (greater of 2% or inflation). The bill repeals the original §13-3403 and takes effect immediately upon passage due to an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula letting political subdivisions (local governments) increase their property tax request authority based on prior-year levies (minus exceptions under §13-3404(1)&(2)). Two components apply in both cases: (i) prior-year tax base × growth percentage; and (ii) prior-year tax base × inflation percentage with different floors. If public safety services (as defined in §13-320) are ≥20% of the property tax‑funded budget, the inflation component uses the greater of zero or the inflation rate. If public safety is <20%, the inflation component uses the greater of two percent or the inflation rate. This amends how much localities may raise property-tax revenue; it is not a direct state appropriation.
