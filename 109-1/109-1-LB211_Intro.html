Summary

LB211 amends calculation of a political subdivision’s property tax request authority under the Property Tax Growth Limitation Act. The preliminary authority is set equal to the prior fiscal year property taxes levied by the county board of equalization less exceptions under §13-3404. Political subdivisions may increase that authority by two components (growth and inflation) based on whether public safety (per §13-320) comprises ≥20% of their property-tax-funded budget. If ≥20%, the inflation component is the greater of zero or the inflation percentage; if <20%, the inflation component is the greater of two percent or the inflation percentage. The bill repeals the original §13-3403 and takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula letting a political subdivision increase its property tax request authority based on prior-year levies (minus exceptions under A.R.S. §13-3404(1)–(2)), its growth percentage, and inflation. If public safety services (per §13-320) are ≥20% of the subdivision’s property-tax-funded budget, the inflation component is the greater of zero or actual inflation (no minimum). If public safety services are <20%, the inflation component is the greater of 2% or actual inflation (a 2% floor). No new agency appropriations are specified; the change alters how local governments’ (political subdivisions’) property-tax increases are calculated.
