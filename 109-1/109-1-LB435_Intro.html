Summary

Bill LB435 amends the Conveyance Safety Act, reallocating duties to the Conveyance Advisory Committee to advise the State Fire Marshal on rules, qualifications, temporary licenses, enforcement, continuing education providers, and disciplinary actions. The State Fire Marshal is authorized to adopt/update conveyance safety standards, grant case-by-case equivalency requests (appealable under the Administrative Procedure Act), register conveyances, and require certificates of inspection before first use. The State Fire Marshal employs a state elevator inspector (and deputies) and establishes inspector qualifications. Licensing applications for elevator mechanics and contractors are submitted to the State Fire Marshal and must permit criminal-history checks via the Nebraska State Patrol/FBI. Owners may satisfy inspection requirements via approved third-party inspection and insurance filings. Procedural provisions for complaint investigations by the State Fire Marshal or state elevator inspector are clarified. Original sections listed are repealed. No appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements involving the State Fire Marshal and state licensing for elevators. Equivalency requests must go to the State Fire Marshal (or designee) and include: a third‑party inspection certificate submitted to the State Fire Marshal, a conveyance insurance policy and statement that it is insured, and payment of an administrative fee established pursuant to section 81‑5,214. Applications for an elevator mechanic license must include name, address, contact email, and telephone. Elevator contractor applications must include subsection (3) info, business name/address, estimated employee count with workers’ compensation evidence (if applicable), proof of general liability/personal injury/property damage insurance, a description of accidents over $1,000, and the name/phone/license number of a Nebraska licensed elevator mechanic employed or contracted in writing. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
