Summary

LB435 amends the Conveyance Safety Act, reallocating duties and authorities among the State Fire Marshal, the Conveyance Advisory Committee, and the state elevator inspector. The Committee’s advisory duties to the State Fire Marshal are clarified (including continuing education and discipline recommendations). The bill removes variances and authorizes the State Fire Marshal to grant individual equivalency requests (appealable under the Administrative Procedure Act). The State Fire Marshal must employ a state elevator inspector (and deputies) under direct supervision and may accept third‑party inspection certificates and insurance filings in lieu of state inspection (subject to an administrative fee set in §81‑5,214). New-installation certificates require contractor submission to the state elevator inspector. License applications to the State Fire Marshal must include contact info and consent to FBI criminal‑history checks via the Nebraska State Patrol; contractor applications must identify a licensed elevator mechanic. No new appropriations or explicit funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures and application requirements tied to the State Fire Marshal and elevator licensing. Equivalency requests (or to the State Fire Marshal’s designee) must include: a third‑party inspection certificate, proof of a conveyance insurance policy and a filed insurance statement, and payment of an administrative fee established pursuant to section 81‑5,214. Elevator mechanic applications must include applicant name, address, contact email and phone. Elevator contractor applications must include the subsection (3) information, business name/address, employee count (and workers’ compensation evidence if applicable), evidence of general liability/personal injury/property damage insurance, a description of accidents causing >$1,000 in injury/damage involving the applicant, and the name/phone/ Nebraska elevator mechanic license number of a contracted or employed licensed mechanic. No explicit appropriations; fee authority referenced.
