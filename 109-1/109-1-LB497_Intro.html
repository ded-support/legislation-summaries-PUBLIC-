Summary

LB497 retains multiple references to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). It (1) treats students placed in foster family homes licensed or approved by DHHS (or in homes under §83‑108.04) as residents of the district where they became foster children unless other determinations apply; (2) specifies that when a ward is placed outside his or her home district and does not reside in a foster home licensed/approved by DHHS, the state shall pay the student's education and required transportation costs to the receiving district "under rules and regulations prescribed by the Department of Health and Human Services"; and (3) treats students in residential settings operated by providers certified or licensed by DHHS as remaining residents of their prior district. The bill contains no separate appropriation or new DHHS budget allocation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language clarifies that students enrolled in private, denominational, parochial, or non‑accredited schools may enroll part‑time, without charge, in a neighboring district to participate in extracurricular activities when their resident district does not offer that activity and the receiving district is the nearest provider. It also includes a limitation reference about credit‑hour enrollment and habitual‑truancy statutes. There are no provisions affecting the Department of Health and Human Services, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS in the provided text.
