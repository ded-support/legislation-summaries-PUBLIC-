Summary

LB497 references the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) in residency and payment provisions for students placed outside their home district. Key DHHS-specific items:

- If a ward is placed outside his/her resident district and does not reside in a foster family home licensed or approved by DHHS, the state shall pay the student's education and required transportation costs to the receiving district or approved institution "under rules and regulations prescribed by the Department of Health and Human Services" (payments not in advance).

- A ward who resides in a foster family home licensed or approved by DHHS is generally deemed a resident of the district where they resided when becoming a foster child, unless §§43-1311/1312 dictate otherwise.

- Residential settings operated by providers certified or licensed by DHHS are addressed for residency determinations. No direct DHHS appropriations or new budget allocations are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language requires a school board to admit, without charge, a student who is enrolled in a private, denominational, parochial, or a school that has opted out of accreditation, for part‑time enrollment solely to participate in extracurricular activities per §79‑2,136, when the student’s resident district does not offer the activity and the admitting district is the closest that does. There are no references to the Department of Health and Human Services, nor any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for that agency.
