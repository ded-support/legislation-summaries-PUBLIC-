Summary

LB497 amends section 79-215 to address residency and payment rules where the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) is involved. Key provisions: (1) Wards placed outside their home district who do not reside in foster homes licensed/approved by DHHS trigger state payment of education and required transportation costs to the receiving district or approved institution, under rules prescribed by DHHS. (2) Students placed in residential settings operated by providers certified or licensed by DHHS remain residents of their prior school district. The bill mandates state payment obligations but contains no specific appropriations, dollar amounts, or new budget line-item allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that school boards must admit, without charge, students who are enrolled in private, denominational, parochial, or in schools that opt out of accreditation/approval (per §79‑1601) for part‑time enrollment solely to participate in extracurricular activities under §79‑2,136 — provided the student’s resident district does not offer that activity and the receiving district is the closest that does. No provisions affect the Department of Health and Human Services, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
