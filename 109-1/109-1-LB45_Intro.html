Summary

LB45 amends statutes governing the Nebraska Training and Support Cash Fund and the Nebraska Worker Training Board. Key agency references and funding changes:

- Nebraska Training and Support Cash Fund: maintained as an investable fund (state investment officer). Expenditures require the Governor's written authorization upon the Commissioner of Labor’s recommendation; the Legislature may direct transfers to the General Fund. Interest from the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund is credited to the cash fund.

- Uses expanded to explicitly include awarding grants under section 3 (grants for job programs for historically underserved youth) and to cover administrative costs; an Administrative Costs Reserve Account within the fund must be allocated to cover anticipated administrative expenses.

- Nebraska Worker Training Board composition specified (includes Commissioner of Labor, Director of Economic Development, Commissioner of Education, community college representative). The board must prepare annual program guidelines for fund-supported workforce activities.

- The Department (Labor) must publish an annual report detailing administrative expenditures; no unrelated departmental costs may be charged to these allocations.

No specific appropriation amounts are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority to award grants under section 3 of the act pursuant to subdivisions (2)(c)–(f) of section 48-622.02, specifying use of grant administrative funds for application review, program auditing, and data management. It bars allocating any portion of administrative costs to unrelated departmental expenses. The (unnamed) department administering the grants must publish an annual report itemizing administrative expenditures to ensure transparency. No specific dollar amounts, appropriations, or funding increases are included.
