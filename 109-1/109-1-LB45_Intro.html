Summary

LB45 amends Neb. Rev. Statutes §§48-622.02 and 48-622.03. It clarifies administration and allowable uses of the Nebraska Training and Support Cash Fund (invested by the state investment officer), adds authority to award grants under a new section, and continues that interest from the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund may be credited to the Cash Fund. Expenditures require the Governor’s written authorization upon the Commissioner of Labor’s recommendation; the Legislature may transfer fund monies to the General Fund or direct transfers to the Sector Partnership Program Fund. The bill creates an Administrative Costs Reserve Account within the Cash Fund to cover anticipated administrative costs and prohibits allocating administrative costs to unrelated departmental expenses. It establishes the seven‑member Nebraska Worker Training Board (including the Commissioners of Labor and Education, the Director of Economic Development, and a community college designee) and requires an annual program plan and public reporting of administrative expenditures.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the (unnamed) department to award grants under section 3 and subdivisions (2)(c)–(f) of section 48-622.02 specifically to fund application review, program auditing, and data management. Administrative funds awarded may not be used for unrelated departmental expenses. The department must publish an annual report itemizing administrative expenditures to ensure transparency. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.
