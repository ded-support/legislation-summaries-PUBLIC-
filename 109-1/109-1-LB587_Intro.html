Summary

LB587 amends Nebraska’s Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act to shorten tenant termination timelines and expand tenant remedies for landlord noncompliance. Key changes: shortens the repeat‑breach termination notice to seven days; clarifies termination procedures if a landlord remedies a breach before the termination date; adds landlord failures to remediate known mold or pest infestations to the list of breaches; authorizes tenants to purchase remediation supplies/services and deduct actual costs from rent; allows recovery for diminution in rental value and for damage to tenant personal property; excuses rent during noncompliance; and permits recovery of substitute‑housing costs and attorney’s fees for deliberate failures. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds tenant remedies when a landlord fails to remediate a known mold problem or pest infestation: tenants may purchase supplies or services to remediate and deduct actual, reasonable costs from rent, and may recover damages for diminution in value of their personal property caused by the landlord's deliberate or negligent noncompliance. The text also clarifies applicability of the remedies subsections. No government agencies, departments, or budget/appropriation changes are mentioned.
