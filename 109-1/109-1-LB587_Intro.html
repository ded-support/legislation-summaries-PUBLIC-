Summary

LB587 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§76-1425 and 76-1427 (Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act). It shortens and clarifies notice/termination timelines for tenant termination following landlord noncompliance and for repeat violations, and expands tenant remedies for landlords' failure to maintain dwelling units or essential services. New remedies allow tenants, after written notice, to (a) procure and deduct reasonable costs for hot/running water, heat, and essential services; (b) remediate and deduct costs for mold or pest infestations; (c) recover diminution in fair rental value and diminution to personal property; (d) be excused from paying rent during the noncompliance period; and (e) recover substitute-housing costs if the landlord's failure is deliberate. Reasonable attorney fees are recoverable. Original sections are repealed. No state agencies, departments, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced or affected.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions expand tenant remedies when a landlord fails to remediate a known mold problem or pest infestation: tenants may (a) purchase supplies or hire services to remediate the condition during the landlord’s noncompliance and deduct actual, reasonable costs from rent, and (b) recover damages reflecting diminution in value of their personal property caused by the landlord’s deliberate or negligent noncompliance.
