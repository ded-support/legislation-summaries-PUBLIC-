Summary

LB343 amends specialty license plate provisions affecting the Department of Motor Vehicles (the “department”). It reduces the specialty plate fee from $70 (before Oct. 1, 2025) to $40 (beginning Oct. 1, 2025). Sixty percent of the initial/renewal fee is credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and forty percent to the Highway Trust Fund. A postage/handling charge the department may assess when mailing plates is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. A $3 fee for transferring specialty plates is likewise credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. The department must provide application forms, may mail plates, furnish temporary stickers to counties at no cost, and may adopt related rules.
