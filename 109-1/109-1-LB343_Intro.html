Summary

LB343 amends section 60-3,104.01 as follows for references to the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV):

- Changes the specialty license plate application fee from $70 to $40 effective October 1, 2025. Sixty percent of initial and renewal specialty plate fees are credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and forty percent to the Highway Trust Fund.

- Allows the DMV to charge a postage and handling fee for mailing plates/registration; that fee is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- Requires the $3 specialty-plate transfer fee be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- Assigns DMV duties: provide application forms, deliver plates/certificates (including by mail), furnish temporary license stickers to counties at no cost, and promulgate related rules.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the DMV: the added language sets a new dollar amount effective October 1, 2025 — "forty dollars." The clause distinguishes the period before October 1, 2025 (left unspecified in the added text) and a clear $40 amount beginning October 1, 2025. The amendment does not include any explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Motor Vehicles.
