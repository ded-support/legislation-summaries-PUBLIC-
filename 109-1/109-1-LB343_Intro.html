Summary

LB343 (amending §60‑3,104.01) directs the State Treasurer to credit 60% of specialty license plate issuance and renewal fees to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund (and 40% to the Highway Trust Fund). Any postage-and-handling fee charged by the department for mailing specialty plates must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. The $3 fee for transferring specialty plates is also remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.
