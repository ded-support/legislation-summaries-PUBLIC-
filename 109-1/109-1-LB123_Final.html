Summary

LB123 amends procedures enforced by the Auditor of Public Accounts and the State Treasurer. Governmental units must submit calculated restricted funds authority with budget filings; the Auditor notifies the governing body and the State Treasurer of budget-limit noncompliance. The State Treasurer must suspend state aid to noncompliant units, hold funds for six months (returned if compliance occurs), and after six months forfeit and redistribute funds to other county recipients or to the Highway Allocation Fund (homestead exemption reimbursements to the General Fund). Forfeiture continues until Auditor confirms compliance; failure to comply for 12 months renders the subdivision ineligible for future state aid until compliance. For municipalities that fail to file required annual audits, the Auditor notifies the State Treasurer, who withholds distributions under sections 39-2511–39-2520 and redistributes withheld funds after six months; 12‑month noncompliance bars future distributions until remedied. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Agencies mentioned: the Auditor of Public Accounts and the State Treasurer.

Summary of additions:

- The Auditor notifies the State Treasurer of noncompliant governmental units; state aid to those units is forfeited and remains forfeited until the Auditor confirms compliance to the State Treasurer.

- Forfeited state aid is redistributed; if no eligible recipient exists, funds are returned to the Highway Allocation Fund.

- Any political subdivision that remains noncompliant 12 months after notice from the Auditor (to the State Treasurer) becomes ineligible for future state-aid distributions until it achieves compliance.

- Specifically, cities and villages failing to comply within 12 months lose entitlement to distributions under sections 39-2511 to 39-2520, regaining entitlement only upon compliance.
