Summary

LB123 amends enforcement of budget, reporting, and audit compliance by directing the Auditor of Public Accounts to prepare budget documents, receive restricted funds authority submissions, and notify governing bodies and the State Treasurer of noncompliance with budget limits (secs. 13-518–13-522). The State Treasurer must suspend state-aid distributions to noncompliant governmental units until compliance; suspended funds are held six months, then forfeited and redistributed to other county recipients or returned to the Highway Allocation Fund (or to the General Fund for homestead-exemption reimbursements). Continued noncompliance for 12 months renders a political subdivision ineligible for future state-aid distributions until compliance. For municipal audit failures, the Auditor notifies the State Treasurer to withhold distributions under sections 39-2511–39-2520; six-month forfeiture and 12-month disqualification rules apply. Original sections 13-522, 19-2907, and 13-3407 are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds enforcement by the Auditor of Public Accounts and the State Treasurer: when the Auditor notifies the State Treasurer of a noncompliant governmental unit, that unit must forfeit state aid until the Auditor notifies the Treasurer of compliance. Forfeited aid remains forfeited and is redistributed—either to the county where the noncompliant unit is located or returned to the Highway Allocation Fund if no eligible recipient exists. If a political subdivision fails to comply within 12 months of the Auditor’s notice to the State Treasurer it becomes ineligible for future state aid (eligibility is restored upon compliance). Cities and villages that remain noncompliant for 12 months lose entitlement to distributions under sections 39-2511–39-2520 until they comply.
