Summary

LB123 amends procedures for the Auditor of Public Accounts and the State Treasurer regarding noncompliant political subdivisions and municipalities.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: prepares budget documents, requires governmental units to submit calculated restricted funds authority, and notifies the governing body and State Treasurer of noncompliance (budget limits or failure to file audits).

- State Treasurer: upon Auditor notice, suspends distribution of state aid and holds funds for six months; if compliance occurs funds are released, if not the funds are forfeited and redistributed to other county recipients or returned to the Highway Allocation Fund (or to the General Fund for homestead-exemption reimbursements).

- Municipal audits: failure to file triggers withholding of distributions under sections 39-2511–39-2520; withheld amounts are redistributed after six months if audit remains unfiled.

- Extended noncompliance (12 months) renders the political subdivision or city/village ineligible for future state distributions until compliance is achieved.

- Repeals original sections 13-522, 19-2907, and 13-3407.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Auditor of Public Accounts must notify the State Treasurer when a governmental unit is noncompliant; the unit forfeits state aid until the Auditor notifies the Treasurer that it is compliant.

- Forfeited state aid remains forfeited and is redistributed; if there is no eligible recipient it is returned to the Highway Allocation Fund per subdivision (a)(i).

- Any political subdivision that fails to reach compliance within 12 months of the Auditor’s delinquency notice is ineligible for future state aid distributions until it becomes compliant.

- Cities and villages similarly lose entitlement to distributions under sections 39-2511 to 39-2520 until compliance is achieved.
