Summary

LB46 amends §68-1017.02 to direct the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to maximize use of federal SNAP options, minimize State General Fund use, staff evaluations, and submit annual reports to the Health and Human Services Committee. DHHS must develop a SNAP outreach plan for approval by the Food and Nutrition Service of the United States Department of Agriculture and may seek federal matching, private gifts, grants, and donations. DHHS is required to establish a Restaurant Meals Program under SNAP (operational by July 1, 2026) and may adopt rules to implement it. The bill references the Department of Labor’s SNAP Next Step Program, encouraging eligible recipients to participate where federal funds and services are available and expressing legislative intent that the Department of Labor use no General Funds for those processes. No explicit dollar appropriations are provided.
