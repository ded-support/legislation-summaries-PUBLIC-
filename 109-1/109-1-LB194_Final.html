Summary

LB194 amends documentary stamp tax exemptions and includes a specific compliance provision: for transfers into a trust that would be exempt if made directly to beneficiaries, the register of deeds must receive a signed statement certifying the exemption, and supporting evidence must be maintained by the signer and made available for inspection by the Department of Revenue. The bill contains no provisions changing funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The addition treats step‑relationships as equivalent to blood relatives for the statute’s family definition and clarifies that the subdivision (5)(a) deed-transfer exemptions apply when property is transferred into a corporation or LLC wholly owned by a single shareholder/member in three cases: the grantor is the owner, spouses transfer to an entity owned by one spouse, or family members (including step‑relations) transfer to an entity owned by a family member. There are no changes to Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or budget allocations—the amendment only clarifies which transfers qualify for exemptions DOR administers.
