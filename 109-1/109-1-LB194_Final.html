Summary

LB194 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §76-902 (documentary stamp tax exemptions). It retains the requirement (subdivision (19)) that, for deeds transferring property into a trust qualifying for an exemption, the register of deeds must be presented a signed statement certifying the exemption and that supporting evidence is maintained and available for inspection by the Department of Revenue. No provisions in the bill alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Revenue to treat step-relationships as equivalent to blood relatives for the family transfer exemption and to apply that exemption to deeds conveying property to a corporation or LLC that is wholly owned by a single shareholder/member in three situations: (1) grantor is the same person as the entity’s sole owner; (2) spouses transfer property to an entity wholly owned by one spouse; (3) family members (including step-relations) transfer property to an entity wholly owned by one family member. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
