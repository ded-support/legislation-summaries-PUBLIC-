Summary

LB194 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §76-902 (documentary stamp tax exemptions). It adds/clarifies exemptions and explicitly requires that, for exempt transfers into a trust, the register of deeds be presented with a signed certification and that supporting evidence be maintained by the signer and be available for inspection by the Department of Revenue. The bill contains no provisions altering funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that "step" relationships count as "blood" relationships when determining family for the deed-transfer exemption in subdivision (5)(a). Clarifies the exemption also applies to deeds transferring property into a corporation or LLC wholly owned by a single person where: the grantor is that owner; spouses transfer to an entity owned by one spouse; or family members (as defined, including step relatives) transfer to an entity owned by one family member. For the Department of Revenue: this changes how it must administer and apply the deed-transfer/exemption rules but contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
