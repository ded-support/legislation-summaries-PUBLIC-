Summary

LB197 amends Nebraska’s Employment Security Law (sections 48-628.09, 48-630, 48-631). Key agency provisions: determinations by the Department of Labor must state reasons and promptly notify parties; claimants continue to receive benefits during appeals unless a hearing officer reverses the allowance; erroneous payments are handled under section 48-665. Adjudicators designated by the Commissioner of Labor may issue redeterminations for computation/identity errors, newly discovered wages, misrepresentations, or errors identified by the Benefit Accuracy Measurement (quality control) program (20 C.F.R. part 602). Redeterminations are limited to two years, require notice, preserve limited appeal rights, and permit the Commissioner to seek revised decisions after final rulings. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a commissioner‑designated representative ("adjudicator") may make a redetermination on a claim, and that a review under the "benefit accuracy measurement program" can trigger a redetermination if it finds an error. It defines the benefit accuracy measurement program as the quality‑control program required by federal regulation at 20 C.F.R. part 602. No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
