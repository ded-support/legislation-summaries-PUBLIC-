Summary

LB197 amends Nebraska's Employment Security Law, affecting the Nebraska Department of Labor and the Commissioner of Labor's adjudicative processes. Key changes: determinations must state reasons and parties be promptly notified; benefits found payable continue during appeal; erroneous benefit overpayments are handled under section 48-665. Redeterminations may be issued by an adjudicator designated by the commissioner for errors in computation/identity, newly discovered wages, misrepresentations of fact, or errors identified by the federal benefit accuracy measurement program (20 C.F.R. part 602). Redeterminations are barred after two years, notice must follow section 48-630, appeals are limited to the issues changed, and the Commissioner may seek revised decisions even after a final hearing officer or court decision. Original sections 48-628.09, 48-630, and 48-631 are repealed. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a representative designated by the commissioner (an "adjudicator") may make a redetermination on claims under §48‑629, and explicitly allows a redetermination when a review under the federal Benefit Accuracy Measurement (BAM) program finds an error. It defines the BAM program as the quality‑control program required by 20 C.F.R. part 602. No specific state agency beyond the commissioner is newly created or named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
