Summary

LB197 amends Nebraska’s Employment Security Law (secs. 48-628.09, 48-630, 48-631) and repeals the originals. Key agency actions: determinations and redeterminations are handled by the Department of Labor and its designated representatives (adjudicators); parties must be promptly notified by the department; appeals are filed with the department under §48-634 and are adjudicated by hearing officers and courts as applicable. New grounds for redetermination include errors identified by the federal benefit accuracy measurement (BAM) program (20 C.F.R. part 602). The bill specifies a two-year limit for redeterminations and confirms that benefits already allowed are paid during appeals. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a "representative designated by the commissioner" is an "adjudicator" who may order a redetermination of a claim and specifically authorizes redetermination when a review under the benefit accuracy measurement (BAM) program finds an error. It defines the BAM program as the quality‑control program required by federal regulation 20 C.F.R. part 602 (the Department of Labor's benefit accuracy measurement requirements). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
