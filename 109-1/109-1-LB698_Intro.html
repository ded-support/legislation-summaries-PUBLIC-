Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Labor. Assigns enforcement authority to the Commissioner of Labor to issue citations when investigations indicate violations of the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act. Specifies notice procedures and maximum administrative penalties ($500 for a first violation; $5,000 for second/subsequent violations). Provides a 15-working-day period to contest citations, with hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act. Unpaid citations bar an employer from contracting with the state or its political subdivisions until paid (contested citations are exempt until resolution). Citations and cited employers' names are public on request, excluding those currently contested. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
