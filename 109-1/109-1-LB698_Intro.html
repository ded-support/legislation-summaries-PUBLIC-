Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Labor and assigns enforcement authority to the Commissioner of Labor. The commissioner must issue citations when investigations indicate violations of the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act, notify employers of proposed administrative penalties (max $500 for a first violation; $5,000 for subsequent violations), and accept contests within 15 working days. The commissioner must provide hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act. Employers with unpaid citations are barred from contracting with the state or political subdivisions until paid (contested citations are not treated as unpaid until resolution). Citations and cited employer names are releasable to the public, except while contested. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
