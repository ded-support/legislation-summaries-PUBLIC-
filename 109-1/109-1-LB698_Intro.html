Summary

LB698 assigns enforcement authority under the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act to the Department of Labor (definition: "Department means the Department of Labor") and directs the Commissioner of Labor to issue citations when investigations reveal possible violations. The bill prescribes notice procedures, administrative penalties (maximum $500 for a first violation; $5,000 for a second or subsequent violation), a 15-working-day period to contest citations with hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act, and a prohibition on contracting with the state or political subdivisions while a citation remains unpaid (contested citations are exempt until resolution). Citations and cited employers’ names are publicly available on request except while contested. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
