Summary

Requires the State Treasurer to transfer $500,000,000 from the Perkins County Canal Project Fund to the Water Sustainability Fund on or before June 30, 2025. The timing and amounts of transfers are to be set by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the State Treasurer to transfer $500 million from the Perkins County Canal Project Fund to the Water Sustainability Fund by June 30, 2025. The timing and installment amounts of that transfer are to be set by the budget administrator of the Budget Division within the Department of Administrative Services. No other appropriations or new funding lines are specified.
