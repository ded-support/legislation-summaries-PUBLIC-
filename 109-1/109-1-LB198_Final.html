Summary

Bill LB198 amends the Pharmacy Benefit Manager Licensure and Regulation Act to regulate pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs), health carriers, health benefit plans, specialty and retail pharmacies, and pharmacy services administrative organizations. Key provisions: define clinician‑administered drugs; prohibit PBMs from excluding accredited Nebraska specialty pharmacies or imposing stricter terms/data reporting; require PBMs/health carriers to meet delivery, notification, appeal, and track‑and‑trace obligations for clinician‑administered drugs and prohibit penalizing providers; bar health plans/carriers/PBMs from forcing exclusive mail‑order use, unilateral prescription transfers, or certain auto‑enrollments (limited exception for maintenance meds); permit network pharmacies to refuse fills if paid below acquisition cost without retaliation; protect retail pharmacies offering delivery; phase out PBM spread pricing (restricted for new contracts 1/1/2026; banned 1/1/2029). No state agencies or budget appropriations are specified. Operative date: January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and new rules governing pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs), specialty pharmacies, health carriers, and health benefit plans. Key provisions: defines "clinician‑administered drug" and specialty pharmacy standards; PBMs cannot apply stricter terms to unaffiliated specialty pharmacies, must limit reporting frequency (generally quarterly), follow federal Drug Supply Chain Security Act track‑and‑trace requirements, and provide delivery/notification (at least five business days) and signature procedures for clinician‑administered drug shipments. Establishes appeal/exception processes if delivery fails or immediate administration is needed; bars PBMs/health carriers from forcing patients/providers to use selected specialty or mail‑order pharmacies, from penalizing providers who decline PBM‑sourced drugs, and from exclusive financial incentives favoring PBM‑affiliated pharmacies. Prohibits spread pricing in new PBM–health plan contracts entered on/after Jan 1, 2026 (except extensions), and bans spread pricing entirely beginning Jan 1, 2029. References federal Drug Supply Chain Security Act; no state agencies are specifically created or funding/appropriations altered.
