Summary

LB222 amends Nebraska Rules of the Road enforcement authorities and officer duties. It explicitly ties powers to enforce motor-vehicle laws to the Superintendent of Law Enforcement and Public Safety and officers of the Nebraska State Patrol (with carrier enforcement officers excepted), and harmonizes §60-683 and §81-2005. New Sections 3–4 restrict other peace officers’ authority to stop or detain vehicle operators (prohibiting stops for nonmoving violations, most DWLS/DWLR stops, and many misdemeanor warrants) and limit equipment-failure stops to situations posing an “immediate, serious threat.” Officers must log/notify dispatch of the stop reason, inform operators, and may not request consent searches in those stops. Vehicle searches generally require a warrant or written consent on a specified bilingual form (with interpreter access); evidence obtained in violation is inadmissible. The bill repeals original sections and takes effect immediately. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds officer conduct and search rules governing "peace officers" and requires pre-stop notification to police dispatch. Key agency-related points:

- Peace officers may not stop or detain drivers for listed nonmoving violations, certain suspended-license cases, or most misdemeanor warrants, except specified offenses.

- Equipment-failure stops allowed only if defect relates to the vehicle and poses an objectively reasonable, immediate, serious safety threat; officer must digitally log or notify dispatch of the primary reason and threat before initiating the stop.

- On contact, officers must state the reason, may not request a consent search under section 4, and may only question about other crimes if plain-view evidence creates reasonable suspicion.

- Vehicle searches require a warrant or written consent from the "authorized person" (operator) using a mandated consent form (English and Spanish, oral explanation, right to counsel, ability to refuse or revoke, interpreter services as needed).

- Evidence obtained in violation is inadmissible.

No agencies are newly created and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
