Summary

This bill amends duties and enforcement authority for peace officers, the Superintendent of Law Enforcement and Public Safety, and officers of the Nebraska State Patrol (including carrier enforcement officers/ division references) and harmonizes Nebraska Rules of the Road provisions. Key agency-related changes:

- Peace officers’ authority is limited: they may not stop/detain vehicle operators for specified nonmoving violations, certain suspended-license cases, and many misdemeanor warrants (lists exceptions).

- Stops for equipment failures require that the officer document/notify dispatch the primary reason, believe the defect poses an immediate, serious safety threat, and inform the operator of that reason.

- Vehicle searches require a warrant or written consent from the operator, using a prescribed bilingual form (English/Spanish) with notice of rights and right to counsel; evidence from unlawful stops/searches is inadmissible.

- References to the Director of Motor Vehicles and carrier enforcement powers remain for inspection and enforcement functions.

No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds limits on police stops, detentions, searches, and consent procedures. Key agency/actor mentions and obligations:

- Peace officers: prohibited from stopping/detaining drivers for many nonmoving violations, suspended-license offenses, or most misdemeanor warrants; may stop for equipment failures only if the defect relates to that vehicle and poses an objectively reasonable, immediate, serious safety threat. Officers must digitally log or notify dispatch the primary reason before initiating such stops, inform drivers of the reason on first contact, refrain from requesting consent searches, and limit questioning to the equipment issue unless plain-view evidence creates reasonable suspicion.

- Dispatch: must be notified or officers must log the stop reason before initiating the stop.

- Courts/criminal proceedings: evidence seized in violation of these rules is inadmissible.

- Search/consent process: defines "authorized person" as vehicle operator; requires written consent forms (English and Spanish), oral explanations, right to consult an attorney, right to refuse/revoke consent, and interpreter use when needed.

No new agencies are created and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
