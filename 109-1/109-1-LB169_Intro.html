Summary

The bill directs the department (Department of Revenue) to expand its annual Tax Expenditure Report to quantify revenue loss from tax exemptions and from failure to impose sales and use tax on services purchased for nonbusiness use. It retains category listings that reference purchases by governmental or quasi‑governmental entities, including political subdivisions, the Nebraska State Fair Board, the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority, licensees of the State Racing and Gaming Commission, and the United States Government. Amendments to gross‑receipts definitions (§77‑2701.16) affect taxable service receipts administered by the department. The Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act (§77‑3005) is clarified to confirm state occupation tax is in addition to sales/use tax and precludes local taxes on such devices except local option sales/use taxes. Operative date: October 1, 2025. No direct appropriations or new funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text enumerates a range of service categories whose gross income is treated under the bill (e.g., chartered flights; clothing cleaning excluding amounts already exempt under §77-2704.14; dating; hair care excluding hair cutting; interior design; lobbying; chartered local passenger transportation including limousines; marketing/telemarketing; massage; nail care; certain personal instruction; ground sightseeing; skin care; pool cleaning/maintenance; tattoo/body modification; telefloral delivery by in‑state florists; travel agency; weight‑loss services). It also explicitly addresses sales and use tax on gross receipts from mechanical amusement devices (excluding local option sales/use taxes). No government agencies, departments, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
