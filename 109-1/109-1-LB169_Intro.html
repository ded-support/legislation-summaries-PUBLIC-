Summary

LB169 amends Nebraska tax law and reporting. The Department of Revenue must expand the annual Tax Expenditure Report to include estimated revenue loss from failure to impose sales and use tax on services purchased for nonbusiness use and to itemize specified service categories. The bill broadens the definition of "gross receipts" to expressly subject many services to sales/use tax (examples include vehicle and property maintenance and repair, personal and professional services, animal specialty and veterinary services except livestock, lodging-related receipts, and various personal care services). The Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act is clarified to treat the occupation tax as additional to state taxes (including sales/use tax) and to prohibit local taxing of such devices except local option sales/use taxes. Operative October 1, 2025. No new appropriations or funding allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a long list of service categories (chartered flights; cleaning of clothing; dating services; hair care and removal; interior design; lobbying; local passenger chartered road transport (e.g., limousines); marketing/telemarketing; massage; nail care; personal instruction (dance, golf, tennis); ground sightseeing; skin care; swimming pool cleaning/maintenance; tattoo/body modification; telefloral delivery; travel agency; weight‑loss services) to the bill’s covered gross‑income provisions. It also expressly includes sales and use tax on gross receipts from operation of mechanical amusement devices (excluding local option sales/use taxes). No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
