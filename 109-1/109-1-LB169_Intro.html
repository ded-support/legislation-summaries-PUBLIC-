Summary

LB169 directs the Nebraska Department of Revenue to expand its Tax Expenditure Report to include estimated revenue losses from failure to impose sales and use tax on services purchased for nonbusiness use and to list many specific service categories. The bill broadens the definition of "gross receipts" to bring numerous services (e.g., vehicle and property maintenance, animal specialty and veterinary services except livestock, personal care, landscaping, many professional and entertainment services, telefloral delivery, travel agency services, etc.) within the sales/use tax base and repeals several statutory sales-tax exemptions. It clarifies that the Mechanical Amusement Device occupation tax is in addition to state taxes, including sales and use tax, and prohibits political subdivisions from levying other taxes on such devices except local option sales and use taxes. Operative date: October 1, 2025. No direct appropriations or new budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary: The added text expands a list of gross‑income exemptions to cover numerous services (chartered flights; clothing cleaning; dating; hair care except cutting; interior design; lobbying; chartered local passenger transport; marketing/telemarketing; massage; nail care; personal instruction; sightseeing by ground vehicle; skin care; swimming pool cleaning/maintenance; tattoo/body modification; telefloral delivery; travel agency services; and weight‑loss services). It also addresses sales and use tax for gross receipts from operation of mechanical amusement devices, excluding local option sales and use taxes. No specific state or federal agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
