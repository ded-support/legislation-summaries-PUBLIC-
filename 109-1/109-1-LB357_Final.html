Summary

LB357 restructures administration of the State Racing and Gaming Commission and the Nebraska Racetrack Gaming Act. It expands licensing (adds a separate three‑year racetrack enclosure license; 20‑year operator licenses), requires fingerprint/background checks processed through the Nebraska State Patrol and FBI (applicant pays costs), and mandates market and socioeconomic studies filed with the Governor and the Legislature’s General Affairs Committee. Financial provisions authorize application/license and oversight fees, a one‑time $5 million authorized gaming operator license fee per racetrack (may be paid $1M/year over five years), a $50,000 annual review fee for 20‑year operator licenses, and a 20% gross gaming revenue tax. The bill creates/renames the Racetrack Gaming Fund to receive fees and certain horseracing receipts, transfers the Racing Cash Fund into it, and directs investment earnings to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024; the Commission remits fines/taxes to the State Treasurer for statutory distribution. It also revises equine‑activity liability exceptions and repeals multiple specified statutes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for Nebraska State Fair Board, county fair boards, county agricultural societies, and qualifying nonprofit livestock/horse exposition organizations to apply to the Racing and Gaming Commission for a racetrack enclosure license (3‑year term; $10,000 application and $10,000 renewal fee). Existing horseracing licensees are deemed licensed until the commission rules. Applicants must submit fingerprints to the Nebraska State Patrol, which will forward them to the FBI and report results to the commission. Fiscal changes: the State Treasurer must transfer any money in the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racing Cash Fund on the act’s effective date to a new Racetrack Gaming Fund. Provisions reference license-fee structures (including an initial $5 million fee payable in $1 million annual installments) and allocation of license fees and gross tax receipts to the commission.
