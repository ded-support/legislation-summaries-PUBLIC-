Summary

LB243 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §23-367 to specify that special sidewalk assessments levied by county government may be collected in the same manner as the collection or foreclosure of county taxes against real estate. The bill repeals the original version of §23-367. No state agencies or departments beyond county governments are referenced, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
