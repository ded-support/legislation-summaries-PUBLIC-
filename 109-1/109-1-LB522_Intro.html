Summary

LB522 establishes annual cost-of-living adjustments beginning January 1, 2026, for weekly workers' compensation income benefits (including survivor benefits) lasting longer than 12 months. The COLA percentage equals the greater of 1% or the twelve‑month percent change in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers, "as prepared by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics." The Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court will determine the applicable COLA each January 1 and apply automatic twelve‑month benefit adjustments. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Labor focus):

- Starting Jan 1, 2026, Nebraska workers' compensation weekly income benefits that continue beyond 12 months will receive automatic annual cost‑of‑living adjustments (COLAs).

- COLA equals the greater of 1% or the 12‑month percent change in the CPI for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers as published by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics (federal DOL/BLS data is the COLA source).

- The Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court will announce the applicable COLA each January; adjustments are applied every 12 months, rounded up to the next whole dollar, and capped by the statutory maximum weekly benefit (with one provision allowing COLA increases to exceed a different statutory cap in section 48‑122.03).

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Nebraska Department of Labor are specified.
