Summary

LB522 adds annual cost-of-living adjustments (COLA) to Nebraska workers’ compensation weekly income benefits lasting longer than 12 months (amending sections 48-121 and 48-122.01), effective beginning 2026. The COLA percentage is defined as the greater of 1% or the twelve‑month percent change in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers as prepared by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. The Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court determines the applicable COLA each January 1 and automatically applies the adjustment every twelve months. The bill contains no appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds automatic cost-of-living adjustments (COLA) to weekly workers’ compensation income benefits that continue beyond 12 months, effective Jan 1, 2026. COLA each year = greater of 1% or the 12‑month change in the CPI‑W (BLS, U.S. Dept. of Labor); the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court will determine the annual COLA. Benefits will be adjusted every 12 months (rounded up to next whole dollar) but not to exceed the applicable statutory maximums in sections 48‑121.01 and 48‑122. Section 48‑122.03’s maximum limitation will not bar COLA increases under section 48‑122. No changes to Department of Labor appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are specified.
