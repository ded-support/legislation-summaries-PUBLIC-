Summary

LB636 authorizes counties to seek state reimbursement for medical services provided to county jail inmates and assigns administration to the Jail Standards Board. The board may define eligible costs, audit county records (at least biennially or as needed), set rules, require counties to retain supporting records two years, deny claims caused by jail or staff misconduct, and request warrants on the State Treasurer to pay approved amounts to county treasurers (credited to county general funds). Claims must be filed within 45 days after each fiscal quarter. The Legislature caps annual appropriations for the Jail Standards Board’s eligible criminal court cost reimbursements at $21 million beginning FY2025-26. LB636 also revises sheriff and distress-warrant fee schedules (dollar amounts, mileage calculation, $10 minimum per service, reporting to county boards, and commissions on tax collections), redirects specified fees to county general funds, and takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures and limits for county jail medical-cost reimbursement administered by the Jail Standards Board. Key agency actions: county boards must certify reimbursement claims and submit them to the Jail Standards Board within 45 days after each quarter end or lose eligibility; the Board may audit county records (at least once every two years), request additional information, and has sole authority to determine reimbursable medical costs and adopt rules. If approved, the Board requests a warrant from the State Treasurer to pay the county treasurer; funds are credited to the county general fund. Reimbursement is barred for costs caused by negligence, recklessness, or intentional misconduct. For FY2025‑26 and thereafter, legislative appropriations to the Jail Standards Board for eligible criminal court cost reimbursement are capped at $21 million annually. The text also authorizes county sheriffs to charge fees for additional officers serving warrants (minimum fee language appears).
