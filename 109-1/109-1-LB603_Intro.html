Summary

LB603 assigns several explicit responsibilities to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DHHS will determine the poverty level used (including any federal poverty index or similar guideline) for client contribution determinations and must adopt and promulgate the client family income schedule as departmental rules and regulations. DHHS may reimburse care management units; reimbursement is to be based on actual casework time units and may include personnel, administration and planning, client eligibility review, contractual services, support services, and other direct and indirect costs. DHHS must adopt standardized reimbursement rates and adjust them at least every three years. Appropriations for DHHS reimbursement of services under sections 81-2229 to 81-2235 and for DHHS program administration must be made separately from funds under the Nebraska Community Aging Services Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: Adds authority for care management clients to make voluntary contributions toward the cost of care management services (per section 81-2230). DHHS must use a client family income schedule based on federal poverty guidelines to determine each client's contribution. No specific appropriations, funding allocations, or dollar amounts are included; the provision establishes a cost-sharing mechanism but does not itself authorize or change state budget appropriations.
