Summary

LB285 prohibits the sale, marketing, or state tax certification of defined "flavored vapor products" (electronic nicotine delivery systems with flavors other than tobacco or menthol). It directs the Tax Commissioner not to issue certifications under the Tobacco Products Tax Act (§77-4011.01) for flavored vapor products. The cigarette tax division of the Tax Commissioner may revoke or suspend licenses or certifications for violations (§59-1523), and cigarettes acquired or imported in violation are contraband subject to seizure and destruction. Retail licensees under §§28-1420–28-1429 are barred from selling flavored vapor products; violations carry a Class III misdemeanor, license revocation/forfeiture, and specified reissuance suspension periods (five years for certain offenses, one year for others). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "flavored vapor product" as any electronic nicotine delivery system that (a) has a taste or smell other than tobacco or menthol distinguishable to consumers (examples listed), (b) is marketed as having such a taste/smell, or (c) is represented by the manufacturer or seller as having such a taste/smell. It prohibits holders of licenses issued under sections 28-1420 to 28-1429 from marketing, advertising, selling, or causing sale of flavored vapor products. It also bars the Tax Commissioner from issuing certifications under section 77-4011.01 for flavored electronic nicotine delivery systems and forbids manufacturers from causing flavored ENDS to be sold at retail in the state. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
