Summary

This bill bans marketing, sale, and Tobacco Products Tax Act certification of "flavored vapor products" (defined) that are electronic nicotine delivery systems. It directs the Tax Commissioner (and the cigarette tax division within the Tax Commissioner’s office) to refuse certification under section 77-4011.01 for such products and authorizes the cigarette tax division to revoke or suspend licenses for violations (including seizure and destruction of contraband cigarettes). Licensing penalties and revocation timelines are retained/clarified (e.g., specified multi‑year license‑bar periods for certain violations). Enforcement actions may include court‑ordered license revocation. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "flavored vapor product" (ENDS with a taste/smell other than tobacco/menthol, marketed as such, or claimed by a manufacturer/seller). New prohibitions: holders of licenses under sections 28-1420 to 28-1429 are barred from marketing, advertising, or selling flavored vapor products; the Tax Commissioner is prohibited from issuing certifications under section 77-4011.01 for any ENDS that is a flavored vapor product; and manufacturers may not cause flavored ENDS to be sold at retail in the state. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
