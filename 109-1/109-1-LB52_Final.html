Summary

The bill amends victim-notification duties for the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). County attorneys must forward victim names/addresses to DCS and DHHS. DCS and DHHS must include victim names in offender files (not public) and accept written victim requests for notification while offenders are under their jurisdiction (with a three‑year post‑jurisdiction request window when DHHS is involved). DCS must notify victims of furloughs/releases (≥24 hours), transfers to community status, community‑based program placements/terminations, escapes/returns, discharges, earliest release/parole calculations, sentence reductions, and victims’ right to submit statements. When commitment petitions are filed, DCS forwards victim names to DHHS; DHHS must notify victims of petitions, escapes/returns, discharge/changes in disposition, furloughs/releases (≥24 hours), and program placements/terminations. DCS and DHHS must adopt implementing rules. Victim contact information held by DCS and DHHS is exempt from public disclosure. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language expands the statutory list of enumerated offenses to include multiple violent and sexual crimes—e.g., manslaughter; motor vehicle homicide; first- and second-degree assault; assault by strangulation/suffocation; terroristic threats; stalking; false imprisonment in the first degree; sexual offenses including sexual abuse by a school employee, sexual assault of a child, sexual abuse of a protected individual; child enticement via electronic communication device; domestic assault (1st/2nd); and sex/labor trafficking (including minors).

Impacts: Department of Correctional Services may see increased caseloads, incarceration and supervision needs tied to these offenses. Department of Health and Human Services may face greater demand for victim services, child protection, mental‑health and treatment programs. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the added text.
