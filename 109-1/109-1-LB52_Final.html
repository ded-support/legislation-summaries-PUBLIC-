Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1850 to (1) require county attorneys to forward victims' names/addresses to the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) (among others) after a felony conviction; (2) require DCS and DHHS to include victims' names in offender files (not public record) and to respond to written victim notification requests; (3) require DCS (or county corrections agencies) to notify victims of furloughs/releases (≥24 hours), transfers to community status, community-based program start/termination, escapes/returns, discharges (when practicable ≥30 days prior), sentence-credit/release-date calculations, sentence reductions, and victims' rights to submit statements; (4) require county attorneys to notify DCS of Mental Health or Sex Offender Commitment petitions and DCS to forward victim information to DHHS; (5) require DHHS to notify victims of commitment-related escapes, discharges/disp osition changes, furloughs/releases, and program participation; (6) direct DCS and DHHS to adopt implementing rules; and (7) exempt victims' contact information held by DCS/DHHS from public disclosure. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added (underlined) language expands the enumerated list of covered offenses to include manslaughter; motor vehicle homicide; first- and second-degree assault (including by strangulation/suffocation); terroristic threats; stalking; first-degree false imprisonment; sexual abuse by a school employee; first-degree sexual assault of a child; child enticement by electronic communication; sexual abuse of a protected individual; domestic assault (first/second degree); and sex and labor trafficking (including minors).

There are no appropriation, funding, or budget-allocation provisions in the added text. Operationally, the Department of Correctional Services could see increased caseloads/incarceration demands and DHHS may face greater need for victim services, treatment, and case management, but no direct fiscal or budget changes are included.
