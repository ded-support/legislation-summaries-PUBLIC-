Summary

The bill amends state motor‑vehicle lettering law to add/clarify exemptions for vehicles of the Department of Correctional Services, Department of Health and Human Services, and the Military Department. Exemptions cover DCS and DHHS vehicles used to apprehend/return escaped offenders or parole violators, to transport DCS offenders and personnel, and to transport DHHS patients and personnel engaged in off‑campus program activities. Military Department vehicles are similarly exempt. Separately, DHHS vehicles (and vocational rehabilitation counselors) are exempt for communicable‑disease control/prevention, drug‑control law enforcement, and related investigations. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for these agencies are made.
