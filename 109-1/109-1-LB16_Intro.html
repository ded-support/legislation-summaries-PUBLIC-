Summary

LB16 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81‑1021 to clarify exceptions to the State motor‑vehicle lettering requirement. It exempts vehicles of the Department of Correctional Services and the Department of Health and Human Services when used to apprehend or return escaped offenders or parole violators, and when transporting DOC offenders and personnel or DHHS patients and personnel engaged in off‑campus program activities. The bill also exempts vehicles of the Military Department. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes for these agencies are included.

No added provisions directly reference the Department of Correctional Services, Department of Health and Human Services, or the Military Department, nor do they appropriate funds to those agencies.
