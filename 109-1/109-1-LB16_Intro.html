Summary

LB16 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1021 to exempt specified state vehicles from conspicuous "State of Nebraska" lettering when used or controlled by the Department of Correctional Services, the Department of Health and Human Services, or the Military Department. Exemptions apply for DOCS and DHHS vehicles used to apprehend/return escaped offenders or parole violators and to transport DOCS offenders/personnel and DHHS patients/personnel on off‑campus program activities; for DHHS vehicles engaged in communicable disease control, public‑health‑endangering prevention activities, drug‑control enforcement, and related investigations; and for Military Department vehicles. The bill contains no appropriations, funding allocations, or other budgetary changes for these agencies.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text creates detailed regulation of consumable hemp: definitions, retail licensing/renewal rules, retailer/manufacturer testing and labeling requirements, training/certification programs, inspection and hearing procedures, civil/ criminal penalties, a 3% excise tax on first distributor-to-retailer sales, and a Nebraska Consumable Hemp Control Commission Cash Fund (fees deposited there; commission remits excise tax receipts to the General Fund; license fees credited to local school funds). It also specifies licensing fees, recordkeeping, and enforcement authorities.

No line in the added text directs funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to the Department of Correctional Services, Department of Health and Human Services, or the Military Department. (There are new criminal penalties that could affect corrections workload or public‑health enforcement responsibilities, but no accompanying appropriations or transfers for those agencies.)
