Summary

The bill amends section 68-911 to place specific responsibilities on the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), including submitting state plan amendments or waivers for: community-based behavioral health coverage (per earlier deadline), school-district–provided direct reimbursable services, and extension of postpartum coverage (seek federal approval by Oct. 1, 2023). DHHS must provide continuous glucose monitor coverage (effective Jan. 1, 2023; expanded Aug. 1, 2024) with intent that no more than $600,000 be appropriated annually from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for implementation. By Oct. 1, 2025 DHHS must submit a waiver/state plan amendment to designate two medical respite facilities, select one in a metropolitan-class city and one in a primary-class city, and—upon approval—report annually to the Legislature. Medical respite services shall be funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.
