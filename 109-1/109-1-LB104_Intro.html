Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and assigns DHHS primary responsibilities under the Family Home Visitation Act. DHHS shall only fund home visitation programs that: include periodic in‑home visits by nurses, social workers, early childhood/health professionals or supervised lay workers; meet listed program goals; and are evidence‑based according to specified standards. Funds may be used for workforce purposes and to assist programs/individuals in obtaining and maintaining provider certification. DHHS may adopt rules to implement the Act and must submit electronic reports (by Feb 15, 2026, 2027, and 2028) to the Legislature with program, outcome, demographic, and funding‑expenditure information. No specific dollar appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines the Department as the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services and makes DHHS responsible for funding only evidence-based family home visitation programs (voluntary, primarily in-home services for pregnant people and families with children ≤5). Funded programs must provide periodic visits by nurses, social workers, early‑childhood/health professionals or trained/supervised lay workers and achieve specified goals (maternal/child health, parenting, development, reduced maltreatment, economic self‑sufficiency, school readiness). DHHS may limit funding to programs meeting detailed evidence‑based standards, may use program funds for workforce development and certification, and may adopt rules. Reporting: DHHS must post electronic reports (by Feb 15, 2026 and Feb 15, 2028) listing program types, outcomes, families served, demographics, total funds expended, models, training, eligibility, waitlists, and referrals. No specific appropriations or dollar allocations are included.
