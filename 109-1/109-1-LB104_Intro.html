Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DHHS is directed to fund only home visitation programs that provide periodic in‑home visits and to fund only evidence‑based programs meeting specified model, training, quality, fidelity, and community‑linkage criteria. State home visitation funds may be used for workforce purposes and to assist programs/individuals in obtaining and maintaining provider certification. DHHS may adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act. No specific appropriations are listed; however the bill requires DHHS to report electronically (no later than Feb 15, 2026, and through Feb 15, 2028) to the Legislature and post on its website program details including types/locations, goals/outcomes, families served, demographics, and the total amount of funds expended. DHHS shall include only federally or state‑funded program outcomes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Designates the Department of Health and Human Services as the administering agency for the new Family Home Visitation Act. Requires the department to fund only periodic, evidence‑based home‑visitation programs (nurse/social‑worker/early‑childhood or trained lay visitors) that accomplish specified maternal/child health, parenting, developmental, safety, and economic outcomes. Defines evidence‑based criteria (model fidelity, external certification, staff training/professional development, quality standards, community linkages). Permits use of home‑visitation funds for workforce purposes and certification costs. Grants the department rulemaking authority. Mandates electronic reports to the Legislature (by Feb 15, 2026 and Feb 15, 2028) with program, outcome, demographic, expenditure, and waitlist data, limited to federally or state‑funded programs. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are included.
