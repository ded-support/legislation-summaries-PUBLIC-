Summary

LB104 designates "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and requires the department to fund only home visitation programs that (1) include periodic in‑home visits for families with children age five or younger or pregnant persons, (2) provide visits by nurses, social workers, early‑childhood/health professionals or supervised lay workers, and (3) are evidence‑based as defined in the bill. Funded program dollars may be used for workforce purposes and to assist programs/individuals in obtaining and maintaining provider certification. The department may adopt rules to implement the Act and must submit electronic reports to the Legislature (by Feb 15, 2026, and through Feb 15, 2028) with program, outcomes, demographic, and expenditure data. The bill contains no specific appropriations or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Family Home Visitation Act," defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and requires DHHS to fund only evidence‑based, periodic home‑visitation programs for pregnant people and families with children ≤5. Funded programs must use nurses, social workers, other professionals or trained/supervised lay workers and target specified outcomes (maternal/infant health, parenting, child development, family well‑being, economic self‑sufficiency, reduced maltreatment, school readiness). Evidence‑based criteria and fidelity requirements are specified. State funds may be used for workforce supports and provider certification/recertification. DHHS may adopt rules and must submit electronic reports (due by Feb 15, 2026 and Feb 15, 2028) including total funds expended. No specific appropriations or new dollar amounts are included.
