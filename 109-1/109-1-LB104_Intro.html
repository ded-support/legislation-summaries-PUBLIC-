Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and requires the department to fund only home visitation programs that (1) provide periodic home visits by nurses, social workers, early childhood/health professionals or supervised lay workers, (2) meet specified goals (e.g., improved maternal/child health, reduced maltreatment), and (3) are evidence-based (model fidelity, certification/association, staff training, quality standards). Funds may be used for workforce purposes and to assist programs/individuals in obtaining/maintaining certification. The department may adopt rules to implement the Act. It must submit electronic reports to the Legislature (by Feb 15, 2026, 2027, and 2028) with program, outcome, demographic, expenditure, and related information; only federally or state-funded program outcomes are to be included. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added Family Home Visitation Act designates the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) as responsible for funding and overseeing voluntary home visitation programs for pregnant people and families with children up to age five. DHHS may fund only periodic, evidence‑based programs that use nurses, social workers, early‑childhood/health professionals or trained supervised lay workers and meet specified outcome goals (maternal/child health, parenting, development, safety, economic self‑sufficiency, school readiness). Evidence‑based criteria and allowable uses of state funds (including workforce development and certification assistance) are defined. DHHS may adopt rules and must publish electronic reports to the Legislature by Feb 15, 2026 and again through Feb 15, 2028, including program details and total funds expended. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.
