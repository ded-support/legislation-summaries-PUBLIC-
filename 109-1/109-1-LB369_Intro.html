Summary

LB369 amends section 43-2101 to address decisionmaking for youth in agency custody. It provides that 18-year-olds may make health-care decisions without parental consent except when they are in the custody of the Department of Health and Human Services pursuant to a juvenile court order. It further allows persons under 19 who are committed to the Department of Correctional Services for secure care to consent to and make decisions about their medical care, mental health services, and related services during commitment without parental consent, subject to section 71-6902. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two consent rules: (c) Individuals 18+ may consent to their own health care without parental/guardian permission, unless they are in the custody of the Department of Health and Human Services under a juvenile court order. (d) Persons under 19 who are committed to the Department of Correctional Services for secure care may consent to and make decisions about their medical, mental‑health, and related services for the duration of the commitment, except as otherwise required by section 71‑6902. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
