Summary

LB484 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-1359 (2024 supplement) to revise definitions for “agricultural land and horticultural land,” excluding land associated with a building or enclosed structure and expressly including wasteland in common ownership/management. It expands “agricultural or horticultural purposes” to include land under conservation easements (per the Conservation and Preservation Easements Act) and land enrolled in unspecified federal or state programs that pay for removal from production. The bill also clarifies treatment of platted/subdivided parcels and defines “farm home site” and “farm site.” No state agencies are specifically named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
