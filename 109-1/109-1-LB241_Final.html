Summary

LB241 (2025) creates a data-privacy statutory framework that defines terms and grants certain private entities an exemption from liability for data-related claims, except when harm results from the private entity’s willful, wanton, or gross negligence. The bill was introduced in the One Hundred Ninth Nebraska Legislature and referred to the Banking, Commerce and Insurance Committee.
