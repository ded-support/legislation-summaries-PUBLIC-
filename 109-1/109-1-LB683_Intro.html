Summary

LB683 requires county assessors to transmit the statutorily mandated real-property valuation notices to the Department of Administrative Services for state printing. The bill does not specify any appropriation, funding allocation, or other budgetary changes for the Department of Administrative Services. No additional departmental duties or funding mechanisms for DAS are detailed elsewhere in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language requires county assessors to transmit property valuation notice materials to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) for state printing. The notices must include prior- and current‑year valuations, prior levies by political subdivisions, current year levy hearing times/places, and prominent protest instructions (deadline June 30). Political subdivisions must report their budget hearing time/place to county assessors by May 15. No specific changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DAS or printing are included in the text.
