Summary

LB683 requires county assessors to transmit the statutorily required real‑property valuation notices to the Department of Administrative Services for state printing. The bill also alters treatment of excess tax collections: political subdivisions must return excess countywide levy proceeds net of collection fee; county treasurers must certify return amounts by July 31 of the following fiscal year; and for fiscal years beginning prior to July 1, 2025, such excess collections are designated as restricted funds in the receiving county's budget under section 13‑518.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text requires each county assessor to transmit property valuation/notice mailings to the Department of Administrative Services for state printing. Notices must include prior and current year valuations and levies, budget hearing times/places (reported by political subdivisions), and a prominent "KNOW YOUR RIGHTS" protest/blurb. Political subdivisions must report budget hearing time/place to county assessors by May 15. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Administrative Services are specified in the text. (Potential printing costs or administrative impacts are not addressed in the provision.)
