Summary

LB450 amends the Property Assessed Clean Energy Act to (1) add grid resiliency and renewable energy to legislative findings and (2) revise definitions and municipal procedures. Key government-related changes: municipalities (defined as counties, cities, or villages) may create clean energy assessment districts and act as district governing bodies; counties may not create districts overlapping a city or village’s corporate or extraterritorial zoning jurisdiction. Municipalities must hold a public hearing (published in a legal newspaper at least ten days prior) before creating a district. Ordinances or, for counties, resolutions must include a public-purpose finding, a model assessment-contract form (municipality, property owner, and optional third‑party lender), designation of an official authorized to sign contracts, application/eligibility rules, assessment collection procedures, and fixed interest/repayment requirements for residential projects. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "grid resiliency improvement" as any acquisition, installation, or modification (on public or private property) intended to anticipate, prepare for, withstand, respond to, and rapidly recover from major power disruptions. Examples listed: backup power generators; backup generators powered by renewable energy; solar panels with battery storage; and smart grid technology. No government agencies or departments are referenced and no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
