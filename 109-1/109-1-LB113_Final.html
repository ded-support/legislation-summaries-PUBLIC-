Summary

LB113 amends multiple liquor statutes and shifts regulatory duties between the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (the commission) and local governing bodies (city/village/county clerks). Key agency actions: the commission may authorize rickhouses and offsite storage, adopt rules on distribution rights, receive and process entertainment-district and license endorsement applications, and must notify local clerks of entertainment-district applications. Local governing bodies may designate/revoke entertainment districts, file such actions with the commission, impose occupation taxes, cancel licenses for cause, set criteria for temporary expansions, and collect an additional $300 entertainment-district license fee payable to the local clerk. The bill contains no statewide appropriations or other budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision related to an entertainment district license holder allowing sale/display and imposes required safety measures: signage, lighting, reduced motor-vehicle speeds when vehicles are near pedestrians. It also bars carrying open alcoholic containers and consuming alcohol on open streets/highways, except when briefly crossing at a designated crosswalk. No government agencies or departments are referenced and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
