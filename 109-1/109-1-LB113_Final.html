Summary

This bill amends the Nebraska Liquor Control Act and primarily affects the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission and local governing bodies (city, village, county) and their clerks. Key agency actions/authorities:

- Commission: retains/expands rulemaking authority on distribution rights and entertainment-district matters; must be notified of local entertainment-district designations/revocations and receives copies of license applications; must authorize offsite storage facilities and rickhouses (per 26 U.S.C. 5178) and be notified of their locations.

- Local governing bodies: may designate/revoke entertainment districts, process and approve entertainment-district licenses per §53-132, impose occupation taxes, and receive a new $300 entertainment-district license fee payable to the local clerk.

- Clerks: receive the $300 fee and confirmation of temporary expansions upon receipt of any local fee or tax.

Budgetary impact: no statewide appropriations; establishes a $300 local license fee and authorizes local occupation taxes and local fee collection. The bill also increases allowable separate physical locations for certain licenses to eight.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds provisions allowing sale or display by at least one holder of an "entertainment district" license, conditioned on safety and conduct rules: required pedestrian safety measures (signage, lighting, reduced motor‑vehicle speeds where vehicles are near pedestrians) and a ban on open alcoholic containers and drinking on public streets/highways except when briefly crossing at a designated crosswalk.
