Summary

LB113 amends Nebraska Liquor Control Act provisions governing manufacturers, craft breweries, microdistilleries, and entertainment-district licenses. Key agency/administrative changes:

- Nebraska Liquor Control Commission ("commission"): may authorize rickhouses meeting 26 U.S.C. 5178 and must be notified of rickhouse/storage locations; may adopt rules on craft brewery and microdistillery distribution rights; receives and processes entertainment-district license applications and is notified of local designations/revocations.

- Local governing bodies (city/village/county) and clerks: must file entertainment-district designations/revocations, may revoke designations, may impose an occupation tax, regulate districts by ordinance, and receive a $300 additional entertainment-district license fee payable to the local clerk.

- License scope/limits: craft brewery and microdistillery licenses may cover up to eight separate physical locations; manufacturers converting from craft-brewery status may continue up to eight retail locations.

- Entertainment-district rules: clarifies commons-area safety requirements, container labeling, limited cross-license alcohol transfer, and prohibition on open containers on streets.

No appropriations or statewide funding changes are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds permission for at least one holder of an “entertainment district” license to sell/display specified items, subject to conditions. Requires reasonable pedestrian safety measures (signage, lighting, and reduced motor‑vehicle speeds where vehicles are near pedestrians). Prohibits carrying open alcoholic containers and consuming alcohol on any open street or highway, except when briefly crossing at a designated crosswalk. No government agencies, departments, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced.
