Summary

LB506 establishes the "Landlord and Tenant Radon Awareness Act." It requires lessors to provide prospective tenants and tenants, on application and upon request, any available radon test results and a prescribed radon-hazard disclosure form. The bill defines radon measurement specialists and radon mitigation specialists as individuals licensed under the Radiation Control Act. "Lessor" may include a government agency or subdivision. Lessors may not withhold security deposits to pay for radon testing or mitigation, except where a tenant installed a mitigation system without consent that was improperly installed (i.e., not by a licensed mitigation specialist). The act applies to leases entered into on or after its effective date. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Landlord and Tenant Radon Awareness Act," requiring lessors (which may include a government agency or subdivision) to disclose radon information to prospective and current tenants, using a prescribed disclosure form. It references licensed personnel under the Radiation Control Act — "radon measurement specialist" and "radon mitigation specialist" — implying oversight and licensure under that statutory framework.
