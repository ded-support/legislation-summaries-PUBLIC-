Summary

LB686 prohibits Nebraska government entities and officials from knowingly keeping lists, records, or registries of privately owned firearms or firearm owners, except records maintained during criminal investigations, prosecutions, or as otherwise required by law. It also bars payment card networks and covered entities from requiring, incentivizing, or assigning merchant category codes that distinguish firearm retailers from other retailers, unless such coding is required by law. Enforcement is vested exclusively in the Nebraska Attorney General, who may investigate, issue notices to cease, seek injunctions, and request civil fines up to $1,000 per violation (with fines stayed pending appeal). No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions and an enforcement scheme enforced solely by the Attorney General. Key points:

- Government entities may not maintain lists/registries of privately owned firearms or owners except records kept in the regular course of a criminal investigation or as otherwise required by law.

- Payment card networks may not require or incentivize use of merchant category codes (MCCs) that distinguish firearm retailers; covered entities may not assign such distinguishing MCCs.

- The Attorney General must investigate alleged violations (including by government entities), issue written notice, and if not remedied within 30 business days, sue for injunction. Courts must enjoin ongoing violations; continued noncompliance after 30 business days may produce civil fines up to $1,000 per violation (court to consider violator’s finances and constitutional harms). Fines are stayed on appeal. The AG has exclusive enforcement authority and the section’s remedies are exclusive.

- No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are added.
