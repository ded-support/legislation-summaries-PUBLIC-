Summary

LB686 prohibits government entities and officials from knowingly keeping lists, records, or registries of privately owned firearms or firearm owners, except records kept in the regular course of a criminal investigation, prosecution, or as otherwise required by law. It bars payment card networks from requiring or incentivizing merchant category codes (MCCs) that distinguish firearm retailers, and prohibits covered entities from assigning such distinguishing MCCs.

Enforcement is vested exclusively in the Nebraska Attorney General, who may investigate, issue a written notice to cease, file for injunctions, and, for noncompliance after injunction, seek civil fines up to $1,000 per violation (with the court weighing violator resources and harm to constitutional rights). A lawful requirement to assign an MCC is an available defense. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Agency: Nebraska Attorney General — given exclusive authority to investigate alleged violations of the new prohibitions on keeping firearm ownership registries and on assigning or requiring merchant category codes that distinguish firearm retailers.

- Enforcement process/timeline: AG must issue written notice; alleged violator has 30 business days to cease. If not, AG may seek a court injunction; noncompliance with an injunction after 30 business days exposes the violator to civil fines.

- Penalties: Civil fines up to $1,000 per violation; courts must consider violator’s financial resources and harm to the state constitutional right to keep and bear arms. Fines are stayed pending appeal.

- Other: Defense exists if a merchant category code was required by law.

- Budget/funding: No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
