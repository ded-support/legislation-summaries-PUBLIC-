Summary

LB195 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-470 to grant immunity to emergency responders and to peace officers or law enforcement employees who, in good faith, obtain from their agency and administer FDA‑approved opioid overdose reversal medications (e.g., naloxone, nalmefene). Such individuals are protected from administrative action, criminal prosecution, and personal civil liability for acts or omissions in providing overdose reversal care, except for willful, wanton, or grossly negligent conduct; agency or organizational liability is not affected. Definitions reference state agencies/authorities: emergency responders licensed under the Emergency Medical Services Practice Act or the EMS Personnel Licensure Interstate Compact; health professionals licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act; law enforcement agencies (police departments, town marshals, offices of sheriff, Nebraska State Patrol); and statutory cross‑references to sections 38‑2806, 38‑2817, and 49‑801. No funding or appropriation provisions are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "opioid overdose reversal medication" as any lifesaving drug FDA-approved to reverse opioid overdose (prescription, provided by a health professional, or over-the-counter), explicitly including naloxone and nalmefene.
