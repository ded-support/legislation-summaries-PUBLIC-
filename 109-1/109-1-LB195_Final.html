Summary

LB195 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28‑470 to provide civil, criminal, and administrative immunity for specified individuals who obtain and administer FDA‑approved opioid overdose reversal medications (e.g., naloxone, nalmefene). Covered individuals include emergency responders (EMS personnel licensed under the Emergency Medical Services Practice Act or the EMS Licensure Interstate Compact), health professionals licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act, peace officers (per §49‑801), and law enforcement employees who obtain such medication from their law enforcement agency (police departments, town marshals, offices of sheriff, Nebraska State Patrol). Immunity does not apply for willful, wanton, or grossly negligent conduct and does not alter liability of the employing EMS organization or law enforcement agency. The bill repeals the original §28‑470. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "opioid overdose reversal medication" as any lifesaving drug approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration to reverse opioid overdose, whether prescribed, provided by a health professional, or sold over‑the‑counter; explicitly includes naloxone and nalmefene. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
