Summary

LB321 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §31-735 governing elections for sanitary and improvement district (SID) boards of trustees. It clarifies agency responsibilities: election commissioners or county clerks receive trustee filings, set the property-record date (≤80 days before the election), and conduct sealed mail-ballot elections (mailing ballots ≥20 days before the election and requiring return by 5 p.m. on election day); clerks of the district must mail election notices ≥65 days before the election; register of deeds records property ownership used to determine voter eligibility. The bill retains provisions for designated corporate/entity representatives, resident vs. all-owner voting classes, and allows boards (per §31-740.01) to reduce trustees from five to three under specified conditions. The original section is repealed. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
