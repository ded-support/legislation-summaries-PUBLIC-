Summary

LB321 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §31-735, revising procedures for sanitary and improvement district trustee elections and specifying roles for local election officials. Key agency/official provisions:

- Candidates file with the county election commissioner (or county clerk where no commissioner) no later than 50 days before the election; filings that designate a candidate as a representative of an entity must include documentation.

- The district clerk must mail election notices at least 65 days before the election using ownership records from the register of deeds as of a date set by the election commissioner or county clerk (not more than 80 days prior).

- The election commissioner or county clerk conducts sealed mail-ballot elections (must be notified by July 1) and must mail ballots at least 20 days before the election; return-envelope and write-in documentation rules are specified.

- The election board may validate post-deadline ownership for voting; trusteeship reduction via amended articles remains governed by §31-740.01.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Original section repealed.
