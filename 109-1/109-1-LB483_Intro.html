Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-416 to specify that any person convicted of violating the controlled-substances provisions, if sentenced to the Department of Correctional Services, must attend appropriate drug-abuse treatment and counseling while incarcerated. No provisions in the bill reference funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text reference the Department of Correctional Services or change its authorities, operations, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations. The additions instead: define permissible cannabis products (including a 300 mg delta-9 THC limit and pill/tincture forms), clarify THC and hemp definitions, make the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission members also serve on the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission, and establish exemptions for conduct or transport lawful under the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Patient Protection Act/Regulation Act. No DOC-specific language or fiscal changes are included.
