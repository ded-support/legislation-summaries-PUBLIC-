Summary

LB584 authorizes a court that seeks more detailed information than provided in the presentence report to commit an offender to the Department of Correctional Services. While so committed, the Department is required to conduct a complete study of the offender as provided in section 29‑2204.03. The bill contains no provisions altering funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary for Department of Correctional Services

- The added text revises felony term ranges, notably reducing the Class IA maximum from 50 years to 20 years and specifying maximum/minimum terms for Classes IB, IC, ID, II, and IIA.

- It clarifies that when the offender was under 18 at the time of the offense, the court must set minimum and maximum terms pursuant to section 28-105.02.

- No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation language for the Department of Correctional Services appears in the added text.

- Operational note (not an appropriation): shorter statutory terms could reduce average incarceration lengths and future prison population and cost projections for the department, but no direct budgetary adjustments are included.
