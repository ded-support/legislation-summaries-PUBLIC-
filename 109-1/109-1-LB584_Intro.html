Summary

LB584 authorizes courts, when additional information beyond a presentence report is needed before sentencing, to commit an offender to the Department of Correctional Services. While in department custody, the Department is required to conduct a complete study of the offender as provided in section 29-2204.03. The bill contains no provisions that change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.
