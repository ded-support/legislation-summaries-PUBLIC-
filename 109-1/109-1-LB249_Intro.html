Summary

LB249 tasks the Nebraska Department of Education (referred to as "the department") with new and revised duties under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. Beginning FY2025‑26 the department must calculate and pay “military impact funding” to districts that received federal impact aid under ESEA §7003 for federally connected active‑duty children, using a per‑pupil formula (per‑pupil federal impact aid vs. per‑pupil general fund tax levy). The bill (1) modifies what constitutes local system formula resources for certain years, (2) voids any state aid, budget authority, or reserve‑percentage certifications for FY2025‑26 completed before the act’s effective date, and (3) requires the department to certify district budget authority and applicable reserve percentages on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 thereafter. The Legislative Appropriations Committee must include amounts necessary to fund those certified state aid figures in its budget recommendations. No specific dollar appropriations are set.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a new ongoing state military-impact aid formula, effective for school fiscal year 2025‑26 and thereafter. The state department (state Dept. of Education implied) must determine and pay "military impact funding" to districts that received a federal ESEA §7003 basic support (impact aid) payment in the prior year and educate federally connected children of active‑duty parents. The bill ties payments to a formula: per‑pupil federal impact aid (prior year §7003 payment ÷ enrolled federally connected active‑duty pupils) and per‑pupil general fund tax levy (prior year local property tax receipts ÷ fall membership excluding those federally connected pupils). Military impact funding = (per‑pupil general fund tax levy − per‑pupil federal impact aid) × number of eligible pupils, floored at zero if negative. No specific appropriation or dollar amounts are provided.
