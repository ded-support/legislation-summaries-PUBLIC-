Summary

LB249 (109th Leg., 1st Sess.) directs the Department (Nebraska Department of Education) to implement a new military impact funding stream beginning FY2025‑26 for districts that received federal impact aid under ESEA §7003 and educate federally connected active‑duty children. The Department must calculate payments per a defined formula (difference between per‑pupil local general fund tax levy and per‑pupil federal impact aid, multiplied by federally connected pupils; floor at zero). The bill resets certification timelines: state aid, district budget authority, and allowable reserve percentages are to be certified by the Department on or before June 15, 2025 and March 1 thereafter; any such certifications made earlier for FY2025‑26 are nullified. The Appropriations Committee must include funding necessary to cover certified state aid in its budget recommendations. The act takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective for school fiscal year 2025‑26 onward, the state "department" (state education department context) must determine and pay military impact funding to eligible school districts. Eligibility requires the district received a federal impact aid basic support payment under §7003 of the federal Elementary and Secondary Education Act in the prior year and educates federally connected children whose parents are on active duty. The statute defines calculation: per‑pupil federal impact aid = prior year federal payment ÷ number of active‑duty federally connected students (fall count); per‑pupil general fund tax levy = prior year local property tax levy ÷ fall membership excluding those federally connected students. Military impact funding = (per‑pupil tax levy − per‑pupil federal aid) × number of active‑duty federally connected students, floored at zero. No specific appropriation or new budget totals are provided.
