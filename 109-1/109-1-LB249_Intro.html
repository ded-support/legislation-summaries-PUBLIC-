Summary

LB249 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. It directs the Nebraska Department of Education to establish "military impact funding" beginning FY2025‑26 for districts that received Section 7003 federal impact aid and educate children of active‑duty service members; the payment equals (per‑pupil local general‑fund tax levy minus per‑pupil federal impact aid) multiplied by the number of such students, floored at zero. The bill revises components of local system formula resources (including adding a percentage of foundation aid for FY2023‑24 and FY2024‑25 and other adjustments). It voids any state aid, budget authority, or reserve certifications for FY2025‑26 completed before the act’s effective date. The Department must certify district budget authority and applicable allowable reserve percentages on or before June 15, 2025 and on or before March 1 annually thereafter. The Legislature’s Appropriations Committee must include amounts necessary to fund those certified state aid amounts in its annual recommendations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the (state) department (presumably the Department of Education) determine and pay "military impact funding" to school districts beginning in school fiscal year 2025–26 and thereafter. Eligibility: districts that received a basic support federal impact aid payment under section 7003 of the federal ESEA in the prior year and educate federally connected children of active-duty service members. Formula: per‑pupil federal impact aid = prior year basic support payment ÷ number of active‑duty federally connected students (fall count); per‑pupil general fund tax levy = prior year local property tax levy receipts ÷ fall membership excluding those federally connected students. Military impact funding = (per‑pupil general fund tax levy − per‑pupil federal impact aid) × number of active‑duty students; funding is zero if the difference is negative. No specific new appropriation or funding source is designated.
