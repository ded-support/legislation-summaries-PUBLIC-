Summary

LB249 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act to require the Department (responsible for K‑12 state aid determinations) to establish and certify a new military impact funding stream beginning FY2025‑26 for districts receiving Section 7003 federal impact aid for federally connected active‑duty students. The Department must calculate, certify budget authority, and set applicable allowable reserve percentages on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 annually thereafter; any prior certifications for FY2025‑26 are void. The bill also alters local system formula resource treatment for FY2023‑24 and FY2024‑25 and mandates the Appropriations Committee include the amounts necessary to fund the newly certified state aid in its budget recommendations. The act takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a military impact aid program, effective school fiscal year 2025-26 and thereafter, administered by "the department" (state education agency) to determine payments to school districts. Eligibility: district must have received a federal impact aid basic support payment under section 7003 of the federal Elementary and Secondary Education Act (ESEA) in the prior year and educate federally connected children with parents on active duty. The payment formula: department computes a per‑pupil federal impact aid (prior year §7003 payment ÷ enrolled federally connected active‑duty pupils) and a per‑pupil general fund tax levy (prior year local property tax receipts ÷ fall membership excluding those federally connected pupils). Military impact funding = (per‑pupil tax levy − per‑pupil federal aid) × number of federally connected active‑duty pupils, floored at zero. No separate appropriation or new budget allocation language is specified.
