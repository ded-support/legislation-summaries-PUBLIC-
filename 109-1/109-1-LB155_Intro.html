Summary

LB155 amends Nebraska statutes on justification for use of force (sections 28-1406, 28-1409, 28-1410). It defines “public officer” as any elected or appointed officer or employee of the State of Nebraska or its political subdivisions (excluding duly authorized law enforcement officers) and excludes law enforcement officers from the term “actor.” The bill clarifies that public officers (and persons assisting them or making arrests) are not required to desist from performing duties due to resistance, and expressly provides there is no duty to retreat before using deadly force when lawfully present in a motor vehicle. No agencies, departments, or programs receive funding changes or appropriations in this bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an exclusion for "any duly authorized law enforcement officer of the State of Nebraska or its political subdivisions" from the definition of "actor." Also clarifies that an actor lawfully present in a motor vehicle is not required to retreat from that vehicle unless the actor was the initial aggressor. No other government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
