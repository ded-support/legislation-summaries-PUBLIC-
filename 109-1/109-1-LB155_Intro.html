Summary

LB155 amends Neb. Rev. Statutes §§28‑1406, 28‑1409, and 28‑1410 (use of force/justification). It clarifies definitions and the scope of who is an "actor" (excluding duly authorized law enforcement officers) and defines "public officer" as any elected or appointed officer or employee of the State of Nebraska or its political subdivisions (excluding law enforcement). The bill explicitly states there is no duty to retreat before using deadly force when lawfully present in a motor vehicle and harmonizes related retreat provisions for dwellings, workplaces, and motor vehicles. It also references peace officers and persons assisting public officers in enforcement contexts. Original sections are repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language clarifies that "actor" does not include any duly authorized law enforcement officer of the State of Nebraska or its political subdivisions. It also references "motor vehicle" as defined in section 28-109 and adds definitions and no‑retreat rules concerning unlawful force and presence in motor vehicles. No state agencies, departments beyond Nebraska law enforcement or political subdivisions are newly created or altered, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations in the added text.
