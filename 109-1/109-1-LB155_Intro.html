Summary

LB155 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 28-1406, 28-1409, and 28-1410. It (1) defines "actor" to exclude duly authorized law enforcement officers of the State of Nebraska and its political subdivisions, and defines "public officer" as any elected or appointed officer or employee of the State of Nebraska or its political subdivisions (excluding law enforcement officers); (2) specifies there is no duty to retreat before using deadly force when lawfully present in a motor vehicle (unless the actor was the initial aggressor) and harmonizes analogous retreat rules for defense of others; and (3) clarifies that public officers, and persons lawfully assisting them or making arrests or preventing escapes, are not required to desist from performing duties because of resistance. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that "actor" — a person using force to invoke the privileges/immunities in sections 28-1406 to 28-1416 — expressly excludes "any duly authorized law enforcement officer of the State of Nebraska or its political subdivisions." It also clarifies that an actor lawfully present in a motor vehicle need not retreat from that vehicle unless the actor was the initial aggressor, and that neither the actor nor the person being protected must retreat farther when lawfully present in another's motor vehicle than from their own. No government funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
