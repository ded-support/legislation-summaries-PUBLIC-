Summary

LB262 appropriates funds to the Legislative Council (Program 1) for payment of Nebraska Legislature members' salaries and other payments under Chapter 68, article 6. It provides $632,982 from the General Fund for FY2025‑26 and $632,982 for FY2026‑27 to the Legislative Council. Expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from these appropriations are capped at $588,000 in each fiscal year. The act becomes operative July 1, 2025, and—due to an emergency clause—takes effect upon passage and approval.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates $632,982 from the General Fund to the Legislative Council (Program 1) for each of FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 to cover legislative member salaries and related payments under Chapter 68, article 6. Although $632,982 is allocated each year, total expenditures from these funds for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $588,000 for FY2025-26 and $588,000 for FY2026-27. No other agencies are affected.
