Summary

LB120 amends the Motor Vehicle Operator’s License Act provisions governing digital images and signatures maintained by the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV). It affirms that the DMV must capture and preserve applicants’ digital images for issuing original, renewal, and replacement operator’s licenses and state identification cards, and that images may be retrieved only by the DMV for renewal/replacement except as authorized. The bill authorizes the DMV to enter agreements with the Nebraska State Patrol for release, use, protection, storage, and retention of images (including posting to the Missing Persons Clearinghouse) and requires the DMV to develop a secure process and enter an agreement with the Secretary of State for voter-identification releases. The DMV may adopt rules to implement these provisions. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language allows the Department of Motor Vehicles to release digital images to the Nebraska State Patrol for posting on the State Patrol’s Missing Persons Clearinghouse, and to enter into an agreement with the State Patrol governing release, use, protection, storage, and retention of those images consistent with the Uniform Motor Vehicle Records Disclosure Act. The State Patrol must implement anti‑fraud protections, create and update security measures for access/storage/retention, and remove an image from the Clearinghouse within three business days after being notified the person has been located. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
