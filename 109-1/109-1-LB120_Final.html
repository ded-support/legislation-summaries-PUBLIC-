Summary

LB120 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60‑484.02 to require the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) to capture and preserve applicants' digital images and signatures for issuing and renewing operator's licenses and state ID cards. Images may be retrieved only by the DMV for renewals/replacements and otherwise released by the DMV only in limited circumstances (to law enforcement, the Secretary of State for voter ID, or to the Nebraska State Patrol for the Missing Persons Clearinghouse). The DMV may enter agreements with the Nebraska State Patrol and the Secretary of State governing release, use, protection, storage, and retention, must develop a secure release process, and may adopt rules and regulations. Unauthorized disclosure by DMV personnel is a Class I misdemeanor. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text authorizes the Department of Motor Vehicles to release digital images (e.g., from motor vehicle records) to the Nebraska State Patrol for posting on the State Patrol’s Missing Persons Clearinghouse when the person is deemed missing. It allows the DMV and State Patrol to enter an agreement governing release, use, protection, storage, and retention of those images consistent with the Uniform Motor Vehicle Records Disclosure Act. The State Patrol must implement protections against fraudulent use, create and update security measures for access/storage/retention, and remove an image from the clearinghouse within three business days after being notified the person has been located. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
