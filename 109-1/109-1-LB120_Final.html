Summary

LB120 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60‑484.02 regarding the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV). Key DMV provisions:

- DMV must capture and preserve digital images and signatures for issuance of operator’s licenses and state ID cards; images may be retrieved only by the DMV for renewals/replacements except as specified.

- The DMV may release images/signatures only under enumerated exceptions (law enforcement/driver‑licensing agencies, Secretary of State for voter ID/registration, and to the Nebraska State Patrol for posting on its Missing Persons Clearinghouse under an agreement).

- The DMV and Nebraska State Patrol may enter agreements governing release, use, protection, storage, retention, and the Patrol must apply security protocols and remove images within three business days after a subject is located.

- DMV must develop the Secretary of State release process and may adopt rules; unauthorized disclosure by DMV personnel is a Class I misdemeanor.

No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language authorizes the Department of Motor Vehicles to provide digital images to the Nebraska State Patrol for posting on the Missing Persons Clearinghouse when an individual is deemed missing. It allows the DMV and State Patrol to enter an agreement governing release, use, protection, storage, and retention of those images consistent with the Uniform Motor Vehicle Records Disclosure Act. The State Patrol must implement anti-fraud protections, maintain/update security measures, and remove images within three business days after notification the person is located. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
