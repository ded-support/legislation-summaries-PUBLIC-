Summary

LB120 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-484.02 to (1) specify that the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) captures and preserves applicants' digital images and signatures for issuance of operator's licenses and state ID cards, and that images may be retrieved only by the DMV for renewals/replacements except as authorized; (2) permit DMV release of images to law enforcement, other states' driver licensing agencies, the Secretary of State for voter ID, and the Nebraska State Patrol (including a formal agreement with the Patrol governing release, use, protection, storage, and retention); (3) require the DMV to develop a secure process and enter an agreement with the Secretary of State for voter-identification image transfers and to adopt rules as needed; and (4) make unlawful disclosure by DMV personnel a Class I misdemeanor. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language allows the Department of Motor Vehicles to release digital images to the Nebraska State Patrol for posting on the State Patrol’s Missing Persons Clearinghouse when an individual is deemed missing. The DMV may enter an agreement with the State Patrol governing release, use, protection, storage, and retention of those images consistent with the Uniform Motor Vehicle Records Disclosure Act. The State Patrol must implement fraud-prevention protections, create and update security measures for access, storage, and retention, and remove images from the Clearinghouse website within three business days after being notified the person has been located. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the DMV are included.
