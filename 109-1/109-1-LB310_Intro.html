Summary

LB310 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-519 to (1) authorize the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to specify additional newborn screening conditions; (2) designate the laboratory for testing and receive reporting of presumptive and confirmed positives; (3) request hospital birthing records for tracking, monitoring, and referral; (4) exercise authority over use, retention, and disposal of neonatal blood specimens and related data, promulgating rules consistent with federal law and accreditation standards (including witness-verified disposal records); (5) permit DHHS to charge a reasonable fee to evaluate research proposals and to prepare/supply specimens for approved research; and (6) require DHHS to prepare parent informational materials. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The bill repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a parental‑opt‑out: infants are not required to undergo the subsection (1) screening if a parent or guardian objects. For the Department of Health and Human Services this changes the department’s operational obligation to obtain screening only with parental consent; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the added text.
