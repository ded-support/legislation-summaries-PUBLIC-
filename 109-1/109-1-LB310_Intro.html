Summary

LB310 amends §71-519 and repeatedly references the Department of Health and Human Services (department). Key provisions: the department may specify additional newborn screening disorders; it designates the laboratory for testing and receives presumptive and confirmed positive results. The department has authority over use, retention, and disposal of collected blood specimens and related information and must adopt rules (consistent with national lab standards and federal law) governing retention, disposal (including witness-verified records), and permitted public-health uses. The department may charge a reasonable fee to evaluate research proposals and to prepare/supply specimens for approved research. The department must prepare informational pamphlets describing its procedures and permitted research uses. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a parental/guardian opt-out: the Department of Health and Human Services may not require an infant to undergo the subsection (1) screening if the parent or guardian objects. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. Practical effect: DHHS must allow and accommodate parental refusals for the referenced infant screening program, which could affect enrollment or implementation but does not change authorized funding.
