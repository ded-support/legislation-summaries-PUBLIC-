Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services to award nursing scholarships of $2,500 per semester to students who meet specified eligibility (Nebraska residency; enrollment or intent to enroll in an approved in‑state nursing program leading to an associate degree, diploma, certificate, or accelerated BSN; intent to practice as an LPN, RN, or nurse aide; and a written two‑year work commitment in Nebraska), subject to available appropriations. The Legislature states intent to appropriate $5,000,000 to DHHS for fiscal year 2025–26 for this purpose.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services will award $2,500 per semester scholarships to eligible nursing students, subject to available appropriations. Eligibility: Nebraska residency; enrollment or intent to enroll in an approved Nebraska nursing program (associate, diploma, certificate, or accelerated BSN); intent to practice as an LPN, RN, or nurse aide after program completion; and a written agreement to work in Nebraska for two years after completion. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $5 million to DHHS for FY2025‑26 for this scholarship program.
