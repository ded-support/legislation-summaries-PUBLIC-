Summary

LB597 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act and changes duties/timelines for the State Department of Education, the Property Tax Administrator, the Tax Commissioner, the Director of Administrative Services, the Auditor of Public Accounts, the Appropriations Committee, the Governor, the Education Committee, the State Board, and the Tax Equalization and Review Commission.

Key agency-related changes:

- Foundation aid: Dept. of Education pays $1,500 per formula student through FY2024-25; beginning FY2025-26 each district receives the greater of $1,500 per student or 7.5% of basic funding. Twenty-four percent of foundation aid is paid from the Education Future Fund. Inclusion of foundation aid as a formula resource falls from 100% (FY2023-24 and FY2024-25) to 60% beginning FY2025-26.

- Local effort rate: beginning FY2025-26 the rate for certification is the maximum levy under §77-3442 less $0.10; yields computed using adjusted valuations.

- Adjusted valuations: county assessor certification by Aug. 20 (amendments by Aug. 31); Property Tax Administrator certifies adjusted valuations to the Dept. of Education by Oct. 10. Agricultural land adjusted valuation rate reduced from 72% to 65% beginning FY2025-26.

- Certification/budget dates: state aid, district budget authority, and allowable reserve percentages certified on or before June 15, 2025 and on or before March 1 each year thereafter; any certifications for FY2025-26 completed before the act’s effective date are null and void.

- Funding/appropriations: Dept. must report necessary funding levels to Governor and legislative committees; Appropriations Committee must include amounts necessary to fund certified state aid. The Director of Administrative Services issues warrants for lump-sum/state aid payments as directed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective for school fiscal year 2025-26 and thereafter, the bill (1) sets a new foundation aid floor for each school district: the greater of $1,500 per formula student or 7.5% of basic funding (per §79‑1007.16); (2) changes how the local effort rate is set: for state aid certification (§79‑1022) it equals the maximum levy authorized under §77‑3442 minus $0.10, while for final state aid calculations (§79‑1065) it is the rate that, when applied to total adjusted valuation of local systems receiving equalization aid under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act (TEEOSA), produces the shortfall that state appropriations by the Legislature plus other local receipts must cover; and (3) fixes adjusted valuation used for aid calculations at 65% of actual value and 65% of special valuation (per referenced §§77‑1359, 77‑1363, 77‑1343).
