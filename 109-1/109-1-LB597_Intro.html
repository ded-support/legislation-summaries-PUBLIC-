Summary

LB597 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act and directs actions by the State Department of Education, Property Tax Administrator, Tax Commissioner/Tax Commission, Director of Administrative Services, Auditor of Public Accounts, Governor, the Legislature’s Appropriations and Education Committees, and the state board.

Key fiscal and administrative changes:

- Foundation aid (Dept. of Education): through 2024‑25 remains $1,500 per formula student; for 2025‑26 onward equals the greater of $1,500 per formula student or 7.5% of basic funding. Twenty‑four percent of foundation aid is paid from the Education Future Fund. Foundation aid counts as a formula resource at 100% for 2023‑24 and 2024‑25, then 60% thereafter.

- Local effort rate (affects local effort rate yield): beginning 2025‑26, certification uses the maximum levy less $0.10 (affecting state/local funding shares).

- Adjusted valuations (Property Tax Administrator/Tax Commissioner): agricultural/horticultural land valuation factor reduced from 72% to 65% for 2025‑26 onward.

- Certification timing: Dept. certifications and required legislative funding reports set for June 15, 2025, and March 1 annually thereafter; prior certifications for FY2025‑26 are void.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds provisions effective for school fiscal year 2025-26 and thereafter affecting school finance under TEEOSA. Key points:

- Foundation aid to each school district = greater of $1,500 per formula student or 7.5% of basic funding (per §79-1007.16).

- For state aid certification (§79-1022) the local effort rate = the maximum levy authorized under §77-3442(2)(a) minus $0.10.

- For final state aid calculation (§79-1065) the local effort rate is set so that (rate × total adjusted valuation of taxable property in local systems receiving equalization aid) plus legislative state aid and other local receipts equals total formula need; each system’s local effort yield = its adjusted valuation × that rate.

- Adjusted valuation for SFY 2025-26 onward is fixed at 65% of actual value (§§77-1359, 77-1363) and 65% of special valuation (§77-1343).
