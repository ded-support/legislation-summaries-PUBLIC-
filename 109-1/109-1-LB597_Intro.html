Summary

LB597 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act and assigns actions to multiple state agencies.

- State Department of Education: determine and certify state aid to the Director of Administrative Services, Auditor of Public Accounts and local systems (certification dates set June 15, 2025 and March 1 thereafter) and report required funding to the Governor, Appropriations Committee and Education Committee.

- Education Future Fund: fund 24% of total foundation aid.

- Property Tax Administrator / Tax Commissioner: county assessors to certify taxable value by Aug. 20 (amendments by Aug. 31); PTA to certify adjusted valuations by Oct. 10; Tax Commissioner to hear objections, certify corrections by Jan. 1, and permit appeals to the Tax Equalization and Review Commission.

- Director of Administrative Services: draw warrants for lump‑sum state aid upon State Board approval.

Fiscal changes: foundation aid (through 2024-25: $1,500 per formula student; from 2025-26 onward: greater of $1,500 per student or 7.5% of basic funding); inclusion of foundation aid as a formula resource reduced to 60% from 2025-26; agricultural land adjusted valuation reduced from 72% to 65% from 2025-26; local effort rate for certification reduced by ten cents beginning 2025-26. Certifications for 2025-26 completed before the act’s effective date are null and void.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds new school-aid mechanics effective FY2025-26. Foundation aid to each school district will be the greater of $1,500 per formula student or 7.5% of basic funding under §79-1007.16. For state aid certification (§79-1022) the local effort rate is set at the maximum levy under §77-3442(2)(a) minus $0.10; for final aid calculations (§79-1065) the local effort rate is the rate that, when multiplied by total adjusted valuation of taxable property in local systems receiving equalization aid under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act (TEEOSA), yields the amount needed when combined with state appropriations and other actual local receipts (§79-1018.01). "Local effort rate yield" is defined as each local system’s adjusted valuation times that rate. Adjusted valuation for FY2025-26 onward is fixed at 65% of actual value (§77-1359/77-1363) and 65% of special valuation (§77-1343). The Legislature is referenced as the source of state appropriations.
