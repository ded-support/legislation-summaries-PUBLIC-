Summary

LB597 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act and affects multiple state agencies.

- State Department of Education: revises foundation aid — for 2025-26 onward each district receives the greater of $1,500 per formula student or 7.5% of basic funding; 24% of total foundation aid must be paid from the Education Future Fund; only 60% (vs. 100% for 2023-24–2024-25) of foundation aid is counted as a formula resource beginning 2025-26. The department must certify state aid amounts to the Director of Administrative Services, Auditor of Public Accounts, and local systems and report required funding to the Governor, the Appropriations Committee, and the Legislature’s Education Committee.

- Property Tax Administrator / Tax Commissioner / Tax Commission: adjusted valuations for agricultural/horticultural land drop from 72% to 65% of actual/special value beginning 2025-26; certification, objection, hearing, and appeal procedures are retained. The local effort rate used for certification beginning 2025-26 is the maximum levy less $0.10 (previously less $0.05), affecting local effort rate yield calculations.

- Director of Administrative Services: receives certification for aid and issues warrants for lump-sum payments when authorized.

- Appropriations Committee: must include the amount necessary to fund certified state aid (certified on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 thereafter) in its budget recommendations.

- Additional provisions nullify any state-aid, budget authority, or reserve-percentage certifications completed for school year 2025-26 prior to the act’s effective date.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds new school funding rules effective for school fiscal year 2025-26 and thereafter. Key points affecting government actors and funding:

- Foundation aid to each school district equals the greater of $1,500 per formula student or 7.5% of basic funding (per §79-1007.16).

- For state aid certification (§79-1022) the local effort rate = the maximum levy under §77-3442(2)(a) minus $0.10.

- For final state aid calculations (§79-1065) the local effort rate is set to produce the amount needed to meet total formula need when combined with state aid appropriated by the Legislature and other local receipts; local effort yield = adjusted valuation × local effort rate.

- Adjusted valuation for aid calculations (2025-26 onward) uses 65% of actual value and 65% of special valuation (per §§77-1359, 77-1363, 77-1343).
