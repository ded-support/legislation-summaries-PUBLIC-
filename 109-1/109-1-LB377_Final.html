Summary

LB377 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §37-101 governing the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission. The commission remains nine members (one from each of eight districts plus one at-large), appointed by the Governor with the consent of a majority of the Legislature. Members must be Nebraska residents and citizens, district appointees must reside in their districts, at least three must be engaged in agricultural pursuits, and no more than five may share the same political party. Members serve four-year terms beginning January 15 and serve until successors qualify; appointments to fill vacancies are for the remainder of the term and do not count as a full term. No person who has served three full terms is eligible for reappointment. The Governor may remove members after a hearing; removal actions are filed with the Secretary of State. The bill repeals the original section. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
