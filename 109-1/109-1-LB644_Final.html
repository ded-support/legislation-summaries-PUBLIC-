Summary

LB644 assigns primary administration and enforcement roles to the Attorney General and the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (NADC), and adds duties for the Secretary of State, Clerk of the Legislature, State Treasurer, and state investment officer.

- Attorney General: establishes the Foreign Adversary and Terrorist Agent Registration Act; requires registration, record retention, public portal posting, transmission of filings to the U.S. Secretary of State, semiannual legislative reports and monthly portal updates, authority to issue civil investigative demands, share data with other governments, adopt rules, and pursue injunctions and penalties.

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission: expands lobbyist/consultant disclosure for representatives of Chinese military companies, authorizes civil penalties (up to $100,000 escalating to $1M), and must pay $50,000 to informants whose tips lead to findings (payment contingent on recovery of penalties).

- State Treasurer: receives remitted penalties for constitutional distribution; state investment officer may invest NADC Cash Fund. Secretary of State to consult on and may adopt attestation rules; Clerk of the Legislature handles lobbyist registration filings.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds multiple enforcement and reporting duties primarily to the Nebraska Attorney General: require registration/supplements by agents of foreign principals, retain and make registration statements public, transmit copies to the U.S. Secretary of State, share information with state and federal agencies and legislative committees, post monthly reports on an AG portal, issue civil investigative demands, and promulgate rules (subject to the Personal Privacy Protection Act). The Secretary of State must consult with the Attorney General on an attestation form and may adopt rules; Directors of Insurance and of Banking and Finance receive attestations on their reports (no more frequently than annually). Civil penalties collected are remitted to the State Treasurer per the Nebraska Constitution; the commission may pay whistleblowers $50,000 contingent on penalty recovery. No explicit new appropriations or budget allocations are provided.
