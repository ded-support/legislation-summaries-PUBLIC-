Summary

This bill creates the Foreign Adversary and Terrorist Agent Registration Act and the Crush Transnational Repression in Nebraska Act and assigns primary administration and enforcement to the Nebraska Attorney General. The Attorney General must collect registrations, maintain public records and a web portal, transmit filings to the U.S. Secretary of State, report semiannually to the Legislature, and may issue civil investigative demands and regulations. The Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (NADC) gains authority to sanction violations of lobbying/consultant disclosure (including enhanced penalties for failures to disclose Chinese military company principals), to pay informants $50,000 contingent on recovery of assessed penalties, and to deposit penalties into the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution. Businesses must attest compliance in filings with the Secretary of State, Director of Insurance, or Director of Banking and Finance. Public entities are barred from contracts with scrutinized/foreign adversarial companies; such companies are ineligible for specified state incentive programs (e.g., ImagiNE Nebraska, Nebraska Advantage acts).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds extensive enforcement and reporting duties primarily for the Nebraska Attorney General: registration, retention, public posting (monthly portal), six‑monthly legislative reports, authority to issue civil investigative demands, share information with state and federal agencies, and to transmit registration copies to the U.S. Secretary of State. The U.S. Department of State is referenced for diplomatic/consular exemptions. The Secretary of State (Nebraska) and the Directors of Insurance and of Banking and Finance must receive or include statutory attestation language on business reports; the Secretary of State may adopt rules. Civil penalties (including those for Chinese military company disclosure violations) are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution. A commission (unnamed) may award $50,000 to whistleblowers contingent on penalty recovery. No new appropriations or specific budget line items are provided.
