Summary

LB644 creates the Foreign Adversary and Terrorist Agent Registration Act and the Crush Transnational Repression in Nebraska Act and assigns primary administration and enforcement duties to the Nebraska Attorney General. The Attorney General must receive and retain registrations and informational material, publish a public portal, transmit filings to the U.S. Secretary of State, share information with state and federal agencies and legislative committees, issue civil investigative demands, adopt rules, and report semiannually to the Legislature. The Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (NADC) is given new lobbyist/consultant disclosure and enforcement authority for activity tied to Chinese military companies, may impose civil penalties, and must pay eligible informants $50,000 contingent on penalty recovery. Penalties collected are remitted to the State Treasurer; the NADC Cash Fund's composition and investment/transfer rules are updated to support administration. The Secretary of State and various state directors have consultative or reporting roles.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds extensive duties for the Attorney General: receive, retain permanently, and publish registration statements and informational materials; transmit copies to the U.S. Secretary of State; post monthly portal reports; exchange data with other states and federal officials; furnish information to state and federal agencies and legislative committees; issue civil investigative demands; promulgate rules; and seek injunctions. The Secretary of State must consult on and receive business attestation filings and may adopt rules. The Directors of Insurance and Banking and Finance must accept attestation on their reporting and set filing frequency (not more than annual). Penalties collected (civil fines for violations) are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Nebraska’s constitution. A commission may levy penalties for specified lobbying violations and pay a $50,000 whistleblower award contingent on penalty recovery. No standalone appropriations or new budget allocations are provided.
