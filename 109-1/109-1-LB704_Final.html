Summary

LB704 amends state child-support review procedures, primarily assigning responsibilities to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DHHS must review IV-D cases and refer orders to the county attorney or authorized attorney for modification when verified financial data show deviation from Supreme Court guidelines or lack of required health coverage. For noncustodial parents incarcerated >180 days, DHHS must initiate review within 15 business days, mail notice to both parents and the incarcerating facility/administrator, request financial information within 30 days, complete review and notify parties within 120 days. If DHHS finds no decrease warranted, it must offer the incarcerated parent a conference (in person or virtual) within 30 days of review completion, accept waivers, and issue a final determination within 30 days after the conference. Final determinations are not contested cases under the APA. No appropriations, funding, or budget changes are included. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for "the department" (child‑support agency) to: initiate a review of a noncustodial parent's child‑support order when the parent is incarcerated; require submission of financial information within 30 days (and to the facility administrator per §47‑1003); and complete the review within 120 days of learning of the incarceration, notifying parties per §43‑512.13. If no decrease is warranted, the department must offer the incarcerated parent a conference (in‑person or virtual) within 30 days, provide mailed notice to both parents with specified contents, review verifiable financials, and issue a final determination within 30 days of the conference (or after a waiver). The department may refer orders to the county attorney or another authorized attorney. Notices to incarcerated parents must be sent to the facility address and administrator. Final determinations are not contested cases under the Administrative Procedure Act. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
