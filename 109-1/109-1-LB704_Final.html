Summary

LB704 amends procedures for the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) in IV-D child support cases. DHHS must review support orders and refer cases to the county attorney or other authorized attorney for filing of modification applications in district court when verifiable financial information meets Supreme Court guideline-variation or health-coverage criteria. For a noncustodial parent newly known to be incarcerated more than 180 days, DHHS must: initiate review within 15 business days, send notice to both parents and the correctional facility administrator, require financials within 30 days, complete review and notify parties within 120 calendar days, and—if a decrease is denied—offer a conference (scheduled within 30 days) with a 30-day final determination post-conference. Determinations are not contested cases under the Administrative Procedure Act. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for the department (child support enforcement) when a noncustodial parent is incarcerated: the department must require parties to submit financial information (within 30 days of notice, also to the facility administrator per §47‑1003), complete its review within 120 days of learning of the incarceration, and notify parties per §43‑512.13. If a reduction is denied, the department must offer the incarcerated parent a conference within 30 days (in‑person or virtual) with specified first‑class mail notice contents and timing (including ability to submit evidence or waive the conference). Within 30 days after the conference the department issues a final determination and may refer the matter to the county attorney or authorized attorney; notices go to the facility address and facility administrator. Final determinations are not treated as contested cases under the Administrative Procedure Act. No funding or appropriation changes are made.
