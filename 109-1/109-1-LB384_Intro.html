Summary

LB384 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-1633 (Property Tax Request Act). It applies to political subdivisions (counties, cities, school districts, community colleges) and adds a requirement that a majority of the elected members of each participating subdivision's governing body attend joint public hearings on proposed property-tax-request increases above the allowable growth percentage. County clerks (or designees) organize the joint hearings; county assessors receive electronic submissions by September 4 and coordinate printing with a service designated by the county board. The bill preserves existing postcard-notice procedures: initial printing charged to the county general fund and the creation/mailing costs (including staff time, materials, postage) apportioned proportionately to participating political subdivisions. The original section is repealed.
