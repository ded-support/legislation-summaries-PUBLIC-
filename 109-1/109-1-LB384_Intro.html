Summary

LB384 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-1633 (Property Tax Request Act). It retains joint public hearing requirements for any county’s political subdivisions (counties, cities, school districts, community colleges) seeking property tax requests above allowable growth, but adds a new attendance mandate: a majority of the elected members of each participating subdivision’s governing body must attend the joint hearing. The county clerk (or designee) must organize the hearing, notify the county assessor and subdivisions by September 4, and deliver a post-hearing report within ten days. Participating subdivisions must electronically send required presentation data to the county assessor by September 4; hearings occur Sept. 14–24 after 6 p.m. The county assessor provides postcard materials to a printer designated by the county board. Initial postcard printing is charged to the county general fund; creation and mailing costs (including staff time, materials, postage) are then allocated proportionately to participating subdivisions. Ordinances/resolutions setting tax requests must be certified to the county clerk by October 15.
