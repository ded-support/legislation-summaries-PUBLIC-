Summary

LB411 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-8,114 (Nebraska Teacher Recruitment and Retention Act). It requires the Department (Nebraska Department of Education) to automatically provide annual retention grants beginning July 1, 2025: $2,500 for years 1–6, $3,000 for years 7–15, and $4,000 from year 16 onward. A one-time high-need retention grant of $5,000 is available for teachers who, on or after July 1, 2025, obtain endorsements in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual-credit and sign a contract to teach in that endorsement; prior high-need recipients are ineligible for a second such grant. The bill repeals the original §79-8,114 and takes effect immediately under an emergency clause. No statewide appropriation or funding source is specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective July 1, 2025, "the department" is directed to automatically provide teacher retention grants. Grant amounts: $2,500 per year for years 1–6; $3,000 per year for years 7–15; $4,000 per year from year 16 onward. A one‑time high‑need retention grant of $5,000 is available (on or after July 1, 2025) to teachers who obtain an endorsement in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual‑credit and sign a one‑year contract to teach in that endorsement area. Teachers may receive only one high‑need grant; recipients of a prior high‑need grant awarded before July 1, 2025, are ineligible for the new grant.
