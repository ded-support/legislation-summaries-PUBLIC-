Summary

Amends the Nebraska Teacher Recruitment and Retention Act (section 79-8,114). The state "department" is directed to automatically provide retention grants beginning July 1, 2025. Grant schedule: $2,500 per year for years 1–6 of full-time Nebraska teaching; $3,000 per year for years 7–15; $4,000 per year from year 16 onward. A $5,000 high-need retention grant is available for teachers (on/after July 1, 2025) who obtain endorsements in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual-credit and sign a contract to teach in that area; teachers are limited to one high-need grant and prior high-need recipients before July 1, 2025, are ineligible. The bill retains a transitional clause for contracts signed on/after April 17, 2024, for the 2024–25 school year, repeals the original section, and takes effect immediately as an emergency. No specific appropriations or funding sources are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective July 1, 2025, "the department" must automatically provide teacher retention grants to eligible Nebraska teachers. Grants are: $2,500 per year for years 1–6 of full‑time Nebraska teaching; $3,000 per year for years 7–15; $4,000 per year beginning in year 16 and thereafter. A single high‑need retention grant of $5,000 is available for teachers who, on/after July 1, 2025, obtain an endorsement in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual‑credit and sign a contract to teach that endorsement area for a year. Each teacher may receive only one high‑need grant; those who already received such a grant before July 1, 2025, are ineligible.
