Summary

LB411 amends the Nebraska Teacher Recruitment and Retention Act (section 79-8,114) to revise eligibility and automatic distribution of teacher retention grants administered by the Department (the department is directed to automatically provide grants beginning July 1, 2025). Grant amounts: $2,500 annually for years 1–6 of full‑time Nebraska teaching; $3,000 for years 7–15; $4,000 from year 16 onward. A one‑time high‑need retention grant of $5,000 is available for teachers who, on/after July 1, 2025, obtain endorsements in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual‑credit and sign a contract to teach in that area; only one high‑need grant per teacher (prior recipients before July 1, 2025 are ineligible). The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective July 1, 2025, "the department" is required to automatically provide teacher retention grants. Grant amounts by years of full‑time Nebraska service: $2,500 per year (years 1–6); $3,000 per year (years 7–15); $4,000 per year (year 16 onward). A one‑time high‑need retention grant of $5,000 is available to a teacher who, on or after July 1, 2025, obtains an endorsement in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual‑credit and signs a contract to teach in that endorsement area for one year. Only one high‑need grant may be received per teacher; teachers who previously received such a grant before July 1, 2025 are ineligible. The text sets grant amounts and administrative responsibility but contains no separate appropriation language.
