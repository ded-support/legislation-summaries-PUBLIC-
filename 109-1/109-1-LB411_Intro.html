Summary

LB411 amends the Nebraska Teacher Recruitment and Retention Act (section 79-8,114). It directs the department (Nebraska Department of Education) to automatically provide annual retention grants beginning July 1, 2025, with amounts tiered by years of full‑time Nebraska teaching: $2,500 (years 1–6), $3,000 (years 7–15), and $4,000 (year 16 onward). It creates a one‑time $5,000 high‑need retention grant for teachers who, on or after July 1, 2025, obtain an endorsement in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual‑credit and sign a contract to teach in that endorsement area. A teacher may receive only one high‑need retention grant; teachers who received such a grant before July 1, 2025, are ineligible for the new $5,000 grant. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately under an emergency clause. No specific appropriation language is included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective July 1, 2025, "the department" must automatically provide teacher retention grants. Eligible teachers receive: $2,500/year in years 1–6, $3,000/year in years 7–15, and $4,000/year from year 16 onward. A one-time $5,000 high-need retention grant is available (on or after July 1, 2025) for teachers who obtain an endorsement in special education, mathematics, science, technology, or dual-credit and sign a one-year contract to teach in that endorsement area at a Nebraska school. Each teacher may receive only one high-need grant; teachers who already received a high-need grant before July 1, 2025, are ineligible for another. The provision names only "the department" as the administering entity; no new funding, appropriation, or budget allocation is specified in the added text.
