Summary

LB138 directs the department to establish a fee‑for‑service pharmacy dispensing fee of $10.38 per prescription for independent pharmacies beginning in FY2024‑25, contingent on implementation of contractual agreements with Medicaid managed care organizations and federal Medicaid state plan approval. The department must determine the actual dispensing fee via a cost‑of‑dispensing survey of participating independent pharmacies every two years and amend all Medicaid managed care organization contracts to authorize a managed‑care dispensing fee consistent with the fee‑for‑service rate. Survey submissions that identify individual pharmacy costs are confidential. The department must electronically report recommendations for interim fee adjustments to the Clerk of the Legislature by December 15, 2024. Independent pharmacies are those owning six or fewer locations. The bill contains no separate appropriation or explicit budget allocation.
