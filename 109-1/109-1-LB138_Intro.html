Summary

LB138 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-9,111 to direct the state department that administers the medical assistance program to set a fee-for-service pharmacy dispensing reimbursement of $10.38 per prescription for independent pharmacies (those owning six or fewer locations) beginning FY2024‑25, pending contractual implementation with Medicaid managed care organizations (MCOs) and federal Medicaid state‑plan approval. The department must amend all Medicaid MCO contracts to authorize a corresponding managed‑care dispensing fee. The department will conduct a cost‑of‑dispensing survey of independent pharmacies every two years to determine the actual fee, keep individual pharmacy cost submissions confidential, and submit recommendations for interim fee adjustments to the Clerk of the Legislature by December 15, 2024.
