Summary

LB138 amends §68-9,111 to direct the department to establish a fee-for-service pharmacy dispensing reimbursement of $10.38 per prescription for independent pharmacies (those owning six or fewer locations) beginning in FY2024-25, contingent on implementation of contractual changes with Medicaid managed care organizations. The department must administer a biennial cost-of-dispensing survey of participating independent pharmacies to determine the actual dispensing fee; any fee change becomes effective after federal approval of the Medicaid state plan. The department is required to amend all Medicaid managed care organization contracts to authorize a managed-care dispensing fee consistent with the fee-for-service amount. Cost submissions that identify individual provider costs are confidential. The department must electronically report recommendations for interim dispensing-fee adjustments to the Clerk of the Legislature by December 15, 2024.
