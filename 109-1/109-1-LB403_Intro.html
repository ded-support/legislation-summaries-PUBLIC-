Summary

Creates an Office of Grants in the executive branch. The office is led by a Director of Grants, appointed by the Governor (serving at the Governor’s pleasure), selected after a nationwide search and required to have at least ten years’ experience writing or managing federal grants. The Director may appoint additional staff "for which adequate funding is available" (no specific appropriation in the bill). Duties: identify national (including federal) grant programs available to state agencies, political subdivisions, and private Nebraska organizations; assist those entities with identification, applications, and compliance; and develop a statewide plan to maximize grant funding by July 1, 2026, with annual updates. Annual July 1 reports to the Legislature are required summarizing the plan, legislative effects on grant access, entities served, and grant awards and dollar amounts attributable to the office’s services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates an Office of Grants in the executive branch headed by a Director of Grants (Governor‑appointed, serves at pleasure, nationwide search, 10+ years federal grant experience) and additional staff as funded. The Office must identify national (including federal) grant programs and assist state agencies, political subdivisions, and in‑state private organizations with identification, applications, and compliance. By July 1, 2026 (and annually thereafter) it must produce a statewide plan to maximize grant funding and submit an annual electronic report to the Legislature summarizing the plan, analyzing recent state legislation's impact on grant eligibility, listing entities served, and reporting count and dollar total of awards obtained through its assistance. No specific appropriations or budget allocations are made; staffing is tied to “adequate funding.”
