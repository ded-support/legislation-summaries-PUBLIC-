Summary

LB687 assigns the Department of Administrative Services the authority and responsibility to review state agency contracts to confirm compliance with the Firearm Industry Nondiscrimination Act. The bill does not include any specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The amendment assigns the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) the authority and responsibility to review state agency contracts to confirm compliance with the new Firearm Industry Nondiscrimination Act. The Act applies to government contracts of at least $100,000 with companies having 10+ full‑time employees and requires vendor verification that they will not discriminate against firearm entities or firearm trade associations; exemptions include sole‑source contracts and situations where no bidder can provide the required verification. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DAS or any other agency are specified in the added text.
