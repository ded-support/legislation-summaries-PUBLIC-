Summary

Section 5 assigns the Department of Administrative Services the authority and responsibility to review state agency contracts to confirm compliance with the Firearm Industry Nondiscrimination Act. The bill includes no language authorizing additional funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language creates a "Firearm Industry Nondiscrimination Act" requiring covered government contracts (companies with ≥10 FTEs, contract value ≥$100,000, entered on/after the act's effective date) to include company verification they will not discriminate against firearm entities/trade associations; exemptions include sole‑source contracts or lack of compliant bidders. The Department of Administrative Services is given authority and responsibility to review state agency contracts for compliance. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DAS oversight are specified.
