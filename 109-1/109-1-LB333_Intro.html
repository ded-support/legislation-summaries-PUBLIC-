Summary

LB333 amends Nebraska's Motor Vehicle Operator's License Act to authorize issuance of hazardous materials (HAZMAT) endorsements for intrastate hauling to any person age 18 or older holding a valid commercial driver's license. The bill references and harmonizes state requirements with federal authorities and rules, including the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA), 49 C.F.R. provisions, the National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners, and federal statutes (Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Act, Motor Carrier Safety Improvement Act, and USA PATRIOT Act). It also preserves the state's role in authorizing third‑party testers and skills examiners. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Adds a provision allowing any person age 18 or older holding a valid commercial driver's license to be issued a hazardous materials endorsement for intrastate transport.
