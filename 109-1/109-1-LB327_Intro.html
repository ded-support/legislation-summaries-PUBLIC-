Summary

LB327 shifts primary fiscal responsibility for probation from counties to the state. Beginning on an implementation date selected by the State Court Administrator (between July 1, 2026, and July 1, 2027), the state — using funds appropriated to the Nebraska Supreme Court — will pay: district probation office operating expenses, office space/facilities, probation staff salaries and travel, and comprehensive IT procurement/management/support. Counties must cooperate with the State Court Administrator on the transition. The Supreme Court may adopt rules to implement the changes. Interpreter standards for court and probation services are set by the Supreme Court; costs for interpreter and auxiliary services for deaf, hard-of-hearing, and non-English-speaking persons will be state-funded via Supreme Court appropriations or other funds/grants. The probation administrator must prepare and submit budget requests through the Supreme Court. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a State Court Administrator‑selected implementation date between July 1, 2026 and July 1, 2027. Beginning on that date, the state (via funds appropriated to the Supreme Court) will assume payment for probation services, covering district office operations, office space/facilities, probation staff salaries and travel, and comprehensive IT procurement/management/support (hardware, phones, mobile devices, scanners/printers, software licensing, networks, internet, technical support). County officials must collaborate with the State Court Administrator to coordinate the transition of payment responsibilities. The Supreme Court may adopt rules as needed to implement these changes.
