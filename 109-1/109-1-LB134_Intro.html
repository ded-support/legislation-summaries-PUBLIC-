Summary

- The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) must issue and design various veteran- and disability-related license plates, compile/maintain a registry of holders of wheelchair-symbol plates and handicapped parking permit holders, furnish temporary license stickers to counties at no cost, and verify applicant eligibility by consulting the registry maintained by the Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) under section 80-414. Applicants for POW, Military Honor, disabled veteran, and Purple Heart plates must register with the DVA.

- Fee/budget changes: $5 alphanumeric Military Honor plate fee credited to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund; $40 personalized Military Honor plate fee split 25% to the DMV Cash Fund and 75% to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund. A $3 transfer fee and any postage/handling recovery are credited to the DMV Cash Fund. If Military Honor plate manufacturing costs exceed statutory plate charges, the excess is first credited to the Highway Trust Fund; remainder to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (DMV, Dept. of Veterans' Affairs)

- DMV: New authority and duties to design and issue specialty plates (disabled veteran, Purple Heart with “Combat Wounded,” wheelchair symbol), require verification before issuance, and require application/proof of disability per §60‑3,113.02 with certification that the disability is permanent. Beginning Jan 1, 2026, DMV must issue replacement plates on renewal; former plates become invalid Feb 1, 2027. Trust-owned vehicles may receive eligible plates if a designated beneficiary qualifies (effective Jan 1, 2026).

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: Provides service‑connected disability ratings (≥10%) used to determine veteran eligibility for “disabled veteran” plates.

- Funding/appropriations: None specified in the added text.
