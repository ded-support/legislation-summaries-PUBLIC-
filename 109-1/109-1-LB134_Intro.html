Summary

- The Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) must verify applicants' eligibility for Military Honor, prisoner-of-war, disabled veteran, and Purple Heart plates by consulting the registry established by the Department of Veterans' Affairs (pursuant to section 80-414) and must make applications available for each plate type. DMV verification is required prior to issuing plates.

- Fee and budget provisions: $5 from alphanumeric Military Honor plate applications is credited to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund. Personalized Military Honor plate fees ($40) are split 25% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 75% to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund. A $3 transfer fee, postage/handling charges, and certain remittances are credited to the DMV Cash Fund. If plate manufacturing costs exceed statutory charges, the excess is first credited to the Highway Trust Fund, remainder to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (DMV & Dept. of Veterans' Affairs):

- DMV to create new veteran-related license plates effective Jan 1, 2026: "disabled veteran" plates (optionally bearing the wheelchair symbol) and Purple Heart plates (bearing a facsimile of the award plus the words "Purple Heart" and "Combat Wounded" and the wheelchair symbol).

- Eligibility: veterans discharged honorable/general and classified by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs as at least 10% service‑connected disabled may apply; trusts may apply if a designated beneficiary qualifies (effective Jan 1, 2026).

- DMV must verify eligibility before issuance and requires the application and permanent‑disability proof per section 60‑3,113.02 (and existing section 60‑385 application requirements).

- Former plate type will be invalid for use beginning Feb 1, 2027.

- No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
