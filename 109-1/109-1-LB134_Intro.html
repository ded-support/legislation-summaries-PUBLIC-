Summary

LB134 assigns the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) duties to design, issue, deliver, and replace specialty plates (Military Honor, prisoner‑of‑war, disabled veteran, Purple Heart, and wheelchair/disabled plates), provide application forms via county treasurers, furnish temporary license stickers, and compile/maintain a registry of wheelchair‑symbol plate holders and holders of handicapped/disabled parking permits. For Military Honor, POW, disabled‑veteran, and Purple Heart plates the DMV must verify applicant eligibility by consulting the registry established by the Department of Veterans' Affairs under section 80‑414. Funding: $5 alphanumeric Military Honor plate fee is credited to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund; $40 personalized Military Honor plate fee is split 25% to the DMV Cash Fund and 75% to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund. A $3 transfer fee, postage/handling fees, and certain replacement/transfer receipts are credited to the DMV Cash Fund; excess manufacturing costs are first credited to the Highway Trust Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Motor Vehicles

- New provisions let the DMV design and issue veteran, disabled‑veteran, and Purple Heart specialty plates (including a wheelchair symbol where specified).

- DMV must verify eligibility before issuance and require the application and proof of disability per section 60‑3,113.02; disability must be certified as permanent.

- Beginning Jan 1, 2026 the DMV will begin issuing replacement plates on renewal; certain older plates become invalid Feb 1, 2027.

- Trusts owning vehicles may apply beginning Jan 1, 2026 if a designated beneficiary qualifies.

Department of Veterans' Affairs

- Eligibility for “disabled veteran” plates requires U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs classification of at least 10% service‑connected disability.
