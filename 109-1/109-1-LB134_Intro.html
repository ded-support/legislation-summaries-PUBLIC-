Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)

- Must compile and maintain a registry of persons issued wheelchair-symbol license plates and holders of handicapped/disabled parking permits.

- Must verify eligibility for Military Honor, prisoner-of-war, disabled veteran, and Purple Heart plates by consulting the registry established by the Department of Veterans' Affairs (per §80-414).

- Will make application forms available through county treasurers, issue plates and temporary stickers (temporary stickers furnished to counties at no cost), may charge postage/handling fees remitted to the DMV Cash Fund, and may summarily cancel plates for fraud.

- Fee and allocation changes: $3 transfer fee remitted to DMV Cash Fund; $5 alphanumeric Military Honor Plate fee credited to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund; $40 personalized Military Honor Plate fee split 25% to DMV Cash Fund and 75% to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund. If manufacturing costs exceed statutory plate charges, the excess is first credited to the Highway Trust Fund, remainder to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA)

- Must maintain the eligibility registry used by the DMV for the veteran-related specialty plates.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (DMV & Veterans' Affairs):

- DMV to offer new disabled‑veteran and Purple Heart license plates beginning Jan 1, 2026; trusts may apply if a designated beneficiary qualifies.

- Veterans eligible: honorable or general (under honorable conditions) discharge and a U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs rating of at least 10% service‑connected disability.

- DMV must verify eligibility before issuance and, on renewals beginning Jan 1, 2026, replace certain existing plates; the old plates become invalid Feb 1, 2027.

- For disability‑bearing plates, applicants must submit the application and proof of disability per section 60‑3,113.02 before issuance/reissuance and show the disability is certified as permanent.

- Purple Heart plates will include a facsimile of the award, specified inscriptions, and the wheelchair symbol.

- No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
