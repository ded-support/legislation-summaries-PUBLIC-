Summary

LB87 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71‑8616 to direct the Nebraska Commission for the Blind and Visually Impaired to develop a statewide support service provider program for persons who are deaf‑blind. The bill includes a legislative intent regarding appropriations to implement the program (no dollar amounts specified in the bill text provided), removes obsolete provisions, repeals the original section, and contains an emergency clause for immediate effect. Referred to the Health and Human Services Committee.
