Summary

LB217 assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) specific duties regarding suicide awareness and prevention training for the child welfare system. DHHS must develop a baseline curriculum and training materials in consultation with statewide and national experts (including the Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition and the Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska), incorporating evidence‑based and trauma‑informed practices. DHHS must provide annual suicide awareness/prevention training for staff who provide direct services or interact with children/families, delivered within existing departmental training programs and completed prior to independent contact. The department must incorporate such training into foster‑home licensure initial and renewal requirements and adopt related rules and regulations; it may issue waivers for non‑safety licensing standards for relative homes. DHHS will also establish standardized training requirements for child‑placing agency employees and may approve agency curricula that meet the baseline. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. The act becomes operative October 1, 2025.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must develop a baseline suicide-awareness-and-prevention curriculum for child welfare training, in consultation with statewide and national behavioral-health and suicide-prevention experts (e.g., Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition, Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska). Training must use evidence-based practices and cover early warning signs, appropriate responses, and trauma-informed care. All DHHS child‑welfare staff who provide direct services or otherwise interact with children/families (and others as the Division of Children and Family Services designates) must receive annual training and complete required training before any independent contact with youth/families; the training is to be delivered within existing DHHS training programs. DHHS must include this training for initial licensure and renewal and establish standardized training requirements for employees of child‑placing agencies, allowing agency curricula if they meet the baseline. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
