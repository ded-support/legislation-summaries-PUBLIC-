Summary

LB217 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to develop a baseline curriculum and training materials on suicide awareness and prevention for child welfare personnel, in consultation with statewide and national behavioral‑health experts. Trainings must incorporate evidence‑based practices (identifying warning signs, effective responses, trauma‑informed care). DHHS must provide annual suicide‑awareness training for all child‑welfare staff who directly serve or interact with children/families, delivered prior to independent contact and within existing department training programs. DHHS rulemaking for foster‑care licensure is amended to require such training for initial licensure and renewals. DHHS will also set standardized suicide‑prevention training requirements for child‑placing agency employees and may approve agency curricula meeting the baseline. Operative date: October 1, 2025. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must develop a baseline, evidence-based suicide awareness and prevention curriculum for child welfare training in consultation with statewide and national behavioral‑health experts (including the Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition and the Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska). Required content includes early warning sign identification, effective responses, and trauma‑informed care. All DHHS child welfare staff who provide or directly interact with children/families, and other personnel designated by the Division of Children and Family Services, must receive annual training and training prior to any independent contact; training will be delivered within existing department programs. DHHS must also: (1) include this suicide prevention training in initial licensure and license renewal processes referenced in sections 71‑1901 to 71‑1906.01, and (2) set standardized pre‑contact training requirements for employees of child‑placing agencies while allowing agencies to use or adapt approved curricula that meet the baseline. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
