Summary

LB217 requires the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to:

- Develop a baseline suicide awareness and prevention curriculum and training materials in consultation with statewide and national behavioral-health experts (e.g., Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition, Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska).

- Provide annual suicide-awareness training for DHHS child-welfare staff who provide direct services or interact with children/families, delivered within existing department training programs and completed before independent contact.

- Adopt rules for foster-care licensure and include the department's suicide-awareness training for initial licensure and renewals.

- Establish standardized suicide-awareness training requirements for employees of child‑placing agencies and may approve agency curricula that meet the DHHS baseline.

Training must incorporate evidence-based practices (early warning signs, appropriate responses, trauma-informed care). Operative October 1, 2025. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must develop a baseline suicide awareness/prevention curriculum for child welfare training, in consultation with statewide and national experts (including the Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition and the Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska). Training must use evidence‑based practices, cover early warning signs, appropriate responses, and trauma‑informed care, and be delivered within existing DHHS training programs. All DHHS child‑welfare staff who provide or directly interact with children/families (and other personnel designated by the Division of Children and Family Services) must receive annual training and complete it before any independent contact with youth/families. DHHS will incorporate these training requirements into initial licensure and renewal (secs. 71‑1901–71‑1906.01) and establish standardized pre‑contact training standards for child‑placing agencies, allowing agency‑specific elements and approval of existing curricula that meet the baseline. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
