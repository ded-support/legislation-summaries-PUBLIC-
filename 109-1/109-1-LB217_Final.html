Summary

LB217 assigns multiple duties to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- DHHS must develop a baseline curriculum and training materials on suicide awareness and prevention for child welfare personnel, in consultation with statewide and national behavioral‑health experts (e.g., Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition, Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska). Training must incorporate evidence‑based and trauma‑informed practices and cover warning signs and effective responses.

- DHHS staff and other Division of Children and Family Services‑designated personnel who provide or directly interact with children/families must receive annual training and training prior to independent contact; training is to be integrated into existing departmental programs.

- DHHS must adopt licensing rules to require such suicide‑prevention training for initial and renewal foster‑care licensure and establish standardized training requirements for child‑placing agency employees, with authority to approve agency curricula that meet the baseline.

- Operative October 1, 2025. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must develop a baseline suicide awareness and prevention curriculum and training materials for the child‑welfare system in consultation with statewide and national behavioral‑health experts (including the Nebraska State Suicide Prevention Coalition and the Behavioral Health Education Center of Nebraska). Training must use evidence‑based practices and cover early warning signs, appropriate responses, and trauma‑informed care. All DHHS child‑welfare staff who provide or directly interact with children/families (and others as designated by the Division of Children and Family Services) must receive this training prior to any independent contact and annually thereafter, delivered within existing DHHS training programs. DHHS will include the training for initial licensure and license renewal (per sections 71‑1901 to 71‑1906.01) and set standardized pre‑contact training requirements for child‑placing agency employees; agencies may use or seek approval for existing curricula that meet the baseline. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
