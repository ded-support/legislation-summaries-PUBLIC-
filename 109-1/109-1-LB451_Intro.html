Summary

LB451 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§69-1317 and 72-2211 to authorize and govern transfers between state funds and clarify administration. It directs the State Treasurer to, beginning in 2025 through 2034, transfer any Unclaimed Property Trust Fund balance in excess of $1 million annually by first depositing $1 million to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and any remainder to the Permanent School Fund; transfers revert to the Permanent School Fund beginning 2035. The bill authorizes the State Treasurer to deduct unclaimed-property administration costs into a newly created Unclaimed Property Cash Fund. It adds the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund (and the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund) as potential revenue sources for the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund. The administrator, commission, and the state investment officer retain spending and investment roles; legislative direction may move funds to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language requires the State Treasurer to reallocate excess balances in the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund. For Nov. 1 each year 2025–2034, any balance over $1 million must be distributed with the first $1 million going to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and the remainder to the Permanent School Fund. Beginning Nov. 1, 2035, any balance over $1 million must be transferred entirely to the Permanent School Fund. Funds referenced include the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund, Unclaimed Property Cash Fund, the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund, and the Permanent School Fund. The Legislature retains direction over the Trust Fund's disposition. No other agencies or new appropriations are identified.
