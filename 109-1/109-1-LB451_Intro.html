Summary

LB451 amends transfer and administration rules for unclaimed property and the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund. It directs the State Treasurer to, on or before November 1 each year 2025–2034, transfer any Unclaimed Property Trust Fund balance in excess of $1 million so that the first $1 million goes to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and any remaining balance to the Permanent School Fund (transfers revert to the Permanent School Fund beginning 2035). The bill authorizes the State Treasurer to deduct unclaimed-property administrative costs into a newly created Unclaimed Property Cash Fund and requires investment of available balances by the State Investment Officer under existing investment acts. It updates the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund statute to explicitly allow transfers from the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund and the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund, assigns administration to the fund administrator and commission, and authorizes fund use for Capitol restoration, collections, promotion, and HVAC project management. The Auditor of Public Accounts and Department of Revenue confidentiality provisions are retained.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds annual transfer requirements for the State Treasurer: on or before November 1 each year 2025–2034, any Unclaimed Property Trust Fund balance over $1,000,000 must be split — the first $1,000,000 to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and any remaining excess to the Permanent School Fund. Beginning in 2035, any Unclaimed Property Trust Fund balance over $1,000,000 must be transferred to the Permanent School Fund. Agencies mentioned: State Treasurer and the Legislature (which may direct the fund). Funds affected: Unclaimed Property Trust Fund (and Unclaimed Property Cash Fund), Capitol Restoration Cash Fund, and Permanent School Fund. No other specific appropriations or dollar amounts beyond the $1,000,000 threshold are specified.
