Summary

LB451 amends the Unclaimed Property and Capitol Restoration statutes. It requires the State Treasurer (on or before Nov. 1 each year, 2025–2034) to transfer any Unclaimed Property Trust Fund balance over $1 million: the first $1 million to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and remaining funds to the Permanent School Fund (pre‑2025 transfers went entirely to the Permanent School Fund; transfers revert to the Permanent School Fund beginning 2035). The bill creates an Unclaimed Property Cash Fund to hold deducted unclaimed‑property administration costs and authorizes investment by the state investment officer. It also amends the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund to explicitly allow legislative‑directed transfers from the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund and Nebraska Capital Construction Fund; the fund is administered by the Capitol administrator, with expenditures approved by the commission. Transfers to the General Fund remain subject to legislative direction.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual transfer schedule from the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund, directed to be executed by the State Treasurer on or before November 1 each year. For 2025–2034, any fund balance in excess of $1,000,000 is split: the first $1,000,000 to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and the remainder to the permanent school fund. Beginning in 2035, any balance above $1,000,000 is transferred entirely to the permanent school fund. Mentions the Unclaimed Property Cash Fund and that transfers are to be made as directed by the Legislature. No other agencies are named and no new appropriations beyond these specified transfers are created.
