Summary

Amends transfers from the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund and establishes related cash‑fund mechanics. Key agencies/officials: State Treasurer (authorized to make the transfers and deduct unclaimed‑property costs), Capitol Restoration administrator and commission (administer/use Capitol Restoration Cash Fund), State Investment Officer (invests fund balances), Legislature (may direct transfers to the General Fund), Auditor of Public Accounts (retains access to confidential records), and Department of Revenue (confidentiality standard reference).

Funding changes:

- On or before Nov. 1 each year 2025–2034, excess balances over $1 million in the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund: first $1 million transfers to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund, remaining to the permanent school fund.

- Beginning 2035, excess balances revert to transfers solely to the permanent school fund.

- State Treasurer may deduct administrative costs into a newly created Unclaimed Property Cash Fund (investable; transfers to General Fund allowed at Legislature's direction).

- Capitol Restoration Cash Fund receipts explicitly include legislative‑directed transfers from the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund and Nebraska Capital Construction Fund; fund used for Capitol restoration, collections, promotional work, and HVAC project manager costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual transfer schedule for the State Treasurer from the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund. Beginning 2025–2034, on or before November 1 each year the Treasurer must transfer any Trust Fund balance in excess of $1,000,000: the first $1,000,000 to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund and any remaining excess to the Permanent School Fund. Beginning 2035, on or before November 1 each year the Treasurer must transfer any Trust Fund balance in excess of $1,000,000 entirely to the Permanent School Fund. Agencies/funds named: State Treasurer, Legislature (directive role), Unclaimed Property Trust Fund/Cash Fund, Capitol Restoration Cash Fund, and the Permanent School Fund.
