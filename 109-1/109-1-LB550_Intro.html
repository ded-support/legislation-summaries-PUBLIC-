Summary

LB550 requires Nebraska school districts to adopt a policy excusing students to attend released‑time courses for religious instruction if parental consent and district conditions are met. Key district obligations: treat excused students as attending for purposes of school funding and attendance requirements; permit academic credit only after secular evaluation using the same criteria as similar courses; restrict released‑time instruction from district property unless allowed under an equal‑access policy that opens facilities to community groups; and not deny released‑time sponsors equal access to funds, benefits, or services available to community groups. The bill authorizes civil actions against school districts for violations (actual damages, equitable/declaratory relief, and attorney’s fees). The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds rules for "released time" courses: students excused to attend such courses are counted as attending their school for purposes of school funding and attendance. School districts may grant academic credit using secular, neutral criteria and must not base credit decisions on religious content. Released time programs and sponsors get equal access to any funds, benefits, or services the school district makes available to community groups. Enforcement allows aggrieved persons/organizations to sue the school district for actual damages, equitable relief, and reasonable attorney fees. No specific new appropriations or agency budget allocations are created; the change affects how student attendance is counted for school funding.
