Summary

LB550 requires Nebraska public school districts to adopt a policy excusing students to attend released‑time courses for religious instruction. For excused periods, students are counted as attending their district for purposes of school funding and compulsory attendance. Districts may award academic credit for such courses only after secular, neutral evaluation comparable to similar courses and without testing religious content or denominational affiliation. Released‑time courses may not be held on district property unless permitted under a neutral equal‑access policy that opens property to community groups. The bill preserves equal access to district funds/benefits for sponsoring entities and allows aggrieved parties to sue the responsible school district for damages, equitable relief, and attorney’s fees. An emergency clause makes the act effective immediately.
