Summary

LB237 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32-960 to permit counties to conduct elections by mail after the county election commissioner or county clerk applies to and receives approval from the Secretary of State. Applications must include a written plan and timetable and provisions for election notices and early-voting ballot applications. Approved counties must follow §§32-953 to 32-959 procedures, with the ballot receipt deadline set by §32-908(2). The election commissioner or county clerk must allow hand-delivery of ballots to their office; provide at least one secure 24-hour ballot drop-box beginning at least ten days before the election through the receipt deadline; maintain at least one in-person voting site at the commissioner’s or clerk’s office open on election day through the receipt deadline; and offer in-person early voting per §32-942. Additional drop-boxes or sites may be provided. The bill repeals the original §32-960. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
