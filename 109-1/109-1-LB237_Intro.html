Summary

LB237 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32-960 to allow a county's election commissioner or county clerk to apply to the Secretary of State to conduct elections by mail for any or all precincts. Applications must include a written election plan and, if approved, follow §§32-953 to 32-959, with the ballot receipt deadline set by §32-908(2). Counties with approved plans must: permit hand delivery of ballots to the election commissioner’s or county clerk’s office; provide at least one secure 24/7 ballot drop-box beginning ≥10 days before the election through the receipt deadline; maintain at least one in-person voting site at the election office open from poll opening through the receipt deadline; and offer in-person early voting per §32-942. The bill repeals the original §32-960. No funding or appropriations are specified.
