Summary

The bill directs the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 of each year beginning October 15, 2026, the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. No provisions in the bill explicitly change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates two new wage schedules the Department of Labor would implement and enforce:

- Youth minimum wage: employers may pay 14–15‑year‑olds $13.50/hour; beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every fifth year thereafter the youth rate rises by 1.5% (rounded to the nearest cent).

- Training wage: set at $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026; beginning Jan 1, 2027 and annually thereafter it increases by 1.5% (rounded to the nearest cent).

No appropriation, funding, or budget allocation language is included in the added text.
