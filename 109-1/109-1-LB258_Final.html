Summary

LB258 assigns two administrative duties to the Nebraska Department of Labor and its Commissioner. Beginning Oct. 15, 2026, and each Oct. 15 thereafter, the Department must calculate and publish the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following Jan. 1. Separately, the Commissioner of Labor must approve on‑the‑job training programs if an employer seeks to extend a new hire’s training‑wage period by an additional 90 days (no more than one‑fourth of total employer hours may be at the training rate). The bill does not include any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds statutory youth and training wage schedules. Employers may pay 14‑ and 15‑year‑old non‑emancipated employees a youth minimum wage of $13.50/hr; that youth wage will increase 1.5% (rounded to nearest cent) beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every five years thereafter. The training wage is set at $13.50/hr through Dec 31, 2026, then will rise 1.5% (rounded to nearest cent) annually starting Jan 1, 2027. The amendment does not specify any Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations or create new financial appropriations; it merely sets wage rates and adjustment schedules (no explicit DOL administrative or budgetary changes are included).
