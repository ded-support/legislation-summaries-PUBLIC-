Summary

The bill directs the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 each year beginning October 15, 2026, the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. No provisions in the bill alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a youth minimum wage and a schedule for the training wage. Key points for the Department of Labor:

- Youth minimum wage: employers may pay $13.50/hour to employees 14–15 years old who are not emancipated. Beginning Jan 1, 2030, and every fifth year thereafter, that youth rate increases by 1.5% (rounded to the nearest cent).

- Training wage: set at $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026. Beginning Jan 1, 2027, and annually thereafter, the training rate increases by 1.5% (rounded to the nearest cent).

- No text provides any changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations; provisions affect wage levels and enforcement/administration responsibilities only.
