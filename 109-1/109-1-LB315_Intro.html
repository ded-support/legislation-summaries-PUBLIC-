Summary

LB315 amends Nebraska corporate statute to make the Secretary of State the central agency responsible for administering biennial reports and occupation taxes for domestic and foreign corporations, but expressly limits (sunsets) those recurring reporting and tax deadlines to periods before January 1, 2026. Duties retained/clarified for the Secretary of State include prescribing report form, receiving reports and occupation taxes, returning nonconforming filings, sending advance notices (mail or electronic), investigating and obtaining information, filing certificates of administrative dissolution or revocation, and maintaining liens for delinquent occupation taxes. The bill preserves rules for tax measurement (including the doubled schedule and $30,000 cap for foreign corporations) and repeals the prior sections. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
