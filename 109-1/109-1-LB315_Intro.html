Summary

LB315 amends Nebraska corporate statutes to impose a sunset on the mandatory biennial report and occupation‑tax regime for domestic and foreign corporations, making the reporting and tax requirements effective only through January 1, 2026. The Secretary of State is the central agency: prescribing report forms, receiving reports and occupation taxes, sending mandated notices to registered agents and foreign corporations, filing reports, returning nonconforming filings, signing and filing certificates of administrative dissolution or revocation, and serving copies. The Secretary may deputize agents to investigate and obtain information (including from state, county, or city officials) to determine tax liability. Delinquent occupation taxes remain liens on corporate assets. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are contained in the bill.
