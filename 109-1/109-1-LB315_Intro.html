Summary

LB315 amends provisions governing biennial reports and occupation taxes for domestic and foreign corporations, adding a sunset for those requirements effective January 1, 2026. The Secretary of State is assigned responsibility to prescribe report forms, collect reports and occupation taxes, send notices, investigate and obtain information (including from state, county, or city officials), and administratively dissolve domestic corporations or revoke foreign corporations’ authority for noncompliance by signing, filing, and serving dissolution/revocation certificates. The existing occupation tax schedule (and the double-rate for foreign corporations, capped at $30,000) is retained. The bill harmonizes statutory language and repeals the prior sections; it contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
