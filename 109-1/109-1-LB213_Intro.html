Summary

Bill LB213 directs the Nebraska State Board of Education to adopt measurable academic content standards for human embryology under the science standards by March 1, 2026, with school integration required by the 2026–27 school year. Required content includes biologically accurate instruction on human embryonic development (including the Carnegie Stages) and use of high‑definition 4D visual recordings showing brain, heart, sex organs, and other vital organ development. The bill preserves a parent/guardian/educational decisionmaker opt‑out. The board must continue adopting and reviewing statewide academic standards (including periodic seven‑year reviews) and the bill repeals and replaces the original statutory section. No departmental funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
