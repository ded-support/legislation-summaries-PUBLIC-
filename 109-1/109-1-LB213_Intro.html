Summary

LB213 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-760.01, directing the State Board of Education to adopt measurable academic content standards (already required for reading, writing, mathematics, science, and social studies) and to: 1) adopt human embryology science standards by March 1, 2026, to be implemented by the 2026-27 school year—including instruction on human development (explicitly citing the Carnegie Stages) and provision of high‑definition 4D visual recordings showing brain, heart, sex organs, and other vital organs; and 2) continue requirements for social studies to include financial literacy and Holocaust/recognized genocide education and for prior computer science standards (due March 1, 2024). The board must review/update standards every seven years, including commonly accepted district standards. Parents/guardians/educational decisionmakers may opt students out of the embryology instruction. The bill takes effect on passage; no funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the board (presumably the state education board) to adopt measurable human embryology academic standards under science standards by March 1, 2026, and integrate them into every school's curriculum by the 2026–27 school year. Required content: accurate biological instruction including the Carnegie Stages of human embryonic development and high‑definition “4D” visual recordings showing development of brain, heart, sex organs and other vital organs. Parents, guardians, or “educational decisionmakers” (per §79‑530) may opt their student out. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
