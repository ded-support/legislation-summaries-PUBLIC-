Summary

The bill amends the criminal background check provisions in section 38-131 and explicitly defines "department" for those provisions as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. Under the amended language, fingerprint-based national criminal history record checks are submitted to the FBI and results are reported to that department; the department and Nebraska State Patrol may adopt rules concerning associated costs. No specific new appropriations, funding amounts, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds Nebraska's adoption of the Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact. Implications for Department of Health and Human Services (as the State Licensing Authority): DHHS would issue/verify qualifying licenses, report licensure, adverse-action, and criminal-background information to the Compact Commission data system, investigate alleged violations, and enforce/adjudicate adverse actions (including revocation or suspension of compact privileges) under Nebraska law. DHHS must monitor the shared database and apply any state jurisprudence requirements. The Compact Commission may establish a budget, charge fees, levy assessments, hire staff, and accept grants — actions that could create costs or assessments for Nebraska. The text includes no explicit appropriations, line-item funding, or mandated budget allocations to DHHS.
