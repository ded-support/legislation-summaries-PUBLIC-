Summary

LB83 amends the criminal-background-check provisions in section 38-131 to require fingerprint submission to the Nebraska State Patrol and FBI, with the Patrol forwarding results and issuing reports to "the department." Applicants must authorize FBI release and pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the check. The bill authorizes the department and the State Patrol to adopt rules on associated costs. For FBI interpretation, "department" is defined as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. No new appropriations, funding allocations, or budgetary changes for the Department of Health and Human Services are provided in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds Nebraska's adoption of the Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact, allowing dentists and hygienists with a qualifying Nebraska license to obtain compact privileges to practice in other member states and vice versa. For the Department of Health and Human Services (as Nebraska’s licensing authority), key impacts: must report licensure, disciplinary, and criminal‑background/check outcomes to the Compact data system; enforce/adjudicate adverse actions (including for compact privileges) and restrict privileges when a Nebraska license is encumbered; participate in Commission rulemaking and compliance monitoring. The Compact creates a multi‑state Commission that may establish a budget, hire staff, charge licensee fees, levy member‑state assessments, and accept grants/donations. No specific state appropriations or dollar amounts are included.
