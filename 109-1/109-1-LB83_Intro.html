Summary

LB83 amends licensure statutes to require criminal background checks for initial health professional licenses (including dentists and dental hygienists). Key provisions referencing the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- Applicants must authorize release of FBI national criminal history record information to the "department."

- The Nebraska State Patrol will forward fingerprints to the FBI and issue a criminal history report to the department.

- The department and Nebraska State Patrol may adopt rules concerning fingerprinting and criminal background check costs; applicants pay the actual costs.

- For FBI interpretation, "department" is defined as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services.

No direct funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to DHHS are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact, creating a multi‑state licensure pathway and a Commission to administer compact privileges, data sharing, rulemaking, investigations, adverse actions, and an online Data System. For Nebraska’s Department of Health and Human Services (as the State Licensing Authority), the bill: requires DHHS to regulate licensees/compact privilege holders, report licensure and disciplinary data to the Commission/Data System, enforce adverse actions and alternative‑program conditions, participate in rulemaking and Commission processes, and comply with monitoring/default procedures. The Commission may charge fees, levy assessments, accept grants/donations, establish a budget, and require State cooperation. No specific appropriations, funding increases, or direct budget allocations to DHHS are included in the added text.
