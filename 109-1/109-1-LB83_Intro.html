Summary

The bill amends the criminal-background-check provision in section 38-131 to require initial-license applicants (including dentists and dental hygienists) to submit fingerprints and to authorize the Federal Bureau of Investigation to release national criminal history record information to "the department." It explicitly defines "the department" for FBI interpretation as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. Applicants must pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the background check; the department and Nebraska State Patrol may adopt related cost rules. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Nebraska adopts the Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact. For the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), acting as the State Licensing Authority, key impacts are:

- DHHS must implement the Compact, issue/monitor qualifying Licenses and Compact Privileges, perform/receive criminal background checks, and report licensure, adverse‑action, and investigative data to the Compact Commission Data System.

- DHHS retains exclusive authority to impose discipline on Nebraska-issued licenses, may take action based on other states’ investigative information, and must prevent licensees in alternative (nonpublic) programs from using Compact Privileges without authorization.

- The Compact Commission will set a budget, charge licensee fees, accept grants/donations, and may levy annual assessments on Participating States; DHHS/Nebraska could therefore incur assessment or administrative costs. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are included.
