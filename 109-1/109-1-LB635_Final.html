Summary

This bill expands the regulatory authority of the State Athletic Commissioner to include professional bare-knuckle mixed martial arts, professional mixed martial arts on ice, professional muay thai, amateur muay thai, and amateur kickboxing. It amends licensing, bonding, and enforcement provisions: promoter license fees set by the commissioner ($100–$300); referee fees $10–$40; matchmaker $10–$100; physicians/managers/judges/timekeepers/contestants $10–$20. Bond minimums are specified ($1,000 for amateur events; $5,000 for professional events). Licensees must report receipts and pay a tax of 5% of gross receipts (including 5% of television rights). The commissioner may adopt rules, limit event numbers, promulgate a banned-substances list, require testing, and impose suspensions/fines for violations (detailed graduated penalties up to lifetime suspension and fines up to $5,000 or 100% of purse). Administrative fines are remitted to the State Treasurer; contested fines follow the Administrative Procedure Act.
