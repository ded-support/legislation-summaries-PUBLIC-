Summary

LB635 expands the State Athletic Commissioner’s regulatory authority to add professional bare‑knuckle mixed martial arts, professional mixed martial arts on ice, professional and amateur muay thai, and amateur kickboxing. The commissioner may adopt rules, set and collect license fees (ranges set for promoters $100–$300; referees $10–$40; matchmakers $10–$100; physicians/managers/judges/timekeepers/contestants $10–$20), require bonds ($1,000 minimum for amateurs; $5,000 minimum for professionals), limit events, and require event reporting. Licensees must pay a tax of 5% of gross receipts (and 5% of television rights) for professional events and 5% for amateur events. The bill authorizes banned‑substance testing, establishes suspension and fine schedules (including tiered fines for positives), requires contested‑case hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act, and remits administrative fines to the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution.
