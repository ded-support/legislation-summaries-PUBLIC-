Summary

LB235 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §76-1446 (Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act). It permits the court to grant additional time for a trial on actions for possession beyond the prior 10–14 day window and retains bench trials (no jury). It narrows early execution of writs of restitution by allowing a writ to be executed fewer than ten days after issuance only if the court finds the defendant poses a true and imminent threat to the safety of other tenants, the landlord, or the landlord’s employees/agents, or if the parties give informed, express agreement. The writ continues to direct the constable or sheriff to restore possession. The plaintiff must follow the Disposition of Personal Property Landlord and Tenant Act and subsection (5) of §76-1414 when removing remaining personal property. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included. The original section is repealed.
