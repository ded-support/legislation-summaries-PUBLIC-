Summary

LB344 amends §46‑740 (groundwater allocation). It retains equal per‑acre irrigation allocations and uniform percentage reductions for acreage rotation, and clarifies that natural resources districts may allow averaging of allocations. The Department of Natural Resources continues to designate areas as fully appropriated (§46‑714) or overappropriated (§46‑713). The bill limits when integrated management plans, rules, or orders may restrict municipal and nonmunicipal commercial/industrial groundwater use in those areas—generally prohibiting such limits before January 1, 2026—and establishes minimum annual allocation bases tied to Industrial Ground Water Regulatory Act permits or the amount necessary for the use (with special treatment for uses <25 million gallons/year). Increases that reduce streamflow must be addressed by integrated management plans under §46‑715. Original section repealed. No appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that allocations to municipalities made before Nov. 1, 2005 remain effective but may be amended by the appropriate natural resources district. It also creates new language addressing allocations made on or after Jan. 1, 2026 for new or expanded commercial or industrial users served by a municipality and indicates such service is subject to controls of a rule or order (agency not specified). No agencies beyond "natural resources district" and municipalities are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
