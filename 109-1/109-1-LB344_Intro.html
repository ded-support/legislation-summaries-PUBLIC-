Summary

LB344 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §46-740 to clarify groundwater allocation in areas the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) has found fully appropriated (§46-714) or designated overappropriated (§46-713). Key agency provisions:

- DNR retains authority to designate fully appropriated/overappropriated areas.

- Natural resources districts (NRDs) may amend municipal allocations made before Nov. 1, 2005.

- Integrated management plans (IMPs), rules, or orders generally may not limit municipal or nonmunicipal commercial/industrial use until Jan. 1, 2026; after that date such users may be limited and base allocations are defined.

- Increases in consumptive use that reduce streamflow must be addressed by IMP controls or incentive programs under §46-715.

- Exemptions remain for users regulated under the Industrial Ground Water Regulatory Act and the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that allocations made on or after January 1, 2026 apply to any new or expanded commercial or industrial user served by a municipality, and confirms prior allocations made before November 1, 2005 remain effective but may be amended by the appropriate natural resources district. The new language also places such municipal users subject to the controls of a rule or order. No specific state agencies or departments beyond "natural resources district" and municipalities are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
