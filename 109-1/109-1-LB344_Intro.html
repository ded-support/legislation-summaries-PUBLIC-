Summary

LB344 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §46-740 regarding groundwater allocation. Key agencies and authorities: the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) retains authority to designate areas as fully appropriated or overappropriated; natural resources districts (NRDs) set allocations and may amend pre‑Nov. 1, 2005 municipal allocations; integrated management plans (IMPs), and controls/incentive programs under §46-715, govern responses to streamflow impacts. The bill bars IMPs, rules, or orders from limiting municipal or nonmunicipal commercial/industrial groundwater use in DNR-designated fully appropriated/overappropriated areas until Jan. 1, 2026, establishes base allocation standards (permit amount under the Industrial Ground Water Regulatory Act or amount necessary for the use, with special treatment for uses <25 million gallons/year), requires IMPs to address consumptive‑use increases that reduce streamflow, and specifies that certain small-use reductions between July 14, 2006, and Jan. 1, 2026, benefit the local NRD. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that municipal water/wastewater allocations made before Nov. 1, 2005 remain in effect but may be amended by the appropriate natural resources district. For allocations made on or after Jan. 1, 2026, it indicates any new or expanded commercial or industrial user served by a municipality is subject to municipal controls (rule or order). Agencies mentioned: natural resources district and municipal authorities. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
