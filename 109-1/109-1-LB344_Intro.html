Summary

LB344 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §46-740 governing ground water allocation and clarifies roles for the Department of Natural Resources and local natural resources districts. Key agency provisions:

- The Department of Natural Resources retains authority to determine areas that are fully appropriated (§46-714) or overappropriated (§46-713). Integrated management plans, rules, or orders interact with those determinations.

- Natural resources districts allocate permissible withdrawals per irrigated acre and may average allocations; reductions or rotations must be uniform unless adjusted for crop consumptive use.

- For nonmunicipal commercial/industrial users, until Jan 1, 2026 minimum annual allocations equal either the Industrial Ground Water Regulatory Act permit amount or the amount needed for the use (including new/expanded uses <25 million gallons/year); after Jan 1, 2026 that base applies and any consumptive increases that reduce streamflow are subject to integrated management controls (§46-715).

- The bill references the Industrial Ground Water Regulatory Act and the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed. The original §46-740 is repealed and replaced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that allocations to municipalities made before Nov. 1, 2005 remain effective but may be amended by the "appropriate natural resources district." For allocations made on or after Jan. 1, 2026, the language addresses new or expanded commercial or industrial users served by a municipality. A new subsection (4)(a) subjects the provision to the controls of a rule or order (agency unspecified). No state agencies beyond natural resources districts are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or referenced.
