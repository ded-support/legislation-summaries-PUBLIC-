Summary

LB125 amends Nebraska law to add and redefine terms (including "nicotine analogue" and "alternative nicotine product"), expand the Tobacco Products Tax Act to cover electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) and alternative nicotine products, and set tax rates: ENDS ≤3 mL at $0.05 per mL; ENDS >3 mL at 10% of purchase/manufacturer price; alternative nicotine products and other non‑cigarette tobacco products at 20% of purchase/manufacturer price; snuff remains $0.44/oz. The bill excludes products regulated by the United States Food and Drug Administration under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. Tax receipts are to be used and distributed pursuant to section 77‑4025; no specific appropriations or new state budget allocations are provided. Operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "nicotine analogue" (substances chemically or neurologically similar to nicotine) and aligns "alternative nicotine product" with section 28-1418.01. Establishes a tax on alternative nicotine products equal to 20% of either (i) the purchase price paid by the first owner or (ii) the price at which a first owner who made, manufactured, or fabricated the product sells it to others. No government agencies, departments, or specific funding/appropriation changes are referenced.
