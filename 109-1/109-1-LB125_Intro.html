Summary

LB125 expands state definitions to include "nicotine analogues" and adds alternative nicotine products and electronic nicotine delivery systems to the Tobacco Products Tax Act. It references the United States Food and Drug Administration for products regulated as drugs or devices (exempting those from the Act). Key tax changes: snuff taxed at $0.44/oz; electronic nicotine delivery systems ≤3 mL taxed $0.05/mL; >3 mL taxed at 10% of the first owner's purchase or sale price; alternative nicotine products taxed at 20% of the first owner's purchase or sale price; other tobacco products taxed at 20%. Tax is imposed on the first owner (with seller liability where sales occur between licensed persons). Revenue collected is to be used and distributed pursuant to section 77-4025. Operative date: January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions define "nicotine analogue" (chemically or pharmacologically similar to nicotine) and incorporate or cross‑reference "Alternative nicotine product" to section 28‑1418.01. They broaden the covered products and establish a tax: a 20% tax on alternative nicotine products, measured either on the purchase price paid by the first owner or the price at which a first owner who made/manufactured/fabricated the product sells it to others.
