Summary

LB125 amends Nebraska law to define and expand regulation of nicotine-related products, including "alternative nicotine products," "electronic nicotine delivery systems," and "nicotine analogue." It excludes products regulated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration from certain state definitions. The bill amends the Tobacco Products Tax Act to impose taxes on first owners: snuff at $0.44/oz; electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) ≤3 mL at $0.05/mL; ENDS >3 mL at 10% of the first owner's purchase/sale price; alternative nicotine products at 20% of first‑owner price; and other tobacco products at 20%. Liability between licensed sellers is addressed per section 77-4009. Revenues are to be used and distributed pursuant to section 77-4025. Operative date: January 1, 2026. No direct appropriations or agency-specific budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions: "nicotine analogue" is a substance with a chemical structure substantially similar to nicotine or with a central‑nervous‑system effect substantially similar to or greater than nicotine. "Alternative nicotine product" is tied to the meaning in section 28‑1418.01. Imposes a tax of 20% on alternative nicotine products, measured either on the purchase price paid by the first owner or the price at which a first owner who made/manufactured/fabricated the product sells it to others. No specific government agencies or departments are mentioned, and no appropriations or budget allocations are included.
