Summary

LB125 expands Nebraska's Tobacco Products Tax Act definitions to include nicotine analogues, "alternative nicotine products," and electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS) containing nicotine or nicotine analogues. It references the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to exclude products regulated as drugs or devices by the FDA from these definitions. Tax changes: snuff remains $0.44/oz; ENDS ≤3 mL taxed $0.05 per mL; ENDS >3 mL taxed 10% of the first owner's purchase price; alternative nicotine products taxed 20% of first-owner purchase price; other tobacco products taxed 20%. Tax is imposed on the first owner; licensed sellers are liable when purchasing from other licensees. Revenues are to be distributed pursuant to section 77-4025. No new appropriations or direct budget allocations are specified. Operative date: January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "nicotine analogue" (chemical structure or CNS effect substantially similar to nicotine, mirroring §28-1418.01) and clarifies "alternative nicotine product" to incorporate nicotine analogues. Establishes a tax on alternative nicotine products equal to 20% of either (i) the purchase price paid by the first owner or (ii) the price at which a first owner who made/manufactured/fabricated the product sells it to others. No government agencies, departments, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
