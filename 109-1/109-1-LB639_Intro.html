Summary

LB639 defines "Department" as the Department of Insurance and "Director" as the Director of Insurance. It requires dental plan carriers to annually submit to the Department, beginning January 1, 2027, a dental loss-ratio report (form and content to be prescribed by the Department) demonstrating compliance with the minimum 85% dental loss ratio. The Department is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to carry out these provisions. The bill grants reporting and rulemaking duties to the Department of Insurance. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates an 85% minimum dental loss ratio effective Jan 1, 2026, and requires dental plan carriers to annually file with the Department of Insurance (beginning Jan 1, 2027) a Department-prescribed report showing incurred claims, earned premiums (net of taxes and regulatory fees), rates, rating schedules and supporting documentation (including historical ratios). If the minimum ratio is not met, carriers must return excess premiums to insureds. The Department of Insurance and the Director are defined parties and are authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement the section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
