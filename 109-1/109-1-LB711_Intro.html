Summary

LB711 amends multiple provisions of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act, reallocating timing and certification responsibilities. The Nebraska Department of Education must determine and certify state aid, school district budget authority, and applicable allowable reserve percentages on or before June 15, 2025, and thereafter on or before March 1 each year; certified aid is reported to the Director of Administrative Services, the Auditor of Public Accounts, local systems, the Governor, and the Legislature’s Appropriations and Education Committees. Prior certifications for FY2025‑26 completed before the bill’s effective date are null and void. The department will approve estimated additional student capacity and administer two‑year new schools adjustments (20% of approved additional capacity × basic funding per formula student); the State Board of Education hears appeals. The Appropriations Committee must include the amount necessary to fund certified state aid in its budget recommendations. The act takes effect immediately.
