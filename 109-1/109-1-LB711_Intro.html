Summary

LB711 amends multiple sections of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act, changing certification and reporting dates and related procedures. Key agencies: the Department of Education (department), State Board of Education, Director of Administrative Services, Auditor of Public Accounts, Governor, and the Legislature’s Appropriations and Education Committees. Major changes: deadlines for the department to determine and certify state aid, school district budget authority, and applicable allowable reserve percentages are set “on or before June 15, 2025, and on or before March 1 each year thereafter.” Certified state aid must be shown as budgeted non‑property‑tax receipts and deducted before calculating local property tax requests; distributions remain ten monthly payments (Sept.–June). Applications and approvals for a two‑year new‑schools adjustment have October 15 and December 1 processing dates. Prior certifications completed before the bill’s effective date for FY2025‑26 are void. The Appropriations Committee must include the necessary funding in its annual recommendation. Emergency clause makes the act effective on passage.
