Summary

• Department of Administrative Services — The bill conditions a transfer of up to $15 million from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Board of Regents for UNMC pancreatic cancer research on receipt of matching private/other funds certified by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services; upon such certification the State Treasurer must transfer an equal amount.

• Department of Health and Human Services — The bill directs an increase of $7,500,000 in FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to DHHS (Program 502) for aid to specified community health centers (Charles Drew, OneWorld, Good Neighbor, Community Action Partnership of Western Nebraska, Nebraska Medicine Midtown, Bluestem, Heartland). Each center receives $700,000; remaining funds distributed proportionally by prior-year uninsured patient counts (Uniform Data System).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: The bill directs that an increase of $7.5 million in each of FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund be transferred to DHHS (Program 502) to provide aid to named community health centers (Charles Drew, OneWorld, East Central/Good Neighbor, Community Action Partnership of Western Nebraska, Nebraska Medicine Midtown, Bluestem Health, Heartland Health). Each center receives $700,000; remaining funds are distributed pro rata based on the prior fiscal year's uninsured patient counts per the Uniform Data System. Funds are for expanded access (e.g., behavioral health, dental, new services/locations, equipment, capital).

Department of Administrative Services: No changes or funding/appropriations affecting DAS are included.
