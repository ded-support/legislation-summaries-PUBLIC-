Summary

LB621 amends §71‑7611 to (1) authorize up to $15,000,000 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to be transferred (on or after July 1, 2022 and before June 30, 2023) to the Board of Regents, University of Nebraska Medical Center for pancreatic cancer research, contingent on receipt of matching funds (up to $15,000,000) certified by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services; upon certification the State Treasurer shall transfer an equal amount. (2) State the Legislature's intent that $7,500,000 in FY2025‑26 and $7,500,000 in FY2026‑27 be transferred from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (Program 502) to aid specified community health centers; each center receives $700,000, with remaining funds distributed proportionately by prior-year uninsured patient counts (Uniform Data System).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: Adds intent that $7,500,000 annually (FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27) be transferred from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to DHHS, Program 502, to aid specified community health centers: Charles Drew Health Center; OneWorld Community Health Centers; East Central District Health Department – Good Neighbor Community Health Center; Community Action Partnership of Western Nebraska; Nebraska Medicine Midtown Health Center; Bluestem Health; and Heartland Health Center. Each center receives $700,000; remaining funds are distributed pro rata based on prior fiscal year uninsured patient counts from the HRSA Uniform Data System. Funds may be used to expand access (e.g., behavioral health, dental), add sites/services, buy equipment, or complete capital projects.

Department of Administrative Services: No changes or appropriations affecting DAS are included.
