Summary

LB621 amends section 71-7611 to (1) direct an increase of $7,500,000 in FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (Program 502) for aid to specified community health centers to expand services (behavioral health, dental, new lines/locations, equipment, capital). Each listed center receives $700,000; remaining funds are distributed proportionately based on the prior fiscal year's uninsured patient counts per the Uniform Data System. (2) Maintain that up to $15,000,000 in transfers from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Board of Regents for UNMC pancreatic cancer research are contingent on matching funds certified by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services; upon such certification the State Treasurer shall transfer an equal amount.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: The bill directs a transfer of $7,500,000 in FY2025‑26 and $7,500,000 in FY2026‑27 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to DHHS (Program 502) to provide aid to specified community health centers (Charles Drew, OneWorld, East Central/Good Neighbor, Community Action Partnership of Western NE, Nebraska Medicine Midtown, Bluestem Health, Heartland Health Center). Each center is to receive $700,000; remaining funds are allocated proportionally based on the prior fiscal year’s uninsured patient counts from the HRSA Uniform Data System. Funds may be used to expand access (e.g., behavioral health, dental), add services/locations, buy equipment, or complete capital projects.
