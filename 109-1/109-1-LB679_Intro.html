Summary

The bill ties annual adjustments of the statutory attorney’s fee awarded in tax-foreclosure decrees to the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, Midwest Region, as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the United States Department of Labor (or its successor agency). Specifically, the fee will be increased beginning January 1, 2027, and each January 1 thereafter by the percentage change in the CPI as measured in August year-over-year. No Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The amendment requires that attorney's fees awarded under the section be increased beginning January 1, 2027, and annually thereafter by the percent change (as of August) in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, Midwest Region, as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the U.S. Department of Labor (or its successor). The Department of Labor is cited only as the CPI publisher; no new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are created.
