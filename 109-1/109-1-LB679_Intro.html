Summary

LB679 ties annual inflation adjustments for the statutory attorney-fee award to the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, Midwest Region, as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the United States Department of Labor (or its successor). The fee baseline ($2,500) is increased January 1, 2027, and each January 1 thereafter by the percentage change in that CPI (August over the prior August). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition allowing counties to conduct "negotiated bulk sale" of multiple tax-delinquent parcels, permit reasonable administrative fees (to be listed on tax certificates), and require removal of parcels sold this way from auction lists. Establishes a baseline reasonable attorney fee of $2,500 and allows courts to award greater amounts as appropriate. The statute directs that the attorney fee awarded will be adjusted upward on Jan 1, 2027, and each Jan 1 thereafter by the percent change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, Midwest Region, as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (U.S. Department of Labor) or successor. No changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.
