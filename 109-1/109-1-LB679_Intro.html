Summary

LB679's only explicit reference to the Department of Labor is that annual adjustments to the statutory baseline attorney's fee (set at $2,500) in tax-foreclosure decrees will be indexed beginning January 1, 2027, and each January thereafter to the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, Midwest Region, as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the U.S. Department of Labor. The bill makes no appropriations, funding changes, or budgetary allocations affecting the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition and rules for "negotiated bulk sale" of tax‑delinquent parcels, allows county boards to set sale terms and a reasonable administrative fee included on tax certificates, and requires removal from auction lists of parcels already sold by negotiated bulk sale. For the Department of Labor connection: attorney fees awarded to plaintiffs will be automatically increased beginning Jan. 1, 2027 and each Jan. 1 thereafter by the year‑over‑year percent change (August-to‑August) in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, Midwest Region, as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the U.S. Department of Labor (or successor). No direct changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.
