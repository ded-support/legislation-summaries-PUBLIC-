Summary

LB303 creates a School Financing Review Commission and amends section 79‑1028.01 (TEEOSA) to revise which district expenditures may be excluded from or added to a district’s general fund budget authority. Agencies and entities named: the Governor (appointing commission members), the Executive Board of the Legislative Council (appointing one member), the State Board of Education (approval authority for exclusions and for demonstrations of net savings from voluntary termination agreements), the Department (responsible for defining distance‑education/telecommunications expenditures for Network Nebraska), the Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council, the School Employees Retirement System of the State of Nebraska, and the Class V School Employees Retirement system. Fiscal changes: employer retirement contributions above baseline rates (7.35% for SERS; 7.37% for Class V) are allowable exclusions; 2024‑25 SERS excess contributions may be carried into 2025‑26 and 2026‑27 budget authority; Network Nebraska, early‑childhood, special grants, special education, and certain voluntary‑termination incentive amounts are clarified as exclusions. Original section repealed; emergency effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds appointment and term rules for a statutory commission: the Executive Board of the Legislative Council will appoint the member described in subdivision (1)(d) in January of odd‑numbered years for two‑year terms (initial appointee within 30 days of the act, serving until a successor in January 2027). Members from subdivisions (1)(a)–(c) serve ex officio. The Governor must appoint members in (1)(e)–(h) within 30 days of the act; initial staggering is specified (some three‑year, some two‑year), thereafter three‑year terms, with gubernatorial appointment to fill vacancies. Fiscal change: for school FY 2025‑26 and 2026‑27, expenditures identified for FY 2024‑25 may be carried over into and included in the general fund budget authority for those years.
