Summary

Creates the School Financing Review Commission (members appointed by the Governor and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council; several members serve ex officio) and establishes appointment terms. The bill repeatedly references the State Board of Education (authority to approve, deny, or modify amounts allowed as exclusions to general fund budget authority; to determine elementary site allowance; to review demonstrations of net savings for termination incentives) and the department (definitions for distance education/telecommunications and Network Nebraska allowances). Other agencies/systems cited: Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council, School Employees Retirement System of the State of Nebraska, the retirement system under the Class V School Employees Retirement Act, the Commission of Industrial Relations, and Emergency Management Act disaster declarations. Budget changes: permits carryover of certain employer retirement contribution expenditures from FY2024‑25 into FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27 and allows districts to exceed general fund budget authority for specified exclusions (telecommunications/Network Nebraska first‑year costs, new elementary sites, early childhood grants, various retirement/termination incentive treatments, special education, special grants, federal impact aid). Emergency clause makes the act effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds appointment and term rules for a commission: the Executive Board of the Legislative Council will appoint the member described in subdivision (1)(d) in January of each odd-numbered year for two-year terms (initial appointee to be appointed within 30 days after the act and serve until a successor in January 2027). Members from subdivisions (1)(a)–(c) serve ex officio. The Governor must appoint members listed in (1)(e)–(h) within 30 days after the act; initial terms begin 30 days after the act with specified staggered lengths, thereafter three-year terms and gubernatorial appointment to fill vacancies. Funding change: for school fiscal years 2025–26 and 2026–27, expenditures from school FY 2024–25 described in (1)(e)(i) may be carried over and included in the general fund budget authority for those years.
