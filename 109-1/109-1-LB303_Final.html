Summary

LB303 creates a School Financing Review Commission (members appointed by the Governor and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council, with specified ex officio positions) and amends §79-1028.01 governing exclusions from and the ability to exceed general fund budget authority under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. The State Board of Education retains authority to approve, deny, or modify exclusion amounts; the department (for distance education allowance) defines telecommunications expenditures. Key budget provisions: employer contributions to the School Employees Retirement System above a 7.35% baseline (and Class V retirement contributions above 7.37%) are excluded from budget authority; FY2024‑25 employer contribution amounts may be carried into FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27 budgets. Other exclusions address Network Nebraska costs, early childhood grants, new elementary sites, special education, grant funds, and federal impact aid. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds membership, appointment, and term rules for a commission: appointments include at least one person with farming experience, one currently certified public school teacher, and two other members; certain appointees may be retired. The Executive Board of the Legislative Council will appoint the (1)(d) member in January of odd-numbered years (two-year terms); members in (1)(a)–(c) serve ex officio; the Governor appoints members in (1)(e)–(h) within 30 days of the act, with staggered initial terms and three-year terms thereafter. Budget change: authorizes carryover of the expenditures described in (1)(e)(i) from school fiscal year 2024–25 into general fund budget authority for school fiscal years 2025–26 and 2026–27 (no specific dollar amounts provided).
