Summary

LB303 amends §79-1028.01, creates a School Financing Review Commission (membership appointed by the Governor and by the Executive Board of the Legislative Council; certain members serve ex officio), and revises when and how school districts may exceed their general fund budget authority under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. The State Board of Education must approve, deny, or modify exclusion amounts. The Department of Education defines Network Nebraska telecommunications expenditures used in an exclusion for a district’s first full year of participation. The bill specifies exclusions (e.g., disaster repairs, judgments, special education, special grants, federal impact aid) and retirement-related employer contributions to the School Employees Retirement System and the Class V School Employees Retirement Act; it allows carryover of 2024‑25 employer contribution expenditures into budget authority for FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27. Original §79‑1028.01 is repealed; the act takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- The Executive Board of the Legislative Council will appoint the member described in subdivision (1)(d) each January of odd-numbered years for two-year terms; the initial appointment must occur within 30 days after the act takes effect and will serve until a successor in January 2027.

- Members in subdivisions (1)(a)–(c) serve ex officio on the commission.

- The Governor must appoint members in subdivisions (1)(e)–(h) within 30 days after the act’s effective date; initial terms begin 30 days after that date, are staggered (some two-year, some three-year initial terms), thereafter three-year terms, and vacancies are filled by the Governor.

- Budget change: for school fiscal years 2025–26 and 2026–27, expenditures made in school FY 2024–25 described in subdivision (1)(e)(i) may be carried over and included in general fund budget authority for those two subsequent school years.
