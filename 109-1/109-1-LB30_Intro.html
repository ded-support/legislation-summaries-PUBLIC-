Summary

LB30 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to allow individuals (for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2025) to subtract income received as overtime compensation paid under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act from federal adjusted gross income. The bill modifies existing income-tax adjustment provisions in that statute.

Government entities/programs referenced include the Tax Commissioner (Nebraska Department of Revenue), the Nebraska educational savings plan trust, the Achieving a Better Life Experience program, the Teach in Nebraska Today Act, the Firefighter Cancer Benefits Act, the Medical Debt Relief Fund, Segal AmeriCorps Education Awards, the Nebraska National Guard, the U.S. Department of Defense, and the U.S. Office of Personnel Management. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are contained in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state tax subtraction, effective for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2025: individuals may subtract from their federal adjusted gross income any overtime compensation paid under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act (to the extent that overtime is included in federal AGI). The text references the Internal Revenue Code and the FLSA. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
