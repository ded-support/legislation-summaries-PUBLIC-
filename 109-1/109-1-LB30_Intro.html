Summary

LB30 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77‑2716 to add a tax subtraction for individuals: for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2025, income from overtime compensation paid under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act is subtracted from federal adjusted gross income. The bill leaves in place numerous existing provisions referencing state and federal entities, including the Tax Commissioner (rulemaking/adjustment authority), the Nebraska Educational Savings Plan Trust (sections 85‑1801–85‑1817), ABLE accounts (77‑1401–77‑1409), the Nebraska long‑term care savings plan, the Medical Debt Relief Fund, the Nebraska National Guard (including 32 U.S.C. duty statuses and federal dual‑status technicians), and federal agencies (U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Office of Personnel Management). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan. 1, 2025, individuals may subtract from their federal adjusted gross income (as defined by the Internal Revenue Code) any overtime compensation they received that is paid under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act. The provision references the federal tax code and the FLSA (administered by the IRS and U.S. Department of Labor, respectively). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
