Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and charges DHHS with establishing the Foster Child Scholarship Program to provide HOPE Scholarships beginning in the 2025–26 school year. DHHS must annually notify educational decisionmakers to increase awareness, may adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act, and must submit an electronic report to the Governor and Legislature by December 1, 2026, and annually thereafter with enrollment counts, total awards, and recipient demographics. The Legislature intends to appropriate $200,000 from the General Fund for each of fiscal years 2025–26 and 2026–27 to DHHS to carry out the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions designate the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to establish the Foster Child Scholarship Program (HOPE Scholarships) beginning in 2025–26 for foster children and their biological siblings to attend qualifying private nonprofit in-state schools. DHHS must: administer eligibility and retention rules (until high school graduation or age 21); annually notify educational decisionmakers of the program; adopt rules and regulations; and submit an annual electronic report (due Dec. 1 beginning 2026) detailing number of recipients, total awards, and recipient demographics (income, grade, race/ethnicity, geography). The bill forbids program provisions from altering state control over participating schools. The Legislature intends to appropriate $200,000 from the General Fund to DHHS for each of FY2025–26 and FY2026–27.
