Summary

LB32 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §49-1474.01 to clarify disclaimer requirements for political materials. Persons who pay for printed campaign materials must include their name and street address; candidates who pay must include name and street address or a post office box. Radio and television ads must include the payer’s name; stations must keep the payer’s street address or PO box on file for at least six months and divulge it on request. The bill directs “the commission” to adopt rules on disclaimer size and placement and to exempt items (e.g., windshield stickers, yard signs, bumper stickers, campaign buttons, balloons). Violations are Class IV misdemeanors. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds candidate disclaimer requirements: except for individuals using personal resources (or as otherwise allowed), candidates must place their name and street address or PO box on printed campaign materials; radio/TV ads must include the candidate’s name, and the station must retain the candidate’s address on file for at least six months and provide it on request. No government agencies or departments are mentioned and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
