Summary

LB609 enacts the Controllable Electronic Record Fraud Prevention Act and imposes regulatory duties on controllable electronic record kiosk operators, including blockchain analytics, written antifraud policies, a full‑time compliance officer, transaction limits, mandatory disclosures and receipts, live customer service hours, and a dedicated law‑enforcement contact. The bill authorizes "the department" to request proof of analytics use and to require additional receipt information. The Consumer Protection Division of the Office of the Attorney General must publish model notice language for gift‑card fraud warnings; the Attorney General may issue written citations and assess civil penalties up to $250 after multiple citations. Section 29‑817 is amended to include "controllable electronic records" in the statutory definition of property for search‑warrant and seizure provisions. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
