Summary

LB609 creates the Controllable Electronic Record Fraud Prevention Act and imposes regulatory requirements on operators of controllable electronic record kiosks. Relevant government entities and duties:

- Consumer Protection Division, Office of the Attorney General: must publish model notice language for gift‑card fraud warnings; may issue written citations for violations and assess civil penalties up to $250 after an initial warning.

- "The department" (as used in the bill): may require additional receipt disclosures and may request evidence that kiosk operators use blockchain analytics to prevent fraud.

- State and local law enforcement agencies and other government agencies: must be listed on kiosk displays and transaction receipts as contacts for reporting fraud; law enforcement has an established dedicated contact requirement for operators.

- Courts and law enforcement: section 29‑817 is amended to explicitly include "controllable electronic records" within the definition of property for search‑warrant provisions.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds multiple obligations involving unspecified "the department," state and local law enforcement, and the Attorney General's office. Key agency points:

- "The department" may require additional disclosures on kiosks/receipts and may request evidence that operators use blockchain analytics.

- Kiosk operators must display contact information for relevant state or local law enforcement and other government agencies for reporting fraud; receipts must list relevant state law enforcement and government agencies. Operators also must maintain a daily-monitored law-enforcement contact method.

- The Consumer Protection Division of the Office of the Attorney General must publish model notice language for gift‑card fraud warnings; the Attorney General may issue citations and impose civil penalties (up to $250 after multiple citations).
