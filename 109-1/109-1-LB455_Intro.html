Summary

LB455 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-144.01 to make employer/insurer injury reports filed with the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court confidential and not subject to public inspection, while prescribing limited exceptions. The Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court (and its administrator) remains the filing and access control authority; the administrator will prescribe forms for employee waivers and inspection requests. An employee may waive confidentiality (waiver stays in effect across employers unless revoked). Permitted disclosures include the employee (or authorized attorney/agent), the employer/insurer/risk pool/third-party administrator (or their attorneys/agents) with written authorization, attorneys involved in claims or litigation, state or federal investigators or statistical compilations, redacted employer injury counts, pleadings/exhibits filed with the court, nonprofit condolence uses for workplace deaths, or by court order. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds confidentiality rules for reports filed with the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court. Key agency references and duties:

- Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court: reports are confidential except as provided; the court may deny inspection unless an exception applies.

- Administrator of the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court: prescribes the form and manner for employees to waive confidentiality and for making inspection/copy requests.

- State or federal government: may obtain reports for investigations, examinations, or to compile statistical information.

Permitted disclosures include when the employee waives confidentiality, the employee (or their attorney/agent), the employer/insurer/risk pool/TPA (or their counsel/agent), certain insurers'/TPAs' attorneys handling related claims, court filings/exhibits, redacted employer injury statistics, nonprofit grief outreach, or by court order. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
