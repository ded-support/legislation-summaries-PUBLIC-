Summary

LB455 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-144.01 to require employers or workers’ compensation insurers to file reportable injury reports with the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court within ten days of notice or knowledge. The bill makes such reports confidential and not open to public inspection except as specified. It authorizes the Court (and its administrator, who prescribes forms and procedures) to disclose reports to the employee (or their counsel/agent), the employer/insurer/risk pool/third-party administrator (or their counsel/agent), attorneys involved in related claims or litigation, state or federal agencies for investigations/examinations or statistical compilation, and in limited other circumstances (e.g., pleadings, court order, redacted employer-level requests). No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds confidentiality rules for reports filed under subsection (1), designating them confidential except as needed by the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court to administer/enforce the Act. The employee may waive confidentiality via a form prescribed by the court administrator; the waiver persists until revoked. The Compensation Court must deny inspection requests unless the employee waived confidentiality or one of specified exceptions applies, including: the employee (or authorized attorney/agent); the employer, insurer, risk-management pool, or third‑party administrator party to the report (or their counsel/agents); attorneys handling related claims or litigation; state or federal investigations or statistical compilations; redacted employer-injury summaries; court filings/exhibits; nonprofits contacting family for condolences/grief services; or a court order. All requests must follow the form/manner prescribed by the court administrator. No funding or appropriation changes.
