Summary

LB455 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-144.01 to require employers or workers’ compensation insurers to file reportable injury reports with the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court within ten days and declares those reports confidential. The Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court (and its administrator) controls access: the administrator prescribes forms for employee waivers and requests, and the court must deny inspection unless an enumerated exception applies (employee waiver; employee’s or employer/insurer/TPA and counsel access with authorization; state or federal investigations/statistical use; redacted aggregated employer injury data; pleadings/exhibits; nonprofit condolence use; or court order). The bill repeals the original section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds confidentiality rules for reports to the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court: reports are confidential except as needed for the Court to administer/enforce the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act. Employees may waive confidentiality (or later revoke) using forms prescribed by the Court’s administrator. The Court must deny inspection requests unless the employee waived or the requester is: the employee (or their attorney/agent); the employer, insurer, risk management pool, or third‑party administrator party to the report (or their attorney/agent); attorneys/agents handling related claims or lawsuits; state or federal government for investigations/examinations or statistical compilation; requesters seeking aggregated employer injury data if the Court redacts employee identities; pleadings/exhibits filed with the Court; nonprofits for condolences/grief services after a workplace death; or by court order. Requests must follow the form/manner the Court administrator prescribes. No funding or appropriation changes.
