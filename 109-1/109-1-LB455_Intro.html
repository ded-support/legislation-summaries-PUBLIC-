Summary

LB455 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-144.01 (Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Act). It requires employers or workers’ compensation insurers to file reportable injury reports with the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court within ten days of notice/knowledge, and makes those reports confidential. The bill specifies the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court (and its administrator) as the custodian, prescribes forms/manner for filings and requests, and limits disclosure except when: the employee waives confidentiality; the requester is the subject employee (or their attorney/agent), the employer/insurer/risk pool/third-party administrator party to the report (or their counsel/agent), parties managing related claims or litigation, state or federal investigators compiling statistics, redacted employer-injury tallies, pleadings/exhibits, nonprofit grief outreach, or by court order. The original section is repealed. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds confidentiality rules for reports filed with the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court: reports are confidential except as needed for the Court to administer/enforce the Workers' Compensation Act. An employee may waive confidentiality; that waiver (or revocation) must follow a form prescribed by the administrator of the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court and remains effective across job changes until revoked. The Court must deny inspection requests unless the employee waived confidentiality or one of specified exceptions applies, including requests by the employee (or their attorney/authorized agent), the employer/insurer/risk management pool/third‑party administrator (or their attorneys/agents), attorneys handling related claims or lawsuits, state or federal investigators compiling statistics, requests to identify employer injury counts with employee redaction, pleadings/exhibits filed with the Court, nonprofits providing condolences/grief services, or by court order. All inspection requests must follow the administrator’s prescribed form. No funding or appropriation changes.
