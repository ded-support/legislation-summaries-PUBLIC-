Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates funds to implement Legislative Bill 644 (109th Leg., 1st Sess., 2025):

- Attorney General (Program 507): $30,000 from the State Settlement Cash Fund for FY2025-26; $0 for FY2026-27. Funds designated to aid carrying out LB644.

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (Program 94): $50,000 from the General Fund for FY2025-26 and $50,000 from the General Fund for FY2026-27 to aid carrying out LB644.

Both appropriations prohibit using these funds for permanent or temporary state employee salaries or per diems.
