Summary

LB644A appropriates funds to implement Legislative Bill 644. It provides the Attorney General (Program 507) $30,000 from the State Settlement Cash Fund for FY2025‑26 (and $0 for FY2026‑27). It provides the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (Program 94) $50,000 from the General Fund for FY2025‑26 and $50,000 from the General Fund for FY2026‑27. Both appropriations include a prohibition on using the funds for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates funding to implement LB 644:

- Attorney General (Program 507): $30,000 from the State Settlement Cash Fund for FY2025-26 and $0 for FY2026-27 to assist in carrying out LB 644. Funds may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (Program 94): $50,000 from the General Fund for FY2025-26 and $50,000 from the General Fund for FY2026-27 to assist in carrying out LB 644. Funds may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.
