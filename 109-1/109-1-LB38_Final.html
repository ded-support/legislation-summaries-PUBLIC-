Summary

LB38 extensively revises the Geologists Regulation Act and the Board of Geologists' authorities and procedures. Key agency provisions:

- Board of Geologists: seven members appointed by the Governor (five professional geologists, one Nebraska geology faculty recommended by a college president, one public member); five-year terms; may designate emeritus members; may adopt rules, code of practice, exams (including Nebraska‑specific exam), and publish specifications; may use Attorney General services or hire counsel; must notify the Secretary of State of license revocations/suspensions/probations.

- Secretary of State: prohibited from issuing certificates of authority, trade-name/service-mark registrations, or business certificates for entities practicing geology unless the board has issued a certificate of authorization or eligibility letter.

- Nebraska Oil and Gas Conservation Commission: statute preserves right to testimony before the Commission.

- National Association of State Boards of Geology: board may accept verified NASBG records in lieu of board forms and authorize attendance at NASBG meetings.

Fees and fiscal provisions:

- Board sets fees within caps: licensure ≤ $300, geologist‑intern enrollment ≤ $100, organizational certificate ≤ $300, emeritus ≤ $100; board may add an administrative collection fee ≤ $100; examination fees set to recover costs.

- Board members serve without compensation but are reimbursed for travel/expenses per sections 81‑1174 to 81‑1177. No direct appropriations or budget allocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies provisions for the state board regulating geologists (the board): composition (five professional geologists, one college/university geology faculty member recommended by the institution president, and one public member), five‑year terms, U.S. citizenship and Nebraska residency, and professional experience/licensure requirements for geologist members. The board may set exams, adopt National Association of State Boards of Geology standards, establish guidelines for references and experience, prepare a Nebraska‑specific statutes exam, and admit/enroll applicants, interns, temporary permits, and out‑of‑state licensees under specified criteria.

Fiscal authorities: the board may charge nonrefundable application/renewal/certificate fees, impose an additional administrative collection fee up to $100, charge for distributing rosters under public‑records fees (84‑712 series), and may waive a first reexamination fee. Emeritus members may be reimbursed per sections 81‑1174 to 81‑1177. No direct appropriations or new budget allocations are provided.
