Summary

LB38 amends Nebraska's Geologists Regulation Act to clarify administration and authority of the Board of Geologists (seven Governor‑appointed members, five‑year terms, eligibility and reimbursement per state law). The board's powers include licensing, issuing seals, certificates of licensure and certificates of authorization to organizations, temporary permits, Nebraska‑specific examinations, roster maintenance, rulemaking, enforcement, and accepting verified records from the National Association of State Boards of Geology. The Attorney General is designated legal advisor; the board may employ counsel. The Secretary of State is prohibited from issuing business certificates or registering trade names containing "geology" unless the board has issued a certificate of authorization or eligibility letter. Fee caps are established: individual licensure up to $300, geologist‑intern enrollment $100, organizational authorization $300, emeritus status $100, plus an administrative collection fee up to $100 and potential roster fees. No appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies provisions under the Geologists Regulation Act establishing the state board’s composition, powers, fee authority, and licensure processes. Key government actors: the Board (appointments made by the Governor; one board seat must be a Nebraska college/university geology faculty member recommended by that institution’s president), Nebraska state statutes referenced for member reimbursement (secs. 81-1174 to 81-1177) and public records fees (secs. 84-712 to 84-712.09). The board may issue certificates of authorization to organizations, adopt exams/standards (including those of the National Association of State Boards of Geology), set application/renewal fees, charge an extra administrative fee up to $100, charge for roster copies, and may waive a first reexamination fee. No state appropriations or other budget allocations are added.
