Summary

LB38 revises the Geologists Regulation Act. It restructures the Board of Geologists (seven governor‑appointed members, qualifications, emeritus status), affirms the Governor's appointment/removal role, and clarifies the board's rulemaking, licensing, examination, and enforcement powers. The bill requires the Secretary of State to withhold corporate certificates, trade name registrations, or business authority that use "geology" unless the board issues a certificate of authorization or eligibility letter. The Attorney General remains legal advisor; the board may employ counsel and use records/exams of the National Association of State Boards of Geology. It preserves testimony before the Nebraska Oil and Gas Conservation Commission. Fee provisions set maximums (licensure ≤ $300; geologist‑intern ≤ $100; certificate of authorization ≤ $300; emeritus ≤ $100; administrative collection fee ≤ $100) and authorize roster/distribution fees. Several statutory sections are repealed. No appropriations or budget reallocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds comprehensive provisions to implement the Geologists Regulation Act, establishing board composition, qualifications, duties, examinations, licensure pathways, and procedural rules. Key agency/actor items:

- A state licensing board (appointments by the Governor; one member must be a Nebraska college/university geology faculty member recommended by that institution’s president).

- References to state statutes for member reimbursement (secs. 81-1174 to 81-1177) and public-record fee authority (secs. 84-712 to 84-712.09).

- Authority to adopt National Association of State Boards of Geology exams/guidelines.

Board composition/qualifications: five professional geologists, one academic geologist, one public member; residency, citizenship, experience, and licensing requirements specified. Licensing/permit rules: education, exam (fundamentals, practice, Nebraska statutes), experience, reciprocity, temporary permits, and emeritus rules. Fee provisions: nonrefundable application/renewal/certificate fees; board may add an administrative charge up to $100 and may waive first reexamination fee. No direct appropriations or budget allocations included.
