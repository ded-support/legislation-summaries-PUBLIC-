Summary

LB489 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-1012 to limit activity around higher‑voltage electric facilities to entities defined as "electric supplier" in §70-1001.01 and to require board approval before such an electric supplier constructs or acquires electric generation facilities or transmission lines/facilities carrying more than 700 volts. Existing exemptions (intra‑service‑area extensions, limited replacement generation, acquisition of lines ≤115 kV with notice, and qualified facilities) are retained. The bill also states a prohibition on certain (unspecified in the excerpt) entities from operating, maintaining, constructing, or acquiring transmission lines and related facilities. No state agency appropriations or other funding changes are included.
