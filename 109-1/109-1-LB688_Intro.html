Summary

LB688 makes it unlawful for any person to advertise an "electronic nicotine delivery system" in Nebraska, using the definition already established in Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-1418.01. The bill contains no references to any specific state agencies or departments, and it includes no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition reference and a statewide ban on advertising electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS): ENDS is defined by referral to section 28‑1418.01, and it becomes unlawful to advertise any ENDS in Nebraska. No government agencies or departments are named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
