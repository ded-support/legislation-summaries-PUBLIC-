Summary

LB251 designates the Department of Banking and Finance as the state regulator across multiple securities, consumer‑finance, and credit‑union provisions and vests the Director with rulemaking authority. The Director gains emergency powers (including possession, conversion, merger, or transfer of charters, assets, liabilities, or branches) to facilitate acquisition of troubled institutions. The Department is named licensing, notice, and enforcement authority for credit unions, loan brokers, money‑transmitter exemptions, consumer protections (including suspected financial exploitation), and certain investment approvals. ATM “switch” operators must file initial and annual notices with the Department (the Department will not regulate switch fees). Banks may establish school savings programs upon notification. Franchise sellers to Nebraska must file a pre‑offer notice and pay a $100 fee; those fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Securities Act Cash Fund, and the Department may request disclosures, deny/revoke exemptions, and act summarily. The Director of Insurance is given rule authority and sole regulation over funding agreements; insurers regulated by DOI are referenced in certain loan‑rate exemptions. No other funding or appropriation changes are specified.
