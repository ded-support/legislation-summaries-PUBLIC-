Summary

LB251 clarifies and shifts regulatory authority for the Department of Banking and Finance and the Department of Insurance. It defines “Department”/“Director” as the Department of Banking and Finance for securities and consumer‑credit provisions; authorizes the Director to adopt rules implementing insider‑credit limits (sec. 8‑143.01) consistent with 12 U.S.C. 84/Regulation O (as of Jan 1, 2025); expands the Director’s emergency powers to effect conversions, mergers, transfers or possession to facilitate acquisition of Nebraska‑chartered financial institutions with post‑action hearing and appeal rights; assigns notice/publication duties, credit‑union application processing, receipt of suspected‑exploitation reports, and approval of additional credit‑union investments to the Department; requires franchise sellers to file a $100 notice with the Department (fees remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Securities Act Cash Fund); and defines the Department in the Consumer Rental Purchase Agreement Act. The Director of Insurance gains rulemaking and sole regulatory authority over issuance, sale, and sellers of funding agreements; insurer loans are noted as exemptions. No new appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Banking and Finance: Adds procedures and limits the director enforces. Entities with pending bank, trust company, or credit union applications may temporarily use protected words (bank, trust, credit union, etc.) but must stop within 30 days if the application is withdrawn or denied. To qualify for an additional 10% lending limit, banks must perfect security interests in livestock or receipts and maintain collateral value at least 115% of the secured amount above statutory thresholds. Applicant banks and credit unions must submit, in a director-prescribed form, an affidavit of publication and proof of payment for required notices. Several federal/regulatory references are fixed as of January 1, 2025.
