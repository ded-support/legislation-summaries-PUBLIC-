Summary

LB416 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §25-21,185.11 to clarify the effect of releases, covenants not to sue, or similar agreements. Key changes: a release discharges the released party from liability to the claimant but reduces the claimant’s claim against other liable parties by the released party’s share as determined by the trier of fact; a released party may not be made or remain a party to the action (or may be dismissed), though that party’s negligence remains relevant under §25-21,185.09; and releases do not abrogate joint-and-several liability of remaining defendants. The original section is repealed. No state agencies or departments are referenced, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection clarifying that a release, covenant not to sue, or similar agreement between a claimant and one or more liable parties does not eliminate the joint and several liability of any other person liable on the same claim. No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
