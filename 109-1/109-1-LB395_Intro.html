Summary

LB395 amends juvenile record‑sealing statutes to impose specific duties for three agencies:

- Department of Motor Vehicles: courts must send notice of a sealing order to the DMV when the sealed record includes impoundment or a prohibition to obtain a license or permit under §43‑287. Sealing orders must include contact information for each government agency subject to the order.

- Department of Health and Human Services: courts must notify DHHS if the juvenile was a ward or DHHS was a party. DHHS may access sealed records for intake, case plans, evaluations, federal reporting, supervision/protection of persons placed with the department, and for specified licensing/certification purposes, and may release information as needed to perform those duties.

- Department of Labor: required to create a website link informing employers they may not ask about sealed records and that employment applications must state applicants are not obligated to disclose sealed records.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two provisions: (1) when a juvenile record is sealed upon termination of probation, the sealing explanation must inform the juvenile they are prohibited from possessing a firearm under §28‑1204.05; (2) authorizes law enforcement to check whether applicants under age 25 have prior juvenile adjudications for felonies or misdemeanor domestic violence when applying for concealed‑handgun permits or certificates to buy/transfer handguns. No budget, funding, or appropriation changes. Department of Labor: no direct effect. Department of Health and Human Services: possible minor administrative role if it or its contractors handle juvenile‑record notifications, but no funding change. Department of Motor Vehicles: no direct effect.
