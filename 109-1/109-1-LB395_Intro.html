Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: court must be sent notice to seal a juvenile record if the record includes impoundment or a prohibition to obtain a license or permit under section 43-287.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): court must be sent notice if the juvenile was a ward of the state or DHHS was a party; DHHS is authorized access to sealed records for juvenile intake, case plans, evaluations, federal reporting, supervision/protection of persons placed with the department, and for licensing/certification under child care statutes; DHHS may release sealed-record information as necessary to perform those duties.

- Department of Labor: required to develop a webpage link informing employers they may not ask applicants about sealed records and that employment applications must state applicants are not obligated to disclose sealed records.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two points: (1) when a juvenile offense has been sealed at probation termination, the juvenile must be informed they are prohibited from possessing a firearm under §28-1204.05; and (2) authorizes law enforcement to check sealed juvenile adjudications for applicants under 25 applying for a concealed handgun permit or a handgun purchase/transfer certificate to determine prior felony or misdemeanor domestic‑violence adjudications. No references to the Department of Labor, Department of Health and Human Services, or Department of Motor Vehicles; no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
