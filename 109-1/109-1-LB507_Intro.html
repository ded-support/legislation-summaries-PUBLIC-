Summary

LB507 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-1129. It directs resident school boards to provide specified services for children with disabilities, including: (a) transportation expenses when a child must temporarily leave the district due to lack of services; (b) transportation when a child must attend a facility other than the normal attendance facility or requires special transportation; (c) certified visiting teachers for homebound children; (d) correspondence instruction; or (e) other instructional methods approved by the Commissioner of Education. Parent/guardian drivers are to be reimbursed at the mileage rate in §81-1176 for actual miles traveled, with payments calculated for one child if multiple children are transported and no payment for mileage not actually driven. Nonresident and resident districts may agree that the nonresident district provide transportation for preschool/child-care enrollees. The bill repeals the original section. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
