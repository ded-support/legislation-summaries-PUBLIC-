Summary

LB507 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-1129 to specify services resident school districts must provide for children with disabilities. Responsibilities fall to the school board and include: reimbursing parents/guardians for transportation at the mileage rate in §81-1176 (for actual miles; when one parent transports multiple eligible children payment is based on one child; no payment for untraveled miles); providing transportation when a child must attend an out-of‑district facility or requires special transportation; supplying certified visiting teachers for homebound children; and offering correspondence or other instructional methods approved by the Commissioner of Education. The nonresident school district may provide transportation by mutual agreement with the district of residence. The bill references reimbursement tied to the statutory mileage rate but contains no separate appropriations or new funding allocations.
