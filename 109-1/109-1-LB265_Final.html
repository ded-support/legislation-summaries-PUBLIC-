Summary

The bill restructures workforce and budget flows affecting DAS, DED, DOL, and DHHS. Department of Labor (DOL) creates and will administer the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund (WDPCF); the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund, Nebraska Training and Support Cash Fund, and Sector Partnership Program Fund terminate July 1, 2025 with balances moved into WDPCF, and state UI tax collections will be paid into WDPCF thereafter. The DOL Commissioner sets the UI tax rate; DOL may award up to $250,000 in Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development pilot grants and administer workforce grant functions. Department of Economic Development retains control of the Job Training Cash Fund and may receive Cash Reserve Fund transfers with specified program priorities. The Department of Administrative Services budget administrator directs timing/amounts of numerous fund transfers (examples: $440 million GF→Cash Reserve, $29.4588 million→Capital Construction). The bill directs transfers totaling $14,820,000 to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund and a $2,950,000 transfer from WDPCF to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: The budget administrator in DAS is authorized to direct timed transfers: $2,950,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the General Fund between 7/1/2025 and 6/30/2026, and $5,000,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to the General Fund between 7/1/2026 and 6/30/2027.

Department of Economic Development: Beginning 10/1/2025–11/1/2026 the department may accept applications for Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot grants (up to $50,000 per award, matching private funds required). Projects begun before application are ineligible; recipients must repay if they cease to qualify. Dept. may adopt rules. Total pilot awards capped at $250,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund.

Department of Labor: The Workforce Development Program Cash Fund may be used for grants, administration, unemployment benefits, studies, and the pilot. The Nebraska Workforce Development Board may submit up to 18 proposals/year; the commissioner may approve/modify and may unilaterally approve up to three annually with reporting triggers.

Department of Health and Human Services: No substantive changes or funding actions identified.
