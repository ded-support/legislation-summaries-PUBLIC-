Summary

The bill centralizes workforce/unemployment funding under the Department of Labor (DOL): the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund, Nebraska Training and Support Cash Fund, and Sector Partnership Program Fund terminate July 1, 2025, with balances and future state UI tax receipts redirected into a new Workforce Development Program Cash Fund administered by DOL. The commissioner sets UI tax rates; the fund may finance workforce grants, administration, unemployment benefits, studies, and a Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot (pilot grants up to $250,000). The final average combined tax rate is reduced 5% for 2025–2029. Sec. 20 directs a $2,950,000 transfer from the Workforce Fund to the General Fund. The Department of Economic Development (DED) assumes the Job Training Cash Fund and it may receive Cash Reserve Fund transfers; DAS is directed to transfer $20,000,000 to the Economic Development Cash Fund. The Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget administrator schedules multiple interfund transfers, including $440,000,000 GF→Cash Reserve Fund and transfers totaling $14,820,000 to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) Cash Fund; DHHS is identified as hospital licensing authority.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Economic Development: creates a Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot (applications Oct 1, 2025–Nov 1, 2026). Eligible manufacturers must document project, cost, and private matching funds at least equal to the grant. Grants equal the private match, capped at $50,000 per applicant; total pilot awards capped at $250,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund. Grants must not fund projects begun before application; repayment required if eligibility lapses. Department may adopt rules.

- Department of Labor: clarifies permitted uses of the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund (including the pilot, grants, admin, unemployment tax/benefit costs, and labor studies). Nebraska Workforce Development Board may submit up to 18 proposals yearly to the Commissioner, who may approve/reject/modify and may approve up to 3 without board approval (reporting required if approvals exceed 25% of unobligated balance).

- Department of Administrative Services: budget administrator directs timing/amounts of transfers — $2,950,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the General Fund (FY2025‑26) and $5,000,000 from Cash Reserve to the General Fund (FY2026‑27).

- Department of Health and Human Services: no substantive changes or funding actions referenced.
