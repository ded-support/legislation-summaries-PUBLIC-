Summary

The bill assigns agency responsibilities and directs multiple fund transfers under Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget-administrator authority. DAS’s budget administrator may set dates/amounts for numerous State Treasurer transfers, including specified one-time transfers (e.g., $440,000,000 from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund; $29,458,800 to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund; $10,000,000 to the Critical Infrastructure Facilities Cash Fund; $10,000,000 to the Youth Outdoor Education Innovation Fund; $28,000,000 from the Jobs & Economic Development Initiative Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund; $3,500,000 and $11,320,000 to the Health & Human Services Cash Fund; periodic transfers to the State Insurance Fund; and a $2,950,000 transfer from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the General Fund).

Department of Labor (DOL) will administer the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund, receive state unemployment insurance tax revenue beginning July 1, 2025, absorb balances of terminating unemployment/training funds on that date, may award up to $250,000 for a manufacturing pilot, and the bill reduces the final average combined tax rate by 5% for 2025–2029. Department of Economic Development (DED) assumes direction of the Job Training Cash Fund and may receive transfers (including from the Cash Reserve Fund). Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) is designated licensing authority for hospitals; specified Cash Fund transfers are directed to DHHS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Economic Development (department administering the pilot): Creates a Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot—eligible manufacturers may apply 10/1/2025–11/1/2026 for grants up to $50,000 (must be matched by private funds). Projects started before application are ineligible; recipients who lose eligibility must repay. The department may award up to $250,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and adopt rules to implement the pilot.

- Department of Labor: Workforce Development Program Cash Fund may be used for labor studies, unemployment-related costs, and the manufacturing pilot. The Nebraska Workforce Development Board may submit up to 18 grant proposals annually; the Commissioner may approve/reject/modify them, approve up to 3 without board concurrence, and must report certain large approvals.

- Department of Administrative Services: The budget administrator in DAS directs timing/amounts of transfers: $2,950,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the General Fund (between 7/1/2025–6/30/2026) and $5,000,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to the General Fund (between 7/1/2026–6/30/2027).

- Department of Health and Human Services: No changes or funding actions referenced.
