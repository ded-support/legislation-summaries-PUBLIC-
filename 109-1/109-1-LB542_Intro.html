Summary

LB542 contains no explicit references to the Department of Transportation. The bill amends statutes on metropolitan utilities district authority and on utility-facility relocation for highway projects, but it does not name or directly address the Department of Transportation, nor does it include any appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations directed to that agency.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of “qualifying utility facility” that expressly covers utility facilities located in, over, along, or under: Interstate highways (and urban extensions), highways formerly designated as Interstates, any highway, parts of the state highway system, and roads/right‑of‑ways under county, city, or village primary authority. “Utility facility” is expanded to include commercial broadband supplier facilities (per §86‑5,104) and community antenna television service facilities (per §18‑2201). The provision operates “notwithstanding any rule, permit, agreement, franchise, or other authorization or law to the contrary.” For the Department of Transportation, this clarifies the types of facilities that may occupy DOT-managed rights‑of‑way and could restrict the applicability of existing permitting/authorization processes; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
