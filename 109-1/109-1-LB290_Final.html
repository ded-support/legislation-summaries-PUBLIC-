Summary

This bill amends duties and grant authority of the Economic Recovery and Incentives Division of the Department of Economic Development. The division shall award grants (50% advance, remainder paid monthly until paid or by Dec. 31, 2026) and may recoup or reduce payments for unused funds. It must prioritize grants to qualified census tracts (QCTs) in metropolitan-class cities and rely on studies under §81-12,242. The division will create a Qualified Census Tract Recovery Grant Program with allocations: up to $10M for QCTs in primary-class cities, up to $10M for QCTs outside metro/primary cities, and remaining funds for metro-class QCTs. Additional capped grants administered by the division include: $90M for a business park (with specified pre-release conditions), $6M for internships/crime prevention, $5M film production, $20M (metro) and $20M (primary) for affordable housing land prep, $5M to a county agricultural society, and $1M for postsecondary financial literacy. Grants using federal ARPA funds must meet Treasury guidance.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires that grant-eligible activity be limited to the boundaries of an inland port district within a metropolitan-class city and expressly prohibits use of grant funds in that city’s downtown and northern downtown areas. For the Department of Economic Development, this narrows where DED-administered grants may be spent but does not specify any new appropriations, funding amounts, or budget reallocations.
