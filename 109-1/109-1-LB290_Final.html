Summary

LB290 amends §81‑12,241 to direct the Economic Recovery and Incentives Division of the Department of Economic Development to administer federal and state grant programs, providing a 50% advance with remaining funds paid monthly until paid in full or by December 31, 2026, and authorizing recoupment or reduction for unused funds. It creates a Qualified Census Tract Recovery Grant Program (caps: up to $10M for tracts in primary‑class cities; up to $10M for tracts outside metro/primary cities; remaining funds prioritized to metro‑class city tracts). It also authorizes the Division to award additional grants, including up to $90M to a nonprofit for a business park (with pre‑release conditions: advisory‑committee participation, separate bank account, 15‑year use restriction, 10‑year pro forma/due diligence, two public input meetings, and inland port support), and specified caps of $6M, $5M, $20M, $20M, $5M, and $1M for other listed purposes. Federal grants must comply with ARPA/Treasury guidance; state grants must align with ARPA intent.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Economic Development: the bill adds that an inland port district may be located within a metropolitan-class city, but expressly bars use of grant funds in that city’s downtown and northern downtown areas. This imposes a geographic restriction on DED-administered grants/eligible expenditures; no new appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are specified.
