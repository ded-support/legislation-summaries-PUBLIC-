Summary

The bill amends duties of the Economic Recovery and Incentives Division of the Department of Economic Development to administer federal and state grant funding. It requires a 50% advance payment to grantees with remaining funds paid monthly until full or by Dec. 31, 2026, and authorizes recovery or reduction of unused funds. The division must create a Qualified Census Tract Recovery Grant Program (allocations: up to $10 million for QCTs in a city of the primary class; up to $10 million for QCTs outside metro/primary cities; remaining funds prioritized for QCTs in a city of the metropolitan class). Additional grant authorizations: up to $90M for a nonprofit to develop a business park (with specified pre-release conditions), $6M internships/crime prevention, $5M film, $20M metro housing, $20M primary-class housing, $5M ag society relief, and $1M financial literacy. Federal ARPA rules and Treasury guidance apply to federal-funded grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a geographic restriction for grants tied to inland port districts within a "city of the metropolitan class": grant funds may not be used in that city's downtown or northern downtown areas. For the Department of Economic Development, this limits where department-administered grant funding may be spent but does not alter appropriation levels or create new funding lines. No changes to funding, budget allocations, or appropriations are specified.
