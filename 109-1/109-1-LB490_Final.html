Summary

LB490 amends the Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act and assigns operational duties to the Department (motor vehicle authority), county treasurers, and the Director of Motor Vehicles. Key agency actions: the Department must implement and operate an electronic title-and-lien system, prescribe forms and certificate numbering, provide system access to licensed dealers and lienholders, note and cancel liens electronically, and issue titles after lien releases. County treasurers must transmit an electronic copy of issued titles to the Department, maintain indices and seals, notify the Department and first lienholders when liens are noted or cancelled, and may destroy records after five years. The Director sets implementation dates for electronic-title provisions; some title eligibility changes begin Jan. 1, 2026. The bill references existing fees for lien notation but contains no new appropriations or specified funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds exclusions and title/lien rules for certain farm equipment: utility trailers; beginning Jan 1, 2026, self‑propelled equipment used exclusively to apply fertilizer/chemicals, floater‑spreader implements, implements of husbandry used mainly for tilling, harvesting or feeding livestock; and, beginning Jan 1, 2026, power‑unit hay grinders or power unit/ grinder combinations when operated without cargo. Confirms that security interests previously perfected under UCC Article 9 retain priority as of their original perfection date. Requires certificate‑of‑title holders to surrender titles to allow lien notation; allows secured parties to obtain a title in the owner’s name and note a lien per sections 60‑144 and 60‑164 if owner fails to obtain title. No agencies or funding/appropriation changes are mentioned.
