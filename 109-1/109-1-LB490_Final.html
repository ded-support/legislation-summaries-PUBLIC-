Summary

LB490 amends the Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act to expand title eligibility and modernize title/lien handling. Key agency roles:

- Department (Director of Motor Vehicles): required to implement and operate an electronic title and lien system, prescribe uniform numbering and electronic forms, receive same‑day electronic copies from county treasurers, note and cancel liens electronically, provide system access to participating licensed dealers and lienholders, and set implementation dates.

- County treasurers: continue to issue and seal paper titles, transmit electronic copies to the department, maintain indices, notify the department of lien notations/cancellations, and may destroy records after five years.

- Licensed dealers (per Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act) and lienholders: may file liens electronically; special rules for inventory and purchase‑money security interests apply.

No changes to appropriations or funding/budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds device categories (utility trailers; beginning Jan 1, 2026, certain self‑propelled fertilizer/chemical applicators, floater‑spreaders, tillage/harvest/feeding implements; and beginning Jan 1, 2026, power‑unit hay grinders or combos) and clarifies lien/title treatment. It references Article 9 of the Uniform Commercial Code for prior perfected security interests, requires certificate‑of‑title holders to surrender title on request to allow lien notation on paper or electronic title records, and allows a secured party to obtain a certificate of title and note a lien per procedures in sections 60‑144 and 60‑164 if the owner fails to obtain title after Jan 1, 2026. No specific government agency is named and there are no funding or appropriation changes.
