Summary

LB490 amends the Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act to expand vehicles eligible for Nebraska title (utility trailers; specified agricultural implements, floater-spreaders, power‑unit hay grinders and certain combinations effective Jan. 1, 2026). It directs county treasurers to issue duplicate titles, transmit an electronic copy to the department on the day of issuance, maintain indices, use a prescribed seal, and destroy older records after five years. The Department of Motor Vehicles must prescribe uniform title numbering, implement an electronic title-and-lien system, provide system access to licensed dealers and lienholders, and note/cancel liens electronically. The Director of Motor Vehicles sets implementation dates referenced in the bill. Procedures for lien notation, notification, cancellation (15‑day rules), and acceptance of electronic certificate records on repossession are specified. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds specified devices (utility trailers; beginning Jan 1, 2026, self‑propelled fertilizer/chemical applicators, agricultural floater‑spreaders, implements of husbandry used primarily for tilling/harvesting/feeding livestock; and, beginning Jan 1, 2026, power‑unit hay grinders or combinations operated without cargo) to the title/lien regime. Security interests perfected under Article 9, UCC, retain priority as of their original perfection date. Certificate‑of‑title holders must surrender titles on request to allow notation of liens; if an owner fails to obtain a certificate of title after Jan 1, 2026, the security‑interest holder may obtain the certificate in the owner’s name and note a lien following sections 60‑144 and 60‑164. Assignment, release, or satisfaction of security interests remains governed by the law under which they were perfected. No state agencies or funding/appropriation changes are mentioned.
