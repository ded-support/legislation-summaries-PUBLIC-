Summary

LB615 (amending Neb. Rev. Stat. §49-1401) prohibits distribution of deceptive or fraudulent deepfakes and synthetic media that depict or misattribute speech or actions to political candidates, requires clear, persistent disclosures for visual and audio synthetic media, and authorizes candidates to seek injunctive or other equitable relief to stop such publications. Exceptions cover radio and television broadcasters (including cable and satellite operators) when material is part of bona fide news coverage or when federal law requires airing candidate ads, online/news publishers that routinely carry news if they disclose inauthenticity, and satire/parody. The bill includes harmonization and severability provisions and repeals the original section. No specific Nebraska state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds disclosure rules and remedies for materially deceptive "deepfake" media: required labels (Image, Video, or Audio); readable on-screen text; video disclosures shown for the full video; audio disclosures spoken at start, end, and every two minutes for audio over two minutes. Gives candidates injunctive/equitable relief to block publication of prohibited deepfakes. Exempts radio/TV broadcasters (including cable/satellite), paid broadcasters who acted in good faith, internet sites and periodicals that clearly disclaim inaccuracy, and satire/parody. References "federal law" obligations for airing candidate ads but names no federal agency and includes no funding, appropriations, or budget changes.
