Summary

LB339 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to administer the state's social services program and to participate in the federal child care assistance/Child Care Subsidy program. DHHS must promulgate rules implementing specified provider/household provisions (operative July 1, 2025), adopt an annual fixed-rate reimbursement schedule effective October 1, and provide a Feb. 1 annual summary report of prior-year subsidy claims (monthly paid day/partial-day units and aggregate reimbursements by age group). DHHS is to collaborate with a private nonprofit on a privately funded evaluation of prior eligibility changes. For costs resulting from earlier income-eligibility changes (July 1, 2021–Sept. 30, 2026), Child Care and Development Block Grant funds (and, if insufficient, TANF funds) may be used; no General Funds shall be used except for administration.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to annually (by Feb. 1) produce, for each licensed provider reimbursed under the federal Child Care Subsidy program in the prior calendar year, a claims summary. The report must list by month: paid full-day units for children under 6 and for children 6 and older; paid partial-day units for those same two age groups; and the aggregate reimbursement totals for each age group. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
