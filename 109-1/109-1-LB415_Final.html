Summary

The bill designates the Department of Labor as the “Department” under the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act and expands the Commissioner of Labor’s enforcement authority (administrative penalties up to $500/$5,000, contest/hearing procedures, public disclosure, and barring employers with unpaid citations from state contracting). It integrates unemployment overpayment recovery with the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act, allowing offsets of overpaid unemployment benefits against gambling winnings. The Department of Revenue, in consultation with the Department of Health and Human Services, the Department of Motor Vehicles, and the Department of Labor, must develop and operate the electronic collection system; DHHS may submit certified debts, and Revenue, DMV, and Labor may submit outstanding state liabilities. Operators remit deductions to Revenue, which credits payments first to DHHS-certified debts, with confidentiality, notice, and appeal procedures involving Revenue and DHHS. No funding or appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Labor: Adds/clarifies that “overpayment” includes unemployment insurance overpayments (section 48-663.01) and explicitly names the Department of Labor as a recipient agency for collection/offset of outstanding state liabilities (including offsets against gambling winnings). Also clarifies paid sick time accrual rules (employees begin accruing after 80 hours; paid sick time provided Jan 1–Oct 1, 2025 counts toward 2025 obligations) under the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Explicitly listed among agencies that may be owed outstanding state liabilities and participate in setoff/collection actions.
