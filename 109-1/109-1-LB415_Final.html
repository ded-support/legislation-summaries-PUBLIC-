Summary

LB415 (as described) makes two agency-specific changes. It designates the Department of Labor as the “Department” under the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act and authorizes the Commissioner of Labor to issue citations, assess administrative penalties, provide hearings and enforcement, and block state contracts for unpaid citations. It requires the Department of Revenue, in consultation with DHHS, the DMV, and the Department of Labor, to create an electronic gambling‑winnings setoff system: DHHS may certify child‑support debts; the DMV and Labor may submit outstanding state liabilities; operators must check the system and remit setoffs to Revenue, which credits DHHS‑certified debts first, then other state liabilities (including unemployment overpayments per amended 48‑665). No appropriations or budget allocations for these agencies are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Labor: Adds an explicit definition that “overpayment” includes unemployment insurance overpayments (sec. 48-663.01) and repeatedly names DOL as an agency that may be owed outstanding state liabilities eligible for collection or setoff (including offsets against gambling winnings). Also clarifies paid‑sick‑time accrual timing (after 80 hours) and that sick leave provided in early 2025 counts toward 2025 employer obligations (policy impact, not agency funding).

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Is expressly listed among agencies that may hold outstanding state liabilities and participate in setoff/collection processes (including gambling winnings setoff).

- Department of Revenue: The bill ties obligor definitions to section 77-2701.25 (Dept. of Revenue setoff authority), expanding the types of debts subject to that process.

- Department of Health and Human Services: No substantive additions affecting DHHS in the added text.

No new appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
