Summary

LB415 directs two primary actions involving the named agencies. First, the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act designates the "Department" as the Department of Labor; the Commissioner of Labor enforces the Act, may issue citations, assess administrative penalties (up to $500 for a first violation, $5,000 for subsequent violations), provide hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act, and bar employers with unpaid citations from state or local contracts. Second, the Department of Revenue must develop and operate a secure electronic gambling-winnings setoff system in consultation with DHHS, the DMV, and the Department of Labor. DHHS may submit certified child‑support debts; DMV and DoL may submit other state liabilities; DoR credits DHHS debts first, then apportions remaining collections pro rata. DHHS, DoR, DMV, and DoL share notification, appeal, rulemaking, confidentiality, and disclosure‑penalty duties. No explicit appropriations or budget allocations are included.
