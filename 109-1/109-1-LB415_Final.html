Summary

The bill designates the "Department" in the Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act as the Department of Labor and directs the Commissioner of Labor to issue citations and proposed administrative penalties for violations. It expands the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act: the Department of Revenue, in consultation with DHHS, the DMV, and DOL, must develop an electronic collections system; DHHS may submit certified debts of $25 or more; Revenue, DMV, and DOL may submit outstanding state liabilities (explicitly including liabilities administered by DOL and DMV). Operators remit setoff amounts to Revenue; Revenue credits DHHS-certified debts first, then other state liabilities; Revenue and DHHS must notify obligors and provide appeal rights. DHHS, DOL, and Revenue have confidentiality and rulemaking authority. Amendments to the Conveyance Safety Act do not affect these four agencies, and the bill includes no explicit appropriations or budget allocations for them.
