Summary

The bill names the Department of Economic Development as administrator of the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund, directs the department to establish a subaccount to fund affordable housing and related land-parcel preparation under the Economic Recovery Act, and authorizes it to seek private or nonstate contributions. Interest earned by the department on grant funds will be applied to the grant program, and grant proceeds not used for a qualified activity within 24 months must be returned to the department for credit to the General Fund. The department is required to award a grant for a prefabricated housing study, set applicant qualifications and deliverables, and receive a presentation and electronic report by April 1, 2026. The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 from the Fund to the Department for that grant. Beginning July 1, 2029, funds held by the department in the Fund shall transfer to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill directs the Department of Economic Development (DED) to award a grant for a statewide study of prefabricated housing. The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 from the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund to DED to fund the grant (for purposes of section 2). Eligible applicants must demonstrate staffing and technical capacity, relevant expertise and experience, stakeholder engagement ability, and commit to deliverables including a comprehensive presentation and an electronic report by April 1, 2026. The study’s scope expressly includes analysis of zoning regulations affecting placement of prefabricated housing across municipalities and counties.
