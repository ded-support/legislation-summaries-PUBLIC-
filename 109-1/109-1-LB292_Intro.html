Summary

- The Department of Economic Development (DED) will administer the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund, establish a subaccount to fund affordable housing and land-parcel preparation under the Economic Recovery Act, and may solicit additional private/nonstate contributions (including from the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority). Interest earned on grant funds is to be applied to the grant program.

- DED shall award a grant to a qualified applicant to conduct a study of prefabricated housing in Nebraska; the grantee must deliver a comprehensive presentation and an electronic report to DED by April 1, 2026.

- The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 from the Fund to DED for the Sec. 2 grant.

- If grant recipients fail to engage in qualified activity within 24 months, proceeds must be returned to DED for credit to the General Fund. Any Fund balances held by DED transfer to the General Fund beginning July 1, 2029.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text directs the Department of Economic Development to administer a grant-funded study on prefabricated housing in Nebraska. The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 from the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund to DED for the grant (section 2). Prefabricated housing is defined to include manufactured homes and other factory-built components. DED must award the grant to a qualified applicant who demonstrates capacity (personnel and technology), a commitment to deliverables, relevant expertise/track record, and ability to engage stakeholders (municipalities, counties, manufacturers, utilities). Deliverables include a comprehensive presentation and an electronic report due April 1, 2026. The study must examine zoning regulations affecting siting of prefabricated housing across municipalities and counties.
