Summary

The bill assigns several responsibilities and funding actions to the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED must establish a subaccount within the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund for Economic Recovery Act housing and land-preparation activities, administer the Fund, and may solicit private/nonstate contributions. Interest earned on grant funds is to be applied to the grant program; grant proceeds returned for nonperformance are credited to DED for deposit to the General Fund. The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 from the Fund to DED for the grant in section 2. DED will award a grant to study prefabricated housing, requiring qualified applicants and a final report/presentation to DED by April 1, 2026. Funds held by DED in the Fund transfer to the General Fund on July 1, 2029.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill directs the Department of Economic Development (DED) to award a grant for a study of prefabricated housing (including manufactured homes per §23‑114 and other offsite‑built components). It specifies applicant eligibility criteria: demonstrated staffing/technical capacity, proven expertise/experience, stakeholder engagement ability, and a commitment to provide a comprehensive presentation and an electronic report to DED by April 1, 2026. The study must examine issues such as zoning regulations affecting siting of prefabricated housing. The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 from the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund to DED to fund the grant described in section 2.
