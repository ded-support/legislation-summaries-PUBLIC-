Summary

LB427 requires the State Treasurer to create and administer (or oversee a third‑party manager for) a Student Savings Account Support Fund and to establish individual educational savings accounts for K–12 students beginning July 1, 2026. The State Treasurer must transfer $1,500 per enrolled student each fiscal year from the Fund into each student's account (available by July 15 or 30 days after application). The state investment officer will invest Fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The State Board of Education must publish and maintain a list of qualified educational expenses (hosted on the State Department of Education website), hear appeals, and may adopt rules. The State Treasurer will monitor accounts, audit, investigate suspected misuse, and refer crimes to the Nebraska State Patrol; restitution is required and funds may revert to the General Fund upon ineligibility, graduation, or death.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Student Savings Account program administered by the State Treasurer (or a third‑party manager under the Treasurer’s supervision). The State Board of Education must publish a list of qualified educational expenses on the State Department of Education website and hear appeals to add expenses. The Treasurer will establish individual accounts beginning July 1, 2026, and each fiscal year transfer $1,500 per enrolled K–12 student from the newly created Student Savings Account Support Fund into each account; fund assets are invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Treasurer will monitor, audit, and investigate misuse and report suspected crimes to the Nebraska State Patrol; misused or unclaimed funds may revert to the General Fund. The State Board may adopt rules.
