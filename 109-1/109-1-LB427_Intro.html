Summary

Creates a Student Savings Account program administered by the State Treasurer (or a third‑party manager under the Treasurer's supervision). Establishes the Student Savings Account Support Fund, which the state investment officer will invest under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. Beginning July 1, 2026, the Treasurer shall create an account per enrolled K–12 student and annually transfer $1,500 from the fund into each account; payments available by July 15 or 30 days after application. The State Board of Education must publish a list of qualified expenses on the State Department of Education website, accept appeals, and may adopt rules. The Treasurer will monitor, audit, and investigate misuse, refer suspected crimes to the Nebraska State Patrol, and require restitution; funds revert to the General Fund if a student no longer qualifies, graduates, or dies.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Student Savings Account program and fund administered by the State Treasurer (or a third‑party manager under the Treasurer’s supervision). The State Board of Education must adopt a list of qualified educational expenses (posted on the State Department of Education website) and hears appeals to add expenses. Beginning 2026–27, parents/guardians/students are informed about applying; starting July 1, 2026 the Treasurer must open an account per enrolled student and annually transfer $1,500 from the newly created Student Savings Account Support Fund into each account (available by July 15 or 30 days after application). The state investment officer invests fund balances per the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Treasurer monitors accounts, audits, investigates misuse, and refers crimes to the Nebraska State Patrol; misused funds must be repaid, and terminated accounts revert to the General Fund. The State Board may adopt rules.
