Summary

LB427 directs the State Treasurer to create and administer a Student Savings Account Support Fund and to establish individual educational savings accounts for K–12 students in approved or accredited public, private, denominational, or parochial schools. The state investment officer will invest fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. Beginning July 1, 2026, the Treasurer (or a third‑party manager under the Treasurer’s supervision) must transfer $1,500 per enrolled student each fiscal year from the Support Fund into each student’s account (available by July 15 or 30 days after application). The State Board of Education must publish and adjudicate a list of qualified educational expenses on the State Department of Education website and may adopt rules. The Treasurer monitors accounts, audits for misuse, refers suspected fraud to the Nebraska State Patrol, and requires restitution; terminated account balances revert to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Student Savings Account program administered by the State Treasurer (or a third‑party manager under the Treasurer's supervision). The State Board of Education must publish a list of qualified educational expenses on the State Department of Education website and hear appeals to add items. The State Treasurer will open individual accounts starting July 1, 2026; each fiscal year $1,500 per enrolled K–12 student will be transferred from the newly created Student Savings Account Support Fund into each student's account. Funds are invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Treasurer will monitor, audit, and investigate misuse and report crimes to the Nebraska State Patrol; improperly used funds require restitution. Unused/terminated accounts revert funds to the General Fund. The State Board may adopt implementing rules.
