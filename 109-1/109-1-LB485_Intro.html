Summary

LB485 requires that personal identifying information of train crew members in reports and in communications with law enforcement arising from railroad fatalities be redacted from public reports and maintained confidentially by law enforcement and others in possession of those records. Unredacted copies may be accessed as needed by: the host or employing railroad; persons with court-ordered authorization; and law enforcement officers, prosecutors, or Public Service Commission staff when necessary for performance of duties. The bill also exempts train crew members from having to display an operator’s license to law enforcement during locomotive or train operations. “Train crew member” is defined to include engineers, conductors, brakemen and other crew operating on rails. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires that personal identifying information of train crew members in railroad-fatality reports and related communications be redacted from public reports and kept confidential by law enforcement and others who possess the records. Unredacted copies are accessible to: the host/employing railroad; persons authorized by court order; and law enforcement officers, prosecutors, or Public Service Commission staff when access is necessary to perform their duties. Establishes that a train crew member (engineer, conductor, brakeman, or any other locomotive/train crew member) is not required to display an operator’s license to law enforcement while operating a train in the state. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
