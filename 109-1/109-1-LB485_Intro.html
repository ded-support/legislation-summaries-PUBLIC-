Summary

LB485 requires that personal identifying information of train crew members in reports and communications concerning railroad fatalities be redacted from public reports and kept confidential by law enforcement and others in possession of those records. Unredacted materials remain accessible when necessary: to the host or employing railroad; to persons authorized by court order; and to law enforcement officers, prosecutors, or Nebraska Public Service Commission staff when needed to perform their duties. The bill also exempts train crew members from being required to display an operator’s license to law enforcement during locomotive or train operation. "Train crew member" is defined to include engineer, conductor, brakeman, and other crew. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require redaction of train crew members' personal identifying information from public reports and communications in railroad-fatality incidents, with law enforcement and others holding those records maintaining confidentiality. Unredacted copies must be made available on request to the host/employing railroad; persons or parties authorized by court order; and law enforcement officers, prosecutors, or Public Service Commission staff when needed to perform their duties. The bill also bars requiring a train crew member to display an operator’s license to law enforcement during train operation. No agencies are allocated new funding or appropriations in these additions.
