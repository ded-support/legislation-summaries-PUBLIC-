Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): Authorizes new organizational and alternate plates and related procedures. Fee allocations: $70 organizational‑plate fee (60% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund; 40% to the Highway Trust Fund); postage/handling fees remitted to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund; $3 transfer fee remitted to that cash fund. Personalized alternate plate fee ($40) credits 25% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 75% to designated recipients. Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit plate ($70) credits 60% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 40% to the designated recipient. DMV to deliver plates, provide forms, and may adopt rules.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Choose Life plate proceeds credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement TANF funds; DHHS will administer the Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund (including plate proceeds) for donation promotion and education.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA): Veterans Employment Program Fund (plate proceeds and other appropriations) administered by DVA for veteran recruitment/education and website; DVA to administer Pets for Vets grants funded by the Pets for Vets Cash Fund (plate proceeds).

- Department of Revenue: Wildlife Conservation Fund may pay expenses incurred by the Department of Revenue in administering the income tax designation program.
