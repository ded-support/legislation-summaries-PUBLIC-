Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): Multiple provisions route specialty and organizational plate fees to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund (e.g., organizational plate fee: 60% to DMV Cash Fund/40% to Highway Trust Fund; $3 transfer fee and postage/handling fees credited to DMV Cash Fund). Personalized alternate plate fees credit 25% to the DMV Cash Fund and 75% to the designated recipient. Nebraska Cornhusker plate fees similarly split 60% DMV Cash Fund / 40% designated recipient. The Czech Heritage plate’s designated recipient is the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Choose Life plate proceeds go to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement TANF funds available to DHHS. DHHS will administer the Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund created from contributions and certain plate proceeds for organ/tissue donation promotion.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: The Veterans Employment Program Fund (funded by section 15 plate proceeds) is administered by the department for veteran recruitment/education. The department will administer or contract a Pets for Vets adoption grant program using the Pets for Vets Cash Fund (also funded by section 15 proceeds).

- Department of Revenue: The Wildlife Conservation Fund may be used to pay expenses incurred by the Department of Revenue in administering the income tax designation program; the State Treasurer shall transfer amounts equal to designated contributions as determined by the Tax Commissioner.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: Choose Life license plate fees are designated to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement federal TANF funds for DHHS.

Department of Motor Vehicles: Expands DMV authority to design and issue many new alternate plates (e.g., Arbor Day, Cornhusker, Czech Heritage, Donate Life, etc.). Czech Heritage plate fees are credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit Plates carry a $70 fee; the State Treasurer will remit 60% to the DMV Cash Fund and 40% to the designated spirit-plate recipient fund.

Department of Revenue: No changes or new allocations identified.

Department of Veterans' Affairs: No direct references or appropriations to the agency. (Pets for Vets plate fees go to a Pets for Vets Cash Fund supporting veterans but not to the Department of Veterans' Affairs.)
