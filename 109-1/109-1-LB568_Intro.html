Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): directs the department to design/issue organizational and alternate license plates, administer applications, and remit postage/handling and certain transfer fees to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. Specific fee allocations: organizational plates — $70 (60% to DMV Cash Fund, 40% to Highway Trust Fund); personalized alternate plates — $40 (25% to DMV Cash Fund, 75% to designated recipient); transfer/issuance fees ($3) remit to DMV Cash Fund. Cornhusker Spirit Plates — $70 (60% to DMV Cash Fund, 40% to Spirit Plate Proceeds Fund).

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Choose Life plate fees credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement TANF; DHHS to administer the Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund (including Donate Life plate proceeds) for donation promotion and related grants.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA): to administer a Veterans Employment Program Fund (receipts per section 15 and appropriations) for veteran recruitment/education and to operate a Pets for Vets grant program funded from the Pets for Vets Cash Fund (receipts per section 15).

- Department of Revenue: permitted to have expenses paid from the Wildlife Conservation Fund for administering the income-tax designation program; State Treasurer to transfer from General Fund an amount equal to contributions designated under section 77-27,119.01 as determined by the Tax Commissioner.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): expands DMV authority to design and issue numerous new alternate license plates (Arbor Day, Breast Cancer, Choose Life, Czech Heritage, Donate Life, Down Syndrome, Josh the Otter, Mountain Lion Conservation, Native American Cultural Awareness, Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit, Nebraska History, Pets for Vets, Prostate Cancer, Wildlife Conservation, etc.). Czech Heritage plate fees are credited to the DMV Cash Fund. Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit Plates carry a $70 issuance/renewal fee; 60% is credited to the DMV Cash Fund and 40% to the Spirit Plate Proceeds Fund.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): proceeds from Choose Life Plates are credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement federal TANF funds.

- Department of Revenue: no changes or references.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: no direct changes or references (Pets for Vets proceeds go to a Pets for Vets Cash Fund, not the department).

- Other funding: creates Home of Arbor Day Plate Cash Fund (administered by Univ. of Nebraska Board of Regents) to fund grants to the Nebraska Statewide Arboretum; funds invested per state investment laws.
