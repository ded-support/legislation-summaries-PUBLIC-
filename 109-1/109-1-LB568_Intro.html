Summary

LB568 affects the following agencies:

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): directs DMV to issue new alternate and organizational plates (including Arbor Day), manage delivery/temporary stickers, and collect related fees. Key fee allocations: organizational plates $70 (60% to Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund, 40% to Highway Trust Fund); $3 transfer fee credited to DMV Cash Fund; alphanumeric alternate plates $5 credited to the designated recipient; personalized-message alternate plates $40 (25% to DMV Cash Fund, 75% to designated recipient); Nebraska Cornhusker spirit plates $70 (60% to DMV Cash Fund, 40% to designated recipient). DMV may charge postage/handling (credited to DMV Cash Fund); manufacturing cost overruns credit the Highway Trust Fund first.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Choose Life plate proceeds credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement TANF; DHHS to administer the Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness and Education Fund (receives Donate Life plate revenues and other contributions).

- Department of Revenue: Wildlife Conservation Fund may pay Department of Revenue (and other agencies) for expenses administering the income tax designation program; State Treasurer transfers equal to designated contributions are specified.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: will administer a Veterans Employment Program Fund and a Pets for Vets Cash Fund (both funded by designated plate proceeds) to support veteran recruitment and pet-adoption grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: required to design and issue a long list of new alternate plates (Arbor Day, Breast Cancer, Choose Life, Czech Heritage, Donate Life, Down Syndrome, Josh the Otter, Mountain Lion, Native American, Cornhusker Spirit, Nebraska History, Pets for Vets, Prostate Cancer, Wildlife, etc.).
