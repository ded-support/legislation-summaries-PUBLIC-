Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to establish criteria for qualified primary care providers serving as primary care medical homes. Beginning no later than January 1, 2027, $5,000,000 annually shall be used to obtain federal financial participation to pay a monthly per‑member fee of not less than $75 to such DHHS‑qualified providers; any remaining funds are to be transferred to the medical assistance program and the Children's Health Insurance Program. Use of these funds requires applicable federal approvals. DHHS is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations necessary to implement the Medicaid Access and Quality Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Highlights affecting the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- Establishes the "Medicaid Access and Quality Act" and directs DHHS to set criteria for qualified primary care providers to serve as primary care medical homes.

- Requires DHHS to adopt rules and regulations necessary to implement the Act.

- Funding: beginning no later than Jan 1, 2027, $5 million annually will be used to draw federal financial participation to pay a monthly per-member fee of at least $75 to qualifying primary care providers (target populations). Any remaining funds will be transferred to the Medical Assistance Program (Medicaid) and the Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP).

- Use of funds is subject to required federal approvals. Legislature intends Medicaid practitioner fee schedule rates and related General Fund appropriations not be reduced below July 1, 2024 levels.
