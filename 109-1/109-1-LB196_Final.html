Summary

LB196 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,356. It allows operation of all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) and utility-type vehicles between sunset and sunrise when used for snow removal within city or village corporate limits or within unincorporated villages. Cities and villages may authorize operation by ordinance; county boards may authorize operation in unincorporated villages by resolution and may impose restrictions. Crossing and operation rules remain subject to existing requirements; electric utility personnel may operate ATVs/utility vehicles outside municipal limits without the sunrise–sunset limitation while performing employment duties. Parades authorized by the State or any department, board, commission, or political subdivision remain exempt. The bill repeals the original section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
