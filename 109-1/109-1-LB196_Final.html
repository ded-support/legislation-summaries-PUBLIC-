Summary

LB196 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,356 to permit operation of all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) and utility-type vehicles (UTVs) between sunset and sunrise when used for snow removal within corporate limits of a city or village or within an unincorporated village. Operators must hold a valid Class O license or farm permit (per §60-4,126), carry liability insurance and produce proof within five days to a peace officer, limit speed to 30 mph, use headlights/taillights, and display a required safety flag. Electric utility personnel are exempt from the daytime-only rule when working outside municipal limits. The bill preserves rules for crossings of controlled-access highways, allows UTV crossings at signalized or stop-sign intersections when authorized by local ordinance or county resolution, and allows agricultural crossings. Cities, villages, county boards, the State of Nebraska, and its departments/boards/commissions/political subdivisions retain authorization and regulatory roles. Original section repealed. No funding or appropriation changes.
