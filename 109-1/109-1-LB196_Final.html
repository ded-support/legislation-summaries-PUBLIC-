Summary

LB196 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,356 to expand permitted operation of all‑terrain vehicles (ATVs) and utility‑type vehicles (UTVs). Key government entities and actions:

- State of Nebraska or any department, board, commission, or political subdivision may authorize parade use exempt from subsection (3).

- Cities and villages may adopt ordinances authorizing ATV/UTV use (including limited nighttime use for snow removal).

- County boards may adopt resolutions authorizing use within unincorporated villages or at intersections outside municipal limits, and may impose restrictions.

- Utility personnel are explicitly allowed to operate outside municipal limits at any hour while performing duties.

- Law enforcement (peace officers) may request proof of required liability insurance.

Other requirements (licenses, insurance, equipment, speed limits) are retained. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
