Summary

The bill appropriates General Fund amounts to the Department of Health and Human Services to implement Legislative Bill 275. For Program 33 it provides $0 for FY2025‑26 and $329,347 for FY2026‑27, with salary/per diem expenditures capped at $0 for FY2025‑26 and $95,442 for FY2026‑27. For Program 354 it provides $0 for FY2025‑26 and $629,165 for FY2026‑27, with the FY2026‑27 amount designated as state aid only; no FY2025‑26 state aid is provided. Funds in Program 354 may not be used for permanent/temporary salaries or per diems.
