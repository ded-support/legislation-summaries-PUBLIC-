Summary

The bill appropriates General Fund amounts to the Department of Health and Human Services to implement LB275. For Program 33: $0 for FY2025‑26 and $329,347 for FY2026‑27, with a cap on permanent/temporary salaries and per diems of $0 in FY2025‑26 and $95,442 in FY2026‑27. For Program 354: $0 for FY2025‑26 and $629,165 for FY2026‑27, with the FY2026‑27 amount designated solely as state aid; no FY2025‑26 state aid is provided. Funds in Program 354 may not be used for state employee salaries or per diems.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS appropriations added to implement Legislative Bill 275 (109th Leg., 2025):

- Program 33: $0 GF for FY2025‑26; $329,347 GF for FY2026‑27. Of the FY2026‑27 funds, up to $95,442 may be used for permanent/temporary salaries and per diems (no salary spending authorized in FY2025‑26).

- Program 354: $0 GF for FY2025‑26; $629,165 GF for FY2026‑27, all designated as state aid and restricted to that purpose. No portion of the Program 354 appropriation may be used for permanent or temporary state employee salaries or per diems.

All funding is General Fund and applies only in FY2026‑27.
