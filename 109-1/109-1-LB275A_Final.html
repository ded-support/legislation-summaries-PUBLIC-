Summary

LB275A appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services to implement LB275. For Program 33: $0 General Fund in FY2025‑26 and $329,347 General Fund in FY2026‑27; permanent/temporary salaries and per diems are limited to $0 in FY2025‑26 and $95,442 in FY2026‑27. For Program 354: $0 General Fund in FY2025‑26 and $629,165 General Fund in FY2026‑27, with the $629,165 designated as state aid for FY2026‑27 (and $0 for FY2025‑26); no permanent or temporary salaries or per diems for state employees may be paid from the funds appropriated to Program 354.
