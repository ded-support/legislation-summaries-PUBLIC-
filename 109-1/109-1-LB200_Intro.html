Summary

LB200 reinstates the Personal Property Tax Relief Act exemptions effective for tax years after 2024 and updates administration and reporting. The Property Tax Administrator and Tax Commissioner implement the exemption and a compensating exemption factor affecting railroads, car lines, public service entities, and air carriers. County assessors and treasurers must certify lost tax revenue to the Tax Commissioner (by Nov. 30); the Tax Commissioner notifies the Director of Administrative Services, who draws warrants against General Fund appropriations for reimbursement in two roughly equal installments after the State Treasurer verifies available funds. Reimbursement distribution formulas to taxing subdivisions are specified (including a 1% county general fund deposit and prescribed allocation methods for public service entities, railroads, car lines, and air carriers). The bill also updates filing deadlines (county assessors to electronically file personal property abstracts with the Property Tax Administrator by July 20) and repeals the original sections amended.
