Summary

Creates the Committee on Pacific Conflict (amending Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-836). Membership: Director of State Homeland Security (chair), Director of Administrative Services, state investment officer, Adjutant General, and three governor-appointed subject-matter experts (including at least one representing a critical infrastructure operator). The Executive Board of the Legislative Council appoints five nonvoting legislative members. The committee may establish an advisory board, consult experts, liaise with federal and other state authorities and research institutions, hold secure briefings with critical‑infrastructure providers, and produce policy recommendations and a confidential report (accessed only with Governor approval). Meetings are quarterly minimum and exempt from the Open Meetings Act; records are not public. The Governor must annually publish a state threat assessment including nonconfidential committee findings. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
