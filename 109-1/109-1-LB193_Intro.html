Summary

Creates the Committee on Pacific Conflict and specifies membership: the Director of State Homeland Security (chair), the Director of Administrative Services, the state investment officer, the Adjutant General, and three Governor‑appointed subject‑matter experts (including at least one representative of critical‑infrastructure operators). It also provides for legislative members appointed by the Executive Board of the Legislative Council as nonvoting participants. The committee may meet at least quarterly, is exempted from the Open Meetings Act, and its records are not public. Powers include convening secure briefings with critical‑infrastructure providers, establishing advisory boards, liaising with federal and other state authorities and research institutions, producing policy recommendations, issuing a public annual state threat assessment (to accompany the Governor’s annual address), and preparing a confidential report accessible only with gubernatorial approval. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
