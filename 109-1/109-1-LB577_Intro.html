Summary

LB577 amends statutes (including sections 71-436.02 and 38-2847) affecting the Department of Health and Human Services to revise licensure and operational requirements for remote dispensing pharmacies. Changes require remote dispensing to occur under real-time audiovisual supervision by a Nebraska‑licensed pharmacist employed by a supervising pharmacy; dispensing must cease if the audiovisual link fails. The remote site must share the same pharmacist in charge as the supervising pharmacy, with that pharmacist ensuring at least one onsite visit per calendar month. The supervising pharmacist may delegate tasks but remains responsible for drug utilization review, final verification, supervision of remote dispensing, and must attempt counseling on all new prescriptions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
