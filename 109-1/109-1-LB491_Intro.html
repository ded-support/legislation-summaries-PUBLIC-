Summary

LB491 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §72-819 to restate legislative intent and adjust provisions related to museum projects. Key agencies: the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission (GPC) and the Nebraska State Historical Society. Provisions:

- GPC must award grants to a federally recognized Indian tribe to construct, develop, and manage a Chief Standing Bear Museum and visitor center.

- Legislative intent to appropriate from the Museum Construction and Maintenance Fund: up to $7,000,000 in FY2025‑26 for construction and up to $7,000,000 in FY2026‑27 for exhibit fabrication and historical interpretation for the Chief Standing Bear project.

- GPC shall also construct, develop, and manage a museum at Fort Robinson State Park, with legislative intent to appropriate up to $7,000,000 in FY2025‑26 from the same fund.

- GPC may execute an MOU or contract with the Nebraska State Historical Society for museum development and exhibits.

The bill repeals the original section.
