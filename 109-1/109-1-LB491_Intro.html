Summary

LB491 amends §72-819 to direct the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission to award grants to a federally recognized Indian tribe to construct, develop, and manage a Chief Standing Bear museum and visitor center. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate up to $7 million from the Museum Construction and Maintenance Fund in FY2025-26 for construction and up to $7 million in FY2026-27 for exhibit fabrication and historical interpretation for that project. The Commission is also directed to construct and manage a museum at Fort Robinson State Park, with intent to appropriate up to $7 million from the same fund in FY2025-26. The Commission may enter an MOU or contract with the Nebraska State Historical Society for museum development and exhibits. The original section is repealed.
