Summary

LB93 amends Nebraska discovery law (sections 29-1912, 29-1913, 29-1918), affecting courts, prosecuting attorneys, and law enforcement agencies. Key agency-related changes:

- Prosecuting attorneys and law enforcement agencies: must permit defendants to inspect/copy investigation materials, including reports developed or received by law enforcement that directly relate to the investigation. Discovery explicitly includes information on computers, electronic communication devices, and digital information.

- Courts: may order access and must specify time/place/manner for defense testing of electronic evidence; may review sealed prosecutorial statements alleging risk of harm to witnesses and make those available to the appellate court.

- Expert witnesses: prosecutors must disclose names, qualifications, and complete opinions with basis/reasons.

- Remedy: prosecutorial neglect or intentional alteration of evidence can render prosecution tests inadmissible.

- Jailhouse informants remain governed by sections 29-4701 to 29-4706.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds disclosure requirements: prosecutors must provide names, qualifications, and complete expert opinions (including bases/reasons) for any expert witness. Reinforces the continuing duty of disclosure (sec. 29-1918). In felony, misdemeanor, or ordinance cases carrying possible imprisonment, courts may order the prosecuting attorney to produce electronic/digital evidence (devices, computers, or analyses) or permit defendants to run comparable tests with their own experts. Defines “computer,” “digital information,” and “electronic communication device” (cross-referencing secs. 28-1343 and 28-833). Requires parties to promptly disclose newly discovered material that is subject to discovery under secs. 29-1912–29-1921 if it was previously requested or court-ordered. Government entities referenced: prosecuting authority/prosecuting attorney and the court. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes included.
