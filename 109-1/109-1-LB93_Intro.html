Summary

LB93 amends Nebraska discovery statutes (sections 29-1912, 29-1913, 29-1918) to expand prosecutorial disclosure and defense access to digital evidence. Key agency roles and effects:

- Prosecuting attorneys: must disclose reports developed or received by law enforcement agencies that directly relate to investigations, provide names/qualifications/opinions of expert witnesses, and make electronic communication devices, computers, and digital information available for defense testing or analysis.

- Law enforcement agencies: reports and digital materials created or received by them are subject to discovery.

- Courts: retain discretion to order time/place/manner for defense testing and to consider safety/coercion concerns; appellate access to sealed prosecutorial showings is preserved.

- Prosecutorial representatives: negligent or intentional alteration of evidence (beyond necessary testing) can bar the prosecution’s test results from admission.

Jailhouse informants remain governed by sections 29-4701 to 29-4706. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds mandatory disclosure by the prosecuting authority of any expert witness: name, qualifications, and a complete statement of opinions including their basis and reasons. Affirms a continuing duty of disclosure under §29‑1918. Authorizes the trial court to order the prosecuting attorney to provide electronic communication devices, computers, digital information, or their scientific analyses to the defendant or to allow the defendant to conduct comparable tests with defense experts. Defines "computer," "digital information," and "electronic communication device" (cross‑referencing §§28‑1343 and 28‑833). Requires prompt disclosure of newly discovered evidence if it is subject to discovery under §§29‑1912 to 29‑1921 and was previously requested or court‑ordered. Agencies/departments implicated: prosecuting authority/prosecuting attorney, trial courts, and municipal governments (city/village ordinances). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
