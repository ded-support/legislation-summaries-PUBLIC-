Summary

Creates the Special Education Teacher Forgivable Loan Program, to be administered by "the department" (program administrator). The department may award up to 25 forgivable loans per academic year per eligible institution to cover remaining in-state tuition (or equivalent) after federal and state grants/scholarships. Recipients must contract to begin teaching special education in a Nebraska K–12 school within one year of graduation and teach five consecutive years (or the equivalent number of years corresponding to loans received). Loan forgiveness occurs annually (generally 20% per compliant year over five years or pro rata for shorter loan histories). Noncompliance may allow rule-based deferments; otherwise remaining balances accrue simple interest at 5% and repayments are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The State Board of Education may promulgate rules. No explicit appropriation or new funding source is specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Special Education Teacher Forgivable Loan Program to be administered by "the department" (program text) to fund in‑state tuition or equivalent at eligible Nebraska institutions. The department may award up to 25 forgivable loans per academic year per eligible institution, must contract with recipients, calculate annual loan amounts, and forgive loans pro rata (generally 20% per compliant year over five years). Recipients must teach special education in Nebraska K–12 schools for required years or repay with 5% simple interest. Repayments are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The department may grant deferments under adopted rules; the State Board of Education may promulgate program rules. No separate appropriation or specific budget allocation is specified.
