Summary

Creates the Special Education Teacher Forgivable Loan Program to be administered by the department (agency authority to award, contract, forgive, defer, or require repayment). The department may award up to 25 forgivable loans per academic year for each eligible institution (state colleges or the University of Nebraska) to cover in-state tuition after federal and state aid. Recipients must contract to teach special education in Nebraska K–12 (five consecutive years or equivalent). Loan forgiveness occurs annually (20% per year over five years, with pro rata rules for shorter loan histories). Noncompliance may trigger deferment under department rules or repayment with 5% simple interest; repayments are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The State Board of Education may adopt rules.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a Special Education Teacher Forgivable Loan Program administered by "the department" (for awarding loans to eligible students at Nebraska state colleges or the University of Nebraska). The department may award up to 25 forgivable loans per eligible institution each academic year; loans cover in-state tuition remaining after federal and state aid. Recipients sign contracts requiring start of special-education teaching in Nebraska within one year of graduation and five years of service (or equivalent to years of loans) for forgiveness. Forgiveness is 20% per compliant year over five years (or prorated for shorter loan histories). Noncompliance may allow deferment per department rules; otherwise repayments bear simple interest at 5% and are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The State Board of Education may adopt program rules. No specific appropriation amounts specified.
