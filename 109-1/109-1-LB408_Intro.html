Summary

Creates the Special Education Teacher Forgivable Loan Program Act to be administered by "the department" (administrative authority designated in the bill). The department may award forgivable loans to eligible students attending Nebraska state colleges or the University of Nebraska (up to 25 loans per academic year per eligible institution). Loans cover remaining in‑state tuition after federal/state aid and require a contract obligating recipients to begin teaching special education in Nebraska within one year of graduation and teach five consecutive years (or an equivalent number of years). The department manages forgiveness (20% per compliant year over five years or pro rata if fewer loan years), deferments by rule, and collection with 5% simple interest on unpaid balances. Repayments are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The State Board of Education may adopt rules. No specific appropriation or funding source is provided.
