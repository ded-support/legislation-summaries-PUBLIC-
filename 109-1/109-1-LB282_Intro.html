Summary

LB282 (2025) authorizes the State Department of Education to accept applications, on department-prescribed forms, from K–12 teachers employed at approved or accredited public, private, denominational, or parochial Nebraska schools for reimbursement of up to $300 for teacher‑purchased school supplies (receipts required), effective for the 2025–26 school year. The State Board of Education must develop and post a policy on qualifying supplies and may adopt rules and regulations to implement the program. The bill contains an emergency clause to take effect upon passage. No specific appropriation or funding source is stated.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Beginning with the 2025–26 school year, teachers in approved or accredited K–12 public, private, denominational, or parochial schools may apply to the State Department of Education (on department-prescribed forms) for reimbursement up to $300 for school supplies, with receipts required. The State Board of Education must create a policy (to be posted on the State Department of Education website) defining qualifying supplies and may adopt rules and regulations to implement the program. The provision specifies the reimbursement amount but does not identify funding sources or appropriate additional appropriations.
