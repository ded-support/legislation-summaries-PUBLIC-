Summary

LB256 adopts the Midwest Interstate Passenger Rail Compact and creates a multistate commission to coordinate and advocate for Midwestern intercity and high‑speed passenger rail. Membership includes each state’s Governor (or designee), a gubernatorial private‑sector appointee, and two legislators. The commission will work with federal agencies, members of Congress, local elected officials, economic development organizations, and state departments/municipalities; may establish an office, hire staff, contract services, assess dues, accept federal/state/local grants and gifts, and must report annually to member Governors and legislatures. Finance: general operations are to be funded by appropriations from compacting states (equal apportionment) when authorized by each legislature; the commission must submit budgets to the officer designated by each party state’s law. Nebraska restrictions: the state may not participate in or appropriate funds for any compact rail project (including planning) unless a legislative bill specifically authorizes it. The bill creates the Midwest Interstate Passenger Rail Compact Cash Fund; the Nebraska State Investment Officer will invest fund balances per state investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Midwest Interstate Passenger Rail Compact commission funded by equal appropriations from compacting states (when authorized by each legislature). The commission may accept donations, grants, gifts or appropriations from the federal government, any party state, or any department, agency, or municipality, but may not issue debt. It must submit budgets to the officer designated by each member state’s law. Nebraska-specific provisions bar the State of Nebraska from participating in or appropriating funds for any rail project (including planning) unless a legislative bill authorizes it. Establishes the Midwest Interstate Passenger Rail Compact Cash Fund (gifts, grants, bequests, or legislative transfers) to support compact initiatives; funds are investable by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.
