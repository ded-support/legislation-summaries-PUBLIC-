Summary

The bill repeatedly cites the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA): it defines “eligible breakfast” and “eligible lunch” as meals reimbursable under USDA regulations (Child Nutrition Act and Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act, as of Jan 1, 2023); it defines “federal reimbursement rate” as the payment levels published by the USDA; and it adopts USDA concepts such as the community eligibility provision and identified student percentage.

On funding, the Legislature states its intent to appropriate General Fund monies to implement the Hunger‑Free Schools Act. The State Department of Education is directed to reimburse qualified schools annually—using formulas tied to USDA reimbursement rates and set per‑meal amounts—to offset the cost of providing free meals.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions assign duties or funding to the state Department of Agriculture. The added text instead references the United States Department of Agriculture only for federal program definitions and federal reimbursement rates for school meals. Administration and reimbursements are assigned to the State Department of Education, which must reimburse qualified schools per specified formulas and the Legislature states its intent to appropriate General Fund money to implement the Hunger‑Free Schools Act. No direct changes to Department of Agriculture budgets, appropriations, or responsibilities are included.
