Summary

LB176 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-1304 (Nebraska Broadband Bridge Act). It continues to vest grant administration in "the commission" (grant deadlines, speed‑test requirements, and enforcement) and requires applications from political subdivisions or Indian tribes to be public–private partnerships with a provider. New application requirements mandate a resolution of project support adopted by each county, city, and village in the project area that includes a project map, permit acknowledgement, and timeline. Matching funds remain required (50% outside high‑cost areas; 25% inside; federal funds excluded from the match). Grants must be completed in 18 months or face graduated repayment. Applicants using equipment on the FCC Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau "Covered List" are ineligible. No new appropriations or statewide budget reallocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that applicants include a resolution of project support adopted by each county, city, and village where the project will be located. Each resolution must at minimum include: (a) a map of the proposed project area; (b) an acknowledgement that the applicant will apply for appropriate permits; and (c) a proposed project timeline. No specific state or federal agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
