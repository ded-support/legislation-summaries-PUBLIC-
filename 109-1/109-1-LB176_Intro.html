Summary

LB176 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-1304 (Nebraska Broadband Bridge Act). Applicants (providers, cooperatives, political subdivisions, Indian tribes) apply to the commission (which sets deadlines and posts them online) for grants for broadband development. Key agency-related changes/requirements:

- Political subdivisions and Indian tribes must apply via public–private partnership with a provider.

- Applications must include resolutions of project support adopted by each county, city, and village in the project area containing a project map, permit acknowledgement, and timeline.

- The commission requires matching funds (50% outside high-cost areas; 25% inside; federal funds exempt), 18-month completion with commission-approved extensions, speed-testing and reporting to the commission, and repayment schedules/penalties for missed deadlines or inadequate speeds.

- Grants are barred for use of equipment on the FCC Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau’s Covered List pursuant to the federal Secure and Trusted Communications Networks Act. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that applicants submit a resolution of project support adopted by each county, city, and village where the project will be located. Each resolution must include: (a) a map of the project area; (b) an acknowledgement that the applicant will apply for appropriate permits; and (c) a proposed project timeline. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
