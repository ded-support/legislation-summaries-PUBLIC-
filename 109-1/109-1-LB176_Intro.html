Summary

LB176 amends §86‑1304 of the Nebraska Broadband Bridge Act, updating application and project requirements overseen by the commission. Key agency-related provisions:

- Applicants (providers, cooperatives, political subdivisions, Indian tribes) apply to the commission; political subdivision or tribal applicants must participate via a public‑private partnership with a provider.

- Matching funds: 50% of development costs outside high‑cost areas, 25% inside; federal funds are exempt from matching.

- Each county, city, and village in the project area must adopt a resolution of project support (must include project map, acknowledgment of permit applications, and proposed timeline).

- The commission sets application deadlines and enforces an 18‑month completion target (with extension rules), repayment schedules for missed deadlines, and required one‑week speed tests; failure after remediation triggers grant repayment.

- Grants are barred for projects using equipment on the FCC’s Covered List (Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau). No specific appropriations or new funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an application requirement that the applicant submit a resolution of project support adopted by each county, city, and village where the project will be located. Each resolution must, at minimum, include: (a) a map of the proposed project area; (b) an acknowledgement that the applicant will apply for appropriate permits; and (c) a proposed project timeline. No state agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are specified.
