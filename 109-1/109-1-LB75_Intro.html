Summary

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Labor.

- Requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, by October 15 each year beginning 2026, the CPI-U–based minimum wage rate that will take effect January 1.

- States employees may submit complaints to the department; the department must forward credible complaints to the appropriate county attorney or pursue its own enforcement and shall develop forms and adopt rules and regulations to implement this process.

- Provides that records and information obtained by the department under the gratuity-records provisions are confidential and discloseable only by court order.

- Requires employer-posted summaries to advise employees how to submit complaints to the department.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds Department of Labor authority and employer obligations for tipped‑employee wage enforcement. Employers must keep 3 years of records (name, address, occupation, rate of pay, pay per period, daily and weekly hours), keep them on premises or make them available on demand (including if kept out of state), and provide sworn statements on commissioner forms. The Department may inspect by appointment, treat obtained records as confidential (not public; disclosure only by court order), develop forms and adopt rules, accept employee complaints, forward credible complaints to county attorneys or pursue enforcement, and must advise employees how to file complaints. Employers are barred from obstructing the commissioner, falsifying/withholding records, retaliating, or paying wages below law. Violations are treated as separate offenses (daily or per week for underpaying) with liquidated damages equal to unpaid wages. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
