Summary

LB75 repeatedly references the Department of Labor and the Commissioner of Labor. Key provisions:

- Defines “Department” as the Department of Labor and “Commissioner” as the Commissioner of Labor.

- Requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish the CPI‑based annual minimum wage by October 15 each year, beginning October 15, 2026.

- Grants the commissioner authority to approve certain training‑wage programs, subpoena records and witnesses, inspect records, and gather testimony.

- Requires employers to make records available to the commissioner (including by demand if kept outside the state) and authorizes the department to receive confidential records (disclosable only by court order).

- Permits employees to file complaints with the department; the department shall forward credible complaints to county attorneys or pursue enforcement and must develop forms and rules.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions identifying "Commissioner" as the Commissioner of Labor and "Department" as the Department of Labor. Requires employers of tipped employees to retain for 3 years records listing employee name, address, occupation, rate of pay, pay per period, and daily/weekly hours; records must be kept on-site or at a suitable location and made available to the Commissioner (out‑of‑state records must be produced on demand). Employers must provide sworn statements on forms the Commissioner prescribes and any nonprivileged information needed for enforcement. All records obtained by the Department are confidential, are not public records, and are disclosable only by court order. The Department may accept complaints from employees (in a manner it prescribes), must develop forms and rules, forward credible complaints to county attorneys or take enforcement itself, and may inspect workplaces. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
