Summary

LB557 amends multiple education statutes. Key agencies/departments and fiscal changes:

- State Department of Education (NDE): will certify school-building percentages qualifying for free/reduced meals (including a 1.6 multiplier for community eligibility) and reimburse resident districts for transportation of option students with disabilities per §79-1144.

- State Board of Education: hears appeals of option-enrollment rejections; option districts must provide written rejection reasons and appeal procedures.

- Commissioner of Education: prescribes option application/withdrawal forms; option districts must notify applicants within ten days.

- School boards: must adopt and publish student-fee policies; may collect fees including transportation per §§79-605/79-611.

- Learning community coordinating council: retained levy and fund-administration authorities (including focus program capital, early childhood, and other specified expenditures) and must administer learning community funds (§79-2111).

- Funding/appropriations: parents of students denied option enrollment become eligible for a $2,500 educational support payment; net option funding formula revised to include converted contract option students and, from FY2023-24, equals net option count × (statewide average basic funding per formula student minus foundation aid). Payments for earlier years referenced to funds under §79-1005.01 and count as a formula resource.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that option-enrollment applications may be submitted at any time and requires district selection standards to be limited to a random selection process and the interests of the student and parent/guardian. Prohibits use of residential address, district capacity measures, or any measures that discriminate by race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status or other protected classes. Establishes a process reference for requesting an "educational support payment" and makes a parent/guardian whose option-enrollment application is rejected eligible for a $2,500 payment for the applicable school year. Defines "student who contributes to the socioeconomic diversity of enrollment" using free/reduced-price lunch certifications pursuant to section 79-2120 and the community eligibility provision. Mentions school districts and learning communities and cites section 79-769.
