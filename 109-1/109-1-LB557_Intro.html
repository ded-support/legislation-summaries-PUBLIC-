Summary

LB557 amends K–12 statutes and assigns roles and financial responsibilities primarily to the State Department of Education, the State Board of Education, the Commissioner of Education, learning community coordinating councils, and local school boards/districts. Key agency actions and funding changes:

- State Department of Education: will reimburse resident districts for transportation costs for option students with disabilities (per §79-1144) and annually certify school-building free/reduced-price lunch percentages for learning communities (§79-2120).

- State Board of Education: adjudicates appeals of option-enrollment rejections.

- Commissioner of Education: prescribes option application and withdrawal forms.

- Learning community coordinating councils: retain levy and fund‑administration authority for certain facilities, focus-program capital, early‑childhood and diversion programs, and must report diversity metrics to the Legislature.

- Funding changes: parents of students denied option enrollment eligible for a $2,500 educational support payment; net option funding formula is revised for FY2023-24 onward to equal net option students multiplied by (statewide average basic funding per formula student minus foundation aid per formula student).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements and definitions affecting school districts and learning communities. Key points:

- School districts must use a random selection process and consider student/parent interest; may not use measures that discriminate by race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status, or other protected classes. Factors listed include residential address and district capacity.

- Establishes a process (referenced to subsection (3)) for requesting an educational support payment.

- Parents/guardians whose option enrollment application is rejected are eligible for a $2,500 educational support payment for the applicable school year.

- Defines “student who contributes to the socioeconomic diversity of enrollment” using free/reduced-price lunch certification under section 79-2120 and the federal community eligibility provision.

Agencies referenced: local school districts and learning communities; certification tied to section 79-2120 (state education certification). No other appropriations beyond the $2,500 payment are specified.
