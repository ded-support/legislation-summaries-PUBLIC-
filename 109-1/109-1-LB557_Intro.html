Summary

LB557 amends multiple education statutes. Key agency roles and fiscal changes:

- Commissioner of Education: prescribes option application and withdrawal forms.

- State Board of Education: hears appeals of rejected enrollment-option applications.

- State Department of Education: reimburses resident districts for transportation of option students with disabilities under section 79-1144 and annually certifies school-building percentages qualifying for free/reduced meals to learning communities and member districts.

- School boards: continue authority over student fee policies and transportation charges.

- Learning community coordinating councils: authority to administer funds under 79-2111, levy for specified purposes (historical reference to pre‑2017–18 levies), fund juvenile-justice diversion programming, and hold public hearings.

Fiscal changes: establishes a $2,500 educational support payment to parents when an option enrollment application is rejected; revises net option funding (FY2023‑24 onward) to equal net option students × (statewide average basic funding per formula student minus foundation aid per formula student); transportation allowance rules clarified (285% of state mileage rate) and excluded for learning‑community students who qualify for free/reduced lunches.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions affect school districts and learning communities. Key points:

- If a student's option-enrollment application is rejected by the school district, the parent or legal guardian is eligible for a $2,500 educational support payment for that school year; the bill adds a process for requesting that payment (subsection (3)).

- Districts must use a random selection process and consider student and parent/guardian interest when handling enrollments.

- The bill defines "student who contributes to socioeconomic diversity" using certification under section 79-2120 (free/reduced-price lunch percentages and the community eligibility provision) to compare school-building rates to the learning community average.

- Statutory references include sections 79-2120 and 79-769. No other state agencies or new appropriations beyond the $2,500 payment are specified in the added text.
