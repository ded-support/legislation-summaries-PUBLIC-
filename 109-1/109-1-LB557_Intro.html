Summary

LB557 amends multiple education statutes. Agencies and bodies affected include the State Department of Education, State Board of Education, Commissioner of Education, learning community coordinating councils (and achievement subcouncils), school boards, resident and option school districts, and the Legislature’s Education Committee.

Key provisions affecting agencies and funding:

- Option districts must notify parents/resident districts (within ten days); the Commissioner prescribes application/withdrawal forms; rejected applicants receive written reasons and appeal instructions to the State Board.

- Parents of students denied option enrollment are eligible for a $2,500 educational support payment for the applicable school year.

- Resident districts must provide transportation for option students with disabilities; the State Department of Education reimburses those costs per existing statute (79-1144).

- Net option funding formula (FY2023-24 onward) is revised: net option funding = net option students × (statewide average basic funding per formula student − foundation aid per formula student).

- The State Department must annually certify school-building free/reduced-price lunch percentages (community eligibility identified student percentage multiplied by 1.6).

- Learning community coordinating councils retain levy and fund-administration authorities and must report to the Education Committee.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures tied to option enrollment and educational support payments. School districts must use a random selection process that considers student/parent interest and may not use criteria that discriminate (race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status, protected classes); listed factors include residential address and district capacity. It creates a process for requesting an educational support payment pursuant to subsection (3). If a student's option enrollment application is rejected by the district, the parent or guardian is eligible for a $2,500 education-support payment for that school year. The changes reference certification under section 79-2120 and section 79-769 (learning community/school building data). No explicit appropriation language appears.
