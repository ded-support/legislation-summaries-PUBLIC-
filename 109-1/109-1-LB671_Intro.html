Summary

LB671 permits the State Department of Education to work with the Department of Labor (and other entities) to develop and implement the Nebraska Teacher Apprenticeship Program. The program uses an apprenticeship model leading to a Commissioner‑issued teaching certificate for completers. The bill expresses legislative intent to appropriate $1,000,000 annually from the Education Future Fund beginning FY2023‑24 to the State Department of Education for the program.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The additions require Boards and the Commissioner of Education to expand reports under §79-528, §79-237, and §79-11,157.01 to include detailed information on K–3 students who display reading deficiencies (including dyslexia characteristics), with counts by grade and by school, and to adopt accreditation rule provisions pursuant to §§79-318 and 79-703.
