Summary

The bill authorizes the State Department of Education to create and administer the Nebraska Teacher Apprenticeship Program and explicitly allows the department to work with the Department of Labor (and others) to develop and implement the program. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $1,000,000 from the Education Future Fund to the State Department of Education for the program for FY2023‑24 and each fiscal year thereafter.
