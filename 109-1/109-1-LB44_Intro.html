Summary

LB44 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29-3001 (postconviction relief). It preserves court procedures: the court that imposed sentence (district court) must serve the county attorney, grant a prompt hearing unless records show no relief, may decide motions without producing the prisoner, and may bar successive similar motions. The one-year filing limitation for verified postconviction motions now runs from the latest of specified events, including a new trigger: the date the prisoner reaches 21 years of age. Other triggers reference the judgment becoming final, discovery of factual predicate, removal of state-created impediments, recognition of new constitutional rules by the U.S. or Nebraska Supreme Court (if made retroactive), and a procedural notice requirement tied to petitions for certiorari to the U.S. Supreme Court (filed in the district court within 30 days). No agencies receive new funding or appropriations.
