Summary

LB44 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29‑3001 governing postconviction relief. It preserves procedural duties of the court that imposed sentence (including serving the county attorney, holding prompt hearings, taxing costs as in habeas cases, and entertaining motions without producing the prisoner) and retains limits on successive motions. The bill revises the one‑year limitations rule: the period runs from the latest of several listed triggers and now explicitly includes (f) the date the prisoner turns 21. It also clarifies subdivision (e) to apply when the U.S. Supreme Court denies certiorari or affirms a Nebraska Supreme Court decision and requires filing a notice in the district court within 30 days after petitioning for certiorari. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new clause (f) specifying "the date on which the prisoner reaches twenty-one years of age" as an applicable date. No government agencies or departments are referenced and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
