Summary

LB44 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29-3001 governing postconviction relief. It preserves that motions are filed in the sentencing court, that notice must be served on the county attorney, and that proceedings are civil with costs taxed as in habeas corpus cases. The one-year limitation for filing a verified postconviction motion is retained but its triggering events are clarified/expanded: timing may run from the conclusion of direct appeal, discovery of the factual predicate, removal of a state-created impediment, recognition of a new constitutional right by the Nebraska Supreme Court or U.S. Supreme Court (if made retroactive), denial of certiorari or affirmance by the U.S. Supreme Court (with a 30-day district court notice requirement after petitioning for certiorari), or the date the prisoner turns 21. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection (f) specifying that the relevant date is the date the prisoner reaches 21 years of age. No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or affected.
