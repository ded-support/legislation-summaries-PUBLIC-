Summary

LB234 (consolidated with Sec. 77-6906, R.R.S.) amends the Urban Redevelopment Act’s definition of “economic redevelopment area.” It directs use of data from the United States Bureau of the Census (most recent federal decennial census or American Community Survey 5‑Year Estimate) to identify qualifying areas where (1) the area’s unemployment rate is at least 150% of the Nebraska statewide rate and (2) the federal census tract’s poverty rate is 20% or higher. The bill also includes any federal census tract directly adjacent to such an area. It repeals the original section. No state agency funding changes, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision expanding the covered area to include any federal census tract directly adjacent to the area described in subdivision (1) (which itself is subdivided into (a) and (b)). No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
