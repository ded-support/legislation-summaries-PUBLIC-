Summary

LB234 (Urban Redevelopment Act) amends section 77-6906 to redefine "economic redevelopment area." The definition now requires (1) an area whose unemployment rate, per the most recent federal decennial census or American Community Survey 5‑Year Estimate by the United States Bureau of the Census, is at least 150% of the statewide average, and (2) a federal census tract poverty rate of 20% or more. The bill also explicitly includes any federal census tract directly adjacent to such an area. The original section is repealed. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or provided in the bill.
