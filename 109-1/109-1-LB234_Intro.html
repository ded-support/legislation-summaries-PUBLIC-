Summary

LB234 (Conrad) amends the Urban Redevelopment Act by redefining "economic redevelopment area" in section 77-6906, Revised Statutes Cumulative Supplement, 2024. The bill bases eligibility on federal data from the United States Bureau of the Census (most recent decennial census or American Community Survey 5‑Year Estimate): an area qualifies if its unemployment rate is at least 150% of the Nebraska average for the same period and the federal census tract's poverty rate is at least 20%. It also includes any federal census tract directly adjacent to such an area. The original section 77-6906 is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
