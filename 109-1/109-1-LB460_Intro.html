Summary

LB460 includes multiple statutory transfers from the State Settlement Cash Fund to other funds to be executed "on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services." Those directed transfers are: $876,998 to the General Fund (on or before June 30, 2018); $1,756,639 to the General Fund (on or before June 30, 2019); $125,000 to the Legal Education for Public Service and Rural Practice Loan Repayment Assistance Fund (on or before April 30, 2018); $150,000 to that same loan repayment fund (on or before July 9, 2018); and $600,000 to the newly created Nebraska Council on Economic Education Cash Fund (on or before July 1, 2025).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Council on Economic Education Cash Fund for University of Nebraska Board of Regents expenses, to be invested by the state investment officer. The Legislature intends to appropriate $300,000 from this cash fund to the Board of Regents in FY2025-26 and $300,000 in FY2026-27. The State Treasurer is directed to transfer $600,000 from the State Settlement Cash Fund into the new cash fund on or before July 1, 2025; the timing and amounts of that transfer are to be set by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services. No direct appropriations to DAS are made.
