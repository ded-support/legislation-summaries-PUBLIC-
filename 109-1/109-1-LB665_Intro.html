Summary

LB665 amends the Animal Health and Disease Control Act. It defines "foreign adversary" by reference to 15 C.F.R. 791.4 (as of Jan 1, 2025). The bill: adopts 9 C.F.R. part 86 (animal disease traceability, as of Jan 1, 2020) by reference but gives the state act, rules, or Department of Agriculture orders precedence if inconsistent; authorizes use of USDA/APHIS/VS program standards; and clarifies who may apply official identification devices (owner, owner's representative, accredited veterinarian, approved tagging site) and limited removal circumstances. Crucially, it prohibits application of electronic identification devices manufactured by entities with direct or indirect ties to a foreign adversary and prohibits the Nebraska Department of Agriculture from requiring use of foreign‑manufactured electronic ID devices. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "foreign adversary" by reference to 15 C.F.R. 791.4 (as of Jan 1, 2025). Establishes state policy prioritizing protection/conservation of livestock and private property rights over government bureaucracy, and frames foreign adversaries using surveillance tech as a security threat. Prohibits within the state the use or application of electronic identification devices for livestock if manufactured by entities with direct or indirect ties to a foreign adversary. Further bars "the department" (unnamed in the excerpt) from requiring use of foreign‑manufactured electronic animal identification for identifying or tracking livestock. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
