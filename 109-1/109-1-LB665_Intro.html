Summary — LB665 (2025)

LB665 amends the Animal Health and Disease Control Act. It (1) adopts by reference federal animal disease traceability rules (9 C.F.R. part 86, as of Jan. 1, 2020) and authorizes the Nebraska Department of Agriculture to employ USDA APHIS Veterinary Services program standards for disease prevention and control; (2) defines "foreign adversary" by reference to 15 C.F.R. 791.4 (as of Jan. 1, 2025); (3) prohibits use in Nebraska of electronic identification devices manufactured by entities with direct or indirect ties to such foreign adversaries and bars the Department of Agriculture from requiring foreign‑manufactured electronic ID devices; and (4) restates related legislative findings. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "foreign adversary" by reference to 15 C.F.R. 791.4 (as of Jan 1, 2025). Establishes Nebraska policy: protect and conserve livestock, prioritize private property rights over government bureaucracy, and treat foreign actors using surveillance tech against livestock production as a security threat. Prohibits application of any electronic identification device for livestock if manufactured by an entity with direct or indirect ties to a foreign adversary. Further bars "the department" (the state agency administering animal identification) from requiring use of foreign‑manufactured electronic animal identification devices to identify or track livestock. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
