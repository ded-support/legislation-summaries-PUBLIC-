Summary

LB515 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §38‑28,104 (Pharmacy Practice Act). It specifies required information on non‑controlled (legend) drug prescriptions before dispensing (patient identifier or “for emergency use”/“for use in immunizations,” drug name, strength, dosage form, quantity, authorized refills, directions, date, prescriber's name, and written signature if applicable). It also allows pharmacists to provide an emergency refill (up to a 30‑day supply or the most recent refill amount, whichever is less) when refill authorization cannot be obtained, provided: (1) the pharmacist documents the prescription from a label, pharmacy record, or common database; (2) the drug is not a controlled substance; (3) it is a maintenance medication; and (4) interruption of therapy would harm the patient. No state agencies or appropriations are named or altered.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for pharmacists, when unable to promptly obtain refill authorization from the prescriber, to dispense an emergency refill up to a 30‑day supply (or the amount of the most recent refill, if less) provided they: obtain prescription information from a prescription label, any pharmacy’s prescription record, or a common database; the drug is not a controlled substance; the drug is a maintenance medication; and, in the pharmacist’s professional judgment, interruption of therapy would be harmful. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
