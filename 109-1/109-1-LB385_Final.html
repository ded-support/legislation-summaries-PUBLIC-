Summary

LB385 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §87-302 (Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act) to expand the list of prohibited deceptive practices—adding items on privacy-policy misstatements, unwanted installation or blocking of file‑sharing programs, deceptive substance labeling, unsolicited checks/billing, expanded anti‑pyramid/chain referral rules, prohibitions on publishing or promoting sexually explicit or obscene material involving minors, trafficking victims, or lack of consent, and inclusion of right-to-list home sale agreements. The bill references other statutes for definitions (e.g., 47 U.S.C. §254; Neb. §§28-807, 28-830, 28-1463.02, 28-318, 81-885.01) and excludes telecommunications/broadband Internet access services from the sexual‑content prohibition. No state agencies or departments are created, named, or reassigned responsibilities, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes. The original §87-302 is repealed.
