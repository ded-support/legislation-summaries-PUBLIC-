Summary

This bill designates the Commissioner of Labor and the Department of Labor as the administering authorities for the new Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act. Health care staffing agencies must register annually with the Department and pay a $1,500 annual registration fee per Nebraska location (or a single $1,500 statewide fee for technology platforms without a physical Nebraska location) payable to the Department. The Commissioner may receive appeals, investigate complaints, conduct random audits, issue subpoenas, and be represented by Department counsel or the Attorney General. The Department must maintain a publicly accessible database of registered agencies, may adopt rules, and Section 81-401 is amended to add this Act to the list of laws the Department administers. Operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act" placing primary administration with the Department of Labor and the Commissioner of Labor. Key Dept. of Labor duties: register agencies annually; collect $1,500 annual fees per operating location (or $1,500 statewide for non‑physical health care technology platforms); operate a public complaints system, investigate complaints, conduct random audits, and exercise subpoena and enforcement authority; maintain a publicly accessible database of registered agencies (dates of approval and expiration/revocation); and adopt rules to implement the Act. Appeal and enforcement procedures are assigned to the Commissioner (with Attorney General support available). No separate appropriations or explicit budget allocations are included; fee revenue is the only funding mechanism specified.
