Summary

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Labor and "Commissioner" as the Commissioner of Labor.

- Requires health care staffing agencies to register annually with the Department of Labor and certify certain conditions.

- Imposes an annual registration fee of $1,500 per Nebraska location (or one $1,500 statewide fee for technology platforms without a physical Nebraska location) payable to the Department.

- Grants the Commissioner authority to receive and investigate public complaints, conduct investigations and random audits, administer oaths, issue and enforce subpoenas, and pursue enforcement actions (with legal representation by Department attorneys or the Attorney General).

- Directs the Department to create and publish a public database of registered agencies and authorizes the Department to adopt rules and regulations.

- Adds the Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act to the Department’s administrative duties. Operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Labor focus):

- Establishes the Commissioner of Labor and the Department of Labor as the registering authority for health care staffing agencies under the new Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act.

- Agencies must annually register with the Department and certify they will not enforce pre‑registration noncompete clauses.

- Annual registration fee: $1,500 per separate Nebraska location (health care technology platforms without a Nebraska physical location pay $1,500 for a statewide application). Renewal applications due ≥60 days before expiration; appeals of citations to the Commissioner within 20 days.

- Department duties: create a public complaint system, investigate complaints, conduct random audits (≥3 business days’ notice), subpoena and compel documents/witnesses, and maintain a public online registration database (including approval and expiration/revocation dates).

- Department may promulgate rules; legal representation provided by departmental attorneys or Attorney General.

- No other appropriations or budget allocations specified.
