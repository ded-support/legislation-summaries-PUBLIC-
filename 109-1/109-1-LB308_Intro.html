Summary

This bill designates the Commissioner of Labor as the enforcing official and the Department of Labor as the administering agency for the new Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act. It requires annual registration with the department and payment of a $1,500 per‑location (or per state for certain technology platforms) registration fee to the Department of Labor. The commissioner must establish a public complaint reporting system, investigate complaints, conduct random audits, and has subpoena and enforcement authority; appeals go to the commissioner and follow the Administrative Procedure Act. The Department of Labor must create and publish a public database of registered agencies on its website and may adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act. Section 81-401 is amended to add the Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act to the department’s administered laws. The Act becomes operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act vesting primary authority in the Department of Labor (Commissioner = Commissioner of Labor). Key Department duties: administer annual registrations (fee $1,500 per operating location; health‑tech platforms without a physical Nebraska location pay $1,500 statewide), process renewals, send notices, and maintain a public online database showing approval and expiration/revocation dates. The Commissioner must establish a public complaint system, investigate complaints, conduct compliance investigations and random audits (≥3 business days' notice), and has subpoena and oath powers; civil enforcement may be handled by department attorneys or the Attorney General. The Department may adopt rules to implement the Act. No separate appropriations or budget allocations are specified beyond the registration fees.
