Summary

LB527A appropriates funds to the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services to implement LB527. For Program 344 it provides $5,765,385 from the Medicaid Access and Quality Fund and $12,279,638 federal funds for FY2025‑26, and $11,758,984 (cash) and $25,045,347 (federal) for FY2026‑27 — all designated for state aid only. For Program 348 it provides $57,392,193 (Medicaid Access and Quality Fund) and $105,013,007 (federal) for FY2025‑26, and $117,056,172 (cash) and $214,182,801 (federal) for FY2026‑27 — also for state aid only. Neither appropriation may be used for permanent/temporary salaries or per diems. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
