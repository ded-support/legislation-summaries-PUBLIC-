Summary

LB77 designates "Department" to mean the Department of Insurance and tasks that department with approving uniform prior‑authorization request forms and considering exemption requests from utilization review agents (including for electronic prior authorization methods). The bill also requires the Department of Health and Human Services to post a clear, accessible process on its website for medical‑assistance (Medicaid) recipients and providers to request exceptions to biomarker‑testing coverage policies. The medical assistance program must cover biomarker testing by January 1, 2028. No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Insurance and gives the department authority to approve uniform prior‑authorization request forms and consider exemption requests from utilization review agents that implement standardized electronic prior‑authorization APIs.

- Imposes Department of Insurance standards and oversight on utilization review agents: physician/clinical‑peer reviewers, citation of written clinical review criteria in denials, mandatory provider discussion opportunities, specific decision timelines (shorter by 2028), requirement to honor prior authorizations for 60 days after plan change, and web‑notice obligations.

- Requires the medical assistance program (DHHS) to cover biomarker testing by January 1, 2028, with specified clinical indications, limits on care disruptions, parity for contractors, and an exception request process to be posted on the Department of Health and Human Services website.

- No explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
