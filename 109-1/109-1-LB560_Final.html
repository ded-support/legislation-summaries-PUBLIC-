Summary

LB560 amends §81-3725 to revise the Nebraska tourism grant programs administered by "the commission" and its executive director. Key agency-related provisions:

- The commission continues marketing-assistance grants for national/international-caliber events; applicants must document out-of-state draw, marketing plans, tracking methodologies, and potential press coverage.

- The commission must establish an innovative tourism grants program (marketing, planning, basic support, regional cooperation) and prohibits use of awards for equipment or capital facility development/improvements.

- Eligibility for innovative grants is expanded to include projects that increase nonlocal, in-state visitors or both nonlocal in-state and out-of-state visitors.

- Funding cap: the commission may award up to $500,000 in innovative tourism grants to any city or village per fiscal year.

- The executive director will convene a technical review committee (minimum three members representing public sector, private sector, and citizens) to score applications and forward recommendations to the commission. Grants recipients must submit final reports within 90 days; the commission shall adopt rules. The original section is repealed.

Adds a requirement that "the commission" administering the innovative tourism grant program may award up to $500,000 in such grants to any eligible city or village per fiscal year. No other agencies or appropriation sources are specified.
