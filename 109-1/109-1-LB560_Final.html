Summary

LB560 amends section 81-3725 to revise the grant programs administered by "the commission" (via its executive director). It retains marketing-assistance grants for national/international-caliber events and expands/clarifies "innovative tourism grants" to support communities or organizations that promote tourism, host events, or increase nonlocal (in-state and/or out-of-state) visitation. Eligible uses include marketing, planning, basic support, and regional cooperation; grants may not fund equipment or capital facilities. The bill caps awards at up to $500,000 in innovative tourism grant funding to any single city or village per fiscal year. The executive director must convene a technical review committee to score applications and forward recommendations to the commission. Recipients must file a final report within 90 days; the commission will adopt rules. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision requiring "the commission" (the program administrator) to award innovative tourism grants of up to $500,000 to any single qualifying city or village each fiscal year. This establishes an annual per‑city/village funding cap; no specific new appropriation source or department beyond "the commission" is named.
