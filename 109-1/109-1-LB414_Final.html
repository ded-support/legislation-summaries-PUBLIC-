Summary

LB414 creates a statewide suicide mortality review team and requires it to: establish a cooperative agreement with the Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System (NVDRS) for data sharing/storage; obtain records from coroners, medical providers, schools, EMS, prescription monitoring, corrections/probation, law enforcement, and employers; and prepare an annual report. County attorneys or the Attorney General may issue subpoenas at a director's request to compel records, which must be entered into NVDRS and provided to the team as aggregate narrative. Law enforcement, with prosecuting attorney approval, may withhold investigative records that would jeopardize a pending prosecution. Review teams must be given requested information within five business days. All review-team materials are confidential and generally exempt from subpoena/discovery. Annual reports are due April 1 (first due 2026) to the Clerk of the Legislature and the Department of Health and Human Services. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds required collaboration and data-sharing between suicide mortality review teams and the Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System, including storing subpoenaed records there in aggregate form. Authorizes the director to request subpoenas issued by a county attorney or the Attorney General to obtain records (death investigations, autopsies, law‑enforcement/probation/corrections reports, medical and mental‑health records, school and employment records, PDMP data, EMS data, toxicology, next‑of‑kin interviews, etc.). Requires agencies to provide requested records within five business days unless a prosecuting attorney approves withholding for active criminal investigations. Establishes confidentiality and evidentiary protections for review team materials and participants. Requires annual electronic reports by April 1, 2026 and each April 1 thereafter to the Clerk of the Legislature, Department of Health and Human Services, and nonprofits, with specified data elements and recommendations. No funding or appropriation changes included.
