Summary

Creates a statewide suicide mortality review team with authority to review suicide deaths and to establish a cooperative agreement with the Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System for sharing and storing review data. Upon request by the bill’s referenced "director," a county attorney or the Attorney General may issue subpoenas for records (coroner and autopsy reports, law‑enforcement records, medical and mental‑health records, EMS data, PDMP data, toxicology, employment, school records, etc.). Agencies must provide requested records within five business days; law enforcement, with prosecuting attorney approval, may withhold records that would jeopardize a pending criminal investigation. All review team records are confidential and exempt from subpoena/discovery/introduction into evidence; team members are protected from compelled testimony about meetings. Annual reports (beginning April 1, 2026) are required to the Clerk of the Legislature, Department of Health and Human Services, and others. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for suicide mortality review teams to enter a cooperative agreement with the Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System (NVDRS) to share and store review-related data. At the director's request, a county attorney or the Attorney General may subpoena records (e.g., coroner, autopsy, law enforcement, medical, probation/corrections, EMS, prescription monitoring) which must be entered into NVDRS and provided to the review team as an aggregate narrative. Review teams must be given requested records within five business days; law enforcement may withhold investigative records with prosecuting attorney approval. All review materials are confidential and generally immune from subpoena or use in proceedings; team members are protected from testimony about review deliberations. Beginning April 1, 2026, and annually, teams must electronically report specified decedent data to the Clerk of the Legislature and the Department of Health and Human Services. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
