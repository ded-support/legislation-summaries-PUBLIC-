Summary

Creates a statewide suicide mortality review team and requires it to (1) enter a cooperative agreement with the Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System (NVDRS) to share/store review data, and (2) submit an annual electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature, the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and nonprofit organizations beginning April 1, 2026, listing specified demographic and cause data for reviewed suicides and the count of suicides not reviewed. Upon request by "the director," a county attorney or the Attorney General may issue subpoenas to compel records (coroner reports, autopsies, law enforcement/probation/corrections records, medical and mental‑health records, EMS, PDMP data, toxicology, school and employment records, next‑of‑kin interviews) to be entered into NVDRS as aggregate narratives. Agencies must provide requested records within five business days; prosecuting attorneys may approve withholding investigative records. Review team records are confidential and generally immune from discovery or admissibility. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for suicide mortality review teams to enter into a cooperative agreement with the Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System (NVDPS) for sharing/storing review data. Allows the director to request subpoenas issued by a county attorney or the Attorney General to compel records (to be entered into NVDPS and provided to the team in aggregate narrative form). Lists sources review teams may access (coroner/autopsy, law enforcement including probation and corrections, medical and mental‑health records, EMS, prescription drug monitoring data, toxicology, employment, school, next‑of‑kin interviews). Agencies must provide requested records within five business days unless law enforcement, with prosecuting attorney approval, withholds records that would jeopardize a pending criminal matter. Materials obtained by review teams are confidential and generally immune from subpoena/discovery. Teams must submit an annual electronic report by April 1 to the Clerk of the Legislature, the Department of Health and Human Services, and nonprofits. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
