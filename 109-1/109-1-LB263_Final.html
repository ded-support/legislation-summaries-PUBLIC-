Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): The Director of Administrative Services is authorized to draw warrants against appropriate state treasury funds up to the act's appropriations; the State Treasurer shall pay such warrants. Workers' compensation premium assessments are prohibited from being paid from the listed programs. The budget division of DAS may administratively transfer General Fund appropriations and/or salary limits among Supreme Court Programs 3–7 (Programs for judges) upon written certification by the State Court Administrator that such transfers are necessary.

- Department of Revenue: Program No. 13 (Tax Commissioner) appropriations and salary limits—FY2025‑26: General Fund $211,572; Cash Fund $64,212; Program Total $275,784; Salary Limit $212,972. FY2026‑27: General Fund $219,088; Cash Fund $66,436; Program Total $285,524; Salary Limit $219,613. The Tax Commissioner must seek additional legislative appropriation or higher salary limit via the deficit process if amounts are insufficient; DAS may not administratively increase the appropriation or salary limit without legislative authorization.
