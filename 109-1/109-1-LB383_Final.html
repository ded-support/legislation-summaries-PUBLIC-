Summary

LB383 affects the three agencies as follows:

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): must order mental‑health evaluations to determine dangerous sex‑offender status for specified convictions at least 180 days before release; evaluations must be completed no later than 150 days prior, with DCS notifying the Attorney General and county attorneys and providing the evaluator’s affidavit. DCS (with DHHS) provides in‑custody sex‑offender treatment; inability to complete treatment due to timing or unavailable programming is not treated as refusal.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): added to the Public Protection Act so assault on a DHHS employee is a specified offense; designated recipient for sexual‑assault reports when victims are under 18, may receive/share child abuse/neglect reports, and may request the AG’s Child Protection Division investigate when a county attorney declines prosecution.

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): must be queried in forfeiture proceedings for titled motor vehicles to identify record owners and lienholders (out‑of‑state vehicles require analogous inquiry of the appropriate agency).

Budget note: the Attorney General must include budget authority for oversight and representation in district‑court appeals of administrative license revocations (sec. 60‑498.04); no other agency‑specific appropriations were identified.
