Summary

LB582 amends the Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act and the Nebraska Tourism Commission Promotional Cash Fund statute.

- Tax administration: Beginning July 1, 2025, distributors (or operators without a revenue-sharing distributor) must pay a quarterly tax on each cash device; the Tax Commissioner prescribes the reporting form and the department collects the tax.

- Tax rate and allocations: 15% of net operating revenue per device. Receipts are remitted to the State Treasurer and credited: 5% to the Charitable Gaming Operations Fund (enforcement/central server), 2.5% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 20% to the General Fund, 47.5% to the Education Future Fund, 17.5% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund, and the remaining 7.5% to the county treasurer where the device is located (split between county and city/village as applicable).

- Tourism fund: The Nebraska Tourism Commission Promotional Cash Fund will consist of revenues submitted by vendors/retailers under section 81-3728 and advertising sales; the commission administers expenditures under the Nebraska Visitors Development Act and the state investment officer may invest fund balances. Operative Oct. 1, 2025; original sections repealed.
