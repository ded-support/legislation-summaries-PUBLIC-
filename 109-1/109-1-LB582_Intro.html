Summary

LB582 amends the Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act and the Nebraska Tourism Commission Promotional Cash Fund.

Key agency actions and funding changes:

- Department of Revenue/Tax Commissioner: beginning July 1, 2025, imposes a 15% tax on net operating revenue of each cash device, collected quarterly and remitted to the State Treasurer. Exempts fraternal benefit societies and qualified veterans organizations.

- State Treasurer: credits collected tax revenue as follows — 5% to the Charitable Gaming Operations Fund (enforcement/central server), 2.5% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 20% to the General Fund, 47.5% to the Education Future Fund, 17.5% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund, and the remaining 7.5% to the county (split half-to-county/half-to-city if device is inside a municipality).

- Nebraska Tourism Commission/State Investment Officer: clarifies that revenues from sale of advertising are remitted to the Nebraska Tourism Commission Promotional Cash Fund; monies may be invested per state investment statutes.

Operative date: October 1, 2025. Original sections repealed.
