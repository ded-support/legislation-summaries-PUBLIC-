Summary

LB582 amends the Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act and related law. Beginning July 1, 2025, distributors (or operators absent a distributor agreement) must remit a quarterly 15% tax on each cash device’s net operating revenue to the Nebraska Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner), due Jan. 1, Apr. 1, July 1, and Oct. 1. The Tax Commissioner forwards receipts to the State Treasurer for credit: 5% to the Charitable Gaming Operations Fund (for enforcement and central‑server maintenance), 2.5% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 20% to the General Fund, 47.5% to the Education Future Fund, 17.5% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund, and the remaining 7.5% to the county treasurer where the device is located (split with municipalities if applicable). The Nebraska Tourism Commission Promotional Cash Fund is revised to receive revenue from vendors/retailers under §81‑3728 and advertising sales; funds are investible by the State Investment Officer. Operative Oct. 1, 2025.
