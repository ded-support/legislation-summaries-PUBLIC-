Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Labor (Sec. 1(3)). It requires the Department of Labor to maintain a public database of violations of sections 1–9 on its public website (Sec. 6). The department is also authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to carry out sections 1–9 (Sec. 9). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Labor focus):

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Labor.

- The department/commissioner is given investigative and enforcement roles: notify employers of violations, provide a 15‑working‑day contest period and hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act, issue final determinations, and notify licensing agencies.

- The department must maintain a public online database of violations and may adopt rules to implement sections 1–9 consistent with federal E‑Verify and civil‑rights requirements.

- License reinstatement requires demonstration that unauthorized workers were terminated and payment of a reinstatement fee to the commission equal to either investigation/enforcement costs or $1,000; enhanced suspension periods apply for breaches involving five or more unauthorized aliens.

- No explicit new appropriations or budget allocations are included; fees are specified to recoup enforcement costs.
