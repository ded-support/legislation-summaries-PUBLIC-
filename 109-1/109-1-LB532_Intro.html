Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Labor.

- The Department of Labor is directed to maintain a public database of violations of sections 1–9 on its public website.

- The Department of Labor may adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement sections 1–9.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Labor focus):

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Labor and is assigned enforcement and administrative duties for sections 1–9.

- The Department must investigate alleged employer violations of E‑Verify/work‑authorization rules, issue determinations, provide contested‑case hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act, and notify licensing agencies of final violations.

- It must maintain a public online database of violations and may adopt rules to implement the sections consistent with federal law.

- The Department may assess/collect reinstatement fees (greater of its investigation/enforcement costs or $1,000) and set additional suspension periods for violations involving five or more unauthorized aliens.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
