Summary

LB675 amends statutes governing the Board of Educational Lands and Funds (BELF). Key changes: BELF must offer the Indian tribe with jurisdiction the first right of refusal to purchase (72-212) or lease (72-232) state school lands located within reservation boundaries. BELF may enter into sales or exchanges of school lands with an Indian tribe with the Governor’s written approval (72-253) and must determine any sale/exchange within a reservation is not detrimental to state interests. Existing BELF authorities—rulemaking for leases, a soil conservation program, cancellation for noncompliance, and exchanges with the U.S. Government—remain. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The prior versions of the amended sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that when school lands within an Indian reservation are offered for sale or lease, the Indian tribe with jurisdiction has the first right of refusal from the Board of Educational Lands and Funds. The Board is authorized, with the Governor’s written approval, to enter into sale or exchange agreements with tribes for state educational lands (including exchanges for lands of equal area or value). Before selling or exchanging reservation lands, the Board must find the action is not detrimental to state interests. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
