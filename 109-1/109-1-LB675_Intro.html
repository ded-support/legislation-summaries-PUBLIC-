Summary

LB675 amends statutes governing the Board of Educational Lands and Funds to grant an Indian tribe with jurisdiction over reservation lands a first right of refusal to purchase or to lease school lands located within reservation boundaries. The bill authorizes the Board, with the Governor’s written approval, to enter into sales or exchanges with an Indian tribe and requires the Board to determine that any sale or exchange within a reservation is not detrimental to state interests. It retains Board authority to negotiate exchanges with the federal government and individuals. Indian reservation and Indian tribe are defined by existing statutory cross‑references. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that when state school/educational lands lie within an Indian reservation the Indian tribe with jurisdiction has the first right of refusal to purchase (for lands covered by sale statutes 72-208, 72-257, 72-257.01, 72-258) or to lease (for lands under lease statutes 72-232–72-239) from the Board of Educational Lands and Funds. It also authorizes the Board, with the Governor’s written approval, to sell or exchange state educational lands for equal-area/value lands and requires the Board to determine such transactions are not detrimental to state interests if the lands are within a reservation. “Indian reservation” and “Indian tribe” are defined by reference to sections 81-2509 and 43-1503. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
