Summary

LB675 amends school-land statutes to change how the Board of Educational Lands and Funds and the Governor handle transactions involving lands within Indian reservation boundaries. Key agency actions:

- Board of Educational Lands and Funds: must give the Indian tribe with jurisdiction a first right of refusal to purchase or lease school lands located within reservation boundaries; may adopt leasing rules, enforce a soil conservation program, and enter into land-exchange agreements with the U.S. Government.

- Governor: must provide written approval for exchanges of state educational lands with individuals or tribes; the Board must find sales/exchanges to tribes are not detrimental to state interests.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds tribal rights and Board authority regarding state school/educational lands. Key points:

- The Board of Educational Lands and Funds must give an Indian tribe with jurisdiction over reservation lands the first right of refusal to purchase or to lease any school lands located within reservation boundaries (references definitions in §§81‑2509 and 43‑1503).

- The Board, with the Governor’s written approval, may enter agreements with an Indian tribe to sell or exchange state educational lands for other lands of equal area or value. If the lands are within a reservation, the Board must first determine the sale/exchange is not detrimental to state interests.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
