Summary

LB209 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§77‑202 and 77‑3506. Key agency references and changes:

- Dept. of Veterans Affairs: §77‑3506 expands homestead exemption eligibility to veterans receiving VA compensation for (a) 100% service‑connected permanent disability or (b) a total disability rating under 38 C.F.R. 4.16. Application/certification timing is revised: the primary category requires VA certification every five years; other veteran/surviving‑spouse categories require annual application but VA certification only every five years.

- County assessors and the Tax Commissioner: retained roles in administering exemptions (including broadband equipment application deadlines and Tax Commissioner‑prescribed forms).

- Governmental subdivisions and public housing authorities are referenced regarding public‑purpose exemptions.

The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes. It takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that the subdivision does not alter property tax exemptions for nonprofit skilled nursing facilities and does not affect the assignment of a "total disability" rating for compensation under 38 C.F.R. §4.16 (the VA disability-rating regulation). Implicates local property tax authorities and the Department of Veterans Affairs’ regulatory framework, but contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
