Summary

LB209 amends Nebraska property tax law (sections 77-202 and 77-3506). Key agencies mentioned: United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), county assessors, and the Nebraska Tax Commissioner; federal programs referenced include the BEAD (Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment) program under the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act.

Major changes:

- For-profit skilled nursing, nursing, and assisted‑living facilities that house Medicaid beneficiaries receive a property tax exemption equal to the facility’s average Medicaid‑occupied bed percentage over the most recent three years (does not affect nonprofit exemptions).

- Homestead exemptions clarified to cover veterans with 100% service‑connected permanent disability or total disability per 38 C.F.R. 4.16, and veterans with 100% temporary service‑connected disability; surviving spouses remain eligible. VA certification required; application timing adjusted (subdivision (2)(a) application every year evenly divisible by five; others annually with certification required only in years evenly divisible by five).

- Broadband equipment exemption procedures: owner applies to county assessor by Dec. 31; assessor must approve within 30 days; forms prescribed by the Tax Commissioner.

No direct appropriations or budget reallocations are specified. The bill takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that the subdivision does not change or reduce: (1) any property tax exemption for nonprofit skilled nursing facilities (administered by state/local taxing authorities), and (2) the assignment of a total disability rating for compensation under 38 C.F.R. §4.16 (a Department of Veterans Affairs regulation). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
