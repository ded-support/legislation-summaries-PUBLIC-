Summary

LB209 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§77-202 and 77-3506. Key agency references and administrative effects:

- United States Department of Veterans Affairs: required to certify veteran or surviving‑spouse status for homestead exemptions; certification for subsection (2)(a) required every five years, for other veteran/surviving‑spouse categories required annually but certification only every five years. The homestead exemption eligibility is clarified to cover veterans drawing compensation for 100% service‑connected permanent disability or a total disability rating under 38 C.F.R. 4.16 and certain surviving spouses.

- County assessors and the Tax Commissioner (forms) remain responsible for processing related exemption applications (including broadband provisions retained elsewhere in §77-202).

- The bill provides a property tax exemption for for‑profit skilled nursing, nursing, and assisted‑living facilities that house Medicaid beneficiaries; the exemption equals the percentage of property tax corresponding to the facility’s average share of Medicaid‑occupied beds over the most recent three years.

No new appropriations, funding allocations, or budgetary changes are included. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that the subdivision does not alter or reduce any property tax exemption for nonprofit skilled nursing facilities and does not affect the assignment of a total disability rating for compensation under 38 C.F.R. §4.16 (a VA regulation). This invokes the Department of Veterans Affairs' regulatory framework but contains no provisions changing funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
