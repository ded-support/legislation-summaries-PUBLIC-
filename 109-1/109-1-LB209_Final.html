Summary

LB209 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§77‑202 and 77‑3506. It expands the §77‑202(1)(d)(i) property‑tax exemption to include for‑profit skilled nursing, nursing, and assisted‑living facilities that house Medicaid beneficiaries, making the exemption a percentage of taxes equal to the facility’s three‑year average share of Medicaid‑occupied beds. The bill preserves county assessor involvement and Tax Commissioner‑prescribed forms for broadband equipment exemptions. In §77‑3506, it clarifies veteran eligibility (including 100% service‑connected permanent disability or a total disability rating under 38 C.F.R. 4.16 and 100% temporary disability) and changes application/certification timing: veterans’ applications and VA certification required in subsequent years evenly divisible by five; other eligible spouses/veterans must apply annually but need VA certification only in years evenly divisible by five. The United States Department of Veterans Affairs is the certifying authority. No appropriations or funding changes are included. The bill takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that the subdivision does not alter property tax exemptions for nonprofit skilled nursing facilities and does not affect assignment of a total disability rating under 38 C.F.R. §4.16 (the VA disability-rating regulation administered by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs). No agencies are newly created or tasked, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
