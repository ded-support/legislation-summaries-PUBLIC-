Summary

LB653 directs the State Treasurer to transfer specified General Fund amounts to the Education Future Fund "on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services." Specifically, it requires a $1,000,000,000 transfer in FY2023‑24 and $250,000,000 in FY2024‑25, and expresses the Legislature's intent that $250,000,000 be transferred in FY2025‑26 and each subsequent fiscal year.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions do not mention or change duties, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Administrative Services. They instead (1) limit district denials of enrollment-option applicants with IEPs (max 16% of denials), require notice to parents when a requested building is at capacity and automatic acceptance for siblings, and require districts to notify applicants of incomplete applications within 10 business days; and (2) authorize the State Department of Education to approve applications to the Education Future Fund for option students whose expected education/support costs are ≥5× the prior-year adjusted average per‑pupil cost, require SDE to pay qualifying districts by Nov. 15 equal to the excess cost (prorated if fund balance is insufficient), and add a funding-purpose statement for these IEP option students.
