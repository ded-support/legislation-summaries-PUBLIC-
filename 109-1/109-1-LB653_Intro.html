Summary

This bill directs two specific roles for the Department of Administrative Services (DAS):

- Budget administrator, Budget Division, DAS: The State Treasurer shall transfer $1.0 billion (FY2023-24) and $250 million (FY2024-25) from the General Fund to the Education Future Fund on dates and in amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of DAS; the Legislature expresses intent for $250 million transfers annually beginning FY2025-26.

- Director of Administrative Services: For monthly special-education reimbursements, the State Treasurer will notify the Director of Administrative Services of available funds in the General Fund and Education Future Fund; the Director shall draw warrants against those appropriated funds upon certification.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No changes reference the Department of Administrative Services; the bill adds nothing to DAS’s duties, appropriations, or budget.

Summary of additions affecting funding and process:

- Districts must limit denials of enrollment-option applications from students with IEPs to 16% of total denials and must automatically accept siblings and notify parents of alternate buildings if a building is at capacity.

- Incomplete applications must be flagged within 10 business days and may be excluded from denial counts.

- For option students with IEPs whose expected costs are ≥5× the prior year’s adjusted average per pupil cost, districts may apply to the State Department of Education (by Oct 15) for payment from the Education Future Fund. The SDE will pay by Nov 15 the excess cost (expected cost minus adjusted average), pro rata reduced if the fund balance is insufficient. Purpose language funds services so districts can accept more option students.
