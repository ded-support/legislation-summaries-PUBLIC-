Summary

LB556 amends juvenile code provisions referencing two agencies:

- Department of Correctional Services: Clarifies that "staff secure juvenile facility" excludes any institution operated by the Department of Correctional Services and prohibits placement, transfer, or assignment of certain categories of juveniles to adult correctional facilities or the Department's secure youth confinement facility.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Authorizes peace officers to deliver certain juveniles to DHHS for temporary placement; DHHS must supervise placements, may consent to emergency medical/psychological/psychiatric treatment, and provides the prescribed certificate used when juveniles are turned over for mental-health evaluation/treatment.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language revises juvenile detention procedures: for felony arrests, the probation officer must recommend detention, release, or alternatives and forward intake to a judge, who consults the officer and may consider (but is not bound by) factors in §43-260.01; for non-felony arrests, criteria are listed that make detention unnecessary or necessary (threat to persons, failure to appear, fleeing/absconding, violent or repeated property offenses, threats to self/others, or agreement with juvenile and county/city attorney).

No provisions reference the Department of Correctional Services or the Department of Health and Human Services, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
