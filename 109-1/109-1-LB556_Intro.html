Summary

LB556 amends juvenile justice statutes to (1) clarify that a "staff secure juvenile facility" does not include any institution operated by the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and to prohibit placing or assigning certain categories of juveniles to adult correctional facilities or the DCS-operated secure youth confinement facility. (2) Require peace officers to deliver custody of juveniles taken under specified noncriminal or protective subsections to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), which must make and supervise temporary placements, may consent to emergency medical/behavioral treatment, and whose temporary custody ends absent a court order within 48 hours. For mental-health-dangerousness detentions, officers must execute a DHHS-prescribed certificate and forward a copy to the county attorney. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Substantive additions (summary):

- Defines "detention" as temporary care in a physically restrictive facility.

- Requires probation officers to recommend detention, release, or alternatives for felony arrests and forward intake to a judge; the judge consults the officer but is not bound by statutory factors.

- For non‑felony arrests, sets criteria judges/probation must consider (threat to persons, flight risk/failure to appear, absconding, violent or multiple property offenses, threats of self‑harm/other‑harm).

- Limits some detentions for non‑felonies to cases where the juvenile’s counsel and the county/city attorney agree, and emphasizes detention only when immediately necessary for protection.
