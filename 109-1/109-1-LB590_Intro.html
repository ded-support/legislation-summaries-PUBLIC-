Summary

The bill authorizes the Department of Transportation to, by rule and regulation, establish, use, and operate a mitigation bank or an in-lieu fee program in accordance with applicable state and federal law. The program’s stated purpose is to provide compensatory mitigation for incidental take of threatened or endangered species and their habitat. The statute also requires that a state or local agency or private party that owns a mitigation bank pay a sum in lieu of ad valorem taxes lost by the county. The bill contains no direct appropriations or other budget allocations to the Department of Transportation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Transportation is authorized to adopt rules to establish, operate, and use a mitigation bank or in‑lieu fee program consistent with state and federal law to provide compensatory mitigation for incidental take of threatened/endangered species and their habitat from permitted activities. The program’s purpose includes restoring, creating, enhancing, or preserving habitat, applying fair and practicable mitigation ratios to offset project impacts, and achieving a net increase in conservation functions and values for affected species and resources. Financially, owners of mitigation banks (state/local agencies or private parties) that acquire land for habitat purposes must pay a sum in lieu of ad valorem taxes to compensate counties for lost property tax revenue. No direct DOT appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
