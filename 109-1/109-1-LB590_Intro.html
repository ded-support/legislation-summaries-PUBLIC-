Summary

LB590 authorizes the Department of Transportation to, by rule and regulation, establish, use, and operate a mitigation bank or an in-lieu fee program in accordance with applicable state and federal law. The program is intended to provide compensatory mitigation for incidental take of threatened or endangered species and their habitat (restoring, creating, enhancing, preserving, or combinations thereof) and to achieve a net increase in conservation functions and values. Owners of such mitigation banks (state, local, or private) must pay a sum in lieu of ad valorem taxes to the county. The bill contains no appropriations or other changes to the Department of Transportation’s budget.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Authorizes the Department of Transportation to adopt rules and establish, use, and operate a mitigation bank or in‑lieu fee program under applicable state and federal law to provide compensatory mitigation for incidental take of threatened or endangered species and their habitat from permitted projects. The program’s purpose includes restoring/creating/enhancing/preserving habitat, using a fair and practicable acreage ratio to offset project impacts, and achieving a net increase in conservation functions and values for affected species and resources. Fiscal change: owners of mitigation banks (state, local, or private) that acquire land for habitat work must pay a sum in lieu of lost ad valorem county taxes. No explicit DOT appropriations or budget allocations are included.
