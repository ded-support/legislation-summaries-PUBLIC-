Summary

LB590 amends section 37-335 to authorize the Nebraska Department of Transportation to, by rule and regulation, establish, use, and operate a mitigation bank or an in‑lieu fee program in accordance with applicable state and federal law. The bill text provided does not specify any appropriations, budget allocations, or other direct funding changes for the Department of Transportation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Transportation may, by rule, establish, use, and operate mitigation banks or in‑lieu fee programs consistent with state and federal law. These programs are authorized to provide compensatory mitigation for incidental take of threatened or endangered species by restoring, creating, enhancing, or preserving suitable habitat, using fair, reasonable, and practicable mitigation ratios, and aiming to achieve a net increase in conservation functions and values. Fiscal change: owners (state/local agencies or private parties) of mitigation banks that acquire land for habitat must pay a sum in lieu of ad valorem taxes to the county. No direct DOT appropriation or budget allocation is specified.
