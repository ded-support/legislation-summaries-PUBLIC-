Summary

LB666 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-1505 to expand and clarify duties of the Nebraska Public Service Commission under the Rural Communications Sustainability Act. After a granting agency completes final payment under a broadband deployment program in a deployment project area served by an incumbent carrier, the Commission, upon request by either the incumbent or the competitive provider, must expeditiously: (1) relieve the incumbent of eligible telecommunications carrier (ETC) and carrier‑of‑last‑resort (COLR) obligations if the granting agency found the competitive provider compliant; (2) determine allocations and distributions of support from the Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund for the project area consistent with the Commission’s procedures; and (3) coordinate with the Federal Communications Commission and consult both carriers to decide whether ETC/COLR obligations tied to that Universal Service Fund support should be transferred to the competitive provider. No direct appropriations are made.
