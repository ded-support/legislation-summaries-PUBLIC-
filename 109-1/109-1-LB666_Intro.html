Summary

LB666 amends §86‑1505 (Rural Communications Sustainability Act), directing the Nebraska Public Service Commission (PSC) to act expeditiously, upon request by an incumbent or competitive provider after a granting agency’s final payment under a broadband deployment program, to: (1) relieve the incumbent carrier of eligible telecommunications carrier (ETC) and carrier‑of‑last‑resort (COLR) obligations in the affected deployment project area if the granting agency found the competitive provider compliant; (2) — consistent with PSC procedural rules — determine allocations and distributions of support from the Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund for that area; and (3) coordinate with the Federal Communications Commission and consult the carriers to decide whether ETC/COLR obligations tied to Nebraska Universal Service Fund support should transfer to the competitive provider. The bill repeals the original section. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are included.

Adds the phrase "or competitive provider expeditiously," authorizing selection or engagement of a competitive provider on an expedited basis. No specific government agency or department is named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
