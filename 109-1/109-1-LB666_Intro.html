Summary

LB666 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86‑1505 (Rural Communications Sustainability Act) to require the Nebraska Public Service Commission to act expeditiously, upon request by either the incumbent carrier or the competitive provider after a granting agency makes final payment under a broadband deployment program, to:

- relieve the incumbent carrier of eligible telecommunications carrier (ETC) and carrier‑of‑last‑resort obligations in the affected deployment project area if the granting agency finds the competitive provider compliant;

- determine allocations and distributions of support from the Nebraska Telecommunications Universal Service Fund for the deployment project area (consistent with Commission procedure rules); and

- in coordination with the Federal Communications Commission and after consulting both carriers, determine whether ETC and carrier‑of‑last‑resort obligations tied to Nebraska Universal Service Fund support should be transferred to the competitive provider.

The original section is repealed. No specific appropriations or dollar changes are included.
