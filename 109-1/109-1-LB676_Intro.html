Summary

LB676 revises the Certified Nurse Midwifery Practice Act and related statutes. Key government entities affected:

- The state board (Board) is charged to monitor CNM scope of practice, recommend disciplinary action, and adopt rules and regulations implementing multiple APRN practice acts for promulgation by "the department" under §38-126. Rulemaking must address approved certification organizations/programs and professional liability insurance.

- The Board may require program accreditation by the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education.

- The bill amends §44-2803 to expressly include certified nurse midwives as "health care providers" for purposes of the Nebraska Hospital‑Medical Liability Act.

The measure removes statutory practice‑agreement requirements and revises definitions and scope provisions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds or clarifies definitions and scope for certified nurse midwives (CNMs). New text names the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education and defines "consultation," "health care provider" (as a professional licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act), and "referral." CNMs may provide broad maternal/newborn and gynecologic care, order tests, prescribe within scope, dispense manufacturer-provided sample medications at no charge, and may pronounce death and sign death certificates where law permits. It requires CNMs to establish collaborative, consultative, and referral networks with other health care professionals. No government funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are added.
