Summary

LB676 amends the Certified Nurse Midwifery Practice Act. It expands duties for "the board" to monitor certified nurse midwives' scope, recommend disciplinary action, and adopt rules and regulations for promulgation by "the department" under section 38-126. The bill allows the board to require midwifery programs be accredited by the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education and recognizes board-approved certifying bodies. It revises definitions (e.g., collaboration, consultation, health care provider) and eliminates practice-agreement requirements while clarifying midwives' referral/consultation responsibilities. The bill also amends section 44-2803 to include certified nurse midwives in the statutory definition of health care provider under the Hospital-Medical Liability Act. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text references the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education and the Uniform Credentialing Act (statutory framework), and cites section 7 of this act and statute 38-605. It defines “health care provider,” “consultation,” and “referral,” and expands/clarifies certified nurse midwife scope (collaboration/consultation/referral duties; prenatal through 28‑day newborn care; gynecologic and primary care for females; ordering tests, prescribing medications, dispensing manufacturer-provided samples, and pronouncing death where lawful). No executive agencies or departments beyond the Accreditation Commission are named, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
