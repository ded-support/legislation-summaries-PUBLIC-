Summary

LB676 amends the Certified Nurse Midwifery Practice Act and related credentialing statutes. It directs the state credentialing board to monitor CNM scope of practice, recommend disciplinary action, and adopt rules (for promulgation by the department) including approved certification organizations and professional liability insurance. The board may require midwifery programs be accredited by the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education. The bill removes practice-agreement requirements, revises definitions (including collaboration and consultation), clarifies CNM scope (prenatal, intrapartum, postpartum, gynecologic, newborn care to 28 days, prescribing within scope, death pronouncement), and adds certified nurse midwives to the Nebraska Hospital‑Medical Liability Act definition of health care provider. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education and defines “health care provider” as a professional licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act. Establishes that certified nurse midwives may consult with physicians or other licensed health care providers, must refer care beyond their scope, and specifies midwifery scope (prenatal/intrapartum/postpartum care, gynecologic and female primary care, newborn care to 28 days, ordering tests/treatments, prescribing within scope, dispensing manufacturer-provided samples, pronouncing death/signing death certificates). No government funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
