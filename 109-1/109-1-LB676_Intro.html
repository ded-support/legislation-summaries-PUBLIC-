Summary

LB676 amends the Certified Nurse Midwifery Practice Act and related credentialing statutes. It expands the Board’s (the licensing board referenced in the Uniform Credentialing Act) duties to monitor CNM scope of practice, recommend disciplinary action, and adopt rules for promulgation by the department (per §38-126), including approved certification organizations/programs and professional liability insurance. The bill authorizes the Board to require Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education accreditation for CNM programs; revises definitions (adding “consultation,” broadening “collaboration” to working with other health care providers, and defining “health care provider” as those licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act); removes practice-agreement language; clarifies CNM scope (prescribing, dispensing manufacturer-provided samples, pronouncing death, newborn care through 28 days). It also amends §44-2803 to expressly include certified nurse midwives under the Nebraska Hospital‑Medical Liability Act. Several statutory sections are repealed. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Accreditation Commission for Midwifery Education and references the Uniform Credentialing Act. Defines "consultation" (CNM seeks physician/other health care provider advice), "health care provider" (licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act), and "referral" (CNM directs patient to another provider). Expands/clarifies certified nurse midwife scope: comprehensive perinatal and gynecologic care, primary female care, newborn care to 28 days, ordering tests/treatments, dispensing manufacturer-provided sample meds, prescribing within scope, and pronouncing death/signing death certificates if allowed by law. Requires collaborative, consultative, and referral networks. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
