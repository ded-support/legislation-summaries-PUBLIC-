Summary

The bill requires the Secretary of State to contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of official voter registration forms. It directs the Secretary of State and the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) to enter an agreement to match statewide voter registration data with DMV records (including citizenship verification); the DMV must also enter an agreement with the Social Security Commissioner. The DMV must make voter registration applications available at issuance/renewal of operator’s licenses and state ID cards, electronically transmit registration data to county election officials within specified timeframes, and provide electronic images/signatures from DMV records for voter registration purposes. The Secretary of State will use DMV data to verify ID numbers on mailed early-voting ballot requests and will supply DMV with voter ID requirement information. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency are specified.
