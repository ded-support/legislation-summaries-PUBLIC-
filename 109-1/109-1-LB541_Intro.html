Summary

The bill requires the Secretary of State to contract with the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) to store and distribute official election forms. It directs the Secretary of State and the Director of the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) to enter an agreement to match statewide voter registration data with DMV records (including citizenship verification); the DMV must also contract with the Social Security Commissioner as required. The DMV must provide voter registration applications at license/ID offices, permit electronic transmission of registration data (including digital image and signature) to county election officials, and assist verification of driver’s license/ID data for mailed‑ballot requests. The Secretary of State will supply DMV with voter‑ID outreach materials. Proceeds from sale of official registration applications are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Election Administration Fund. No other bill sections reference or appropriate funds for DAS or DMV.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions tighten election procedures—defining “hand count,” limiting it to voter‑marked paper ballots (including ballots marked by assistive machines), requiring covert‑watermark and signature verification, mandating secure ballot drop‑box standards (marking, lighting, tamper resistance, 24/7 video, pre‑election inspections, repair deadlines, public release of recordings within five days, 22‑month retention), requiring deputy registrars and early‑ballot applicants to present valid photographic ID, limiting employer agents, and providing for hand‑count audits (at least 25% and recount options). No language references the Department of Administrative Services or the Department of Motor Vehicles, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes affecting those agencies in the added text.
