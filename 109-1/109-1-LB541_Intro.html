Summary

The bill requires the Secretary of State (SOS) to contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of official voter‑registration forms. SOS must provide the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) with instructions and materials on voter‑ID requirements and how to obtain free photographic ID; the DMV will make the voter registration application available at its offices and must complete applications taken there by close of business on the third Friday before an election. The DMV and SOS must enter an agreement to match the statewide voter registration list with DMV records (including citizenship) to verify registrations; the DMV director must also contract with the Social Security Commissioner. DMV and SOS must electronically transmit registration data to county election officials (generally within 10 days, five days near deadlines). Proceeds from sale of registration applications are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Election Administration Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions affect the Department of Administrative Services or the Department of Motor Vehicles, and there are no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.

Summary of additions (election-related):

- Defines "hand count" (manual count/recount of physical ballots; includes ballots marked by disability-assist machines) and requires verification of covert watermark and signature comparison for early ballot envelopes.

- Requires valid photographic identification for voter registration and for requesting/returning early ballots; limits who may act as an agent (employer restrictions).

- Sets secure ballot drop-box standards: conspicuous marking, lighting, tamper-resistance, continuous video surveillance, inspection schedule, repair deadlines, closure after deadline, recordings retained 22 months and made public within five days after last retrieval.

- Requires independent, nonpartisan IT assessment of vote-counting systems and provides for hand counts of at least 25% of ballots to verify device tabulation; preserves option for full manual recount.
