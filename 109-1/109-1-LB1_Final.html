Summary

LB1 amends definitional provisions in Initiative Law 2024, No. 437 (Nebraska Medical Cannabis Patient Protection Act) and Initiative Law 2024, No. 438 (Nebraska Medical Cannabis Regulation Act) to correct an incorrect subdivision reference adopted by voters. Changes clarify definitions (e.g., “cannabis,” “allowable amount,” “caregiver,” “health care practitioner,” “registered cannabis establishment,” and “registration”) and retain cross‑references to licensing under the Uniform Credentialing Act and to health care facility and home health agency definitions (sections 71‑413 and 71‑417). The bill identifies the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission in the regulatory act. It repeals the original sections and includes an emergency clause making the act effective on passage. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
