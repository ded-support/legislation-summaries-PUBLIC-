Summary

LB1 corrects subdivision references in two voter-adopted medical cannabis initiatives (Initiative Law Nos. 437 and 438) by amending their definitions. It clarifies the exclusion of hemp as defined in section 2-503, defines the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission, and repeats references to regulated entities and professionals (health care practitioners licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act, health care facilities per section 71-413, and home health agencies per section 71-417). The bill repeals the original conflicting sections and includes an emergency clause making it effective on enactment. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
