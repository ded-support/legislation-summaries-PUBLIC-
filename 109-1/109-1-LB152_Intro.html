Summary

References to the Department of Revenue: The bill directs the Department of Revenue to determine the percent change in total assessed value of all real property used to calculate minimum annual Property Tax Credit Act funding for tax year 2029 (prior-year minimum + that percentage increase + $75 million) and for tax year 2030 onward (prior-year minimum + that percentage increase).

Funding/allocations established or amended:

- Property Tax Credit Act minimums: tax year 2025 $430M; 2026 $445M; 2027 $460M; 2028 $475M; tax year 2029 = prior minimum + Dept. of Revenue–determined percentage increase + $75M; tax year 2030+ = prior minimum + Dept. of Revenue–determined percentage increase.

- School District Property Tax Relief Act totals: tax year 2024 $750M; 2025 $780M; 2026 $808M; 2027 $838M; 2028 $870M; 2029 $902M; 2030+ increase by 3% annually.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a uniform homestead exemption: beginning January 1, 2026, the first $100,000 of a homestead’s actual value is exempt from taxation. It preserves and expands existing homestead policy and specifies that the exemptions in sections 77-3506, 77-3507, and 77-3508 remain in addition to this new $100,000 exemption. The added text does not mention the Department of Revenue, nor does it include any new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for administering the change.
