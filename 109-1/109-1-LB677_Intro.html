Summary

LB677 requires the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission to coordinate with the Department of Agriculture, Department of Revenue, and Department of Health and Human Services on cultivation, processing, testing, distribution, transportation and sale compliance. It creates a Medical Cannabis Control Fund (fees, gifts, grants; application fees remitted to the State Treasurer credited to the fund) to finance commission operations; bonding premiums for commission employees are paid from the General Fund. Department of Correctional Services employees (and jail employees/peace officers) are barred from holding medical cannabis licenses; persons sentenced to DOC under amended Section 28-416 must receive drug‑abuse treatment while incarcerated. Vehicle‑lettering exemptions are extended to DOC and DHHS (for offender transport, communicable disease control, drug investigations), to the Military Department, and to Agriculture for special investigations. Sales/use tax proceeds from aircraft sales/leases are credited to the DOT Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund; medical cannabis sales to qualified patients/caregivers are taxed at 4%, with dispensary proceeds credited to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of additions affecting specified agencies:

- Department of Agriculture: Required to collaborate with the Medical Cannabis Commission on regulation. Commission rules for cultivators include pesticide, residue testing, destruction, record‑keeping and waste disposal standards—areas overlapping agricultural expertise.

- Department of Correctional Services: Possession or use of medical cannabis is prohibited in jails, correctional facilities, and youth centers; employees of a jail or the Department of Correctional Services are disqualified from holding licenses.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Required collaborator with the commission; health‑care practitioner recommendations and health‑care facility accommodations/restrictions addressed (patient access, consumption limits).

- Department of Revenue: Collaborative role; application and license fees are credited to a new Medical Cannabis Control Fund. Sales/use tax proceeds on dispensary sales to qualified patients are directed to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund (via State Treasurer/Revenue administration).

- Department of Transportation: Commission may set transporter standards (insurance, timeframes, vehicle requirements); no direct DOT funding changes.

- Military Department: No references or fiscal changes.
