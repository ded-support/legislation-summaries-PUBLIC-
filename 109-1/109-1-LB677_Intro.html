Summary

LB677 requires the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission to coordinate with the Departments of Agriculture, Revenue, and Health and Human Services on cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transportation, testing, and sale. It creates a Medical Cannabis Control Fund to receive application/renewal fees and other receipts (vertical $100,000; cultivator $25,000; products manufacturer $20,000; dispensary $25,000; transporter $5,000; testing facility $25,000; $5,000 renewal) for administration, enforcement, and salaries/benefits; bond/insurance premiums for commission employees handling public funds are paid from the State General Fund. Department of Correctional Services employees (and jail employees/peace officers) are barred from holding medical‑cannabis licenses; persons sentenced to DCS must attend drug‑abuse treatment. The bill exempts DHHS, DCS, the Military Department, and the Department of Agriculture (for special investigations) from certain state vehicle‑lettering rules. Tax changes direct a 4% medical‑cannabis sales tax to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund, the Tax Commissioner retains the Revenue Distribution Fund, and aircraft sales/use tax proceeds go to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund (transactions on/after 7/1/2023).

- Department of Revenue: will collect sales/use taxes on medical-cannabis sales and handle remittal of commission application fees to the Medical Cannabis Control Fund; sales and use tax proceeds from dispensary sales are credited to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund.
