Summary

LB677 requires the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission to coordinate with the Department of Agriculture, Department of Revenue, and Department of Health and Human Services to ensure cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transportation, testing, and sale of medical cannabis comply with the Act. The bill bars certain public employees — specifically including Department of Correctional Services staff and peace officers — from holding medical cannabis licenses. Persons convicted of controlled‑substances offenses and sentenced to the Department of Correctional Services must receive drug‑abuse treatment and counseling while incarcerated. The bill exempts vehicles used by the Department of Health and Human Services, Department of Correctional Services, Military Department, Department of Agriculture (for special investigations), and Tax Commissioner enforcement staff from state vehicle‑lettering requirements. Fiscal change: sales/use tax proceeds from aircraft sales or leases are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Agriculture: Added duty to collaborate with the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission on cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transport, testing, and sale. No direct funding or appropriation changes.

- Department of Correctional Services: Employees of jails or the Department of Correctional Services are barred from holding licenses; possession/use of medical cannabis is prohibited in jails, correctional facilities, and youth centers. No funding changes.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Required to collaborate with the Commission; health-care facility consumer-access and restriction provisions added (may restrict smoking/vaping, specify locations, but cannot unreasonably limit access unless federal benefits/licenses at risk). No funding changes.

- Department of Revenue: Required to collaborate with the Commission; sales/use tax proceeds from dispensary sales are allocated (proceeds credited to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund). Medical Cannabis Control Fund created; licensing/application fees (listed amounts) are remitted to that fund for administration/enforcement. Bonding premiums for commission employees are paid from the General Fund.

- Department of Transportation and Military Department: No substantive mentions or funding changes in the added text.
