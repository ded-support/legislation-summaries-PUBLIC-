Summary

LB677 assigns interagency roles and funding changes across several departments. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must collaborate with the Department of Agriculture, Department of Revenue, and Department of Health and Human Services to implement and oversee medical cannabis (cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transportation, testing, sale). A Medical Cannabis Control Fund is created to receive application fees, gifts, grants, and other non‑penalty revenue; fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for that fund and may be used for commission administration and enforcement, including salaries/benefits. Commission employees handling public funds must be bonded or insured, with bonding premiums paid from the General Fund. The Department of Correctional Services is barred from holding medical cannabis licenses and must provide sentenced persons drug‑abuse treatment/counseling. All sales/use tax from aircraft sales/leases is credited to the Dept. of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund (effective July 1, 2023). DHHS, DCS, Department of Agriculture, and the Military Department receive specified state‑vehicle lettering exemptions for investigative, transport, apprehension, and disease/drug‑control duties.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Agriculture: Named as a collaborator with the Medical Cannabis Commission on regulation. Commission rules must cover cultivation (including permitted/prohibited pesticides, pesticide-residue testing, destruction/disposal), with Agriculture coordination.

- Department of Correctional Services: Employees of jails or the Department of Correctional Services are barred from holding medical cannabis licenses; possession/use of cannabis is prohibited in jails, correctional facilities, and youth centers.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Identified as a collaborator with the Commission on implementation and oversight; health-care facility/practitioner definitions and patient recommendation requirements are added.

- Department of Revenue: Identified as a collaborator; application fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to a new Medical Cannabis Control Fund. Sales/use tax proceeds from dispensary medical cannabis sales are directed to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund.

- Department of Transportation & Military Department: No substantive duties, budget changes, or new responsibilities assigned in the added text.

- Funding/appropriations: Medical Cannabis Control Fund created for commission administration; bonding premiums for commission employees paid from the General Fund; civil penalties remitted to State Treasurer per constitutional disposition.
