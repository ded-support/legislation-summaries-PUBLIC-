Summary

LB499 amends statutes governing the Crime Victim’s Reparations Committee (Reissue Revised Statutes §§81-1802 to 81-1804). Key changes: public members’ terms now begin October 1; to establish staggered service, one of the two public members appointed under subdivision (2)(b)(i) and one of the two appointed under subdivision (2)(b)(ii) will serve initial two-year terms, with all subsequent terms being four years; vacancies are filled for the remainder of the term; and members whose terms have expired may continue to serve until a successor is appointed. The bill repeals the original sections. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
