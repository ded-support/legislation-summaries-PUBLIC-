Summary

LB498 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. It directs the Department (state education agency) to calculate foundation aid: $1,500 per formula student for 2023-24 and 2024-25, and beginning 2025-26 a per‑student amount indexed annually to the prior calendar year's CPI‑U. Twenty‑four percent of total foundation aid is to be paid from the Education Future Fund. For 2023-24/24-25 all foundation aid is a formula resource; for 2025-26 onward 60% is a formula resource.

The Department must certify aid, district budget authority, and applicable reserve percentages to the Director of Administrative Services, Auditor of Public Accounts, and local systems on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 annually thereafter, and report funding needs to the Governor, Appropriations Committee, and Education Committee. Prior certifications for 2025‑26 are void. The Appropriations Committee must include the necessary state aid amount in its budget recommendations. Emergency clause makes the act effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual CPI-based index to foundation aid starting in school year 2025‑26. For 2025‑26 and each year after, each district’s foundation aid = (prior year’s foundation aid paid per formula student, increased by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers as published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics for the previous calendar year) × (the district’s number of formula students). The provision references school years 2023‑24 and 2024‑25 but gives the CPI indexing effective 2025‑26. No specific dollar appropriations or budget line items are included.
