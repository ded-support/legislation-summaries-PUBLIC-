Summary

Creates timing and funding rules administered by the state education department and other state fiscal offices under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act. Key agency actions and budget changes:

- Department (state education department) must certify foundation aid amounts and district budget authority on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 each year thereafter, and report necessary funding levels to the Governor, the Appropriations Committee, and the Education Committee; certifications are sent to the Director of Administrative Services, the Auditor of Public Accounts, and local systems.

- Foundation aid: $1,500 per formula student for FY2023-24 and FY2024-25; from FY2025-26 forward the per-student amount is indexed annually by CPI‑U. 24% of total foundation aid is paid from the Education Future Fund.

- Treatment: certified state aid shown as budgeted non‑property‑tax receipts and deducted before calculating local property tax requests; aid distributed in ten monthly payments (Sept–June), with small payments lumped in December.

- Any certifications for FY2025-26 completed prior to the act’s effective date are null and void.

- Appropriations Committee must include the amount necessary to fund the certified state aid in its budget recommendations.

- Emergency clause: effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula for foundation aid beginning in school fiscal year 2025‑26: each district's aid equals the prior year’s foundation aid per formula student, increased by the percent change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) for the previous calendar year as published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics, multiplied by the district’s number of formula students. No specific appropriation amounts are set; the change ties future state payments to the BLS CPI‑U.
