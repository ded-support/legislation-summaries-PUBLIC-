Summary

LB498 requires the Department of Education to recalculate and timely certify foundation aid, district budget authority, and allowable reserve percentages on or before June 15, 2025, and on or before March 1 each year thereafter, and nullifies any such 2025-26 certifications completed before the bill’s effective date. Foundation aid: $1,500 per formula student for 2023-24 and 2024-25; beginning 2025-26 per‑student aid is the prior year amount increased by the annual CPI‑U percentage. Twenty-four percent of total foundation aid each year must be paid from the Education Future Fund. For 2025-26 and thereafter, only 60% of foundation aid is counted as a formula resource (100% for 2023-24 and 2024-25). The department must certify amounts to the Director of Administrative Services, Auditor of Public Accounts, Governor, Appropriations Committee, Education Committee, and local systems; the Appropriations Committee must include certified state aid in its budget recommendations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an inflation‑indexing formula for state foundation aid starting in school year 2025‑26: each district’s aid equals the prior year’s foundation aid per formula student, increased by the prior calendar year’s percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) as published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics, multiplied by the district’s current number of formula students. The only federal agency named is the BLS (CPI‑U data). No specific dollar appropriations are set; the change mandates how future education funding is calculated.
