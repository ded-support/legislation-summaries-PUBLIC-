Summary

LB498 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act and directs the department (Department of Education) to determine and certify foundation aid, district budget authority, and allowable reserve percentages on or before June 15, 2025, and March 1 annually thereafter. Certifications are to be sent to the Director of Administrative Services, the Auditor of Public Accounts, and local systems; funding-level reports go to the Governor, the Appropriations Committee, and the Education Committee. Fiscal changes: foundation aid is $1,500 per formula student for FY2023-24 and FY2024-25, then indexed by the annual CPI-U thereafter; 24% of total foundation aid will be paid from the Education Future Fund; foundation aid counts as 100% of formula resources for 2023-24 and 2024-25 but 60% beginning FY2025-26. Any state-aid or budget certifications for FY2025-26 completed before the act’s effective date are void. The bill is effective immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula for foundation aid beginning with school fiscal year 2025-26: each district's foundation aid equals the prior year's foundation aid per formula student, increased by the prior calendar year's percent change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) as published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics, then multiplied by the district's number of formula students. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are set; the Bureau of Labor Statistics is the only federal agency named.
