Summary

LB259 (Faithful Delegate to Federal Article V Convention Act) prescribes how Nebraska’s Legislature will select, instruct, oversee, and, if necessary, remove members of a state delegation to a U.S. Article V convention. Key government bodies named: the Committee on Committees (nominates one delegate and one alternate per legislative caucus), the full Legislature (elects delegates/alternates, adopts/amends instructions, determines unauthorized votes), the Executive Board of the Legislative Council together with the Lieutenant Governor (serve as the advisory committee when the Legislature is not in session), the Speaker of the Legislature (notifies convention officers of disqualifications), and the Secretary of State (receives delegates’ oaths). Delegates receive no pay but may be reimbursed under statutes sections 81-1174 to 81-1177. Gifts from registered lobbyists are prohibited (violations Class III misdemeanor). No new appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statutory framework for selecting, instructing, and policing state Article V convention delegates. Agencies/departments mentioned and roles:

- Legislature: adopts delegate instructions, elects delegates/alternates, determines unauthorized votes, fills vacancies.

- Committee on Committees of the Legislature: nominates one delegate and alternate per legislative caucus.

- Executive Board of the Legislative Council and Lieutenant Governor: form the advisory committee (chair = executive board chair; Lt. Governor breaks ties) to act when the Legislature is not in session.

- Speaker of the Legislature: must notify convention officers of disqualifications.

- Secretary of State: receives delegates' oaths.

- References to Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act, Secretary of the U.S. Senate and Clerk of the U.S. House (lobbyist registries), and reimbursement rules in sections 81-1174 to 81-1177.

Funding/appropriations: delegates/alternates receive no compensation but may be reimbursed for expenses under existing statutes; no new appropriations specified.
