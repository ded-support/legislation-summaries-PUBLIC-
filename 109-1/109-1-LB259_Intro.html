Summary

LB259 (Faithful Delegate to Federal Article V Convention Act) establishes procedures for Nebraska’s participation in a federal Article V convention. Key state entities/roles: the Nebraska Legislature (elects delegates and alternate delegates via Committee on Committees nominations), the Article V Convention Committee (delegates form committee; Legislature elects chair and vice-chair), the Executive Board of the Legislative Council and the Lieutenant Governor (serve as the advisory committee; chair of the executive board serves as advisory chair; Lt. Gov. may break ties), and the Secretary of State (files delegates’ oaths). The bill references state expense reimbursement statutes (sections 81-1174 to 81-1177) and the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act (gift prohibition from registered lobbyists; Class III misdemeanor penalty). It also requires notification by the Speaker to the convention presiding officer of any disqualified delegate. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates procedures for Nebraska's Article V convention delegation. Key state entities: the Nebraska Legislature (which elects delegates/alternates, adopts instructions, and determines unauthorized votes); the Committee on Committees (nominates delegates); the Article V Convention Committee (delegates constitute this committee); the Executive Board of the Legislative Council and the Lieutenant Governor (serve as the advisory committee, chair is executive‑board chair, Lt. Governor breaks ties); the Speaker of the Legislature (must notify committee chair and convention presiding officer of disqualifications); and the Secretary of State (files delegates' oaths). Federal officer offices (Secretary of the U.S. Senate, Clerk of the U.S. House) are referenced for lobbyist reporting. Delegates receive no compensation but may be reimbursed under existing statutes (secs. 81‑1174 to 81‑1177). No new appropriations or budget allocations are made. Gift restrictions and a Class III misdemeanor penalty are included.
