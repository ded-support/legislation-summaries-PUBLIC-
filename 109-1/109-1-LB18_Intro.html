Summary

LB18 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-1236 (Small Wireless Facilities Deployment Act) to specify duties for a right-of-way authority and requirements for wireless providers. Key agency-related provisions:

- Authority: prohibited from granting exclusive right-of-way arrangements; may charge nondiscriminatory fees (or charge nothing) consistent with §86-1239; may impose objective, nondiscriminatory design/concealment measures in pre‑2019 historic districts; may require repair of right-of-way damage and recover reasonable costs; may approve height exceptions subject to nondiscriminatory rules; must determine compliance with the federal Americans with Disabilities Act and that installations do not obstruct travel, public safety, or utility operations before permitting new/modified poles.

- Wireless providers: granted permitted-use rights for collocation and pole work subject to height limits and authority conditions.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires any permitting authority to, before installing or modifying a utility pole in a right‑of‑way, verify the installation: (1) complies with the federal Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 and its federal implementing regulations as they existed on Jan 1, 2025; (2) does not obstruct or hinder usual travel or public safety in the right‑of‑way; and (3) does not obstruct another utility’s legal use of the right‑of‑way or impede safe operation or service. If the authority finds noncompliance with these criteria, it must deny the installation. No state or agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
