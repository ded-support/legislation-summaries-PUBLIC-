Summary

LB18 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-1236 (Small Wireless Facilities Deployment Act) to specify duties and limits for an “authority” managing rights-of-way and requirements for wireless providers. Key agency-related provisions: authorities may not enter exclusive right-of-way arrangements; may charge nondiscriminatory fees per §86-1239 (or waive them) only if they charge other entities; and may require repair/restoration of right-of-way damage and recover documented costs. Authorities must verify new or modified utility poles meet federal Americans with Disabilities Act requirements, do not impede travel or public safety, and do not obstruct utilities; noncompliant installations must be denied. Authorities may impose objective, published design/concealment measures in historic districts (citing 47 C.F.R. 1.1307(a)(4)). No funding or appropriation changes. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the responsible authority (e.g., local/state permitting authority) to, before installing any new or modified utility pole in a right‑of‑way, confirm the installation: (1) complies with the federal Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 and applicable federal regulations as of Jan 1, 2025; (2) does not obstruct usual travel or public safety in the right‑of‑way; and (3) does not impede other utilities’ lawful use or safe operation. If criteria aren’t met, the authority must deny permission. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
