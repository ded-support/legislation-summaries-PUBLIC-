Summary

LB561 directs the Department of Transportation to create a statewide "bridge map" (identifying prohibited bridge structures) by January 1, 2026, review it annually, and publish the latest version on the department's public website. The department (or Nebraska State Patrol for applicable highways) may issue special permits, including new overweight raw‑milk vehicle permits, and may revoke individual permits for violations or revoke all such permits if they risk loss of federal aid. The department may adopt rules, require financial security to cover roadway damage, make permit applications available in county treasurers’ offices (and other locations), and grant limited length exceptions to meet axle-weight limits. The department may sue to recover bridge‑repair costs caused by prohibited crossings; recovered funds must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Roads Operations Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Dept. of Transportation):

- Defines Department of Transportation as lead agency for new "overweight raw‑milk vehicle" permits (max gross weight 107,500 lbs and axle weights per federal bridge formula). Vehicles must carry the bridge map, avoid prohibited bridges, and not use the Interstate unless both DOT and FHWA authorize on the permit.

- DOT must create a statewide "prohibited bridge" map by Jan 1, 2026, review it annually, and publish updates on its website.

- DOT may revoke permits for violations (with written‑notice/APA appeal rights) and may revoke all permits if federal aid is at risk.

- DOT may pursue owners for bridge repair costs; recovered funds go to the Roads Operations Cash Fund.

- DOT may grant reasonable vehicle‑length exceptions to meet axle limits.

No new appropriations or line‑item budget allocations are specified.
