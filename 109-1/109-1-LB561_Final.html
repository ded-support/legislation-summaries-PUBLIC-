Summary

- Authorizes the Department of Transportation (DOT), along with the Nebraska State Patrol for interstate routes, to issue special/continuing permits (including new overweight raw‑milk vehicle permits) and to make permit applications available in county treasurers' offices or other locations the DOT selects.

- Requires DOT to create, publish on its public website, and annually review a bridge map identifying prohibited bridge structures for overweight raw‑milk vehicles (map due before Jan 1, 2026).

- DOT may revoke overweight raw‑milk permits for violations or if DOT determines permits risk loss of federal aid; revocations are subject to written notice and appeal under the Administrative Procedure Act.

- DOT may grant vehicle‑length exceptions to meet axle weight limits.

- DOT may sue to recover costs when an overweight raw‑milk vehicle damages a prohibited bridge; recovered funds are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Roads Operations Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates an overweight raw‑milk vehicle permit regime administered by the Department of Transportation (DOT). Key DOT duties and powers: define and publish a statewide “bridge map” by Jan 1, 2026 (annual updates; post to DOT website) identifying prohibited bridges; issue and revoke overweight raw‑milk permits for violations (written notice required; appeals under the APA); authorize limited Interstate travel only with FHWA approval; grant limited vehicle‑length exceptions to meet axle weight limits; revoke all permits if DOT determines federal aid is at risk. DOT may sue to recover bridge‑repair costs when an overweight raw‑milk vehicle damages a prohibited bridge; recovered monies must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Roads Operations Cash Fund. No direct new appropriations are specified.
