Summary

LB382A appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program 571, to implement Legislative Bill 382. It provides $2,000,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for FY2025-26 and $2,000,000 from the same fund for FY2026-27. Each fiscal-year appropriation is designated as $2,000,000 in Cash Funds for state aid and may only be used for that purpose. The bill prohibits using these appropriated funds for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates to the Department of Health and Human Services (Program 571) $2,000,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for FY2025‑26 and $2,000,000 for FY2026‑27 (total $4,000,000 across the biennium) to support implementation of Legislative Bill 382 (109th Leg., 2025). Each annual appropriation is designated as $2,000,000 Cash Funds restricted for state aid only. The funds may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.
