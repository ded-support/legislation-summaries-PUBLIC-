Summary

LB50 amends section 77-6204 to require the county treasurer to distribute all revenue received from the Department of Revenue (pursuant to §77-6203) as follows: 5% to the community college area where the renewable energy facility is located; the remainder to local taxing entities that would have otherwise received personal property tax revenue from depreciable personal property used directly to generate electricity from wind, solar, biomass, or landfill gas. The bill also explicitly prohibits the Department of Revenue from retaining any revenue collected under sections 77-6201 to 77-6204 for distribution, use, transfer, pledge, or allocation to or from the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a 5% carve‑out of specified renewable energy generation facility revenue to the community college area where the facility is located, with the remainder to be distributed pursuant to the existing subdivision (1)(b) scheme. For the Department of Revenue, this is an allocation change in revenue distribution responsibilities—no new appropriation or separate funding line is created. The net effect is that other recipients under subdivision (1)(b) will receive slightly less overall funding because 5% is redirected to the local community college area.
