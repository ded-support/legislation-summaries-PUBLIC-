Summary

Authorizes the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska to design and construct student residential facilities for nursing and allied health in Norfolk. State-supported project cost: $23,000,000. Legislature intends annual appropriations of $1,150,000 for 20 years. The Board may enter long‑term financing agreements with private parties through June 30, 2045, may use conveyance/lease or lease‑back arrangements to facilitate financing, and may schedule payments monthly, quarterly, or semiannually; it may not pledge the State’s credit except for legislative appropriations. On or before August 31, 2045, unused project funds from the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund and the General Fund held in the treasury or by trustees must be transferred to the General Fund. Legislative intent protects appropriations until obligations are satisfied but not beyond FY ending June 30, 2045. Also an intended $250,000 appropriation to the Board for educational technology in Norfolk.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates authority for the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska to design and build student residential facilities in Norfolk. State-supported project cost: $23,000,000, funded by the Legislature via intent to appropriate $1,150,000 annually for 20 years. Regents may enter long‑term financing agreements with private parties (payments through June 30, 2045), may convey/lease/lease‑back property to facilitate financing, and may schedule payments monthly/quarterly/semiannually. Regents may not pledge the State's credit except for legislative appropriations. Any project funds from the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund or the General Fund held in the treasury or by trustees and not needed for financing agreements must be transferred to the General Fund by August 31, 2045. Appropriations may not be reduced until related contracts/securities are paid but will not extend beyond FY ending June 30, 2045. Also declares intent to appropriate $250,000 to the Regents for educational technology in Norfolk.
