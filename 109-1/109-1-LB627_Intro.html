Summary

Provides the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska authority to design and construct student residential facilities for nursing and allied health in Norfolk. State-supported project cost: $23,000,000. The Legislature intends annual appropriations of $1,150,000 for 20 years to support the project. The Board may enter long‑term financing contracts (payments not to extend beyond June 30, 2045), may convey/lease or lease back facilities to facilitate financing, and may schedule payments monthly, quarterly, or semiannually. The Board may not pledge the State's credit except for legislative appropriations. Any unneeded funds appropriated from the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund and the General Fund held in the treasury or by trustees shall be transferred to the General Fund by August 31, 2045. Appropriations for the project should not be reduced until related contracts and securities are completed or paid, but not beyond FY ending June 30, 2045. Also intends a $250,000 appropriation to the Board for educational technology in Norfolk.
