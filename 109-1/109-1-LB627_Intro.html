Summary

Bill LB627 authorizes the University of Nebraska Board of Regents to design and construct student residential facilities for nursing and allied health in Norfolk. It establishes a state-supported project cost of $23,000,000 and expresses legislative intent to appropriate $1,150,000 annually for 20 years to fund the project. The Board may enter long‑term financing contracts with private entities (payments not to extend beyond June 30, 2045), and may convey, lease, or lease back facilities to facilitate financing; the Board may not pledge the State’s credit except for legislative appropriations. Unused funds from the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund and the General Fund related to the project held in the treasury or by trustees must be transferred to the General Fund by August 31, 2045. The Legislature also intends to appropriate $250,000 to the Board for educational technology for Norfolk facilities.
