Summary

LB434 amends the State Fire Marshal statutes (section 81-505.01) to specify that fees for fire safety inspections of foster family homes (as defined in §71-1901) may be paid by the Department of Health and Human Services. No specific appropriation, funding amount, or budget allocation to DHHS is included in the bill.

For DHHS: the added text creates/adjusts fee provisions that direct collected fees to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the designated account(s).
