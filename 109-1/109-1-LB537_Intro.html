Summary

LB537 directs the Department of Labor to administer the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and to provide workforce development grants. The department must award grants evenly across the three congressional districts. Grants are to be used for developing job skills, money management, communication skills, job preparation, and job placement. Applicants must demonstrate one-to-one private fund matching, and grant recipients must report to the Department of Labor on job placement and self‑sufficiency outcomes. The Legislature intends to transfer $1,500,000 from the General Fund to the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund for FY2025‑26 for the department to provide these grants.
