Summary

LB537 directs the Department of Labor to administer the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and to provide workforce development grants, to be awarded evenly among Nebraska’s three congressional districts. Grants must be used for job skills, money management, communication, job preparation, and placement; applicants must demonstrate one-to-one private matching. Grant recipients must report to the Department of Labor on job placement and self-sufficiency outcomes for individuals served. The Legislature intends to transfer $1,500,000 from the General Fund to the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund for FY2025-26 for the department to provide these grants.
