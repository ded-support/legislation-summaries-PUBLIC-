Summary

LB571 (2025) requires public power districts that operate a defined benefit pension plan to begin, January 1, 2026, paying a cost-of-living adjustment to retirees who are not eligible for Social Security. The adjustment must equal the annual COLA determined by the federal Social Security Administration. No state agencies other than public power districts and the federal SSA are referenced; the bill contains no express appropriations, funding allocations, or budgetary provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective Jan. 1, 2026, any public power district that operates a defined benefit pension plan must provide retirees who are not eligible for Social Security a cost‑of‑living adjustment equal to the annual COLA set by the federal Social Security Administration.
