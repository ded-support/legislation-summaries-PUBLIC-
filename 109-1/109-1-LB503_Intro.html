Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns these specific responsibilities to it:

- Receive and process county applications for "American energy friendly" designation on a department-prescribed form; designate qualifying counties within 30 days and notify the county board; maintain a current list on its website of applicants/designated counties.

- Collect and enforce the nameplate capacity tax (including a referenced tax rate), require annual owner reports (filed March 1), and prorate tax changes when a county gains or loses designation.

- Separately identify tax proceeds from this section and remit them to the county treasurer where the facility is located within 30 days of receipt.

- Adopt and promulgate rules and regulations necessary to implement the section.

No direct changes to department appropriations or other state budget appropriations are provided in the bill text.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key provisions affecting the Department of Revenue (the "department"):

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Revenue.

- The department must accept county applications (on a department-prescribed form) and, if statutory conditions are met, designate a county as an "American energy friendly county" and notify the county board within 30 days of receipt.

- The department must maintain a current list on its website of counties that have applied/been designated.

- The department may adopt rules and regulations to implement the section.

- The department is tied to tax administration: the increased nameplate-capacity tax for owners of privately developed renewable facilities is prorated in the first year a county gains or loses the designation (based on days designated).

No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the department are specified.
