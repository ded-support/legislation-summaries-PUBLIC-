Summary

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Revenue.

- The Department of Revenue receives county applications for "American energy friendly" designation, must act within 30 days to grant designation, and maintain an up-to-date list of applicant/designated counties on its website. The department may adopt rules to implement these provisions.

- The department is directed to collect the nameplate capacity tax, enforce this tax statute, and may promulgate necessary rules and regulations.

- Owners must file annual nameplate capacity reports with the Department by March 1; taxes are due April 1 and paid quarterly.

- The department will prorate any increased nameplate capacity tax based on the number of days it designated a county as American energy friendly in a year.

- The Department must separately identify tax proceeds and remit them to the county treasurer within 30 days of receipt.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns these responsibilities to it: receive county applications for "American energy friendly county" designation; decide and notify counties within 30 days if statutory requirements are met; maintain an up‑to‑date list on its website of counties that have applied/been designated; adopt rules and regulations to implement the program; and apply a proration rule for the increased nameplate‑capacity tax in the first year a county gains or loses designation (tax increase prorated to days designated). No new appropriations, funding lines, or budget allocations for the Department are specified.
