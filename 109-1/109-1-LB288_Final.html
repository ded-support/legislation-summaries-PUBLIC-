Summary

The bill amends multiple affordable- and workforce-housing provisions involving the Department of Economic Development (DED). Key DED actions and fiscal provisions:

- DED may not require new-construction or rental-conversion projects funded from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to satisfy other department program application requirements (Sec. 10).

- DED shall prioritize funding applications using available funds reduced by actual administrative costs, may contract for services, and may recapture unused or unspent awards (recaptured funds credit to the Trust Fund) (Secs. 11–12).

- DED must include its administration budget, planned fund uses, and project-level summaries (including grant amounts requested) in its annual report (Sec. 12).

- DED will administer the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund, create a subaccount for Economic Recovery Act parcel preparation, seek private funds, apply interest to grants, and return or transfer unexpended funds to the General Fund under specified timelines (Sec. 16).

- Matching for workforce housing grants is reduced from 50% to 25% for awards on/after July 19, 2024; DED updates construction/value limits annually using the Producer Price Index (Secs. 15, 7).

The Department of Labor is not referenced in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development

- New provisions govern Affordable Housing Trust Fund awards: the department may recoup funds if a recipient fails to complete agreed housing units (pro rata) and must credit recaptured amounts back to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund.

- Recapture also applies to homebuyer assistance provided by recipients — repayment from net sale proceeds goes back to the Fund.

- The department may impose sanctions for misuse, including suspension from applying for up to 24 months, other administrative measures (not unduly harming reputation), and standard criminal referrals for fraud/theft.

- The department may not require new-construction or rental-conversion projects funded by the Trust Fund to meet other department program application requirements and shall not restrict construction/sale of units funded from specified sources.
