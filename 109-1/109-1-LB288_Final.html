Summary

LB288 assigns several new duties and funding controls to the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED may not require Affordable Housing Trust Fund–funded new construction or rental-conversion projects to meet other department program requirements. DED shall prioritize funding to fully utilize available funds net of administrative costs, may recapture unused or unspent award funds (crediting recaptures to the Trust Fund), and must report extensive Trust Fund details — including the department's current administration budget and planned use/distribution — in its annual status report. Workforce housing grants: competitive awards (through FY2026-27) capped at $10 million per nonprofit over two years; matching requirement reduced from 50% to 25% for awards on/after July 19, 2024. DED will administer the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund, create a subaccount for land-parcel work, apply interest to grants, require return of inactive grants after 24 months, and transfer remaining fund balances to the General Fund on July 1, 2029. The bill references the U.S. Department of Labor’s BLS Producer Price Index for annual indexing.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development

- Prohibits the department from imposing additional program requirements on new construction or rental-conversion projects that receive Affordable Housing Trust Fund (AHTF) funding.

- Authorizes administrative sanctions for misuse of funds, including disqualification of a qualified recipient from AHTF applications for up to 24 months and other actions short of reputational harm; criminal acts (e.g., fraud, theft) remain subject to appropriate enforcement.

- Requires recapture of AHTF dollars when awardees fail to complete agreed housing units; recaptured sums are credited back to the AHTF. Also requires recipients to recapture and return homebuyer assistance from net sale proceeds when assisted homes are sold.
