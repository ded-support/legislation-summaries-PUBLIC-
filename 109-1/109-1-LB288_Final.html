Summary

- Department of Economic Development (DED) responsibilities:

- Administer the Nebraska Affordable Housing Act and Affordable Housing Trust Fund: fund applications in priority order, contract for services or provide funds to other governmental entities, recapture misused or unexpended funds (crediting recaptures to the Trust Fund), and proportionally recapture funds for incomplete projects.

- Annual reporting requirements: include funded applications, recipients, locations, amounts, leveraged funds, units/occupancy, expected rents/payments, projected employment/community investment, revenue into the Trust Fund, total applications, year-end balance, appropriated funds actually expended, DED’s current administration budget and planned use/distribution of funds, plus project summaries.

- Establish and award workforce housing investment grants (director authority); grant caps ($10M per grantee over two years), changed matching requirements (50% pre‑7/19/24; ≥25% on/after 7/19/24), and unallocated funds roll forward.

- Create/administer the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund, including a subaccount for Economic Recovery Act land-prep, apply interest earnings to grants, require return of grants to General Fund if inactive 24 months, and transfer remaining fund balance to the General Fund on July 1, 2029.

- Require annual certification and reporting from nonprofit grantees to be included in DED’s status report.

- Department of Labor (U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics):

- DED shall annually update specified housing cost thresholds using the Producer Price Index for all commodities published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development

- Prohibits DED from imposing other department program application/requirement conditions on new construction or rental-conversion projects receiving Affordable Housing Trust Fund (AHTF) funds.

- Grants DED enforcement options for misuse of AHTF funds, including recapture, up to 24-month disqualification from AHTF applications, and other measures that avoid undue reputational harm; illegal acts (e.g., fraud, theft) remain separately actionable.

- Requires DED to recapture a pro rata share of awarded AHTF funds when a recipient fails to complete agreed housing and to credit recaptured dollars back to the AHTF.

- Requires recipients to recapture and return AHTF-provided homebuyer assistance from net sale proceeds when the assisted home is sold.
