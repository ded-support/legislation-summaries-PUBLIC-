Summary

LB338 (2025) bars life insurers, disability insurers, and long-term care insurers from using genetic information or genetic test results—absent a clinical diagnosis—to deny, cancel, limit coverage, set premium differentials, or impose preexisting-condition exclusions. Insurers also may not require or solicit genetic information or consider an individual's genetic-testing decisions for underwriting. Permitted actions include reviewing an applicant's medical record, asking about family history, and relying on documented clinical diagnoses. The bill cites the federal Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (29 U.S.C. 1182) and uses the definitions in Neb. Rev. §48-236. No state agencies are assigned new responsibilities, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.
