Summary

LB424 references the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics by defining "inflation rate" as the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U), as prepared by that federal agency for the 12‑month period ending August 31. That inflation rate is used (subject to a 3% cap) in the bill's allowable growth percentage that limits year‑to‑year increases in property tax bills. The bill contains no changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text defines "inflation rate" as the year-over-year change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) as produced by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics. That CPI-U figure is used to set the allowable growth percentage (capped at 3%) which limits year-to-year increases in property tax bills, excluding increases due to property improvements. No changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
