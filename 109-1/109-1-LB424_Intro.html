Summary

LB424 references the U.S. Department of Labor in defining "inflation rate" as the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U), as prepared by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, for the 12‑month period ending August 31 of the year the property tax bill is determined. That CPI‑U figure is used to set the "allowable growth percentage" (the lesser of the inflation rate or 3%) that caps annual increases in individual property tax bills. The bill contains no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provision caps year‑to‑year property tax bill increases at the lesser of (a) the inflation rate or (b) 3%. It defines “inflation rate” as the 12‑month change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) as prepared by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics for the period ending August 31 of the tax year. Improvements to a parcel are excluded from the cap. There are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes for the Department of Labor or BLS in this text; the Department’s role is limited to supplying the CPI‑U data used to calculate the cap.
