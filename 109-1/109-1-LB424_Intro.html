Summary

LB424 defines "inflation rate" as the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) as prepared by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, for the 12‑month period ending August 31 of the year the property tax bill is determined. The bill uses that inflation rate to establish allowable property tax growth. No Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text caps year‑to‑year increases in a parcel’s property tax bill at the "allowable growth percentage," defined as the lesser of the annual inflation rate or 3%. "Inflation rate" is defined as the 12‑month percent change in the CPI for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) for the period ending August 31, as prepared by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics. Property tax bills cannot rise more than that allowable growth over the prior year, except for increases attributable to improvements to the parcel. No changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
