Summary

The bill defines "inflation rate" as the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) as prepared by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics, for the 12‑month period ending August 31 of the year the property tax bill is determined. No provisions in the bill alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language caps annual increases in an individual property tax bill to the "allowable growth percentage," defined as the lesser of (a) the inflation rate or (b) 3%. The "inflation rate" is specified as the 12‑month change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) as prepared by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics. The cap does not apply to tax increases caused by property improvements. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor or any other agency are created or altered.
