Summary

LB514 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §14-102 to add a new power for cities of the metropolitan class to regulate any housing authority within their boundaries. Authorized actions include imposing code enforcement and pest-control requirements on housing authority–owned/controlled properties; mandating regular inspections; requiring compliance with city rental-registration ordinances; setting penalties for code violations and mismanagement; and requiring monthly reports to the city council. The bill repeals the original section. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for cities of the metropolitan class to regulate their housing authorities. Measures include requiring code enforcement and pest control for authority-owned/controlled properties; regular inspections; enrollment of authority‑managed properties in the city's rental registration program; penalties for code violations and poor property management; and monthly reporting to the city council. No agencies beyond the city, its housing authority, and the city council are referenced, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
