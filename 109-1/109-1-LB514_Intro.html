Summary

LB514 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §14-102 to expressly authorize cities of the metropolitan class, by ordinance, to regulate housing authorities. New municipal powers include requiring code enforcement and pest control on housing authority–owned/controlled properties; regular inspections; mandatory registration of housing authority-managed units under any city rental-registration ordinance; establishment of penalties for code or management failures; and monthly reporting from the housing authority to the city council. The bill repeals the original section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for a metropolitan-class city to regulate its housing authority. Requirements include: city code enforcement and pest control for all housing-authority‑owned/controlled properties; regular inspections of all managed properties; mandatory registration under the city's rental registration ordinance; penalties for code violations and mismanagement; and monthly updates to the city council. Government entities referenced: the housing authority, the metropolitan-class city (and its code enforcement function), and the city council. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
