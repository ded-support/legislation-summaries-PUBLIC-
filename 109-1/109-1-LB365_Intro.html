Summary

LB365 amends §68‑911 to require the Department of Health and Human Services to, among other duties:

- Provide coverage and reimbursement for self‑measured blood pressure monitoring services by January 1, 2026.

- Provide coverage for continuous glucose monitors by January 1, 2023, expanded August 1, 2024 (includes gestational diabetes); intent that no more than $600,000 annually be appropriated from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for the expansion, with any excess funded by that Fund.

- Seek/submit state plan amendments or waivers to extend postpartum coverage (by Oct. 1, 2023) and to designate two medical respite facilities (by Oct. 1, 2025); medical respite services to be funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

- Provide electric personal‑use breast pumps and ten lactation consultation visits by Jan. 1, 2025; Legislature intends a 145% rate increase appropriation for lactation consultations.

- Provide translation/interpretation coverage by Jan. 1, 2024 and maximize federal matching funds; funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to, by January 1, 2026, provide coverage and reimbursement under the Medical Assistance program for self-measured blood pressure monitoring services for all MA-eligible persons. Covered items/services must include: a validated BP device; appropriately sized and replacement cuffs; patient education/training on device setup/use, clinical validation/calibration, and measurement procedures; and collection/transmission of patient/caregiver BP data to providers to inform treatment. No funding, appropriation, or budget-allocation changes are specified.
