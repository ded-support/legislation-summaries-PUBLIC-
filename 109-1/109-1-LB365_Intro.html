Summary

LB365 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-911 to impose specific duties on the Department of Health and Human Services (the department). Key provisions require the department to: provide coverage and reimbursement for self-measured blood pressure monitoring services by Jan. 1, 2026 (device, appropriately sized/ replacement cuffs, patient training, and data reporting); provide continuous glucose monitor coverage per prior deadlines and expansion (with intent that no more than $600,000 annually be appropriated from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund and excess funded by that fund); seek federal approval to extend postpartum coverage; submit a waiver/state plan for two medical respite facilities (funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund); provide electric personal-use breast pumps and ten lactation consultations (legislative intent for an appropriation equal to a 145% rate increase); and cover translation/interpretation services (funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

By January 1, 2026, the Department of Health and Human Services must provide coverage and reimbursement under the medical assistance program for self-measured blood pressure monitoring for all eligible beneficiaries. Covered services must include: (a) a validated blood pressure monitor; (b) appropriately sized and replacement cuffs as needed; (c) patient education and training on device setup, use, clinical accuracy, calibration, and measurement procedures; and (d) collection of patient- or caregiver-reported readings for submission to providers to inform or adjust treatment plans. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.
