Summary

LB365 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to take multiple Medicaid actions and makes specific funding intentions:

- By Jan 1, 2026, DHHS must provide coverage and reimbursement for self-measured blood pressure monitoring services (device, cuffs, training, data collection).

- By Jan 1, 2025, DHHS must cover electric personal-use breast pumps and a minimum of ten lactation consultation visits; the Legislature intends the appropriation for those visits equal a 145% increase over DHHS’s current lactation consultation rate.

- By Jan 1, 2024, DHHS must cover and reimburse necessary translation/interpretation services and maximize federal funding; those services are to be funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

- By Jan 1, 2023, DHHS must provide continuous glucose monitor coverage; effective Aug 1, 2024 eligibility expands. The Legislature intends up to $600,000 annually be appropriated from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for that expansion (excess also funded from the Fund).

- By Oct 1, 2023, DHHS must seek federal approval to extend postpartum Medicaid coverage to at least six months.

- By Oct 1, 2025, DHHS must submit a waiver/state plan amendment to designate two medical respite facilities for homeless adults in the expansion population; those services shall be funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund and DHHS must report annually after approval.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requirement for the Department of Health and Human Services to, by January 1, 2026, provide coverage and reimbursement under the medical assistance program for self-measure blood pressure monitoring services for all eligible recipients. Covered services must include a validated blood pressure device, appropriately sized (and replacement) cuffs, patient education/training on device setup/use, calibration and measurement procedures, and patient/caregiver data reporting to providers for treatment planning. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation language is included.
