Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services. It requires physicians who provide an abortion‑inducing drug to file a monthly report with the Department within 30 days after the end of the calendar month in which the drug was provided, including information specified by the Department's rules and regulations and a Department‑produced standard form. Required report data include physician name, drug and dates, follow‑up date, documented adverse events, follow‑up treatment, and referral purpose; reports must exclude personally identifying information. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and assigns it responsibility for receiving physician reports on provision of abortion-inducing drugs. Physicians must file a monthly report (within 30 days after the end of the calendar month) using a department standard form; the report must include physician name, drug(s) and dates provided, follow‑up visit date, documented adverse events, subsequent treatment, and referrals, but may not include any woman's personally identifying information. The department may adopt rules and regulations to implement reporting and must produce the standard form. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
