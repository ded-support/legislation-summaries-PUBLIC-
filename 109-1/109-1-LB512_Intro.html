Summary

LB512 defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and assigns the department responsibility for collecting reports on provision of abortion‑inducing drugs. Physicians must file a report with the department within 30 days after the end of the calendar month in which the drug was provided. The department must produce a standard reporting form. Required report fields include physician name; drug name and dates; follow‑up visit date; documentation of adverse events; follow‑up treatment; and referral purpose. Reports must exclude personally identifying information. The bill contains no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added Chemical Abortion Safety Protocol Act requires physicians to file de-identified reports with the Department of Health and Human Services within 30 days after the end of the month in which an abortion-inducing drug was provided. Reports must use a department-produced standard form and include the physician’s name, drug(s) and dates, follow-up visit date (follow-up required 3–14 days post‑provision), documented adverse events, any follow‑up treatment, and referral purpose. The department may adopt rules/regulations specifying additional required information. Reports must exclude personally identifying patient information. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
