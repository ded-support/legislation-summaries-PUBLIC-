Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Requires any physician who provides an abortion‑inducing drug to file a monthly report with DHHS within 30 days after the end of the calendar month in which the drug was provided. Reports must include physician name, drug name and dates, follow‑up visit date (if any), documented adverse events, follow‑up treatment, and referral purpose, in addition to information required by DHHS rules and regulations. DHHS must produce a standard reporting form. Reports must not include personally identifying information. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions define “Department” as the Department of Health and Human Services and require physicians who provide abortion‑inducing drugs to file monthly reports with the Department (within 30 days after the end of the calendar month in which the drug was provided). The Department must produce a standard reporting form and may adopt rules specifying additional required data. Required (non‑identifying) report fields include physician name; drug name(s) and dates provided; follow‑up visit date; documentation of adverse events; any follow‑up treatment; and purpose of any referral. Reports must exclude personally identifying information. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are specified.
