Summary

LB674 directs the State Treasurer to transfer $250 million from the Perkins County Canal Project Fund to the General Fund and $250 million to the Water Sustainability Fund on or before June 30, 2025. Both transfers are to occur on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services, giving that budget administrator authority to schedule and size the transfers.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the State Treasurer to transfer two separate $250 million sums (total $500 million) from the Perkins County Canal Project Fund by June 30, 2025: one to the General Fund and one to the Water Sustainability Fund. Both transfers are to occur on dates and in amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the Budget Division within the Department of Administrative Services, giving that DAS official control over timing and phasing of the transfers.
