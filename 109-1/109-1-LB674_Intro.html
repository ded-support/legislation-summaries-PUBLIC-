Summary

The bill directs the State Treasurer to transfer a total of $500 million from the Perkins County Canal Project Fund by June 30, 2025: $250 million to the General Fund and $250 million to the Water Sustainability Fund. Both transfers are to occur on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the State Treasurer to make two transfers from the Perkins County Canal Project Fund by June 30, 2025: $250 million to the General Fund and $250 million to the Water Sustainability Fund. The timing and specific amounts of each transfer are to be set by the budget administrator in the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services, giving that DAS official directional control over the transfers' schedule and allocation. Total redirected funds: $500 million.
