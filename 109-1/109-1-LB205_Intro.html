Summary

LB205 restricts admissible evidence and awards for medical damages in Nebraska civil actions and imposes a $1,000,000 cap on noneconomic damages against commercial motor vehicle carriers. It directs courts to admit only specified measures of past and future medical costs: actual amounts paid; insurer-allowed amounts plus the claimant’s share when private coverage exists; or, for uninsured or Medicare/Medicaid patients, 120% of the applicable Medicare reimbursement rate or 170% of the state Medicaid rate. Claimants must disclose health coverage status and any letters of protection; transfers of accounts receivable to factoring companies and purchase amounts must be disclosed. For commercial motor vehicle cases (as defined at §60‑316), juries or judges must first find noneconomic damages then the court must reduce awards to the $1,000,000 limit. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds rules limiting admissible evidence and recoverable medical damages in personal-injury/wrongful-death cases: defines "letter of protection"; limits past unpaid charges to amounts actually paid, insurer obligations, or (if uninsured or on Medicare/Medicaid) 120% of the Medicare reimbursement rate or 170% of the applicable state Medicaid rate; requires disclosure if medical receivables were sold, of the purchaser and purchase price, and disclosure of the claimant's health coverage and any letter-of-protection referrals (including attorney referrals). Establishes a $1 million cap on noneconomic damages for claims involving commercial motor vehicles requiring a CDL, with juries/courts instructed to first find full damages then reduce awards to the cap. Government programs referenced: Medicare and Medicaid (including state Medicaid rates). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
