Summary

LB654 appropriates General Fund transfers to the Department of Agriculture (Program 78): $300,000 for FY2025-26 and $300,000 for FY2026-27, designated as state aid. The funds are earmarked to purchase assistive technology and equipment for farmers and ranchers through the Nebraska AgrAbility program where such items are not eligible for U.S. Department of Agriculture funding. The act contains an emergency clause and takes effect upon passage.

Appropriates $300,000 General Fund for FY2025-26 and $300,000 General Fund for FY2026-27 to the Department of Agriculture, Program 78 (state aid).
