Summary

LB43 amends Nebraska electric law to add and clarify agency roles and certification duties. It (1) defines "military installation" to specifically include certain U.S. Air Force silos and bases (per 31 C.F.R.); (2) requires owners of generation facilities or transmission >700 volts located within a ten-mile radius of a military installation to certify to the board (and notify its executive director) that no electronic-related equipment or components are manufactured by a listed foreign adversary, with a NERC Critical Infrastructure Protection (CIP) compliance exemption for retail electric suppliers and a similar NERC-based compliance option for privately developed renewable projects interconnected at ≥100 kV meeting specified size thresholds; (3) preserves board authority to preapprove otherwise prohibited equipment, issue acknowledgements within ten days, enforce compliance (including hearings and cessation orders), and bring actions in the name of the State. The Game and Parks Commission is required consultation on wildlife impacts. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for electric suppliers and facility owners near military installations to certify to "the board" that generation, transmission, or related equipment within a ten‑mile radius contains no electronic-related equipment or components made by a defined "foreign adversary" (per 15 C.F.R. 791.4) and to notify the board if compliance lapses. The definition of covered sites explicitly references certain U.S. Air Force ballistic missile silos and bases (31 C.F.R. 802.211(b)(3) and 802.227(c)). Owners of larger facilities tied to the grid (≥100 kV and specified MVA thresholds) must instead certify compliance with North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) critical infrastructure protection standards. The board may preapprove use of foreign‑adversary equipment only if no reasonable alternative exists and avoiding use would cause greater harm to the state or residents. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
