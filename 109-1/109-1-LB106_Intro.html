Summary

LB106 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60‑6,257 to revise motor vehicle window tinting standards. It prohibits nontransparent or colorized sunscreening on windshields below the AS‑1 line and bars red, yellow, or amber sunscreening above the AS‑1 line. It sets luminous reflectance and visible light‑transmission limits at 20% for front side windows and for rear/side windows behind the front seat (with exceptions for multipurpose vehicles, vans, and buses) and requires federally compliant safety glazing for campers, motor homes, pickup covers, and similar equipment. The bill defines AS‑1 and related terms and repeals the original section. No state agencies, appropriations, or budget changes are referenced.
