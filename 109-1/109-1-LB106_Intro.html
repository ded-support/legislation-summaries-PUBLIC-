Summary

LB106 (Hughes) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,257 to revise motor vehicle window-tint restrictions. Key provisions: prohibits tint that reduces the driver's clear view or substantially impairs visual access into a vehicle; bars nonclear windshield sunscreening below the AS-1 line or red/yellow/amber sunscreening above it; requires front side windows to have luminous reflectance ≤ 20% and light transmission ≥ 20%; applies the same limits to rear/side windows behind the front seat except for multipurpose vehicles, vans, and buses; and requires windows on campers, motor homes, pickup covers and similar units to meet federal safety glazing standards (49 C.F.R. 571.205). Introduced to the Transportation and Telecommunications Committee. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included; the bill repeals the original section.
