Summary

LB559 creates new financial-crime offenses (skimmer installation/use; continuing criminal enterprise for financial transaction offenses) and expands forfeiture authority for covered offenses to include equipment, components, peripherals, software, hardware, accessories related to electronic communication devices. Procedural changes require the prosecuting authority to specifically plead forfeiture in the criminal information and authorize district-court pretrial and post‑conviction forfeiture hearings and intervention by third‑party claimants.

Fiscal/program provisions: the Financial Fraud Victims' Reimbursement Fund is created and administered by the Attorney General, funded by assets forfeited from financial transaction offenses. The State Investment Officer invests fund assets under existing state investment statutes. The Attorney General prescribes application processes, determines qualifying victim reimbursements (pro rata if insufficient funds), and holds funds for at least 36 months; thereafter remaining amounts are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutionally directed distribution. No direct appropriation or budget allocation is specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Financial Fraud Victims' Reimbursement Fund, to be administered by the Nebraska Attorney General. The fund is to consist of assets forfeited from "financial transaction offenses" (per section 28-1601). The state investment officer will invest any available funds pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Attorney General will set application procedures, determine qualifying losses, and distribute reimbursements (pro rata if claims exceed available funds). Funds transferred into the fund remain there until the later of 36 months after transfer or resolution of applications filed in that period; thereafter remaining amounts are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution. No specific appropriation amounts are provided.
