Summary

The bill vests broad administration of the new Nebraska EPIC consumption tax in the Department of Revenue (DOR): rulemaking (by Sept. 1, 2025), forms and tax‑exempt certificate applications online by Oct. 1, 2027, continuing education, audits, summons, liens/garnishment/seizure, designation of tax collectors and bond requirements, limited collection of specified 2027 taxes during calendar year 2028, and maintenance of reporting systems (hotline and awards). Proceeds are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund; the Tax Commissioner must deliver an annual report beginning 2029. DOR confidentiality is strengthened (unauthorized disclosure a Class IV felony) with specified disclosure exceptions and limited tax‑collection agreement terms (only for finally assessed, unappealed taxes; up to 12 months). Interest on final assessments accrues from entry date as a lump sum. The Department of Health and Human Services must certify developmental disability for homestead exemption on DOR‑prescribed forms; certification need not be renewed annually unless a change is requested. No direct appropriations are specified in the provided text.

Department of Revenue — Major new duties and deadlines: collect and enforce a statewide 7.5% "consumption tax" effective Jan 1, 2028 (replacing state income, sales, property, motor vehicle, and inheritance taxes as of Dec 31, 2027); remit proceeds to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund; create tax-exempt certificate rules by Sept. 1, 2025 and an electronic/paper application by Oct. 1, 2027; administer registration, reporting, audits, summonses, penalties, large-seller weekly remittances and security requirements, fraud awards/hotline, recordkeeping, receipts rules, and confidentiality/disclosure provisions; maintain employee training and begin an annual audit report to the Legislature in 2029.
