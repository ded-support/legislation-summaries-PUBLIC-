Summary

The bill gives the Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner) broad administration and enforcement authority for a new consumption tax: register/audit sellers; collect/remit tax receipts (remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund); design forms and tax‑exempt certificates; designate collectors and require surety; summon records, seize assets, garnish wages, file liens; set penalties and appeals; operate a hotline and fraud‑reward program; publish monthly interest rates; maintain employee training; and provide an annual in‑person report to the Legislature’s Revenue Committee beginning 2029. Rules are required by Sept. 1, 2025; applications available by Oct. 1, 2027. Collection‑agreement recoveries are limited to finally assessed, unappealed taxes and capped at 12 months. Department of Revenue employees face a Class IV felony for unauthorized disclosure; permitted disclosures and deidentified reporting provisions are detailed. Beginning 2028, agencies (including Revenue) must file annual budget requests by Sept. 15 subject to CPI limits. The Department of Health and Human Services must certify developmental‑disability homestead exemption eligibility on forms prescribed by the Department of Revenue; annual recertification is not required unless the medical condition changes. No specific appropriations to either agency are provided.

Department of Revenue

- Replaces Nebraska income, sales/use, property, motor vehicle, and inheritance taxes with a statewide 7.5% "consumption tax" effective Jan 1, 2028; Dept. of Revenue (Tax Commissioner) administers registration, collection, audits, enforcement (liens, garnishment, criminal penalties), large-seller remittance/security rules, tax‑exempt certificates (rules by 9/1/2025; application online/paper by 10/1/2027), transitional inventory credit, and other compliance provisions.

- Consumption tax receipts are collected by the Department of Revenue and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund; the State Treasurer disburses revenue and the Dept. of Administrative Services administers/disburses agency shares.

- Departmental obligations: maintain employee continuing education; begin annual audit/activity reporting to the Legislature in 2029; penalty-abatement and confidentiality rules updated.
