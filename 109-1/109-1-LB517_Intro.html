Summary

LB517 amends duties of the State School Security Director (appointed under §79‑2,143) within the Nebraska Department of Education. School districts are required to provide the director with safety and security plans, annual inspection information for protective door assemblies, door‑locking systems, and double‑entry doors, and any other security information requested (with submitted materials subject to withholding under Neb. Rev. Stat. §84‑712.05). The director must establish protective door‑assembly standards, including requiring each school board to cause annual inspections by trained inspectors, collect plans per accreditation rules, recommend minimum standards to the State Board of Education, oversee related training/programs, and implement the School Safety and Security Reporting System Act. No funding or appropriations are specified. The original §79‑2,144 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for school boards to establish standards for protective door assemblies in school buildings and to provide annual inspection information for protective door assemblies, door‑locking systems, and double‑entry doors within the school district. Each school board must cause annual inspections of all applicable protective door assemblies to be conducted by a trained inspector with appropriate knowledge, training, and experience in door assemblies and building security. Defines "protective door assemblies" to include doors with panic or fire‑exit hardware, door assemblies in exit enclosures, electronically controlled egress doors, and doors with special locking arrangements (e.g., delayed egress, sensor‑released egress, elevator‑lobby doors). No state agencies beyond local school boards are named and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
