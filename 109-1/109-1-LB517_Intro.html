Summary

LB517 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-2,144 to expand duties and reporting requirements tied to the State School Security Director (appointed under §79-2,143) and the State Department of Education. School districts must provide the director with accreditation-related safety/security plans, annual inspection information for protective door assemblies, door-locking systems, and double-entry doors, and any other security information requested (subject to withholding under §84-712.05(9)). The director will set minimum standards (to the State Board of Education), establish protective-door-assembly standards requiring annual inspections by qualified inspectors, conduct statewide school-security assessments, oversee behavioral/mental-health training per §79-2,146, and administer the School Safety and Security Reporting System Act. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that school districts/school boards maintain annual inspection information for protective door assemblies, door‑locking systems, and double‑entry doors, and establishes standards requiring each school board to cause annual inspections of applicable protective door assemblies by a trained inspector with appropriate knowledge/training/experience in door assemblies and building security. Defines "protective door assemblies" to include doors with panic or fire‑exit hardware; door assemblies in exit enclosures; electronically controlled egress doors; and doors with special locking arrangements (e.g., delayed‑egress, sensor‑released, elevator‑lobby). No state agency or funding/appropriation changes are specified.
