Summary

LB175 amends §74‑1335 to allow a landowner with property on both sides of a railroad right‑of‑way to file a written complaint with the Nebraska Department of Transportation alleging an inadequate or unsafe crossing. The Department must investigate, hold hearings, and issue necessary orders. The Department may require overhead, underground, or grade separations, wing fences at underground crossings, or relocation of existing crossings. Funding detail: where a special crossing costs more than $1,500, the landowner must pay one‑half of the expenses in excess of $1,500.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a broad definition of “successor in interest” to include agents, successors, assignees, trustees, receivers, or any person acquiring interests/rights in railroad land — explicitly covering holders of servient estates and rights of reversion. For the Department of Transportation, this clarifies who may be treated as the railroad landowner for notices, transactions, property management, and enforcement actions. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Transportation are included.
