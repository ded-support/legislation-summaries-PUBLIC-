Summary

LB175 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §74-1335 to expressly include a railroad’s "successor in interest" and directs that any landowner with property on both sides of a railroad right-of-way may file a written complaint with the Department of Transportation if a crossing is inadequate or unsafe. The Department of Transportation must investigate, hold hearings, and issue necessary orders. The department may require overhead, underground, or grade crossings, wing fences, or relocation of existing crossings. For special crossings costing more than $1,500, the landowner must pay one-half of the expense amount exceeding $1,500. The bill contains no changes to DOT appropriations or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds/clarifies a definition of “successor in interest” to include any agent, successor, assignee, trustee, receiver, or other person acquiring interests or rights in railroad land — explicitly including owners/holders of servient estates or rights of reversion. Also inserts repeated language to cover references to “its successor in interest.” No Department of Transportation programs, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
