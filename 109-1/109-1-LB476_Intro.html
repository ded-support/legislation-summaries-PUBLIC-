Summary

LB476 assigns the Department of Agriculture a primary investigatory role under the Foreign‑owned Real Estate National Security Act. Any person may notify the Department or the Attorney General of suspected violations; the Department shall investigate. If the Director of Agriculture has reasonable suspicion, the director must refer the matter to the Attorney General or to outside counsel retained by the Department for enforcement. Referred matters may lead the Attorney General or retained counsel to issue subpoenas and commence actions. The court may approve reimbursement of investigation and litigation costs and expenses to the Attorney General or retained outside counsel from divestment proceeds. No direct appropriations or other budgetary allocations to the Department are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added section creates a permanent exemption from the Foreign‑owned Real Estate National Security Act for property interests that were acquired either (a) while the holder was present in the U.S. as an employment‑authorized alien under 8 C.F.R. 274a.12, or (b) while the holder did not meet the definition of a nonresident alien. Such interests cannot later be voided, divested, or otherwise subjected to the Act so long as the holder keeps the interest, even if the holder later becomes a nonresident alien. The exemption expressly excludes restricted entities and their agents/trustees/fiduciaries. No Department of Agriculture references or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
