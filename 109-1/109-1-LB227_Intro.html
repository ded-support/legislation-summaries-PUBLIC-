Summary

The bill addresses Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) involvement only in medicaid nonemergency medical transportation and contracts with DHHS or its contractors. Key provisions:

- Designations/permits may authorize carriers to provide medicaid nonemergency medical transportation under contract with (i) DHHS, (ii) medicaid-managed care organizations under DHHS contract, or (iii) DHHS agents per section 75-303.01.

- In evaluating such authorizations, the Public Service Commission must consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (or designee) of DHHS's Division of Medicaid and Long-Term Care.

- Transportation network companies or participating drivers may not transport persons under DHHS contracts without specific commission authorization; the commission must grant authorization unless a protestant proves existing carriers meet the need or the applicant would impair existing carriers.

- No appropriations, funding, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that when the commission considers authorizing providers to deliver Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) under a contract with the Department of Health and Human Services, a Medicaid managed‑care organization under contract with the department, or an agent acting for the department, the commission must consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care (or designee). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or mentioned.
