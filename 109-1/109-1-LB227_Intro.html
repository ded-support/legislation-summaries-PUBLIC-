Summary

LB227 amends carrier authorization rules involving the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). For designations and permits to provide Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) under contracts with (i) DHHS, (ii) Medicaid-managed care organizations under DHHS contract, or (iii) agents acting on DHHS’s behalf, the Public Service Commission must consult the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (or designee) when determining public interest and necessity. The bill also requires transportation network companies and participating drivers to obtain specific commission authorization before providing transportation for persons under contract with DHHS or DHHS contractors; such authorization must be granted unless protested on specified competitive-impact grounds. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that when the commission considers granting authority to provide Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) under a contract with (i) the Department of Health and Human Services, (ii) a Medicaid managed‑care organization contracting with the department, or (iii) another agent working on the department’s behalf per §75‑303.01, the commission must consult the Director of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care (Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care) or their designee. Existing allocation of burdens of proof for applicants and protestants and the public‑interest standard remain. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are included.
