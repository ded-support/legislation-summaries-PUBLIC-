Summary

LB474 strengthens regulatory authority for three agencies.

Department of Banking and Finance (DBF): defines “Department/Director,” expands supervision under the Money Transmitters and Nebraska Installment Loan and Sales Acts, authorizes hiring, rulemaking, NMLS participation, background checks/fingerprints, third‑party audits, multistate cooperation, cease‑and‑desist orders, examinations and charging exam/investigation costs to licensees. Establishes licensing/renewal/branch fees (including $1,500 application / $750 renewal for money transmitters and a $50,000 charter fee for digital asset depositories), required surety bonds (base $50,000; mortgage bond scaled by volume), and administrative fines (up to $5,000/violation). Collected fees/fines remit to the State Treasurer; certain fees credit the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund.

Department of Insurance (DOI): adds notice and policy provisions for life/endowment policies (15‑day pre‑lapse notice, effective Jan 1, 2026) and requires retail installment insurance premiums conform to DOI filings; Director to set (by Jan 1, 2026) a tax up to 6% on non‑Medicare HMO direct‑writing premiums, remitting receipts to the Medicaid Access and Quality Fund.

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): allows credentialing terminations without corrective plans when DHHS or another agency restricts a provider or in fraud/imminent‑harm cases; must amend the Medicaid state plan or file federal documents by Aug 1, 2025, to obtain federal matching funds for the Medicaid Access and Quality Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Banking and Finance — Expands and modernizes the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act and the Installment Loan & Sales Act. Adds detailed definitions, licensing, fingerprint/background requirements, application content and timelines, examiner and investigation powers, multistate/NMLS participation, net‑worth, surety‑bond and permissible‑investment rules, delegated‑agent reporting, enforcement (administrative fines up to $5,000 per violation/day), and director authority to accept third‑party audits and interstate exam reports. Sets specific fees (money transmitter: $1,500 application, $750 annual renewal) and surety bond/net‑worth thresholds; remits all departmental fees, charges and costs to the State Treasurer for credit to the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund; fines remitted to the State Treasurer per the Nebraska Constitution.

Department of Insurance — New requirement that policies issued/delivered on/after Jan 1, 2026 include at least 15 days’ electronic or mailed notice to owner (and assignee) prior to termination or lapse for nonpayment; assignees given same standing.

Department of Health and Human Services — No changes or appropriations identified.
