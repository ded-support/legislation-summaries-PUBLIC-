Summary

Appropriates $5,000,000 from the Securities Act Cash Fund for FY2025-26 and $5,000,000 from the same fund for FY2026-27 to the Department of Banking and Finance, Program 66, to implement provisions of Legislative Bill 468 (109th Legislature, 2025). Each fiscal-year appropriation is designated as Cash Funds for state aid and must be used only for that purpose. The bill prohibits using these appropriated funds for permanent or temporary employee salaries and per diems. Contains an emergency clause making the act effective upon enactment.
