Summary

LB602 (the "Delete Act") assigns primary administrative duties to the Nebraska Secretary of State and enforcement to the Attorney General. The Secretary of State must: maintain a public registry of data brokers (annual registration by Jan. 31; fee set by the Secretary, not to exceed $300); publish registration and deletion-mechanism information on its website; establish an accessible statewide deletion mechanism by Jan. 1, 2028 (features, security, free consumer access); select independent third-party auditors for triennial audits of data brokers and receive audit reports; adopt rules to implement the Act; and may charge data brokers an access fee limited to the Secretary's reasonable costs. The Attorney General may seek civil penalties, injunctive relief, and recover attorney fees and costs for violations. Civil penalties include $200 per day to the Secretary of State for each deletion request a data broker fails to complete; other registration-related penalties are referenced. No direct appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the Secretary of State and enforcement powers for the Attorney General. Key changes affecting agencies:

- Secretary of State must publish data-broker registration info and create an accessible deletion mechanism by Jan 1, 2028 (security, accessibility, authorized‑agent support, consumer interfaces, auditing support).

- Data brokers must annually register with the Secretary of State (by Jan 31), pay a fee up to $300, provide breach/audit/metric disclosures, and use the deletion mechanism beginning Aug 1, 2028.

- The Secretary of State may charge data brokers an access fee limited to the agency's reasonable costs, select independent auditors, adopt rules, and receive civil‑penalty payments ($200/day per uncompleted deletion request).

- The Attorney General may collect civil penalties, seek injunctive relief, and recover attorney fees.

No general appropriations or budget line‑item allocations are added; provisions authorize user fees and penalties only.
