Summary

LB74 amends section 49-1446.03 of the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to clarify and expand allowable campaign-committee expenditures. Permitted uses include continued operation of campaign offices; social events for campaign workers/constituents; public-opinion activities; repayment of pre‑election campaign loans; newsletters and communications; gifts of acknowledgment (limited to $50 per individual annually); meals, lodging, travel, and conference fees for officeholders, their staff, minor children, and immediate family when related to duties or candidacy; and child care for officeholders. It explicitly applies similar conference/travel provisions to the candidate committee for the Governor. The bill repeals the original section. No appropriations or budgetary changes are included. The bill was assigned to the Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee.
