Summary

LB312 amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act. It expands eligible student loan programs to include nurse anesthesia and dietitian nutritionist programs and adds nurse anesthetists and dietitian nutritionists to loan-repayment eligibility. It sets financial limits: student loans $30,000/year (caps: $120,000 for medical/dental/doctorate mental‑health; $30,000 for master's mental‑health/PA); medical resident incentive $40,000/year (cap $120,000); loan repayments $30,000/year (cap $90,000) for physicians/psychiatrists/dentists/psychologists and $15,000/year (cap $45,000) for PAs, NPs, nurse anesthetists, pharmacists, PTs, OTs, dietitian nutritionists, and mental‑health practitioners. Full debt repayment is authorized for qualifying psychiatrists/psychologists/mental‑health practitioners meeting five‑year practice and community‑supervision treatment criteria. The bill preserves departmental authority to set terms and requires using federal American Rescue Plan Act funds first for agreements entered by 12/31/2024 (usable through 12/31/2026) without a local-match requirement. It repeals the prior sections.
