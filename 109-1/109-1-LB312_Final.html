Summary

LB312 amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act to add nurse anesthetists and dietitian nutritionists as eligible for student loans and loan-repayment awards. It revises award limits: student loans capped at $30,000 per academic year (overall caps: $120,000 for medical/dental/doctorate-level mental health; $30,000 for master's-level mental health and physician assistant students); medical resident incentives $40,000 per residency year (cap $120,000); loan repayments set at $30,000/year (up to $90,000) for physicians, psychiatrists, dentists, and psychologists, and $15,000/year (up to $45,000) for physician assistants, nurse practitioners, nurse anesthetists, pharmacists, physical and occupational therapists, dietitian nutritionists, and mental health practitioners. The administering department retains authority over agreement terms. Agreements entered by Dec. 31, 2024 must first use federal American Rescue Plan Act funds (no local match required), with those federal funds to be spent by Dec. 31, 2026. Original sections are repealed.
