Summary

LB116 amends the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act to specify that the approving board (Governor, State Treasurer, chairs of the Investment Council and State Board of Public Accountancy, and an economics professor) "for administrative and budget purposes only" shall be considered part of the Department of Revenue. The bill does not appropriate funds to the Department of Revenue nor change departmental budget or funding authority; other fund transfers and appropriation intents (e.g., Convention Center Support Fund distributions) are specified in the bill but do not expressly allocate budget or appropriations to the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language imposes limits on state assistance: annual and aggregate amounts for eligible facility projects may not exceed the maximum aggregate appropriation approved under the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act. It clarifies eligible uses—acquisition, construction, improvement, equipping, expansion and maintenance of visitor attractions/facilities (per section 81‑3720)—and directs county governing bodies to use the fund for expanding, improving, and maintaining such facilities. There is no explicit change to the Department of Revenue’s duties, nor any direct appropriation, funding transfer, or department budget allocation described in the added text.
