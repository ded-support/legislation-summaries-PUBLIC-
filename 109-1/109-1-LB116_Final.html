Summary

LB116 designates the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act board as, "for administrative and budget purposes only," part of the Department of Revenue. The bill creates a Convention Center Support Fund: following annual certification and audit, the State Treasurer transfers certified amounts to that fund; monies may be invested and the Legislature may direct transfers to the General Fund. The Legislature intends to appropriate from the fund to approved political subdivisions up to 70% of state sales tax revenue tied to eligible facilities (including sales at facilities, primary/secondary box office admissions, associated hotels and nearby retailers), with assistance prohibited from being used as an operating subsidy. The remaining 30% is to be transferred to the Civic and Community Center Financing Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions reference or alter the Department of Revenue. The added language clarifies definitions (e.g., "applicant," "eligible facilities"), authorizes state assistance for eligible facilities under the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act, and establishes that such assistance is subject to an annual and aggregate maximum appropriation (amount not specified). It also directs county governing bodies to use designated funds to expand, improve, and maintain visitor attractions and facilities. No explicit changes to Department of Revenue authorities, budget, or specific appropriations to the Department are included.
