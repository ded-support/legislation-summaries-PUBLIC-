Summary

LB116 designates the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act board (Governor, State Treasurer, chairpersons of the Investment Council and State Board of Public Accountancy, and an appointed economics professor) as, for administrative and budget purposes only, part of the Department of Revenue. The bill does not include any explicit appropriations, budget line items, or direct funding increases or transfers to the Department of Revenue itself.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions assign duties, revenues, transfers, or budget authority to the Department of Revenue. The added language clarifies applicant/eligible‑facility definitions, references state assistance limits — "annual and an aggregate amount of not more than the maximum aggregate appropriation" approved under the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act — and authorizes county governing bodies to use designated funds to expand, improve, or maintain visitor attractions/facilities pursuant to section 81‑3720. In sum: funding caps and permitted uses are specified, but there are no direct funding, appropriation, or budget changes affecting the Department of Revenue.
