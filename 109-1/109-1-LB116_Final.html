Summary

LB116 designates the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act board as part of the Department of Revenue "for administrative and budget purposes only." The bill contains no direct appropriations, funding transfers, or budget allocations to the Department of Revenue itself; it merely places the board within the Department administratively for budgeting/administration. No other provisions explicitly reference the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the underlined additions reference or reassign responsibilities, funding, or administrative duties to the Department of Revenue. The changes instead:

- Define "applicant" as the political subdivision seeking assistance and clarify eligible project geography (contiguous or noncontiguous within the applicant’s boundaries).

- Add language establishing an annual and aggregate cap on state assistance under the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act (refers to a "maximum aggregate appropriation") but does not specify dollar amounts.

- Direct county governing bodies to use the fund to expand, improve, and maintain visitor attractions and facilities.

Net effect for Department of Revenue: none explicit — no new appropriations to DOR, no specified budgetary transfers, and no change in DOR duties described.
