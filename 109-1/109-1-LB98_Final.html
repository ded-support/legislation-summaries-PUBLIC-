Summary

LB98 amends definitions of "all-terrain vehicle" and "utility-type vehicle" in multiple statutes (sections 60-103, 60-135.01, 60-305, 60-358.01, and 60-6,355), affecting the Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act, the Motor Vehicle Registration Act, and references in the Nebraska Rules of the Road. Key changes: ATVs are defined as motorized off‑highway devices ≤50 inches wide, traveling on three or more nonhighway tires, designed for operator only or operator plus one passenger; UTVs are devices ≤74 inches wide, ≤180 inches long (including bumper), traveling on four or more nonhighway tires. Vehicles modified with after‑market equipment beyond statutory requirements are ineligible for registration under the Motor Vehicle Registration Act. Original sections are repealed and the bill takes effect immediately by emergency clause. No funding or appropriation changes.
