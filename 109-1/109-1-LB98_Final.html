Summary

LB98 amends definitions for "all-terrain vehicle" and "utility-type vehicle" in Nebraska statutory law—specifically sections 60-103, 60-135.01, 60-305, 60-358.01, and 60-6,355 (Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act, Motor Vehicle Registration Act, and Nebraska Rules of the Road). Key changes: ATVs are defined as motorized off-highway vehicles ≤50 inches wide, on three or more nonhighway tires, and designed for operator use only or for operator plus one passenger; utility-type vehicles are defined as motorized off-highway vehicles ≤74 inches wide, ≤180 inches long (including bumper), and on four or more nonhighway tires. Modified/retrofitted ATVs/UTVs with after-market equipment not required by specified statutes are ineligible for registration. Original sections are repealed and the bill takes effect immediately as an emergency measure. No government agencies or funding/appropriation changes are mentioned.
