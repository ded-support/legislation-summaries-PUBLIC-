Summary

LB118 amends the Pharmacy Practice Act (section 38-2866.01) to revise pharmacist supervision rules. A pharmacist may supervise any combination of pharmacy technicians and pharmacist interns up to a total of four people. Pharmacist interns must be supervised at all times while performing intern functions unless they are receiving experiential training directed by the accredited pharmacy program in which they are enrolled. When a pharmacist supervises four technicians/interns, at least one of those individuals must be a certified pharmacy technician as defined in section 38-2890. The bill repeals the original version of section 38-2866.01. Referred through the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
