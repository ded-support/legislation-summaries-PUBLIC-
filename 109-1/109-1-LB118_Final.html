Summary

LB118 (2025) amends the Pharmacy Practice Act (Neb. Rev. Stat. §38-2866.01). Key changes: a pharmacist may supervise up to four combined pharmacy technicians and pharmacist interns; pharmacist interns must be supervised at all times while performing intern functions except when receiving experiential training directed by their accredited pharmacy program; and when a pharmacist supervises four individuals, at least one must be a certified pharmacy technician (see §38-2890). The bill repeals the prior version of §38-2866.01. Committee: Health and Human Services. No agencies, departments, or budgetary appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary
