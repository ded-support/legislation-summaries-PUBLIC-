Summary

LB118 (Hardin, Raybould) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §38-2866.01 (Pharmacy Practice Act). It sets a pharmacist supervision limit of up to four combined pharmacy technicians and pharmacist interns; requires pharmacist interns to be supervised at all times while performing intern functions except when receiving experiential training through their accredited pharmacy program; and requires that when a pharmacist supervises four such individuals, at least one must be a certified pharmacy technician pursuant to §38-2890. The bill was considered by the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee and repeals the original §38-2866.01. No state agencies or departments are newly created or altered by name, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a staffing requirement: when a pharmacist supervises four pharmacy technicians or pharmacist interns, at least one must be a certified pharmacy technician as defined by section 38‑2890.
