Summary

LB129 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-407 to bar counties, cities, villages, and other political subdivisions from enacting or enforcing ordinances, codes, resolutions, rules, regulations, or policies that restrict or prohibit any energy source when that source is used, delivered, converted, or supplied by: natural gas utilities, natural gas transmission companies, retail marketers or dispensers of propane, or retail marketers of other energy sources to serve their authorized customers. The bill defines "energy source" in statute and exempts municipal-owned/operated natural gas utilities (ordinances governing them) and regulations of retail propane marketers/dispensers. The original section is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a broad definition of "energy source" to include aviation fuel, biofuel, compressed/liquified natural gas, diesel (including renewable diesel and biodiesel blends), electricity for EV charging, gasoline (including ethanol blends), gas distillates, hydrogen, and liquified petroleum gas. Also adds a category covering "a retail marketer of any other energy source." No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are referenced.
