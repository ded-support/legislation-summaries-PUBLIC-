Summary

LB129 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-407 to define "energy source" and prohibit counties, cities, villages, or other political subdivisions from enacting or enforcing ordinances, codes, resolutions, rules, regulations, or policies that restrict or prohibit any energy source when delivered, supplied, converted, or used by: (a) a natural gas utility; (b) a natural gas transmission company; (c) a retail marketer or dispenser of propane; or (d) a retail marketer of any other energy source. The bill exempts ordinances governing a natural gas utility owned/operated and directly controlled by a city or village and exempts regulation of retail marketers/dispensers of propane. The original §13-407 is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of “energy source,” listing fuels and power sources that include aviation fuel; biofuel; compressed or liquified natural gas; diesel (including renewable diesel and biodiesel blends); electricity for charging electric vehicles; gasoline (including ethanol blends); gas distillates; hydrogen; and liquified petroleum gas. Also expands covered parties to include “a retail marketer of any other energy source.” No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced or changed.
