Summary

LB405 amends §53-123.17 of the Nebraska Liquor Control Act (and repeals the original section), clarifying provisions for local entertainment district licenses. Key governmental actors and responsibilities:

- Local governing bodies (city or village councils): may designate or revoke entertainment districts, file designations with the Commission, regulate districts by ordinance, impose an occupation tax within their liquor license jurisdiction, and cancel licenses for cause.

- Nebraska Liquor Control Commission: receives filings, must be notified of applications, provides application copies to local clerks, and processes/decides appeals from local cancellations.

- Clerks of local governing bodies: receive the $300 additional entertainment district license fee payable by applicants.

No state appropriations or other budgetary changes are made.
