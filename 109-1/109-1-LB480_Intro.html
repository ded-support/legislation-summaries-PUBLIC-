Summary

LB480 amends the Water Recreation Enhancement Act (sections 37-1802 and 37-1803). It broadens eligible projects at Lake McConaughy, Lewis and Clark State Recreation Area, and Niobrara State Park from narrowly defined construction projects (e.g., marinas, event center and lodge) to include other identified projects that expand water access and recreational opportunities. The bill grants the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission authority to implement the Act, including entering contracts for construction, management, legal, audit and consulting services and forming public‑private partnerships. The commission must engage local stakeholders. The Legislature also encourages political subdivisions holding Federal Energy Regulatory Commission licenses to enter into land use, lease, or purchase agreements consistent with FERC rules. Commission members and employees are barred from having financial interests in related transactions. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
