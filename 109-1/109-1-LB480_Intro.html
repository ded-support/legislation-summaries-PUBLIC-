Summary

LB480 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§37-1802 and 37-1803 (Water Recreation Enhancement Act). Key agency actions and provisions:

- Grants the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission authority to administer projects at Lake McConaughy, Lewis and Clark State Recreation Area, and Niobrara State Park, broadening project scope from specific marina/event-center builds to “other projects identified” to improve water access and recreational opportunities.

- Authorizes the Commission to enter contracts for construction, management, legal, audit, and consulting services and to form public–private partnerships.

- Directs the Commission to engage local stakeholders.

- Encourages political subdivisions holding FERC licenses to contract for use, lease, or sale of adjacent lands to promote economic development.

- Prohibits financial interests by Commission members/employees in related property transactions or contracts.

No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds repeated authorization to carry out projects to increase recreational opportunities and enjoyment of Niobrara State Park, including infrastructure projects to improve general access and water access.
