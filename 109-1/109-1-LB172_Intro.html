Summary

LB172 requires the Department of Correctional Services to order mental-health evaluations for certain incarcerated persons (specified sex-offense convictions or repeat registrants) to determine whether they are dangerous sex offenders. Evaluations must be ordered at least 180 days before scheduled release, with written findings provided no later than 150 days before release, and prosecuting authorities must be notified. Persons who refuse or fail to complete sex-offender treatment offered by DCS or the Department of Health and Human Services are included, though inability to complete due to insufficient time or programming is not treated as refusal. The Department of Health and Human Services is designated recipient for sexual‑assault reports involving victims under 18; confidential victim information may be shared with DHHS for child abuse/neglect investigations, and DHHS may request the Attorney General’s Child Protection Division to investigate or prosecute if a county attorney declines. No appropriations or budgetary changes for DCS or DHHS are specified.
