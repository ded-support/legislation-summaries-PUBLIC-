Summary

LB520 amends Reissue Revised Statutes to redefine "bed and breakfast establishment," carve specific food-safety obligations into the Nebraska Pure Food Act, and add fire- and life-safety requirements to the State Fire Code. The State Fire Marshal is directed to include bed-and-breakfast provisions in the State Fire Code and to enforce them via inspections, plan review, and code compliance (including existing plan‑review fees and a $50 penalty for late submissions). The bill requires the unspecified "department" to offer a food‑handler examination and certify bed‑and‑breakfast operators. Operational requirements (smoke/heat alarms, emergency lighting, egress or sprinkler compliance, fire extinguishers, kitchen suppression for larger operations) are placed on establishments. No appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included. Original sections amended are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds extensive health and fire-safety requirements for bed and breakfast establishments. Key agency role: “the department” must certify operators (by exam), approve thawing methods, and may require demonstrated testing/maintenance procedures for battery-powered smoke alarms and emergency lighting. New mandates cover food safety (pasteurized Grade A milk only; limits on home-canned goods; temperature controls; utensil/hand hygiene; exclusion of infected food handlers), storage and sanitation, and laundry/animal separation. Fire/safety rules require smoke/heat detectors, egress standards (secondary egress or sprinkler exemption), 18-month compliance window to install secondary egress or a sprinkler system, specified fire extinguishers, and kitchen fire-suppression hoods for B&Bs serving hot meals to >10 guests. No funding or appropriation changes included.
