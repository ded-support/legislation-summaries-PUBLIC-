Summary

LB520 amends statutes governing bed and breakfast (B&B) establishments. It requires the State Fire Marshal to incorporate B&B-specific provisions into the State Fire Code, to enforce the Code via inspections, compliance orders, and plan review, and to assess a $50 penalty for plans submitted after construction in addition to the existing plan review fee (per section 81-505.01). The bill also requires the unnamed "department" referenced in the Nebraska Pure Food Act to offer a food-handler certification examination for B&B operators. New safety requirements to be enforced under the Fire Code include smoke alarms and heat detectors, emergency egress lighting, a smoking ban, primary and secondary egress or sprinklers (with an 18-month compliance window), minimum fire extinguisher ratings by floor and kitchen, and hood fire suppression for kitchens serving hot meals to more than ten guests. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds detailed food-safety and fire/safety requirements for bed-and-breakfasts and assigns limited oversight to "the department" (unspecified in the text) to: approve thawing methods and offer the food-handler certification exam operators must pass. Food rules include storage, temperature controls, use of pasteurized Grade A milk, limited home-canned goods, hygiene and exclusion of communicable-foodborne workers, utensil/sanitation rules, and required indicating thermometers. Fire/safety rules require smoke alarms, heat detectors, emergency lighting, indoor smoking prohibition, primary and secondary egress (with sprinkler-system exemptions), an 18‑month compliance period to add egress or sprinklers, specified fire-extinguisher ratings per floor/kitchen, and a fire-suppression hood for kitchens that serve hot meals and host over ten guests. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
