Summary

LB586 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32-944 to require county sheriffs and directors (or designated staff) of county/city jails and detention centers to ensure voting access for individuals unable to vote in person housed at those facilities. It requires a facility plan that: informs restricted individuals of their voting rights and registration/verification options; provides reasonable access to verify/register; designates a jail location for ballot return to be collected by two registered voters unaffiliated with the same party; and establishes a ballot-deficiency cure process. The election commissioner or county clerk must adopt procedures consistent with the Election Act and Secretary of State rules. Noncompliance by a sheriff or facility director is subject to a civil penalty assessed by the district court and payable by the county. No appropriations or funding changes are included. The bill repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties and procedures for county jails/detention centers to ensure eligible incarcerated or “restricted” individuals can verify, change, or register to vote; access voter registration services; return ballots at an on‑site location collected by two registered voters from different political parties; receive information on return methods/locations/deadlines; and have opportunities to cure ballot deficiencies. Assigns operational responsibility to county sheriffs or facility directors/designated staff and requires election commissioners or county clerks to adopt conforming procedures per the Election Act and rules promulgated by the Secretary of State. Noncompliance by a jail/facility is subject to a civil penalty assessed by the local district court and payable by the county. No new appropriations or specific budget allocations are provided.
