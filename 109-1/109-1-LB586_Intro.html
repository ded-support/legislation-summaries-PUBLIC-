Summary

LB586 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32-944 (Election Act) to require county sheriffs and directors or designated staff of city/county jails and detention centers to implement facility-specific plans ensuring voting access for individuals unable to vote in person. Plans must address training, notification of voting/registration/curing procedures, reasonable access, a designated jail location for ballot return (collected by two registered voters of different parties), and deadlines. County election commissioners or county clerks must adopt procedures consistent with the Election Act and rules of the Secretary of State. Noncompliance by a sheriff or facility director is subject to a civil penalty set by the district court and payable by the county. The bill repeals the original §32-944. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires county sheriffs (or facility directors/designated staff) to: provide jailed eligible voters access to verify/change/register to vote, supply information on ballot-return methods/locations/deadlines, provide a jail location where ballots may be returned to two registered voters of different parties, and offer a process to cure ballot deficiencies. Election commissioners or county clerks must adopt procedures consistent with the Election Act and rules promulgated by the Secretary of State. Noncompliance by a county sheriff/director/designated staff is subject to a civil penalty set by the district court where the jail is located, payable by the county. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified beyond the county-payable civil penalty.
