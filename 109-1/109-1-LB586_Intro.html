Summary

LB586 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32-944 (Election Act). It directs county election commissioners or county clerks to develop plans, consistent with Secretary of State rules, to ensure voting access for individuals housed in county jails/detention centers who cannot vote in person. Plans must address voter registration/verification access, ballot return locations within facilities (collected by two registered voters not of the same party), methods/deadlines for return, and procedures to cure ballot deficiencies. County sheriffs, facility directors, or designated staff must carry out duties under the Election Act. Noncompliance by jail officials exposes the county to a civil penalty set by the local district court. The bill repeals the prior version of §32-944. No appropriations or budget changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires county jails/detention centers to ensure restricted/incarcerated eligible voters can verify/change/register to vote, access ballot return locations inside the facility (collected by two registered voters of different parties), receive information on return methods/locations/deadlines, and have a process to cure ballot deficiencies. Assigns operational responsibility to the county sheriff or facility director/designated staff, with the election commissioner or county clerk required to adopt procedures consistent with the Election Act and Secretary of State rules. Noncompliance by a county jail/direct facility subjects the county to a civil penalty set by the local district court. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are mentioned.
