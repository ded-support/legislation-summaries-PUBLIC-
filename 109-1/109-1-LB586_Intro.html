Summary

LB586 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §32‑944 to require county election officials, in coordination with county sheriffs, jail/detention center directors or designated staff, and election commissioners or county clerks, to develop and implement a written plan ensuring incarcerated or otherwise restricted individuals housed in county jails/detention centers can verify, change, or register to vote, return ballots (including a designated on‑site collection location and use of two registered voters of different party affiliation to collect ballots), and cure ballot deficiencies. Election commissioners or county clerks must adopt procedures conforming to the Election Act and rules promulgated by the Secretary of State. Noncompliance by sheriffs or facility directors/staff subjects the county to a civil penalty set by the district court. The bill repeals the original section. No appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for county jails/detention centers to provide restricted (incarcerated) individuals with information and reasonable access to verify, change, or register to vote; a designated on-site location for ballot return; instructions on return methods, location, and deadlines; and a process to cure ballot deficiencies. Ballots at facilities must be returned for collection by two registered voters who are not affiliated with the same political party. The county sheriff or facility director/designated staff must perform duties consistent with the Election Act. The election commissioner or county clerk must adopt procedures conforming to the Election Act and Secretary of State rules. Noncompliance by a sheriff or facility director/staff subjects the county to a civil penalty, determined by the local district court. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
