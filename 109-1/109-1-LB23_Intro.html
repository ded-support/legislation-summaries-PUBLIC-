Summary

The bill directs the Department of Economic Development to establish a program to award grants to nonprofit organizations to operate bike‑sharing programs. The department must determine eligibility, require reporting to verify grant funds were used for their intended purpose, and provide a process for return of funds not used as authorized. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $250,000 from the General Fund to the Department of Economic Development to establish this program.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Economic Development to create a grant program funding nonprofit-run bike‑sharing operations. The department will determine eligible nonprofit recipients, issue grants, require recipient reporting to verify proper use, and set a process for returning unspent or misused funds. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $250,000 from the General Fund to the department to establish the program.
