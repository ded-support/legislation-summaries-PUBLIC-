Summary

The bill directs the Department of Economic Development to establish a program to award grants to nonprofit organizations to operate bike‑sharing programs. The department must determine eligibility, require reporting to verify grants were used for intended purposes, and provide a process for return of funds not used as authorized. The Legislature intends to appropriate $250,000 from the General Fund to the Department to establish the program.
