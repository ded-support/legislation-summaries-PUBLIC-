Summary

LB147 modifies Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-3306 to state that public or private entities "may" add fluoride to drinking water "in the amount and manner prescribed by the rules and regulations of the Department of Health and Human Services." The Department of Health and Human Services is thus given rulemaking authority to specify fluoride amount and application procedures. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS are included in the bill.
