Summary

The bill designates the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) as the implementing agency for the Family Home Visitation Act and requires DHHS to: fund only evidence-based home visitation programs (including workforce and certification support); adopt rules; and submit electronic reports (due Feb. 15, 2026–2028) with program, outcome, demographic, and expenditure data. DHHS must file federal state-plan/waiver amendments: to implement targeted case management for evidence-based nurse home visiting services by Oct. 1, 2025 (for Medicaid postpartum mothers and infants ≤6 months) and for medical respite facility designation; DHHS is directed to expand Medicaid coverage (breast pumps, lactation consultations, continuous glucose monitors) with specified funding intents. Funding: the Legislature intends these services to be financed from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (MMCEPF), not the General Fund; MMCEPF’s uses are expanded to include evidence-based nurse home visiting and related services, and investment earnings are credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The additions establish the Family Home Visitation Act under the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DHHS may fund only periodic, evidence‑based home visitation programs (defined by model fidelity, workforce training, certification, community links, standards, etc.) serving families with children ≤5 or pregnant persons; funds may be used for workforce development and provider certification. DHHS may adopt rules and must submit electronic reports to the Legislature (due by Feb 15, 2026, and through Feb 15, 2028) detailing program types, outcomes, populations served, expenditures, and related data. By Oct 1, 2025 DHHS must seek CMS approval for a Medicaid state plan amendment to secure federal matching funds for targeted case management providing nurse home visits to Medicaid postpartum mothers and infants ≤6 months, with legislative intent to fund these services from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (not the General Fund).
