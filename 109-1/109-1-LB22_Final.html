Summary

This bill designates the Department (Department of Health and Human Services) as responsible for the Family Home Visitation Act and directs the Department to: fund only periodic, evidence‑based home visitation programs (including workforce and certification support); adopt rules; and submit electronic reports (Feb. 15, 2026–2028) detailing program types, outcomes, expenditures, and demographics. By October 1, 2025 the Department must seek federal approval (state plan amendment/waiver) to implement targeted case management for evidence‑based nurse home visiting services for postpartum Medicaid mothers and infants ≤6 months. The Legislature expresses intent that these services (and related items including medical respite, translation services, continuous glucose monitor coverage, and lactation services) be funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund rather than the General Fund; 68‑996 is amended to expressly allow use of that fund for evidence‑based home visitation and nurse home visiting services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services must fund only evidence-based, periodic home-visitation programs (nurses, social workers, other professionals or trained supervised lay workers) that meet defined model, staffing, training, quality, and fidelity standards and achieve specified goals (maternal/child health, parenting, child development, safety, economic self-sufficiency, school readiness). DHHS may adopt rules, may use program funds for workforce development and certification, and families may decline services. Reporting to the Legislature (electronic, posted on DHHS website) is required by Feb 15, 2026 and 2028 with program, outcome, demographic, expenditure, and waitlist data (state/federal funded programs only). By Oct 1, 2025 DHHS must seek CMS approval (state-plan amendment) for Medicaid targeted case management for evidence‑based nurse home visits for postpartum mothers and infants ≤6 months; Legislature intends funding from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund, not the General Fund.
