Summary

LB22 designates the Department of Health and Human Services (department) as responsible for the new Family Home Visitation Act, requiring the department to fund only evidence-based home‑visitation programs, permit use of funds for workforce and certification, adopt rules, and submit electronic reports (by Feb 15, 2026–2028) including amounts expended. The department must submit CMS state plan amendments/waivers for: evidence‑based nurse home‑visiting targeted case management (by Oct 1, 2025) — with legislative intent that these services be funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund and not the General Fund — and other coverage changes (postpartum extension, medical respite designation, breast pumps, lactation services, translation/interpretation). The Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund is amended to explicitly allow spending for evidence‑based nurse home‑visiting services and related specified programs; specific appropriation intents include a $600,000 cap for CGM implementation and a 145% rate increase for lactation consultation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions designate the Department of Health and Human Services as responsible for the Family Home Visitation Act and require the department to fund only evidence‑based, periodic home‑visitation programs meeting specified staffing, model, quality, and outcome criteria. DHHS may adopt rules, may use program funds for workforce development and certification, and must publish electronic reports (due Feb 15, 2026, and again through Feb 15, 2028) detailing program types, outcomes, families served, expenditures, models, eligibility, and more. By Oct 1, 2025 DHHS must seek CMS approval via a state‑plan amendment to obtain federal matching funds for targeted case management delivering evidence‑based nurse home visits to postpartum mothers and Medicaid‑enrolled children up to six months. The Legislature intends funding to come from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (section 68‑996), not the General Fund. No direct new General Fund appropriation is specified.
