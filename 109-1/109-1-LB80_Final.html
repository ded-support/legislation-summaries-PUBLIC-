Summary

The bill explicitly defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and assigns DHHS specific child‑protection responsibilities: authority to conduct investigations, creation of internal Review, Evaluate, and Decide teams, and designation of non‑court‑involved matters as department‑opened cases. DHHS must provide notifications (including written notice and central‑registry placement when appropriate) after investigations, notify relevant military installations for military family cases, and negotiate memoranda of understanding with in‑state installations that operate family advocacy programs covering information‑sharing and confidentiality. The department may adopt implementing rules. An amendment to the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act likewise defines "Department" as DHHS. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the Department of Health and Human Services regarding child‑abuse/neglect reports involving military families: after contacting the family and confirming the subject is a military family member, DHHS must notify the appropriate military installation with which it has a memorandum of understanding (MOU). DHHS must negotiate MOUs with each Nebraska installation that has a Military Family Advocacy Program to set procedures for identifying military‑family subjects, information sharing, and confidentiality, and may adopt rules to implement the section. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
