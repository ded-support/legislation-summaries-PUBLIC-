Summary

The bill clarifies that "department" means the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for both the Child Protection and Family Safety Act and (by amending §42‑903) the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act. It assigns DHHS responsibility for investigations, alternative/non‑court responses, comprehensive assessments, central‑registry determinations, and internal Review, Evaluate, and Decide Teams. DHHS must provide written notice of investigation results and registry decisions to subjects, notify the Commissioner of Education when a subject is a school employee, and notify appropriate military installations when a subject is a military family member. DHHS must negotiate memoranda of understanding with Nebraska military installations that have Military Family Advocacy Programs and may adopt rules to implement these duties. The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes for DHHS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the Department of Health and Human Services regarding child-abuse reports involving military families: after contacting the family and confirming the subject is a military family member, DHHS must notify the appropriate military installation (per new section 32). DHHS must negotiate memoranda of understanding with each Nebraska installation that has a Military Family Advocacy Program to set procedures for identifying military-family subjects, sharing information, and maintaining state/federal confidentiality. DHHS is authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement these requirements. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
