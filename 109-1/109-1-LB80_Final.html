Summary

The bill designates the "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (Sec. 31; Sec. 40 amending 42‑903) and assigns DHHS explicit child‑protection duties: conduct investigations and implement traditional and alternative responses; manage non‑court‑involved cases; operate and maintain the central child‑abuse/neglect registry and provide notice to subjects regarding entry, amendment, expungement, and mandatory expungement hearings (Secs. 31, 33). DHHS must contact and notify appropriate military installations (per negotiated MOUs) when a report involves military families, may adopt rules to implement MOUs and information‑sharing/confidentiality, and must establish internal Review, Evaluate, and Decide Teams for report review (Sec. 32). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) regarding child abuse/neglect reports involving military families: after contacting the family and determining the subject is a military family member, DHHS must notify the appropriate military installation and negotiate memoranda of understanding with each Nebraska installation that has a Military Family Advocacy Program. MOUs must set procedures for identifying military-family subjects, information sharing required by the section, and confidentiality consistent with state/federal law. The department may adopt rules and regulations to implement these requirements. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
