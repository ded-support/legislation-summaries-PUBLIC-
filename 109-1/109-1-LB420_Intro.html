Summary

LB420 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84‑1501 to define composition and appointment rules for the Public Employees Retirement Board (PERB). The board will have nine appointed members plus the state investment officer as a nonvoting, ex officio member. Seven appointees must be active or retired participants from systems administered by PERB (School Employees Retirement System, Nebraska Judges Retirement System, Nebraska State Patrol Retirement System, Retirement System for Nebraska Counties, State Employees Retirement System, and a Class V school retirement system teacher). Two appointees must be non‑state employees with ≥10 years’ experience in organizational management or actuarial/benefit‑plan administration. Appointments are made by the Governor with Legislative approval; terms are staggered and vacancies filled by gubernatorial appointment. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
