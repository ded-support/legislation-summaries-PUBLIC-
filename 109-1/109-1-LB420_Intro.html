Summary

LB420 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-1501 to revise composition and appointment details for the Public Employees Retirement Board. The board will consist of nine appointed members plus the state investment officer as a nonvoting, ex officio member. Seven appointed members must be active or retired participants in systems administered by the board; two appointed members must be nonemployees with at least ten years’ experience in organizational management or actuarial/benefit-plan administration. Specified member seats represent: School Employees Retirement System (administrator and teacher), Nebraska Judges Retirement System, Nebraska State Patrol Retirement System, Retirement System for Nebraska Counties, State Employees Retirement System, and (beginning Sept. 1, 2024) a Class V school teacher seat (previously nonvoting ex officio). Governors appoint members with legislative approval; terms are staggered, vacancy and removal procedures are defined. Original section repealed. No appropriations or funding changes.
