Summary

LB420 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-1501 to revise composition, appointment, and term rules for the Public Employees Retirement Board. The board will consist of nine appointed members and the state investment officer as a nonvoting, ex officio member. Seven appointed members must be active or retired participants in systems administered by the board; two appointed members must not be state or local employees and must have at least ten years’ experience in organizational management or actuarial/employee benefit plan administration. Specified representation is required from the School Employees Retirement System (one administrator, one teacher), Nebraska Judges Retirement System, Nebraska State Patrol Retirement System, Retirement System for Nebraska Counties, State Employees Retirement System, and — beginning Sept. 1, 2024 — a teacher from a Class V School Employees Retirement Act system (previously nonvoting). Governors appoint members with legislative approval; terms are generally five years and staggered per statute. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
