Summary

LB518 amends §83‑1,100.03 to require the Department of Correctional Services, in consultation with the Board of Parole, to: adopt rules to reduce the number of inmates who serve their entire sentence and are released without supervision; ensure parolees receive at least nine months of supervision; and require that each parole-eligible inmate be assessed using a validated risk-and-needs assessment provided by the department, with assessed risk, criminal history, program completion, institutional conduct, and related factors incorporated into release decisions. The department and board must jointly submit an annual electronic report (by Feb. 1) to the Legislature, Supreme Court, and Governor detailing rates and characteristics of unsupervised releases and recommending policy/practice changes. No appropriations or funding changes are specified.
