Summary

LB518 amends section 83-1,100.03 and requires the Department of Correctional Services to consult with the Board of Parole in adopting rules to reduce inmates who serve full sentences and are released without supervision. The department must provide a validated risk-and-needs assessment used in parole decisions and help ensure parolees receive a minimum of nine months of supervision. The bill also requires the department and the board to jointly submit an annual electronic report (due February 1) to the Legislature, Supreme Court, and Governor documenting: the percentage of department inmates released without supervision; characteristics of those released (felony class, offense type, most recent risk assessment, reentry-plan status, reasons for release); and department/board recommendations to reduce such releases. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
