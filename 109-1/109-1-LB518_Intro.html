Summary

Under LB518, the Department of Correctional Services (the department) must consult with the Board of Parole to adopt rules reducing the number of inmates who serve full sentences and are released without supervision. The rules must ensure parolees receive at least nine months of supervision, prioritize meaningful transition periods, and require parole-eligible inmates be assessed using a validated risk-and-needs assessment provided by the department. Release decisions must incorporate assessed risk, criminal history, program completion, institutional conduct, and related factors. The department and the board must jointly submit an annual electronic report (due February 1) to the Legislature, Supreme Court, and Governor documenting the percentage released without supervision, characteristics of those released, and joint recommendations for policy and practice changes. No changes to funding or appropriations are included.
