Summary

LB518 amends §83‑1,100.03 to require the Department of Correctional Services (department), in consultation with the Board of Parole, to help adopt rules to reduce inmates who serve full sentences and are released without supervision. Rules must ensure parolees receive at least nine months of supervision, prioritize meaningful transition periods, and require that each parole-eligible inmate be assessed with a validated risk-and-needs assessment provided by the department; assessed risk, criminal history, program completion, institutional conduct, and other individual factors must inform release decisions. Annually by February 1, the department and board must electronically report to the Legislature, Supreme Court, and Governor on percentages and characteristics of inmates released without supervision and provide policy/practice recommendations. No appropriations or funding changes are included.
