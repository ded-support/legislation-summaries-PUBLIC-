Summary

The bill affects the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) as follows. DHHS: juveniles may be placed in DHHS temporary care and custody when secure detention is not required, and DHHS is made responsible under section 43‑290(5) to pay for a committed juvenile’s support, study, or treatment costs not covered by the juvenile’s parent. DCS: the Nebraska Crime Victim Fund, as directed by the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice, may transfer amounts to the DCS Facility Cash Fund (and the Supreme Court Automation Cash Fund) to pay initial implementation costs of Laws 2010, LB510; DCS and the Supreme Court will determine and certify amounts. After reimbursement the transfer authority terminates and remaining receipts are reallocated (75% Victim’s Compensation Fund; 25% Reentry Cash Fund). The bill includes no direct appropriations or line‑item budget allocations to either department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary focused on the agencies:

- Department of Correctional Services: The added language broadly exempts individuals described in section 1 from court- and supervision‑related fees (including probation, program participation, and other local fees). This reduces or eliminates fee collection related to supervision/programs for those individuals. No statutory appropriations or replacement funding for DOC are provided.

- Department of Health and Human Services: The bill bars assessment of health‑related costs against juveniles described in section 1 and permits courts to inquire about existing insured/uninsured coverage (and order continuation of deductible payments when coverage exists). DHHS is not assigned new funding or explicit program/appropriation changes.

- Fiscal note: counties are directed to pay certain waived fees “in the same manner as other claims,” shifting some costs to counties. No explicit state appropriations or budget allocations appear in the added text.
