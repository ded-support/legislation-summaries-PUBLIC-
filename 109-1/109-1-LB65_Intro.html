Summary

LB65 affects DHHS and the Department of Correctional Services (DCS). For DHHS, the bill retains authority to accept juveniles into temporary care/custody when secure detention is unnecessary (sec. 43‑254), requires DHHS to pay costs of a committed juvenile’s support, study, or treatment not covered by the parent (sec. 43‑290), and keeps existing procedures for remitting support payments and agency transfers to the Director of Administrative Services. Placements in state (non‑adult) institutions are charged to the committing county on a per‑diem set by the institution head. For DCS, the Nebraska Crime Victim Fund may, when available, transfer certified amounts to the DCS Facility Cash Fund (and the Supreme Court Automation Cash Fund) to pay initial implementation costs of Laws 2010, LB510; distributions revert after reimbursement (sec. 33‑157). No new appropriations or specific budget line items are established.

Summary (focus: Dept. of Correctional Services, Dept. of Health and Human Services)

- The added language exempts any "individual described in section 1 of this act" from court- and program-related fees and costs across multiple statutory provisions: probation fees, court costs, laboratory/sample collection fees, juvenile pretrial diversion and filing fees, and other local program participation fees.

- For Department of Correctional Services implications: probation-related fees and fees for participation in non‑probation programs may not be imposed on these individuals, shifting any otherwise chargeable fees to counties (fees "shall be paid by the county" in some instances). This likely reduces fee revenue and shifts costs to county budgets; no DCS appropriation is provided.

- For Department of Health and Human Services implications: courts may inquire about existing health coverage for juveniles and may order parents/guardians to continue paying deductibles if coverage exists, but the bill does not create new DHHS funding or appropriations.

- No explicit changes to state appropriations or direct budget allocations for DCS or DHHS are included.
