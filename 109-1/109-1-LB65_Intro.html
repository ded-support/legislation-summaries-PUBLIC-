Summary

LB65 amends juvenile placement and payment rules for DHHS and preserves a funding transfer mechanism for DCS. For DHHS, section 43-254 allows temporary placement or custody of juveniles with DHHS when secure detention is not required, except juveniles alleged under subdivisions (1), (2), (3)(b), or (4) of §43-247 may not be placed or supervised by DHHS. Section 43-290(5) makes DHHS responsible for costs of a juvenile’s support, study, or treatment when committed to DHHS and not paid by a parent, creating a departmental fiscal obligation but no new line-item appropriation. For DCS, §33-157 continues authority for the Commission to direct transfers from the Nebraska Crime Victim Fund to the DCS Facility Cash Fund (and Supreme Court Automation Cash Fund) to cover initial LB510 (2010) implementation costs as certified by DCS; remaining funds revert to victim compensation and reentry accounts.

Summary (impact on Department of Correctional Services and Department of Health and Human Services):

- Individuals described in section 1 are exempt from assessment of court, probation, program, local, juvenile, and related fees or costs (including program fines, participation fees, sample/drug‑test collection fees, and filing fees).

- For probation and correctional‑related fees, the bill explicitly bars requiring fees as a condition of probation and prohibits courts or government entities from charging local program fees — shifting any such charge burden in one clause to the county to pay claims in the usual manner. This affects Department of Correctional Services’ fee collection and supervision funding sources.

- For DHHS/juvenile health matters, courts may inquire about insured/uninsured coverage and may order continued payment of deductibles if coverage exists, but may not assess juvenile or guardian fees; sample collection/drug‑testing fees are also waived for covered individuals.

- No new appropriations or specific budget allocations are provided; only one provision notes county payment of waived fees.
