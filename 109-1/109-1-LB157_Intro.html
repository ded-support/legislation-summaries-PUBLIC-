Summary

- Sec. 2 defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue.

- Sec. 3(3): Beginning for taxable years on/after Jan. 1, 2027, the Department must adjust the Child Tax Credit amounts, phase‑out thresholds, and reduction increments for inflation using the same percentage used to adjust individual income tax brackets under section 77‑2715.03.

- Sec. 4: Beginning in 2027, the Department must submit an annual report to the Legislature on Child Tax Credit usage by July 15 each year.

- Sec. 5 grants the Department rulemaking authority to implement the Act.

- Sec. 7(7)(d) authorizes the Department of Revenue to recapture a $5,000 home‑purchase tax credit if the residence is sold or ceases to be the claimant's primary residence within five years.

- No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns it administration duties: beginning tax year 2027 the Department must inflation‑adjust the child tax credit amount, the income thresholds where the credit phases down, and the phase‑down increments using the same percentage applied to individual income tax brackets (section 77‑2715.03). The Department must submit an annual report on credit usage to the Legislature by July 15 each year and may adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are included.
