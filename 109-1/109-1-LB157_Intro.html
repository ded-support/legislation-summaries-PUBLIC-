Summary

- Sec. 2 defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue.

- The Department is directed to (a) make inflation adjustments beginning for taxable years on/after Jan. 1, 2027 to the Child Tax Credit amounts, thresholds, and reduction increments; (b) submit an annual report to the Legislature on Child Tax Credit usage beginning in 2027 (due July 15 each year); and (c) adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Child Tax Credit Act.

- Separately, under existing law amended here, the Department of Revenue is given authority to recapture a prior residence-purchase tax credit if statutory conditions are not met.

- No explicit appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions designate the Department of Revenue as the administering agency for a new Child Tax Credit (effective for tax years beginning or deemed to begin on/after Jan. 1, 2026). A "qualifying child" is age six or younger, claimed as a dependent, and has an SSN or ITIN. The credit is refundable, generally $1,000 per qualifying child with income-based phaseouts that vary by filing status. Beginning Jan. 1, 2027, the Department must annually index the credit amount, phaseout thresholds, and reduction increments for inflation using the same percentage as individual income tax bracket adjustments (section 77-2715.03). The Department must submit an annual usage report to the Legislature by July 15 and may adopt rules to implement the Act. No specific appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
