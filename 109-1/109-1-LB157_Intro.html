Summary

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue (Sec. 2).

- Directs the Department to annually adjust, beginning 2027, the Child Tax Credit amounts, income thresholds, and reduction increments for inflation using the same index as individual income tax brackets (Sec. 3(3)).

- Requires the Department to submit an annual report on Child Tax Credit usage to the Legislature by July 15 each year, beginning in 2027 (Sec. 4).

- Grants the Department rulemaking authority to implement the Child Tax Credit Act (Sec. 5).

- Specifies the Department of Revenue may recapture a residence-purchase tax credit if conditions are not met (Sec. 77-2715.07(7)(d) as amended).

- Adds the Child Tax Credit Act to the list of refundable credits administered under section 77-2715.07 (Sec. 77-2715.07(8)).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Revenue focus):

- Designates "Department" as the Department of Revenue and makes the Child Tax Credit Act effective for tax years beginning on or after Jan 1, 2026.

- Requires the Department to administer a refundable credit of $1,000 per qualifying child (age ≤6, claimed as dependent, SSN or ITIN). Credit phases out by filing status with specified AGI thresholds and reduction increments.

- Beginning in 2027 the Department must index the credit amount, phaseout thresholds, and reduction increments for inflation (per the same percentage used for income tax bracket adjustments).

- Beginning in 2027 the Department must submit an annual usage report to the Legislature by July 15 and may adopt rules/regulations to implement the Act.

- No explicit appropriations, funding lines, or budget allocations for the Department are included in the added text.
