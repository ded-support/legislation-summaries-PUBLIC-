Summary

LB24 amends probation fee and revocation statutes. District and county courts must assess a $30 one-time administrative enrollment fee and monthly programming fees ($25 for standard probation; $35 for intensive supervision and participants in non‑probation programs that use probation personnel under interlocal agreements funded in part by §29‑2262.07). Clerks of court remit collected fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the Probation Program Cash Fund. Courts may remove non‑probation program participants for fee default; waivers are available for persons previously found indigent or who show undue hardship. Adult probationers received under the Interstate Compact are assessed while actively supervised by Nebraska probation authorities. For felony probationers, revocation for substance‑abuse or noncriminal violations is permitted only after the probationer has served 90 days of cumulative custodial sanctions in the current term.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for courts to waive the fees referenced in §29-2262(2)(m) and (o) if the offender was previously found indigent in the case or, after a hearing, if payment would be an undue hardship. The waiver applies only while the probationer or a participant in a non-probation-based program is unable to pay the monthly probation programming fee. Also allows the probation term to be extended, at the joint request of the probationer and the prosecutor, while a motion or information to revoke probation is pending or until the statutory maximum probation period expires; the court must accept the extension if the probationer has counsel or knowingly and voluntarily requests it in open court. Agencies mentioned: courts and prosecutors. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
