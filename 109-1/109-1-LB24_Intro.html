Summary

LB24 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§29-2262.06 and 29-2267 to (1) require courts to assess a $30 one-time administrative enrollment fee and monthly probation programming fees ($25 for standard probation; $35 for intensive supervision and for participants in non‑probation programs that use probation personnel under interlocal agreements funded per §29-2262.07); (2) direct clerks of court to remit collected fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the Probation Program Cash Fund; (3) preserve waiver authority where indigence or undue hardship (including limited income, work/school status, or physical/mental disability) is shown and bar revocation for nonpayment if the probationer is financially unable and so states under oath with a court finding; and (4) revise revocation procedure: revocation requires a hearing with clear-and-convincing proof, felony revocations for substance-abuse or noncriminal violations require the probationer to have served 90 cumulative days of custodial sanctions in the current term, and courts may extend probation at joint request until resolution if counsel or a valid waiver is present.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two court-administered protections: (1) Courts must waive fees referenced in subdivisions (2)(m) and (o) of §29-2262, in whole or part, for offenders previously found indigent in that case or when payment would be an undue hardship (limited income, work/school status, or disability). The waiver applies only while the probationer/participant cannot pay the monthly probation programming fee. (2) When a motion or information to revoke probation is filed, the probation term may be extended at the joint request of the probationer and the prosecutor until final resolution or the statutory maximum. The court must accept such extensions if the probationer has counsel or affirmatively makes the request freely, knowingly, and intelligently in open court. No state agencies beyond courts/prosecutors are newly created or any funding/appropriations changed.
