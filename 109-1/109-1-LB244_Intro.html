Summary

LB244 (Sanders) amends section 49-1402 of the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act (Reissue Revised Statutes) to revise the Legislature’s findings regarding the need for increased accountability, disclosure, and prevention of conflicts of interest in state and local elections and governmental service. The bill repeals the original section and replaces the findings language to emphasize disclosure of campaign funding, prevention of corruption and appearance of corruption, and guidance for public officials and employees. No state agencies or departments are newly created or altered, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
