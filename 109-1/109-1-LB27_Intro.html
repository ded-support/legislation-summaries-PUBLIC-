Summary

This bill amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act and related statutes. It creates the Nebraska Rural Health Advisory Commission and expands the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund’s permissible uses to include loan repayments under the Act. The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) will execute and pay loan-repayment agreements (exempt from the State Procurement Act) and must use only specifically appropriated monies for dentist-specific agreements. General loan-repayment agreements require equal state and local funding and DHHS makes payments directly to recipients. New dentist-specific agreements (for dentists with <5 years’ practice and no prior program assistance) require five years’ practice in Nebraska, specified Medicaid patient thresholds, payments up to $60,000/year for up to five years, and full repayment with 8% simple interest on breach. The Legislature intends a $1.5 million appropriation from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to DHHS for FY2025‑26. The state treasurer and state investment officer administer the fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a DHHS-administered loan repayment program for dentists who treat Medicaid patients. Eligible dentists must have practiced <5 years and not previously received Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act aid. Agreements (exempt from the State Procurement Act) require five years of in-state practice and a commission‑specified minimum share/number of Medicaid patients. DHHS pays recipients directly for qualified educational debt—up to $60,000 per year for up to five years (maximum $300,000). Recipients must document use of funds. Breach requires repayment to the state of 100% of funds plus 8% simple annual interest; obligations cancel on death or total permanent disability. DHHS may only use specifically appropriated funds; Legislature intends to appropriate $1,500,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for FY2025‑26 for this purpose.
