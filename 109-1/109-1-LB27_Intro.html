Summary

This bill amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act and the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund. It (1) explicitly allows the Excess Profit Fund to be used for loan repayments under the Rural Health Act and confirms fund investment by the state investment officer with investment earnings credited to the General Fund beginning October 1, 2024; (2) restates the Act’s purposes, including creating the Nebraska Rural Health Advisory Commission; (3) creates a new dentist loan-repayment pathway administered by the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for early-career dentists who commit to five years’ practice in Nebraska with specified Medicaid patient thresholds, authorizing DHHS payments up to $60,000/year (maximum $300,000) for up to five years; (4) requires repayments under that dentist option to be made only from specifically appropriated funds and expresses legislative intent to appropriate $1.5 million from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to DHHS for FY2025–26.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a loan repayment program administered by the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), exempting agreements from the State Procurement Act, to recruit dentists who treat Medicaid patients. Eligible dentists must have <5 years' practice and no prior Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act assistance. Agreements (in lieu of section 71-5668 contracts) require five years' Nebraska practice and a commission‑determined Medicaid patient threshold. DHHS may pay qualified educational debt up to $60,000/year per recipient (up to $300,000 total); recipients must verify funds were used for debt. Breach requires full repayment with 8% simple interest; obligations cancel on death or total permanent disability. DHHS may only use specifically appropriated funds; Legislature intends to appropriate $1.5 million from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for FY2025‑26.
