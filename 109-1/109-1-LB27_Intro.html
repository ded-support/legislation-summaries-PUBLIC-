Summary

Bill amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act and the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund statutes. It explicitly allows the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to be used for Rural Health Act loan repayments and directs investment earnings (beginning Oct. 1, 2024) to the General Fund. It restates purposes (including creation of the Nebraska Rural Health Advisory Commission) and adds a dentist-specific loan-repayment option administered by the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Eligible dentists (less than five years in practice, no prior program aid) may contract directly with DHHS for up to $60,000 per year for five years (maximum $300,000 total), must meet Medicaid patient-share requirements, and face full repayment with 8% interest on breach. Agreements are exempt from the State Procurement Act. The Legislature intends to appropriate $1,500,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to DHHS for FY2025‑26 for these repayments.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a DHHS‑administered loan repayment program for dentists who treat Medicaid patients, added to the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act. DHHS (the "department") may enter agreements exempt from the State Procurement Act; a commission will set required Medicaid patient thresholds and verify use of payments. Eligible dentists must have <5 years' practice and no prior program assistance, commit to five years' practice in Nebraska, and receive up to $60,000/year (maximum $300,000 per recipient) toward qualified educational debt. Breach triggers full repayment plus 8% simple interest; obligations cancel on death or total permanent disability. DHHS may only pay from specifically appropriated funds. The Legislature intends a $1.5 million appropriation from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to DHHS for FY2025‑26 for these repayments.
