Summary

LB236 amends §13-910 of the Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act to permit tort claims for child abuse or sexual assault of a child when the harm is a proximate result of a political subdivision's or its employee's failure to (a) control a person in its charge or (b) protect a person in its care, custody, or control. The change preserves existing immunities otherwise listed in §13-910. The bill references related statutes including the Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act and State Boat Act (affecting DMV-related title issuance), the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act (employee-claims exclusion), and the Healthy Pregnancies for Incarcerated Women Act (as an exception). No agencies are newly created or renamed, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds liability where child abuse or sexual assault is a proximate result of a political subdivision—or its employee—failing to exercise reasonable care to (i) control a person it has taken charge of, or (ii) protect someone in its care, custody, or control from harm by a non‑employee actor. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
