Summary

LB236 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-910 (Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act) to permit tort claims previously barred by immunity when the harm from child abuse or sexual assault of a child is a proximate result of a political subdivision or its employee failing to exercise reasonable care to (a) control a person in its charge or (b) protect a person in its care, custody, or control. The bill preserves existing enumerated immunity exceptions (e.g., discretionary acts, inspections, recreational-activity claims) and retains prior Motor Vehicle Certificate of Title Act and State Boat Act references (including electronic dealer services under section 60-1507). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds civil liability where child abuse or sexual assault results proximately from a political subdivision—or its employee—failing to exercise reasonable care to (i) control a person it has taken charge of, or (ii) protect someone in its care, custody, or control from harm by a non‑employee actor. The provision targets political subdivisions (e.g., counties, municipalities) and their employees. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
