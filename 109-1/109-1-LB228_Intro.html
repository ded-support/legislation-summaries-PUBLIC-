Summary

LB228 amends definitions used for tax-incentive performance audits (section 50-1209). It designates the Department of Labor to: determine "distressed area" pursuant to the Nebraska Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act; receive employer reporting of employment used to define "full‑time worker" and "high‑quality job" (two consecutive quarterly wage reports); publish the Nebraska average weekly wage by October 1 annually; and assign NAICS code designations used to define "high‑tech firm" and "renewable energy firm." These Department of Labor data and determinations feed the office's five‑year audit schedule and evaluation criteria. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are included.
