Summary

LB228 amends tax-incentive audit definitions to reference the Department of Labor as the official source for several data points: (1) "Distressed area" is determined by the Department of Labor under the Nebraska Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act; (2) "Full‑time worker" and "high‑quality job" require employment to be reported to the Department of Labor on two consecutive quarterly wage reports; (3) "Nebraska average weekly wage" is the Department of Labor's annual report (by Oct. 1); and (4) NAICS code designations for "high‑tech firm" and "renewable energy firm" are those assigned by the Department of Labor. No changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement to include planning documents and, if applicable, any correspondence between the office and an audited agency (e.g., Department of Labor) as part of the referenced records/submissions. No language in the added text changes funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.
