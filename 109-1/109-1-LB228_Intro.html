Summary

LB228 amends the Legislative Performance Audit Act definitions used for tax-incentive performance audits to reference the Department of Labor. The bill specifies: (1) “distressed area” is determined by the Department of Labor under the Nebraska Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act; (2) “full‑time worker” and “high‑quality job” require reporting to the Department of Labor on two consecutive quarterly wage reports; (3) “high‑tech firm” and “renewable energy firm” are defined by NAICS codes as assigned by the Department of Labor; and (4) the Nebraska average weekly wage must be the most recent figure reported by the Department of Labor by October 1 annually. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are included.
