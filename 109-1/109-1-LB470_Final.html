Summary

LB470 permits attorneys to use unsworn declarations, signed and dated under penalty of perjury, in lieu of sworn declarations, verifications, certificates, statements, oaths, or affidavits when an attorney's signature is required. Exceptions: it does not apply to depositions, oaths of office, or oaths required to be taken before a specified official other than a notary public. The bill prescribes two model declaration statements (for execution inside or outside Nebraska) and references Nebraska law for out‑of‑state executions. No state agencies or departments are created or modified, and the bill contains no funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that where an attorney's sworn declaration, verification, certificate, statement, oath, or affidavit is required because the attorney represents a party, an unsworn, written declaration signed by the attorney “under penalty of perjury” may be used with the same force and effect (excluding depositions, oaths of office, or oaths required before a specified official other than a notary public). Two prescribed forms are provided: one for executions inside Nebraska and one for executions outside Nebraska (the latter references “under the laws of the State of Nebraska”). No state agencies are newly created or referenced beyond the State of Nebraska and notary public; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
