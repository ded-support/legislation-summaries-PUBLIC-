Summary

LB470 allows an attorney’s unsworn written declaration, signed and dated under penalty of perjury, to substitute for any sworn declaration, verification, certificate, statement, oath, or affidavit when the attorney’s signature is required—except for depositions, oaths of office, or oaths required to be taken before a specified official other than a notary public. It prescribes two declaration forms: one for execution outside Nebraska (states “under penalty of perjury under the laws of the State of Nebraska”) and one for execution within Nebraska. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Permits attorneys representing parties in actions to substitute an unsworn declaration, verification, certificate, or statement signed under penalty of perjury for a sworn declaration, affidavit, oath, or similar written attestation (excluding depositions, oaths of office, or oaths required before officials other than a notary). Provides two specimen statements: one for execution outside Nebraska referencing Nebraska law, and one for execution within Nebraska. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
