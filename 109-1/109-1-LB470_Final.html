Summary

LB470 authorizes attorneys to substitute unsworn declarations "under penalty of perjury" for sworn declarations, verifications, certificates, statements, or affidavits required by law in cases where the attorney's signature is required—except for depositions, oaths of office, or oaths required to be taken before a specified official other than a notary public. The bill prescribes the exact declaration language for executions inside and outside Nebraska. No specific state agencies or departments are named; the text references the laws of the State of Nebraska and notary publics. The bill does not include any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that whenever law requires an attorney’s sworn declaration, verification, certificate, statement, oath, or affidavit (other than for depositions, oaths of office, or oaths before officials other than a notary), an attorney may instead submit an unsworn written declaration signed and dated under penalty of perjury. Provides two model forms: one for execution outside Nebraska referencing Nebraska laws, and one for execution within Nebraska. No state agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
