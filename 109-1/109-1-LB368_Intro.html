Summary

LB368 creates the Nebraska Youth in Care Bill of Rights and assigns specific duties to the state child welfare agency (referred to in the bill as "the department"), guardians ad litem, caseworkers, and the courts. Key agency-related provisions:

- The department must facilitate sibling joint placement/visitation (per §43-1311.02), reunification and family visitation as required by state/federal law, provide transition plans and post-adoption information (per §43-1311.03), maintain confidentiality consistent with law, and ensure access to vital documents.

- Caseworkers must be trained on the rights, deliver paper notices to youth aged 14+, discuss rights on defined timelines (within 72 hours, within two weeks, then quarterly), and explain grievance procedures.

- Guardians ad litem must discuss rights within two weeks of appointment and every six months, and raise suspected infringements to the court.

- The department must acknowledge grievances within three business days and provide appeal information under the Administrative Procedure Act.

- The Nebraska Strengthening Families Act Committee (§43-4716) will monitor implementation.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds detailed child-welfare duties and oversight. Key government actors named or implicated:

- "The department" (child welfare agency) must facilitate sibling placements/visitation, reunification, family visitation, confidentiality, access to vital records, transition plans for youth (including parenting youth), and explain denials; must acknowledge foster‑care grievances within three business days and provide grievance/Administrative Procedure Act review information.

- Caseworkers must be trained on and provide paper notices of youth rights, discuss them on set timelines, and obtain acknowledgments.

- Guardians ad litem (appointed under §43‑272.01) must discuss rights soon after appointment and semiannually and raise alleged infringements to the court.

- Courts and the Nebraska Strengthening Families Act Committee (§43‑4716) will monitor implementation.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
