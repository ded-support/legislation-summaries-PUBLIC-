Summary

LB114 amends multiple Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) provisions for driver records and creates a fund for a new operator’s license system. Key DMV provisions:

- DMV may furnish certified driving-record abstracts for $3 each. Revenue from subsections (1)–(5) is remitted to the State Treasurer and credited 41 2/3% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund, 25% to the General Fund, and 33 1/3% to the Records Management Cash Fund.

- Monitoring-service searches are charged $0.30 each; $0.24 per search is credited to the DMV Cash Fund and $0.06 as provided in subsection (2).

- Driver-header extracts are $30 per 1,000 records, credited to the DMV Cash Fund. For certain record requests $2 per individual record and $22 per 1,000 (for requests over 2,000) are credited to the License Plate Cash Fund.

- Creates the Operator’s License Services System Replacement and Maintenance Fund (funded by amounts credited under §60-483) to build/maintain a new operator’s license services system; implementation by July 1, 2032. Investment earnings in the fund are credited to the General Fund beginning October 1, 2024. Act operative July 1, 2025; emergency clause included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds required remittance from each DMV fee: $0.24 of every fee must be sent to the State Treasurer and credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund, and $0.06 must be credited as specified in subsection (2). Exceptions for records fees: $2.00 per individual record and $22.00 per 1,000 records (for requests over 2,000 records) are to be credited to the License Plate Cash Fund.
