Summary

The bill amends multiple provisions governing the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV). It specifies DMV authority to provide driver records and certified abstracts, establishes fees (e.g., $3 per abstract; $0.30 per monitoring search; $30 per 1,000 header records), and directs remittance of revenues to the State Treasurer: 41 2/3% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund, 25% to the General Fund, and 33 1/3% to the Records Management Cash Fund. Monitoring-search fees allocate $0.24 to the DMV Cash Fund and $0.06 as in subsection (2). The bill credits $2 per individual record (and $22 per 1,000 for large requests) to the License Plate Cash Fund. It creates the Operator's License Services System Replacement and Maintenance Fund, funded by amounts credited under section 60-483, for building and maintaining a new operator's license system; investment earnings are credited to the General Fund. The act is operative July 1, 2025, and takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The amendment directs that for each fee the Department of Motor Vehicles collects, $0.24 must be remitted to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and $0.06 remitted as specified in subsection (2). It further specifies that fees for record requests—$2.00 per individual record and $22.00 per 1,000 records for requests exceeding 2,000—are to be credited to the License Plate Cash Fund. No new appropriations or total dollar amounts beyond these per‑fee allocations are provided.
