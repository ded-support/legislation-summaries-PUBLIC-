Summary

LB554 creates the Nebraska Health Professions Commission and explicitly names the chief medical officer of the Department of Health and Human Services (or designee) as a commission member. The bill otherwise assigns review, reporting, and rulemaking roles to the commission, board, and a "director," but does not explicitly tie additional duties or authority to the Department of Health and Human Services beyond that membership. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS or for the commission are included in the provided text.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Health Professions Commission to review credentialing and scope‑of‑practice applications and conduct directed reviews and public factfinding. The commission must collect and analyze workforce, public‑health, quality, access, and affordability data; hold public hearings; evaluate proposals under statutory criteria; forward annual recommendations to the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee by October 1; and file reports with the board, the DHHS director, and the committee chair. The chief medical officer of the Department of Health and Human Services (or designee) is a statutory member. The commission may monitor impacts of enacted changes and initiate reviews. No changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.
