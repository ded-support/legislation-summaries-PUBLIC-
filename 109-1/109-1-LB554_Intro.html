Summary

LB554 creates the Nebraska Health Professions Commission and explicitly designates the chief medical officer of the Department of Health and Human Services (or the chief medical officer’s designee) as a commission member. The bill does not include any explicit changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Health Professions Commission to review credentialing and scope-of-practice proposals, hold public factfinding hearings, collect and analyze workforce, public health, quality, access, and affordability data, and issue annual recommendations to the Legislature. For the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): the commission must include the DHHS chief medical officer or a designee; it must file its report with the board, the DHHS director, and the Health and Human Services Committee chair; and it may monitor/evaluate public health and health‑care quality impacts of enacted changes. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
