Summary

LB255 expands uses of the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund and directs related state entities. Key provisions:

- Creates/affirms the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund (funded by transfers from the Nebraska Opioid Recovery Trust Fund).

- Authorizes appropriations from that fund to the Nebraska Supreme Court for problem‑solving courts to order medication‑assisted treatment (FDA‑approved medications plus counseling/behavioral therapies).

- States legislative intent to appropriate $1,000,000 in FY2025‑26 and $1,000,000 in FY2026‑27 from the fund to the Supreme Court for that purpose.

- Requires the State Court Administrator to ensure each judicial district has at least one problem‑solving court and to submit annual outcome reports to the Legislature.

- Permits “the division” to fund public‑private opioid prevention/treatment infrastructure projects (including capital/renovation); limits administrative distribution costs to 5%.

- Authorizes investment of fund balances by the State Investment Officer. Act takes effect immediately (emergency).
