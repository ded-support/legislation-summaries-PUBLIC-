Summary

LB255 expands uses of the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund and assigns implementation responsibilities to state court entities. Key points for agencies and funding:

- Supreme Court: May receive appropriations from the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund to support problem solving courts (drug, veterans, mental health, DUI, reentry, young adult, etc.) to order FDA‑approved medication‑assisted treatment (MAT) combined with counseling and behavioral therapies.

- State Court Administrator: Must track/evaluate problem solving court outcomes and submit annual reports to the Legislature; Supreme Court to operate/establish courts under its rules.

- The fund: Created from transfers from the Nebraska Opioid Recovery Trust Fund; may be used by "the division" for public‑private opioid prevention/treatment infrastructure projects (including capital construction/renovation). Administrative distribution costs capped at 5%.

- State investment officer: Invests any idle fund balances per applicable state investment acts.

- Appropriations intent: $1,000,000 for FY2025‑26 and $1,000,000 for FY2026‑27 to the Supreme Court for MAT.

- Emergency clause: effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authorization and legislative intent for the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund to provide appropriations to the Colorado Supreme Court to support problem‑solving courts in ordering medication‑assisted treatment (FDA‑approved medications plus counseling and behavioral therapies) per section 24‑1302. Specifically, it states money from the fund may be appropriated to the Supreme Court and expresses the Legislature’s intent to appropriate $1,000,000 for FY 2025‑26 and $1,000,000 for FY 2026‑27 to the Supreme Court for this purpose.
