Summary

LB255 amends statutes to allow and prioritize use of the newly created Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund (funded by transfers from the Nebraska Opioid Recovery Trust Fund) for Supreme Court problem-solving courts to order medication-assisted treatment (MAT). The State Court Administrator must track and report outcomes and ensure each judicial district has designated problem-solving courts; the Supreme Court promulgates related rules. The bill authorizes the fund, as appropriated, for the division to support local and state public–private partnerships for nonprofit and for‑profit opioid prevention and treatment infrastructure projects (including capital construction/renovation), with administrative distribution costs capped at 5%. The state investment officer may invest fund balances under applicable investment acts. Intent to appropriate $1,000,000 in FY2025‑26 and $1,000,000 in FY2026‑27 to the Supreme Court is stated. The act takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds legislative intent that money from the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund be appropriated to the Colorado Supreme Court to support problem‑solving courts in ordering medication‑assisted treatment (FDA‑approved medications plus counseling and behavioral health therapies) for substance use disorders (per §24‑1302). Specifies intent to appropriate $1,000,000 in FY 2025‑26 and $1,000,000 in FY 2026‑27 from that cash fund to the Supreme Court for this medication‑assisted treatment.
