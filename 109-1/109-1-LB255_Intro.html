Summary

LB255 directs use of the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund (funded by transfers from the Nebraska Opioid Recovery Trust Fund) to support problem solving courts and opioid treatment infrastructure. Key agencies/departments and actions:

- Supreme Court: eligible to receive appropriations from the fund to enable problem solving courts to order FDA‑approved medication‑assisted treatment (MAT); Legislature intends separate appropriations so each judicial district can operate at least one specified problem solving court.

- State Court Administrator: required to track/evaluate outcomes and submit annual reports to the Legislature by June 1.

- "The division": authorized to distribute fund moneys for local/state public‑private partnerships (including capital construction/renovation); administrative costs capped at 5% of distributions.

- State Investment Officer: to invest any fund balances per state investment law.

Fiscal intent: $1,000,000 appropriated to the Supreme Court for FY2025‑26 and $1,000,000 for FY2026‑27. Original sections 24‑1302 and 71‑2492 are repealed; act takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authorization and legislative intent for the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund to provide appropriations to the Colorado Supreme Court to support problem‑solving courts in ordering FDA‑approved medication‑assisted treatment (combined with counseling and behavioral health therapies) under section 24‑1302. Specifies intent to appropriate $1,000,000 for FY 2025‑26 and $1,000,000 for FY 2026‑27 from that fund to the Supreme Court for this purpose.
