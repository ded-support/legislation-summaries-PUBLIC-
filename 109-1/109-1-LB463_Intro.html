Summary

LB463 assigns new responsibilities and grant authority primarily to the State Department of Education and the state school security director. Key changes:

- Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund: adds eligible use for grants to cover costs associated with school cardiac emergency response plans; fund investments are managed by the state investment officer and investment earnings are credited to the General Fund (beginning Oct 1, 2024).

- State Department of Education / state school security director: must collect school safety plans, establish a cardiac emergency response plan template (with specified elements), provide training for cardiac emergency response teams and threat assessment teams, and administer the School Safety and Security Reporting System.

- Grants: the Department administers grants (educational service units may apply/contract for nonpublic schools), must report annually to the Legislature, and the Legislature intends to appropriate $10 million from the School Safety and Security Fund for the grant program. The School Safety and Security Fund is administered by the Department and invested by the state investment officer.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a required cardiac emergency response plan template for schools (per section 6) incorporating American Heart Association or similar core elements and specifying: creation of a school cardiac emergency response team; AED placement and routine maintenance on school grounds; required training for designated personnel (e.g., coaches, school nurses, athletic trainers) and team members in CPR/AED use; annual drills; coordination with local emergency medical services; and annual school board review.
