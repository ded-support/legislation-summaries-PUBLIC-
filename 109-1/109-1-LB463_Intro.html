Summary

LB463 assigns the State Department of Education and the state school security director responsibility for a statewide cardiac emergency response program: creating a model plan template, providing training for cardiac emergency response teams, and requiring schools to develop response plans. The department may award grants (to school districts and educational service units, including for approved nonpublic schools) to fund qualifying projects and costs associated with cardiac emergency response plans and AED placement/maintenance. The Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund is expanded to permit grants for cardiac emergency response plan costs. The department must report annually to the Clerk of the Legislature on grant awards. The School Safety and Security Fund remains administered by the State Department of Education for security infrastructure grants, and the bill states legislative intent to appropriate $10 million from that fund to the department for the grant program. Investments are managed by the state investment officer; certain investment earnings are credited to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statewide school cardiac emergency response framework: a template (aligned with American Heart Association or similar standards) requiring AED placement and maintenance, designated school cardiac emergency response teams, training (first aid/CPR/AED) for specified staff, annual drills, coordination with local emergency medical services, and annual school board review. Defines "cardiac emergency response plan" and "cardiac emergency response team." It mandates that "the department" provide training for teams serving public and nonpublic schools (training to be reasonable in length). The bill also authorizes grants for costs of implementing plans (sec. 7); no specific appropriations or dollar amounts are listed.
