Summary

LB189 amends section 71-7611 to condition a transfer of up to $15,000,000 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Board of Regents (University of Nebraska Medical Center) for pancreatic cancer research on receipt of matching private or other funds. Receipt of matching funds must be certified by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services; upon such certification the State Treasurer is directed to transfer an equal amount. The authorized transfer window is on or after July 1, 2022, but before June 30, 2023.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text reference the Department of Administrative Services or assign duties, funding, or rulemaking to that agency.

Funding/appropriations changes included:

- Creation of the Paid Family and Medical Leave Insurance Fund, funded by private donations, legislative transfers, covered‑individual contributions, and employer donations. The commissioner administers the fund; the State Treasurer credits contributions and transfers.

- One-time transfer: $5,558,000 moved from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the new fund on October 1, 2026 to cover upfront administrative costs; it must be repaid by the commissioner no later than October 1, 2030 when the fund is solvent.

- Contributions by covered individuals begin on an implementation date on or before January 1, 2027. The state investment officer may invest fund balances.
