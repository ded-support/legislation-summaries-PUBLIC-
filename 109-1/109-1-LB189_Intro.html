Summary

LB189 amends the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund statute (71-7611) to retain a provision authorizing a transfer of $15 million from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the University of Nebraska Medical Center for pancreatic cancer research (to occur on or after July 1, 2022, but before June 30, 2023). That transfer is expressly made contingent upon receipt of matching private or other funds "certified by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services." Upon such certification, the State Treasurer is directed to transfer an equal amount from the Health Care Cash Fund to the Board of Regents.
