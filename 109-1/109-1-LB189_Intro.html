Summary

LB189 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-7611 to condition transfers from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund for pancreatic cancer research. The State Treasurer is directed to transfer up to $15,000,000 to the Board of Regents/UNMC between July 1, 2022 and June 30, 2023, contingent on receipt of matching private or other funds certified by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services. Upon such certification, the State Treasurer must transfer an equal amount (up to the $15 million cap). The Department of Administrative Services’ role is limited to certifying the matching funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions explicitly amend duties, appropriations, or funding for the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). The added text creates a Paid Family and Medical Leave Insurance Fund (administered by the Commissioner of Labor), funds it with contributor payments, private donations, and a one‑time $5,558,000 transfer from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund on Oct. 1, 2026 (repayable by Oct. 1, 2030). Contributions begin on an implementation date on or before Jan. 1, 2027; the commissioner sets contribution rates and may adjust them. Investment authority is assigned to the state investment officer under existing investment acts, and the State Treasurer handles transfers. The bill encourages using existing state data/technology (e.g., unemployment systems) to implement the program but does not assign DAS specific responsibilities or provide DAS appropriations.
