Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to establish and maintain the medical‑cannabis patient/registered‑caregiver registry, issue/renew confidential registry cards, process applications, and promulgate rules. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must collaborate with DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Department of Revenue on implementation and oversight. It creates a Medical Cannabis Control Fund (fees, gifts, grants; investable and transferable to the General Fund) and splits license/application fees 50% to that Fund and 50% to local treasurers for school funds; bond/insurance premiums for commission staff are payable from the State General Fund. The Department of Economic Development will administer a Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund (accepting appropriations), provide technical assistance/mentorship and a grant program for social‑equity/disproportionately impacted applicants. Department of Correctional Services employees are ineligible for licenses; DCS must provide drug‑treatment to sentenced persons and receives vehicle‑lettering exemptions (shared with DHHS, Agriculture, and the Military Department). Courts must notify the Department of Motor Vehicles of certain clean‑slate orders/juvenile abstracts. Effective July 1, 2023, aircraft sale/lease sales/use tax proceeds are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Agriculture: Added duty to collaborate with the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission, Dept. of Revenue, and DHHS on regulation and oversight; no direct funding changes.

- Department of Economic Development: Assigned administration of the new Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund (may hold gifts, grants, legislative appropriations) and to run technical assistance, mentorship, and a grant program for social equity applicants and disproportionately impacted areas. Funding: eligible to receive appropriations and a share of cannabis tax proceeds (see DOR).

- Department of Correctional Services: Employees/jail staff prohibited from holding cannabis licenses; Cannabis Community Reinvestment Fund may support re-entry programs for formerly incarcerated persons; no explicit DOC appropriation.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Designated as the agency running the patient/caregiver registry, issuing registry cards, rulemaking for caregivers, and collaborating with the commission; no explicit new funding.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Added notice/recording role in clean-slate/expungement process and a carve-out that clean-slate orders do not affect commercial CDL eligibility; no funding change.

- Department of Revenue: Responsible for crediting sales/use tax proceeds from dispensary sales: 50% to Medical Cannabis Control Fund, 25% to Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund, 25% to Cannabis Community Reinvestment Fund; continues tax/fee collection duties.

- Department of Transportation: No new agency-specific duties or appropriations; transport standards/insurance for cannabis movers are set by the commission.

- Military Department: Not referenced; no changes.
