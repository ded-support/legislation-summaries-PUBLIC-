Summary

The bill assigns DHHS to operate the qualified patient/registered caregiver registry and enrollments, and creates a Medical Cannabis Control Fund (fees, gifts, grants) to finance commission administration (including salaries/benefits) and allow legislative transfers to the General Fund. Application fees are split 50/50 between that Fund and local treasurers (credited to local school funds); bonding premiums for commissioners and staff are paid from the General Fund. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must coordinate with DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Department of Revenue. Department of Economic Development (DED) will administer a new Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund (gifts, contributions, legislative appropriations), provide technical assistance, grants, mentorships, and invest available funds per state investment law. Department of Correctional Services employees are barred from licenses; sentenced persons must attend drug treatment. Courts must notify the DMV of certain clean‑slate orders and juvenile drug penalties trigger abstracts to the DMV. State vehicle‑lettering exemptions are expanded for Correctional Services, DHHS, Agriculture, and the Military Department. Sales/use tax from aircraft transactions is credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions affecting agencies and funding:

- Department of Health and Human Services: defined as “Department”; must run the patient/caregiver registry, issue registry cards, adopt caregiver/health‑facility rules, maintain confidentiality, and collaborate with the Medical Cannabis Commission.

- Department of Agriculture: required to coordinate with the Commission on regulation, cultivation, processing, testing, transport, and sale.

- Department of Revenue: included as a collaborator; sales/use tax on dispensary sales is allocated: 50% to the Medical Cannabis Control Fund, 25% to the Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund, and 25% to the Cannabis Community Reinvestment Fund.

- Department of Economic Development: will administer the Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund (gifts/legislative appropriations allowed), provide technical assistance/mentorship, and run grant programs for social equity/disproportionately impacted areas.

- Department of Correctional Services: employees barred from holding licenses; bill funds re‑entry programs via the Reinvestment Fund and creates resentencing/clean‑slate processes affecting incarcerated persons.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: to receive notice of clean‑slate orders; clean‑slate does not affect commercial driver’s license obligations.

- Department of Transportation and Military Department: no direct budget or program changes specified.
