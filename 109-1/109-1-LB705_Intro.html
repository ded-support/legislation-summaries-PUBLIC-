Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to operate the medical cannabis registry. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must collaborate with DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Department of Revenue on cultivation, processing, distribution, transportation, testing, and sale regulation. The Department of Economic Development (DED) will administer the Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund, provide technical assistance/mentorship and mentorship pairings, and operate a business development grant program for social equity/disproportionately impacted applicants; the Fund may receive gifts, bequests, appropriations, and be invested per law. Employees of the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) are ineligible for medical cannabis licenses; DCS must provide treatment/counseling for sentenced persons. Financial changes: creation of a Medical Cannabis Control Fund; initial license fees split 50/50 between that Fund and local treasurers (school funds); permissive transfers to the General Fund; bond/insurance premiums paid from the General Fund. DOT: aircraft sales/use tax proceeds go to the Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund; related highway tax distributions retained. DMV must receive court notices for clean‑slate and impoundment/license actions. Vehicles of DHHS, DCS, Department of Agriculture, and the Military Department are exempted from standard state lettering.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions by agency (concise):

- Department of Health and Human Services: Defined as the “Department” for the patient program; establishes/maintains the patient/caregiver registry, issues registry cards, sets confidentiality, surrender/destruction rules, and may adopt rules for registered-caregiver entities.

- Department of Economic Development: Administers the Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund (gifts/Legislative appropriations permitted), provides technical assistance, grants, mentorship, and a business development grant program for social equity/disproportionately impacted areas.

- Department of Revenue: Allocates sales/use tax revenue from medical cannabis sales: 50% to the Medical Cannabis Control Fund, 25% to the Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund, 25% to the Cannabis Community Reinvestment Fund. Also collaborates with the commission.

- Department of Agriculture: Required to collaborate with the commission on regulation of cultivation, processing, testing, etc.

- Department of Correctional Services: Employees of jails or the Department are disqualified from holding licenses; cannabis prohibited in correctional facilities.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Must be notified of certain sealed adjudications; clean-slate orders do not alter commercial CDL obligations.

- Department of Transportation and Military Department: No substantive mentions or funding changes in the added text.
