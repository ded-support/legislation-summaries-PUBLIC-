Summary

The bill assigns agency roles in the medical cannabis system and other programs. DHHS will establish and maintain the patient/caregiver registry, issue registry cards, adopt related regulations, and administer enrollment and appeals; DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Department of Revenue must collaborate with the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission on regulation/oversight. The Department of Economic Development will define “disproportionately impacted areas,” administer a new Medical Cannabis Business Equity Fund (including gifts/legislative appropriations, investable by the state investment officer), and provide technical assistance, mentorship, and grantmaking for social equity applicants. Jail and Department of Correctional Services employees are barred from cannabis licenses; persons sentenced to DCS must attend drug‑abuse treatment. Courts must notify the Department of Motor Vehicles about certain clean‑slate and license‑impound orders. Vehicle‑lettering exemptions are extended to DCS, DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Military Department. Fiscal: a Medical Cannabis Control Fund is created (fees/gifts/grants; 50% of license fees to the fund, 50% to local school funds), bond premiums paid from the General Fund, transfers to the General Fund allowed, and aircraft sales/use tax proceeds are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.
