Summary

LB2 outright repeals terminated statutory provisions establishing and governing: the Children and Juveniles Data Feasibility Study Advisory Group; the Crimes Against Children Fund; the Industrial Recovery Fund; the Medical Cannabidiol Pilot Study; the Municipal Natural Gas System Emergency Assistance Act; and the Nebraska Economic Development Task Force. It specifically repeals sections 19-5601 through 19-5608, 28-463 through 28-469, 50-435, 81-1213, 81-1429.01 (Reissue Revised Statutes of Nebraska), and section 43-1306 (R.R.S. Cumulative Supp., 2024). The bill does not name state departments or agencies for reassignment nor include any new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
