Summary

- Department of Correctional Services: Section 11 directs the department (with the Division of Parole Supervision and the Board of Parole) to establish a "second chance relief" program to identify eligible committed offenders, regularly provide lists of eligible or soon-to-be-eligible offenders to the Board of Parole, and assist offenders in applying for commutations. The department's role is incorporated into the Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act by amending section 83-1,135 to include section 11.

- Department of Health and Human Services: The Clean Slate provisions exclude offenses involving assault on a Department of Health and Human Services employee and require that, when an order to seal an adjudication is entered, notice be sent to DHHS if the person was a ward of the state at the time or if DHHS was a party.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Courts must notify the DMV when a sealed adjudication included impoundment or prohibition to obtain a license or permit under section 43-287.

No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Correctional Services: Establishes a "second chance relief" program with the Division of Parole Supervision and Board of Parole. The department must regularly identify committed offenders who are or will be eligible (within five years) and provide lists to the Board of Parole. The Board of Parole conducts risk assessments, involves community stakeholders, recommends commutations to the Board of Pardons, and, if parole is granted, develops reentry plans, mentorship, restorative-justice options, and monitoring.

Department of Health and Human Services: Will receive notice to seal records when an adjudication involved a ward of the state or when DHHS was a party. Assaults on DHHS employees are excluded from clean-slate eligibility.

Department of Motor Vehicles: Will receive notice to seal records when an adjudication included impoundment or prohibitions on licensing under section 43-287.

No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are added.
