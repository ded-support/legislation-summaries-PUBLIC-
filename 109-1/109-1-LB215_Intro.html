Summary

LB215 assigns specific duties to the Department of Correctional Services (department): it must identify committed offenders eligible (or nearing eligibility) for the new “second chance relief” commutation program, provide regular lists to the Board of Parole, and collaborate with the Division of Parole Supervision and Board of Parole to implement the program. The Board of Parole will assess risk, involve community stakeholders, recommend commutations to the Board of Pardons, and—if parole is granted—develop reentry plans, mentorship, restorative-justice options, and parole monitoring (including regular parole officer check‑ins). Under the Clean Slate provisions, courts must notify the Department of Motor Vehicles when an adjudication included license impoundment/prohibition and must notify the Department of Health and Human Services if the person was a state ward or DHHS was a party; those agencies must seal records as ordered. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Correctional Services (the "department"): required to create a "second chance relief" program with the Division of Parole Supervision and Board of Parole; regularly identify committed offenders who are or will become eligible, provide lists to the Board of Parole, and support applicants. If parole/commutation is granted, the Board of Parole (working with DOC/Division) must produce individualized reentry plans (job placement, housing, education, mental health), mentorship, restorative‑justice options, supervision/monitoring, and ongoing program evaluation.

- Department of Health and Human Services: courts must notify DHHS to seal records when the sealed adjudication involved a person who was a state ward or DHHS was a party.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: courts must notify DMV to seal records when the sealed adjudication included impoundment or a prohibition on obtaining a license/permit under §43‑287.

No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
