Summary

LB295 touches the Department of Labor in three limited ways. It adopts the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Price Index (CPI‑U) as the index for annual cost‑of‑living adjustments and directs use of a substitute DOL index if CPI is discontinued; it also cites the DOL’s Medical Price Index to justify a separate medical cost‑of‑living supplemental annuity. The bill clarifies retirement coverage for Department of Labor employees employed before July 1, 1984 and paid from Title III or other federal funds: such employees remain excluded from the State Employees Retirement Act unless their contributory retirement plan/contract is terminated, in which case they become members effective the first day of the first pay period after termination and receive vesting credit for prior participation. No Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions specifically reference or alter duties, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor. The added text amends Public Employees Retirement Board rules: it recognizes DHS/USCIS extensions of ID validity, creates a hardship exception to repayment of inadvertent benefit overpayments (including gross distributions), adjusts the method for calculating a 20‑hour‑per‑week threshold, clarifies allowable remote retirement‑planning access, and restricts use of state retirement system names/logos in financial solicitations. No Department of Labor budget or funding changes are included.
