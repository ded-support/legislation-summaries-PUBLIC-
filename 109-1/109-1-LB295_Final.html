Summary

LB295 references the U.S. Department of Labor for cost‑of‑living adjustments, specifying the CPI‑U (Bureau of Labor Statistics, U.S. DOL) as the index for annual COLAs and directing that, if discontinued, a substitute index published by the U.S. DOL be selected (selection by the board until Sept. 1, 2024; thereafter by the retirement board). The bill also cites the U.S. DOL’s Medical Price Index to support an annual medical COLA for eligible annuitants. It amends the State Employees Retirement Act as applied to Nebraska Department of Labor staff by excluding employees employed before July 1, 1984 and paid from Title III or other federal funds from the Act’s “employee” definition, but provides that if the prior contributory retirement plan/contract under section 48‑609 is terminated those employees become eligible under the Act (with vesting credit) effective the first day of the first pay period after termination. No Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
