Summary

LB295 references the Department of Labor in two ways. First, it uses U.S. Department of Labor/Bureau of Labor Statistics indices — the CPI‑U for annual cost‑of‑living adjustments and the Medical Price Index for supplemental medical COLAs — and directs selection of a substitute U.S. Department of Labor index if the CPI‑U is discontinued (selection by the board before Sept. 1, 2024, or by the retirement board on/after that date). Second, it modifies State Employees Retirement Act treatment of Department of Labor employees: those employed before July 1, 1984 and paid from Title III or other federal sources remain excluded from “employee” status unless a pre‑Jan. 1, 2018 contributory plan/contract is terminated; upon termination they become members of the State system effective the first pay period and receive vesting credit for prior participation. No Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text affect the Department of Labor, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes for that agency.

Substantive additions address public retirement administration: accept DHS/USCIS‑extended identity documents (and state learner’s permits) as valid; authorize the Public Employees Retirement Board to waive repayment of inadvertent benefit overpayments when repayment would cause significant hardship; allow independent lump‑sum payments; revise how the 20‑hours/week threshold is calculated; clarify meeting/travel reimbursement and electronic access rules for retirement‑planning materials (time limits and access window); and restrict use of retirement system names/logos in financial solicitations, requiring prominent disclaimers to avoid misleading affiliation or endorsement.
