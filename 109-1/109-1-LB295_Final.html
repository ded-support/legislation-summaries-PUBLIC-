Summary

The bill references the U.S. Department of Labor in two ways. First, it ties retirement cost‑of‑living adjustments to federal inflation measures published by the Department of Labor: annual COLAs use the CPI‑U (and, beginning Sept. 1, 2024, the board may select any substitute index also published by the Department of Labor) and it cites the Department of Labor’s Medical Price Index for an annual supplemental medical COLA. Second, it addresses Department of Labor employees covered by a contributory retirement plan: it retains an exclusion for employees hired before July 1, 1984 who were paid from Title III or other federal funds, but provides that if the contributory plan/contract is terminated those employees become State Employees Retirement Act members effective the next pay period and active participants on termination receive vesting credit for prior service. No appropriations, funding reallocations, or budget changes to the Department of Labor are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text reference the Department of Labor or change its responsibilities. The amendments: accept DHS/USCIS‑extended identity documents and state learner’s permits; authorize hardship exceptions to repay inadvertent benefit overpayments; revise hours‑threshold calculation language; limit electronic retirement‑planning access; permit lump‑sum payments and certain board travel reimbursements; and restrict use of public‑retirement system names in private solicitations with required disclaimers. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are included.
