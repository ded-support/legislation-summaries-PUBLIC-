Summary

LB229 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-604 (Employment Security Law) to exclude "service by a marketplace network contractor for a marketplace network platform" from the definition of employment, provided four conditions and definitions for "marketplace network contractor" and "marketplace network platform" are met. Agencies or officials referenced: the commissioner (who may approve employer elections under §48-604(3)(a)), the Secretary of Labor (federal certification under section 3304), the Public Service Commission and its Division of Motor Carrier Services (referenced in §48-604(6)(q) regarding motor carrier rules), and federal agencies/Congress (in various federal-law cross-references). The bill contains no provisions changing funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new carve‑out defining and treating "marketplace network contractors" as independent contractors when (1) there is a written independent‑contractor agreement with the marketplace network platform, and the platform does not set mandatory hours, bar outside work, or terminate for refusing a specific request; (2) defines "marketplace network contractor" and "marketplace network platform" by written agreement, digital‑only request intake, and absence of a physical platform business location; and (3) excludes services for employers listed elsewhere in the statute (subdivisions (4)(a), (4)(b), or §48‑603.01) and services for which federal taxes must be paid that are creditable against the Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA) or required for full FUTA credit under the state Employment Security Law. Mentions FUTA and the state unemployment compensation fund/Employment Security Law; no new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
