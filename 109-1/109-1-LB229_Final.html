Summary

LB229 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-604 (Employment Security Law) to exclude “marketplace network contractor” services for a “marketplace network platform” from the definition of employment when specified conditions are met (written independent‑contractor agreement; platform does not set mandatory availability hours, bar outside work or use of other platforms, or terminate for declining a specific request). It defines “marketplace network contractor” and “marketplace network platform” and preserves exceptions where services are for employers described in §48-604(4)(a)/(4)(b) or where federal tax law requires coverage. The statute references the commissioner (administrating unemployment law), the U.S. Secretary of Labor, the Federal Unemployment Tax Act, the Public Service Commission, and the Division of Motor Carrier Services. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new exception treating “marketplace network contractors” as independent contractors if a written agreement states that status and the platform does not set mandatory hours, bar outside work (except while actively providing services), or punish refusal of individual requests. Defines “marketplace network contractor” and “marketplace network platform” (digital‑only request channels). Excludes this exception for employers covered by specified statutory subsections (48‑603.01 and subdivisions 4(a)/(b)) and where federal tax rules (including the Federal Unemployment Tax Act) require coverage creditable against the state unemployment compensation fund or otherwise mandate coverage under the state Employment Security Law. No specific government agencies, appropriations, or funding changes are named.
