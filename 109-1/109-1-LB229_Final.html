Summary

LB229 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-604 (Employment Security Law) to specify that services by a "marketplace network contractor" for a "marketplace network platform" are not employment—subject to written contractor designation and operational criteria—and defines those terms. Relevant government entities referenced: the statute’s "commissioner" (administrative authority under the Employment Security Law), the U.S. Secretary of Labor and the Federal Unemployment Tax Act (federal tax coverage/credit considerations), Congress, federal agencies (for work-training/program exceptions), the Public Service Commission and the Division of Motor Carrier Services (motor carrier/lessor rules), and references to the Internal Revenue Code. The bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new carve-out and definitions for "marketplace network contractor" and "marketplace network platform." A contractor qualifies if a written agreement labels them an independent contractor, the platform cannot set mandatory hours, cannot bar outside work except while on-platform, and cannot fire the contractor for declining a specific request. Defines contractors (use platform, paid via digital network, no on-site platform location in-state) and platforms (digital/app-only intake, not phone/fax/in-person). Exempts services for certain employers (see subdivisions cited) and any services subject to federal tax rules that permit credit against state unemployment contributions or require coverage under the Employment Security Law (references FUTA and state unemployment compensation). No specific agencies or departments are named and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
