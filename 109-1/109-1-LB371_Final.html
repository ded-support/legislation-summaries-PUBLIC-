Summary

LB371 (2025) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§25-3502 and 25-3503 (Uniform Civil Remedies for Unauthorized Disclosure of Intimate Images Act). Key changes: expands “depicted individual” and “intimate image” definitions to cover images created by computer generation or digital manipulation; treats such nonconsensual computer-generated/digitally manipulated images as potentially “private”; clarifies consent, identifiability, and harm standards; and preserves causes of action against persons who intentionally or recklessly disclose private intimate images. The bill repeals the prior versions of those sections. No state agencies, departments, appropriations, or budgetary changes are referenced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language expanding covered material to explicitly include content produced "by means of computer generation or digital manipulation" and clarifies clause (C) to cover images or content "created by means of computer generation or digital manipulation without the consent of the depicted individual" (and an alternate phrasing concerning information displayed in connection with an intimate image). No government agencies, departments, appropriations, or funding changes are mentioned.
