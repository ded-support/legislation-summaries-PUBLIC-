Summary

LB371 amends the Uniform Civil Remedies for Unauthorized Disclosure of Intimate Images Act to expressly cover images created by computer generation or digital manipulation. Key changes:

- Expands definitions so a "depicted individual" and "private" intimate images include computer-generated or digitally manipulated images made without consent.

- Creates a civil cause of action for intentional or threatened disclosure of private intimate images when the defendant knew or recklessly disregarded lack of consent, the image’s privacy, and identifiability.

- Clarifies that consent to creation or prior consensual sharing does not by itself permit further disclosure; nonconsensual sexual conduct or uncovering preserves a reasonable expectation of privacy even if created in public.

No state agencies or departments are mentioned. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that covered material includes images created or altered "by means of computer generation or digital manipulation," including those "created ... without the consent of the depicted individual," and material "from information displayed in connection with an intimate image."
