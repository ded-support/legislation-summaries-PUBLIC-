Summary

LB371 amends sections 25-3502 and 25-3503, Revised Statutes Cumulative Supplement, 2024, updating the Uniform Civil Remedies for Unauthorized Disclosure of Intimate Images Act. Key changes: (1) expands "depicted individual" to include images produced by computer generation or digital manipulation; (2) defines such computer-generated or digitally manipulated images as potentially "private" when created without consent; (3) clarifies terms (consent, disclosure, identifiable, intimate image, sexual conduct, harm) and confirms a cause of action where an identifiable depicted individual suffers harm from intentional or threatened disclosure of a private intimate image without consent, where the defendant knew or recklessly disregarded nonconsent, privacy, and identifiability; (4) preserves that prior consent to creation or prior consensual disclosure alone does not establish consent to subsequent disclosure. The bill repeals the original sections. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations are referenced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that covered material includes images (A) created by computer generation or digital manipulation without the depicted person’s consent and (B) images produced from information displayed in connection with an intimate image.
