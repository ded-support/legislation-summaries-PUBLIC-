Summary

The bill assigns specific duties to the Department of Labor: (1) to work with the human trafficking task force to develop or select multilingual informational posters (including a toll‑free hotline) for placement statewide (sec. 81‑1430(3)(a)); (2) to work with the task force and lodging businesses to approve and determine delivery of optional human‑trafficking training for hotel and similar public‑lodging employees (sec. 81‑1431(2)); and (3) to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the hotel/employee training and prevention provisions (sec. 3(4)). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that the task force may collaborate with the Department of Labor and hotels to approve and determine delivery of employee human‑trafficking training. The Department of Labor is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the section. The provision grants hotels discretion to provide approved training, adopt reporting procedures, and implement prevention policies, and provides limited liability protection for compliant operators. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are included.
