Summary

LB320 assigns the Department of Labor three explicit roles related to human‑trafficking prevention: (1) collaborate with the human trafficking task force to develop or select multilingual informational posters (including the National Human Trafficking Resource Center number) for placement statewide; (2) work with the task force and hotels/similar lodging establishments to approve and determine delivery of optional employee training on human trafficking; and (3) adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the bill’s hotel/training provisions. The bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Labor focus):

The added language authorizes the Department of Labor to work with the existing task force to approve and determine delivery of human‑trafficking training for hotel and similar public lodging employees. It also expressly grants the Department authority to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the section. The provision lists required training topics and allows hotels to implement training, reporting procedures, and prevention policies (with limited liability protection), but contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
