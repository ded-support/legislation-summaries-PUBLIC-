Summary

This bill amends duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (department) regarding Medicaid home-and community-based waivers. Key department-specific provisions:

- Department shall administer the waivers; use an objective assessment (published on the department website) subject to advisory board/stakeholder approval. Assessment may not reduce an individual's budget to force removal from a setting; redetermination requests completed within 30 days; an independent mediated review administered outside the department.

- Department to provide service coordination directly or by contract; individuals/legal representatives choose the provider.

- Department must maximize federal funding and require eligible residents to apply for/accept Medicaid and other applicable benefits.

- Residential settings added as options for additional funding priority groups; the department must create an expedited form/process for priority one and respond within five calendar days.

- If funds are insufficient, the department shall provide comparable day services to specified transitioning individuals and, by Sept. 15 annually, notify the Legislature’s Health and Human Services and Appropriations Committees of estimated need and net additional resources required. Federal CMS approval is required for the military-dependent priority.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- Must publish the objective assessment formula on its website and obtain approval from the Governor’s developmental disability advisory board or the required stakeholder group.

- The assessment cannot lower an individual’s budget so they are removed from their setting.

- Residential settings must be offered as an option for funding priorities 1, 2, 3, and 5, and may be offered for priorities 4 and 6 through the assessment process.

- Redetermination requests from individuals/families/guardians must be completed within 30 days; DHHS must create an expedited form/process for consideration of funding priority 1 and respond within five calendar days of submission.

- Assessment must be person‑centered and reflect the individual support plan.

- An independent mediated review must be administered outside DHHS; the individual or legal representative selects the review entity.

No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
