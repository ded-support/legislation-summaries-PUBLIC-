Summary

LB562 amends Game and Parks statutes administered by the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission and touches the Department of Motor Vehicles (Director of Motor Vehicles) and county treasurers. Key agency provisions and fiscal changes:

- Commission authority: establishes/resets permit types and caps fees (resident annual motor-vehicle permit ≤ $50; nonresident = 2× resident; temporary resident permit ≤ $10; nonresident = 2× resident). Active-duty military permit set at $5. Commission to set exact fees by rule; may offer temporary reduced rates.

- Permit issuance: permits may be sold via commission offices, vending, authorized vendors, county offices, and permit agents; permit agents may collect and retain an issuing fee per section 37-406.

- Mountain sheep permits: resident application fee ≤ $34; auctions may authorize up to two additional permits; auction proceeds restricted to perpetuation/management of mountain sheep, elk, and deer.

- Motorboat registrations/stamps: three‑year registration fees specified by class; up to $10 of each registration may be used for the Aquatic Invasive Species (AIS) Program. Non‑Nebraska boats must buy an AIS stamp ≤ $30 plus issuance fee (section 37-406); stamp proceeds remitted to the State Game Fund.

- DMV role: implementation date and enhanced owner identification/data requirements for boat registration.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that an issuing fee will be charged pursuant to section 37-406 and that the number of permits issued shall not exceed the number awarded under subsection (2). Clarifies that a partner, officer, shareholder, or beneficiary may be designated as the qualifying landowner by a partnership, corporation, or trust under rules in subdivision (2)(a). No specific government agencies or departments are named, and no changes to appropriations, budget allocations, or new funding are included beyond the cross-reference to the existing fee statute.
