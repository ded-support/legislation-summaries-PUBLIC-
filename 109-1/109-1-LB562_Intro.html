Summary

LB562 amends Game and Parks and motorboat statutes. Key agency actions and fiscal changes:

- Nebraska Game and Parks Commission: may set annual motor-vehicle park permit fees (resident ≤ $50; nonresident = 2×), temporary permits (resident ≤ $10; nonresident = 2×), active-duty military permit $5, and collect an issuing fee per §37‑406. Permits may be sold via commission offices, county offices, designated private agents, vending, and online.

- Mountain sheep permits: resident application fee ≤ $34; auctions limited to two additional permits; auction proceeds restricted to mountain sheep, elk, and deer perpetuation/management.

- Limited landowner permits: acreage and eligibility clarified (e.g., 80‑acre resident threshold; 320‑acre nonresident), fee caps (resident elk limited permit ≤ ½ regular; nonresident elk limited permit ≤ 3× resident).

- Motorboat registration (three‑year): Class 1 $28; Class 2 $51; Class 3 $72.50; Class 4 $120. Up to $10 of each registration may fund the Aquatic Invasive Species Program. AIS one‑year stamp ≤ $30 plus issuance fee; stamp proceeds to the State Game Fund. Director of Motor Vehicles to coordinate implementation and expanded owner ID requirements.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds three provisions: (1) references an issuing fee under section 37‑406; (2) clarifies that the number of permits cannot exceed those awarded under subsection (2); and (3) allows a partner, officer, shareholder, or beneficiary to be designated as the “qualifying landowner” for a partnership, corporation, or trust per the rules in subdivision (2)(a). No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
