Summary

LB562 amends statutes administered primarily by the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission and touches the Director of Motor Vehicles and county treasurers. Key agency provisions and fiscal changes:

- Game and Parks Commission: sets maximum fees for vehicle area permits (resident annual ≤ $50; nonresident = 2× resident; resident temporary ≤ $10; active-duty permit $5; disabled‑veteran permits free and perpetual). Commission may offer reduced-rate combinations and adopt rules.

- Permit distribution/agents: permits may be sold via commission offices, county offices, vendors and designated private agents, who may collect and retain an issuing fee pursuant to §37‑406.

- Mountain sheep permits: resident application fee ≤ $34; no more than two additional auction permits; auction revenues restricted to mountain sheep/elk/deer management.

- Limited landowner permits: deer/antelope limited permits = ½ regular fee (resident and nonresident structures); limited elk resident ≤ ½ regular, nonresident ≤ 3× resident; pre-season landowner permits (up to eight) cost $5 each.

- Motorboats/Director of Motor Vehicles: three‑year boat registration fees set by class (Class 1 $28; Class 2 $51; Class 3 $72.50; Class 4 $120). Up to $10 of each registration may fund the Aquatic Invasive Species Program; AIS stamps ≤ $30 plus issuance fee; stamp proceeds remit to the State Game Fund. Registration applications processed by county treasurers; Motor Vehicles Director to set implementation date and require expanded owner identification.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a cross-reference to an issuing fee under section 37-406, limits the number of permits to the number awarded under subsection (2), and explicitly allows a partner, officer, shareholder, or beneficiary to be designated as the "qualifying landowner" for a partnership, corporation, or trust per the rules under subdivision (2)(a). No specific government agencies or departments are named and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
