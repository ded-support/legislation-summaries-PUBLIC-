Summary

LB562 amends Game and Parks statutes, primarily affecting the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission (the commission), county treasurers, and the Director of Motor Vehicles. Key agency-related changes and fiscal provisions:

- Commission may set permit fees by rule. Annual motor‑vehicle park permit capped at $50 for residents; nonresident = 2× resident. Temporary permit capped at $10 for residents; nonresident = 2×. Active‑duty military permit $5. Commission may offer reduced‑rate permits for events.

- Commission and designated county offices or private permit agents may collect and retain an issuing fee (per §37‑406).

- Mountain sheep resident application fee capped at $34 (nonrefundable); up to two additional permits may be auctioned; auction proceeds restricted to mountain sheep, elk, and deer perpetuation/management.

- Limited‑permit fees: deer/antelope limited permits = 1/2 regular fee (resident and nonresident rules); limited elk permit fees: resident ≤1/2 regular, nonresident ≤3× resident.

- Motorboat registration fees set by class; up to $10 of each registration may fund the Aquatic Invasive Species Program. Aquatic invasive species stamp fee capped at $30 plus issuing fee; stamp proceeds remitted to the State Game Fund.

- Director of Motor Vehicles to set implementation date and registration identity requirements; county treasurers handle boat registration filings.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that permits (and an issuing fee referenced to section 37-406) shall not exceed the number awarded under subsection (2). Clarifies that a partner, officer, shareholder, or beneficiary may be designated as the qualifying landowner for a partnership, corporation, or trust per rules and regulations under subdivision (2)(a). No specific government agencies or departments are named and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
