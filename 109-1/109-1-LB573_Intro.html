Summary

LB573 (Meat and Poultry Workers and Contractors Protection Act) assigns enforcement and oversight duties primarily to "the commissioner" (statutorily charged to):

- Receive, investigate, resolve complaints and assess civil penalties up to $15,000 for violations; develop a compliance‑monitoring process within 90 days; and bring civil actions on behalf of aggrieved workers or contract growers.

- Require covered industrial operators to submit disaster mitigation plans, provide workers' compensation coverage under the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act for illnesses/injuries during disasters, offer specified severance and lost‑revenue payments after events, and prohibit retaliatory discrimination.

- Require employers (25+ employees) to provide employees information on federal collective‑bargaining and FLSA rights and authorize penalties up to $15,000 for misinformation, intimidation, or wrongful terminations.

The commissioner must appoint a Workers and Contractors Task Force (specified membership, includes two nonvoting legislative Agriculture Committee members), convene regular meetings, and submit an annual report to the commissioner and Legislature. No appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds enforcement, reporting, and penalty duties primarily to "the commissioner" (who must receive and investigate complaints, develop a compliance-monitoring process within 90 days, require covered industrial operators to provide employee information on collective-bargaining rights, and may bring court actions). The federal Occupational Safety and Health Administration and the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act are referenced. Covered industrial operators must: submit disaster mitigation plans; provide workers workers'‑comp coverage for injuries during disaster events; pay 12 weeks' severance for terminations within 60 days after an event; and compensate contract growers for 180 days' lost revenue. The commissioner may assess civil penalties up to $15,000 for violations. The commissioner must appoint a Workers and Contractors Task Force (specified membership, meeting requirements, regional stakeholder meetings) to report annually to the commissioner and the Legislature. No appropriations or new funding allocations are included.
