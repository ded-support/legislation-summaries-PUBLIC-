Summary

LB573 creates the "Meat and Poultry Workers and Contractors Protection Act" and assigns primary enforcement duties to the commissioner (who must receive disaster mitigation plans, monitor compliance, investigate complaints, develop a compliance process within 90 days, assess civil penalties up to $15,000, and may sue or bring actions on behalf of aggrieved workers/contract growers). During a declared "disaster mitigation event" covered industrial operators must offer Nebraska Workers' Compensation coverage for related injury/illness for the event and two years after, provide 12 weeks' severance for workers terminated within 60 days, and pay affected contract growers lost revenue equal to the prior 180 days. Operators with 25+ employees must notify workers of collective-bargaining/FLSA rights; the commissioner may penalize misinformation, intimidation, or wrongful termination. The bill creates a commissioner-appointed Workers and Contractors Task Force (includes legislative nonvoting members from the Legislature's Agriculture Committee) that must meet regularly and submit annual reports to the commissioner and Legislature. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates duties for "the commissioner" (who will receive, investigate, and enforce complaints) to: require covered industrial operators to file disaster mitigation plans; develop a compliance‑monitoring process within 90 days; require employers (25+ employees) to provide workers information on collective‑bargaining/NLRB and FLSA rights; and assess civil penalties up to $15,000 for violations or anti‑union misconduct. References federal OSHA and the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act for workplace safety/compensation standards and permits commissioner civil suits on behalf of workers. Establishes a Workers and Contractors Task Force (appointed by the commissioner, with two nonvoting legislative Agriculture Committee members) to meet regularly and report annually to the commissioner and Legislature. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
