Summary

LB274 amends the Child Care Licensing Act's licensing, inspection, and background-check processes. Key agency actions and changes:

- Department (state licensing agency) must require applicants to show liability insurance of at least $200,000 per occurrence before issuing a license and verify ongoing coverage at each unannounced inspection. If proof is not provided within three business days after notice, the department will suspend the license until verification.

- Department may investigate licensees/applicants, delegate sanitation inspections to local environmental health personnel, and request fire-safety inspections from the State Fire Marshal.

- Nebraska State Patrol will handle fingerprint transmission to the FBI for national criminal history checks; the department may seek federal funds to help cover fingerprinting costs and may adopt implementing rules with the State Patrol.

- Background checks remain portable; no direct appropriations or new budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that licensees must maintain required liability insurance while children are in their care. Department inspectors must request and verify current proof of insurance at every unannounced inspection and investigation. If a licensee cannot produce proof or coverage has lapsed, the department will notify the licensee and require proof within three business days; failure to do so results in license suspension. Licenses are reinstated when the department receives and verifies required proof per section 71-1911.03. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
