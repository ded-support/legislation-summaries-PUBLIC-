Summary

LB274 amends the Child Care Licensing Act’s oversight and screening procedures. The department must obtain written proof of liability insurance of at least $200,000 per occurrence before issuing a license and verify current coverage at unannounced inspections; failure to produce proof within three business days results in license suspension until coverage is verified. The department may investigate applicants and inspect facilities, may delegate environmental health inspections to local personnel, and may request State Fire Marshal fire-safety inspections (with notification when authority is delegated). Nebraska State Patrol will collect fingerprints and transmit them to the FBI for national criminal history checks; the department will provide eligibility documentation and will seek federal funds to offset fingerprinting costs, though no new state appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that inspectors must request and verify current proof of required liability insurance at each unannounced inspection and each investigation. If a licensee cannot produce proof or lets coverage lapse, "the department" must notify the licensee and allow three business days to provide proof; failure to do so results in license suspension. Licenses are reinstated when the department receives and verifies current insurance per section 71-1911.03. The provision references section 71-1911.03; no specific agency beyond "the department" and inspectors is named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
