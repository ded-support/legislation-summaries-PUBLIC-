Summary

LB274 amends the Child Care Licensing Act to tighten insurance verification, inspection, and background-check procedures. The Department (agency administering licensing) must obtain written proof of liability insurance of at least $200,000 per occurrence before issuing a license and verify current coverage at each unannounced inspection; failure to provide proof within three business days triggers license suspension until coverage is verified. The Department may request State Fire Marshal inspections and may delegate sanitation and facility investigations to local environmental health personnel or city/village/county authorities. The Nebraska State Patrol will process fingerprints and transmit them to the FBI; the Department will provide criminal-history documentation, require periodic background checks, and seek federal funds to offset fingerprinting costs. If federal funds are insufficient, applicants/staff or licensees bear the cost; the Department may cover costs if funding becomes available. No direct appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirement that licensees must continuously maintain required liability insurance and that inspectors must request and verify current proof at each unannounced inspection and each investigation. If proof is missing or coverage lapsed, the department will demand proof within three business days and will suspend the license if proof is not provided; licensure is restored when the department verifies the insurance per section 71‑1911.03. No agencies beyond "the department"/inspectors are newly created or referenced, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
