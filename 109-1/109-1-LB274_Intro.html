Summary

LB274 amends the Child Care Licensing Act to (1) require applicants to provide written proof of liability insurance of at least $200,000 per occurrence before licensure and obligates licensees to maintain that coverage; inspectors must request and verify current proof at each unannounced inspection and the department will suspend a license if proof is not provided within three business days. (2) Clarify inspection and investigation authority: the department (licensing agency) may investigate, inspect, and delegate sanitation inspections to qualified local environmental health personnel; inspections may be conducted by city, village, or county per statute. (3) Allow the department to request fire‑safety inspections from the State Fire Marshal. (4) Require fingerprint‑based national criminal history checks processed by the Nebraska State Patrol and FBI; the department will seek federal funds to offset costs. No new appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that licensees maintain required liability insurance while caring for children. At each unannounced inspection inspectors must request and verify current proof of insurance; if proof is missing or coverage lapsed the department must notify the licensee to provide proof within three business days and will suspend the license if not received. Licensure is reinstated when the department receives and verifies required proof (per section 71-1911.03). Also requires inspectors to request and verify proof of liability insurance at each investigation. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
