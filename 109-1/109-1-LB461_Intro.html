Summary

LB461 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §16-1027 governing municipal firefighter retirement benefits. The bill clarifies optional benefit forms established by the retirement committee and provided through the retirement system (including annuity contracts the system may purchase and distribute). For firefighters with retirement dates on or after Jan. 1, 1997, optional payment frequencies are specified as annual, quarterly, monthly, or a single lump-sum; lump sums remain available for earlier retirees only if the city adopted that option. Retirees may defer first payment to the first day of any specified month prior to age 70. The city council retains authority to set lump-sum amounts after committee review. If a minimum pension cannot be provided from a firefighter’s retirement value, the city must use funds from the retirement system’s unallocated employer account to provide the required benefit. The bill was referred to the Nebraska Retirement Systems committee.
