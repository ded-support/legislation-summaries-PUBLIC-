Summary

LB206 amends the Nebraska Criminal Code to impose enhanced penalties for specified offenses committed in a "disaster area" during an "emergency period." "Disaster area" is defined to include areas subject to: a Governor emergency proclamation (see §81-829.40), a local emergency declaration (§81-829.50), a Presidential major disaster or emergency declaration under the Stafford Act (42 U.S.C. 5121 et seq.), or a mandatory evacuation order by the appropriate local, state, or federal official. The emergency period defaults to 30 days if no end date is provided. Covered offenses include various assaults, robbery, arson, burglary, theft, criminal mischief, criminal trespass, and related inchoate and accessory offenses. Penalties are increased by one classification (a Class I misdemeanor enhanced to a Class IV felony). The prosecuting attorney must plead and prove the enhancement allegations beyond a reasonable doubt. No changes to funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an enhanced‑penalty provision: specified violent and property crimes committed in a “disaster area” during an “emergency period” receive the next higher penalty (Class I misdemeanors become Class IV felonies). “Disaster area” is defined to include areas subject to: a Governor’s emergency proclamation (section 81-829.40), a local emergency declaration (section 81-829.50), a Presidential major disaster/emergency declaration under 42 U.S.C. 5121 et seq., or a mandatory evacuation order by the appropriate local, state, or federal official. The prosecuting attorney must allege and prove the enhancement beyond a reasonable doubt in the indictment/information. No agencies are allocated funding and no appropriations or budget changes are included.
