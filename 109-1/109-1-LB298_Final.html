Summary

The bill creates an Office of Inspector General for the Nebraska Correctional System, defines "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services (DOC), and expands the deputy public counsel's authority to oversee DOC facilities, DHHS mental‑health institutions, and Department of Veterans' Affairs institutions. DOC must notify the Inspector General of inmate deaths, serious injuries, and employee hospitalizations and provide timely, secure access to records, facilities, personnel and electronic files; access to inmate/parolee medical or mental‑health records generally requires consent except when a death is investigated. The Tax Commissioner retains tax administration but must furnish returns to the Auditor/Legislative Audit Office on Department premises and may disclose limited tax information to Department of Labor (employment/security/contractor statutes), DMV (IFTA delinquencies), DHHS and courts (support enforcement), and Department of Insurance (tax‑incentive programs). DAS must provide fiscal data to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and adjust the $400,000 project threshold every four years. Staffing for the Public Counsel and Inspector General is to be within available appropriations; no new appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an Office of Public Counsel and creates Inspector General offices for Nebraska Child Welfare and the Nebraska Correctional System under the Division of Legislative Oversight. Key impacts for the listed agencies:

- Department of Health and Human Services (and its Juvenile Services Division): must notify the Inspector General when criminal investigations involving death or serious injury commence and conclude; must provide access to records/personnel and may enter information‑sharing agreements for secure electronic access to case files.

- Department of Correctional Services (department overseeing prisons/parole): similar notification and access/negotiation provisions; secure data‑sharing permitted.

- All listed agencies (Administrative Services, Insurance, Labor, Motor Vehicles, Revenue, Veterans' Affairs) are subject to the new office's and Legislative Audit Office's subpoena and audit powers to compel records/testimony within 30 days; expedited court review and contempt procedures apply.

- Legislative Audit Office established; staffing funded "within the amount available by appropriation" to the Division of Legislative Oversight. No specific new appropriations or budget allocations for the named agencies are provided.
