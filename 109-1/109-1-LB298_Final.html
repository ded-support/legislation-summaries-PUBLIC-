Summary

The bill creates and expands oversight and data‑sharing roles across agencies. It establishes an Office of Inspector General of the Nebraska Correctional System within Legislative Oversight with investigatory/subpoena authority over the Department of Correctional Services. The Office of Public Counsel gains designated deputy counsels for corrections and for institutions (including DHHS and Department of Veterans' Affairs facilities). DHHS must establish a statewide toll‑free child abuse/neglect line and cooperate with the Child Welfare Inspector General. The Department of Revenue’s disclosure rules are expanded to share limited taxpayer data with the Department of Labor, Department of Motor Vehicles (IFTA delinquency), DHHS, and Department of Insurance, and to permit Auditor/Legislative Audit Office reviews under safeguards. DAS must furnish fiscal information to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and is included in taxpayer confidentiality protections. Several new oversight/staffing roles are to be filled "within the amount available by appropriation"; no direct appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds creation/expansion of legislative oversight entities (Office of Public Counsel; Inspectors General for Nebraska Child Welfare and for the Nebraska Correctional System) with broad audit, inspection, investigation, and subpoena powers. For the listed agencies (Department of Administrative Services; Insurance; Labor; Correctional Services; Health and Human Services; Motor Vehicles; Revenue; Veterans' Affairs): employees and records of any agency may be required to be produced or to testify (30‑day compliance), may be audited or inspected, and disputes are adjudicated in Lancaster County on expedited timetables. Agencies may object on privilege and negotiate production terms but subpoenas may be sought if no agreement. Protections: witnesses may have counsel (agency counsel may not represent them); witness fees/travel follow court rates; costs of counsel are not charged to the oversight offices or Legislative Council. The Legislative Auditor will be funded “within the amount available by appropriation” through the Division of Legislative Oversight; no specific appropriations or dollar changes are included.
