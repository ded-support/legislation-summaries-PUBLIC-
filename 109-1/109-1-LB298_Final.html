Summary

The bill creates an Office of Public Counsel with deputies, including a deputy for corrections (oversight of the Department of Correctional Services and county/municipal jails) and a deputy for institutions (oversight of Department of Health and Human Services mental‑health institutions and veterans institutions operated by the Department of Veterans' Affairs). It establishes an Office of Inspector General for the Nebraska Correctional System within Legislative Oversight; DOCS must notify the IG of inmate deaths, serious injuries, hospitalizations and provide personnel/record access (medical/mental‑health records require individual consent except in death investigations). DHHS, juvenile services and youth rehabilitation centers must report child‑welfare incidents to the Inspector General. The Department of Administrative Services Accounting Administrator must furnish data to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst; DAS (with the Tax Commissioner and State Treasurer) is barred from unauthorized taxpayer disclosure. The Department of Revenue retains tax administration and must permit sampled, on‑premises auditor access to returns under strict safeguards. Department of Labor, DMV, DHHS and Department of Insurance may receive limited tax information to administer specified programs. Staffing for the Public Counsel and Inspector General is limited to amounts available by appropriation, and agencies must report to Legislative Oversight when implementation used additional appropriated funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates expanded Legislative oversight: establishes Office of Public Counsel, Inspector General offices for Nebraska Child Welfare and for the Nebraska Correctional System, and a Legislative Audit Office within the Division of Legislative Oversight with subpoena and audit powers. For the Department of Health and Human Services: gives the child‑welfare IG broad access to records/personnel, requires the department to notify the IG when criminal investigations involving death/serious injury begin/conclude, allows negotiated disclosure or subpoena if needed, and protects employee interview rights. For the Department of Correctional Services: parallel IG authority, notification, secure electronic information‑sharing, audits/inspections, negotiation or subpoena authority, and expedited court review (Lancaster County; court decision within 20 days; 10‑day appeals). For Departments of Administrative Services, Insurance, Labor, Motor Vehicles, Revenue, Veterans' Affairs (and other agencies): subject to performance audits and committee‑authorized subpoenas to produce records/testimony within 30 days; counsel/witness protections apply. No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
