Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services. Requires the department to approve signage for newborn safety devices and be notified of any device failures needing removal. Directs the department to develop and publish on its website procedures to allow anonymous provision of medical history, reconsideration of surrender, and paternity testing for surrendered newborns. Fiscal provisions: intent to appropriate $15,000 to the department for FY2025-26 to award grants for installation (not purchase or ongoing operation, except if lack of funding jeopardizes continued use) of newborn safety devices; and subject to available funding, intent to appropriate $65,000 to the department for FY2024-25 and $10,000 annually thereafter to develop, implement, and maintain a public information program (website, literature, media, training, and device awareness).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Health and Human Services):

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Health and Human Services and is charged with approving device signage, receiving notifications of device failures, and administering related procedures.

- DHHS must publish on its website procedures to allow anonymous submission of a surrendered newborn's medical history, reconsideration of surrender, and paternity testing.

- DHHS oversight includes signage approval (multilingual), and notification/documentation requirements for device inspections, repairs, and removals.

- Funding: the Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $15,000 to DHHS for FY2025–26 to award grants for installation (not purchase or ongoing operation) of newborn safety devices, except where lack of funds would jeopardize continued use.
