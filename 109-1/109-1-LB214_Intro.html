Summary

"Department" is defined as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Key DHHS provisions:

- DHHS must develop and publish on its website procedures to allow anonymous submission of a surrendered newborn's medical history, reconsideration of surrender, and paternity testing.

- Signage for newborn safety devices must be approved by DHHS; staff must notify DHHS of device failures.

- Intent to appropriate $15,000 to DHHS for FY2025-26 to award grants for installation (not purchase/ongoing operation) of newborn safety devices, unless lack of funds jeopardizes continued use.

- Subject to available funding, intent to appropriate $65,000 to DHHS for FY2024-25 and $10,000 annually thereafter to create and maintain a public information program (website, literature distribution, media materials, training, and device awareness).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions (Department = Department of Health and Human Services; newborn infant = ≤90 days) and creates technical and operational standards for “newborn safety devices.” Key DHHS responsibilities and budget items:

- Approve device signage; receive notifications of device failures requiring removal; oversee documentation and daily inspections performed by site staff.

- Administer grants: Legislature intends to appropriate $15,000 to DHHS for FY2025‑26 to award grants for installation (not purchase or ongoing operation) of devices, with an exception if lack of funding jeopardizes continued use.

- Develop and publish on its website procedures to allow anonymous submission of a newborn’s medical history, reconsideration of surrender, and paternity testing.
