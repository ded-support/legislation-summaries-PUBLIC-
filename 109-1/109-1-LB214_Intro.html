Summary

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services.

- Requires the Department to develop and publish procedures (on its website) to allow anonymous submission of medical history, reconsideration of surrender, and paternity testing for surrendered newborns.

- Requires staff at device sites to notify the Department of any device failure and mandates Department approval of signage.

- Legislative intent to appropriate $15,000 to the Department for FY2025-26 to award grants for installation (not purchase or ongoing operation, unless lack of funding jeopardizes use) of newborn safety devices.

- Amends intent language to appropriate $65,000 to the Department for FY2024-25 and $10,000 annually thereafter, subject to available funding, to develop/maintain a public information program (including a permanent interactive website, statewide literature distribution, educational materials, training, and public awareness of newborn safety devices).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Dept. of Health and Human Services focus):

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and requires DHHS oversight of newborn safety device program.

- DHHS must approve signage (English, Spanish, other local languages) and receive notifications of device failures; staff at sites must document inspections/repairs.

- DHHS must develop and publish on its website procedures allowing anonymous submission of a surrendered newborn's medical history, reconsideration of surrender, and paternity testing.

- The Legislature intends to appropriate $15,000 to DHHS for FY 2025–26 to award grants for installation (not purchase or ongoing operation) of newborn safety devices, unless funding shortfalls would jeopardize continued use.
