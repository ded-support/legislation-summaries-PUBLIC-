Summary

LB340 adopts the Asbestos Trust Claims Transparency Act and the Asbestos Claims Priorities and Over‑Naming Reform Act. It directs Nebraska courts to enforce claimant obligations to file all applicable asbestos trust claims, produce trust‑claim materials and affidavits, and allows defendants (not less than 60 days before trial) to move the court to compel additional trust filings. Courts may delay trial, order production, and apply setoffs for amounts paid or payable by trusts; trust materials are admissible and not privileged. The bill permits defendants to seek discovery from asbestos trusts with claimant cooperation. The reform act establishes medical prima facie standards (qualified physicians, AMA Guides), limits discovery until prima facie is shown, restricts consolidation absent consent, and applies to new filings. It amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §25‑224 to toll limitations for non‑malignant asbestos conditions until diagnosis meeting the prima facie standard or death. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures requiring claimants to file all asbestos trust claims, produce trust claim materials and affidavits, and supplement them; allows defendants to move courts (and courts to order) additional trust filings, production and a 90‑day delay before trial. Makes trust claim materials and trust governance documents admissible and subject to discovery (claimant cannot assert privilege); permits defendants setoffs for amounts paid or payable by trusts. Establishes medical prima facie standards for impairment and malignant conditions referencing the AMA Guides and American Thoracic Society standards and requires board certification (American Board of Internal Medicine or American Osteopathic Board of Internal Medicine) for certain physicians; courts must hold evidentiary hearings and limit discovery until prima facie proof. Refers to “the court” and asbestos trusts. No executive agencies, departments, appropriations, or budget changes are specified.
