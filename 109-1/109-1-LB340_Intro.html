Summary

LB340 enacts the Asbestos Trust Claims Transparency Act and the Asbestos Claims Priorities and Over‑Naming Reform Act, imposing court-directed procedures and evidentiary rules. Courts may require claimants to file or supplement asbestos trust claims, produce trust governance documents (deemed admissible and not privileged), and provide affidavits; defendants may move 60 days before trial and a court cannot set trial earlier than 90 days after compliance. Courts must hold evidentiary hearings to determine prima facie impairment under specified medical standards (AMA Guides, American Thoracic Society) and may limit discovery until prima facie proof is resolved. Defendants receive setoffs for trust recoveries, allocated proportionally. The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §25‑224 to toll the limitations period for non‑malignant asbestos conditions until a qualifying diagnosis or death. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds detailed procedures for asbestos litigation: claimants must file all applicable asbestos trust claims, produce trust‑claim materials and affidavits, and supplement filings; courts may order additional trust filings, delay trials, admit trust materials as evidence, and allow defendants discovery from trusts and setoffs for trust payments. Creates the "Asbestos Claims Priorities and Over‑Naming Reform Act" with definitions (including AMA Guides) and medical report standards, requires evidentiary hearings to establish prima facie impairment, limits discovery until prima facie is shown, and allows limited consolidation and tolling of statutes of limitations. Mentioned entities: courts, asbestos trusts, American Medical Association, American Thoracic Society, American Board of Internal Medicine, American Osteopathic Board of Internal Medicine. No government agencies, appropriations, or budget changes are referenced.
