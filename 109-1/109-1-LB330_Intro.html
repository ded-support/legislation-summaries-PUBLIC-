Summary

The bill amends section 77-27,132 to direct that, for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023, all proceeds of the state sales and use taxes imposed under section 77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (as defined in section 3-101) be credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. These proceeds are allocated from the Revenue Distribution Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No direct changes affect the Department of Transportation.

Summary of added provisions:

- Effective Oct 1, 2025, state sales tax generally set at 5%, with exceptions: 2.75% for transactions inside portions of "good life" districts located within city/village limits, and a 15.5% rate on alcoholic liquor sales. Transactions in good life districts will receive the reduced rate provided elsewhere in statute.

- Proceeds of sales/use taxes from alcoholic liquor, for transactions on/after Oct 1, 2025, are to be credited 50% to a newly created Alcohol Addiction Prevention and Treatment Fund and 50% to the Education Future Fund; amounts determined quarterly by the Tax Commissioner.

- The Alcohol Addiction Prevention and Treatment Fund (administered by DHHS) is established for prevention/treatment and may be invested per state investment law.
