Summary

LB330 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-27,132 to direct that, for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023, all proceeds of the state sales and use taxes imposed under §77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (per §3-101) be credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. The Tax Commissioner remains responsible for crediting these receipts.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text affect the Department of Transportation.

Summary of changes affecting funding/allocations:

- Effective October 1, 2025, the bill sets state sales tax rules (5% general rate; 2.75% within specified "good life" districts; special treatment for alcoholic liquor sales).

- For transactions on or after October 1, 2025, proceeds of sales/use taxes from alcoholic liquor are to be split 50% to a newly created Alcohol Addiction Prevention and Treatment Fund and 50% to the Education Future Fund; quarterly amounts to be determined by the Tax Commissioner.

- The Alcohol Addiction Prevention and Treatment Fund is created, administered by DHHS, may be invested by the state investment officer, and is to be used for alcohol addiction prevention and treatment.

- No specific appropriations or dollar amounts to DOT are included.
