Summary

LB330 amends revenue distribution (section 77-27,132) to direct all proceeds of sales and use taxes imposed under section 77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (per section 3-101) to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023. No other explicit Department of Transportation appropriations or allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary focused on the Department of Transportation:

The added language sets new sales tax rules effective October 1, 2025, including a 5% statewide rate with special lower rates for designated "good life district" areas and a higher rate on alcoholic liquor. It creates an Alcohol Addiction Prevention and Treatment Fund (administered by DHHS) and directs that proceeds from sales/use taxes on alcoholic liquor be split 50% to that Fund and 50% to the Education Future Fund, with quarterly allocations determined by the Tax Commissioner. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text are directed to or impact the Department of Transportation.
