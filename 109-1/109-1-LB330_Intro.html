Summary

LB330 amends the revenue distribution statute to direct aviation sales-tax revenue to the Department of Transportation. Specifically, for transactions on or after July 1, 2023, all proceeds of the state sales and use taxes (under §77-2703) on the sale or lease of aircraft (per §3-101) are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. This change appears in the Revenue Distribution Fund allocation provisions. No dollar amounts or additional DOT appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the underlined additions affect the Department of Transportation (no new duties, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DOT).

Summary of additions relevant to funding: beginning Oct 1, 2025, statewide sales tax rates are revised (including reduced rates for transactions inside designated Good Life districts and a higher rate on alcoholic liquor). For transactions on or after Oct 1, 2025, 50% of sales/use tax proceeds from alcoholic liquor are credited to a newly created Alcohol Addiction Prevention and Treatment Fund (administered by DHHS) and 50% to the Education Future Fund; the Tax Commissioner will determine quarterly credits. The new DHHS fund may be invested by the state investment officer and is dedicated to alcohol addiction prevention and treatment.
