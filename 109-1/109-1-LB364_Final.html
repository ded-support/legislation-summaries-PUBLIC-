Summary

Amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §37-342. Retains the commission's authority to enter long-term leases, lease-purchase agreements, or other agreements for state park purposes. Requires the commission to obtain approval from the Legislature — or, when the Legislature is not in session, the Executive Board of the Legislative Council — before issuing an indication of intent to incorporate land or facilities owned or developed by a local governmental subdivision into the state park system. When seeking that approval, the commission must provide an estimated fiscal impact that specifies the extent costs can be paid from cash funds and the extent likely to require General Fund appropriations. Repeals the original section.
