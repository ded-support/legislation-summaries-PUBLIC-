Summary

LB91 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-650.01 concerning conveyance of electric distribution systems. It clarifies that a public power district or public power and irrigation district that has acquired an electric distribution system (including by lease) and has fully paid or has reserves sufficient to redeem indebtedness for those assets must convey its right, title, and interest in the distribution system (distinct from generating plants and transmission lines) to a requesting city or village. Request procedures remain: a governing-body resolution, 30 days' public notice, and a potential referendum if petitioned; the municipal clerk administers petitions. A city or village may seek a declaratory judgment with the district to define "distribution system." Existing bond-refinancing and additional-bonding exceptions are retained. No funding or appropriation changes. The original section is repealed.
