Summary

LB91 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-650.01 governing transfers of electric distribution systems between public power providers and municipalities. It applies to public power districts and public power and irrigation districts and requires conveyance of all right, title, and interest in distribution systems to a city or village upon the municipality's resolution request once bonds are paid or reserves sufficient for redemption exist. Municipal notice, a 30‑day referendum petition process (threshold: signatures equal to 20% of last municipal election vote), and filing with the municipal clerk are retained. In absence of agreement, a city/village may seek a declaratory judgment joined with the district to define “distribution system.” The bill preserves limits on refinancing and debt issuance tied to pledged distribution‑system revenue and permits issuance of bonds for additional facilities. The original section is repealed.
