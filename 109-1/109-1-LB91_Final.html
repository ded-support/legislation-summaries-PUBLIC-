Summary

Bill LB91 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-650.01 governing transfers of electric distribution systems from public power districts and public power and irrigation districts to cities or villages. It requires districts that have paid or reserved sufficient funds to redeem bonds/obligations tied to their systems to convey distribution systems (excluding generation and transmission) upon a municipal resolution request, subject to published notice and a potential referendum if a petition (signed by electors equal to ≥20% of the last municipal election turnout) is filed. Cities/villages may seek declaratory judgment to define "distribution system." The bill preserves limits on refinancing existing indebtedness, permits issuance of bonds for new facilities with pledged revenue, and authorizes district boards to allocate revenue attributable to additional facilities. Original section repealed. No appropriations or budget changes.
