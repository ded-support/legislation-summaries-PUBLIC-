Summary

LB91 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-650.01 governing transfers of electric distribution systems between public power entities and municipalities. It requires a public power district or public power and irrigation district that has retired or reserved funds for indebtedness to convey its electric distribution system — explicitly distinguished from generating plants and transmission lines — to a city or village upon a duly adopted municipal resolution (subject to 30‑day public notice and a potential referendum). The bill retains: processes for declaratory-judgment determination of what constitutes the distribution system; protections allowing refinancing without enlarging pledged obligations; authority to issue bonds for additional facilities; and the district board’s ability to allocate revenue attributable to additional facilities. Agencies/actors referenced include public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, city and village governing bodies, municipal clerks, and district boards of directors. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
