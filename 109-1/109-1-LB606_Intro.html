Summary

LB606 consolidates parole supervision by eliminating the Division of Parole Supervision and the Director of Supervision and Services and vesting those powers and duties in the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and its Director. DCS must operate facilities; develop treatment, reentry and parole programs; maintain Board of Parole records and complete electronic offender files; implement validated risk‑and‑needs assessments, performance metrics, and related training; and may pilot a technical‑violation residential housing program. DCS may contract with private providers for community work‑release and reentry centers, use available Board funds for reentry housing, and is intended to receive a $1,000,000 General Fund appropriation for the Community Work Release and Reentry Centers Act. Parolees pay a $25 monthly fee credited to the Parole Program Cash Fund for DCS use. The Department of Health and Human Services may subpoena confidential records for licensing investigations, and DCS is an authorized recipient of certain records for lifetime community supervision. Department of Revenue employees under section 77‑366 are not defined as law enforcement officers.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Correctional Services: New provisions require the department to maintain all records and files for the Board of Parole, specifically including records for persons under lifetime community supervision (sec. 83‑174.03), and expressly allow the department to keep daily records related to the Board of Pardons. The additions also clarify departmental/director implementation authority and emphasize that continued in‑custody correctional treatment, medical care, or vocational/other training may be provided when it substantially improves an offender’s ability to lead a law‑abiding life after release.

Department of Health and Human Services / Department of Revenue: No substantive duties, funding changes, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS or Revenue are added.
