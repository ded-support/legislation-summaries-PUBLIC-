Summary

The bill transfers parole‑supervision functions to the Department of Correctional Services (DCS), abolishing the Division of Parole Supervision and the Director of Supervision and Services and vesting related powers in the Director of Correctional Services. DCS is given expanded responsibilities to operate and staff facilities, administer parole services, maintain Board of Parole records, set performance metrics, adopt validated risk‑and‑needs assessments, create individualized program/reentry plans, train parole officers, run a technical‑parole‑violation housing pilot, and oversee community work‑release and reentry centers (standards, staffing, approvals, inspections, records, monitoring). Parolees will pay a $25 monthly parole programming fee to the State Treasurer credited to a Parole Program Cash Fund for DCS use. The Legislature intends a $1,000,000 General Fund appropriation to implement the Community Work Release and Reentry Centers Act. DCS staff and contractors must cooperate with investigations; DHHS may access confidential records under DCS subpoena for licensure probes. Employees of DCS and specified Department of Revenue employees are excluded from the definition of law enforcement officer.
