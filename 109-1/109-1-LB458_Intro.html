Summary

LB458 amends section 77-1804 to require county treasurers to forward an electronic copy of the list of real property subject to tax sale to the Property Tax Administrator, who must compile a statewide list and publish that compiled list on the website of the Department of Revenue. The bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text mainly expands housing definitions and mandates that duplexes, manufactured homes, and at least one accessory dwelling unit be allowed under municipal zoning, sets accessory unit size/fee limits (application fee cap $250), and defines vacant/abandoned property criteria and streamlined tax-deed procedures for land banks (including affidavit to the county treasurer and a $150 administrative fee charged to the property owner). It also directs review of a $20 fee every five years beginning 2030. No language specifically alters Department of Revenue duties, nor does it create any new appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.
