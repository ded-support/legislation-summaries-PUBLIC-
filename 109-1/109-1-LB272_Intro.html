Summary

LB272 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-3506 to expand homestead property-tax exemptions. Beginning January 1, 2026, the bill adds veterans rated at least 10% but less than 100% service‑connected disabled (and their qualifying surviving spouses) to eligible classes; the exemption for these veterans is a percentage of the exempt amount equal to the veteran’s VA disability rating. The United States Department of Veterans Affairs is specified as the source for required certification of veteran status. The bill also adjusts application/certification timing: applicants under subdivision (2)(a) must provide certification every fifth year, while applicants under (2)(b)–(g) must apply annually but need VA certification only every fifth year. No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a pro rata homestead tax exemption tied to a veteran’s disability rating: the exempt amount for homesteads described in subdivision (2)(g) will equal the veteran’s VA disability percentage. Effective January 1, 2026, expands eligibility to veterans discharged honorable or general (under honorable conditions) who receive compensation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs for a service‑connected disability rated at least 10% but less than 100%, and who are not already eligible for total exemption under sections 77‑3526 to 77‑3528; it also covers an unremarried surviving spouse and a surviving spouse who remarries after age 57. No agencies beyond the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs are added, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
