Summary

LB272 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77‑3506 to expand homestead tax exemptions tied to United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) disability ratings. Effective January 1, 2026, veterans rated at least 10% but less than 100% service‑connected disabled (and qualifying surviving spouses) are eligible for a partial exemption equal to the VA disability percentage. The bill requires VA certification of eligibility; applicants under subsection (2)(a) must reapply and recertify in years evenly divisible by five, while applicants under (2)(b)–(f) and the new (g) must apply annually but need VA recertification only in years evenly divisible by five. The original section is repealed. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a tax exemption tied to VA disability and expands eligible veterans/surviving spouses. Specifically:

- Exempts from taxation on qualifying homesteads a percentage of the exempt amount equal to the veteran’s disability rating as determined by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs.

- Effective January 1, 2026, expands eligibility to veterans discharged honorable/general who receive VA compensation for a service‑connected disability of at least 10% but under 100%, and to their unremarried surviving spouses or spouses who remarry after age 57.

No agencies beyond the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs are referenced, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
