Summary

LB272 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-3506 to expand homestead tax exemptions and to specify certification requirements from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). Beginning January 1, 2026, veterans rated 10–99% service‑connected disabled (and qualifying surviving spouses) become eligible for a pro rata exemption equal to their VA disability percentage. The bill requires VA certification of veteran/spouse status: certain 100% categories require application and VA certification every five years, while other eligible categories require annual application but VA certification only every five years. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or provided in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a tax-exemption tied to VA disability: a homestead exemption equal to the veteran’s disability percentage. Creates (effective Jan 1, 2026) eligibility for that pro rata exemption for veterans discharged honorable or general (under honorable conditions) who receive compensation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs for a service‑connected disability of at least 10% but less than 100%, and for their unremarried surviving spouses or spouses who remarry after age 57, provided they are not already eligible for total exemption under sections 77-3526 to 77-3528. No agencies beyond the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs are added, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
