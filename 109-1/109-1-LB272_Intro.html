Summary

LB272 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-3506 to expand homestead tax exemptions for veterans and surviving spouses. Beginning January 1, 2026, veterans with a service-connected disability rated at least 10% but less than 100% (and their qualifying surviving spouses) become eligible for a prorated exemption equal to the veteran’s VA disability percentage. The bill requires applicants to include certification from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs; applications remain annual for most categories, but VA certification need only be submitted every fifth year for applicable applicants. No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two provisions: (1) for homesteads described in subdivision (2)(g), the tax-exempt amount is prorated — the exemption percentage equals the veteran’s VA disability percentage; and (2) effective January 1, 2026, expands eligibility to veterans discharged honorable or general (under honorable conditions) who receive 10%–99% service‑connected disability compensation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, and to their unremarried surviving spouses or spouses who remarried after age 57. No funding, appropriations, or other agencies are referenced.
