Summary

LB178 directs the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (commission) to establish mandatory server alcohol training, approve programs, certify individuals, and maintain certification lists. Approved programs must issue completion certificates and report participants to the commission. The commission may charge enrollment fees (up to $30) and certification fees (up to $20); all fees are collected, remitted to the State Treasurer, and credited to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund. The fund’s purposes are expanded to cover administrative costs (including salaries/benefits), electronic regulatory transactions, industry education, enforcement training, regulatory equipment, and administering the new training requirements. Funds may be transferred to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction and invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment statutes. Retail license noncompliance may lead to suspension, cancellation, or revocation. Effective Jan 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission to create and administer a mandatory server alcohol training and certification program (see section 6 and section 53-117.03). The commission may charge an enrollment fee up to $30; fees are collected by the commission and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund. Beginning January 1, 2026, retail licensees may only employ persons involved in alcohol sale/service, security, or age verification who hold the commission‑approved certification (or obtain it within 90 days of hire). Licensees must keep employee certificates (or proof of <90 days employment) on the premises. The commission may suspend, cancel, or revoke licenses for noncompliance.
