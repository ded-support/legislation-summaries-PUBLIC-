Summary

This bill directs the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission to establish and administer a mandatory server alcohol-training program (effective Jan. 1, 2026), approve provider programs, require issuance of completion certificates and submission of participant names, and enforce employer compliance (licenses may be suspended, canceled, or revoked). The commission may set enrollment fees (not to exceed $30) and certification fees (not to exceed $20) to defray administrative costs, including salary and benefits. All fees collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund. The fund’s uses are expanded to include administering the new training requirement; funds may be transferred to the General Fund by the Legislature and are invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a mandatory server alcohol-training program to be run by the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (the commission) under section 6 and 53-117.03. The commission may charge an enrollment fee up to $30; all fees are collected by the commission and remitted to the State Treasurer for deposit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund. Effective January 1, 2026, retail licensees may only employ staff who have completed commission‑approved training and obtained certification (or obtain certification within 90 days). Licensees must keep employee certificates (or proof of <90 days employment) on the premises. The commission may suspend, cancel, or revoke licenses for noncompliance.
