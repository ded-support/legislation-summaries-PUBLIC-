Summary

LB178 directs the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission to establish and approve mandatory server alcohol-training programs, issue certificates of completion, and maintain a list of certified persons. Beginning January 1, 2026, retail liquor licensees may employ staff who sell, serve, mix alcohol, provide security, or verify age only if certified (or become certified within 90 days); licensees must keep employee certificates on premises. The commission may charge enrollment fees (up to $30) and certification fees (up to $20); all fees are collected by the commission and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund. The fund’s purposes are expanded to include administering these training provisions, and to cover administrative costs (including salary/benefits), electronic regulatory transactions, education, enforcement training, and equipment. The Legislature may direct transfers from the fund to the General Fund; investments handled by the state investment officer.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission to create and administer a mandatory server alcohol training and certification program (per section 6 and 53-117.03). The commission may charge an enrollment fee up to $30; fees are collected by the commission and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund. Effective January 1, 2026, retail licensees may only employ persons who have completed (or obtain within 90 days of hire) the commission‑approved training and certification for roles involving sale/service/mixing of alcohol, security, or age verification. Licensees must keep employee certificates or proof of recent hire; violations can lead to suspension, cancellation, or revocation of the license by the commission.
