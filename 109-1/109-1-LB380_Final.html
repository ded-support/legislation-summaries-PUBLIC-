Summary

LB380 directs the Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care of the Department of Health and Human Services to:

- Require medicaid managed‑care contractors to comply with federal and state laws and rules for mental‑health and substance‑use‑disorder coverage, including EPSDT for children.

- Publish contractor surveys, financial analyses, contract audits, and parity compliance results.

- Ensure provider access and parity for behavioral health versus medical/surgical services via review of claims, reimbursement procedures, network adequacy, and payment‑rate adequacy.

- Establish monthly electronic communications to all Medicaid providers about contract changes.

- Define network adequacy and annually post the Division’s assessment criteria and each MCO’s compliance on the Department website.

The bill does not specify any direct appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations to DHHS.

Summary (Department of Health and Human Services):

- DHHS (Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care) must enforce contractor compliance with federal/state laws on mental health and substance use disorder (MH/SUD) coverage, make contractor surveys, financial analyses, contract audits and parity reports public, ensure access and parity with medical/surgical providers, define and annually publish network‑adequacy criteria and each MCO’s compliance, and establish monthly electronic communications to Medicaid providers about MCO contract changes.

Program integrity contractors must provide appeal procedures/timelines, contact info, and respond to provider inquiries within 10 business days.
