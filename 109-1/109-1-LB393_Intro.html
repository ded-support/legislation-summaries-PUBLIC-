Summary

This bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-612 to (1) require the State Treasurer to transfer Cash Reserve Fund amounts to the General Fund only upon certification by the Director of Administrative Services that the General Fund cash balance is inadequate (with transfers reversed upon Director notification of sufficiency), and (2) make multiple specified Cash Reserve Fund transfers to various funds on dates and in amounts directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services: $2,425,000 to the Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Revolving Fund (FY2024‑25 window); $4,000,000 to the General Fund (FY2024‑25); $29,458,800 to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund (FY2024‑25); $3,500,000 to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund (FY2024‑25); $25,000,000 to the Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund by June 30, 2025; and $10,000,000 to that fund in FY2025‑26.
