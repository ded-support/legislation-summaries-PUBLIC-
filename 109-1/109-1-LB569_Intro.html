Summary

LB569 amends §38-1216 of the Emergency Medical Services Practice Act to add a statutory duty for the Nebraska Board of Emergency Medical Services to undertake a review and evaluation of the act and statewide emergency care needs and to submit an annual electronic report to the Nebraska Legislature. The required aggregate report items include: counts of EMS telephone/call volumes (by organizational status and primary caregiver level), regional average response times, patient age ranges, complaint types, provider impressions, patient dispositions, number of active Nebraska‑licensed EMS providers, and comparisons with other national regions or states. The bill repeals the prior version of §38-1216. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires an annual statewide report compiling aggregate EMS data for Nebraska, including: total EMS telephone calls; calls by EMS organizational status; calls by primary patient caregiver level of care; average regional response times; patient age ranges; nature of complaints; provider impressions; patient dispositions; number of active licensed EMS providers in Nebraska; and comparisons with other national regions/states. No specific government agency or department is named to produce the report, and no funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
