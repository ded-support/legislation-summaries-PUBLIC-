Summary

LB387 amends §83-170 (Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act) to explicitly define "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and "Director" as the Director of Correctional Services. It clarifies "facility" to mean any institution operated by the department and retains "person committed to the department." The bill adds definitions for "restrictive housing" and "solitary confinement," describing confinement conditions applicable to inmates in department-operated facilities, and references the Director of Supervision and Services (per §83-1,101). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
