Summary

This bill amends county road vacation and section‑line road procedures and assigns duties to county and municipal officials.

Key agency/office actions and changes:

- County boards: may open and work section‑line roads; may propose vacating roads previously declared under prior section 39‑1410 and, for those, may do so without requiring a study. Resolutions must describe the road and (after hearing) state conditions/disposition. Vacation requires a two‑thirds board vote.

- County highway superintendent (or designee): to study road use and submit a 30‑day written report when required; reports retained by the county clerk as permanent records.

- County surveyor or another licensed land surveyor: must perform monumentation and related work; if corners are lost, locate per §§23‑1907/23‑1908.

- County clerk: retain resolutions/reports.

- Planning and public works directors and city governing bodies (metropolitan/primary/first class): must be served/approve vacations when within city zoning jurisdiction.

- Register of deeds: county must file certified vacating resolution within 30 days for indexing.

No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural authority for county governments: county boards may, by resolution, propose vacating or abandoning public roads previously declared public under former section 39-1410 and may do so without conducting a study. The county board must set a public hearing (triggered either by receipt of the report under §39-1722 or by the board’s resolution). The county superintendent (or a superintendent-designated person) is directed to carry out certain duties related to notices, monumenting, and other actions; copies/notice must be mailed to persons listed in §39-1724. Sections 23-1907 and 23-1908 are referenced for monumenting duties. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
