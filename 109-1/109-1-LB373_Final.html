Summary

LB373 amends Nebraska road statutes, affecting county-level road administration and road vacation procedures. Key agencies/departments referenced and duties:

- County board: may open section-line roads (section lines declared public but not mandatory), propose vacating roads previously declared under prior section 39-1410 without requiring a study, adopt resolutions, hold hearings, require two-thirds vote to vacate, and obtain city governing-body approval when within a city's zoning jurisdiction. Must file a certified copy of vacating resolution with the county register of deeds within 30 days.

- County highway superintendent (or designee): conduct studies when required.

- County surveyor or a professional land surveyor: perform preservation/location of government corners (or follow sections 23-1907/23-1908 if lost).

- County clerk: retain resolutions/reports as permanent records.

- City planning and public works directors and city governing bodies: notified/approval roles for roads in city zoning areas.

- Townships: may receive offers when applicable.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations. Original sections repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for county boards to propose vacating or abandoning public roads previously declared under former section 39-1410, and to set public hearings after either receiving the report required by section 39-1722 or passing a resolution to vacate. Specifies notice procedures (mailing to persons identified under section 39-1724) and allows a directed superintendent or designated person to carry out field actions (including placing monuments/survey markers; references sections 23-1907 and 23-1908). No state agencies beyond county boards and county superintendents (or designated persons) are created or altered, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes referenced.
