Summary

LB373 amends Nebraska road statutes (sections 39-1410, 39-1722, 39-1724, 39-1725). Key agency actions and roles:

- County board: may open section-line roads when public good requires; may propose/vote to vacate or abandon public roads (two-thirds vote required); must specify disposition conditions; must file certified vacating resolution with the county register of deeds within 30 days.

- County highway superintendent (or designee): may be directed to study road use and submit written reports retained by the county clerk.

- County surveyor or a licensed professional land surveyor: must perform work to perpetuate government corners and plant monuments; lost corners located per state survey statutes.

- Governing body of metropolitan/primary/first-class cities and their planning and public works directors: prior approval required for vacations/abandonments within city zoning jurisdiction; must receive mailed notice before hearings.

- Townships: may receive offers to relinquish roads in township-operating counties.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures empowering county boards to propose vacating or abandoning previously declared public roads (those under former section 39-1410), allows such actions without requiring a study, and requires the county board to set a public hearing (time/date/place) and mail notices to affected persons (per section 39-1724). It also assigns duties to a directed superintendent or designated person to carry out tasks such as causing survey monuments to be placed. References include sections 23-1907, 23-1908, 39-1722 and 39-1724. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
