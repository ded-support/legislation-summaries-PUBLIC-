Summary

LB373 amends statutes governing county road designation, maintenance, and vacation procedures, affecting county and municipal officials. Key agency impacts:

- County boards: retain authority to open/maintain section-line roads (section lines now declared public roads but not required), to initiate vacations/abandonments, and must adopt resolutions setting hearings and recording outcomes.

- County highway superintendent or designee: may be directed to study road use and submit written reports to the county board; reports and resolutions are permanent records in the county clerk’s office.

- County surveyor or, if none, a licensed professional land surveyor: must perpetuate government corners with monuments and records; lost corners located per §§23-1907–1908.

- Planning and public works directors and governing bodies of metropolitan/primary/first-class cities: receive notice and must approve vacations when within city zoning jurisdiction.

- Register of Deeds: must receive and index vacating resolutions within 30 days.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures authorizing county boards to propose vacating or abandoning public roads previously declared under former section 39‑1410, including: no study requirement for such vacations/abandonments; triggering a public hearing either upon receipt of the report required by section 39‑1722 or by county board resolution; county responsibility to fix the hearing time/place; and required notice to persons identified in section 39‑1724. The additions also reference sections 23‑1907 and 23‑1908 concerning causing and placing monuments and assign duties to the directed superintendent or other designated person to cause those monuments to be set. No state agency other than county boards and the county superintendent (or designated person) is newly created or mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
