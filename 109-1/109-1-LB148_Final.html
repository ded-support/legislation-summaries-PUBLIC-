Summary

The bill amends criminal-background-check provisions (sec. 38-131) to require fingerprint submission to the Nebraska State Patrol and an FBI national criminal history check for initial licensure applicants (including dentists and dental hygienists). Applicants must authorize FBI release of results to the department and pay actual fingerprinting/background-check costs. The Nebraska State Patrol will forward fingerprints to the FBI and provide the criminal-history report to the department. The bill explicitly defines "department" for FBI interpretation as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. No new appropriations or other funding allocations to DHHS are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

This bill adopts the Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact. For the Department of Health and Human Services (as Nebraska’s State Licensing Authority), key additions: it becomes the agency responsible for implementing Compact Privileges (issuing/denying, investigating, and imposing adverse actions or alternative-program conditions), reporting licensure and disciplinary data to the Compact’s Data System, and monitoring that database. DHHS must follow Commission rules and participate in rulemaking, oversight, dispute resolution, and default/withdrawal processes (including continuing recognition of Compact Privileges for 180 days after withdrawal). The Compact authorizes the interstate Commission to establish a budget, levy assessments, charge fees, accept grants/donations, and require participating states to pay assessments or administrative costs. No specific appropriations, dollar amounts, or state budget allocations for DHHS are included in the text.
