Summary

LB148 requires applicants for initial dental and related professional licenses to submit fingerprints for national FBI criminal-history checks. The Nebraska State Patrol forwards fingerprints to the FBI and must report results to "the department," defined for FBI interpretation as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. The bill authorizes the department to receive those reports, adopt rules concerning associated costs, and—upon board recommendation—issue licenses. No appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes for the Department of Health and Human Services are contained in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language adopts the Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact, creating Compact Privileges for interstate practice and a multi‑state Commission to oversee licensure data sharing, adverse‑action reporting, rulemaking, investigations, and enforcement. It requires Participating State licensing authorities to submit licensure, disciplinary, and investigative information to a shared data system and to enforce adverse actions and alternative‑program restrictions. Although the Compact authorizes the Commission to establish a budget, levy assessments, charge fees, accept grants/donations, and hire staff, the text contains no direct appropriation, funding directive, or specific budget allocation for Nebraska's Department of Health and Human Services. DHHS (if it serves as the State Licensing Authority) would have operational/reporting obligations and potential exposure to assessments or fee collection but no explicit new state funding in the bill.
