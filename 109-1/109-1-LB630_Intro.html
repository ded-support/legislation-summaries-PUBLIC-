Summary

LB630 amends the Occupational Therapy Practice Act. Key government entities and actions:

- "Board" (the occupational therapy regulatory board) retains authority to approve training courses and examinations and to identify subjects for the national licensure examination.

- "Department" is directed to issue certificates for physical‑agent and instrument‑assisted modalities to occupational therapists who meet board‑approved criteria and to authorize occupational therapy assistants to set up/implement superficial thermal agent modalities after board‑approved training/exam, only under onsite supervision of a certified occupational therapist.

- The Hand Therapy Certification Commission (or equivalent entity recognized by the board) is referenced as an approved credentialing body.

- Examination results from the national licensure entity are sent to the board for verification.

- Temporary license provisions and a revised definition of supervision are included.

- Original statutory sections listed are repealed.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds licensure and practice provisions for occupational therapy. Key agency references: the state licensing board (the "board") and the entity that administers the national licensure examination. New provisions: temporary licenses expire on issuance of a permanent license, denial, 180 days, or notification of exam failure; applicants must take the national exam per the exam entity's rules and the exam results are sent to the board. Expands and clarifies practice scope (comprehensive evaluation domains, performance patterns/skills, client factors, intervention methods including prevention, health promotion, occupational justice, and group interventions). Defines supervision as a collaborative accountability process where the occupational therapist is accountable for assistants/aides. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
