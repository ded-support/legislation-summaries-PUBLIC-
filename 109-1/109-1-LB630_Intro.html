Summary

LB630 amends the Occupational Therapy Practice Act. Key agency actions:

- The board (state occupational therapy licensing board) retains authority to approve training courses and examinations, identify subjects for the national licensure exam, and recognize equivalent certification entities (e.g., Hand Therapy Certification Commission).

- The department is directed to issue certificates authorizing occupational therapists to apply physical‑agent and instrument‑assisted modalities and to authorize occupational therapy assistants to set up/implement superficial thermal modalities when board‑approved training/exams and onsite supervision requirements are met.

- Temporary licenses may be issued to applicants but only to practice in association with a licensed occupational therapist; examination results are sent to the board.

- Supervision is defined with clear accountability for therapists and assistants.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds licensing and scope provisions for occupational therapy. Key agency references: the state occupational therapy licensing board (the “board”) and the entity that administers the national licensure examination. New provisions require applicants to follow the exam administrator’s rules, and exam results must be sent to the board to verify entry‑level competency. A temporary license now expires on issuance of a permanent license, on denial, 180 days after issuance, or upon notification of exam failure. Substantive additions define required evaluation domains (ADLs, IADLs, performance patterns/skills, client factors), intervention approaches (remediation, compensation, maintenance, health promotion, prevention), specific interventions (therapeutic occupations, training, remediation, injury prevention, occupational justice, group interventions), and a supervision standard making therapists accountable for assistants/aides. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
