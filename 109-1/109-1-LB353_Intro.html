Summary

LB353 prohibits labor organizations representing public school employees or public school employers from including any contract term (entered or renewed after the bill’s effective date) that restricts when a public school employee may join or terminate union membership. It amends §79-873 to retain written payroll-deduction authorizations (including notice of the right to refuse and requirement that revocation be written) and preserves the organization’s ability to certify deduction amounts that bind the employee and the school district, except as limited by the new timing restriction. The bill repeals the prior version of §79-873 and contains an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Prohibits labor organizations (or employers of public school employees) from including, in any membership contract entered or renewed after the act’s effective date, restrictions on when a public school employee may join or terminate union membership. It preserves an exception referenced in section 1 of the act. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
