Summary

LB168 enacts the "340B Contract Pharmacy Protection Act," prohibiting manufacturers, their agents/affiliates, and third‑party logistics providers from denying, restricting, or conditioning delivery/acquisition of 340B drugs to locations authorized by 340B entities, or from requiring data beyond what federal law mandates. Enforcement: the Nebraska Attorney General or any county attorney may sue in the name of the State of Nebraska for injunctions or other relief to prevent violations. The bill includes severability and an emergency clause making it effective on enactment. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "340B Contract Pharmacy Protection Act," defining "340B drug," "340B entity," and "Manufacturer." It bars manufacturers, their agents/affiliates, and third‑party logistics providers from denying, restricting, or conditioning supply or delivery of 340B drugs to locations authorized by 340B entities, and from requiring submission of claims, utilization, purchasing, or other data as a condition for access unless federal law requires it. Enforcement authority is vested in the Nebraska Attorney General and county attorneys, who may sue in the name of the State for injunctions or other relief. The provision defers to federal law where applicable. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are mentioned.
