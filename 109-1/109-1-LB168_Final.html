Summary

Creates the "340B Contract Pharmacy Protection Act." Defines 340B drugs and 340B entities by reference to federal law (42 U.S.C. 256b). Prohibits manufacturers, their agents or affiliates, and third‑party logistics providers from denying, restricting, or prohibiting acquisition or delivery of 340B drugs to locations authorized by 340B entities, and from conditioning such access on submission of claim, utilization, encounter, medical, purchasing, or other data, except where federal law requires otherwise. Enforcement: Nebraska Attorney General or any county attorney may bring actions in the name of the State for injunctions or other relief to prevent violations. Includes a severability clause, a requirement that the Act not conflict with applicable federal law, and an emergency clause making it effective on passage. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "340B Contract Pharmacy Protection Act," defining 340B drug/entity by reference to 42 U.S.C. §256b (as of Jan 1, 2025) and "manufacturer" by reference to Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-7438. It bars manufacturers, their agents/affiliates, and third‑party logistics providers from denying or restricting acquisition or delivery of 340B drugs to locations authorized by 340B entities, and from conditioning access on submission of non‑federally required data. Enforcement: the Nebraska Attorney General or any county attorney may sue in the name of the State of Nebraska for injunctions or other relief. Bill preserves supremacy of federal law and compatible Nebraska law. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
