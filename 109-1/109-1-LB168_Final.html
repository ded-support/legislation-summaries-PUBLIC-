Summary

LB168 enacts the "340B Contract Pharmacy Protection Act." It authorizes the Nebraska Attorney General or any county attorney to bring state actions (including injunctions) to enforce prohibitions on manufacturers, their agents/affiliates, or third‑party logistics providers from: (1) denying or restricting acquisition or delivery of 340B‑priced drugs to locations authorized by 340B entities (as defined by 42 U.S.C. 256b); and (2) conditioning such access on submission of non‑federally required data. The bill includes a severability clause, clarifies it must not conflict with federal law, contains an emergency clause making it effective on enactment, and contains no appropriations or budget changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "340B Contract Pharmacy Protection Act," defining "340B drug," "340B entity" (per 42 U.S.C. 256b as of Jan 1, 2025), and "Manufacturer" (per Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-7438). Prohibits manufacturers, their agents/affiliates, and third‑party logistics providers from denying, restricting, or conditioning delivery or acquisition of 340B drugs to locations authorized by 340B entities, and from requiring non‑federally‑mandated data as a condition of access. Enforcement: the Nebraska Attorney General or any county attorney may sue in the name of the State for injunctions or other relief. Includes a savings clause deferring to compatible federal or other state law. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes included.
