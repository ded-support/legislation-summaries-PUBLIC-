Summary

LB495 amends the Property Tax Request Act (Neb. Rev. Stat. §§77‑1632 and 77‑1633). It narrows the scope of the special joint‑hearing and expanded notice procedures in §77‑1633 to "county, city, or school district," removing community colleges from that definition. As a result, county clerks, county assessors, county boards, and participating political subdivisions (counties, cities, school districts, learning communities, sanitary and improvement districts, natural resources districts, and educational service units) will still follow §77‑1633 joint public‑hearing, postcard‑mailing, website‑posting, cost‑allocation, and reporting duties, but those requirements no longer apply to community colleges, which remain subject to the standard §77‑1632 hearing/notice process. The bill contains an emergency clause and takes effect on enactment. No changes to appropriations or funding allocations are made.
