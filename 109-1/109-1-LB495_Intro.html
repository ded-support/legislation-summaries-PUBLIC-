Summary

LB495 amends the Property Tax Request Act (§§77‑1632, 77‑1633). Key agency effects:

- Removes community colleges from the definition of "political subdivision" in §77‑1633 and thus excludes them from the joint public‑hearing/oversight procedures that apply when a county, city, or school district seeks to increase its property tax request above the allowable growth percentage.

- Assigns joint‑hearing organization and notification duties to the county clerk (must notify the county assessor by Sept. 4) and requires the county assessor to provide postcard data to a county‑designated printer.

- Provides that initial postcard printing costs are paid from the county general fund and then proportionally charged to participating political subdivisions based on parcel counts; postcards must be mailed at least seven days before the hearing.

- Continues deadlines for certification to the county clerk (on or before Oct. 15). Emergency clause makes the act effective on enactment.
