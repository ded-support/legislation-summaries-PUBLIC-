Summary

LB246 amends the Nebraska Pure Food Act (sections 81‑2,239, 81‑2,240, 81‑2,282) and the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act (section 87‑302) to add a statutory definition of "cultivated‑protein food product" and to classify such products as adulterated. The bill prohibits manufacturing, producing, importing, distributing, promoting, offering, or selling adulterated food—including cultivated‑protein food products—and makes violations a deceptive trade practice under state law. It retains references to the Food Code and Current Good Manufacturing Practice standards adopted by reference, harmonizes related provisions, and repeals the prior sections. No state agencies or departments are explicitly named in the bill, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "cultivated-protein food product" as a food resembling animal tissue but produced from manufacturing cells grown in vitro (including stem-cell–derived processes). Prohibits producing, importing, promoting, displaying for sale, or attempting to sell such products. Creates an additional civil consequence: violations committed in the course of business are also treated as deceptive trade practices, without altering liability under the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. References enforcement/penalty via section 81-2,282. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes in the added text.
