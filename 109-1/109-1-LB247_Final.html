Summary

LB247 amends provisions administered by the Department of Environment and Energy concerning solid‑waste disposal fees and the Petroleum Release Remedial Action Cash Fund. Landfill/processing operators must pay a disposal fee of $2.34 per applicable unit quarterly to the department. Receipts are split 65% to the Integrated Solid Waste Management Cash Fund (for the department’s response to spills/environmental emergencies; regulation, investigation, remediation and monitoring; CERCLA nonfederal cost‑share, O&M and related activities) and 35% to the Waste Reduction and Recycling Incentive Fund (grants to local governments and reimbursements for dump‑site remediation, with political‑subdivision reimbursements capped at 5% of prior‑year disposal fee revenue). The Petroleum Release Remedial Action Cash Fund (administered by the department) receives fee revenue, settlements, gifts and appropriations and may be used for remedial actions, department response costs, State Fire Marshal fee collection expenses, State Claims Board‑approved claims, and investment earnings are credited to the fund. The bill declares an emergency and becomes effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority to provide cost‑share, operation, and maintenance support for remediation of Superfund sites to cover nonfederal costs (including in‑kind services) under the federal Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act of 1980 (42 U.S.C. 9601 et seq.). The provision is tied to activities through June 30, 2025. No specific dollar amounts or new appropriations are included in the added text.
