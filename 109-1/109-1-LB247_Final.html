Summary

LB247 amends statutes administered by the Department of Environment and Energy governing solid-waste disposal fees and the Petroleum Release Remedial Action Cash Fund.

Key agency actions and fiscal changes:

- Imposes a disposal fee of $2.34 per specified unit of solid waste. Fee receipts: 65% to the Integrated Solid Waste Management Cash Fund (administered by the department) to cover department direct/indirect costs including spill response, CERCLA nonfederal cost‑share/operation & maintenance for Superfund sites, regulation, investigation, remediation and monitoring; department may seek cost recovery.

- 35% to the Waste Reduction and Recycling Incentive Fund for grants and reimbursements to political subdivisions (reimbursement disbursements capped at 5% of prior fiscal year disposal‑fee revenue; director to seek geographic distribution).

- Petroleum Release Remedial Action Cash Fund (administered by the department): continues funding sources and allowable uses, and limits department CERCLA cost‑share/O&M payments to up to $1.5 million per fiscal year through June 30, 2025. Investment earnings credited to the fund; State Treasurer, State Fire Marshal, State Claims Board and state investment officer referenced for administration.

The bill repeals the original sections, declares an emergency, and states an operative date of July 1, 2025, while taking effect upon passage and approval.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority to provide cost‑share, operation, and maintenance funding for remediation of Superfund sites to cover nonfederal costs (including in‑kind services) under the federal Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act of 1980 (42 U.S.C. 9601 et seq.).
