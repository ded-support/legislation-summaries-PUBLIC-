Summary

Bill LB452 declares the Legislature’s intent to appropriate funds to the Department of Health and Human Services: $500,000 in FY2025-26 and $500,000 in FY2026-27 from federal funds. The amounts are designated as Federal Funds (specifically federal Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) funds) and are earmarked solely for court-appointed special advocate state aid. The bill includes an emergency clause, making it effective upon passage and approval.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Legislature intends to appropriate $500,000 in FY2025-26 and $500,000 in FY2026-27 in federal funds to the Department of Health and Human Services for state aid. Each year’s appropriation specifically designates $500,000 Federal Funds for Court Appointed Special Advocate (CASA) state aid and restricts use exclusively to that purpose. The funding source is federal Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) in both fiscal years.
