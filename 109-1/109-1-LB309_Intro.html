Summary

LB309 (2025) creates the Safe Battery Collection and Recycling Act and delegates administration to the Nebraska Department of Environment and Energy ("department"). Key agency actions: review and approve battery stewardship plans (120-day review clock), require periodic plan updates, audit plans after five years, host approved plans and annual reports on the department website (with proprietary exemptions), review out-of-state studies and report to the Legislature's Natural Resources Committee by May 30, 2028, and adopt implementing rules. Enforcement authorities: Attorney General and county attorneys may seek injunctions; civil penalties remit to the State Treasurer. Fiscal provisions: establishes the Battery Stewardship Cash Fund, consisting of fees collected under section 4, administered by the department and restricted to implementing the Act; funds may be invested by the State Investment Officer under existing state investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Department of Environment and Energy as the lead agency to administer the Safe Battery Collection and Recycling Act: approving and reviewing battery stewardship plans (plans due Jan 1, 2027; department must act within 120 days), posting approved plans and annual reports on its website, reviewing out‑of‑state studies and reporting to the Legislature (Natural Resources Committee by May 30, 2028), and adopting rules. Establishes the Battery Stewardship Cash Fund, funded by fees under section 4, administered by the department and invested by the state investment officer under state investment laws; monies may be used only to carry out the Act. Civil penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer; Attorney General and county attorneys may enforce.
