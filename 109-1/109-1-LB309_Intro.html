Summary

LB309 designates the Department of Environment and Energy as the lead agency to implement the Safe Battery Collection and Recycling Act. The department must review and approve or deny battery stewardship plans (120-day review), post approved plans and reports (excluding proprietary data), require plan amendments and participant notifications, adopt rules, and report to the Legislature’s Natural Resources Committee by May 30, 2028. Civil enforcement may be pursued by the Attorney General or county attorneys; civil penalties collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution. The bill creates the Battery Stewardship Cash Fund, to be administered by the department and funded by fees collected under section 4; funds are restricted to carrying out the Act and may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment statutes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Department of Environment and Energy (defined as "the department") as the lead agency to review, approve, conditionally approve, deny, and post battery stewardship plans and amendments, with specified timelines (plan submissions by Jan 1, 2027; department action within 120 days; revised plan decisions within 90 days). The department must publish approved plans and annual reports (excluding proprietary data), review out‑of‑state studies and report to the Legislature's Natural Resources Committee by May 30, 2028, and may adopt implementing rules. Creates a Battery Stewardship Cash Fund, consisting of fees from section 4, administered by the department and investable by the state investment officer under Nebraska investment law. Civil penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer; enforcement may involve the Attorney General or county attorneys.
