Summary

LB567 directs the Nebraska State Board of Education to adopt a model policy on community engagement. School districts are authorized (but not required) to adopt local policies consistent with that model. No state agencies beyond the State Board of Education and local school districts are referenced, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
