Summary

- The bill expands the definition of "administrator" to expressly include an administrator of a Department of Correctional Services facility (or designee).

- Multiple provisions authorize holding persons taken into emergency protective custody, or subjects in sex-offender commitment proceedings, in a Department of Correctional Services facility as an appropriate location for admission or custody; dangerous sex offenders may be held in DOC facilities and shall not be admitted to a medical facility for emergency custody unless a medical or psychiatric emergency requires it.

- The written emergency-custody certificate required from law enforcement must be prescribed and provided by the Department of Health and Human Services; copies are forwarded to the county attorney.

- No direct appropriations or specified budget amounts for either agency appear.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Regional Mental Health Expansion Pilot Program administered by the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice to fund one county law enforcement agency to add mental‑health beds regionally. The commission must develop grant eligibility, application and audit rules, identify grant funding, and may adopt rules; it will coordinate with the Attorney General. No specific appropriation or dollar amounts are included.

Department of Correctional Services: added as an explicit potential placement site ("Department of Correctional Services facility") and is referenced in provisions allowing admission to jails/other correctional facilities under the pilot and permitting videoconferenced evaluations/hearings.

Department of Health and Human Services: not directly altered or allocated funding in the added text; existing references to medical facilities and section 71‑919 remain, and videoconferencing for evaluations/hearings is authorized.
