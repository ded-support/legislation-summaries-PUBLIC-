Summary

LB386 adds explicit references to the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Key provisions:

- "Administrator" definition is amended to include administrators of Department of Correctional Services facilities.

- Multiple sections authorize holding, admitting, evaluating, or detaining persons in jails or Department of Correctional Services facilities for emergency protective custody, evaluation, or sex-offender commitment when appropriate (including use of jail mental-health beds created under the Regional Mental Health Expansion Pilot Program).

- The written certificate executed by the arresting officer is to be "prescribed and provided by the Department of Health and Human Services."

- The Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice must identify grant funding for the pilot program; the bill includes no specific appropriations or dollar allocations to DHHS or DCS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Creates the "Regional Mental Health Expansion Pilot Program" administered by the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice to fund a single county law enforcement agency to add mental‑health beds and foster regional MOUs.

- The commission must develop grant rules, application and audit requirements, identify grant funding, and may adopt rules; it will coordinate with the Attorney General. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are included.

- The bill explicitly permits temporary placement of individuals in jails or Department of Correctional Services facilities that have available mental‑health beds under the pilot until a medical facility is available, and expands use of videoconferencing for evaluations/hearings.

- The Department of Health and Human Services is not assigned new duties or funding in the added text; no DHHS budget or appropriation changes are specified.
