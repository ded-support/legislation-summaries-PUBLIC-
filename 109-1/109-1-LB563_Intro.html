Summary

LB563 amends the Motor Vehicle Registration Act (sections 60-301, 60-302, 60-3,101, and 60-3,104) to define "commercial fertilizer trailer" and authorize the Department (the agency charged by the Act) to issue permanent trailer license plates for such trailers. It: (1) adds the new definition; (2) exempts these permanent plates from the six‑year reissuance/validation‑decal cycle; (3) requires return of the permanent plate to the Department if the registrant no longer owns the trailer; and (4) makes the registrant liable for the full registration fee on renewal for trailers still owned. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed. Original statutes are repealed and provisions harmonized.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "commercial fertilizer trailer" — a trailer owned by an individual or business that sells agricultural fertilizer or chemicals. Establishes that permanent trailer license plates issued under section 6 must be returned to "the department" if the registrant no longer owns the trailer, and that the registrant remains liable for the full registration fee for any commercial fertilizer trailer still owned at renewal. No agencies beyond "the department" are named and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
