Summary

LB563 amends the Motor Vehicle Registration Act to (1) add a definition of "commercial fertilizer trailer" (a trailer owned by an individual or business that sells agricultural fertilizer or chemicals as a going concern) and (2) authorize permanent trailer license plates for commercial fertilizer trailers. The bill directs the department (the motor vehicle registration authority) to treat such plates as exempt from the six‑year reissue cycle, requires return of the permanent plate if the registrant no longer owns the trailer, and makes the registrant liable for the full registration fee on renewal for trailers still owned. The bill amends sections 60‑301, 60‑302, 60‑3,101, and 60‑3,104 and repeals the original sections. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.

Adds a definition of "commercial fertilizer trailer" as a trailer owned by an individual or business that sells agricultural fertilizer or chemicals. Requires that if a permanently‑plated commercial fertilizer trailer is no longer owned by the registrant, the permanent plate must be returned to "the department" (unnamed), and the registrant remains liable for the full registration fee on any trailer still owned at renewal.
