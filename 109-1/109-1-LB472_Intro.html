Summary

LB472 creates the Office of Regulatory Management within state government, led by a Director appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the Governor. The office will supervise a baseline, publicly published catalog of agencies' regulatory requirements and publish biennial reports showing changes and estimated compliance costs for a median Nebraska citizen or business. All state departments, agencies, offices, and divisions must conduct internal reviews (initial reviews due within six months) and provide evaluations, needs assessments, and supporting due diligence to the office on request; updates are required every two years or within 60 days of a request. The office reviews agencies' identification of systemic problems, requires cost–benefit and monetized/nonmonetized estimates, may request reevaluation if best practices were not used, and must publish its assessments. The office may not authorize or reject other agencies' regulatory actions. No funding appropriations or budget allocations are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Office of Regulatory Management under a Director appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the Governor. The office will supervise a baseline, publicly published catalog of all regulatory requirements, require every Nebraska state agency/department/office/division to conduct and submit internal reviews (initially within six months, then biennially or within 60 days of request), and publish a biennial report estimating changes and compliance costs for the median citizen or business. The office will collect, assess, and publish agencies' evaluations and due diligence for new rules, can ask agencies and the Governor to reevaluate rulemaking if best practices weren’t used, but cannot approve or block agency regulations. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
