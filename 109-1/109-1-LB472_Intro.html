Summary

Creates the Office of Regulatory Management, led by a Director appointed by the Governor, to support and oversee state agencies' regulatory functions. The office must build and publish a baseline catalog of all regulatory requirements, require every state department/agency/division to submit internal reviews (initially within six months, then updated every two years or within 60 days of request), and publish a biennial report showing changes and estimated compliance costs for a median citizen or business. The office will collect, analyze, and publish agencies' evaluations, needs assessments, and cost‑benefit analyses, insist on empirical justification for regulatory action, and may request agencies and the Governor to reevaluate deficient actions. The office may not approve or reject agency regulations. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Office of Regulatory Management under a Director appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the Governor. The office will supervise, support, and regulate other state agencies' regulatory functions and must be staffed appropriately. Agencies must inventory and submit all current regulatory requirements to the office (initial reviews due within six months; updates on request or every two years, or within 60 days of an update request). The office will maintain and publish a transparent baseline catalog and biennial report showing changes and estimated compliance costs for the median Nebraska citizen or business. The office collects and publishes agencies' evaluations and due diligence, requires rigorous evidence, cost–benefit and monetized/quantitative (or qualitative) estimates for new rules, may request reevaluation if best practices were not used, but cannot approve or reject agency rules. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
