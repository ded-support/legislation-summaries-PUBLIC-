Summary

Creates the Office of Regulatory Management, led by a Director appointed by the Governor, to supervise and support other state agencies' regulatory functions. The office must staff and maintain a transparent baseline catalog of all regulatory requirements, requiring every Nebraska department, agency, office, and division to submit an initial internal review within six months and biennial updates (or within 60 days of request). The office will collect, analyze, and publish agency evaluations, needs assessments, and cost‑benefit analyses (including estimated compliance costs for a median citizen or business) and may request agencies and the Governor to reevaluate actions that do not follow prescribed best practices. The office may publish assessments but has no authority to approve or reject agency regulations. No appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Office of Regulatory Management under the Regulatory Management Act, headed by a Director appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the Governor. The office will staff and supervise programs to support and regulate other state agencies, but does not have authority to approve or reject their regulatory actions. It must build and publish a transparent baseline catalog of all regulatory requirements, require every state department/agency to conduct and submit internal reviews on specified timelines (initially within six months, then updates within 60 days or biennially), and publish a biennial report including estimated compliance-cost changes for a median citizen or business. The office will collect, analyze, and publish agencies’ evaluations/duediligence for new rules, enforce analytic standards (e.g., evidence, cost‑benefit, monetized or quantitative estimates where feasible), and may ask agencies and the Governor to reevaluate actions that fail to follow best practices. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
