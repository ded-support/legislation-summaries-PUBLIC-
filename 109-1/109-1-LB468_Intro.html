Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: The budget division administrator may direct State Treasurer transfers from the Securities Act Cash Fund to the General Fund — $29 million on or before June 30, 2026, and $23 million on or before each June 30, 2027, 2028, and 2029.

- Department of Insurance: Securities registration fees are credited to the Department of Insurance Cash Fund. The Director must transmit insurance premium tax receipts annually: 50% to the State Treasurer, 30% to the General Fund, 10% to the Mutual Finance Assistance Fund, and 10% to counties (by population), subject to specified exceptions.

- Department of Revenue: Retains discretion to review county assessor sale determinations, must publish compiled delinquent-sale lists online, will collect/adjust the renewable-energy nameplate-capacity tax and remit proceeds to county treasurers, and the 3% collection fee is credited to the Department of Revenue Property Assessment Division Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue

- New duty: beginning Jan 1, 2027 and annually thereafter, DOR must adjust a specified tax rate by the average annual percentage change in total statewide property taxes over the most recent 10 years (round to nearest dollar).

- Creates a 1% tax on clear market value of property (or beneficial interests) received by each person in excess of $100,000 for decedents dying on/after July 1, 2025; proceeds are to be distributed to counties (credit to county general funds) according to certified property-tax shares, with 10% allocated to counties by population share.

- Legislative intent to review specified fees every five years beginning 2030.
