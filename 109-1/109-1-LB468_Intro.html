Summary

The bill affects the three named agencies as follows:

- Department of Administrative Services: authorizes the budget division administrator to direct State Treasurer transfers from the Securities Act Cash Fund to the General Fund—$29 million on or before June 30, 2026, and $23 million on or before each June 30 in 2027, 2028, and 2029.

- Department of Insurance: assigns administration of securities registrations for insurance-company securities to the Director of Insurance; registration fees are credited to the Department of Insurance Cash Fund. The Director must remit specified insurance‑related taxes with distribution instructions, including a 10% population‑proportionate share to counties and specified exceptions/remittances for fire, workers’ compensation, and health‑insurer taxes.

- Department of Revenue: Tax Commissioner will design documentary stamp tax stamps, promulgate rules, and issue declaratory rulings; require annual ImagiNE Nebraska personal‑property exemption claims by May 1 with determinations certified by August 1; allow payment of certain exemption fees with sales/use tax returns; publish county real‑property sale lists (collection fees credited to the Department of Revenue Property Assessment Division Cash Fund); and administer/adjust a renewable nameplate capacity tax starting Jan 1, 2027, remitting proceeds to county treasurers within 30 days.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: new mandates — beginning Jan 1, 2027 and annually thereafter DOR must adjust a specified tax rate by the average annual percent change in total statewide property taxes over the most recent 10-year period, rounding to the nearest whole dollar. The bill also adds (effective for decedents dying on or after July 1, 2025) a 1% tax on the clear market value of property/beneficial interests received by each person in excess of $100,000 (estate/benefit threshold). Multiple provisions express legislative intent to review specified fees beginning in 2030 and every five years thereafter.

- Department of Administrative Services: no underlined changes identified.

- Department of Insurance: no underlined changes identified.

- Funding/appropriations: beginning July 1, 2025 the State Treasurer shall distribute $5,000,000 annually from the Securities Act Cash Fund to counties pro rata by population.
