Summary

The bill directs several fiscal and administrative actions for the three agencies. Department of Administrative Services (DAS): the State Treasurer must transfer specified amounts from the Securities Act Cash Fund to the General Fund—$29 million by June 30, 2026, then $23 million each in 2027–2029—on dates/amounts set by DAS’s budget administrator; beginning July 1, 2025, $5 million annually from that fund is to be distributed proportionately to counties. Department of Insurance: Securities Act registration fees are credited to the Department of Insurance Cash Fund, and the Director must, after audit, remit insurance‑related tax shares (50% to the State Treasurer, 30% to the General Fund, 10% to the Mutual Finance Assistance Fund, 10% to counties). Department of Revenue: authority over declaratory rulings; post county sale lists online; retain a 3% collection fee to the DOR Property Assessment Division Cash Fund; adjust/collect the renewable nameplate capacity tax (rate adjusted Jan 1, 2027 per a 10‑year property tax change) and remit proceeds to county treasurers within 30 days.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: no underlined additions affecting DAS were included.

- Department of Insurance: no underlined additions affecting DOI were included.

- Department of Revenue: adds a standing mechanism requiring the Department of Revenue, beginning January 1, 2027 and each January 1 thereafter, to adjust the (specified) tax rate by the average annual percentage change in total property taxes levied statewide over the most recent ten-year period; non‑whole-dollar results are rounded to the nearest whole dollar. The bill also expresses legislative intent to review specified fees at least once every five years beginning in 2030.

- Funding/appropriations: beginning July 1, 2025 the State Treasurer must distribute $5 million annually from the Securities Act Cash Fund to counties pro rata by population.
