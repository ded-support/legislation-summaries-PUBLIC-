Summary

The bill authorizes several fiscal and administrative changes for the named agencies. Department of Administrative Services: the budget administrator may direct State Treasurer transfers from the Securities Act Cash Fund to the General Fund — $29 million on or before June 30, 2026, and $23 million on or before each June 30, 2027–2029. Department of Insurance: securities-registration fees for insurance-company issuers are credited to the Department of Insurance Cash Fund; the Director must transmit related tax receipts with allocations of 50% to the State Treasurer, 30% to the General Fund, 10% to the Mutual Finance Assistance Fund, and 10% to counties pro rata by population. Department of Revenue: the Property Tax Administrator retains a 3% collection fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Property Assessment Division Cash Fund; the department must publish county real-property sale lists, defer overturning county sale‑qualification determinations absent its own review, and will adjust, collect, and remit nameplate-capacity tax proceeds to county treasurers with annual rate adjustments beginning Jan. 1, 2027. No other provisions affect these agencies.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: New statutory duty — beginning Jan. 1, 2027 and annually thereafter the Department must adjust a specified tax rate by the average annual percent change in total statewide property taxes over the most recent 10 years; non‑whole dollar results are rounded to the nearest dollar. The text also includes legislative intent that certain fees be reviewed every five years beginning 2030. No explicit appropriation or new funding for the Department is provided.

- Department of Administrative Services: No changes or funding/appropriation actions affecting DAS appear in the added text.

- Department of Insurance: No changes or funding/appropriation actions affecting DOI appear in the added text.

- Other fiscal change: Beginning July 1, 2025 the State Treasurer will distribute $5.0 million annually from the Securities Act Cash Fund to counties, allocated by county population.
