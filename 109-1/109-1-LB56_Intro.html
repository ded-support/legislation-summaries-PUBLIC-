Summary

LB56 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,102 to require that post-mortem alcohol/drug test results be included in reports under §§60-6,101–60-6,104 and be tabulated monthly by the Department of Transportation. The bill specifies that this information—including the identity of the deceased and any measured alcohol or drug levels—will be public information and may be released or disclosed as provided by the Department of Transportation. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provision requires medical facilities and mortuaries to comply with a peace officer’s request to obtain a blood draw from a driver or pedestrian described in subsection (1). There are no Department of Transportation-specific duties, program changes, or references in the added text, nor any funding, appropriation, or budget allocation adjustments for the Department of Transportation.
