Summary

The bill requires that information from post‑mortem alcohol/drug testing (for drivers or pedestrians who die within four hours of a motor vehicle crash) be included in reports under sections 60‑6,101 to 60‑6,104 and be tabulated monthly by the Department of Transportation. It specifies that this information — including the identity of the deceased and measured alcohol/drug levels — is public and may be released or disclosed as provided by the department. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Transportation are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection requiring medical facilities and mortuaries to comply with a peace officer’s request to draw blood from a driver or pedestrian described in subsection (1). No provisions reference the Department of Transportation, and the amendment contains no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations affecting DOT.
