Summary

LB412 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 43‑1401 and 43‑1411 concerning paternity actions. Key government entities referenced: juvenile courts (including county or separate juvenile courts), county courts, county sheriffs, counties, and the State of Nebraska. The bill: (1) authorizes third‑party complainants to intervene in juvenile proceedings to establish paternity (with an affidavit) and bars intervention if parental rights have been terminated; (2) directs juvenile courts to order genetic testing per §43‑1414 and may require the child be made available for testing; (3) waives any filing fee for the intervention complaint; and (4) allows the juvenile court to allocate genetic‑testing costs to the complainant, the county, or the state at its discretion.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds criteria and a definition related to juvenile-court-ordered paternity/genetic testing (within statutes 43-1401 to 43-1418 and excepting subsection (3) of 43-1411). The juvenile court may consider: the child's age; the child's relationship with any presumptive parent, any parent established by acknowledgment or court finding, and the complainant; whether establishing paternity would benefit or harm the child; and any other relevant factors. Defines "child" as a person under 18, including those born out of wedlock. No government agencies/departments are referenced and no funding or appropriation changes are made.
