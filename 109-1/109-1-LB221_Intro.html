Summary

LB221 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §50‑1301 to address prolonged vacancies in statutorily created boards, commissions, and similar entities. For a seat statutorily required to be filled by gubernatorial appointment that has been vacant six months or more, the legislative committee of jurisdiction may accept applications and forward appointment recommendations to the Governor. If the Governor does not fill the seat within three months after receiving those recommendations, the Executive Board of the Legislative Council is authorized to make the appointment. The bill names the Governor, legislative committee of jurisdiction, and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council as the relevant actors. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original section 50‑1301 is repealed.
