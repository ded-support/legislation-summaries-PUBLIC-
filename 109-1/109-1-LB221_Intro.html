Summary

LB221 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §50-1301 to add legislative findings about growth of boards and commissions and to establish procedures for filling extended vacancies in gubernatorial appointments. If a gubernatorial-appointed seat is vacant six months or more, the legislative committee of jurisdiction may accept applications and forward recommendations to the Governor. If the seat remains vacant three months after recommendations are forwarded, the Executive Board of the Legislative Council may make the appointment. The bill references the Legislature, the Governor, legislative committees of jurisdiction, and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council. The original section is repealed. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a procedure for filling gubernatorial vacancies on statutory boards, commissions, and similar entities: if a gubernatorial-appointed seat is vacant six months, the legislative committee of jurisdiction may accept applications and forward recommendations to the Governor; if the seat remains vacant three months after those recommendations are sent, the Executive Board of the Legislative Council may make the appointment. Mentions the Governor, the Legislature, legislative committees of jurisdiction, and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
