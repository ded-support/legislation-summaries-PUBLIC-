Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Revenue responsibility to implement the First‑Time Homebuyers Savings Account Act. Duties include adopting rules and regulations; creating and making available required forms (account designation, beneficiary designation, annual report specifying transactions and supporting documentation, and a transaction report for withdrawals); and receiving those reports and any IRS Form 1099s with the taxpayer's Nebraska return. The Department of Revenue is directed to collect a 10% penalty on certain nonqualified withdrawals and remit penalties to the State Treasurer per the Nebraska Constitution. The act becomes operative January 1, 2026. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are provided in the bill.

Highlights affecting the Department of Revenue:
