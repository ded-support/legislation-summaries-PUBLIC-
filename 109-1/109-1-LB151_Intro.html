Summary

LB151 assigns the Department of Revenue (department) administrative duties for the new First‑Time Homebuyers Savings Account Act. The department must provide forms for account designation and beneficiary designation, annual reports, and transaction reports; adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act; and receive annual reports, IRS Form 1099s, and transaction reports from account holders. The department is directed to collect statutory penalties (10% on certain withdrawals) and remit them to the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution. The bill expressly relieves financial institutions of duties to report or verify account use. No appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue responsibilities: adopt rules and create forms to implement the First‑Time Homebuyer Savings Account program (account designation, beneficiary designation, annual report, transaction report). Account designations and beneficiary designations must be submitted on department forms by the individual income tax filing deadline; annual reports and any IRS Form 1099 must be included with the Nebraska income tax return; transaction reports are required on withdrawals. The Department will collect 10% penalties on nonqualified withdrawals and remit those penalties to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution. Financial institutions are explicitly not required to verify, label, report, or administer accounts for the Department. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.
