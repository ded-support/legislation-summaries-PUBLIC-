Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Revenue these duties under the First‑Time Homebuyers Savings Account Act: adopt and promulgate implementing rules and create required forms (account designation, beneficiary designation, annual report, transaction report with minimum content); receive account designations and beneficiary designations submitted by the individual by the individual income tax filing date; receive annual reports, any IRS Form 1099 issued for the account, and transaction reports on withdrawals; collect 10% penalties on nonqualified withdrawals and remit penalties to the State Treasurer in accordance with Article VII, section 5. The bill contains no appropriations or changes to departmental funding.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue responsibilities: adopt rules and implement the First‑Time Homebuyers Savings Account Act; create and provide required forms (account designation, beneficiary designation, annual account report listing transactions and required documentation, and withdrawal/transaction report documenting eligible home costs and remaining balances). Account holders must file the annual report, any IRS Form 1099, and transaction reports with their Nebraska income tax return by the filing deadline in section 77‑2768. The Department will collect a 10% penalty on nonqualified withdrawals (with limited exceptions) and remit those penalties to the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department are included in the added text.
