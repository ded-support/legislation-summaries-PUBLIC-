Summary

LB151 assigns the Department of Revenue (referred to in the bill as "the department") responsibility for implementing the First‑Time Homebuyers Savings Account Act. The Department must adopt and promulgate rules and create required forms (account designation, beneficiary designation, annual report, transaction report). Account holders must submit annual reports, any IRS Form 1099, and transaction reports to the Department. The Department of Revenue is directed to collect a 10% penalty on certain nonqualified withdrawals and remit those penalty receipts to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution. No appropriations or other budget/funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Revenue):

- The Department of Revenue must adopt rules and create forms to implement the First‑Time Homebuyers Savings Account Act (effective tax years beginning Jan 1, 2026).

- Required forms: account designation, beneficiary designation, an annual report (to accompany Nebraska income tax return) listing account transactions and required documentation, and a transaction report on withdrawals detailing eligible home costs applied and remaining balances.

- Account designation and beneficiary forms must be filed by the usual Nebraska income tax return deadline (section 77‑2768).

- The Department will collect the statutory 10% penalty on nonqualified withdrawals and remit proceeds to the State Treasurer per constitutional distribution rules.

- No explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocation is provided for the Department.
