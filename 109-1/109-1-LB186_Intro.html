Summary

LB186 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §53-123.17 (Nebraska Liquor Control Act) governing entertainment districts. Local governing bodies may designate or revoke districts and must file actions with the commission. The Nebraska Liquor Control Commission processes entertainment-district license applications, notifies the local clerk and provides an application copy. Eligible holders (retail, craft brewery, microdistillery, or manufacturer's licenses) may obtain annual entertainment-district licenses issued/renewed on the same schedule as underlying licenses. The bill imposes a $300 additional annual entertainment-district license fee payable to the local governing body’s clerk and explicitly authorizes local governing bodies to impose an occupation tax on entertainment-district businesses under section 53-132. Local governing bodies may cancel licenses for cause; canceled licensees may appeal to the commission under section 53-134. An emergency clause is included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions of where holders of an "entertainment district" license may sell: it distinguishes cities by class. For primary or metropolitan-class cities, five specified area criteria apply (items I–V). For first- and second-class cities, villages, or unincorporated county areas, permitted sale areas are (i) within an entertainment district designated by the local governing body and (ii) areas shared by authorized licensees holding entertainment district licenses. No state agencies or departments besides local governing bodies are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
