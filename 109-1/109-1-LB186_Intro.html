Summary

Bill LB186 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §53-123.17 (Nebraska Liquor Control Act). It assigns operational duties to local governing bodies and the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission: local governments designate or revoke entertainment districts, file those actions with the Commission, may regulate districts by ordinance, impose an occupation tax, and cancel licenses within their liquor-jurisdiction; the Commission must be notified of entertainment-district license applications, receive a copy, and process and adjudicate appeals per existing provisions. Financial provisions: an additional annual entertainment-district license fee of $300 is payable to the clerk of the local governing body, and local jurisdictions may impose an occupation tax on licensees. The bill contains an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions of areas where an entertainment-district license holder may sell or display (distinguishing city classes). For cities of the primary or metropolitan class it enumerates specific area criteria; for cities of the first/second class, villages, and unincorporated county areas it covers: (1) areas within an entertainment district designated by the local governing body and (2) areas shared by authorized licensees holding entertainment-district licenses. No state agencies or departments are named and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
