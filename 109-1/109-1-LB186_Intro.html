Summary

LB186 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §53‑123.17 governing “entertainment districts.” Key government actors: local governing bodies, the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (the commission), and local clerks. Changes/agency actions:

- Local governing bodies may designate or revoke entertainment districts, regulate them by ordinance, impose an occupation tax on entertainment‑district licensees, and receive a $300 additional annual entertainment‑district license fee payable to the local clerk.

- The commission must accept/process applications on its forms, notify the local clerk and provide a copy of the application, and adjudicate appeals of local cancellations under existing statutory procedures.

- Clarifies commons‑area requirements (e.g., closure to vehicular traffic in larger cities) and license issuance/renewal follows underlying retail/craft/manufacturer licenses.

The bill includes an emergency declaration. No statewide appropriation is specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions of areas where an entertainment district license display may be sold. For cities of the primary or metropolitan class it creates several specified area categories (items I–V). For cities of the first class, second class, villages, and unincorporated county areas it allows sales: (I) within an entertainment district designated by the local governing body, and (II) in areas shared by authorized licensees holding entertainment district licenses. Only a "local governing body" is mentioned; no state agencies or departments are added and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
