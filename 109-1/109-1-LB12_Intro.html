Summary

LB12 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §25-228 to remove the statute of limitations for civil actions arising from child sexual assault (offenses under §§28-319.01 and 28-320.01). No time limit will apply to actions against: (1) the individual perpetrator for violations occurring on or after August 24, 2017 (or earlier if not previously time-barred); and (2) other persons or entities for violations occurring on or after the bill’s effective date (or earlier if not previously time-barred). The bill preserves exceptions under the State Tort Claims Act and the Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act and clarifies that criminal prosecution is not required to pursue a civil claim. No funding or appropriations are provided. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision that, except as otherwise provided by the State Tort Claims Act and the Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act, eliminates any statute-of‑limitations for actions "against The or Any" where the violation occurred on or after this act’s effective date, or earlier if the claim was not already time‑barred. No specific government agencies or departments are named beyond the referenced tort‑claims statutes, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes included.
