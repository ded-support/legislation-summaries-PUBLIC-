Summary

LB12 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §25-228 to remove time limitations on civil actions arising from violations of the child sexual assault statutes (sections 28-319.01 and 28-320.01). Civil suits against the individual perpetrator are unlimited if the offense occurred on or after August 24, 2017 (or earlier if not previously time-barred). Civil suits against any other person or entity (not the direct perpetrator) are unlimited for offenses occurring on or after the bill’s effective date (or earlier if not previously time-barred). The State Tort Claims Act and the Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act remain exceptions. Criminal prosecution is not required to pursue a civil action. No agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations are named or modified. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision removing any statute-of-limitations for suits against entities covered by the State Tort Claims Act or the Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act, except where those Acts themselves provide otherwise. The no-time-limit rule applies to violations occurring on or after the bill’s effective date and to earlier violations that were not already time-barred. No agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are referenced.
