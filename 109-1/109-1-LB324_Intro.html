Summary

LB324 amends the Business Improvement District Act (sections 19-4027, 19-4029.01, and 19-4029.04). Key agency actions:

- City councils retain authority to hold hearings, hear protests and rule on written protests.

- Proceedings to create or modify a BID terminate if, after a mailed self‑addressed postcard vote, less than 50% of returned postcards favor creation/modification (applies separately to special assessments and to occupation taxes).

- Counties (county treasurer tax rolls) must be used to mail notices and postcards to record property owners; notices and postcards also mailed to users of space when an occupation tax is proposed.

- Cities must accept neighborhood association registrations (area, contact, service preference) and provide notice per the association’s requested method.

No appropriations or changes to funding allocations are included. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations. The added language clarifies voting procedures for creating or modifying business improvement districts: postcards for voting (including a self‑addressed postcard when a special assessment is proposed) may be returned by record owners or users of space as authorized by the referenced statutory subdivisions (sections 19-4027 and 19-4029.04). It also confirms that if less than 50% of returned postcards from record owners or users favor the proposal, the district (or modification) is not approved.
