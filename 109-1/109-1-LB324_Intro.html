Summary

LB324 amends the Business Improvement District Act's protest and notice procedures. Key government actors: city councils conduct hearings, rule on written protests, and may continue hearings; cities must publish hearings, mail notices (with self‑addressed voting postcards when a special assessment or occupation tax is proposed), and administer neighborhood‑association registration rules; county treasurers’ tax rolls identify taxable property owners; registered neighborhood associations receive notice in their requested manner. Proceedings to create or modify a district terminate if, after postcard voting, fewer than 50% of returned postcards from affected record owners (for special assessments) or users of space (for occupation taxes) vote in favor. The bill repeals the original cited sections. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language clarifies postcard voting procedures for creation or modification of business improvement districts: postcards must be mailed under §19‑4029.01 to record owners and to users of space; a modified or new district is defeated if fewer than 50% of returning record‑owners (and a corresponding proportion of returning users of space) vote in favor. It also expressly allows self‑addressed postcards and references methods for returning postcards under §§19‑4027 and 19‑4029.04. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
