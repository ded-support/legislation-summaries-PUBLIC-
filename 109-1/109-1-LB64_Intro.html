Summary

LB64 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §44-3614 to revise eligibility and premium rules for Medicare supplement policies for individuals under 65 who qualify by reason of disability. Premium differentials by age are permitted if actuarially sound, not excessive, inadequate, or unfairly discriminatory; premiums for a disabled individual under 65 may not exceed 150% of the premium for a similarly situated 65-year-old. The bill also provides that such disabled, under-65 individuals become subject to the same open enrollment rules applicable to newly eligible 65-year-olds beginning the first day of the month they turn 65. No state agencies, departments, or budgetary appropriations are named or changed. The original section is repealed.
