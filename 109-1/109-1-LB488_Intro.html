Summary

LB488 amends probation and juvenile custody statutes. Key agency actions:

- Office of Probation Administration/probation administrator: authorized to adopt rules, promulgate a statewide graduated-response matrix for juveniles, and provide implementation and training to probation officers.

- Probation officers and chief probation officers: may impose administrative/custodial or graduated-response sanctions; may request peace officers to assume custody of probationers or juveniles when risk of flight or danger exists.

- Peace officers: required to arrest and assume custody of probationers or take juveniles into temporary custody upon a probation officer’s request.

- County attorneys: must be notified after detention and decide promptly whether to authorize release or file motions to revoke probation; sentencing/adjudicating courts receive reports and revocation filings.

- Detention procedures: detained individuals held in jail/detention facilities; juvenile hearings required within 24 hours.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for law enforcement: when requested by a probation officer, a peace officer (law enforcement) must arrest and assume custody of the probationer, and must take a juvenile into temporary custody if a probation officer requests assistance under subsection (6) of §43-286.01.
