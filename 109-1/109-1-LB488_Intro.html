Summary

LB488 amends criminal and juvenile probation procedures and assigns responsibilities to key agencies:

- Office of Probation Administration: authorized to create a statewide graduated‑response matrix, provide implementation and ongoing training to probation officers, and promulgate rules and regulations (including ensuring prompt court review of custodial sanctions).

- Probation officers/Chief probation officers: may impose administrative or custodial sanctions, arrest probationers without a warrant when flight risk or danger exists, and take juveniles into temporary custody in similar circumstances.

- Peace officers: obligated to assist and assume custody of probationers or juveniles when called by a probation officer.

- County attorneys and sentencing/juvenile courts: must be promptly notified via written reports after arrests or detentions and decide whether to file motions to revoke probation or authorize release.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a duty requiring peace officers to arrest and assume custody of a probationer when requested by a probation officer. It also requires peace officers to take juveniles into temporary custody upon a probation officer's request for assistance, referencing subsection (6) of §43‑286.01. No state agencies beyond "peace officer" and "probation officer" are newly created or mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
