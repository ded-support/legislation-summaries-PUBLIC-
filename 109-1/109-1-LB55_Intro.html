Summary

The bill states legislative intent to appropriate $1,500,000 for FY2025‑26 from the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for the Medical Assistance program. Funds are to reimburse non‑hospital mental health providers serving Medicaid‑eligible participants who are also Medicare‑eligible when the Medicare rate for behavioral health services is lower than the then‑current Medicaid rate. The bill also expresses intent to sustain Medicaid rates for such mental health providers under Program 348 (Medical Assistance).

DHHS: Legislative intent to appropriate $1,500,000 for FY2025‑26 from the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (Program 348, Medical Assistance). Funds are designated to reimburse non‑hospital mental health providers who serve Medicaid‑eligible, Medicare‑eligible (dual‑eligible) participants when Medicare reimbursement is lower than then‑current Medicaid rates for behavioral health services.
