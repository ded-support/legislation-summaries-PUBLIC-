Summary

LB55 states the Legislature’s intent to appropriate $1,500,000 for FY2025‑26 from the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for the Medical Assistance program. The funds are intended to reimburse non‑hospital mental health providers who serve Medicaid‑eligible persons also eligible for Medicare when the Medicare rate for behavioral health services is lower than the Medicaid rate. The bill also expresses intent to sustain Medicaid rates for such mental health providers under Program 348, Medical Assistance.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill directs a FY2025–26 appropriation of $1,500,000 from the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) — Medical Assistance — to reimburse non‑hospital mental health providers who serve dual‑eligible (Medicaid/Medicare) participants when Medicare pays a lower rate than prevailing Medicaid behavioral‑health rates. It also states legislative intent to sustain Medicaid rates for those mental‑health providers under Program 348, Medical Assistance.
