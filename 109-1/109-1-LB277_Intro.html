Summary

LB277 amends procedures for grand juries when a death occurs during apprehension or while in custody. It requires district courts to call a grand jury upon certification by the county coroner/coroner’s physician. The court must appoint a special prosecutor (minimum five years’ criminal/felony litigation experience) who will select a team of three peace officers trained in homicide investigation from outside the jurisdiction. Local law enforcement must secure the scene, preserve evidence, investigate, and transfer assembled materials to the special prosecutor. A grand jury must be impaneled within 30 days unless extended. Any involved officer or detention personnel may not return to duty until grand jury proceedings conclude. If a licensed physician certifies a natural death and the county attorney (or staff) presents that finding, a special prosecutor need not be appointed. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirement that the court appoint a special prosecutor with at least five years’ experience in criminal litigation, including felony cases (the text contemplates use of prosecutors from outside the local jurisdiction). It requires that any law enforcement officer or detention personnel involved in a death not return to duty until the grand jury finishes its investigation. Agencies implicated: the courts (appointing authority), prosecutor’s offices, law enforcement agencies, and detention/corrections authorities. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
