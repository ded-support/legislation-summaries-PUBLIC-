Summary

LB277 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29-1401 to require district courts to convene a grand jury when the county coroner or coroner’s physician certifies a death occurred while a person was being apprehended by, or in the custody of, law enforcement or detention personnel. The court must appoint a special prosecutor with at least five years’ felony/criminal litigation experience who will select a three-officer homicide-investigation team from outside the jurisdiction to examine evidence and report to the special prosecutor. Local law enforcement must secure the scene and investigate until evidence is transferred. A grand jury must be impaneled within 30 days unless extended for compelling reasons. If a licensed physician certifies natural causes and the county attorney presents that finding to a grand jury, a special prosecutor need not be appointed. Officers involved may not return to duty until the grand jury completes its investigation. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Adds a requirement that the court appoint a special prosecutor with at least five years’ experience in criminal litigation, including felony work.
