Summary

LB319 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to maximize Nebraska participation in the federal Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), seek federal funding, minimize General Fund use, hire necessary staff, and submit an annual report (by Dec. 1) to the Legislature detailing options, actions, caseload, costs, and expenditures. DHHS must develop a USDA Food and Nutrition Service–approved state outreach plan, may accept gifts/grants/donations, partner with counties/nonprofits, submit the plan to FNS/USDA for approval and request federal matching funds, and may be exempt from implementation if private or federal funds are insufficient. DHHS shall create a TANF‑funded policy to establish categorical SNAP eligibility (including a temporary increase of gross income eligibility to 165% FPG through 10/1/2025), eliminate most asset limits (liquid assets capped at $25,000), and annually evaluate administrative costs and federal funding availability. The Department of Labor may administer a SNAP Next Step employment program (participants encouraged 131–165% FPG); the Legislature intends no General Funds be used by Labor for those processes.
