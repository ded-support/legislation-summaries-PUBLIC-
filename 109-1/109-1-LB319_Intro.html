Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): must pursue all federal SNAP options, maximize federal funding and minimize use of General Funds, employ staff to evaluate options, and submit an annual report (by Dec. 1) to the Health and Human Services Committee detailing options, actions, caseload, costs and expenditures. DHHS must develop a SNAP outreach plan meeting Food and Nutrition Service (USDA) criteria, may accept gifts/grants/donations, partner with counties/nonprofits, seek federal matching funds, and is exempt from implementing the plan if private or federal funds are insufficient to cover implementation or increased caseload. DHHS shall create a TANF‑funded categorical‑eligibility policy (including temporary gross‑income expansion to 165% FPG) and annually report (by Dec. 15) on costs, recommendations, and federal fund availability.

- Department of Labor: may administer the SNAP Next Step employment program in partnership with DHHS; recipients 131–165% FPG were to be encouraged to participate (through 9/30/2023). The Legislature intends that the Department of Labor use no General Funds for those processes.

- United States Department of Agriculture (Food and Nutrition Service): DHHS must submit the state outreach plan to FNS for approval and request available federal matching funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) eligibility restriction: anyone convicted of a felony for possession, use, or distribution of a controlled substance (per 21 U.S.C. 862 as of Jan 1, 2025) is eligible for SNAP only if they (1) have completed their sentence or (2) are currently serving parole, probation, or post‑release supervision.

Impacts by agency:

- Department of Agriculture (USDA/FNS): Primary implementer — must apply and verify the new eligibility standard; may incur administrative verification workload but no explicit funding or appropriation changes are included.

- Department of Labor: No direct role or funding changes specified.

- Department of Health and Human Services: No funding or appropriation changes specified; no direct programmatic role mandated by this text.
