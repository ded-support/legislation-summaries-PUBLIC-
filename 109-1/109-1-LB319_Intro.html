Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): required to apply for and use SNAP options to maximize federal funding and minimize General Fund use; employ staff and submit an annual report (to the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee) including options considered, actions taken, number served, and all costs/expenditures. DHHS must develop a state outreach plan meeting USDA Food and Nutrition Service criteria, may accept gifts/grants/donations, partner with counties/nonprofits, request federal matching funds, and is exempt from implementing the plan if private/federal funds are insufficient to cover implementation and increased caseload costs. DHHS shall create a TANF-funded categorical eligibility policy (gross income limit increased to 165% until Oct 1, 2025) and annually report evaluations, administrative cost impacts, and federal funding availability.

- Department of Labor: may administer the SNAP Next Step Program; participation encouraged for certain households (131–165% of poverty) subject to available federal funds, and the Legislature intends no use of General Funds by the Department of Labor for those processes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (impact by agency):

- Department of Agriculture (USDA): Adds an eligibility restriction to SNAP under 21 U.S.C. 862 (as of Jan 1, 2025): persons convicted of a felony involving possession, use, or distribution of a controlled substance may receive SNAP only if they (a) have completed their sentence or (b) are serving parole, probation, or post‑release supervision. USDA would need to update eligibility rules, verification procedures, caseworker guidance, and IT/recertification processes to implement this limitation.

- Department of Labor (DOL): No direct funding or appropriation changes; potential operational impact limited to coordination with reentry/employment programs for affected individuals.

- Department of Health and Human Services (HHS): No funding changes; role limited to potential program coordination or referrals for supportive services.
