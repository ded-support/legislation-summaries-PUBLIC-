Summary

LB318 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68‑911 and directs the Department of Health and Human Services to take multiple actions and seek federal approvals, with specific deadlines:

- Submit state plan amendments/waivers for community-based secure residential/subacute behavioral health services (by 7/1/2009), school‑district direct‑reimbursable services (by 10/1/2014), extended postpartum coverage (seek approval by 10/1/2023), and coverage for incarcerated youth under Medicaid/CHIP (submit by 9/30/2025).

- Provide coverage for continuous glucose monitors (by 1/1/2023; expanded eligibility effective 8/1/2024). Legislature intends no more than $600,000 annually be appropriated from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for that expansion.

- By 10/1/2025, submit waiver/SPAs to designate two medical respite facilities and report annually; those services — and translation/interpretation services and certain program components — are funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

- Provide electric personal‑use breast pumps and at least ten lactation consultations (by 1/1/2025); appropriation for consultations intended to reflect a 145% rate increase.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services, by September 30, 2025, to submit a state plan amendment to CMS to ensure Medicaid and CHIP coverage for youth while incarcerated pending disposition of charges: Medicaid for youth under 21, CHIP for youth under 19, and former foster-care youth up to age 26. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are specified.
