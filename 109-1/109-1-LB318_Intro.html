Summary

LB318 amends section 68-911 and directs the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) to take or seek multiple Medicaid/CHIP actions, including:

- Submit a state plan amendment by Sept. 30, 2025 to CMS to provide coverage for incarcerated youth (Medicaid: <21; CHIP: <19; former foster youth ≤26) while detained pending disposition.

- Submit/seek prior state plan amendments/waivers for community-based secure residential/subacute behavioral health services, school-based direct reimbursable services, extended postpartum coverage (to ≥6 months), and coverage for medical respite facilities (two facilities designated via waiver/state plan by Oct. 1, 2025).

- Provide coverage for continuous glucose monitors (with Aug. 1, 2024 expansion); electric personal-use breast pumps and 10 lactation consultations by Jan. 1, 2025; and translation/interpretation services by Jan. 1, 2024.

Funding/appropriations: certain services (continuous glucose monitors, medical respite, translation/interpretation) are funded by the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund; lactation consultation appropriation is intended to reflect a 145% rate increase (specified amount).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to submit a state plan amendment to CMS by September 30, 2025, to ensure Medicaid covers youth under age 21, the Children's Health Insurance Program covers youth under age 19, and former foster care youth up to age 26, while those individuals are incarcerated in a public institution pending disposition of charges. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the added text.
