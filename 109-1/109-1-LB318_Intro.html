Summary

The bill amends duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (the department). Key DHHS mandates and deadlines include: submit a state plan amendment to CMS by September 30, 2025, to cover incarcerated youth (Medicaid, CHIP, and former foster-care youth as specified); earlier and existing directives to seek/submit state plan amendments or waivers for community-based behavioral health, school-based services, continuous glucose monitor coverage, postpartum extension, medical respite facilities (by October 1, 2025), breast pumps and lactation consultations (by January 1, 2025), and translation/interpretation services. Funding provisions: up to $600,000 annually from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for glucose-monitor implementation (excess also from that fund); medical respite and translation services funded by that fund; lactation consultation appropriations intended to be a 145% rate increase over current departmental rates.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to submit a State Plan Amendment to CMS by September 30, 2025 to secure coverage while detained pending disposition: Medicaid for youth under 21, CHIP for youth under 19, and former foster-care youth up to age 26. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
