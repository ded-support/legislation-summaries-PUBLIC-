Summary

LB388 revises procedures and responsibilities for judicial nominating commissions, affecting the Governor, the Nebraska Supreme Court (including the Clerk of the Supreme Court and designated Supreme Court member/chair), the Nebraska State Bar Association (Executive Council and Executive Director), the Secretary of State, and the Attorney General. Key agency-related changes:

- Clerk of the Supreme Court: may solicit lawyer nominations electronically via the Court’s attorney-licensure portal, mail/electronically deliver ballots, and convene the counting board (Clerk, Secretary of State, Attorney General or alternates).

- Nebraska State Bar Association Executive Council/Director: nominated candidates if insufficient party representation; designates alternate lawyer members and fills alternate vacancies upon Clerk notification.

- Governor: appoints judicial and alternate citizen members and fills citizen vacancies when notified.

- Supreme Court member rules, residency/party-registration requirements, vacancy and alternate selection processes, hearing timelines, and automatic termination on residency or party-registration changes are clarified.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added text (agencies and budget impacts):

- Nebraska Supreme Court: nominations may be submitted electronically via the Supreme Court’s attorney licensure portal by Oct 1 of even-numbered years; nominations must be completed by Nov 1. The Clerk of the Supreme Court is empowered to use certified prior election results to advance alternate lawyer members to fill vacancies when appropriate.

- Judicial Nominating Commission: vacancy rules clarified — vacancies from resignation are to be filled promptly by an alternate lawyer member of the same political party, or by a nonpartisan alternate if none exist; temporary vacancies caused by disqualification/inability are filled but end after candidate names are sent to the Governor. Members or alternates seeking a judgeship must resign before notice publication.

- Nebraska State Bar Association (Executive Council): upon notification by the Clerk, the Executive Council must designate sufficient lawyers to fill unexpired-term vacancies who meet statutory requirements.

- Budget/funding: no changes to appropriations or funding are included.
