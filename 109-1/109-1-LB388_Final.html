Summary

LB388 amends procedures for judicial nominating commissions and assigns roles to state officials and the Nebraska State Bar Association. Key agency actions and changes:

- Governor: appoints Supreme Court judge members, citizen members, and fills citizen-member vacancies.

- Nebraska Supreme Court / Clerk of the Supreme Court: Clerk manages electronic or mailed lawyer-member nominations and ballots (via the Court’s attorney licensure portal), publishes hearing notices, delivers certified results, and assures ballot secrecy. Supreme Court judge designated to each commission chairs meetings (no vote).

- Executive Council of the Nebraska State Bar Association: nominates or appoints alternate lawyer members, fills alternate-lawyer vacancies upon Clerk notification, and supplies candidates when electronic nominations are insufficient.

- Secretary of State and Attorney General: serve on the ballot-counting board with the Clerk (or their alternates).

Other provisions: four-year terms, alternates fill vacancies in order, eight voting members required at votes, hearing timelines (first hearing within 60 days; nominations to Governor within 90 or 120 days), automatic termination for residency or party-registration changes. No appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural rules and agency roles for judicial nominating commissions. Key agencies: Nebraska Supreme Court (attorney licensure portal — electronic receipt of applications by Oct. 1 of even-numbered years), Clerk of the Supreme Court (authorized to fill vacancy by moving up alternates using certified prior election results and to notify the Executive Council), the Judicial Nominating Commission, the Executive Council of the Nebraska State Bar Association (to designate lawyers to fill vacancies when notified), the Nebraska State Bar Association, and the Governor (receives candidate names). New rules: electronic filing deadline, priority order for filling vacancies (alternate of same party → nonpartisan alternate → Clerk-selected alternate by prior vote totals), requirement that lawyer commissioners resign before seeking a judgeship, and residency-change release. No funding or appropriation changes.
