Summary

The bill amends duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) regarding SNAP. DHHS must apply for and use all federal SNAP options, seek to maximize federal funding and minimize General Fund use, employ necessary staff, and submit an annual report to the Legislature on options, actions, caseload, and costs. DHHS must develop a state outreach plan that meets Food and Nutrition Service (U.S. Department of Agriculture) criteria, may accept gifts/grants/donations, partner with counties/nonprofits, submit the plan to FNS and request federal matching funds, and may be exempt from implementation if private/federal funds are insufficient. DHHS shall create a TANF-funded categorical eligibility program (allowing gross-income up to 165% of OMB poverty guidelines as permitted) and may require employment/training participation per federal law. Original section repealed.

- Department of Health and Human Services (state "department"): Prohibits the department from seeking, applying for, accepting, or renewing SNAP work‑requirement waivers under 7 U.S.C. 2015(o) (as of Jan 1, 2025), except it may use the federal statutory option to grant exemptions under 7 U.S.C. 2015(o)(6).
