Summary

LB192 amends §68-1017.02 relating to the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program. It directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to pursue all federal SNAP options to maximize federal funding and minimize use of state General Funds, employ staff to evaluate options, and submit the required legislative report. DHHS must develop a state outreach plan meeting Food and Nutrition Service (USDA) criteria, may apply for and accept gifts, grants, and donations, partner with counties/nonprofits, submit the plan to USDA FNS for approval, and request available federal matching funds; implementation is excused if private or federal funds are insufficient to cover costs. The bill authorizes a TANF-funded program/policy to establish categorical eligibility (including an option to raise gross income eligibility to 165% of OMB poverty guidelines where allowed) and repeals the original section.
