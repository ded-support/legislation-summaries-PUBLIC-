Summary

- The Job Training Cash Fund is placed under the direction of the Department of Economic Development; the department must establish subaccounts (including one for funds transferred from the Cash Reserve Fund) and give preference to job‑training activities in enterprise or opportunity zones. The department must also establish a subaccount for training grants for small businesses, allocate prior‑year investment earnings to that subaccount, and administer uses specified for job training.

- The Intern Nebraska Cash Fund is created; the Department of Economic Development (or its contractors) may use up to 5% of any appropriation from that fund for administrative services.

- The State Treasurer is directed to transfer unobligated balances remaining in the Intern Nebraska Cash Fund to the General Fund by June 30, 2026, as directed by the budget administrator. Sections implementing the internship grant program are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language requires the State Treasurer to transfer any unobligated balances in the Intern Nebraska Cash Fund to the General Fund by June 30, 2026, on dates/amounts set by the budget administrator of DAS. For the Department of Economic Development, this effectively sweeps remaining unobligated Intern Nebraska funds out of the program and into the General Fund. No new appropriations or replacement funding for DED are provided in this text; it only directs reversion of unobligated cash balances.
