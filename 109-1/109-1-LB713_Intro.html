Summary

LB713 amends the School Employees Retirement Act (sections 79-902 and 79-930). It clarifies definitions and actuarial methods (different mortality tables and interest-rate determination for employees hired before vs. on/after July 1, 2017) and updates the index used for lump-sum estate settlements. Agency references include the Public Employees Retirement Board (retirement board) and the Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS) — including NPERS’ director and board for 180‑day return-to-work determinations and appeals. The bill also references the Commissioner and State Department of Education, Board of Trustees of Nebraska State Colleges, Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska, and community college boards of governors. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
