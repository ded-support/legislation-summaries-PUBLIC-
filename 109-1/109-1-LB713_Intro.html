Summary

LB713 amends the School Employees Retirement Act (sections 79‑902 and 79‑930) to eliminate the definition of "prior service" and harmonize related statutory definitions and return‑to‑work provisions. The bill clarifies when a member is deemed to have terminated employment, when contributions and service credit resume, and limits interim work (volunteer/substitute) following separation. It identifies the Public Employees Retirement Board as the retirement board and assigns the Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS) director authority to make 180‑day separation determinations, with appeals to the Public Employees Retirement Board. The bill references the State Department of Education/Commissioner of Education, Nebraska State Colleges Board of Trustees, University of Nebraska Board of Regents, community college boards, and state/local employers. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
