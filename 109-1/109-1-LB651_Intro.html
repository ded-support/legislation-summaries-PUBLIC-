Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to operate the medical cannabis patient/caregiver registry and directs the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission to collaborate with the Department of Agriculture and the Department of Revenue (and DHHS) on cultivation, processing, testing, distribution, transportation, and sale. It creates a Medical Cannabis Control Fund (fees, gifts, grants) to finance commission operations; application fees are split 50% to that Fund and 50% to local treasurers (credited to local school funds). Bonding premiums for commissioners, the executive director, and commission employees are paid from the State General Fund. Department of Correctional Services employees may not hold licenses; persons sentenced to that department under the amended controlled‑substances provisions must attend drug‑abuse treatment. DHHS, the Department of Correctional Services, the Military Department, and the Department of Agriculture have specified vehicle‑lettering exemptions. Sales/use tax on aircraft is credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund; motor‑vehicle sales/lease tax allocations flow to the Highway Trust Fund (with amounts above 5% to the Highway Allocation Fund) and a designated 0.25% sales tax is split 85%/15% between those funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: defined as "the Department" to run the patient/caregiver registry, issue/renew registry cards, adopt rules, coordinate disposal/temporary transport authorizations, and oversee related enforcement.

- Department of Agriculture: required to collaborate with the Medical Cannabis Commission on cultivation, processing, testing, pesticide and safety standards.

- Department of Revenue: directed to credit sales/use tax proceeds from dispensary sales to the new Medical Cannabis Control Fund; licensing/application fees remitted via State Treasurer (50% to the Control Fund, 50% to local treasurers/school funds).

- Department of Correctional Services: jails/correctional facilities prohibited as consumption/possession locations; employees of jails or the Department are ineligible for licenses.

- Department of Transportation: subject to new transport rules (insurance, vehicle/timeframe requirements); prohibitions on consuming/inhaling/transporting cannabis in vehicles and while working on transportation property.

- Military Department: not referenced; no new duties or funding.

- Funding: Medical Cannabis Control Fund created for commission operations; bonding/insurance premiums for commissioners and staff paid from the General Fund.
