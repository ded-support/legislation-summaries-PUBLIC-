Summary

The bill assigns DHHS responsibility for operating the medical cannabis patient registry (enrollments, issuing/renewing/denying/verifying cards), adopting program rules, coordinating authorized transport/disposal, and overall program oversight. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must collaborate with the Departments of Agriculture and Revenue and DHHS on cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transport, testing, and sale. Department of Correctional Services (DCS) employees and jail staff are ineligible for medical cannabis licenses; persons sentenced to DCS for controlled‑substances violations must attend drug‑abuse treatment and counseling while incarcerated. Funding changes: the State (General Fund) pays bonding/insurance premiums for commissioners and commission staff; the Medical Cannabis Control Fund is created to receive fees/gifts/grants; application fees split 50% to that Fund and 50% remitted to local treasurers for school funds. Tax allocation: all sales/use tax proceeds on aircraft sales/leases (effective 7/1/2023) are credited to the Dept. of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. Vehicle‑lettering exemptions are added for DHHS, DCS, the Military Department, and (for investigative purposes) the Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: defined as the "Department." It will operate the patient/caregiver registry, issue/renew registry cards, set rules (including for health care/home health entities and transport/administration limits), coordinate disposal/delivery of patient-held cannabis and provide temporary transport authorizations.

- Department of Correctional Services: staff/employees of a jail or the Department are barred from holding licenses; possession/use of medical cannabis is prohibited in jails, correctional facilities, and youth rehabilitation centers.

- Department of Agriculture: required to collaborate with the Medical Cannabis Commission on implementation; cultivation rules include pesticide, testing, destruction, recordkeeping, and waste-disposal requirements that implicate agricultural oversight.

- Department of Revenue: to collaborate with the Commission. Sales/use tax proceeds on dispensary sales are credited to the Medical Cannabis Control Fund.

- Funding/appropriations: Medical Cannabis Control Fund created (fees, gifts, grants; 50% of application fees credited there); 50% of application fees go to local school funds; commission/employees’ bonding premiums paid from the State General Fund.

- Department of Transportation and Military Department: no substantive duties, funding, or appropriation changes specified.
