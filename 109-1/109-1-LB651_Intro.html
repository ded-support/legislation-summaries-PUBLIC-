Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) primary responsibility for the medical cannabis patient registry, issuance/renewal/denial of registry cards, coordination of patient cannabis disposal/transport authorizations, and rulemaking under the Medical Cannabis Patient Protection Act. The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must collaborate with DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Department of Revenue on implementation and oversight. Jail and Department of Correctional Services (DCS) employees are barred from holding medical cannabis licenses; persons convicted under the amended controlled substances provision who are sentenced to DCS must attend drug treatment and counseling. Fiscal changes create a Medical Cannabis Control Fund (fees/gifts/grants) to fund commission operations, fund commissioners’ bond/insurance from the State General Fund, and split initial application fees 50/50 between that fund and local school funds. Sales/use tax proceeds on aircraft (since July 1, 2023) are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. Vehicles used by DHHS, DCS (specified transport/apprehension duties), the Military Department, and the Department of Agriculture (special investigations) are exempted from State lettering requirements.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Agriculture: required to "work collaboratively" with the Medical Cannabis Commission on implementation (cultivation, processing, testing, transport oversight). No direct appropriations or new funding specified.

- Department of Correctional Services: employees of jails or the Department are disqualified from holding cannabis licenses; cannabis use/possession remains prohibited in jails, juvenile correctional facilities, and youth rehab centers.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): designated as the "Department" to run the patient/caregiver registry, issue/renew registry cards, coordinate disposal and temporary transport authorizations, adopt rules for patient protections, and collaborate with the Commission. No explicit new funding appropriations for DHHS.

- Department of Revenue: must cooperate with the Commission. Financial changes: creation of the Medical Cannabis Control Fund (to be used for administration/enforcement); 50% of license application fees credit the Fund; proceeds of sales/use tax on dispensary sales to qualified patients are credited to the Medical Cannabis Control Fund.

- Department of Transportation: not explicitly named, but the bill empowers the Commission to set transport rules (insurance, vehicle standards, timeframes) and reaffirms vehicle/DUI-related prohibitions. No funding changes.

- Military Department: not referenced; no changes.
