Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to operate the medical cannabis registry (30‑day application decisions; issuance/renewal of registry cards; disposal/transport authorizations; rulemaking). The Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission must collaborate with DHHS, the Department of Agriculture and the Department of Revenue on regulation of cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transportation, testing and sale. Department of Correctional Services (DCS) employees (and jail employees and peace officers) are barred from holding medical cannabis licenses; persons convicted under amended controlled‑substances provisions and sentenced to DCS must attend treatment and counseling. Fiscal changes: creation of a Medical Cannabis Control Fund; application fees split 50/50 between that Fund and local treasurers (credited to local school funds); bonding premiums for commissioners/executive staff paid from the State General Fund. Separately, sales/use tax proceeds on aircraft sales/leases (from July 1, 2023) are credited to the DOT Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. DHHS, DCS, Military Department, and Department of Agriculture vehicles receive limited exemptions from state lettering for specified operational uses.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (focus on named agencies):

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Defined as "the Department." DHHS will run the patient/caregiver registry, issue/renew registry cards, process applications (30-day target), coordinate authorized transport/disposal of patient-held cannabis, adopt rules to implement the Patient Protection Act, and collaborate with the Medical Cannabis Commission.

- Department of Agriculture: Explicitly required to work collaboratively with the Commission on cultivation, processing, manufacture, distribution, transportation, testing, and sale; cultivation standards (pesticides, testing, waste) set by the Commission.

- Department of Revenue: Required collaborator; statute directs tax/fee flows. A Medical Cannabis Control Fund is created for commission use. License/application fees: 50% to that fund and 50% to local treasurers (credited to school funds). Proceeds of sales/use taxes on dispensary cannabis sales are credited to the Medical Cannabis Control Fund.

- Department of Correctional Services: Employees of jails or the Department are disqualified from licensure; possession/use is prohibited in jails/correctional facilities/youth centers.

- Department of Transportation & Military Department: no substantive new duties or funding changes directed to them in the added text (transport rules referenced generally, but no agency-specific allocations).

- Funding note: bond/premium costs for commissioners and certain commission employees paid from the General Fund; Medical Cannabis Control Fund authorized to fund commission operations.
