Summary

LB273 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §30-3417 governing powers of attorneys‑in‑fact under powers of attorney for health care. Key government-related provisions: county courts retain final authority to adjudicate incapacity — a principal’s objection prevails over an attorney‑in‑fact’s decision unless the county court determines the principal is incapable (cross‑references §30‑3412). Health care providers are not required to accept an attorney‑in‑fact’s decisions until presented with a signed original or copy of the power of attorney. The bill also defers to a treating physician’s determination when life‑risk to a pregnant principal is asserted (limits decisions affecting an unborn child) and references §30‑3418 for end‑of‑life decision exceptions. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a medical-exception clause tying authorization to the principal’s treating physician: if an act would result in the death of the principal’s unborn child (and the unborn child is likely to die), it applies only when the principal’s treating physician determines the principal’s life is at risk. No government agencies or departments are referenced and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
