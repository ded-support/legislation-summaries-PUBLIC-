Summary

LB273 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §30-3417 to revise the authority of attorneys-in-fact under a power of attorney for health care. Key changes: an attorney-in-fact may not consent to any act that would cause the death of the principal’s unborn child when the principal is pregnant and the child is likely to reach live birth, unless the treating physician determines the principal’s life is at risk; prohibitions remain on decisions to withhold/withdraw life-sustaining treatment or artificially administered nutrition and hydration except as provided in §30-3418. The attorney-in-fact’s authority only arises after a determination of incapacity under §30-3412, and a principal’s objection prevails unless a county court finds incapacity. Health care providers may require a signed original or copy of the POA before accepting decisions. No funding or appropriations are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an exception covering situations that would result in the death of the principal's unborn child — i.e., where the unborn child is likely to die unless the principal's treating physician determines the principal's life is at risk.
