Summary

LB523 establishes the Student Teacher Compensation Act. The State Department of Education must create a stipend program providing $4,000 per student‑teaching semester to eligible student teachers who apply. The department must annually report to the Legislature and Governor on numbers of applicants and recipients and aggregate demographic and educator‑shortage area data (no individual or school identifiers). The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate funds annually to the State Department of Education for stipends; the department may also pursue grants, private donations, or federal funding. The State Board of Education may adopt rules and determine acceptable accredited teacher education programs (accreditation recognized by the U.S. Department of Education). The act is operative July 1, 2025, and contains an emergency clause.
