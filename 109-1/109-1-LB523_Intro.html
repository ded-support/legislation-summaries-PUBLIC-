Summary

LB523 creates the Student Teacher Compensation Act, directing the State Department of Education to administer a stipend program that provides $4,000 per student‑teaching semester to eligible student teachers who apply. The department must report annually to the Legislature on stipend counts, average and total amounts, distribution by district and school type, and aggregated, non‑identifying feedback from participants and host schools. The Legislature states its intent to appropriate annual funds to the State Department of Education for these stipends; the department may also pursue grants, private donations, or federal funds. The State Board of Education is authorized to adopt rules to implement the Act. The bill becomes operative July 1, 2025, and takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
