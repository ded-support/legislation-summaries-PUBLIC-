Summary

LB130 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-4602 to clarify monthly and annual reporting and to revise automatic transfers from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund. The Tax Commissioner must publish monthly actual General Fund net receipts and comparisons to the Nebraska Economic Forecasting Advisory Board forecast and prior-year receipts, and a fiscal‑year summary comparing actual and certified estimated receipts (per §§77-4601 and 77-4603). Within 15 days after year-end the Tax Commissioner computes (a) the surplus (actual minus certified estimate) and (b) 50% of a growth-based formula; if the surplus is positive the greater amount is certified to the Director of Administrative Services. Upon that certification the State Treasurer transfers the certified sum from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund (immediate transfer of the surplus portion; remainder by end of the following fiscal year), reduced as needed to keep the Cash Reserve Fund at or below 16% of budgeted General Fund expenditures (excluding reappropriations/encumbrances). Legislature may authorize exceeding 16%. Emergency clause: effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds automatic year-end calculations and transfers involving the Tax Commissioner, Director of Administrative Services, and State Treasurer. Within 15 days after each fiscal year the Tax Commissioner must compute (i) the excess of actual over estimated General Fund net receipts and (ii) half of a growth-based formula using recent receipt increases. If (i) is positive, the Tax Commissioner certifies the larger of the two amounts to the Director of Administrative Services; upon that director’s certification the State Treasurer transfers the certified sum from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund (the portion equal to (i) immediately; any remainder by the end of the next fiscal year). Transfers are reduced to prevent the Cash Reserve Fund exceeding 16% of budgeted General Fund expenditures unless the Legislature authorizes otherwise.
