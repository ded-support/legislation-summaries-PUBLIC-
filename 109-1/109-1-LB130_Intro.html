Summary

LB130 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-4602 governing General Fund net receipts and transfers to the Cash Reserve Fund. It requires the Tax Commissioner to publish monthly and annual statements comparing actual General Fund net receipts to forecasted and prior-year receipts (forecasts by the Nebraska Economic Forecasting Advisory Board). Within 15 days after each fiscal year the Tax Commissioner must calculate (a) actual minus certified estimated net receipts and (b) 50% of actual receipts times the difference between the most recent annual growth rate and the 20-year average growth (excluding the lowest year). If (a) is positive, the Commissioner certifies the larger amount to the Director of Administrative Services; upon that director’s certification the State Treasurer transfers that amount from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund (immediate transfer of (a) amount, remainder by end of the subsequent fiscal year). Transfers are limited so the Cash Reserve Fund does not exceed 16% of budgeted General Fund expenditures, unless the Legislature authorizes a higher balance. The bill repeals the prior version of the section and takes effect immediately (emergency).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Tax Commissioner, within 15 days after each fiscal year, to calculate: (i) actual General Fund net receipts minus the certified estimate, and (ii) 50% of actual receipts times the amount the most recent annual growth exceeds the 20‑year average growth (excluding the lowest year). If (i) is positive, the Commissioner must certify the larger of the two amounts to the Director of Administrative Services. Upon that certification, the State Treasurer transfers that amount from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund: an amount equal to (i) immediately, with any remainder by the end of the next fiscal year. Transfers are reduced so the Cash Reserve Fund does not exceed 16% of budgeted General Fund expenditures (excluding reappropriations/encumbrances), though the Legislature may authorize a higher balance for capital or emergency needs.
