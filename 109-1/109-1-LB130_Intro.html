Summary

LB130 amends §77-4602 to revise reporting and automatic transfer rules tied to General Fund net receipts. It directs the Tax Commissioner (within 15 days after each month and fiscal year) to publish comparisons using forecasts from the Nebraska Economic Forecasting Advisory Board and, after each fiscal year, to calculate: (i) actual minus estimated General Fund net receipts and (ii) fifty percent of a growth-based formula. If (i) is positive, the Tax Commissioner must certify the larger amount to the Director of Administrative Services. Upon that certification, the State Treasurer transfers the certified sum from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund—an amount equal to (i) immediately and any remainder by the end of the next fiscal year. Transfers are reduced if they would push the Cash Reserve Fund above 16% of budgeted General Fund expenditures; the Legislature may authorize exceeding 16%. The Director of Administrative Services, Tax Commissioner, State Treasurer, and the Forecasting Advisory Board are implicated. The bill takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text assigns the Tax Commissioner responsibility to calculate, within 15 days after each fiscal year, two measures of excess General Fund net receipts: (i) actual minus certified estimated receipts (per §§77-4601, 77-4603) and (ii) 50% of a growth-based formula comparing the most recent annual receipt increase to the 20-year average (excluding the lowest year). If (i) is positive, the Tax Commissioner must certify the larger amount to the Director of Administrative Services, who triggers the State Treasurer to transfer that certified sum from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund. Transfers are split: an amount equal to (i) immediately and any remainder by the end of the following fiscal year, but reduced if the transfer would push the Cash Reserve Fund above 16% of budgeted General Fund expenditures (unless the Legislature authorizes exceeding 16% for specific needs).
