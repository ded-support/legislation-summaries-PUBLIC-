Summary

- The Department of Health and Human Services (department) shall administer the state's social services program and may contract for services.

- The department must participate in the federal child care assistance/subsidy program, implement income-eligibility and transitional-assistance rules, and adopt an annual fixed-rate schedule for child-care reimbursement (effective Oct. 1 each year).

- The department shall create a child-care workforce pilot (Oct. 1, 2025–Sept. 30, 2028), set eligibility parameters, allow 12 months continuous eligibility without cost-share for qualifying employed household members, permit rulemaking (operative July 1, 2025), and permit certain enrollment accommodations.

- The department must submit electronic annual reports (Dec. 1, 2026–2028) detailing county-level monthly enrollments, employment tenure before enrollment, and program expenditures (monthly aid disbursed and average household cost).

- The department shall collaborate with a nonprofit on an independent evaluation of prior income-eligibility changes if privately funded (evaluation due July 1, 2024).

- Funding: Child Care and Development Block Grant funds (and, if insufficient, TANF funds) shall cover costs from the 2021 eligibility changes through Sept. 30, 2026; General Funds may not be used for those costs except administration.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services must create and administer a pilot within the federal Child Care Subsidy program to provide child care assistance to the child care workforce. The pilot runs October 1, 2025–September 30, 2028. Eligibility: households ≤85% of state median income, an applicant/household member listed in the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System, working ≥20 hours/week as (or at) a licensed child care provider, Head Start, or Early Head Start, and meeting federal non‑income requirements. Enrollees remain eligible 12 continuous months with no cost‑share if employment is maintained. DHHS must submit annual electronic reports to the Legislature (Dec 1, 2026–2028) detailing monthly enrollment by county and employment type, tenure before enrollment, and program expenditures (monthly aid disbursed and average household cost). No explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocation provisions are included.
