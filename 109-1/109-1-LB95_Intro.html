Summary

The bill amends section 68-1206 to direct the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to administer state social services, contract for services, and participate in the federal child care assistance/Subsidy programs. DHHS must provide child care assistance under specified income-eligibility rules; create a child care workforce pilot (Oct. 1, 2025–Sept. 30, 2028); promulgate rules; adopt an annual fixed-rate reimbursement schedule for providers; and submit electronic annual pilot reports (Dec. 1, 2026–2028) including enrollment and monthly expenditure data. Funding: Child Care and Development Block Grant (CCDBG) funds shall pay costs from the 2021 income-eligibility changes (July 1, 2021–Sept. 30, 2026), with TANF funds as backup; General Funds may not be used for those costs except for administration.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services must create a pilot program (Oct 1, 2025–Sept 30, 2028) providing child care assistance to the child care workforce under the federal Child Care Subsidy program. Eligibility: household income ≤85% of state median income, an applicant/household member listed in the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System, and working ≥20 hours/week as self-employed or employed at a licensed child care program, Head Start, or Early Head Start; must meet federal non‑income eligibility rules. Enrolled households receive 12 continuous months of assistance without a cost‑share if employment is maintained. DHHS must file annual reports to the Legislature (Dec 1, 2026–2028) detailing enrollment by county/employment type, tenure before enrollment, and monthly program expenditures. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
