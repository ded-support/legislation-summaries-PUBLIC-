Summary

The bill assigns these duties to the Department of Health and Human Services: administer the state's social services program and contract for services; participate in the federal Child Care Subsidy program; create a child-care workforce pilot (Oct. 1, 2025–Sept. 30, 2028); promulgate rules (subsections (2)(b)(v) and (2)(b)(vi) operative July 1, 2025); and electronically submit annual reports (Dec. 1, 2026–Dec. 1, 2028) detailing monthly enrolled children/households by county and employment type, counts of short- and longer-tenured child-care workers, and program expenditures including monthly aid disbursed and average household cost. The Department must collaborate with a private nonprofit for an independent evaluation if private funding is available (evaluation due July 1, 2024). The bill contains no direct appropriations to the Department nor revises its budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services must create a pilot child-care assistance program (under the federal Child Care Subsidy) for the child-care workforce from Oct 1, 2025, through Sept 30, 2028. Eligibility: households at or below 85% of state median income with an applicant listed in the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System who works ≥20 hours/week in licensed child care, Head Start/Early Head Start, or is self-employed at a licensed child care program; must meet federal non‑income rules. Enrollees retain eligibility for 12 continuous months without a cost‑share if employment is maintained. DHHS must submit annual electronic reports to the Legislature (Dec 1, 2026–2028) including monthly enrolled children/households by county and employment type, counts by employment tenure (<2 months vs >2 months), and program expenditures (monthly aid disbursed and average monthly household cost). No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation language is included.
