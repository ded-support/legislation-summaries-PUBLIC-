Summary

The bill amends section 68-1206 and assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) responsibility to administer the state's social services program, to contract for services, and to participate in the federal child care assistance/subsidy program. DHHS must create a child care workforce pilot (Oct. 1, 2025–Sept. 30, 2028), promulgate implementing rules (operative July 1, 2025), and electronically submit annual pilot reports to the Legislature (Dec. 1, 2026–Dec. 1, 2028) including monthly enrollment by county and employment type, employment-duration breakdowns, program expenditures, and average monthly household cost. DHHS shall collaborate with a private nonprofit for an independent evaluation if privately funded. Funding language directs Child Care and Development Block Grant (CCDBG) funds (July 1, 2021–Sept. 30, 2026) to cover related costs, allows TANF funds if CCDBG is insufficient, and prohibits use of General Funds for those costs except administration.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services must create a pilot child care assistance program for the child care workforce under the federal Child Care Subsidy program, effective October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2028. Eligibility: household income ≤85% of state median; an applicant/household member listed in the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System working ≥20 hours/week in a licensed child care program, Head Start, or Early Head Start (including self‑employed); and meeting federal non‑income requirements. Enrollees retain eligibility for 12 continuous months without cost‑share while employed as required. DHHS must file annual electronic reports to the Legislature (Dec 1, 2026–2028) with participation and expenditure metrics. No direct funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are specified.
