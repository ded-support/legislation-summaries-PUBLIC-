Summary

LB547 amends Nebraska law to expand and clarify tax exemptions for disabled and blind veterans. It (1) exempts one motor vehicle used for personal transportation by a disabled or blind veteran whose disability or blindness is recognized by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs and who was discharged or separated with an honorable characterization (application per section 60-3,189), and (2) exempts mobile homes owned and occupied by such veterans if the VA recognizes the condition as service‑connected and the veteran was discharged honorable or general (under honorable conditions). "Disabled veteran" is defined by reference to 5 U.S.C. 2108 (as of Jan 1, 2025). Operative date: January 1, 2026. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included.
