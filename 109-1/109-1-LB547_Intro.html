Summary

LB547 amends Nebraska tax statutes to modify veteran-related exemptions. It aligns the definition of "disabled veteran" with federal law (5 U.S.C. §2108 as of Jan 1, 2025) and requires that a veteran’s disability or blindness be recognized by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs as service‑connected. Changes affect (1) a motor vehicle tax exemption for one vehicle owned/used by a disabled or blind veteran who was discharged or separated with an honorable characterization, and (2) a property (mobile home) tax exemption for disabled or blind veterans discharged with honorable or general (under honorable conditions) characterizations. The bill becomes operative January 1, 2026, repeals the original sections, and contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definitional provision: “veteran” is defined to have the same meaning as in 5 U.S.C. 2108 as that section existed on January 1, 2025. No federal agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
