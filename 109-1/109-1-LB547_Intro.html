Summary

LB547 amends Nebraska tax law to clarify eligibility for motor vehicle and mobile‑home tax exemptions for disabled and blind veterans. It ties "disabled veteran" to the federal definition in 5 U.S.C. §2108 (as of Jan 1, 2025) and requires that the disability or blindness be recognized by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs as service‑connected. The bill retains discharge/ separation characterizations (honorable or general under honorable conditions where specified), repeals the original statutory sections, and becomes operative January 1, 2026. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget reallocations are included.
