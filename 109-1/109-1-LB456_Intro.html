Summary

LB456 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-146.02 to strengthen enforcement tools of the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court. A three-judge panel may, after notice and hearing, request the Director of Insurance to suspend or revoke an insurer’s authorization to write workers’ compensation coverage (Chapter 44), suspend or revoke a risk management pool’s authority to provide group self-insurance under the Intergovernmental Risk Management Act (see §44-4319), or revoke approval of a self-insurer under §48-145. At the administrator’s request the Attorney General may file show-cause motions and participate as a party. The compensation court may examine insurers’, pools’, self-insurers’, and their agents’ workers’ compensation records, subject to work-product and attorney-client privileges, and may adopt implementing rules. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
