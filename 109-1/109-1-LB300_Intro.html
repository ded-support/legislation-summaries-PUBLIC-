Summary

LB300 amends statutes to extend the Superintendent Pay Transparency Act to require superintendent and educational service unit (ESU) administrator compensation to comply with that Act. It (1) defines benefit, compensation (including employer retirement and insurance contributions), beginning-teacher compensation, and salary; (2) caps total compensation paid by a school district or ESU to a superintendent or ESU administrator at five times the district’s/ESU’s beginning-teacher compensation for the same contract year; (3) makes contracts violating the cap invalid, prohibits expenditure of district/ESU funds on such contracts, requires forfeiture and return of excess payments, and mandates filing notice with the Commissioner of Education within 30 days; and (4) requires pre-approval and post-approval public publication of proposed/approved contracts and reasonable estimates of current and future costs. The bill references school boards, school districts, ESUs, the Commissioner of Education, and the School Employees Retirement Acts.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "benefit," "compensation," "compensation for a beginning teacher," and "salary," specifying that benefits include employer retirement contributions (School Employees Retirement Act and Class V), annuities, insurance premiums, severance, and other employer-paid amounts. Requires school districts and educational service units (and their boards) to cap superintendent/administrator total compensation at no more than five times the beginning teacher compensation in the same district/ESU. Contracts exceeding the cap are invalid, such payments may not be expended, and excess compensation must be forfeited and returned; a notice (including the maximum allowed compensation) must be filed with the Commissioner of Education within 30 days. No new appropriations or budget allocations are created.
