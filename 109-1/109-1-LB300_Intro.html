Summary

LB300 amends state statutes to expand and operationalize the Superintendent Pay Transparency Act. It requires boards of education (Class I–V school districts) and boards of educational service units (ESUs) to set superintendent and ESU administrator compensation in compliance with the Act. Key provisions: defines benefit, compensation, salary, and beginning-teacher compensation; caps superintendent/administrator total compensation at five times the district’s or ESU’s beginning-teacher compensation; treats cross-payments between districts and ESUs as compensation of the paying entity; invalidates contracts exceeding the cap and requires forfeiture and return of excess pay and a notice to the Commissioner of Education within 30 days. Boards must publish proposed contracts/amendments, cost estimates, and the statutory maximum at least three days before consideration and post approved contracts and cost estimates within two days (website publication allowed). No appropriations or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and limits under the Superintendent Pay Transparency Act affecting school districts, educational service units (ESUs), school boards, and superintendents/administrators. Key points:

- Defines "benefit," "compensation," "salary," and "compensation for a beginning teacher," and explicitly includes employer retirement and insurance contributions (School Employees Retirement Act; Class V School Employees Retirement Act) and tax-deferred plans (IRC §§125, 403(b), 457).

- Prohibits contracts that would pay a superintendent or ESU administrator more than five times the district/ESU beginning-teacher compensation for the same year; dual compensation is attributed to the employing district/ESU.

- Violating contracts are invalid; excess pay must be forfeited and returned to the district/ESU, and a notice must be filed with the Commissioner of Education within 30 days.

- No new appropriations or funding increases are made.
