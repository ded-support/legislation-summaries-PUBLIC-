Summary

LB109 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§44-4606 and 44-4610 to restrict actions by pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs) and insurers. Key provisions: PBMs may not prohibit or penalize pharmacies/pharmacists from disclosing specified treatment, authorization, cost, or plan-incentive information; may not bar disclosure to the director, law enforcement, or state/federal officials (subject to confidentiality procedures); may not terminate or penalize pharmacies for reporting PBM practices or sharing contracts with the director under complaint review; must limit patient cost-sharing to the lesser of plan cost-share or cash price (amounts counting toward deductibles/out-of-pocket maxima); and may not limit retail community pharmacy dispensing or delivery choices. The bill invokes severability and repeals the original sections. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that dispensing may occur under delegation and supervision or by a pharmacist and may take place in a physician's office, hospital outpatient infusion center, pharmacy, or other clinical setting. It prohibits pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs) from restricting a covered person’s choice of how a retail community pharmacy dispenses or delivers prescription drugs and from prohibiting retail community pharmacies (text continues).
