Summary

LB109 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§44-4606 and 44-4610 to limit pharmacy benefit manager (PBM) contract terms and insurer/health-plan restrictions. It protects pharmacists and pharmacies from contractual prohibitions or penalties for disclosing treatment, alternative therapies, utilization decisions, PBM practices, or total prescription costs; permits disclosure to the insurance director, law enforcement, or state/federal officials (with confidentiality safeguards); and bars PBMs from terminating or penalizing pharmacies for such disclosures or for sharing contracts with the director under the Pharmacy Benefit Manager Licensure and Regulation Act. The bill requires point-of-sale patient payments to be the lesser of plan cost-share or cash price and counts those payments toward deductibles/out-of-pocket limits consistent with federal Public Health Service Act §2707. It also preserves retail community pharmacy choice and modifies PBM specialty pharmacy network provisions. The insurance director is the primary state official referenced. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.
