Summary

The bill assigns primary administrative authority over the Kratom Consumer Protection Act to the Department of Revenue, empowering the Department to register processors, adopt rules, require product testing (including by a Department‑selected laboratory), impose civil penalties (first offense up to $1,000; second up to $5,000; third/subsequent $5,000–$20,000 plus a three‑year processor sales ban), and allow affected parties a hearing before the Tax Commissioner. It also preserves the Department’s tax‑amnesty administration and annual reporting duties (forms/procedures, personnel, technology, revenue). The bill creates a Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund, specifying allocations: at least 80% of amnesty proceeds to the General Fund, 10% (capped at $500,000) to the Enforcement Fund (excess to General Fund); initial Enforcement Fund proceeds are appropriated to the Department to hire enforcement personnel. Thereafter 20% (up to $750,000) of revenue generated by those hires and 10% of §77‑367 contract revenues are deposited to the Fund; transfers are authorized and Fund investment earnings are directed to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions involving the Department of Revenue (the "department"):

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Revenue.

- Enforcement powers: on evidence a product may be adulterated, the department may require independent third‑party testing by a laboratory of its choosing. The department may adopt rules to implement the Act.

- Penalties imposed by the department on processors/retailers: first violation up to $1,000; second up to $5,000; third and subsequent violations $5,000–$20,000 and, for processors, a three‑year ban on selling their kratom products in Nebraska. A clean four‑year period resets status to "first violation."

- Due process: determinations require notice and a hearing before the Tax Commissioner if requested.

- Forfeiture: conviction or materially false application statements can forfeit registration and monies paid.

- Funding: registration/registration‑fee monies shall be used by the Department of Revenue to defray administration costs of the Act.
