Summary

LB207 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-3,191 to set specific registration fees for alternative-fuel motor vehicles. It establishes a $75 fee for motorcycles and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles, a $150 fee for other alternative-fuel vehicles, and a commercial-vehicle provision making registration for alternative-fuel vehicles over 7,500 pounds three times the applicable fee. County treasurers collect the fees and remit them to the State Treasurer for credit to the Highway Trust Fund. The bill repeals the original section. Agencies explicitly affected: county treasurers and the Office of the State Treasurer (Highway Trust Fund receipt).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an alternative-fuel vehicle registration fee schedule: commercially registered alternative-fuel vehicles over 7,500 lbs pay three times the applicable alternative-fuel vehicle fee (as referenced in subdivision (1)(c)), and all other alternative-fuel motor vehicles pay $150.
