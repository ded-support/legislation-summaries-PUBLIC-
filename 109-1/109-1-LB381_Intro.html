Summary

LB381 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-974 governing program integrity and recovery-audit contractors for the Medical Assistance program. Key agency references and changes:

- "The department" (Medicaid administering agency) must coordinate with contractors and federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) at audit commencement and retain statutes/policies in audit records.

- CMS and the department conduct/coordinate program integrity audits; recovery audits must exclude capitated Medicaid managed-care claims, non-primary insurance claims, and claims currently or previously audited; audits of managed-care claims must be coordinated with the managed care organization.

- New provider protections/timelines: one-year payment lookback (except fraud), 180-day determination letters, 10-business-day onsite notice, 90-day post-audit documentation window, 60-day no-recoupment after adverse notice, appeal processes, and public annual contractor metrics on the department website.

- Annual December 1 electronic report to the Legislature on contracts, parties, issues, estimated recoveries, and savings.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for the department and its contractors overseeing audits and overpayment recovery. Key points:

- The department must provide written justification to a provider before commencing an audit.

- Payments become final after one year from payment date (except for fraud).

- Records requests are limited to documents relevant and proportional to the services audited (per subsection 12).

- Onsite audits require at least ten business days' advance notice; requested documentation after an onsite audit cannot be required sooner than 90 days from the provider’s request date.

- Overpayment notices must include detailed information (beneficiary, dates, amount, claim identifiers, evidence/basis, payment method/date, adjustment procedure, appeal rights, recovery method) and overpayments cannot be recouped for at least 60 days after notice.

- Contracts that violate these rules are unenforceable.

- Program integrity and audit-recovery contractors may not recoup until appeals are exhausted (unless credible fraud) and must provide appeal procedures, contact info at audit commencement, and answer provider questions within ten business days.

- An incomplete provision requires the department to submit an electronic filing. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
