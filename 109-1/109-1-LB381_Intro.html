Summary

LB381 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68‑974 (Medical Assistance Act) to tighten procedures, transparency, and coordination for program integrity and recovery audit contractors working with the Department (state Medicaid agency) and the federal Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). Requirements include: contractor review limited to claims paid within one year (except provider fraud); 180‑day deadline for final audit determinations after receipt of requested materials; records requests proportional and limited (≤200 records per 180 days) with 45 days for provider response; use of licensed specialty clinicians for clinical audits; exclusion/coordination of capitated Medicaid managed‑care claims and prohibition on duplicate audits; prohibition on extrapolated overpayment calculations without evidence of sustained error; withholding recoupment until appeals are exhausted (unless credible fraud); annual publication of contractor performance metrics on the Department website; and an annual December 1 electronic report to the Legislature on contracts, parties, estimated recoveries, and savings. No new appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions impose procedural limits on audits by "the department" and its contractors (program integrity contractor; audit recovery contractor). Key requirements: written justification to the provider for commencing an audit; records requests limited to documents relevant and proportional to the services audited; one-year finality for payments (no adjustment after one year except for provider fraud); advance notice of onsite audits of at least ten business days and a good‑faith effort to schedule; providers given at least 90 days to supply requested documentation after an onsite audit; overpayment notices must include detailed identification and explanation (beneficiary, dates, amounts, claim numbers, basis/evidence, payment method, appeals procedure, recovery method) and recovery cannot begin sooner than 60 days after notice. Program integrity contractors must provide appeal procedures at audit start, respond to provider questions within ten business days, and generally may not recoup until appeals are exhausted absent credible fraud. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included. An electronic submission requirement by the department appears incomplete.
