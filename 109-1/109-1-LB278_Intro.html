Summary

LB278 amends section 44-4109.01 (insurance provider participation rules) to preserve a pre-termination opportunity for a provider to enter and complete a corrective action plan, except in cases of fraud, imminent harm, or when the provider’s ability to provide services has been restricted by action (including probation or compliance agreements) by the Department of Health and Human Services or another governmental agency. No provisions in the bill reference funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds/clarifies that “provider” includes (a) a practice and (b) an individual holding a visiting faculty permit under §38‑2045, and includes a provision beginning “An insurance arrangement shall not be prohibited…” (text fragmentary). No language in the provided addition addresses the Department of Health and Human Services, nor does it include any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
