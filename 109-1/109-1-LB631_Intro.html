Summary

LB631 creates (amends) the School Emergency Response Mapping Fund and assigns administration to the State Department of Education. The fund may include legislative transfers and gifts, grants, or bequests; the department will use it to award grants for implementation of mapping data under section 79-3110. Funds available for investment are to be managed by the state investment officer pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Legislature states intent to transfer $4,000,000 from the General Fund to this Fund for FY2025-26. The bill repeals the original section 79-3111 and contains an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment.
