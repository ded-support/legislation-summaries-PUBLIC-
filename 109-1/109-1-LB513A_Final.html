Summary

LB513A appropriates funds to implement LB513. Agencies and amounts:

- Nebraska Supreme Court:

- Program 3: $26,838 (FY2025‑26), $53,775 (FY2026‑27); salary/per diem caps $23,631 and $47,616.

- Program 4: $21,976; $44,023; caps $19,242 and $38,774.

- Program 6: $250,398; $501,519; caps $218,585 and $440,449.

- Program 7: $205,999; $412,530; caps $179,257 and $361,202.

(All from General Fund.)

- Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court (Program 526): $21,574 (FY2025‑26) and $43,211 (FY2026‑27) from the Compensation Court Cash Fund; salary/per diem caps $18,736 and $37,753.

- Tax Equalization and Review Commission (Program 131): $12,051 and $24,283 from the General Fund; caps $10,465 and $21,087.

The act takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause.
