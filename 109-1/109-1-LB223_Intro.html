Summary

LB223 amends the Nebraska Fair Housing Act to add “lawful source of income” and protection for persons subject to public-assistance requirements. It defines lawful source of income (e.g., social security, child support, veterans benefits, federal/state/local housing assistance and vouchers). Agency impacts and duties:

- Equal Opportunity Commission: expanded enforcement, investigation, conciliation, data collection (including protected characteristics and lawful source of income), and required electronic annual reporting to the Legislature; authority to adopt rules and enter cooperative agreements with HUD and with testing organizations (prohibits pay-for-performance compensation to testers).

- State Fire Marshal: retains administration of accessibility standards.

- County attorneys: explicitly authorized to enforce the Act and must notify the commission of complaints.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of “lawful source of income” to include social security, child support, foster care subsidies, alimony, veterans benefits, any federal/state/local public general or housing assistance (including vouchers under 42 U.S.C. 1437f), housing-assistance payments/credits, and other lawful income. It clarifies such assistance may be paid directly, indirectly, or on behalf of a renter or attributed to a landlord. It also states the Nebraska Fair Housing Act does not bar otherwise nondiscriminatory measures that benefit public-assistance recipients (e.g., housing limited to such recipients or favorable loan/lease/mortgage terms). Government references are to federal, state, and local assistance programs and the federal voucher authority (42 U.S.C. 1437f, i.e., HUD Section 8); no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
