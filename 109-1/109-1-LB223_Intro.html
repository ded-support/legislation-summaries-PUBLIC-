Summary

LB223 amends the Nebraska Fair Housing Act to add "lawful source of income" and compliance with public assistance or housing-assistance program requirements as protected categories. Administration and enforcement remain with the Nebraska Equal Opportunity Commission (the commission), with the State Fire Marshal responsible for accessibility standards and county attorneys authorized to enforce the Act (with the commission notified of county complaints). The bill defines lawful source of income (including Social Security, child support, foster-care subsidies, alimony, veterans benefits, and public housing vouchers). It expands the commission’s investigative, conciliation, subpoena, hearing, and publication powers; requires electronic annual reporting to the Legislature and public data collection that includes lawful source of income; authorizes cooperative agreements with HUD and testing organizations while prohibiting testing-payments tied to conciliations/settlements. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "lawful source of income" to include social security, child support, foster care subsidies, alimony, veterans benefits, and any form of federal, state, or local public general assistance or housing assistance (whether paid to renters or landlords), explicitly citing vouchers under 42 U.S.C. 1437f. Also clarifies the Nebraska Fair Housing Act does not prohibit otherwise nondiscriminatory actions intended to benefit recipients of public assistance (e.g., housing limited to such recipients or favorable loan/lease/mortgage terms for them). Agencies/departments referenced: federal, state, and local public assistance and housing assistance programs and federal low‑income housing assistance under 42 U.S.C. 1437f (federal housing voucher program). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
