Summary

LB223 amends the Nebraska Fair Housing Act to add "lawful source of income" (including public assistance and housing vouchers) as a protected characteristic. Agencies/departments affected:

- Nebraska Equal Opportunity Commission: retains primary administration of the Act; gains explicit authority to investigate, conciliate, publish, and collect data (including lawful source of income) and must submit an annual electronic report to the Legislature. May enter cooperative agreements with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development and with testing organizations; tester compensation may not be tied to conciliations, settlements, or reasonable-cause findings.

- State Fire Marshal: continues to administer accessibility standards under specified statutes.

- County attorneys: granted authority to enforce the Act and must notify the commission of complaints.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a broad definition of “lawful source of income,” explicitly including social security, child support, foster care subsidies, alimony, veterans benefits, any federal/state/local public general or housing assistance (including vouchers under 42 U.S.C. §1437f), housing-assistance payments or credits, and other lawful income. Also clarifies that the Nebraska Fair Housing Act does not bar otherwise nondiscriminatory actions intended to benefit public-assistance recipients (e.g., housing or favorable loan/lease terms limited to such recipients). Mentions federal, state, and local public assistance programs (and a statutory reference to federal housing assistance under 42 U.S.C. §1437f); no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
