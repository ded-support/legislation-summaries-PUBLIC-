Summary

LB223 amends the Nebraska Fair Housing Act to add "lawful source of income" (defined to include Social Security, child support, public housing vouchers, other public assistance, etc.) and protections for compliance with public assistance requirements. Agencies and roles affected:

- Nebraska Equal Opportunity Commission: remains primary administrator of the Act; gains explicit authority to investigate, conciliate, hold hearings, collect demographic data (including lawful source of income), publish annual electronic reports to the Legislature, adopt rules, and enter cooperative agreements with HUD under the Fair Housing Assistance Program. The commission may contract with testing organizations but may not tie tester compensation to case outcomes.

- State Fire Marshal: retains administration of accessibility standards/specifications (sections 81-5,147 and 81-5,148).

- County attorneys: granted authority to enforce the Act with the same powers as the commission and must notify the commission when a complaint is filed.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a broad definition of “lawful source of income” to the Nebraska Fair Housing Act to include income from Social Security, child support, foster care subsidies, alimony, veterans benefits, and any federal, state, or local public general or housing assistance (explicitly citing vouchers under 42 U.S.C. §1437f — the federal housing voucher/Section 8 program). It bars discrimination based on lawful source of income but clarifies that the Act does not prohibit nondiscriminatory programs that specifically benefit public-assistance recipients (e.g., housing limited to such recipients or favorable loan/lease terms). No state agencies are newly created or renamed, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
