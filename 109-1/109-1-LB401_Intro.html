Summary

LB401 makes targeted changes affecting the Department of Revenue and the Department of Motor Vehicles. For sales and use tax enforcement, when penalties are collected by a county treasurer or the Department of Motor Vehicles the statutory penalty is fixed at $5 (replacing the general 10% or $25 penalty). The Department of Revenue must receive annual information returns from publicly traded partnerships electing partnership treatment, reporting unit‑holder name, address, TIN and other requested data for holders with in‑state income over $500. Additional Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner) changes: tolling of the deficiency‑notice period when the Commissioner seeks a court order to compel records (with specified exceptions); federal entity extensions that extend notice periods to partners/shareholders/members for entity items; and a requirement that refund‑denial notices state facts/reasons and become final after 30 days absent review. No appropriations or budget changes are included.
