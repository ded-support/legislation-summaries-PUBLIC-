Summary

LB401 amends statutes affecting two agencies. For the Department of Motor Vehicles (amending §77‑2708) it preserves a reduced $5 penalty when a penalty is collected by a county treasurer or the DMV under specified subdivisions of §77‑2703. For the Department of Revenue (amending §77‑2727) it exempts publicly traded partnerships from remittance requirements if the partnership agrees to file an annual information return with the Department reporting unit‑holder name, address, TIN, and other requested information for holders with Nebraska‑source income over $500. No appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for either agency are included.

Department of Revenue
