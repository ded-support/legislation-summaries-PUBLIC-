Summary

LB401 amends revenue statutes to (1) retain a reduced late‑filing/late‑payment sales and use tax penalty of $5 when those penalties are collected by a county treasurer or the Department of Motor Vehicles, and (2) require a publicly traded partnership (treated as a partnership and electing the reporting condition) to file an annual information return with the Department of Revenue listing unit holders with in‑state income exceeding $500. The bill sometimes refers to the "Tax Commissioner" rather than explicitly naming the Department of Revenue. It contains no explicit appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for either the Department of Motor Vehicles or the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (focus: Department of Revenue; no effect on DMV)

- Requires the Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner) to include in notices—such as notices of proposed deficiency determinations—a written statement detailing the facts, circumstances, and reasons supporting determinations that a taxpayer did not report the correct tax or did not make an overpayment.

- Clarifies that, for returns filed for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan. 1, 2022, an election/credit may be made on the applicable Nebraska income tax return for that same taxable year regardless of the year the tax is paid to Nebraska or deducted federally.

Fiscal/budgetary impact: No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. No provisions affect the Department of Motor Vehicles.
