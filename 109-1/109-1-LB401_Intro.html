Summary

The bill makes two agency-specific tax changes. For the Department of Motor Vehicles, it reduces the penalty for late sales/use tax returns to $5 when the penalty is being collected by a county treasurer or the DMV (replacing the standard 10% or $25 penalty). For the Department of Revenue, it exempts publicly traded partnerships (treated as partnerships) from the partnership remittance rule provided the partnership files an annual information return with the Department reporting unit-holder name, address, taxpayer identification number, and other requested information. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency are included.
