Summary

LB565 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-901.03 (Administrative Procedure Act) to tighten requirements for agency guidance documents. Key agency obligations: publish each guidance and an index of current rules/guidance in one public location and online; submit the index to the Clerk of the Legislature annually by December 31; include on the first page a prescribed notice and an estimated fiscal-impact description covering state agencies, political subdivisions, and regulated persons/businesses; and post agency decisions publicly. Individuals may request revision, repeal, or conversion of guidance to rules; agencies must respond within 60 days. Agencies are barred from issuing guidance from July 1, 2025, to July 1, 2027, except for federal compliance; guidance may not exceed federal requirements. Guidance and provider bulletins issued July 1, 2022–July 1, 2025, are revoked pending rulemaking unless federally required. The bill contains both an operative date (July 1, 2025) and an emergency clause; no funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires state agencies (and political subdivisions) to stop issuing guidance documents from July 1, 2025 to July 1, 2027 except when needed to comply with new or amended federal rules. Any agency guidance must not impose stricter requirements on Nebraska residents or businesses than federal rules. All guidance documents and provider bulletins issued between July 1, 2022 and July 1, 2025 are revoked until they undergo formal rulemaking under the Administrative Procedure Act, unless explicitly required for federal compliance. No specific agencies are named and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
