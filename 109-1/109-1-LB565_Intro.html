Summary

LB565 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-901.03 (Administrative Procedure Act) to impose new transparency, notice, and procedural requirements on state agencies. Agencies must post guidance documents and an annual online index of current rules and guidance, and deliver that index electronically to the Clerk of the Legislature by December 31 each year. Each guidance document’s first page must contain a specified advisory notice and an estimated fiscal-impact description for state agencies, political subdivisions, and regulated entities. The bill creates a 60-day agency-response requirement for written requests to revise, repeal, or convert guidance into rules and requires publication of related agency decisions. It bars issuing guidance between July 1, 2025, and July 1, 2027, except for federal compliance, prohibits guidance more stringent than federal law, and revokes guidance/provider bulletins from July 1, 2022–July 1, 2025 unless federally required. No appropriations or budget changes are included. An emergency clause makes the act effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Agencies must include a description, with estimated quantification, of fiscal impacts on state agencies, political subdivisions, and regulated persons/businesses.

- State agencies are barred from issuing guidance documents from July 1, 2025, through July 1, 2027, except when needed to comply with new or amended federal rules.

- Guidance documents may not impose requirements stricter than federal law.

- All guidance documents and provider bulletins issued between July 1, 2022, and July 1, 2025, are revoked pending formal rulemaking under the Administrative Procedure Act, unless explicitly required for federal compliance.

- No explicit changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are made.
