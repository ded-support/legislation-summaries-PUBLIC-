Summary

LB565 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-901.03 (Administrative Procedure Act) to impose new requirements on state agencies regarding guidance documents. Agencies must post guidance at one public location and on their website, publish an annual index of applicable rules/guidance and submit it to the Clerk of the Legislature by Dec. 31, and place on each guidance’s first page a prescribed notice and an estimated fiscal-impact description covering state agencies, political subdivisions, and regulated entities. Agencies must respond within 60 days to written requests to revise, repeal, or convert guidance into rules and publicly post those decisions. The bill bars issuance of new guidance from July 1, 2025, to July 1, 2027, except for federal compliance, prevents guidance from exceeding federal requirements, and revokes guidance/provider bulletins dated July 1, 2022–July 1, 2025 pending rulemaking unless federally required. Emergency clause makes it effective on passage. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires agencies to include an estimated fiscal-impact description for state agencies, political subdivisions, and regulated persons/businesses. Prohibits any agency from issuing guidance documents between July 1, 2025 and July 1, 2027 unless needed to comply with new or amended federal rules. Mandates that guidance may not impose stricter requirements on Nebraska residents or businesses than federal law. Revokes all guidance documents and provider bulletins issued July 1, 2022–July 1, 2025 pending formal rulemaking under the Administrative Procedure Act, except those explicitly required for federal compliance. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
