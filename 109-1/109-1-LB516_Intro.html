Summary

LB516 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) statutory framework be amended by outright repeal of sections 68-130 and 81-1139.01, Reissue Revised Statutes of Nebraska. The bill removes a statutory requirement tied to counties to provide office space for DHHS and eliminates those identified obsolete provisions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included or changed in the bill.
