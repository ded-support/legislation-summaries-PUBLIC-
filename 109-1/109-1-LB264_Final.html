Summary

The bill centralizes fund-transfer authority with the Department of Administrative Services (DAS): the DAS budget administrator directs timing/amounts and certifies numerous transfers, and certain fund investment earnings are redirected to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024. Major agency fiscal provisions include: DMV — creation of replacement/maintenance funds, required motor‑carrier system implementation by 7/1/2025, and multiyear transfers to the General Fund (including $3.0M annually from a motor‑carrier fund and one‑time multimillion amounts); Department of Revenue — $2.0M (FY25‑26) and scheduled Tobacco Products transfers ($9.5M FY25‑26 & FY26‑27; $9.0M FY27‑28 & FY28‑29); Department of Health and Human Services — transfers of $3.224M (FY25‑26) and $1.47M (FY26‑27) and a $1.0M pilot intent appropriation; Banking and Finance — Settlement Cash Fund with $1.0M (FY25‑26) and $0.75M (FY26‑27) transfers; DOT/DED — a Transportation‑administered Economic Opportunity Program capped at $20M (ends 6/30/2025); DED — Transformational Projects matching authority (15%, up to $50M/year, intent cap $300M); Labor — administration of Sector Partnership and Workforce Development cash funds; Veterans’ Affairs — administers Military Installation Development and Support Fund with 50% match requirement. Correctional Services may use Prison Overcrowding Contingency funds for an inmate classification study.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: given authority (via its budget division) to direct timing/amounts of numerous fund transfers to/from the General Fund and other accounts; required to create a subaccount in the Nebraska Soil and Water Conservation Fund for Environmental Trust Fund transfers. Major Cash Reserve actions include $45,897,000 to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund (by 6/30/2026), $100,000 to the Self‑Insured Liability Fund (by 6/30/2025), intent to phase $200M to the Transformational Project Fund beginning FY2027‑28, and $147,000,000 to the General Fund in FY2026‑27.

- Department of Banking and Finance: $1,000,000 (FY25‑26) and $750,000 (FY26‑27) from its Settlement Cash Fund to the General Fund; fund may also be transferred to the Nebraska Opportunity Grant Fund per Legislature.

- Department of Economic Development: remaining Economic Development Cash Fund and Intern Nebraska balances to General Fund (FY25‑26); $4.4M from Jobs & Economic Development Initiative to General Fund (FY25‑26); site/building fund receipts/transfers re: military fund.

- Department of Labor: transfers from Employment Security Special Contingent ($1.5M), Workforce Development ($1.0M to GF; $2.0M to Opportunity Grant), Nebraska Training & Support ($2.25M FY25‑26; $0.5M FY26‑27), and Sector Partnership ($0.2M) to GF or other funds.

- Department of Correctional Services: $500,000 from Prison Overcrowding Contingency Fund to the General Fund (FY25‑26).

- Department of Health and Human Services: $3.224M (FY25‑26) and $1.47M (FY26‑27) from the HHS Cash Fund to the General Fund.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: multiple transfers affecting DMV funds — e.g., $5.5M (FY25‑26) and $1.5M (FY26‑27) from vehicle title/registration system fund to GF; $7.5M (FY25‑26) and $6.0M (FY26‑27) from DMV Cash Fund to Operator's License System fund; $12.5M from DMV Cash Fund to GF (FY26‑27); ignition interlock and motor carrier cash fund transfers also directed.

- Department of Revenue: $2.0M from Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund to the General Fund (FY25‑26 and FY26‑27); $250,000 redirected to the Property Assessment Division Cash Fund; miscellaneous receipts balances may be transferred to GF.

- Department of Transportation: termination of the Transportation Infrastructure Bank Economic Opportunity Program (no new letters after 5/1/2025); transfers from Transportation Infrastructure Bank and Motor Carrier Services funds to GF and to DMV as specified (multi‑year).

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: no direct fund transfers specified.

- Military Department: $25.5M from the Military Installation Development and Support Fund to the Site and Building Development Fund (FY25‑26); fund subject to legislative transfer direction.
