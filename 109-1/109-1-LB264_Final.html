Summary

LB264 centralizes fiscal authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget administrator, authorizing timing, certification, administrative transfers, warrant draws, and crediting certain investment earnings to the General Fund. DAS is assigned numerous directed transfers (notably $147M to the General Fund in FY2026‑27, $10M to School Safety, $25M and $10M to the Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund, capital construction transfers, and intent to begin $200M Cash Reserve transfers to a Transformational Project Fund in FY2027‑28).

Agency-specific provisions/funding:

- DMV: new motor carrier system; multiple interfund transfers including $500K (FY25‑26)/$250K (FY26‑27) to GF, $3M transfers to DMV Cash Fund, $7.5M/$6M to operator license replacement fund, and $12.5M (FY26‑27) to GF.

- Revenue: $2M/year (FY25‑26, FY26‑27) from Enforcement Fund to GF; creates Tobacco Products Admin Cash Fund with mandated transfers ($9.5M FY25‑26 & FY26‑27; $9M later) and an Unclaimed Property Cash Fund.

- Banking & Finance: creates Settlement Cash Fund; transfers of $1M (FY25‑26) and $750K (FY26‑27) to GF.

- Economic Development: administers ImagiNE Revolving Loan Fund, advises Economic Opportunity Program and Transformational Project reviews.

- Transportation: administers Economic Opportunity Program with DED (up to $20M, ends 6/30/25).

- Labor: administers Sector Partnership and Workforce Development cash funds; may transfer to GF or Opportunity Grant Fund.

- Health & Human Services: receives directed transfers ($3.224M FY25‑26; $1.47M FY26‑27; additional $3.5M) and will administer a post‑acute care transition pilot (legislative intent $1M).

- Veterans’ Affairs & Military Department: Veterans’ Affairs administers Military Installation Development & Support Fund requiring ≥50% public/private match; references Nebraska Military Department.

- Correctional Services: may use Prison Overcrowding Contingency Fund for an inmate‑classification study.
