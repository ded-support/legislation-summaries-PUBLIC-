Summary

The bill gives the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) broad budget authority to direct timing/amounts of numerous State Treasurer transfers, administratively move Building Renewal Allocation funds, create a Soil & Water subaccount, credit certain investment earnings to the General Fund (beginning Oct. 1, 2024), draw warrants for K–12 disbursements, and direct specific transfers (including a $3.5M Cash Reserve transfer to HHS and an intended $200M transfer to the Nebraska Transformational Project Fund beginning FY2027‑28).

Department of Motor Vehicles must implement a new motor‑carrier system by 7/1/2025 and undergo multiple cash‑fund reallocations (notably $12.5M to the General Fund in FY2026‑27; $7.5M FY2025‑26 and $6M FY2026‑27 to operator license system replacement; other multi‑year transfers).

Department of Revenue creates tobacco and marijuana administration cash funds with scheduled GF transfers (tobacco: $9.5M FY25‑26 and FY26‑27, then $9M thereafter), directs unclaimed‑property confidentiality, and shifts certain enforcement/property assessment transfers.

Department of Banking and Finance establishes a Settlement Cash Fund with permitted transfers to the General Fund ($1.0M FY25‑26; $0.75M FY26‑27).

Department of Economic Development administers ImagiNE Revolving Loan and Transformational Project funds (up to $300M total; 15% state match capped $50M/year) and other grant/loan programs.

Department of Labor administers Workforce Development and Sector Partnership funds (with DED participation).

Department of Transportation administers a capped ($20M) Economic Opportunity Program (terminating 6/30/2025).

Department of Health and Human Services is directed to run a $1.0M pilot (legislative intent), remit tobacco‑settlement receipts for Health Care Cash Fund transfers, and has specified cash‑fund transfers to the General Fund (approx. $3.224M FY25‑26; $1.47M FY26‑27).

Department of Veterans' Affairs (including the Military Department) administers the Military Installation Development & Support Fund (50% public/private match required).

Department of Correctional Services may use the Prison Overcrowding Contingency Fund for an inmate‑classification study.
