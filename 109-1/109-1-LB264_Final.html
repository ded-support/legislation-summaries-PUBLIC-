Summary

The bill centralizes extensive cash‑management authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget administrator, directing numerous State Treasurer transfers, creation/administration of several DAS funds (Ferguson House, Community College State Dependents, Building Renewal subaccounts), and crediting investment earnings of multiple funds to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024. DAS is authorized large Cash Reserve Fund moves including transfers to the General Fund ($147 million in FY2026‑27), Nebraska Capital Construction Fund ($29.46M and later $45.897M), Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund ($25M by 6/30/25 and $10M FY2025‑26), and an intent to transfer $200M to a Transformational Project Fund beginning FY2027‑28. Department‑specific provisions: DMV must build a new motor‑carrier system and faces multiple multi‑million fund transfers between DMV funds (including $12.5M to the General Fund and redirected fund earnings). Revenue creates tobacco and marijuana administration funds with scheduled General Fund transfers ($9.5M/year beginning FY2025‑26). Banking & Finance gets a Settlement Cash Fund (settlement transfers to GF permitted). DED, DOT and Labor administer economic, workforce and loan programs (DOT/DED Economic Opportunity Program capped at $20M). HHS receives/can transfer funds and a $1M pilot appropriation intent. Department of Veterans’ Affairs/Military administer a Military Installation Development & Support Fund with matching requirements. Correctional Services may use overcrowding contingency funds for an inmate classification study.
