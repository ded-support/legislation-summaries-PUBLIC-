Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and directs the DMV to prescribe safety/security title and manufacturer‑assignment forms (ATV/UTV certificates must state "manufactured for off‑road use"), select security paper, and may contract for secure power‑of‑attorney forms. The DMV will design and issue specialty plates, provide applications via county treasurers, may mail plates/registration (postage/handling remitted to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund), and may appoint staff or certified instructors to examine license applicants. DMV personnel or county treasurers must collect fees and the surcharge for school and LPE‑learner's permits. Fiscal changes: a $50 special‑interest plate fee is split $25 to the DMV Cash Fund and $25 to the Highway Trust Fund (manufacturing cost overruns credited first to the Highway Trust Fund). By Jan 1, 2024 the DMV must record motorcycle safety course completion on driving records. For transactions on/after July 1, 2023, sales/use tax on aircraft sales/leases is credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Dept. of Motor Vehicles / Dept. of Transportation):

- DMV: Adds all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) and utility‑type vehicles (UTVs) into the Motor Vehicle Registration Act for titling, registration, and issuance of license plates (per section 60‑3,100). Titles must reference "manufactured for off‑road use." Registration fee set at $10 for ATVs and $10 for UTVs. UTV defined (≤74" width, ≤180" length incl. bumper, dry weight ≤2,000 lbs, four+ nonhighway tires); excludes golf cars and low‑speed vehicles.

- DOT: Permits operation of registered ATVs/UTVs on county and township roads outside municipal limits, but prohibits use on the interstate system, state highways, expressways, freeways, and where local ordinance bans them.

- Budget: No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
