Summary

LB690 affects the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and Department of Transportation (DOT) as follows. For special‑interest vehicle plates, the $50 fee is split $25 to the DMV Cash Fund and $25 to the Highway Trust Fund; the DMV may impose a postage/handling charge for mailing plates that is remitted to the DMV Cash Fund. If plate manufacturing costs exceed the statutory charge, amounts otherwise payable to the DMV Cash Fund are first diverted to the Highway Trust Fund to cover the excess, with any remainder to the DMV Cash Fund. The bill defines “Department” as the DMV under the Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act, requires applicants to submit motorcycle‑safety course proof to the DMV, and directs the DMV to record course completion on driving records by Jan 1, 2024. It also redirects sales/use tax proceeds from aircraft transactions (on/after July 1, 2023) to the DOT Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)

- Adds all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) and utility‑type vehicles (UTVs) into the Motor Vehicle Registration Act: they require certificate of title, issuance of license plates under section 60-3,100, and registration when operated on highways pursuant to section 60-6,356.

- Sets registration fees at $10 for ATVs and $10 for UTVs.

- Defines UTV technical criteria (≤74" width, ≤180" overall length, dry weight ≤2,000 lb, four+ nonhighway tires) and excludes golf cars/low‑speed vehicles.

Department of Transportation (DOT)

- Clarifies where registered ATVs/UTVs may be operated: allowed on highways generally but prohibited on the Interstate System, state highways, expressways, freeways, and any local ordinance‑prohibited roads.
