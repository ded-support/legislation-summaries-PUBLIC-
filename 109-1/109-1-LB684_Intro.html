Summary

The bill centralizes juvenile services and reporting across multiple agencies. Department of Administrative Services will receive biannual juvenile and adult probation reports (Jan. 15, July 15) and administer fiscal transition provisions: FY2025‑26 Supreme Court appropriations/salary limits are voided and reallocated to the Office of Probation Administration, with unpaid obligations payable from unexpended program balances per DAS accounting. DHHS must apply for Title IV‑E reimbursement, may contract with the Juvenile Probation Agency as a Title IV‑E surrogate, and remit recovered funds to the State Treasurer crediting the Juvenile Probation Program Cash Fund; DHHS/Office of Juvenile Services community‑based and juvenile parole funds will transfer to the Juvenile Probation Agency to fund expansion. DHHS will prioritize Medicaid waivers for departmental wards and transitioning juveniles and assist with IDs/birth certificates (fees waived). DMV will receive adjudication abstracts and sealing‑order notifications affecting licenses. Department of Labor must update employer guidance on sealed juvenile records. Department of Correctional Services may access sealed records. Legislative intent directs DHHS, DOL, DED and DCS to coordinate integrated programs or operating agreements.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Administrator must send biannual electronic reports to DAS' budget division. Unpaid financial obligations certified as valid encumbrances are to be routed to DAS' accounting division; the bill voids certain FY2025‑26 appropriations/salary limits for Supreme Court programs and reallocates those amounts to the new Office of Probation Administration (Agency No. XXX). The administrator will prepare and submit the agency budget/appropriation requests per law.

- Funding/appropriations: State pays agency salaries, travel, drug testing, electronic‑surveillance equipment, and interpreter services (from Supreme Court appropriations or other funds). Counties initially fund district office maintenance but are reimbursed per administrator formula. A Juvenile Probation Contractual Services Cash Fund is created to hold payments from local political subdivisions to fund probation services.

- Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Correctional Services, Department of Health and Human Services, and Department of Motor Vehicles: not referenced or affected in the added text.
