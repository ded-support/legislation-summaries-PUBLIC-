Summary

LB126 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §10‑126 to revise municipal bond redemption rules. Bonds generally become callable at the issuer’s option five years after issuance, with explicit exceptions for public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, metropolitan utilities districts, cities of the metropolitan and primary classes, housing authorities, certain large revenue issues of cities/villages, large school‑district bonds, bonds issued by the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska and the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges, and county bonds sold via competitive sale. Issuers may agree to call premiums (up to 4% for large single issues; up to 2% for districts under Chapters 31 or 39). The bill permits exempt entities to set their own redemption terms, authorizes calls by governing‑body resolution or ordinance (including conditioning on issuance of refunding bonds), and requires deposit with the paying agent to make a call effective. No appropriations or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that county bonds sold to an underwriting firm via a competitive sale (including the sale method in section 10‑145) may be issued with a call for redemption that is conditioned on the obligor’s issuance of refunding bonds or a similar event that produces funds necessary to prepay the bonds. Mentions counties and the statutory sale method (sec. 10‑145); no executive agencies, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or specified.
