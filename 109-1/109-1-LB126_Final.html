Summary

LB126 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §10-126 governing optional redemption of local government bonds. It retains the general rule that bonds issued by counties, cities, villages, school, drainage, irrigation and other municipal corporations are callable after five years, but explicitly exempts bonds of public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, the metropolitan utilities district, cities of metropolitan and primary class, housing authorities, certain large revenue bond issues, and now also bonds issued by the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska and the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges. The bill caps call premiums (up to 4% generally; 2% for districts under Chapters 31 or 39), permits those exempt entities to set redemption terms, clarifies calling procedures, and repeals the original section. No appropriations or budgetary allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that county bonds sold to an underwriting firm via a competitive sale (including the method in section 10‑145) may have an ordinance provision conditioning a call for redemption on the issuance of refunding bonds by the obligor or a similar event that generates funds for prepayment. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.
