Summary

LB126 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §10-126 governing municipal bond redemption. It clarifies which issuers are subject to the five-year optional-call restriction and expressly excludes bonds of public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, metropolitan utilities districts, cities of the metropolitan and primary classes, housing authorities, large revenue or large-issue school district bonds, bonds sold by counties via competitive sale, and bonds issued by the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska and the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges. Districts created under Chapters 31 or 39 have special annexation-call rules. Issuers may pay call premiums (up to 4%, 2% for Chapter 31/39 districts for large issues). Governing bodies may call bonds by resolution/ordinance conditioned on refunding issuance, and calls require sufficient deposit with the paying agent. No appropriations or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new clause allowing county bonds sold to an underwriting firm via a competitive sale (per section 10-145) to include call-for-redemption provisions that are conditioned on the issuance of refunding bonds or a similar event that generates funds for prepayment. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and no changes to appropriations, funding allocations, or budgets are included.
