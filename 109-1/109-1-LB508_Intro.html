Summary

This bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §55-117 and affects the Nebraska National Guard, the Governor, the Adjutant General, and local police authorities. It (1) limits ordering the Nebraska National Guard into active combat for federal service to instances when the U.S. Congress has declared war or has lawfully called forth the Guard under the U.S. Constitution; (2) requires the Governor to review all current federal orders placing Guard members into active combat for compliance and, if noncompliant, to take action to immediately return those members to Nebraska; (3) requires the Governor to report findings and actions to the Legislature’s Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee within 30 days of completing the review; and (4) defines "active combat." No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds limits and procedural duties concerning the Nebraska National Guard and federal activation. Key agencies mentioned: the Nebraska National Guard, the Governor, the United States Congress, and the Legislature's Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee. New provisions: the Guard may not be ordered into "active combat" unless Congress has declared war or cited the Guard to execute federal laws, suppress insurrection, or repel invasions per the U.S. Constitution. The Governor must review all current federal orders placing Guard members into active combat, return any members if an order is noncompliant, and report the review and actions to the Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee within 30 days of completing the review. "Active combat" is defined in three parts. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
