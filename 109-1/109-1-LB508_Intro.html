Summary

LB508 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §55-117 and repeals original §55-118. It affects the Nebraska National Guard, the Governor, the Adjutant General, local police authorities, and the Legislature's Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee. Key changes:

- Prohibits ordering the Nebraska National Guard into active combat under federal service unless Congress has declared war or has lawfully called forth the Guard under the U.S. Constitution.

- Requires the Governor to review every federal order that has placed Nebraska National Guard members into active combat to assess compliance with that prohibition.

- Directs the Governor to take immediate action to return Guard members to Nebraska if an order is found noncompliant, through the Adjutant General as necessary; local police remain subject to gubernatorial control when Guard is ordered out.

- Requires the Governor to report the review findings and any responsive actions to the Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee within 30 days after completing the review.

- Defines "active combat" (three-part definition).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds limits and procedures regarding the Nebraska National Guard and federal orders. Key agencies/entities:

- Nebraska National Guard: may not be ordered into active combat unless the U.S. Congress has declared war or "called forth" the Guard to execute federal laws, suppress insurrection, or repel invasions.

- Governor: must review every current federal order sending Guard members into active combat for compliance; if an order is noncompliant, the Governor must take actions to immediately return those members to Nebraska.

- Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee of the Nebraska Legislature: Governor must report the review results and any actions taken within 30 days after completing the review.

- United States Congress referenced as the authorizing body.

"Active combat" is defined (armed conflict; hazardous service in a foreign state related to armed conflict; duty via an instrumentality of war). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
