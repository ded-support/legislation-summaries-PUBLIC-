Summary

LB508 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §55-117 (Military Code). It: (1) bars the Nebraska National Guard from being ordered into active combat under federal service unless the U.S. Congress has declared war or has "called forth" the Guard pursuant to the U.S. Constitution to enforce federal law, suppress insurrection, or repel invasions; (2) requires the Governor to review all current federal orders placing Guard members into active combat for compliance with that restriction and to take any actions necessary to immediately return noncompliant members to Nebraska; (3) requires the Governor to report the review and any actions to the Legislature’s Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee within 30 days after completion; (4) defines "active combat;" and (5) preserves the Governor’s control through the Adjutant General (with local police subject to that control when Guard is ordered). No funding or appropriation changes. Section 55-118 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that the Nebraska National Guard may not be ordered into active combat unless Congress has declared war or otherwise called the Guard under the U.S. Constitution. Requires the Governor to review all current federal orders that placed Guard members into active combat for compliance; if an order is noncompliant the Governor must take action to immediately return those members to Nebraska. The Governor must report the review and any actions to the Legislature’s Government, Military and Veterans Affairs Committee within 30 days of completing the review. Defines “active combat” by three criteria. No agencies receive new funding and no appropriations or budget changes are specified.
