Summary

LB604 amends initiative and referendum procedures (secs. 32-1405, 32-1412). Key agency duties and procedural changes:

- Secretary of State: requires filing of petition object/text and sworn sponsor list before circulating; transmits text to the Revisor of Statutes; keeps filings confidential for five days after the Revisor’s review; must provide petition form to sponsor within five business days and post measure text/notice on its website until the filing deadline. If refusing to place a measure on the ballot for legal insufficiency (other than signature insufficiency), the Secretary must announce the refusal and state reasons when posting circulation notice; failure to state reasons waives those defenses.

- Revisor of Statutes: must review proposed measures and suggest form/draftsmanship changes within ten business days.

- Courts: mandamus suits go to Lancaster County District Court; suits challenging sufficiency (other than signature insufficiency) must be filed within 60 days of the Secretary’s posting (or by the petition-filing deadline if earlier); the sponsor of record is a necessary party defendant in injunction/removal suits. If practicable, the Nebraska Supreme Court may hear non-signature insufficiency suits in original jurisdiction; appeals to the Court of Appeals within ten days.

No funding or appropriations are included. Original sections repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural duties for the Secretary of State and timing/venue rules for legal challenges. Key points:

- Secretary of State must post the measure text and a notice that it is in circulation on the Secretary of State website until the petition filing deadline.

- If the Secretary refuses to place a measure on the ballot for any legal insufficiency other than insufficient signatures, the Secretary must announce that refusal on the same date as the circulation posting and state the reasons; failure to state reasons waives those defenses.

- Suits challenging petition legal sufficiency (other than signature insufficiency) must be filed within 60 days of the circulation notice—or by the petition filing deadline if the 60th day falls after that deadline.

- When practicable, the Nebraska Supreme Court shall hear such suits under its original jurisdiction (section 24-204) on petition.

- Parties may assert substantive facial insufficiency without ripeness dismissal.

No agencies beyond the Secretary of State and the Nebraska Supreme Court are added; no funding or appropriation changes.
