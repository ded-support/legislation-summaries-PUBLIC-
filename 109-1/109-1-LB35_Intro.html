Summary

LB35 amends section 70-1014.02 governing exemptions for privately developed renewable energy generation facilities. Key agency references and actions:

- "Board" (and its executive director) receives written notice/certifications from facility owners and must issue exemption acknowledgments within ten days if requirements are met; the board may sue in the name of the State for failure to meet decommissioning security requirements unless a local governmental entity has enacted decommissioning rules.

- Nebraska Game and Parks Commission must be consulted to identify avoidance/mitigation for protected species during planning/design.

- Private suppliers must enter a joint transmission development agreement with the electric supplier owning transmission ≥60 kV; agreements must align with applicable regional transmission organization directives.

- Compliance with NERC critical infrastructure protection rules is required where applicable; facilities within 10 miles of military installations must avoid components from foreign adversaries per 15 C.F.R. 7.4 or meet NERC standards.

- Clarifies eminent domain authority for consumer‑owned electric suppliers and preserves limits on private suppliers’ retail sales authority.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
