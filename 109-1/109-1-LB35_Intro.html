Summary

LB35 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-1014.02 to revise exemption requirements for privately developed renewable energy generation facilities. Key agency references and duties:

- The unspecified "board" (and its executive director) must receive written notice ≥30 days before construction, review certifications (decommissioning plan, security bond by year six unless a local government has enacted decommissioning rules), and issue a written acknowledgement within ten days if requirements are met; the board may bring enforcement actions for bond-plan noncompliance.

- Private developers must consult the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission to identify measures to avoid, minimize, or mitigate impacts to protected species during planning/design.

- Projects must enter a joint transmission development agreement with the electric supplier owning ≥60 kV facilities; applicable regional transmission organizations govern interconnection terms.

- Projects within 10 miles of a military installation must exclude components from foreign adversaries or comply with NERC critical infrastructure protection standards; developers must notify the board of noncompliance.

The bill reiterates eminent-domain limits and presumption of public use for consumer-owned suppliers. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
