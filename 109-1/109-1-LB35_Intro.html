Summary

LB35 amends section 70-1014.02 to revise exemption procedures for privately developed renewable energy generation facilities. Key agency actions:

- The (unnamed) state board and its executive director must receive written notice, certifications, decommissioning plans and security instruments, and issue exemption acknowledgments within ten days.

- The Nebraska Game and Parks Commission must be consulted to identify measures to avoid, minimize, or mitigate impacts to protected species during planning and design.

- Private electric suppliers must enter joint transmission development agreements with the electric supplier owning transmission ≥60 kV; regional transmission organizations and NERC critical infrastructure compliance are referenced for facilities near military installations.

- Local governmental entities retain authority to enact decommissioning requirements; consumer‑owned electric suppliers retain eminent‑domain authority for necessary transmission.

No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included. The bill repeals the prior version of the section.
