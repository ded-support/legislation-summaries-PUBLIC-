Summary

LB35 amends procedures for privately developed renewable energy facilities, emphasizing roles for state and local agencies. Key agency provisions:

- "Board" (and its executive director) receives developer notices, issues exemption acknowledgments within 10 days, may sue in the name of the State for failure to meet decommissioning security requirements, and receives copies of bonds/instruments.

- Private developers must consult the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission to identify measures to avoid or mitigate impacts to protected species.

- Developers must enter joint transmission development agreements with the electric supplier that owns transmission ≥60 kV and comply with applicable regional transmission organization processes.

- Facilities near military installations must meet 15 C.F.R. 7.4 related foreign-adversary restrictions or NERC critical-infrastructure requirements.

- Local governmental entities may enact decommissioning rules that supersede state bond requirements.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
