Summary

The bill reallocates duties, defines memberships, and sunsets multiple advisory bodies involving DHHS, DAS, DED, DOL, DOT, and DMV. Key DHHS actions: ex officio membership on several committees; staffing/coordination of the Early Childhood Interagency Coordinating Council; administration of the Women's Health Initiative Fund (able to accept/remit funds through July 1, 2026); lead agency for the Critical Incident Stress Management Program with expanded post‑July 1, 2026 responsibilities; and an annual child‑welfare metrics report to the Health and Human Services Committee. The Climate Assessment Response Committee, Children's Behavioral Health Task Force, Foster Care Reimbursement Rate Committee, and other named bodies terminate July 1, 2026. DAS assumes Suggestion Award Board and Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee duties July 1, 2026. DOL will produce multilingual human‑trafficking posters (through July 1, 2026). DED participates on a Rural Broadband Task Force and mental‑health advisory committee. DOT and DMV each have one representative on a temporary Nebraska Safety Center Advisory Council. The Rural Broadband Task Force Fund (appropriations/transfers and gifts/grants/bequests) is created, administered by NITC, and terminates July 1, 2026, with remaining balances reverting to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: Beginning July 1, 2026, DAS will assume duties of the Suggestion Award Board and the Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee (both terminated July 1, 2026).

- Department of Health and Human Services: On and after July 1, 2026, DHHS will operate the Critical Incident Stress Management program—coordinating response, providing equipment, recruiting/training peers, appointing a program director (DHHS employee), setting policy, managing planning and budget development, program evaluation, QA/certification, regional identification, and rulemaking. DHHS also takes over appointment/participation roles previously held by the council/other agencies. The State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services will assume duties of the State Advisory Committee on Substance Abuse Services (the latter terminates July 1, 2026).

- Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Motor Vehicles, Department of Transportation: No underlined changes identified.

- Funding/appropriations: Rural Broadband Task Force Fund terminates July 1, 2026; remaining balances transfer to the General Fund. No explicit appropriations or new budget allocations specified.
