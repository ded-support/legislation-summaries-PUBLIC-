Summary

The bill revises agency memberships, duties, and limited fund authorities. Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) gains seats on multiple councils (Climate Assessment Response Committee; Women's Health Initiative Advisory Council; State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services), must annually report detailed child‑welfare data, administers the Women's Health Initiative Fund (gifts/grants/fees; may receive federal funds to the State Treasurer before 7/1/2026; proceeds reimburse initiative/council expenses but not abortion referrals/services), leads the Critical Incident Stress Management Program (expanded duties after 7/1/2026), and provides staff/reimbursement roles on early‑childhood and foster‑care bodies. Department of Economic Development joins the Rural Broadband Task Force and mental‑health advisory. Department of Labor develops multilingual human‑trafficking materials and is added to a Nebraska Safety Center Advisory Council that also includes DOT and DMV. DAS will assume certain committee duties beginning 7/1/2026. A Rural Broadband Task Force Fund is created (may include legislative appropriations; terminates 7/1/2026). No new line‑item appropriations to these agencies are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: Beginning July 1, 2026, DAS assumes duties of the Suggestion Award Board and the Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee. No specific appropriation changes listed.

- Department of Health and Human Services: On and after July 1, 2026 DHHS will operate the Critical Incident Stress Management program (coordination, equipment, recruitment/training, director appointment, planning and budget development, program evaluation, QA, regional support) and will assume certain appointment duties previously by the Council. The State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services will take on substance-abuse advisory duties as the Substance Abuse Committee terminates. No explicit dollar appropriations specified, but DHHS gains budget/management responsibility for the program.

- Departments of Economic Development, Labor, Motor Vehicles, and Transportation: No substantive changes, duties, or funding adjustments for these agencies are included.

- Funding note: Several advisory/task force funds and committees terminate July 1, 2026; the Rural Broadband Task Force Fund balance is directed to the State General Fund.
