Summary

The bill assigns roles and responsibilities to specified agencies and creates several funds and time‑limited bodies. DHHS gains multiple statutory roles (ex officio Climate Assessment Response Committee member; Title V Administrator on the Women's Health Initiative Advisory Council; lead for the Critical Incident Stress Management Program with evolving duties after July 1, 2026; member/staff support roles on several advisory bodies) and is named in enhanced child‑welfare reporting requirements. DAS’s director joins the Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee and, beginning July 1, 2026, DAS assumes duties of the Suggestion Award Board and that committee. DED’s director (or designee) joins the Rural Broadband Task Force and the Housing Office sits on the State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services. DOT, DMV, and DOL each have representatives on the Nebraska Safety Center Advisory Council; DOL must develop multilingual human‑trafficking posters and coordinate placements. Funding changes: a Women's Health Initiative Fund is created (gifts/grants/fees; investable); a Rural Broadband Task Force Fund (Legislative appropriations/transfers and gifts; terminates July 1, 2026 with balances to the General Fund); several reimbursements specified (Early Intervention/Quality Child Care funds; Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: Beginning July 1, 2026 DAS will assume duties of the Suggestion Award Board and the Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee. No specific appropriations or new funding lines are included.

- Department of Health and Human Services: On and after July 1, 2026 DHHS will administer the Critical Incident Stress Management program—coordinating response, supplying equipment, recruiting and training peers, appointing a program director (DHHS employee), setting policy, managing planning and budget development, quality assurance, regional identification, and rulemaking authority. Also, the State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services will assume duties of the State Advisory Committee on Substance Abuse Services (the latter terminates). No explicit new appropriations are specified.

- Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Motor Vehicles, Department of Transportation: no substantive additions, duties, or funding changes appear in the added text.
