Summary

The bill assigns roles, reporting, and budget responsibilities to DHHS, DAS, DED, DOL, DOT and DMV. DHHS is named ex officio on the Climate Assessment Response Committee, must provide/coordinate staff for the Early Childhood Interagency Coordinating Council, serve as nonvoting ex officio on the Foster Care Reimbursement Rate Committee, report annual child‑welfare service data (including budget allocations), administer a newly created Women's Health Initiative Fund (apply/receive/remit funds to the State Treasurer; investable funds managed by the state investment officer), and become lead agency for the Critical Incident Stress Management Program effective July 1, 2026. DAS assumes duties of the Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee and the Suggestion Award Board beginning July 1, 2026. DOL must produce multilingual human‑trafficking posters and sits on a temporary Nebraska Safety Center advisory council alongside DOT and DMV. DED’s director (or designee) and its Housing Office join broadband and mental‑health advisory bodies. A Rural Broadband Task Force Fund (legislative appropriations/transfers, gifts/grants) is created and terminates July 1, 2026, with remaining balances reverting to the General Fund. Climate committee and children’s behavioral health expenses may be reimbursed from specified state funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Beginning July 1, 2026, DAS will assume duties of the Suggestion Award Board and the Vacant Building and Excess Land Committee (both terminate July 1, 2026). No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Also, the State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services will assume duties of the State Advisory Committee on Substance Abuse Services (the latter terminates July 1, 2026).
