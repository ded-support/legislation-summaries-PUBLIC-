Summary

LB184 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. sections governing tobacco licensing to regulate nitrous oxide. It (1) prohibits tobacco specialty stores from selling or distributing objects containing nitrous oxide and makes violations subject to misdemeanor penalties and possible court-ordered license revocation; (2) bars delivery sales of nitrous-oxide-containing objects by licensed retailers and makes knowingly transporting them via common carrier a Class I misdemeanor; and (3) bans sale/distribution of devices specifically designed for inhaling nitrous oxide for recreational use. All unlawfully sold items are subject to seizure, forfeiture, and destruction, with costs charged to the violator. The cigarette tax division of the Tax Commissioner may revoke or suspend affected licenses after notice and hearing. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions and penalties on sale, distribution, and transport of objects containing nitrous oxide, especially by holders of tobacco specialty store licenses (refs. §§28-1420 to 28-1429). Violations carry misdemeanor penalties (ranging from Class III to Class I/II depending on offense and provision), possible license revocation by a court, and designation as a deceptive trade practice under the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. Common carriers that knowingly transport such items are criminally liable. All unlawfully sold items are subject to seizure, forfeiture, and destruction, with costs charged to the person from whom they are seized. Exemptions: shipments to a foreign trade zone under 19 U.S.C. §81a and actions by government employees in official duties. No state agencies are newly created or assigned funding; no appropriations or budget changes are specified.
