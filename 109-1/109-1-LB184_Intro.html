Summary

LB184 prohibits retail and delivery sales of nitrous oxide (and sale of devices specifically designed for recreational inhalation) by tobacco specialty stores and by licensees subject to the state's tobacco licensing statutes. Enforcement and penalties include Class III/II/I misdemeanors (escalating by offense), court-authorized revocation and forfeiture of tobacco licenses, and seizure and destruction of contraband at the violator’s expense. The bill explicitly authorizes the cigarette tax division of the Tax Commissioner to suspend or revoke related licenses under tax statutes. Exceptions include shipments to foreign trade zones and acts by government employees in the course of official duties. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions and penalties on sale, distribution, and transport of nitrous oxide-containing products. Licensees under sections 28‑1420 to 28‑1429 (tobacco specialty store licensees) may not sell or allow removal of such objects; violations carry escalating criminal penalties (Class II → Class I misdemeanors), possible license revocation/forfeiture and delayed reissuance per section 28‑1429. Delivery sales to consumers are banned; common carriers who knowingly transport such items for violators face Class I misdemeanors. Violations also constitute deceptive trade practices under the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. Items sold in violation are subject to seizure, forfeiture, and destruction, with costs borne by the person from whom seized. Exceptions: shipments to a foreign trade zone under 19 U.S.C. 81a et seq. and actions by government employees in official duties. Separate ban on products specifically designed for recreational inhalation with Class III (first) and Class II (subsequent) misdemeanors. No agencies named and no funding or appropriation changes.
