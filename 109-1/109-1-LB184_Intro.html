Summary

LB184 prohibits sale or distribution of nitrous oxide in tobacco specialty stores and by licensed sellers (including delivery sales), and bans sale of devices specifically designed to inhale nitrous oxide for recreational use. Violations carry criminal penalties (Class III up to Class I misdemeanors depending on offense and repetition), authorize seizure, forfeiture, and destruction of products with costs borne by the violator, and allow court-ordered revocation and forfeiture of tobacco licenses with one- and five-year reissuance bars. The cigarette tax division of the Nebraska Tax Commissioner may revoke or suspend licenses under the referenced tobacco statutes for violations and treats contraband cigarettes as subject to seizure and destruction. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions on selling, distributing, or furnishing objects containing nitrous oxide: tobacco specialty stores (licensees under sections 28-1420–28-1429) may not sell or allow removal of such items; delivery sales to consumers are barred; and items designed for recreational inhalation are banned. Penalties escalate from Class III to Class I/II misdemeanors for repeat offenses, courts may revoke/forfeit tobacco specialty store licenses, and common carriers who knowingly transport violative items face Class I misdemeanors. Violations also constitute deceptive trade practices under the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. All violative products are subject to seizure, forfeiture, and destruction at the violator’s expense. Exceptions: shipments to a federal foreign‑trade zone (19 U.S.C. 81a et seq.) and government employees acting in official duties. No changes to funding or appropriations are included.
