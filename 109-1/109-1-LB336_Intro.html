Summary

LB336 creates a Task Force on Supported Employment. Relevant to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and Department of Labor (DOL):

- Membership: the task force includes as nonvoting members an employee of DHHS’s Division of Developmental Disabilities and an employee of DHHS’s Division of Behavioral Health, each appointed by the Governor, and an employee of the Department of Labor appointed by the Governor.

- Deliverable and timing: the task force must submit an electronic report and action plan to the Legislature and Governor by July 1, 2027; the task force terminates December 31, 2027.

No sections in the bill appropriate funds or make budgetary allocations to DHHS, DOL, or the task force.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Task Force on Supported Employment that includes nonvoting appointees from the Department of Labor (one employee appointed by the Governor) and two Department of Health and Human Services employees (one each from the Division of Developmental Disabilities and the Division of Behavioral Health, appointed by the Governor). The task force must review prior supported‑employment reports and evaluations, produce an action plan addressing workforce barriers, untapped potential, projected workforce numbers, and effects of work on benefits, and submit a report by July 1, 2027. It terminates December 31, 2027. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
