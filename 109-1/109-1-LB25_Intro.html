Summary

LB25 appropriates $5,000,000 from the General Fund for FY2025-26 to the Department of Administrative Services, Program 245, exclusively for state aid to support interoperable communications for volunteer departments (per section 35-901) via a grant program. Grants may fund mobile radios, programming, installation and related equipment, require a one-to-one local match, and are capped at $3,500,000 per applicant. The appropriation is restricted to the stated purpose. The act contains an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates $5,000,000 General Fund for FY2025‑26 to the Department of Administrative Services (Program 245) for a state aid grant program to fund interoperable communications for volunteer departments (per §35‑901). Funds may only be used to provide mobile radios, programming, and installation. Grants require a 1:1 local match and are limited to a maximum award of $3,500,000 per applicant.
