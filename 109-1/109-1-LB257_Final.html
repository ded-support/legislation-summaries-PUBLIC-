Summary

This bill directs state licensing and enforcement actions across multiple agencies:

- Department (credentialing): with board recommendations, may issue licenses/certificates based on out-of-state licensure for marriage and family therapists and other mental-health credentials, and implements interstate compact privileges for counselors and occupational therapists. The board gains rulemaking authority over training/certification for occupational therapy modalities (including dry needling) and supervision/delegation limits.

- Department (child care licensing): requires written proof of liability insurance ($100,000 per occurrence) before licensing and continuous coverage thereafter; allows State/political subdivisions to use risk pools or self-insure; inspectors must verify coverage at unannounced inspections and may suspend licenses if coverage is not restored within three business days.

- Nebraska State Patrol/FBI: fingerprinting for national criminal history checks for child care staff, with checks every five years; checks are portable. Department may seek federal funds to offset fingerprinting costs.

- State Fire Marshal and local environmental health personnel: may perform delegated fire and sanitation inspections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions assign duties to "the department" (acting with the recommendation of "the board") to issue licenses or certificates by endorsement to marriage and family therapists and to certify occupational therapists for specific modalities (including dry needling) when board-approved training/exams are met. Inspectors acting for the department must verify required liability insurance at unannounced inspections and investigations; the department will notify licensees and suspend licenses if proof is not provided within three business days, restoring licensure upon verification. The bill repeatedly references the board, the department, and inspectors; no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
