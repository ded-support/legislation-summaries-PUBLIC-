Summary

LB28 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to allow individuals, for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2025, to subtract tip income (to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income) from Nebraska taxable income. The bill amends the income-modification statute administered by the Tax Commissioner (Nebraska Department of Revenue) and repeals the original section. The statute already references and interacts with federal agencies and programs (Internal Revenue Service, U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Office of Personnel Management, and the federal Social Security Act) and state entities (Nebraska National Guard; Nebraska educational savings plan trust; Medical Debt Relief Fund), but LB28 contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state tax subtraction for individuals: beginning with tax years on or after Jan. 1, 2025, taxpayers may subtract tip income (to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income) from their federal AGI for state tax purposes. No specific government agencies or budget appropriations are mentioned.
