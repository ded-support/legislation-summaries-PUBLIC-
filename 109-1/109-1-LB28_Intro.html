Summary

LB28 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to add a new adjustment permitting individuals to subtract tip income (to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income) for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2025. The section continues to reference the Tax Commissioner for rulemaking related to net operating loss and other income modifications. The statute also addresses income rules affecting Nebraska National Guard members and cites federal agencies (U.S. Department of Defense and U.S. Office of Personnel Management) in the context of military retirement reporting. The bill repeals the original section. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision (effective for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2025) allowing individuals to subtract from their federal adjusted gross income any tip income that was included in federal AGI. The text cites the Internal Revenue Code of 1986; no specific government agencies, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
