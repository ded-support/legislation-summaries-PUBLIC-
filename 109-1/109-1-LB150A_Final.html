Summary

LB150A appropriates federal funds to the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice (Program 155) to implement Legislative Bill 150. It provides $146,056 in FY2025‑26 and $147,609 in FY2026‑27. Of those amounts, total expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $97,078 for FY2025‑26 and $101,932 for FY2026‑27.
