Summary

LB510 amends Nebraska tax and Good Life district law. Key agency roles and fiscal changes:

- The Department (state agency administering Good Life districts) retains authority to approve/terminate districts, apply eligibility tests, accept evidence of investment commitments, transmit application materials to affected cities/villages, and—with city consent—approve district development/design standards after notice and hearing.

- State sales tax treatment changed: the reduced 2.75% state rate for transactions inside Good Life districts is extended but, commencing July 1, 2025, applies only after the city or village has imposed a local sales or occupation tax of at least 2.75%.

- Applicants must certify anticipated diversion of state sales tax will be offset by sales taxes on project development.

- Districts: 30-year duration, acreage limits, termination triggers based on investment commitments (10%/3 years; 50%/7; 75%/10).

- Cities must obtain voter approval to appropriate local revenues (including local option sales/use and occupation taxes); elections filed with the election commissioner or county clerk.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a July 1, 2025 change to state sales tax: the statewide rate becomes 5.5%, but transactions within portions of a Good Life district located inside a city or village are taxed at a reduced 2.75% only after that city or village has imposed at least a 2.75% local sales or occupation tax on those transactions. States that, on/after July 1, 2025, transactions in Good Life districts follow the reduced state rate per section 77-2701.02. Directs "the department" to accept evidence so that a property owner or the city/village is treated as the Good Life district applicant for purposes of the Good Life acts, and prohibits the department from terminating a Good Life district within three years of its creation. Bars cities/villages from using eminent domain to acquire property in a Good Life district to give or sell to private parties. No appropriations or budget allocations are included.
