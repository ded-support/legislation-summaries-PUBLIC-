Summary

This bill amends procedures for selecting Nebraska's presidential electors and assigns specific duties to state officials.

- Governor: must serve certificates of appointment by registered or certified mail; notify electors of meeting time/location; include a security feature and vacancy/amendment language in the state's certificate of ascertainment submitted under 3 U.S.C. 5; fill vacancies if electors fail to elect replacements by 3 p.m.

- Secretary of State: receives convention certifications; provide each elector a list and a presidential/vice‑presidential ballot; examine and accept only ballots consistent with each elector’s pledge; affix the state seal to abstracts.

- Board of State Canvassers: open county abstracts, prepare and sign the statewide abstract, and treat the presidential canvass as the return of votes for party electors—electors receiving the highest statewide votes are deemed elected.

Other: bill expressly provides for five presidential electors and requires electors to pledge to vote for the statewide highest‑vote presidential/vice‑presidential candidates. No funding or appropriation changes.
