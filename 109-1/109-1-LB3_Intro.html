Summary

Bill LB3 revises Nebraska law on selection, certification, and duties of presidential electors. Key agency provisions:

- Governor: receives certified elector names from party conventions; serves certificates of appointment by registered/certified mail; designates meeting location and time (2 p.m., first Tuesday after the second Wednesday in December); must include a security feature and vacancy statements in the state's certificate of ascertainment and submit an amended certificate if substitutes are appointed; appoints electors to fill vacancies if not filled by 3 p.m. on meeting day.

- Secretary of State: receives certifications from conventions, is provided lists/ballots for electors, examines and accepts only ballots consistent with electors' pledge, and affixes the state seal to the canvass abstract.

- Board of State Canvassers: authorizes the Secretary of State to open county abstracts and declares presidential vote returns that constitute election of a party’s electors.

Other provisions: conventions select five electors; electors must pledge to vote for the state’s highest‑vote presidential and vice‑presidential candidates; refusal or voting contrary to the pledge vacates the office. No changes to funding or appropriations.
