Summary

Bill LB3 amends Nebraska law governing presidential electors. Key executive and administrative roles:

- Governor: must serve certificates of appointment by registered or certified mail; notify electors of meeting time/location; include a security feature and specific statements when submitting the state's certificate of ascertainment; appoint substitutes for vacancies after the electors fail to fill them by 3 p.m.

- Secretary of State: receives elector certifications from party conventions; must provide each elector with a presidential/vice‑presidential ballot, examine ballots, and accept only those marked consistent with an elector's required pledge.

- Board of State Canvassers: continues to canvass statewide presidential returns as the vote for the corresponding party's electors; declares the electors of the highest‑vote party elected.

- State party organizations: conventions must select and certify five presidential electors to the Governor and Secretary of State.

The bill imposes procedural and certification requirements but contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
