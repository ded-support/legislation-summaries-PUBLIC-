Summary

LB3 amends Nebraska law governing selection and certification of presidential electors. Key agency roles:

- Governor: receive convention-certified elector names; serve certificates by registered/certified mail; notify electors of meeting time; include a security feature in the federal certificate of ascertainment; appoint substitutes for vacancies and submit amended certificates as needed.

- Secretary of State: receive certifications from party conventions; be provided lists by the Governor; supply and examine presidential/vice‑presidential ballots; accept and count only ballots consistent with each elector’s required pledge; affix the state seal to canvass abstracts.

- Board of State Canvassers: authorize opening county abstracts, prepare statewide abstracts, and declare the party whose electors receive the highest statewide vote as elected.

Other changes: conventions must select five presidential electors; electors must pledge to vote for the presidential/vice‑presidential candidates who received the highest number of votes statewide (effectively a statewide winner‑take‑all allocation). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
