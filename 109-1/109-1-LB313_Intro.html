Summary

LB313 amends the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act to (1) clarify subrogation rights of employers and workers' compensation insurers, explicitly allowing employer or insurer subrogation against third parties and permitting recovery beyond amounts actually paid as compensation; excess recoveries, after collection expenses, must be paid promptly to the employee and treated as an advance on future compensation. (2) Update third‑party settlement rules to require written agreement of the employee and the employer's workers' compensation insurer (or the employer if uninsured), or court approval as fair and reasonable. If distribution is contested, the court must deduct recovery expenses, pay one‑third of the remainder to the employee, reimburse the employer/insurer for past or future compensation obligations, and pay any remaining balance to the employee. No agency appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a three-step priority for distributing a recovery (after reasonable collection expenses): (a) one-third of the remainder to the employee or their personal representative; (b) from the next balance, the employer or workers' compensation insurer is reimbursed for all compensation already paid or that it may be obligated to pay; and (c) any remaining balance goes to the employee or their personal representative. No government agencies, departments, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced.
