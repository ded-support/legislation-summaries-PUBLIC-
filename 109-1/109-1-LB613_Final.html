Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: Officials and employees of the Department of Administrative Services are explicitly included among persons prohibited from disclosing confidential tax information; unauthorized disclosure is a Class I misdemeanor.

- Department of Labor: The Tax Commissioner may disclose tax information to the Department of Labor as necessary to administer the Employment Security Law, the Contractor Registration Act, or the Employee Classification Act.

- Department of Revenue: The bill clarifies Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner) duties: collect and remit municipal local-option sales taxes; notify municipalities annually (by March 1) of estimated refunds and, beginning with the 2025 notification, include adjustments for prior-year differences; provide municipalities lists of retailers on request and, upon written request and proper municipal certification, supply confidential sales/use tax returns and return information (10 business days to prepare; secure electronic transmission allowed); accept annual municipality requests for lists of qualifying businesses (due June 30). The Department is protected from liability for municipal impermissible disclosures. No appropriations or budgetary allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: Added duties to provide municipalities with information on qualifying businesses and tax returns. The Tax Commissioner must comply with municipal requests and may disclose returns/return information to a certified municipal employee solely to verify collection/analysis/forecasting, allocation among municipalities, or that taxes are used as voted. Municipalities may annually (by June 30) request from the Department of Revenue a list of businesses that filed for incentives under the Employment and Investment Growth Act, Nebraska Advantage, ImagiNE Nebraska, or Urban Redevelopment Act. Qualifying businesses must provide information to municipalities by June 30 each year. If tax-levy proceeds are received but the originating incorporated municipality is not identified within six months, the amount is credited to the Municipal Equalization Fund.

- Department of Administrative Services & Department of Labor: No changes, funding shifts, or appropriations affecting these agencies are included in the added text.
