Summary

LB613 amends statutes on tax information disclosure and local tax administration as follows:

- Department of Revenue / Tax Commissioner: may provide municipalities lists of retailers and, upon municipality certification, confidential sales and use tax returns/return information to a certified municipal representative (department allowed ten business days to prepare; secure electronic transmission permitted; unauthorized further disclosure is a Class I misdemeanor). The Department of Revenue must notify municipalities of pending refunds and remits monthly collections after deducting refunds and crediting 3% of the remainder to the Municipal Equalization Fund. Municipalities may annually request lists of qualifying businesses that applied for certain tax incentives. The Department of Revenue is insulated from liability for impermissible disclosures by municipalities.

- Department of Administrative Services: named among officials prohibited from disclosing taxpayer information.

- Department of Labor: may receive tax information as necessary to administer Employment Security, Contractor Registration, and Employee Classification laws.

- Budget items: 3% credit to Municipal Equalization Fund; municipality-held amounts for certain tax refunds excluded from specified budget and reserve limitations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue (DOR)

- Requires DOR/Tax Commissioner to provide municipalities, on request, information (excluding detailed business-specific revenue/expense/operations data) and to send such information when requested.

- Allows a certified municipal official to share copies of returns/return information with another municipal employee solely to: (i) verify collection/analysis/forecasting accuracy, (ii) verify allocation among municipalities, or (iii) verify taxes are used as voted.

- Mandates qualifying businesses to supply information to the municipality by June 30 each year.

- Authorizes municipalities to annually (by June 30) request from DOR a list of businesses that filed for incentives under the Employment and Investment Growth Act, Nebraska Advantage, ImagiNE Nebraska, or Urban Redevelopment Act.

- If tax proceeds are received but the originating incorporated municipality is unidentified after six months, funds are credited to the Municipal Equalization Fund.

Department of Administrative Services / Department of Labor

- No changes, funding, or appropriations affecting DAS or DOL are included.
