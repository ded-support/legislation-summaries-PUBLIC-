Summary

- Department of Revenue: retains authority to collect state and incorporated municipal sales/use taxes, remit proceeds monthly after refunds and a 3% deduction to the Municipal Equalization Fund, and notify municipalities of significant refunds with adjusted deduction timing for certain cities. The department must provide municipalities lists of retailers and qualifying businesses on request, supply confidential sales/use tax returns to certified municipal representatives under conditions, certify property tax exemptions, and enforce data‑disclosure requirements for certain Nebraska Advantage large data center projects. Municipal amounts held for sales/use tax refunds are excluded from specified municipal budget and reserve limitations.

- Department of Labor: may receive tax return information necessary to administer the Employment Security Law, Contractor Registration Act, and Employee Classification Act.

- Department of Administrative Services: along with the Tax Commissioner and State Treasurer, its employees are prohibited (Class I misdemeanor) from unauthorized disclosure of taxpayer information.

Department of Revenue

- Tax Commissioner may provide municipalities requested information so long as it does not disclose business-specific revenue, expenses, or operations.

- A certified individual may share returns/return information with another municipal employee solely to verify collection/analysis/forecasting accuracy, allocation between municipalities, or that taxes are used for the voter‑approved purpose.

- Qualifying businesses must supply information to the municipality by June 30 annually. Municipalities may annually request (by June 30) from the Department of Revenue a list of businesses that filed applications for specified state tax‑incentive acts.
