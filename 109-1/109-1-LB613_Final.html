Summary

LB613 amends disclosure and administration provisions involving the Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner), and references the Department of Labor and Department of Administrative Services. Key agency provisions:

- Department of Revenue: retains collection/remittance of municipal local option sales taxes, credits 3% (after refunds) to the Municipal Equalization Fund, and must notify municipalities of pending refund deductions (with revised annual estimate/adjustment reporting beginning 2025). Expands/clarifies authority to provide municipalities lists of retailers, to furnish confidential sales/use tax returns to a certified municipal representative (10 business days to prepare; secure electronic transmission allowed), and to provide lists of qualifying businesses on June 30 annually. For certain Nebraska Advantage/data‑center projects, Tax Commissioner/data disclosure requirements to municipalities are mandated.

- Department of Labor: may receive tax return information necessary to administer Employment Security, Contractor Registration, or Employee Classification laws.

- Department of Administrative Services: referenced in confidentiality prohibition; unauthorized disclosure by its employees is a Class I misdemeanor.

- Budget impact: amounts held by municipalities for specified sales/use tax refunds are excluded from municipal restricted fund, cash reserve, and Property Tax Growth Limitation Act calculations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue: Adds that the Tax Commissioner must, upon request (by June 30 annually), provide municipalities a list of businesses that applied for tax incentives under the Employment and Investment Growth Act, Nebraska Advantage Act, ImagiNE Nebraska Act, or Urban Redevelopment Act, provided the information excludes detailed data on any particular business’s revenue, expenses, or operations. A municipal official certified under this provision may share copies of returns/return information with another municipal employee solely to verify collection/analysis/forecasting, allocation between municipalities, or that taxes are spent as voter‑approved. If tax proceeds are received but the originating incorporated municipality cannot be identified within six months, amounts are credited to the Municipal Equalization Fund.

Department of Administrative Services and Department of Labor: no changes, funding, or appropriations affected.
