Summary

LB352 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-760.05, directing the State Board of Education and the State Department of Education to implement statewide student-achievement and student-discipline tracking using the department’s student identifier system. The State Board must publish a de-identified, searchable discipline database (by district, building, offense, discipline type, school resource officer/law enforcement involvement, restraint, and seclusion). School districts must report individual student incidents (suspensions, expulsions, alternative placements, physical intervention/restraint/seclusion, offenses triggering long-term discipline, and law-enforcement contact) and designate/publish a discipline data coordinator. The Department must annually analyze and electronically report aggregated and disaggregated achievement and discipline data to the Governor, Legislature, school districts, educational service units, and learning communities and post it publicly. The Commissioner of Education may enforce compliance, including accreditation actions. Original section repealed. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for the State Board of Education and the Department of Education to create and publish a de‑identified, publicly searchable student discipline database (searchable by district, school, offense, discipline imposed, school resource officer/law enforcement involvement, restraint, and seclusion). Each school district must designate a discipline data coordinator and post that contact on its website. The department must publish an annual, disaggregated report (by demographics and discipline types and with academic/progress indicators) on its website without identifying individual students. The Commissioner of Education is given enforcement authority to notify noncompliant districts, allow time to cure, and take remedial action—including treating persistent, willful noncompliance as an accreditation rules violation. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
