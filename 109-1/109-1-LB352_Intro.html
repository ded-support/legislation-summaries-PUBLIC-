Summary

LB352 directs the State Board of Education and the State Department of Education to implement statewide, student‑level systems (using the department’s student identifier) to track academic achievement and student discipline. School districts must provide individual assessment and discipline data. The State Board must publish a de‑identified, publicly searchable discipline database (by district, building, offense, discipline type, law‑enforcement involvement, restraint, and seclusion) and require districts to designate and publish contact information for at least one discipline data coordinator. The Department of Education will annually analyze and electronically report aggregated and disaggregated achievement and discipline data to the Governor, Legislature, school districts, educational service units, and learning communities, including progress indicators for specified student groups. The Commissioner of Education may enforce district noncompliance, up to accreditation actions. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- The State Board of Education must create a public, de‑identified, searchable student-discipline database (searchable by district, school, offense, discipline imposed, school resource officer/law‑enforcement involvement, restraint, and seclusion).

- School districts must track incidents and annually report them to the State Board and publish a discipline‑data coordinator contact on the district website. The Department of Education will make the report available on its website and require disaggregation by demographics, discipline types, and academic/progress indicators; reports must not identify students.

- The Commissioner of Education may notify districts of noncompliance and, after a cure period, impose remedial action up to treating noncompliance as an accreditation rules violation.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
