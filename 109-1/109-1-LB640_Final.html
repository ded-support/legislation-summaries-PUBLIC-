Summary

LB640 (Judiciary Committee) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §47-502 concerning persons confined in city or county jails, including those serving custodial sanctions for parole or probation violations. After the first 15 days of confinement, the bill directs that the aggregate remaining term—including the sum of any consecutive sentences imposed at the same time or imposed during confinement—be reduced one day for each day the person does not commit a breach of discipline or other violation of jail regulations. The bill repeals the original section. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are created or altered.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that “the aggregate of” includes the sum of all consecutive sentences a person receives, whether imposed at the same time or at any later time during that sentence or confinement. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
