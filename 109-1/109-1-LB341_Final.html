Summary

The bill requires that when a minor is a ward of the state and a settlement is paid in cash, check, draft, or direct deposit, payment be made into a trust account or subaccount established by the Department of Health and Human Services to receive settlement funds for the ward. The account must earn interest for the benefit of the ward, and DHHS must provide notice of the deposit to the ward by personal service or first-class mail. The bill contains no explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocation for DHHS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions let a custodian settle a minor’s claim (≤ $40,000, excluding medical liens and fees) without court approval if no guardian/conservator exists, with funds placed in restricted accounts or annuities. For Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): if the minor is a state ward and settlement is paid by cash/check/draft/direct deposit, payment must go into a trust account or subaccount established by DHHS that earns interest “for the benefit of the ward and the department,” and DHHS must notify the ward of the deposit by personal service or first-class mail. Withdrawals from such accounts are limited (court order, custodian direction, upon age 19, or death). No appropriations, budget allocations, or explicit funding changes are specified.
