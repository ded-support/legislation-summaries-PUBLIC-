Summary

LB341 requires that when a minor is a ward of the state and a settlement is paid in cash, check, draft, or direct deposit, payment must be made into a trust account or trust subaccount established by the Department of Health and Human Services to receive settlement funds for the ward. The account must earn interest for the ward’s benefit, and the Department must notify the ward of the deposit by personal service or first-class mail. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for settling minor claims when no guardian/conservator exists. For Department of Health and Human Services specifically: if the minor is a state ward and settlement is paid in cash/check/draft/direct deposit, funds must be deposited into a DHHS-established trust account or subaccount that earns interest for the ward; DHHS must notify the ward of the deposit by personal service or first-class mail. Withdrawals from such accounts are restricted (court order, custodian direction, at age 19, or on death). No language in the added text creates new appropriations, funding transfers, or budget allocations for DHHS.
