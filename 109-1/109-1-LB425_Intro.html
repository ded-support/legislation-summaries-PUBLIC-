Summary

LB425 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-3506 to expand homestead tax-exemption eligibility and adjust documentation requirements. The bill adds a new category (effective January 1, 2026) for veterans rated at least 80% but less than 100% service‑connected disabled; the exemption for that category is a percentage of the exempt amount equal to the veteran’s VA disability percentage. The United States Department of Veterans Affairs is the certifying agency for disability status. Applications: veterans in subsection (2)(a) require VA certification every five years; applicants in (2)(b)–(g) must apply annually, but VA certification is required only in years evenly divisible by five. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a property tax exemption tied to a veteran’s VA disability rating: for homesteads described in the new subdivision, a percentage of the exempt amount equal to the veteran’s disability percentage is exempt from taxation. Effective January 1, 2026, eligible recipients include veterans discharged honorable or general who receive compensation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs for service‑connected disability of at least 80% but less than 100%, and qualifying surviving spouses (unremarried or remarried after age 57); excludes those already eligible for total exemption under sections 77‑3526 to 77‑3528. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
