Summary

LB496 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,111 (Rules of the Road) governing animals and animal-drawn vehicles on roadways. Riders/drivers of animals, animal-drawn vehicles, farm tractors, or implements of husbandry retain the rights and duties of vehicle drivers except where inapplicable. If their slowness obstructs traffic they must move to the nearest shoulder, except where subsection (3) applies. Subsection (3) (defining livestock) requires motor vehicle operators to exercise caution when approaching/herding livestock, stop if given a raised-hand distress signal (unless stopping would cause an accident), turn off the engine if requested, and yield right-of-way to livestock on the highway. No state agencies or departments are referenced and there are no funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection defining "livestock" (horse, mule, donkey, cattle, swine, sheep, goat) and requires drivers on a highway to: exercise caution when approaching or passing people leading/herding livestock; promptly stop if given a distress signal (raising a hand), unless moving is necessary to avoid an accident; turn off the vehicle engine if requested by the person leading/driving the livestock; and yield right of way to livestock being led or driven. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
