Summary

Amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,111 to clarify roadway rules for animals and livestock. Riders/drivers of animals, animal-drawn vehicles, farm tractors, and implements of husbandry retain the rights and duties of vehicle drivers and must, when obstructing traffic, pull to the nearest shoulder. Defines "livestock" (horse, mule, donkey, cattle, swine, sheep, goat) and imposes four duties on motorists encountering persons leading/herding livestock: use caution when approaching/passing, promptly stop if given a distress signal (unless stopping would cause an accident), turn off the engine if requested, and yield the right of way to the livestock. Repeals the original section. No state agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
