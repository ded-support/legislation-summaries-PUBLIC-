Summary

LB121 amends section 13‑2039 of the Integrated Solid Waste Management Act. It adds a prohibition on land disposal of solar panels, wind turbine blades, and their component parts. The bill continues existing prohibitions and conditions on disposal of lead‑acid batteries, waste oil, tires, discarded household appliances, and unregulated hazardous wastes (which must be disposed at a licensed hazardous‑waste facility). It preserves the role of "the department" in issuing permits and approvals (e.g., landfill methane recovery, authorizing certain tire‑shred/engineering projects, and receiving race‑sponsor tire disposal plans) and the authority of "the council" to require additional notification information. It also directs state and local governmental entities to prefer composted materials for land maintenance and references compliance with the Engineers and Architects Regulation Act for specified projects. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
