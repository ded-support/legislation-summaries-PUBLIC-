Summary

LB305 assigns several Department of Revenue responsibilities related to the new Preceptorship Tax Credit Act and other tax provisions:

- Requires applicants to submit an affidavit and any other documentation the Department of Revenue requires; the department must determine completeness, approve qualifying applications, and certify approved credit amounts to licensed physicians.

- Caps approvals at $1,000,000 in credits per fiscal year, with priority for rural preceptors and prorating of credits when the annual limit is exceeded.

- Grants the department rulemaking authority to implement the Preceptorship Tax Credit Act.

- Adds the Preceptorship Tax Credit Act to the list of income tax credits in section 77-2715.07.

- Separately, the Department of Revenue may recapture a residence purchase credit if residency requirements are not met.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue duties: require an affidavit and any other documentation it prescribes to verify a licensed physician served uncompensated as a preceptor and met Preceptorship Tax Credit Act requirements; determine completeness and eligibility; approve and certify credit amounts. The department may adopt rules to implement the Act. Fiscal/financial effect: the department may approve up to $1,000,000 in tax credits per fiscal year, give approval priority to rural preceptors, prorate credits among requests received on the day the annual cap is exceeded, and prohibit transfer/sale of credits. No explicit appropriations or direct budget allocations are added; the provision imposes a $1M annual cap on credits (a potential revenue impact).
