Summary

LB202 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §38-2037 (Medicine and Surgery Practice Act). It preserves disciplinary authority (per §§38-178, 38-179 and procedures in §38-196) for physicians, osteopathic physicians, and physician assistants who fail to comply with statutes on signing birth and death certificates (§§71-603.01, 71-604, 71-605, 71-606). It also adds an explicit protections clause: a license to practice medicine and surgery (MD/DO) shall not be subject to disciplinary measures for an applicant’s or licensee’s expression of an opinion in public or professional forums (First Amendment intent). The bill repeals the original §38-2037. No agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a legislative intent protecting First Amendment rights of physicians and osteopathic physicians to express differing medical opinions in public or professional forums.
