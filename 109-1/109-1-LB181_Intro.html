Summary

LB181 requires the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to expand administration and federal-plan filings for the Young Adult Bridge to Independence Act to include young adults not lawfully present in the U.S. It amends verification rules so lawful-presence checks are not required for program participation. DHHS must: (1) submit a Title IV‑E state plan amendment (deadline previously set Oct 1, 2023) to secure IV‑E funding for newly eligible youth; (2) by Oct 1, 2025, apply to CMS to amend the Medicaid state plan to cover program participants (including non‑lawfully present under‑21s); and (3) adopt implementing rules by Oct 1, 2025. If youth are ineligible for Medicaid/CHIP, DHHS must pursue other coverage options, including potential General Fund payments.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the state "department" administering the Young Adult Bridge to Independence program to ensure medical care for participating young adults through federal Medicaid (Title XIX) or CHIP (Title XXI). If federal coverage is unavailable, the department must pursue other options, including payment from the state's General Fund. By October 1, 2025 the department must apply to the federal Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to amend the Medicaid state plan to provide medical assistance to program participants eligible for Medicaid or the federal Children's Health Insurance Program (42 U.S.C. 1397aa et seq.), explicitly including young adults under 21 who are not lawfully present in the U.S. Implementation/effective date references are set for October 1, 2025.
