Summary

LB588 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43-512. It retains that applicants file written applications with the Department of Health and Human Services and directs the department to investigate, report findings to county or authorized attorneys when parents/stepparents can contribute, determine allowance/denial and set monthly assistance levels. Beginning September 10, 2025, the department must calculate and tie the maximum monthly payment level to the annual inflation factor. The department is required to adopt rules for prevention services, work‑transition programs, and transitional benefits (including up to 12 months of medical assistance) and may employ authorized attorneys. No appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are specified.
