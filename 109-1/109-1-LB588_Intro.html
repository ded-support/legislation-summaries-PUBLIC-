Summary

The bill retains and clarifies multiple duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DHHS is the agency to receive applications for aid to dependent children, to investigate applications and file findings with the county or authorized attorney when a parent/stepparent can contribute, and to determine allowance and monthly assistance amounts. Beginning September 10, 2025, the bill ties the maximum monthly payment level to the annual inflation factor. DHHS must adopt rules establishing prevention/work-incentive programs and transitional benefits (including up to 12 months of medical assistance) and hears appeals to the department's chief executive officer. No explicit appropriations, new funding amounts, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language sets September 10, 2025 as the date for a specified calculation and requires that the result be tied/indexed to the annual inflation factor. For the Department of Health and Human Services, this means any DHHS-mandated rate, payment, or threshold governed by that calculation will be adjusted based on the annual inflation factor as of that date. No explicit appropriations, funding amounts, or new budget allocations are included in the underlined text.
