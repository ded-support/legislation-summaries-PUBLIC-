Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43-512 to specify Department of Health and Human Services (department) responsibilities: accepting written applications for aid to dependent children; conducting investigations and referring findings to the county or authorized attorney where parental support is available; and determining allowance and monthly assistance amounts. Payments remain by unit size per §68-1713, and beginning September 10, 2025 the maximum monthly payment level is to be calculated and tied to the annual inflation factor. The department must adopt rules providing services to prevent dependency, establish work incentive/ training programs, and issue rules implementing transitional benefits (five-month transitional payments, child care, and up to 12 months medical assistance) consistent with federal law. Authorized attorneys may be department employees. No appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
