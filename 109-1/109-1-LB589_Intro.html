Summary

LB589 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79‑1142. Key agencies: the State Board of Education (sets the support‑services percentage cap), the Department (Nebraska Dept. of Education) (administers reimbursements, audits documentation, and reports), the State Treasurer and Director of Administrative Services (certify and draw warrants), and the Governor plus the Legislature’s Appropriations and Education Committees (receive annual reports).

Funding and budget provisions:

- Department reimburses school districts/cooperatives 80% of allowable excess special‑education costs in the following fiscal year; if General Fund appropriations are insufficient, payments are pro rata and the remainder is paid from the Education Future Fund.

- The Board’s support‑services percentage cannot exceed (10% minus the Legislature’s set‑aside percentage).

- Separate set‑asides from special‑education appropriations for services/supports must equal prior year requests plus unpaid amounts.

- Payments scheduled monthly (minimum seven installments beginning in December).

- Amounts received under the Extraordinary Increase in Special Education Expenditures Act are subtracted from reimbursements and transferred to the Education Future Fund.

Operational change:

- Special‑education substitute costs are allowable; districts must provide four days/year of professional leave for special‑education staff, supported by substitutes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that allowable and reimbursable special education costs explicitly include substitute service costs. It requires each school district to provide special education staff four days of professional leave per year, with substitute personnel covering those days; the leave must be used onsite solely to complete essential documentation for students on the staff’s caseload. No specific state agency (e.g., department of education) is named and no dollar amounts, appropriations, or new funding allocations are specified—only that substitute costs are eligible for reimbursement.
