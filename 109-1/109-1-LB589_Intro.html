Summary

LB589 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-1142. Key agency actions and fiscal changes:

- State Board of Education: establishes the percentage cap for support‑service reimbursable costs (not to exceed 10% minus the Legislature’s set‑aside percentage).

- Department (Nebraska Department of Education): reimburses school districts, cooperatives, and ESUs 80% of total allowable excess special‑education costs; may prorate payments if General Fund appropriations (minus set‑asides) are insufficient, with remaining portions of the 80% drawn from the Education Future Fund.

- State Treasurer and Director of Administrative Services: Treasurer certifies available General Fund and Education Future Fund balances monthly; Director draws warrants for payments.

- Department must set aside separate appropriation amounts for special‑education and support‑service reimbursements based on estimates and may audit claims.

- Districts must provide four days professional leave for special‑education staff (supported by substitutes).

- Department must report annually (by Nov. 15) to the Governor and the Appropriations and Education Committees on costs and reimbursements.

- Repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that substitute costs tied to special education programs are allowable and reimbursable. Requires each school district to provide special education staff four days per year of on-site professional leave, supported by substitute personnel, solely to complete essential documentation for students on their caseloads. No specific state agency is named and no dollar amounts or new appropriations are specified.
