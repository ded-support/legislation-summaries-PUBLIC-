Summary

LB589 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-1142 (special education reimbursement). Key agencies: State Board of Education (sets percentage cap for support-services reimbursement), Nebraska Department of Education (administers reimbursements, audits documentation, and files annual reports to the Governor, Appropriations Committee, and Education Committee), State Treasurer, and Director of Administrative Services (certifies funds and draws warrants). Funding changes/clarifications:

- Reimburses school districts 80% of total allowable excess special education costs; if General Fund appropriations are insufficient, payments are prorated from the General Fund and the remainder paid from the Education Future Fund.

- Requires Legislature-set aside amounts for support services and special education reimbursements; board cap on support-services percentage tied to that set-aside.

- Payments made monthly (min. seven payments); transfers tied to Extraordinary Increase payments move to the Education Future Fund.

Operational change: allowable costs include substitute costs and districts must provide four days/year professional leave for special-education staff, supported by substitutes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that allowable/reimbursable special education and support costs explicitly include substitute service costs. Requires each school district to provide special education staff four days of professional leave annually, with substitute personnel provided, to be used onsite solely to complete essential documentation for students on their caseloads. No specific state agency is named and no explicit new appropriation or dollar amount is indicated beyond making substitute costs reimbursable.
