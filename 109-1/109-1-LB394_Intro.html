Summary

LB394 updates the Weights and Measures Act's incorporated NIST/NCSNM handbooks and requires copies of those handbooks be filed with the Secretary of State, the Clerk of the Legislature (electronically), and the Department of Agriculture. The bill contains no provisions that explicitly change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to the Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Agriculture — Weights & Measures: The bill increases inspection/testing fees (effective 2025) for specified devices. Examples shown: up to 35‑lb capacity scales $37 → $43; loading‑rack meters $110 → $128; liquid petroleum gas meters $135 → $157; liquid fertilizer/herbicide meters $125 → $145; liquid feed meters $125 → $145; cryogenic meters $176 → $204; mass‑flow (all liquid) $252 → $293; electric vehicle chargers $294 → $340. It also adds that if a fee remains unpaid 90 days after its due date, the related weighing and measuring establishment permit is void. No appropriations, new funding streams, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
