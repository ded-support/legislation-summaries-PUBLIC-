Summary

LB394 adopts updated National Conference on Weights and Measures/NIST handbooks (as of Jan. 1, 2025) by reference and requires that copies of those adopted handbooks be filed with the Secretary of State, the Clerk of the Legislature (electronically), and the Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Agriculture (weights & measures), the bill raises 2025 fees for inspected devices/permits: up through 35 lb capacity $37 → $43; loading‑rack meters $110 → $128; LPG meters $135 → $157; liquid fertilizer/herbicide meters $125 → $145; liquid feed meters $125 → $145; cryogenic meters $176 → $204; mass‑flow meters (liquids) $252 → $293; electric vehicle chargers $294 → $340. It also adds that a weighing/measuring establishment permit is void if the related fee remains unpaid 90 days after its original due date. No explicit appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes are included.
