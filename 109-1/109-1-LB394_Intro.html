Summary

The bill requires that copies of the NIST/NCCWM handbooks adopted by reference be filed with the Department of Agriculture.

Fiscal and fee provisions: annual registration fee set at $4; a device fee (due Aug. 1) is set annually by the director on or before July 1 and may be increased/decreased by the same percentage across device categories but may not exceed specified capped amounts (column B). The director must set fees so estimated annual revenue does not exceed 107% of program cash fund appropriations for the Weights and Measures program and fiscal year‑end cash balances do not exceed 17% of those appropriations. Delinquent accounts incur an administrative fee equal to 50% of annual fees per month (not to exceed 100%); fees and administrative fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Weights and Measures Administrative Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Agriculture (weights & measures functions): the bill raises 2025 inspection/permit fees (rounded to nearest dollar) for specified devices — e.g., up through 35 lb capacity: $37 → $43; loading‑rack meters: $110 → $128; LPG meters: $135 → $157; liquid fertilizer/herbicide meters: $125 → $145; liquid feed meters: $125 → $145; cryogenic: $176 → $204; mass flow (all liquid): $252 → $293; electric vehicle chargers: $294 → $340. It also adds that any unpaid fee 90 days after its due date voids the related weighing/measuring establishment permit. No separate appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are specified.
