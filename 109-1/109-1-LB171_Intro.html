Summary

LB171 amends Nebraska income tax law. Key agencies/actions:

- Tax Commissioner (Nebraska Department of Revenue functions): continues to adjust individual tax brackets for inflation, prescribe and publish updated tax rate schedules, prepare tax tables, and may require taxpayer use of tables. Adjustments reference the Consumer Price Index published by the federal Bureau of Labor Statistics and earlier Internal Revenue Code methodology for limited years.

- Legislature: enacts rate changes described below.

Tax changes:

- Individual: the third bracket is 5.01% through 2025 and drops to 4.99% for taxable years beginning on/after Jan. 1, 2026; the top (fourth) bracket phases down and becomes 4.99% on/after Jan. 1, 2026.

- Corporate: tax set at 5.20% for taxable years beginning in 2025 and 4.99% for taxable years beginning on/after Jan. 1, 2026.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
