Summary

LB171 amends Nebraska income tax law. It (1) modifies individual income tax rate schedules, phasing certain top bracket rates downward so that "rate three" becomes 4.99% and "rate four" becomes 4.99% effective for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026; (2) changes corporate rates to a flat 5.20% for 2025 and a flat 4.99% for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026. The bill retains Tax Commissioner duties to adjust brackets for inflation, publish updated rate schedules and tax tables, and sets indexing based on the Consumer Price Index (Bureau of Labor Statistics) and prior Internal Revenue Code provisions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Original sections are repealed.
