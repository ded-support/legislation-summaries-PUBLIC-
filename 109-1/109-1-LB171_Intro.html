Summary

LB171 amends Nebraska individual and corporate income tax statutes and assigns administrative duties to the Tax Commissioner. Key agency references:

- Nebraska Tax Commissioner: directed to update and publish tax rate schedules and tables, adjust individual tax brackets for inflation using specified methods, and prescribe new schedules when brackets or rates change.

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: CPI data specified for inflation adjustments.

- Internal Revenue Code (section 1(f)) referenced for historical adjustment methodology.

Substantive changes: phased reductions in higher individual income tax rates, culminating in a 4.99% top individual rate for taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2026; corporate income tax set at 4.99% on all taxable income effective Jan. 1, 2026. Original sections are repealed. No direct funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
