Summary

LB629 creates the "County Initiative and Referendum Act," reserving initiative and referendum powers to the qualified electors of each county. It establishes that the Act governs use of initiative to enact and referendum to amend or repeal measures affecting county governance. Key government entities affected: county governments and county boards (measures include ordinances, charter provisions, and resolutions within a county board's legislative authority). The bill defines "circulator" (a person soliciting petition signatures) and excludes actions authorized under the Nebraska Advantage Transformational Tourism and Redevelopment Act. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
