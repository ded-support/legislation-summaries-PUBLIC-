Summary

LB629 creates the County Initiative and Referendum Act, reserving to qualified electors of each Nebraska county the powers of initiative (to enact) and referendum (to amend or repeal) for measures affecting county governance. The bill governs use of those powers at the county level, defines "circulator" (a person soliciting petition signatures) and "measure" (an ordinance, charter provision, or resolution within a county board’s legislative authority), and excludes actions authorized under the Nebraska Advantage Transformational Tourism and Redevelopment Act. No state agencies beyond county governments or county boards are newly created or altered, and the bill includes no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "County Initiative and Referendum Act," reserving initiative and referendum powers to qualified electors of each county and subjecting county governance measures to that Act. It recognizes county boards as the local legislative authority whose ordinances, charter provisions, or resolutions may be enacted or amended/repealed via initiative or referendum. Defines "circulator" (person soliciting petition signatures) and limits "measure" to matters within a county board's authority, excluding actions authorized under the Nebraska Advantage Transformational Tourism and Redevelopment Act. No state agencies or departments are newly created or referenced, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
