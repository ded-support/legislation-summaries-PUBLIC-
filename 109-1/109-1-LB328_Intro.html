Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Economic Development (DED) to administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and the Site and Building Development Fund. DED must include in its annual status report detailed fund activity, including appropriated funds actually expended and the department’s current administration budget and planned distributions. The Site and Building Development Fund remains administered by DED; the bill reiterates legislative intent to transfer $5 million from the General Fund for each of FY2022‑23 and FY2023‑24 and to appropriate $500,000 from the fund for FY2024‑25 to DED for competitive studies of large commercial/industrial sites. The Department of Revenue (via the Tax Commissioner) is referenced for documentary stamp design, and taxpayers may seek declaratory rulings under Department of Revenue rules.
