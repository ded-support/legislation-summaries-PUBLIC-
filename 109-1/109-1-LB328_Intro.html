Summary

LB328 assigns administration and reporting duties to the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED shall administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and the Site and Building Development Fund and include detailed fund reporting in its annual status report (applications funded, recipients, project locations and amounts, leveraged funds, units created, year‑end balance, appropriated funds expended, and the department’s current administration budget and planned distributions). The bill preserves legislative authority to direct transfers from those funds to the General Fund and other named funds. It reiterates legislative intent to transfer $5 million from the General Fund to the Site and Building Development Fund for FY2022‑23 and FY2023‑24 and to appropriate $500,000 from that fund for FY2024‑25 to DED for site studies. The Department of Revenue is referenced as the agency whose rules allow taxpayers to seek declaratory rulings on documentary stamp tax disputes.
