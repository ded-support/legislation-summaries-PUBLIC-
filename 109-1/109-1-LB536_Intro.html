Summary

The bill designates the Department of Economic Development as the “department.” The department will accept grant applications October 1–November 1, 2025, review them in receipt order, and may approve grants (up to $250,000 each, equal to documented private matching funds) subject to available funding. The department may not approve projects started before application and may require repayment if eligibility lapses. The department will administer the newly created Manufacturing Modernization Pilot Investment Fund, which consists of legislative transfers and any gifts, grants, or bequests, and whose assets may be invested by the state investment officer. The department may adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act. The Legislature intends to appropriate [XXXX] dollars from the General Fund to carry out the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions create and charge the Department of Economic Development with administering the new Manufacturing Modernization Pilot Investment Fund and the grant program. The department will accept applications Oct. 1–Nov. 1, 2025, review them in order received, approve eligible applicants subject to available funding, and may adopt rules to implement the program. Grants (up to $250,000) require one-to-one private matching funds and cannot fund projects commenced before application; recipients who later fail eligibility must repay grants. The Fund consists of legislative transfers and any gifts/grants and will be invested by the state investment officer. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate an unspecified [XXXX] dollars from the General Fund.
