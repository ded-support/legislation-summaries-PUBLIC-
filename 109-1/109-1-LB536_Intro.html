Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Manufacturing Modernization Pilot Investment Act and designates the Department of Economic Development as the administering agency. The Department will: accept applications Oct 1–Nov 1, 2025; review them in receipt order; approve eligible projects subject to available funding; issue grants from a new Manufacturing Modernization Pilot Investment Fund (grants up to $250,000 each). Grants must be matched one-to-one with private funds and equal the documented private match (capped at $250,000). The Department may not approve projects begun before application and may require repayment if eligibility lapses. The Fund (to be administered by the Department) comprises legislative transfers and gifts and will be invested per state law. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate an unspecified General Fund amount ([XXXX] dollars). The Department may adopt rules to implement the program.
