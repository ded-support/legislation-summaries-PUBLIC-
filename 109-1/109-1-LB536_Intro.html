Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Manufacturing Modernization Pilot Investment Act" and assigns administration to the Department of Economic Development. The department will: establish and administer the Manufacturing Modernization Pilot Investment Fund (comprised of legislative transfers and gifts), run a grant application window (Oct 1–Nov 1, 2025), process applications in receipt order, approve grants subject to fund availability, notify applicants, enforce one-to-one private matching, and adopt rules to implement the program. Eligible manufacturers (NAICS 31–33, ≥3 years, ≥51% manufactured‑goods revenue, ≥3 FTEs) may receive grants up to $250,000 for smart‑technology capital investments. Funds in the new fund may be invested by the state investment officer. The bill expresses legislative intent to appropriate an unspecified [XXXX] dollars from the General Fund.
