Summary

The bill explicitly grants the Department of Revenue authority to recapture a previously enacted home‑purchase income tax credit if the claimant sells, transfers, or stops using the residence as a primary residence within five years of the taxable year the credit was claimed. Separately, it amends the Property Tax Credit Act, specifying minimum relief amounts through tax year 2024 (e.g., $275M for 2020–2022; $360M for 2023; $395M for 2024), directs past transfers from the General Fund to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund ($105M by Aug 1, 2007; $115M by Aug 1, 2008), and preserves legislative authority to transfer funds from the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund to the General Fund. No other provisions explicitly mention the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a new refundable renter's income tax credit, effective for tax years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan. 1, 2025. The credit applies to Nebraska residents who rent their primary residence and equals the greater of 4% of annual rent or $200, capped at $1,000. It is a refundable credit against tax under the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967. Department of Revenue implications: administer, calculate, process refunds, and update forms/filing systems to accommodate the new credit. The text contains no explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department.
