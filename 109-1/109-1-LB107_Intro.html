Summary

- The bill gives the Department of Revenue explicit recapture authority over the new $5,000 residence purchase income tax credit (extremely blighted areas): if the claimant sells, transfers, or stops using the residence as their primary residence within five years, the Department may recapture the credit.

- The amended Property Tax Credit Act (77-4212) lists statutory funding/allocations: historical and minimum relief amounts include $105 million (tax year 2007), $115 million (2008), $224 million (2017), $275 million (tax years 2020–2022), $360 million (2023), and $395 million (2024). It also retains specified transfers from the General Fund to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund ($105M by Aug 1, 2007; $115M by Aug 1, 2008) and preserves legislative authority to transfer funds from the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a new refundable renter tax credit effective for tax years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan. 1, 2025. The Department of Revenue must administer a credit against Nebraska income tax for qualifying residents who rent their primary residence equal to the greater of 4% of rent paid or $200, capped at $1,000. Because the credit is refundable, DOR will process refunds and handle eligibility verification, form and system changes, and taxpayer guidance. The provision contains no explicit appropriations, funding source, or budget allocation for DOR implementation or to cover the revenue impact.
