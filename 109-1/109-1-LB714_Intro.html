Summary

LB714 (operative Jan. 1, 2026) revises motor vehicle tax and fee collection and allocations. The department (DMV) will annually determine taxes/fees and notify registrants. County treasurers retain 2% of collected motor vehicle tax and 2% of motor vehicle fee for administrative costs. The State Treasurer will receive 1.3% of motor vehicle tax proceeds for credit to the Vehicle Title and Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund. Remaining motor vehicle tax proceeds are allocated by tax district: generally 40% to counties, 37% to local school systems/districts, and 23% to cities/villages, with specified exceptions (nonmunicipal districts and counties containing a metropolitan-class city). Motor vehicle fees fund the newly created Motor Vehicle Fee Fund; the State Treasurer distributes it quarterly (70% to counties, 30% to municipalities) pro rata based on recent Highway Allocation Fund allocations. Funds are investable by the state investment officer and restricted to roads, bridges, and streets.
