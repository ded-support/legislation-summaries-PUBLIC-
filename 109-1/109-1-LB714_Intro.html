Summary

LB714 amends motor vehicle tax and fee administration. The department (statutory motor vehicle agency) will continue to determine and notify registrants of annual taxes and fees. County treasurers retain 2% of motor vehicle tax proceeds and 2% of motor vehicle fees for costs. The State Treasurer receives 1.3% of motor vehicle tax proceeds for credit to the Vehicle Title and Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund. Remaining motor vehicle tax proceeds are allocated by tax district: generally 40% county, 37% local school system/district, 23% city/village (special allocations for non‑city districts and counties with a metropolitan class city). A Motor Vehicle Fee Fund is created: counties receive 70% and municipalities 30% of the fund quarterly in proportions matching recent Highway Allocation Fund distributions. Funds are for road, bridge, and street purposes. Operative January 1, 2026.
