Summary

LB714 amends motor vehicle tax and fee administration (effective Jan. 1, 2026). The Department (responsible for annual tax/fee determinations and notices) will have county treasurers retain 2% of motor vehicle tax proceeds and 2% of motor vehicle fees for costs. The bill directs a 1.3% transfer of tax proceeds to the State Treasurer for credit to the Vehicle Title and Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund. Remaining motor vehicle tax proceeds are allocated to county (40%), local school systems (37%), and city/village (23%) with specified exceptions (63% county where no city/village; in counties with a metropolitan-class city, 38% county/25% city). Motor Vehicle Fee Fund receipts are remitted to the State Treasurer, invested by the state investment officer, and distributed quarterly (70% to counties, 30% to municipalities) for road, bridge, and street purposes. Original sections repealed.
