Summary

LB714 revises administration and revenue distribution for motor vehicle taxes and fees. The Department (motor vehicle agency) must annually determine and notify registrants of tax and fee amounts. County treasurers may retain 2% of motor vehicle tax proceeds and 2% of motor vehicle fees for costs. The bill directs 1.3% of motor vehicle tax proceeds to the State Treasurer for credit to the Vehicle Title and Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund; the remainder is allocated to counties (40%), local school systems (37%), and cities/villages (23%) with specified exceptions (no municipality or metropolitan-class city adjustments). County treasurers remit motor vehicle fees to the State Treasurer for the Motor Vehicle Fee Fund, which is created and distributed quarterly: 70% to counties and 30% to municipalities in proportions matching recent Highway Allocation Fund shares. Funds are investible by the State Investment Officer and restricted to road, bridge, and street purposes. Operative Jan 1, 2026.
