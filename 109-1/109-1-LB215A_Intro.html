Summary

LB215A appropriates General Fund dollars to the Nebraska Board of Parole (Program 358) to implement Legislative Bill 215 (2025). It provides $122,000 for FY2025‑26 and $125,966 for FY2026‑27. Of those amounts, total expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $85,400 for FY2025‑26 and $88,015 for FY2026‑27.
