Summary

LB402 amends the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act to add the Department of Labor as a claimant for unemployment-benefit overpayments and to authorize the Department of Labor to submit certified debts ($25+) to the electronic collection system. The Department of Revenue, in consultation with the Department of Health and Human Services and the Department of Labor, must develop and implement the secure collection system; outstanding state tax liability is defined to include liabilities administered by the Department of Revenue, the Department of Labor, and the Department of Motor Vehicles. Remittances are to be credited pro rata: first DHHS-certified debts, then Department of Labor-certified debts, then outstanding state tax liability. DHHS, the Department of Labor, and the Department of Revenue share confidentiality, notification, hearing, and rulemaking duties. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Department of Labor

- Authorized to establish/set off casino, pari‑mutuel, sports wagering, and cash‑device winnings against debts assigned to it for unemployment overpayments (section 48‑663.01).

- May submit certified debts of $25+ to collections (unless legitimately disputed); overpayment debts submitted continuously and may fluctuate with actual amounts.

- Upon Department of Revenue remittance notification (sec. 9‑1306), Labor must notify the obligor within 20 days, providing basis, intent to apply winnings, 30‑day contest right and hearing address; mailed hearing requests are effective on mailing. Labor must hold hearings, adjust amounts if incorrect, and allow appeals under the Administrative Procedure Act.
