Summary

LB402 expands the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act and the Employment Security Law to involve the Department of Labor, Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and Department of Revenue (and references the Department of Motor Vehicles in the definition of "outstanding state tax liability"). Key agency provisions:

- Department of Revenue will develop a secure electronic collection system in consultation with DHHS and the Department of Labor.

- DHHS and the Department of Labor may submit certified debts of $25+ (including unemployment overpayments) to the system; Revenue may submit outstanding state tax liabilities (including liabilities administered by DMV and Labor).

- Operators remit deducted amounts to Revenue; Revenue credits payments pro rata: DHHS first, then Labor, then outstanding tax liabilities.

- Revenue, DHHS, and Labor must notify winners and provide contest/hearing rights; confidentiality and rulemaking authority for Revenue, DHHS, and Labor are included.

No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor (DOL)

- May set off casino, parimutuel, sports-wagering, or cash-device winnings against debts assigned to DOL for unemployment insurance overpayments (section 48-663.01).

- May submit certified debts of $25+ to the collection system (unless legitimately disputed); collections are continuous and amounts can fluctuate.

- May apply winnings pro rata against certified debts until satisfied.

- After Department of Revenue notification under §9-1306 of a remittance related to a DOL-certified debt, DOL must notify the obligor within 20 days, provide claim basis, intent to apply winnings, 30-day contest period and hearing address; hearing requests are effective on mailing, hearings must be granted and amounts adjusted if incorrect, appeals follow the Administrative Procedure Act.

Department of Revenue

- Role limited to providing remittance notifications to trigger DOL action.
