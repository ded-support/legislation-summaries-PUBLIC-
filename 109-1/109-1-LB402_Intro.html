Summary

LB402 amends the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act to: add the Department of Labor as a claimant able to submit certified debts (overpayments of unemployment benefits under the Employment Security Law) for setoff against casino/parimutuel/sports-wagering/cash-device winnings; require the Department of Revenue, in consultation with the Department of Health and Human Services and the Department of Labor, to develop and operate the electronic collection system; permit DHHS and DOL to submit certified debts of $25+; specify pro rata remittance crediting by the Department of Revenue (first DHHS, then DOL, then outstanding state tax liability); assign notice, hearing, confidentiality, and appeal processes to DHHS, DOL, and Revenue; and authorize DHHS, DOL, and Revenue to adopt rules. The Department of Motor Vehicles is added to the definition of agencies administering outstanding state tax liability. No funding or appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor

- May set off casino, parimutuel, sports-wagering, or cash-device winnings against unemployment benefit overpayments assigned to the department.

- May submit certified debts of $25+ to the collections system unless legitimately disputed; submissions are continuous and debt amounts may fluctuate.

- Winnings can be credited pro rata against certified DoL debts until satisfied.

- Must send written notice to the obligor within 20 days after Department of Revenue remittance under §9-1306, detailing the claim, intent to apply winnings, a 30-day contest period, hearing address, and waiver consequences.

- Hearing requests are effective on mailing; DoL must grant hearings, adjust amounts if incorrect, and appeals follow the Administrative Procedure Act.

Department of Revenue

- Identified only as the remitting notifier under §9-1306; no funding changes.

Department of Health and Human Services; Department of Motor Vehicles

- No substantive changes or funding/appropriation actions noted.
