Summary

LB612 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §24‑514 to require the State of Nebraska, using funds appropriated to the Nebraska Supreme Court, to pay 50% of county courts’ operational costs. The statute continues to require the state (via Supreme Court appropriations) to pay all salaries, benefits, and education/travel expenses for county court judges and employees. The bill also preserves that property purchased by a county for county court use remains county property and repeals the original section. Agencies affected: State of Nebraska (budget/general fund), the Nebraska Supreme Court (appropriation recipient/administrative payer), county courts, and county governments.
