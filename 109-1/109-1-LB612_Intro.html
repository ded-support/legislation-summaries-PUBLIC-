Summary

LB612 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §24-514 to expand state funding for county courts. It requires the State of Nebraska, using funds appropriated to the Nebraska Supreme Court, to continue paying salaries, benefits, and education/travel expenses for county court judges and staff and to also pay 50% of county courts’ operational costs. The bill preserves that property purchased as a county court expense remains county property and repeals the original section. Agencies affected: State of Nebraska (executive funding responsibility), the Nebraska Supreme Court (source/manager of appropriated funds), county courts, and counties. No specific dollar amounts are set.
