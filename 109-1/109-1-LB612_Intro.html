Summary

LB612 (2025) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §24-514 to require the State of Nebraska, using funds appropriated to the Nebraska Supreme Court, to pay fifty percent of county courts' operational costs. The statute already directs the state (via Supreme Court appropriations) to cover all salaries, benefits, and judge/employee education and travel expenses for county courts. The bill preserves that county-owned property purchased as a county court expense remains county property and repeals the original section. Agencies affected: Nebraska Supreme Court (appropriation authority), county courts, and county governments.
