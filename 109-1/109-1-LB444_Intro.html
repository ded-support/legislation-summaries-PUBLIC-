Summary

LB444 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75‑307 to clarify regulatory insurance requirements for certificated intrastate motor carriers (common and contract), motor carriers transporting household goods under §75‑304.03, and motor carriers transporting railroad employees under §75‑304.04. The commission is authorized to prescribe rules for filings, approvals, and maintenance of surety bonds, insurance policies, self‑insurer qualifications, or other securities; to require bonds or insurance to cover judgments for bodily injury, death, or property loss; and to require security to compensate shippers. When setting required policy rates, the commission must require at least $1,000,000 in uninsured/underinsured coverage per occupant. The commission may classify carriers by operational hazard and household‑goods value. The section excludes transportation network companies. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
