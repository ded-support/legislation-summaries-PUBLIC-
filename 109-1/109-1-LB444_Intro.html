Summary

LB444 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75-307 to require the commission to administer proof-of-coverage, bonding, self-insurance or other security filings for certificated intrastate motor carriers, motor carriers transporting household goods under §75-304.03, and motor carriers transporting railroad employees under §75-304.04. The commission retains discretion to set amounts, require bonds or policies to cover property losses, and classify carriers by operational hazards and household-goods value. The bill adds an explicit minimum of $1,000,000 in uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage per occupant for required policies and continues to exempt transportation network companies. The measure repeals the original section. No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
