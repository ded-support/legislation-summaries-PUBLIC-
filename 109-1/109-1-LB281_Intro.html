Summary

LB281 revises Nebraska nursing governance. It consolidates and clarifies statutory references to the Board of Nursing (amending sections 38-204, 38-605, 38-703, 38-904) and removes separate qualifications and duties for a Board of Advanced Practice Registered Nurses. The State Board of Health remains responsible for appointments and must attempt representative membership. The Board of Nursing composition is specified: eight registered nurses, two licensed practical nurses, and three public members, with registered-nurse seats delineated (including four advanced practice registered nurses). It updates qualifications and staggered terms for APRN members (effective Jan 1, 2026) and sets preferred APRN representation (CRNA, nurse practitioner, clinical nurse specialist, certified nurse midwife). The board’s duties are harmonized, including administering the Nurse Licensure Compact and reporting to the coordinated licensure information system; the statutory allowance for disclosure of personal identifying information to that system is included. Sections 38-205, 38-206, and 38-2307 are repealed. No funding or appropriation changes are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Starts Jan. 1, 2026, terms for advanced practice registered nurses on the board are staggered: initial appointments of one 3‑year, one 4‑year, and one 5‑year term; all subsequent appointments are five years. The board’s preferred APRN composition is one certified registered nurse anesthetist, one nurse practitioner, one clinical nurse specialist, and one certified nurse midwife; if that mix isn’t possible, vacancies may be filled from the applicant pool. No state agencies beyond the board are newly created or referenced, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
