Summary

LB281 consolidates and updates Nebraska nursing regulation. It removes a separate Board of Advanced Practice Registered Nurses and standardizes multiple statutory references to the Board of Nursing. The bill revises Board of Nursing composition and member qualifications (details for practical nurse, associate/diploma, baccalaureate educators, staff nurses, nursing service administrators, LPN members, and APRN members), prescribes APRN member term lengths effective January 1, 2026, and sets preferred APRN representation (CRNA, NP, CNS, CNM). The State Board of Health retains appointment responsibilities and representational directives. The board’s duties are clarified to include administering the Nurse Licensure Compact and authorize the department to report personal identifying information, including social security numbers, to the compact’s coordinated licensure information system. Sections 38-205, 38-206, and 38-2307 are repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made or changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds provisions, effective Jan. 1, 2026, governing advanced practice registered nurses serving on the board: initial staggered appointments of one three‑year, one four‑year and one five‑year term, with all subsequent appointments set at five years. Specifies preferred APRN representation on the board: one certified registered nurse anesthetist, one nurse practitioner, one clinical nurse specialist and one certified nurse midwife; if that composition cannot be met, vacancies may be filled from the applicant pool. No state agency beyond "the board" is newly created or identified and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
