Summary

- Secretary of State shall contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of election forms and provide instructions to the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) about voter-identification requirements and how to obtain free photographic ID.

- The Secretary of State and the DMV must enter an agreement to match the statewide voter registration list with DMV records (including citizenship) to verify registration information. The DMV will prescribe a voter-registration portion on driver’s license/ID and commercial license/permit applications, transmit applicant registration data (and digital image/signature) to county election officials within set timelines, and may still issue credentials if the voter-registration portion is incomplete or declined.

- Social Security numbers may be furnished to the Department of Revenue under section 77-362.02.

- Veteran designation on credentials requires registration with the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs registry.

- No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
