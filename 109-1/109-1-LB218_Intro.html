Summary

LB218 assigns and clarifies agency roles related to voter registration and driver licensing. Key provisions:

- Department of Administrative Services: Secretary of State may contract with DAS for storage and distribution of election forms.

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): required to enter into data‑matching and information‑sharing agreements with the Secretary of State to verify voter registration (including citizenship), prescribe and transmit voter registration portions on license/ID and specialty permit applications, and provide digital images/signatures to the Secretary of State. DMV application language must advise applicants that their information will be used to register or update voter records and permit an opt‑out.

- Department of Revenue: may receive social security number information as authorized under section 77-362.02.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: DMV veteran‑designation on licenses/cards available only if registrant is enrolled with the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs registry.

No explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds voter‑registration duties for the Department of Motor Vehicles: DMV must collect and transmit an applicant’s voter information to the election commissioner/county clerk unless the applicant affirmatively opts out. Applicants must be advised that their information will be used to register or update their registration and given an opportunity to decline. DMV may still issue licenses/IDs if the voter portion is incomplete or refused, but must transmit whatever information was provided. Commercial learner’s permit/commercial driver’s license applicants must answer the voter‑registration question. No provisions affect the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Revenue, or Department of Veterans’ Affairs, and no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
