Summary

LB218 requires/updates interagency actions and data-sharing:

- Department of Administrative Services: Secretary of State shall contract with DAS for storage and distribution of election forms.

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): directs DMV to (a) enter agreement with Secretary of State to match DMV database with the statewide voter registration list and to prescribe/collect a voter‑registration portion on license, ID, employment‑permit, medical‑hardship, and commercial license applications; (b) transmit registration data (including digital image/signature) to election officials and may issue credentials even if registration portion is incomplete; and (c) provide voter‑registration advisement language to applicants.

- Department of Revenue: DMV may furnish social security information to the Department of Revenue under specified statutes.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: veteran designation on credentials requires registration with the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs.

No appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Motor Vehicles: Adds a voter‑registration workflow for driver’s license/state ID/CLP applicants. Applicant data will be transmitted to the county election official unless the applicant affirmatively opts out; applicants must be informed that their information will be used to register or update voter records and be given the opportunity to decline. The DMV may still issue licenses/IDs if the voter‑registration portion is incomplete or refused, but any completed portion (even incomplete) will be transmitted. Commercial learner’s permit/commercial driver’s license applicants must answer the voter‑registration question.
