Summary

- Secretary of State shall contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of election forms.

- The Department of Motor Vehicles must: enter into an agreement with the Secretary of State to match DMV records with the statewide voter registration list (including citizenship verification); incorporate a voter‑registration portion into operator's‑license, state‑ID, employment driving permit, medical hardship permit, and commercial license/CLP application forms; transmit applicant data (including digital image/signature) electronically to county election officials; and may still issue licenses/IDs if the voter‑registration portion is incomplete or declined.

- Social Security number use is authorized to furnish information to the Department of Revenue under section 77‑362.02.

- Veteran designation on licenses requires registration with the Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs registry.

- No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (underlined additions):

- Department of Motor Vehicles / Department of Administrative Services: Applicants for operator's licenses or state ID must be given a voter-registration portion that is transmitted to the county election official unless the applicant expressly opts out. Applicants must be advised: "We will use your information to update your voter registration record or register you to vote," and given an opportunity to decline. If the voter portion is partially completed or refused, the department may still issue the license/ID but must transmit whatever voter information was provided. Commercial learner's permit/commercial driver's license applicants are required to answer the voter-registration question.

- Department of Revenue / Department of Veterans' Affairs: No changes or responsibilities in the added text.

- Funding/appropriations: None specified.
