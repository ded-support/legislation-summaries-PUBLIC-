Summary

The bill directs the Department of Revenue to annually calculate and certify state replacement payments to educational service units (ESUs) that lose property‑tax levy authority. Certification goes to the State Treasurer and each ESU. Payment amounts: for 2029, equal to the ESU’s property taxes for tax year 2027 increased by 3.5%; for 2030 and thereafter, each ESU’s prior‑year distribution increased by 3.5%. Distributions are made in two equal installments, on or before March 31 and August 31 each year.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language eliminates educational service units' property tax levy authority beginning FY2028-29 and creates a state-funded replacement payment stream beginning in 2029. For 2029 each ESU receives its 2027 levy amount increased 3.5%; for 2030+ each ESU receives the prior year distribution increased 3.5%. The Department of Revenue is assigned to annually calculate each ESU's distribution and certify those amounts to the State Treasurer and the ESU. Payments are made in two equal installments, on or before March 31 and August 31. The excerpt mandates state distributions but contains no separate appropriation or detailed budget-account language.
