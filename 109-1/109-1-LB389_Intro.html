Summary

The bill directs state distributions beginning in 2029 to offset educational service units' lost property-tax revenue after elimination of their levy authority. For 2029 each ESU's payment equals its property taxes for tax year 2027 increased by 3.5%; for 2030 and thereafter the amount equals the prior year's distribution increased by 3.5%. The Department of Revenue is charged to annually determine and certify each ESU's distribution amount to the State Treasurer and the ESU. Distributions are made in two equal payments, on or before March 31 and August 31.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key points affecting the Department of Revenue (DOR):

- Starting FY 2028-29, educational service units (ESUs) lose authority to levy property taxes.

- Beginning in 2029 the state must pay ESUs amounts to offset lost levy revenue: for 2029 each ESU receives its tax‑year 2027 levy amount increased by 3.5%; for 2030 onward each ESU receives the prior year's distribution increased by 3.5%.

- The DOR is required to annually calculate each ESU's distribution amount and certify those amounts to the State Treasurer and the ESU.

- Payments are made in two equal installments: on or before March 31 and on or before August 31.

- The provision creates a recurring state payment obligation to ESUs but contains no separate appropriation language or specified funding source in the added text.
