Summary

The bill requires the Department of Revenue to annually determine and certify to the State Treasurer and each educational service unit (ESU) the state distribution amounts that will replace ESU property tax levies beginning in 2029. Funding is defined as: for 2029, the ESU payment equals the property taxes levied by the ESU for tax year 2027 increased by 3.5%; for 2030 onward, each ESU payment equals the prior year's payment increased by 3.5%. The Department of Revenue must certify those amounts and distributions are to be made in two equal payments—on or before March 31 and on or before August 31.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue duties: Beginning in 2029 the Department of Revenue must annually calculate and certify to the State Treasurer and each educational service unit (ESU) the state distribution amounts that replace ESU property-tax levies. Policy/funding changes: ESUs lose levy authority beginning FY2028-29; the state will pay ESUs amounts equal to each ESU’s tax-year-2027 levies increased 3.5% for 2029, then increase each subsequent year’s distribution by 3.5%. Payments are made in two equal installments (on or before March 31 and August 31). No explicit appropriation or change to the Department of Revenue’s budget is included in the added text (only a recurring administrative certification and calculation duty).
