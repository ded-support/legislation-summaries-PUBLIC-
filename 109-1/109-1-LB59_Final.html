Summary

LB59 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §2-3224 governing natural resources districts. It authorizes district fund disbursements not only by written instruments signed by the treasurer (or an authorized signer) but also by electronic payment systems, explicitly including electronic funds transfers and automated clearinghouse transfers. The bill requires the treasurer’s written authorization for others to sign to be filed with the district secretary. If the treasurer’s bond does not cover losses from fraudulent or unauthorized acts by such authorized signers, the district must procure and file a corporate surety bond approved by two district officers (not the treasurer). The secretary must report newly filed or changed bonds at each board meeting. No appropriations or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds payment and signature provisions for the treasurer: defines acceptable payment methods to include (a) checks signed by the treasurer and (b) electronic payment systems, specifically electronic funds transfers and automated clearinghouse transfers. Also authorizes the treasurer to designate another person to sign on the treasurer’s behalf and establishes delegation/authorization procedures for such signatures. No other agencies or departments are referenced and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
