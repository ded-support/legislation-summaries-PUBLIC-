Summary

LB59 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §2-3224 governing natural resources districts. It authorizes district funds to be disbursed not only by written instrument signed by the treasurer (or an authorized designee) but also by electronic payment systems, explicitly including electronic funds transfers and automated clearinghouse transfers. The bill requires any written authorization permitting another to sign for the treasurer be filed with the district secretary. If the treasurer’s bond does not cover losses caused by authorized signatories, a corporate surety bond must be procured and filed, approved by two district officers other than the treasurer. The secretary must report bond filings or changes at each board meeting. The original section is repealed. No appropriations or budget allocations are included.
