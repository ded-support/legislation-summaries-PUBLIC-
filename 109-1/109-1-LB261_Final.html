Summary

The bill establishes biennial appropriations, transfers, and administrative authorities for multiple agencies and tasks DAS oversight. Department of Administrative Services (DAS) is authorized to draw warrants, time large General Fund transfers (including $422M/$442M to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund, $780M/$808M to School District Property Tax Relief, and $242M/year to the Education Future Fund), monitor agency appropriations (including IMS/OCIO), create/administrate funds and reappropriations, and coordinate DCS decommissioning. Department of Correctional Services: major operating appropriations (~$395.9M → $403.3M total) and GF of $363.1M → $375.1M, plus authorization of $135M (FY26) and $10M (FY27) from the Capital Construction Fund for a ~1,512‑bed facility and set‑asides for reentry/pre‑apprenticeship grants. DHHS: large program appropriations (Administration ~$639.7M → $657.3M; Medical Assistance ~$5.71B → $5.657B; Developmental Disabilities ~$535.5M → $544.2M) and targeted public‑health/state‑aid grants. Other notable allocations: Agriculture $22.11M → $22.64M; Banking & Finance enforcement ~$7.99M and $1.95M annually; Labor Division of Employment ~$61.9M → $62.7M; DMV enforcement reduced ~$54.4M → $44.5M with CF reappropriations; Revenue increases for Homestead, school/property tax credits; DOT transfers from Highway Cash Fund to Roads Operations $543.9M → $550.7M and caps for salaries; Veterans' Affairs ~$100.0M → $102.2M; Military Department emergency, tuition assistance and match construction funding.
