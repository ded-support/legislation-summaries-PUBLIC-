Summary

The bill centralizes extensive budget and administrative authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS): drawing warrants, timing and directing major transfers and reappropriations, monitoring allotments and agency appropriations, administratively transferring certified unexpended balances and salary‑limit exceptions, accepting gifts and creating cash/revolving/trust funds, and overseeing OCIO/Information Management Services and state building/capital programs.
