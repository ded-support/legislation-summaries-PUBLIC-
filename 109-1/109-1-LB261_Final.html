Summary

The bill establishes agency appropriations, transfer authorities, and monitoring duties across multiple state agencies. Department of Administrative Services (DAS) must draw warrants, create/accept funds, administratively adjust salary‑limit lines, schedule major cash transfers (Property Tax Credit, School District Credit, Education Future Fund and many cash‑fund transfers), oversee state building projects (Capitol HVAC $33.33M) and monitor appropriations for Agriculture, Banking, Economic Development, Insurance, Labor, Information Management Services (Program No.172/OCIO), Correctional Services, Veterans’ Affairs, Environmental programs, and others. Key agency funding highlights: Agriculture ~$22.11M (FY26); Banking & Finance enforcement funds ~$9.94M; Economic Development programs ~$224M combined across programs; Labor Division of Employment ~$61.9–$62.7M; Insurance enforcement ~$14.6M; Revenue major tax relief credits (School Property Tax Credit $780M/$808M; Homestead Exemption ~$160M/$170M); Transportation large transfers/construction ($543.9M/$550.7M to Roads Operations; construction ~$1.04B/yr); Motor Vehicles enforcement and plate funds (multi‑million transfers); Correctional Services GF operations ~$363M/$375M plus $135M NCCF capital (FY26) for new facility contingent on decommissioning; DHHS totals in the billions (Medical Assistance ~$5.7B) with numerous public‑health and program grants; Veterans’ Affairs ~$100–102M; Military Dept. program funding and construction match.
