Summary

The bill centralizes budget authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS): DAS may draw warrants up to enacted appropriations, accept gifts/create funds, reappropriate certified encumbrances/unexpended balances, administratively transfer appropriations and salary limits, and direct specific cash transfers (notably Property Tax Credit $422M/$442M; School Property Tax Relief $780M/$808M; Education Future Fund $242M/year). DAS must monitor appropriations across many agencies and programs.

Key agency appropriations and changes:

- Agriculture (Program No.78): $22.113M (FY25‑26) and $22.636M (FY26‑27), incl. $653k GF, $420k FF, $300k GF for AgrAbility.

- Banking & Finance enforcement: $7.994M (Financial Institutions CF) and $1.951M (Securities CF) annually.

- Insurance enforcement: $14.581M/year (incl. FF).

- Labor (Employment Division): ~$61.9M/$62.7M with ~$9.88M CF workforce state aid and $2.5M FF.

- DMV: Enforcement CF $54.36M/$44.48M, plate funds and up to $8M CF reappropriation.

- Revenue: Homestead Exemption GF $159.917M/$170.303M; large property tax credit transfers.

- DHHS: Medical Assistance ~$5.71B/$5.657B with multiple CF/FF earmarks (behavioral health, tobacco cessation, etc.).

- Correctional Services: Operations GF $363.06M/$375.14M; NCCF site‑planning $135M (FY25‑26) and $10M (FY26‑27) for a ~1,512‑bed facility.

- DOT: Highway Cash transfers to Roads Ops $543.9M/$550.7M; major construction/maintenance appropriations.

- Veterans’ Affairs: $100M/$102.2M total funding; Military Department receives emergency and federal project funding and capital reappropriations.

- Information Management/OCIO: Operations Revolving Fund ~$133.74M/$135.16M; DAS to monitor IMS programs.
