Summary

LB417 assigns the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska authority to administer the Nebraska Promise Program and enacts the College Promise Act. It creates a College Promise Fund administered by the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education to reimburse community and state colleges for tuition waivers (eligible Nebraska residents with family income < $65,000, GPA ≥2.5; up to 2 years at community college or 4 years at state college). Colleges must waive remaining tuition after federal grants and state scholarships; the Legislature intends to fully fund the Fund annually equal to total waiver value. The state investment officer may invest idle Fund moneys per existing investment statutes. The bill also amends duties of the State Board of Education and State Department of Education (assessment, reporting, career‑readiness tests) and creates a Governor‑appointed technical advisory committee. An emergency clause makes the act effective upon approval.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions identify the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education as administrator of a new "College Promise Fund" to distribute money to Nebraska community colleges and state colleges; the fund will consist of legislative transfers and the Legislature intends to fully fund it each year equal to the total value of tuition waivers. The state investment officer may invest available fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. Community colleges/state colleges must verify eligibility, notify applicants within 45 days (with appeal rights through the Coordinating Commission), and waive 100% of remaining tuition after federal grants and state scholarships for eligible low‑income Nebraska residents. The State Board (and the Department) must recommend and implement career‑readiness assessments (free to 12th‑grade public students) and require school districts to report individual scores.
