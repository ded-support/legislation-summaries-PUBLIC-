Summary

The bill amends placement restrictions to bar use of the "secure youth confinement facility operated by the Department of Correctional Services." Under amended section 43-251.01, juveniles described in subdivision (3) of §43-247 may not be placed in an adult correctional facility, the DOCS-operated secure youth confinement facility, or a youth rehabilitation and treatment center. Juveniles described in subdivisions (1), (2), or (4) of §43-247 may not be assigned or transferred to an adult correctional facility or the DOCS-operated secure youth confinement facility. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for juveniles aged 13–15 charged with certain enumerated felonies: juvenile court has exclusive original jurisdiction; after disposition a county attorney may move to transfer the case to district court; the court must hold a hearing within 15 days, the juvenile has counsel, and the court will retain the case unless the county attorney proves by a preponderance that the juvenile is not amenable to rehabilitation. Decisions must be issued within 30 days and are immediately appealable on an expedited schedule. If granted, charges are filed in district court and the juvenile is arraigned as an adult; statutes of limitation are tolled for one year after transfer or final appeal. Admissions by the juvenile are generally inadmissible except for specified uses. No provisions change funding, appropriations, or the Department of Correctional Services' budget.
