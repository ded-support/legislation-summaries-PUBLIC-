Summary

LB407 amends Nebraska’s Juvenile Code to restrict placement of juveniles in adult or state-operated secure confinement. Specifically, juveniles described in subdivision (3) of §43-247 may not be placed in an adult correctional facility, the secure youth confinement facility operated by the Department of Correctional Services, a youth rehabilitation and treatment center, or committed to the Office of Juvenile Services. Juveniles described in subdivisions (1), (2), or (4) of §43-247 may not be assigned or transferred to an adult correctional facility or the Department of Correctional Services’ secure youth confinement facility. The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes for the Department of Correctional Services.

Summary (Department of Correctional Services focus):

The additions give juvenile court exclusive original jurisdiction over 13–15‑year‑olds for certain enumerated felonies and allow a county attorney, after juvenile disposition, to move to dismiss the juvenile petition and refile charges in district (criminal) court. The bill sets tight timelines (hearing within 15 days, decision within 30), a preponderance‑of‑evidence standard that the youth is not amenable to rehabilitation to transfer, expedited appeals, tolling of statutes of limitations for at least one year after transfer, and limits on admissibility of juvenile statements with narrow exceptions.
