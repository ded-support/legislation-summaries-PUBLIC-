Summary

LB407 expressly bars placement or transfer of certain juveniles to the secure youth confinement facility operated by the Department of Correctional Services. Specifically (section 43-251.01): juveniles described in subdivision (3) of section 43-247 may not be placed in an adult correctional facility, the Department of Correctional Services’ secure youth confinement facility, a youth rehabilitation and treatment center, or committed to the Office of Juvenile Services; juveniles described in subdivisions (1), (2), or (4) of section 43-247 may not be assigned or transferred to an adult correctional facility or the Department’s secure youth confinement facility. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for juveniles ages 13–15 charged with specified felonies: juvenile court has exclusive original jurisdiction and may transfer cases per section 8. After disposition, the county attorney may move to dismiss the juvenile petition and refile in district court; the court must hold a hearing within 15 days, the juvenile has counsel and may present evidence, and the court must decide within 30 days with written findings. The court retains the case unless the county attorney proves by a preponderance that the juvenile is not amenable to rehabilitation. Orders are immediately appealable to the Court of Appeals on an expedited docket. If transferred, the juvenile is arraigned as an adult and applicable statutes of limitations are tolled for one year. Most admissions in these proceedings are inadmissible later, with limited exceptions. No changes to funding, appropriations, or Department of Correctional Services budgets are included.
