Summary

LB696 amends the Professional Landscape Architects Act and primarily affects the State Board of Landscape Architects. Key agency-related changes:

- Board duties: maintain a public roster (name and last-known address) and may charge a copy fee (under sections 84-712 to 84-712.09); accept Council of Landscape Architectural Registration Boards (CLARB) records in lieu of board forms and adopt CLARB exams/guidelines.

- Licensure/renewal: licensure effective on issuance; board may require professional development; duplicate certificate fee up to $50; late renewal penalties escalate monthly up to double the original fee and no renewals after one year.

- Discipline and funds: civil penalties (up to $10,000 per offense) are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution; costs assessed are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the State Board of Landscape Architects Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the regulating board (the board responsible under the Professional Landscape Architects Act) to provide copies of the board’s roster on request and to charge a fee for such copies consistent with sections 84‑712 to 84‑712.09 and board rules. It also makes examination materials subject to disclosure under those same statutory public‑records provisions. References licensure under the Professional Landscape Architects Act. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified beyond the board’s fee‑setting authority.
