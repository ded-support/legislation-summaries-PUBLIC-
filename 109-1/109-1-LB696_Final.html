Summary

LB696 amends the Professional Landscape Architects Act, primarily affecting the State Board of Landscape Architects. Key agency-related changes: the board must maintain a public roster with each licensee’s name and last-known address and may charge a fee for copies; it may accept Council of Landscape Architectural Registration Boards (CLARB) council records in lieu of board forms; examination materials are not public records; licensure is effective on issuance. Renewal and fee provisions reference board-set expiration dates and allow increased late fees (10% per month up to double the renewal fee) and board-imposed continuing professional development. Disciplinary actions require a two-thirds board vote; civil penalties (up to $10,000 per offense) are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution, and recovery of board costs (including attorney and hearing officer fees) is credited to the State Board of Landscape Architects Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the licensing board under the Professional Landscape Architects Act to provide copies of the complete roster to any requester and to charge a fee for such copies in accordance with sections 84‑712 to 84‑712.09 and the board’s rules and regulations. It also treats examination materials as records subject to disclosure under those same statutory provisions. Agencies mentioned: the licensing board (Board of Landscape Architects) and statutory sections 84‑712 to 84‑712.09. No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
