Summary

LB696 amends Nebraska’s Professional Landscape Architects Act, reallocating responsibilities and authorities of the State Board of Landscape Architects. Key actions for the Board: maintain and provide a public roster (for a fee under sections 84-712 to 84-712.09); accept Council of Landscape Architectural Registration Boards records and examinations; treat exam materials as nonpublic; issue, replace (fee up to $50), and set renewal/late‑renewal rules (late renewals increase 10% per month to a 2x cap; no renewals after one year); and impose professional development requirements. The Board’s disciplinary powers and sanctions are clarified. Financial provisions: civil penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution pursuant to Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution, and costs collected are credited to the State Board of Landscape Architects Cash Fund. Original statutory sections listed are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and procedural provisions for the licensing board (the Board under the Professional Landscape Architects Act). Key points:

- Defines "direct supervision" as having full professional knowledge and control over landscape-architecture work.

- Authorizes the board to provide copies of the complete roster of professional landscape architects on request and to charge a fee for such copies consistent with sections 84-712 to 84-712.09.

- Requires the board to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations governing these processes.

- Clarifies that examination materials are records subject to disclosure under sections 84-712 to 84-712.09.

No new appropriations or budget allocations are made; the only financial provision permits fee collection for roster copies.
