Summary

LB159 (Introduced by Guereca; Judiciary Committee) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §29-2260 to require sentencing courts, when deciding whether to withhold imprisonment for non‑mandatory misdemeanor or felony convictions, to consider an offender’s status as a victim of abuse or human trafficking as a mitigating factor to be accorded weight in favor of withholding sentence. The bill retains existing statutory guidance on juvenile adjudications under section 43-247 and application of the Nebraska Juvenile Code. No state agencies or departments beyond the courts and juvenile system are newly directed to act, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
