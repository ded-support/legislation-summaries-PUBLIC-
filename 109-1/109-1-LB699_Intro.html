Summary

LB699 amends §77-6831 of the ImagiNE Nebraska Act. Key agency actions and fiscal provisions:

- Tax Commissioner: issue rules, forms, certificates; determine and certify eligibility for personal property exemptions to taxpayers and affected county assessors by Aug. 1; administer sales/use tax exemption procedures.

- Director: collect an administrative fee equal to 0.5% of incentives (excluding personal property exemptions).

- State Treasurer: receive collected fees and credit them to a newly created ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund.

- Department of Economic Development: administer the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund; fund may also receive other amounts appropriated by the Legislature.

- State investment officer: invest fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Direct payment permits issued under §77-2705.01 on agreement execution; taxpayers must file exemption claims with Tax Commissioner and county assessors.

No specific appropriation amounts are set.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that when a contractor buying materials under the cited subdivisions has elected to be taxed as the consumer of building materials (per §77-2701.10(2) or (3)): if the contractor already paid sales or use tax, they must certify the amount to the taxpayer and the taxpayer may claim a refund as if they paid the tax; if the contractor did not pay sales or use tax, the contractor’s purchases are treated as tax-exempt as if purchased by the taxpayer. No state agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations are mentioned or changed.
