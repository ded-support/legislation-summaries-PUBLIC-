Summary

LB699 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-6831 (ImagiNE Nebraska Act). Key agency duties and funding changes:

- Tax Commissioner: authorized to issue rules, regulations, certificates, and forms; certify exemption eligibility to taxpayers and county assessors; administer sales/use tax reporting and refunds; oversee direct payment permits under §77-2705.01.

- Department of Economic Development: designated administrator of a newly created ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund for program administration.

- State Treasurer: receives and credits collected fees to the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund.

- Director (dept. director) collects an administrative fee equal to 0.5% of incentives (excluding personal property exemptions); fee may be withheld from refunds or paid directly; application fee credit allowed.

- State investment officer: may invest fund balances under existing state investment statutes.

- County assessors: receive exemption claim filings and Tax Commissioner certifications.

The bill repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provision treats contractors who elect to be taxed as the consumer of building materials as follows: if such a contractor already paid sales or use tax on materials, the contractor must certify the amount to the taxpayer and the taxpayer may claim a refund as if the taxpayer had incurred the tax; if the contractor has not paid tax, those purchases are exempt as if made by the taxpayer.
