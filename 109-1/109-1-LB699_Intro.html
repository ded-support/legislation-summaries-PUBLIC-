Summary

LB699 amends the ImagiNE Nebraska Act (section 77-6831). Key agency actions and fiscal changes:

- Tax Commissioner: authorized to issue rules, regulations, certificates, and forms; to issue direct payment permits at agreement execution; and to determine and certify eligibility for personal property tax exemptions to taxpayers and county assessors.

- Director (collection agent): collects a 0.5% administrative fee on incentives (except personal property exemptions) when incentives are received or used; fee may be paid directly or via withholding of refunds; a credit is allowed for fees paid with the application.

- State Treasurer: receives fee receipts for credit to the newly created ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund.

- Department of Economic Development: administers the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund, which consists of collected fees and any legislative appropriations and is used to administer the Act.

- State investment officer: may invest available fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that when a contractor (purchasing under subdivisions (2)(a)(iii)–(v)) has elected to be taxed as the consumer of building materials under §77-2701.10(2) or (3): if the contractor already paid sales or use tax, the contractor must certify the amount to the taxpayer and the taxpayer may claim a refund as if they had paid the tax; if the contractor has not paid sales or use tax, the contractor’s purchases are treated as tax‑exempt as if purchased directly by the taxpayer. No state agency or department is named, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budgets are included.
