Summary

LB689 amends the School Employees Retirement Act (§79‑930) and the Class V School Employees Retirement Act (§79‑992). Agencies referenced: Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS) — including the NPERS director and NPERS board — the Class V retirement administrator, the retirement board, and school districts. Key agency-related changes: members returning as regular employees must immediately resume contributions and accrue service credit (including prior temporary/substitute work); members who do not establish regular-employee status remain noncontributing and do not accrue or purchase service credit. For members who applied for or received benefits, NPERS’ director may determine whether the 180‑day post‑separation period has elapsed upon receipt of clear and convincing evidence (45‑day submission window); such determinations may be appealed to the board, whose decision is final. A parallel determination and appeal process is established for Class V members via the administrator and board. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
