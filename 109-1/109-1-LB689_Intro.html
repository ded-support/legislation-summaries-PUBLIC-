Summary

LB689 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§79-930 and 79-992 (School Employees Retirement Act and Class V School Employees Retirement Act) and repeals the original sections. Key agency actions and officials:

- Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS): clarifies return-to-work rules; members who separate but have not applied for or received benefits may work as temporary, substitute, or volunteer but generally do not contribute or earn service credit unless they become a regular employee, at which point contributions and service credit resume.

- NPERS director: may determine whether a member satisfied the 180-day post‑separation period (clear-and-convincing evidence required); determinations may be appealed to the NPERS board, whose decision is final.

- For Class V (school district) members: parallel provisions assign the administrator authority to make 180‑day determinations, with appeals to the retirement board; defines intermittent/substitute/temporary work exceptions.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
