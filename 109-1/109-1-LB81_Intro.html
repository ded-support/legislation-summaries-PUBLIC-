Summary

- The bill defines "Department" to mean the Department of Revenue (77-6702).

- The department is tasked with determining the allowable growth percentage used in the Nebraska Property Tax Incentive Act (77-6702(1)).

- The department must set the credit percentage so total refundable tax credits equal specified amounts: $548,000,000 for taxable years in calendar 2022 and $560,700,000 for calendar 2023; for calendar 2024 the total is the prior-year maximum increased by the allowable growth percentage (77-6703(2)).

- The department must provide forms and schedules to verify eligibility and allocate school district taxes (77-6703(3)).

- No direct appropriations or other departmental budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language directs the Department of Revenue to calculate an "allowable growth percentage" equal to the statewide percentage change in total assessed value of all real property from the prior year. For taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) in calendar year 2024, the department must set the credit percentage so the total credits equal the prior year's maximum credits increased by that allowable growth percentage. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
