Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns it these duties: determine the allowable growth percentage; set the annual credit percentage so total refundable tax credits equal specified amounts (calendar-year totals of $548,000,000 for 2022 and $560,700,000 for 2023, and for 2024 the prior year's maximum increased by the department‑determined allowable growth percentage); and provide forms and schedules to verify eligibility and allocate school district taxes. The credits are refundable against Nebraska income or certain franchise taxes and the department administers the credit percentage calculation and related documentation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition directing the Department of Revenue to calculate the "allowable growth percentage" as the year‑over‑year percentage change in total state real property assessed value. It also requires the department to set the credit percentage for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) in calendar year 2024 so total credits equal the prior year's maximum credits increased by that allowable growth percentage. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
