Summary

The bill defines "department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns it three specific duties: (1) determine the allowable growth percentage used in credit calculations; (2) set the annual credit percentage so total refundable credits equal specified statewide amounts — $548,000,000 for taxable years in 2022 and $560,700,000 for taxable years in 2023, with 2024’s total equal to the prior year’s maximum increased by the allowable growth percentage; and (3) provide forms and schedules to verify eligibility and allocate school-district-tax-based credits. The Department’s actions directly determine the statewide allocation and administration of the refundable tax-credit funding.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition and an administrative formula for credits under the Department of Revenue.

- Defines “allowable growth percentage” as the year‑over‑year percentage increase (if any) in total statewide assessed real property value, as determined by the department.

- Requires the Department of Revenue, for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin in calendar year 2024, to set the credit percentage so total credits equal the prior year’s maximum credits increased by that allowable growth percentage.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
