Summary

Defines "department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns it these roles: determine the "allowable growth percentage"; set the annual credit percentage so total refundable credits for school district taxes equal specified statewide amounts—$548,000,000 for taxable years beginning in calendar year 2022, $560,700,000 for 2023, and for 2024 the prior-year maximum increased by the allowable growth percentage; and provide forms and schedules to verify eligibility and allocate school district taxes. The credits are refundable and are taken against Nebraska income tax or the franchise tax.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language gives the Department of Revenue authority to determine the "allowable growth percentage" — the year‑over‑year percent change in total assessed real property value statewide — and requires the department to set the credit percentage for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) in calendar year 2024 so that total credits equal the prior year's maximum credits increased by that allowable growth percentage. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.
