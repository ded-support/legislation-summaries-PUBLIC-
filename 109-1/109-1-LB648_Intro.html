Summary

The bill amends revenue distribution to direct all sales-and-use-tax proceeds from the sale or lease of aircraft (per section 3-101) to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023. This allocation is added to the distribution scheme in section 77-27,132. The act's operative date is July 1, 2026. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are specified.
