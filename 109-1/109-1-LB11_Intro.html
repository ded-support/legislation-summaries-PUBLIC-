Summary

LB11 appropriates General Fund moneys to implement the Nebraska Statewide Workforce and Education Reporting System Act. For FY2025‑26 it provides $130,893 and for FY2026‑27 $179,795 to each of the following: Nebraska Department of Education (Program 25); Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges (Program 48); Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska (Program 781); and Agency No. 83 — Aid to Community Colleges (Program 151). The Aid to Community Colleges amounts are designated as state aid and must be disbursed by the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education on behalf of the community colleges. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
