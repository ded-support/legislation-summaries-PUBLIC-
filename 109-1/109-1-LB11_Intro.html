Summary

LB11 appropriates General Fund amounts to implement the Nebraska Statewide Workforce and Education Reporting System Act. It directs the State Department of Education (Program 25), the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges (Program 48), and the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska (Program 781) to each receive $130,893 for FY2025-26 and $179,795 for FY2026-27. Agency No. 83 — Aid to Community Colleges (Program 151) receives the same amounts; those sums are designated as state aid to community colleges and shall be disbursed on the colleges' behalf by the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education. The bill contains an emergency clause making it effective upon passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds General Fund appropriations to support the Nebraska Statewide Workforce and Education Reporting System Act as follows: each of the State Department of Education (Program 25), Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges (Program 48), Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska (Program 781), and Agency No. 83 — Aid to Community Colleges (Program 151) receives $130,893 for FY2025‑26 and $179,795 for FY2026‑27. The amounts for Program 151 are designated as state aid to community colleges and must be disbursed by the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education on behalf of the community colleges. All amounts are from the General Fund.
