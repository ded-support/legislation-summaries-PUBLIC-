Summary

LB240 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §18-2147 (Community Development Law). It requires redevelopment authorities to send notices of any provision dividing ad valorem taxes on forms prescribed by the Property Tax Administrator to the county assessor on or before July 1 of the effective year; failure leaves affected taxes undivided and fixes the redevelopment valuation at the last certified pre-effective-year value for the remaining division period. The bill reiterates tax-allocation mechanics: the levy portion continues to fund public bodies, excess ad valorem taxes are allocated to an authority special fund to pay bonds/loans/indebtedness for redevelopment, and interest/penalties are paid to public bodies. For metropolitan-class city projects producing housing funds, the authority and city must use best efforts to allocate at least 30% to single-family housing and adopt a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest-income, long-term affordability. Immediate effect (emergency).
