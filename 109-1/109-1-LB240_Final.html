Summary

LB240 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §18-2147 (Community Development Law). Key administrative changes: the Property Tax Administrator must prescribe the notice form and the redevelopment authority must send notice of a tax-division provision to the county assessor on or before July 1 of the effective year. The county assessor must notify the authority and owner of redevelopment project valuations; protests go to the county board of equalization (decision within 30 days; county clerk mails decision within 7 days); appeals may be filed with the Tax Equalization and Review Commission. The county treasurer is notified when authority debt is retired so taxes revert to public bodies. For metropolitan-class cities, the authority and city must prioritize affordable housing use of divided ad valorem tax funds (best efforts to allocate ≥30% annually to single-family housing and a qualified allocation plan). Failure to timely notify causes taxes to remain undivided. The bill takes effect immediately; no direct new appropriations are specified.
