Summary

LB240 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §18-2147 (Community Development Law). Key agencies/entities: redevelopment authority (per §18-2103), county assessor, county board of equalization, county clerk, county treasurer, Property Tax Administrator, Tax Equalization and Review Commission, and cities (including metropolitan class). The bill prescribes that the authority must send notice of any provision dividing ad valorem taxes to the county assessor on Property Tax Administrator forms on or before July 1 of the year the provision takes effect. Failure to provide timely notice causes taxes for affected years to remain undivided and be paid to the public bodies; remaining tax-increment valuations use the last certified pre‑effective-year valuation. The bill reiterates allocation rules: base levy to public bodies, tax increment to an authority special fund to repay bonds/loans, interest/penalties to public bodies; division terms: up to 20 years for extremely blighted areas, otherwise up to 15 years. Act takes effect immediately.
