Summary

LB240 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §18-2147 (Community Development Law). Key agencies/departments: redevelopment authority (per §18-2103), county assessor, county board of equalization, county clerk, county treasurer, Property Tax Administrator, Tax Equalization and Review Commission, and cities of the metropolitan class.

Major changes:

- Notice rule: the authority must send notice of a tax-division provision to the county assessor on forms prescribed by the Property Tax Administrator on or before July 1 of the year of the provision’s effective date.

- Failure to timely notify causes affected ad valorem taxes to remain undivided; remaining future divisions use the last certified valuation prior to the effective date.

- Appeals/process timing: protests to county board of equalization (30 days); board decision within 30 days; clerk mails decision within 7 days; appeals to TERC within 30 days.

- Tax division/funding: taxes split between public bodies and an authority special fund to service bonds/loans; authorities may use single or separate funds. For metropolitan-class cities, authorities and cities must strive to allocate ≥30% of such funds to single-family housing and adopt a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest‑income, longest‑term projects.

Effective immediately due to emergency clause.
