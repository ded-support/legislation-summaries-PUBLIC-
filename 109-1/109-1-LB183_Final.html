Summary

LB183 amends the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act. Key agency actions and fiscal provisions:

- State Treasurer: continues publishing and mailing owner notices; must record holder reports and deposit unclaimed property receipts (including from §69‑1307.05) into the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund, pay claims and related expenses, and may deduct costs into a newly created Unclaimed Property Cash Fund. Both funds may be transferred to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction.

- Fiscal flows: on or before Nov. 1 annually, any Trust Fund balance over $1,000,000 is distributed to the permanent school fund. Money in the Cash Fund may be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Records/confidentiality: social security numbers, DOBs, and last-known addresses receive Department of Revenue–level confidentiality; Auditor of Public Accounts retains unrestricted access. Unclaimed property records may be withheld from public or shared with other states only on a reciprocal basis.

- Finder rules: commercial/professional locators cannot charge fees until 24 months after publication/disclosure; professional finder fees capped at 10% with disclosure requirements to owners.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language bars commercial or professional locators from charging fees in connection with property presumed abandoned. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
