Summary

LB183 amends the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act, affecting primarily the State Treasurer and related state fiscal and oversight entities. Key agency provisions:

- State Treasurer: continues publication and mailing of abandoned-property notices; may withhold unclaimed property records from the public and restrict access by commercial/professional locators for 24 months after publication. Creates an Unclaimed Property Cash Fund to pay unclaimed-property costs; transfers to the General Fund require legislative direction.

- Fiscal allocations: On or before Nov. 1 each year, any Unclaimed Property Trust Fund balance exceeding $1,000,000 is distributed to the Permanent School Fund. Funds may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment laws.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: retains unrestricted access to confidential owner records.

- Department of Revenue: owner SSN, DOB, and last-known address are afforded the same confidentiality as tax return information.

- Limits professional finders’ fees to 10% and imposes disclosure requirements. Original sections repealed.
