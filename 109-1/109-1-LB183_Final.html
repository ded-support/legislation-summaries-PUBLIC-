Summary

LB183 amends the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act, primarily affecting the State Treasurer's administration of unclaimed property. The bill clarifies publication and mailed-notice duties of the State Treasurer and restricts public inspection and commercial copying of holders' records; records containing Social Security numbers, dates of birth, and last-known addresses are treated as confidential on par with Department of Revenue tax return information, with the Auditor of Public Accounts retaining unrestricted access. Professional finders are capped at a 10% fee and must provide specified disclosures; finders may not charge until 24 months after names are published or disclosed. All unclaimed property receipts (including section 69-1307.05 receipts) are deposited into the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund; balances over $1,000,000 (as of November 1) transfer to the permanent school fund. A new Unclaimed Property Cash Fund may hold deducted administrative costs and is investable by the state investment officer; legislative direction may move funds to the General Fund.
