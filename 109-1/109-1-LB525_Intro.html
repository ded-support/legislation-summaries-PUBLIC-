Summary

LB525 enacts the Agricultural Data Privacy Act. Key government roles and fiscal provisions:

- Attorney General: granted exclusive enforcement authority to sue controllers or processors for injunctive relief or civil penalties (penalty amount left unspecified in the bill).

- State Treasurer: required recipient of any civil penalties, to be distributed per Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution.

- Attorney General duties: must publish controller/processor responsibilities and consumer rights on the AG website and provide an online complaint submission mechanism.

The bill disallows a private right of action. No appropriations or other budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Agricultural Data Privacy Act" to regulate agricultural data (definitions, consent, rescission) and limit controllers' and processors' use, sale, or conditioning of services on such data; bans contract provisions waiving these rights and disallows a private right of action. The Attorney General is given exclusive enforcement authority to seek injunctive relief and civil penalties (amount left as a placeholder). All civil penalties must be remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution per Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. The Attorney General must publish guidance on controller/processor responsibilities and consumer rights on the AG website and provide an online complaint mechanism. No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
