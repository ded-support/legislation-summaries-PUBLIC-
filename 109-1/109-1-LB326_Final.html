Summary

LB326 explicitly defines "Department" as the Department of Insurance and "Director" as the Director of Insurance. Key provisions referencing the Department/Director:

- Insurers must respond to a written inquiry from the Department within 15 working days.

- Health insurance lead generators and insurers (other than agents, brokers, consultants) must reimburse the Department for the expense of examinations under the Insurers Examination Act.

- The Director is authorized to examine, investigate, issue charges/hearings, and to transmit covered claims to the Nebraska Property and Liability Insurance Guaranty Association.

- Mutual insurance holding companies must file annual financial statements and a confidential encumbrance disclosure with the Director by March 1 and an independent audit by June 1.

The bill contains no explicit state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to the Department.

Adds definitions and regulatory duties relevant to the Department of Insurance: creates/clarifies terms including "customer," "health insurance lead generator," "lead generating device," and "recording." Permits the association to provide covered benefits/services and appoint service providers, and requires exhaustion of state/federal program remedies before association payment.
