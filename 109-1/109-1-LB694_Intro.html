Summary

The bill amends Section 39-210 (tourist-oriented directional sign panels) to require any applicant seeking qualification to provide the Department of Transportation assurance of compliance with applicable anti-discrimination laws, explicitly adding military or veteran status as a protected characteristic. Noncompliance renders a business ineligible for panel placement and may require removal of unlawfully erected advertising devices. Qualified panels must also conform to the Federal Beautification Act and the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices as adopted under section 60-6,118. Other enacted provisions do not affect the Department of Transportation, and no DOT funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds "military or veteran status" as a protected class across multiple statutes—including the Nebraska Fair Employment Practice Act—defines it to include active duty service (including reserve/National Guard), those discharged honorably or with general (under honorable conditions) characterization, and dependents as defined in 50 U.S.C. §3911. Preserves veterans' preference programs (secs. 48-225 to 48-231, 48-238), allows otherwise nondiscriminatory benefits or limits for veterans/servicemembers (housing, loans, discounts), and clarifies this is not meant to duplicate USERRA or require special treatment for absent servicemembers. No provisions amend or create Department of Transportation funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
