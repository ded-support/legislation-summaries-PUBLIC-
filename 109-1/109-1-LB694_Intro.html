Summary

LB694 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §39-210 to require any activity seeking placement on tourist‑oriented directional sign panels to provide the Department of Transportation assurance that the activity complies with applicable anti‑discrimination laws, explicitly adding military or veteran status. Activities found to violate those laws are ineligible for sign panels and must remove unlawfully erected advertising devices. Existing conformity requirements with the Federal Beautification Act and the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices remain unchanged. The bill makes no changes to Department of Transportation funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary: The added text inserts "military or veteran status" as a protected characteristic across multiple Nebraska statutes (including the Nebraska Fair Employment Practice Act and public‑accommodation and housing provisions). It defines "military or veteran status" (active duty, honorably discharged veterans, and certain dependents), clarifies that veterans' preference programs and otherwise nondiscriminatory benefits or limits for veterans/servicemembers (e.g., housing, discounts, favorable loan terms) remain allowed, and states the inclusion does not duplicate or alter federal USERRA protections. Relevant statutory exceptions and cross‑references (e.g., sections 20‑322, 38; veterans preference sections 48‑225 to 48‑231 and 48‑238) are preserved. No provisions in the added text change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Transportation.
