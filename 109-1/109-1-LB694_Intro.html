Summary

LB694 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §39-210 to require entities seeking placement on tourist-oriented directional sign panels to provide the Department of Transportation assurances they comply with applicable anti-discrimination laws, explicitly adding military and veteran status. Activities that violate those laws lose eligibility to appear on panels. The bill also reiterates that qualifying activities must remove unlawfully erected advertising devices and that panels must conform with the Federal-Aid Highway Beautification Act and the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices. No provisions change Department of Transportation funding, appropriations, or budget allocations; repealed sections listed do not affect DOT.
