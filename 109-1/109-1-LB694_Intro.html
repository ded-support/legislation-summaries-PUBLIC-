Summary

The bill amends eligibility for tourist‑oriented directional sign panels by requiring applicants to provide the Department of Transportation an assurance they comply with applicable anti‑discrimination laws, explicitly adding military or veteran status to the listed protected characteristics. Entities found to violate those laws become ineligible for sign panels and must remove any unlawfully erected advertising devices. Other examined excerpts contain no additional references to the Department of Transportation and the bill includes no DOT appropriations, funding changes, or budget reallocations.
