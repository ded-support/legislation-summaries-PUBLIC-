Summary

The bill directs the Secretary of State and the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) to enter an agreement to match the statewide voter registration list with DMV records to verify applicant information, including citizenship. Under the agreement the DMV must: enter a Social Security Administration agreement per 42 U.S.C. 405(r)(8); provide a combined voter-registration application for issuance/renewal of operator’s licenses and state ID cards; electronically transmit registration data (including digital image and signature) to county election officials; and notify election officials when a registrant surrenders a Nebraska license/ID to another state for cancellation. The Secretary of State may contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of printed election forms. No appropriations or budget changes for either agency are specified.
