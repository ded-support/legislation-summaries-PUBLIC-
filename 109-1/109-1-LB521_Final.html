Summary

LB521 directs the Department of Motor Vehicles to cooperate with the Secretary of State to match DMV records to the statewide voter registration list and to: provide a combined voter‑registration application at license/ID issuance; electronically transmit registration data (including digital image and signature) to county election officials within specified timelines; and notify counties when a registrant surrenders a Nebraska license/ID to another state. The Secretary of State may obtain DMV electronic images/signatures, issue voter‑ID/outreach guidance, require compliance reporting, and contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of printed forms. The bill includes no explicit appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for DAS or the DMV.
