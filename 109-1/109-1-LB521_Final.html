Summary

The bill requires the Secretary of State and the Department of Motor Vehicles to enter an agreement to match the statewide voter‑registration list against DMV records (including citizenship). It directs the DMV to prescribe and make available a combined voter‑registration form for license/ID applicants, electronically transmit registration data and provide applicants’ digital images and signatures to the Secretary of State, and enter an agreement with the Social Security Administration for needed data. The Secretary of State must contract with the Department of Administrative Services for storage and distribution of printed forms and issue guidance to the DMV on public distribution of voter‑identification information. No appropriations or budget allocations for DAS or DMV are specified.
