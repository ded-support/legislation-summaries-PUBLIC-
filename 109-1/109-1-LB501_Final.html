Summary

LB501 amends Nebraska property tax statutes governing assessment relief for real property suffering significant damage. Key agency actions and deadlines: county assessors (in counties with population ≥150,000) must provide preliminary valuation notice by Jan.15 and complete assessments by March 25; property owners must report damaged property to the county clerk by July 15 on a Tax Commissioner form; county assessors must inspect and file reports with the county board of equalization by July 20; the county board adjusts current-year assessed values and issues notices and protest decisions (appealable to the Tax Equalization and Review Commission). County treasurers are excepted from immediate tax-collection duties for qualifying damaged property. Local officials (assessors, sheriffs, constables, city council members, village trustees) must notify county treasurers of removals/demolitions. No funding or appropriation changes are included. The bill takes effect immediately.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the county assessor must inspect and review all properties for which a report is filed under this section and submit a comprehensive report to the county board of equalization by July 20 of the current assessment year. The report must use a form prescribed by the Tax Commissioner and include all reports filed by property owners. No agencies beyond the county assessor, county board of equalization, and the Tax Commissioner are mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
