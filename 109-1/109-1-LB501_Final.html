Summary

LB501 amends procedures and deadlines for assessing real property that suffers "significant property damage." Key government actors and changes:

- Property owners must report damaged property to the county clerk by July 15; Tax Commissioner prescribes the form.

- County assessors must inspect reported properties and submit a report to the county board of equalization by July 20.

- County boards of equalization adjust assessed value to the value at the time of damage, may meet June 1–July 25 (or to Aug 10 with an extension), and must notify owners; protests may be filed with the board (30 days) and appealed to the Tax Equalization and Review Commission (30 days).

- Mobile homes moved under §77-3708 with accelerated tax may seek refunds under §77-1734.01 for reductions ordered by the county board.

- Assessors, sheriffs, constables, city council members, and village trustees must notify county treasurers of removals/demolitions; county treasurers are excepted from immediate tax collection/distress for damaged property.

- Emergency effective date. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for the county assessor to inspect and review all properties for which owners file reports under this section and to submit a comprehensive report to the county board of equalization by July 20 of the current assessment year. The report must use a form prescribed by the Tax Commissioner and include all such property-owner reports. No agencies beyond the county assessor, county board of equalization, and the Tax Commissioner are mentioned, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
