Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §38-3113 to include institutions accredited by the Department of Health and Human Services among settings whose staff who, having performed specified duties for two years prior to Sept. 1, 1994, may use the title "psychologist associate" in the context of that employment. Use of the title is limited to the subdivision's described duties and supervision rules may be adopted as provided. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a School Psychologist Interstate Licensure Compact establishing a multi‑state Commission to administer interstate licensure, define terms, set eligibility and investigatory requirements, promulgate Rules, and provide dispute-resolution and enforcement processes. Financing authority allows the Commission to accept grants/donations, lease/purchase property, hire staff, and require annual assessments from Member States and fees from licensees (allocated by Rule) to fund its budget. Impact on Department of Health and Human Services: none direct — no new DHHS appropriation or earmark is made. Indirectly, if DHHS serves as a State Licensing Authority it would be required to share licensure data and may bear any state assessment/administrative costs under the Compact.
