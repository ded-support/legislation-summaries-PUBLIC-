Summary

The bill amends section 38-3113 to incorporate the School Psychologist Interstate Licensure Compact and revises exemptions under the Psychology Practice Act. Explicit references to the Department of Health and Human Services: persons who performed duties described in the subdivision while employed in institutions accredited by the Department of Health and Human Services (among other agencies) for at least two years prior to September 1, 1994, may use the title "psychologist associate" in that employment context, subject to limits described. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a School Psychologist Interstate Licensure Compact establishing definitions, Member State participation rules, a multi‑state Commission (powers, meetings, rulemaking, dispute resolution), information‑sharing requirements, and financing via member assessments, licensee fees, grants, and donations. No line items, appropriations, or direct funding are provided to the Department of Health and Human Services.
