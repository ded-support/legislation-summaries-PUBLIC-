Summary

LB624 appropriates General Fund dollars to the Office of the State Treasurer: $10,000,000 for FY2025-26 and $10,000,000 for FY2026-27, designated as state aid and restricted for that purpose. The Treasurer is to contract to provide education scholarships for low‑income and at‑risk elementary and secondary students to expand access to approved or accredited private, denominational, or parochial schools. The bill states legislative intent to appropriate $10,000,000 annually thereafter to the State Treasurer for the same scholarship purpose.
