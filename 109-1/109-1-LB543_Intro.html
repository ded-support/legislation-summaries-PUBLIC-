Summary

LB543 authorizes the Department (DMV) to deliver license plates or registration certificates by mail or alternative shipping and permits the DMV to charge a postage-and-handling fee limited to actual cost; that fee is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. The bill also: (1) establishes a $50 annual nonrefundable fee for requesting a single front-or-rear plate, remitted to the Highway Trust Fund; (2) maintains a per-plate fee cap (not to exceed $4.25) and changes the remittance destination for fees under §60-3,102 from the Highway Trust Fund to a License Plate Cash Fund beginning with the 2029 issuance cycle; and (3) retains duplicate-registration and replacement-plate fees ($1 and $2.50).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key DMV changes (effective on an implementation date set by the director on or before Jan. 1, 2029):

- Allows registrants to choose annual or multiyear (2- or 3-year) registrations; multiyear renewals require payment of fees and taxes for each year, including fees under sections 60-3,141 and 60-3,156.

- Permits the department to mail license plates or registration certificates to applicants or county treasurers via secure delivery; if mailed to the applicant the department may charge a postage/handling fee limited to actual cost. That fee is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- Allows operation/towing with proof of registration for 30 days without a mailed plate.

- Beginning with the 2029 plate issuance cycle, all fees collected under this section are to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the License Plate Cash Fund.
