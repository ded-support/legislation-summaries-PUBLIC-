Summary

LB543 authorizes the Department (Motor Vehicles) to deliver license plates and registration certificates by secure mail or alternative shipping, with an implementation date to be set by the director on or before January 1, 2029. The department may charge a postage and handling fee (cost-recovery) remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. The bill permits multiyear (two- or three-year) registrations (implementation by director by Jan. 1, 2029). It changes fee remittances: license-plate fees currently credited to the Highway Trust Fund will, beginning with the 2029 license plate issuance cycle, be credited to the License Plate Cash Fund. The single-front-plate option fee ($50 plus decal cost) continues to be remitted to the Highway Trust Fund; duplicate/replacement plate and certificate fees are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective on an implementation date set by the DMV director on or before Jan. 1, 2029, the bill adds: a voluntary multiyear registration option (two- or three-year terms) with all applicable fees and taxes (including fees in sections 60‑3,141 and 60‑3,156) paid for each year at renewal; authority for the DMV to mail license plates or registration certificates (or deliver to county treasurers) via a secure process; and permission for the DMV to charge a postage/handling fee no greater than actual cost, remitting that fee to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. It also allows a vehicle to be operated up to 30 days on proof of registration without a mailed plate, and directs that, beginning with the 2029 plate cycle, fees collected under this section be credited to the License Plate Cash Fund. No specific appropriation amounts are included.
