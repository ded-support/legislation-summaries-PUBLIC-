Summary

References to the Department of Motor Vehicles (the "department") and related fiscal changes in LB543:

- The department may deliver license plates and registration certificates by mail or alternative secure shipping beginning on an implementation date on or before Jan 1, 2029, and may charge a postage/handling fee not to exceed actual cost; that fee is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- The department may adopt rules for a staggered registration system for apportioned vehicles (including collection of up to 18 months of fees) and may offer multiyear (2–3 year) registrations beginning by Jan 1, 2029.

- The department sets a per-plate fee (to cover plates/decals) not to exceed $4.25. Beginning with the 2029 plate cycle, fees under that section shift from the Highway Trust Fund to the License Plate Cash Fund.

- A single-front-plate option carries a $50 annual nonrefundable fee (plus decal cost) remitted to the Highway Trust Fund.

- Duplicate fees: $1 for duplicate registration certificate/decals; $2.50 for duplicate/replacement plates.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Effective on an implementation date set by the DMV director no later than Jan. 1, 2029, registrants may choose annual or multiyear (2- or 3-year) registrations; multiyear renewals require payment of fees and taxes for each year up front, including the fees in sections 60-3,141 and 60-3,156.

- Beginning by that same implementation date, the DMV may mail license plates or registration certificates (or deliver via an alternative shipping service) through a secure process and may charge a postage/handling fee limited to actual cost recovery. That fee must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- Beginning with the 2029 license‑plate issuance cycle, all fees collected under this section are to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the License Plate Cash Fund.
