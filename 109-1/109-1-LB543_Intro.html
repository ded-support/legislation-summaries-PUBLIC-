Summary

The bill directs the Department of Motor Vehicles (the department) to: permit delivery of license plates and registration certificates by mail or alternative secure shipping and to charge a postage/handling fee remitted to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund (60-3,100(3)); design, procure, and furnish validation decals to county treasurers and secure competitive bids whenever plates are changed (60-3,100(1),(2)(ii)); set a per-plate fee not to exceed $4.25 (60-3,102(1)); continue remitting plate fees to the Highway Trust Fund, with fees credited instead to the License Plate Cash Fund beginning with the 2029 issuance cycle (60-3,102(2)); and adopt rules for a staggered registration system for apportioned vehicles (60-392(6)). The bill also retains specified duplicate/ replacement fees ($1 for duplicate registration/decals; $2.50 per duplicate/replacement plate).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key DMV changes (implementation date on or before Jan 1, 2029, as designated by the director):

- Allows optional multiyear registrations (2- or 3-year) in addition to annual; at multiyear renewal all fees and taxes must be paid for each year, including fees under sections 60-3,141 and 60-3,156.

- DMV may mail license plates or registration certificates to applicants or county treasurers via a secure delivery process.

- If mailed to an applicant, DMV may charge a postage/handling fee up to actual cost; that fee must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund.

- Consumers showing proof of registration may operate a vehicle for 30 days without a mailed plate.

- Beginning with the 2029 plate issuance cycle, fees collected under this section are to be remitted to the License Plate Cash Fund.
