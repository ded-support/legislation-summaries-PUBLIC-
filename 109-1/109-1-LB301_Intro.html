Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: bill requires the Department to prescribe the attestation format for applicants to verify U.S. citizenship, lawful presence, or eligible-alien status (including eligible aliens employed or training as law enforcement officers) and allows agencies to accept electronic attestations substantially similar to the DAS format.

- Department of Labor: employees of the Department of Labor who are active participants in a contributory retirement plan or contract (under former section 48-609) at the date of that plan's termination shall be granted vesting credit for years of participation.

- Department of Correctional Services: employees of the Department are expressly excluded from the statutory definition of "law enforcement officer."

- Department of Revenue: employees under section 77-366 are likewise excluded from the "law enforcement officer" definition.

No explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included.

Summary (impact on specified agencies):

- Department of Administrative Services: must prescribe the attestation format for applicants claiming eligible-alien status and law-enforcement employment/training to receive the listed public benefits.

- Department of Labor: required to provide Employment Security Law benefits to eligible aliens employed as, or training to become, law enforcement officers.

- Department of Correctional Services: as a state law-enforcement employer, must treat eligible aliens the same as other officers for employment-related public benefits (retirement under listed state acts, deferred compensation, scholarships, etc.) and extend family/dependent benefits regardless of lawful presence.

- Department of Revenue: not directly referenced; no changes to tax administration or revenue duties.
