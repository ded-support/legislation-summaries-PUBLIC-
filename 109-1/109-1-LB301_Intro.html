Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: The bill requires applicants for specified public benefits to attest in a format prescribed by the Department of Administrative Services that they are U.S. citizens or lawfully present; applicants who are eligible aliens employed as or training to become law enforcement officers must also attest in a DAS-prescribed format to that status.

- Department of Labor: Employees of the Department of Labor who were active participants in a contributory retirement plan or contract that is terminated (per prior law) shall be granted vesting credit for years of participation.

- Department of Correctional Services: Employees of the Department of Correctional Services are explicitly excluded from the statutory definition of "law enforcement officer."

- Department of Revenue: Employees of the Department of Revenue under section 77-366 are likewise excluded from the "law enforcement officer" definition.

No appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes are included.
