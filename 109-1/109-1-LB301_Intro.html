Summary

LB301 directs the Department of Administrative Services to prescribe the attestation format for applicants verifying U.S. citizenship, lawful presence, or — for eligible aliens — employment or training as law enforcement officers; state agencies may adopt substantially similar electronic filing. The bill excludes employees of the Department of Correctional Services and employees of the Department of Revenue (per §77-366) from the statutory definition of "law enforcement officer." It also provides that if a prior contributory retirement plan (per former §48-609) is terminated, employees of the Department of Labor who were active participants at termination shall be granted vesting credit. The bill contains no direct appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions (focus on listed agencies):

- Department of Administrative Services: must prescribe an attestation format for applicants seeking the specified public benefits; applicants must attest they are an "eligible alien" employed as or training to become a law enforcement officer.

- Department of Labor: extends Employment Security Law (unemployment) benefits to eligible aliens employed as or training to become law enforcement officers.

- Department of Correctional Services: eligible aliens employed as, or training to become, law enforcement officers (which would include applicable correctional officers) are explicitly entitled to employment-related public benefits — retirement (County Employees, Nebraska State Patrol, Police Officers, State Employees Retirement Acts), deferred compensation, scholarships/financial aid (including First Responder Recruitment and Retention Act), and other employment-related benefits. Family/dependents are likewise eligible regardless of lawful presence.

- Department of Revenue: no substantive changes, funding, or appropriations affecting Revenue are included.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. The section is enacted under 8 U.S.C. 1621(d).
