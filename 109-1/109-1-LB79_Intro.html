Summary

LB79 requires a trial court, on a defendant's motion, to dismiss any claim of the defendant’s direct negligence in civil actions involving a commercial motor vehicle (as defined in Neb. Rev. Stat. §60‑316) that requires a commercial driver's license if the defendant stipulates the alleged negligent person was the defendant’s employee or independent contractor and was acting within the course and scope of employment. If those stipulations are made and the employee/contractor’s negligence is found to have caused the damages, the defendant’s liability is limited to respondeat superior. No state agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires that in civil suits involving commercial motor vehicles requiring a CDL, a trial court must dismiss any claim of the defendant’s direct negligence if the defendant stipulates that (a) the alleged negligent person was the defendant’s employee or independent contractor and (b) that person was acting within the course and scope of employment or as the defendant’s independent contractor at the time. If the stipulation is made and that person’s negligence caused or contributed to the harm, the defendant’s liability is limited to respondeat superior. "Commercial motor vehicle" is defined by cross‑reference to section 60‑316. No government agencies or departmental funding or appropriations are mentioned or changed.
