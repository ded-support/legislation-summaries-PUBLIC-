Summary

LB79 requires Nebraska trial courts to dismiss a defendant’s direct‑negligence claim in civil cases involving a commercial motor vehicle that requires a commercial driver’s license if the defendant stipulates that the alleged negligent person was the defendant’s employee or independent contractor and was acting within the course and scope of employment or as an independent contractor. If such a stipulation is made and the employee/contractor’s negligence is found to have caused or contributed to damages, the defendant’s liability is then adjudicated solely under respondeat superior. The bill relies on the definition of “commercial motor vehicle” in section 60‑316. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text allows a defendant in a civil action involving a commercial motor vehicle that requires a commercial driver’s license to move to dismiss any claim of the defendant’s direct negligence by stipulating that the negligent person was the defendant’s employee or independent contractor and was acting within the scope of employment or as the defendant’s contractor. If those stipulations are made and the employee/contractor’s negligence caused the damages, the defendant’s liability is limited to respondeat superior. “Commercial motor vehicle” is as defined in section 60-316. No government agencies, departments, or budget/appropriation changes are mentioned.
