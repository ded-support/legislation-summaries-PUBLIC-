Summary

LB652 terminates the Board of Educational Lands and Funds on July 1, 2027, and directs the Department of Administrative Services to assume control of all school lands formerly managed by the board. The department must sell those lands as existing leases expire, give the current lessee a right of first refusal, and otherwise sell at public auction. Proceeds from sales are to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the permanent school fund. Any funds remaining in the temporary school fund on July 1, 2027, are to be distributed to the state's school districts.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The amendment terminates the Board of Educational Lands and Funds on July 1, 2027 and transfers control of all school lands it managed to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) on that date. DAS must sell those lands as existing leases expire; the current lessee has a right of first refusal to buy, otherwise DAS will sell at public auction. Sale proceeds are to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the state's permanent school fund. Any monies remaining in the board's temporary school fund on July 1, 2027 will be distributed to school districts. No separate appropriations or direct DAS budget increases are specified.
