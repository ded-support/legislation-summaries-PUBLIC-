Summary

LB149 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§79-263, 79-265, and 79-267, repeals the originals, and outright repeals §79-265.01. It removes the statutory prohibition on suspending students in pre-K through grade two (harmonizing student-discipline provisions), clarifies that principals may impose short-term suspensions (up to five school days) and restates grounds for long-term suspension, expulsion, or reassignment. School districts must continue annually to report to the State Department of Education an assurance that the district has the required one-year firearm-expulsion policy and provide details of any expulsions under that policy. Superintendents and local school boards retain authority to modify expulsions on an individual basis. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
