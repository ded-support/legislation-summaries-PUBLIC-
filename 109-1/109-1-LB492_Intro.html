Summary

LB492 amends the Nebraska Juvenile Code to specify that juvenile court jurisdiction includes any juvenile voluntarily relinquished pursuant to section 43-106.01 to the Department of Health and Human Services or to any child-placement agency licensed by the Department of Health and Human Services (amended §43-247(7)). The bill repeals the original sections replaced. It contains no changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a procedural defense to juvenile truancy adjudication: if, after subtracting specified excludable absences, a child has accrued fewer than 20 unexcused absences in a school year, the petition may be defended. Excludable absences include lack of adequate transportation; the school's failure to coordinate agreed interventions/attendance supports; physical or mental illness excused by a parent/guardian (with or without a doctor's note); or absences otherwise excused by the school or court. Referrals must indicate which unexcused absences, if any, were excused by a parent/guardian for physical or mental illness. No language in the added text directs funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to the Department of Health and Human Services; no explicit DHHS fiscal responsibilities are established.
