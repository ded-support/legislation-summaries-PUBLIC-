Summary

LB492 retains juvenile-court jurisdiction over any juvenile voluntarily relinquished under section 43-106.01 to the Department of Health and Human Services or to a child-placement agency licensed by the Department of Health and Human Services (amending section 43-247). No provisions in the bill alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text refer to or assign duties, funding, or budget changes to the Department of Health and Human Services. The amendments redefine "habitually truant" (ages 13–16) as 20+ unexcused absences with documented educational loss; create a defense if, after excluding specified excusable absences, the child has fewer than 20 unexcused days; list exclusions (lack of transportation, school failure to coordinate agreed interventions/attendance supports, physical/mental illness excused by parent/guardian with or without a doctor’s note, or other school/court excusals); and require referrals to note which absences were excused for physical/mental illness. No appropriations or DHHS responsibilities are added.
