Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it several duties. The department must establish a Small Business Investment Program to award grants to microloan delivery and technical-assistance organizations and innovation hubs, coordinate statewide microlending support, leverage nonstate funds, and may use awards for match requirements, revolving loan funds, or guaranty funds. The department may award up to $3,000,000 annually under that program. Under the Small Business Assistance Act the department must create and administer grant programs (start-up grants up to $25,000; existing small-business grants up to $12,500) and contract with private entities to provide counseling in redevelopment areas and qualified census tracts. The department must submit biennial reports to the Governor and Legislature and may require reporting from contractors and grant recipients.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies definitions and scope for the Department of Economic Development: establishes "economic redevelopment area" (areas with average unemployment ≥150% of the state rate and ≥20% poverty by census tract) and defines "qualified census tract" by cross‑reference to 26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I) as of Jan 1, 2025. Expands covered parties to include private entities located in those areas and entities with which the department contracts. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
