Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it several duties: establish a small business investment program to award grants to microloan delivery and technical-assistance organizations and innovation hubs (the department may award up to $3 million annually and may identify/coordinate other state and federal funding); create and administer a Small Business Assistance grant program (grants up to $25,000 for startup individuals and up to $12,500 for small businesses ≤5 years); contract with private entities to provide specified counseling services in economic redevelopment areas and qualified census tracts; and submit biennial reports to the Governor and Legislature listing demand, grant and counseling recipients and amounts, impacts, and requiring contractors/grantees to provide necessary reporting.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added definitions and scope for the Department of Economic Development:

- "Qualified census tract" is adopted by reference to 26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I) as of Jan 1, 2025.

- "Economic redevelopment area" is defined by two objective thresholds: area unemployment at least 150% of the state rate (per the most recent decennial census or ACS 5-year estimate) and a census-tract poverty rate of 20% or more.

- The statute explicitly covers private entities located in those areas and entities with which the department contracts.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
