Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development for the Business Innovation Act and the Small Business Assistance Act. It requires the department to establish a small business investment program and authorizes the department to award up to $3,000,000 per year under that program. The department must create and administer a Small Business Assistance grant program providing up to $25,000 to individuals starting a small business and up to $12,500 to owners of small businesses under five years old. The department shall contract with private entities to provide professional counseling services (accounting, business planning, HR, technology coaching, etc.) for businesses in economic redevelopment areas or qualified census tracts. The department is required to submit periodic reports to the Governor and Legislature (including grant and counseling recipients and impact metrics) and may require contractors and recipients to provide information to fulfill reporting obligations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds/clarifies definitions relevant to the Department of Economic Development: establishes "qualified census tract" by reference to 26 U.S.C. 42(d)(5)(B)(ii)(I) as in effect on Jan. 1, 2025; and defines "economic redevelopment area" as a Nebraska area with average unemployment at least 150% of the state rate and a federal census-tract poverty rate of 20% or more. The change also explicitly covers one or more private entities located in such areas and entities with which the department contracts. No language in the provided text alters funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Economic Development.
