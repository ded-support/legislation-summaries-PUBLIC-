Summary

LB233 amends statutes governing the Nebraska State Insurance Program (sections 84-1607, 84-1608, 84-1609). It transfers and consolidates provisions allowing optional, employee-paid special coverages into the group life (84-1607) and group health (84-1608) insurance provisions—examples include accidental death and dismemberment, additional life amounts, dental, disability, disability income replacement, and dependent coverage—with uniform availability and employee choice to elect or decline. Section 84-1609 is amended to require the Nebraska State Insurance Program to include coverage for in vitro fertilization beginning July 1, 2025. The bill repeals the original sections and declares an emergency. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Nebraska State Insurance Program to include coverage for in vitro fertilization beginning July 1, 2025. The program is granted discretion in how it provides that coverage.
