Summary

LB605 requires each school board to adopt a policy governing student participation as a transgender student in school-sponsored extracurricular activities and in activities run by any athletics or activities association of which the school is a member. The bill directs school administrators or athletic directors to submit a written application identifying the student as transgender and listing desired activities. School boards must determine eligibility under the adopted policy, require documentation (current registration, a written statement from student and parent/guardian, corroborating statements from others), and, when association approval is needed, submit required documentation and cooperate with that association. Participation on sex-separated teams defaults to the biological sex on the student’s birth certificate unless board approval is granted. The bill contains no funding or appropriation provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for school boards (with school administrators or athletic directors) to: receive written requests from transgender students listing desired activities; determine eligibility under locally adopted policy; and, when applicable, submit eligibility applications and required documentation to any athletics or activities association of which the school is a member. Authorizes sharing student registration, a written statement from the student and parent/guardian, and corroborating documentation from others with those associations. If approved, school boards must cooperate with associations to obtain determinations. Establishes that eligibility for sex-separated teams defaults to the biological sex on the birth certificate unless approved under the local transgender or cross‑gender policy. Clarifies the policy does not encourage medical transition and does not alter existing cross‑gender exceptions. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
