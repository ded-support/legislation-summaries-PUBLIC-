Summary

Section 1 (amending §48‑225) revises the definition of "disabled veteran" to include an individual who is receiving compensation, disability retirement benefits, or a pension under a public statute administered by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs or a military department. The bill contains no provisions altering funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Military Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies veterans' preference rules: defines "preference eligible" (veteran or spouse), limits a servicemember spouse's preference to while the servicemember is on active status and up to 180 days after discharge, and defines "servicemember." Requires specified documentation (including proof if killed in the line of duty or died due to service). If two or more preference eligibles are considered, veterans preference must be applied equally. Spouses must provide proof of the servicemember's active status and marriage; similar proof requirements are extended to private employers. No language affects the Military Department's funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
