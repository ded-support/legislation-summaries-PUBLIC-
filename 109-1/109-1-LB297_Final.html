Summary

LB297 amends calculation of the combined tax rate in the Employment Security Law (section 48-649.03). It specifies that the state's reserve ratio and planned yield are to be calculated using "total wages for contributory employers" (defined as all remuneration paid by a contributory employer). The numerator for the reserve ratio includes amounts available in the Unemployment Trust Fund and the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund and certain employer combined-tax receivables. The commissioner (administrative official under the Employment Security Law) may lower the scheduled yield factor if the state's reserve ratio is 0.70% or greater. For tax year 2025 only, the bill fixes rate-category 12 at 0.48; otherwise the average combined tax rate is assigned to category 12. No new appropriations or direct changes to funding levels are made. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
