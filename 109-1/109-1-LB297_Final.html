Summary

LB297 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-649.03 (Employment Security Law) governing calculation of employers' combined tax rates. It specifies the state's reserve ratio uses amounts available to pay benefits in the Unemployment Trust Fund and the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund and uses "total wages for contributory employers" as the denominator. The commissioner retains authority to lower the scheduled yield factor when the reserve ratio is 0.70% or greater. The bill sets the average combined tax rate normally assigned to category 12, but fixes category 12's rate at 0.48 for tax year 2025. Filing-delinquent employers remain assignable to category 20 unless corrected. No new appropriations or budget allocations are created. An emergency clause makes the act effective on passage.

Adds a temporary rate change for contributory employers: for tax year 2025 only, the "category twelve" rate is set at 0.48. No state agencies or departments are mentioned and there are no explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
