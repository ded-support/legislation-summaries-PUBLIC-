Summary

LB566 includes a recapture provision assigning authority to the Department of Revenue to recapture the residential purchase income tax credit if the claimant sells or transfers the residence or stops using it as a primary residence within five years after the end of the taxable year the credit was claimed. The bill contains no provisions making changes to Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
