Summary

LB667 amends §60-1438 of the Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act to revise dealer compensation rules for diagnostic work and parts. Key agency-related provisions: dealers may establish an average parts markup by submitting sequential customer repair orders; manufacturers have 30 days to audit and 45 days after approval for the markup to take effect. A dealer may appeal a denial to the Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Board, with the manufacturer bearing the burden to justify denial under the Act; an unreasonable denial is an Act violation subject to board enforcement. The bill defines compensation when a franchisor furnishes parts at no or reduced cost (uses "fair wholesale value") and preserves manufacturer/distributor audit and recall/warranty obligations. Claim/timing rules (six-month filing, 30-day approval/payment, audit periods) are clarified. Introduced in the Legislature and heard by the Transportation and Telecommunications Committee. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds rules under the Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act governing dealer compensation and rate review. Key points:

- Denials must be made pursuant to the Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act.

- When calculating part compensation, only retail sales not involving warranty repairs are used; markups for listed recommended maintenance items (oil, filters, fluids, brakes, spark plugs, wipers, tires, etc.) are excluded.

- If a franchisor supplies a part free or at reduced cost for warranty work, the franchisor must pay the dealer the dealer's cost plus the dealer's markup, multiplied by the part's “fair wholesale value,” defined as the greatest of dealer paid amount, franchisor price-schedule cost, or cost of a substantially identical franchisor-listed part.

- A manufacturer/distributor may request up to 100 additional dealer repair orders (from a selected 90-day window) to verify rates, may adjust payments, and dealers have 30 days to rebut; requests cannot be made more than once within 12 months.
