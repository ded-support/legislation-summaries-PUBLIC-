Summary

The bill expands the Department of Banking and Finance’s regulatory and fee authority. It adds specific fees (Sec. 8‑602: $50,000 charter fee for digital asset depositories; $500 investigation fee for digital asset depository branch/merger/conditional‑charter matters; Sec. 8‑2711: $1,500 nonrefundable license application fee), authorizes the Director to recover administration and enforcement costs by imposing proportionate fees, and requires applicants/licensees to pay investigation, examination, and related expenses. The Director may participate in multistate supervision and use the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry and retains confidentiality, examination, reporting, and inspection powers. All fees, charges, and costs under the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund; fines are remitted for constitutional distribution. The bill contains no direct appropriations or new funding authorizations to the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds extensive money-transmitter authority and procedures to the Department of Banking and Finance. Key department powers: define department/director; adopt rules; enter multistate and federal information‑sharing agreements; use the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System and Registry; accept third‑party audits; hire analytical/exam software; conduct/summarily accept examinations; summon witnesses; and impose/collect proportionate fees, examination costs, and penalties. Licensing: $1,500 nonrefundable application fee; license decisions within 120 days (60 days for control acquisitions, deemed approved if not decided); applicants/licensees must meet net‑worth and surety bond requirements. Fiscal provisions: the department must remit all fees, charges, and costs collected under the act to the State Treasurer for credit to the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund; fines collected are remitted to the State Treasurer per the Constitution.
