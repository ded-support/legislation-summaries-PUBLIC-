Summary

LB473 expands the Department of Banking and Finance’s authority under the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act. It establishes new fees (including a $50,000 charter fee for digital asset depositories and a $500 investigative fee for branch/merger/conditional‑charter requests), retains a $1,500 nonrefundable license application fee and specified minimum investigative fees, and grants the Director rulemaking, cost‑recovery and proportionate‑fee authority. The department may accept third‑party or multistate exams/audits, use analytic systems, and participate in multistate supervision. Licensees must provide records and pay examination costs; failure to respond within 21 days can trigger fines (up to $5,000 per violation) and investigation cost charges. The department may issue a notice of cancellation if a licensee fails to maintain its surety bond. All fees and charges collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund; fines are remitted per Article VII, §5. No other appropriations or budget changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions (Nebraska Money Transmitters Act) designate the Department of Banking and Finance (and its Director) as the supervising authority, expanding its powers to: enter agreements with federal/state regulators and multistate organizations; use the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System/Registry and analytical/examination tools; accept third‑party audits and out‑of‑state exam reports; conduct and coordinate examinations (including multistate processes); require records, background checks, reports, bonds, net‑worth and permissible‑investment standards; and impose fees, costs, fines, suspensions, and enforcement orders. Financial flows: all fees, charges and costs collected under the Act are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund; fines are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution. No separate new appropriations or line‑item budget allocations are specified.
