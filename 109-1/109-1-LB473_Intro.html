Summary

The bill expands authorities and fee provisions for the Department of Banking and Finance. It adds to the department fee schedule a $50,000 charter fee for digital asset depositories under the Nebraska Financial Innovation Act and a $500 investigation fee for branch, merger/acquisition-of-control, or conditional-charter-extension filings for digital asset depositories, while retaining a $1,500 nonrefundable license application fee (Secs. 8-602; 8-2710/8-2713). The Director may recover enforcement and supervisory costs through proportionate fees (Sec. 8-2705(2)), and licensees must pay examination costs (Sec. 8-2707(5)). The department may share/accept confidential examination information in multistate supervision and use the Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry for records and fee processing; receipts must list a department contact for complaints. Other provided text contains no additional changes to the department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting the Department of Banking and Finance:

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Banking and Finance and "Director" as its Director, and expands the director's authorities (exams, investigations, rulemaking, multistate cooperation, use of analytics/contractors, acceptance of external audits/reports, and use of the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System and Registry).

- The director may recover administrative costs by imposing and collecting proportionate fees and may require licensees to pay reasonable examination costs. A nonrefundable $1,500 application fee is established (waiver authority retained).

- Fiscal handling: all fees, charges, and costs collected under the act are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund; fines are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution per Neb. Const. art. VII, §5.

- No direct appropriations or new budget line-item allocations for the Department are included.
