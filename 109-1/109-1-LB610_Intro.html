Summary

LB610 assigns several duties and authorities to the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) related to supplemental reimbursement for ground emergency medical transport. Key provisions:

- The department must specify required evidence, data submissions, and recordkeeping that governmental entities must provide when seeking supplemental reimbursement on behalf of eligible providers.

- By January 1, 2026, the department must seek any necessary federal approvals to implement the program and may limit reimbursement to Title XIX–allowable costs.

- The department shall submit claims and annual assurances to obtain federal financial participation and may implement an intergovernmental transfer program only if federal participation/approval is available.

- The department’s chief executive officer may return or refuse intergovernmental transfers or adjust payments to maintain Medicaid compliance and must declare the program inoperative if federal determinations require it.

- No specific state appropriations or dollar amounts are included; payments are contingent on federal approval and federal financial participation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services, in 2026, to seek any necessary federal approvals to implement subsection (2) of the Ground Emergency Medical Transport Act as of January 1, 2025. Implementation must be based on the actual and allowable costs defined by regulations that existed on January 1, 2025, and must comply with any other applicable federal requirements. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
