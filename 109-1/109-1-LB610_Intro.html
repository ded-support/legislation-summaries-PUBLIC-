Summary

LB610 revises the Department of Health and Human Services’ duties under the Ground Emergency Medical Transport Act. Key DHHS provisions: seek necessary federal approvals by January 1, 2026; specify the evidence, data submission, and records governmental entities must provide to support certifications and claims; limit the program to costs allowable under Title XIX as of January 1, 2025; implement the intergovernmental transfer program only if federal funding and approvals are available; authorize the department’s CEO to refuse or return IGTs and adjust payments to ensure Medicaid compliance; and submit claims and annual assurances to CMS that only allowable expenditures are claimed. The bill allows increased capitation payments to begin as of January 1, 2025 if federal approval is obtained. No direct state appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must base ground emergency medical transport payments on "actual and allowable" costs as defined by regulations in effect on January 1, 2025, and comply with any applicable federal requirements. In 2026 the department is directed to seek any federal approvals needed to implement subsection (2) of the Ground Emergency Medical Transport Act (implementation referenced to January 1, 2025). The added text does not specify any new appropriations, funding amounts, or budget allocations.
