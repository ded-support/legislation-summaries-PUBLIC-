Summary

References to the Department of Revenue in LB709:

- The bill creates the Adoption Tax Credit Act (effective for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2026) and explicitly authorizes the Department of Revenue to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to carry out that Act. The Act provides a refundable Nebraska credit equal to 10% of the federal adoption expenses credit (26 U.S.C. §23). The new Adoption Tax Credit is added to the list of refundable credits in Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2715.07(2)(d).

- Separately, §77-2715.07(7)(d) confirms the Department of Revenue’s authority to recapture the residence-purchase tax credit if the taxpayer disposes of or ceases primary residence use within five years.

No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Adoption Tax Credit Act" effective for tax years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2026. It allows a refundable Nebraska income tax credit equal to 10% of the taxpayer's federal adoption expenses tax credit under 26 U.S.C. §23, for taxpayers otherwise eligible for the federal credit. The Department of Revenue is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act. No specific appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included in the added text.
