Summary

LB92 creates the Residential Tenant Clean Slate Act, directing Nebraska trial courts to seal (grant "clean slate relief" for) eviction records in specified circumstances—automatic sealing after dismissal, when a writ of restitution is reversed/vacated or never executed, COVID-19 nonpayment evictions between March 13, 2020–June 30, 2021, when a defendant was a minor, or when a judgment violated statutory protections—and allows tenant petitions for sealing after three years in other cases. Procedural requirements assign duties to trial courts: petition filing, notice to prior parties, a 30‑day objection period, hearings if objected to (objector bears burden), and that orders are final and appealable. No state agencies or executive departments are named, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Residential Tenant Clean Slate Act," defining terms (tenant, landlord, residential tenancy, eviction proceeding, trial court) and requiring the trial court to immediately issue record-sealing ("clean slate") orders under section 5 when an eviction is dismissed. Gives tenants grounds to petition the trial court for sealing (including vacated/reversed judgments, unwritten/unexecuted writs of restitution, evictions for nonpayment during the COVID-19 emergency Mar 13, 2020–Jun 30, 2021, named minors, or certain statutory-violation judgments) or to petition after three years from writ/final judgment. Petitions are filed in trial court, served on prior parties (30 days to object); no objection -> grant if criteria met; objections trigger a hearing with the objector bearing the burden. Grants/denials are final and appealable per section 25-1902. No executive agencies or departments are created or mentioned, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
