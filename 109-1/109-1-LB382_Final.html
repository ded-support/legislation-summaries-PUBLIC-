Summary

LB382 amends funding and reimbursement for aging services. It expands uses of the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to include reimbursement of actual costs for eligible activities and services under section 81-2222 and retains investment/crediting provisions (State Treasurer returns; state investment officer investments; earnings credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024). The Legislature intends to appropriate $2,000,000 in FY2025-26 and $2,000,000 in FY2026-27 from that fund to the state's eight area agencies on aging (equal shares). Section 81-2224 is amended to require "the department" to reimburse each designated area agency on aging 75% of actual costs from (1) state appropriations (including the specified fund), (2) federal allocations (e.g., Older Americans Act), and (3) other funds; payments are monthly and prorated if funds are insufficient.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds legislative intent to appropriate $2,000,000 in FY2025‑26 and $2,000,000 in FY2026‑27 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to the state's eight area agencies on aging. Funds are for providing or reimbursing actual costs of eligible activities and services as defined in section 81‑2222 and will be distributed equally to each of the eight agencies. References cash fund appropriations described in section 2 of the act.
