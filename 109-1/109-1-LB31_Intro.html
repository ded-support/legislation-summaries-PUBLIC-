Summary

Requires the State Board of Education to develop and distribute a model policy governing school district use of student surveillance, monitoring, and tracking technology. The model must require districts to inventory tools and provide: vendor names/contact information; purchase and maintenance costs; tool descriptions and privacy protections; opt-out availability; whether data is shared with law enforcement or used for discipline; accommodations for students with disabilities/IEPs; how biometric and personally identifiable information is stored/shared/sold; and available remedies (including under the Consumer Protection Act, the Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act, and section 79-2,104). School districts must post the inventory on their websites and provide hard copies on request. Each school board must adopt a written policy conforming to the model by May 1, 2026, to take effect for the 2026–27 school year. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Education (via the State Board of Education) must develop and distribute a model policy on school use of student surveillance, monitoring, and tracking technology. School districts must inventory tools and provide vendor contact info, purchase/maintenance costs, tool descriptions and privacy protections, opt‑out options, law‑enforcement/data‑sharing and disciplinary implications, disability accommodations, biometric/PII storage/sharing/sale practices, and available remedies (including Consumer Protection Act, Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act, and section 79‑2,104). Districts must post the inventory online and provide hard copies on request. By May 1, 2026, each school board must adopt a written policy conforming to the state model for implementation in school year 2026–27 (with discretion to add provisions). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
