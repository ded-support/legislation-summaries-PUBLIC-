Summary

LB287 amends the Nebraska Housing Agency Act (section 71-1572) to assign specific bed‑bug responsibilities to the housing agency for a city of the metropolitan class. It defines "bed bug" and "pest control professional" (a commercial applicator licensed under the Pesticide Act). Requirements include pre‑rental visual inspections and disclosure of adjacent-unit infestations; prohibition on showing or renting units known or reasonably suspected to be infested; complaint response timelines (acknowledge and inspect within five days; remediate within 14 days if infestation is confirmed); inspection of adjacent units; advance notice to tenants for entry; five‑day tenant notification of pest‑professional determinations; and two‑year retention of complaint and treatment records. The bill makes the housing agency financially responsible for investigation and remediation costs. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the housing agency of a city of the metropolitan class regarding bed bugs. Defines "bed bug" and "pest control professional" (must be a commercial applicator licensed under the Pesticide Act). Requires pre‑rental visual inspections, prohibits showing/renting units known or reasonably suspected to be infested, and mandates disclosure to prospective tenants if adjacent units are infested or being treated. On notification of suspected infestation the agency must: acknowledge the complaint within 5 days; inspect or retain a licensed pest control professional within 5 days; inspect adjacent units within 5 days; provide remediation within 14 days of confirmed infestation; give tenants advance notice of entry; notify affected tenants of the professional's determination within 5 days; and keep written records (including chemicals used) for two years. The housing agency is financially responsible for investigation and remediation costs.
