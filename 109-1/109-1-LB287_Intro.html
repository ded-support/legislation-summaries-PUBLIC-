Summary

LB287 amends the Nebraska Housing Agency Act (section 71-1572) to assign specific bed‑bug responsibilities to housing agencies for cities of the metropolitan class. The agency must visually inspect units before renting, may not show or lease units it knows or reasonably suspects are infested, and must disclose adjacent-unit infestations to prospective tenants. On complaint, the agency must acknowledge within five days, inspect or retain a pest control professional (a commercial applicator licensed under the Pesticide Act) within five days, remediate confirmed infestations within 14 days, inspect adjacent units within five days, provide advance notice before unit entry, notify affected tenants of determinations within five days, and retain complaint/remediation records for two years. The housing agency is made responsible for all investigation and remediation costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the "housing agency for a city of the metropolitan class," including mandated bed‑bug prevention and response actions. Key agency obligations: pre‑rental visual inspections and disclosure of adjacent unit treatments; refusal to show/rent units known or reasonably suspected to be infested; acknowledge complaints within 5 days; inspect or hire a licensed pest control professional within 5 days; remediate confirmed infestations within 14 days; inspect adjacent units within 5 days; provide tenant notice before entry and notice of pest professional determinations within 5 days; keep written complaint/control records for two years. Fiscal effect: the housing agency is expressly made responsible for all investigation and remediation costs (no specific appropriation provided).
