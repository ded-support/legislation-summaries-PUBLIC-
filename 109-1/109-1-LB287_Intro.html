Summary

LB287 amends the Nebraska Housing Agency Act to impose specific bed‑bug duties on housing agencies for cities of the metropolitan class. Agencies must visually inspect units before renting, refuse to show/rent vacant units known or reasonably suspected to be infested, disclose adjacent-unit infestation/treatment status, and upon complaint: acknowledge within five days, secure investigatory services from a pest control professional (licensed under the Pesticide Act) within five days, remediate confirmed infestations within 14 days, inspect adjacent units, provide advance notice before unit entry and timely notice of pest‑control determinations, and keep two years of written records (including chemicals used). The housing agency is made financially responsible for investigation and remediation costs. The bill repeals the prior version of section 71-1572.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Makes the housing agency for a city of the metropolitan class responsible for bed‑bug prevention and response. Defines "bed bug" and "pest control professional" (a commercial applicator licensed under the Pesticide Act). Requirements include pre‑rental visual inspections, prohibition on renting units known or reasonably suspected to be infested, disclosure of adjacent unit treatments, and specific complaint timelines: acknowledge complaints within 5 days, inspect or hire a licensed pest control professional within 5 days, remediate within 14 days if infestation is confirmed, inspect adjacent units, provide tenant notices, and keep written records (including chemicals used) for two years. Fiscal effect: the housing agency is explicitly made responsible for the costs of investigation and remediation; no separate appropriation is provided.
