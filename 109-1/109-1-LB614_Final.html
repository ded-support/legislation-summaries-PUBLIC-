Summary

Bill LB614 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§16-901 and 17-1001 to restate extraterritorial zoning rules and farm-building exemptions. Agencies/departments referenced and affected:

- Cities of the first class, second class, and villages: may apply municipal zoning, building, electrical and plumbing ordinances in their extraterritorial zoning jurisdiction (ETJ); ETJ distances remain two miles (first-class) and one mile (second-class/village) generally, but are reduced to one mile and one-half mile respectively for purposes of sections 70-1001 to 70-1020.

- County boards: must receive written notice at least 30 days before final city/village action affecting ETJ and may comment.

- Boards of adjustment (per §§19-907–19-915): may exempt qualifying farm buildings (farmstead ≥20 acres, ≥$1,000 annual production).

- Joint planning commissions/departments: where present, the 30-day notice requirement does not apply.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for cities of the first class—and separately for cities of the second class or villages—to allow their board of adjustment (acting under sections 19-907 to 19-915) to exempt certain farm buildings within their extraterritorial zoning jurisdiction from zoning, property-use, building, electrical and plumbing ordinances (and, for first‑class cities, ordinances authorized by section 16-240) when consistent with the municipality’s comprehensive development plan and documented long‑range population/economic projections. Defines “farm building” by reference to section 77-1359: agricultural use on a farmstead of 20+ acres producing $1,000+ of farm products annually. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
