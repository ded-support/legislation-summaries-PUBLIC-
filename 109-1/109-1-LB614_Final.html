Summary

LB614 amends Neb. Rev. Statutes §§16-901 and 17-1001 governing extraterritorial zoning. It clarifies ETJ distances (city of the first class: generally 2 miles, but 1 mile for chapters 70-1001–70-1020; second-class cities/villages: generally 1 mile, but 1/2 mile for those same chapters). Cities and villages may apply municipal zoning, building, electrical and plumbing ordinances within their ETJ but may exempt certain farm buildings by action of the board of adjustment when consistent with the comprehensive plan. The bill defines qualifying farm buildings (per §77-1359; farmstead ≥20 acres and ≥$1,000 annual production). It requires written notice to the county board 30 days before final ETJ zoning decisions, with exceptions for counties >100,000 population or where a joint planning commission/department exists. Original sections are repealed. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for local boards of adjustment to exempt certain farm buildings from zoning, building, electrical, plumbing, and related ordinances within a city’s or village’s extraterritorial zoning jurisdiction. The exemption may be granted by the board of adjustment of a city of the first class, city of the second class, or village if the structure is consistent with the comprehensive development plan and required population/economic projections (citing sections 19-907 to 19-915 and section 16-240). Defines "farm building" by reference to section 77-1359: on a 20+ acre farmstead producing ≥ $1,000/year in farm products. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
