Summary

LB614 amends extraterritorial zoning statutes for Nebraska municipalities. Key agency/department actions:

- Cities of the first class: general ETZJ remains two miles, but for purposes of sections 70-1001–70-1020 the ETZJ is set to one mile.

- Cities of the second class and villages: general ETZJ remains one mile, but for purposes of sections 70-1001–70-1020 the ETZJ is set to one-half mile.

- Board of adjustment may exempt specified farm buildings (defined by reference to section 77-1359, on farmsteads ≥20 acres producing ≥$1,000/yr) from municipal ordinances within ETZJ when consistent with the comprehensive plan.

- Cities/villages must notify the county board at least 30 days before final action on zoning changes affecting ETZJ; county boards may comment within 30 days. Notification requirements do not apply if the county’s population (per the U.S. Bureau of the Census) exceeds 100,000 or there is a joint planning commission/department.

- Original sections 16-901 and 17-1001 are repealed.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for cities of the first class, and for cities of the second class or villages, to exempt certain farm buildings located in their extraterritorial zoning jurisdiction from zoning, property‑use, building, electrical, plumbing and related ordinances. Exemptions are made by the municipal board of adjustment pursuant to statutory procedures (sections 19‑907 to 19‑915) and must be consistent with the jurisdiction’s comprehensive development plan, including documented population and economic projections. "Farm building" is defined by reference to section 77‑1359 as agricultural buildings on farmsteads of 20+ acres producing $1,000+ in annual farm products. References also include ordinances authorized by section 16‑240. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
