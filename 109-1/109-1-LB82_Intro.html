Summary

LB82 adopts the Cosmetology Licensure Compact into Nebraska law and amends several sections of the state's Cosmetology Practice Act. It creates a Compact Commission (with an Executive Committee) to administer a multistate licensure system and a centralized Data System. Member States' State Licensing Authorities remain the primary regulators, select one delegate to the Commission, handle complaints and discipline within their borders, and must submit licensure and adverse-action data to the Commission. The Commission may adopt rules, hire staff, lease property, initiate legal actions, and convene meetings. Funding authorities include levying annual assessments on Member States, charging fees to licensees for multistate licenses and renewals, accepting grants/donations, and establishing a budget with annual financial reviews. The bill contains no specific Nebraska appropriation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Cosmetology Licensure Compact establishing a multistate Compact Commission and Executive Committee to administer multistate cosmetology licenses. State Licensing Authorities in Member States retain primary licensing, disciplinary, investigatory, and enforcement responsibilities and must submit uniform license and adverse-action data to a Commission-operated Data System. The Commission may adopt rules, hire staff, lease/purchase property, contract services, maintain a budget, borrow, accept gifts/grants, and cooperate with law enforcement and courts. Funding: the Commission may levy annual assessments on Member States (allocated by formula), charge fees to licensees, accept donations/grants, and must secure funds before incurring obligations; annual financial reviews are required. Judicial venue for Commission actions is federal court in D.C. or where its principal office is located.
