Summary

LB82 adopts the Cosmetology Licensure Compact and amends Nebraska's Cosmetology, Electrology, Esthetics, Nail Technology, and Body Art Practice Act to implement it. Key state actors: each Member State's State Licensing Authority (responsible for issuing, investigating, disciplining, and delegating an administrator to the Compact Commission) and the state's executive and judicial branches (charged with enforcement). The Compact establishes a Compact Commission and Executive Committee to administer multistate licenses, operate a centralized Data System for licensure/investigative information, promulgate binding rules, and initiate enforcement or dispute actions (including in federal court). Financing: the Commission may adopt a budget, hire staff, accept grants/donations, levy annual assessments on Member States, and charge fees to licensees to cover operations; it must conduct annual financial reviews. The bill does not appropriate state funds directly.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a Cosmetology Licensure Compact creating a interstate "Commission" and related Executive Committee to oversee a multistate licensing program. Key government actors: each Member State's State Licensing Authority (and its delegate), state executive and judicial branches, governors and state legislative leaders (for default/termination notices), law enforcement agencies (for cooperation), and federal courts (United States District Court for D.C. or district where the Commission's principal office is located) as venue for litigation. The Commission must develop/operate a centralized Data System; Member States must submit uniform licensure and disciplinary data. Financial provisions authorize the Commission to establish a budget, levy annual assessments on Member States (allocated by formula), charge licensee fees (initial and renewal), accept grants/donations, borrow money, and keep audited financial records subject to annual review. The Commission may not pledge Member State credit and must include the financial review in its annual report.
