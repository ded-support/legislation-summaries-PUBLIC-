Summary

LB82 adopts the Cosmetology Licensure Compact, creating a multistate licensure system and a Compact Commission with an Executive Committee. State Licensing Authorities remain the primary regulators and disciplinarians for cosmetology within their states, must cooperate with the Commission, and must submit licensure, investigative, and adverse-action data to a Commission-managed Data System. The Commission may adopt binding rules, hire staff, contract services, own property, initiate litigation, and hold meetings. Financing: the Commission may accept grants/donations, levy annual assessments on Member States, and charge fees to licensees (including application and renewal fees) to fund its operations, must establish a budget, keep financial records, and obtain an annual financial review. The bill amends Neb. statutes (including sections 38-1001 and 38-1004) to incorporate the Compact. No direct state appropriations or specific Nebraska budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds creation of a Compact Commission and requires each Member State's State Licensing Authority to cooperate with it. Key governmental entities named: the Commission (and its Executive Committee), Member State State Licensing Authorities, state executive and judicial branches, governors and legislative leaders, law enforcement agencies, and federal courts (U.S. District Court for D.C. or where the Commission's principal office is located).

Fiscal and administrative provisions: the Commission may adopt rules, hire staff, buy/lease property, borrow, purchase insurance, and maintain a Data System. It may accept grants/donations, assess annual fees on Member States (allocated by rule) and impose fees on licensees to fund operations, establish a budget, keep audited financial records, and cannot pledge Member State credit without approval. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.
