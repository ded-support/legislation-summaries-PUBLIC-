Summary

LB358 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §83-178 (Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act). It directs the Department (and its director) to establish and maintain individual files for committed offenders and requires file review before classification, transfer, preparole, or parole decisions. Committed offenders may access their own individual file by written request to the facility chief executive officer and may obtain medical records from the provider under §§71-8401–71-8407; the department may withhold mental‑health/psychological records when appropriate. The Office of Public Counsel retains inspection authority under §§81‑8,240–81‑8,254 but must have offender consent to access medical/mental‑health records and may not further disclose those records except as authorized by law. No funding or appropriation changes.
