Summary

LB358 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §83‑178 (Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act). It directs the department and its director to maintain individual committed‑offender files and requires file review before classification, transfer, preparole preparation, or parole release. It grants committed offenders access to their own department file upon written request to the facility chief executive officer. Medical records remain obtainable from the provider under §§71‑8401 to 71‑8407, while the department may withhold mental‑health/psychological records when appropriate. The Public Counsel and the Office of Public Counsel retain inspection authority under §§81‑8,240–81‑8,254, but access to and disclosure of an offender’s medical or mental‑health records require the offender’s consent and are otherwise restricted. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
