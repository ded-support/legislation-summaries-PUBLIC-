Summary

LB539 amends Nebraska handgun regulation statutes and affects the Nebraska State Patrol, local chiefs of police and sheriffs, mental health boards, and federally licensed firearms importers/manufacturers/dealers (18 U.S.C. 923).

Key agency provisions:

- Nebraska State Patrol: may be furnished only the criminal-history information necessary to determine eligibility (including concealed-carry qualification); must ensure accuracy and update records made available to NICS; shall provide chiefs of police or sheriffs only a determination of whether an applicant is disqualified from purchasing or possessing a handgun.

- Immunity: persons, agencies, or mental health boards reporting in good faith are immune from liability.

- Penalties: knowingly causing the State Patrol to request records without reasonable belief the subject applied is a Class II misdemeanor; knowingly providing false statements or false ID in connection with a handgun purchase/transfer is a Class IV felony.

- Federally licensed dealers must inspect a valid certificate and photo ID before transfer.

The bill repeals numerous related statutory sections. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that criminal-history record checks for firearms or explosives cover individuals qualified for a concealed-carry permit under section 69-2430 or otherwise required by law/section to undergo such checks. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are referenced.
