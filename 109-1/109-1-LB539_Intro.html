Summary

LB539 amends Nebraska handgun statutes and reallocates responsibilities for criminal-history checks to the Nebraska State Patrol. The Patrol is restricted to receiving only information necessary to determine fitness for concealed-carry permits or firearm transfer disqualifications and must provide only to an applicant’s local chief of police or sheriff whether the applicant is disqualified. Entities (including mental-health boards) reporting in good faith are granted immunity from liability. The bill creates a Class II misdemeanor for knowingly causing the Patrol to request checks without reasonable belief an application exists and retains a Class IV felony for materially false statements or false identification in handgun transactions. Multiple statutory sections are repealed; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that criminal history record information (CHRI) checks for firearms or explosives purposes apply to persons qualified for a concealed carry permit under § 69-2430 or otherwise subject to CHRI checks by law. No specific government agency or department is named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
