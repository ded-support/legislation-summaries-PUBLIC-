Summary

LB48A appropriates $1,000,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), Program 33, for FY2025‑26 and $1,000,000 for FY2026‑27 to aid in carrying out Legislative Bill 48 (2025). Expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from these appropriated funds are capped at $60,000 for each of FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates $1,000,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund to DHHS (Program 33) for FY2025‑26 and another $1,000,000 for FY2026‑27 to support implementation of Legislative Bill 48 (109th Leg., 1st Sess., 2025).
