Summary

LB449 amends §39‑1365.01 to revise state highway planning and revenue allocation. It requires the department responsible for the state highway system to develop a specific, long‑range plan, present annual plans and the §39‑1365.02 report to the Legislature by December 1, and submit them to the appropriate legislative standing committees for review. The plan must (1) designate portions of the state highway system as expressways and (2) account for highway revenue by each district identified in §39‑1102, stipulating that not less than 70% of revenue raised in each district be allocated to projects within that district. The bill repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a district-level accounting and allocation requirement for highway revenue: agencies must account for highway revenue raised by each district referenced in section 39-1102, and at least 70% of revenue raised in each district must be allocated to projects within that same district.
