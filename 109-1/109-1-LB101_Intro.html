Summary

LB101 amends Nebraska’s Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act to (1) affirm a tenant’s right to a jury trial and bar rental-agreement waivers of rights under state or federal law; (2) require complaints for restitution to be filed with the clerk of the district or county court and permit inclusion of a jury demand; (3) revise summons content and service rules (including a three‑day service window, five‑day return, and filer affidavit) to notify defendants of the right to a jury trial; and (4) impose specific duties on courts—informing defendants of jury rights, inquiring about jury demands in county court, scheduling jury trials as practicable, and allowing courts to require tenants to deposit accrued rent if a tenant-requested jury trial is continued. If judgment for possession is entered, the court issues writs of restitution directing the constable or sheriff to restore possession. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made. Original cited sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural provisions for courts handling possession actions: complaints may include a jury demand and must notify defendants of their right to a jury trial. If a defendant has not demanded a jury by first appearance, the court must inform them and, in county court cases, ask whether they demand a jury or elect a bench trial. If tried by the court, the trial must occur 10–14 days after summons; if by jury, it must be scheduled as soon as practicable. If a tenant requests a continuance beyond the initial date, the court may require the tenant to deposit accruing rent with the clerk of court. No agencies beyond courts/county courts and the court clerk are created or given funding; no appropriations or budget changes are specified.
