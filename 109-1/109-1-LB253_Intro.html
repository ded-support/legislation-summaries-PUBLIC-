Summary

LB253 requires health insurers, nonprofit health service plans, health maintenance organizations, and Nebraska’s medical assistance program (administered by the Department of Health and Human Services) to cover biomarker testing for diagnosis, treatment, management, or monitoring when supported by medical/scientific evidence, including FDA approvals/labels, FDA-indicated drug tests and warnings, CMS national or Medicare administrative contractor local coverage determinations, or nationally recognized clinical guidelines. The medical assistance program must implement coverage by January 1, 2026; its contractors must provide testing at the same scope, duration, and frequency. Prior authorization decisions must be issued within 72 hours (nonurgent) or 24 hours (urgent). Insurers and DHHS must post an accessible exception/appeals process on their websites. The bill contains no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds mandatory coverage standards for biomarker testing, including for the state medical assistance program (required no later than Jan 1, 2026). Tests must be covered when supported by medical/scientific evidence such as: FDA-approved/cleared labeled indications, FDA‑indicated tests for drugs, FDA drug label warnings/precautions, CMS national coverage determinations or local Medicare administrative contractor determinations, and nationally recognized clinical practice guidelines. Entities contracting with the medical assistance program must provide testing at the same scope/duration/frequency as the program. Prior authorization decisions/notifications must occur within 72 hours (nonurgent) or 24 hours (urgent) for insurers and the medical assistance program (or their third‑party agents). Exception request processes must be posted on insurer websites and the Department of Health and Human Services website. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
