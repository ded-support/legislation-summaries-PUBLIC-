Summary

LB253 requires coverage of biomarker testing for diagnosis, treatment, management, or monitoring when supported by medical/scientific evidence, including FDA-labeled indications, FDA drug test indications/warnings, CMS national or Medicare administrative contractor local coverage determinations, and nationally recognized clinical guidelines. It directs Nebraska health insurers, nonprofit health service plans, and HMOs to provide such coverage in ways that limit care disruptions and to post an exception process on their websites. Prior‑authorization decisions must be issued within 72 hours for nonurgent and 24 hours for urgent requests. The state medical assistance program must implement equivalent biomarker coverage by January 1, 2026; contractors must provide testing at the same scope/frequency, follow the same prior‑auth timeframes, and post the exception process on the Department of Health and Human Services website. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that biomarker testing be covered when supported by medical/scientific evidence, including FDA-approved/cleared test indications and drug labels, CMS national coverage determinations or local Medicare administrative contractor decisions, and nationally recognized clinical guidelines. Requires the state medical assistance program (Medicaid) to cover biomarker testing by Jan 1, 2026, and obligates entities contracting with the program to provide equivalent scope, duration, and frequency. Sets prior‑authorization decision timelines (72 hours nonurgent, 24 hours urgent) and requires an exceptions/appeals process be posted on the Department of Health and Human Services website. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.
