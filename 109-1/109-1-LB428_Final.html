Summary

LB428 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-532 to require school districts to notify parents, guardians, and educational decisionmakers at least 15 days before administering surveys about student drug, vape, alcohol, or tobacco use (via the school's electronic notification system or mail). Notices must describe survey content, purpose, age-appropriateness, data use, access, privacy protections, and disclosure plans. Parents may request a copy, review the survey in person, or exempt their child. School personnel may not disclose personally identifiable student information except as required by federal or state law. Surveys soliciting sexual information are prohibited for kindergarten through grade six. The bill references compliance with FERPA, PPRA, COPPA, and the Children's Internet Protection Act (as of specified dates). No funding or appropriation changes are included.
