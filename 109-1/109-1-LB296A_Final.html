Summary

LB296A affects the State Department of Education. It specifies an appropriation of $0 from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund for both FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27 to the State Department of Education (Program 161) to aid in carrying out Legislative Bill 296. The bill establishes limits on expenditures from the funds appropriated in this section for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems: not to exceed $160,197 for FY2025‑26 and $165,403 for FY2026‑27. No other agencies or budget changes are referenced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an appropriation clause directing funds from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund to the State Department of Education (Program 161) to support implementation of Legislative Bill 296 (109th Legislature, 2025). However, the appropriation amounts are $0 for both FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27. The provision caps total expenditures from the appropriated funds for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems at $160,197 for FY2025‑26 and $165,403 for FY2026‑27.
