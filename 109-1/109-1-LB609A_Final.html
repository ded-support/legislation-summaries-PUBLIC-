Summary

LB609A appropriates funds to the Department of Banking and Finance, Program 65, from the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund: $232,777 for FY2025-26 and $185,061 for FY2026-27 to aid implementation of LB609 (2025). It caps total expenditures from these appropriations for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems at $85,000 for FY2025-26 and $87,125 for FY2026-27.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Appropriates $232,777 (FY2025-26) and $185,061 (FY2026-27) from the Financial Institution Assessment Cash Fund to the Department of Banking and Finance, Program 65, to implement provisions of Legislative Bill 609 (109th Leg., 1st Sess., 2025).

- Caps payroll-related spending from these appropriated funds at $85,000 (FY2025-26) and $87,125 (FY2026-27) for permanent/temporary salaries and per diems.
