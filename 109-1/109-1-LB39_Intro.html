Summary

LB39 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. § 8-124.01 to require that, within 30 days of a vacancy on a bank’s board of directors, the bank notify the Department of Banking and Finance of the vacancy. After vacancies are filled (typically by appointment within 90 days), the bank must apply to the Department for approval of the appointed director under § 8-126. The bill repeals the original § 8-124.01. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Banking and Finance are included.
