Summary

LB39 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §8-124.01 to require a bank to notify the Department of Banking and Finance within 30 days of a vacancy on its board of directors. It retains procedures: vacancies to be filled within 90 days by appointment of remaining directors (appointees serve until the next directors' election; appointment optional if a minimum of five directors remain) and requires the bank to apply to the Department for approval of any appointed director under section 8-126. The bill repeals the original §8-124.01. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes for the Department of Banking and Finance are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires banks to notify the Department of Banking and Finance of any vacancy on their board of directors within 30 days of the vacancy. This is an added administrative reporting requirement; no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
