Summary

LB269 references the Department of Revenue in two places:

- Retailer notice of sales/use tax changes or terminations may be provided via the Department of Revenue website or other electronic means.

- The Tax Commissioner must adopt rules requiring the Department of Revenue to verify addresses in the state's address-based boundary database and to verify appropriate sales and use tax rates used on returns when online or remote transactions are included.

The bill contains no specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocation changes for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill adds requirements directing the Tax Commissioner to adopt rules obligating the Department of Revenue to verify (1) addresses in the state's address‑based boundary database and (2) that the correct sales and use tax rates are applied on returns when online or remote transactions are included. It also requires the state to maintain a separate zip‑code boundary database mapping each 5‑ and 9‑digit ZIP to the proper tax rates and jurisdictions (to be used only as allowed by the address‑database provision). For purposes of the Streamlined Sales and Use Tax Agreement, the ZIP database must apply the lowest combined tax rate where multiple rates exist. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
