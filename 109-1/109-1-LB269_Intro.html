Summary

LB269 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-27,143 to: allow notice to retailers to be provided via the Department of Revenue website or other electronic means; and require the Tax Commissioner to adopt rules obligating the Department of Revenue to verify addresses in the state's address-based boundary database and to verify appropriate sales and use tax rates reported on returns that include online or remote transactions. The bill contains no changes to Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a Tax Commissioner rulemaking requirement directing the Department of Revenue to verify addresses in the state's address‑based boundary database and to verify that appropriate sales and use tax rates are applied on returns when online or remote transactions are included. Establishes a separate state‑maintained zip‑code boundary database (5‑ and 9‑digit) mapping zip areas to correct tax rates/jurisdictions, limited to the uses in subsection (6). For the streamlined sales and use tax agreement, the zip‑code database must apply the lowest combined tax rate when multiple rates exist in a zip area. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
