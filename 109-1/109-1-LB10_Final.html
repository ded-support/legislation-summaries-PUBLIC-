Summary

LB10 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-7455 to require wholesale drug distributors (excluding original manufacturers) to verify all transactions on a drug pedigree before further distribution. It authorizes "the department" to receive prescription drugs and supplies under the Prescription Drug Donation Program Act and to dispense them through licensed personnel during, or in preparation for, a Governor-declared state of emergency, and to distribute such items to individuals impacted by the emergency. The original section is repealed and the bill takes effect immediately as an emergency measure. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for "the department" to receive prescription drugs and supplies under the Prescription Drug Donation Program Act and to (a) dispense them via licensed personnel during or in preparation for a Governor‑declared state of emergency and (b) receive and distribute them to individuals impacted by such a state of emergency. No agencies beyond "the department" and the Governor are named; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
