Summary

LB10 amends section 71-7455, R.R.S. Neb. It requires wholesale drug distributors (other than the original finished-form manufacturer) to verify all pedigree transactions before further distribution. The bill authorizes "the department" to receive prescription drugs and supplies directly under the Prescription Drug Donation Program Act and to dispense them via licensed personnel during, or while preparing for, a Governor-declared state of emergency. The department may also receive and distribute donated drugs and supplies to individuals impacted by such an emergency. The original section is repealed and the act takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for "the department" to receive prescription drugs and supplies under the Prescription Drug Donation Program Act and to (a) dispense them via licensed personnel during or while preparing for a Governor‑declared state of emergency and (b) receive and distribute them to any individual affected by such an emergency. No agencies beyond "the department" and reference to the Governor's office are named; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
