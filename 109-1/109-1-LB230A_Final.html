Summary

The bill appropriates funds from the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund to the Department of Revenue, Program 102, to implement Legislative Bill 230. It provides $251,010 for FY2025-26 and $121,600 for FY2026-27. Of those appropriated funds, total expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $88,600 for FY2025-26 and $91,400 for FY2026-27.
