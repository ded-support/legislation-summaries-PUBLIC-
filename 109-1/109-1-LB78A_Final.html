Summary

The bill appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program 514, to implement Legislative Bill 78. It directs (1) $830,000 from the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund for FY2025‑26 and (2) $1,135,000 from the same fund for FY2026‑27 to DHHS. Both appropriations are Cash Funds designated for state aid only. The bill prohibits using these appropriated funds for permanent or temporary employee salaries and per diems.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The amendment appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program 514, to implement Legislative Bill 78 (109th Leg., 1st Sess., 2025): $830,000 (FY2025‑26) and $1,135,000 (FY2026‑27), both from the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund. Each fiscal year’s amount is designated as Cash Funds for state aid only. The appropriation may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.
