Summary

This bill amends Neb. racetrack gaming law to (1) require each authorized gaming operator to obtain and pay for an annual independent CPA audit of its Nebraska operations, including internal control reports, and to submit the audit electronically to the State Auditor (Auditor of Public Accounts) and the State Racing and Gaming Commission within six months (unless extended); (2) direct the Auditor to review audits for calculation errors or deficiencies—particularly those affecting gross gaming revenue or tax—and to report findings to the Governor, Legislature, and the Commission, and to exercise prescribed examination powers over operator books, records, funds, and accounts; and (3) explicitly permit the Racing and Gaming Commission to use the Racing and Gaming Commission's Racetrack Gaming Fund for administration and for any audit, examination, or review authorized under the new audit section. Investment earnings of the fund are credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires each authorized gaming operator to obtain, at its own expense, an annual independent CPA audit (including internal control report) of all Nebraska financial transactions. The audit must be submitted electronically within six months after fiscal year-end to the Auditor of Public Accounts and the gaming commission (with possible written extension from the Auditor). The Auditor of Public Accounts must review the audit for calculation, accounting, or other deficiencies—particularly those affecting gross gaming revenue or the gaming tax—and report results electronically to the Governor, the Legislature, and the gaming commission. No new appropriations or government funding changes are specified; operators bear audit costs.
