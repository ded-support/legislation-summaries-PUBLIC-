Summary

LB280 amends Nebraska racetrack gaming law to expand regulatory oversight and clarify fund uses. Key agency actions:

- State Racing and Gaming Commission: retains staffing/authority; receives operators' audit reports; may use the Racing and Gaming Commission's Racetrack Gaming Fund for administration and for any audit, examination, or review authorized by the bill.

- Auditor of Public Accounts (APA): authorized to receive and review annual independent CPA audits (including internal control reports) filed by each authorized gaming operator (operator-paid) within six months of fiscal year end; to review for calculation errors or deficiencies affecting gross gaming revenue or gaming tax; to report findings to the Governor, Legislature, and the gaming commission; and to examine operators’ books, records, funds, and accounts as prescribed.

- State investment officer: investment earnings on the Racetrack Gaming Fund are credited to the General Fund beginning October 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual audit requirement for each authorized gaming operator in Nebraska: operators must, at their own expense, hire an independent certified public accountant licensed in Nebraska to audit annual financial statements and report on internal controls covering all Nebraska operations. Audit reports must be submitted electronically to the Auditor of Public Accounts and the gaming commission within six months after year‑end (unless the Auditor grants a written extension). The Auditor of Public Accounts will review reports for mathematical errors, improper accounting, or deficiencies—especially those affecting gross gaming revenue or the annual gaming tax—and will report the review results electronically to the Governor, the Legislature, and the gaming commission. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
