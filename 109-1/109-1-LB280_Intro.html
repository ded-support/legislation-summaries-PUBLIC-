Summary

LB280 amends Nebraska gaming statutes to (1) expand the duties and authorities of the State Racing and Gaming Commission to implement the new audit provisions in section 7; (2) require each authorized gaming operator to obtain, at its expense, an annual independent CPA audit (including internal control report) and submit it electronically to the Auditor of Public Accounts and the Racing and Gaming Commission within six months (extensions allowed by the Auditor); and (3) give the Auditor of Public Accounts authority to review those audit reports for errors or deficiencies, examine operators’ books, records, funds, and accounts as prescribed, and report findings electronically to the Governor, Legislature, and gaming commission. The Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund (license/application fees) is explicitly authorized to be used for administration and for any audit, examination, or review required or authorized under section 7. The fund’s investment remains with the state investment officer; investment earnings are credited to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual audit requirement for each authorized gaming operator: operators must pay for an independent certified public accountant (licensed in Nebraska) to audit annual financial statements and internal controls covering all Nebraska operations. Audit reports must be submitted electronically within six months after fiscal year‑end to the Auditor of Public Accounts (APA) and the gaming commission, unless the APA grants a written extension. The APA will review audits for mathematical errors, improper accounting, or deficiencies—particularly those affecting gross gaming revenue or gaming tax—and report results electronically to the Governor, the Legislature, and the gaming commission. No state appropriation changes; operators bear audit costs.
