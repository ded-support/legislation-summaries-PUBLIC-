Summary

LB280 amends Nebraska racetrack gaming law to (1) authorize the State Racing and Gaming Commission to administer and enforce a new annual audit requirement (section 7) and to use the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund to pay for any audit, examination, or review required or authorized under that section; and (2) require every authorized gaming operator to obtain, at its expense, an annual independent CPA audit (including internal control report) of Nebraska operations and submit it electronically to the Auditor of Public Accounts and the gaming commission within six months (subject to extension). The Auditor of Public Accounts must review audit reports for mathematical or accounting deficiencies—especially those affecting gross gaming revenue or gaming tax—and report results to the Governor, the Legislature, and the gaming commission, and may examine operators’ books, records, funds, and accounts as prescribed. Investment earnings on the fund are credited to the General Fund. No specific dollar appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require each authorized gaming operator to obtain, at its own expense, an annual independent CPA audit (including an internal control report) of all Nebraska financial transactions for the preceding fiscal year. The audit must be submitted electronically within six months after year-end to the Auditor of Public Accounts and the gaming commission (Auditor may grant a written extension). The Auditor of Public Accounts will review the audit for calculation errors, improper accounting, or other deficiencies—particularly those affecting gross gaming revenue or the annual gaming tax—and will report the review results electronically to the Governor, the Legislature, and the gaming commission. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
