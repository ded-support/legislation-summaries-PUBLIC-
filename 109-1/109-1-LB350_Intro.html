Summary

LB350 references the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and the Department of Correctional Services (DOCS) as follows:

- DHHS: Judges may commit defendants to DHHS for competency-restoration treatment (state hospitals, other state facilities, or contract providers). DHHS may file alternative treatment plans with the court. DHHS provides the emergency protective-custody certificate form. The bill requires DHHS to reimburse counties that continue to lodge defendants committed for competency restoration: counties are reimbursed for lodging, food, medical services, transportation, and related costs at $100 per day (per defendant) after the first 30 days, with annual CPI-based adjustments. The state pays maintenance and care costs during court-ordered treatment.

- DOCS: Emergency protective custody may place individuals in a Department of Correctional Services facility; persons with prior listed sex-offense convictions must be admitted to a jail or DOCS facility unless a medical/psychiatric emergency requires hospital care.
