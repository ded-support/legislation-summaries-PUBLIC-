Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Judges may commit defendants to DHHS for competency restoration, including state hospitals, other state-owned/-operated facilities, or contract providers under DHHS-proposed alternative treatment plans (court review within five days). DHHS must file such reports with the court. If a DHHS-committed defendant remains lodged in a county jail, DHHS shall reimburse the county for lodging (daily lodging, food, medical services, transportation, other necessary costs). Reimbursement is $100 per day per defendant for each day after the first 30 days, adjusted annually each July 1 by the CPI-U.

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): Emergency protective custody placements may include admission to a Department of Correctional Services facility. Persons with prior sex-offense convictions taken into emergency custody must be admitted to a jail or DCS facility unless a medical/psychiatric emergency requires a medical facility; transfer occurs once the emergency has passed.
