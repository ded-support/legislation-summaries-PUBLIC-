Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Courts may commit defendants to DHHS for competency restoration, including placement in state hospitals, other state-operated facilities, or contract providers under court‑approved alternative treatment plans. DHHS must reimburse counties when a DHHS‑committed defendant remains lodged in county jail: reimbursement covers daily lodging, food, medical services, transportation, and other necessary costs. The daily lodging rate is $100 per day after the first 30 days, adjusted annually each July 1 by the CPI‑U. DHHS also prescribes the written certificate form used by law enforcement for emergency protective custody.

- Department of Correctional Services: Emergency protective custody admissions may include Department of Correctional Services facilities; persons with certain prior sex‑offense convictions must be admitted to a jail or Department of Correctional Services facility unless a medical/psychiatric emergency exists.
