Summary

LB167 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-1566 to extend the termination date of the Nebraska Litter Reduction and Recycling Act from September 30, 2025, to September 30, 2030. It directs "the department" named in the statute to review and evaluate achievement of the act’s purposes and the need for continuation, with that review completed at least six months before the termination date. The bill repeals the original section and contains an emergency clause, making it effective on passage. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
