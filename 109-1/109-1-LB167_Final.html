Summary

LB167 amends section 81-1566 (Nebraska Litter Reduction and Recycling Act) to set the Act’s termination date at September 30, 2030. The bill requires "the department" (as referenced in the statute) to review and evaluate achievement of the Act’s purposes and the need for continuation at least six months prior to termination. The original section is repealed. An emergency clause makes the bill effective upon enactment. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or specified. The Legislature retains authority to extend the Act.
