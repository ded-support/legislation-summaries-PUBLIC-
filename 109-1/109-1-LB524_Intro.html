Summary

LB524 directs the State Treasurer to transfer $1,000,000,000 from the General Fund to the Education Future Fund in fiscal year 2023‑24 and $250,000,000 in fiscal year 2024‑25 on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services. These provisions tie the timing and amounts of those specified transfers to instructions from DAS's budget division.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text requires the department to automatically distribute retention grants to the employing school district, which must then pass the funds to eligible paraeducators. It establishes paraeducator eligibility and grant amounts: $1,000 for paraeducators contracted and averaging ≥28 hours/week in school years 2025‑26 or 2026‑27, with proportional (prorated) amounts for those averaging <28 hours. No specific appropriation, funding source, or budget allocation for the Department of Administrative Services is identified in the added language.
