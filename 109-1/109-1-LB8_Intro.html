Summary

The bill gives the Department of Revenue these explicit roles and authorities: (1) recapture authority for the residence-purchase tax credit—the Department may recapture the credit if the claimant sells/transfers the residence or stops using it as a primary residence within five years; (2) administration of the Sustainable Aviation Fuel Tax Credit—producers must register with the Department, provide prescribed third‑party certifications and any other information the Department requires, submit applications on Department forms, and the Department will consider applications in receipt order, approve qualifying applications, certify approved credit amounts to producers, and issue tax credit certifications to be attached to returns. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds multiple references to the "Sustainable Aviation Fuel Tax Credit Act," specifies that the credit becomes refundable in 2028, and requires the Department of Revenue to consider tax credit applications in the order received. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included in the added text.
