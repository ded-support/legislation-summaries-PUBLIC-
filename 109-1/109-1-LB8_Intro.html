Summary

LB8 assigns administrative responsibilities to the Department of Revenue regarding tax credits. Under the amended Sustainable Aviation Fuel Tax Credit Act, producers must register with the department, provide third‑party certification in forms/manners prescribed by the department, submit applications to the department (which will consider applications in receipt order), and, if qualified, the department will approve and certify the amount of credits to the producer. Producers must attach the tax credit certification received from the department to their tax returns. Separately, the bill makes the residence‑purchase credit subject to recapture by the Department of Revenue if the claimant disposes of or stops using the residence as a primary residence within five years. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Sustainable Aviation Fuel Tax Credit Act to the list of statutes the Department of Revenue administers, specifies the credit becomes refundable in 2028, and requires the department to process credit applications in the order received. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
