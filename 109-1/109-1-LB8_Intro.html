Summary

- Registration, certification, and administration: Producers of sustainable aviation fuel must register with the Department of Revenue and provide third‑party certification of compliance with applicable CORSIA or similar methodology requirements; the Department may require additional information.

- Application and approval: Producers apply on a Department‑prescribed form. The Department of Revenue will consider applications in receipt order, determine qualification, approve applications, and certify the amount of tax credits to the producer.

- Claiming credits: Producers must attach the tax credit certification issued by the Department of Revenue to their tax return to claim credits.

- Recapture authority: The Department of Revenue may recapture the residence purchase tax credit if the taxpayer disposes of or ceases primary use of the residence within five years.

- Fiscal note: The bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding authorizations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.
