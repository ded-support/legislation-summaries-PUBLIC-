Summary

LB355 assigns several responsibilities to the Department of Economic Development (DED): allocate state financial assistance to certified development districts (50% equally, 20% by population per the latest federal decennial census—DED may determine unincorporated county population if census data are unavailable—and 30% by proportional share of local governments) and verify local dues, capping assistance accordingly. Fees equal to 0.5% of ImagiNE Nebraska incentives are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund, which DED will administer. DED must annually update workforce-housing construction cost limits using the Producer Price Index published by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics. DED will administer job-training grants, audit recipients, and require annual reporting on trainee outcomes and matching contributions. The bill also amends the Nebraska Innovation Hub Act to define “Department” and “Director” as DED and its director; operative date July 1, 2025, with an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary: The added language lets the Department of Economic Development use American Community Survey (5‑Year) estimates when the latest federal decennial census data aren’t available and explicitly authorizes the Department to determine the applicable figures. There are no substantive changes affecting the Department of Labor in this insertion, and no new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
