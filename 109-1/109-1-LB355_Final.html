Summary

The bill defines "Department" and "Director" in the Nebraska Innovation Hub Act as the Department of Economic Development (DED) and its director. It expands DED duties: distributing state financial assistance to certified development districts using a 50% equal-share / 20% population / 30% local-government-share formula and determining county unincorporated population when federal decennial census data are unavailable. DED will administer the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund, funded by a 0.5% administrative fee on incentives (fees remitted to the State Treasurer and invested by the state investment officer). DED also oversees job training grants, requiring business applications, audits, and annual trainee/outcome reporting. The federal Bureau of Labor Statistics Producer Price Index is specified as the basis for annual updates to workforce housing cost caps used in DED rulemaking.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text replaces the prior fallback language about population with a directive to use the American Community Survey (ACS) 5‑Year Estimates. Effect on agencies:

- Department of Economic Development: removes/limits its role in “determining” population when decennial census data is unavailable; population counts will instead rely on ACS 5‑Year Estimates.

- Department of Labor: no substantive changes or responsibilities added.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
