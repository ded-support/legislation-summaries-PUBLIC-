Summary

LB355 clarifies and expands duties of the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED must distribute state financial assistance to certified development districts using a 50/20/30 allocation formula (50% split equally; 20% by population proportion; 30% by share of local governments), may determine unincorporated county populations when census data are unavailable, and must verify local dues so state aid does not exceed local contributions. DED will administer the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund—financed by a one‑half percent fee on awarded incentives remitted to the State Treasurer—to support the ImagiNE Nebraska Act. DED also will receive and audit job‑training grant applications and require annual reporting by recipients. The bill designates “Department” and “Director” in the Nebraska Innovation Hub Act as DED and its director, who will designate iHubs. DED must update workforce‑housing construction cost limits annually using the Producer Price Index from the U.S. Department of Labor (BLS).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "American Community Survey 5‑Year Estimate" as the specified fallback population data source when the latest federal decennial census is not available and directs the Department of Economic Development to determine population using that ACS 5‑year estimate. No provisions affect or assign duties to the Department of Labor. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
