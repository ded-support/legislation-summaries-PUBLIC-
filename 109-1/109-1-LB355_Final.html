Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Economic Development (DED) responsibility for: distributing state aid to certified development districts (50% equally, 20% prorated by population using the latest federal decennial census or DED determination if unavailable, and 30% by proportional local-government share), verifying local matching dollars, and administering distributions. Under ImagiNE Nebraska, a 0.5% administrative fee on incentives is remitted to the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund, which DED administers and invests to fund program administration. Businesses seeking job-training grants must submit plans to DED; grant recipients are subject to DED audit and annual reporting. DED will update workforce-housing construction cost limits annually using the Producer Price Index from the U.S. Department of Labor (BLS). The Nebraska Innovation Hub Act is amended to define “Department” and “Director” as DED and its Director. No Department of Labor program changes or agency-level appropriations/budget reallocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language lets the Department of Economic Development use American Community Survey (ACS) 5‑Year Estimates to determine population when the latest federal decennial census data are not available. No provisions affect the Department of Labor. There are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
