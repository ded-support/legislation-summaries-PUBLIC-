Summary

References to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) in LB143:

- DHHS-prescribed rules and regulations govern state payment (not in advance) of education and required transportation costs to receiving school districts or approved institutions for students who are wards placed outside their resident district.

- A student who is a ward and resides in a foster family home licensed or approved by DHHS is deemed a resident of the district where they lived when they became a foster child (unless otherwise determined).

- Residential settings operated by providers certified or licensed by DHHS (or enrolled in Medicaid Title XIX/XXI) do not change a non-ward student’s resident district and trigger the contracting/payment provisions for educational services.

No specific appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included in the bill.
