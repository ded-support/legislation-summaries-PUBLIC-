Summary

LB469 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §76-1401 (Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act) to impose a duty on the State Court Administrator to develop a form that must be included in eviction notices. The bill directs the State Court Administrator (judicial branch administrative office) to prepare that form. The act repeals the original section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
