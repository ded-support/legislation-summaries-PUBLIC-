Summary

LB703 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81‑2604 governing the Commission on African American Affairs. It adds a new duty requiring the commission to develop an exchange program with the Republic of Ghana and states the Legislature’s intent to appropriate $1,000,000 annually for that program beginning in FY2026‑27. The commission’s existing roles remain—coordinating programs and working with other state and federal government agencies, elected officials, and the Governor’s office. The bill repeals the original §81‑2604.
