Summary

LB678 directs General Fund appropriations to the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges (Program 48): unspecified sums ($XXX) for FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27. Funds are earmarked for salary increases required by negotiated agreements with the Nebraska Association of Public Employees, the State College Education Association, and the Nebraska State College System Professional Association; to cover minimum wage increases required by the Wage and Hour Act; and to cover increased health insurance premiums. The bill includes an emergency clause making it effective on enactment.
