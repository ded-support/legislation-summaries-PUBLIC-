Summary

LB180 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §73-813 to add explicit procurement exemptions. Department of Insurance contracts for financial or actuarial examinations; rehabilitation, conservation, reorganization, or liquidation of licensees; and professional services related to residual pools or excess funds under the agency's control are excluded from the procurement requirements of sections 73-807, 73-815, 73-816, and 73-817. The bill likewise excludes Department of Transportation contracts for all road and bridge projects from those statutes. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
