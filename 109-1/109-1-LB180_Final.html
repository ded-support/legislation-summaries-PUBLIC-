Summary

LB180 amends section 73-813 to add explicit procurement exceptions. It exempts Department of Insurance contracts for financial or actuarial examinations; for rehabilitation, conservation, reorganization, or liquidation of licensees; and for professional services related to residual pools or excess funds under the agency's control from the requirements of sections 73-807, 73-815, 73-816, and 73-817. It also exempts Department of Transportation contracts for all road and bridge projects from those same procurement provisions. The bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
