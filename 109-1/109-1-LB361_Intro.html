Summary

LB361 amends the Nebraska Fair Employment Practice Act to (1) prohibit covered employers, including state and other governmental agencies, from discriminating or retaliating against individuals for reporting work‑related injuries, filing claims, or seeking remedies under the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Act, and declares the Act’s remedies supplemental to workers’ compensation; (2) establish a right to jury trial in civil actions under the Act, explicitly applying this right to suits against state and governmental agencies; and (3) clarify that state and governmental agencies may be sued under the Act in the same manner as other employers. Original sections 48‑1125 and 48‑1126 are repealed. No funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that it is unlawful for a covered entity to discriminate or retaliate against someone for reporting a work‑related injury, filing a claim, or seeking remedies under the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Act. Clarifies that rights and remedies under the Nebraska Fair Employment Practice Act are supplemental to those under the Workers’ Compensation Act. Establishes a right to a jury trial in civil actions under the Fair Employment Practice Act, expressly including cases brought against state and governmental agencies. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
