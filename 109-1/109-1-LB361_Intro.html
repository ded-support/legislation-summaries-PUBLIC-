Summary

LB361 amends the Nebraska Fair Employment Practice Act to (1) prohibit covered employers from discriminating or retaliating against individuals who report work‑related injuries, bring claims, or seek remedies under the Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Act, and to state that the Act’s remedies are supplemental to workers’ compensation; and (2) establish a right to jury trial in civil actions under the Act, explicitly including cases against state and governmental agencies. Section 48-1126 is revised to confirm state and government agencies may be sued under the Act like other employers. Sections 48-1125 and 48-1126 are amended and the originals repealed. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that it is unlawful for a "covered entity" to discriminate or retaliate against someone for reporting a work‑related injury, filing a claim, or seeking remedies under the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act. States that rights and remedies under the Nebraska Fair Employment Practice Act are supplemental to those under the Workers' Compensation Act. Establishes a right to a jury trial in any civil action under the Fair Employment Practice Act, explicitly including suits against state and governmental agencies. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included. The language is incorporated into sections 1–3 of the act.
