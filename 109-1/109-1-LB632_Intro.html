Summary

LB632 requires health care facilities that perform elective abortions to provide individual disposition of aborted fetal remains by cremation or interment (burial), and makes it unlawful to dispose of such remains in any other manner. If cremation or burial is not possible, disposition shall follow directions from the Nebraska Board of Health. The bill also specifies that facilities are not required to notify the woman undergoing the abortion of the method of disposition. No funding appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text directs that the Legislature’s purpose is to protect public health and respect the dignity of unborn children by requiring health care facilities that perform elective abortions to ensure dignified, safe disposition of fetal remains. It defines “remains of an aborted unborn child,” makes unlawful any disposal method not authorized by the section, and requires individual disposition by cremation or burial—or, if those are not possible, as directed by the Board of Health. No notice to the patient is required. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
