Summary

LB115 amends section 77-3105 to require the volunteer department’s certification administrator to file a certified list of qualifying volunteer emergency responders with the Department of Revenue by February 15 each year (with a copy to the local governing body). The bill establishes a refundable Nebraska income tax credit of $1,000 per listed volunteer for any taxable year in which the volunteer appears on that list; volunteers claim the credit by submitting the certification with their state income tax return. No explicit appropriations, funding transfers, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included in the bill.
