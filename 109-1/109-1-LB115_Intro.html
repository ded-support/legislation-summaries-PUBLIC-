Summary

LB115 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-3105 to require the volunteer department’s certification administrator to file a certified list of qualifying volunteer members with the Department of Revenue no later than February 15 each year (a copy must also be provided to the local governing body). Members on that list are entitled to a refundable income tax credit of $1,000 for each taxable year they are listed; the credit is claimed by attaching the certification to the state income tax return. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.
