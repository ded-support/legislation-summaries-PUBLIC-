Summary

LB608A appropriates General Fund dollars to the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education (CCPE), Program 692, to implement Legislative Bill 608. It provides $781,647 GF for FY2025‑26 and $1,351,495 GF for FY2026‑27, with both fiscal-year amounts designated solely for state aid. The appropriation explicitly prohibits using these funds for permanent or temporary salaries and per diems for state employees.
