Summary

LB294 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-3720 governing the County Visitors Improvement Fund. The fund remains administered by the county governing body with advice from the county visitors committee (per §81-3717). Grants may be awarded to public or nonprofit-owned visitor attractions for facility expansion, exhibits, construction, planning, or development. Annual grant payments are capped at the greater of proceeds from a county 1% sales tax dedicated to the Fund or $5,000 and may be made for terms up to 20 years; recipients may pledge grant payments to secure bonds, which are payable solely from those grant funds and are not county obligations. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause.
