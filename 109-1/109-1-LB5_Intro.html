Summary

LB5 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-470 to broaden legal immunity for use, prescribing, dispensing, and administration of opioid antagonists (e.g., naloxone) and defines covered actors and entities. It protects: health professionals licensed under the Uniform Credentialing Act; family/friends and school personnel who obtain/administer an opioid antagonist; emergency responders (licensed under the Emergency Medical Services Practice Act or via the EMS Interstate Compact) using supplies from their EMS organization; and peace officers or law enforcement employees using agency-provided supplies. Protected persons are immune from administrative action, criminal prosecution, and (with exceptions for willful, wanton, or gross negligence) personal civil liability. The bill defines law enforcement agencies (police departments, town marshals, sheriffs, Nebraska State Patrol) and law enforcement employees, and clarifies that organizational liability is not affected. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included. The prior section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "opioid antagonist" to mean naloxone hydrochloride or any opioid antagonist approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration for emergency reversal of known or suspected opioid overdose. No government funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
