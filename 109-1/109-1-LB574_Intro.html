Summary

LB574 establishes procedural and workplace protections for certified firefighters and emergency personnel. It defines “complaint,” “formal investigation,” and “interview,” requires investigations be completed in a reasonable timeframe, and mandates a written summary of complaints before interviews. Interviews must be audio recorded; firefighters may have legal or union representation; written investigation outcomes must be provided; complaint materials remain confidential unless disclosure is legally required or consented to; polygraph exams are prohibited; and firefighters may seek damages for false complaints and pursue grievances. The bill also affirms rights to safe working conditions, training, equipment, mental-health services, confidentiality of personal information, off-duty political activity, and access to personnel and training records. No state agencies, departments, or budget appropriations are specified.

Adds procedural and employment protections for firefighters (defined to include certified firefighters, emergency medical technicians, and paramedics). Key provisions require timely formal investigations, written complaint summaries before interviews, audio-recorded interviews, the right to legal or union representation, confidentiality of complaints/statements/recordings (unless law or consent requires disclosure), notice of investigation outcomes, ability to seek damages for false complaints, access to grievance processes, prohibition on compelled polygraphs, workplace safety, training/equipment access, mental‑health services, personnel/training record access, and off‑duty political activity.
