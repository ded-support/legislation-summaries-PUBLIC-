Summary

LB574 establishes procedural rights and protections for certified firefighters and emergency personnel (including EMTs and paramedics) during formal investigations ordered by a commanding officer. Key provisions require written complaint summaries before interviews, audio-recording of interviews, confidentiality of complaint materials (unless disclosure is legally required or consented), notice of investigation outcomes, access to personnel and training records, representation by legal or union representatives, prohibition of compulsory polygraph exams, ability to seek damages for false complaints, grievance rights, access to mental health services, and employer obligations for safe working conditions, training, equipment, and support. No state agencies or departments are identified, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations.
