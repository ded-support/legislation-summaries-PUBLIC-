Summary

LB574 applies to certified firefighters and emergency personnel (including EMTs and paramedics). It requires that formal investigations ordered by a commanding officer be completed in a reasonable time; provide a written complaint summary and written notice of the outcome; allow legal or union representation; record interviews by audio; preserve confidentiality of complaints, statements, recordings, and personal information absent legal requirement or written consent; prohibit compelled polygraph exams; and permit damages for false complaints and challenge of discipline through grievance or legal channels. The bill also guarantees safe working conditions, access to training, equipment, mental‑health services, off‑duty political activity, and access to personnel/training records. No state agencies or budget appropriations are named or modified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds comprehensive firefighter protections and procedural requirements for agencies that investigate firefighters: definitions (complaint, firefighter, formal investigation, interview); mandates that formal investigations be timely; written complaint summaries provided before interviews; interviewees may have legal or union representation; interviews must be audio‑recorded; investigators must send written outcomes; complaints, statements, and recordings remain confidential absent law or consent; firefighters can seek damages (including attorney fees) for false complaints; may use grievance/union/legal channels; and cannot be compelled to take polygraphs. Also guarantees safe working conditions, access to training, equipment, support, and mental‑health services; confidentiality of personal information; off‑duty political activity allowed; and access to personnel and training records.
