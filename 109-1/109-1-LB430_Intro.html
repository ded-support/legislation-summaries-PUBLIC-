Summary

LB430 amends student-discipline statutes (sections 79‑265, 79‑265.01, 79‑268). It modifies short‑term suspension procedures: principals must, within 24 hours (or up to 72 hours if reasonably necessary), send written notice to student and parent/guardian detailing the conduct, reasons for suspension, prior school interventions, resources available, and an actionable plan to keep the student in school; principals must document reasonable efforts to confer with parents/guardians before or at return. It adds an exception allowing suspension of pre‑K through grade‑2 students for violent behavior capable of causing physical harm (in addition to bringing a deadly weapon). Long‑term suspension/expulsion notices must likewise include resources and an actionable behavior plan and confirm rights to a hearing and to complete missed schoolwork. No agencies outside school districts, principals, or superintendents are created or funded, and no appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require schools to document (before suspension) actions taken to try to discontinue or alleviate a student’s behavior, list resources the school can provide or recommend to assist the student, and set out an actionable plan to manage the behavior going forward with the goal of keeping the student in school. The additions also clarify misconduct definitions (including bringing prohibited items and violent behavior causing physical harm). No specific government agencies or departments are referenced, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
