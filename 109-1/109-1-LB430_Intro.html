Summary

Bill LB430 amends sections 79-265, 79-265.01, and 79-268 of Nebraska law governing school discipline. Key agency/department roles: school districts must develop elementary disciplinary policies; school principals and district superintendents carry notification, investigation, suspension, and hearing duties; the Student Discipline Act procedures apply.

Notable changes:

- Short-term suspension notice (principal) must be sent within 24–72 hours and include conduct, reasons, prior school interventions, available resources, and an actionable plan to keep the student in school; principals must document efforts to confer with parents.

- Pre-K–2 suspension prohibition gains exceptions for bringing a deadly weapon onto school property/vehicles/activities and for violent behavior causing potential physical harm.

- Long-term suspension/expulsion procedures require decision and superintendent filing within two school days, certified-mail notice with resources, actionable remediation plan, hearing rights, ability to complete missed work, records/witness disclosure, and a hearing-request form. Principals may suspend pending hearings to prevent interference or injury.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The prior versions of the amended sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require schools to document: actions taken to discontinue or alleviate a student's behavior before suspension; resources the school can provide or recommend; and an actionable plan for handling the behavior going forward focused on keeping the student in school. The additions also clarify that conduct including bringing items onto school property and violent behavior causing physical harm to students or staff are covered. No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
