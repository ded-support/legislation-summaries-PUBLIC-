Summary

LB430 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 79-265, 79-265.01, and 79-268, reallocating procedural duties among school officials. Key changes:

- School districts/elementary schools: must adopt policies providing in‑school disciplinary alternatives for pre-K–2; an exception now permits suspension of pre-K–2 students who bring a deadly weapon (per §28-109) or engage in violent behavior capable of causing physical harm.

- Principals: must investigate before short‑term suspension; within 24 hours (plus up to 48 additional hours) send parents a written statement detailing conduct, prior school interventions, available resources, and an actionable plan to keep the student in school; make reasonable effort to meet parents when student returns.

- Superintendents/principals: long‑term discipline recommendations must be filed within two school days; notices to families (by registered/certified mail) must include evidence summaries, resources, hearing and appeal procedures, and a hearing‑request form. Mandatory reassignment is subject to these procedures.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for schools to document: actions taken to discontinue or alleviate a student's behavior before suspension; resources the school can provide or recommend to assist the student; and a forward-looking, actionable plan focused on maximizing strategies to keep the student in school. Also clarifies that engaging in violent behavior capable of causing physical harm to another student or school employee is addressed. No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
