Summary

LB21 adopts the Uniform Unlawful Restrictions in Land Records Act, authorizing property owners to record an amendment removing an "unlawful restriction" from deeds or other title documents. The amendment must identify the owner, affected real property, and the original document, include a conspicuous statutory statement, be executed and acknowledged for recordation, and be recorded in the land records of each county where the original document is recorded (i.e., county land records/recorders' offices). The bill preserves the validity of restrictions that are not unlawful and clarifies such amendments (or later conveyances) do not extend a restriction's duration. No state agencies are tasked with oversight in the excerpt, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural requirements for owner-filed amendments under the Uniform Unlawful Restrictions in Land Records Act: the amendment must identify the owner, affected property, and original document; include a specified conspicuous statement; be executed/acknowledged and recorded in each county where the original document is recorded; and not affect enforceability of restrictions that are lawful or revive restrictions that would otherwise expire. Provides a model form with fields for owner name, property address/legal description, and the document being amended. No government agencies or departments are mentioned and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
