Summary

LB21 adopts the Uniform Unlawful Restrictions in Land Records Act, providing a procedure for owners to amend recorded instruments to remove unlawful restrictions. Amendments must identify the owner, affected real property, and the original document; include a conspicuous statutory statement; be executed and acknowledged as required for recordation; and be recorded in the land records of each county where the original document appears (i.e., county recorders/registries). The bill preserves the validity of restrictions that are not unlawful and states that the amendment or a later conveyance does not republish a restriction that would otherwise expire. No state agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for an owner to record an amendment under the Uniform Unlawful Restrictions in Land Records Act: the amendment must identify the owner, affected real property, and the original document; include a prescribed conspicuous statement removing an “unlawful restriction”; be executed and acknowledged as required for recordation; and be recorded in the land records of every county where the original document is recorded. It clarifies the amendment does not affect restrictions that are lawful or constitute a republication that would extend an otherwise expiring restriction. A sample form (owner name, property address/legal description, and the amended document) is provided. No state agencies beyond county land-records/registrar-of-deeds offices are specified, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
