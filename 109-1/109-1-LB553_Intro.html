Summary

LB553 amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act to add accredited dietitian nutritionist programs and licensed dietitian nutritionists as eligible for student loans and loan repayment. The bill assigns administration to the department (which executes agreements, makes direct loan‑repayment payments, and enforces defaults—requiring 150% repayment plus 8% simple interest) and requires the commission to include dietitian nutritionist practice when designating health profession shortage areas. Funding limits: student loans up to $30,000/year (program caps unchanged); resident incentives $40,000/year (max $120,000); loan repayments $30,000/year (physicians, psychiatrists, dentists, psychologists; max $90,000) and $15,000/year (physician assistants, nurse practitioners, pharmacists, physical and occupational therapists, dietitian nutritionists, mental health practitioners; max $45,000). Agreements entered by 12/31/2024 must use ARPA federal funds first (no local match required) and those federal funds must be used by 12/31/2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds "dietitian nutritionist" throughout program and eligibility language for Nebraska programs, explicitly recognizing a "dietitian nutritionist licensed under section 38-1813" as an eligible category. The changes insert dietitian nutritionist terminology into lists of program types and participant/qualifier criteria. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
