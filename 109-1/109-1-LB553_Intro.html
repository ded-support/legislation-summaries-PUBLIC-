Summary

LB553 amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act to add dietitian nutritionist education programs and licensed dietitian nutritionists to eligible student-loan and loan-repayment categories. The bill directs the commission to include dietitian nutritionist practice when designating health profession shortage areas. It retains loan and repayment caps (student loans up to $30,000/year; residency incentives $40,000/year; loan repayments $30,000/year for physicians/psychiatrists/dentists/psychologists and $15,000/year for physician assistants, nurse practitioners, pharmacists, PTs, OTs, dietitian nutritionists, and mental health practitioners) and preserves full-debt forgiveness provisions for certain mental-health practitioners meeting five-year service conditions. Loan-repayment agreements remain between the department and local entities, require equal state/local funding (payments made directly to recipients), include default repayment (150% plus 8% simple interest), and retain ARPA-related language prioritizing federal funds and waiver of local match for ARPA-funded agreements.

Adds a "dietitian nutritionist program" to the list of Nebraska programs and enumerates eligible provider types, explicitly including "a dietitian nutritionist licensed under section 38‑1813" (Nebraska statute).
