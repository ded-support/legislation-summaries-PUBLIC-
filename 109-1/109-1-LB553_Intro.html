Summary

LB553 amends the Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act to add dietitian nutritionist programs and licensed dietitian nutritionists to student loan and loan-repayment eligibility. It directs the commission to include dietitian nutritionist practice when designating health profession shortage areas. Financial terms: student loans remain capped at $30,000 per recipient per academic year (program caps retained); medical resident incentives up to $40,000 per residency year (max $120,000); loan-repayment limits set at $30,000/year (up to $90,000) for physicians/psychiatrists/dentists/psychologists and $15,000/year (up to $45,000) for physician assistants, nurse practitioners, pharmacists, physical/occupational therapists, dietitian nutritionists, and mental health practitioners. The department will make payments; state and local entities provide equal funding for repayments. Default repayment is 150% plus 8% simple interest. ARPA federal funds must be used first for certain agreements and need not require equal local funding. Original sections are repealed.
