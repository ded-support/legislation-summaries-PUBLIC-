Summary

LB553 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§71-5662, 71-5663, 71-5665, and 71-5668 to add dietitian nutritionists to eligibility for Rural Health Systems and Professional Incentive Act student loans and loan-repayment programs. The bill directs the (unnamed) commission to include dietitian nutritionist practice when designating health profession shortage areas. The Department (responsible for program administration) will execute exempt agreements with recipients and local entities and make loan-repayment payments directly. State and local entities provide equal funding for repayments, subject to caps: $30,000/year (physicians, psychiatrists, dentists, psychologists) and $15,000/year (physician assistants, nurse practitioners, pharmacists, physical and occupational therapists, dietitian nutritionists, and mental health practitioners). Student-loan limits remain (e.g., $30,000/academic year; certain program total caps). ARPA federal funds continue to be prioritized for specified agreements and may be used without local matching for the covered period.
