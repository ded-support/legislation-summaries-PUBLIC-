Summary

LB657 amends the Nebraska Criminal Code (section 28-101) to create the offense of "assault on a sports official." It defines "athletic contest" and "sports official" (including those duly registered by local, state, regional, or national organizations that provide training). The offense is committed when a person intentionally or knowingly causes bodily injury to a sports official immediately before, during, or after an athletic contest, and is classified as a Class IV felony. Referred to the Judiciary Committee. No agencies, departments, or budget appropriations are named or altered, and the bill contains no funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "athletic contest" and "sports official" (including those registered with local, state, regional, or national organizations that provide education/training). Establishes the offense of assault on a sports official when a person intentionally or knowingly causes bodily injury to such an official immediately before, during, or after an athletic contest, with knowledge (or reasonable awareness) that the victim is a sports official. Classifies the offense as a Class IV felony. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
