Summary

LB657 amends section 28-101 of the Nebraska Criminal Code to add a new offense, "assault on a sports official." It defines "athletic contest" and "sports official" (including referees, umpires, etc., who are registered with or members of local, state, regional, or national organizations that provide education/training to officials). The offense occurs when a person intentionally or knowingly causes bodily injury to a sports official immediately before, during, or immediately after an athletic contest, and the offender knows or reasonably should know the victim is a sports official. Violation is a Class IV felony. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
