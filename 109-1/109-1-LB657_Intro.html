Summary

LB657 amends the Nebraska Criminal Code (section 28-101) to create the new offense of "assault on a sports official." The bill defines "athletic contest" and "sports official" (including officials registered with local, state, regional, or national organizations that provide training), and makes it a Class IV felony to intentionally or knowingly cause bodily injury to a sports official immediately before, during, or after an athletic contest when the offender knows or should know the victim is an official. No state agencies or departments are newly created or specified, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new offense creating "assault on a sports official." Key points: defines "athletic contest" to include interscholastic, intercollegiate, amateur or professional contests; defines "sports official" as referees/umpires/linesmen (or similar) who are duly registered by or members of local, state, regional, or national organizations that provide education/training to officials. Criminalizes intentionally or knowingly causing bodily injury to a sports official immediately before, during, or immediately after an athletic contest where the offender knows or should know the victim is an official. Classifies the offense as a Class IV felony. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes.
