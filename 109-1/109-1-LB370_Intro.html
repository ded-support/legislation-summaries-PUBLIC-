Summary

LB370 amends the Nebraska Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. Key government‑related points: it references postsecondary institutions (per §85‑2403), the Nebraska Uniform Athlete Agents Act (athlete‑agent registration), the Securities Act of Nebraska (financial advisor registration), and attorneys admitted by order of the Nebraska Supreme Court as permitted professional representatives. The bill bars state and local governmental entities from investigating, penalizing, restricting, or requiring reporting about activities protected by the Act. Agency agreements are generally nonpublic records. Public postsecondary institutions can be sued under the Act only to the extent allowed by the State Tort Claims Act, State Contract Claims Act, or State Miscellaneous Claims Act, and actions must be brought within one year. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and substantive rules under the Nebraska Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. Key additions:

- Defines collaborators and “third party” paying for a student‑athlete’s NIL or athletic reputation.

- Prohibits adverse government action (complaints, investigations, penalties, reporting requirements or restrictions) against student‑athletes, agents, institutions, supporters, NIL collectives, or third parties for activities protected by the Act.

- Requires written contracts when anticipated annual NIL payments to a student‑athlete are at least $5,000, specifying payer contact, total compensation, payment schedule, athlete obligations, contract term, modification/termination options, and withholding conditions.

- Treats agency agreements as nonpublic records (except as otherwise provided) and sets agent eligibility, disclosure, attestation, notification, and conduct rules; voids nonconforming or upfront‑payment agreements.

No state agencies or departments are newly created or named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
