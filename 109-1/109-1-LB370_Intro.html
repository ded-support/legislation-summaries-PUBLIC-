Summary

LB370 (Introduced to the Business and Labor Committee) amends Nebraska’s Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. Relevant government references: it cites the Nebraska Supreme Court for attorney admission (professional representation may include attorneys admitted by order of the Court). It clarifies that civil actions against public postsecondary institutions are subject only to remedies permitted under the State Tort Claims Act, State Contract Claims Act, or State Miscellaneous Claims Act and imposes a one‑year statute of limitations for such suits. The bill contains no changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary: The additions create detailed definitions and contract requirements for name‑image‑likeness (NIL) deals and athlete‑agent agreements, require agent disclosures/attestations of compliance with state licensing rules, and bar certain adverse government actions (e.g., investigations, penalties, reporting requirements) against parties engaging in activities protected by the Nebraska Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. Government entities are not specifically named; the text references state and federal courts and makes agency agreements non‑public records except as provided by section 48‑3604.
