Summary

LB370 amends the Nebraska Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. It references postsecondary institutions (per §85‑2403), the Nebraska Uniform Athlete Agents Act (certificate/registration), the Securities Act of Nebraska (financial advisors), and attorney admission by the Nebraska Supreme Court for professional representation. The bill requires written contracts for anticipated NIL payments of $5,000+ and imposes agent qualification, disclosure, and conduct rules (including criminal-history and anti‑pay provisions). Agency agreements are generally nonpublic. It expands parties able to sue for violations and limits suits against public postsecondary institutions to the extent permitted by the State Tort Claims Act, State Contract Claims Act, and State Miscellaneous Claims Act, with a one‑year limitations period. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The additions (underlined) do not create or name any new government agencies or departments and make no changes to funding or appropriations. Key agency-related points:

- Agency agreements are declared not to be public records except as provided in section 48‑3604.

- Governmental action is restricted: the bill bars accepting complaints, opening investigations, imposing penalties, restricting activity, or requiring reporting against student‑athletes, athlete agents, postsecondary institutions, athletic program supporters, NIL collectives, or third parties for activities protected by the Nebraska Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act.

- Other provisions define parties/third parties, require written contracts for anticipated payments ≥ $5,000/year, set agent eligibility and disclosure requirements, require prompt notice if an agent loses eligibility, and void noncompliant agreements.
