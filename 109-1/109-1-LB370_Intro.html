Summary

LB370 amends the Nebraska Student-Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. Relevant government entities: postsecondary institutions (as defined in §85-2403) and public postsecondary institutions are specifically addressed — the bill requires written contracts for payments ≥ $5,000 involving a name/image/likeness collective, postsecondary institution, or third party, and provides that public postsecondary institutions may be sued under this Act only to the extent allowed by the State Tort Claims Act, State Contract Claims Act, or State Miscellaneous Claims Act, with a one-year limitations period. The Supreme Court is referenced for attorney admission as qualified professional representation. Agency agreements are generally not public records. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions (Parties; Third party) and new compliance rules under the Nebraska Student‑Athlete Name, Image, or Likeness Rights Act. Mentions state or federal civil courts (as grounds for disqualifying an athlete agent if charges/convictions exist) and references "section 48‑3604" and "this state" regarding agency‑agreement public‑record status and agent licensure. Requires written contracts when anticipated annual NIL payments to a student‑athlete meet or exceed $5,000, specifying payer identity, total compensation, payment timing, athlete obligations, term dates, revision/termination options, and nonpayment terms. Prohibits certain payments by agents (e.g., loans to induce representation) and voids contracts offering upfront benefits for future NIL rights. No changes to government funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
