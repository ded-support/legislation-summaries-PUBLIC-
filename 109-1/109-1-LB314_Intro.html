Summary

LB314 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act. It clarifies the role and voting rules of the administering board (a majority of all members required for action; one member must be the Governor) and requires the board to consider an applicant’s fiscal/economic capacity. Approvals tied to voter-approved general obligation bonds or to issuance of a building permit become permanent only after those conditions; otherwise temporary approvals are void.

For privately owned sports complexes, state assistance may be used to: repay bonds for public infrastructure related to the complex; lease portions to a political subdivision at fair-market rent (≤20 years); promote publicly accessible sporting events; or repay nonprofit coapplicant debt subject to voter approval. Funding limits: ≤10 years for arts/cultural or sporting event promotion, ≤5 years for complexes in second-class cities/villages. For large public stadiums approved on/after July 19, 2024, up to 100% of final project cost may be funded by state assistance (per §13-3108).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "lease" as a contractual lease between the coapplicants identified in §13-3102(1)(b) for use of an eligible sports arena at fair‑market rent for up to 20 years. Establishes that an approval tied to the §13-3103(1)(f) requirement is a temporary approval: it converts to a permanent approval if a building permit for the eligible sports arena is issued within 24 months, and it becomes void if no permit is issued within 24 months. The text refers generally to a "board" but does not name any specific agency or department, and contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
