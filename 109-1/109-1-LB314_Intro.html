Summary

LB314 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act (amending §13-3103 and §13-3106). It defines "lease" for coapplicants and clarifies allowable uses of state assistance for privately owned sports complexes: repayment of bonds borrowed for related public infrastructure, leasing to political subdivisions (coapplicants) at fair‑market rent for up to 20 years, promotion of public sporting events, and repayment of nonprofit corporation debt subject to voter approval. It caps state-funded promotion at 10 years (arts/cultural and sporting events) and 5 years for complexes in second‑class cities or villages. For large public stadiums approved on/after July 19, 2024, state assistance may fund up to 100% of final project costs. §13-3106 also clarifies temporary board approvals (tied to voter approval or issuance of a building permit within 24 months) and board quorum/voting requirements (majority, including the Governor).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "lease" as any contractual lease between the coapplicants identified in §13-3102(1)(b) for use of an eligible sports arena at fair‑market rent for up to 20 years. Establishes that an approval given solely to satisfy the §13-3103(1)(f) requirement is a temporary approval: it becomes permanent if a building permit for the eligible sports arena is issued within 24 months of that temporary approval; if not, the temporary approval expires.
