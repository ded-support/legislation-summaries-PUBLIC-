Summary

LB314 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act. It narrows eligible uses of state assistance for privately owned sports complexes to: repayment for public infrastructure costs; leasing portions to a political subdivision (defined as a contractual lease between coapplicants at fair-market rent for up to 20 years); promotion of publicly available sporting events; or repayment to a nonprofit coapplicant subject to voter approval. It caps state-funded promotion: 10 years for arts/cultural events, 10 years for sporting events, and 5 years for complexes in cities of the second class or villages. For large public stadiums approved on or after July 19, 2024, state assistance may cover up to 100% of final project cost. It revises board approval procedures—approvals tied to voter approval of bond issues or issuance of a building permit within 24 months—and requires a majority board vote with the Governor as a member. Original sections 13-3106 and 13-3103 are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "lease" as a contractual lease between the coapplicants referenced in §13-3102(1)(b) for an eligible sports arena at fair market rent, term not to exceed 20 years. It also makes approvals required by §13-3103(1)(f) temporary: the board’s temporary approval becomes permanent if a building permit for the arena is issued within 24 months; if not, the temporary approval is void. Agencies implicated: the approving board and the building permit authority. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
