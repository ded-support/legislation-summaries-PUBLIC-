Summary

LB314 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act (secs. 13-3103, 13-3106). It clarifies eligible uses of state assistance for privately owned sports complexes: repayment of bonds/debt for related public infrastructure or for debt issued by a nonprofit coapplicant (subject to voter approval under §13-3110); leasing facilities to political subdivisions for governmental use (defined as contracts up to 20 years at fair-market rent); and promotion of public sporting events. It caps state-funded promotion at 10 years (arts/cultural events also 10 years) and limits funding to 5 years for complexes in second-class cities or villages. For large public stadiums approved on or after July 19, 2024, state assistance under §13-3108 may fund up to 100% of final project cost. Board approval rules are revised—certain approvals are temporary pending voter approval or issuance of a building permit within 24 months—and board actions require a majority vote including the Governor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "lease" as any contractual lease between the coapplicants identified in §13-3102(1)(b) for use of an eligible sports arena at fair‑market rent for up to 20 years. Establishes that approvals tied to §13-3103(1)(f) are temporary: they convert to permanent if a building permit for the arena is issued within 24 months of the temporary approval by the board; otherwise the temporary approval voids. No state agencies beyond "the board" (the statute's approving authority) are newly created or referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
