Summary

Bill LB51 ratifies the National Crime Prevention and Privacy Compact (34 U.S.C. 40316) to enable authorized interstate exchange of criminal history record information for noncriminal‑justice purposes. The Superintendent of Law Enforcement and Public Safety is directed to execute the compact and serve as the state's compact officer (or designate a designee) to administer it. The Nebraska State Patrol is designated as the state repository for criminal history record information and is authorized to take necessary actions, including adopting rules, regulations, and procedures for cooperative exchange with federal authorities. The bill preserves existing Nebraska State Patrol obligations under state law (including the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act) and does not change public access provisions. No funding or appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text ratifies the National Crime Prevention and Privacy Compact and assigns implementation and administration responsibilities to state officials and agencies. The Superintendent of Law Enforcement and Public Safety must execute the compact and (or designee) serve as the state's compact officer. The Nebraska State Patrol is designated as the state repository for criminal history record information and is authorized to perform all actions necessary to carry out the compact, including adopting rules, regulations, and procedures for interstate/federal exchanges for non‑criminal‑justice purposes. Ratification continues until expressly renounced by statute and does not change the Patrol’s existing statutory duties or public access to criminal history records. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are provided or changed.
