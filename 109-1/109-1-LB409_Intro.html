Summary

LB409 amends membership of the Nebraska Power Review Board (NPRB). After the first vacancy among the current "three additional persons" following the bill's effective date, the five-member board must include: at least one engineer, at least one attorney, at least one licensed journeyman electrician affiliated with a nonprofit labor organization holding a 501(c)(5) exemption (selected from a list provided by that organization), and two other persons. Appointments remain gubernatorial with legislative approval. The bill retains statutory pay and reimbursement provisions: board members $100/day (capped at $7,000/year), the Southwest Power Pool Regional State Committee designee $250/day (capped at $35,000/year) with an aggregate SPP-related cap of $40,000/year. The NPRB must continue filing reports to the Governor, Clerk of the Legislature (electronically), Department of Environment and Energy, and the Natural Resources Committee. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a post‑effective‑date transition for the board’s composition: when the first member appointed under subdivision (1)(a)(iii) vacates or their term expires after the act takes effect, the board will thereafter be five members. Those five must include at least one engineer, one attorney, one licensed journeyman electrician who is affiliated with a nonprofit labor organization for electrical workers (501(c)(5)) and selected from that organization’s recommended list, and two other persons. No state agencies are named and there are no funding or appropriation changes.
