Summary

The bill specifies that for physical brand inspections performed outside the brand inspection area, the Nebraska Brand Committee may charge an actual-expense fee that includes mileage calculated "at the rate established by the Department of Administrative Services." No appropriations, funding transfers, or other budgetary changes involving the Department of Administrative Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language addresses Nebraska Brand Committee fees and surcharges: it authorizes a brand-inspector travel surcharge up to $20 per inspection (collected by the inspector from the person requesting or required to have the inspection) and sets a feedlot registration/annual renewal fee of $0.50 multiplied by the registered feedlot’s maximum livestock capacity. It also clarifies applicability to cattle shipped to/from qualified dairies. There are no provisions naming the Department of Administrative Services, nor any explicit changes to DAS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
