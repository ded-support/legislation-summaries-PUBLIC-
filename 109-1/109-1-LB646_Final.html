Summary

LB646 amends the Livestock Brand Act to allow the Nebraska Brand Committee to charge an actual-expense fee for physical inspections performed outside the brand inspection area, which explicitly may include mileage calculated "at the rate established by the Department of Administrative Services." The provision also authorizes an hourly travel/inspection charge (not to exceed $30/hour). Such fees are to be charged and collected by the brand committee and may be assessed against persons committing violations. No other references to the Department of Administrative Services or changes to appropriations, funding, or broader budget allocations appear in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and provisions governing the Nebraska Brand Committee: designates “Brand committee” as the Nebraska Brand Committee; authorizes a brand-inspector travel surcharge up to $20 per inspection location, to be collected by the inspector from the person requesting or required to obtain the inspection; and sets a registration and annual renewal fee for registered feedlots equal to $0.50 multiplied by the feedlot’s maximum livestock capacity. No language in the added text references the Department of Administrative Services, and there are no appropriations, funding transfers, or budget allocations affecting that agency.
