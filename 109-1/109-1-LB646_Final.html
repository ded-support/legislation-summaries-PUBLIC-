Summary

LB646 amends section 54-1,108 to authorize the Nebraska Brand Committee to charge an actual-expense fee for physical brand inspections performed outside the brand inspection area. That fee may include mileage calculated at the rate established by the Department of Administrative Services and an hourly rate (not to exceed $30/hour) for travel and inspection time. The brand committee shall charge and collect the fee; when tied to investigations of alleged brand-law violations, the fee may be charged against the person committing the violation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds brand committee authority to impose a surcharge (up to $20) per inspection location to cover brand inspector travel; the surcharge is collected by the inspector and paid by the person requesting or legally required to have the inspection. Also adds/clarifies a registration and annual renewal fee equal to $0.50 multiplied by the registered feedlot’s maximum livestock capacity. No references to the Department of Administrative Services and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DAS are included.
