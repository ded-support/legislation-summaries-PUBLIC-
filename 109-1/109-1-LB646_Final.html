Summary

The bill references the Department of Administrative Services only to specify that mileage for out‑of‑area brand inspections is reimbursed "at the rate established by the Department of Administrative Services." For such inspections the Nebraska Brand Committee may charge an actual‑expense fee that includes mileage at the DAS rate plus an hourly travel/inspection charge (capped at $30/hour). The bill does not appropriate funds to DAS or change any state budget or appropriation for DAS. Fees and surcharges related to brand inspections remain collectible by the brand committee and may be charged to the requesting party or the person found in violation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions: authorize the Nebraska Brand Committee to impose a travel surcharge (up to $20) per inspection location to cover brand inspector travel, collected by the inspector from the requester or legally responsible person; set a registration/annual renewal fee equal to $0.50 multiplied by a feedlot’s maximum livestock capacity; and clarify applicability for cattle shipped to/from qualified dairies. There is no mention of the Department of Administrative Services and no changes to DAS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
