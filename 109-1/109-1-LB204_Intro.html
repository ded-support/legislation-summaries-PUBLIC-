Summary

LB204 (Biometric Autonomy Liberty Law) establishes state protections for individuals’ biometric data and defines "public entity" to include the State of Nebraska, state and local agencies, political subdivisions, Nebraska courts, and the Legislative Council. The State Department of Education defines the seventh‑grade lexile for consent language. The Attorney General is authorized to issue subpoenas, seek injunctive relief, and bring civil actions (including recovery of economic damages) for violations; enforcement is tied to the Nebraska Consumer Protection Act (section 59‑1602). Law enforcement agencies may use biometric data for legitimate purposes, and processors may disclose data when cooperating with law enforcement or pursuant to warrants/subpoenas. The Department of Motor Vehicles is exempted for facial‑recognition fraud detection; HIPAA‑covered health data and certain federal statutes are also excluded. No funding or appropriation changes are included. The act becomes operative January 1, 2026.
