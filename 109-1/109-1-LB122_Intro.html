Summary

LB122 requires each public school board, on and after the act’s effective date, to prominently display in English the U.S. national motto ("In God We Trust") and Nebraska’s state motto ("Equality Under the Law") on a poster or plaque at least 8.5" x 11" in each classroom or another prominent location in each school building where students can read it daily. The bill authorizes school boards to accept private contributions to defray implementation costs. No state agency other than local school boards is named, and the bill makes no appropriations or changes to state budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires each school board, effective on the act's effective date, to prominently display the U.S. national motto "In God We Trust" and Nebraska's state motto "Equality Under the Law" in legible English on a poster or plaque at least 8.5" x 11" in each classroom or another prominent location in each school building visible to students daily. Allows school boards to accept contributions to defray implementation costs. No state agencies or departments are named and no new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
