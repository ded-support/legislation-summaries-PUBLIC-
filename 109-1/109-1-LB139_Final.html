Summary

LB139 amends provisions of the Real Property Appraiser Act and the Nebraska Appraisal Management Company Registration Act. It authorizes the Real Property Appraiser Board to use contingent dismissal agreements with remedial measures for credential holders and appraisal management companies (AMCs) and clarifies that successfully completed contingent dismissals are not disciplinary actions. The bill continues board authority to investigate, issue cease-and-desist orders, convene hearings, contract for technical assistance, and seek enforcement through the Attorney General and district court. It requires reporting disciplinary actions to federal authorities under Title XI and to the Appraisal Subcommittee per the AMC rule. For criminal-history checks, individual 10%+ AMC owners must submit fingerprints to the Nebraska State Patrol for FBI checks, and the board shall pay the Nebraska State Patrol’s costs for those fingerprint-based national checks. Original sections are repealed.
