Summary

LB139 amends enforcement and registration provisions under Nebraska's Real Property Appraiser Act and Appraisal Management Company (AMC) Registration Act. Key agency actions and requirements:

- The Real Property Appraiser Board may investigate alleged violations, contract for technical assistance, enter contingent dismissal agreements with remedial measures (for credential holders or AMCs), and report disciplinary actions to federal authorities as required by Title XI. A successfully completed contingent dismissal is not disciplinary action.

- AMCs must have owners (>10%) submit fingerprint cards/electronic prints for national criminal history checks (Nebraska State Patrol and FBI). The board is directed to pay the Nebraska State Patrol for those fingerprint-based checks.

- The board may issue cease-and-desist orders, seek enforcement through the Attorney General, and must report AMC violations/discipline to the Appraisal Subcommittee per Title XI/AMC rule.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the board charged with enforcing the Real Property Appraiser Act to: (1) contract with or use qualified persons for technical assistance in investigations; and (2) enter into contingent dismissal agreements with remedial measures not only with credential holders but also with appraisal management companies. It also clarifies that a successfully completed contingent dismissal agreement is not disciplinary action. No state agencies beyond the enforcing "board" are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
