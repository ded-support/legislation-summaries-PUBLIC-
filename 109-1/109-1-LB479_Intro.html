Summary

LB479 amends the distribution of sales and use tax revenue to direct all proceeds from the sales/use taxes imposed under section 77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (as defined in §3-101) to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023. The bill becomes operative October 1, 2025, and repeals the original version of §77-27,132.
