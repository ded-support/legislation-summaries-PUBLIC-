Summary

LB552 prohibits public postsecondary institutions in Nebraska—those governed by the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska, the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges, and community college governing boards—from maintaining a "diversity, equity, and inclusion" (DEI) office or implementing DEI actions that influence hiring, provide preferential treatment, adopt race-/gender‑referenced policies, require employee participation in DEI programs, or use public funds for DEI programs or offices. Exceptions include sexual‑harassment training; offices of licensed attorneys for legal compliance; statements required for grants/accreditation (e.g., support for first‑generation, low‑income, underserved students or compliance certifications); certain data collection, research, student organization activities, and short‑term speakers. Employees may sue institutions for violations and seek injunctive relief. No specific appropriations or new funding are provided; the bill bars public spending on DEI programs/offices.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions tying implementation to the appropriate governing boards: the Board of Regents (University of Nebraska), the Board of Trustees (Nebraska State Colleges), or community college governing boards. Defines "diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) office/program" broadly (activities that influence hiring, promote differential treatment or race-/gender‑referenced policies/programs). Prohibits public postsecondary institutions from requiring participation in DEI programs as a condition of employment or enrollment and from spending public money on DEI programs or offices. Preserves activities for legal compliance (offices staffed by attorneys), sexual‑harassment training, grant/accreditation statements, demographic data collection, scholarly research, student organization activities, and short‑term guest speakers. Employees compelled to attend may sue; prevailing employees can obtain injunctive relief. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are included.
