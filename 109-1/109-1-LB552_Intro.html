Summary

LB552 prohibits public postsecondary institutions in Nebraska from maintaining a "diversity, equity, and inclusion" (DEI) office or conducting DEI programs that (a) influence hiring apart from color‑blind/sex‑neutral processes, (b) provide differential treatment or special benefits based on race, color, ethnicity, gender identity, or sexual orientation, (c) implement policies/procedures or programs referenced to those traits, or (d) require participation or condition enrollment/functions on such programs. "Appropriate governing board" is defined as the Board of Regents (University of Nebraska), the Board of Trustees (Nebraska State Colleges), or a community college governing board. The bill also prohibits spending public money on DEI offices/programs. Exceptions include sexual‑harassment training, attorney‑staffed compliance offices, grant/accreditation statements, demographic data collection, scholarly research, recognized student activities, and short‑term guest speakers. Employees may sue for injunctive relief; no appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and restrictions affecting public postsecondary institutions governed by the Board of Regents (University of Nebraska), Board of Trustees (Nebraska State Colleges), and community college governing boards. Defines "diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) office/program" and prohibits requiring participation in DEI programs or spending public money on DEI offices/programs. Preserves exceptions allowing sexual‑harassment training; legal offices staffed by attorneys for compliance; grant- or accreditation-related statements; data collection; scholarly research; recognized student organization activities; and short-term guest speakers. Allows employees to sue institutions for violations and obtain injunctive relief. No specific appropriations or new budget allocations are made.
