Summary

LB685 amends section 84-612 to (1) require certification by the Director of Administrative Services that the General Fund cash balance is inadequate before the State Treasurer may transfer cash from the Cash Reserve Fund to the General Fund, and (2) vest the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services with authority to direct the dates and amounts for numerous statutory Cash Reserve Fund transfers. The bill specifically directs a transfer of $1,000,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to the School Safety and Security Fund "as soon as administratively possible" after the bill's effective date (with the subaccount to be established by the Department of Education). Multiple other statutory transfers in the section remain subject to timing/amount direction by the DAS budget administrator.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill directs the Department of Administrative Services to create a subaccount to receive a $1,000,000 transfer from the Cash Reserve Fund. The State Treasurer must transfer that $1,000,000 to the School Safety and Security Fund “as soon as administratively possible,” on dates/amounts as directed by the budget administrator in DAS’s Budget Division. Money in the DAS-managed subaccount will be used to provide grants under section 79-3108 to fund installation of Underwriters Laboratories Standard 1037 exterior secure master key boxes at schools.
