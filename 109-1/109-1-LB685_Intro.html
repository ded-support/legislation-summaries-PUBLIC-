Summary

Key references to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS):

- Transfers from the Cash Reserve Fund to the General Fund require certification by the Director of Administrative Services that the General Fund cash balance is inadequate; such transfers must be reversed upon notification by the Director that sufficient funds exist (84-612(2)).

- Numerous statutory transfers from the Cash Reserve Fund to specific funds are to be executed “on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services” (84-612, multiple subsections).

- The bill directs the State Treasurer, as soon as administratively possible after the act’s effective date and as directed by the DAS budget administrator, to transfer $1,000,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to the School Safety and Security Fund (84-612(31)). The Department of Education must establish a subaccount for that $1,000,000 to fund exterior secure master key boxes (79-3109(2)).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text directs a $1,000,000 transfer from the Cash Reserve Fund into the School Safety and Security Fund to finance exterior secure master key box grants. The budget administrator in the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services is authorized to direct the timing and amounts of that transfer. The bill also requires "the department" to establish a subaccount for the transferred $1,000,000; funds in that subaccount must be used to provide grants pursuant to section 79‑3108 for installing the mandated exterior secure master key boxes. No other DAS programmatic duties or additional appropriations are specified.
