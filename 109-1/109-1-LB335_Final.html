Summary

LB335 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-2303 (In the Line of Duty Dependent Education Act) to revise definitions used in the Act and explicitly add “stepchild” to the definition of “child” for dependents of Nebraska law enforcement officers or firefighters killed in the line of duty. Defined institutions/agencies referenced include the community college system, Nebraska state college system, the University of Nebraska (state university), and volunteer fire departments (as defined in §35-1303). The bill references the In the Line of Duty Dependent Education Benefit (§85-2304) and governing bodies that set tuition and fees. The original §85-2303 is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Adds the phrase "or the stepchild," expanding the definition to expressly include a stepchild. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations are mentioned or affected.
