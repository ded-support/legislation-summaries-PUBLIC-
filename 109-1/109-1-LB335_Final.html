Summary

LB335 amends the In the Line of Duty Dependent Education Act (section 85‑2303) to modify definitional language. It explicitly adds "stepchild" to the definition of "child" eligible for the Act's education benefit. The bill also restates definitions for entities and authorities: community colleges (community college system), state colleges (Nebraska State College System), state universities (University of Nebraska), and volunteer fire departments (per section 35‑1303). It confirms that tuition and fees are those set by the governing bodies of state universities, state colleges, or community colleges. No executive agencies, new programs, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.
