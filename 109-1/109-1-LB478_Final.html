Summary

LB478 locates the Winery and Grape Producers Promotional Fund for administrative purposes in the Department of Agriculture. Revenues credited to the fund (including the winery charge in section 53-304, funds received under subsections 5 or 6 of section 53-123.15, excise taxes under section 2-5603, and gifts/grants/bequests) are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to that fund. The Department of Agriculture, under the direction of and in cooperation with the Nebraska Grape and Winery Board, shall use the fund to develop and maintain research, marketing, promotion, and related programs for grapes, fruits, berries, honey and other agricultural products for the wine industry (including funding viticulture/enology experts). Funds are restricted to those purposes, may carry over, and are investable per state law.
