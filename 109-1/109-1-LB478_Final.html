Summary

The bill locates the Winery and Grape Producers Promotional Fund administratively in the Department of Agriculture. Revenues credited to the fund include the $20 per 160-gallon charge paid by Nebraska wineries, fees remitted from certain shipping licenses (manufacturer direct-sales and retail direct-sales under section 53-123.15), excise taxes under section 2-5603, and gifts/grants/bequests. The Department of Agriculture, at the direction of and in cooperation with the Nebraska Grape and Winery Board, shall use the fund for research, development, marketing and promotion of grapes, wines and related agricultural products, including hiring viticulture/enology experts. Unexpended balances may carry forward and funds are investable under state investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text affect the Department of Agriculture. The bill instead creates: (1) an auction permit for auction houses to sell alcohol from closed retailers or private collections (notification/inventory and rulemaking required); (2) a shipping license for out‑of‑state wholesalers limited to shipments to Nebraska wholesalers, subject to the existing wholesaler shipping license fees (per §§53‑124, 53‑124.01) which are collected by the commission and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund; and (3) an annual reaffirmation with a renewal fee up to $30 for designated primary sources of supply, plus an exemption from a specified reporting requirement for certain licensed retailers. No appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes to the Department of Agriculture are included.
