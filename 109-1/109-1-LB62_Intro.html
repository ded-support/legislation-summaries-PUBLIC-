Summary

LB62 appropriates $19,000,000 from the General Fund for FY2025‑26 to the Department of Administrative Services (Program 245). DAS is directed to provide those funds to the Office of the Chief Information Officer to: (1) establish interoperable communications between state agencies and volunteer departments (portable and mobile radios, programming, installation), and (2) fund a grant program for training and connecting volunteer departments to the Statewide Radio System. Grant eligibility is based on number/type of radios required; the CIO may not charge subscription, programming, or training fees to volunteer departments after a grant. The Legislature intends all grant funds distributed by June 30, 2028. The bill takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill appropriates $19,000,000 from the General Fund for FY2025-26 to the Department of Administrative Services (Program 245). DAS must transfer these funds to the Office of the Chief Information Officer to run a grant program that equips volunteer departments and agencies for interoperability with the Statewide Radio System — including portable/mobile radios, programming, installation, and interoperability training. Eligibility is based on each volunteer department's size and radio needs. Grantees may not be charged subscription, programming, or training fees after receiving a grant. Legislature intends all grants distributed by June 30, 2028.
