Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: directs DAS to create and publish a public online database of school-district financial information (revenue sources, expenditures, balance sheets from most recent audit reports). The DAS budget administrator must set an implementation date on or before January 1, 2026. School districts must provide annual and historical (back to 2005, if available) audit data; DAS must update the database within 30 days of receipt.

- Department of Economic Development: beginning with academic year 2024-25, the Department of Economic Development, in collaboration with the Nebraska State Colleges and University Boards (and in one provision in consultation with the Coordinating Commission), will designate eligible programs/shortage areas for Nebraska Career Scholarships based on periodic workforce reviews.

- Department of Health and Human Services: DHHS licensing/approval of foster or residential providers affects student residency determinations. When wards are placed outside their home district, the state shall pay the student's education and required transportation costs under rules and regulations prescribed by DHHS (payments not made in advance).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: New requirement to build, host and publish an online database of each school district's financials (revenue sources, expenditures, and balance sheet from the most recent audits). The budget division administrator must set an implementation date on or before Jan 1, 2026. Districts must supply audit-derived data and annual updates (department to refresh within 30 days) and provide historical data back to 2005 when available. Confidential information exempted by law need not be disclosed.

Department of Economic Development: No changes in the added text.
