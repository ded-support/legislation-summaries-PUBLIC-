Summary

LB306 directs three state agencies as follows:

- Department of Administrative Services: required to develop, maintain, and post a public database of school-district financial information (audit-based revenue, expenditures, assets/liabilities). The DAS budget administrator will set an implementation date on or before January 1, 2026. School districts must provide current and annual updates (and available historical data beginning 2005); DAS must update the database within 30 days of receipt.

- Department of Economic Development: will designate eligible programs of study for Nebraska Career Scholarships (in collaboration with the Board of Trustees, Board of Regents, or Coordinating Commission) based on periodic workforce-need reviews.

- Department of Health and Human Services: foster homes and residential service providers must be licensed/approved by DHHS for residency determinations; the state will pay education (and required transportation) costs for wards placed outside their resident district under rules and regulations prescribed by DHHS.

- Funding/appropriations: Boards and the Coordinating Commission shall allocate amounts appropriated under the Nebraska Career Scholarship Act among campuses/private colleges; the commission must establish a separate budget subprogram for private-college scholarship allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services (DAS): New requirement for DAS to develop, maintain, and publish online a school-district financial database (revenue sources, expenditures, and balance sheets showing assets/liabilities) using most recent audit reports. The budget division’s administrator must set an implementation date on or before January 1, 2026. School districts must provide audit-derived data annually (and historical data back to 2005 if available); DAS must update the database within 30 days of receipt. Confidential or legally exempt information need not be disclosed.

Department of Economic Development and Department of Health and Human Services: No substantive additions, funding changes, or appropriation/budget allocations affecting these agencies appear in the underlined text.
