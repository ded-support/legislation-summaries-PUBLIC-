Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: directs DAS to develop and publish a public database of financial information from all Nebraska school districts (revenues, expenditures, balance sheet items) using most recent audit reports; the budget administrator must set an implementation date on or before Jan. 1, 2026. School districts must provide annual updates (database updated within 30 days) and historical data from 2005 if available; confidential records remain protected.

- Department of Health and Human Services: maintains authority to prescribe rules/regulations for state payment of education and required transportation costs when a ward is placed outside his/her resident district; foster family homes licensed or approved by DHHS determine district residency for wards; residential settings operated by DHHS‑certified/licensed providers (or Medicaid‑enrolled) affect residency, contracting, and reimbursement responsibilities.

- Department of Economic Development: will designate eligible programs of study for Nebraska Career Scholarships (in collaboration with relevant governing boards and other agencies) based on periodic workforce-need reviews.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: Requires DAS to create and publish a public online database of each school district’s financials (revenues, expenditures, balance sheet with assets/liabilities) from most recent audits; DAS must set an implementation date no later than January 1, 2026. School districts must submit audit-derived data annually (and historical data back to 2005 if available); DAS must update the database within 30 days of receipt. Confidential or legally exempt records are excluded.

Department of Economic Development and Department of Health and Human Services: No provisions, funding changes, appropriations, or budget allocations affecting DED or DHHS appear in the added text.
