Summary

LB680 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79‑1204 to clarify the role and mission of educational service units (ESUs). ESUs are designated as providers of core and requested services to member school districts and must act cooperatively with the State Department of Education in planning and implementing statewide long‑range educational goals. The State Board of Education retains authority to set minimum accreditation standards for ESUs to ensure taxpayer accountability, equitable statewide services, and program quality. ESUs may administer federal, state, and private funds for participating districts "when appropriate and as funds become available," may contract with nonmember districts, nonpublic schools, other ESUs, and other public agencies (via the Interlocal Cooperation Act and Joint Public Agency Act), and may use ESU funds to pay board membership dues. No specific appropriations or funding allocations are made.
