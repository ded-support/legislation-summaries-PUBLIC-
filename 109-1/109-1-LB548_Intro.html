Summary

LB548 authorizes political subdivisions (e.g., municipalities) that own and operate natural gas distribution systems to enter contracts to sell natural gas to Nebraska-based industrial consumers whose facilities require at least 3 billion BTU per day on an average day. Contracts must be for a minimum two-year term (excluding renewals). The industrial customer must not be a customer of any investor‑owned or other governmentally‑owned gas system for the operations receiving service at the time the contract is made. For contract purposes, the facility location is deemed within the political subdivision’s service area. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authorization allowing any political subdivision that owns and operates a natural gas system (e.g., a municipal or other government utility) to contract to sell natural gas to industrial consumers located in Nebraska that average at least 3 billion BTU/day. Key provisions: contracts must run at least two years (excluding renewals); the industrial customer must not already be served by any investor‑owned or governmentally‑owned gas system for the operations to receive the service at time of contracting; and the customer’s facility is deemed within the political subdivision’s service area solely for the contract. No specific state agencies, appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are established or changed.
