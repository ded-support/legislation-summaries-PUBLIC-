Summary

LB548 permits a political subdivision (e.g., a municipality) that owns and operates a natural gas system to contract to sell natural gas to an industrial consumer located in Nebraska that requires at least 3 billion Btu per day on an average day. Contracts must run at least two years (excluding renewals). The industrial consumer may not, at the time the contract is entered, be a customer of any investor‑owned or governmentally‑owned gas system for the operations to be served. For contract purposes, the facility’s location is treated as within the political subdivision’s gas service area. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for any political subdivision that owns and operates a natural gas system to contract to sell gas to industrial consumers located in Nebraska that average at least 3 billion BTU/day. Contracts must be at least two years (excluding renewals). The industrial customer may not be a customer of any investor‑owned or governmentally‑owned gas system for the operations to receive service when the contract is made. The facility is deemed within the political subdivision’s service area for the contract. No specific state agencies or departments, nor any funding or appropriations, are mentioned.
