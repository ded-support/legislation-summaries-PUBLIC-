Summary

LB245 adopts by reference several National Institute of Standards and Technology handbooks (Handbook 44, Handbook 130, and Handbook 133, as of Jan. 1, 2025) for weights and measures. It requires that copies of the adopted handbooks be filed with the Secretary of State, the Clerk of the Legislature (filed electronically), and the Department of Agriculture. The bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations directed to the Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Agriculture: the bill adds higher inspection/permit fees and procedural rules for food-related and weighing/measuring permits. Annual permit fees for Salvage Operation, Commissary, and Establishment are raised to $171. Effective 2025, scale inspection fees are updated (new rates): up to 35 lb $20; multiunit scales $114; >35–1,000 lb $36; >1,000–4,000 lb $72; >4,000–50,000 lb $83; >50,000–150,000 lb $88; over 150,000 lb $192. Rounding rules (nearest dollar or one‑half dollar) and a provision voiding a weighing/measuring permit if fees remain unpaid 90 days after due date are added. Educational institutions, health care facilities, nursing homes, and governmental organizations are exempt from owner fees, but outside food service providers used by those entities must pay applicable fees. No direct appropriations or new budget allocations are specified.
