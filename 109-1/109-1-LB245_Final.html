Summary

LB245 requires that copies of the National Institute of Standards and Technology handbooks adopted by reference for weights and measures be filed with the Secretary of State, Clerk of the Legislature, and the Department of Agriculture (copies filed with the Clerk must be electronic). The bill also establishes fee-setting and cash-fund limits: fee revenues for the Nebraska Pure Food Act and the Weights and Measures Act may not exceed 107% of program cash fund appropriations, and fiscal year‑end cash fund balances may not exceed 17% of those appropriations. Fee adjustments must be proportionally distributed; late and administrative collection fees are remitted to the Pure Food Cash Fund and the Weights and Measures Administrative Fund, respectively. The bill updates device fee-setting procedures and maximums.
