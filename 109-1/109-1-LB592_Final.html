Summary

The bill requires the State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator of the Department of Administrative Services to determine the appropriate state fund types for the ABLE program to comply with federal law and state policy. It directs the State Treasurer to annually report, prior to transferring funds, the amounts transferred from the ABLE Expense Fund to the State Investment Officer's Cash Fund to the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services (and electronically to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst). Program startup and operating costs are to be paid from the Treasury Management Cash Fund (and transfers from the expense fund as permitted), in amounts authorized by legislative appropriation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added clause makes amounts in the referenced account immune from levy, execution, judgment, garnishment or other judicial seizure to satisfy debts of the account’s beneficiary or owner. It is an asset-protection provision that overrides other law. There are no provisions in this text altering Department of Administrative Services funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
