Summary

- The State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator of the Department of Administrative Services will jointly determine the state fund types required to comply with section 529A and state policy.

- The State Treasurer must annually—and prior to any transfer to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund—report amounts transferred during the previous fiscal year to the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services and to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst (the LFA report must be submitted electronically).

- Funding/appropriation provisions: the ABLE Expense Fund is fee-funded; the State Treasurer will transfer from that fund a pro rata share of the budget appropriated to the Nebraska Investment Council to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund. Until the program is self-sustaining, establishment and operating costs are payable from the Treasury Management Cash Fund and, as permitted, from expense-fund transfers in amounts authorized by legislative appropriation, with legislative intent to reimburse startup costs when self-sustaining.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary for Department of Administrative Services:

The added language creates a blanket protection for amounts in the specified account, declaring they are immune from levy, execution, judgment, garnishment, seizure, appropriation, or other judicial enforcement to satisfy debts or liabilities of the account owner or beneficiary, “notwithstanding any other provision of law.” There are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Administrative Services in this provision.
