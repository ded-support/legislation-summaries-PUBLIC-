Summary

LB418 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §38-1424 (Funeral Directing and Embalming Practice Act) to explicitly allow a nonlicensed funeral director and embalmer to officiate at a funeral ceremony. The bill retains existing disciplinary grounds tied to violations of state law or rules of "the department or other body having regulatory powers" (i.e., the agency charged with enforcing the Act) and clarifies that licensed practitioners may continue pre-need sales under the Burial Pre-Need Sale Act. Introduced to and reviewed by the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee, the bill repeals the prior version of §38-1424. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
