Summary

LB97 reorganizes DMV fee authority and fund allocations and creates two new DMV funds. It (1) establishes the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund (administered by the DMV director) and a DMV Ignition Interlock Fund, (2) directs specific fees—including a $10 identification‑inspection fee, NMVTIS reporting fees charged to wrecker/salvage dealers (capped at reporting cost), mailing/postage for plate delivery, and portions of operator’s license/ID, commercial license, ignition interlock, and 24/7 permit fees—to be credited to those funds or to county funds as specified, (3) shifts license‑plate production fee receipts to the License Plate Cash Fund beginning with the 2029 plate cycle (previously to the Highway Trust Fund), and (4) authorizes DMV to collect fees, issue credentials (including electronically), adopt ignition‑interlock rules, administer indigent payments from the Ignition Interlock Fund, credit receipts to the Motor Carrier Services Division Distributive Fund, and allows legislative-directed transfers of DMV Cash Fund balances (including to the General Fund or the Vehicle Title & Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund). Investment earnings on the DMV Cash Fund are credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key DMV additions:

- Defines "amphibious vehicle" and exempts such vehicles from specified statutory sections if validly registered under the Motor Vehicle Registration Act.

- Title rules: tighter bonded-certificate requirements—applicants must send certified‑mail notice to lienholders; unresponsive lienholders have 30 days and issuance extinguishes liens.

- Identification inspections: $10 fee per inspection, remitted to State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund; no fee when a U.S. Government Certificate to Obtain Title is presented.

- Registration options: allows annual or 2‑ or 3‑year multiyear registrations (implementation by director on/before Jan 1, 2029); multiyear renewals pay fees/taxes for each year.

- License plates: one plate generally issued/displayed; department may mail plates/registration and charge a postage/handling fee (remitted to DMV Cash Fund). Beginning with 2029 plate cycle, related fees remit to the License Plate Cash Fund.

- Ignition interlock: DMV must review records to determine applicant eligibility and prior convictions before issuing permits.
