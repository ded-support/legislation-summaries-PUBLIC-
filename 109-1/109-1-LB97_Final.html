Summary

The bill expands Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) authority over titles, identification inspections, electronic reporting by wrecker/salvage dealers, and issuance/renewal/replacement of operator licenses, state IDs, commercial credentials, permits and ignition‑interlock permits (including preserved digital image/signature methods and device/program rules). It authorizes DMV staff or county treasurers to collect statutory fees and creates a Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund (Director‑administered) to receive receipts, county reimbursements, a $10 identification‑inspection fee, capped wrecker/salvage reporting fees, and postage/handling fees for mailed plates/registration. Beginning with the 2029 plate cycle, certain license‑plate fees shift from the Highway Trust Fund to the License Plate Cash Fund. The bill specifies allocation splits for operator/permit and ignition‑interlock fees, allows legislative transfers from the DMV Cash Fund to the General Fund or the Vehicle Title & Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund, credits fund investment earnings to the General Fund (from 10/1/2024), and creates an Ignition Interlock Fund for indigent assistance (transferable to the General Fund); prior transfers to the Violence Prevention Cash Fund are preserved.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV):

- Identification inspections: a $10 fee per inspection is added, remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund; no fee when a U.S. Government Certificate to Obtain Title is presented.

- Registration/plates: persons may opt for 2- or 3-year registrations (must prepay fees/taxes each year, including fees in §§60-3,141 and 60-3,156). The director may begin secure mail delivery of plates/registration by an implementation date on or before Jan 1, 2029; the DMV may charge a postage/handling fee (remitted to the DMV Cash Fund). Proof of registration allows 30 days operation without a mailed plate.

- Fee flow change: beginning with the 2029 plate cycle, all fees under the section are remitted to the License Plate Cash Fund.

- Titles/permits: bonded-title notice procedures added (certified mail, 30‑day lien extinguishment); DMV must screen applicants before issuing ignition‑interlock permits (eligibility, prior convictions, no‑drive periods).
