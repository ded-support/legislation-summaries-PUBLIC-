Summary

LB97 broadens and clarifies Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) authority and makes multiple fiscal changes. The DMV may prescribe forms, approve identification inspections, issue/reissue bonded, duplicate and certified titles (and recall bonded titles), use digital images/signatures, electronically issue/renew/replace commercial licenses and ID cards, and administer exams/receipts. Fee changes: a $10 ID‑inspection fee, NMVTIS reporting fees from wrecker/salvage dealers, and postage/handling for mailed plates are credited to the DMV Cash Fund; ignition‑interlock and 24/7 sobriety permit fees are allocated among the DMV Cash Fund, a new DMV Ignition Interlock Fund (to assist indigent installation/maintenance and subject to transfer to the General Fund), and county general funds as specified. The bill allows county reimbursements to the DMV, legislative transfers from the DMV Cash Fund to specified funds, directs some receipts to the Motor Carrier Services Division Distributive Fund, and redirects DMV Cash Fund investment earnings to the General Fund beginning October 1, 2024.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key DMV changes:

- New definitions/exemptions: “amphibious vehicle” defined; such vehicles with valid registration are exempt from specified statutory sections.

- Title/registration procedures: bonded-title applicants must notify secured-party lienholders by certified mail; liens extinguish if no response within 30 days.

- Identification inspections: $10 fee per inspection payable to State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund; no fee when a U.S. Government Certificate to Obtain Title is presented.

- Registration/plates: optional 2- or 3-year multiyear registrations allowed (implementation date on or before Jan 1, 2029); fees/taxes for each year due at renewal. Beginning on department implementation (on or before Jan 1, 2029) the DMV may mail plates/registration and charge a postage/handling fee (remitted to the DMV Cash Fund). Beginning with the 2029 plate cycle, related fees are remitted to the License Plate Cash Fund.

- Ignition interlock: DMV must review records/driving abstracts to determine applicant eligibility before issuing permits.

- Temporary operation: proof of registration permits operation for 30 days without a mailed plate.
