Summary

Appropriates state General Fund dollars to the Nebraska Attorney General, Program 507, to implement Legislative Bill 504. Amounts: $72,149 for FY2025‑26 and $147,752 for FY2026‑27. Includes spending caps on permanent and temporary salaries and per diems drawn from these appropriations: maximum $50,000 in FY2025‑26 and $103,000 in FY2026‑27. Funds are explicitly designated to aid the Attorney General in carrying out LB504.
