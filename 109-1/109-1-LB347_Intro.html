Summary

LB347 repeals Neb. Rev. Stat. §75‑148, eliminating the statutory conditions on common carriers’ issuance of securities that were tied to the Public Service Commission. In effect, the bill removes the specific PSC authority/responsibility established by that section to condition or regulate such securities issuances. No changes to agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the bill.
