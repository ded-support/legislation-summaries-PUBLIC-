Summary

This bill defines “Department” as the Department of Revenue and assigns several regulatory duties to it. The department (via the Tax Commissioner) may conduct background checks at any time—including fingerprinting and an FBI criminal-history check through the Nebraska State Patrol—require personal history reports, and perform background investigations and facility inspections. Applicants and licensees must pay background-check costs and any fees the department determines. The Tax Commissioner has authority to deny, suspend, or revoke licenses. Operators and distributors must remit amounts withheld for outstanding debts or state tax liabilities to the Department of Revenue for credit against such liabilities (per section 9-1306). The bill also establishes license fees: distributor fees of $200 per cash device (max $10,000; biennial) and a $10,000 biennial manufacturer license.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for Department of Revenue licensing reviews: applicants and licensees must submit a department personal‑history form and are subject to fingerprinting and an FBI criminal‑history check via the Nebraska State Patrol—both on a biennial basis and at any time—to determine whether the department should deny, suspend, cancel, revoke, or terminate a license. The department may also conduct background investigations and inspect applicant or licensee facilities. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
