Summary

LB177 designates "Department" as the Department of Revenue and repeatedly authorizes the department to: conduct background checks (including fingerprinting and FBI criminal-history checks) of applicants and licensees for operator, distributor, and manufacturer cash-device licenses; require submission of a department personal history report; conduct investigations and facility inspections; and determine whether there is cause to deny, suspend, revoke, or terminate licenses. Applicants and licensees must pay background-check costs and any required fees as determined by the department. Operators and distributors must remit amounts withheld from winnings to the Department of Revenue to be credited against outstanding state tax liabilities. No appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes to the Department of Revenue are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language authorizes the Department of Revenue to require fingerprinting and an FBI criminal history check (via the Nebraska State Patrol) of applicants and licensees for the covered license, both at application and on a biennial basis or "at any time." Applicants/licensees must submit a department personal history report and may be subject to background investigations and inspections of their facilities. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are stated.
