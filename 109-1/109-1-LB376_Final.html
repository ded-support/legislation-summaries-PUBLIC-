Summary

The bill imposes multiple new duties on the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): mandatory physician abortion reporting with DHHS publishing an annual public report by June 30; DHHS to file an annual effectiveness report by Dec. 1 and a Nebraska Prenatal Plus Program report by Dec. 15 through 2029; creation of an amino‑acid elemental formula assistance program (up to 50% of OOP costs, capped at $12,000 per 12‑month period, first‑come/first‑served); establishment of a lead‑poisoning prevention program and a statewide cancer registry with mandatory reporting; furnishing mental‑health commitment/discharge data to the State Patrol for the firearm‑purchase database; and pursuing a 1915(c) waiver for a family support program (up to $10,000/year per participant, max 850 participants), to be administered only as funded and reported through 2027. Budget and funding changes: the Legislature must annually appropriate amounts from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund derived from the 1¢ and 2¢ cigarette tax (registry capped at $200,000; no more than half the two‑cent funds to UNMC/Eppley), and the State Treasurer is directed by the Department of Administrative Services’ budget administrator to transfer $7,000,000 from that Cash Fund to the General Fund. DHHS budget submissions must include goals, benchmarks, and progress reports filed with the Director of Administrative Services; DAS’s budget division will coordinate benchmark development.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: Adds temporary scope-of-practice language for emergency medical personnel — an "advanced technician" is authorized to perform visualized intubation and, until December 31, 2025, to perform all acts authorized for an emergency medical technician‑intermediate. The text also inserts several statutory cross‑references and multiple temporary/sunset dates (e.g., June 30, 2028; December 15, 2029; December 31, 2027) but does not specify program funding.

Department of Administrative Services: The underlined text contains no substantive provisions affecting DAS and contains no appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes for either agency.
