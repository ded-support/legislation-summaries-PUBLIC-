Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to implement and report on multiple public‑health programs and to coordinate budgeting with the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). DHHS must collect physician abortion reports (annual public report by June 30); submit an annual §43‑512 electronic report (by Dec. 1); continue Prenatal Plus reporting through Dec. 15, 2029; establish a Medicaid‑aligned lead poisoning prevention program and a statewide cancer registry (annual reports; registry funding capped at $200,000). Funding changes: Legislature to appropriate from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund amounts equal to one and two cents of the cigarette tax for cancer research/registry; a one‑time $7 million transfer from that cash fund to the General Fund is directed by the State Treasurer per DAS budget administration. DHHS must create an amino‑acid formula program (covers up to 50% OOP, max $12,000/12 months) and seek a 1915(c) waiver for a family support program (max $10,000/participant annually; 850 participants). ICF/DD Reimbursement Protection Fund allocations: $55,000 admin; $312,000 community services plus federal match; $1,000,000 to General Fund; remainder to rate rebasing. Agencies must file budget forms with the DAS Director; DAS budget division will work with DHHS on goals, benchmarks, and budget processes.
