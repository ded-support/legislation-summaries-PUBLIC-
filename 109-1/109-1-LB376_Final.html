Summary

LB376 makes multiple changes affecting the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and touches the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget functions. Key DHHS provisions: require physician abortion reporting and annual DHHS statistics; Prenatal Plus annual reports to the Legislature through 2029; counties must maintain DHHS offices/services at no cost to DHHS through June 30, 2028; DHHS to cover up to 50% (max $12,000/12‑month) of out‑of‑pocket costs for amino acid–based elemental formula; establish a Family Support Program (DHHS Division of Developmental Disabilities) with $10,000 per‑participant caps, up to 850 participants, subject to appropriations and annual reporting through 2027 (ARPA funds may be used); ICF/DD Reimbursement Protection Fund allocations ($55,000 admin, $312,000 community services, $1,000,000 to General Fund; remainder to rate increases); expanded medical‑assistance authorities and contractor transparency; lead‑poisoning prevention program and infectious‑disease notification duties. Budgeting: agencies file budget forms with the DAS Director; DHHS budget must include goals/benchmarks; annually appropriate cigarette‑tax HHS Cash Fund moneys for cancer research/registry (registry capped $200,000) and a $7,000,000 transfer from that fund to the General Fund as directed by the DAS budget administrator.
