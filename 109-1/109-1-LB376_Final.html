Summary

The bill assigns multiple operational, reporting, and funding responsibilities to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and assigns budget oversight roles to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). DHHS duties include receiving physician abortion reports and publishing an annual public report; annual program reporting (due Dec. 1) and continued Nebraska Prenatal Plus reporting through Dec. 15, 2029; receiving/furnishing mental‑health/commitment data for the firearm‑purchase database; investigating facility infectious‑disease notifications; operating the lead‑poisoning program and statewide cancer registry; and developing a developmental‑disabilities quality improvement plan with goals/benchmarks included in the biennial budget to the DAS Director. DHHS must create an amino‑acid elemental‑formula assistance program (up to 50% of OOP, max $12,000/12 months) and apply for a 1915(c) waiver to run a capped family support program ($10,000/applicant, ≤850 participants) with ARPA funds as available. Medical‑assistance/managed‑care contract limits are tightened (admin ≤12%, profit ≤3%, MLR 85%, incentives ≤2%). Specified ICF/DD and other allocations include $55,000 admin, $312,000 community services, and $1,000,000 to the General Fund. Appropriations: cigarette‑tax‑derived HHS Cash Fund monies (registry ≤$200,000; research limits) and a required $7,000,000 transfer from that cash fund to the General Fund as directed by the DAS budget administrator. DAS will receive agency budget forms, enforce deadlines, collaborate on DHHS goals/benchmarks, administer allotments and position authorizations, and may review long‑vacant positions.
