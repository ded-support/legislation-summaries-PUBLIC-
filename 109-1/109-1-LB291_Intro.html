Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it two explicit roles. Under the Aid to Municipalities Act the Department will receive and process municipal grant applications (on forms it prescribes), consider them in receipt order, and may approve grants within available appropriations; no individual grant may exceed $5 million. Amendments to section 13-522 require the Auditor to notify the Department (for cities and villages) of budget noncompliance; the State Treasurer and, where applicable, the Department must suspend distribution of state aid to the noncomplying municipal unit until compliance. Suspended funds are held six months and may be forfeited and redistributed if noncompliance continues.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Department of Economic Development as the defined "Department" administering the Aid to Municipalities Act. Municipalities apply to the Department on a Department-prescribed form (request amount and project description). The Department must process applications in receipt order and may approve grants only within available appropriations; individual grants are capped at $5 million. The Department also is to receive notifications of noncompliance (for cities or villages). No new dollar appropriations or specific budget allocations are included—approvals are constrained to existing/available appropriations.
