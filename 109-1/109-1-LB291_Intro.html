Summary

The bill designates "Department" to mean the Department of Economic Development and places administration of the new Aid to Municipalities Act with that Department. Municipalities apply to the Department on a prescribed form; the Department reviews applications in receipt order and may approve grants within available appropriations (individual grants capped at $5 million). The Act makes state aid to municipalities part of the defined "state aid." Under amended section 13-522, if a city or village is found noncompliant with budget limits, the Auditor must notify the Department (and State Treasurer), and the Department, with the Treasurer, shall suspend distribution of state aid to the unit until compliance; suspended funds are held six months then forfeited and redistributed if noncompliance persists.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The addition establishes the "Aid to Municipalities Act" and designates the Department of Economic Development as the administering "Department." The Department will prescribe application forms, accept municipal grant applications, review them in the order received, and may approve grants up to $5 million each, subject to available appropriations. It also will receive notifications of governmental-unit noncompliance (alongside the State Treasurer). No specific new appropriation amounts or budget line-item allocations are provided—approval authority is limited only by available appropriations and the $5 million per-grant cap.
