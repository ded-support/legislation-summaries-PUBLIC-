Summary

LB291 creates the Aid to Municipalities Act and designates the "Department" as the Department of Economic Development. Municipalities apply to the Department on a prescribed form for grants to fund specified infrastructure projects. The Department reviews applications in receipt order and may approve awards within available appropriations; no single grant may exceed $5 million. The bill amends section 13-522 to require the Auditor to notify the State Treasurer and, for cities or villages, the Department of Economic Development of budget noncompliance. The State Treasurer and, if applicable, the Department must then suspend distribution of state aid to the noncomplying unit; suspended funds are held six months and forfeited and redistributed if compliance is not achieved.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Aid to Municipalities Act," defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development, and assigns the Department responsibility for administering municipal infrastructure grants. Municipal applications must state the amount requested and describe the project. The Department will consider applications in receipt order and may approve grants only within available appropriations; individual grants are capped at $5 million. The bill also requires notification of noncompliance to the State Treasurer and, where the unit is a city or village, to the Department of Economic Development. No new appropriation amounts are specified.
