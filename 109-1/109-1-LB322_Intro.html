Summary

LB322 explicitly names agency employees in enhanced-offense definitions: it treats employees of the Department of Correctional Services as public safety officers and specifically includes employees of DOCS-operated secure youth confinement facilities when the offender is committed there. It also treats Department of Health and Human Services employees as public safety officers only where the offender is committed under the Sex Offender Commitment Act. The habitual‑criminal mandatory minimum terms are to be served in a Department of Correctional Services adult correctional facility. The bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (focus: Department of Correctional Services & Department of Health and Human Services)

- Employees of the Department of Correctional Services are newly listed as "public safety officers" and therefore covered by the bill's enhanced assault offenses and protections.

- Employees of the Department of Health and Human Services are added as "public safety officers" only where the offender is committed as a dangerous sex offender under the Sex Offender Commitment Act.

- The amendments extend assault definitions/protections (including striking with bodily fluids and related degrees of assault) to these employees where applicable.

- No language in the provided additions establishes or changes any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency.
