Summary

• The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue.

• The Department of Revenue is the licensing and regulatory authority for fantasy contest operators: it receives applications and a nonrefundable $5,000 application fee, conducts background investigations, issues or denies licenses, and must provide denial grounds and relied-upon documents as permitted by law.

• The department may adopt rules (except rules regulating individual contest mechanics or labeling contests as sports wagering), require geolocation and anti-fraud/money‑laundering controls, set compulsive‑gaming standards, and coordinate investigations with law enforcement (including provision of account‑level data).

• The Department will administer the newly created Fantasy Sports Consumer Protection Fund; all license and application fees collected by the Department are credited to that fund.

• Civil penalties and monies collected by the Department are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and makes the department the primary regulator for fantasy contests. Key department powers and duties: adopt rules (including compulsive/problem gaming standards), require geolocation technology, mandate fraud/money‑laundering and unauthorized‑account controls, conduct background investigations (including criminal history checks and resubmissions), license or deny operators (with written grounds), cooperate with law enforcement, and incorporate fantasy self‑exclusion into existing programs. The department may not regulate individual contest rules, platform design, or label contests as sports wagering. Funding: establishes the Fantasy Sports Consumer Protection Fund, administered by the department, to receive and use all license and application fees; funds may be invested by the state investment officer. Civil penalties collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution.
