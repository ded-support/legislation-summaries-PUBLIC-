Summary

LB708 (Ibach) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-1504, which defines the six community college areas for the Nebraska community college system. The bill revises county boundary assignments among those areas — notably adding Dawson County to the Mid‑Plains Community College Area (while retaining Red Willow) — and retains existing provisions allocating specific Cherry and Boone County precincts to Western, Northeast, and Central areas. The bill repeals the original section and replaces it with the amended boundaries. No state agency other than the community college system is referenced, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
