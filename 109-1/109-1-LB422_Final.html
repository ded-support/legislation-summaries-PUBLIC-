Summary

LB422 amends the Nebraska Uniform Real Property Transfer on Death Act to require transfer-on-death deeds to include a warning that the Department of Health and Human Services may require revocation of the deed by the transferor, the transferor's spouse, or both, in order to qualify or remain qualified for Medicaid assistance. The bill also notes that designated beneficiaries may be personally liable to account for Medicaid reimbursement to the extent of the property's value. No provisions in the bill make changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language requires transfer-on-death deeds executed after the act’s effective date to include a warning that property insurance may expire 30 days after the transferor’s death unless the beneficiary is added as an insured. It specifies coverage ends at the earlier of the policy period’s normal end, the beneficiary obtaining other coverage, or 30 days after death only if the premium for days beyond the policy period is paid. No provisions affect the Department of Health and Human Services and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
