Summary

LB467 amends the Utilization Review Act to require health carriers and utilization review agents, by January 1, 2028, to implement and maintain a prior-authorization application programming interface conforming to federal regulation 45 C.F.R. 156.223(b) (as of Jan. 1, 2025). Prior-authorization requests for drugs are excluded; "drug" is defined by 45 C.F.R. 156.221(b)(1)(v) as of Jan. 1, 2025. The bill updates the Act's citation to include this new section and repeals the original section 44-5416. It references federal Code of Federal Regulations standards (45 C.F.R.), under HHS/CMS regulatory authority. No state agency-specific duties, appropriations, or funding changes are contained in the bill.
