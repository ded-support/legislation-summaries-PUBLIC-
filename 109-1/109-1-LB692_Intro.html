Summary

LB692 amends the School District Property Tax Limitation Act. It requires the department to compute each school district’s annual property tax request authority using a revised formula: prior-year property tax request (net of any amount that exceeded prior authority) plus prior-year non‑property‑tax revenue (excluding special building fund investment income), increased by the district’s base growth percentage, then reduced by current-year non‑property‑tax revenue (with June 1 data‑gaps defaulting to prior‑year amounts). The department must certify the result to each school board. The bill also allows districts to carry forward unused property tax request authority but expressly excludes carryforward of increases attributable to base growth percentage changes; the department must account for unused authority to school boards. Original sections are repealed. No appropriations or funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language applies to school districts: when calculating the current property tax request it requires subtracting any prior-year amount by which the district’s property tax request exceeded its statutory authority under section 79‑3405. It explicitly excludes from that subtraction any increases in request authority that resulted from increases to the district’s base growth percentage under section 79‑3405. No state agencies or departments are named, and no direct appropriations or budgetary transfers are created—this is a technical adjustment to how districts’ property tax requests are calculated.
