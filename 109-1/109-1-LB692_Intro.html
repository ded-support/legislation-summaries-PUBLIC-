Summary

LB692 amends the School District Property Tax Limitation Act by specifying administrative duties for "the department" (the agency responsible for calculating school district tax authority). The department must annually calculate and certify each district's property tax request authority using a multi-step formula: adjust prior-year property tax request for any prior overages, add prior-year non-property-tax revenue minus prior-year special building fund investment income, apply the district's base growth percentage, then subtract current-year non-property-tax revenue (with any categories lacking June 1 data deemed equal to the prior year) and correct known documentation errors. The bill also limits what unused request authority may be carried forward—excluding increases resulting from base growth percentage increases under §79-3405—and requires the department to calculate and report unused authority to each school board. Original sections are repealed. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a deduction to the property tax calculation for school districts: subtract any amount by which the prior year's property tax request exceeded the district's property tax request authority under section 79‑3405. Excludes from that deduction any increases in property tax request authority that resulted from increases to the district's base growth percentage under section 79‑3405. No specific state agency or funding appropriation is named.
