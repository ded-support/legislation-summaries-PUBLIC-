Summary

LB41A appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services to implement Legislative Bill 41 (2025), designated to Programs 344 and 348.

- Program 344: FY2025‑26 — $8,798 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund and $19,223 federal; FY2026‑27 — $7,655 Cash and $16,305 federal.

- Program 348: FY2025‑26 — $69,777 Cash and $85,802 federal; FY2026‑27 — $72,860 Cash and $86,780 federal.

All specified amounts are earmarked for state aid only. Appropriated funds may not be used for permanent or temporary salaries or per diems for state employees.
