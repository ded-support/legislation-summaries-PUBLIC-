Summary

LB26 (2025) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-929.01 to expand the definition of "health care professional" for purposes of sections 28-929, 28-929.02, 28-930, 28-931, and 28-931.01. The bill retains the Emergency Medical Services classifications as defined under the Emergency Medical Services Practice Act and references hospital and health clinic definitions in §§71-419 and 71-416. It adds: (a) physicians or other licensed/certified/registered practitioners who practice at a hospital or health clinic, and (b) any other employee of a hospital or health clinic, thereby bringing those hospital/clinic employees within the referenced assault-related statutes. The original §28-929.01 is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection (b) stating that "Any other employee of a hospital or health clinic" is covered. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
