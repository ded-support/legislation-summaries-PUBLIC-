Summary

LB594 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,179.01 to prohibit physically holding or otherwise using a handheld wireless communication device while operating a motor vehicle in motion. Explicit exemptions cover persons performing official duties as law enforcement officers, firefighters, ambulance drivers, or emergency medical technicians; employees/contractors of utility service providers responding to utility emergencies; and drivers in emergency situations communicating with emergency services. The bill allows device use without touching the device or to initiate a function with a single touch. Violations are traffic infractions, assessed license points under §60-4,182, and fined $200 (first), $300 (second), $500 (third+). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original section is repealed.

Adds exceptions to the ban on physically holding or supporting a handheld wireless device while driving: employees or contractors of utility service providers responding to utility emergencies; persons communicating or attempting to communicate with emergency services; and use when the device is not touched or when initiating a function with a single touch.
