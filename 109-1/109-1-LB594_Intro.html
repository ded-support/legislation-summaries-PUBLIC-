Summary

LB594 (introduced by Rep. Prokop; referred to Transportation and Telecommunications) amends §60-6,179.01 to prohibit the use of handheld wireless communication devices while operating a motor vehicle. Explicit exemptions are provided for law enforcement officers, firefighters, ambulance drivers, emergency medical technicians performing official duties, and employees/contractors of utility service providers responding to utility emergencies; an emergency driver communicating with emergency services is also exempt. The bill clarifies device definitions and allows functions initiated without touching the device or with a single touch. Violations are traffic infractions with license points assessed per §60-4,182 and fines of $200/$300/$500 (first/second/third+ offenses). No funding or appropriations are included; the original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds narrow exemptions to the handheld‑device-while-driving prohibition: employees or contractors of a utility service provider responding to a utility emergency, and persons communicating or attempting to communicate with emergency services, are exempt. It also permits using a handheld wireless device while driving if used without touching it or to initiate a function with a single touch. No specific government agencies or departments are named beyond generic "utility service provider" and "emergency services," and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
