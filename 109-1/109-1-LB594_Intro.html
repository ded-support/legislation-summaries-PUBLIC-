Summary

LB594 amends §60‑6,179.01 regarding handheld wireless device use while driving. Exemptions explicitly apply to personnel performing official duties: law enforcement officers, firefighters, ambulance drivers, and emergency medical technicians; employees or contractors of utility service providers responding to utility emergencies; and drivers communicating with emergency services during an emergency. The section does not apply to commercial motor vehicle operators where §60‑6,179.02 governs. Violations remain traffic infractions with points assessed on the operator’s license pursuant to §60‑4,182 and fines of $200 (first), $300 (second), and $500 (third+). No state agency appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds exceptions to the ban on physically holding or supporting a handheld wireless device while driving: exempts employees or contractors of utility service providers responding to utility emergencies and persons communicating or attempting to communicate with emergency services. Also clarifies the prohibition does not apply when the device is used without touching it or to initiate a function with a single touch. No government agencies or departments are named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
