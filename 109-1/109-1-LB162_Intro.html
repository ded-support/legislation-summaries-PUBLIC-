Summary

LB162 creates the Child Care Safety and Security Act and establishes the Child Care Safety and Security Fund, to be administered by the Nebraska State Department of Education (SDE) with monies transferred by the Legislature and investable by the state investment officer under existing state investment acts. SDE, subject to available appropriations, will competitively award grants to educational service units to fund "designees" (e.g., school districts, cities/villages, local health departments, law enforcement, early childhood support agencies, local emergency management agencies) via memoranda of understanding. Grants support emergency response notification systems, safety/reunification materials, and training; funding for each listed designee activity is capped at $1,000 per year. SDE must report annually to the Clerk of the Legislature; the State Board of Education may adopt rules.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Child Care Safety and Security Fund, administered by the State Department of Education (SDE) with money transferred by the Legislature and investable balances managed by the state investment officer under Nebraska investment law. Subject to available appropriations, SDE shall competitively award grants to educational service units (ESUs) on behalf of "designees" (e.g., public school districts, cities/villages, local public health departments, law enforcement, early childhood support agencies, local emergency management agencies) for emergency notification systems, safety/reunification materials, and training. ESUs/designees must enter MOUs; certain designee payments are capped at $1,000 per year per listed function. SDE must file an annual electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature by Dec. 1. The State Board of Education may adopt rules to implement the Act.
