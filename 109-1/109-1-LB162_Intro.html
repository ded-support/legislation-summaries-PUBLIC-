Summary

LB162 (Child Care Safety and Security Act) establishes a Child Care Safety and Security Fund administered by the State Department of Education. The fund consists of money transferred by the Legislature and is investable by the state investment officer under existing state investment law. Subject to available appropriations, the department competitively awards grants to educational service units (ESUs) to fund designees—public school districts, cities/villages, local public health departments, law enforcement, early childhood support agencies, or local emergency management agencies—via memoranda of understanding. Grants support emergency response notification systems, safety/reunification materials, and training; funding to a designee for each listed function is capped at $1,000 per year. ESUs must submit applications and approved security/reunification plans. The State Board of Education may adopt rules. The department must file an annual electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Child Care Safety and Security Fund, to be administered by the Nebraska State Department of Education (SDE) and funded by transfers from the Legislature; fund assets may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment laws. SDE, subject to available appropriations, will competitively award grants to educational service units (ESUs) on behalf of designees (public school districts, cities/villages, local public health departments, law enforcement, early childhood support agencies, or local emergency management agencies) for emergency notification systems, training, and reunification materials. Grants to designees for administering notification systems, distributing materials, and providing training are each capped at $1,000 per year. SDE must file an annual electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature; the State Board of Education may adopt implementing rules.
