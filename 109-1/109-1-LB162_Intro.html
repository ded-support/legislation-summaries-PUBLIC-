Summary

Creates the Child Care Safety and Security Act and the Child Care Safety and Security Fund, administered by the State Department of Education (SDE). The fund consists of money transferred by the Legislature and is invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment law. Subject to available appropriations, SDE competitively awards grants to educational service units to fund designated public entities (designees—e.g., school districts, cities/villages, local public health, law enforcement, early childhood support agencies, local emergency management) for emergency response notification systems, training, and safety/reunification materials. Grant limits: funding for each designee activity (notification system, distribution of materials, training) shall not exceed $1,000 per year. Educational service units must use memoranda of understanding with designees. SDE must submit an annual electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature. The State Board of Education may adopt rules to implement the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Child Care Safety and Security Fund, administered by the State Department of Education (SDE) and funded by money transferred by the Legislature; the state investment officer will invest fund balances under existing state investment law. Subject to appropriations, SDE competitively awards grants to educational service units on behalf of "designees" (public school districts, cities/villages, local public health, law enforcement, early childhood support agencies, or local emergency management agencies) for emergency notification systems, training, and reunification/safety materials. Educational service units must enter MOUs with designees to qualify. Grant limits: up to $1,000 per designee per listed activity (notification administration, materials distribution, and training). SDE must file an annual December 1 electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature (aggregate grant usage and denials); the State Board of Education may adopt rules to implement the Act.
