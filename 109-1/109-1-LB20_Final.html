Summary

LB20 defines "agricultural self-generation facility" (≤100 kW, on same premises as the owner's electric account, using methane, wind, solar, biomass, hydropower, or geothermal, no backfeed device, not net‑metered, used for agricultural/horticultural purposes) and "local distribution utility" (owner/operator of distribution equipment, including public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, electric cooperatives, and municipal electric systems). The bill prohibits a local distribution utility from denying electric service to a customer with such a facility; requires the owner‑generator to notify the utility before installation; and subjects owner‑generators to the utility’s interconnection requirements, safety standards, rates, charges, and service agreement terms. Utilities may set rates/fees to fully recover costs. No changes to state agency budgets, appropriations, or funding are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "agricultural self-generation facility" (≤100 kW, on-site, owner-controlled, uses methane/wind/solar/biomass/hydro/geothermal, prevents backfeed, not for net metering) and clarifies "local distribution utility" to include public power districts, public power and irrigation districts, electric cooperatives, and municipal electric systems. Prohibits those utilities from denying service to customers with such facilities. Owner-generators must notify the local distribution utility and comply with the utility's interconnection, safety, rate/charge, and service-agreement requirements; utilities may set rates/fees to fully recover their costs. References statutes 77-1359 and 70-2002. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
