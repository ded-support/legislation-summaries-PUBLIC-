Summary

LB9 expands regulatory, enforcement, and tax authority primarily for the Nebraska Tax Commissioner, with roles for the Attorney General, Governor, State Treasurer, and peace officers. Key agency changes:

- Tax Commissioner: new reporting and audit authorities for manufacturers, importers, stamping agents, and retailers; extended deficiency-notice periods (3 years, 6 years for fraud); rulemaking; license suspension/revocation; civil/administrative penalties (Tobacco Products Tax Act penalties up to $5,000); authority to seize/forfeit contraband cigarettes and tobacco products and direct destruction or law-enforcement use. Immunity provided for the commissioner, staff, and peace officers for seizures.

- Attorney General: may seek injunctions for violations and calculate escrow shortfalls.

- Governor: advises/consents to commissioner hires.

- Compensation and enforcement expenses: staff paid from tobacco-related revenue; administrative expenses may be paid by General Fund warrants but not to exceed 4% of collections. Effective January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions tying "nicotine analogue" and "alternative nicotine product" to section 28-1418.01 and imposes a tax on alternative nicotine products equal to 20% of the first owner's purchase price or the first owner's sale price. Empowers the Tax Commissioner to examine returns, notify deficiencies (generally within 3 years, 6 years for fraud/evasion/large omissions), and deliver notices by mail or personal service. Grants the Tax Commissioner authority to suspend or revoke licenses issued under sections 28-1420 to 28-1429 and to impose administrative penalties up to $5,000 after notice and hearing under the Administrative Procedure Act; appeals follow that Act. Declares untaxed tobacco products contraband and authorizes seizure by the Tax Commissioner, the commissioner's agents/employees, or any peace officer on the commissioner's direction; forfeited items are to be destroyed or used for law enforcement and then destroyed. No funding or appropriations changes.
