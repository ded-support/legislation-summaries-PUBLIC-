Summary

LB9 expands state regulation to cover nicotine analogues, alternative nicotine products, and electronic nicotine delivery systems. Key agencies and changes:

- Tax Commissioner / Cigarette Tax Division: broadened reporting, audit, deficiency-notice, license suspension/revocation, and enforcement authority (including seizure/forfeiture of contraband tobacco products without warrant). May hire inspectors/clerks with Governor's advice and consent and use AG, county attorneys, sheriffs, or other peace officers for enforcement.

- Attorney General: may seek injunctions, calculate escrow shortfalls, recover investigation costs and fees, and issue enforcement notices.

- Governor: sets compensation for Tax Commissioner employees.

- State Treasurer: receives remitted profits as ordered by courts.

- Federal interaction: requires manufacturers/importers to provide Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau or Customs reporting or 6103 consent.

Budget/funding: administrative enforcement expenses paid by warrants against the General Fund, capped at 4% of collections. New taxes imposed on ENDS and alternative nicotine products (specified per‑unit and percentage rates). Operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions linking "alternative nicotine product" and "nicotine analogue" to section 28-1418.01 and defines "nicotine analogue." Expands Tax Commissioner authority under the Tobacco Products Tax Act: examine returns, notify of deficiencies (notice deadlines—generally 3 years; 6 years for fraud/omission), and accept written extensions of the assessment period. Imposes a 20% tax on alternative nicotine products based on the first owner's purchase price or the first owner's sale price if manufactured by that owner. Grants enforcement powers: seize contraband tobacco without a warrant; direct return when no willful tax evasion; require forfeited items be destroyed or used for law enforcement then destroyed; assess seizure/destruction costs to the owner; suspend/revoke licenses (sections 28-1420–28-1429) after notice/hearing and impose administrative penalties up to $5,000. Appeals governed by the Administrative Procedure Act. No funding or appropriation changes.
