Summary

LB9 expands regulation and taxation authority primarily under the Tax Commissioner. Key agencies and fiscal provisions:

- Tax Commissioner/cigarette tax division: broadened enforcement powers (license suspension/revocation, administrative penalties), authority to employ inspectors/clerks with Governor's advice/consent, call Attorney General, county attorneys, sheriffs, or other peace officers for aid, and to seize contraband tobacco products without warrant. Notices of deficiency may be mailed or personally served; extended timeframes for certain violations apply. Operative Jan 1, 2026.

- Attorney General: authorized to act on behalf of the Tax Commissioner (directory enforcement, injunctions, recovery of investigation and litigation costs).

- State Treasurer: receives court-ordered remittances.

- Federal agencies: manufacturers/importers must submit federal excise reports or consent to federal disclosure to the Tax Commissioner (Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau, U.S. Treasury).

- Fiscal/appropriation changes: new taxes—electronic nicotine delivery systems ≤3 ml at $0.05/ml; >3 ml at 10% of purchase price; alternative nicotine products at 20% of purchase price; other tobacco products at 20%. Stamping-agent license fee $500 credited to the General Fund. Administrative/administration expenses payable by warrants against the General Fund, capped at 4% of collections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions linking "nicotine analogue" and "alternative nicotine product" to section 28-1418.01 and imposes a tax on alternative nicotine products equal to 20% of the purchase price paid by the first owner or the first owner's sale price. Expands enforcement authority for the Tax Commissioner: examine returns, issue deficiency notices (generally within 3 years, 6 years for fraud/omission), and extend mailing periods by agreement. Authorizes suspension/revocation of licenses under sections 28-1420 to 28-1429 and administrative penalties up to $5,000 after notice/hearing; appeals follow the Administrative Procedure Act. Declares untaxed tobacco products contraband, allowing seizure without warrant by the Tax Commissioner, agents, or peace officers; forfeited goods are to be destroyed or used for law enforcement and costs borne by the owner. No new appropriations or budget allocations specified.
