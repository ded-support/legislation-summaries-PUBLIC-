Summary

LB170 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2704.24 to remove the sales-tax exemptions for candy and soft drinks, redefines related terms (candy, soft drinks, prepared food, etc.), and takes effect October 1, 2025. The bill text cross-references the federal Food and Drug Administration for recommended cooking guidance and was referred to the Legislature’s Revenue Committee. No state agency (beyond these references) is explicitly assigned new duties in the text, and the bill contains no changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "candy" and "soft drinks." "Candy" is defined as sugar/honey or sweetener preparations combined with chocolate, fruits, nuts, etc., in bar/drop/piece form and excludes preparations containing flour or requiring refrigeration. "Soft drinks" are defined as nonalcoholic beverages with natural or artificial sweeteners and exclude beverages containing milk or milk substitutes or beverages with more than 50% vegetable or fruit juice. No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
