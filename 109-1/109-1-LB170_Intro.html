Summary

LB170 (Revenue Committee) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2704.24 to eliminate the sales and use tax exemptions for candy and soft drinks. The bill redefines key terms (including candy, soft drinks, food and food ingredients, prepared food, dietary supplement, and food sold through vending machines) and harmonizes related provisions. Prepared-food exceptions (e.g., bakery items, certain raw animal foods, manufacturing classifications) remain specified. The bill becomes operative October 1, 2025, and repeals the original section. No state agencies or departments are expressly named in the text, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitional language for "candy" and "soft drinks."

- Candy: preparations of sugar, honey, or other natural/artificial sweeteners combined with chocolate, fruits, nuts, or flavorings in bars, drops, or pieces; excludes products that contain flour or require refrigeration.

- Soft drinks: nonalcoholic beverages containing natural or artificial sweeteners; excludes beverages with milk/milk substitutes (soy, rice) or those that are more than 50% vegetable/fruit juice.

No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
