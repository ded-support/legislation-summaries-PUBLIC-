Summary

LB170 (2025) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2704.24 to eliminate the sales and use tax exemptions for candy and soft drinks and to redefine terms (e.g., candy, soft drinks, prepared food). The bill cites federal standards, referencing the Food and Drug Administration’s Food Code (chapter 3, part 401.11) and 21 C.F.R. §101.36 for dietary supplement labeling. It becomes operative October 1, 2025, and repeals the original section. Agencies/departments explicitly referenced: the federal Food and Drug Administration and the Nebraska Legislature (as enacting body). No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are contained in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and listing for candy and soft drinks:

- Defines "candy" as sugar/honey/sweetener preparations with chocolate, fruits, nuts, etc., in bar/drop/piece form; excludes items containing flour or requiring refrigeration.

- Inserts "candy, soft drinks" into the referenced enumeration.

- Defines "soft drinks" as nonalcoholic beverages with natural or artificial sweeteners; excludes beverages containing milk or milk substitutes or those with greater than 50% vegetable/fruit juice by volume.

No government agencies, departments, or any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
