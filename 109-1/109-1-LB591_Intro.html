Summary

LB591 amends the Nebraska Installment Loan Act (sections 45-1003 and 45-1004). It clarifies licensing authority tied to "the department" (the licensing department) by expanding when a license is required: nonfinancial persons who market, own, hold, acquire, service, or otherwise engage in loan activity with an installment-loan borrower must be licensed, including where a financial institution originally made the loan. The bill exempts affiliates whose in-state activities are limited solely to securitization provided servicing rights remain with the licensee or are assigned to a financial institution, licensee, or permittee. It defines affiliate and control (ownership of 25% of voting shares or control of a majority of director elections). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are contained.
