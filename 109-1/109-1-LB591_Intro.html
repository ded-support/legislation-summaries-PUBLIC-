Summary

LB591 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§45‑1003 and 45‑1004 to expand licensing requirements under the Nebraska Installment Loan Act. It requires any person (not a financial institution) who, at or after the time a loan is made by a financial institution, markets, owns, holds, acquires or services that loan to obtain a license. It also clarifies that a license from the department is required to make installment loans or to service, hold or acquire ownership interests in such loans or otherwise engage with borrowers. The bill exempts affiliates whose in‑state activities are limited solely to securitization where servicing rights remain with the licensee or transfer to a financial institution, licensee, or permittee, and defines “affiliate” and “control.” No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
