Summary

Bill LB591 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§45-1003 and 45-1004 to revise licensing under the Nebraska Installment Loan Act. It clarifies that the department (the state licensing authority) must issue licenses for persons that, if not financial institutions, market, own, hold, acquire or service installment loans made by financial institutions. It expands licensing to persons who service, hold or acquire ownership interests in, or otherwise engage in loan activity with, installment-loan borrowers. It exempts affiliates of a licensee from licensure when their in‑state activities are limited solely to securitization and servicing rights remain with (or are transferred to) the licensee, a financial institution, or a permittee. The bill defines “affiliate” and “control” (25% voting interest or control of director elections) and repeals the original sections. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
