Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) is designated to administer a state social services program and may contract with other social agencies. DHHS must provide child care assistance to families with incomes from 130% to 400% of the federal poverty level beginning October 1, 2026, and implement eligibility rules (including a 10% earned-income disregard after 12 continuous months and sliding-scale cost-sharing). Beginning October 1, 2025, DHHS must allow assistance when an applicant is a primary caregiver and permit enrollment outside a household member’s program, and shall adopt related rules and regulations. DHHS will set annual fixed-rate reimbursement schedules (effective October 1 each year). Funding: a 0.52% payroll tax (0.39% employer / 0.13% employee) shall fund the program; General Funds are prohibited for costs arising from the income-eligibility changes beginning October 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS will administer expanded social services and a child-care subsidy program. Key DHHS duties: contract for services, adopt rules, set an annual fixed rate schedule (effective Oct 1 each year) for child-care reimbursements (cannot exceed providers' private rates; may set separate rates for infants or children with special needs). Effective Oct 1, 2026, DHHS must provide child-care assistance to families with incomes 130%–400% of FPL; ongoing eligibility allows a 10% gross earned income disregard after 12 continuous months and at each redetermination, and assistance is cost-shared on a sliding scale with family contributions capped at transitional child-care rates (as of Jan 1, 2025). Beginning Oct 1, 2025, DHHS must allow reasonable workplace accommodations or permit assistance when an eligible household member is a program employee/primary caregiver, and permit enrollment in alternate programs. Funding: a 0.52% payroll tax (employer 0.39%, employee 0.13%) will fund the program; General Funds are prohibited from covering state costs (including administration) arising from the income-eligibility changes beginning Oct 1, 2026.
