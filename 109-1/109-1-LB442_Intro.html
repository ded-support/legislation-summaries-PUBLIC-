Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) is directed to administer the state social services program and may contract for purchase of services at prevailing or departmental cost rates; statutory maximums for aid to dependent children apply only to public assistance grants, not social services. DHHS must establish and administer a state child care subsidy: providing assistance to families with incomes 130%–400% of FPL beginning October 1, 2026; determining eligibility (including a 10% earned‑income disregard after 12 continuous months and use of a sliding cost‑share plan with family contributions capped at existing transitional rates); adopting rules and a fixed annual reimbursement rate schedule (effective each October 1 and revised yearly); and prioritizing highest assistance for qualified apprentices. Funding: a 0.52% payroll tax (0.39% employer / 0.13% employee) will fund the program; General Funds may not be used for related state costs, including administration, beginning October 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must expand and administer state social services to provide child care assistance to families with incomes from 130%–400% of FPL beginning Oct. 1, 2026, and adopt rules to implement the changes. DHHS will enforce Child Care Licensing Act criminal‑history checks for participating providers, apply a 10% gross‑earned‑income disregard after 12 continuous months, use a sliding cost‑share with family contributions capped at transitional program rates (as of Jan. 1, 2025), and create reasonable‑accommodation and enrollment exceptions effective Oct. 1, 2025. DHHS will set an annual fixed reimbursement rate schedule (effective Oct. 1 each year), with separate rates allowed for infants and children with special needs, not exceeding providers’ private rates. Funding: the program is financed by a 0.52% payroll tax (0.39% employer / 0.13% employee); General Funds cannot be used for costs (including administration) from the eligibility changes beginning Oct. 1, 2026. The program must prioritize qualified apprentices for highest assistance.
