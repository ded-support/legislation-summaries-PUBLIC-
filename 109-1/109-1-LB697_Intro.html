Summary

LB697 amends the Pharmacy Practice Act. Key agency-related changes:

- Board of Pharmacy: membership specified as eight members — five actively practicing pharmacists (including at least one hospital pharmacist), one pharmacy technician, and two public members.

- Compounding: requires compliance with USP chapters 795 and 797 as of Jan 1, 2023; permits compounding for practitioner-ordered care, research/teaching, or office use by outsourcing facilities operating under 21 U.S.C. §353b or Neb. §71-470; restricts compounding of FDA-withdrawn products, essentially duplicate approved drugs (unless shortage or allergy), or products the FDA/board prohibit.

- Delegated dispensing at public health clinics: clarifies who may write prescriptions (including practitioners affiliated under a delegated dispensing permit), limits refills to contraceptives, mandates specific labeling and documentation, and requires documented daily availability of the delegating or on-call pharmacist.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that two members must be from an outsourcing facility operating under federal law (21 U.S.C. §353b) or Nebraska statute (section 71-470), or be a Nebraska‑licensed practitioner affiliated with a public health clinic under a delegated dispensing permit. No state agencies or departments beyond those referenced by statute are newly created or assigned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
