Summary

LB697 amends the Pharmacy Practice Act. Key agency impacts:

- Nebraska Board of Pharmacy: membership set at eight (five actively practicing pharmacists, including at least one hospital pharmacist; one pharmacy technician; two public members).

- Compounding oversight: persons authorized to compound must follow USP/NF chapters 795 and 797 as of Jan 1, 2023. Outsourcing facilities operating under 21 U.S.C. 353b or section 71-470 may compound for office use only. Hospital compounding may occur across hospitals in the same health system or formal network. Reconstitution, beyond-use dating, and prohibited compounded drugs (FDA-withdrawn, copies except in shortage/allergy, or board/FDA-designated noncompoundable) are specified.

- Public health clinics: delegated dispensing permits clarified — prescriptions may be by onsite practitioners or affiliated practitioners under the permit; refills limited to contraceptives; labeling, formulary, and mandatory availability/documentation of a delegating or on-call pharmacist required.

No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that two members may be representatives of an outsourcing facility operating under federal law 21 U.S.C. 353b (FDA-regulated compounding outsourcing facilities) or under Nebraska statute section 71-470, or practitioners licensed in Nebraska who work with public health clinics under a delegated dispensing permit. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
