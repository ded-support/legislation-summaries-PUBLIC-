Summary

- Department of Economic Development (DED) shall use the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to finance community affordable housing projects, the new Statewide Housing Assistance Program, and departmental expenses "as appropriated" by the Legislature.

- DED sets matching requirements for for‑profit recipients (minimum 10%), must attempt to allocate not less than 30% of trust‑fund monies to each congressional district, announce a ≥90‑day application period, develop a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest‑income projects and homebuyers, and may recapture unused awards.

- The Statewide Housing Assistance Program will be administered by a nonprofit contracted by DED, provides downpayment loans (max $30,000) secured by liens, and operates under DED rules.

- DED must report annually detailed fund receipts (including amounts from section 76‑903) and its administrative budget and planned distributions.

- The Tax Commissioner/Department of Revenue designs documentary stamp tax stamps and issues declaratory rulings; the department may not overturn county assessor sale‑qualification determinations absent its own review.

- Documentary stamp tax allocation: of $2.55 per $1,000, $0.50 remains with the register of deeds; $1.25 is credited to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund, $0.25 to the Site and Building Development Fund, $0.25 to the Homeless Shelter Assistance Trust Fund, and $0.30 to the Behavioral Health Services Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Statewide Housing Assistance Program administered by a nonprofit contracted by the Department of Economic Development. The program provides statewide downpayment assistance (up to $30,000 per homebuyer) as a repayable loan secured by a lien; repayment is due on transfer of title. Eligibility, application and other rules are set by the department; assistance is limited to owner‑occupied primary residences. Priority for awards is given to lowest‑income buyers and buyers in designated enterprise zones, federal opportunity zones, or areas declared extremely blighted. No Department of Revenue duties, funding lines, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the provided text.
