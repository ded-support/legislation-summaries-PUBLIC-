Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED): DED may use the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to finance community affordable housing projects, the new Statewide Housing Assistance Program (SHAP), and departmental expenses as appropriated. DED must attempt to allocate at least 30% of available funds to each congressional district, announce ≥90‑day application periods for projects and homebuyers, adopt a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest‑income projects and homebuyers, and may contract a nonprofit to administer SHAP. SHAP provides liened, repayable downpayment loans up to $30,000 per buyer. DED must report recipients, funding amounts, and its administration budget in its annual status report.

Department of Revenue: The documentary stamp tax is $2.55 per $1,000; registers retain $0.50 and remit the remainder, which the State Treasurer credits $1.25 to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund, $0.25 to the Site and Building Development Fund, $0.25 to the Homeless Shelter Assistance Trust Fund, and $0.30 to the Behavioral Health Services Fund. The department shall not overturn county assessor sale‑qualification determinations without review.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Statewide Housing Assistance Program to be administered by a nonprofit contracted by the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED will adopt rules governing applicant eligibility. The program provides downpayment assistance as a loan (secured by a lien, repayable on title transfer), limited to $30,000 per homebuyer, for primary residences only. Priority for assistance is given to lowest‑income buyers and those in designated enterprise zones, federal opportunity zones, or areas declared extremely blighted. No changes to or actions by the Department of Revenue are specified, and the text contains no new appropriations, funding sources, or budget allocations.
