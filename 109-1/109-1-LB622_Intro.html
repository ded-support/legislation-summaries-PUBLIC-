Summary

- Department of Economic Development (DED): DED shall use the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to finance loans, grants, credit enhancements, the new Statewide Housing Assistance Program, and departmental expenses as appropriated by the Legislature (Sec. 58-705). DED must allocate at least 30% of available annual funds to each congressional district, announce a ≥90-day application period, develop a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest‑income projects and homebuyers, may contract for services or provide funds to governmental entities, and may recapture unused awards (Secs. 58-708, 58-706). The Statewide Housing Assistance Program will be administered by a nonprofit contracted by DED, providing lien‑secured downpayment loans up to $30,000 (Sec. 6). DED must report annually its budget for administration and planned fund distribution, including amounts for projects and administrative purposes (Sec. 58-711).

- Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner): The Tax Commissioner designs documentary stamp tax stamps and issues declaratory rulings; registers collect the tax. From each $2.55 collected, $0.50 stays with the county and $1.25 is credited to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund (with specified credits to other funds); the Department will not overturn county assessor sale determinations without its own review (Secs. 76-903, 77-1327).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Statewide Housing Assistance Program to be administered by a nonprofit contracted by the Department of Economic Development. The program provides statewide downpayment assistance (limited to $30,000 per homebuyer), available to applicants defined in department rules. Assistance must be used for a primary residence, is structured as a loan secured by a lien, and is repaid on transfer of title. Priority for trust-fund assistance is given to lowest-income homebuyers located in designated enterprise zones, federal opportunity zones, and areas declared extremely blighted under section 18-2101.02. No changes to the Department of Revenue or explicit appropriations/funding amounts are included.
