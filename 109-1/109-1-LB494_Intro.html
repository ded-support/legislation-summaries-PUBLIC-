Summary

LB494 requires the State Treasurer to transfer $750 million from the General Fund to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in fiscal year 2024‑25 "on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services." This grants the DAS budget division’s budget administrator authority over the timing and phasing of that FY2024‑25 transfer.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No new duties, appropriations, or budget allocations are imposed on the Department of Administrative Services. The added language establishes legislative intent (beginning FY 2025-26) to transfer from the General Fund whatever amount is needed to fund the property tax relief required by §77-7305(1)(a) into the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund. Relief will be delivered as property tax credits on tax statements and credited against school district taxes. Money transferred under §77-4602, if any, is added to the minimum required transfer. The Tax Commissioner/State Treasurer provisions govern certification and transfers to the Cash Reserve Fund but do not alter DAS funding or responsibilities.
