Summary

The bill directs the State Treasurer to transfer $750 million from the General Fund to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in fiscal year 2024‑25, with the timing and installment amounts to be set by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions (Department of Administrative Services: none specified)

- New certification/transfer rule: If one-year/ two-year revenue calculations meet specified positive/negative conditions, the Tax Commissioner must certify a calculated positive amount to the State Treasurer, who will transfer that amount to the Cash Reserve Fund.

- Property tax relief funding (effective FY2025-26): The Legislature intends that the General Fund will transfer whatever amount is necessary to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund to provide the property tax credits required by section 77-7305. Credits will appear on property tax statements and offset school district taxes. The total relief equals a defined minimum amount, plus any additional transfers to the fund made under section 77-4602 for that tax year.

No language in the added text references the Department of Administrative Services or directs any appropriations or budget changes to that agency.
