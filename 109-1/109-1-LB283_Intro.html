Summary

LB283 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-901 (Medical Assistance Act) to require the Department of Health and Human Services to file specified state plan amendments. The bill directs DHHS to take those filing actions as prescribed and repeals the original section. No provisions in the bill reference changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department.
