Summary

The bill enacts administrative, regulatory, and fiscal changes affecting multiple state agencies. Key fiscal actions: the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) may certify unpaid encumbrances to its accounting division; DAS’s budget division may time/direct statutory transfers (including specified transfers from the Water Resources and Water Sustainability funds); investment earnings from the Jobs and Economic Development Initiative Fund are diverted to the General Fund (beginning Oct. 1, 2024) with up to $20 million made available for site selection/feasibility/flood‑mitigation (Economic Development). Motor‑fuel tax receipts are allocated 66% to the Highway Cash Fund for the Department of Transportation, 17% to counties, 17% to municipalities. The Department of Revenue receives a new Litter Fee Collection Fund and retains administration of ethanol/biodiesel credit rules. DHHS: surety forfeitures and related receipts credit the DHHS Cash Fund for remediation uses; DHHS gains several ex officio board/hearing roles. DAS procurement preferences, personnel‑system exemptions for Correctional Services/DHHS/Veterans’ Affairs, and expanded pesticide enforcement authorities at Agriculture are also established.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (effective July 1, 2025):

- Department of Administrative Services: Responsible for accounting/processing certified valid encumbrances for any unpaid Department of Natural Resources obligations as of June 30, 2025.

- Department of Agriculture; Banking and Finance; Economic Development; Insurance; Labor; Correctional Services; Health and Human Services; Motor Vehicles; Revenue; Transportation; Veterans' Affairs; Military Department: No substantive changes, funding shifts, or new duties for these agencies appear in the added text.

- Budget/appropriations: All appropriations and salary limits previously enacted for Agency No. 29 (Department of Natural Resources) are nullified and those amounts are appropriated to Agency No. 84 (Department of Water, Energy, and Environment).

- Other: DNR positions and employees transferring to the new department retain personnel rights and continuous service; a Chief Water Officer role is created.
