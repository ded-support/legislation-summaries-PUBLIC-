Summary

LB317 reallocates duties, funds, and statutory roles among state agencies. It transfers appropriations and salary limits from the Department of Natural Resources to the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment, treats DNR staff as transferred, and requires the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) to certify unpaid DNR obligations as valid encumbrances. DAS’s budget administrator directs multiple statutory fund transfers (notably $1,000,000 from the Water Resources Cash Fund to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund by June 30, 2025 and other Water Sustainability Fund transfers) and a $200,000 transfer from the Clean‑burning Motor Fuel Development Fund; DAS also manages warrants, purchasing preferences, rebate review, and a public contract database. The Department of Agriculture gains expanded pesticide regulatory and enforcement authority (fines up to $5,000) and reimbursement powers from the Pesticide Administrative Cash Fund, hosts the Riparian Vegetation Task Force, and oversees certain weights‑and‑measures approvals. DHHS is added to several boards/committees, may participate in groundwater hearings, and may receive forfeited sureties for site care. DOT, DED, and Revenue are named stakeholders for flood/infrastructure planning; Revenue administers ethanol tax credits and a new Litter Fee Collection Fund. Banking & Finance, Insurance, Labor, Motor Vehicles, Veterans’ Affairs, Correctional Services, and the Military Department are added as official recipients of session law distributions and receive limited statutory references (appointments/exemptions).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a July 1, 2025 merger of the Department of Natural Resources into the Department of Environment and Energy, renaming it the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment and creating a Governor‑appointed, Legislature‑confirmed Chief Water Officer (reports to the Director). DNR positions transfer to the new department with continuous service and retained personnel/bargaining rights; existing DNR rules remain effective. Any appropriations and salary limits previously enacted for Agency No. 29 (DNR) are voided and reappropriated to Agency No. 84 (Department of Water, Energy, and Environment). Outstanding DNR financial obligations as of June 30, 2025, must be certified to the accounting division of the Department of Administrative Services for subsequent processing. No other listed agencies’ budgets or authorities are altered by this text.
