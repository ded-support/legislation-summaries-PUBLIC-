Summary

The bill assigns program and fiscal duties across agencies. Department of Economic Development (DED) establishes a Community Development Assistance Act to certify community betterment programs, set per‑program tax‑credit amounts, approve/disapprove proposals within 45 days and transmit certifications to the Tax Commissioner; total annual credits are capped at $350,000. DED also reviews Urban Redevelopment Act applications, may authorize up to $8 million in incentives, refunds most of the $500 fee if not approved, and must provide application materials to the Department of Revenue (DOR).

Department of Revenue: administers food‑donation tax credits (up to $500,000 in FY2025‑26), compiles/publishes county tax‑sale property lists, expands enforcement/audit/confidentiality rules, and may share taxpayer data with the Department of Labor for specified statutes. County treasurers in counties 150,000+ must remit $1 of each of the first 5,000 vehicle‑registration collection fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the DOR Enforcement Fund.

Department of Motor Vehicles: continue monthly collection/remittance of vehicle sales tax, deposit its collection fee to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund (forfeited on rule violations), and certain penalties fixed at $5.

Department of Administrative Services: budget administrator to schedule a $750 million General Fund transfer to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in FY2024‑25. Disclosure of taxpayer information by employees of the Tax Commissioner, State Treasurer, or DAS is made a Class I misdemeanor (with enumerated exceptions).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development: Adds definitions and assigns the department/director authority to certify community betterment programs, adopt rules on approvals and tax-credit amounts, review local consistency, issue written decisions within 45 days, and transmit approvals to the Tax Commissioner and Director of Insurance.

Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner): Required to prescribe forms/rules for claiming the new credits and to allow credits against specified income/tax sections when certified. Receives copies of department decisions. New limits and timing: total Community Development Assistance Act credits capped at $350,000 per fiscal year; a moratorium on new program certifications for businesses for 2026–2029; potential reopening in 2030 with up to $6 million/year. For applications filed on/after Jan 1, 2026, credit = 10% of investment, capped at $150,000 per application. Unused 2025 credits may be carried forward.

Department of Administrative Services, Department of Agriculture, Department of Labor, Department of Motor Vehicles: No changes or funding/appropriation actions in the added text.
