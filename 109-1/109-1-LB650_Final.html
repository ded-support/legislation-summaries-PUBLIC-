Summary

The bill assigns roles, authorities, and funding limits across agencies. Department of Economic Development (DED) administers a new Community Development Assistance Act (certifying programs, rulemaking, issuing tax‑credit authorizations, 45‑day approval deadline) with credits up to 40% (five‑year carryover) and a $350,000 statewide annual cap; DED also reviews Urban Redevelopment Act applications (90‑day deadline for complete filings), charges a $500 application fee (largely refundable), and is subject to an $8 million total incentives approval cap and reporting to the Department of Revenue. Department of Revenue (DOR) will publish county tax‑sale property lists, administer a food‑donation tax credit (max $500,000 in FY2025‑26, prorated if oversubscribed), expand enforcement/audit/confidentiality authorities, and receive remittances to the Nebraska Incentives Fund. Department of Motor Vehicles collects vehicle sales tax, may retain a collection fee (to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund), and counties must remit $1 per early registrations in large counties to the State Treasurer for credit to the DOR Enforcement Fund. Department of Administrative Services directs a $750 million FY2024‑25 General Fund transfer to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund and makes unauthorized disclosure of taxpayer affairs by DAS officials a Class I misdemeanor. The Tax Commissioner may disclose return information to the Department of Labor for specified enforcement programs.
