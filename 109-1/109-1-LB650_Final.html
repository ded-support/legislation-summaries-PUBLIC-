Summary

The bill assigns specific roles and fiscal limits to multiple state agencies. Department of Economic Development (DED) creates the Community Development Assistance Act, may certify transferable community betterment tax credits (40% per contributor; $350,000 annual cap), must act within 45 days, and administer Urban Redevelopment approvals ( $500 application fee to the Nebraska Incentives Fund; $8 million aggregate cap) and related rulemaking. Department of Revenue is the administering agency for numerous tax-credit programs (application, certification, prorating) and adopts new annual caps (e.g., food-donation $500K in FY2025‑26; Relocation Incentive $1M; Cast & Crew and Shortline Rail $500K each; staged reverse‑osmosis caps to $1M). It will publish compiled tax‑sale property lists; receive $1 per registration (first 5,000) in large counties for the Enforcement Fund; and enforce/recapture credits. DMV collects/remits vehicle sales tax, deposits fees to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund, and certain penalties reduced to $5. DAS employees gain misdemeanor protection against unlawful taxpayer‑info disclosure, and DAS will direct a $750M General Fund transfer to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in FY2024‑25 with ongoing transfers thereafter. Department of Labor may receive taxpayer return information to administer employment/contractor statutes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED): defines "department" and "director," gives DED authority to certify community betterment programs, adopt rules (including credit amounts), review proposals within 45 days, and transmit approvals in writing to the Tax Commissioner and Director of Insurance. DED sets per-applicant credit limits (generally up to 40% of contributions) and a program-year cap.

Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner): required to provide forms and methods to claim the credit, accept DED certifications, grant credits against specified income and sales tax provisions, and certify amounts to the State Treasurer for transfers under specified formulas (affecting the Cash Reserve and School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund).

Other agencies (Admin. Services, Agriculture, Economic Development—covered above, Labor, Motor Vehicles): no substantive changes or funding/appropriation duties assigned to Dept. of Administrative Services, Dept. of Agriculture, Dept. of Labor, or DMV in the added text.
