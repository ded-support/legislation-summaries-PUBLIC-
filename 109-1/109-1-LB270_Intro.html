Summary

LB270 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2711 with provisions affecting the Department of Revenue, Department of Labor, and Department of Administrative Services:

- Department of Revenue: Expands audit access by requiring the Tax Commissioner to make tax returns/return information available to the Auditor of Public Accounts/Legislative Audit for audits of the Department of Revenue under specified statutes (including new references). Confidential returns must be audited on Department premises; audit work papers are to be stored securely in the Department of Revenue. The Department must also provide municipalities (via a certified municipal designee) sales/use tax permit and remittance information and is protected from liability for municipal disclosures.

- Department of Labor: Permits disclosure of tax information to the Department of Labor as necessary to administer the Employment Security Law, Contractor Registration Act, or Employee Classification Act.

- Department of Administrative Services: Employees are included among officers prohibited (Class I misdemeanor) from impermissible disclosure of tax return information.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language lets a municipality‑certified reviewer notify the Auditor of Public Accounts of any suspected irregularity or discrepancy found in the documents described earlier; the Auditor may then conduct an audit under the authority of subsection (11). There is no mention of the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Labor, or Department of Revenue in the added text, and no new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are authorized or referenced. No direct fiscal impact on those agencies is indicated.
