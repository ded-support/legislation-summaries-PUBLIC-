Summary

LB270 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2711 with provisions affecting the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Labor, and Department of Revenue. It preserves a Class I misdemeanor prohibition on disclosure of taxpayer information by officials, explicitly including employees of the Department of Administrative Services. It expressly permits disclosure of tax information to the Department of Labor as necessary to administer the Employment Security Law, the Contractor Registration Act, or the Employee Classification Act. It requires the Department of Revenue to provide confidential returns and return information to the Auditor of Public Accounts for audits of the Department (audit work papers to be stored on Department premises), to supply municipalities lists of retailers, and to furnish certified municipal representatives confidential sales/use tax information (with timing, on‑premises review, nondisclosure limits, and a nonliability provision for the Department). No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subsection allowing an individual certified by a municipality (per subdivision (b)) who identifies a suspected irregularity in the reviewed documents (per subdivision (a)) to notify the Auditor of Public Accounts, who may then conduct an audit under subsection (11). The follow-up language signals that subsection (d) is subject to that new (c) exception. There is no reference to the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Labor, or Department of Revenue, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for those agencies.
