Summary

LB270 amends Neb. Rev. §77-2711 regarding disclosure and audit of tax information.

- Department of Revenue (via the Tax Commissioner) must allow the Auditor of Public Accounts/Legislative Audit to inspect tax returns and return information to audit the Department of Revenue; confidential returns may be audited only on Department of Revenue premises and audit work papers are to be stored securely in the Department of Revenue.

- The Department of Revenue must provide confidential sales/use tax returns and remittance amounts to a municipality’s certified representative (with ten business days’ notice); that representative may notify the Auditor of Public Accounts of suspected discrepancies, which may prompt an audit. The Department of Revenue is not liable for impermissible municipal disclosures.

- Disclosure to the Department of Labor for administering Employment Security, Contractor Registration, or Employee Classification laws is authorized.

- Retains Class I misdemeanor penalty for improper disclosure by Tax Commissioner, State Treasurer, or Department of Administrative Services personnel.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
