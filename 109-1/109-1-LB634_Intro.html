Summary

LB634 creates the Legislative Sunset Review Act and establishes a Legislative Sunset Review Committee (members to include the chair of the Appropriations Committee and five legislators appointed by the Executive Board of the Legislative Council with geographic representation). The committee will receive staff support from legislative divisions and be appointed each regular session beginning after January 1, 2026; initial reviews begin in 2027 with each reviewable entity reviewed every five years. Reviewable entities must submit detailed performance, compliance, fiscal and advisory‑committee reports to the Clerk of the Legislature. The committee may request assistance and inspect records of reviewable entities and state agencies and may exercise standing‑committee powers. The bill abolishes the Legislature’s Planning Committee, becomes operative Jan. 1, 2026, and repeals specified statutory sections. No appropriations or funding changes are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds creation and structure of a Legislative Sunset Review Committee within the Legislature (chair = Appropriations Committee chair, plus five legislative appointees by the Legislative Council Executive Board; staff support from legislative divisions). Requires the Committee to schedule entity reviews beginning 2027 and every five years, publish the schedule on the Legislature's website, and receive electronic reports to the Clerk of the Legislature from each "reviewable entity." Reports must include performance measures, compliance with public records/Open Meetings Act and state purchasing goals for small/minority businesses, Auditor of Public Accounts recommendations, advisory‑committee details, federal funding impacts, and other data. The Committee (and staff) may access/request assistance from reviewable entities, state agencies, and officers and inspect records. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
