Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns the Department primary administration of the new Property Tax Circuit Breaker Act. Qualifying taxpayers must apply to the Department with specified documentation; the Department must approve or deny and certify approved credit amounts within 30 days. The Department may adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act. The bill also adds the Property Tax Circuit Breaker Act to the list of refundable income tax credits in section 77-2715.07 and preserves the Department of Revenue's recapture authority for certain purchaser credits. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and makes the Department the administrator of a new refundable Property Tax Circuit Breaker credit (effective for taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2026). The Department must accept applications with specified information (residence address, property tax or rent paid, federal AGI, assessed value for owners, and any other required documentation), determine eligibility, approve qualifying applications within 30 days, and certify the approved credit amount to taxpayers for attachment to their income tax returns. The Department is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are included.
