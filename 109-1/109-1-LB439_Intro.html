Summary

This bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue and assigns the department primary administrative responsibilities for the new Property Tax Circuit Breaker Act. The department will: receive and require documentation for applications; determine eligibility and approve and certify credit amounts within 30 days of receipt; and adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act. The bill also preserves the Department of Revenue’s recapture authority over a separate residence-purchase tax credit (allowing the department to recapture the credit if residency requirements are not met). No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Property Tax Circuit Breaker Act" and designates the Department of Revenue as the administering agency. For tax years beginning on or after Jan 1, 2026, the Department will accept applications, verify eligibility, approve or deny within 30 days, and certify refundable income tax credits to qualifying homeowners and renters who reside in their Nebraska principal residence at least six months. The Department may adopt rules. The credit equals 50% of property tax or rent paid above a threshold (5% of federal AGI), subject to limits ($4,000 max; $5,000 for seniors) and a cap on taxable value used to compute property taxes. No specific appropriations, funding provisions, or budget allocations for the Department are included.
