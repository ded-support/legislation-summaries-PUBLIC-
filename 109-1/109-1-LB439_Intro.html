Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue (Sec. 2).

- Qualifying taxpayers must apply to the Department of Revenue for the new Property Tax Circuit Breaker credit and may be required to provide documentation as determined by the department (Sec. 3(5)(e)).

- The department must approve qualifying applications within 30 days and certify the approved credit amount to the taxpayer (Sec. 3(6)); taxpayers must attach that department-issued certification to their income tax return (Sec. 4).

- The Department of Revenue is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the act (Sec. 6).

- The bill preserves Department of Revenue recapture authority for an existing homebuyer credit (Sec. 77-2715.07(7)(d)).

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Revenue focus):

- The bill names the Department of Revenue as the administering agency for the new Property Tax Circuit Breaker Act (effective for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on/after Jan 1, 2026).

- The Department must receive and review applications, which must include residence address, property tax or rent paid, federal AGI, assessed value (for owners), and any other documentation the Department requires.

- If eligible, the Department must approve and certify the credit amount to the taxpayer within 30 days; taxpayers attach that certification to their income tax return.

- The Department is authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act.

- No changes to Department funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
