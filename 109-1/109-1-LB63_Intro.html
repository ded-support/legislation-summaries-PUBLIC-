Summary

LB63 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §9-1103, redefining terms tied to racetrack gaming and sports wagering and repeals the original section. It designates the State Racing and Gaming Commission as the regulatory authority with specific powers to:

- approve designated sports wagering areas and the boundaries of licensed racetrack enclosures in accordance with the Nebraska Constitution and law;

- issue racing and authorized gaming operator licenses;

- define and determine prohibited participants and exclude sporting events from wagering; and

- authorize the systems of sports wagering. The bill also references keno conducted under the Nebraska County and City Lottery Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
