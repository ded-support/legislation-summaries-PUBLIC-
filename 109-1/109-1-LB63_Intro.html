Summary

LB63 amends section 9-1103 of the Nebraska Racetrack Gaming Act to revise definitions governing sports wagering. It retains "commission" as the State Racing and Gaming Commission and clarifies the commission’s authorities: approving designated sports wagering areas, excluding sporting events from wagering, determining prohibited participants, and issuing racing and gaming licenses. The bill narrows "authorized sporting event" (explicitly excluding parimutuel wagers, fantasy sports, minor‑league events, and high‑school‑level or lower events, among others) and revises "sports wagering" exclusions (including certain wagers on individual collegiate athletes and minors). It also references keno conducted under the Nebraska County and City Lottery Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
