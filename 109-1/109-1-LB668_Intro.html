Summary

LB668 amends multiple statutes referencing the Department of Health and Human Services (department). Key department-specific provisions:

- Requires the department to provide written notice before entering a person into the Adult Protective Services or child protection central registries, including rights to appeal, to request amendment/expungement, or removal; a pending appeal blocks entry until the department issues a final order.

- Confirms the central child-protection registry is maintained by the department; the department must update name-change orders and may adopt rules.

- Authorizes the department to charge a records‑check fee (not to exceed $5), remit receipts to the State Treasurer for credit to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund, waive fees for hardship, and use fees to defray costs.

- Requires that case managers for DHHS‑custody and most noncourt cases be department employees (with contracted providers allowed for specified prevention cases) and assigns their decisionmaking and oversight duties.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural protections for persons the Department of Health and Human Services would enter on abuse/neglect registries: the department must notify the subject of the right to contest, send that notice at least 14 days before entry on the central child-protection registry (per §28-718), and allow an appeal (within 14 calendar days of notice) under the Administrative Procedure Act. If a timely appeal is filed, the department may not enter the person on the registry until a final departmental order. Also clarifies a prevention-case manager may be a contracted provider; a “prevention case” is defined by FFPSA-related criteria (a juvenile candidate for foster care, no ongoing child-welfare services needed to ensure safety, and voluntary family engagement). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
