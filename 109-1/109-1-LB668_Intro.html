Summary

LB668 amends multiple statutes referencing the Department of Health and Human Services (the department):

- Requires the department to notify subjects before entering them on the Adult Protective Services central registry or the central registry of child protection cases, and bars entry while a proper appeal is pending until the department issues a final order.

- Authorizes the department to amend, expunge, or remove identifying information or substantiated reports from registries and to provide related notices and appeal processes.

- Directs the department to maintain the child protection central registry, include court name-change cross‑references, and report annually to the Governor and relevant legislative committee chairpersons.

- Permits the department to charge a records‑check fee not to exceed $5 per request, remits collected fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund, and use fees to defray records‑check costs.

- Specifies that case managers for juveniles in DHHS care must be department employees, except contracted providers may serve for defined prevention cases.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) — summary of added provisions:

- DHHS must notify an individual of the right to contest before entering them on the adult or child central registry and send that notice at least 14 days before entry.

- The notice starts a 14‑calendar‑day appeal window for the person alleged to have abused/neglected/exploited.

- Appeals are to be conducted under the Administrative Procedure Act; if a proper appeal is filed, DHHS may not enter the person on the registry until a final departmental order.

- Clarifies that the subject may appeal the department’s decision.

- Allows the case manager for prevention cases to be a contracted provider and defines "prevention case" (juvenile is a Families First candidate for foster care, no ongoing child‑welfare services are required to keep the child safe, and the family voluntarily accepts community services).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
