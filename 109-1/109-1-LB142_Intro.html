Summary

LB142 amends section 79‑1003 of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act by updating definitional language used to calculate state aid. Key government entities referenced: the State Department of Education (designated "Department"), the State Board of Education, local school boards, the Educational Service Unit Coordinating Council, the School Employees Retirement System of the State of Nebraska, the Class V School Employees Retirement Act system, and the Clerk of the Legislature (for lobbyist reporting). The bill clarifies what is excluded from "general fund operating expenditures" (e.g., categorical funds, federal impact aid, specified tuition/transportation items, levy‑override receipts calculation, lobbyist payments) and caps recognition of employer/school district retirement contributions to amounts above specified contribution-rate baselines (7.35% and 7.37%). No new appropriations or direct funding allocations are enacted; the changes are definitional and affect how aid and budget measures are calculated.
