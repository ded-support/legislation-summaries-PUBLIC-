Summary

LB459 assigns responsibilities to the Nebraska Department of Environment and Energy, declares legislative intent, and establishes a Home Weatherization Clearinghouse. The bill directs the Clearinghouse to perform duties related to home weatherization programs and coordination, subject to requirements of any relevant federal, state, or local grant program. The provided text does not specify any appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations.
