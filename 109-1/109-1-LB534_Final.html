Summary

LB534 authorizes payment of specific claims against the State and approves agency write‑offs. It appropriates $1,195,691.75 from the General Fund for settlements to be paid through Program 536, Agency 65; $827,500.00 from the General Fund for indemnification claims via Program 592, Agency 65; $1,260,000.00 from the Workers’ Compensation Claims Revolving Fund for workers’ compensation payments via Program 593, Agency 65; and $1,446,454.12 from the State Insurance Fund via Program 594, Agency 65. Total appropriations equal $4,729,645.87. The Director of Administrative Services is directed to draw warrants and the State Treasurer to pay upon receipt of releases. The State Claims Board approved write‑offs totaling $1,767,570.24 for: Department of Veterans’ Affairs; DMV; Department of Transportation; Department of Labor; Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems; Department of Environment and Energy; and Department of Health and Human Services. The act takes effect immediately.
