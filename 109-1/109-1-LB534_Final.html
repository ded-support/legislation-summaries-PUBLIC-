Summary

LB534 appropriates funds to pay state settlements, indemnification, workers' compensation, and tort claims settled by the Attorney General or State Claims Board. Payments will be processed through Agency 65 (Programs 536, 592, 593, 594). Key funding allocations:

- General Fund appropriations: $1,195,691.75 (Sec.1) and $827,500.00 (Sec.2) — total $2,023,191.75.

- Workers' Compensation Claims Revolving Fund: $1,260,000.00 (Sec.3).

- State Insurance Fund (revolving): $1,446,454.12 (Sec.4).

The Director of Administrative Services is directed to issue warrants and the State Treasurer to pay upon receipt of releases. The State Claims Board approved write-offs totaling $1,767,570.24 for: Department of Veterans' Affairs; Department of Motor Vehicles; Department of Transportation; Department of Labor; Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems; Department of Environment and Energy; and Department of Health and Human Services. The act takes effect immediately.
