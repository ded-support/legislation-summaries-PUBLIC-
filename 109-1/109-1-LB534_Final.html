Summary

LB534 directs payment of settled claims and authorizes agency account write-offs. Claims settled by the Attorney General or State Claims Board are to be paid through Agency 65 as follows: Program 536 (General Fund) $1,195,691.75; Program 592 (General Fund) $827,500.00; Program 593 (Workers' Compensation Claims Revolving Fund) $1,260,000.00; Program 594 (State Insurance/Revolving Fund) $1,446,454.12. The Director of Administrative Services is authorized to draw warrants and the State Treasurer to pay upon receipt of releases. The State Claims Board approved write-offs by state agencies: Department of Veterans' Affairs $14,983.58; Department of Motor Vehicles $14,296.88; Department of Transportation $120,741.50; Department of Labor $90,339.82; Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems $49,591.34; Department of Environment and Energy $53,256.46; Department of Health and Human Services $1,424,360.66. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds appropriations to pay settled claims and directs payment processing through Agency 65 (Programs 536, 592, 593, 594). Key funding changes:

- General Fund appropriations: $1,195,691.75 for miscellaneous/line-of-duty claims (Program 536) and $827,500.00 for indemnification claims (Program 592) — total General Fund $2,023,191.75.

- Workers' Compensation Claims Revolving Fund: $1,260,000.00 for workers' comp claims (Program 593).

- State Insurance Fund: $1,446,454.12 for tort claims (Program 594).

The Attorney General and State Claims Board settlements are authorized for payment; the Director of Administrative Services and State Treasurer are directed to issue warrants upon receipt of releases. The State Claims Board approved write-offs for: Dept. of Veterans' Affairs; Dept. of Motor Vehicles; Dept. of Transportation; Dept. of Labor; Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems; Dept. of Environment and Energy; Dept. of Health and Human Services (total write-offs $1,767,570.24).
