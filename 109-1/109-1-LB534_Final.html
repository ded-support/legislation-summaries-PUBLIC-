Summary

LB534 directs payments and write-offs for settled claims and authorizes related appropriations. Agency 65 will disburse claim payments through Program 536 (state settlements, General Fund $1,195,691.75) and Programs 592 (indemnification, General Fund $827,500.00), 593 (workers’ compensation, Workers’ Compensation Claims Revolving Fund $1,260,000.00), and 594 (tort/State Insurance Fund $1,446,454.12). The Director of Administrative Services is authorized to draw warrants and the State Treasurer to pay upon receipt of full releases; the Attorney General, State Claims Board, and Nebraska Workers’ Compensation Court are involved in settlements. The State Claims Board approved agency write-offs totaling $1,767,570.24 from: Dept. of Veterans’ Affairs; Dept. of Motor Vehicles; Dept. of Transportation; Dept. of Labor; Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems; Dept. of Environment and Energy; and Dept. of Health and Human Services. The act takes effect immediately.
