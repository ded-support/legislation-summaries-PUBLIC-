Summary

LB17 amends the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act to prohibit most landlord fees, limit allowable fees, require written disclosure of total rent and itemized mandatory/optional fees, and require landlords to provide copies of any consumer reports plus the name, address, and phone number of each consumer reporting agency used. Enforcement: violations are treated as violations of Nebraska’s Consumer Protection Act (section 59-1602) and may be enforced by the Attorney General or by private civil action. Landlords who knowingly violate the section may incur civil penalties up to $300 per violation, plus court costs and attorney fees. The bill preserves rights under the state Credit Report Protection Act and the federal Fair Credit Reporting Act and references liability for improper dissemination of criminal history or FCRA violations. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds limits on landlord fees and strong disclosure requirements. Landlords may only charge rent, security deposits (per §76-1416), returned-payment fees up to landlord cost, limited late fees, and application fees limited to the actual cost of obtaining a consumer report from a consumer reporting agency. Landlords must provide copies of any consumer/tenant screening reports and identify the consumer reporting agencies used, and must disclose total rent and itemized mandatory/optional fees before application. Optional services require written informed consent and opt-out. Violations are enforceable under the Consumer Protection Act by the aggrieved person or the Attorney General, with civil penalties up to $300 per violation plus costs. No funding or appropriation changes.
