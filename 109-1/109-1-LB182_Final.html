Summary

- The bill designates "Department" in the Child Care Tax Credit Act as the Department of Revenue and makes the Department the administering agency for that credit: taxpayers apply to the Department, submit documentation, and the Department approves and certifies credit amounts in receipt order up to a $2.5 million annual cap.

- For the Affordable Housing Tax Credit, pass‑through entities or taxpayers must notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before claiming allocated, transferred, sold, or assigned credits and provide the transferee's tax identification number; the Department prescribes the notification form.

- If Nebraska affordable housing tax credits are recaptured or disallowed, the Department of Revenue's declaration increases the claimant's tax liability in the year of declaration.

- The Department may receive verification from DHHS (for certain child care provider billing) but may use that information only for administration of the Child Care Tax Credit Act.

- Certified credits may be used to offset income, premium/retaliatory, or franchise taxes as specified; no direct appropriations or new funding lines are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue impacts:

- Pass‑through entity rules: owners/allocating entities must notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before a taxpayer claims Nebraska affordable housing tax credits, providing the transferee’s tax identification number; notification format determined by the department.

- Administrative treatment: credits may be used to offset premium/retaliatory taxes and franchise taxes; credits claimed against retaliatory tax are not to trigger additional retaliatory tax and are treated as a payment of tax for purposes of subsection (1) of §77‑2734.03.

- Effective date: changes to §§77‑2502, 77‑2503, and 77‑2506 apply to taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2024.

- No added funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are included.
