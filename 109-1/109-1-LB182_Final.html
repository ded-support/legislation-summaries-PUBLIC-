Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue for the Child Care Tax Credit Act.

- Affordable Housing Tax Credit: pass‑through entities or taxpayers allocating, transferring, selling, or assigning credits must notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before claiming the credit and provide the transferee’s tax identification number; the notification format is prescribed by the Department. If Nebraska housing credits are recaptured or disallowed, the Department of Revenue declares the disallowance/recapture, which increases the claimant’s tax liability in that year.

- Child Care Tax Credit: taxpayers apply to the Department of Revenue; the Department approves and certifies credit amounts, processes applications in receipt order, and may approve credits up to an annual cap of $2,500,000. The Department may use DHHS verification only for direct administration of the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require pass-through entities (and taxpayers transferring credits) to notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before a tax return claims any allocated, transferred, sold, or assigned Nebraska affordable housing tax credit, using a department-prescribed method and providing the transferee’s tax identification number. Pass-through entities may allocate credits among owners or further transfer/sell/assign credits to taxpayers. Credits may offset income, premium/retaliatory, or franchise taxes and are treated as tax payments for section 77-2734.03(1). Changes apply to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2024. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.
