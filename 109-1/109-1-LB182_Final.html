Summary

Key provisions referencing the Department of Revenue:

- Defines "Department" as the Department of Revenue for the Child Care Tax Credit Act.

- Affordable Housing Tax Credit: pass‑through entities or taxpayers allocating, transferring, selling, or assigning credits must notify the Department of Revenue (including transferee tax ID) at least 30 days before a return claiming the credit; notification format prescribed by the Department.

- Recapture: Nebraska affordable housing tax credits recaptured or disallowed increase the taxpayer's liability in the year the Department of Revenue declares the recapture/disallowance.

- Child Care Tax Credit: taxpayers apply to the Department (documentation required); the Department approves/certifies credits, considers applications in receipt order, and may approve credits up to an annual cap of $2,500,000.

- Department may only use DHHS verification information for direct administration of the child care credit.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds rules requiring pass-through entities and taxpayers to notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before claiming or using allocated, transferred, sold, or assigned Nebraska affordable housing tax credits, providing the transferee's tax identification number in a department-prescribed format. Credits may be allocated among partners/members/shareholders, further reallocated, or sold/assigned; they may offset individual income taxes, certain insurer premium/retaliatory taxes, and franchise taxes. A taxpayer using the credit against retaliatory taxes is not subject to additional retaliatory tax, and any claimed credit is treated as a tax payment for purposes of section 77-2734.03. The changes apply to taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2024. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.
