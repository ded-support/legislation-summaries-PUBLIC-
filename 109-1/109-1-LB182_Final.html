Summary

This bill designates the Department of Revenue as the administering agency for the Child Care Tax Credit Act and assigns several specific duties to the Department. It requires pass-through entities and taxpayers to notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before claiming allocated, transferred, sold, or assigned Nebraska affordable housing tax credits, providing the transferee’s tax identification number in the manner prescribed by the Department. The Department is authorized to declare Nebraska affordable housing tax credits disallowed or recaptured, which increases the claimant’s tax liability in the year of declaration. For the Child Care Tax Credit, the Department receives applications, approves and certifies credit amounts in receipt order (subject to a $2.5 million annual approval cap), and must limit use of DHHS verification data to administration of the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Revenue focus):

- Expands pass‑through rules for the Nebraska affordable housing tax credit, allowing allocation among partners/members/shareholders and permitting transfer, sale, or assignment of credits between taxpayers.

- Requires any pass‑through entity or taxpayer that allocates, transfers, sells, or assigns a credit to notify the Department of Revenue at least 30 days before a return claiming the credit is filed, and to provide the transferee’s tax identification number in the manner prescribed by the Department.

- Clarifies credits may offset income tax, certain insurance premium/retaliatory taxes, and franchise taxes; claiming such credits won’t trigger additional retaliatory tax and is treated as a tax payment for section 77‑2734.03(1).

- Effective for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after January 1, 2024.

- No direct changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.
