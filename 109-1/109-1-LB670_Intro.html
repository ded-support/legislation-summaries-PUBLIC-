Summary

The bill amends section 43-2606 to require the Department of Health and Human Services to adopt and promulgate mandatory training rules for child care and school-age-care providers. Key provisions: preservice orientation and annual inservice training (capped at four hours); licensed child care programs must show completion of a department‑approved preservice orientation before receiving a provisional license; use of the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System to document and verify staff training for reporting programs; required training topics (e.g., SIDS, abusive head trauma, crying plans, child abuse); the department must provide/arrange statewide training opportunities and inform providers at registration/licensure/renewal or annually; providers receiving inservice training must submit their Social Security number to the department; DHHS must review and recommend updates to these rules at least every five years. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require each public and nonpublic school to adopt and annually update a safety plan (may use the state school security director's model) covering fire, weather, vehicle evacuation and other drills. School districts must provide their plan to the state school security director every three years; the director may offer suggestions, training and resources. The State Board of Education is barred from requiring more than four hours of annual inservice training for prekindergarten teachers, paraeducators, and home visitors (districts may mandate additional training). Initial evidence‑based training is capped at 12 hours, and the bill directs recommending best practices/minimum standards to nonpublic schools. No duties, funding changes, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services are specified.
