Summary

The bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to adopt and promulgate rules for mandatory training for child care and school-age-care providers. Requirements include a preservice orientation and annual inservice training limited to no more than four hours. Licensed child care programs must complete a DHHS‑approved or -delivered preservice orientation before receiving a provisional license. For programs reporting to the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System, DHHS shall use that system to document staff training levels and verify minimum training requirements. DHHS must provide or arrange statewide training opportunities and information at registration/licensure/renewal, require attendees to submit their Social Security number, and review and recommend rule updates to the Governor at least every five years. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require each public and nonpublic school board to adopt and annually update a safety plan (fire, tornado/ weather, vehicle evacuation, and other drills as appropriate). Districts must submit their safety plan to the state school security director every three years; the director may offer suggestions, training, and resources and provide a model plan. The State Board of Education is barred from requiring more than four hours of annual inservice training for teachers, paraeducators, and pre-K home visitors (local boards may impose more). Initial evidence-based training is capped at 12 hours; recommendations and minimum safety standards may be issued to nonpublic schools. Department of Health and Human Services is not mentioned and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
