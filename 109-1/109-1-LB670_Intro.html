Summary

The bill amends section 43-2606 to assign the Department of Health and Human Services authority to adopt mandatory training rules for child care and school‑age‑care providers, requiring preservice orientation and annual inservice training (capped at four hours). Licensed programs must complete department‑approved preservice orientation before provisional licensure. For programs reporting to the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System, the department will document staff training levels and verify minimum requirements. Required training topics include SIDS, abusive head trauma, crying plans, and child abuse. DHHS must provide or arrange statewide training, collect Social Security numbers from inservice trainees, and review/recommend rule updates to the Governor at least every five years. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require each school district and nonpublic school to adopt and annually update a safety plan (using the state school security director's model as a guide) that includes fire, tornado/weather, vehicle evacuation, and other deemed drills; plans must be provided to the state school security director every three years. The state school security director may offer training/resources and make recommendations to nonpublic schools on best practices and minimum standards. The State Board of Education is barred from requiring more than four hours of annual inservice training for teachers, paraeducators, or prekindergarten home visitors (districts may require more); an initial evidence‑based training is limited to 12 hours. No changes to the Department of Health and Human Services or any appropriations/budget allocations are included.
