Summary

The bill amends section 43-2606 to assign the Department of Health and Human Services responsibility to adopt rules for mandatory preservice orientation and annual inservice training (annual inservice limited to four hours) for child care and school‑age‑care providers. Licensed child care programs must complete a department‑approved or -delivered preservice orientation before receiving a provisional license. Beginning January 1, 2020, the department shall use the Nebraska Early Childhood Professional Record System to document staff training levels and verify minimum training. Required content includes SIDS, abusive head trauma, crying plans, and child abuse. The department must provide or arrange statewide training and information at registration/licensure or annually, require providers receiving inservice training to submit their Social Security number, and review/update rules for the Governor at least every five years. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language requires each public and nonpublic school to adopt and annually update a safety plan (using the state school security director’s model if desired) that at minimum addresses fire, tornado/weather, vehicle evacuation, and other drills. Districts must submit their safety plan to the state school security director every three years; the director may offer suggestions, training, and resources. The State Board of Education is barred from imposing more than four hours of annual inservice training for teachers, paraeducators, or pre-K home visitors (local boards may require more). It also limits initial evidence‑based training to no more than 12 hours for personnel as outlined in school safety plans. No provisions mention the Department of Health and Human Services, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
