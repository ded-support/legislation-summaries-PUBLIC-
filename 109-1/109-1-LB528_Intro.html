Summary

Defines "department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it authority to establish a grant program to procure annual licenses for preexisting three‑dimensional, game‑based learning platforms for middle and high schools. The department must develop application/prioritization criteria, accept developer applications, and may adopt rules. Grants funded with lottery funds pursuant to section 79‑3501 shall be awarded from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund (administered by the State Department of Education); grant funds may be used only to procure licenses (not for R&D). Grantees must provide access to all Nebraska school districts; awards must be made by July 1 annually. The Department of Economic Development must file an annual report to the Clerk of the Legislature by December 1. The statute allocates 8.75% of the ten percent lottery transfer to these subsection (2) grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting the Department of Economic Development (DED):

- "Department" is defined as the Department of Economic Development.

- DED may approve grant applications for learning platforms, but grant funds may only be used to procure or purchase an annual license for a preexisting platform (not for R&D).

- Eligible platforms must align academic content with State Board of Education standards (high school and equivalent middle‑school standards), comply with ISO 27001, and demonstrate statewide implementation experience.

- Approved grants authorized by DED will be awarded from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund administered by the State Department of Education.

No new appropriations or budget line‑item changes are specified; funding is to come from the existing SDE Improvement Grant Fund.
