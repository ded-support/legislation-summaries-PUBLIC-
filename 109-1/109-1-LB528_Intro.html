Summary

LB528 designates the Department of Economic Development (DED) to establish a grant program to procure annual licenses for preexisting three‑dimensional, game‑based learning platforms for middle and high schools. Grants are funded with lottery funds under §79‑3501 and awarded from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund (administered by the State Department of Education). DED must develop application/prioritization criteria, prescribe the application process, require awarded platforms be made available to all Nebraska school districts, award grants by July 1 each year, and submit an annual report to the Clerk of the Legislature by December 1. DED may adopt rules to implement the program. Fiscal allocation: of the 10% lottery transfer to the Improvement Grant Fund, 8.75% is earmarked for the subsection (2) learning‑platform grants (1.25% remains for subsection (1) grants).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text designates “Department” as the Department of Economic Development and gives the department authority over grant approval for learning platforms. Grants may only purchase or procure annual licenses for preexisting learning platforms (no R&D funding). Approved platforms must: align academic content with State Board of Education middle/high‑school standards, comply with ISO 27001 data‑security standards, and show experience with statewide implementation. While the Department of Economic Development approves applications, awarded grants are to be paid from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund (administered by the State Department of Education). No specific appropriation amounts or new budget allocations are included.
