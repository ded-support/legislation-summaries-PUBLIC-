Summary

Defines the Department of Economic Development ("department") and charges it with establishing a grant program to procure annual licenses for preexisting three-dimensional, game-based learning platforms for middle and high schools (science/chemistry/physical science/mathematics focus). Grants are funded with lottery funds pursuant to §79-3501 and awarded from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund (administered by the State Department of Education). The department will set application criteria, approve developer applications, require full statewide district access, restrict funds to license procurement (not R&D), and must award grants by July 1 annually. The department must file an annual report to the Clerk by December 1 and may adopt rules. Funding allocation: of the 10% transferred under §79-3501, 8.75% is reserved for these subsection (2) grants and 1.25% for subsection (1) grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Economic Development):

- “Department” is defined as the Department of Economic Development.

- The department may approve grants to purchase annual licenses for preexisting learning platforms; grant funds may not be used for platform research or development.

- Eligible platforms must align with State Board of Education academic standards for middle/high school, comply with ISO 27001 data-security standards, and show experience with statewide implementation.

- Grants approved by the Department of Economic Development are to be awarded from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund, administered by the State Department of Education.

- No specific appropriation amounts or new budget allocations are included.
