Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Economic Development (DED) to establish and administer a grant program to procure annual licenses for preexisting three‑dimensional, game‑based learning platforms for middle and high schools (STEM-focused). DED must: develop prioritization criteria; accept developer applications; award grants (by July 1 annually) from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund (administered by the State Dept. of Education); require platform access for all Nebraska school districts; and submit an annual December 1 report to the Clerk of the Legislature on recipients and program effectiveness. Grants are limited to license procurement (no R&D) and must meet listed content and security criteria. Funding: grants are funded with lottery funds under section 79‑3501; of the 10% transferred to the Improvement Grant Fund, 8.75% is allocated for the subsection (2) grants DED administers (1.25% for subsection (1)).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text assigns “Department” to the Department of Economic Development and makes DED responsible for approving grant applications for learning platforms. Grants may only fund procurement/purchase of an annual license for a preexisting learning platform (not R&D). Eligible platforms must align academic content with State Board of Education standards (high school or equivalent for middle school), comply with ISO 27001 data‑security standards, and demonstrate statewide implementation experience. Approved grants are to be awarded from the State Department of Education Improvement Grant Fund, administered by the State Department of Education. No specific new appropriations, dollar amounts, or changes to budget allocations are included.
