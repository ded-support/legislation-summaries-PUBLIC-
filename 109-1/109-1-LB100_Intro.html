Summary

The bill requires the Director of Economic Development to electronically file an annual report (first due July 1, 2026) to the Legislature documenting: (a) the percentage of economic development funds awarded to (i) individuals starting new businesses or (ii) individuals/entities that provide services to businesses established within the previous five years and based in Nebraska; and (b) the total dollar amount of such awards. Beginning July 1, 2026, the Department of Economic Development is encouraged (not required) to allocate five percent of its economic development funds to those recipients. "Economic development funds" is defined as amounts budgeted for programs administered by the Department, including community development block grants. No specific appropriations or mandatory budget transfers are imposed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting the Department of Economic Development (effective July 1, 2026):

- The Director must annually file a report with the Legislature (by July 1 each year) showing (a) the percentage and (b) the total dollar amount of economic development funds awarded to (i) individuals starting new businesses and (ii) individuals/entities that provide services to businesses established within the previous five years and based in Nebraska.

- The Department is encouraged (not required) to award 5% of its economic development funds to those same startups/service providers.

- "Economic development funds" are defined as amounts budgeted for programs the Department administers, including Community Development Block Grants.

- No mandatory appropriation changes to the Department’s budget are prescribed.
