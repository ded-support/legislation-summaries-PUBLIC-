Summary

LB100 directs the Director of Economic Development to file an annual electronic report (by July 1, 2026, and each July 1 thereafter) to the Legislature showing (a) the percentage of economic development funds awarded to (i) individuals starting new businesses and (ii) individuals/entities providing services to businesses established within the past five years with principal place in Nebraska, and (b) the total dollar amounts awarded for those categories. Beginning July 1, 2026, the Department of Economic Development is encouraged to award five percent of its economic development funds to those same categories. "Economic development funds" is defined as amounts budgeted for programs administered by the Department, including community development block grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an annual reporting requirement for the Director of Economic Development (first report due July 1, 2026) to file with the Legislature the percentage and total dollar amount of "economic development funds" awarded to (a) individuals starting new businesses and (b) entities providing services to businesses established within the previous five years whose principal place of business is in Nebraska. Beginning July 1, 2026, the Department of Economic Development is encouraged (not required) to direct five percent of its economic development funds to those early-stage, in-state businesses. "Economic development funds" is defined to mean amounts budgeted for programs administered by the Department, including Community Development Block Grants. No explicit new appropriations or mandatory budget reallocations are specified.
