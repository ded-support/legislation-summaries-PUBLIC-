Summary

LB432 designates “Department” as the Department of Correctional Services and “Director” as the Director of Correctional Services. The bill authorizes the department/director to determine execution method (lethal injection or nitrogen hypoxia); requires the director to create, modify, and maintain a written execution protocol; select substances and equipment; document procurement procedures; designate and define responsibilities and training for an execution team; and maintain confidentiality of team identities. Executions must occur at a department facility under the director’s supervision, with the director controlling who may be present. The director may notify the Governor if military assistance is deemed necessary, and must return execution proceedings to the court clerk. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
