Summary

LB432 assigns primary responsibility for carrying out death sentences to the Department of Correctional Services and its Director. The bill (a) defines “Department” and “Director”; (b) makes the department responsible for selecting the execution method (lethal injection or nitrogen hypoxia) and for creating, modifying, and maintaining a written execution protocol; (c) authorizes the Director to select substances or equipment, designate and train an execution team, and supervise executions at a department facility; (d) makes execution-team identities confidential; (e) empowers the Director to seek military assistance and requires the Director to return execution proceedings to the sentencing court. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and administrative authority for the Department of Correctional Services and its Director: defines “Department” and “Director,” and defines “lethal injection” (IV injection) and “nitrogen hypoxia” (inhalation of pure nitrogen). Gives the Director authority to determine the method of execution (lethal injection or nitrogen hypoxia), select equipment for nitrogen hypoxia, and designate qualified persons to administer substances under the execution protocol for lethal injection. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
