Summary

LB682 (amending Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-770) requires school districts, at a parent/guardian's request, to issue a certificate of attendance to students receiving services under the Special Education Act who are 17 and have not completed their individualized education program (IEP). It permits such students to participate in a high school graduation ceremony (one certificate and one ceremony), preserves the district’s obligation to continue special education services, and allows participation in a later ceremony if the student subsequently earns a diploma. The bill cites Neb. sections 79-729 and 79-730 and repeals the original §79-770. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
