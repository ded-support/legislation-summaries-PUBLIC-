Summary

LB682 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-770 to require school districts, at a parent or guardian's request, to issue a certificate of attendance to students receiving services under the Special Education Act who are 17 and have not completed their individualized education program. The certificate permits a one-time participation in the high school graduation ceremony with diploma recipients; districts must continue providing special education services and may allow a later ceremony if the student subsequently earns a diploma. The bill cross-references graduation requirements ( §79-729) and high school equivalency ( §79-730) and repeals the original section. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
