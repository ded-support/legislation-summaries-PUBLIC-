Summary

LB682 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-770. It requires school districts, at a parent or guardian's request, to issue a certificate of attendance to a student receiving special education services under the Special Education Act who is at least 17 and has not completed his or her individualized education program. Districts must allow such students to participate in the high school graduation ceremony with diploma recipients; a student may receive only one certificate and participate in only one ceremony based on it. Issuance of the certificate does not relieve the district’s obligation to continue special education services. The change clarifies that receipt of a certificate does not prevent a student from later earning a high school diploma under district graduation rules (§79-729) or a high school equivalency diploma (§79-730); districts may permit an additional ceremony if a diploma is later awarded. No funding or appropriation provisions are included. The original section is repealed.
