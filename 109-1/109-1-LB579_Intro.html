Summary

LB579 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§84-712 and 84-712.01 to (1) prohibit charging fees for public-record requests made by members of the Legislature and (2) impose timing requirements on custodians. Custodians (public bodies, public entities, public officials, and state agencies) must, within four business days of receiving a written request, provide an estimated cost and either access/copies (if equipment is available), a written denial with required information, or a written explanation when fulfillment cannot be completed within four business days, including the earliest practicable fulfillment date and a chance to narrow/prioritize the request. Requesters then have ten business days to respond; absent a response the custodian will not proceed. The bill also preserves that state agencies offering records via the §84-1204 portal obtain fee approval under §§84-1205.02–84-1205.03 and may include approved portal fees. No appropriations or budgetary allocations are contained.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language exempting members of the Legislature from fees for requests under this section — “No fee shall be charged under this section for a request made by a member of the Legislature,” with an exception referenced in subdivision (3)(i)/subsection (3).
