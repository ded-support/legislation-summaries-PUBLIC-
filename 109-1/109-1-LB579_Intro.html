Summary

Bill LB579 amends Nebraska public‑records law governing custodians (public bodies, public entities, public officials and their departments, agencies, counties, cities, villages, tax‑supported districts, boards, bureaus, commissions, councils, subunits, and committees). Key agency‑focused changes:

- Members of the Legislature: custodians may not charge any fee for requests.

- Custodians must, within four business days of receipt, provide an cost estimate and either access/copies, a written denial (with required info), or a written explanation with the earliest practicable fulfillment date and opportunity to narrow/ prioritize for extensive requests.

- Requesters have 10 business days to respond; custodians may require deposits if estimated costs exceed $50.

- Fee rules: resident requests exclude salary costs for the first eight cumulative staff hours; nonresidents’ fees may include prorated salary and attorney review.

- State agencies providing records via an electronic portal (per §84‑1204) must obtain fee approval under §§84‑1205.02–84‑1205.03.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an explicit fee exemption for members of the Legislature: no fee may be charged under this section for a request made by a legislator, except as otherwise provided in subdivision (3)(i)/subsection (3).
