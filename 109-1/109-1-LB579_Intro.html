Summary

LB579 amends Nebraska public‑records statutes to (1) prohibit custodians, public bodies, public entities, and public officials from charging fees for public‑record requests made by members of the Legislature, and (2) impose response deadlines and procedural requirements. A custodian must, within four business days of receiving a written request, provide access or copies (if copying equipment is available), a written denial with statutory notice, or a written explanation with the earliest practicable fulfillment date and cost estimate; requesters then have ten business days to respond. The bill clarifies fee limits (actual added cost), deposit authority if estimated cost exceeds $50, labor‑charge rules (first eight cumulative hours exempt for Nebraska residents), and that state agencies using an 84‑1204 portal must obtain fee approval under §§84‑1205.02–84‑1205.03. No appropriations or budgetary allocations are made. It also enumerates covered entities (state, counties, cities, villages, political subdivisions, tax‑supported districts, and their agencies/boards/committees).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an explicit fee exemption: requests made by a member of the Legislature are not subject to fees under this section, except as limited by subdivision (3)(i). No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
