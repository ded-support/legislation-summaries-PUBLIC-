Summary

LB617 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-102 (Workers' Compensation Act) to expressly abolish certain employer defenses. Under the amended language, it shall not be a defense that (1) the employee was negligent (except when negligence was willful or the employee was intoxicated), (2) the injury was caused by a fellow employee's negligence, or (3) the employee assumed risks due to unsafe premises or appliances. The bill also repeals the original §48-102. No state agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary provisions.
