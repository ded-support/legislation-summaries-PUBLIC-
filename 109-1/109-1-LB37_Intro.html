Summary

LB37 enacts the Railroad Safety Act and assigns regulatory authority for railroad carrier safety to the Nebraska Public Service Commission (PSC) by amending §75-109.01 to expressly reference the new Act (and existing statutes 74-918, 74-919, 74-1323, 75-401–75-430). The bill references the Federal Railroad Administration (FRA) for certain definitions and timetables (main line criteria). It sets operational safety requirements (e.g., maximum train lengths for hazardous-substance trains) and preserves that the PSC will not oversee labor relations. The act becomes operative July 1, 2026, repeals the prior §75-109.01, and outright repeals §17-225, R.R.S. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Railroad Safety Act," with findings and extensive definitions. Key government references: "Commission" is defined as the Nebraska Public Service Commission; several federal authorities/standards are cited (multiple 49 C.F.R. provisions and timetables filed with the Federal Railroad Administration) to define terms like accident, incident, hazardous substance, main line (≥5 million gross tons/year per FRA filings), and others. New operational limits: trains carrying hazardous substances may not exceed 8,500 feet on any state main line; in civil suits, if a train exceeded 7,000 feet and length caused harm, a plaintiff's recovery may not be reduced for comparative fault. The Act also states it does not confer PSC jurisdiction over railroad labor relations. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
