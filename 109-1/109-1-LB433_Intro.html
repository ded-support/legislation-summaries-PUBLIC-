Summary

LB433 amends State Personnel and State Employees Retirement Act provisions affecting several named agencies:

- Department of Banking and Finance and Department of Insurance: deputies and examiners are exempted from the State Personnel System (except those who remain covered).

- Department of Correctional Services: chief executive officers of all correctional facilities and the department medical director are exempt; pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, and psychologists employed by the department are also exempt.

- Department of Health and Human Services and Department of Veterans' Affairs: pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, psychologists, service area administrators, and facility operating officers are exempt from the State Personnel System.

- Department of Labor: active participants in a terminated contributory retirement plan/contracts under former section 48-609 shall receive vesting credit for years of participation.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations appear in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Departments of Banking and Finance; Insurance; Labor; Correctional Services; Health and Human Services; and Veterans' Affairs:

- Deputy directors and attorneys hired after the bill’s effective date will serve at the will of the agency director; their salaries are set by the director and approved by the Governor.

- incumbents in those roles may permanently elect to convert to at‑will status by written notice; until they do they remain in the State Personnel System.

- Temporary employees who return to state service without a 120‑day break must rejoin the retirement system within 30 days; the definition of "temporary employees" is clarified for certain legislative hires.
