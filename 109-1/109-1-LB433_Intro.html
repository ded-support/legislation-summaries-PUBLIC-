Summary

LB433 amends State Personnel and State Employees Retirement Act provisions affecting specific agencies:

- State Personnel System exemptions: adds or clarifies exemptions for deputies and examiners of the Department of Banking and Finance and the Department of Insurance; chief executive officers of facilities operated by the Department of Correctional Services and the department medical director; all pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, and psychologists employed by the Department of Correctional Services; and pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, psychologists, service area administrators, and facility operating officers of the Department of Health and Human Services and the Department of Veterans' Affairs. Several Veterans' Home chief executive positions are explicitly listed.

- Retirement membership: if a specified contributory retirement plan/contract is terminated, Department of Labor employees who were active participants at termination are to receive vesting credit for years of participation.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the named agencies (Banking and Finance; Insurance; Labor; Correctional Services; Health and Human Services; Veterans’ Affairs): the bill makes deputy directors and attorneys hired after enactment "at‑will," with salaries set by the agency director and approved by the Governor. Incumbent deputy directors/attorneys may irrevocably elect at‑will status in writing; until they do they remain in the State Personnel System. Temporary employees who were prior contributing retirement members and return before a 120‑day break must resume retirement system participation within 30 days. The bill also broadens the temporary‑employee definition (affecting legislative hires). No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for these agencies are included in the added text.
