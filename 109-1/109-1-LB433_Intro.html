Summary

LB433 amends State Personnel System and retirement membership provisions as follows:

- Department of Correctional Services: chief executive officers of all department facilities and the department medical director are exempted from the State Personnel System; personnel employed as pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, or psychologists by the department are likewise exempt.

- Department of Health and Human Services and Department of Veterans' Affairs: personnel employed as pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, psychologists, service area administrators, or facility operating officers are exempted. Chief executives of specified veterans' homes are also listed as noncovered.

- Department of Banking and Finance and Department of Insurance: deputies and examiners are exempted from the State Personnel System as provided in statutory cross-references, except those who remain covered.

- Department of Labor: employees who were active participants in a terminated contributory retirement plan shall be granted vesting credit for years of participation.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Departments of Banking and Finance, Insurance, Labor, Correctional Services, Health and Human Services, and Veterans' Affairs: deputy directors and attorneys hired after this act become at‑will employees, with salaries set by the agency director and subject to Governor approval. Incumbent deputy directors and attorneys may elect at any time to convert to at‑will status (the election is final); until they elect, they remain in the State Personnel System. Returning temporary employees who were prior retirement system contributors must rejoin the retirement system within 30 days if they return before a 120‑day break. The definition of temporary employee is clarified to include certain legislative hires, grant‑funded positions, and special projects. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
