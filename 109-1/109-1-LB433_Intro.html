Summary

LB433 amends exemptions from the State Personnel System and retirement membership rules as follows:

- Department of Banking and Finance and Department of Insurance: Deputies and examiners (per sections 8‑105 and 44‑119) are exempted from the State Personnel System, except those who remain covered.

- Department of Correctional Services: Chief executive officers of all facilities and the department medical director are exempt; personnel employed as pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, or psychologists by the department are also exempt.

- Department of Health and Human Services and Department of Veterans' Affairs: Personnel employed as pharmacists, physicians, psychiatrists, psychologists, service‑area administrators, or facility operating officers are exempt. Specific veterans' home chief executive officers are listed as exempt.

- Department of Labor: If the contributory retirement plan/contract under section 48‑609 is terminated, active Department of Labor participants will be granted vesting credit for years of participation.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language makes deputy directors and attorneys in the Departments of Banking and Finance, Insurance, Labor, Correctional Services, Health and Human Services, and Veterans' Affairs (and all other state agencies) hired after the bill’s effective date subject to at‑will employment. Directors set their salaries, subject to gubernatorial approval and credential-based levels. Current incumbents may elect to become at‑will (the election is irreversible); until they do they remain in the State Personnel System. Separately, returning temporary employees with less than a 120‑day break must rejoin the retirement system within 30 days, and the temporary‑employee definition is expanded to cover certain legislative limited‑term, grant‑funded, and special‑project hires. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are changed.
