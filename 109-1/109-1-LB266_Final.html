Summary

LB266 forbids local governments—defined as any city, village, county, or any agency or subdivision thereof—from enacting or enforcing ordinances that have the effect of imposing rent controls on private property. Exceptions allow ordinances that increase affordable housing supply via land‑use or inclusionary housing requirements and voluntary, contract‑based programs where property owners agree to rent restrictions. The prohibition overrides home‑rule charters, and any conflicting local ordinance is declared null and void. No state agencies, departments, or budgetary changes, appropriations, or funding shifts are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statewide prohibition on local governments (defined to include cities, villages, counties and their agencies or subdivisions) from enacting or enforcing ordinances that impose rent control on private property. "Ordinance" is defined broadly to include ordinances, resolutions, rules, or regulations. Exceptions permit local measures that (1) increase the supply of affordable housing through land‑use or inclusionary housing requirements, or (2) create voluntary, contract‑based programs in which private owners agree to limit rents. The prohibition overrides home rule charters, and any conflicting local ordinance is declared null and void. No state agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations changes are referenced.
