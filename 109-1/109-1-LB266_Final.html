Summary

LB266 prohibits local governments — defined as cities, villages, counties and any agency or subdivision thereof — from enacting or enforcing any ordinance (including resolutions, rules or regulations) that would have the effect of imposing rent controls on private property. Exceptions allow ordinances that (1) increase affordable housing supply via land‑use or inclusionary housing requirements, or (2) create voluntary, contractual programs in which private property owners agree to limit rents. The prohibition applies notwithstanding home‑rule charters, and any ordinance violating the bill is declared null and void. No state agencies are assigned new duties and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statewide ban on local rent-control ordinances. Defines "local government" to include cities, villages, counties and their agencies/subdivisions, and bars them from enacting or enforcing ordinances that impose rent controls on private property, except where state law otherwise permits or under two exceptions: (1) ordinances adopted to increase affordable housing via land‑use or inclusionary housing requirements; and (2) voluntary, contract‑based programs landlord(s) opt into that limit rent increases. The provision overrides home‑rule charters and declares any violating ordinance null and void. No state agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations changes are specified.
