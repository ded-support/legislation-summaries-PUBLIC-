Summary

LB441 amends the Building Construction Act (section 71-6401) to authorize state agencies, counties, cities, and villages that require building-permit inspections to permit virtual inspections under conditions specified by the bill. Authorized inspectors must be credentialed with the issuing state agency, county, city, or village, or be third-party inspectors registered or licensed with the State of Nebraska; self-inspections are excluded. Virtual inspection eligibility: residential buildings under three stories and 10,000 sq ft, with onsite personnel listed and live interaction required (except nonstructural reinspections, which may use video/photo). All permit or structural inspections must be performed by authorized inspectors, and inspection records must be made public if the structure is standing when requested. No appropriations or funding changes are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and inspection rules for entities issuing building permits: "authorized inspector" (credentialed by the state agency, county, city, or village issuing permits, or a third‑party inspector registered/licensed with the State of Nebraska; excludes self‑inspectors) and "inspection records" (pass/fail results with reasons tied to the permit address). Requires state agencies, counties, cities, or villages to allow virtual inspections for residential buildings under three stories and under 10,000 sq ft if the permit holder provides an onsite personnel list and the inspection is live with both parties; nonstructural reinspections may use photos/video. Mandates use of an authorized inspector for building or structural inspections and makes inspection records public if the structure remains standing. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
