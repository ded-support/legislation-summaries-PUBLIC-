Summary

LB441 amends the Building Construction Act (section 71-6401) to authorize virtual inspections and to require public access to certain inspection records. It defines "authorized inspector" as an individual credentialed by the state agency, county, city, or village issuing permits or a third‑party inspector registered/licensed with the State of Nebraska. State agencies, counties, cities, and villages that require inspections may allow live virtual inspections for residential buildings under three stories and 10,000 sq ft if the permit holder provides an onsite personnel list; nonstructural reinspections may use submitted video or photos. All required inspections for permits or structural projects must be performed by an authorized inspector, and inspection pass/fail records must be made public if the structure is standing at request time. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and procedures for building inspections applicable to any state agency, county, city, or village. Key points:

- Defines "authorized inspector" (credentialed by the state agency, county, city, village issuing permits, or a State-registered/licensed third-party inspector); excludes self‑inspections.

- Permitting entities may allow virtual inspections for residential buildings under three stories and <10,000 sq ft if the permit holder provides an onsite personnel list and the inspection is live (except nonstructural reinspections may use submitted video/photos).

- Requires authorized inspectors for inspections with a structural scope.

- Inspection records must be public if the structure is still standing when requested.

No funding, appropriations, or budget changes.
