Summary

LB531 prohibits the Department of Economic Development from requiring projects that receive funding from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to comply with the International Energy Conservation Code (IECC) as prescribed. The bill also creates an exception to the existing requirement that buildings constructed with state funds meet the 2018 IECC. No provisions in the bill change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Economic Development or the Affordable Housing Trust Fund.
