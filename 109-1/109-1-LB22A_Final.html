Summary

The bill appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program 348, to implement Legislative Bill 22. For FY2025‑26 it provides $380,628 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (cash) and $694,972 federal funds. For FY2026‑27 it provides $774,002 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (cash) and $1,377,198 federal funds. Each year's amounts are designated as state aid and may be used only for that purpose. The appropriated funds may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.
