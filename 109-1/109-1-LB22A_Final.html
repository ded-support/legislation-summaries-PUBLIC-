Summary

The bill appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program 348, to implement Legislative Bill 22. Appropriations:

- FY2025-26: $380,628 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (cash) and $694,972 federal funds.

- FY2026-27: $774,002 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (cash) and $1,377,198 federal funds.

The cash and federal amounts included in each year’s appropriation are designated for state aid only. The bill expressly prohibits using these appropriated funds for permanent or temporary employee salaries and per diems.

Department of Health and Human Services — Program 348: Appropriates funds to implement Legislative Bill 22 (109th Leg., 1st Sess., 2025). FY2025‑26: $380,628 cash from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund and $694,972 federal funds (total $1,075,600). FY2026‑27: $774,002 cash and $1,377,198 federal funds (total $2,151,200). All amounts are designated as state aid and may be used only for that purpose. The appropriated funds may not be expended for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.
