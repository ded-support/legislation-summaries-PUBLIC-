Summary

LB519 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §83-4,114.02 to require the Nebraska Department of Correctional Services to provide independent confirmatory testing, upon inmate request, before imposing disciplinary measures for drug or alcohol violations. The requirement covers positive urinalysis results and any positive screening, field, presumptive, or spot test of items suspected of being or containing contraband drugs. If the confirmatory test remains positive, the inmate must pay the cost of that test. The bill repeals the original section. No new appropriations are included; cost recovery is imposed on inmates when confirmation is positive.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language (subsection (1)(a)–(b)) expressly includes “urinalysis” and “any screening test, field test, presumptive test, or spot test of an item suspected of being or containing a contraband drug.” No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the excerpt.
