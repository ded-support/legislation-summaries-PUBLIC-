Summary

LB293 amends the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act. It requires any self‑funded health benefit plan offered by a PEO to use a third‑party administrator licensed in Nebraska, hold plan assets in an ERISA section 403 trust, maintain actuarially sound reserves, notify participants if the plan is self‑funded, and file quarterly and annual reports with the Department (Department of Labor). Reports must include financials, an actuary’s adequacy statement, evidence of stop‑loss coverage from an insurer licensed in Nebraska naming the PEO as insured (with 30‑day termination notice to the PEO and the Commissioner of Labor), and a compliance certificate. The Department may hold compliance hearings, issue orders to comply, and revoke a PEO’s registration for noncompliance. The bill also treats PEOs as employers for sponsoring retirement/welfare plans and exempts single‑PEO plans from MEWA registration. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds enforcement and reporting duties for the state "department" (the agency overseeing PEO registration) and references the Commissioner of Labor. PEO-offered health plans may be fully insured or, if self-funded, must use a licensed TPA, hold assets in an ERISA-style trust, maintain actuarially determined reserves, notify participants that the plan is self-funded, and file quarterly plus an annual report with the department within 90 days of plan fiscal year-end. Annual reports must include a financial statement, an actuary's sufficiency statement, confirmation of stop‑loss insurance (issued by a state‑licensed insurer that must give the PEO and the Commissioner of Labor 30 days' written notice before termination), and a compliance certificate. The department may hold hearings (30 days' notice), issue compliance orders (to be met within 30 days), and revoke PEO registration under the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act for failure to comply. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
