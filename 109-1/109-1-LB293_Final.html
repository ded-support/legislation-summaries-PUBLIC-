Summary

LB293 amends the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act (sections 48‑2706 and 48‑2708). Key agency-related provisions:

- Requires professional employer organizations (PEOs) offering self-funded health benefit plans to file quarterly reports and an annual report within 90 days after the plan fiscal year with "the department" detailing: financial statements; an actuary's solvency statement; confirmation of stop‑loss insurance (aggregate excess >125% of expected claims) issued by an insurer licensed in Nebraska; and a certificate of compliance. Stop‑loss policies must notify the PEO and the Commissioner of Labor at least 30 days before termination.

- Authorizes the department to hold compliance hearings, issue orders to comply within 30 days, and revoke a PEO's registration for noncompliance.

- Declares PEO‑sponsored employee welfare/health plans a single employee welfare benefit plan, exempting them from the Multiple Employer Welfare Arrangement Act, and treats the PEO as the employer under the Small Employer Health Insurance Availability Act; insurers must accept and insure eligible employees.

No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for professional employer organizations (PEOs) that offer health plans: plans must be fully insured or, if self‑funded, use a licensed third‑party administrator, hold assets in an ERISA-style trust, maintain actuarially sound reserves, notify employees the plan is self‑funded, and file with the department quarterly and within 90 days after plan fiscal year end. Quarterly/annual reports to the department must include a financial statement, an actuary’s adequacy statement, evidence of aggregate stop‑loss insurance naming the PEO and requiring the insurer to give the PEO and the Commissioner of Labor 30 days’ written notice before termination, and a certificate of state-law compliance. The department (via 30 days’ written notice) may hold compliance hearings, issue corrective orders within 30 days after a hearing, and revoke PEO registration under the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act for failure to comply. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
