Summary

Bill LB293 amends the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act, imposing regulatory requirements enforced by the Department of Labor (department) and the Commissioner of Labor. Self-funded PEO health plans must use a third‑party administrator licensed in Nebraska, hold plan assets in an ERISA-style trust, maintain actuarially sound reserves, provide quarterly and annual reports to the department, and carry stop‑loss insurance issued by an insurer licensed in Nebraska that names the PEO as insured and provides 30 days' termination notice to the PEO and Commissioner. The department may hold compliance hearings (30‑day notice), issue 30‑day corrective orders, and revoke PEO registration for noncompliance. The bill also specifies state-law treatment of PEO-sponsored welfare and small‑employer health plans. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for state enforcement and reporting related to professional employer organizations' (PEOs) self‑funded health plans. Key agencies/roles:

- "The department" (state oversight agency) must receive quarterly reports and an annual filing within 90 days of plan fiscal year end; may hold compliance hearings with 30 days' notice, issue compliance orders within 30 days after a hearing, and revoke a PEO's registration under the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act if the PEO fails to comply.

- Commissioner of Labor must be named on stop‑loss insurance termination notices; stop‑loss insurers must be licensed in the state and provide 30 days' written notice to both the PEO and the Commissioner before termination.

Also requires use of licensed third‑party administrators and licensed insurers. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
