Summary

LB406 revises section 2-1201 to define and restate provisions for the State Racing and Gaming Commission. Key agency/authority items: members (seven) are appointed by the Governor and subject to legislative confirmation; the Governor may remove members for cause. Membership composition, term lengths, geographic and partisan limits, vacancy filling, and prohibitions on financial interests in licensed racetrack enclosures or authorized gaming operators (per the Nebraska Racetrack Gaming Act) are specified. Compensation is set at $1,000 per month, adjustable every two years by up to the CPI change; members are reimbursed for expenses per sections 81-1174 to 81-1177 and must be bonded/insured under section 11-201. The original section is repealed.
