Summary

Bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §2-1201 governing the State Racing and Gaming Commission. The Commission remains a seven‑member body appointed by the Governor with legislative confirmation; members may be removed for cause by the Governor. Membership rules specify one member from each congressional district (as of Jan 1, 2010) and four at‑large seats with staggered terms (including historical appointment dates and two at‑large members added May 26, 2021). Limits: no more than four members of one political party, residency caps by district and county, and gubernatorial appointment to fill vacancies. Compensation is set at $1,000 per month, adjustable biennially up to the CPI‑W change; members receive expense reimbursement per §§81‑1174 to 81‑1177 and must be bonded/insured per §11‑201. Members may not hold financial interests in licensed racetracks or authorized gaming operators. The original section is repealed.
