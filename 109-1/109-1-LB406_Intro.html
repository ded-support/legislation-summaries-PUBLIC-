Summary

LB406 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §2-1201 to (re)define the State Racing and Gaming Commission. Key provisions: the seven-member commission is appointed by the Governor with legislative confirmation and removable for cause; membership set by congressional-district representation (as of Jan 1, 2010) plus at-large seats with specified initial appointment dates and subsequent four-year terms; political- and residency-based limits on composition; vacancies filled by gubernatorial appointment. Member pay is $1,000 per month, adjustable biennially up to the change in the CPI for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers. Members are reimbursed under §§81-1174–81-1177, must be bonded/insured per §11-201, and may not hold financial interests in licensed racetracks or authorized gaming operators. The bill repeals the original section. No new appropriations are specified.
