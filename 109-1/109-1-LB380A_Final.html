Summary

Appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services to implement LB380.

- Program 33: $150,000 from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund for FY2025-26 and $150,000 for FY2026-27. Expenditures from these appropriations for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are limited to no more than $100,000 in each fiscal year.

- Program 348: FY2025-26 — $2,118,003 from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund and $4,212,005 federal funds; FY2026-27 — $4,386,006 Cash Fund and $8,424,011 federal funds. Specified amounts in this program are designated for state aid only. Funds appropriated in Section 2 may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.
