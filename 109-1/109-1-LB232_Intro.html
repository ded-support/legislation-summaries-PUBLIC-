Summary

LB232 amends Nebraska insurance law to require insurers issuing life policies subject to assignment as security for indebtedness to provide known assignees at least 30 days’ advance notice of pending lapse/termination. Insurers may send electronic notice (assignee must request it in writing before default; request becomes effective within 10 business days) and may charge the owner up to $2.50 per notice. Assignees must promptly send a release upon termination of assignment.

The bill also allows Nebraska residents age 65+ with individual premium-paying life policies to designate one third-party to receive notices of cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional renewal (written designation and acceptance required; termination procedures specified).

No state agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Key additions:

Requires insurers to give any known assignee (for policies assigned as security) at least 30 days' notice of pending lapse/termination. Insurers must electronically notify assignees who request notice; insurers may charge the policy owner up to $2.50 per notice. Specifies written/electronic request procedures, effective timing, required info, and that assignees must mail a release upon termination of rights.

- Defines "senior-citizen insured" (Nebraska resident age 65+) and permits such insureds to designate one third-party to receive notices of cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional renewal. Specifies designation, acceptance, termination, dual transmission to insured and designee, and annual insurer notice of the option.
