Summary

LB232 requires private life insurers to provide advance notice to assignees and to designated third parties in certain circumstances.

Key provisions:

- Section 1: For life policies assigned as security, insurers may not lapse/terminate for premium default unless any known assignee receives notice at least 30 days prior. Assignees may request electronic notice (written request, effective 10 business days after receipt); insurers may charge up to $2.50 per notice. Assignees must deliver releases upon termination of assignment.

- Section 2: Defines "senior-citizen insured" (Nebraska resident age 65+) and allows such insureds to designate one third‑party to receive notices of cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional renewal (written/electronic acceptance required). Annual notice to seniors of this option is required unless already designated.

No state agencies or departments are named, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds insurer notice and third‑party designation rules for life policies. Key points: insurers must give any known assignee at least 30 days’ notice before a lapse/termination of a life policy assigned as security; electronic notice is required if the assignee requests it, and insurers may charge up to $2.50 per notice. Assignees must make a written (certified mail or electronic) request before default, effective within 10 business days, and must provide contact, insured name, and policy number; assignees must mail a release upon termination of their assignment. Separately, defines “senior‑citizen insured” (Nebraska resident 65+) and requires insurers to allow such seniors to designate one third‑party to receive notices of cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional renewal (with written acceptance and contact); termination procedures, duplicate notice to the insured, no designee liability, and annual insurer notice of the option are added. No government agencies are mentioned and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
