Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds consumer-protection requirements for life insurers (Nebraska references):

- For life policies assigned as collateral, insurers must notify any known assignee at least 30 days before lapse/termination; electronic notice is allowed and insurers may charge up to $2.50 per notice. Assignees must request such notices in writing (certified mail or electronically) before default; requests take effect within 10 business days. Assignees must mail a release upon termination of their assignment. Excludes nonrenewal and does not alter assignees’ contractual rights.

- For individual life policies on Nebraska senior citizens (age 65+), insurers must allow the insured to designate one third-party to receive cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional renewal notices (written designation via certified mail or electronically; effective in 10 business days). Designees may terminate; receiving notices does not create liability. Insurers must annually inform seniors of this option unless a designation exists.
