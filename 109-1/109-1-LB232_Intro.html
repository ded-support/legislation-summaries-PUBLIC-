Summary

LB232 requires insurers issuing life insurance in Nebraska to notify assignees that a policy subject to assignment as security for indebtedness will lapse or terminate. Insurers must provide at least 30 days' notice to any known assignee; electronic delivery is permitted if requested, and insurers may charge the policyowner up to $2.50 per notice. Assignees must make written requests (including contact, insured name, and policy number) before default; requests become effective within ten business days. Assignees must promptly mail releases upon termination of assignment. For individual policies on Nebraska residents aged 65+, insurers must allow the owner/insured to designate one third‑party to receive notices of cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional renewal (designation and acceptance in writing; effective within ten business days). The bill references no state agencies and contains no funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds insurer obligations and notice procedures (no state agencies or funding changes). Key points:

- Requires insurers to give any known assignee of a life policy assigned as collateral at least 30 days' notice before lapse/termination; insurers must provide electronic notice if the assignee requests it. Insurers may charge the owner or policy for the reasonable cost of such notice, capped at $2.50 per notice.

- Specifies how assignees request notice (written, certified mail or electronic, effective within 10 business days; must provide contact, insured name, policy number) and to mail a release when assignment rights end. Does not apply to nonrenewal or alter contractual assignee rights.

- Defines "senior-citizen insured" (65+) and requires insurers to allow a senior to designate one third-party to receive cancellation, nonrenewal, and conditional-renewal notices, with similar written/electronic procedures, termination rules, dual transmission to insurer and designee, annual notice of the option, and a liability disclaimer for designees.
