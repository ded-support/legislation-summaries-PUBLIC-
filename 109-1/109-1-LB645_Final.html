Summary

This bill amends funding and administrative provisions for Nebraska retirement systems.

Key agencies/funds:

- Retirement Board: continues actuarial certification of state deposits to the School Retirement Fund and Service Annuity Fund.

- Director of Administrative Services and State Treasurer: State Treasurer transfers certified state deposits on the Director’s warrant from appropriated funds.

- State Investment Officer: invests Service Annuity Fund pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Appropriations Committee/Legislature: may review/recommend additional transfers to Class V school districts.

Funding changes:

- Employee contribution rates to the School Retirement Fund become tiered effective July 1, 2025 (9.75%, 8.75%, 8.0%, or 7.25% based on funded ratio).

- State contribution previously a flat 2% phases to a funded-ratio–based schedule (2%, 0.7%, or none) beginning July 1, 2025.

- For Class V systems, from July 1, 2025 the state will transfer 2% of member compensation (plus specified service‑annuity amounts) to Class V districts.

- Appropriation mechanics remain: transfers drawn from state-appropriated funds as certified. Emergency clause makes the act effective on enactment.

Also adjusts Nebraska State Patrol survivor benefits (effective dates noted).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Agencies/departments referenced: the state, Class V school districts and their district retirement systems, the School Retirement Fund, and the retirement board (annual actuarial valuation per §84-1503).

- Effective July 1, 2025: employee contribution rates to the School Retirement Fund become a funded‑ratio sliding scale (9.75% if <96%; 8.75% if 96–<98%; 8.0% if 98–<100%; 7.25% if ≥100% of actuarial assets). Deposits are transmitted with employer contributions.

- Beginning in 2025: the state must make additional annual funded‑ratio based deposits into the School Retirement Fund (2.0% of member compensation if funded ratio <96%; 0.7% if 96–<100%; none if ≥100%).

- For Class V districts, the state will annually transfer an amount equal to 2% of member compensation (plus the amount under §79‑966). The Legislature states this does not create new state liability for district funding obligations.

- Effective July 1, 2027: surviving spouse/children benefits are increased to 100% of the officer’s annuity as specified.
