Summary

Provides amendments affecting the Nebraska School Retirement Fund, the Service Annuity Fund, Class V school district retirement systems, and the Nebraska State Patrol Retirement System.

Key agency/finance changes:

- State Retirement Board/Director of Administrative Services/State Treasurer: continue to certify and effectuate annual state deposits and transfers; transfers to the Service Annuity Fund remain payable and invested by the State Investment Officer under state investment law.

- Class V school districts: beginning July 1, 2025 the state must transfer to Class V districts an amount equal to 2.0% of members' compensation plus the actuarially determined service-annuity payment; districts may petition the Legislature’s Appropriations Committee for additional transfers.

- School Retirement Fund: beginning July 1, 2025 employee contribution rates become tiered by funded ratio (9.75%, 8.75%, 8.00%, or 7.25%); state contribution similarly becomes tiered (2.0%, 0.7%, or 0%), calculated July 1 each year.

- Nebraska State Patrol/State Patrol Retirement Fund: survivor benefits increase from 75% to 100% of an officer’s annuity for surviving spouses beginning July 1, 2027 (affects post- and pre-retirement deaths).

- Emergency clause: act effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds required transfers and contribution schedules affecting the State, Class V school districts, the district retirement systems, the School Retirement Fund, and the retirement board (which must produce the annual actuarial valuation under section 84-1503). Key funding changes:

- Beginning July 1, 2025 the State must annually transfer to Class V districts’ retirement funds an amount equal to 2% of members’ compensation (in addition to other statutory transfers) payable on or before July 1. Legislature clarifies this does not shift district funding liability to the State.

- Employee contribution rates (effective July 1, 2025) are set on funded-ratio tiers: 9.75%, 8.75%, 8.00%, or 7.25% of compensation.

- State deposits into the School Retirement Fund (also beginning 2025) vary by funded ratio: 2.0%, 0.7%, or none. Deposits transmit with employer contributions.

- Survivor annuity benefits for officers increase to 100% for spouses/eligible children beginning July 1, 2027.
