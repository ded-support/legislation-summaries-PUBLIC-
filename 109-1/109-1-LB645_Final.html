Summary

This bill amends retirement funding and benefit provisions and names responsible state actors. Key agency roles:

- Retirement Board and its actuary: certify state deposits and determine required transfers to the School Retirement Fund and Service Annuity Fund; administrator of Class V systems must provide member data.

- Director of Administrative Services and State Treasurer: execute certified state transfers/appropriations to the School Retirement Fund.

- State investment officer: invest Service Annuity Fund assets.

- Legislature/Appropriations Committee: may review and authorize additional transfers to Class V district systems.

Funding changes:

- Employee contribution to the School Retirement Fund becomes a tiered rate (7.25%–9.75%) effective July 1, 2025 based on funded ratio.

- State contribution to the School Retirement Fund becomes tiered (0%–2% of payroll) from July 1, 2025 based on funded ratio.

- State continues targeted transfers to Class V district funds and the Service Annuity Fund to pay normal cost and amortize unfunded liabilities; additional transfers require legislative approval.

Nebraska State Patrol: survivor benefits increase from 75% to 100% of the officer’s annuity beginning July 1, 2027 in applicable circumstances. The act is effective on passage due to an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds required state transfers and new contribution formulas for school retirement plans and survivor benefits. Key agencies/entities: the State, Class V school districts and their retirement systems, the School Retirement Fund, and the retirement board (annual actuarial valuation per §84-1503). Funding changes:

- Beginning July 1, 2025, employee contribution rates to the School Retirement Fund are tiered by funded ratio: 9.75%, 8.75%, 8.00%, or 7.25% of compensation.

- State deposits into the School Retirement Fund are tiered by funded ratio: 2.0% of member compensation if <96%; 0.7% if 96%–<100%; none at ≥100%. Transfers to Class V district retirement systems are to be made annually by July 1. Legislature states these state transfers do not assume district funding liabilities.

- Survivor annuity benefits changed to 100% of officer’s annuity to surviving spouse and dependent children beginning July 1, 2027.
