Summary

Bill LB645 amends state retirement funding and administrative procedures, naming agencies and fiscal actions:

- Retirement Board: certifies annual state deposits to the School Retirement Fund; administrator of Class V systems must supply membership data for actuarial calculations.

- State Treasurer and Director of Administrative Services: execute transfers from appropriated state funds on the Board’s warrant.

- State Investment Officer: may invest amounts in the Service Annuity Fund under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Appropriations Committee/Legislature: may consider supplemental transfers to Class V districts upon request following an actuarial finding.

Key funding changes:

- Employee contributions to the School Retirement Fund become funded‑ratio‑based starting July 1, 2025 (9.75%, 8.75%, 8.0%, or 7.25% depending on funded ratio).

- State deposits to the School Retirement Fund become funded‑ratio‑based (2.0%, 0.7%, or 0%) beginning July 1, 2025.

- Beginning July 1, 2025, the state will transfer 2% of compensation annually to Class V school districts for their retirement systems; Service Annuity Fund transfers for normal cost and amortization continue.

- Nebraska State Patrol Retirement: survivor benefits increase to 100% of an officer’s annuity effective July 1, 2027.

The act contains an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds funding and benefit rules for Class V school district and statewide school retirement systems. Key points:

- State must annually transfer to a Class V district’s retirement fund an amount equal to 2% of members’ compensation beginning FY 2026 (transfer due by July 1); statute clarifies this does not shift district liability to the state.

- Employee contribution rates to the School Retirement Fund (set July 1 each year, based on the funded ratio from the actuarial valuation prepared for the retirement board under §84-1503) are tiered beginning July 1, 2025: <96% = 9.75%; 96–<98% = 8.75%; 98–<100% = 8.00%; ≥100% = 7.25%. Deposits transmitted with employer contributions.

- State deposits into the School Retirement Fund (also based on funded ratio) beginning 2025: <96% = 2.0% of pay; 96–<100% = 0.7%; ≥100% = none.

- Effective July 1, 2027, surviving spouses and dependent children receive 100% of an officer’s annuity (spouse for life; children under 19 divided equally).
