Summary

LB397 directs actions by the Director of Insurance (defined as "director") in amended section 44-3,158: the Director may contract (up to five years) with one or more workers' compensation insurers to provide assigned‑risk coverage, consider cost, loss‑control/claims handling and insurer financial condition when selecting insurers, require insurer/industry loss‑sharing and assessments tied to premiums written or pool contributions, and promulgate implementing rules. The bill also terminates the Workplace Safety Consultation Program Cash Fund and requires the State Treasurer to transfer any remaining balance to the General Fund. The bill contains no explicit provisions referencing the Department of Labor.
