Summary

LB397 amends §44-3,158 to (a) define “director” as the Director of Insurance and (b) authorize the Director of Insurance to contract (up to five years) with one or more insurers to provide assigned-risk workers’ compensation coverage, using specified selection criteria; to structure agreements where an assigned-risk insurer covers a portion of losses with remaining losses assessed against all Nebraska workers’ compensation insurers and intergovernmental risk pools; and to establish an alternative shared assigned-risk system and adopt rules if needed. The bill repeals the prior versions of affected sections. It also terminates the Workplace Safety Consultation Program Cash Fund and directs the State Treasurer to transfer any remaining balance to the General Fund. No explicit Department of Labor provisions appear.
