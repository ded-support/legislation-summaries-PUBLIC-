Summary

LB132 (Kauth) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §60-6,273 to specify that evidence a person was not wearing an occupant protection system or three-point safety belt at the time of injury is admissible in civil proceedings for determining (1) liability or proximate cause and (2) mitigation of damages. The bill repeals the original section. No state agencies or departments are named or affected, and there are no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language clarifying that a "civil proceeding" includes actions undertaken to determine (1) liability and (2) mitigation. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
