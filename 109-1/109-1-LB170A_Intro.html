Summary

The bill directs the State Treasurer to transfer $100,000,000 from the General Fund to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in FY2025‑26 and another $100,000,000 in FY2026‑27, with transfer dates and amounts to be set by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services. It appropriates $100,000,000 (FY2025‑26) and $100,000,000 (FY2026‑27) from that Fund to the Department of Revenue, Program 121, for implementation of LB170. Each year’s appropriation is Cash Funds designated solely for state aid and may not be used for permanent/temporary employee salaries or per diems.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): The budget administrator in DAS’s budget division is authorized to direct the timing and amounts of two transfers from the General Fund to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund — $100,000,000 to be transferred during FY2025-26 and $100,000,000 during FY2026-27.

- Department of Revenue: The bill appropriates $100,000,000 from the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund to the Department of Revenue (Program 121) for each of FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 to implement Legislative Bill 170 (2025). Each appropriation is designated as $100,000,000 Cash Funds for state aid only. These funds may not be used for permanent/temporary salaries or per diems of state employees.
