Summary

LB549 amends §§79-804, 79-805, and 79-814.01 to permit local school boards to employ (including as volunteers) chaplains—defined as licensed/ordained clergy trained for secular settings—without holding state teaching or administrative certificates. School boards must adopt policies on employment, discipline, continued education, and termination. Chaplains are subject to criminal history checks conducted by the Nebraska State Patrol (including FBI fingerprint checks at the Commissioner of Education's request); the Commissioner may deny eligibility for employment based on specified convictions. The State Department of Education/Commissioner retains notification and eligibility functions; county treasurers’ withholding and board personal-liability provisions in §§79-804/79-805 are made inapplicable to chaplains. Boards set applicant fees for background checks, limited to actual direct costs. Requests for closed hearings invoke the Open Meetings Act. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "chaplain" and authorizes local school boards to employ (including volunteers) chaplains to provide academic, career, emotional, and behavioral supports without requiring a state teaching or services certificate. Chaplains must undergo a criminal history record check pursuant to section 79-814.01 and pay any required fees; the Commissioner of Education may deny eligibility based on that check and, if denied, the school board may not employ the individual. School boards must adopt policies governing employment, discipline, continued education, and termination of chaplains. The provision does not compel boards to hire chaplains and excludes chaplains employed under section 1 of the act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
