Summary

LB549 authorizes local school boards to employ (including volunteer) chaplains without a Nebraska teaching/admin certificate. Key government roles and changes:

- Commissioner of Education: may order criminal-history checks, and may deny a chaplain’s eligibility based on felony or certain misdemeanor convictions; notification/eligibility rules apply.

- Nebraska State Patrol: conducts fingerprinting and national criminal history checks at the commissioner's request and reports results to the commissioner.

- State Department of Education: retains existing personnel reporting framework (annual fall personnel report); chaplains are exempted from certificate registration requirements.

- County treasurer and school-board liability provisions that permit withholding of funds or personal liability for employing uncertified teachers/administrators are amended so they do not apply to chaplains.

- Applicants bear the actual direct costs of required background checks (per statute).

No appropriations, new funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "chaplain" and authorizes local school boards to employ (including volunteers) clergy to provide academic, career, emotional and behavioral health supports without requiring a state teaching or specialist certificate. Requires applicants to undergo a criminal history record information check pursuant to section 79-814.01 (applicant pays any required fees) and allows the Commissioner of Education to deny eligibility based on that check. Requires school boards to adopt policies governing employment, discipline, continuing education, and termination of chaplains. Clarifies hiring is optional and exempts chaplains covered by section 1 of the act. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
