Summary

LB549 authorizes school boards to employ chaplains (including volunteers) to provide academic, career, emotional, and behavioral supports without holding a state teaching or administrative certificate. Key government entities and roles:

- Commissioner of Education / State Department of Education: retains authority to require criminal history checks, deny chaplain eligibility based on felony or misdemeanor convictions for abuse/neglect/sexual misconduct, and be notified of certain personnel actions. Chaplains are exempted from certificate registration and related liability provisions in sections 79-804 and 79-805.

- Nebraska State Patrol: conducts state and national criminal history checks and transmits reports to the Commissioner; fingerprints forwarded to the FBI as requested.

- Local school boards: must adopt employment policies, ensure checks, and set processing fees (borne by applicant and limited to actual direct costs).

- County treasurer: prior withholding provisions for uncertified staff remain but do not apply to chaplains.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. Original sections amended/repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that school boards may hire (including volunteers) chaplains—defined as licensed/ordained/endorsed clergy trained for secular settings—to provide academic, career, emotional, and behavioral supports. Chaplains are not required to hold teaching or certification credentials. Chaplains must undergo a criminal history record information check under §79-814.01 and pay any fees; the Commissioner of Education may deny employment eligibility based on that check. School boards must adopt policies on hiring, discipline, continuing education, and termination for uncertified chaplains. The provision does not mandate school boards to employ chaplains and excludes chaplains covered by section 1 of the act. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are added.
