Summary

Bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-602. It directs the department responsible for vital records to update standard registration and certification forms to support registration, statistical research or quality improvement, and consistency with federal forms. It expands authorized recipients of health data and statistical research information to include the United States Public Health Service (or its successor), government health agencies, statewide quality improvement initiatives, and researchers approved through a department-established application process. The department must publish at least annual, de-identified analyses of information received on its website. Confidentiality is preserved and any release under the section does not convert the information into public records. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the responsible state agency publish at least annually on its website information about statewide quality improvement initiatives and a clear, expedited application process for those initiatives. No specific agency is named and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
