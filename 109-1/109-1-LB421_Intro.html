Summary

LB421 requires authorized gaming operators to check the state's collection system (per the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act) before paying sports-wagering winnings. If a winner has a debt or outstanding state tax liability, the operator must deduct the identified amount and remit the deducted funds to the Department of Revenue to be credited against that liability under section 9-1306.

The bill also revises gaming-tax distributions. For non-sports gross gaming revenue: 75% to the State Treasurer (2.5% Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 2.5% General Fund, 70% Property Tax Credit Cash Fund) and 25% to the county treasurer (county or split with city/village). For sports-wagering gross revenue: remitted to State Treasurer as 3% Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 3.5% Racetrack Gaming Fund, 3.5% Racing Cash Fund, and 90% Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions define online sports wagering/platform providers, require wagering to be run by authorized gaming operators (licensed racetrack or partner), permit servers outside Nebraska subject to federal law, limit operators to one platform/provider, and require operators/providers to submit platform controls to the Racing and Gaming Commission under a specified review timeline before offering wagering.

For the Department of Revenue: sports-wagering gross gaming tax collected under section 9-1203 is to be remitted to the State Treasurer and allocated: 3% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 3.5% to the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund, 3.5% to the Racing Cash Fund, and 90% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. No direct appropriations to the Department are added.
