Summary

LB421 directs that, prior to paying sports-wagering winnings, authorized gaming operators must check the state's collection system and, if a winner has a debt or outstanding state tax liability, deduct the identified amount and remit the deduction to the Department of Revenue to be credited against that debt under section 9-1306 (Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text creates definitions and a regulatory framework for online sports wagering and establishes tax remittance for gross gaming revenue from sports wagering. Under the amendment (section 9-1203), the Department of Revenue would collect and remit sports-wagering tax receipts to the State Treasurer as follows: 3.0% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 3.5% to the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund, 3.5% to the Racing Cash Fund, and 90.0% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. No direct changes to Department of Revenue appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
