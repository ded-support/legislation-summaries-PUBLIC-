Summary

LB421 requires authorized gaming operators to check the state collection system before paying any sports-wagering winnings. If a winner has a debt or outstanding state tax liability identified in that system (per the Gambling Winnings Setoff for Outstanding Debt Act and definitions in section 9-1303), the operator must deduct the identified amount from the winnings, remit the deducted amount to the Department of Revenue, and credit it against the debtor's liability under section 9-1306; any net winnings are paid to the winner.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and a regulatory framework for online sports wagering, including operator/platform-provider roles, server-location rules, and commission approval timelines for platform controls. For the Department of Revenue: the bill directs collection/remittance of the tax on gross gaming revenue from sports wagering to the State Treasurer with the following distribution: 3.0% to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund, 3.5% to the Racing and Gaming Commission’s Racetrack Gaming Fund, 3.5% to the Racing Cash Fund, and 90.0% to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. The measure implies the Department will collect and remit these taxes but contains no new appropriations or direct budget allocations to the Department of Revenue.
