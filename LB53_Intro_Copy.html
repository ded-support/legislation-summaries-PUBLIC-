Summary

LB53 declares the Legislature’s intent not to criminalize pregnancy or punish people for pregnancy outcomes and establishes both criminal and civil immunity for individuals regarding their own pregnancy outcomes (including stillbirth, miscarriage, intentional termination, or any non–live-birth outcome). The bill does not mention or direct any specific state agencies or departments, nor does it include any funding, appropriations, or budgetary provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a prohibition on citation, arrest, or prosecution for any person based on the outcome of their own pregnancy (including stillbirth, miscarriage, intentional termination, or any non–live birth) and grants civil‑action immunity for such pregnancy outcomes.
