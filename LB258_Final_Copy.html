Summary

The bill requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 each year (beginning October 15, 2026), the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. It also authorizes an employer to extend a new employee’s 90‑day training‑wage period for an additional 90 days while the employee participates in on‑the‑job training that (a) requires necessary technical or personal skills and (b) is approved by the Commissioner of Labor. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are specified.

Summary for Department of Labor:

- Establishes a youth minimum wage of $13.50/hour for employees ages 14–15 who are not emancipated; the youth rate will increase by 1.5% (rounded to nearest cent) beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every fifth year thereafter.
