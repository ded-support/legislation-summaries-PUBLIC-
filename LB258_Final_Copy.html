Summary

LB258 requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 each year commencing October 15, 2026, the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. The bill also authorizes the Commissioner of Labor to approve on-the-job training programs that permit employers to extend a new-employee training wage for an additional 90-day period. No provisions in the bill change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two wage schedules: a youth minimum wage allowing employers to pay $13.50/hour to employees age 14–15 (not emancipated), with that youth rate increasing 1.5% (rounded to the nearest cent) beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every five years thereafter; and a training wage set at $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026, then increasing 1.5% (rounded) annually beginning Jan 1, 2027. The amendment does not include any Department of Labor-specific funding, appropriation, or budget allocations. (Under existing law, the Department of Labor would implement/enforce any new minimum-wage requirements, but no new funding for those duties is provided in the added text.)
