Summary

The bill requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 each year (commencing October 15, 2026), the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. No funding, appropriations, or other budgetary changes or allocations for the Department of Labor are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a youth minimum wage and a separate training-wage schedule. Key points affecting the Department of Labor:

- Establishes a youth minimum wage of $13.50/hour for employees aged 14–15 (non‑emancipated). That youth rate rises by 1.5% (rounded to the nearest cent) beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every five years thereafter.

- Sets a training wage of $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026; beginning Jan 1, 2027 the training wage increases annually by 1.5% (rounded).

- Impacts for the Department of Labor: update regulations and employer guidance, revise postings/forms, enforce compliance and monitoring. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
