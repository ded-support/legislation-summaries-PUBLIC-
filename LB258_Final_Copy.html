Summary

The bill requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 each year beginning October 15, 2026, the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Labor):

- Establishes a youth minimum wage of $13.50/hour for employees ages 14–15 (not emancipated). Beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every fifth year thereafter it increases by 1.5%, rounded to the nearest cent.

- Sets a training wage at $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026. Beginning Jan 1, 2027 and annually thereafter the training wage increases by 1.5%, rounded to the nearest cent.

- Implications for the Department of Labor: responsibility to implement, update rules/guidance, and enforce the new rates for covered youth and trainees. The amendment contains no explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.
