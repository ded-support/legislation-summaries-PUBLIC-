Summary

LB258 requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish, no later than October 15 each year beginning October 15, 2026, the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1. The bill also ties eligibility for an extended (second 90‑day) training‑wage period to on‑the‑job training that is approved by the Commissioner of Labor. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor are in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary for Department of Labor: The amendment sets a youth minimum wage of $13.50/hour for employees aged 14–15 (non‑emancipated). Starting Jan 1, 2030 and every five years thereafter the youth rate rises by 1.5%, rounded to the nearest cent. A training wage is set at $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026; beginning Jan 1, 2027 it will increase annually by 1.5%, rounded to the nearest cent.
