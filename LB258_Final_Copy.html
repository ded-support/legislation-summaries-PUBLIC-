Summary

LB258 requires the Nebraska Department of Labor to calculate and publish the minimum wage rate that will take effect the following January 1 no later than October 15 of each year, commencing October 15, 2026. The bill includes no provisions altering Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds youth and training wage rules: employers may pay 14–15‑year‑olds (non‑emancipated) a youth minimum wage of $13.50/hour. The youth wage will rise by 1.5% (rounded to nearest cent) beginning Jan 1, 2030 and every fifth year thereafter. The training wage is set at $13.50/hour through Dec 31, 2026, then will increase by 1.5% (rounded) annually beginning Jan 1, 2027.

Department of Labor implications: DOL would implicitly be responsible for administering and enforcing these new youth/training wage rates (guidance, compliance, investigations), but the text contains no explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
