Summary

LB409 amends membership and reporting provisions for the Nebraska Power Review Board. Appointments remain by the Governor with legislative approval and four‑year terms, but once one current member vacancy/term occurs the five‑member board must include: at least one engineer, one attorney, and at least one licensed journeyman electrician who is affiliated with a nonprofit labor organization holding a 501(c)(5) exemption and selected from a list provided by that organization, plus two additional persons. Conflicts restrictions and recusal timelines remain. The bill preserves member pay rates and caps (standard members $100/day to $7,000/yr; the Southwest Power Pool Regional State Committee designee $250/day to $35,000/yr; SPP‑related pay capped at $40,000/yr) and reimbursement rules. The board must submit a biennial report with annual data to the Governor, Clerk of the Legislature (electronically), and the Department of Environment and Energy, which will consider the data in its own reports. Original section repealed.
