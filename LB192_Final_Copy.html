Summary

The bill amends section 68-1017.02 to direct the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to maximize Nebraska participation in the federal Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), seek federal funding, minimize use of state General Funds, and employ staff to evaluate available options and produce an annual report to the Legislature. DHHS may apply for and accept gifts, grants, and donations and must submit a state outreach plan to the Food and Nutrition Service of the United States Department of Agriculture for approval and federal matching funds. DHHS may partner with counties or nonprofits, be exempt from implementing outreach if insufficient private/federal funds exist, create a TANF-funded categorical eligibility policy, limit seeking SNAP work‑requirement waivers, and may require participation in employment and training per federal law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the state agency that administers SNAP (typically the Department of Agriculture or the Department of Health and Human Services) not to seek, apply for, accept, or renew federal waivers of SNAP work requirements, except that the agency may use the federal statutory option to exempt individuals under 7 U.S.C. 2015(o)(6). It also authorizes the department to require non‑exempt recipients to participate in SNAP employment and training programs under 7 U.S.C. 2015(d). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
