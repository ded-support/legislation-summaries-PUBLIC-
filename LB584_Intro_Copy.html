Summary

LB584 authorizes (section 29-2204(5)) a court that wants more detailed information before imposing sentence to commit an offender to the Department of Correctional Services. While so committed, the Department must conduct a complete study of the offender as provided in section 29-2204.03. The bill contains no provisions altering funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary for Department of Correctional Services:

- The bill revises felony sentencing language for Classes IA, IB, IC, ID, II and IIA (clarifying maximum and minimum terms) and adds a new subsection requiring that when the offender was under 18 at the time of the offense, the court must set the sentence's minimum and maximum as provided by section 28-105.02.

- No provisions in the added text alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.
