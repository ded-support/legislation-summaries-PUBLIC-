Summary

LB108 amends the Cities of the First Class Firefighters Retirement Act (sections 16-1024 and 16-1025). Key agency/financial effects:

- Cities of the first class and the Firefighters Retirement System: scheduled employee contribution increases to 8.7% (10/1/24–9/30/25), 10.7% (10/1/25–9/30/26), and 12.7% (from 10/1/26). Employer (city) contributions increase to 13% (through 9/30/25), 14% (10/1/25–9/30/26), and 15% (from 10/1/26).

- Carveout (effective Mar 1, 2025): firefighters and cities in cities with >60,000 population located in counties >100,000 (and covered by an absolute coverage group) remain at 6.5% employee and 13% employer contributions.

- Offsets (effective July 20, 2024): a 6.2% salary offset applies to contributions for firefighters and to city contribution obligations for firefighters covered by an absolute coverage group, except for the large-city/county carveout above.

- Cities must pick up employee contributions (treated as employer contributions under federal tax law per IRC sec. 414(h)); accounts retain interest. Unallocated employer accounts and transfer rules remain. Emergency clause: effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two contribution requirements effective March 1, 2025, tied to the local retirement system:

- Firefighter contributions: Firefighters who (1) work for a city with >60,000 population located in a county with >100,000 population, (2) participate in the retirement system, and (3) are in an “absolute coverage group” must contribute 6.5% of salary to the retirement system.

- Employer (city) contributions: Cities of the first class meeting the same population/county criteria must contribute 13% of the periodic salary for each participating firefighter covered by an absolute coverage group.

Agencies/departments affected: municipal governments (cities of the first class) and the local firefighter retirement system. No separate appropriations or line-item budget allocations are specified beyond the mandated contribution percentages.
