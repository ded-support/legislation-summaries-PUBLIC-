Summary

LB41 (2025) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-502.03 to modify statutory requirements for blood testing of pregnant women related to communicable diseases. The bill also includes a legislative statement of intent regarding payment for certain blood tests for pregnant women but does not specify appropriations, funding amounts, or new budget allocations. The measure was introduced Jan. 9, 2025, referred to the Legislature’s Health and Human Services Committee, and repeals the original version of the section.
