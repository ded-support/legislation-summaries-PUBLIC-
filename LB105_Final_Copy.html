Summary

LB105 amends statutes governing public power and public power and irrigation districts to permit dividing voting precincts for establishing operating-area and chartered-territory boundaries and for district elections, with divided precincts described by section/township/range. Districts must transmit precinct-division and retail-customer voting certifications with election certification to the Secretary of State; counties may bill districts for additional election costs (payable within 30 days). The Nebraska Power Review Board retains approval authority over subdivision plans (including adding out‑of‑charter county representation for irrigation districts and approving county-line-based subdivisions in certain multi‑county districts). After each federal decennial census, metropolitan-area districts must redistrict following county lines and precincts set by county election commissioners or clerks. An emergency clause makes the act effective on enactment. No appropriations or funding changes are included.
