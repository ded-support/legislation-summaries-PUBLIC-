Summary

LB1058 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-3725, assigning the Nebraska Tourism Commission responsibility to develop and administer: (1) marketing assistance grants for national/international-caliber events that attract out-of-state visitors and generate press; and (2) "innovative tourism" grants to communities/organizations that increase nonlocal instate or out-of-state visitation. Applicant requirements include documentation of out-of-state draw, marketing plans, impact-tracking methodologies, and expected press coverage. Innovative grants may fund marketing, planning, basic support, and regional cooperation but explicitly exclude equipment or capital facility projects. The commission may award up to $500,000 in innovative tourism grants to any single city or village per fiscal year. The bill repeals the prior version of §81-3725 and contains an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment.
