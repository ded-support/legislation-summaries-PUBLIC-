Summary

LB1058 amends section 81-3725 of the Nebraska Visitors Development Act, assigning the Nebraska Tourism Commission authority to establish additional grant programs. Key provisions: the Commission must create marketing-assistance grants for national/international-caliber events that attract out‑of‑state visitors and provide related documentation and impact‑tracking; it must establish innovative tourism grants (marketing, planning, basic support, regional cooperation) that may not fund equipment or capital facilities; and it caps awards at up to $500,000 in innovative tourism grants to any single city or village per fiscal year. The bill also adds a tourism-and-visitor-promotion grant program, states legislative intent regarding funding, repeals the prior codified version of the section, and takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.
