Summary

LB758 amends Nebraska law to allow 501(c)(3) charitable organizations to claim nontestamentary interests by presenting a prescribed affidavit to the holder of the interest or any person with information. Required attachments include the organization's IRS determination or group exemption letter, state certificate of existence, decedent death evidence, corporate resolution authorizing the affiant, and a W-9. Holders must transfer property within 45 days or provide requested information within 30 days or face a statutory right of action, except where a timely notice of reasonable delay applies (including court orders or conflicts with specified federal banking/AML statutes and regulations). The bill adds this affidavit-based proof as acceptable evidence of ownership for motor-vehicle certificate-of-title applications (references the department and county treasurer functions). No funding or appropriation changes are included. Original sections recodified/repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a procedure allowing 501(c)(3) charities to claim a decedent's interest in property by submitting an affidavit plus documentation (IRS determination or group-exemption letter, state certificate of existence, death certificate/probate notice/obituary, corporate resolution, and a completed IRS Form W‑9). It cites the Internal Revenue Service and the Internal Revenue Code (section 501(c)(3)). Holders must pay/transfer the property (45 days) or provide requested information (30 days), or face suit; transferees are discharged as if dealing with a personal representative. Reasonable delay includes court orders or when compliance would cause a financial institution to violate specified federal banking/AML statutes (12 U.S.C., 31 U.S.C.) or Treasury regulations (31 C.F.R. 1000–1019). No funding or appropriation changes.
