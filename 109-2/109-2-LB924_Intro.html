Summary

LB924 amends levy-authority and allowable uses for Nebraska learning communities (sections 77-3442, 79-2104, 79-2111). It authorizes learning community coordinating councils to levy up to $0.005 per $100 valuation for leasing/purchasing and remodeling elementary learning center facilities and to cover up to 50% of estimated capital costs for approved focus school/program projects; it retains a $0.015 per $100 levy for early childhood programs, staff, contracts and pilots with a 10% cap for administrative staff. Proceeds may be used to reduce bonded indebtedness (transferred to member districts’ special building funds) and include repayment/interest conditions if facilities are repurposed. The bill references the Nebraska Department of Education (Commissioner/designee), educational service units, school districts, county officials (assessors/clerks/treasurers), the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (Chief Water Officer), and the Department of Transportation (Division of Aeronautics). No direct state appropriations are created.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions authorize the learning community to lease or purchase facilities and to lease or purchase administrative staff for the learning community, and add an obligation to partner with public and private entities to help increase high school graduation rates. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
