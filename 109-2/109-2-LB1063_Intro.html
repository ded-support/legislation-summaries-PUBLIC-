Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Banking and Finance and "Director" as the Director of Banking and Finance. The bill authorizes the Director to prescribe application forms and content, require certifications and supporting information that applicants, key individuals, and persons in control are not "foreign adversary persons," and verify those certifications. The Director is directed to (1) presume lack of such proof indicates unfitness for licensure or control acquisitions, (2) within 30 days issue forms/instructions and an order requiring information showing applicants are not foreign adversary persons, and (3) within 60 days request supplemental information from existing licensees/applicants and initiate revocation proceedings under section 8-2734 for noncompliance or reasonable suspicion. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates new foreign-adversary screening and reporting duties for the Department of Banking and Finance (the director under the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act). Key points:

- Defines “foreign adversary person” and requires applicants, licensees, key individuals, and persons in control to certify they are not such persons and provide verifiable supporting information.

- Director may presume lack of character/fitness and deny, block acquisition, or revoke licenses if certification/verification is not established.

- On enactment the rules apply to all existing and future licenses; within 60 days the director must request supplemental information from licensees/applicants and may initiate revocation proceedings for noncompliance; within 30 days the director must issue forms/instructions and an order imposing the new information requirements.

- No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are added or changed.
