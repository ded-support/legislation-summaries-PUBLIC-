Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Banking and Finance and "Director" as the Director of Banking and Finance.

- Applicants and key individuals must certify they are not "foreign adversary persons" and supply information enabling the Director to verify those certifications.

- The Director may prescribe, change, or update application forms and content (to align with Nationwide Mortgage Licensing System and Registry standards), waive requirements, and accept substitute information.

- Within 30 days of enactment the Director must issue forms, instructions, and an order requiring information sufficient to show an applicant is not a foreign adversary person.

- Within 60 days the Director must request supplemental information from existing licensees and applicants; failure to timely respond or reasonable suspicion of foreign-adversary ties authorizes the Director to institute license-revocation proceedings under section 8-2734.

- The bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Banking and Finance)

- Adds a statutory definition of “foreign adversary person” tied to 15 C.F.R. 791.4 and requires applicants, key individuals, and persons in control to certify they are not foreign adversary persons, with the director authorized to verify certifications.

- Establishes a presumption that applicants, acquirers, or controllers who cannot prove they are not foreign adversary persons are unfit for money transmitter licensure or control.

- Applies requirements retroactively to all existing licenses and applications.

- Requires the director to: prescribe forms/instructions within 30 days; within 60 days request supplemental information from licensees/applicants; and initiate revocation proceedings for noncompliance or reasonable suspicion.

- Requires certain exempt entities to notify affected individuals and the Attorney General if they learn data was shared with a foreign adversary (during license or within three years after expiration).

No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
