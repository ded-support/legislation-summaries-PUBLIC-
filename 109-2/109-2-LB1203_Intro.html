Summary

LB1203 requires county treasurers and county clerks to maintain lists of checks issued by their offices that remain uncashed after two years (including payee and amount). Checks under $100 become void three years after issuance and cease to be county obligations; such void checks may later be submitted to the county board, which may approve payment. Void checks must be charged off in county books, and the county treasurer shall credit the proceeds (including interest) to the county general fund. Those void checks are removed from the definition of intangible personal property and are not subject to the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act. No separate appropriations or state agency budget changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires county treasurers and county clerks to maintain a list of checks uncashed after two years (payee and amount). Checks under $100 become void three years after issuance, cease to be county obligations, but may be presented to and approved by the county board. Voided checks must be charged off in county accounting, and the treasurer must credit the principal and any interest to the county general fund. Voided checks are no longer intangible personal property and are excluded from the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act. No specific appropriations are provided.
