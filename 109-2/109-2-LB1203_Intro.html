Summary

LB1203 requires county treasurers and county clerks to maintain a list of checks issued by their office that remain uncashed after two years, including payee name and amount. Checks under $100 become void three years after issuance and cease to be county obligations; such voided checks may thereafter be submitted to the county board for approval of payment. Voided checks must be charged off in county accounting, and the treasurer must credit the proceeds, including any interest, to the county general fund. Voided checks under this provision are removed from owners' intangible personal property and are not subject to the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires county treasurers and county clerks to track checks uncashed after two years (amount and payee). Checks under $100 become void after three years and no longer are county obligations; claimants may seek payment approval from the county board. Void checks must be charged off in county records and the county treasurer must credit the proceeds (including interest) to the county general fund. Such voided checks also cease to be intangible personal property and are excluded from the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act. No statewide appropriation is created; the provision reallocates small unclaimed check amounts into county general funds.
