Summary

This bill assigns multiple duties and authorities to the Department of Agriculture and changes related fund flows and fees. Key departmental provisions and fiscal changes:

- Dept. powers expanded under the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Act (inspections, sampling, quarantines, enforcement, certification; inspection fees/time defined). Fees due 1/1/2027 set by statute; thereafter director sets fees within caps; delinquent fees carry administrative penalties remitted to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund.

- Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund created; State Treasurer to transfer Nebraska Potato Development Fund balances to it on that fund's termination.

- Noxious Weed Cash Fund created/credited (including pesticide-registration proceeds and weed-book sales); State Treasurer to transfer Weed Book Cash Fund to Noxious Weed Cash Fund by 1/1/2027.

- Pesticide registration fee set at $200 (max $250); allocation: $50 to Noxious Weed Cash Fund, $50 to Buffer Strip Incentive Fund, $55 to Natural Resources Water Quality Fund, remainder to Pesticide Administrative Cash Fund.

- Dept. to administer renamed Nebraska Commercial Fertilizer and Beneficial Substances Act; inspection fees, reporting deadlines, and a Fertilizers and Beneficial Substances Administrative Fund are established for departmental administration; fund may be used for program costs and transfers to the General Fund as directed by the Legislature.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Agriculture):

- Creates definitions and regulatory coverage for "beneficial substances" (e.g., plant biostimulants, soil amendments; excludes primary/secondary nutrients, micronutrients, pesticides, unmanipulated manure, commercial fertilizer).

- Designates "product" to include both commercial fertilizers and beneficial substances and defines "ton."

- Requires product registration before distribution beginning Jan 1, 2027; registration to be accompanied by a $50 per‑product fee.

- Authorizes an inspection fee (set by the director, not to exceed $0.15 per ton) to apply, beginning Jan 1, 2027, to commercial fertilizers and beneficial substances; fee borne by the distributor.

- Requires semiannual tonnage reports (net tons distributed) to the department, with recurring filing deadlines (July 31 and January 31) beginning 2027/2028.

- Establishes/recognizes a Fertilizers and Beneficial Substances Administrative Fund for department programs.

- Fiscal transfers: Potato Development Fund monies on termination go to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund; Weed Book Cash Fund balances must transfer to the Noxious Weed Cash Fund by Jan 1, 2027.
