Summary

LB948 assigns and clarifies multiple authorities and funding flows for the Department of Agriculture. Key points:

- Expands department enforcement under the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Act (inspections, sampling, treatment, quarantines, orders) and authorizes license/inspection fees; director sets fees after 2027; delinquent administrative fees remitted to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund.

- Creates/affects funds and transfers: Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund (State Treasurer to transfer Nebraska Potato Development Fund balance to it); Noxious Weed Cash Fund (receives proceeds from pesticide registration fees and Weed Book receipts; Weed Book Cash Fund funds transferred to Noxious Weed Cash Fund); Noxious Weed Cash Fund may receive General Fund appropriations.

- Pesticide registration fee set at $200 (cap $250); fee distribution: $50 to Noxious Weed Cash Fund, $50 to Buffer Strip Incentive Fund, $55 to Natural Resources Water Quality Fund, remainder to Pesticide Administrative Cash Fund; late fees to Pesticide Administrative Cash Fund.

- Establishes Fertilizers and Beneficial Substances Administrative Fund for department program costs; fertilizer/beneficial-substance registration and inspection fees (max $0.15/ton; minimum $10) and delinquent fees credited thereto; director may set rates within appropriation-based limits.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds statutory regulation of "beneficial substances" under the Department of Agriculture. Key fiscal provisions:

- New definitions and product scope: "beneficial substance" and "product" (includes commercial fertilizers and beneficial substances).

- Registration and fees: products must be registered beginning Jan 1, 2027; $50 registration fee per product.

- Inspection fee revenue: director may set an inspection fee not to exceed $0.15 per ton for commercial fertilizers and, beginning Jan 1, 2027, beneficial substances; fee is paid by the distributor to the ultimate user (revenue to department funds).

- Reporting: semiannual tonnage reports required (deadlines starting 2027/2028) to support oversight.

- Funds/transfers: directs State Treasurer to transfer Nebraska Potato Development Fund upon termination to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund; transfers Weed Book Cash Fund to the Noxious Weed Cash Fund by Jan 1, 2027. These provisions establish and route new/existing revenues to departmental plant-health funds.
