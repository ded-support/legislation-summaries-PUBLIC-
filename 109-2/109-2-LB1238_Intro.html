Summary

LB1238 reallocates cigarette- and tobacco-product tax revenue and directs related appropriations. The State Treasurer is required (effective July 1, 2026) to deposit 50% of cigarette-tax receipts to the General Fund and distribute the remainder: 2% to the Nebraska Outdoor Recreation Development Cash Fund; 10% to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund; 26% to the Building Renewal Allocation Fund (until Deferred Building Renewal Act purposes fulfilled); 5% to the Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Cash Fund; 6% to the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund; and 1% to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund, with any residual going to Capital Construction. The Tobacco Products Tax Act taxes snuff, ENDS, alternative nicotine products, and other tobacco products at 30% of the first-owner purchase price. The Department of Health and Human Services shall receive appropriations from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund equal to 3.5% (registry and related expenses, $200,000 cap) and 6.5% (cancer research grants) of the cigarette tax, with distribution limits to UNMC/Eppley and other Nebraska medical colleges. The act is operative July 1, 2026, and takes effect on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that beginning July 1, 2026, and each fiscal year thereafter the State Treasurer must deposit an amount equal to 1% of the referenced tax into the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund. To satisfy this deposit, the State Treasurer may reduce the tax distribution otherwise directed to the General Fund under subsection (2). No specific dollar appropriation is stated—only a one‑percent set‑aside of the tax.
