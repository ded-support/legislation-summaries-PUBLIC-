Summary

LB1238 reallocates cigarette- and tobacco-product tax receipts and specifies taxing rates and appropriations. The Tax Commissioner continues collection; the State Treasurer will distribute cigarette-tax proceeds beginning July 1, 2026 as follows: 50% to the General Fund; 2% to the Nebraska Outdoor Recreation Development Cash Fund; 10% to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund; 26% to the Building Renewal Allocation Fund; 5% to the Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Cash Fund; 6% to the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund; and 1% to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund, with any leftovers to the Capital Construction Fund. Tobacco-product taxes (snuff, ENDS, alternative nicotine products, others) are set at 30% of first-owner purchase price or sale price. The Department of Health and Human Services shall receive annual appropriations from the Health and Human Services Cash Fund equal to 3.5% of the cigarette tax for cancer research/registry (registry capped at $200,000) and 6.5% for cancer research grants (no more than half to UNMC/Eppley). Act operative July 1, 2026; emergency clause enacted.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a recurring fiscal allocation and adjusts percentage distributions. Beginning July 1, 2026, the State Treasurer must deposit an amount equal to 1% of the referenced tax into the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund each fiscal year; if needed, the Treasurer will reduce transfers to the General Fund (per subsection (2)) to effect this deposit. The amendment also revises various percentage allocations tied to purchase-price/tax distributions (examples shown include 30%, 50%, 2%, 10%, 26%, 5%, 6%, and 3.5%/6.5% entries), affecting how proceeds are split among recipients (e.g., retail dealers/first owners).
