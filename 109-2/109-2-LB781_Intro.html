Summary

- The bill defines "Department" to mean the Department of Motor Vehicles and formally defines "Director" as the Director of Motor Vehicles and the "Division" as the Division of Motor Carrier Services within the Department of Motor Vehicles.

- The Director of Motor Vehicles must adopt standards for an informal dispute-settlement procedure substantially complying with 16 C.F.R. part 703 (as of Jan 1, 2026).

- The Department and its personnel who custody motor-vehicle records must disclose records (excluding social security numbers) upon verification of identity and purpose for specified uses, including for government agencies, courts, insurers, employers verifying CDL/CLP information, and the Commercial Driver License Information System.

- The Director is authorized to participate in and file Nebraska's plan under the Unified Carrier Registration plan and agreement (Unified Carrier Registration Act).

- No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
