Summary

LB958 assigns multiple duties and program changes to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- DHHS to implement a home-and-community-based services waiver: develop assessment tools and communications, ensure services meet federal Medicaid standards, require supervisory review before reductions, and submit reports to Legislative Oversight by Aug 1, 2026 and Aug 1, 2027.

- Under the Medical Assistance Act DHHS must maintain client confidentiality, provide the maximum retroactive coverage allowed by federal law, and file an annual summary/analysis to the Governor and Legislature by Dec 1 with detailed metrics (applications, enrollments, fair hearing links, community engagement data, concurrent enrollment and vendor information).

- Nebraska Prenatal Plus Program is placed within DHHS; DHHS to report annually (Dec 15 through 2034) on mothers served, services, and birth outcomes; eligible services expanded to include breastfeeding support sessions.

- DHHS to administer LIHEAP eligibility at 150% of FPL and limit crisis payments to $800/year (with exceptions).

- Legislative intent to fund the state portion of Medicaid doula reimbursement from the vital statistics subfund of the Health and Human Services Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summarized additions affecting the Department of Health and Human Services:

- Requires DHHS to clearly and promptly notify waiver participants (or guardians) of eligibility determinations, assigned service tiers, assessment results, and appeal rights. Services must be individualized per federal HCBS waiver rules, sufficient to prevent unnecessary institutionalization, and any reduction in tier/hours requires immediate supervisory review before implementation.

- DHHS must submit electronic reports by Aug 1, 2026 and Aug 1, 2027 to Legislative Oversight, including detailed community engagement metrics (denials/approvals, exemptions, verification sources, contracted vendors) and concurrent enrollment data.

- DHHS must provide the maximum retroactive Medicaid coverage allowed under federal law (sec. 71112, Public Law 119‑21) as of Jan 1, 2026.

- Establishes a doula workgroup/definitions and states legislative intent to fund the state portion of doula reimbursement from the Health & Human Services Cash Fund’s vital statistics subfund.

- Authorizes crisis assistance payments up to $800 per program year, with discretion to exceed for extenuating circumstances.
