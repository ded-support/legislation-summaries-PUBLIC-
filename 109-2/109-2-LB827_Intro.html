Summary

LB827 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §2-3232 to expand natural resources districts’ (NRDs) authorities. NRDs retain coordination requirements with the University of Nebraska (and its Agricultural Research Division within the Institute of Agriculture and Natural Resources) for studies and demonstration projects and are explicitly authorized to establish or participate in programs to educate residents or research practices to improve water quality, water-quantity management, and soil health. Such programs may include demonstration projects and educational events, and NRDs may enter into agreements with agricultural producers, nonprofits, companies, service organizations, political subdivisions, public or private postsecondary institutions, or state and federal agencies. The bill repeals the original section. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for natural resources districts to create or join programs researching or educating on water quality, water‑quantity management, and soil health, including demonstration projects (per §2‑3232) and public/producer educational events. Districts may contract or partner with agricultural producers, nonprofits, companies, service organizations, political subdivisions, public/private postsecondary institutions, and state or federal agencies to run such programs. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
