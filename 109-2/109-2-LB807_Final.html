Summary

The bill amends the Noxious Weed Control Act to continue a grant program administered by the director from the Noxious Weed and Invasive Plant Species Assistance Fund. It explicitly authorizes the Department of Agriculture to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement that section. The director is also authorized to annually apply for conservation funding from the Natural Resources Conservation Service of the United States Department of Agriculture. No specific appropriations, new funding amounts, or budget reallocations to the Department of Agriculture are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language creates a grant program (administered by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment) for weed management entities to remove riparian vegetation to improve natural streamflow and to acquire related equipment. Eligible applicants are weed management entities, weed control authorities, and natural resources districts. Applicants apply to DWEE on a form to be prescribed by Jan 1, 2027; DWEE may prioritize projects in interstate-compact/decree areas and may adopt rules. No reference to the Department of Agriculture, and no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
