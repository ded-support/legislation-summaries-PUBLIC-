Summary

LB1151 treats each shipment into Nebraska by holders of certain shipping licenses as a sale establishing Nebraska tax nexus and requires those licensees to collect applicable taxes and remit sales taxes to the Department of Revenue (excise taxes are remitted monthly to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission). Apart from that remittance requirement, the bill assigns tax reporting, collection, bond, and enforcement duties to the Commission and local bodies and includes no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a “regional craft brewery” definition (20,000–200,000 barrels/year) and creates a new regional craft brewery license under the Nebraska Liquor Control Act that: authorizes aggregate production across up to eight physical locations; allows sales to beer wholesalers and retail sales for on- and off‑premises consumption (with other retail licenses required for non‑brewery products); permits obtaining related licenses (annual catering, special designated, entertainment district, promotional farmers market); and allows authorized offsite storage of tax‑paid product with commission approval, location notice, and perpetual inventory (consumption at storage prohibited). The commission may adopt rules. No provisions change Department of Revenue responsibilities, nor do they include any new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
