Summary

LB1151 treats each shipment into Nebraska by holders of shipping licenses under §53-123.15(3), (5), or (6) as a sale creating Nebraska tax nexus. Licensees must collect all applicable state and local taxes and remit sales taxes to the Department of Revenue (excise taxes are remitted monthly to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission). The bill otherwise amends the Nebraska Liquor Control Act but contains no direct appropriations, budgetary allocations, or other changes to Department of Revenue funding.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Revenue focus):

- The bill creates a "regional craft brewery" (20,000–200,000 barrels/year) licensed under the Nebraska Liquor Control Act, allowing production across up to eight physical locations, sales to wholesalers, and onsite/offsite retail under specified conditions.

- It permits authorized offsite storage of tax‑paid product if approved by the commission and requires separate perpetual inventories at both the brewery and the storage site; consumption at storage sites is prohibited.

- No provisions directly amend Department of Revenue authority, appropriations, or funding. There are no new budget allocations.

- Potential indirect impacts: additional recordkeeping/verification and coordination with the liquor commission for tax‑paid inventory tracking and compliance, but no explicit funding or staffing changes are specified.
