Summary

LB988 amends the Community Development Law to define "workforce housing" with specific construction-cost caps (owner-occupied ≤ $275,000; rental ≤ $200,000) and directs the Department of Economic Development to update those unit-cost thresholds annually based on the most recent change in the Producer Price Index for All Commodities, as published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. No other provisions explicitly reference the Nebraska Department of Labor or the U.S. Department of Labor, and the bill contains no direct appropriations or funding changes for either agency.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions tighten redevelopment rules: inspectors designated by the governing body may declare structures permanently uninhabitable only if unstable/collapsing, allowing elemental intrusion, or posing unresolved safety hazards; areas generally cannot be re‑declared substandard/blighted within 20 years without gubernatorial approval; voter approval is required before the Division of Taxes revenue may be used for a public transportation project; once a redevelopment project is paid off, the governing body treasurer must redirect any excess revenue to the taxing entity; and “debt and indebtedness” is limited to principal, interest, premiums, and related financing liabilities for the specific project. No provisions explicitly affect the Department of Economic Development or the Department of Labor, and no direct appropriations or agency budget allocations are added.
