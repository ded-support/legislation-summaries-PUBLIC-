Summary

LB900 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77‑1720 governing fees, commissions, and mileage for distress warrants used to collect taxes. It sets the fee for issuing each warrant at $20 and ties mileage to the county sheriff rate in §33‑117. Commission on taxes collected by distress and sale is set at $0.10 per dollar for amounts up to $500 and $0.08 per dollar for amounts over $500. All fees, mileage, and commissions are assessed to the taxpayer and collected as part of the original tax. If mileage is advanced under §33‑117 and later recovered with the tax, the recovered mileage is paid to the county treasurer along with fees and commission and credited to the county general fund. The bill repeals the original version of §77‑1720.
