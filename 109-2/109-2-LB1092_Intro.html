Summary

LB1092 assigns the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) responsibility to design and issue new alternate plates, including Back the Blue (available Jan 1, 2027) and Iconic-Design plates. Iconic-Design alphanumeric/personalized applications carry a $100 fee. The DMV Cash Fund is the designated recipient for Czech Heritage plates. Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit plate fees ($70) are split 60% to the DMV Cash Fund and 40% to the designated recipient (Highway Trust Fund).

The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) will administer the newly created Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, which receives monies credited under section 60-3,163.02 (including license-plate fees) and may award grants for provider transportation, infrastructure, safety, capital, maintenance, and equipment. Choose Life plate fees are designated to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement federal TANF funds administered by DHHS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: directs the DMV to design new "Back the Blue" and multiple "Iconic‑Design" license plates (in consultation with law‑enforcement associations). Back the Blue and two Iconic‑Design types (black‑ and white‑background) available Jan 1, 2027; two additional Iconic options on director‑set implementation dates. Iconic alphanumeric/personalized plates carry a $100 initial/renewal fee. Alternate plate fees from Back the Blue go to the Back the Blue Cash Fund; Iconic‑Design fees go to the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund.

- Department of Health and Human Services: DHHS will administer the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund (funded by gifts, grants, plate fee proceeds, statutory credits, and legislative transfers). Funds are investable by the state investment officer and may be awarded as grants for provider capital/equipment, transportation fleet, infrastructure and safety upgrades; funds cannot be used for general program operations. No direct appropriation amounts are specified.
