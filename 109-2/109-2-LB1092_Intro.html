Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): The bill directs the DMV to issue new alternate plates, including Back the Blue and multiple Iconic-Design plates. Iconic-Design plates carry a $100 issuance/renewal fee. Czech Heritage plate fees are credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. For Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit Plates, a $70 fee is split by the State Treasurer: 60% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 40% to the designated recipient (Highway Trust Fund). Application dates for certain plates begin January 1, 2027.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Choose Life plate fees are designated to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to supplement federal TANF funding for DHHS. The bill creates the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, administered by DHHS (funded by gifts, grants, legislative transfers, and monies credited under section 60-3,163.02). DHHS may award grants from that fund; it cannot be used for general operation or administration of the state’s developmental disabilities program.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Authorizes new specialty plates—"Back the Blue" (design in consultation with law‑enforcement associations) and multiple "Iconic‑Design" plates (black/white and two optional retrospective designs). Back the Blue applications begin Jan 1, 2027; Iconic Black/White also Jan 1, 2027; other Iconic designs on director’s implementation date. Iconic alphanumeric/personalized plates carry a $100 initial/renewal fee. Designated recipients of alternate plate fees are specific cash funds (see below).

- Department of Health and Human Services: Creates the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, administered by DHHS, funded by gifts/grants/license‑plate fees (including monies credited under section 60‑3,163.02) and legislative transfers. Fund income may be invested per state law; DHHS may award grants for capital, equipment, transportation fleet, infrastructure/safety upgrades for developmental‑disability providers. Fund cannot be used for general program operations. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.
