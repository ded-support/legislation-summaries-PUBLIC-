Summary

LB804 establishes confidentiality and privilege for peer support counseling provided to personnel of law enforcement agencies (per §81‑1401), emergency services providers (public employers of firefighters, dispatchers, EMS), and the Nebraska National Guard. It defines peer support specialists, peer support teams, and employee assistance programs, makes communications in peer support sessions nonpublic and inadmissible in judicial, administrative, arbitration, or discovery proceedings, and preserves confidentiality among specialists and EAP staff. Exceptions include threats of suicide or criminal acts, mandated reports of abuse/neglect, admissions of criminal conduct, and disclosures with participant or estate consent. The bill also preserves admissibility of information otherwise obtained in the course of employment and allows EAP‑to‑employer fitness‑for‑duty communications. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds confidentiality and privilege protections for peer support counseling involving law enforcement agencies, emergency services providers, and the Nebraska National Guard. Defines covered parties and terms, including law enforcement agency/officer (per section 81-1401), emergency services provider/personnel, employee assistance programs (which may be established by a law enforcement agency, emergency services provider, or the Nebraska National Guard), peer support specialists, and peer support teams. Communications in such sessions are confidential, not public records, and inadmissible in adjudicatory proceedings, with limited exceptions (suicide/criminal threats, abuse reporting, admissions of criminal conduct, or participant/estate consent). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
