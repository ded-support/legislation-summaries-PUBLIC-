Summary

LB804 creates a confidential peer support counseling privilege for personnel of law enforcement agencies, public emergency services providers (fire, dispatch, EMS), and the Nebraska National Guard. Designated peer support specialists and peer support teams, and employee assistance programs established by those agencies, may lead sessions; communications in such sessions are confidential, not public records, and inadmissible in judicial, administrative, arbitration, or other adjudicatory proceedings (including discovery). Communications among peer specialists and between specialists and employee assistance program supervisors/staff are likewise confidential and permitted. Exceptions include threats of suicide or criminal acts, required reports of abuse/neglect, admissions of criminal conduct, and disclosures with an express consent (including by a decedent’s representative). The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and confidentiality protections for peer support counseling involving law enforcement agencies, emergency services providers (public employers of firefighters, dispatchers, EMS), and the Nebraska National Guard. It authorizes employee assistance programs established by a law enforcement agency, emergency services provider, or the Nebraska National Guard and recognizes peer support specialists/teams designated by those entities. Communications in such sessions are confidential, not public records, and inadmissible in adjudicatory proceedings, with limited exceptions (threats of suicide/crime, required abuse reports, admissions of criminal conduct, or express consent). The bill names supervisors/staff of employee assistance programs as covered. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
