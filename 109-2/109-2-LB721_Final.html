Summary

Creates the Intergenerational Care Facility Incentive Grant Program and directs the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) to administer it. The Legislature intends to appropriate $300,000 from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for FY2024-25 to the department for these grants. The department must develop eligibility, application, and audit requirements in collaboration with statewide nursing home associations and other stakeholders; meet with those associations before October 1, 2024, to identify regulatory barriers and recommend non-safety-impacting remedies; and award grants to nonprofit and proprietary facilities on a first-come (date-of-application) basis.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds eligibility limits for DHHS‑administered grants: facilities (including nonprofit and proprietary) may not apply for a grant under this section if they previously received one (applies to grants for construction or expansion). No language in the added text changes appropriations, funding amounts, or DHHS budget allocations.
