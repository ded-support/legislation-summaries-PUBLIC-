Summary

- Department of Economic Development: the bill explicitly designates the Department of Economic Development to administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund as part of its comprehensive housing affordability strategy. The Affordable Housing Trust Fund shall receive money pursuant to section 76-903 and other sources. Transfers from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to specified funds (General Fund, Behavioral Health Services Fund, Lead‑Based Paint Hazard Control Cash Fund, Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund, Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund, and Site and Building Development Fund) may occur prior to July 1, 2027; transfers are prohibited on and after July 1, 2027.

- Department of Revenue: taxpayers disputing documentary stamp tax liability may seek a declaratory ruling under Department of Revenue rules. The Department of Revenue may not overturn a county assessor’s sale‑qualification determination unless the department’s review finds it incorrect.

- Funding/allocation change: section 76-903 sets revised documentary stamp tax distributions (different allocations for transfers before vs. on/after Jan 1, 2032), including specified cents credited to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and multiple housing-related funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Effective July 1, 2027: no transfers may be made from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund.

- Rate/timing: For conveyance documentary stamp taxes before Jan 1, 2032, the bill keeps a $3.00 per $1,000 rate, with $0.50 directed to the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund and $0.50 to the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund. Beginning Jan 1, 2032 the rate is set at $2.32 per $1,000.

- Department of Revenue impact: registers of deeds retain $0.50 of each $2.32 to county general funds; the remainder is remitted to the State Treasurer for specified credits. The Dept. of Revenue must implement the new collection/retention and remittance mechanics.

- Budget allocations (state-level): per $2.32 remitted, credits are $0.95 to Affordable Housing Trust Fund, $0.25 to Site and Building Development Fund, $0.25 to Homeless Shelter Assistance Trust Fund, $0.30 to Behavioral Health Services Fund, $0.07 to Domestic Violence & Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund.

- Department of Economic Development: affected as a likely recipient/administrator of the Site and Building Development Fund (receives $0.25 per $2.32). No separate appropriation line items are added in the text.
