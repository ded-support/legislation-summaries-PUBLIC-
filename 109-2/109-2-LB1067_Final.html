Summary

- Department of Economic Development: the bill explicitly directs the Department of Economic Development to administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund as part of its housing affordability strategy. It allows transfers from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to specified funds (General Fund; Behavioral Health Services Fund; Lead‑Based Paint Hazard Control Cash Fund; Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund; Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund; Site and Building Development Fund) prior to July 1, 2027, and prohibits transfers from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund on or after July 1, 2027.

- Department of Revenue: taxpayers may seek a declaratory ruling under rules promulgated by the Department of Revenue. The Department of Revenue also is restricted from overturning a county assessor’s sale‑qualification determination unless its review determines the assessor’s determination is incorrect.

- Documentary stamp tax: the tax rate falls from $3.32 to $2.32 per $1,000 on Jan 1, 2032, and post‑2032 allocations drop removal of the prior 50¢ credits to the Rural and Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Funds; the 50¢ county retention and 95¢ credit to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund remain.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Beginning July 1, 2027, money shall not be transferred out of the Affordable Housing Trust Fund.

- For documentary-stamp tax on transfers on or after Jan. 1, 2032: of each $2.32 collected under section 76-901 the register of deeds retains $0.50 (county general fund); the remainder is remitted to the State Treasurer, who credits $0.95 to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund, $0.25 to the Site and Building Development Fund, $0.25 to the Homeless Shelter Assistance Trust Fund, $0.30 to the Behavioral Health Services Fund, and $0.07 to the Domestic Violence and Sex Trafficking Survivor Housing Assistance Fund.

- Department of Revenue (and registers of deeds) are assigned collection/remittance responsibilities and the specified county retention.

- Department of Economic Development is not named directly, but the Site and Building Development Fund receives a dedicated $0.25-per-$2.32 revenue stream; no itemized appropriation totals are provided.
