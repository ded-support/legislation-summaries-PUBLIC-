Summary

LB1198 requires any public or private entity selling electronic tickets to a "large public event" (venue capacity ≥5,000) to provide a free paper ticket to purchasers on request, delivered or made printable prior to the event. Enforcement is assigned to the Nebraska Attorney General. Violations carry a civil penalty equal to $10 multiplied by the total number of tickets sold for the affected event. Collected penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution in accordance with Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution. No other appropriations or agency funding changes are specified.
