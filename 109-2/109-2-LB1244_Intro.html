Summary

LB1244 (Gov. request) amends Nebraska tax law and reporting requirements with several agency implications:

- Department (Revenue): expands the Tax Expenditure Reporting Act duties to quantify revenue loss from exemptions and from failure to tax services purchased for nonbusiness use and to list detailed exemption categories (including services).

- Tax Commissioner: retains and is referenced for rulemaking and adjustments tied to tax computations and reporting.

- Nebraska Job Creation and Mainstreet Revitalization Act oversight: no new applications after June 30, 2026; credits cannot be allocated, issued, or claimed after December 31, 2035 (affecting credit administration).

- Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act: clarifies the occupation tax is additional to state taxes, including sales/use tax; limits local taxation to local option sales/use taxes.

- Other agencies mentioned in exemption lists: Nebraska Investment Finance Authority, Nebraska State Fair Board, State Racing and Gaming Commission.

No explicit funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The bill contains operative dates and an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text lists numerous specified services (including veterinary services on livestock as defined in section 54‑183) as covered items and explicitly includes "purchases by licensees of the State Racing and Gaming Commission." It also references sales and use tax on gross receipts from mechanical amusement devices through June 30, 2026 (excluding local option taxes). No other government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations in the added text.
