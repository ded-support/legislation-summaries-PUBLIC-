Summary

LB1244 amends tax reporting and multiple tax statutes with direct impacts on state agencies.

- Nebraska Department of Revenue: expands the Tax Expenditure Report to quantify estimated revenue loss from untaxed services, list sales/use tax expenditures by category, and review major tax exemptions for which state general funds offset revenue loss.

- Tax Commissioner: retains/executes authority to adjust income‑tax thresholds referenced in the bill (e.g., Social Security reduction thresholds).

- Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act: imposes state sales and use tax on gross receipts from mechanical amusement device operations; political subdivisions may not impose additional device taxes except local‑option sales/use taxes.

- Nebraska Job Creation and Mainstreet Revitalization Act: no new applications after June 30, 2026; credits cannot be allocated, issued, or used after December 31, 2035.

- Other state entities referenced in reporting or tax adjustments include the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority, Nebraska State Fair Board, State Racing and Gaming Commission, Nebraska educational savings plan trust, and the Medical Debt Relief Fund.

The bill includes operative dates and an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a long list of service categories to the statute governing gross income treatment (including veterinary services on livestock as defined in section 54-183, animal grooming, nail/skin/tattoo/body‑modification exclusions when medical, and many professional and personal services). It specifically references purchases by licensees of the State Racing and Gaming Commission and notes inclusion of sales and use tax (excluding local option taxes) with a June 30, 2026 date. No new appropriations, funding, or budget allocations or other state agency funding changes are specified.
