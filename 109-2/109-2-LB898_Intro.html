Summary

LB898 amends Power Review Board statutes and the Open Meetings Act.

- Nebraska Power Review Board (and its executive director): retains authority to approve/deny generation and transmission applications; must receive applicant reports of required public meetings for proposed generation >10 MW; may consider regional transmission organization (RTO) planning and notices; may sue the owner for failure to meet decommissioning bonding obligations; must acknowledge exemptions for qualifying privately developed renewable projects within ten days.

- Game and Parks Commission: project proponents must consult the Commission to identify measures to avoid/minimize impacts to listed species during planning/design.

- Regional transmission organizations and the North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC): RTO planning may inform board decisions; facilities near military installations must meet or certify compliance with NERC critical infrastructure protection requirements or certify absence of foreign-adversary components (subject to board review).

- Other referenced entities: consumer-owned electric suppliers, local governmental decommissioning authorities, Governor (Emergency Management Act), and various public power entities affected by Open Meetings Act changes.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
