Summary

LB1136 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-905 (Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act). Key agency/departamental provisions:

- Requires each political subdivision to post on its website the official or law department authorized to receive tort claims, identifying that person/department by name, title, and mailing address.

- Permits governing bodies to designate claims be filed with the subdivision’s law department.

- Filing with the listed official or law department constitutes substantial compliance.

- If the subdivision fails to keep the website information accurate and up to date, statutory deadlines for submitting a written claim and for filing suit under §§13-919 and 13-920 are tolled.

- Confirms the duty of the receiving official to present claims to the governing body and retains existing content requirements for written claims.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original §13-905 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires every political subdivision to post on its website home page a notice naming the official or law department authorized to receive claims, giving that person/department's name, title and a mailing address. Filing a claim with the named official/department counts as substantial compliance with the statutory claim-filing requirement. If the subdivision fails to keep that information accurate and current, the time limits to submit a written claim and to file suit under sections 13-919 and 13-920 are tolled.
