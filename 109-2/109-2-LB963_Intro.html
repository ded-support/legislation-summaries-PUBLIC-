Summary

The bill amends the Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act to explicitly define "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and "Director" as the Director of Correctional Services. It clarifies "committed offender" and "person committed to the department" to include individuals sentenced or committed to facilities operated by or to the department (excluding certain juvenile adjudications). "Facility" is defined as any institution operated by the department. The bill further provides that sentencing to a specific facility within the department constitutes commitment to the department and authorizes the Director to designate any suitable residence facility or institution — whether state-operated or not — as a place of confinement and to transfer committed persons among locations. Original sections 83-170 and 83-176 are repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
