Summary

LB824 amends the School Employees Retirement Act and the Class V School Employees Retirement Act to clarify agency roles and return-to-work rules. It authorizes the retirement system administrator/NPERS director to make 120‑day separation determinations and receive retirement applications; members may appeal to the Public Employees Retirement Board within 30 days, and the board’s decision is final and not judicially reviewable. The administrator must accept clear-and-convincing evidence submitted within 45 days after the later of a bona fide separation or board receipt of an application. Temporary, substitute, or volunteer post‑separation work is not treated as a termination and has limited contribution/service‑credit rights. Direct-rollover of refunds over $1,000 is governed by board designation (transition rule through Sept. 1, 2024). The bill also updates interplay with the State Department of Education, state agency school-plan employers, and retains Nebraska Investment Council and related investment/governance roles. No new appropriations or budget allocations are included; several statutes are repealed.
