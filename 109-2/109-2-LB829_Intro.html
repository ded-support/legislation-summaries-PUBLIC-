Summary

LB829 amends duties of the Auditor of Public Accounts (APA). Examinations must follow Government Auditing Standards for financial audits and attestation engagements and, except as authorized (e.g., joint audits with the Legislative Oversight Committee), do not include performance audits. The bill requires any audited entity (excluding state colleges and the University of Nebraska) receiving a management-letter comment to provide the APA a detailed corrective‑action plan within six months; the APA may investigate and must electronically report findings to the Governor, the appropriate legislative standing committee, and the Legislature’s Appropriations Committee and ensure the Appropriations Committee receives the report for entry into its budget hearing record. The statute references contractual audit fees being deposited into the Auditor of Public Accounts Cash Fund and a 14% annual interest rate on delinquent audit fees.
