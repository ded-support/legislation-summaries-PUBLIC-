Summary

LB944 amends section 68‑911 of the Medical Assistance Act. It directs the Department (Medicaid administrator) to begin full reimbursement, effective August 1, 2026, for hospice services provided to nursing facility or intermediate care facility residents — payments must be made directly to the hospital or intermediate care facility, not the hospice provider, and must not reflect any discount. The bill also continues multiple CMS state-plan/waiver directives (including school-based direct reimbursement in consultation with the State Department of Education, postpartum coverage extension, medical respite waivers, CGM and breast‑pump coverage, translation services, targeted nurse home‑visiting, and expanded psychology services). Funding/appropriations: multiple provisions are to be financed from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (section 68‑996), including a $600,000 annual appropriation cap for CGM implementation, medical respite, translation services, and intended funding (not General Fund) for nurse home‑visiting; lactation consultations target a 145% rate increase.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

By August 1, 2026, "the department" must fully reimburse hospice care services for individuals residing in nursing facilities or intermediate care facilities who would be eligible for those facility services under the medical assistance program as if not receiving hospice. Reimbursement must be paid directly to the hospital or intermediate care facility (not to the hospice provider) and cannot include any discount. No specific new appropriation, funding increase, or budget allocation is stated in the added text.
