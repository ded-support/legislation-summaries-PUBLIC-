Summary

The bill amends regulatory and information-sharing duties among the Department of Banking and Finance (DBF), Department of Revenue (DoR), and Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- DBF (as “department” under the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act) may require certifications/forms (including foreign‑adversary certifications), assess a nonrefundable $1,500 license application fee, request supplemental information, initiate revocations, and must prescribe required forms/orders within 30 days of the operative date.

- DBF and DoR may exchange money‑transmitter data; DoR may share information with DBF. On request, DBF may claim a licensee’s surety bond to satisfy remittance tax liabilities.

- DoR will collect a 25% excise tax on certain remittances to specified foreign‑adversary countries (credited to the General Fund), administer refunds (including expedited military refunds), adopt rules, and use electronic funds transfer.

- DHHS (in vulnerable‑adult provisions) may provide information to financial institutions; DHHS claims under §68‑919 receive payment priority despite estate payment order rules.

No agency appropriations or other budget line‑item allocations are specified beyond the General Fund crediting of excise tax revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Banking and Finance: New licensing controls under the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act require applicant/key‑person certifications that they are not "foreign adversary persons"; director may presume lack of fitness and pursue license revocation if certifications/info not provided. Changes apply to existing licenses; director must send supplemental information requests within 60 days and prescribe new forms within 30 days. DBF must provide money‑transmitter data to the Department of Revenue as requested and may receive shared information from Revenue. On Revenue request, DBF may claim against a licensee's surety bond for unpaid remittance taxes.

- Department of Revenue: Imposes a 25% excise tax on remittances to foreign adversary countries (except Cuba and Venezuela). Remittance providers collect and remit quarterly; Revenue credits receipts to the General Fund, may share tax information with DBF, adopt rules, and establish expedited refunds for military.

- Department of Health and Human Services: No provisions or funding changes affecting DHHS in the added text.
