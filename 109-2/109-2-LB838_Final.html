Summary

LB838 designates the Department of Banking and Finance (DBF) as the supervising "department" under the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act, expanding the director’s authority over certification, investigations, application forms, supplemental‑information requests, and license revocations, and establishing a $1,500 nonrefundable license application fee. DBF must provide money‑transmitter data to the Department of Revenue (DOR) and, at DOR’s request, may claim against a licensee’s surety bond for unpaid remittance tax. DOR will administer a new 25% excise tax on certain cash remittances to listed foreign adversary countries, collect it quarterly, may adopt rules, and remit receipts to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. DHHS is referenced for vulnerable‑adult protections and estate‑claim priority. The bill includes no explicit appropriations or agency budget‑allocation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Banking and Finance (DBF): new duties to (1) assist the director in enforcing amended Nebraska Money Transmitters Act (prescribe forms, request supplemental certifications within set timeframes), (2) provide money-transmitter data to the Department of Revenue (DOR) on request, and (3) allow DOR to request DBF to make claims against a licensee's surety bond to satisfy the new tax. DBF/Director may initiate revocation proceedings for noncompliant licensees.

- Department of Revenue (DOR): authorized to administer a new 25% excise tax on remittance transfers to residents of designated “foreign adversary” countries (Cuba and Venezuela excepted), set exemptions, require quarterly collection/remittance, promulgate rules (including expedited refunds for servicemembers), share tax-related information with DBF, and remit receipts to the General Fund.
