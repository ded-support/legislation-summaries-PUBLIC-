Summary

LB900 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-1720 governing distress warrants for tax collection. It sets the issuing fee at $20 per warrant and provides mileage at the county sheriff rate established in §33-117. It establishes commission rates on taxes collected by distress and sale: $0.10 per dollar on sums up to $500 and $0.08 per dollar on amounts over $500. The bill directs that fees, mileage, and commissions be taxed to the delinquent parties and collected with the tax. Mileage paid under §33-117 when taxes are not collected by distress is preserved; if mileage is later recovered with the tax, that mileage amount is payable to the county treasurer and must be credited to the county general fund. The original section is repealed.
