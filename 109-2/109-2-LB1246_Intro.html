Summary

LB1246 directs agency-specific duties as follows:

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): defines "budget administrator" as the budget division's administrator and requires the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority (NIFA) to record all public-fund revenues, balances, and expenditures and to submit reports to the budget administrator (DAS), the Legislative Fiscal Analyst, and the Director of Economic Development — including a condition-of-funds report 20 days before each regular legislative session and monthly transaction accountings. NIFA must furnish related records on request.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): prescribes Affordable Housing Trust Fund procedures administered by DED — annual aim to allocate at least 30% of funds to each congressional district, a 90‑day application period, and a grant disbursement schedule (80% upon approval, 20% at project completion beginning July 1, 2026). Requires quarterly and annual reporting by recipients (effective Oct. 1, 2025 and July 1, 2027), authorizes recapture of unused or incomplete-project funds (credited to the Trust Fund), and allows DED to prioritize funding net of its actual administrative costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): the bill defines "budget administrator" as the DAS budget division administrator and requires the authority to record all public‑fund revenues, balances, and expenditures in an accounting system and to submit a condition report at least 20 days before each regular legislative session. The budget administrator may request and must receive monthly accountings of transactions (grant/loan payments, invoices, administration/contracting expenditures) and any records related to expenditure or planned expenditure of public funds.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): the Director of Economic Development must receive the same condition reports and monthly accountings and may request related records. Beginning July 1, 2027, the department will set annual reporting for qualified recipients to track obligated funds, repayment terms, balances, and housing‑equity estimates; noncompliance can require return of awards and transfer of recoverable housing equity to the department.

- No explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are made.
