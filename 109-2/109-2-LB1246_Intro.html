Summary

LB1246 requires interaction between the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority (authority), the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), and the Department of Economic Development (DED). It defines the budget administrator as the DAS budget-division official and obligates the authority to record all public-fund activity and to submit condition reports and monthly (or on-request) accountings of public-fund transactions to the DAS budget administrator, the Legislative Fiscal Analyst, and the DED Director.

For DED, the bill: sets a goal to allocate at least 30% of Affordable Housing Trust Fund dollars to each congressional district; mandates a ≥90-day application period; requires DED to disburse 80% of grant funds at approval and 20% at project completion (effective July 1, 2026); imposes quarterly and, beginning July 1, 2027, annual reporting/recordkeeping and recapture rules; and directs DED to fund applications in priority order using available funds less actual administrative costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Defines "budget administrator" as the Budget Division budget administrator within the Department of Administrative Services (DAS).

- Requires the authority to report public-fund status to the DAS budget administrator, the Legislative Fiscal Analyst, and the Director of Economic Development at least 20 days before each regular legislative session.

- Requires monthly accounting to those same recipients (and on request) detailing payments on grant/loan awards, invoices, and administration/contracting expenditures, and to furnish any requested authority records.

- Directs the Department of Economic Development to adopt annual reporting requirements beginning July 1, 2027, for qualified recipients to report obligations, repayment terms, unspent/repaid balances and housing-award equity estimates; noncompliance may trigger return of awards and transfer of recoverable housing-equity positions to the department.

- No explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included.
