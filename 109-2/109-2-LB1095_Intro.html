Summary

LB1095 directs the Nebraska Investment Council and the state investment officer to prohibit investment of Public Employees Retirement Board retirement-system assets in defined "restricted entities" (primarily certain China-related entities and specified U.S. government lists), identify any existing holdings, and divest from them as soon as practicable consistent with fiduciary duties. Appointed council members are confirmed as fiduciaries for retirement systems and state funds (with liability protections where Class V school systems manage their own investments). Reporting and notification duties: monthly operational reports from the state investment officer to the Governor, State Treasurer, Auditor of Public Accounts, Department of Administrative Services, and the council; semiannual electronic reports to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee and Clerk listing each restricted entity, asset class, and amounts (first due Jan 15, 2027). The State Treasurer must publish and semiannually update a restricted-entity list on its website by Oct 1, 2026, and notify the council and state investment officer. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are changed. The bill declares an emergency and takes effect upon enactment.
