Summary: LB875 amends the Equipment Business Regulation Act (sections 87-704 and 87-706). It prohibits suppliers from including dealer-agreement terms that waive compliance with the Act or state law, or that select out‑of‑state venue or law. It bars suppliers from forcing dealers to accept unordered equipment/parts, imposing unique amendment terms unless applied to similarly situated dealers, or altering/terminating agreements for events beyond a dealer’s control (unless in security‑agreement default). Renewal cannot be conditioned on major capital changes without at least one yearʼs prior written demand (with a historic caveat). Suppliers must maintain repair‑parts availability and provide at least an annual surplus‑parts return program with specified return-credit rules; the bill removes any authorization for a dealer to waive that return opportunity. Original sections are repealed. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two prohibitions on dealer agreements: (1) they may not include any term purporting to waive compliance with the Equipment Business Regulation Act or any other state law applicable to those agreements; and (2) they may not require jurisdiction/venue outside the state or mandate the application of another state’s law to disputes. No state agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.
