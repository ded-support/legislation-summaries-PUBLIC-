Summary

LB875 amends the Equipment Business Regulation Act (Reissue Revised Statutes §§87‑704, 87‑706). It prohibits suppliers from requiring dealers to accept unrequested equipment or features, imposing amendatory agreements unless imposed on similarly situated dealers, conditioning renewals on undisclosed capital construction, terminating agreements for events beyond a dealer’s control (except security‑agreement default), and including contract clauses that waive compliance with the Act or require out‑of‑state venue or law. It requires suppliers to maintain parts availability and to offer an annual surplus‑parts return program (dealer may return parts equal to at least 6% of prior‑12‑month parts purchases; credit at least 85% of listed price; credit issued within 90 days), with eligibility and return rules. No state agency or department is identified, and the bill includes no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes. Original §§87‑704 and 87‑706 are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two prohibitions on dealer agreements: (1) they cannot include any clause that waives compliance with the Equipment Business Regulation Act or other state laws, and (2) they cannot require disputes to be resolved outside the state or under another state's laws. No government agencies or funding changes are mentioned.
