Summary

LB864 designates the Department of Labor as the administering agency for the Intern Nebraska internship program (definition: “Department means the Department of Labor”) and assigns related duties to it, including developing a biennial qualified action plan (submitted to the Governor) and an annual report to the Governor and Legislature. Financial provisions: the department may not allocate more than $1,500,000 per fiscal year from the Job Training Cash Fund or its subaccounts for the program. Individual grant limits are set (maximum $7,500 per internship; application/award limits for businesses). The Intern Nebraska Cash Fund is created to finance the program, and the Department of Labor (or its contractor) may use up to 5% of any appropriation for administrative services.
