Summary

LB864 designates the Department of Labor as the administering agency for a statewide student internship grant program. The bill authorizes the department to award and disburse internships, develop biennial qualified action plans (submitted to the Governor), adopt rules, require recipient reporting, enter contracts with Nebraska nonprofits, and use external data agreements. Funding provisions: the department may not allocate more than $1,500,000 in any one fiscal year from the Job Training Cash Fund or its subaccounts for the internship program, may receive public or private funds, and the newly created Intern Nebraska Cash Fund will hold legislative transfers and donations for the program. The Department of Labor, or its contractor, may use up to 5% of any appropriation for administrative services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds new provisions (sections 2–5) to the state Labor statutes by assigning them to Chapter 81, Article 4—thereby placing the material under the statutory domain overseen by the Department of Labor. No language in the added text creates or changes any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.
