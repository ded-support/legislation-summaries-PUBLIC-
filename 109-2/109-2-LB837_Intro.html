Summary

LB837 establishes rounding rules for cash transactions and cash wages. It permits sellers or payors to round cent amounts: if the final cent digit is 1, 2, 6, or 7, round down to the nearest 5-cent increment; if 3, 4, 8, or 9, round up to the nearest 5 cents. Transactions totaling 1 or 2 cents must be rounded up to 5 cents. The provision does not apply to noncash methods (negotiable instruments, electronic fund transfers, checks, gift cards, money orders, credit cards, etc.). The bill does not reference any government agencies, departments, or contain changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a cash‑rounding rule for persons selling goods/services or paying cash wages: if the total cash transaction (including taxes) ends in 1, 2, 6, or 7 cents, round down to the nearest 5 cents; if it ends in 3, 4, 8, or 9 cents, round up to the nearest 5 cents; but totals of 1 or 2 cents must be rounded up to 5 cents. Exempts non‑cash payments (checks, cards, EFTs, gift cards, money orders, etc.). No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
