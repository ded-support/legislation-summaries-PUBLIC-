Summary

LB837 establishes rounding rules for cash transactions and cash wages: sellers or payers may round a total (including taxes) down to the nearest 5 cents if the final cent digit is 1, 2, 6, or 7; may round up if the final digit is 3, 4, 8, or 9; and must round up totals of 1 or 2 cents to 5 cents. The provision applies only to cash payments, cash transfers, and cash wages and excludes noncash methods (checks, cards, electronic fund transfers, gift cards, money orders, etc.). The bill does not reference any state agencies or departments and contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a cash‑transaction rounding rule: cash sales, cash transfers, or cash wage payments may be rounded to the nearest five cents—round down when the cents digit is 1,2,6,7; round up when it is 3,4,8,9; and must be rounded up to five cents when the total is one or two cents. Exempts non‑cash payments (cards, checks, EFTs, gift cards, money orders). No government agencies, departments, funding, or appropriations are mentioned.
