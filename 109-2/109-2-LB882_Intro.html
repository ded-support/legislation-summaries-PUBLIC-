Summary

LB882 amends homestead exemption procedures (Secs. 77-3506, 77-3512). Key agency roles and changes:

- United States Department of Veterans Affairs: VA certification remains required to establish eligibility for certain veteran categories, but for exemptions under subdivisions (2)(c), (d), and (e) certification need only be renewed in subsequent years that are evenly divisible by five (rather than every year).

- County assessor: continues to receive homestead exemption applications; veterans and surviving spouses qualifying under subdivisions (2)(a), (b), or (f) are no longer required to refile in subsequent years.

- County board: may extend annual filing deadline to July 20 by majority vote.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a surviving spouse who received a homestead exemption under subdivision (2)(b) or (f) — including surviving spouses of veterans/servicemen/servicewomen — who remarries before age 57 loses the exemption and must notify the county assessor of the remarriage within a reasonable time. The only government office referenced is the county assessor. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
