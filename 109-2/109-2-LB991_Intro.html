Summary

Bill LB991 amends §60-6,175 to authorize school districts to install, operate, or contract for stop‑signal‑arm camera systems on district‑owned school buses. Data collected must include imprinted/embedded date, time, and location; buses with cameras must display a warning readable at 100 feet. School districts may provide copies of collected data. Enforcement: a peace officer must review camera data before a law enforcement agency (as defined in §79‑2702) may issue a citation; citations must be mailed to the registered owner within six days. Registered owners may appeal to the issuing law enforcement agency within 30 days and the agency must rescind the citation if evidence shows the owner was not the driver. The bill amends and replaces the original §60‑6,175. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for school districts to install, operate, or contract with private vendors for stop‑signal‑arm camera systems on school buses, and requires date/time/location to be embedded in collected data and visible warning signage on buses. School districts may share camera data or copies. Enforcement: a peace officer must review camera data before issuing a citation; citations are mailed to the registered vehicle owner within six days; owners may appeal to the law enforcement agency that employed the officer within 30 days and present evidence of non‑driver status; that law enforcement agency must rescind the citation if it finds adequate evidence. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
