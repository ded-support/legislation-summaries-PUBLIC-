Summary

LB991 authorizes school districts to install, operate, or contract with private vendors to provide, operate, or support stop‑signal‑arm camera systems on school buses. Collected data must bear date, time, and location; buses with cameras must display a warning readable at 100 feet. School districts may disclose or copy collected data.

The bill amends enforcement in §60‑6,175 to permit citation for stop‑arm violations based on camera data only after a peace officer reviews the data. Citations must be mailed to the registered owner within six days. Registered owners may appeal to the law enforcement agency (as defined in §79‑2702) within 30 days; the agency must rescind the citation if evidence shows the owner was not the driver. No funding or appropriation provisions are included.

Summary: School districts are authorized to install or operate stop‑signal‑arm camera systems on district-owned school buses or contract with private vendors to install, operate, or support such systems. Data collected must embed date, time, and location; districts may provide or copy that data. Buses with cameras must display a warning readable from 100 feet. The registered owner may appeal to the law enforcement agency that employed the officer within 30 days and present evidence they were not the driver; the agency must rescind the citation if it finds adequate evidence. “Law enforcement agency” is defined by reference to section 79‑2702.
