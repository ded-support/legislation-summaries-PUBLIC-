Summary

LB1250 authorizes cities (primary, first-, or second-class) and villages to sell or transfer municipally owned waterworks, sewer systems, or water systems to an Indian tribe when: (1) the facility is located and operates entirely within that tribe’s tribal lands; and (2) the sale or transfer is approved by the city’s or village’s qualified electors. The governing body must submit the proposition to voters at a general or special city/village election (it may be placed on the ballot with other special-election matters), and votes are canvassed and declared as in other local elections. “Indian tribe” is defined as federally recognized or state‑acknowledged tribes. The bill contains no agency-specific mandates, funding changes, or appropriations.
