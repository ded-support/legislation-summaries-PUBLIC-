Summary

LB1250 authorizes Nebraska cities (primary, first-class, second-class) and villages to sell or transfer municipally owned waterworks, sewer systems, or water systems to an Indian tribe if: (1) the facility is located and operates entirely within that tribe’s tribal lands, and (2) the sale or transfer is approved by the city’s or village’s qualified electors at a general or special city/village election. The city or village governing body must submit the question to voters in the usual manner; votes are canvassed and declared as in other local elections. "Indian tribe" is defined as federally recognized or state‑acknowledged. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
