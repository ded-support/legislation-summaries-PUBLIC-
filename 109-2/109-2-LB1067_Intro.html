Summary

- Department of Economic Development: The bill specifies the Affordable Housing Trust Fund shall be administered by the Department of Economic Development as part of its comprehensive housing affordability strategy. The Affordable Housing Trust Fund will receive revenue pursuant to section 76-903.

- Department of Revenue: Taxpayers may seek a declaratory ruling under rules promulgated by the Department of Revenue. The Department of Revenue may not overturn a county assessor's determination about whether a transaction qualifies as a sale unless the department's review finds the assessor's determination incorrect.

- Funding/disbursement (per section 76-903): Each $3.82 documentary stamp tax is allocated, including $0.95 to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and $0.75 each to the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund and the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund.
