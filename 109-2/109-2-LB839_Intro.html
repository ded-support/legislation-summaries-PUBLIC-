Summary

LB839 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §19-5504 (Municipal Density and Missing Middle Housing Act) to revise cities' reporting requirements to the Legislature’s Urban Affairs Committee. Each city must electronically submit a biennial report (by July 1 every two years) detailing zoning and housing metrics, including: residential zoning overviews; areas permitting multifamily/middle housing; five-year new-construction and annexation breakdowns by housing type; per-unit cost estimates; use of density bonuses/incentives; accessory dwelling unit allowances; incentives for affordable housing; percentages of residential areas declared substandard, blighted, or extremely blighted under the Community Development Law; demographic housing-need analysis; historic multifamily unit counts and Fair Housing Act accessibility compliance; and status of affordable housing action plans under §19-5505. The Urban Affairs Committee may require public presentations. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed. The original section 19-5504 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds reporting of (1) the number of multifamily housing units built within the city and issued an initial certificate of occupancy since March 13, 1991, and (2) how many of those qualify as "covered multifamily dwellings" and were designed/constructed to meet the accessibility requirements of the federal Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 3601 et seq.; definition per 42 U.S.C. 3604). No government agencies or departments are newly created or referenced beyond the FHAct citation, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
