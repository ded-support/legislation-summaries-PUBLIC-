Summary

LB839 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §19-5504 to require each city to electronically submit, on or before July 1, 2021 and biennially thereafter, a detailed report to the Urban Affairs Committee of the Legislature on municipal zoning and actions affecting affordable housing. Required report items include: zoning overviews; percentages of residential areas permitting multifamily and middle housing; five-year new construction and annexation breakdowns by housing type; per-unit cost estimates; use of density bonuses/incentives; accessory dwelling unit allowances; incentives/regulatory relief; percentages of residential areas declared substandard, blighted, or extremely blighted under the Community Development Law; demographic housing-need analyses; multifamily unit counts since 1991 with Fair Housing accessibility compliance; and status of affordable housing action plans. The Urban Affairs Committee may require a public presentation. The original section is repealed. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirement to report two counts: (1) multifamily housing units built within the city and issued initial certificates of occupancy since March 13, 1991, and (2) how many of those are "covered multifamily dwellings" that were designed and constructed to meet accessibility requirements of the federal Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 3601 et seq.). Defines "covered multifamily dwelling" by reference to 42 U.S.C. 3604. No state agencies or departments are named and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
