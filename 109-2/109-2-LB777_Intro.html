Summary

LB777 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-908, expanding duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) in administering the Medical Assistance (Medicaid) program. DHHS must prepare and electronically submit an annual summary and analysis to the Governor and Legislature by December 1, with detailed metrics (applications approved/denied; eligibility determinations; case closures and enrollment by program, geography, and race/ethnicity; ex parte renewals; processing times; call metrics; fair hearing outcomes and link to redacted decisions). New reporting specifically requires detailed tracking and public disclosure related to community engagement requirements, concurrent enrollment identification (including external vendor contracts and links), and descriptions of cost sharing and payment sources. The bill references DHHS authority over waivers/managed care, school-based health centers, and federally qualified health center reimbursement. No appropriations or budget changes are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds reporting and implementation requirements for the state Medicaid agency ("the department") and related state contracts. Key provisions require the department to: provide the maximum retroactive Medicaid coverage allowed by federal law; publish detailed status and metrics about proposed and implemented community‑engagement requirements (implementation plans, affected recipients, counts of denials/approvals/verification failures, exemption and compliance details, re‑enrollment rates at 90 days and 12 months, and data sources); identify external vendors the state contracts to assess community engagement or concurrent enrollment (with links to each contract); report counts and re‑enrollment rates for cases terminated for concurrent enrollment; and describe cost‑sharing, premiums, copays, and deductibles (amounts and payment sources). No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
