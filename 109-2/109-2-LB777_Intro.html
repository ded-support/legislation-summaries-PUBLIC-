Summary

LB777 amends §68-908, assigning the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) additional eligibility and expanded reporting duties as administrator of the Medical Assistance (Medicaid) program. DHHS must continue rulemaking, contracts, waivers/managed care (including under the Nebraska Behavioral Health Services Act), confidentiality, and retroactive coverage. The bill requires an annual report to the Governor and Legislature (by Dec. 1) with detailed program metrics and breakdowns by eligibility category, local public health district, and race/ethnicity; links to redacted fair hearing decisions; and expanded disclosures on community engagement requirements, concurrent enrollment identification, and cost‑sharing amounts/payment sources. It also requires DHHS to list external vendors used to verify compliance or identify concurrent enrollees, including links to each vendor contract. No appropriations or funding changes are provided. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires "the department" administering medical assistance to: provide the maximum retroactive coverage permitted by federal law; report detailed status of proposed/implemented community engagement requirements (implementation plan, authority/effective date, affected recipients; counts of denials, verification failures, additional-information requests, exemptions and types, approvals by compliance type, re‑enrollments within 90 days and 12 months); list data sources used to verify compliance and identify external vendors the state contracts to assess compliance, with links to each contract. Also requires reporting on concurrent‑enrollment cases and vendors used to detect them (with contract links) and re‑enrollment rates after termination (90 days and 12 months). Finally, requires a description of cost sharing (premiums, copays, deductibles), their amounts, and the payment source for collected cost sharing. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
