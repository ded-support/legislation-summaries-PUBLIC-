Summary

The bill amends §81-407 to (1) confirm the Department of Labor as administrator of the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and specify permitted uses (workforce development grants; program administration; unemployment insurance tax and benefit costs; labor studies; and Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot Investment Act purposes); (2) add the Nebraska Youth Pre‑Apprentice Conservation Program Fund as an allowable transfer destination from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund (transfers may also be made to the General Fund or Nebraska Opportunity Grant Fund) at the Legislature’s direction; and (3) retain the Commissioner of Labor’s authority over grant approvals and related reporting. It also authorizes transfers to be made administratively as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language authorizes the budget administrator in the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services to direct administrative transfers “after the effective date of this act, on such dates and in such amounts” and explicitly adds the Nebraska Youth Pre‑Apprentice Conservation Program Fund as a fund subject to those directions. No specific dollar amounts, appropriations, or new budget allocations are provided. There is no mention of the Department of Labor or any change affecting that agency.
