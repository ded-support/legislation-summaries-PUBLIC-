Summary

LB1052 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-257 to explicitly authorize a school board or board of education to impose emergency exclusion, short- or long-term suspension, expulsion, or mandatory reassignment of a student for conduct violating board rules, provided such actions comply with procedures in the Student Discipline Act. The bill harmonizes statutory language with that act and repeals the original §79-257. No state agencies beyond local school boards/boards of education are named, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary provisions.
