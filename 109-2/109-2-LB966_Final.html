Summary

LB966 (Hunger-Free Schools Act) references the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) in its nutrition definitions: eligible meals are those served under USDA child nutrition programs, and the "federal reimbursement rate" is defined as the payment published by the United States Department of Agriculture under the federal Child Nutrition Act and the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act (rates referenced as of January 1, 2026). The bill includes an appropriation of $1,151,180 Cash Funds for state aid (to be used only for that purpose) and expressly prohibits using those funds for permanent or temporary salaries and per diems for state employees. No other Department of Agriculture entities are mentioned.
