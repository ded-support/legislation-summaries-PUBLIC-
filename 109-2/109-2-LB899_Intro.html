Summary

LB899 amends provisions administered by the Nebraska Board of Engineers and Architects. Key agency references and changes:

- Board of Engineers and Architects: clarifies seal requirements (design set by the board; signature and date must be placed across or adjacent without obscuring name/number); board rulemaking authority for seal application and for increasing certain building-size exemptions under the Negotiated Rulemaking Act; board may adopt NCEES examinations/guidelines, set fees, issue one-time temporary permits, and treat exam materials as nonpublic.

- Department of Water, Energy, and Environment: retained as the permitting authority referenced for earthmoving and livestock-waste facility exemptions.

- State building code (section 71-6403) limits referenced; Consumer Price Index (Bureau of Labor Statistics) used for adjusting public-works dollar threshold.

- Accreditation bodies referenced: Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET, Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board, National Architectural Accrediting Board.

No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations. Original sections cited are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the date of a seal's application be placed adjacent to, and not obscure, the licensee's name or license number on the seal page. Adds that an application for licensure as a professional engineer must be submitted with a fee set by "the board" (the licensing board). No specific state agencies beyond the board are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
