Summary

LB899 amends provisions administered by the state Board of Engineers and Architects. It (1) prescribes seal content and requires the licensee’s signature and date to be placed across or adjacent to the seal, authorizes the board to adopt rules for seal application, and maintains board authority over seal use and discipline; (2) retains and clarifies exemptions to architectural regulation and authorizes the board (via the Negotiated Rulemaking Act) to adjust size limits and to adjust the public‑works dollar threshold every five years using the Consumer Price Index published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics; (3) references the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment for permit-related exemptions (earthmoving and livestock waste facilities); and (4) revises engineer enrollment, licensure, temporary‑permit, and examination procedures, including board adoption of National Council of Examiners for Engineering and Surveying exams. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two requirements: placement of the seal-application date so it does not obscure the licensee’s name or license number on the seal page; and that an application for licensure as a professional engineer must be submitted with a fee established by “the board” (i.e., the licensing board). No specific state agency beyond “the board” is named, and there are no appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes—only an applicant fee set by the board.
