Summary

LB1087 creates the Nebraska‑Ireland Commission and explicitly places it, for administrative and budgetary purposes, within the Department of Economic Development. The Department of Economic Development will administer a newly established Nebraska‑Ireland Fund to defray the commission’s administrative expenses and support its activities. The fund may receive legislative transfers, monies raised by or for the commission, federal funds, gifts, grants, and other sources; investment of fund balances is assigned to the state investment officer under existing state investment statutes, with earnings credited to the fund. The bill contains no specific appropriations or dollar amounts. The Department of Agriculture is not referenced.
