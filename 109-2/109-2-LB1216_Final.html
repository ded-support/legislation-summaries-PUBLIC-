Summary

This bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §83‑182 to assign specific educational duties to the Department of Correctional Services (through the Director of Correctional Services), the State Department of Education, and the State Board of Education. The director must offer educational services and programming to committed persons under age 21 who have not earned a high school diploma or equivalent and must ensure special education services as required by state and federal law. The State Department of Education will determine substantially similar minimum standards and a core curriculum (language arts, social science, science, mathematics; optional vocational, computer, arts, physical fitness), and the State Board may adopt rules to implement the section. Programs may be provided directly by the department or via partnerships with educational institutions. No funding or appropriation changes are included. The original §83‑182 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirement that the director must provide educational services/programming to persons committed to the department who are under 21 and lack a high‑school diploma, including required special education services under state and federal law; participation is voluntary except as needed to satisfy legal obligations. The State Department of Education will determine minimum standards and a core curriculum (language arts, social science, science, mathematics) and may allow vocational, computer, arts, and physical fitness offerings; programs may be run by the department or via partnerships with educational institutions. The State Board of Education may adopt rules to implement and ensure compliance. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.
