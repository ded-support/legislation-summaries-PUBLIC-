Summary

LB1081 (2026) requires that, beginning January 1, 2027, state agencies and political subdivisions (defined to include cities, counties, school districts, public power and natural resources districts, interlocal and joint public agencies, etc.) may not use public funds to purchase U.S. or Nebraska flags unless the flags are 100% manufactured in the United States. Exceptions apply to hand‑held flags under 8" x 6". The bill amends §72‑2205 to require the State Capitol administrator to comply when acquiring flags (existing proceeds continue to be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Capitol Restoration Cash Fund) and amends §79‑708 to require school governing bodies to comply when purchasing flags (costs remain payable from school general operating funds). No new appropriations or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "political subdivision" and "state agency" (explicitly including departments, commissions, boards, offices, councils, authorities, and other constitutionally or statutorily created state entities). Beginning January 1, 2027, any state agency or political subdivision is prohibited from using public funds to purchase U.S. or Nebraska flags unless the flag is 100% manufactured in the United States from U.S.-grown or -produced materials. Exempts hand-held flags and flags smaller than 8"×6"; when acquiring those, the "administrator" and governing bodies must comply with section 1 of the act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
