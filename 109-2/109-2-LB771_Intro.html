Summary

LB771 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75-327 governing transportation network companies (TNCs). It requires TNCs to file and keep current the rates they use (including dynamic pricing) with the commission and to charge consistent rates; otherwise TNCs remain largely exempt from rate regulation. When using dynamic pricing, TNC apps must give a clear visible notice, require passenger confirmation, and provide a fare estimator. The Governor may permit or prohibit dynamic pricing in any political subdivision named in a state of emergency proclamation or by executive order; prohibition is limited to the time specified. The bill also maintains limits that rides be prearranged and drivers work no more than 12 hours per 24-hour period, repealing the original section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Gives the Governor discretion to allow or prohibit "dynamic pricing" in any political subdivision named in a gubernatorial state-of-emergency proclamation; any prohibition must be issued in the proclamation or by executive order and is limited to the time period the Governor specifies. Outside declared emergency areas, dynamic pricing is generally permitted unless another provision restricts it. No other agencies or departments are mentioned and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
