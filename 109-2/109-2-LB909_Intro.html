Summary

LB909 references the two agencies as follows:

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): Board of Parole shall forward victim names/addresses to DCS; DCS must include victims' names in inmate files (not public); victims may request notifications from DCS; DCS (or county corrections) must notify victims of furloughs, transfers to community status, escapes/returns, releases, discharge, program placements, earliest release/parole calculations, sentence reductions, and victims' rights to submit statements. DCS must forward victim names/addresses to DHHS when a commitment petition is filed. DCS may assign offenders to incarceration work camps and the Director may assign offenders, with covered-offense exclusions.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Victims under 18 reporting sexual assault must report to DHHS; confidentiality exceptions permit sharing with DHHS for child-abuse investigations; DHHS must notify victims regarding petitions or changes in mental-health/sex-offender commitments.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text creates a new offense prohibiting sexual penetration or sexual contact by a law enforcement officer with a "minor" (defined as age 16–18), and criminalizes patterns/schemes to do so. Consent is expressly not a defense. Definitions reference existing law (law enforcement officer per §81‑1401; sexual contact/penetration per §28‑318). Penalties: sexual penetration by an officer = sexual abuse by a law enforcement officer in the first degree (Class IIA felony); sexual contact = second degree (Class IIIA felony); pattern/scheme = third degree (Class IV felony).

Impacts on agencies: convictions would place offenders under Department of Correctional Services custody consistent with felony classifications, potentially increasing DOC caseloads. The Department of Health and Human Services is not mentioned and no new victim‑service or health program funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
