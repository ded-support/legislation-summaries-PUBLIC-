Summary

LB909 provisions referencing the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- County attorneys must forward victims' names/addresses for felony convictions to the Board of Parole, DCS, county corrections agencies, DHHS, and Board of Pardons; victims may waive. Agencies must include the victim in the offender file (not public) and adopt rules to implement notice duties.

- DCS/county corrections must notify victims of furloughs/releases/transfers, escapes/returns, participation in or completion of community programs, discharges, sentence‑calculation information, sentence reductions, and victims' rights to submit statements.

- DCS shall forward victim names/addresses to DHHS when a county attorney files a mental‑health or sex‑offender commitment petition; DHHS must notify victims of commitment proceedings, escapes, discharges, furloughs, and program releases for committed persons.

- For victims under 18, reporting sexual assault to DHHS qualifies as a report. Victim contact information maintained by DCS and DHHS is exempt from public disclosure.

- No funding or appropriation changes included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new offense, "sexual abuse by a law enforcement officer," defining law enforcement officer, minor (ages 16–18), sexual contact/penetration, barring consent as a defense, and creating three felony degrees (Class IIA for penetration, Class IIIA for contact, Class IV for pattern/scheme). It also inserts the new offense into statutory lists of "covered offenses" alongside other sexual‑abuse provisions (including abuse of inmates, parolees, detainees).

Impacts on agencies:

- Department of Correctional Services: will likely see effects on prosecutions, sentencing, inmate classification, confinement for these covered offenses, and related custody/parole/registration processing.

- Department of Health and Human Services: may see increased involvement for victim services, reporting, and investigations involving minors.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
