Summary

LB1116 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act and affects the following state entities and funds:

- State Treasurer: transfers certified amounts to a newly created Sports Arena Facility Support Fund.

- State Investment Officer: invests fund assets under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Legislature: appropriates distributions from the Support Fund to approved political subdivisions per statutory formulas (generally up to 70% of specified state sales tax revenues for most facilities; 25% for sports complexes in cities of the second class or villages). Excess after year ten may revert to the General Fund.

- Fund and grant limits: overall cap of $100 million per facility; large public stadiums limited to $25 million total and $1.25 million annually (no payments before July 1, 2027), though approvals on/after July 19, 2024 may fund up to 100% of final cost within those caps.

- Residual tax allocations: portions directed to the Support the Arts Cash Fund, Convention Center Support Fund, or Civic and Community Center Financing Fund as specified.

- Board procedures: public hearing within 30 days of application review and board decision within 30 days after hearing; temporary approvals become permanent upon voter approval or issuance of building permit.

- Effective immediately due to emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds deadlines for "the board" to decide on applications: the board must determine approval within 30 days after completing its review (or, for applications filed on/after the act's effective date, within 30 days after the public hearing required by section 13‑3105). For applications filed before the effective date, the decision deadline is 30 days after that public hearing or 30 days after the effective date, whichever is later. Applications are deemed approved unless the board finds the project ineligible or that state assistance would not be in the state's best interest.
