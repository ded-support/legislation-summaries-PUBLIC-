Summary

LB1116 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act. Key state actors and funds affected:

- Board (Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Board): must hold a public hearing within 30 days after review; applications decided within 30 days after that hearing; approval tied to voter approval or building-permit milestones for some preliminary approvals; board quorum and voting clarified.

- State Treasurer: transfers certified amounts into the newly referenced Sports Arena Facility Support Fund.

- State investment officer: invests fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and State Funds Investment Act.

- Legislature: appropriates distributions from the Support Fund to political subdivisions.

Funding/limits:

- State assistance sourced from specified state sales tax revenues: up to 70% for most facilities (25% for sports complexes in second‑class cities/villages).

- General cap of $100M per facility; large public stadiums limited to $25M total and $1.25M/year, with no payments before July 1, 2027 (but §13‑3103 also allows up to 100% of final cost for large stadiums approved on/after July 19, 2024).

- Ten‑year funding limits for arts/cultural or sporting event promotion and for small-city sports complexes; twenty years for large public stadiums.

- Residual revenue allocations: portions to Support the Arts Cash Fund, Convention Center Support Fund, Civic and Community Center Financing Fund, or General Fund as specified.

- Emergency clause: effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the board to decide an application within 30 days after completing its review. For applications filed on/after the act's effective date, the 30‑day clock runs from the public hearing held under section 13‑3105. For applications filed before the effective date, the board has 30 days from that hearing or 30 days from the effective date, whichever is later. Applications are deemed approved unless the board finds the project ineligible or that state assistance would not be in the state's best interest. Mentions only "the board" and the public hearing under section 13‑3105; no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
