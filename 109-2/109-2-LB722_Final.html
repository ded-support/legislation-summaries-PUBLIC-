Summary

LB722 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-812 to expand allowable uses of the Behavioral Health Services Fund. The Department/Division of Behavioral Health may now use fund monies for housing-related assistance for very low-income adults with substance use disorder and may include such individuals in landlord risk mitigation payment eligibility. Money transferred into the fund under §76-903 must be used for these housing supports; if obligations are met, the Division may distribute up to 20% of that money to regional behavioral health authorities for housing acquisition or rehabilitation. The Division will set a distribution formula in consultation with regional behavioral health authorities and the Department and must contract with each regional authority. Funds remain investable by the state investment officer under existing state investment laws; no new appropriation is created.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds/clarifies statutory language to refer to "an individual" and to include "a diagnosis with a v‑code, a disorder, a disability, or substance use disorder," and expressly states (clause (iv)) that "Substance use disorder" has the same meaning as in section 71‑430. No government agencies, departments, or any funding/appropriations or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
