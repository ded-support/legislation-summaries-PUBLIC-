Summary

The bill amends section 77-5723 to add a requirement (subsection (6)(e)) that a company must update the Department of Revenue annually on any changes affecting its timetable for expected sales tax refunds. If the company fails to do so, the Tax Commissioner may defer any pending sales tax refunds until the company complies. No appropriations or other funding changes for the Department of Revenue are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text lets taxpayers with active Nebraska Advantage tier 6 agreements elect to have the bill’s amendments to §77‑5723 apply to their agreement by making a one‑time payment of $90,000 within a time period set by the Tax Commissioner. The Department of Revenue (via the Tax Commissioner) administers the election window and fee collection. If the taxpayer does not elect and pay, the preexisting §77‑5723 provisions remain in effect. No specific appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes for the Department are included.
