Summary

LB1191 amends the Nebraska Advantage Act with two provisions involving the Department of Revenue and related fees. It requires companies to update the Department of Revenue annually about any changes affecting the timetable for sales tax refunds; the Tax Commissioner may defer pending sales tax refunds if a company fails to comply. The bill also specifies nonrefundable application fees by project tier ($1,000 tier 1; $2,500 tiers 2, 3, 5; $5,000 tier 4; $10,000 tier 6) to be credited to the Nebraska Incentives Fund, and authorizes a one-time $90,000 fee for taxpayers with active tier 6 agreements who elect to adopt the bill’s changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner): the bill adds a compliance deadline requiring tier 2, 4, and 5 Nebraska Advantage projects to meet required employment and investment levels by the end of the sixth year after application. For existing tier 6 agreements still active on the bill’s effective date, taxpayers may opt in to the bill’s changes by making a one‑time election and paying a $90,000 fee within a period the Tax Commissioner prescribes; if they do, the amended section 77‑5723 will apply to their agreement. Absent election/payment, prior law remains. The Tax Commissioner must set the payment window, collect the $90,000 fee, and implement/administer the election and compliance monitoring. No other appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
