Summary

Bill LB889 amends the State Electrical Act (sections 81-2118 and 81-2143). Key agency-related changes:

- State Electrical Division and the State Electrical Board: licenses/registrations expire December 31 of even-numbered years; applicants must provide a social security number. The Board must set exam, issuance, and renewal fees by unanimous vote at levels "necessary to cover the costs" of the Division and Board while avoiding unnecessary surpluses. The Board must file an annual fee report with the Attorney General and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst (LFA) by July 1; the LFA report must be submitted electronically.

- Enforcement (Board authority): expands criminal provisions to make certain acts a Class IV felony, including knowingly making false statements, performing electrical work while claiming a license, failing to request inspections, obstructing inspectors, or failing to comply with the Act; a family-member exemption for unlicensed work is retained.

No direct appropriations or new budget allocations are included. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds license/registration renewal windows: primary renewal "beginning October 1 through November 31 of even-numbered years" upon fee payment, with a late-renewal period from December 1 through December 31 of even-numbered years. Also clarifies that subdivision (1)(b) does not prohibit a person from performing electrical work without a license for close family members (parent, stepparent, spouse, descendants, grandparents, siblings, cousins, uncles, aunts, including half‑blood or by adoption/marriage). No government agencies, departments, or budget/appropriation changes are referenced.
