Summary

The bill requires licensees under both the Nebraska Lottery and Raffle Act and the Nebraska Small Lottery and Raffle Act to register each progressive‑jackpot, multi‑draw lottery/raffle (e.g., Queen of Hearts) with the Department of Revenue and to post the game rules at the sale/conduct location. It authorizes sales via vending/dispensing devices and grants the Department of Revenue rule‑making authority to regulate such games. The bill’s preamble states it provides powers and duties to the Department of Revenue. It also reduces the required prize‑payout floor for lotteries/raffles conducted under the new progressive‑jackpot section from 65% to 50% of gross proceeds. No direct appropriations or other budget allocations to the department are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Authorizes licensed organizations and qualifying nonprofits to run progressive‑jackpot, multi‑draw lotteries/raffles (e.g., “Queen of Hearts”).

- Requires each such game to be registered with the Department of Revenue and game rules to be posted where the game is run or tickets are sold.

- Allows ticket sales through vending/dispensing devices.

- Grants the Department of Revenue authority to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations governing these games.

- Notes a provision that a lottery/raffle under section 4 need only allocate 50% of gross proceeds to prizes.

No provisions in the added text change Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or departmental budget allocations.
