Summary

LB799 assigns reporting and compilation duties to the materiel division of the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). The division (defined as DAS’s materiel division) will receive annual service-contract reports from cities, counties, and state agencies, compile them, and electronically submit a comprehensive report to the Governor, Clerk of the Legislature, and Urban Affairs Committee by September 1 each year. The division must also publish the report on the DAS website. For enforcement, the division will notify any noncompliant city or county, after which the State Treasurer must suspend all state-aid distributions to that jurisdiction until the required report is submitted; suspended distributions are restored once the report is filed. The bill includes no direct appropriations to DAS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Service Contract Reporting Act" and assigns the materiel division of the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) these duties: receive annual service-contract reports from cities, counties, and state agencies (due Aug. 1); compile and electronically submit a comprehensive statewide report to the Governor, Clerk of the Legislature, and Urban Affairs Committee (due Sept. 1); and post the report on DAS’s website. DAS must notify noncompliant cities/counties; upon such notice the State Treasurer is directed to suspend state-aid distributions to that jurisdiction until the report is filed (suspended funds are restored after filing). No explicit appropriations or new DAS funding or budget allocations are included.
