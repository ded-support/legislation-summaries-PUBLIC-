Summary

LB1155 tightens confidentiality and limited disclosure of tax-return information and clarifies agency-specific uses of that information. For the Department of Revenue, auditors (Auditor of Public Accounts and Legislative Audit Office) must notify the Tax Commissioner in advance, follow Department confidentiality safeguards, use a secure on-site storage area or secure remote access, keep standardized request records, restrict access to necessary personnel, report on procedures, and return records; the Tax Commissioner may permit other state tax officials to inspect returns only for tax enforcement and must compile school-district income data (including total AGI) with rules to protect taxpayer confidentiality. The bill allows specified disclosure exceptions for the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Labor, Department of Motor Vehicles, and Department of Insurance for limited enforcement/oversight purposes, preserves criminal penalties for unauthorized disclosure, and limits Inspector General staffing to amounts appropriated through the Division of Legislative Oversight.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text assign duties, funding, appropriations, or budget changes to the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Insurance, Department of Labor, Department of Health and Human Services, Department of Motor Vehicles, or Department of Revenue.

Substantive additions impose juvenile-facility reporting and oversight requirements: electronic submission to the Division of Legislative Oversight (annual by Sept. 15, beginning 2026) of facility-level and individual-level data (total beds, juveniles served, individual confinement occurrences and total confinement hours in hours/minutes, counts of incidents <1 hour, <4 hours, and >24 hours). Reports must redact PII, follow a DLO-prescribed format, and room confinement over one hour requires written supervisor approval.
