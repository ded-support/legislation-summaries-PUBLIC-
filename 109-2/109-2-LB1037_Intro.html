Summary

LB1037 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§77-2602.05, 77-2602.06, and 77-2603.01. It requires the Governor (or designated representative) to enter good-faith negotiations with federally recognized Nebraska tribes—commencing within 60 days of a written request and with at least one session every 90 days while active—over collection, allocation, refund and remittance of cigarette and other tobacco-product taxes and related escrow under the Master Settlement Agreement. The bill limits grounds for declining or delaying talks (e.g., tribal regulation of cannabis) and prescribes required agreement elements (duration, administration, enforcement, waiver of sovereign immunity, division of proceeds, dispute resolution, reporting/auditing). It preserves the Tax Commissioner’s refund process and allows negotiated refund formulas (capped at taxes paid by tribal entities). Section 77-2603.01 expressly permits tribal cigarette stamps under such agreements. No direct appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Governor (or a designated representative) to enter into good‑faith negotiations with any federally recognized Nebraska tribe that submits a written request. Negotiations must begin within 60 days, include at least one session every 90 days while active, and continue until an agreement or a joint written impasse. The Governor may choose whether to execute an agreement but may not refuse, terminate, or unreasonably delay negotiations based primarily on a tribe's lawful exercise of authority on matters unrelated to cigarette/tobacco taxation (e.g., tribal cannabis regulation). The negotiation scope explicitly covers collection, allocation, and refunds of cigarette and other tobacco taxes and amounts held or paid into escrow under section 69‑2703 and related Master Settlement Agreement statutes. No agencies beyond the Governor’s office are named, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
