Summary

LB1139 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §42-371 to make a Title IV-D Division payment history from the Department of Health and Human Services admissible as prima facie evidence that child or spousal support payments are current. Such a payment history (showing payments current for the prior 12 months or the life of the order, whichever is shorter) operates to release the statutory lien for purposes of transferring a specific parcel of real property. A certified copy from the Title IV-D Division is likewise prima facie evidence and is valid for 30 days after certification. No funding, appropriation, or budgetary provisions for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a child- or spousal-support judgment creates a lien on a debtor’s real or personal property that attaches when a payment is due and ends when the payment is made. It authorizes the Title IV‑D Division of the Department of Health and Human Services to provide a current payment history showing that support payments have been made as ordered for the past 12 months (or for the order’s entire duration if shorter); that record is prima facie proof the payments are current and, for purposes of transferring a specific parcel of real property, operates to release the lien. No changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
