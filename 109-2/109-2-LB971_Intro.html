Summary

LB971 (Guereca) requires that health coverage issued, delivered, or renewed in Nebraska — including individual and group sickness-and-accident policies, hospital/medical/surgical policies (except specified-disease or limited-benefit plans), and self-funded employee benefit plans to the extent not preempted by federal law — cover prescribed asthma inhalers. Insurers must cover one rescue and one maintenance inhaler without any deductible, copayment, coinsurance, or other cost-sharing. High-deductible health plans (per IRC §223(c)(2)) may require application of the plan’s annual deductible only if otherwise HSA eligibility would be lost. The bill references Nebraska statute section 44-3,131 and the Internal Revenue Code; it contains no provisions for new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
