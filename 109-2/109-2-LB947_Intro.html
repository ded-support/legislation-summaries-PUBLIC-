Summary

LB947 directs several Department of Agriculture-related funding and administrative changes:

- Creates a Noxious Weed Cash Fund (administered by the director) funded by pesticide registration fee proceeds (per 2-2634), transfers (including from the Weed Book Cash Fund), gifts, reimbursements, and possible General Fund appropriations for noxious weed control.

- Establishes a Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund: all monies received under the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Act are remitted by the department to that fund for program administration.

- Terminates the Nebraska Potato Development Fund on July 1, 2026, with its balance transferred to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund.

- Sets pesticide registration fees (base $200; cap $250) and allocates each fee: $50 to Noxious Weed Cash Fund, $50 to Buffer Strip Incentive Fund, $55 to Natural Resources Water Quality Fund, remainder to the Pesticide Administrative Cash Fund.

- Abolishes the Weed Book Cash Fund; proceeds and remaining balances transfer to the Noxious Weed Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Agriculture:

- A specified fund is terminated July 1, 2026; the State Treasurer must transfer its balance to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund as soon as administratively practicable.

- All money in the Weed Book Cash Fund must be transferred by the State Treasurer to the Noxious Weed Cash Fund by January 1, 2027.

- The department is authorized to encourage/promote, collect, distribute or dispose of any remaining books in its possession.

- The bill also includes language about fee/certification timing and that fees be calculated based on any portion of an hour (references to 2027–2028), though the funding impact of that change is not explicit.
