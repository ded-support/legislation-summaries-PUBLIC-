Summary

LB947 addresses the Department of Agriculture. It terminates the Nebraska Potato Development Fund effective July 1, 2026 and directs the State Treasurer to transfer any remaining money from that fund to the Plant Protection and Plant Pest Cash Fund. It requires the State Treasurer, by January 1, 2027, to transfer all money in the Weed Book Cash Fund to the Noxious Weed Cash Fund and mandates that proceeds from sale of the Weeds of the Great Plains publications be remitted to the State Treasurer and credited to the Noxious Weed Cash Fund. The bill notes taxes and fees collected by the Department of Agriculture had been credited to the Nebraska Potato Development Fund. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.
