Summary

LB921 defines "Department" as the Department of Labor and makes the department the statutorily designated recipient and enforcer of the Nebraska Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act. Employers must provide written notice to the department (including affected employees' addresses) when planning a business closing or mass layoff, and must notify the department if workers are covered by a collective bargaining agreement. The department must maintain confidentiality of employee names/addresses, may receive employer demonstrations that separate actions negate aggregation, shall enforce the Act (including civil penalties), and may adopt rules and regulations to implement it. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Nebraska WARN Act with the Department of Labor designated as the enforcing agency. Key Department-related provisions: employers must serve written notice to the department (including employee addresses) and the department must keep employee names/addresses confidential; the department may adopt rules to implement the Act; the commissioner must develop a list of Spanish interpreters for employer use. The Act gives the department enforcement authority and establishes a civil penalty (up to $100 per day) as the exclusive remedy (no injunctions). Employers must provide a named referral agent at each worksite (information in the non‑English language), and that agent must compile and assist employees with local community service contacts. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
