Summary

LB748 amends Nebraska’s 529 trust statutes. The State Treasurer remains trustee, but the State Investment Officer has fiduciary authority over investments and selects investment options under policies of the Nebraska Investment Council, which may adopt investment rules and approve fees. The State Treasurer and the Accounting Administrator of the Department of Administrative Services will determine state fund types for IRC §529 compliance. The trust’s Program, Expense, and Administrative Funds are separately administered; the Expense Fund is funded by fees assessed to the Program Fund, may pay trust costs and Meadowlark Act purposes, and must transfer a pro rata share to the State Investment Officer’s Cash Fund to cover investment-management expenses per section 72-1249.02. Annual transfer reporting to DAS’s Budget Division and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst is required. The bill also authorizes use of trust funds for recognized postsecondary credential programs.
