Summary

LB809 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-331 to constrain local governments (cities, villages, counties, and their agencies/subdivisions). It prohibits local ordinances that (1) bar landlords from refusing to lease to applicants whose rent source includes federal or other housing-assistance programs and (2) impose rent control on private property, subject to two exceptions: land-use or inclusionary-housing measures that increase affordable housing and voluntary, contractual owner participation programs that limit rent. The provision overrides any home-rule charter; ordinances violating it are declared null and void. No state agencies, departments, or budgetary appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state preemption: except where other state law expressly allows, local governments may not adopt or enforce ordinances that prevent landlords from refusing to rent privately owned single‑family, multi‑unit, or commercial rental property to applicants whose lawful rent source includes funding from a federal or other housing assistance program. No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
