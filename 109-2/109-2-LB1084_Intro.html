Summary

The bill amends provisions governing emergency protective custody and the Sex Offender Commitment Act to explicitly reference state agencies as follows:

- Department of Correctional Services (DOCS): persons taken into emergency protective custody may be admitted to a DOCS facility; convicted sex offenders must be admitted to a jail or DOCS facility unless a medical/psychiatric emergency requires temporary medical care; the term “administrator” includes the administrator of a DOCS facility.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): law enforcement shall use a written certificate “prescribed and provided by the Department of Health and Human Services” when admitting a person to custody.

No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.
