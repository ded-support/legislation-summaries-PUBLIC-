Summary

"Department" is defined as the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED is directed to administer the Civic and Community Center Financing Fund, using it to award grants for: construction/renovation of civic and recreation centers; preservation/rehabilitation (including demolition/reuse) of historic buildings or districts; and construction/upgrades of public spaces. DED will evaluate applications under section 13-2707 and may conditionally approve grants subject to limits: grants minimum $15,000; assistance capped at 50% of project cost; one 13-2704.01 and one 13-2704.02 grant per applicant every two years; and a five-year sale restriction on assisted facilities. Maximum grant amounts vary by municipal class (e.g., primary-class cities/tribal governments up to $2,250,000, rising to $3,375,000 when the fund balance reaches specified thresholds). DED must prioritize cities of the second class and villages, prefer new applicants, and the Legislature intends unobligated appropriations be reappropriated for subsequent grant periods.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds application and eligibility rules for demolition/reuse of properties the State Historic Preservation Officer (SHPO) oversees: if a property is listed or eligible for the National Register, applicants must show either (1) a certified building professional (PE/architect or chief local code official) has deemed it a substandard and abandoned commercial property with SHPO concurrence, or (2) the city/village and SHPO have agreed to preservation-based mitigation. Defines "certified building professional" and "substandard and abandoned commercial property." Budget/finance change: explicitly permits the related fund to pay reasonable and necessary costs incurred by the SHPO in performing duties under section 19-1202. No specific appropriation amounts are provided.
