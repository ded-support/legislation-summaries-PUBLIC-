Summary

LB849 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2704.09 to add over-the-counter (OTC) drugs to the list of items exempt from Nebraska sales and use taxes. Existing exemptions for insulin, mobility-enhancing equipment (when prescribed), prescription drugs and specified durable medical equipment, home medical supplies, prosthetics, and oxygen equipment remain. The bill defines OTC drug by reference to 21 C.F.R. §201.66 (as of Jan 1, 2026). It references the Medical Assistance Act (the state medical assistance/Medicaid program) and the Uniform Credentialing Act (licensees authorized to issue prescriptions). The act becomes operative October 1, 2026, repeals the original section, and includes no changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.
