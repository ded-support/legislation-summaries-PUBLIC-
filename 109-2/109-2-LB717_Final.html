Summary

LB717 centralizes regulation in the Department of Banking and Finance by defining "Department" and "Director" accordingly across multiple statutes and assigning the department authority over charters, licensing, examinations, audits, investigations, rules (including remote work), enforcement, intergovernmental agreements, and certain reporting (e.g., AML, quarterly lists of controllable electronic‑record addresses). It requires notices and filings (60‑day control acquisition notices with Director action within 30 days plus a 30‑day extension option; switch operational notices; annual audited net‑worth, earnings/operations, mortgage condition, and timely breach notifications) and permits licensees to report suspected exploitation of vulnerable/senior adults. Fee changes: $100 franchise filing fee (credited to the Securities Act Cash Fund), $50 mortgage‑loan‑originator notification fee, $75 branch‑office fee, and licensee-paid exam/investigation charges under §§8‑605/8‑606. The only Department of Insurance reference exempts loans by insurance companies from the statutory interest‑rate limitation. No appropriations or other budget allocations are included.
