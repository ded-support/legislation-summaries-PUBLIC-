Summary

LB717 designates the "Department" and "Director" as the Department of Banking and Finance and its Director, explicitly authorizes the Director to adopt rules (including to implement federal Regulation O), and makes the Department the primary regulator for: state‑chartered bank acquisitions; interstate branching and merger oversight; the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act (including kiosk and authorized‑delegate reporting); digital asset depository entities under the Nebraska Financial Innovation Act; anti‑exploitation reporting coordination; and approval of additional credit union investments. It expands licensee duties (notifications of name changes, data breaches, bankruptcies/adverse actions; audits, annual reports; record production) and grants examination, subpoena, rulemaking (including remote‑work rules), enforcement, and cancellation authority. Fee changes: $50 filing for mortgage‑loan‑originator hires, $75 per branch notice, $100 franchise filing; franchise filing receipts are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Securities Act Cash Fund, and examination charges remain payable by licensees. The only Department of Insurance reference is an exemption for loans made by insurers from certain interest‑rate limits. No other appropriations or budget reallocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Banking and Finance

- Adds an exemption to the Nebraska Money Transmitters Act for payroll processors meeting conditions: <20 employees (or serving <50 Nebraska employees), no felony convictions for principals, no revoked financial/professional licenses, and not otherwise engaged in money transmission.

- Requires lenders to disclose, on a director‑prescribed worksheet or similar form, whether a refinance of an existing installment loan or a residential mortgage provides a "net tangible benefit." Provides an enumerated (non‑exclusive) list of what constitutes net tangible benefit (e.g., lower rate, lower payment, shorter amortization, switching to fixed rate, removing negative amortization or balloon payment, cash‑out exceeding closing costs, avoiding foreclosure, eliminating private (mortgage) insurance, consolidating loans).

Department of Insurance

- No direct agency funding or appropriation changes. Note: the residential refinance disclosure explicitly lists elimination of private mortgage insurance as a net tangible benefit (relevant to insurers).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are added.
