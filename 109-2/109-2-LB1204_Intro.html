References to the Department of Revenue in LB1204:

- Owners must file annual nameplate-capacity reports (by March 1) with county treasurers and provide a copy to the Department of Revenue on forms prescribed by the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions assign the Department of Revenue (DOR) administrative and oversight duties for the new nameplate capacity tax. Owners must file annual nameplate-capacity reports (copy to DOR) on forms prescribed by DOR. The DOR will: review filings for accuracy/consistency; audit facilities to verify reported capacity; prescribe report forms and statewide audit/verification standards; establish procedures for penalty assessment for late/false filings or underpayment; and ensure consistent tax calculation/collection across counties. County treasurers must annually report amounts collected and revenue distributions to DOR for audit/verification. No explicit new appropriations or funding/budget allocations to DOR are included in the added text.
