Summary

LB740 designates the "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (Sec. 3) and expresses legislative intent that the Department seek federal approval to cover supportive housing services under Medicaid (Sec. 2(2)(a)). The bill states a policy goal to authorize Medicaid reimbursement for supportive housing services (Sec. 2(1)(e)) and to expand permanent supportive housing statewide. The bill text provided contains no references to the Department of Economic Development and includes no explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocation language.
