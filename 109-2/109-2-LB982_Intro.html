Summary

LB982 appropriates $10,000,000 from the Nebraska General Fund for FY2026‑27 to the Nebraska Public Service Commission, designated to Program 793 (Broadband Bridge) for continued implementation of the Broadband Bridge Program. The bill includes an emergency clause, making the appropriation effective upon enactment.
