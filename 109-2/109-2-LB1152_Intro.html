Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns it authority to administer the New Taxpayer Recruitment Grant Act. Key department duties: prescribe the grant application form; accept and consider applications in received order and notify approvals within 30 days; approve grants only within available appropriations; disburse grants (50% at award, 50% upon meeting half the household goal) and reaward unspent remainder; receive semiannual program outcome reports; adopt rules and regulations. Fiscal provisions: creates the New Taxpayer Recruitment Grant Cash Fund to be administered by the department to finance grants; fund sources include legislative transfers, gifts, grants, and bequests; fund investments follow existing state investment law. Grant limits: maximum $250,000 per applicant per fiscal year.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Economic Development):

- The Department of Economic Development is defined as the administering agency and must prescribe application forms, consider applications in receipt order, and notify qualifying applicants within 30 days.

- The department will award grants to finance new taxpayer recruitment program administration and household relocation incentives, subject to available appropriations.

- Grants are capped at $250,000 per applicant per fiscal year and are paid 50% at award and 50% upon meeting half the program’s household goal; unmet halfway goals suspend the second payment and remaining funds may be reawarded.

- A New Taxpayer Recruitment Grant Cash Fund is created and administered by the department; it receives legislative transfers and gifts/grants/bequests (including federal) and may be invested by the state investment officer.

- The department may adopt rules to implement the Act.
