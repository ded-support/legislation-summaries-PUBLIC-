Summary

LB822 references the United States Department of Labor only as the source for annual cost‑of‑living adjustments to in‑the‑line‑of‑duty compensation. Section 81-8,317(2)(b) sets the 2023‑and‑after compensation amount equal to the prior year’s amount increased by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) for the 12 months ending June 30, "as published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics." The bill contains no Department of Labor funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The underlined additions do not affect or reference the Department of Labor and contain no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations for that agency.

Substantive additions instead clarify police/fire retirement matters for "cities of the first class": definitions (city, tenured firefighter/police), disability and survivor benefit standards, vesting/retirement age rules, rules for transfers between termination and new-employment cities, option to purchase annuities, governance of city retirement systems (e.g., six‑member retirement committee with four police representatives), and annual reporting/review requirements (including June 30 filing). No Department of Labor responsibilities or budget changes are created.
