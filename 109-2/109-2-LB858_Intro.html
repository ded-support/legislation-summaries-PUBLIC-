Summary

LB858 amends Laws 2025, LB261, sec. 107 (Agency No. 25 — Department of Health and Human Services, Program No. 502 — Public Health Aid). FY2025-26 appropriations: $7,083,060 General Fund and $9,339,000 Cash Funds (program total $16,422,060). FY2026-27: $7,083,060 General Fund and $10,230,000 Cash Funds (program total $17,313,060). Specified allocations include: $2.1M GF annually to seven federally qualified community health centers ($300,000 each); $1.4M GF annually for dental services ($200,000 each); FY25-26 and FY26-27 distributions of $325,000 GF and $750,000 Cash (Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund) pro rata to the seven centers based on uninsured clients, plus an additional $500,000 Cash in FY26-27 (and intent to increase Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund transfers by $500,000 annually). Other DHHS allocations: $300,000 GF annually to tribally owned urban FQHCs; $500,000 Cash (FY26-27) to Midtown Health Center; and multiple public health and minority health funding lines as detailed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The addition directs the Department of Health and Human Services to include $500,000 Cash Funds in FY2026-27 (part of a program cash fund increase from $9,339,000 to $10,230,000 and a program total increase from $16,422,060 to $17,313,060) to support seven federally qualified community health centers (Federal Program 330). Funds will be distributed proportionally based on uninsured client counts reported to HRSA and paid to Charles Drew, OneWorld, East Central/Good Neighbor, Community Action Partnership of Western Nebraska, Midtown, Bluestem, and Heartland Health Center. Uses include expanding access (behavioral health, dental), new services/locations, equipment, or capital projects. The Legislature intends an ongoing $500,000 annual transfer from the Nebraska Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund to the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to sustain this aid.
