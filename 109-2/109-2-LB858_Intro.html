Summary

LB858 amends Laws 2025, LB261, §107 for AGENCY NO. 25 — Department of Health and Human Services, Program No. 502 (Public Health Aid). Appropriations: FY2025‑26 — $7,083,060 General Fund and $9,339,000 Cash Fund (program total $16,422,060); FY2026‑27 — $7,083,060 General Fund and $10,230,000 Cash Fund (program total $17,313,060).

Key allocations: $2.1M GF annually for seven Federal Program 330 community health centers ($300,000 each); $1.4M GF annually for dental services ($200,000 each); FY2025‑26 $325,000 GF and $750,000 Cash (NHCCF) and FY2026‑27 an added $500,000 Cash for proportional uninsured‑client distributions among the seven centers; $300,000 GF annually for tribally owned urban FQHCs; FY2026‑27 $500,000 Cash for Midtown Health Center. Public health departments: $2,898,246 GF and $5,605,000 Cash annually with specified distributions. Minority health: $31,713 GF and $1,526,000 Cash annually; second district centers: $28,101 GF and $1,349,000 Cash annually. The Department is directed to distribute specified funds (including per‑capita minority allocations). The bill states legislative intent to increase transfers from the Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund to the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund by $500,000 beginning FY2026‑27.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For DHHS: program cash funding increases from $9,339,000 to $10,230,000 (program total from $16,422,060 to $17,313,060). Included is a new $500,000 Cash Funds appropriation (FY2026-27) from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to be distributed to seven federally qualified community health centers (Charles Drew, OneWorld, East Central/Good Neighbor, Community Action Partnership of Western Nebraska, Midtown, Bluestem, Heartland). DHHS will allocate amounts proportionally based on each center’s uninsured client count reported on the Uniform Data System. Funds may be used to expand access (including behavioral health or dental), add services or locations, purchase equipment, or complete capital projects. Legislature intends a $500,000 annual transfer increase from the Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund to support this.
