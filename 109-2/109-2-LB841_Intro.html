Summary

LB841 amends Neb. Rev. Statutes §§79-1139 and 79-1164 related to special education. Key agency-related provisions:

- School districts: may enroll only students identified/verified under §§79-1137–79-1138; must provide services as directed by each student's IEP; may not implement IEP changes without consent of the parent, legal guardian, educational decisionmaker, or surrogate unless documented reasonable efforts fail to elicit a response within ten calendar days or approval is obtained through a hearing. The bill also assigns both the burden of proof and burden of production in hearings to the school district.

- Department: upon petition under §79-1162, assigns hearings to a hearing officer who conducts proceedings, issues findings and a final decision within 45 days (extensions allowed), and delivers decisions by certified mail to parties and the Commissioner of Education.

No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included. The original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "educational decision maker" and "surrogate" and requires each school district to provide special education per a student's individualized education program (IEP). Prohibits a district from changing an IEP without consent from the parent, guardian, educational decision maker, or surrogate unless the district documents reasonable efforts with no response within ten calendar days or obtains approval through a hearing under sections 79-1161 to 79-1167. The school district is assigned the burden of production and proof. No state agencies or departments beyond school districts are newly referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
