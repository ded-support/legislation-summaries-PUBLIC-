Summary

LB841 amends special education procedure statutes. It directs school districts to provide special education only to students identified and verified under §§79‑1137–79‑1138 and to implement services as stated in a student's IEP. School districts may not change an IEP without parental, guardian, educational decisionmaker, or surrogate consent except after documented unsuccessful consent attempts or following a hearing. The bill assigns the Nebraska Department of Education to appoint a hearing officer, requires the hearing officer to issue a final decision within 45 days (with limited extensions), and requires delivery of decisions to the parties and the Commissioner of Education. It places both the burden of proof and the burden of production on the school district. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "educational decision maker" (court‑designated educational decisionmaker) and "surrogate" (assigned under §79‑1161). Requires school districts to provide special education per a student's IEP as verified under §§79‑1137–79‑1138. Prohibits changing an IEP without consent of the parent, guardian, educational decision maker, or surrogate, except when (a) reasonable efforts to obtain consent fail and no response is received within 10 calendar days, or (b) a hearing under §§79‑1161–79‑1167 approves the change. Assigns the burden of proof and production to the school district. No agencies beyond school districts and courts are named; no funding or appropriation changes.
