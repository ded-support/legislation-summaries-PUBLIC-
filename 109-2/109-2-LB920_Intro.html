Summary

LB920 assigns additional powers and duties to the Tax Commissioner and the Department of Revenue related to regulation and enforcement of cash devices. The bill requires manufacturers/distributors to pay a $500 application fee and operators to pay a $250 annual decal fee; those fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- The Department of Revenue must approve technical standards for age‑verification mechanisms on cash devices (electronic ID authentication, prevent play until age 21, scan before each session and voucher/redemption).

- The Department may examine and audit retail establishments operating cash devices without prior notice to verify compliance.

- A Nebraska Child Care Aid Fund is created to consist of funds received pursuant to section 77-3012, legislative transfers, and gifts/grants; money in the fund is to be invested by the state investment officer and used for state child care subsidies.

- The bill directs 68.95% of the identified revenue allocation to the Nebraska Child Care Aid Fund (per the added percentage language referencing distributions under section 77-3012).
