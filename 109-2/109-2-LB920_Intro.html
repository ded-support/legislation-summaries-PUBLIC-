Summary

This bill assigns additional powers and duties to the Department of Revenue (via the Tax Commissioner), including seizure and sealing authority for noncompliant cash devices; suspension/revocation of licenses; rulemaking (including advertising/packaging standards); review of distributor/manufacturer/operator documents; approval of device locations; examination/audit authority; and information requests with specified response deadlines.

Manufacturer/distributor application fee ($500) and annual decal fee ($250) are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

The bill imposes a 15% tax on net operating revenue of cash devices, to be collected by the Tax Commissioner and remitted to the State Treasurer with specified allocations: 2.5% Charitable Gaming Operations Fund; 2.5% Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund; 0.9% General Fund; 3.4% Nebraska Tourism Commission Promotional Cash Fund; 13.35% Property Tax Credit Cash Fund; 68.95% Nebraska Child Care Aid Fund; and 8.4% to county/city distribution.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- The Department of Revenue is given regulatory and enforcement roles: it will approve technical standards for cash-device age‑verification systems and may examine/audit retail establishments operating cash devices without prior notice to verify compliance.

- New age‑verification requirement: devices must electronically authenticate government IDs to prevent play under age 21; standards must be department‑approved.

- Funding change: 68.95% of the specified revenue is allocated to a newly created Nebraska Child Care Aid Fund (receipts pursuant to §77-3012, transfers, gifts/grants). The fund will finance state child‑care subsidies and be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment statutes.
