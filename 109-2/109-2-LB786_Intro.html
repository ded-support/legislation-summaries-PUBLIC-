Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): required to design and issue Military Honor Plates, make application forms available via county treasurers, verify applicant eligibility by consulting the Department of Veterans' Affairs registry (sec. 80-414), may charge postage/handling (remitted to the DMV Cash Fund), and may cease issuing plates if statewide uptake is under 100/year over any prior consecutive two-year period.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: maintains the eligibility registry the DMV must consult for plate issuance.

- Fees and allocations: $5 alphanumeric-plate fee credited to the designated recipient (Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund); $40 personalized-plate fee split 25% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 75% to the designated recipient (Military Department Aid Fund); $3 transfer fee and postage/handling recoveries credited to the DMV Cash Fund. If plate manufacturing costs exceed statutory plate fees, the excess is credited first to the Highway Trust Fund, remainder to the designated recipient.

- Military Department: creates the Military Department Aid Fund administered by the Adjutant General, funded by plate proceeds, gifts, grants, legislative transfers, and investments; the Adjutant General will promulgate rules (by Jan 1, 2027) and award nonrepayable grants to needy uniformed service members.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles: required to create new license-plate designs honoring each armed force and persons who served; fees for those plates are routed to designated recipients.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs / Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System: fees from certain veteran/military plates are designated to the Nebraska Veteran Cemetery System Operation Fund.

- Military Department: creates the Military Department Aid Fund, administered by the Adjutant General. Funding sources: gifts, grants, bequests, public/private receipts, amounts credited under section 60-3,122.04 (license-plate fees), and legislative transfers. Funds may be invested; earnings revert to the fund. The Adjutant General may award non‑repayable grants to Military Department members pursuant to rules (to be adopted by Jan 1, 2027) limiting eligibility to needy uniformed members. No specific dollar appropriations are included.
