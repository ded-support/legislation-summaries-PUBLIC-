Summary

The bill designates the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) as the "Department" under the Child Protection and Family Safety Act and grants DHHS authority to investigate reports. Schools and the Commissioner of Education must immediately notify DHHS if an option/enrollment election or transfer is filed for a student who is the subject of a child‑protection notice; DHHS may issue a confidential notice preventing the student's transfer or disenrollment for 14 days or until further notice. Such notices are confidential investigation records, and DHHS may adopt rules to implement these provisions. Separately, the State Department of Education may partner with the Department of Labor to develop a Nebraska Teacher Apprenticeship Program, and the Legislature intends to appropriate $1,000,000 annually from the Education Future Fund to the State Department of Education for that program.
