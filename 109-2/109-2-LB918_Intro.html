Summary

LB918 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act (sections 13-3103 and 13-3108). Key agency/program changes:

- Creates/uses the Sports Arena Facility Support Fund; directs the State Treasurer to transfer certified amounts into the fund and the state investment officer to invest fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Expands applicant/eligibility and allowable uses for state assistance (including privately owned concert venues and sports complexes), and limits promotional funding to ten years for arts/cultural and sporting event support and for sports complexes in second‑class cities/villages.

- Funding rules/formula retained and clarified: generally up to 70% of specified state sales tax receipts (25% for certain small sports complexes); total state assistance caps of $100M (general) and $25M for large public stadiums, with a $1.25M annual cap and no payments before July 1, 2027. Excess amounts may transfer to the General Fund.

- Quarterly distributions timing, interfund transfers (Support the Arts Cash Fund, Convention Center Support Fund, Civic and Community Center Financing Fund), and ineligibility rules between this program and Civic and Community Center grants are specified.

- Emergency effective date.
