Summary

This bill amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to add disclosure and registration requirements related to "Chinese military companies." Key government entities affected:

- Clerk of the Legislature (forms approved by the Executive Board of the Legislative Council): lobbyists must register before lobbying and must disclose if their principal is a Chinese military company, including an affirmative acknowledgment that the principal is a foreign adversary of the United States.

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (the commission): required to receive filings from consultants representing Chinese military companies (including the same affirmative acknowledgment); may issue enforcement orders (cease-and-desist, filing, costs) and assess existing civil penalties (generally up to $5,000 per violation).

- State Treasurer: all penalties collected under the enhanced lobbyist-disclosure provision are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution pursuant to Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution.

Fiscal changes: civil penalties for failing to make the required acknowledgement/disclosure are set at $100,000 per violation, increasing by $100,000 for subsequent violations up to $1,000,000 per violation. The commission must pay $50,000 to any person whose information leads to a finding of such a violation, contingent on the commission recovering the civil penalty.
