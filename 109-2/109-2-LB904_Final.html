Summary

LB904 amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to add registration and disclosure duties relating to "Chinese military companies." Key agencies and actions:

- Clerk of the Legislature: receives lobbyist registration (form approved by the Executive Board of the Legislative Council) including mandatory disclosure if the principal is a Chinese military company.

- Executive Board of the Legislative Council: approves lobbyist registration form.

- The Commission (Nebraska accountability/disclosure commission referenced throughout): will receive consultant filings for persons influencing on behalf of a Chinese military company, may impose existing enforcement remedies (including up to $5,000 per violation), and must pay an informant $50,000 if their tip produces a finding, contingent on recovery of civil penalties.

- State Treasurer: receives civil penalties remitted under Article VII, §5. New civil-penalty schedule for lobbyist disclosure violations: $100,000 for first violation, increasing by $100,000 per subsequent violation up to $1,000,000 per violation.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a pre-activity reporting requirement: any person employed, retained, or authorized as a consultant for a Chinese military company must, before conducting influencing activity in Nebraska, file a commission-prescribed form with "the commission." The form must include the consultant’s name, permanent and office addresses; the Chinese military company’s name and address; a description of the consultant’s business; the matters they expect to influence; an affirmative acknowledgment that they are acting as an agent of a foreign adversary; and the name/address of any referenced legislative official (text truncated). No agencies beyond "the commission" are named; no funding or appropriation changes are included.
