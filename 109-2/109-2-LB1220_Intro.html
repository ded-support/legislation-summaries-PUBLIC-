Summary

LB1220 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §37‑314, imposing duties and procedural limits on the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission. The commission must fix, prescribe, and publish rules/regulations and may adopt commission orders (including those implementing U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service conservation orders) on seasons, bag limits, gear, and take. Orders and rules must be based on investigation and reliable data. The commission must hold at least one public hearing (per §37‑104) for each proposed order or rule, post hearing notice on its website at least 30 days in advance, and an order is not effective until 15 days after website posting. Emergency season closures/reopenings require majority vote at a valid special meeting (§37‑104), are delayed 24 hours, and are limited to grave danger to life, property, or populations; the commission must make efforts to notify news media. Violations are a Class III misdemeanor with a minimum $100 fine. Original section 37‑314 is repealed. No appropriations or funding changes.
