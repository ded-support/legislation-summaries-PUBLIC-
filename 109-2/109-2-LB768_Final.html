Summary

- Department of Economic Development (DED)

- Continues to administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and is required to adopt rules, monitor projects, allocate (attempt to allocate) at least 30% of available trust‑fund monies to each congressional district, and announce grant/loan application periods of at least 60 days.

- May contract with statewide agents or instrumentalities to carry out program duties and may provide or contract funds to other governmental entities.

- New reporting and accounting requirements: prescribe annual reporting of awards/repayments (beginning July 1, 2027), record use of public funds, and may recapture improperly used or unspent funds (credited to the Affordable Housing Trust Fund).

- Administers Rural and Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Funds, may accept legislative transfers/grants/private contributions, may receive contributions from the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority, and must transfer remaining balances in those funds to the General Fund beginning July 1, 2032.

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS)

- The budget administrator of DAS's budget division must be granted access, upon request, to the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority's accounting system for grant/loan programs involving public funds.

- The State Building Administrator (DAS), in consultation with the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment, may specify updated energy code editions, additional efficiency/renewable requirements, and waivers based on life‑cycle cost analysis; agencies receiving funding must provide such analyses.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (focused by agency):

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): The bill requires that, when the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority (NIFA) administers grant/loan programs involving public funds for a state agency, DAS’s budget division (budget administrator) must be given access, on request, to the authority’s accounting system alongside the state agency and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): Beginning July 1, 2027, DED must adopt annual reporting rules for qualified recipients to report funds obligated, repayment terms, and unspent/returned balances, plus accounting of specified housing awards (including equity estimates). Noncompliant recipients may be forced to return awards and transfer recoverable housing-equity positions. DED may contract with a statewide public/private nonprofit or state instrumentality to act as its agent under the Nebraska Affordable Housing Act.
