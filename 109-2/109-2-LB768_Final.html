Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED)

- DED will administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund and the Rural and Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Funds, including grant programs, qualified allocation plans, disbursement, recapture, and reporting requirements.

- Requires DED to (a) allocate not less than 30% of available Affordable Housing Trust Fund dollars to each congressional district, (b) announce grant/loan application periods of at least 60 days, and (c) require land identification before new-construction applications.

- DED may contract with agents or other governmental entities, seek nonstate contributions (including from the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority), establish subaccounts (Middle Income Fund for Economic Recovery Act land preparation), and must implement reporting (beginning July 1, 2027) and recapture provisions. Specified fund transfers and end‑dates (e.g., transfers to the General Fund July 1, 2032) are maintained.

Department of Administrative Services (DAS)

- The budget administrator (budget division, DAS) must be given access to the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority’s accounting system when the authority administers public‑fund grants/loans under agreement with a state agency.

- The State Building Administrator (DAS) may adopt/specify updated energy code editions, additional energy/renewable requirements, and lifecycle‑cost‑justified waivers for state buildings and will consult with the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment on rulemaking and code specifications.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: Added that when the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority (or similar authority) administers grant/loan programs using "public funds" under a state-agency agreement, the authority must record program accounting and allow the state agency, the Legislative Fiscal Analyst, and the budget administrator of the budget division of DAS access to that accounting system on request.

- Department of Economic Development: Beginning July 1, 2027, DED must prescribe annual reporting by qualified recipients to track obligated funds, repayment terms, unspent/repayed balances, and awards tied to recoverable housing assistance (including equity estimates). Noncompliance can trigger return of awards and transfer of equity positions. DED may contract with a statewide nonprofit or state instrumentality to serve as its agent under the Nebraska Affordable Housing Act.

- Department of Labor: No changes affecting the Department of Labor identified.
