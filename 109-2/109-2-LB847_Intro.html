Summary

Creates the Nebraska Office of Registered Apprenticeship within the Department of Labor, to be operated by the commissioner, and designates the office as the state apprenticeship agency and registration agency. Duties include establishing apprenticeship standards and credentials, approving/denying registrations, creating competency-based frameworks, providing reciprocal approval for out-of-state registrations, resolving apprenticeship disputes, and conducting outreach. The Department of Labor is responsible for implementation, enforcement, and rulemaking under the act. Amends section 48-649.01 to require the commissioner to set the state unemployment insurance tax rate annually and to allocate the combined tax so that, when the state rate is nonzero, not less than 50% is employer contribution and not more than 50% is the state rate; employers with combined rates of 5.4% or higher have a zero state rate. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Office of Registered Apprenticeship within the Department of Labor, managed by the commissioner or designee. The office will: serve as the state apprenticeship/registration agency; set standards for pre‑apprenticeships, youth apprenticeships, registered apprenticeships, and related instruction; approve or deny registration of programs and sponsors (with written reasons and opportunity to amend); allow apprentice enrollment while sponsor approval is pending but prohibit training until approval; develop a state apprenticeship completion credential consistent with 29 C.F.R. 29.5; establish competency‑based frameworks; enable reciprocal federal approval of out‑of‑state registrations (subject to state wage/hour and ratio rules); resolve apprenticeship disputes; conduct outreach/education; and implement and enforce the Act, including rulemaking. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
