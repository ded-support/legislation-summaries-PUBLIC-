Summary

LB737 amends section 81-6,122 to require the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), in collaboration with the Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Transportation, and Department of Veterans' Affairs, to develop a comprehensive Olmstead strategic plan for serving qualified persons with disabilities in the most integrated community settings. The DHHS chief executive shall convene a team to develop the plan, appoint a stakeholder advisory committee, arrange for independent consultant review, and provide the completed plan and periodic progress reports to the Legislature. The bill also requires annual joint legislative hearings to assess Olmstead progress. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are contained in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Legislature’s Health and Human Services and Urban Affairs Committees to hold an annual joint public hearing (by Oct 1) to review Olmstead plan progress in housing, employment, education, community supports, and transportation, and to identify possible legislative fixes; committees must file an electronic report with the Clerk by Dec 31.
