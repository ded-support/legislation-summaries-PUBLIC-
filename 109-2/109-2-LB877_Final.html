Summary

LB877 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28‑405 and repeals the original section (Sec. 2), comprehensively updating Nebraska’s controlled‑substance schedules (II–V) to expressly list numerous synthetic opioids (including fentanyl analogs), synthetic cannabinoids, substituted phenethylamines, tryptamines, synthetic cathinones, and certain benzodiazepine‑like compounds. The bill cross‑references and incorporates federal regulatory determinations and lists, including the Drug Enforcement Administration (U.S. Department of Justice) exempted products list (as of Jan. 31, 2022), the U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services, and the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. §360b). It excludes specified FDA‑approved products (e.g., certain dronabinol/cannabidiol formulations and FDA‑approved crystalline psilocybin) and exempts xylazine when used in FDA‑permitted animal drugs, investigational uses, or pharmacist/veterinarian compounding. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds specific controlled substances — including psilocin; 2,5-dimethoxy-α-methylphenethylamine (2,5‑DMA); methyl-2H-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one; and the benzodiazepine/related compounds etizolam, flualprazolam, and flubromazolam. No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced in the added text.
