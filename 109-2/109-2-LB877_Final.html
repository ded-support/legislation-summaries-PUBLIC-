Summary

LB877 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-405 (Uniform Controlled Substances Act) to expand controlled‑substance schedules to include numerous specific substances and broad classes (e.g., fentanyl analogs, synthetic cannabinoids, substituted phenethylamines/tryptamines, synthetic cathinones, certain benzodiazepine analogs, xylazine variants, and multiple anabolic steroids). The bill expressly defers to federal determinations and exemptions, citing the Drug Enforcement Administration (U.S. Department of Justice) exempted‑products list (as of Jan. 31, 2022), the Food and Drug Administration, and the U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 360b) for FDA‑approved product exclusions (e.g., dronabinol, certain psilocybin polymorphs). It repeals the prior version of §28‑405 (Revised Statutes Supplement, 2025). No state funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary
