Summary

LB1039 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-2704 governing memoranda of understanding (per §79-2703) between schools and law enforcement/security agencies for school resource officers (SROs) or security guards. Key agency-related provisions:

- Requires SROs/security guards to complete at least 20 hours of school-based law-enforcement training; requires one administrator per school with an assigned SRO/security guard to receive the same 20 hours.

- Prohibits SROs/security guards from administering corporal punishment (per §79-295).

- Directs school districts and law enforcement/security agencies to maintain records on student referrals for prosecution, including referral reason and federally identified demographic characteristics, and to adopt policies on parent notification (in the parent's language), advising students of constitutional rights, referral criteria (per §79-262), and a complaint process to the employing law enforcement or security agency.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a prohibition preventing school resource officers and school security guards from administering corporal punishment to students as defined in section 79-295. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
