Summary

LB1039 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-2704 to strengthen memoranda of understanding governing school resource officers (SROs) and contracted security guards. Key agency-related provisions require: 20 hours of school-based law-enforcement training for each SRO/security guard and for a minimum of one administrator at each school with an assigned SRO; a prohibition on SROs/security guards subjecting students to corporal punishment (per §79-295); law-enforcement and school policies for parental notification in an understandable language, advising students of constitutional rights prior to questioning, and criteria (per §79-262) distinguishing disciplinary matters from law-enforcement referrals; systematic recordkeeping of SRO referrals including reasons and federally identified student demographics; and a complaint process directed to the relevant law enforcement or security agency. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a prohibition preventing any school resource officer or school security guard from subjecting a student to corporal punishment as defined in section 79‑295. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or referenced.
