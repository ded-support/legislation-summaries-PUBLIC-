Summary

Amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-670 to bar consumer-owned utilities (public power districts/cooperatives) from using eminent domain to acquire privately owned electric generation facilities built to serve a single industrial customer with projected new load >1,000 MW, where the facility is co-located/adjacent, has equivalent interconnection, and is approved by the Nebraska Power Review Board. Conditions include executed long-term commercial agreements preserving local retail service rights, a contractual waiver of eminent-domain authority, and prohibition on resale. The industrial customer must pay all costs, fees, congestion charges, and system upgrade costs incurred by consumer-owned utilities. The Attorney General may represent districts in condemnation proceedings; districts must reimburse the state for AG costs. No direct appropriations or new budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a prohibition on consumer‑owned utilities exercising eminent domain to acquire privately owned generation built to serve a single‑site industrial load >1,000 MW, conditioned on several requirements. Key government body: the Nebraska Power Review Board must approve the facility. Other conditions include negotiated long‑term contracts preserving the consumer‑owned utility’s exclusive retail service rights and resource‑supply arrangements, a contractual waiver of eminent‑domain authority, a ban on resale by the industrial customer, and the industrial customer’s obligation to pay all costs, fees, congestion charges, and grid‑upgrade expenses incurred by affected consumer‑owned utilities. No appropriations, budget allocations, or changes to state funding are included.
