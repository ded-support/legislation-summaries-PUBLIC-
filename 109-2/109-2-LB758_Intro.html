Summary

LB758 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §30-2715 to add a statutory process allowing 501(c)(3) charitable beneficiaries to claim nonprobate interests or obtain information by presenting an affidavit to the property holder or any person with relevant information. The affidavit must include the charity's IRS determination letter, a certificate of existence (or equivalent) from its state/country of incorporation, proof of the decedent's death or probate notice, a corporate resolution authorizing the affiant, and a completed IRS Form W-9. The bill references the Internal Revenue Service and federal Internal Revenue Code requirements and invokes notarization. Upon receipt of a compliant affidavit the holder must pay, deliver, or transfer the property (or provide information); the bill also creates an enforcement right of action. No state agency budgetary changes, appropriations, or funding allocations are included.
