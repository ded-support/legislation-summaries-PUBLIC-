Summary

The bill imposes agency-specific duties and reporting, with limited budget directions. Department of Insurance must collect insurer shared‑savings incentive data annually and deliver an aggregate electronic report to the Legislature beginning July 1, 2027 and biennially thereafter. Department of Labor may not use General Funds to implement the short‑time compensation program (must use available federal funds), must develop multilingual human‑trafficking posters and coordinate placement by July 1, 2026, and adopts the federal DOL CPI as a fee adjustment benchmark. DHHS is financially responsible for its evaluations/plans and court‑ordered custody/treatment, may house non‑corrections inmates in surplus institutional space (with cost recovery to senders), and may provide certain post‑discharge psychotropic medications. DAS must receive sole‑source contract approvals/justifications within three business days, prepare executive budget material with DHHS performance metrics, and the DAS budget administrator will time a $1,000,000 transfer from the Water Resources Cash Fund to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund between July 19, 2024 and June 30, 2025. DECD and Revenue must file joint electronic reports (annual by Oct. 31), appear at joint committee hearings (Dec. 15, even years), disclose incentive/credit/project data biennially with confidentiality protections, DECD will assign investor ID numbers, and Revenue—consulting with DHHS—must verify employer eligibility for a specified tax credit.
