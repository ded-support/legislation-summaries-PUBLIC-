Summary

LB1048 makes agency-specific changes across multiple departments.

- Department of Insurance: insurers must report shared‑savings incentive program data; DOI to submit an aggregate report to the Legislature by July 1, 2027, and biennially thereafter.

- Department of Labor: bars use of General Funds to implement short‑time compensation (must use federal funds); requires development and placement of multilingual human‑trafficking posters and coordination with the task force by July 1, 2026.

- Department of Administrative Services: director must receive sole‑source contracts and emergency justifications within three business days; DAS budget division prepares agency spending estimates for the Governor’s budget; DAS budget administrator directs timing/amounts of a $1,000,000 transfer from the Water Resources Cash Fund to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund.

- Department of Economic Development and Department of Revenue: expand joint electronic reporting, confidentiality and project‑level reporting timetables; Revenue must create an employer‑credit verification process in consultation with DHHS; DHHS also to work with the budget division on performance goals.

- Department of Health and Human Services: may temporarily use surplus institutional space to house inmates from other state institutions (subject to transfer limits); retains and clarifies authority to assess/collect patient/relative liability, adopt rules, recover unpaid amounts, accept voluntary payments, treat certain unpaid amounts as estate claims, and must provide prescribed medications to eligible indigent persons discharged after mental‑health‑board‑ordered treatment. No new DHHS appropriations are specified.
