Summary

LB1022 revises Nebraska teacher-certification law. Key agencies/departments and fiscal provisions:

- State Board of Education: continues rulemaking for issuance, renewal, conversion, suspension, and revocation of certificates/permits.

- Nebraska Department of Education / Commissioner of Education: issues certificates/permits, verifies out‑of‑state credentials, and must provide a web portal for endorsement and certificate applications.

- Certification Fund: created/authorized to receive certification fees remitted to the State Treasurer; used by the department to pay certification costs and to reimburse school districts for hiring replacements when employees serve on board advisory committees (advisory committee member travel reimbursed under state travel statutes).

- Fee caps: endorsements up to $55; certificate/permit issuance up to $65.

- State Investment Officer: may invest Certification Fund monies under state investment law.

- Legislature: may direct transfers from the Certification Fund to the Education Future Fund.

The bill also removes the human relations training requirement for certification and repeals the original sections.
