Summary

- Department of Revenue: required to perform quinquennial (every five years beginning July 1, 2030) adjustments to multiple statutory dollar limitations, generally by applying the five‑year cumulative change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) and rounding to the nearest $100. Specific adjustments/indices include: HSA/medical savings account levy exemption, veterans' pension/property exemption, exempt personal property and annuity/life‑insurance cash‑value limits (CPI‑U), the homestead allowance (adjusted by the Nebraska Housing Price Index prepared by the Federal Housing Finance Agency), and the statewide homestead dollar limitation (adjusted to statewide average single‑family residential value using county certification per section 77‑3506.02).

- Department of Labor: the Commissioner of Labor shall adopt regulations prescribing the minimum‑wage multiple equivalent for nonweekly pay periods for garnishment limits.

- Department of Health and Human Services: claims filed under section 68‑919 retain payment priority notwithstanding the payment order in section 30‑2487.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue: Multiple provisions require the Department of Revenue to automatically adjust specified dollar limitations every five years beginning July 1, 2030. Most adjustments are tied to the cumulative five‑year percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U, U.S. Dept. of Labor, BLS) and must be rounded to the nearest $100. One adjustment is tied to the five‑year change in the Housing Price Index for Nebraska; another ties a limit to the statewide average single‑family residential value (using county-certified values per section 77‑3506.02). Department of Labor: no substantive changes to state Department of Labor duties—text only cites the federal BLS CPI. Department of Health and Human Services: no changes. No new appropriations or funding allocations are included.
