Summary

LB871 directs the Department of Revenue to perform five-year inflation adjustments beginning July 1, 2030 for multiple statutory dollar limitations: medical/health savings account protections (indexed to CPI‑U), a disabled‑veteran exemption, certain exempt personal property and survivor allowances (CPI‑U), life‑insurance/annuity cash‑value exemptions (CPI‑U), the homestead allowance (indexed to the Federal Housing Finance Agency Nebraska Housing Price Index), and the statewide homestead value cap (adjusted to statewide average single‑family residential value using county‑certified values). The bill also requires the Commissioner of Labor to adopt regulations establishing an equivalent multiple of the state minimum wage for nonweekly pay periods for garnishment calculations. Section 30‑2323 preserves an exception giving priority to claims filed by the Department of Health and Human Services under section 68‑919. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (underlined additions):

- Department of Revenue: required to adjust multiple statutory dollar limitations every fifth year beginning July 1, 2030. Adjustments equal the current limitation multiplied by the cumulative five‑year percentage change of a referenced index, with results rounded to the nearest $100. One set of adjustments uses the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U); another uses the Housing Price Index for Nebraska; one adjustment is tied to the statewide average single‑family residential value calculated from county‑certified values per section 77‑3506.02.

- Department of Labor: cited only as the source of CPI‑U data (Bureau of Labor Statistics).

- Department of Health and Human Services: not affected or mentioned.

No appropriation, funding, or budget allocation changes are included.
