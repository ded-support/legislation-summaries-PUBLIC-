Summary

LB1077 amends section 48-146.03 of the Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act to revise rules for deductible workers' compensation policies. Key provisions:

- Insurers may decline to offer a deductible policy if a credit investigation shows the employer lacks financial ability to cover deductible amounts.

- Insurers must service and, if necessary, defend all claims arising during the policy period, including claims payable from the deductible, and must report payments (including deductible payments) to the Workers' Compensation Court as required.

- Employees of employers electing a deductible policy cannot be required to pay any deductible or to relinquish their statutory right to choose a physician (section 48-120); violations are a Class II misdemeanor.

The bill repeals the original section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
