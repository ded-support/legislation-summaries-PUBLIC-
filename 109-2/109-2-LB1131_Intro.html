Summary

- The bill creates the Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act. "Department" is defined as the Department of Revenue (DOR).

- DOR must administer and annually distribute refundable Nebraska income tax credits (effective taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2027) totaling $6,000,000 annually, allocated as:

- $480,000 equally to qualifying tribal domestic violence/sexual assault programs;

- $300,000 to a statewide nonprofit coalition that has an affiliation agreement with the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to provide services under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act;

- $5,220,000 distributed among entities by (i) $2,088,000 equally to the tribal programs and the DHHS‑affiliated coalition, (ii) $2,505,600 by population, and (iii) $626,400 by service‑area square miles.

- DOR may adopt rules, must process notifications when credits are sold (seller must notify DOR 30 days before purchaser claims the credit), and is directed to distribute all credits each taxable year.

Summary (focus: Dept. of Health and Human Services; Dept. of Revenue)

- Creates the "Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act," effective for tax years beginning on/after Jan 1, 2027, establishing refundable income tax credits totaling $6,000,000 annually.

- Funding allocation: $480,000 to tribal-run domestic violence/sexual assault programs; $300,000 to a statewide coalition of nonprofits that have an affiliation agreement with the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to provide Protection from Domestic Abuse Act services; $5,220,000 distributed among providers (including $2,088,000 equally to the tribal programs and the DHHS-affiliated coalition, $2,505,600 by population, $626,400 by service-area square miles).

- Department of Revenue (DOR) is defined as the administering agency, must distribute the credits annually, may adopt rules, and must be notified (with purchaser TIN) 30 days before any credit transfer is claimed.
