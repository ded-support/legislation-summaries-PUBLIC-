Summary

LB1131 creates the Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act (operative Jan 1, 2027). It authorizes refundable Nebraska income tax credits totaling $6,000,000 annually: $480,000 equally to qualifying tribal domestic violence/sexual‑assault programs; $300,000 to a statewide coalition of nonprofits that have an affiliation agreement with the Department of Health and Human Services under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act; and $5,220,000 to be distributed among providers (including $2,088,000 equally to the tribal and coalition groups, $2,505,600 by program service‑area population, and $626,400 by service‑area square mileage). The bill defines "department" as the Department of Revenue, requires the Department of Revenue to distribute the credits each taxable year, authorizes rulemaking, and requires credit sellers to notify the Department of Revenue prior to a purchaser claiming transferred credits.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act" (effective for tax years beginning/treated as beginning on/after Jan 1, 2027). Establishes $6.0 million annually in refundable Nebraska income tax credits administered by the Department of Revenue, distributed each year as: $480,000 equally to tribal-run domestic violence/sexual assault programs; $300,000 to a statewide coalition of nonprofits that have an affiliation agreement with the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to serve victims under the Protection from Domestic Abuse Act; and $5,220,000 allocated (a) $2,088,000 equally to the tribal programs and the DHHS‑affiliated coalition, (b) $2,505,600 by program service‑area population, and (c) $626,400 by service‑area square miles. Credits are transferable/sellable with 30‑day prior notice to Revenue; Revenue may adopt implementing rules.
