Summary

LB891 amends the Child Care Licensing Act and assigns duties primarily to the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) and the Nebraska State Patrol, with FBI involvement for fingerprint-based checks. The department must: maintain a complaint-tracking system identifying licensing violations found via complaints, unannounced inspections, or self-reports; conduct unannounced inspections (or delegate to city, village, or county) and verify liability insurance, suspending licenses for lapses; authorize provisional work after qualifying state background results under 45 C.F.R. 98.43; and adopt rules to include volunteers in the definition of staff and related ratios. The Nebraska State Patrol transmits fingerprints to the FBI and reports results to the department. The department will seek federal funds to offset fingerprint/background-check costs; absent sufficient federal funds, applicants/staff bear costs (the department may pay if funding becomes available). The volunteer provision sunsets Dec. 31, 2028. Political subdivisions may not impose residency requirements on family child care homes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements directing "the department" (under the Child Care Licensing Act) to:

- Authorize prospective child‑care staff to begin work after qualifying national criminal history results from the staff member's state of residence, with mandatory supervision until all background check components are complete.

- Allow providers, staffing agencies, or substitute staff pools to rely on a prior qualifying background check (within five years, previously provided to the department, and with employment gap ≤180 days) instead of re‑submitting a national check.

- Require documentation of employment eligibility to identify licensing violations found via complaint, unannounced, or self‑report investigations.

- Permit volunteers to count in staff‑to‑child ratios (including unsupervised ratios if qualified) subject to background checks, supervision, and training; and direct the department to promulgate rules to define such volunteers as staff.

Also references federal regulation 45 C.F.R. 98.43. Section sunsets Dec. 31, 2028. No funding or appropriation changes.
