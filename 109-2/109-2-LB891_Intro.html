Summary

LB891 amends the Child Care Licensing Act and assigns duties primarily to the Department of Health and Human Services (the department), the Nebraska State Patrol, and the Federal Bureau of Investigation. Key agency provisions:

- DHHS must maintain a complaint-tracking system identifying licensing violations from complaints, unannounced inspections, or self-reports and adopt implementing rules.

- Background-check regime: applicants and staff must submit fingerprints to the Nebraska State Patrol for transmission to the FBI for national criminal history checks (and periodic five‑year rechecks, NCIC/sex‑offender/state registries searches). DHHS may authorize supervised provisional employment per 45 C.F.R.

- DHHS will seek federal funds to offset fingerprinting/check costs; absent funds, costs fall to applicants/staff; DHHS and the State Patrol may set cost rules.

- Volunteers may count in staff‑to‑child ratios if they meet background, supervision, and qualification requirements (sunsets 12/31/2028).

- Political subdivisions are barred from imposing residency requirements on family child care homes.

No specific appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds state child‑care licensing rules tying state practice to 45 C.F.R. §98.43. The state department (the child‑care licensing agency) may authorize a prospective child‑care worker to begin work after receiving qualifying national criminal history results from the applicant’s state of residence, with supervised placement until all background checks finish. Providers/agencies need not request a new national check if the applicant had a qualifying background check within five years, the department previously provided that result to the first provider, and the applicant is currently employed in‑state or separated ≤180 days. The department must document licensing violations found in investigations and must adopt rules treating volunteers and any persons who count in staff‑to‑child ratios as “staff”; volunteers may count if they meet background, supervision, and qualification requirements. Provisions sunset Dec. 31, 2028. No funding or appropriations changes.
