Summary

LB1172 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-1034 and (by repeal) replaces the original. Key agency: the Nebraska Power Review Board (NPRB). Major provisions affecting NPRB and regulated entities:

- Public power districts, public power & irrigation districts, electric membership associations, electric cooperative companies, municipalities with generation, and registered municipal groups must notify the NPRB in writing before a final decision to close or decommission any dispatchable generation facility >100 MW. Notices must include recommended worker/community transition activities. NPRB may hold a closed hearing (generally within 60 days) and will issue advisory recommendations within 60 days after the hearing or a no‑hearing determination. Records remain confidential until the entity publicly announces a closure, subject to statute exceptions.

- Electric suppliers must maintain 75% dispatchable capacity, certify compliance annually to the NPRB, and file a compliance plan within 60 days if out of compliance.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that electric suppliers keep 75% of their generation portfolio as “dispatchable” capacity. Suppliers must annually certify compliance to the Nebraska Power Review Board; if noncompliant they must submit a remediation plan to the Board within 60 days. The provision defines “dispatchable electricity” (generation available on demand or with adjustable output) and “capacity” (maximum power a plant can supply at a point in time). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
