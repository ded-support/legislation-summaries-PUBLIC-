Summary

LB861 amends the Building Construction Act (section 71-6401) to require that any state agency, county, city, or village that mandates inspections as part of a building permit or structural project must use an "authorized inspector" — defined as a credentialed inspector with the permitting entity or a third‑party inspector registered or licensed with the State of Nebraska (excluding self‑inspections). It defines "inspection records" as pass/fail results, including reasons for failure or deviations from project specifications, and requires those records be made available to the public if the inspected structure is standing when the records are requested. The bill repeals the original section; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Adds definitions and inspection requirements.
