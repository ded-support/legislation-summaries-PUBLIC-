Summary

LB1133 authorizes specific claim payments, agency write-offs, and related administrative actions.

Appropriations/Payments:

- General Fund: $51,791.88 to Rodney Gerald Hoelscher (Program 536, Agency 65); $354,456.23 to the Trust Account of Fiedler Law Firm on behalf of Kurt Von Minden (Program 592, Agency 65). Total General Fund: $406,248.11.

- Workers' Compensation Claims Revolving Fund: $882,500.00 to four claimants via trust accounts (Program 593, Agency 65): $400,000; $162,500; $20,000; $300,000.

Administrative/Agency actions:

- Payments processed by the Director of Administrative Services and State Treasurer upon receipt of releases.

- State Claims Board approved agency write-offs: State Fire Marshal $856.00; Department of Health and Human Services $1,032,144.79; Department of Transportation $98,759.21; Department of Labor $1,708,343.43; Department of Veterans' Affairs $2,647.70.

The bill takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.
