Summary

- Defines the Division of Motor Carrier Services as part of the Department of Motor Vehicles.

- Repeatedly references the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): DHHS (or its medicaid-managed care contractors/agents) may contract for intrastate medicaid nonemergency medical transportation; carriers operating under DHHS contracts must meet DHHS requirements (training, driver standards, background checks, service quality).

- The Public Service Commission must consult with DHHS (Director of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care) when authorizing medicaid transportation designations (prior to 1/1/2027) and shall adopt rules in consultation with DHHS for contractor insurance, equipment, driver qualifications for DHHS‑utilized contractors.

- Beginning 1/1/2027, carriers must obtain a PSC license to provide intrastate medicaid nonemergency medical transportation; licenses are annual, renewable, carry a commission‑set fee capped at $250, and may be suspended or revoked for noncompliance.

- No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are altered.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Added requirement that, beginning Jan 1, 2027, anyone providing intrastate Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation to DHHS, a DHHS-contracted Medicaid managed‑care organization, or a DHHS agent must hold a commission-issued license under section 75-311. Licensing rules: application, fitness standards, optional hearing, one‑year term with annual renewal, fee set by the commission not to exceed $250, and suspension/revocation procedures (with notice and hearing). Rates negotiated with a state agency are exempt from Public Service Commission rate regulation. Transportation network companies with a permit under §75‑324 have limited exceptions.

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): No substantive changes, funding, or appropriations affecting DMV are included.

- Funding/Appropriations: None specified.
