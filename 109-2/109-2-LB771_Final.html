Summary

LB771 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75‑327 to require transportation network companies (TNCs) to file and keep current the rates they use (including dynamic pricing) with the commission and to charge consistent rates. If a TNC uses dynamic pricing, its app must show a clear visible indication, obtain explicit passenger confirmation, and provide a fare estimator. Dynamic pricing is prohibited for seven days after any Governor‑declared state of emergency that affects transportation of persons, suppliers, goods, or equipment, limited to areas in the Governor's proclamation; the Governor may further regulate dynamic pricing by executive order during emergencies. The bill repeals the original section and contains an emergency clause making it effective on passage. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a seven‑day ban on dynamic pricing following any Governor‑declared state of emergency that affects transportation of people, suppliers, goods, or equipment, limited to areas named in the Governor’s emergency proclamation. Grants the Governor authority to impose further regulation of dynamic pricing during such emergencies by executive order. No other agencies or departments are mentioned and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
