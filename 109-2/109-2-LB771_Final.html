Summary

LB771 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75‑327 governing transportation network companies (TNCs). It requires TNCs to file and keep current with the commission the rates used on their apps (including any dynamic pricing) and to charge consistently with those filings, while otherwise exempting TNCs from commission rate regulation under sections 75‑101 to 75‑158. App requirements for dynamic pricing are added: clear visible notice, an affirmative passenger confirmation, and a fare estimator. Dynamic pricing is prohibited for seven days after any Governor‑declared state of emergency that impacts transportation within areas identified in the proclamation; the Governor may further regulate dynamic pricing by executive order. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Prohibits dynamic pricing for seven days after a gubernatorial state of emergency that affects transportation of people, suppliers, goods, or equipment. The ban applies only within areas specified in the Governor’s emergency proclamation. The Governor is also authorized to further regulate dynamic pricing during any state of emergency by executive order. No other agencies or departments are named, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
