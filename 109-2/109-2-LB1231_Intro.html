Summary

LB1231 assigns administration and funding actions for two state funds. The Animal Damage Control Cash Fund is administered by the Department of Agriculture, and the Legislature intends to appropriate $26,000 from that fund for FY2026‑27 to the Department to carry out the animal damage control program. The Panhandle Improvement Project Cash Fund is administered by the Department of Economic Development; DED must develop grant application criteria and the fund may provide grants (up to $995,000 to a third‑congressional‑district county for fairground renovation and up to $5,000 to a village under ten for a community facility). The bill authorizes transfers from the Panhandle Improvement Project Cash Fund to the Animal Damage Control Cash Fund at the Legislature’s direction and directs the State Treasurer to transfer any unobligated Panhandle fund balance to the Animal Damage Control Cash Fund on or after July 31, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Agriculture: The bill expresses the Legislature’s intent to appropriate $26,000 for FY2026‑27 from the Animal Damage Control Cash Fund to the Department of Agriculture for the animal damage control program (per §81‑2,236). The State Treasurer is directed to transfer any unobligated balance remaining in the Panhandle Improvement Project Cash Fund to the Animal Damage Control Cash Fund as soon as practicable on or after July 31, 2026.
