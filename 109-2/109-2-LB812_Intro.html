Summary

LB812 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-908 and assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) responsibility for administering the medical assistance program. It authorizes the department to enter contracts and interagency agreements; adopt rules, fee schedules, waivers, and managed-care plans (including under the Nebraska Behavioral Health Services Act); and take other actions needed to operate the program. It clarifies coverage and reimbursement for services provided through school-based health centers (including FQHC reimbursement rates) and requires the department to maintain applicant/recipient confidentiality. The department must prepare and electronically submit an annual summary and analysis to the Governor and Legislature by December 1, listing specified enrollment, eligibility, redetermination, processing, call-center, and fair-hearing metrics. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are specified.
