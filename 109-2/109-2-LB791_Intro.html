Summary

LB791 amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to impose a contribution limit for candidates and ballot question committees. Beginning January 1, 2027, no person may contribute more than $3,500 in total to a candidate or ballot question committee during an election period; committees must refund excess contributions within ten days and report the contributor name, address, amount received, receipt date, and return date. A candidate’s personal funds are exempt. Beginning January 1, 2029, and biennially thereafter, the limit is adjusted by the cumulative change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics, rounded to the nearest $100. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made or changed by this bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Establishes a $3,500 per-person contribution cap to any candidate or ballot question committee per election period effective Jan. 1, 2027. Committees must refund excess contributions within 10 days and report them on subsequent campaign statements (name, address, amount, date received, date returned). A candidate’s own personal funds are exempt. "Election period" is defined as the calendar year of the election. Beginning Jan. 1, 2029, the limit is adjusted every two years for inflation using the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics, rounded to the nearest $100. No agencies other than the BLS are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
