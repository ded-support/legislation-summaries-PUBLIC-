Summary

Bill LB791 amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to impose a statewide contribution cap: beginning January 1, 2027, no person may give more than $3,500 in the election period to a candidate or ballot‑question committee. Committees must refund within ten days and disclose any excess contributions received and returned. Candidates’ personal funds are exempt. Beginning January 1, 2029, and biennially thereafter, the limit is adjusted by the cumulative percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics, rounded to the nearest $100. The bill repeals the original section. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a contribution limit and indexing rule. Key points involving government entities:

- Establishes a $3,500 per-person contribution cap to a candidate or ballot-question committee for an election period, effective January 1, 2027, with refund/reporting requirements (refund within 10 days; subsequent campaign statements must disclose contributor name, address, amount, date received, and date returned).

- Exempts a candidate’s own personal funds.

- Beginning January 1, 2029, and biennially thereafter, the cap will be adjusted by the cumulative percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U) published by the Federal Bureau of Labor Statistics; amounts rounded to the nearest $100.

- Defines “election period” as the calendar year of the election.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
