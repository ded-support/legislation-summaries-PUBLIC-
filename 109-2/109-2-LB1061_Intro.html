Summary

LB1061 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-502 to set minimum residence rules governing boards of state postsecondary institutions must use to determine in-state tuition/fee eligibility. It defines qualifying categories including: 180 days habitual presence with intent to reside; parental/guardian residence or continuity of student intent; federal tax dependency on a Nebraska-resident parent; marriage to a Nebraska resident; aliens with 180 days’ presence and intent to become lawful permanent U.S. residents; staff members (or dependents) who join the University of Nebraska, Nebraska state colleges, or community college areas immediately before a term; active-duty service members (or their spouses/dependents) assigned to Nebraska; current Nebraska National Guard members; and recipients of National Service Trust educational awards (42 U.S.C. §12602, as of Jan 1, 2021). The bill repeals the original §85-502. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
