Summary

The bill centralizes budget authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS), authorizing DAS/Budget Division to adjust salary/per diem limits, draw warrants, monitor agency appropriations, and administratively transfer certified unexpended balances between programs. DAS is directed to execute major General Fund transfers (e.g., Property Tax Credit Cash Fund ~$422M→$437M; School District Property Tax Relief Credit ~$780M→$791M; Education Future Fund $242M/year) and reappropriate most Nebraska Capital Construction Fund balances (less $2M).

Department highlights: DHHS receives targeted increases and earmarks (Children’s Health Insurance $36M cash/year; Developmental Disability Aid jumps substantially, Medical Assistance funding declines year‑to‑year; $745K from Medicaid excess profit fund to Program 33). Veterans’ Affairs total rises (~$105.2M→$111.1M; GF increase). Agriculture Program 78 totals ~$21.85M→$22.01M with GF down and $300K/year for Nebraska AgrAbility. Correctional Services: workforce recruitment up and vocational/life‑skills grants ($4M GF plus cash funds). Labor, Revenue, Transportation, Motor Vehicles, Military Department, Information Management Services, and Insurance receive specified fund transfers, appropriations, and reporting directives.
