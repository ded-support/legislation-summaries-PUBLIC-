Summary

The bill directs appropriations, transfers, and administrative authority across multiple agencies. Department of Administrative Services may draw warrants, adjust salary limits, monitor Agriculture and DHHS appropriations, administratively transfer certified unexpended DHHS balances, time major General Fund transfers (e.g., Property Tax Credit ~$422M/$437M; School Property Tax Relief ~$780M/$791M), reappropriate most Nebraska Capital Construction Fund to DAS Program 921, and funds OCIO operations (~$133.7M→$134.4M) while reducing the State Building Division revolving fund (~$93.4M→$53.8M). Department of Health and Human Services: Children's Health Insurance $36M/yr, Mental Health and Community‑Aging allocations, $745K/yr from Medicaid excess profits, major Medical Assistance GF decline (~$1.085B→$850.3M) and increased Developmental Disability Aid. Other changes: Agriculture program ~$21.85M→$22.01M with AgrAbility $300K/yr; Labor workforce development ~$9.88M/yr plus a $266K/$272K transfer; Correctional Services recruitment GF up (~$487K→$973K); Veterans' Affairs GF up (~$47.3M→$52.9M); Transportation and DMV fund transfers; Revenue homestead exemption GF increases and a $43.9K/yr transfer to Revenue Enforcement; Economic Development GF/Cash reductions and targeted state aid.
