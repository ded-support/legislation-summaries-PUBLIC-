Summary

The bill makes agency-specific regulatory and procurement changes and creates a revenue fund. Key provisions:

- Department of Economic Development: prohibits requiring Affordable Housing Trust Fund–financed new construction or rental conversions to comply with section 72‑805 (IECC) or to obtain building‑plan approval from the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment.

- Department of Insurance: exempts its contracts for financial/actuarial examinations, rehabilitation/conservation/reorganization/liquidation of licensees, and related professional services from procurement rules.

- Department of Health and Human Services: the CEO (or designee) is added to the Water Well Standards and Contractors’ Licensing Board; State Fire Marshal inspections explicitly include investigations at DHHS request for foster care, certain program providers, residential child‑caring agencies, and licensed health care facilities.

- Department of Transportation: exempts all road and bridge project contracts from procurement rules.

- Department of Revenue: creates the Litter Fee Collection Fund; the Tax Commissioner deposits a withheld collection/administration fee into it, the fund is expressly appropriated to DOR, and available balances are invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

No other direct appropriations or budget allocations for DED, DOI, DHHS, or DOT are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text directly affect the Department of Economic Development, Department of Insurance, Department of Health and Human Services, Department of Revenue, or Department of Transportation — there are no changes to their duties, funding, or appropriations.

Key additions concern Water/Energy/Environment functions: creation of a Water Quality and Quantity Cash Fund (fees, gifts, grants remitted to the State Treasurer; invested per state law); authority to charge application and annual fees for NPDES and pretreatment permits beginning July 1, 2027; civil penalty range ($2,500–$10,000/day) and enforcement procedures for geologic CO2 storage violations; local authority over pools, camps, mobile home parks (inspection fees limited to actual cost); Environmental Safety Cash Fund to terminate Dec. 31, 2026, with remaining funds transferred to the General Fund.
