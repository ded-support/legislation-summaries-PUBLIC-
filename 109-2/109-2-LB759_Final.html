Summary

The bill (1) bars the Department of Economic Development from requiring Affordable Housing Trust Fund recipients to meet sec. 72‑805 International Energy Conservation Code standards or to obtain building‑plan approval from the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment. (2) Adds the Department of Health and Human Services director (or designee) as an ex officio member of the Water Well Standards and Contractors' Licensing Board and requires the State Fire Marshal to perform and report fire‑safety investigations at DHHS‑requested licensed foster care providers, specified child‑care programs, residential child‑caring agencies, and health care facilities. (3) Expands procurement exceptions to explicitly include Department of Insurance contracts for financial/actuarial exams, rehabilitation/reorganization/liquidation, and services for residual pools/excess funds, while excluding Department of Transportation road and bridge contracts. (4) Authorizes the Tax Commissioner to deduct an administrative collection fee from the litter fee into a Litter Fee Collection Fund “shall be appropriated to the Department of Revenue” (funds may be invested). (5) Directs the State Treasurer to transfer specified Health and Human Services Cash Fund balances to the Environmental Safety Cash Fund on July 1, 2021; that fund terminates Dec. 31, 2026, with remaining balances reverting to the General Fund.
