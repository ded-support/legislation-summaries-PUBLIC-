Summary

LB1079 amends §29-2221 (habitual criminal enhancements). It prescribes mandatory minimum and maximum imprisonment terms to be served in Department of Correctional Services adult correctional facilities: generally a 10‑year minimum and up to 60 years; a 25‑year minimum (up to 60) for specified serious offenses or defined combinations of prior felonies; a 3‑year minimum (up to 20) where the current and prior felonies are violations of subsection (3) of §28‑416; and a 3‑year minimum (up to 20) for violations of §§28‑509–28‑518 when two or more prior felonies are similar. The bill maintains court procedures requiring the indictment/information to allege habitual status (not disclosed to juries) and a separate court hearing before sentencing. The original §29‑2221 is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a sentencing enhancement: when a felony violates sections 28-509 to 28-518 and the defendant has two or more prior felony convictions for those same or substantially similar offenses (including convictions from other states or federal law), the mandatory minimum sentence is three years and the maximum is twenty years. The enhancement applies even if one or more prior convictions did not include a term of imprisonment. No government agencies, departments, or funding/appropriation changes are mentioned.
