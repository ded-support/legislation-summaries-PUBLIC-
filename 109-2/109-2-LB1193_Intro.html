Summary

LB1193 requires owners of nameplate‑capacity–taxed renewable generation facilities and energy storage resources to file an annual report (prior calendar year nameplate capacity) with the county treasurer and provide a copy to the Department of Revenue on forms prescribed by the Department. The Department of Revenue shall review reports for accuracy, may audit facilities, and may adopt rules prescribing report form/content, audit standards, uniform calculation/collection procedures, and penalty assessment processes. County treasurers must annually report tax collections and distributions to the Department for audit/verification. The bill directs distribution of revenues (for taxes levied on/after Jan. 1, 2027: 95% to counties, 5% to community college areas) and prohibits the Department from retaining or transferring such revenue to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue duties are expanded for the new nameplate capacity tax on energy storage resources (effective Jan 1, 2027). Owners must file capacity reports with county treasurers and provide copies to the Department, which will review reports for accuracy and may audit facilities. The Department is directed to prescribe report forms, establish audit procedures/standards, ensure consistent tax calculation and collection across counties, and set procedures for assessing penalties. County treasurers must annually report collections and distributions to the Department for audit/verification. No explicit changes to the Department of Revenue’s funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
