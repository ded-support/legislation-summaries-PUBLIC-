Summary

LB1193 assigns several administrative and oversight roles to the Department of Revenue related to the nameplate capacity tax. Owners must file annual nameplate-capacity reports (filed with county treasurers) and provide copies to the Department on forms the Department prescribes. The Department shall review reports for accuracy, may audit facilities to verify reported capacity, and may adopt rules establishing report forms, audit procedures/standards, uniform tax calculation/collection, and penalty-assessment procedures. County treasurers must annually report amounts collected and distributions to the Department for audit/verification. The bill also bars the Department from retaining any revenues from these taxes for distribution, use, transfer, pledge, or allocation to or from the General Fund. (Also revises distribution: effective Jan 1, 2027, 95% to counties, 5% to community college areas.)

Summary (Department of Revenue focus):

- The Department of Revenue will prescribe the form/content of owner reports of nameplate capacity and receive copies from county treasurers.

- It must review those reports for accuracy, consistency, and compliance and may audit facilities to verify reported nameplate capacity.

- The Department is directed to establish audit procedures/standards, procedures to ensure consistent tax calculation/collection across counties, and procedures for assessing penalties for reporting/payment failures.

- County treasurers must annually report total tax collections and distributions to the Department for audit/verification.

- Revenue distribution for taxes effective Jan 1, 2027: 95% to counties and 5% to community college areas; county treasurers remit per section 77-6204 within 30 days.

- No explicit new appropriations or additional Department funding are provided.
