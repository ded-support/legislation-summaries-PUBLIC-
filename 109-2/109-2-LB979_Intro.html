Summary

LB979 amends Game and Parks Commission statutes. Key agency actions and fiscal changes:

- Game and Parks Commission: broadens authorized uses of the State Game Fund (includes deer/antelope/elk damage abatement); may set and collect fees for fish and wildlife education, remitting proceeds to a newly defined Game and Parks Commission Educational Fund. The Educational Fund also receives specified credits, gifts, and other legislative appropriations and is investable by the state investment officer.

- Game Law Investigation Cash Fund: clarifies funding sources (Game Law revenue, commission allocations, donations), authorizes investment by the state investment officer, and requires annual, detailed electronic reporting to the Clerk of the Legislature.

- Licensing/fees: expands electronic issuance, allows agents an issuance fee (up to $4), establishes or adjusts many permit, application, preference-point, paddlefish, and motorboat registration fees and allocations (including up to $10 per registration for Aquatic Invasive Species Program). Aquatic invasive species stamp proceeds remit to the State Game Fund.

- Other agencies: Director of Motor Vehicles and county treasurers involved in motorboat registration implementation and processing; State Treasurer receives remitted fees.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Agency affected: Nebraska Game and Parks Commission (referred to as "the commission") and the State Treasurer (receipt of funds).

- Funding/fees: The commission may accept gifts, grants, bequests, and other contributions to the Game and Parks Commission Educational Fund. It may establish and collect reasonable fees for fish and wildlife education programs (limited to staffing/material costs); proceeds go to the State Treasurer for credit to the Educational Fund.

- Use of funds/authorized expenditures: Adds or clarifies allowable expenditures for enforcement/abatement investigations to include investigative vehicles, specialized investigatory equipment, digital investigative services, and specialized investigative training.

- Permits/fee caps: Lifetime resident and nonresident fishing/combination permit fees are capped at not more than twenty times the annual fee as set by the commission under §37-327; the commission will promulgate related rules on preference/bonus points.

- Enforcement: Raises criminal penalty to a Class III felony when aggregate value of illegally traded wildlife exceeds $10,000 within three consecutive years.
