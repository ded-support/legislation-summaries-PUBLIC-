Summary

Amends §77‑6827 (ImagiNE Nebraska Act) to require applicants to disclose any contracts with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) and to bar the director administering the Act from approving incentive applications for taxpayers that have entered into contracts with ICE. The bill retains the $5,000 nonrefundable application fee, which is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund. Existing procedural provisions — the director’s 90‑day decision period (with tolling for information requests) and the prohibition on new applications after December 31, 2030 — remain in place.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement to disclose any contracts between the taxpayer and the U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), and prohibits the director from approving applications for taxpayers that have such contracts with ICE. No other agencies are mentioned. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
