Summary

LB1236A appropriates funds to the Legislative Council, Program 123, from the Nebraska Statutes Distribution Cash Fund: $25,000 for FY2026‑27 and $25,000 for FY2027‑28 (total $50,000) to aid implementation of LB1236. The appropriation specifically prohibits using these funds for permanent or temporary state employee salaries and per diems. The act includes an emergency clause and takes effect upon passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates $25,000 from the Nebraska Statutes Distribution Cash Fund for FY2026-27 and $25,000 for FY2027-28 to the Legislative Council (Program 123) to support implementation of LB 1236 (109th Legislature, 2nd Session, 2026). Funds are restricted: they may not be used to pay permanent or temporary state employee salaries or per diems.
