Summary

The bill amends the Automatic License Plate Reader Privacy Act to define "alert" as including data held by the Department of Motor Vehicles (alongside state criminal-justice systems, FBI NCIC, FBI kidnappings and missing persons list, the Missing Persons Information Clearinghouse, and manually entered plate numbers relevant to investigations). It also revises the automatic license plate reader system definition and explicitly excludes speed control enforcement systems from that definition. No provisions in the bill reference funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Motor Vehicles.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions define "highway work zone" and a new "speed control enforcement system" (devices that detect speed and photograph rear license plates). Such systems may be used in work zones with workers only if entry signage warns of their use and a marked law‑enforcement vehicle/officer is present. Systems may record only when a vehicle exceeds the limit by more than 11 mph. The bill also clarifies that these speed control systems are not "automatic license plate reader systems" under Nebraska Rules of the Road.

Impact on Department of Motor Vehicles: no explicit new DMV duties, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
