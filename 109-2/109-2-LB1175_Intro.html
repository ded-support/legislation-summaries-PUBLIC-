Summary

LB1175 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §86-1704 (Parental Rights in Social Media Act). It authorizes civil actions against a social media company or third-party vendor for violations, specifies available relief (preliminary/equitable or declaratory relief, damages, and discretionary attorney fees), and expands recoverable actual damages for minors or their parents to explicitly include harms such as injury, self-harm, addiction, and sexual exploitation. The bill also treats a social media platform as a "product" for purposes of product liability actions under §25-21,180 and repeals the original §86-1704. No state agencies or departments are named, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary provisions.
