Summary

LB1028 (amends the Livestock Waste Management Act) requires each large concentrated animal feeding operation (per 40 C.F.R. 122.23, as of Jan 1, 2026) to register its nutrient management plan with "the department," update it annually, and have the plan and supporting documentation published on the department's website. Entities applying livestock waste/litter must fund establishment and maintenance of cover crops (unless perennial cover exists) and a minimum 30-foot perennial-grass buffer adjacent to running waterways. Those entities must also annually compensate buffer landowners for production losses at prices determined by the department. The department is assigned registration, public-posting, and price-setting responsibilities. No state appropriations or other funding allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for large concentrated animal feeding operations (per 40 C.F.R. 122.23, as of Jan 1, 2026) to register and annually update nutrient management plans with "the department" and post plans/supporting documents on the department's website. Requires cover crops on acres where livestock waste/litter is applied (unless perennial cover exists) with establishment and maintenance costs borne by the entity implementing the plan. Requires a minimum 30‑foot perennial grass buffer adjacent to running waterways, with those costs also paid by the implementing entity. The implementing entity must annually compensate landowners for production losses on buffered acres at prices set by the department. No explicit new appropriations or agency budget allocations are specified.
