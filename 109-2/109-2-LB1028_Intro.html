Summary

LB1028 amends the Livestock Waste Management Act to require each large concentrated animal feeding operation (as defined by 40 C.F.R. 122.23, effective January 1, 2026) to register and annually update its nutrient management plan with "the department" and publish the plan and supporting documentation on the department's website. Entities applying livestock waste or litter under such plans must establish and maintain cover crops (unless perennial cover exists) and, where application is adjacent to running waterways, a minimum 30-foot perennial-grass buffer. Establishment and maintenance costs for cover crops and buffers are to be paid by the implementing entity. The implementing entity must also annually compensate landowners for production losses on buffered acres at prices determined by the department. The bill encourages use of regenerative agricultural practices. No direct state appropriations or agency budget increases are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Uses the federal regulatory definition of a “large concentrated animal feeding operation” (40 C.F.R. 122.23, as of Jan 1, 2026).

- Requires each such operation to register and annually update its nutrient management plan with “the department,” and post the plan and supporting documents on the department’s website.

- Assigns financial responsibility to the entity implementing the nutrient management plan for establishing and maintaining cover crops and for creating/maintaining a 30-foot perennial-grass buffer adjacent to waterways.

- Requires that entity to annually compensate buffer landowners for production losses at prices set by the department.

- No explicit new appropriations to the department are specified; the department is given authority to determine compensation prices.
