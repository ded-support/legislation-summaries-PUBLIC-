Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) responsibility for approval of ignition interlock devices, issuance of ignition interlock permits only upon proof of installation, and review of applicants' driving records and eligibility before permit issuance. The DMV director is directed to adopt rules governing device approval, installation, and program administration. Ignition interlock service facilities must notify the DMV if a device installed pursuant to a DMV order is disabled, bypassed, or altered. The Department of Motor Vehicles Ignition Interlock Fund is created, administered by the DMV, may receive fees under section 60-4,115, may be invested by the state investment officer, may fund indigent persons' device costs per 60-6,211.05, and may be used for transfers to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Department of Motor Vehicles Ignition Interlock Fund administered by the DMV. The fund may receive fee revenue under section 60-4,115, is investable by the state investment officer under applicable state investment laws, and may be transferred to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction and as allowed by section 60-6,211.05. Ignition‑interlock service facilities must notify the DMV if a device installed pursuant to a DMV order is disabled, bypassed, or altered (failure to notify is a Class V misdemeanor). The DMV is also implicated in issuing ignition‑interlock permits and enforcing related orders. No explicit new appropriations or specific budget allocations are included.
