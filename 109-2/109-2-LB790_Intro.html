Summary

LB790 amends §83-1,119 (Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act). Key agencies/departments and actions:

- Department (the correctional department referenced in statute): must develop a matrix of rewards, graduated administrative sanctions, and custodial sanctions; may use county jails that contract with the department as contract facilities. A custodial sanction of up to 30 days in a correctional or contract facility is designated the most severe response short of revocation.

- Parole officers: may impose administrative sanctions for technical or substance-abuse violations (with parolee acknowledgement) or submit reports to the Board of Parole.

- Board of Parole: receives reports, may dismiss charges, impose custodial sanctions (up to 30 days), issue warrants, and — for substance-abuse violations — may revoke parole under the Act; must adopt rules to implement the section.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures when a parole officer suspects a parolee of a substance-abuse violation but no flight or public-safety risk: the parole officer must either (a) impose administrative sanctions scaled to risk/severity if the parolee signs written acknowledgment and agrees to the sanction (if the parolee refuses, the officer must follow option (b)), or (b) submit a written report to the Board of Parole. The Board of Parole may dismiss, determine a violation, impose up to 30 days custodial sanction in a correctional or contract facility, revoke parole under the Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act, or issue an arrest warrant. Agencies/departments: parole officers, Board of Parole, correctional/contract facilities; references the Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
