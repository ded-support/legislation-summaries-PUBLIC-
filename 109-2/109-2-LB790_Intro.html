Summary

LB790 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §83-1,119 (Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act). It directs the Department (departmental parole staff) to create a matrix of rewards, graduated administrative sanctions, and custodial sanctions (with a 30-day custodial sanction in a Department facility or county contract facility as the most severe response in lieu of revocation). Parole officers may impose administrative sanctions or submit reports to the Board of Parole. For substance abuse violations the Board may dismiss, impose up to 30 days custodial sanction, issue a warrant, or revoke parole under the Act; technical violations generally permit administrative sanctions or up to 30 days custody but do not enumerate revocation. Parole officers may arrest parolees who pose flight or safety risks; peace officers may assist. The Board must adopt implementing rules. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures for handling suspected substance-abuse parole violations. Parole officers may either: (a) impose administrative sanctions (based on risk, severity, and response) if the parolee signs a written acknowledgement and agreement — or, if the parolee declines to acknowledge, must proceed to (b); or (b) submit a written report to the Board of Parole. The Board of Parole may dismiss, determine a violation, impose up to 30 days’ custodial sanction in a correctional or contract facility, revoke parole under the Nebraska Treatment and Corrections Act, or issue an arrest warrant. No agencies beyond the Board of Parole and correctional/contract facilities are created or mentioned, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
