Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and requires the medical director to develop standardized medical-care protocols to be used by the Department for inmates' chronic and acute conditions.

- The Office of Inspector General may investigate allegations involving Department employees or persons under contract with the Department, including deaths, serious injuries, and medical neglect of individuals in the Department's custody or supervision.

- The Department must report deaths, serious injuries, and employee hospitalizations to the Inspector General as soon as reasonably possible and must notify the Inspector General when related criminal investigations begin and end.

- The Inspector General must audit the Department's compliance with the Medical Standards for Incarcerated Individuals Act (first by May 15, 2028, then annually) and deliver investigation reports to the Director of Correctional Services within three business days; the Director has 30 business days to respond and must submit a corrective action plan.

- No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds duties for the Department of Correctional Services (DCS): the department’s medical director must develop and implement standardized medical-care protocols for inmates’ chronic and acute conditions (ongoing monitoring; timely access to medications, diagnostics and specialists; documentation/tracking; routine screening/early detection). DCS will be audited for compliance annually (first report due May 15, 2028) by the office/Inspector General, which must also provide investigation reports to the Director of Correctional Services within three business days. Death investigations with suspected medical neglect require a public report within 15 days after certain procedures. The Director has 30 business days to respond and submit corrective action plans. Annual public summary reports (redacted) are required. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
