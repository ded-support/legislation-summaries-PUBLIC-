Summary

LB745 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-730 to clarify that the Commissioner of Education may issue a diploma of high school equivalency to a non‑graduate who: (1) has been a Nebraska resident for at least 30 days immediately before application or whose final credit‑earning high school attendance was in a Nebraska school; (2) demonstrates, via approved tests and other criteria determined by the Commissioner, educational attainment equivalent to a typical high school graduate; and (3) is unable to secure a diploma from the high school last attended. The bill repeals the original section. No agencies beyond the Commissioner of Education are referenced and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
