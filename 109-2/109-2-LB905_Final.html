Summary

LB905 makes multiple agency-specific changes.

- Department of Agriculture: revises definitions in the Nebraska Potato Inspection Act and authorizes the director to end compulsory potato inspection if a petition representing ≥51% of previous commercial potato acreage (per state–federal reports) is filed.

- Department of Transportation: adds two DOT representatives to the Board of Public Roads Classifications and Standards (board expenses paid by DOT) and directs transfers from the Highway Trust Fund into the Highway Cash Fund/Roads Operations Cash Fund while authorizing DOT to expend and invest those funds for state highway acquisition, construction, maintenance and related purposes; investment income is credited to the Highway Cash Fund.

- Department of Administrative Services: centralizes surplus/real‑property management, easement approvals, deed/lease execution, and requires DAS to seek authorization to donate 43.55 acres to Northeast Community College (sale proceeds within 10 years revert to the General Fund). DAS also may support the Nebraska Information Technology Commission administratively.

- Information Management Services: IMS may review DAS records, set chargeable rates; receipts go to the Information Management Revolving Fund (expenditures by legislative appropriation); investment earnings on that fund will be credited to the General Fund beginning Oct 1, 2024.

- Department of Economic Development: receives priority referrals for surplus state property disposals and adds a Housing Office representative to the State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services.

- Department of Labor: must produce multilingual human‑trafficking informational posters and oversee their placement.

- Department of Health and Human Services: designated lead for a statewide critical incident stress management program; required to implement standardized foster‑care reimbursement tiers and administer the Breast and Cervical Cancer Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: Bill clarifies “Department” as DAS, specifies Director/State Building Administrator authority tied to section 72-813, and references DAS roles under the Information Technology Infrastructure Act and utility/department orders.

- Department of Agriculture: Adds definitions in the Nebraska Potato Inspection Act (person; potato grower = at least 3 acres; potato shipper = 180,000+ lbs sold/used).

- Department of Transportation: Allows current members (as of June 30, 2026) of the Board of Examiners of County Highway and City Street Superintendents to be presumptively eligible for the Board of Public Roads Classifications and Standards and serve four-year terms.
