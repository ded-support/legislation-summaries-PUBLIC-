Summary

LB905 assigns or clarifies agency authorities and creates several funds and transfer rules. Department of Administrative Services (DAS) is made responsible for vacant/excess state property, may order disposals and execute conveyances, must approve utility easements, and is directed to seek approval to donate 43.55 acres to Northeast Community College (sale within 10 years remits proceeds to the General Fund). Department of Agriculture’s definitions under the Potato Inspection Act are revised and the director may use state–federal acreage reports and order termination of compulsory inspection if petitions represent ≥51% of prior commercial acreage. Department of Economic Development must receive surplus‑building/land notices and may refer buyers/lessees (referrals are a final priority); a Housing Office representative is added to the State Advisory Committee on Mental Health Services. Information Management Services’ administrator may centralize IT, set billing rates, and credit charges to the Information Management Revolving Fund (expenditures need legislative appropriation); investment earnings on that fund will be credited to the General Fund beginning Oct 1, 2024. Department of Transportation gains two board representatives, will pay Board of Public Roads expenses, and will receive/remit trust‑fund shares and county highway receipts into new Highway Cash and Roads Operations Cash Funds to be expended/invested by DOT. DHHS is designated lead for foster‑care reimbursement tiering, Bridge to Independence reporting, the statewide Critical Incident Stress Management program, and administers the Breast and Cervical Cancer Cash Fund. Vacant Building and Excess Land Cash Fund proceeds may be used for maintenance/disposal or transferred to the General Fund; proceeds from sale of state military property are likewise remitted to the General Fund (less expenses). No specific dollar appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Bill adds/clarifies a definition designating "Department" as DAS, and specifies roles/authority of the Director/State Building Administrator (including references to §72‑813) and for purposes of the Information Technology Infrastructure Act. This appears to assign/clarify IT and building/utility responsibilities under DAS.

- Department of Agriculture: Adds definitions to the Nebraska Potato Inspection Act — “person,” “potato grower” (actual grower of at least three acres in Nebraska) and “potato shipper” (sells or uses 180,000 pounds of Nebraska potatoes in a year).

- Department of Transportation: Members serving on the Board of Examiners of County Highway and City Street Superintendents on June 30, 2026, are made presumptively eligible to serve on the Board of Public Roads Classifications and Standards without meeting normal criteria; such members serve four‑year terms from their original appointment and may be reappointed.
