Summary

LB878 requires the State of Nebraska (state agencies and other state employers) to provide paid maternity leave to eligible state employees. Key provisions: six weeks of paid leave for full‑time employees (prorated for part‑time); leave usable only from birth/adoption up to six months and no more than six weeks in any 12‑month period; leave generally may not be charged against sick, vacation, compensatory, or other leave and runs concurrently with FMLA where applicable. Intermittent use allowed only by mutual written agreement or medical certification. Employees must provide 30 days’ notice when practicable. Employers must reinstate employees to the same or equivalent position and may not pay out unused maternity leave at termination. Retaliation against employees for using leave is prohibited. The bill does not specify any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
