Summary

LB732 requires that if a court finds a health care practitioner knowingly violated the Act’s prohibition on gender‑altering procedures for persons under 19, the court must mail a certified copy of the final order to both the Attorney General and the Department of Health and Human Services. The bill also authorizes the Attorney General to sue and collect civil penalties, which are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, section 5, of the Nebraska Constitution. The bill does not establish any direct appropriations or budget allocations to the Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text requires courts finding a health care practitioner knowingly violated section 71-7304 to mail a certified copy of the final judgment to the Attorney General and the Department of Health and Human Services. The Attorney General may, within 20 years, sue to enjoin violations and recover $25,000 per violation; each gender‑altering procedure or each prescription/provision of cross‑sex hormones or puberty blockers in violation counts as a separate violation. Civil penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. No specific funding, appropriation, or budget allocation to the Department of Health and Human Services is provided.
