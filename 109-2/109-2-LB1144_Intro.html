Summary

LB1144 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 68‑927 and 68‑928 (Medical Assistance Act). At the department's request (the agency administering state benefit programs, including the medical assistance program), licensed insurers and self‑funded insurers must provide coverage information without an individual's authorization for eligibility determinations and benefit coordination. Such requests must specify the individual and be answered within 30 days unless good cause is shown. Coverage information for limited‑benefit policies is restricted to whether coverage exists and a description, and may be used only for eligibility determinations. Entities issuing a health plan (explicitly including service benefit plans, managed care organizations, pharmacy benefit managers, and other parties legally responsible for payment) must respond to department claims submitted within three years of service and may not deny claims solely for late submission, form type, or point‑of‑sale documentation when department conditions are met; responses must be provided within 30 days. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires entities that issue health plans—including service benefit plans, managed care organizations, pharmacy benefit managers, or any party legally responsible for payment—to respond to requests from "the department" about claims submitted within three years of the date of service. Such issuers may not deny a department-submitted claim solely for late submission, claim form type/format, or lack of point-of-sale documentation if (1) the department submitted the claim within three years of service and (2) the department begins enforcement action within six years of claim submission. Issuers must provide the requested information to the department within 30 days unless good cause is shown. Requests must specify the individual covered. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
