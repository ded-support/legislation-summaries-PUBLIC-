Summary

LB923 amends county administrative duties and recordkeeping:

- County clerk (or designee) must maintain a perpetual inventory of county personal property in counties with ≥150,000 population (per most recent federal decennial census).

- County clerk, county board, and county treasurer: restrengthens warrant controls—clerks may issue warrants only on board-approved claims; must keep publicly accessible warrant records; may prepare/issue warrants electronically or mechanically (including summary warrants); expands acceptable payment methods to treasury-established electronic/telephonic/mechanical or UCC article 4A transfers; authorizes chairperson and clerk signatures by delegation, facsimile, or electronic means; treasurer may establish transfer systems and signature procedures with internal controls.

- County clerk must maintain confidential military Discharge Records (accessible to the veteran, county veterans service officer, or recognized post service officer) and may transfer them to the State Archives of the Nebraska State Historical Society after the federal OMPF is archival or after 62 years.

- Outright repeal of section 23-1313 (registration of farm/ranch/home names).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the county clerk (or the clerk’s designee) to: 1) combine warrants for individual funds into a single summary warrant that lists each fund and requested amount, and 2) transfer a veteran’s Discharge Record to the State Archives of the Nebraska State Historical Society for permanent preservation once the veteran’s federal Official Military Personnel File becomes a public archival record or after 62 years, whichever is later. The federal decennial census is referenced for determinations. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
