Summary

This bill amends county administrative duties and records handling:

- County clerk (or designee) must maintain a perpetual inventory of county personal property in counties with population ≥150,000 (per most recent federal decennial census).

- County clerk may issue warrants only after county board approval, must maintain public-accessible warrant records, may implement electronic/mechanical warrant preparation, and may combine fund-specific warrants into summary warrants. Warrant definition expanded to include electronic, telephonic, UCC article 4A, and other funds-transfer systems. Chairperson and clerk signatures may be delegated, facsimile, or electronic.

- County treasurer may establish electronic/telephonic/mechanical funds-transfer systems with internal control safeguards; such systems satisfy signature requirements.

- County clerk must keep Discharge Record confidential (accessible only to the veteran, county veterans service officer, or post service officer) and may transfer it to the State Archives of the Nebraska State Historical Society after OMPF is archival or 62 years.

- Outright repeal of section 23-1313. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authorization for the county clerk (or the clerk's designee) to combine individual fund warrants into a single summary warrant listing each fund and requested amount. It also permits the county clerk to transfer veterans' Discharge Records to the State Archives of the Nebraska State Historical Society for permanent preservation once the veteran's federal Official Military Personnel File is a public archival record or after 62 years, whichever is later. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
