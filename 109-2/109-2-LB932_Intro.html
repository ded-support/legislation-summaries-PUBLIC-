Summary

LB932 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to add two new individual income-tax deductions effective for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026: qualified tips (26 U.S.C. §224) with a $25,000 cap and AGI-based phaseouts, and qualified overtime compensation (26 U.S.C. §225) with $12,500 (individual) / $25,000 (joint) caps and AGI-based phaseouts. The Tax Commissioner (Nebraska Department of Revenue) will administer these modifications as part of state income-tax adjustments. The bill references multiple state programs and funds (Nebraska educational savings plan trust; ABLE/achieving a better life experience; Give to Enable Support Cash Fund; Medical Debt Relief Fund/Act; Long-Term Care Savings Plan; Firefighter Cancer Benefits Act; Teach in Nebraska Today Act) and the Nebraska National Guard. No new appropriations or budget allocations are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan. 1, 2026, the bill adds two new deductions under the Internal Revenue Code (administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury/IRS):

- A deduction for qualified tips (26 U.S.C. §224) included in federal adjusted gross income, capped at $25,000 per taxpayer per year, with a phaseout of $100 for each $1,000 of federal AGI above $150,000 (single) or $300,000 (married filing jointly).

- A deduction for qualified overtime compensation (26 U.S.C. §225) included in federal AGI, capped at $12,500 for individuals and $25,000 for married filing jointly, with the same phaseout thresholds and rate.

No agency appropriations, new funding, or budget allocations are specified.
