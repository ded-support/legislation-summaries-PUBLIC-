Summary

LB932 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to add two new individual income-tax deductions effective for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2026: (1) qualified tips (26 U.S.C. §224), deductible up to $25,000 annually with AGI-based phaseouts ($150,000 individual / $300,000 married filing jointly); and (2) qualified overtime compensation (26 U.S.C. §225), deductible up to $12,500 for individuals and $25,000 for married filing jointly with the same phaseouts. The section references the Tax Commissioner and various existing state programs and funds (e.g., Nebraska educational savings plan trust, Give to Enable Support Cash Fund, Medical Debt Relief Fund, Nebraska National Guard) and federal agencies (U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Office of Personnel Management). No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective for tax years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan. 1, 2026, the bill allows new deductions from federal adjusted gross income for: (1) qualified tips (defined by 26 U.S.C. §224), capped at $25,000 per year with a phase‑out starting at $150,000 AGI for individuals ($300,000 married filing jointly); and (2) qualified overtime compensation (defined by 26 U.S.C. §225), capped at $12,500 for individuals and $25,000 for joint filers, with the same phase‑out thresholds and $100 reduction per $1,000 excess AGI. The provisions reference the Internal Revenue Code (26 U.S.C.)—administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury/IRS—but include no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
