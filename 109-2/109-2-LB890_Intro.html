Summary

The bill repeatedly assigns responsibilities and receipts to the Department of Revenue. The Tax Commissioner (Dept. of Revenue) may require background checks (including FBI/Nebraska State Patrol fingerprint checks) and charge applicants for the costs. License and fee changes: distributor biennial license fee set at $200 per cash device (max $10,000); manufacturer license fee $10,000; application and annual decal fees set ($500/$250 prior to 2027; $650/$350 in 2027) and thereafter adjusted annually by the Department of Revenue for inflation using the Producer Price Index published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics (with specified rounding). All such fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. Occupation tax is $70 for 2027 and thereafter inflation‑adjusted by the Department of Revenue; occupation tax receipts are split 20% to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund and 80% to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue: Establishes phased fee schedule and annual inflation adjustments for license/application fees and cash-device decal fees—$500 (pre‑1/1/27), $650 (2027), then annually adjusted on Jan 1 using the Producer Price Index (all commodities) and rounded to the nearest $50. Sets occupation tax for non‑cash mechanical amusement devices at $35 (pre‑1/1/27), $70 for 2027, then annually PPI‑adjusted and rounded to the nearest $5. The Department of Revenue must remit collected license fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

Department of Labor: No budget or appropriation changes; its Bureau of Labor Statistics PPI is specified as the inflation index to be used for fee adjustments.

Funding allocation: occupation taxes split 20% to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund and 80% to the General Fund.
