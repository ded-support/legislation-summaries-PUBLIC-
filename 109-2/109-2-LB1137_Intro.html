Summary

The bill adds and clarifies several provisions involving the Department of Insurance (department). It criminalizes knowingly making false entries in material documents filed with or required by the Department of Insurance (Insurance Fraud Act amendments). It directs the Department of Insurance to strictly enforce insurer obligations under subdivision (13) of §44-1540 (reasonable, accurate explanations for claim denials or compromise offers). Public adjuster provisions require contracts and certain compensation terms to be provided to or available for inspection by the department or its director (no redaction of compensation; contracts must be available for inspection), and insurers must verify a public adjuster’s license with the department upon receiving an insured-signed authorization. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds multiple consumer-protection and licensure provisions affecting the Department of Insurance (DOI). Key points: expands prohibited conduct enforceable under the Public Adjusters Licensing Act (unlicensed representation/operation, unlawful rebates, knowingly misrepresenting claim damages); defines and bans "post‑loss assignments" (voids such contracts; licensed public adjusters may accept assignments only to the extent of their fee); requires insurers, upon receiving a public adjuster notification, to verify the adjuster’s license with the DOI; mandates homeowner cancellation notices and a five‑business‑day cancellation right for certain contracts; prohibits adjuster advertising of damage without inspection and offering to pay/waive deductibles as inducements. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
