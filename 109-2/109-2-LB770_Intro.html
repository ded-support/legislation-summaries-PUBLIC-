Summary

LB770 amends distribution of revenue received from the Department of Revenue (per §77-6203). After the county treasurer receives those funds, the treasurer may retain 1% for collection costs; the remainder is distributed with 5% to the community college area where the renewable energy facility is located and the balance allocated among eligible local taxing entities by a levy-weighted formula. The bill also expressly prohibits the Department of Revenue from retaining any revenue collected under §§77-6201–77-6204 for distribution, use, transfer, pledge, or allocation to or from the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language authorizes the Department of Revenue to retain 1% of all revenues it receives under section 77-6203 to cover its costs before remitting the remainder to recipients (e.g., reclamation districts or county agricultural societies). No specific dollar amounts, new appropriations, or other budget allocations are specified in the text.
