Summary

The bill designates the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) as "the department" in the Child Protection and Family Safety Act; defines “person” to include DHHS employees and “protected individual” as those in DHHS care or custody (28-322.04). It requires DHHS to: notify a student's current school and the Commissioner of Education to block transfers to an exempt school when the subject of a child‑abuse report is the student's parent, guardian, or educational decisionmaker; establish procedures to carry out that notification process; and adopt rules governing response timelines, transfers between alternative and traditional responses, Review, Evaluate, and Decide Team criteria, report intake/categorization, and hotline operation. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS-specific additions:

- Prohibits transfer of a student to an "exempt" (non‑accredited) school while a child abuse/neglect report involving the student’s parent, guardian, or educational decisionmaker is under review by a Review, Evaluate, and Decide Team or during a traditional response.

- DHHS must notify the student’s current school and the Commissioner of Education that any election under §79‑1601 to transfer to an exempt school is invalid until further notice, and later notify them when the restriction is lifted.

- Notices are confidential investigation records and may include only the student’s name and whether transfer is allowed.

- DHHS must establish procedures and may adopt rules/regulations to implement these provisions.

- No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.
