Summary

LB767 (Certified Help Options in Claims Expertise for Veterans Act) requires individuals with access to veterans' medical or financial information to "maintain a certification from the National Association for Veterans Rights or an accreditation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs." The bill also expressly states it does not "apply to, limit, or expand the requirements imposed on agents, attorneys, or other representatives accredited or regulated by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs." No sections in the provided text make changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
