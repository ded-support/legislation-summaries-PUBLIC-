Summary

LB767 (Certified Help Options in Claims Expertise for Veterans Act) requires individuals with access to veterans' medical or financial information to maintain either certification from the National Association for Veterans Rights or accreditation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs. The bill expressly states it does not alter, limit, or expand requirements imposed on agents, attorneys, or other representatives accredited or regulated by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes for the Department of Veterans' Affairs are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires providers handling veterans' clients to avoid international data centers, not access clients' login credentials, run identity and criminal-history background checks on anyone accessing medical/financial records, and hold either National Association for Veterans Rights certification or accreditation from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs. Violations are treated as deceptive trade practices under the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. The bill explicitly does not alter, expand, or limit requirements imposed on agents, attorneys, or other representatives accredited or regulated by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, and it does not apply to state or county veterans service officers. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
