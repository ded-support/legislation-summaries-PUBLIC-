Summary

LB854 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28‑101 (Nebraska Criminal Code) to require law enforcement officers to wear "appropriate identification" (name and badge number plus employing agency name or initials) and generally prohibit facial coverings or personal disguises while performing duties. "Law enforcement agency" explicitly includes municipal police departments and town marshals, county sheriffs' offices, the Nebraska State Patrol, Class I railroad police departments, other states' law enforcement agencies, and federal law enforcement agencies. "Law enforcement officer" also includes federal officers, National Guard members on active service by gubernatorial direction, and persons acting on their behalf. Exemptions cover covert/undercover operations and specific protective uses (medical masks/respirators, ballistic/chemical protection, helmets, clear face shields, etc.). Violation is a Class III misdemeanor. No funding or appropriation changes are included. The original §28‑101 is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that law enforcement officers wear visible identification and not wear facial coverings or disguises while performing duties in the state, with exceptions for covert/undercover operations and specified safety/health situations (disease masks, bodily-fluid or toxin protection, high-risk incident protection, clear shields, SCBA for underwater, motorcycle helmets, protective eyewear). Defines "law enforcement agency" to include municipal police, town marshals, county sheriffs, the Nebraska State Patrol, Class I railroad police, other states' and federal agencies; "officer" includes federal officers, National Guard members on active state service, and persons acting on their behalf. Violation is a Class III misdemeanor. No funding or appropriation changes.
