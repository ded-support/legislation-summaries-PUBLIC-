Summary

LB989 appropriates $500,000 from the General Fund to the Military Department (Program 544) for FY2026‑27 to support the Nebraska Nonprofit Security Grant Program. Of that amount, $450,000 is designated as General Fund state aid and must be used solely for that purpose. Expenditures from the appropriation for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $45,000 for FY2026‑27.
