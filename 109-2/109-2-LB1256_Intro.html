Summary

LB1256 amends section 81-829.39 of the Emergency Management Act to revise definitional language. It (1) clarifies "emergency management" functions to explicitly include snow and ice removal and flood management alongside firefighting, police, medical, search and rescue, radiological preparedness, hazardous materials response, evacuation, emergency welfare and transportation, utility restoration, and related civilian-protection activities; (2) continues to define "emergency management worker" broadly to include paid, volunteer, auxiliary personnel and instructors and students in programs approved by the Nebraska Emergency Management Agency (NEMA); and (3) reiterates the State Emergency Response Team as established under sections 81-829.52–81-829.54. The bill references the Governor, Legislature, federal authorities, and political subdivisions. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
