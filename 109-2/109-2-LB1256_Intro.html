Summary

LB1256 (2026) amends section 81-829.39 of the Emergency Management Act, revising definitions used statewide. Key agency/department points:

- Expands the definition of "emergency management" to explicitly include snow and ice removal and flood management alongside existing functions (utilities restoration, firefighting, police, medical, hazardous materials response, evacuation, radiological preparedness, etc.).

- Clarifies that "emergency management worker" includes instructors and students in programs approved by the Nebraska Emergency Management Agency and workers from state, local, interstate, or international entities serving under state or political-subdivision authority or request.

- Confirms the "state emergency response team" is established by state authority under sections 81-829.52–81-829.54 to supplement local response.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
