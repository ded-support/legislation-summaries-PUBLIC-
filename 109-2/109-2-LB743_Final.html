Summary

Bill LB743 amends the Police Officers Retirement Act (§16-1007). It clarifies optional benefit forms administered by the retirement committee and provided under the retirement system, adding that a retiring officer may elect a single lump-sum payment or one or more partial payments of the officer’s retirement value, and may defer the first payment to the first day of any month prior to age 70. Annuity purchases provided by the retirement system may be distributed to officers, terminating system obligations. For officers entitled to statutory minimum pensions, actuarial-equivalent conversions apply; if choosing a lump-sum, the actuarial value may be the average cost of three comparable annuity contracts selected by the officer, the retirement committee, and the city. If retirement value is insufficient to meet minimum benefits, the city must transfer funds to the officer’s employer account. Operative October 1, 2026. No state appropriations or new funding allocations are specified.
