Summary

LB965 assigns multiple new roles and definitions to the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DCS is defined as the “Department” for sexual‑abuse provisions; “person” is expanded to include DCS employees, contractors, and designees. Sexual‑abuse offenses are explicitly expanded to cover inmates, parolees, probationers, and problem‑solving court participants. DCS is designated a public‑safety agency; correctional officers are included in Brady‑Giglio procedures and employment protections. DCS must receive victim information from county attorneys, notify victims of furloughs, releases, escapes, community‑status transfers and release‑date calculations, forward victim names to DHHS on mental‑health or sex‑offender commitment petitions, and may place eligible inmates in work camps (excluding specified sexual and capital offenders). DHHS receives reports concerning victims under 18, may receive confidential child‑abuse investigation information, must notify victims of commitments/escapes/discharges/program releases, and assaults on DHHS employees are subject to enhanced penalties. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Correctional Services (DOCS)

- The bill explicitly defines "Department" as the Department of Correctional Services and includes correctional officers in the definition of "officer" and DOCS in the list of "public safety agency" entities.

- Adds procedural protections and notice/hearing rights for officers named in Brady‑Giglio disclosures, confidentiality requirements for officers' personal information, and prohibitions on discipline solely for being named.

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

- Expands reportable/registerable sex offenses to include "sexual abuse by a child welfare service provider" (section 13) and similar conservator/guardian offenses (section 12), making such convictions subject to the Sex Offender Registration Act even if from other jurisdictions.

Funding/Appropriations

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
