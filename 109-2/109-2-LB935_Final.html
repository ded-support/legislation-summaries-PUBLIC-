Summary

LB935 includes provisions affecting the three agencies as follows:

- Department of Correctional Services (DOC): Courts must impose a determinate term for a misdemeanor when sentenced concurrently or consecutively with specified Class III/IIIA/IV (or higher) felony sentences, unless the defendant is committed to DOC under §29‑2204.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): For sexual‑assault victims under 18, DHHS (with providers/peace officers) must provide statutorily required victim‑rights information and host materials on its website. The Title IV‑D Division’s certified support payment history is prima facie evidence that child/spousal support payments are current and may release or substantiate liens for 30 days.

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): The budget administrator is authorized to direct timing and amounts of multiple transfers from the State Settlement Cash Fund to the General Fund and other funds, with specified dollar amounts and dates.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: No added provisions or funding changes affecting DAS in the underlined text.

Department of Correctional Services: No added provisions or funding changes affecting the Department of Correctional Services.

Department of Health and Human Services: New language makes a Title IV-D Division payment history from DHHS (showing support payments current for the prior 12 months or life of the order, whichever is shorter) prima facie evidence to release a child or spousal support lien for purposes of transferring a specific parcel of real property. Fiscal action included: the State Treasurer is directed to transfer $10,000 from the State Settlement Cash Fund to the Legal Education for Public Service and Rural Practice Loan Repayment Assistance Fund on the section’s operative date.
