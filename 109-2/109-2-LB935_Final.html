Summary

LB935 includes agency-specific provisions for three departments:

- Department of Administrative Services: The budget administrator may direct timing and amounts of multiple transfers from the State Settlement Cash Fund to the General Fund, Nebraska Capital Construction Fund, and specified loan‑repayment funds. Investment earnings on the Settlement Cash Fund will be credited to the General Fund beginning October 1, 2024.

- Department of Correctional Services: Commitments to the department under §29‑2204 are made a condition permitting courts to impose determinate misdemeanor sentences when such sentences run concurrently or consecutively with certain felony terms.

- Department of Health and Human Services: For sexual‑assault victims under 18, DHHS must provide prescribed victim‑rights information in accessible formats and host/downloadable materials on its website. Title IV‑D support payment histories from DHHS are prima facie evidence of current payments for 30 days and may release or rebut liens in property transfers.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services: The bill adds that a current child- or spousal-support payment history from the Title IV-D Division of DHHS (or district court clerk for spousal support) is prima facie evidence payments are current and releases a support lien for purposes of transferring specific real property. Department of Administrative Services: no additions or budgetary changes affecting DAS are included. Department of Correctional Services: no additions or budgetary changes affecting the Department of Correctional Services are included. Additional funding change (general): the State Treasurer must transfer $10,000 from the State Settlement Cash Fund to the Legal Education for Public Service and Rural Practice Loan Repayment Assistance Fund on the bill's operative date.
