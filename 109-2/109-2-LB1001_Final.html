Summary

The bill places the Charitable Gaming Division within the Department of Revenue, assigning it administration and enforcement duties for multiple gambling statutes and requiring annual electronic reports to the Governor, Legislature, Auditor, and Attorney General. It creates a Charitable Gaming Operations Fund: 40% of taxes under specified sections (9‑239, 9‑344, 9‑429, 9‑648) is made available to the Division and the State Racing and Gaming Commission for administration/support, and 60% transfers to the General Fund. From July 1, 2019, through June 30, 2026, $100,000 per quarter is transferred from the Operations Fund to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund. The Division may assess fees to the Operations Fund; taxes under §77‑3012 fund Mechanical Amusement Device enforcement and central server maintenance. Gaming winners’ setoffs and casino withholdings for outstanding state liabilities are remitted to and credited by the Department. The Department may regulate gift enterprises, bar promotional tickets resembling approved pickle cards, and seek injunctions in Lancaster County.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds responsibilities for the department (Department of Revenue) to register progressive-jackpot/multi-draw lotteries (e.g., "Queen of Hearts") run by licensed organizations or qualifying nonprofits, require game rules to be posted where tickets are sold/played, and authorize the department to adopt rules and regulations governing those games. It also sets that lotteries under the referenced section need only 50% of gross proceeds for prizes. No explicit changes to Department of Revenue funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
