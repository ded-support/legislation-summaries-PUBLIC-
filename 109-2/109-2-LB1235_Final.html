Summary

LB1235 amends liquor and medical cannabis law. It requires local governing bodies to file entertainment-district designations with the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission and authorizes a consumption-only entertainment-district license ($25 fee payable to the commission and remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund) and a $300 local entertainment-district fee payable to municipal clerks.

It creates the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission (three ex officio Nebraska Liquor Control Commission members plus up to two gubernatorial appointees, six-year terms), sets commissioner salaries at $12,500 annually, and establishes the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission Cash Fund (Legislative transfers, fees, gifts, grants; excludes civil penalties) to finance administration and enforcement. The state investment officer may invest fund balances. The commission may set registration fees up to $50,000 and must require FBI national criminal-history checks via the Nebraska State Patrol (fee payable to the patrol).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a consumption-only class of entertainment district license administered by the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission for businesses inside locally designated consumption-only areas. Applicants pay a $25 fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission Rule and Regulation Cash Fund; local governing-body approval is required before the commission issues the license.

Establishes the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission salary ($12,500 annually per member) and creates the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission Cash Fund, funded by legislative transfers and fees/gifts/grants (excluding civil penalties) for administration and enforcement. Money may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment statutes. The commission may set fees up to $50,000. Applicants must submit two fingerprint sets to the FBI via the Nebraska State Patrol and pay the patrol’s record-check fee, authorizing release to the commission.
