Summary

The bill substantially expands Department of Revenue authority, funding streams, and interagency data sharing, and narrows an ImagiNE Nebraska eligibility definition for the Department of Banking and Finance.

Key Department of Revenue provisions:

- Creates the Domestic Violence & Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act (effective tax years beginning 1/1/2027) with annual refundable credit allocations: $240,000 (tribal programs); $150,000 (statewide coalition affiliated with DHHS); $1,044,000 (equally to qualifying entities); and $1,566,000 apportioned by population ($1,252,800) and area ($313,200).

- Requires DHHS and Revenue to share confidential information for administration, with use limits and penalties.

- Expands fee/assessment authority (collection/assessment fees, $40 redetermination filing fee, recovery of collection costs); establishes numerous license/application/decal fees and directs proceeds to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

- Creates a Charitable Gaming Division and Operations Fund, designating 40% of specified gaming taxes for division administration (60% to the General Fund) and allowing transfers to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund; terminates the Miscellaneous Receipts Fund (7/1/2026) and transfers balances to the Enforcement Fund.

- Grants rulemaking/administration authority over renewable energy credits, home‑buyer credit recapture, winnings setoffs, and the Mechanical Amusement Device Tax Act.

Department of Banking and Finance:

- Limits "financial services" eligible for ImagiNE Nebraska benefits to entities licensed by the Department of Banking and Finance or the SEC.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue

- Creates the refundable Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act (taxable years beginning on/after Jan 1, 2027) with annual credit allocations: $240,000 to tribal DV/sexual assault programs; $150,000 to a statewide coalition of DHHS‑affiliated nonprofits; $1,044,000 equally to specified shelters/nonprofits; and $1,566,000 distributed to those entities ( $1,252,800 by population, $313,200 by square miles). Credits are transferable (with DOR notification) and DOR may adopt rules.

- Imposes collection and assessment fees: $25 or 10% (whichever greater) on delinquent claims and assessments, plus actual collection costs; authorizes a $40 filing fee for certain redetermination petitions.

- Establishes a 10% excise tax on retail kratom sales (beginning Jan 1, 2027), remitted monthly to the General Fund.

- Directs occupation tax receipts: 20% to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund and 80% to the General Fund.

- Requires retailers/registrants recordkeeping and electronic reporting as prescribed.

Department of Health and Human Services

- Authorized to share confidential information with DOR (and receive DOR data) for proper administration beginning Oct 31, 2026; DHHS‑affiliated nonprofits are eligible for a portion of the new tax credits. No direct appropriations added.

Department of Labor and Department of Banking and Finance

- No changes, funding, or allocations affecting these agencies are included.
