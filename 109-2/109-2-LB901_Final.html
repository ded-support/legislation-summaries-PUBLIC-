Summary

The bill substantially expands Department of Revenue (DOR) authority, funding streams, and interagency data‑sharing and creates new dedicated funds. DOR will administer a $3.0M refundable Domestic Violence & Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit (tax year 2027), allocated $240,000 to tribal programs, $150,000 to a DHHS‑affiliated statewide coalition, $1,044,000 to nonprofit shelters, and $1,566,000 via population/area formula; credits may be sold with seller notice to DOR. DOR may exchange confidential information with DHHS beginning Oct. 31, 2026. DOR gains rulemaking/enforcement over mechanical amusement/cash devices, new licensing/decals/fees (schedules effective 2025–2027) and collection/assessment fees, and may cap renewable energy credits usable statewide at $50,000. The bill creates a Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund and a Charitable Gaming Operations Fund, redirects gaming/device taxes (detailed percentage remittances) to multiple funds (including Behavioral Health Services Fund) and institutes a 10% kratom excise (effective Jan. 1, 2027) remitted to the General Fund. Department of Banking and Finance is referenced for “financial services” licensing eligibility in ImagiNE Nebraska definitions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue (DOR)

- New "Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act" (taxable years beginning/ deemed Jan 1, 2027): $3,000,000 total refundable income tax credits annually, allocated (examples) $240,000 to tribal programs, $150,000 to a DHHS‑affiliated statewide coalition, $1,044,000 shared among shelters/nonprofits, and $1,566,000 split by population ($1,252,800) and square miles ($313,200). Credits are transferable; sellers must notify DOR 30 days before purchaser claims them. DOR may adopt rules and must distribute credits annually.

- New delinquent-collection and assessment charges: $25 or 10% (whichever greater) added to delinquent claims and to assessments/notices; DOR may add actual collection costs; up to 50% of balance may be added when assigned to a collection agency. DOR may charge a $40 filing fee for redetermination petitions and may register delinquent tax claims as judgments. Collections remitted to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund and other specified funds.

- Excise tax on retail kratom sales: 10% starting Jan 1, 2027; monthly returns, recordkeeping, and remittance to the General Fund; DOR enforcement duties and removal of adulterated products from registries.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

- Eligible coalition receiving $150,000 under the new tax‑credit program (must have an affiliation with DHHS).

- Beginning Oct 31, 2026, DHHS must, on request, share confidential information with DOR for proper administration; use limited to statutory purposes and remains subject to confidentiality/penalties.

- Department of Labor and Department of Banking and Finance

- No substantive additions or funding changes affecting these agencies in the underlined text.
