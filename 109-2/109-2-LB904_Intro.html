Summary

LB904 amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to add disclosure and registration requirements for lobbyists and for consultants representing "Chinese military companies."

Key agencies/departments:

- Clerk of the Legislature: receives lobbyist registrations on forms approved by the Executive Board of the Legislative Council.

- Executive Board of the Legislative Council: approves registration form.

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission ("the commission"): receives consultant filings, may impose enforcement orders, fines, and pay tipsters.

- State Treasurer: receives civil penalties for distribution under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution.

Budget/funding effects:

- Civil penalties for failing to disclose representation of a Chinese military company: $100,000 for each violation, escalating by $100,000 up to $1,000,000 per violation; collected penalties remitted to the State Treasurer.

- The commission may pay an informant $50,000 if a violation is found, contingent on recovery of the civil penalty. The commission retains authority to assess up to $5,000 per general violation and recover hearing costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a pre-activity registration requirement with "the commission" (form prescribed by the commission) for any person employed, retained, or authorized as a consultant for a Chinese military company before they begin influencing activity in Nebraska. Required filings must include: consultant name and residence/office addresses; the Chinese military company's name and address; description of the consultant's business activity; the specific matters the consultant will seek to influence; an affirmative acknowledgment that the consultant is acting as an agent of a foreign adversary of the United States; and the name and address of any official in the legislative (text cuts off).
