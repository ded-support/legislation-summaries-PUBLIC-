Summary

- Department of Economic Development: Prohibits the department from requiring Affordable Housing Trust Fund–funded new construction or rental conversion projects to comply with the International Energy Conservation Code or to obtain building-plan approval by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment.

- Department of Insurance: Affirmed exemption of Department of Insurance contracts (financial/actuarial examinations; rehabilitation, conservation, reorganization, or liquidation of licensees; and related professional services) from state procurement statutes.

- Department of Health and Human Services: The agency’s chief executive (or designee) is added as a member of the Water Well Standards and Contractors' Licensing Board; its rules are referenced for facility closure/decommissioning/decontamination standards for licensed operators.

- Department of Revenue: The Litter Fee Collection Fund is created and appropriated to the Department of Revenue; the Tax Commissioner may withhold an administrative collection fee to be deposited in that fund.

- Department of Transportation: Contracts for all road and bridge projects remain exempt from specified state procurement requirements.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language grants the Water, Energy, and Environment department (via the Chief Water Officer or designee) authority to enter property to conduct surveys, tests, borings, and other work related to locating, acquiring, or constructing the Perkins County Canal Project. Such entry is not deemed trespass; the department must pay for any actual demonstrable damage, and unresolved damage claims may be pursued under section 76-705.
