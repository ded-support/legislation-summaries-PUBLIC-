Summary

The bill updates motor vehicle, veterans, and health agency authorities and creates several new dedicated funds. DMV: expands title/registration rules (bonded motorboat titles, "In Transit" placards, transporter recordkeeping), phases single rear license-plate display (1/1/2029), may mail plates/registrations and charge postage/handling (credited to the DMV Cash Fund), requires in-person state ID renewal at least once every 16 years with new digital image, mandates electronic SR‑22 filings by 7/1/2027, and will issue specialty plates (fee allocations: $5 alphanumeric Military Honor; $40 personalized—25% to DMV Cash Fund, 75% to designated recipient; $3 transfer fee to DMV Cash Fund). DOR: shares situs/registration/tax authority and may impose a 50% late fee (credited to the Highway Trust Fund). DHHS: may award grants, administers a Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital & Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, and receives Choose Life plate fees (to the HHS Cash Fund). DVA: maintains the Military Honor registry and administers a Women Veterans Cash Fund. Military Department: establishes the Military Department Aid Fund (recipient of most personalized Military Honor plate revenues beginning 1/1/2027) and may award nonrepayable grants to needy service members. Manufacturing-cost shortfalls are first credited to the Highway Trust Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: creates the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, administered by DHHS, funded by gifts/grants/alternate license-plate fees and legislative transfers; funds invest via state investment officer; DHHS may award grants and must publish application form by Jan 1, 2027.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: authorizes numerous new specialty plates (e.g., Back the Blue, Blackout, Choice Color, Scarlet & Cream, Honoring Women Veterans, University Museum) with set fees and specified revenue allocations (Blackout $100 — 30% to the Developmental Disabilities Provider Fund, 70% to DMV Cash Fund; Choice Color/Scarlet & Cream fees credited to DMV Cash Fund); changes in "In Transit" display rules effective Jan 1, 2029; SR-22 proof electronic transmission from July 1, 2027.

- Department of Revenue: shares authority with DMV to determine presumed in-state ownership for tax/registration; appeal procedures; unpaid taxes incur 50% late fee credited to the Highway Trust Fund.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: administers Women Veterans Cash Fund (funded by plate fees) for women veterans events; funds invested.

- Military Department: creates Military Department Aid Fund, administered by the Adjutant General, funded by gifts/grants/plate-fee credits and legislative transfers; funds invest; grants to needy Military Department members authorized beginning Jan 1, 2027.

- Department of Transportation: no direct substantive changes or appropriations in the added text.
