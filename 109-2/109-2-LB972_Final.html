Summary

The bill revises vehicle-title and plate rules and creates several agency‑administered cash funds. DMV: establishes a bonded motorboat title process (fees $50/$20 remitted to the DMV Cash Fund), authorizes in‑transit placards and $10 transporter certificates/plates, permits mailing plates with postage/handling remitted to the DMV Cash Fund, sets single rear‑plate use from 1/1/2029, and requires DMV to issue Military Honor Plates using the Department of Veterans' Affairs registry for eligibility verification. Department of Revenue: partners with DMV on situs determinations; a 50% late penalty on unpaid taxes/fees is remitted to the Highway Trust Fund. Plate fee allocations: Military Honor Plate fees ($5 alphanumeric to designated recipients; $40 personalized split 25% to DMV Cash Fund/75% to designated recipient, with personalized proceeds shifting to the Military Department Aid Fund on 1/1/2027); Choose Life plate proceeds to the DHHS Cash Fund. New funds: Military Department Aid Fund, DHHS Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, and Women Veterans Cash Fund; the Military Department, Nebraska State Patrol, and DHHS are authorized to award or receive grants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): New specialty plates and display rules (including In Transit changes effective 1/1/2029). DMV will design/issue multiple new plates, collect associated fees, remit to State Treasurer, and credit specified percentages to designated funds (examples: Blackout plates $100 — 30% to Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, 70% to DMV Cash Fund; Choice Color and Scarlet & Cream plates fees credited to DMV Cash Fund). DMV and Dept. of Revenue may make presumptive ownership determinations for out‑of‑state vehicles, notify residents to register/pay taxes, and handle appeals; unpaid amounts incur a 50% late fee credited to the Highway Trust Fund.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Creation of the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, DHHS‑administered, funded in part by plate fee credits (sec. 60‑3,163.02) and legislative transfers; DHHS may award grants and adopt rules.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: Creation of the Women Veterans Cash Fund, DVA‑administered, funded by plate fee credits (sec. 60‑3,163.02) for women‑veteran events.

- Military Department: Creation of the Military Department Aid Fund, Adjutant General‑administered, funded by gifts, plate fee credits (sec. 60‑3,122.04), and legislative transfers; may award non‑repayable grants to needy service members beginning 1/1/2027.
