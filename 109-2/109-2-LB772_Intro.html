Summary

The bill centralizes cash‑fund control and creates/adjusts agency funds and transfer schedules across multiple departments. Department of Administrative Services (DAS) is authorized to draw warrants for numerous program funds (including motor vehicle licensing, geologists, certified public accountants, and Correctional Services facility funds), to establish a Soil & Water Conservation subaccount, to administer vacant‑building/excess‑land funds, and to schedule and direct multiple State Treasurer transfers from settlement, tobacco, visitors promotion and other cash funds to the General Fund. Department of Insurance fees are credited to its cash fund with mandated transfers to the General Fund: $14 million by June 30, 2026 and $11 million by June 30 in 2027, 2028 and 2029. Department of Revenue gains new enforcement, charitable gaming, racetrack gaming, petroleum‑collection and other cash funds with specified revenue splits and quarterly $100,000 transfers to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund through June 30, 2026. Departments of Banking and Finance, Motor Vehicles, Agriculture, Labor, Economic Development, Correctional Services, and the Military Department each receive new or restructured cash funds and defined transfer/administration authority.
