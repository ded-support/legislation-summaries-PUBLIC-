Summary

The bill centralizes cash‑fund management and directed transfers across several agencies. The Department of Administrative Services (DAS) is given authority to issue warrants/disbursements (including for Geologists, CPAs, Correctional Services Facility Cash Fund, and State Visitors Promotion), create subaccounts/subfunds, and the budget administrator is directed to schedule numerous transfers from various cash funds to the General Fund (including specified Historic transfers, a $500,000 transfer from the Nebraska Motor Vehicle Industry Licensing Fund, and timing/amounts for other funds).

Department of Banking and Finance: creates a Securities Act Cash Fund and a Settlement Cash Fund; directs transfers from the Securities Act fund to the General Fund ($34M by 6/30/2026; $28M annually 2027–2029).

Department of Insurance: creates an Insurance Cash Fund with transfers to the General Fund ($14M by 6/30/2026; $11M annually 2027–2029).

Department of Revenue: creates Charitable Gaming and Racetrack Gaming funds (40% of specified gaming tax for operations, 60% to General Fund; quarterly $100,000 transfers to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund through 6/30/2026), establishes an Enforcement Fund (10% of amnesty proceeds, cap $500K; initial FY appropriation for investigators/agents; future deposit percentages and $750K cap apply), credits certain receipts to implementation costs, and authorizes a $150,000 annual administrative deduction to the Petroleum Release Remedial Action Collection Fund (appropriated to DOR).

Department of Agriculture: eligible for Motor Fuel Tax Enforcement/Collection fund reimbursements; certain administrative cash funds (Seed, Pure Food, Fertilizers/Soil Conditioners) dedicated to department administration and specified transfer directions.

Department of Labor: establishes Contractor & PEO Registration Cash Fund and Workforce Development Program Cash Fund for enforcement, grants, and UI/admin functions.

Department of Correctional Services: establishes a Facility Cash Fund with DAS‑processed certified disbursements.

Department of Motor Vehicles: creates Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund, Vehicle Title & Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund, and a DMV Cash Fund with permitted transfers and earnings treatment.

Military Department: establishes a Military Department Cash Fund administered by the Adjutant General.
