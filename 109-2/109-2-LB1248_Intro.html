Summary

Bill LB1248 amends Laws 2025, LB261, section 280, revising appropriations for the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (Agency No. 84), Program No. 513 — Environmental Quality. Key fiscal changes: FY2025‑26 — General Fund $5,593,323; Cash Funds $42,816,431; Federal $45,798,301; Program total $94,208,055. FY2026‑27 — General Fund $43,593,323; Cash Funds $43,115,996; Federal $46,175,320; Program total $132,884,639. Salary limit rises from $18,821,791 to $19,216,295. Earmarks: state aid set aside (FY25‑26: $18,645,601 Cash, $24,538,513 Federal; FY26‑27: $38,000,000 General, $18,645,601 Cash, $24,529,499 Federal). Up to $6,000,000 of unexpended Cash Fund balances from the Department of Natural Resources is reappropriated for metropolitan utilities districts’ lead service line replacement grants. The Department of Administrative Services will monitor appropriations. The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a FY2026-27 appropriation of $38,000,000 in General Funds as aid within the named program to award a grant to a "city of the first class" that operates a publicly owned wastewater treatment facility. Eligible facility must be subject to a National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit and have documented increases in industrial wastewater load causing treatment capacity or compliance problems tied to a calcium caseinate food‑ingredient manufacturing facility.
