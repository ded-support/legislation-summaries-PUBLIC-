Summary

LB1086 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-2005 governing the community college gap assistance program. It specifies applicant eligibility outcomes (ability to be accepted to and complete eligible programs and postsecondary credit programs, obtain and maintain full-time employment). The committee administering the program may award assistance up to the full amount of eligible costs but must deny applications when total eligible costs are covered by other public or private funding sources. Importantly, the committee is prohibited from treating benefits available through the State of Nebraska’s SNAP Employment and Training (SNAP E&T) program (per 7 C.F.R. 273.7 as of Jan 1, 2026) as a disqualifying funding source. The bill repeals the prior section and takes effect immediately due to an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that, for purposes of determining eligibility for community college gap assistance, the committee must not count any benefit available to an applicant under the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program Employment and Training (SNAP E&T) program as administered by the State of Nebraska pursuant to 7 C.F.R. 273.7 (as of Jan. 1, 2026). Mentions the State of Nebraska and the referenced federal regulation; no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.
