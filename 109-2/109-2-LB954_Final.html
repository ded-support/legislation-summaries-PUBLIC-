Summary

The bill requires the Department of Revenue to recalculate a taxpayer’s base-year employees for a tier 6 Nebraska Advantage project when the taxpayer sells or transfers part of the business operations and those operations continue under an entity not in the same unitary group. The recalculation subtracts equivalent employees at the sold/transferred operations from the base-year employee count on the last Form 312N filed before the sale or transfer. Exceptions: no recalculation if the sold operations cease within 24 months or if the primary purpose of the transfer was to close a location. Credits generated before the sale/transfer are not recalculated. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Revenue to recalculate a tier 6 project taxpayer’s base‑year employee count when the taxpayer sells/transfers parts of the business covered by the agreement and those operations continue under an entity outside the taxpayer’s unitary group. The recalculation is done by subtracting the equivalent employees at the sold/transferred operations from the base‑year employees reported on the last Form 312N before the sale/transfer. Exemptions: the rule does not apply if the sold/transferred operations cease within 24 months or the sale/transfer’s primary purpose was to close a location. Credits or incentives already generated before the sale/transfer are not recalculated. Applies to agreements entered after Dec. 31, 2016. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
