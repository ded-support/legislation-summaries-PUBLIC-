Summary

LB829 amends duties of the Auditor of Public Accounts. Audits must follow Government Auditing Standards (2024 Revision) for financial and attestation engagements and generally exclude performance audits. Entities (except state colleges and the University of Nebraska) receiving a management-letter comment must submit a detailed corrective-action plan to the Auditor within six months; the Auditor may evaluate and must electronically report findings to the Governor, the appropriate legislative standing committee, and the Appropriations Committee and ensure inclusion in Appropriations Committee budget hearing records. The bill affirms the Auditor may charge political subdivisions for audits (fees to cover costs deposited into the Auditor of Public Accounts Cash Fund) and assess 14% interest on delinquent audit fees. The Auditor must prescribe and publish certain annual report forms, maintain an online budget/actual reporting system for political subdivisions, may conduct joint audits with the Legislative Oversight Committee, and the act takes effect immediately.
