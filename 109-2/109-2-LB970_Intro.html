Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services (department) to pursue multiple Medicaid actions and coverage mandates, including:

- Submit a state plan amendment by Oct. 1, 2026 to expand EPSDT to add an early‑literacy promotion/intervention program for children birth–5.

- Submit/seek waivers or state plan amendments and provide coverage for specified services (community-based behavioral health, school‑provided EPSDT services, CGMs, extended postpartum coverage, medical respite for homeless adults, electric personal‑use breast pumps and lactation consultation visits, translation/interpretation services, targeted case management for nurse home visiting, and psychology services) by the dates listed in the bill.

- Funding directives: several provisions are to be funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (including CGM implementation limits—intent no more than $600,000 annually—medical respite, translation services, and targeted case management) and an intent that lactation consultation appropriations equal a 145% rate increase over the current rate.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

By October 1, 2026 the Department of Health and Human Services must seek CMS approval via a state plan amendment to expand EPSDT (early and periodic screening, diagnosis, and treatment) to add an early literacy promotion and intervention program during well‑child visits for ages birth–5. The program will provide evidence‑based screening, referrals, and age‑appropriate literacy materials and guidance for children on medical assistance. Funding: the department is to pursue federal matching funds; no direct state appropriation is specified.
