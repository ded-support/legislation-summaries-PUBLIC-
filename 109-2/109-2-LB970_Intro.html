Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services (the department) to take multiple Medicaid actions and expansions, including:

- Submit a state plan amendment by October 1, 2026 to expand EPSDT to add an early literacy promotion/intervention program for children birth–5.

- Provide specified coverages by set dates: continuous glucose monitors (by Jan 1, 2023; expansion effective Aug 1, 2024), electric personal-use breast pumps and minimum lactation consultations (by Jan 1, 2025), translation/interpretation services (by Jan 1, 2024), targeted case management for nurse home visiting (seek SPA by Oct 1, 2025), and psychology services by advanced-level practitioners (by Jan 1, 2026).

- Funding/appropriations: intent that up to $600,000 annually be appropriated from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund for the glucose-monitor expansion; lactation consultations funded at a 145% rate increase over current departmental rates; multiple provisions to be funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (medical respite, translation services, nurse home visiting).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services, by October 1, 2026, to seek CMS approval (via a state plan amendment) to obtain federal Medicaid matching funds to expand EPSDT benefits to add an early literacy promotion and intervention program during well‑child visits for children birth through age five. The program must provide evidence‑based screening, referrals, and age‑appropriate materials and guidance for children enrolled in medical assistance as part of standard well‑child care. No explicit state appropriations or dollar amounts are specified; the provision seeks federal matching funds contingent on CMS approval.
