Summary

LB969 requires the Nebraska Auditor of Public Accounts to develop, maintain, and publish online searchable financial databases for municipalities: cities of the metropolitan, primary, first and second classes, and villages. Each database must include revenue sources, expenditures, and balance sheets (assets and liabilities) drawn from the most recent municipal audits. Municipalities must provide current data annually and historical financial information beginning with calendar year 2016; the Auditor must update databases within 30 days of receipt. The Auditor must set implementation dates on or before January 1, 2027. Disclosure exclusions align with existing state and federal confidentiality and public-record law. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that the Auditor of Public Accounts (APA) create, maintain, and publish on its website a statewide financial database for cities of the metropolitan, primary, first, and second classes and for villages. The APA must include revenue sources, expenditures, and balance sheets (assets/liabilities) from the most recent municipal audits (and audit reports filed under section 19-2905 where cited), set an implementation date on or before January 1, 2027, and update the database within 30 days after receiving annual updates. Cities/villages must supply current and historical (from 2016) financial data. Confidential or nonpublic records are exempt. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
