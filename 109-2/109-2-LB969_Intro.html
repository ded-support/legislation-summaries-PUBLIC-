Summary

LB969 directs the Nebraska Auditor of Public Accounts to develop, maintain, and publish on its website financial databases for municipalities. Although the bill’s caption references counties and municipalities, the operative sections require databases for cities of the metropolitan, primary, first and second classes and for villages. Each database must include revenue sources, expenditures, and a balance sheet (assets and liabilities) drawn from the most recent municipal audits (or audits filed under §19‑2905). The Auditor must set implementation dates no later than January 1, 2027, update databases within 30 days of receiving annual submissions, and collect historical data back to calendar year 2016 where available. Disclosure exclusions follow federal/state confidentiality rules and Neb. Rev. Stat. §84‑712.05. The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Auditor of Public Accounts to develop, maintain, and post on its website searchable financial databases for all city classes (metropolitan, primary, first, second) and villages, with an implementation date on or before January 1, 2027. Databases must include revenue sources, expenditures, and balance sheets (assets and liabilities) drawn from most recent municipal audits (including audits filed under section 19-2905). Cities/villages must provide necessary current data annually and historical financial data back to 2016 when available; the Auditor must update the database within 30 days of receipt. Confidential or nonpublic records remain exempt. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
