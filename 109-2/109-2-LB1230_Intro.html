Summary

LB1230 amends vehicle title and landlord-tenant statutes to treat mobile homes like titled vehicles and to harmonize disposal procedures. Key state and local actors affected:

- Nebraska Department of Motor Vehicles (Department) / Director of Motor Vehicles: retains authority to issue certificates of title (including acceptance of electronic title records after repossession) and to set the implementation date for electronic title provisions.

- County treasurers: may issue and retain certificates of title, accept alternative proof of ownership (including county assessor assessment records) for manufactured/mobile homes, and process applications after repossession or statutory transfers.

- County assessors: their assessment records are authorized as proof of ownership for title issuance.

- State Treasurer: continues to receive unclaimed sale proceeds for disposition under the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act.

- Courts of record: remain sources of ownership orders.

No changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included. Original referenced statutory sections are repealed and replaced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Substantive additions reference the Disposition of Personal Property Landlord and Tenant Act (including section 76-1459) and clarify application to mobile home spaces and mobile home parks, self-service situations, and mobile homes. The text specifies that a notice need not describe the contents of trunks or of mobile homes that are locked in a manner deterring immediate access.
