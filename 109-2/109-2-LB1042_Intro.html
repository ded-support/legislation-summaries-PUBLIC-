Summary

LB1042 amends Nebraska’s Uninsured and Underinsured Motorist Insurance Coverage Act (sections 44‑6410 and 44‑6411). It expressly bars stacking of UM/UIM limits for vehicles insured under the same policy or under separate policies held by the same person or by related persons residing in the same household, while permitting stacking for separate policies held by different policyholders who are not related household members. Recovery when entitled to multiple such policies is capped at the highest single policy limit for policies held by the same person or related household members. The bill clarifies payment priority (coverage on the occupied vehicle is primary; exhausted primary makes other UM/UIM excess) and a three‑tier order of priority among applicable policies. No state agencies or departments are named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that uninsured/underinsured motorist (UM/UIM) limits for two or more vehicles insured under separate policies may be added together/stacked to determine available coverage for an injured person, regardless of policy language, when the policies are held by different policyholders who are not related persons residing in the same household. It also inserts the qualifier ", if applicable" after the phrase referencing policies held by the same person or related household members. No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
