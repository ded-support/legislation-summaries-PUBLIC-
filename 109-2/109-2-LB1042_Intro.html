Summary

LB1042 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 44-6410 and 44-6411 (Uninsured and Underinsured Motorist Insurance Coverage Act) to clarify stacking and priority rules. Key changes: (1) bars stacking of UM/UIM limits for two or more vehicles insured under the same policy or under separate policies held by the same person or by related persons residing in the same household; (2) expressly permits stacking between separate policyholders who are not related and do not reside together; (3) caps recovery for household-related multiple policies at the highest single policy limit; and (4) clarifies payment priority when multiple policies apply (occupied vehicle first, pedestrian claim next, then other noninvolved-vehicle coverage; and occupied nonowned vehicle coverage is primary). No state agencies or departments are referenced and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a mandatory “stacking” rule for uninsured/underinsured motorist (UM/UIM) limits: regardless of policy language, limits from two or more vehicles insured under separate policies held by different policyholders who are not related and do not reside together may be combined to determine the available coverage for any one accident. The text also inserts clarifying phrasing (“if applicable” and “In the event an injured person is entitled to coverage under more than one policy”). No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
