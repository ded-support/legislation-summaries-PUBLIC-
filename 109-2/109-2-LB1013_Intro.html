Summary

LB1013 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §83-107.01 concerning institutions under the supervision of the Department of Health and Human Services. It lists the official DHHS-run institutions (Beatrice State Developmental Center; Lincoln and Norfolk Regional Centers; Youth Rehabilitation and Treatment Center–Kearney) and requires that, while DHHS operates YRTC–Kearney, it be a single-sex treatment center (boys or girls, not both simultaneously). The department must ensure safe and appropriate gender separation at any youth rehabilitation and treatment facility. In an emergency (excluding inadequate staffing), DHHS may house both genders at YRTC–Kearney, YRTC–Geneva, or another compliant facility for up to seven days with gender separation. The bill also directs that DHHS-run institutions providing juvenile inpatient/subacute substance abuse or behavioral-health residential treatment not delay necessary or court-ordered treatment. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
