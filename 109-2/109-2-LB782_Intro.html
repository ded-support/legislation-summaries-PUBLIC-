Summary

LB782 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §9-648 to require counties, cities, and villages that conduct lotteries to submit a quarterly tax equal to one percent of gross proceeds to "the department," remitted within 30 days on department forms. The department forwards collections to the State Treasurer for credit to the Charitable Gaming Operations Fund. Deficiencies accrue interest and penalties as provided in the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967. The bill repeals the original section, sets an operative date of July 1, 2026, and includes an emergency clause declaring the act effective upon passage.
