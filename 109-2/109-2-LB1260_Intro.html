Summary

LB1260 revises administration and allocation of motor vehicle taxes and fees.

- The department (administrative agency responsible for motor vehicle tax/fee notices) continues to determine taxes/fees and deliver notices.

- County treasurers: retain 1% of motor vehicle tax proceeds prior to 7/1/2028 and 2% thereafter; retain 2% of motor vehicle fees for costs.

- State Treasurer: receives a transfer (credit) to the Vehicle Title and Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund — 1% before 7/1/2028 and 1.2% on/after that date; receives remaining motor vehicle fees into a newly created Motor Vehicle Fee Fund and makes quarterly distributions (74% to counties, 26% to municipalities in proportions matching the Highway Allocation Fund). Motor Vehicle Fee Fund receipts are restricted to road/bridge/street purposes and count as local matchable revenue.

- Allocations of remaining motor vehicle tax proceeds among counties, local school systems, and cities/villages are restructured with different percentage splits before and after 7/1/2028.

- Beginning October 2028, the department will calculate, and the State Treasurer will pay within five business days, a quarterly motor vehicle tax supplement to each local school system equal to 0.48% of the motor vehicle tax allocated to that system. The Legislature intends to appropriate annually beginning FY2028-29 amounts sufficient to pay these supplements.

- Section affecting school funding: the Department of Education’s local system formula resources (TEEOSA) explicitly include motor vehicle tax and motor vehicle tax supplement receipts.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- County treasurers: retention of motor vehicle tax proceeds increases from 1% to 2% for payments on/after July 1, 2028.

- State Treasurer: will receive a transfer to the Vehicle Title and Registration System Replacement and Maintenance Cash Fund equal to 1% before July 1, 2028 and 1.2% on/after that date. The State Treasurer must pay motor vehicle tax supplements to local school systems within five business days of receiving the department’s report.

- The department (responsible for calculating) must quarterly compute, beginning October 2028, a motor vehicle tax supplement equal to 0.48% of the prior three months’ motor vehicle tax allocated to each local school system and report amounts to the State Treasurer.

- Allocation of remaining proceeds (effective on/after July 1, 2028) is set by district type: metro city (26% county / 48% school / 26% city), other city/village (31.5% county / 48% school / 20.5% city/village), or non‑municipal (52% county / 48% school).

- Legislature intends to annually appropriate sufficient funds beginning FY 2028–29 to pay these supplements.
