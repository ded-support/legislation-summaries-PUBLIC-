Summary

This bill amends FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27 appropriations for the Department of Labor and the Department of Economic Development.

Department of Labor (Agency 23)

- Division of Employment: salary limit increases from $23,368,597 to $23,873,143. Workforce development state aid: $9,883,387 (FY25‑26) and $9,880,259 (FY26‑27). Administration: $116,613 (FY25‑26) and $119,741 (FY26‑27). Additional state aid: $1,600,000 (FY25‑26); FY26‑27 amount redacted. Federal state aid: $7,904,743 each year. Federal Sec. 903(d)/(f) funds of $2,500,000 annually for Employment Security/public employment offices. Unexpended workforce development Cash Fund balance reappropriated.

- Division for Protection of People and Property: General Fund $764,157 (FY25‑26), $0 (FY26‑27); federal estimates $822,851 and $828,634.

Department of Economic Development (Agency 72)

- Business Incentives: General Fund $2,460,522 each year; Cash Funds $54,000,000 (FY25‑26) and $50,000,000 (FY26‑27). Includes $50,000,000 from Nebraska Transformational Project Fund each year as state aid. ImagiNE Revolving Loan Fund: $4,000,000 (FY25‑26), $0 (FY26‑27). Unexpended Cash Fund balance reappropriated. An emergency clause makes the act effective on passage.
