Summary

LB821 amends statutes governing the Nebraska Investment Council (NIC) and the Public Employees Retirement Board (PERB)/Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS). Key agency changes:

- Nebraska Investment Council: requires an independent analysis of investment returns for each PERB-administered retirement system (waivable if assets < $1M) and an annual report (due April 10) on portfolios, strategies, officer duties/limitations, organizational structure and that analysis; report to be presented to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee.

- Public Employees Retirement Board / NPERS: eliminates certain employer-verification and written plan-of-action provisions; requires an annual report (due April 10) on funding policy, administrative costs/fees by fund/plan, member education, director duties/limitations, organizational and internal control structure; present to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee.

- Fiscal and administrative provisions: retirement-system expenses generally paid from investment income; directs the State Treasurer to transfer funds for (a) the Legislative Council Retirement Study Fund and (b) Class V School Employees Retirement payment/processing funds; PERB may bill employers for Class V transfer work and related costs. Other existing duties (actuarial procurement via DAS materiel division, compliance audits, hiring attorney/internal auditor) remain. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Nebraska Investment Council, no later than March 31 each year, to prepare an annual report—including the analysis required by subsection (2)—and present that report to the Legislature’s Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee at a public hearing. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
