Summary

LB814 revises property-valuation rules for agricultural and horticultural land and for land receiving special valuation. It sets valuation at 75% of actual (or special) value before Jan 1, 2027—except that school-district taxes levied to pay principal and interest on bonds approved on or after Jan 1, 2022, use a 50% valuation—and at 50% of actual (or special) value on and after Jan 1, 2027. The commission (pursuant to §77-5022) retains authority to adjust class/subclass values in any county or taxing authority so levels fall within acceptable ranges, which are changed for agricultural/horticultural and special-valuation lands to 44–50% on or after Jan 1, 2027 (previously 69–75% with a 44–50% bond exception). No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds phased valuation thresholds effective January 1, 2027: for applicable items the on-or-after-Jan-1-2027 valuation is set at either 50% of actual value or 50% of its special valuation (as defined in §77-1343), and in other clauses at 44–50% of actual value (or 44–50% of special valuation per §77-1343). No government agencies or departments are mentioned, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
