Summary

LB1177 creates the Child Care Cash Fund to be administered by the Department of Health and Human Services. The fund may receive transfers authorized by the Legislature from the General Fund, private gifts, grants or donations, and other legislatively directed funds; monies may be invested by the state investment officer per applicable state law. DHHS shall use the fund for the child care subsidy program (continuity of care, timely provider payments, reimbursement rates, administrative costs, quality improvement and workforce supports, and other legislatively authorized child care uses). The fund may not supplant the state's required match. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate "XXX" annually from the fund. DHHS must submit an electronic annual report to the Legislature detailing amounts credited/expended and specified subsidy application and enrollment metrics.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Child Care Cash Fund, to be administered by the Department of Health and Human Services. The fund may receive Legislative transfers from the General Fund, private gifts/grants/donations, and other Legislature‑directed funds, and may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment law. DHHS may use the fund for the child care subsidy program, including continuity of care, timely provider payments, reimbursement rates to support provider stability and access, administrative costs, quality improvement and workforce supports, and other legislatively authorized child‑care uses. The fund may not supplant the state's required match for the subsidy. The Legislature intends an annual appropriation from the fund (amount shown as XXX). DHHS must submit an annual electronic report detailing receipts, expenditures, and monthly application/eligibility/service metrics.
