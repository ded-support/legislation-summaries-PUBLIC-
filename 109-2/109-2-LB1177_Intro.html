Summary

Creates the Child Care Cash Fund to be administered by the Department of Health and Human Services. The fund may consist of legislative transfers from the General Fund, private gifts/grants/donations, and other funds as directed; available balances may be invested by the state investment officer. DHHS is to use the fund for the child care subsidy program (continuity of care, timely provider payments, adequate reimbursement rates, administrative costs, quality and workforce supports, and other legislatively authorized child-care uses). The fund may not supplant the state's required match. The Legislature intends to appropriate "XXX" annually from the fund. DHHS must submit an annual electronic report detailing amounts credited/expended, monthly families/children served by eligibility level/reason, monthly application counts (received/accepted/denied), denial reasons, and pending applications.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill creates the Child Care Cash Fund to be administered by the Department of Health and Human Services. The fund may receive Legislature-authorized transfers from the General Fund, private gifts, grants or donations, and other legislatively directed funds; available balances may be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and State Funds Investment Act. DHHS may use the fund for the child care subsidy program, including continuity of care, timely provider payments, reimbursement rates to support provider stability and access, necessary administrative costs, quality improvement and workforce supports, and other legislatively authorized child care uses. The fund may not supplant the state's required match. The Legislature intends to appropriate an annual amount (placeholder XXX) from the fund. DHHS must submit an electronic annual report detailing receipts/expenditures, monthly families/children served (by income/eligibility reason), applications received/accepted/denied (with denial reasons), and pending applications.
