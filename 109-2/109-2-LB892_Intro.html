Summary

LB892 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §38-1716 (Massage Therapy Practice Act). It reiterates that massage therapy establishments must be licensed by the department and that the department may not issue or renew a license until statutory requirements are met. The bill clarifies that engaging in massage therapy outside a licensed establishment is prohibited except when specifically permitted by the board. The bill repeals the original section. No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced or changed. Agencies affected: the department responsible for licensure under the Massage Therapy Practice Act and the board that may grant exceptions for off-site practice.
