Summary

The bill assigns roles and new fiscal authorities to three agencies. Department of Economic Development (DED) — led by the Director — administers Grow the Good Life and ImagiNE programs, may contract with the Tax Commissioner, jointly adopt rules with the Department of Revenue (DOR), and must jointly report annually (accrual basis) with DOR. DED manages the Site and Building Development Fund (≥40% to nonmetropolitan areas; generally 100% matching required; subaccount for inland ports; may award specified grants including a $2,000,000 city grant) and the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund (receives a 0.5% administrative fee). Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner) processes credits/refunds (ImagiNE refunds within 180 days), audits/recovers erroneous amounts, administers Nebraska Advantage, and houses the Convention Center financing board. Department of Labor provides wage data, calculates statewide average hourly wage for eligibility, and operates a $300,000 grant program for workforce retention after ownership changes. Key fiscal items: $5,000 Grow the Good Life application fee (to Nebraska Incentives Fund); wage‑retention credits capped $5M/year ($50M total); Nebraska Advantage fees $1,000–$10,000 (and $90,000 election fee for certain Tier 6); Defense Efforts credits capped $4M/year ($40M total).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED)

- The Director of Economic Development reviews/approves applications, executes agreements with employers, may set terms, and must include provisions allowing the Department of Revenue to verify headquarters and employment. DED may access Tax Commissioner records, contract with the Tax Commissioner for services, jointly adopt rules with DOR, and co‑author an annual fiscal (GASB) report to the Legislature.

Department of Revenue (DOR)

- DOR administers credit claims (forms), may disclose agreement/credit information to acquiring taxpayers as needed, may waive recapture for unavoidable circumstances, and will jointly report with DED. Credits reduce income tax liability and may be applied against employer withholding.

Department of Labor (DOL)

- Must establish a grant program (to economic development organizations) capped at $300,000 total to support workforce retention/attraction; grants awarded within ten years after the ownership change and prioritized for largest employers.

Funding/allocations

- $5,000 nonrefundable application fee to the Nebraska Incentives Fund; wage retention credit caps: $5 million per year and $50 million total statewide.
