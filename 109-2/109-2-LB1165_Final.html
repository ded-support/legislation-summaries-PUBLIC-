Summary

The bill assigns roles and funding rules to the Department of Economic Development (DED), Department of Labor (DOL), and Department of Revenue (DOR)/Tax Commissioner. DED administers the Grow the Good Life Act, the ImagiNE Nebraska Cash Fund (0.5% of incentives remitted to the State Treasurer), and the Site and Building Development Fund (may finance loans/grants/credit enhancements and department expenses as appropriated). Applicants generally must provide 100% matching funds; at least 40% of Site and Building funds must serve nonmetropolitan areas. Key funding caps: $5,000 nonrefundable application fee credited to the Nebraska Incentives Fund; wage‑retention credits capped at $5 million annually and $50 million total. DOL’s Office of Labor Market Information sets the statewide average hourly wage and must run a workforce grant program (award cap $300,000) and provide employment/wage data on request. The Tax Commissioner issues rules/forms, certifies exemptions, processes refunds (within 180 days), audits eligibility, and may recover erroneous refunds/credits (deficiency determinations within three years). DED may contract with the Tax Commissioner; DED and DOR must jointly file a GASB‑compliant annual report and appear at a joint legislative hearing. The Convention Center board is administratively and budgetarily part of DOR.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED)

- DED (the Director) administers applications, approves/denies within 30 days, executes agreements with employers, and must include provisions allowing the Department of Revenue to verify HQ location and employment thresholds.

- DED may access Tax Commissioner records, contract with the Tax Commissioner for support services, jointly adopt rules with the Department of Revenue and Governor, and must jointly file an annual accrual-basis report with DOR by Oct. 31 and appear at a Dec. 15 joint legislative hearing. Reports list agreements, parties, and biennial, agreement-specific credit usage (with confidentiality protections).

Department of Revenue (DOR)

- Tax Commissioner administers credit claims/forms, withholding offsets, recapture (treated as tax underpayment with lien/interest), and may waive recapture for unavoidable circumstances.

- DOR may disclose agreement/credit info to acquiring taxpayers as needed and share records with DED. Application fee ($5,000) is remitted to State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Incentives Fund. Wage-retention credit caps: $5M/year and $50M total.

Department of Labor (DOL)

- Must create a grant program for economic development organizations to support employer retention/relocation plans; total grants capped at $300,000 and awarded within ten years of the ownership change, with prioritization by employer size if funds are limited.
