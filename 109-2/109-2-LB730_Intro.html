Summary

LB730 requires public governing bodies and state agencies to designate restrooms and locker rooms by sex and restrict use to the designated sex. Specifically:

- Governing bodies of public schools must mark each group restroom as “for use by females,” “for use by males,” “single‑occupancy,” or “for families,” and prohibit males from female restrooms and vice versa, subject to limited exceptions (custodial/maintenance, emergency assistance, coaches/authorized staff, and parents/caregivers accompanying minors or persons with disabilities).

- Governing bodies of public postsecondary institutions must adopt analogous restroom/locker‑room policies and nonretaliation provisions for good‑faith reporters.

- Each state agency must designate locker rooms in state‑owned facilities by sex, follow similar access restrictions and exceptions, and, in rulemaking, enforcement, and administrative adjudication, define an individual’s sex as either male or female.

The bill contains no explicit funding, appropriation, or budget provisions. ADA protections for persons with differences in sex development are preserved.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for: public school governing bodies, public postsecondary governing bodies, and state agencies. They must designate restrooms/locker rooms (female, male, single-occupancy/family), adopt implementing policies, and prohibit opposite-sex use of facilities in most circumstances, with narrow exceptions (custodial/maintenance, emergencies, authorized staff taking steps to avoid exposure, parents/caregivers, and ADA‑protected accommodations for persons with disorders/differences of sex development). Governing bodies must include visitor conduct rules and prohibit retaliation for good‑faith reporting. It directs every state agency, in rulemaking, enforcement, and adjudication, to define sex as either male or female. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
