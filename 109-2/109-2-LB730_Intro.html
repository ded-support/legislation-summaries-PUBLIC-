Summary

LB730 requires state and educational entities to classify and control access to restrooms and locker rooms by sex.

- Public schools: governing bodies must post designation (for females, for males, single-occupancy, or family) at each group restroom, adopt implementing policies (including visitor conduct), prohibit opposite-sex use with limited exceptions (maintenance, emergencies, ADA-covered differences in sex development, parental/caregiver situations), and prohibit retaliation against good‑faith reporters.

- Public postsecondary institutions: governing bodies must similarly designate restrooms and locker rooms, adopt policies, apply the same exceptions and nonretaliation protections.

- State agencies: must designate locker rooms in state-owned facilities and follow the same use and exception rules. Additionally, every state agency must define an individual's sex as either male or female for administrative rulemaking, enforcement, and adjudication.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for public K–12 school and public postsecondary institution governing bodies to post restroom/locker‑room designations (for females, for males, single‑occupancy, or family) and to adopt implementing policies. Bars allowing persons of one sex to use facilities designated for the opposite sex, with limited exceptions (custodial/maintenance, emergencies, coaches/authorized staff, and parental/caregiver assistance; ADA accommodations for individuals with differences of sex development). Requires each state agency to designate locker rooms in state‑owned facilities similarly and prohibits cross‑sex use with similar exceptions. Directs state administrative bodies to define an individual's sex as either male or female in rulemaking, enforcement, and adjudication. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
