Summary

LB998 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to expand state income tax exclusions for members of the Nebraska National Guard. For taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2025 and before Jan. 1, 2027, members may exclude 100% of income from specified 32 U.S.C. duty statuses (e.g., drills, annual training, active guard/reserve, operational support), employment as 32 U.S.C. federal dual‑status technicians with the Nebraska National Guard, and service in state active duty. For taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2027, the exclusion is extended to income from service in either 10 U.S.C. or 32 U.S.C. military duty status, dual‑status technician employment, and state active duty. The Tax Commissioner’s statutory role in income tax administration remains. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a tax exclusion for members of the Nebraska National Guard. For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2027, income that is included in federal adjusted gross income and received from (1) service in a 10 U.S.C. or 32 U.S.C. military duty status while a Nebraska National Guard member, (2) employment as a 32 U.S.C. federal dual‑status technician with the Nebraska National Guard, or (3) service in state active duty may be 100% excluded. References the Internal Revenue Code. No agencies are allocated funding and no appropriations or budget changes are specified.
