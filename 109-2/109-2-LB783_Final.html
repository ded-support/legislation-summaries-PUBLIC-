Summary

LB783 adopts the Uniform Assignment for Benefit of Creditors Act and amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §76-902 to exempt assignments made under that Act from the documentary stamp tax. The amendment retains existing trust-transfer exemptions and requires the register of deeds be presented a signed certification; supporting evidence for that exemption must be maintained by the certifier and be available for inspection by the Department of Revenue. The bill contains no changes to appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting the Department of Revenue:

- Assignees are required to "comply with all requirements of the Internal Revenue Service and state and local taxing authorities" (i.e., state Department of Revenue rules) when administering an assignment.

- Post‑assignment taxes incurred by the assignee are expressly listed as administrative costs payable from the assignment estate and are paid before many creditor distributions.

- Claims with federal priority (e.g., 31 U.S.C. 3713) are addressed in the payout order; state tax claims are treated through the assignee’s compliance and payment duties above.

No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to the Department of Revenue are created by the added text.
