Summary

LB952 (2026) restructures court-related funds and funding authorities. Key provisions:

- Creates the Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund, administered by the State Court Administrator, to support court and probation programs; eligible receipts include fees, grants, donations, and contractual payments from political subdivisions. Funds are to be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment law.

- Clarifies the Commission on Public Advocacy Operations Cash Fund will consist of remittances under §33-156 and is intended to be the commission’s sole funding source; investments follow state law.

- Creates then terminates the Supreme Court Reports Cash Fund and the Court Appointed Special Advocate Fund, directing the State Treasurer to transfer remaining balances to the General Fund; the latter section terminates Jan 1, 2027.

- Repeals several statutory sections related to these programs. Emergency clause makes the act effective on passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund," to be administered by the State Court Administrator (Judicial Branch). The fund will finance court and probation programs that enhance justice administration and community safety, and will be funded by fees, grants, donations, gifts, and payments from contractual agreements with political subdivisions. Funds available for investment are to be managed by the State Investment Officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. On the act's operative date the fund terminates and the State Treasurer must transfer any remaining balance to the General Fund. The section sunsets January 1, 2027.
