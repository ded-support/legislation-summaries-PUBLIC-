Summary

The bill imposes a remittance transfer excise tax and assigns the Department of Revenue responsibility for its administration. Remittance transfer providers must collect the tax from senders and remit it to the Department of Revenue quarterly. The Department may adopt rules and forms, establish an expedited refund process for military members/dependents charged in error, accept electronic funds transfers for collections and refunds, and share tax-related information with the Department of Banking and Finance. All taxes received by the Department of Revenue are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. Upon request by the Department of Revenue, the Department of Banking and Finance may claim against a licensee’s surety bond to recover unpaid tax.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions assign the Department of Revenue (DOR) responsibility to collect and administer a new remittance-transfer excise tax: remittance providers must remit tax quarterly to DOR, and DOR treats the tax like sales/use tax under §§77-2707 to 77-2711. DOR may adopt rules/forms, use electronic funds transfer for collection/refunds, and share related information with the Department of Banking and Finance (DBF). DOR must establish an expedited refund process for active-duty military or dependents erroneously charged (proof of military ID required); ordinary refunds follow existing sales-tax claim deadlines. All taxes received are credited to the State Treasurer for the General Fund. No specific appropriations, budget increases, or dedicated funding for DOR are included. DOR may request DBF to claim against a licensee’s surety bond for unpaid tax.
