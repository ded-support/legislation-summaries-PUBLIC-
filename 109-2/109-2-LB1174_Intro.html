Summary

Key provisions referring to the Department of Revenue in LB1174:

- Remittance transfer providers must collect the imposed remittance-transfer tax and remit it quarterly to the Department of Revenue.

- The tax is administered under sales/use tax procedures (sections 77-2707 to 77-2711).

- The Department of Revenue may share tax-related information with the Department of Banking and Finance and may request that department pursue claims against a licensee’s surety bond to recover unpaid tax.

- The Department must establish an expedited refund process for active-duty military members/dependents erroneously charged, accept proof of military status, adopt rules/forms for payment/ reporting, and may use electronic funds transfer for collections/refunds.

- All receipts are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. No direct appropriations to the Department of Revenue are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a remittance-transfer excise tax administered by the Department of Revenue (Dept.). Key Dept. duties and authorities:

- Collect remittance-transfer tax (licensees remit quarterly to Dept.); enforcement and procedures governed by existing sales/use tax statutes (sections 77-2707 to 77-2711).

- Share tax-related information with the Department of Banking and Finance and, at Banking’s request, enable claims against a licensee’s surety bond for unpaid tax.

- Establish an expedited refund process and accept proof of military status for exempt active-duty members/dependents; adopt rules, forms, and permit electronic funds transfers and filings.

- All tax receipts go to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund.

No direct appropriations or new Dept. budget allocations are specified.
