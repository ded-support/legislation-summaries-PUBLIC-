Summary

The bill amends the Medical Assistance Act to (1) explicitly define "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and (2) restrict the department’s authority to submit any 1915(c) waiver or amendment that would introduce an individual cost limit, reduce the aggregate number of service hours, or narrow eligibility criteria. Such waiver submissions require prior legislative approval. The bill also clarifies that "waiver" refers to applications submitted by the department to the federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to obtain legislative approval before submitting any 1915(c) HCBS waiver application or amendment that would (1) introduce an individual cost limit, (2) reduce the aggregate number of service hours, or (3) narrow eligibility criteria. It also defines "nursing facility level of care" as a Medicaid recipient needing assistance with two or more activities of daily living who may receive services in a community setting provided the waiver program remains cost‑neutral. No explicit changes to appropriations or new funding/budget allocations are included.
