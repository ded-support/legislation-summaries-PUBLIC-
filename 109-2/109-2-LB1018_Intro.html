Summary

LB1018 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §49-1446.02 (Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act) to restate and clarify the list of expenditures committees are prohibited from making. Prohibited items include: clothing and medical/dental expenses for candidates/their immediate families; automobile installment payments; mortgage or rent for a candidate’s permanent residence; satisfaction of personal debts (except reportable campaign loans under §49-1456(2)); personal services (except reportable campaign services under §49-1455); office supplies/staff/furnishings for the public office (with an exception tied to §49-1446.01(2)); and, rules limiting cross‑payment between candidate and ballot‑question committees. The bill repeals the original section. No specific state agencies or departments are named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a ballot question committee may act to influence voters regarding nomination or election of a candidate, and that a candidate committee may provide goods, materials, services, or facilities for a ballot question committee — specifically allowing a candidate committee to pay for broadcast ads produced by the ballot question committee (to obtain lower advertising rates). Clarifies this does not bar a candidate or candidate committee from expressing positions on ballot questions, initiatives, or referenda. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no appropriations, funding, or budget allocation changes.
