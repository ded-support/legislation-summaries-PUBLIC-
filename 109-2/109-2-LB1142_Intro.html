Summary

- Designates the Department of Economic Development (DED) as the agency to administer the Nebraska Visitors Development Act (defines "Department" as DED). DED will appoint a Director of Tourism, locate and staff the Nebraska Tourism Commission, absorb commission employees, adopt rules, run grant programs, and administer the State Visitors Promotion Cash Fund, the Nebraska Tourism Promotional Cash Fund, and the Tourism Conference Cash Fund.

- Requires DED to comply with Department of Administrative Services (DAS) procurement rules for contracts and references the DAS budget division's budget administrator in directing a $1,000,000 transfer from the State Visitors Promotion Cash Fund to the General Fund on or before June 30, 2019.

- Directs the Department of Transportation to erect and maintain highway tourism markers and determine their exact locations (with public safety consideration).

- Fiscal provisions: 10% of the cash-device tax is allocated to the Nebraska Tourism Promotional Cash Fund; beginning October 1, 2024, investment earnings on the State Visitors Promotion Cash Fund are credited to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Economic Development (DED): Adds/clarifies that DED will administer the Nebraska Visitors Development Act and establishes a Director of Tourism within DED. DED may employ staff and contract for services; procure and evaluate data; develop/approve state marketing campaigns; run a promotional/technical-assistance/state-aid program for local governments and the tourism industry; cooperate with public and private partners; and adopt rules to implement the Act. The Director of Economic Development appoints the Director of Tourism (salary set by the Director of Economic Development; serves at pleasure). For administrative purposes the commission will be located and staffed within DED.
