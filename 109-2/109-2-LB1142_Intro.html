Summary

LB1142 transfers administration of the Nebraska Visitors Development Act to the Department of Economic Development (DED). DED will appoint a Director of Tourism, staff the Nebraska Tourism Commission, absorb commission employees, run grant programs, and administer these funds: State Visitors Promotion Cash Fund, Nebraska Tourism Promotional Cash Fund, and Tourism Conference Cash Fund. Revenue from vendor/retailer sales and 10% of the cash-device net‑operating‑revenue tax are credited to the Nebraska Tourism Promotional Cash Fund. The State Visitors Promotion Cash Fund may be used in addition to General Fund appropriations; investment earnings of that fund are credited to the General Fund beginning Oct. 1, 2024, and a $1,000,000 transfer (directed by the budget administrator of the Department of Administrative Services) is referenced. DED contracts are subject to the State Procurement Act and Department of Administrative Services rules. The Department of Transportation will erect and maintain highway tourism markers and determine their locations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED)

- Establishes DED as administrator of the Nebraska Visitors Development Act and creates a Director of Tourism position appointed by the Director of Economic Development (serving at that director’s pleasure; salary fixed by the Director of Economic Development).

- Gives DED authority to hire staff and contract for services, procure and evaluate data, develop/approve state marketing campaigns, run a program providing promotional services, technical assistance and state aid to local governments and the tourism industry, cooperate with other governments and private entities, and adopt rules to implement the Act.

- States the tourism commission will be located and staffed within DED and directs active coordination/partnerships with other state agencies.

Department of Administrative Services and Department of Transportation

- No provisions, funding changes, or appropriations affecting DAS or DOT are included.
