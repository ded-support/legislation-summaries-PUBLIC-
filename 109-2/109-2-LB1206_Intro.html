Summary

LB1206 (2026) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to exempt from Nebraska state income taxation income received by certificated teachers, paraeducators, and paraprofessionals (paraprofessional defined by reference to 20 U.S.C. §7011 and including paraeducators; private schools also referenced). The statute retains the Tax Commissioner’s rulemaking authority for tax modifications. The bill text cites existing state programs/funds in §77-2716, including the Nebraska educational savings plan trust (secs. 77-1415–77-1430), the Achieving a Better Life Experience program (secs. 77-1401–77-1409), the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund, the Medical Debt Relief Fund, and provisions relating to the Nebraska National Guard. Federal agencies referenced include the U.S. Department of Defense and the U.S. Office of Personnel Management for retirement reporting. No appropriations, funding transfers, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions: “Paraprofessional” is defined by reference to federal law (20 U.S.C. §7011, as it existed on Jan 1, 2026) and expressly includes paraeducators. “Private school” is defined to mean any private, denominational, or parochial school offering elementary or high‑school instruction. No specific executive agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
