Summary

LB1206 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to exempt from state income tax income received by certificated teachers, paraeducators, and paraprofessionals. The Tax Commissioner (Nebraska Department of Revenue) is the implementing authority for modified federal adjusted gross income computations under the amended section. The bill references several state-administered programs and funds that affect income tax treatment, including the Nebraska Educational Savings Plan Trust, the achieving a better life experience (ABLE) program, the Give to Enable Support Cash Fund, the Nebraska long-term care savings plan, the Medical Debt Relief Fund, and the Teach in Nebraska Today Act. It also references the Nebraska National Guard and federal agencies that report retirement income (U.S. Department of Defense and U.S. Office of Personnel Management). No changes to appropriations, state funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines two terms: "paraprofessional" adopts the meaning in federal statute 20 U.S.C. 7011 as it existed on Jan. 1, 2026 (explicitly including paraeducators), and "private school" covers private, denominational, or parochial elementary or high schools. No government agencies or departments are directly named; the provision references a federal education statute (20 U.S.C. 7011). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
