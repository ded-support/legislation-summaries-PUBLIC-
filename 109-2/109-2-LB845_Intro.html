Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Support Act and requires DHHS to provide staff and support to the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council and to collaborate with the council on related activities.

- DHHS is directed to develop a comprehensive Olmstead-related strategic plan for services to qualified persons with disabilities in collaboration with the Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Transportation, Department of Veterans' Affairs, Department of Correctional Services, State Department of Education, University of Nebraska, and the Equal Opportunity Commission. The DHHS chief executive shall convene the planning team and stakeholder advisory committee and submit the plan and periodic reports to the Legislature.

- The State Advisory Committee on Mental Health and Substance Use Services will include three DHHS representatives (mental health, social services, Medicaid) and one representative from the Housing Office, Community and Rural Development Division of the Department of Economic Development.

- The bill creates the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council Fund to receive federal funds, grants, and gifts; funds are remitted to the State Treasurer and are subject to applicable investment and expenditure conditions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council Fund" to receive federal funds, grants, and gifts for purposes of the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Support Act. Funds must be remitted to the State Treasurer, expenditures are subject to any donor/award conditions, and idle balances may be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.
