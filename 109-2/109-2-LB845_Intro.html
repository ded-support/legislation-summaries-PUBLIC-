Summary

LB845 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to rename/create and staff the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council, provide council support, and compensate members for expenses per state law. DHHS must submit periodic State Alzheimer's Plan updates and may, with the council's advice, terminate funding to an area agency on aging by disapproving its area plan. The bill creates the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council Fund to receive federal funds, grants, and gifts (remitted to the State Treasurer; expenditures subject to conditions). DHHS is also required to develop an Olmstead-related strategic plan in collaboration with the Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Transportation, and Department of Veterans' Affairs. The State Advisory Committee on Mental Health and Substance Use Services includes one representative from the Housing Office of the Community and Rural Development Division of the Department of Economic Development.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds creation of the "Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council Fund." The fund will consist of federal funds, grants, and gifts for purposes authorized by the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Support Act; expenditures are subject to any donor/award conditions. Receipts are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the fund, and available money is invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.
