Summary

LB1007 (2026) amends the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act. It (1) bars landlords from including nondisclosure provisions that prohibit tenants from disclosing the contents of a rental agreement and makes such provisions unenforceable (with tenants able to recover actual damages and reasonable attorney fees for deliberate use); and (2) prohibits a landlord from retaining prepaid rent or a security deposit if the landlord received it prior to entering a rental agreement and the prospective tenant declines to sign, requiring prompt return and preserving existing remedies (court costs, attorney fees, and, if willful, liquidated damages up to one month's rent or twice the deposit, whichever is less). The bill preserves the existing security-deposit cap (one month’s rent, limited pet deposit) but exempts housing agencies organized under the Nebraska Housing Agency Act. It requires returned-but-unclaimed deposits to be reported to the State Treasurer under the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Adds an item (e) stating a landlord “agrees not to disclose the contents of the rental agreement.” It also adds a new provision requiring a landlord who receives prepaid rent or a security deposit before a rental agreement is formed to return those funds if the prospective tenant declines to enter the agreement.
