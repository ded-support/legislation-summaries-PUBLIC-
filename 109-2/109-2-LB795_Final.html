Summary

LB795 adds agency-specific provisions to Nebraska criminal law. It requires persons convicted under the controlled substances provision (amendment to §28-416), if sentenced to the Department of Correctional Services, to attend appropriate drug treatment and counseling. It explicitly lists "a Department of Health and Human Services employee" as a protected person, making assault on a DHHS employee a covered offense under the Public Protection Act (amendment to §28-1354) and extending second-degree (§28-930), third-degree (§28-931), and motor-vehicle assault (§28-931.01) protections to DHHS employees. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for either agency are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions create quantity-based felony tiers for fentanyl offenses: 140+ grams — Class IB felony; 28–<140 grams — Class IC felony; 10–<28 grams — Class ID felony. "Fentanyl" is defined broadly to include fentanyl, its isomers/esters/ethers, their salts, and related Schedule I substances. No provisions in the added text reference the Department of Correctional Services or the Department of Health and Human Services, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes included.
