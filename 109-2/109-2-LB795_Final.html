Summary

LB795 amends controlled‑substance statutes and contains two agency-specific provisions. Section 2 (amending §28‑416) requires any person convicted under that section who is sentenced to the Department of Correctional Services to attend appropriate drug‑abuse treatment and counseling. Section 3 (amending §28‑1354) adds Department of Health and Human Services employees to the list of protected persons for racketeering and expressly brings assaults on DHHS employees — including first-, second-, and third‑degree assault and motor‑vehicle assault (see §§28‑930, 28‑931, 28‑931.01) — within enhanced offense protections. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language creates specific fentanyl quantity thresholds and felony classifications: possession or distribution of fentanyl (broadly defined to include isomers, esters, ethers, salts, and related Schedule I substances) is a Class IB felony at 140+ grams, Class IC at ≥28 to <140 grams, and Class ID at ≥10 to <28 grams. No sections reference the Department of Health and Human Services or provide new programmatic duties. Implications for the Department of Correctional Services: potential for increased incarceration and supervision workload due to elevated felony penalties, but the bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for DOC or DHHS.
