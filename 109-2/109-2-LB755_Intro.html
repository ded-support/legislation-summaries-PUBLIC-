Summary

LB755 (Emergency Service Peer Support Act) authorizes Nebraska "emergency service agencies" — defined to include law enforcement agencies, fire departments, emergency medical services, correctional facilities, hospitals, dispatch centers, rescue services, and other emergency response entities — to establish or approve peer support programs and approve/train peers (who may be current, former, or other approved individuals). Agencies must develop program policies, may discipline peers who violate confidentiality, and may refer employees internally or to programs of other agencies; participation cannot be mandated. Communications in peer support sessions are privileged and exempt from public disclosure under sections 84-712 to 84-712.09, with specified exceptions. The bill provides civil immunity for peers acting in good faith. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Emergency Service Peer Support Act establishing privileged, confidential peer‑support programs for employees of "emergency service agencies" (defined to include law enforcement agencies, fire departments, emergency medical services, correctional facilities, hospitals, dispatch centers, rescue services, and similar emergency‑response entities). Communications in peer support sessions are inadmissible in civil, criminal, administrative, or arbitration proceedings; peers and program staff cannot be compelled to testify or produce records. Records are exempt from disclosure under sections 84‑712 to 84‑712.09. Exceptions to confidentiality include threats of future suicide/homicide/felonious assault, legally mandated reports (e.g., domestic violence, sexual assault, child/elder abuse), admissions of criminal conduct, or admissions indicating refusal to protect others. Agencies must adopt program policies and discipline peers who violate confidentiality; agencies may cross‑refer employees to other agencies' programs and participation is voluntary. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.
