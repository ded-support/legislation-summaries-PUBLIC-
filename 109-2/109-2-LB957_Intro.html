Summary

LB957 amends the unlawful membership recruitment statute (section 28-1351) to explicitly include assault against a Department of Health and Human Services employee (first, second, or third degree, and motor-vehicle assault under section 28-931.01) among the predicate offenses that can support a charge of unlawful membership recruitment. The bill does not contain any appropriations, funding, or budgetary provisions affecting the Department of Health and Human Services.

Summary (Department of Health and Human Services implications)

- The added provisions impose strict civil liability on a parent, guardian, or their spouse for all damages if a child under 14 knowingly possesses a handgun or handgun ammunition (subject to an existing statutory exception).

- Creates a new criminal offense when a person stores or leaves a loaded firearm unsecured, knows a child under 14 may access it, the child gains access without parental consent, and the child displays or uses the firearm in public or to cause injury or death. Exceptions: unlawful entry by another person and statutorily justified use. Penalty: Class I misdemeanor.
