Summary

LB725 amends the State Electrical Act, affecting the State Electrical Board and the State Electrical Division. Key agency-related changes:

- The State Electrical Board retains authority to set examination, licensing, registration, and inspection fees by unanimous vote; fees must cover costs incurred by the State Electrical Division and the board and avoid unnecessary surpluses.

- The board must annually file fee reports with the Attorney General and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst (the LFA report must be submitted electronically).

- All license/registration applications must include the applicant’s Social Security number; licenses expire December 31 of even-numbered years with specified renewal windows and a 10% late renewal penalty.

- Inspection procedures: requests must be submitted to the board as prescribed; inspections scheduled within one week; inspection fees due at or before commencement and forwarded with the request; local jurisdictions with ordinances may be exempt from state inspection fees.

- Clarifies exemptions for municipal/public utility employees and pump contractors under other Acts.
