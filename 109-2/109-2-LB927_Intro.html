Summary

LB927 amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to define “foreign national” and “preliminary activity” and to add new reporting, certification, and prohibition requirements affecting ballot question committees and independent-expenditure filers.

Agencies and entities referenced:

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (referred to as “the commission”): must provide independent-expenditure report forms, receive campaign and independent-expenditure filings, assess late‑filing fees, enforce fines for prohibited foreign‑national contributions/expenditures, and may adopt rules to implement the provisions.

- Secretary of State: petition filing dates with the Secretary of State trigger campaign‑statement filing schedules for ballot question committees.

- Courts and state or local government entities: investigations and court actions must shield lawful donors’ identities; courts and government entities are restricted from collecting donor identity information except as directly related to alleged violations.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and restrictions on foreign nationals regarding ballot-question activity. Key agency/authority items:

- Refers to "the commission" (under the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act) with authority to adopt rules and regulations to implement the section.

- Requires ballot-question committees and treasurers to obtain donor affirmations and file certifications (including that no preliminary activity was funded by foreign nationals and that donors have not accepted >$100,000 from foreign nationals in the prior four years); independent-expenditure reports require similar certifications.

- Establishes prohibitions on foreign nationals making, soliciting, directing, or participating in contributions/expenditures related to ballot questions.

- Prescribes civil fines (committee: prohibited amount or $100,000, whichever greater; foreign national: prohibited amount plus $100,000).

- Directs that investigations under the Act shield lawful donors and bars state/local government entities or courts from collecting donor identities for tax-exempt organizations except when directly related to an alleged violation.

No funding or appropriations changes.
