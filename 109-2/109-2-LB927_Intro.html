Summary

LB927 amends the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to add definitions for "foreign national" and "preliminary activity" and to strengthen disclosure and prohibition requirements related to ballot-question activity. Key government actors and changes:

- Secretary of State: petition filing dates remain the triggers for ballot-question committee reporting schedules.

- Commission (Nebraska political accountability commission authority): receives statements of organization, campaign and independent-expenditure reports; may adopt rules; must receive certifications from treasurers/donors that contributions did not originate with foreign nationals or aggregate over $100,000 from foreign nationals in the prior four years; independent-expenditure reports (>$250) must include the same certification.

- Courts and state/local government entities: investigations must shield lawful donor identities; courts/entities may not collect donor identities except when directly related to an alleged violation.

- Enforcement: bans foreign-national contributions/expenditures for ballot questions, permits certain U.S. subsidiaries under conditions, and imposes fines (prohibited amount or $100,000 minimum; foreign nationals fined amount plus $100,000).

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions (including “foreign national” and “preliminary activity”) and bars foreign nationals from making, soliciting, directing, or controlling contributions or expenditures related to ballot questions. Requires ballot question committees and independent-expenditure filers to certify donors are not foreign nationals and that they (and donors) have not received over $100,000 from foreign nationals in the prior four years. Treasurers must obtain donor affirmations on receipt of contributions. Enforcement: committees face a fine equal to the prohibited contribution or $100,000, whichever is greater; foreign nationals face the prohibited amount plus $100,000. The commission is authorized to adopt rules to implement the section. Investigations or court actions under the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act must shield lawful donors’ identities, and no state or local government entity, court, or officer may collect donor identities except when directly related to an alleged violation.
