Summary

LB1031 requires the Legislature to appropriate $1,500,000 from the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for FY2025‑26 and $1,500,000 for FY2026‑27. The funds are earmarked to DHHS’s medical assistance program (Medicaid) to reimburse non‑hospital mental health providers for behavioral health services to beneficiaries dually eligible for Medicaid and Medicare. For dual eligibles, the higher of the Medicaid or Medicare rate must be paid for behavioral health services. The bill also states legislative intent to sustain Medicaid rates for those mental health providers serving dually eligible participants.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Legislature directs a transfer of $1,500,000 in FY2025‑26 and $1,500,000 in FY2026‑27 from the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to the Department of Health and Human Services for the medical assistance program. Funds are to reimburse non‑hospital mental health providers who deliver behavioral health services to Medicaid‑eligible, dual‑eligible (Medicaid and Medicare) beneficiaries. For dual‑eligible recipients, the higher of the Medicaid or Medicare rate must be paid. The Legislature also states its intent to sustain Medicaid rates for these mental health providers.
