Summary

LB822 references the Department of Labor in amended section 81-8,317: In‑the‑Line‑of‑Duty Compensation is set at $250,000 for 2022 and, for 2023 and thereafter, is indexed annually by the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers as published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics (12‑month period ending June 30). Separately, the bill authorizes mayor and council to levy, within the limits of section 77-3442, to provide required amounts to pay or fund Cities of the First Class police pension plans. No other Department of Labor references or explicit appropriations changes appear.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions amend Police Officers Retirement Act rules for "cities of the first class," clarifying definitions (e.g., tenured police/officer/firefighter), disability (complete inability while in the line of duty), retirement eligibility and benefit treatment (including purchased annuity contracts), portability on qualifying employment changes between cities, and procedures for terminated/retired officers. They set retirement-system governance (a six‑member committee with four police members and city‑selected members), require annual reports (chairperson by June 30) and periodic reviews, and declare the subject a matter of statewide concern. No language refers to or changes the Department of Labor, and there are no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations included.
