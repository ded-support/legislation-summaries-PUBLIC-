Summary

LB1185 enacts the "Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act." It assigns enforcement authority to the Nebraska Attorney General, who may bring civil actions on behalf of the State or aggrieved persons against operators of conversational AI services. Available remedies include equitable or declaratory relief, actual damages, recovery of litigation expenses (court costs, reasonable attorney fees, investigative and deposition costs), and civil penalties of at least $1,000 per violation, capped at $500,000 per operator. The bill states no private right of action and shields a model developer from liability for violations committed by a third-party operator. Operative date: July 1, 2027. No appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes are included.
