Summary

LB1185 enacts the Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act and vests enforcement authority in the Nebraska Attorney General to bring civil actions on behalf of the State or aggrieved persons against operators of conversational AI services. Available remedies include equitable and declaratory relief, actual damages, recovery of costs and attorney fees, and civil penalties of at least $1,000 per violation (capped at $500,000 per operator). The bill bars a private right of action and provides that developers of AI models are not liable for violations by third‑party operators. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included. Operative date: July 1, 2027.
