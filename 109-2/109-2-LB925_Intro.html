Summary

LB925 contains no explicit reference to the Department of Health and Human Services. Therefore the bill does not assign duties to DHHS nor does it change any DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

(For context only: the bill assigns enforcement/notification duties to an unspecified "department" related to suspension and pro-rata forfeiture of state funds to political subdivisions, but that entity is not identified as the Department of Health and Human Services.)

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions give the Department of Health and Human Services (the “department”) authority to determine when a political subdivision is violating the bill’s anti-camping/related rules and to notify the State Treasurer of noncompliance. Upon that notice the State Treasurer must suspend distribution of state funds to the subdivision; the subdivision forfeits a daily pro‑rata share of awarded state funds (award amount divided by days in the funding period). Suspension continues until DHHS certifies compliance. Any suspended funds held six months or longer are forfeited to the General Fund. No new appropriations or budget increases for DHHS are included.
