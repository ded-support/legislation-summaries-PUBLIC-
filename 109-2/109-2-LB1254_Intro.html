Summary

Bill LB1254 amends the Nebraska Criminal Code (section 28-101) to add a new offense: it defines "electronic smoking device" and "nicotine analogue" (cross-referencing section 28-1418.01) and makes it unlawful to sell at retail, offer for retail sale, or distribute for retail promotion in Nebraska any electronic smoking device manufactured on or after the act's effective date that lacks a required "child safety feature." Violations are a Class IV misdemeanor. Exemptions include interstate transport and warehouse/distribution center storage. The bill repeals the original section 28-101. No state agencies are directed to implement the measure and there are no changes to funding or appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for "electronic smoking device" (devices delivering nicotine/tobacco via heating/electronic means) and references "nicotine analogue" per section 28-1418.01. Prohibits retail sale, offering for retail sale, or retail distribution/promotion in the state of any such device manufactured on or after the act's effective date unless it includes a child safety feature. Violations are a Class IV misdemeanor. Exempts interstate transportation and storage in warehouses/distribution centers. No government agencies or departments are referenced and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
