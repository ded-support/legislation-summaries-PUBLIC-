Summary

LB833 amends statutes governing the Nebraska State Colleges and related oversight. Key agencies/departments:

- Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges: retains authority to confer BA/BS and must approve certain new graduate offerings; Chadron State and Wayne State may independently award an MBA subject to Board approval.

- University of Nebraska: retains sole responsibility for specialist and master’s graduate programs and all baccalaureate professional programs (with statutory exceptions), and primary statewide responsibility for instruction, research, and public service in agriculture and natural resources.

- Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education: must authorize/approve any state-college master’s or doctoral programs (including doctoral education degrees) upon demonstration of compelling need.

Chadron, Peru, and Wayne State Colleges must prioritize new baccalaureate programs to meet rural and regional needs. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified. Original sections amended are repealed.

Adds language directing that programs and doctoral-level development be prioritized based on the needs of rural Nebraska, with emphasis on doctoral degrees in education.
