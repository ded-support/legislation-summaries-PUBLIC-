Summary

The bill creates the Community Reinvestment Fund, to be administered by the (unnamed) department and used for grants for childcare/early childhood development, community development initiatives, healthcare, healthy food access, homeless services, and re‑entry housing. The fund consists of transfers under section 4(3), bequests, donations, gifts, and grants, and any funds available for investment shall be managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. Beginning January 1, 2028, the department, in consultation with the Department of Health and Human Services, will administer an annual grant program with specified award proportions: childcare/early childhood services—15% of grants; community development initiatives—15%; federally qualified health centers—30%.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development (DED)

- Requires covered businesses to file annual community reinvestment reports by April 1 starting 2028; DED will publish weighted ratings (Outstanding/Satisfactory, Needs to Improve, Noncompliance).

- Outstanding/Satisfactory businesses get website recognition, eligibility preference for state façade improvement grants administered by DED, and consideration in local licensing; Needs to Improve triggers a DED technical assistance plan.

- Noncompliance (failure to file or material misstatement) = fines up to $10,000 per location initially and up to $50,000 for subsequent violations; administrative hearings and appeals follow the Administrative Procedure Act.

Funding/appropriations

- Creates the Community Reinvestment Fund, administered by DED, funded by transfers under sec. 4(3) of the act plus bequests, donations, gifts, and grants; monies may be invested by the state investment officer.

- Beginning Jan 1, 2028, DED (in consultation with DHHS) will award annual grants with specified allocations (example percentages listed: childcare/early childhood 15%, community development 15%, federally qualified health centers 30%).
