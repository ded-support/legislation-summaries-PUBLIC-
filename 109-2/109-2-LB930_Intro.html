Summary

LB930 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77‑2716 to add a new subtraction (new subsection (27)) effective for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2027. Qualified retirees—those employed full time as firefighters or certified law‑enforcement officers for at least 20 years and age 60 or older—may reduce federal adjusted gross income by annual retirement benefits, up to $100,000 per year, to the extent those amounts are included in federal AGI. The statute (in other provisions) references the Tax Commissioner, the Nebraska National Guard, the Nebraska Educational Savings Plan Trust, the Firefighter Cancer Benefits Act, and federal agencies such as the U.S. Department of Defense and the U.S. Office of Personnel Management. LB930 makes no changes to agency responsibilities and contains no appropriations, funding, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state tax exclusion (referencing the Internal Revenue Code of 1986) effective for taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2027: retirees who served full time as firefighters or certified law‑enforcement officers for at least 20 years and are age 60+ may reduce their federal adjusted gross income by annual retirement benefits—to the extent included in AGI—up to $100,000 per year. No agencies receive new funding or appropriation changes.
