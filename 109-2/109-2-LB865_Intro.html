Summary

This bill amends sections 77-2701 and 77-2701.04 of the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967 and adds a new section creating a sales-and-use tax exemption for a three-day annual holiday (12:01 a.m. last Friday of July through 11:59 p.m. Sunday) for: (1) child care supplies, (2) clothing/footwear, and (3) school supplies, each item priced $100 or less. The bill enumerates qualifying child care and school supply items and specifies exclusions and administration rules (items used in a trade or business, cell phones, allocation of discounts in mixed transactions, rain checks, exchange/return procedures, shipping/backorder treatment, seller time zone for timing, and a 60-day documentation requirement for refunds). It becomes operative January 1, 2027, and repeals the original sections. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statewide sales-tax holiday: sales and use tax is waived from 12:01 a.m. on the last Friday of July through 11:59 p.m. that Sunday for qualifying items priced $100 or less—child care supplies, clothing/footwear, and a long enumerated list of school supplies. Defines eligible child care and school items, excludes business-use items and cell phones, and sets rules on discount allocation, single-unit articles, rain checks, exchanges, and returns (requires proof of tax paid for refunds within 60 days). The seller’s time zone controls timing. No state agencies or departments are referenced and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
