Summary

This bill amends the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967 (sections 77-2701 and 77-2701.04) to create a sales-and-use tax holiday. Between 12:01 a.m. on the last Friday of July and 11:59 p.m. the following Sunday, sales taxes are exempted on child care supplies, clothing/footwear, and school supplies priced at $100 or less per item. The bill lists qualifying and excluded items, sets rules for discounts, rain checks, exchanges, returns, and time-zone determinations, and repeals the original sections. Operative date: January 1, 2027. No state agency funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or provided in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a sales-tax holiday: sales and use taxes are waived for specified child care, clothing/footwear, and school supply items (each ≤ $100) sold between 12:01 a.m. on the last Friday of July and 11:59 p.m. Sunday of that weekend. Detailed lists define “child care supplies” and “school supplies.” Exclusions and rules cover items for trade/business, cell phones, single-unit articles, discount allocation for mixed transactions, rain checks, exchanges/returns (including a 60‑day proof-of-tax rule), and seller‑location time zone for timing. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.
