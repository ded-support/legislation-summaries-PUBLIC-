Summary

LB843 directs the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to maximize federal SNAP options and funding, minimize General Fund use, hire necessary staff, and submit an annual December 1 report to the Legislature detailing options considered, actions taken, caseload, and costs. DHHS must develop a USDA Food and Nutrition Service–compliant state outreach plan, may accept gifts/grants/donations, partner with counties or nonprofits, request USDA matching funds, and may be exempt from implementing the plan if private or federal funds are insufficient. DHHS shall create a TANF‑funded program/policy to expand categorical eligibility (including raising gross income eligibility to 165% of OMB guidelines and eliminating most asset limits) contingent on available federal participation funds. The Department of Labor administers the SNAP Next Step Program in partnership with DHHS (with legislative intent that Labor not use General Funds). DHHS must submit an annual SNAP waiver to USDA by October 1, 2026, to waive refugee/immigrant eligibility changes.
