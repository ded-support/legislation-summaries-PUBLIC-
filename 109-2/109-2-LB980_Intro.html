Summary

LB980 amends Nebraska’s Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act to allow waiver of the right to a jury trial in rental agreements and to revise procedural rules for landlord possession actions. Courts: summons content is expanded; summary-judgment procedures and court-initiated summary judgment are authorized; courts may award court costs and reasonable attorney’s fees to the prevailing party in jury trials and sanction bad-faith affidavits. Clerks of court: courts may require defendants to deposit accrued rent with the clerk when a jury trial continuance is requested. Law enforcement: writs of restitution may direct the constable or sheriff to restore possession within ten days. No agencies receive new funding or appropriations. Original sections repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No government agencies or executive departments are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Substantive additions address court procedures: parties may waive jury trial with specified notices; bench trials must be scheduled 10–14 days after summons while jury trials are set as soon as practicable; courts may require defendants to deposit accrued rent if they request a continuance of a jury trial. The bill expands summary-judgment rules (timing, required evidentiary citations, court authority to treat facts as undisputed, sua sponte consideration, and sanctions including fee-shifting and contempt). Courts may award court costs and reasonable attorney fees to prevailing parties in jury trials.
