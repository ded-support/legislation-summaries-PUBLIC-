Summary

LB980 amends Nebraska’s Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act to: allow contractual waiver of the right to a jury trial; require summonses in possession actions to notify defendants of jury-rights and potential recovery of court costs and reasonable attorney fees; shorten service/return timing; permit courts to schedule expedited bench trials (10–14 days) or promptly set jury trials and to require defendants who delay jury trials to deposit accruing rent with the court clerk. The bill expands summary‑judgment procedures (including court‑initiated consideration), authorizes sanctions and fee awards for bad‑faith filings, and directs writs of restitution to constables or sheriffs. It requires compliance with the Disposition of Personal Property Landlord and Tenant Act. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural rules governing possession actions: permits contractual waiver of jury trial with specified tenant notices; requires bench trials be held 10–14 days after summons while jury trials be scheduled as practicable; allows courts to require defendants seeking continuances to deposit accruing rent with the court clerk. Substantively expands summary-judgment procedure (timing, required supporting materials, treatment of disputed facts, court-initiated consideration) and authorizes courts to sanction bad-faith affidavits by awarding court costs and reasonable attorney fees or other sanctions. Agencies mentioned are courts and the clerk of the court. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
