Summary

LB911 assigns two duties to the Department of Health and Human Services. The department must (1) establish clinical review protocols for applied behavior analysis (ABA) services under the Behavior Analyst Practice Act that exceed 20 hours per week per client or 12 consecutive months of ongoing authorization without documented functional progress, to ensure continuing medical necessity and prevent overutilization; and (2) adopt and promulgate rules specifying when dual licensing arrangements, waivers, or exemptions are necessary for ABA programs that operate extended‑day, summer, or wraparound care components. The bill contains no appropriations or funding changes for the department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services to: (1) establish clinical review protocols for applied behavior analysis (ABA) services that exceed 20 hours/week per client or continue 12 consecutive months without documented functional progress, to confirm continuing medical necessity and prevent overutilization; and (2) adopt rules specifying when dual licensing, waivers, or exemptions are required for ABA programs that run extended‑day, summer, or wraparound care. It also limits when ABA providers must obtain child‑care licensure. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
