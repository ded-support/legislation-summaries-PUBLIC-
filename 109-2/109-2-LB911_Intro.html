Summary

The bill directs the Department of Health and Human Services to: (1) establish clinical review protocols for applied behavior analysis (ABA) services that exceed either 20 hours/week per client or 12 consecutive months of authorization without documented functional progress, to ensure continuing medical necessity and prevent overutilization; and (2) adopt and promulgate rules specifying when dual licensing, waivers, or exemptions are required for ABA programs that operate extended-day, summer, or wraparound care components. No provisions in the bill amend, appropriate, or allocate funding to the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS duties: establish clinical review protocols for applied behavior analysis (ABA) when services exceed 20 direct service hours/week per client or when authorization continues 12+ months without documented functional progress, to ensure continuing medical necessity and prevent overutilization. DHHS must also adopt and promulgate rules specifying when dual licensing, waivers, or exemptions are required for ABA programs that operate extended‑day, summer, or wraparound care. The bill exempts pure medically‑necessary ABA from child‑care licensure unless it provides naps/meals/nonclinical recreation or >10 cumulative nontherapeutic hours/week per child. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
