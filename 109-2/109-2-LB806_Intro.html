Summary

The bill amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §81-12,147 to direct the Department of Economic Development to use the Site and Building Development Fund to finance loans, grants, subsidies, credit enhancements and other financial assistance for industrial site and building development and to cover department administration expenses as appropriated by the Legislature. It adds: (1)(m) authorization for a grant to a city of the first class in a county under 50,000 population affected by a sudden, significant private‑sector closure or downsizing to mitigate economic impacts via building or infrastructure improvements; (2) continued use of a subaccount to assist inland port authorities; and an intent to appropriate "XX dollars" from the General Fund to implement (1)(m).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new grant program (subdivision (m)) providing grants to cities of the first class in counties with under 50,000 residents that are hit by a sudden, significant private‑sector closure or downsizing. Grants are limited to necessary improvements to buildings or infrastructure tied to the closed/downsized private entity to mitigate local economic harm. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate "XX dollars" from the General Fund to implement this grant program. If the Department of Economic Development is the administering agency, this creates a new program responsibility and would require receiving/managing the specified General Fund appropriation and disbursing grants to eligible cities.
