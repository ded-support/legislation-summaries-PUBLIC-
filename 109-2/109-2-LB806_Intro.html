Summary

The amendment directs the Department of Economic Development (DED) to use the Site and Building Development Fund to finance loans, grants, subsidies, credit enhancements and other financial assistance for industrial site and building development and to cover department expenses as appropriated by the Legislature. DED is further authorized to use the fund’s subaccount to provide financial assistance to inland port authorities for shovel‑ready commercial and industrial sites. The bill adds an explicit DED-eligible grant (subdivision (1)(m)) for a city of the first class in a county under 50,000 impacted by a sudden, significant private‑sector closure to mitigate economic impacts via building or infrastructure improvements. The Legislature expresses intent to appropriate "XX dollars" from the General Fund to implement subdivision (1)(m).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new grant category: eligible cities of the first class located in counties under 50,000 population that suffer a sudden, significant private‑sector closure or downsizing may receive grants to mitigate economic harm by making necessary improvements to buildings or infrastructure tied to the closed/downsized entity. The Legislature states its intent to appropriate "XX dollars" from the General Fund to carry out this grant. For the Department of Economic Development, this creates a new grant program obligation (subject to assignment in the bill) and an intended General Fund appropriation (amount unspecified) to be administered or allocated through its budget.
