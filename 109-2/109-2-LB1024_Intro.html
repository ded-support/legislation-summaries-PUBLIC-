Summary

LB1024 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§79-724 and 79-760.01 to require instruction on the history of communism and to assign specific duties to education authorities. The State Board of Education must adopt measurable social studies standards (including financial literacy, Holocaust/recognized genocides, and — by January 1, 2027 — instruction on the history of communism) and review standards every seven years. Each school board must annually appoint a three-member committee on American civics to hold public meetings, approve and make curricula accessible, ensure alignment with state standards (including required civics assessments or approved alternatives), and report findings to the board. Superintendents and school boards are held directly responsible for compliance. The bill repeals the original sections; it contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that, by January 1, 2027, instruction on the history of communism must be provided as required by section 1 of the act. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and the amendment includes no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
