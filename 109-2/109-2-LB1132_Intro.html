Summary

The bill amends duties of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) under the Medical Assistance Act, including deadlines and funding directives:

- By Oct. 1, 2026, DHHS must submit a state plan amendment to CMS to cover FDA‑approved long‑acting injectable and extended‑release medications for serious mental illness or substance use disorder administered in hospitals, EDs, or inpatient settings (including state psychiatric facilities).

- Other DHHS directives (with deadlines) include coverage expansions for continuous glucose monitors, postpartum extension, medical respite (waiver/SPAs due Oct. 1, 2025; two facilities designated), electric personal breast pumps and ten lactation consults (by Jan. 1, 2025), translation/interpretation services (by Jan. 1, 2024), nurse home‑visiting targeted case management (seek SPA by Oct. 1, 2025), and psychology services by advanced‑level practitioners (by Jan. 1, 2026).

- Funding: multiple provisions direct use of the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund; a $600,000 annual appropriation cap for a CGM expansion and an intent that lactation consults be funded at a 145% rate increase.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must, by October 1, 2026, submit a state plan amendment to CMS to secure Medicaid coverage for FDA‑approved long‑acting injectable and extended‑release medications when given for serious mental illness or substance use disorder in hospitals, emergency departments, or other inpatient settings (including state hospitals and state psychiatric facilities). No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are specified.
