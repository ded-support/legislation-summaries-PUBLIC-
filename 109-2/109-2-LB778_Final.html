Summary

LB778 amends eligibility and award rules under the Civic and Community Center Financing Act. It bars municipalities that have applied for/received grants under the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act (and, for cities of the primary class, any that received such grants) and municipalities that received Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act funding from receiving Civic and Community Center grants. For July 1, 2027–June 30, 2028, the administering department must (1) restrict eligibility to municipalities not barred by those provisions, (2) give preference to municipalities partnered with a certified creative district, and (3) may award grants in amounts set in coordination with the Nebraska Arts Council, which shall set a minimum grant of $100,000. Tribal governments remain eligible to apply. No direct appropriations are specified.
