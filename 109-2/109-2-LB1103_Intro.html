Summary

LB1103 amends the Nebraska State Patrol Retirement Act. It raises the mandatory retirement age for state patrol officers to 65 and updates the deferred retirement option plan (DROP). Key agency/finance provisions:

- DROP eligibility requires attainment of normal retirement age and 25 years of service; DROP deposits are held in member-directed defined contribution accounts under IRC §414(k) and subject to §415 limits.

- While in DROP, members stop making required contributions; the Nebraska State Patrol is not assessed the employer contribution under §81-2017(2) and no such amount is credited to the State Patrol Retirement Fund.

- DROP investments are directed by members; the retirement system, the state, the board, and the state investment officer are not liable for investment results; administrative fees are charged to members' DROP accounts.

- Cost-of-living adjustments are not applied to benefits during the DROP period.

No new appropriations are created.
