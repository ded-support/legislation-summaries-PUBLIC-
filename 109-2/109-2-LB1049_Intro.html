Summary

LB1049 requires two administrative changes affecting state agencies:

- Legislative Fiscal Analyst: Must prepare and submit an economic impact report, along with existing fiscal notes, for any bill that would trigger regulatory action by a state agency. Reports must include a cost‑benefit analysis (direct and indirect costs/benefits; estimated compliance costs; numbers and types of affected businesses/localities; impacts on private property and state expenditures, including administrative expenses; key assumptions and uncertainties). State agencies are required to cooperate and provide relevant information.

- Secretary of State: Continues duties to maintain and publish the Nebraska Administrative Code, remit related fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the Secretary of State Cash Fund, and must now annually submit to the Clerk of the Legislature an agency‑indexed report listing the total number of new and amended rules and regulations adopted that year.

No appropriations, new funding, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for the Legislative Fiscal Analyst (LFA) to prepare and submit an economic impact report, along with fiscal notes, to the appropriate legislative standing committee for any bill that would require regulatory action by an agency (per §84-901). The report must include a cost‑benefit analysis covering direct benefits, societal cost savings, number and identity of affected businesses/localities, compliance costs, impacts on private property and state expenditures (including administrative expenses), indirect costs, and key assumptions. State agencies must cooperate by providing relevant information. The LFA must also file an annual, agency-indexed report with the Clerk of the Legislature listing totals of new and amended rules adopted that year. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.
