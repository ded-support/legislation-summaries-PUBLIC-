Summary

LB797 amends municipal contracting law (sections 16-321 and 17-568.01). It revises obligations of city and village engineers and expressly authorizes boards of public works, when so authorized by the mayor and governing body, to use their engineering staff, hire consultants, and purchase materials and labor for system extensions and improvements. The bill raises the contract-price thresholds that require city council or village board approval and formal advertising for bids and codifies tiered no-bid thresholds for municipal electric utilities based on gross annual retail sales. Agencies affected include city engineers, village engineers, boards of public works, mayors, city councils, and village boards of trustees. No statewide appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds tiered dollar limits tied to municipal electric utilities' size: up to $500,000 where a utility's gross annual retail sales exceed $20 million; up to $750,000 where sales exceed $30 million; and up to $1,000,000 where sales exceed $75 million. The only government entity referenced is the municipal electric utility. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no explicit appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes included.
