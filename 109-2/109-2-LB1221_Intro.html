Summary

LB1221 amends section 68‑901 of the Medical Assistance Act and requires "the department" to adopt definitions to implement community engagement and work requirements for individuals experiencing short‑term hardship under the federal One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Pub. L. 119‑21). Specified definitions: "extended period of time" (overnight stays, repeated multi‑trip travel, or sustained absences that interfere with requirements); "inpatient hospital services" (per 42 C.F.R. 412.3, deemed to begin on date of admission and including related outpatient/observation care); "outside of the individual's community" (travel outside the county of residence); and "serious or complex medical condition" (per 42 C.F.R. 440.315). The original section 68‑901 is repealed. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires "the department" (acting to implement/administer the federal One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Pub. L. 119-21) to adopt four specific definitions for applying community‑engagement or work‑requirement exemptions for short‑term hardship events: (1) "Extended period of time" — one or more overnight stays, repeated travel of two+ trips, or sustained absence that reasonably interferes with requirements; (2) "Inpatient hospital services" — inpatient admission per 42 C.F.R. 412.3, deemed to begin at admission and to include related outpatient/observation/diagnostic/recovery/follow‑up care; (3) "Outside of the individual's community" — travel outside the individual's county of residence; and (4) "Serious or complex medical condition" — conditions listed in 42 C.F.R. 440.315, including medically necessary Medicaid coverage. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
