Summary

- Sec. 10 creates the Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund, to be administered by the Department of Health and Human Services for programming and facilities providing juvenile mental health services; fund balances may be invested by the state investment officer pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.

- Sec. 11 amends public-records law to (a) exempt security-related standards, procedures, policies, plans, specifications, diagrams, access lists, and other security-related records of the Lottery Division of the Department of Revenue from public disclosure (while preserving disclosure of contract payments, prize amounts, and most prize-winner names/locations), and (b) allow the Department of Health and Human Services to redact personally identifiable information in vital event records.
