Summary

Creates the Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund, to be administered by the Department of Health and Human Services for programming and facilities providing juvenile mental health services; fund balances may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment acts. Amends public-records law to (1) permit the Department of Health and Human Services to redact personally identifiable information in vital event records, and (2) continue to allow the Lottery Division of the Department of Revenue (and its contractors) to withhold security-related records, while explicitly prohibiting withholding of amounts paid to contractors, prize amounts, and prize-winner names for awards under $250,000. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting agencies:

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Creates the Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund, to be administered by DHHS for programming and facilities providing juvenile mental health services. Funds available for investment will be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.
