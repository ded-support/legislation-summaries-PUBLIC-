Summary

LB876 amends section 29-4315 to require that, upon an initial interaction arising from a sexual assault, the Department of Health and Human Services (for victims under 18) shall provide information explaining victims' rights under the Sexual Assault Victims' Bill of Rights Act and related law. The information must be presented in clear (fifth-grade) language, be accessible to persons with visual disabilities, and available in all major languages spoken in Nebraska. Required content includes that a victim need not participate in the criminal justice system or undergo an evidentiary exam to retain rights, contact information for domestic violence/sexual assault professionals and advocates, available state and federal victim relief, and law enforcement protections. The bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes affecting the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Immediate Protection from Abuse Act," authorizing law enforcement to issue standardized immediate protective orders when arresting someone for a domestic- or sexual‑assault offense and there are reasonable grounds of threat. Orders must use a specified form, list grounds, protected persons, expiration (automatic at 11:59 p.m. three days after issuance), service, filing with the district court clerk, and entry into court electronic case management and protection/ restraining-order databases. Presiding judges review orders and may dissolve or modify only upon clear and convincing evidence; violations carry criminal penalties (first offense Class I misdemeanor; subsequent offenses Class IV felony). No provisions assign duties to, affect, or provide funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services.
