Summary

LB1104 assigns explicit investigation and enforcement roles to the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and the Department of Revenue (DOR). Both agencies may determine that a vehicle or trailer kept >30 days in Nebraska has situs in-state and that a Nebraska resident is avoiding required taxes or fees, based on factors such as purchase, use, ownership affiliation, insurance coverage, or other evidence. Such determinations create a rebuttable presumption that the resident is the actual owner, must register the vehicle in Nebraska, and owes motor-vehicle taxes/fees and applicable sales or use tax. DMV or DOR must notify the presumed owner to register and pay within 30 days. Appeals: DMV appeals go to the Director of Motor Vehicles (hearing officer; Administrative Procedure Act; costs may be assessed); DOR appeals proceed under section 77-2709. Unpaid amounts incur a 50% late fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Highway Trust Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions authorize the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and Department of Revenue (DOR) to determine that a motor vehicle or trailer has been kept >30 days in Nebraska and thus has situs here, based on specified nexus factors (purchase, operation/storage, ownership affiliation, insurance coverage, or other evidence). Such determinations create a rebuttable presumption that a Nebraska resident is the owner, must register the vehicle, and owes motor-vehicle taxes/fees and sales/use tax. DMV or DOR must notify the presumed owner and require compliance within 30 days. DMV appeals go to the Director of Motor Vehicles (hearing officer, APA procedures); DOR appeals follow section 77-2709. Unpaid amounts incur a 50% late fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Highway Trust Fund. Existing statutory penalties also apply.
