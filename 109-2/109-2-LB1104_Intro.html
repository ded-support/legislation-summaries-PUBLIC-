Summary

LB1104 authorizes the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and the Department of Revenue (DOR) to determine that a vehicle or trailer kept in Nebraska more than 30 days has situs here and that a resident owner is avoiding required taxes/fees. Specified factors (e.g., Nebraska initial purchaser, operation/storage in-state, insurance coverage, resident affiliation with nominal owner) support a rebuttable presumption that the resident must register the vehicle and pay motor vehicle taxes/fees and sales/use tax. DMV or DOR must notify the presumed owner, who has 30 days to pay or appeal (DMV appeals to the Director with a hearing officer under the Administrative Procedure Act; DOR appeals under §77‑2709). Unpaid amounts incur a 50% late fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Highway Trust Fund; other penalties in §§60‑3,164 and 60‑3,170 apply.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates authority for the Department of Motor Vehicles or Department of Revenue to deem a vehicle or trailer kept >30 days in Nebraska and establish situs based on specified ties to a Nebraska resident. Such a determination creates a rebuttable presumption that the resident is the owner, must register the vehicle in Nebraska, and owes motor vehicle taxes, fees, and sales/use tax. The agency making the determination must notify the resident, who has 30 days to pay or appeal. DMV appeals go to the Director of Motor Vehicles (hearing officer; APA procedures); Revenue appeals follow section 77-2709. Unpaid amounts incur a 50% late fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Highway Trust Fund. No other appropriations or budget changes.
