Summary

LB1227 amends state building, energy, and electrical code statutes.

- Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (DWEE): counties, cities, or villages must notify DWEE if they delete portions of specified International Building/Residential Code chapters. Local energy codes may only be adopted after a local determination that the code is "cost efficient" (expected implementation costs < five‑year energy savings), using DWEE energy impact studies. Local authorities must submit analyses to DWEE when waiving Nebraska Energy Code requirements; DWEE reviews and reports findings.

- State Electrical Board / certification: local jurisdictions may provide for inspection of electrical installations by certified electrical inspectors; only certified holders may be appointed, and political subdivisions must provide copies of certificates to the board. State inspection still does not apply to routine maintenance.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original sections amended are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibition on local electrical codes that differ from standards set by the State Electrical Board (per the State Electrical Act), while allowing counties/cities/villages to have certified electrical inspectors perform inspections. Requires counties, cities, and villages to assess and find new local energy codes "cost efficient" before adoption; they may not adopt codes judged not cost-efficient. "Cost efficient" is defined by five‑year energy savings outweighing implementation cost increases, using energy impact studies published by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment. No funding or appropriations changes included.
