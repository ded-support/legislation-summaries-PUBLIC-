Summary

LB1192 amends section 13-2603 of the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act to specify that the Act’s board (Governor, State Treasurer, chair of the Nebraska Investment Council, chair of the Nebraska State Board of Public Accountancy, and an appointed economics professor) "for administrative and budget purposes only, ... shall be considered part of the Department of Revenue." The bill does not appropriate funds to the Department of Revenue or change any Department budget or funding allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the phrase "unless such facility is located in a city of the primary class," creating a location-based exception. The amendment does not reference the Department of Revenue and contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for that agency.
