Summary

LB1027 amends Nebraska electric-generation statutes to remove certain exemptions for privately developed renewable generation and impose new board oversight and preconstruction certification requirements. Key state entities and processes affected:

- The board (and its executive director) gains explicit authority to require preconstruction written notice/certifications for private renewable projects, review hearings, and enforcement (including a $500 fine and orders to cease construction if certifications are not timely).

- Owners must consult the Game and Parks Commission on wildlife/species mitigation.

- Projects within 10 miles of military installations must certify no electronic components from foreign adversaries or certify NERC critical infrastructure protection compliance.

- Owners must submit decommissioning plans and post security (bond or similar) by year six, and enter joint transmission development agreements with the transmission-owner electric supplier; a power purchase agreement with a public power district is required.

- Regional transmission organizations’ planning/construct directives are given consideration.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are enacted.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the owner certify to the board that it has entered a power purchase agreement with a public power district for a privately developed renewable energy facility. After receipt of all required written notices and certifications under subsection (2), the board must hold a hearing to determine compliance with the section and may approve or deny construction. No executive agencies beyond the board and public power districts are named, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
