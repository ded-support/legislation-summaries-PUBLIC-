Summary

LB1166 amends section 79-958 of the School Employees Retirement Act. It ties employee contribution rates for the School Retirement Fund to the fund’s funded ratio (per the annual actuarial valuation prepared for the retirement board under §84-1503) with graduated rates beginning July 1, 2025: <96% = 9.75% of compensation; 96–<98% = 8.75%; 98–<100% = 8.00%; ≥100% = 7.25%. It sets annual calculation and effective-date rules for those rates (transitional rules for 2025–2027, then July 1 calculation with Sept. 1 effectiveness thereafter). Employers must deposit 101% of required employee contributions to the School Retirement Fund and transmit them to the retirement board concurrently with employee contributions. Employer “pickup” of member contributions continues and is treated as employer contributions under federal §414(h)(2). The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Sets effective dates for employee contribution rates calculated July 1 each year:

- 7/1/2025 rate applies 7/1/2025 until before 7/1/2026.

- 7/1/2026 rate applies 7/1/2026 until before 9/1/2027.

- Beginning in 2027, the rate calculated each July 1 takes effect on September 1 of that year and runs until the next September 1.
