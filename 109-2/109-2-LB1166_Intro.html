Summary

Bill LB1166 amends section 79-958 of the School Employees Retirement Act. It ties employee contribution rates to the School Retirement Fund’s funded ratio (per the retirement system’s annual actuarial valuation reported to the retirement board) and establishes tiered employee rates effective July 1, 2025: <96% = 9.75%; 96%–<98% = 8.75%; 98%–<100% = 8.00%; ≥100% = 7.25%. It also specifies annual calculation and implementation dates for those rates (initial schedules for 2025–2027, then July 1 calculations effective each Sept. 1). Employers must deposit 101% of required employee contributions to the School Retirement Fund and transmit deposits alongside employer contributions. Employer pickup of member contributions remains treated as employer contributions under IRC §414(h)(2). The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds timing rules for employee contribution rates: the rate calculated July 1, 2025 applies starting July 1, 2025 until before July 1, 2026; the rate calculated July 1, 2026 applies starting July 1, 2026 until before September 1, 2027; and beginning in 2027 (and each year thereafter) the rate calculated July 1 will apply starting September 1 of that year and remain in effect until the following September 1. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
