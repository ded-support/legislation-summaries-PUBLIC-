Summary

LB893 amends section 79-808 to require the State Department of Education to create and make available an online interpersonal relations training course and accompanying test as an alternative to the current human relations training for applicants for a Nebraska substitute teacher's certificate. The department may charge a fee for the course/test not to exceed the cost of providing them. The State Board (board) may issue a temporary certificate (up to two years) to applicants who have not completed human relations training and may grant a substitute certificate to applicants who either have the required human relations training or have completed and passed the department’s interpersonal relations course and test. Advisory committee members are reimbursed per sections 81-1174–81-1177; districts hiring replacements for such members are reimbursed from the Certification Fund. No new appropriation is specified. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that the board may grant a Nebraska substitute teacher certificate to applicants who either have the required human relations training or who complete and pass an online "interpersonal relations" course and test as determined by the department. The State Department of Education must create and provide that online course and test as an alternative to existing human relations training. The department may charge a fee for the course/test limited to recovering the costs of providing the service. No specific new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
