Summary

LB761 directs the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (department) to revise fee collection and fund allocations. Key actions:

- Creates the Water Quality and Quantity Cash Fund for Clean Water Act (section 402) program costs; department remits application and annual NPDES/pretreatment permit fees (beginning July 1, 2027) to the State Treasurer for credit to this fund. Fees cannot exceed the department's administration or service costs. Permits under section 54-2428 are exempt.

- Hazardous-waste generator and permittee fees must cover—but not exceed—the department's direct and indirect regulatory costs; receipts are credited to the Integrated Solid Waste Management Cash Fund.

- Water well registration fee standardized to $200 per well (plus applicable statutory fees), collected by the department.

- Livestock Waste Management fees must be reviewed annually to ensure they cover 40% of prior-year program costs; receipts credit the Livestock Waste Management Cash Fund.

The bill revises related statutory citations and repeals prior versions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a Water Quality and Quantity Cash Fund and directs "the department" to remit all related fees, gifts, grants, reimbursements, and transfers to the State Treasurer for credit to that fund. The fund may be invested by the state investment officer under existing investment laws and is to pay reasonable direct and indirect costs to develop and administer programs regulating Clean Water Act (section 402) discharges. Beginning July 1, 2027, the department may charge application fees ( capped to reimburse application processing) and annual fees (capped at actual cost of services) for NPDES and pretreatment permits; permits under section 54-2428 are exempt. Also specifies that fees collected under section 81-1505(13)(c) must be used by the department to implement related rules (and to cover hazardous‑waste/solid‑waste regulatory costs).
