Summary

LB761 directs the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment to restructure fee collection and fund flows and creates a new dedicated fund.

Key agency actions and fiscal changes:

- Department to collect increased water‑well registration fees ($200 per well/series/groups as specified) and hazardous‑waste and NPDES/pretreatment permit fees.

- Integrated Solid Waste Management Cash Fund is amended to receive hazardous‑waste fees (per 81‑1505(13)(c)) and forfeited financial‑assurance funds; fees must be used to implement hazardous‑waste rules. Funds are remitted to the State Treasurer and may be invested by the state investment officer.

- The Water Quality and Quantity Cash Fund is created to receive NPDES application and annual permit fees (effective July 1, 2027), gifts, grants, and transfers to fund Clean Water Act (section 402) program costs; livestock permits under §54‑2428 are exempt.

- Livestock Waste Management Cash Fund continues to receive animal‑feeding operation fees; the department must adjust fees annually to cover 40% of program costs and submit a program report reviewed by the Legislature’s Appropriations Committee. Transfers from that fund to the General Fund may be made by the Legislature.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds fee and fund provisions for the state environmental department: fees collected under subdivision (13)(c) of §81-1505 must be used to implement related rules and to cover direct and indirect costs of hazardous-waste regulation. Creates the Water Quality and Quantity Cash Fund; the department must remit all related fees, gifts, grants, reimbursements, and transfers to the State Treasurer for credit to the fund. Money in the fund may be invested by the state investment officer under applicable state investment laws. Beginning July 1, 2027, the department may charge application and annual fees for NPDES and pretreatment permits (capped at actual administrative costs or necessary reimbursement); permits under §54-2428 are exempt.
