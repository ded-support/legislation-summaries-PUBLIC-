Summary

LB1125 amends §85‑422 to specify that any balance in the University of Nebraska Facilities Program exceeding amounts required for debt service (excluding amounts in the trust fund under §85‑421(7)) shall be distributed proportionally between the Board of Regents and the Department of Administrative Services. The split is based on the total amounts contributed to the program by the Board pursuant to §85‑419 and by the state. This distribution applies beginning with the fiscal year starting July 1, 2009, through the fiscal year ending June 30, 2062, on December 31, 2063, or when all related financing obligations are discharged, whichever occurs first.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions require the University of Nebraska to obtain prior legislative (or Executive Board if the Legislature is not in session) approval before acquiring a controlling or sole interest in any hospital or health care facility valued over $100 million, and bar primary-affiliate hospitals/academic health centers of UNMC from being owned or controlled by a for‑profit or out‑of‑state entity unless the Attorney General — in consultation with the Board of Regents — determines it is in the public interest. No Department of Administrative Services responsibilities, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
