Summary

Bill updates the Nebraska Municipal Land Bank Act. Key agency/department items:

- Board composition and appointments: voting members (minimum seven) appointed by a municipality’s chief executive and confirmed by two‑thirds of its governing body; nonvoting members must include the municipality’s planning director (or designee) and a governing‑body appointee. Clerk roles specified for removal hearings.

- Removal authority: for multi‑municipal banks, removal is by the chief executive of the municipality in which the majority of land‑bank property is located, subject to governing‑body review.

- Powers/financial interactions: land banks may borrow from municipalities, the state, federal funds, or private lenders, and procure state/federal insurance or guarantees; bond issuance is prohibited after Nov. 14, 2020. Contracts may be executed with municipal agencies or departments to perform functions on behalf of one another.

- Property rules: jurisdictional holding limits by city class; commercial acquisitions limited (e.g., three years vacancy or failed nuisance abatement); requires community benefits agreements when holding property for private/nonprofit entities over one year.

- Reporting: boards must report to creating municipality after each meeting or quarterly and submit an annual electronic report to the Chair of the Legislature’s Urban Affairs Committee by March 1 with specified financial and property details.

No new appropriations or state budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds residency requirements and reporting and community-benefits conditions for land banks. Key agency references: municipal land banks, the creating municipality, counties, and properties held under agreements with nonprofit or private entities per subsection 18-3408(8). Changes:

- Board membership: each voting member must reside in the creating municipality, within 10 miles, in its extraterritorial zoning jurisdiction, or in the county; a majority must reside in the creating municipality (with location-of-property clarifications where property is held under 18-3408(8)).

- Deposited property held on behalf of others for more than one year may require the depositing entity to enter a community benefits agreement (land bank plus local community groups) committing to specified benefits (e.g., affordable housing, local hiring).

- Reporting: board must provide an electronic report after each meeting or at least quarterly, including whether property is held on behalf of a nonprofit/private entity under 18-3408(8) and the intended use of such property.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
