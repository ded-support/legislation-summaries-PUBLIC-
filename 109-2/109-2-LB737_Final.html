Summary

LB737 amends §81-6,122 to require the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to lead development of a comprehensive Olmstead strategic plan in collaboration with, among others, the Department of Economic Development, Department of Labor, Department of Transportation, and Department of Veterans' Affairs. The DHHS chief executive officer must convene a team to draft the plan, appoint a broad stakeholder advisory committee, retain an independent consultant for analysis, submit the completed plan to the Governor and Legislature, and report progress periodically. The Legislature’s Health & Human Services and Urban Affairs Committees must hold an annual joint public hearing on Olmstead progress (including housing, employment, education, community supports, and transportation) and file findings. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed or specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Health & Human Services and Urban Affairs legislative committees to hold a joint public hearing by October 1 each year to assess Olmstead-plan progress in housing, employment, education, community supports, and transportation, and to submit an electronic report of findings to the Clerk by December 31. Departmental impact: DHHS is the primary subject of oversight; employment and transportation assessments implicate the Departments of Labor and Transportation; Economic Development and Veterans' Affairs may be consulted but are not assigned duties. Adds that DHHS is not required to provide the report referenced in subdivision (2)(d). No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
