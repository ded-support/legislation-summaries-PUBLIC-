Summary

LB905 makes agency‑specific changes across several departments. Department of Agriculture: repeals the Nebraska Potato Development Act and Division of Potato Development, revises potato inspection definitions, and permits termination of compulsory inspection if petitioning acreage meets ≥51% of prior year. Department of Administrative Services: centralizes responsibility for identifying, transferring, maintaining, selling, leasing and disposing of vacant/excess state buildings/land, authorizes easement approvals and donations (with resale proceeds within 10 years remitted to the General Fund), and administers the Vacant Building and Excess Land Cash Fund (with legislative‑authorized transfers to the General Fund) and IT administrative support. Department of Economic Development: must be notified of surplus property for buyer/lessee referral and gains an added Housing Office member on the mental health advisory committee. Department of Health and Human Services: implements standardized foster‑care reimbursement tiers, leads Critical Incident Stress Management, manages the Breast & Cervical Cancer Cash Fund, and receives Bridge to Independence reports. Department of Transportation: creates Highway Cash Fund and Roads Operations Cash Fund with transfers from the Highway Trust Fund and specifies expenditure authority; County Highway/City Street Act receipts are credited to the Highway Cash Fund. Department of Labor: develops multilingual human‑trafficking posters with a toll‑free hotline for specified locations. Information Management Services: clarifies administrator billing; charges go to the Information Management Revolving Fund (expenditures by appropriation) and beginning Oct. 1, 2024 investment earnings on that fund are credited to the General Fund. The act is effective on passage.
