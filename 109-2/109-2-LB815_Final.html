Summary

- Creates/clarifies the Motor Fuel Tax Enforcement and Collection Cash Fund (fund sources include appropriations and transfers under sections 39-2215 and 66-489) and specifies the fund "shall be used exclusively for the costs of the Department of Revenue in carrying out its duties" and for "other related costs for the Department of Agriculture and the Nebraska State Patrol;" transfers to the General Fund allowed by the Legislature.

- Assigns Department of Revenue responsibility to collect and administer the petroleum release remedial action fee, to issue licenses, suspend/cancel noncompliant licenses, and adopt rules.

- Authorizes the Department of Revenue to withhold up to $150,000 per fiscal year for direct collection/admin costs (prorated if partial year); withheld amounts deposit into the Petroleum Release Remedial Action Collection Fund, which is appropriated to the Department of Revenue (transfers to General Fund permitted).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Imposes a new diesel surcharge (0.25 of one cent per gallon) beginning October 1, 2026, on diesel that is (a) indelibly dyed/chemically marked per Treasury regs or (b) >0.05% sulfur or fails a cetane‑index minimum of 40 and is indelibly dyed per EPA regs.

- Revenue treatment: first $140,000 of these receipts each fiscal year go to the Motor Fuel Tax Enforcement and Collection Cash Fund; all remaining receipts go to the Agricultural Alcohol Fuel Tax Fund (remitted to the State Treasurer). The Department of Revenue would administer/collect and remit these taxes (not explicitly stated but implied).

- Declares Nebraska Ethanol Board the state agency to promote ethanol and tightens board composition to require multiple members be in‑state ethanol producers (appointments and term transition dates specified beginning September 1, 2026).

- No direct appropriations or new departmental budget line items for the Departments of Agriculture or Revenue are specified.
