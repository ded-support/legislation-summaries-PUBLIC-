Summary

LB815 provisions referencing the Department of Revenue and Department of Agriculture:

- Creates a 0.25¢/gallon tax on certain dyed/chemically marked or specified diesel beginning October 1, 2026. The first $140,000 of annual receipts are credited to the Motor Fuel Tax Enforcement and Collection Cash Fund; remaining receipts go to the Agricultural Alcohol Fuel Tax Fund. The Motor Fuel Tax Enforcement and Collection Cash Fund is to be used exclusively for costs of the Department of Revenue in carrying out specified duties and for related costs of the Department of Agriculture and the Nebraska State Patrol; legislative-direction transfers to the General Fund remain permitted.

- Revises the petroleum release remedial action fee administration: the Department of Revenue collects the fee, may deduct up to $150,000 per fiscal year for collection/admin costs (deposited to a Petroleum Release Remedial Action Collection Fund), and that fund is appropriated to the Department of Revenue (with possible transfers to the General Fund).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: Beginning October 1, 2026, a new diesel tax of one-quarter of one cent per gallon is imposed on indelibly dyed diesel or dyed diesel with >0.05% sulfur or cetane <40. The first $140,000 of annual receipts go to the Motor Fuel Tax Enforcement and Collection Cash Fund; all remaining annual receipts are credited to the Agricultural Alcohol Fuel Tax Fund. Dept. of Revenue would administer collection and remittance.

- Department of Agriculture: The bill declares the Nebraska Ethanol Board the state agency to promote ethanol and related products, directing statewide market development, research, education, and policy/regulatory support. Board membership is revised to require multiple ethanol producers (appointments and staggered initial terms beginning after Sept. 1, 2026). No other direct appropriations to the departments are specified.
