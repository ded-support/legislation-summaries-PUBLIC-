Summary

LB973 assigns the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) custody and operational control of most state-owned or leased buildings, and authorizes the DAS director to appoint an administrator with authority to lease and provide parking. Beginning July 1, 2028, DAS must provide adequate offstreet parking for all state employees at no cost. The bill creates a Parking Revolving Fund to receive parking charge receipts and authorizes DAS to expend those funds to construct, operate, and maintain parking for state vehicles, employees, and visitors. The system of charges for state buildings is amended to include amounts to fund parking (in addition to operating and building renewal costs); other rental proceeds remain credited to the State Building Revolving Fund or State Building Renewal Assessment Fund. Buildings/grounds owned or operated by the Department of Correctional Services and certain Department of Transportation facilities are exempted from specified DAS maintenance/charging provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (effective July 1, 2028):

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): Assigned responsibility to provide adequate off‑street parking for all state employees at all state‑owned or -leased sites, at no cost to employees. DAS is also directed to fund that parking per subdivision (4)(d) of §81‑1108.15.

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): Must have adequate off‑street employee parking at its facilities; implementation/funding will be handled under the DAS responsibility/funding directive beginning 7/1/2028.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): Same requirement for adequate off‑street employee parking at DOT sites; funding/construction obligations are tied to the DAS funding directive.

Funding/appropriations: The added text requires DAS to fund parking beginning 7/1/2028 but contains no specific appropriation amounts or line‑item budget allocations.
