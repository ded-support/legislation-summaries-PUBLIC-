Summary

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): DAS is named custodian of the state laboratory, Governor’s Mansion, and other state-owned/leased buildings except listed exemptions. The DAS director must appoint an administrator who may lease parking and property, establish equitable parking charges, and expend a newly created Parking Revolving Fund for constructing, operating, maintaining parking for state vehicles, employees, and visitors. Receipts from parking charges are credited to the Parking Revolving Fund; other building charge proceeds continue to credit the State Building Revolving Fund and State Building Renewal Assessment Fund. Beginning July 1, 2028, the DAS must provide adequate off‑street parking for all state employees without cost to employees, and the system of charges must fund that parking.

- Department of Correctional Services: buildings/grounds owned, leased, or operated by DOC are exempt from DAS maintenance/administration duties.

- Department of Transportation: state facilities rented by or leased for nonoffice use by DOT are exempt from specified DAS duties.
