Summary

LB979 amends statutes governing the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission and related funds and administration.

Key agency/budget provisions:

- State Game Fund: proceeds from permits/publications remain appropriated to the Commission for propagation, enforcement, boating safety education, boating facilities, and deer/antelope/elk damage abatement. $2 from each annual resident fishing and each combination permit is earmarked for fish hatcheries and distribution.

- Game Law Investigation Cash Fund: clarified use for investigations; funded by Game Law revenue, commission allocations, and donations; annual detailed fiscal report to the Clerk of the Legislature required; funds invested by the state investment officer.

- Game and Parks Commission Educational Fund: may receive fees (Commission-authorized educational program fees), gifts, and credits under section 60-3,163.02; fees remitted to State Treasurer for credit to the fund.

- Licensing/fees: agents may retain up to $4 per issued license as clerical reimbursement; motorboat registration fees set by class with up to $10 per registration for the Aquatic Invasive Species Program; aquatic invasive species stamp fee cap established; proceeds remitted to the State Game Fund.

- Administrative: Director of Motor Vehicles to coordinate vessel registration implementation with the Commission; county treasurers handle initial vessel applications.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority and fee provisions for the Game and Parks Commission. The commission may establish and collect reasonable fees for fish and wildlife education programs (fees limited to staffing/material costs) and must remit proceeds to the State Treasurer for credit to the Game and Parks Commission Educational Fund. Fee caps: resident lifetime fishing/combination permits capped at 20× the annual fee; nonresident lifetime permit similar; turkey permit application fee capped at $9; preference-point fees up to $24 (resident) and $72 (nonresident). The commission may set other permit/application fees (per §37‑327) and adopt related rules. Text also lists allowable investigative resources (investigative vehicles, specialized equipment, digital services, training) for abatement investigations.
