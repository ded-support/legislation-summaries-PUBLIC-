Summary

LB979 makes multiple changes affecting the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission and related state funds and agencies.

- Creates/updates funds and reporting: establishes the Game Law Investigation Cash Fund (commission-funded and donation-funded) with annual, detailed expenditure reports to the Clerk of the Legislature; expands the Game and Parks Commission Educational Fund and authorizes fees for fish/wildlife education to be remitted to it. Investment of fund balances authorized to the state investment officer.

- Appropriations/use of State Game Fund: clarifies State Game Fund appropriations to the commission (propagation, hatcheries, boating safety, boating facilities, deer/antelope/elk damage abatement, publications). Directs $2 from each resident annual fishing and $2 from each combo permit to fish hatcheries.

- Fees and allocations: authorizes electronic licensing, allows agents to retain up to $4 per transaction; sets or updates caps for application and permit fees (deer, antelope, elk, turkey, paddlefish, mountain lion limited permits, lifetime permit cost including aquatic habitat stamp), auction proceeds for mountain sheep restricted to species management, and controlled-shooting-area license fee (up to $258). Motorboat registration fees specified; up to $10 of each registration may fund the Aquatic Invasive Species Program; aquatic invasive species stamp fee cap and proceeds remitted to State Game Fund.

- Other agencies: Director of Motor Vehicles, county treasurers, and State Treasurer are given roles for registration, application processing, and fund crediting. Penal provision: unlawful trade/possession of wildlife taken in violation of Game Law is a Class I misdemeanor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Agencies mentioned: Game and Parks Commission and the State Treasurer.

Key provisions affecting funding or fees:

- The commission may establish and collect reasonable fees for fish and wildlife education programs; fees are limited to staffing/material costs and proceeds must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Game and Parks Commission Educational Fund.

- Permit fee caps: resident lifetime fishing/combination permits may not exceed 20× the annual fee; similar caps apply to nonresident lifetime permits.

- The commission may set a nonrefundable turkey application fee up to $9.

- The commission may charge up to $24 (resident) and $72 (nonresident) for issuance of a preference point; it must adopt rules for issuing preference points.

- Language authorizes use of resources for abatement/investigations including investigative vehicles, specialized equipment, digital investigative services, and specialized training.
