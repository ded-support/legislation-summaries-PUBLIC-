Summary

LB896 amends §33-110, R.R.S. Neb., affecting county clerk fees. County clerks continue to receive no fee for specified official duties (issuing certificates of election; acting as clerk of the county board; taking acknowledgments of county claims; attesting/certifying documents authorized by the county board or required by state departments; recording Army/Navy discharges and certified copies for compensation/disability claims). A 25¢ charge remains for other certificates. The bill sets a $50 fee to the county clerk for issuance of a marriage license, related oaths/affirmations, and recording the certificate, and a $16 fee for each certified copy of a marriage record. All such fees are deposited to the county general fund. The original section is repealed.
