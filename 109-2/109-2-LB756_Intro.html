Summary

LB756 revises Nebraska tax-exemption procedures for disabled or blind U.S. veterans (service‑connected disability recognized by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs) and their surviving spouses for one motor vehicle and for mobile-home property tax exemptions. It requires affected individuals to apply to the county treasurer within specified registration windows; the county treasurer must approve or deny within 20 days, and denials may be appealed to the county board of equalization within 20 days. For organizational vehicle exemptions, the county treasurer recommends status to the county board within 20 days; the board holds a hearing on ten days’ notice, decides within 30 days, and its decision may be appealed to the Tax Equalization and Review Commission within 30 days. The Tax Commissioner promulgates rules. Operative date: January 1, 2027. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds wording to reorganize and clarify who qualifies for the exemption: it inserts a "person:" label and divides the provision into numbered subsections (1) and (2), where (1) covers an individual and (2) covers a surviving spouse of a disabled or blind veteran who would have qualified at the veteran's death and who has not remarried. It also updates an internal cross‑reference (from subdivision (2)(a) to subdivision (1)). No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned or changed.
