Summary

LB1096 creates the Preventing Lethal Agricultural and National Threats Act and explicitly defines "Department" to mean the Department of Agriculture. It defines "high‑risk agricultural pathogen or pest" to include the APHIS list (as of Jan. 1, 2026) and agents designated under section 5 of the bill. The bill prohibits knowingly importing such high‑risk pathogens or pests into Nebraska except when the importer possesses a permit or authorization from the Department of Agriculture. (Penalties for violations are set elsewhere in the bill.) The bill contains no references to the Department of Revenue and includes no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for either department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Preventing Lethal Agricultural and National Threats Act," defining "Department" as the Department of Agriculture and defining "high-risk agricultural pathogen or pest" to include the USDA APHIS list (as of Jan 1, 2026) and any agent designated under section 5. It bars importing such pathogens into the state unless the Department of Agriculture issues a permit and makes violations felonies (Class III; elevated to Class IIA for concealment, foreign‑government involvement, or similar aggravating conduct). No provisions alter funding, appropriations, or budgets, and the Department of Revenue is not addressed.
