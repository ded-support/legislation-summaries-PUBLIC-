Summary

Amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §37-420 to require issuance to qualifying Nebraska veterans, without fee, of a combination fishing, fur-harvesting, and hunting permit plus habitat, aquatic habitat, and Nebraska migratory waterfowl stamps. Eligibility: Nebraska resident, honorable or general discharge, and either a U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) disability rating of 50%+ or receiving a VA pension for total and permanent disability. Veterans with a VA rating of 10%+ may buy special combination permits for $90 (three years) or $135 (five years). The commission may issue free vehicle-hunting permits to physically disabled persons, adopt rules, and free permits are perpetual until ineligibility. Repeals original section. No appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision allowing Nebraska resident veterans to obtain a combined fishing and hunting permit plus habitat, aquatic habitat, and Nebraska migratory waterfowl stamps upon application and payment — $90 for a three‑year special permit or $135 for a five‑year special permit. Eligibility: honorable or general (under honorable conditions) discharge and a VA disability rating of 10% or greater by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs. No state agency appropriations or other funding changes are specified.
