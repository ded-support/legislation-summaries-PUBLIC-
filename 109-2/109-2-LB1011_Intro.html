Summary

LB1011 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-121 (Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act). Key agency references and obligations:

- Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court (and any judge of the compensation court) may order vocational rehabilitation; the bill explicitly entitles employees to temporary disability compensation while undergoing physical/medical rehabilitation and while undergoing vocational rehabilitation whether voluntarily offered by the employer or ordered by the Court.

- Temporary disability benefits continue until the later of (a) a permanent-impairment determination or a loss-of-earning-capacity evaluation, or (b) 30 days after notice (per new subdivision (6)).

- Employers terminating temporary benefits must provide copies of evidence relied upon; if non-treating-physician evidence is relied on, the employer must pay for an independent medical examiner chosen by the employee.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that temporary disability pay continues until the later of (a) a permanent impairment determination or issuance of a loss‑of‑earning‑capacity evaluation, or (b) 30 days after the employee is given notice. Benefits may only cease after a 30‑day notice. On termination, the employer must provide copies of all evidence used to stop payments. If the employer relied on non‑treating physicians, the employee may obtain an independent medical examiner (chosen by the employee) at the employer’s expense. No state agencies or appropriations are mentioned.
