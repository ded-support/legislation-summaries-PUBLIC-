Summary

LB1034 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§79‑2701 and 79‑2702. Key agency references and changes:

- State Department of Education: defined as “Department” for these sections (now also explicitly tied to section 3 of the act).

- Law enforcement agencies: definition clarified to include municipal police departments, offices of town marshals, county sheriffs, the Nebraska State Patrol, and departments to which a deputy state sheriff is assigned.

- Peace officers and school resource officers (SROs): SROs are defined as peace officers whose primary duty is assignment to a school; transient or event-driven law enforcement responses are excluded from the SRO definition.

- Security agencies/security guards: contractors and guards are defined and explicitly distinguished from law enforcement (no arrest authority).

- New statutory intent: schools must not facilitate immigration enforcement activities on school grounds absent a valid judicial warrant; schools must comply with lawful court orders and exigent‑circumstance responses.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an obligation for schools to maintain safe, orderly learning environments and to respect the privacy and constitutional rights of students and school employees. It prohibits school employees from facilitating immigration‑enforcement activities on school grounds except when presented with a valid judicial warrant, requires compliance with lawful court orders, and mandates response to exigent circumstances. Mentions "section 3 of this act." No specific government agencies are named beyond references to immigration enforcement and the courts; no funding or appropriations are included.
