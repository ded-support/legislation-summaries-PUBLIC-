Summary

LB1034 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§79-2701 and 79-2702. It states legislative intent on school safety and clarifies that "Department" means the State Department of Education. It defines "law enforcement agency" to include municipal police departments, offices of town marshals, county sheriffs, the Nebraska State Patrol, and departments referenced in §84-106; "peace officer" by reference to §28-109; and delineates "school resource officer," "security agency" (contractors), and "security guard." The bill directs schools to respect student and employee privacy and prohibits school employees from facilitating immigration enforcement on school grounds absent a valid judicial warrant (while allowing compliance with lawful court orders and exigent circumstances). No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new duty for schools to: maintain safe, orderly learning environments; respect student and employee privacy and constitutional rights; refrain from having school employees facilitate immigration enforcement on school grounds except when presented with a valid judicial warrant; comply with lawful court orders; and respond to exigent circumstances. Affects local education agencies/school staff, immigration enforcement authorities, and the judiciary. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
