Summary

LB846 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716.01 to revise individual income tax deduction rules. Key changes: (1) personal exemption credits (beginning tax year 2018) are computed using the number of child and dependent credits on the federal return (plus filing-status adjustments) with the annual credit indexed using the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the federal Bureau of Labor Statistics; (2) standard deduction amounts and indexing methods for various tax-year ranges are updated; and (3) for taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2026, itemizers may subtract federal itemized deductions except that state or local income tax amounts are limited to the applicable cap provided under Internal Revenue Code section 164(b)(7) for years after 2029. The bill repeals the original section. No agencies are allocated new funding or appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision (effective Jan. 1, 2026) tying the state subtraction from federal adjusted gross income to federal rules: individuals who itemized on their federal return may subtract the greater of the state standard deduction or their federal itemized deductions (per IRC §63(d)), but the portion for state/local income taxes is limited by the applicable cap in IRC §164(b)(7) for taxable years beginning after 2029, using that section as it existed on Jan. 1, 2026. The text cites the Internal Revenue Code (federal tax law, administered by the IRS/Treasury). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
