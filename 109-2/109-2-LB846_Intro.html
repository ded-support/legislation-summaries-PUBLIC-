Summary

LB846 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716.01 to revise individual income tax deduction rules. It continues the post‑2018 personal exemption credit formula and CPI-based indexing, specifies fixed and inflation‑adjusted standard deduction amounts for various filing statuses (using the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers published by the federal Bureau of Labor Statistics), and updates rounding/indexing methods. For taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2026, itemizers may subtract the greater of the Nebraska standard deduction or federal itemized deductions, except the state/local income tax portion is limited to the applicable cap set under section 164(b)(7) of the Internal Revenue Code as it existed Jan. 1, 2026 (applicable to years after calendar year 2029). The bill repeals the original section. No agency appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added subsection (b) (effective for taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2026) lets taxpayers who itemized federal deductions subtract from state federal adjusted gross income the greater of the state standard deduction or their federal itemized deductions, but excludes the portion for state/local income taxes to the extent that amount exceeds the limitation set by IRC section 164(b)(7) as it exists on Jan. 1, 2026, for years after 2029. No government agencies or departments are named and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
