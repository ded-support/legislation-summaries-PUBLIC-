Summary

LB738 amends the alternate license plate statute with several explicit agency references and funding provisions:

- Designates the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund as the alternate-fee recipient for Czech Heritage Plates.

- Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit Plates: $70 initial/renewal fee; State Treasurer credits 60% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 40% to the alternate recipient.

- Personalized Support Our Troops Plates: $70 fee; 25% of the fee credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 75% to the designated recipient (Veterans Employment Program Fund).

- Creates the Women Veterans Cash Fund, to be administered by the Department of Veterans' Affairs, funded by credits under section 60-3,163.02, usable for events focused on women veterans and investable under state investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Motor Vehicles: directs the DMV to design a specialty "Honoring Women Veterans" license plate (in consultation with a nongovernmental veterans support organization) showing a woman soldier with "women veterans" at the top and "honoring her service" at the bottom. The plates' designated recipient is the Women Veterans Cash Fund.

Department of Veterans' Affairs: establishes the Women Veterans Cash Fund, to be administered by DVA. The fund will consist of money credited under section 60-3,163.02, may be used by DVA for events focused on women veterans, and any investable balances are to be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are provided.
