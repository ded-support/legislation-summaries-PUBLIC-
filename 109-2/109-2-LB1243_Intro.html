Summary

LB1243 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-2,136 to require school boards and districts to permit part-time enrollment for students who are residents/admitted under §79-215 and who attend private, denominational, parochial, or schools electing nonaccreditation under §79-1601, and to adopt related policies. Boards must allow such students to participate in extracurricular activities (per §79-2,126) on the same basis as public-school students; nonaccredited schools set academic eligibility standards. For activities governed by an athletics/activities association, participating part-time students must be enrolled in at least five credit hours per semester. Policies may not prefer full-time students. Participation does not create entitlement to general transportation or reimbursement under §79-611. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a prohibition on school districts requiring students to be enrolled in a minimum number of school-offered credit hours to participate in extracurricular activities that are not regulated by an athletics or activities association. No state agencies, departments, or budget, funding, or appropriation changes are mentioned.
