Summary

LB1026 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §70-1001.01 to (re)define terms used in the state's electric statutes, explicitly defining "Board" as the Nebraska Power Review Board. It prohibits any electric supplier from retiring, shutting down, ceasing operation of, or substantially altering operation of an electric generation facility (including privately developed renewable facilities) if customers are awaiting service, except where required by federal or state law, the facility is mechanically unsafe, damaged beyond repair by a natural disaster, or continued operation is uneconomical. The supplier's governing body must adopt a resolution stating one of those reasons before action. The bill harmonizes related provisions and repeals the original section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Prohibits an electric supplier from retiring, shutting down, ceasing operation of, or substantially altering any electric generation facility — including privately developed renewable facilities — if the supplier has customers waiting for service, except where: (a) the action is required by federal or state law; (b) the facility is mechanically unsafe; (c) the facility is irreparably damaged by a natural disaster; or (d) continued operation is uneconomical. Requires the electric supplier’s governing body to adopt a resolution identifying the reasons for the proposed action and to cite at least one of the four exceptions. No specific government agencies or departments are named beyond references to “federal or state law,” and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
