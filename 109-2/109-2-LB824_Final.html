Summary

LB824 revises the School Employees Retirement Act and the Class V School Employees Retirement Act to redefine "termination of employment" and tighten return‑to‑work rules. It authorizes the Public Employees Retirement Board and the Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS) director to determine bona fide separations and enforce a 120‑day bar (measured from the later of separation or the board’s receipt of a retirement application) that affects eligibility for benefits, distributions, and contribution requirements. The system administrator may require verification within 45 days; appeals are decided finally by the retirement board. Temporary, substitute, or volunteer returns are narrowly treated (only for §79‑991) and do not earn service credit or permit contributions until a formal termination under §79‑978(42). Transfer of management to the retirement board does not shift school districts’ funding obligations or liabilities to the State, NPERS, the retirement board, the Nebraska Investment Council, or the State Investment Officer. Refunds over $1,000 default to direct rollovers. The bill includes repeal clauses and an emergency effective date. No appropriations or budget changes.
