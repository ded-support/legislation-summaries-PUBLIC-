Summary

LB848 amends the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967 (amending sections 77-2701 and 77-2701.04) to create a three-day annual state and local sales-and-use tax holiday (12:01 a.m. first Friday in August through the following Sunday). Exemptions apply to specified personal-use items within price caps: clothing ($100 or less), school supplies ($50 per purchase), computer software ($350 or less), graphing calculators ($150 or less), and personal computers/peripherals ($1,500 or less). The bill adds rules on layaway, discounts, bundled transactions, delivery timing, exchanges/returns, and documentation for refunds. No state agency or department is explicitly named; the changes operate within existing Nebraska tax statutes and enforcement by tax authorities. Operative date: January 1, 2027. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a three-day state and local sales-and-use tax holiday beginning at 12:01 a.m. on the first Friday in August through midnight Sunday. Exemptions apply to qualifying purchases for personal use only: clothing (≤ $100), school supplies (≤ $50 per purchase), computer software (≤ $350), graphing calculators (≤ $150), and personal computers/peripherals (≤ $1,500). Rules cover bundled sales, layaway (must be paid and delivered during the period or ordered for immediate delivery during the period), how seller discounts and coupons are treated, allocation of discounts when mixed taxable/exempt items are purchased, backordered deliveries, and exchange/return documentation (60-day rule). No government agencies, departments, or changes to funding/appropriations are mentioned.
