Summary

LB848 amends sections 77-2701 and 77-2701.04 of the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967 and adds a new section creating an annual three-day state and local sales-and-use tax holiday (12:01 a.m. first Friday in August through the following Sunday). Exemptions (personal use only) cover: clothing priced ≤ $100; school supplies ≤ $50 per purchase; computer software ≤ $350; graphing calculators ≤ $150; and personal computers/peripheral devices ≤ $1,500. The bill includes provisions governing bundled sales, discounts, layaway sales, delivery timing, exchanges, and returns. Operative date is January 1, 2027, and original referenced statutory sections are repealed. No state agency is explicitly named in the bill, and it contains no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a three-day annual state and local sales-and-use tax holiday from 12:01 a.m. on the first Friday in August through midnight the following Sunday. Exempts: clothing ≤ $100, school supplies ≤ $50 per purchase, computer software ≤ $350, graphing calculators ≤ $150, and personal computers/peripherals ≤ $1,500 — personal-use only. Includes rules on bundled sales, layaway (exemption only if final payment/delivery or order/acceptance occur during the period), discount and coupon treatment (third‑party‑reimbursed coupons not treated as seller discounts), allocation of transaction-wide discounts, out‑of‑stock deliveries, exchanges/returns, and a 60‑day documentation requirement for tax refunds. No specific state agencies or departments are named and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
