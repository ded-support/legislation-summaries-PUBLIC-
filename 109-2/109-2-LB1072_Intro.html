Summary

The bill centralizes budget transfer authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget administrator, authorizing numerous State Treasurer transfers and timing directives among cash funds, the Cash Reserve Fund, General Fund, and capital accounts, and certifying matching funds for a contingent $15M NU Medical Center transfer. Key agency provisions:

- Health and Human Services (DHHS): a 988 Emergency Lifeline Cash Fund seeded ($3,478,000) from the 911 fund; DHHS to receive 988 support, administer the Gamblers Assistance Program, transmit specified HHS Cash Fund transfers to the General Fund ($7,806,629 FY2026; $1,000,000 FY2027), and a $500,000 transfer to UN Eppley.

- Revenue: creates a Charitable Gaming Division and fund with revenue splits (40% operations/60% General Fund), mandated transfers (quarterly $100,000 to Compulsive Gamblers Assistance through 6/30/2026), lottery earnings reallocation (from 7/1/2026), a Tobacco Products Admin Cash Fund ($11.5M FY25‑26; $12.5M FY26‑27), and modest Enforcement Fund transfers (~$43,900).

- Insurance: new Department of Insurance Cash Fund with required transfers to the General Fund ($17.2M by 6/30/2026; $16.8M each June 30, 2027–2029).

- Economic Development: administers multiple grant/aid funds (including ARPA recovery grants, Municipality Infrastructure Aid, Affordable Housing Trust), audits Teleworker employers, caps job‑training grants, remits recoveries to the General Fund, and partners with Labor on data.

- Labor: administers Employment Security funds; Special Contingent Fund earnings credited to the General Fund (from 10/1/2024); MOU with Economic Development.

- Correctional Services: creates an Insurance Proceeds Fund; administers Inmate Welfare and Club Accounts; funds may be transferred to the General Fund.

- Motor Vehicles: creates a DMV Ignition Interlock Fund for indigent costs and provides multi‑year motor‑carrier/plate production transfers (~$3.55M FY25‑26; ~$2.59M FY26‑27).

- Agriculture: allows use and transfers from the Commercial Feed Administration Cash Fund for feed, animal health, and USDA cooperative reporting.

- Veterans' Affairs: transfers of $2.5M in FY2026 and FY2027 from the Nebraska Veterans' Aid Fund to the Department of Veterans' Affairs Cash Fund.
