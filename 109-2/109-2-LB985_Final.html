Summary

LB985 amends Nebraska Probate Code provisions governing courts, the Supreme Court (rulemaking), county courts, the Public Guardian, court‑appointed physicians, guardians ad litem, and conservators. Key agency‑related changes:

- Courts: expressly authorize in‑person or virtual attendance at incapacity, guardianship, and conservatorship hearings; may appoint counsel or guardian ad litem; may send visitors to investigate; must follow Supreme Court rules for standby‑guardian activation and training.

- Supreme Court: rulemaking authority referenced for standby‑guardian procedures and training schedules.

- Public Guardian: barred from serving as a standby guardian.

- County courts: issue letters of guardianship/standby guardianship; receive inventories and annual reports; may limit conservator powers and require court permission to move a protected person out of state.

- Guardians/conservators: new monitoring and quarterly contact, recordkeeping, inventory and mailing requirements, and a 20‑ward cap for permanent guardianships.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for guardians and ward/court participation. Key agency: the court — it may receive documentation of missed contacts, can modify or waive monitoring rules, and is the forum for hearings. New duties for guardians: quarterly personal contact with wards (in person or virtual if adequate), written visit records, documented attempts if contact fails, efforts to enable wards to attend hearings in person or virtually, and a cap prohibiting appointment as a permanent guardian/conservator if already serving 20+ permanent wards (temporary guardianships/conservatorships excluded). Also affirms minors/persons under protection have the right to attend hearings virtually or in person. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
