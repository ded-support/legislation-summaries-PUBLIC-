Summary

LB815 assigns several responsibilities and fiscal provisions to the Department of Revenue in the petroleum release remedial action fee statute (66-1521). Application for required petroleum-release licenses must be made to the Department of Revenue; failure to obtain a license is a Class IV misdemeanor, and the Department may suspend or cancel licenses for nonpayment of the fee. The Department may adopt rules and is charged with collecting the fee. It must deduct and withhold administrative collection costs (capped at $150,000 per fiscal year, prorated if collected part-year), depositing those amounts into the Petroleum Release Remedial Action Collection Fund, which is appropriated to the Department of Revenue (with Legislative-authorized transfers to the General Fund). Fee revenue priorities and reimbursement limits (including up to $3 million/year for certain investigations) remain specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: beginning October 1, 2026 a new tax of one-quarter of one cent (0.25¢) per gallon is imposed on certain dyed/chemically marked diesel (and specified high‑sulfur/low‑cetane dyed diesel). All taxes collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Agricultural Alcohol Fuel Tax Fund. (No separate appropriation language is added.)

- Department of Agriculture / Nebraska Ethanol Board: the bill declares a statewide policy prioritizing ethanol production, processing, research, market development, and regulatory expertise and designates the Nebraska Ethanol Board as the state agency for that purpose. Board membership is revised so that a specified member must be an in‑state ethanol producer beginning Sept. 1, 2026, and two additional ethanol‑producer members must be appointed within 30 days after that date (staggered initial terms ending Aug. 31, 2028 and Aug. 31, 2029). No other direct budget appropriations to the department are specified.
