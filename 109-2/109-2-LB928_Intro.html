Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) specific post‑removal duties. After a child is removed, the person or court must notify DHHS to identify, locate, and provide written notice to adult relatives and fictive kin. When notified or on voluntary placement, DHHS must, within 30 days, identify, locate, and notify noncustodial parents, grandparents, parents with custody of siblings, adult siblings, aunts, uncles, cousins, adult relatives, and fictive kin (with a domestic‑violence exception). DHHS must investigate names/locations (including asking the child in an age‑appropriate way) and file to the court within 30 days the names, relationships, contact methods, and responses. The bill defines “department” as DHHS and defines “fictive kin.” No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies definitions relevant to child-welfare law: “Indian child” is referenced to §43-1503; “fictive kin” is aligned to §71-1901 and defined as an adult not related by blood, marriage, or adoption who either previously lived with the child or is a trusted adult with a preexisting, significant relationship; “sibling” is defined to include those treated as siblings under Nebraska law even if parental rights were terminated or disrupted; and “person” expressly includes entities such as partnerships, LLCs, agencies, and corporations. No Department of Health and Human Services funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are added or changed in the provided text.
