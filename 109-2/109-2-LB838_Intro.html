Summary

LB838 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§8-2901 to 8-2903 concerning financial exploitation of vulnerable or senior adults. It explicitly defines "financial institution" to include banks, credit unions, and similar entities "whether chartered by the Department of Banking and Finance, the United States, or a foreign state agency." The bill contains no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition for "authorized contact" and establishes liability protections: financial institutions may decline to implement or to interact with an authorized contact without civil/criminal/administrative liability (except for gross negligence or willful misconduct), are not liable for an authorized contact's actions, and may refuse contact if they reasonably suspect exploitation or that interaction is not in the customer's best interest. Individuals acting as authorized contacts who act in good faith and with reasonable care are also immune from liability. No provisions assign duties, funding, appropriations, or budget changes to the Department of Banking and Finance.
