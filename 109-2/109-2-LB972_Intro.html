Summary

LB972 revises Department of Motor Vehicles authorities and fee flows. Key DMV changes: DMV to issue bonded motorboat titles; bonded-title fees ($50 for boats model year ≥1990; $20 for older) remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the DMV Cash Fund. DMV may mail license plates/registrations and charge postage/handling (credited to the DMV Cash Fund). The bill creates an "In Transit" placard and authorizes transporter certificates/plates (fee $10), with the DMV retaining records, inspecting bona fide status, and prescribing display. The DMV will stop accepting new special‑interest plate applications after 12/31/2028 and discontinue renewals thereafter; existing special‑interest fee allocation ($50 fee split $25 DMV Cash Fund / $25 Highway Trust Fund) and priority of manufacturing costs to the Highway Trust Fund remain. The DMV director may appoint examiners and waive certain road tests. One excerpt refers only to "the department" without naming DMV or Transportation and contains no funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- DMV: Bonded motorboat title applicants must send certified-mail notice to secured-interest holders; if no response within 30 days, issuance extinguishes the lien.

- "In Transit" placard/sticker rules clarified for dealers, towing and operation of unregistered vehicles/trailers (administration details assigned to vehicle registration processes).

- Special‑interest license plates are phased out: no new applications accepted on/after Jan 1, 2029; renewals end Dec 31, 2028.

- New "Choice Color Plates" (solid black, red, green, blue with white characters) authorized; fees set at $70 (alphanumeric) and $100 (personalized). All Choice Color Plate fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund; applications begin Jan 1, 2027.

- DMV may waive certain ignition‑interlock requirements and expand remote/in‑person renewal options for Class O/M licenses.

- Insurance SR‑22 certificates must be transmitted electronically to DMV beginning July 1, 2027.

- No explicit Department of Transportation budget appropriations or new DOT funding allocations are included.
