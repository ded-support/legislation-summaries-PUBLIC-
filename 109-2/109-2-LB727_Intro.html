Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services. The Department is required, in consultation with law enforcement agencies, to approve one or more educational training programs on responding to anaphylaxis, including epinephrine autoinjector use; approved programs must provide a certificate of completion. The Department is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department are specified in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS is defined as the Department of Health and Human Services and is given three key roles: approve, in consultation with law enforcement, one or more educational training programs (including certification on epinephrine autoinjector use); set the prerequisite that officers must present a certificate of completion before an agency may provide an autoinjector or before an officer may administer one on duty; and adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the section. The provision permits — but does not require — agencies to obtain/autoinjectors or officers to carry/administer them. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
