Summary

Creates the Recreational Vehicle Industry Regulation Act and moves regulation of recreational vehicles out of the Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act. Expands the Nebraska Motor Vehicle Industry Licensing Board (chaired by the Director of Motor Vehicles) to include two new gubernatorial appointees—a licensed recreational vehicle dealer and a licensed recreational vehicle manufacturer—whose three-year terms begin January 1, 2027. Grants the board rulemaking, licensing, investigatory, and enforcement authority, including hiring a hearing officer and employing investigators. Civil penalties (up to $1,000 per violation) may be imposed by the board and must be remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutionally prescribed distribution. Amends definitions in the Uniform Standard Code for Manufactured Homes and Recreational Vehicles to recognize dealers licensed under the new act and retains prior seals issued by the Department of Health and Human Services Regulation and Licensure or the Public Service Commission. Operative date: October 1, 2026. No direct appropriations or new funding streams are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a state regulatory board to administer the new Recreational Vehicle Industry Regulation Act with authority to license dealers, manufacturers, and distributors; adopt and enforce rules; employ a hearing officer; and use investigators to inspect licensees. The board’s seal will authenticate records; the chair must review board actions if a controlling number of members are active market participants. Governor must appoint one dealer and one manufacturer board member by Jan. 1, 2027, each for three-year terms starting Jan. 1, 2027. Civil penalties (up to $1,000 per violation) are payable to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. Actions and appeals use county courts and the Administrative Procedure Act. The bill references existing statutes (Motor Vehicle Registration Act; Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act) but contains no new appropriations or budget line-item funding.
