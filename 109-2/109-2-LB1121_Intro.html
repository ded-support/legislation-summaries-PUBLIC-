Summary

LB1121 creates the Recreational Vehicle Industry Regulation Act (operative Oct. 1, 2026) and places regulation of RV dealers, manufacturers, and distributors under the Nebraska Motor Vehicle Industry Licensing Board (the board), chaired by the Director of Motor Vehicles. The board gains authority to issue/revoke licenses, adopt rules, employ a hearing officer, use investigators to inspect licensees, and impose civil penalties (up to $1,000 per violation) remitted to the State Treasurer per the Nebraska Constitution. The board’s membership is expanded to add one licensed recreational vehicle dealer and one licensed recreational vehicle manufacturer (appointments by Jan. 1, 2027). The bill amends motor-vehicle and manufactured-home statutes to exclude RVs from certain motor vehicle provisions and to recognize the Public Service Commission and DHHS roles for seals. No direct appropriations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a new state regulatory Board under the "Recreational Vehicle Industry Regulation Act" with authority to issue/revoke licenses, adopt and enforce rules, employ a hearing officer, and use investigators to inspect licensed dealers' facilities. The Board's seal authenticates records. License appeals proceed under the Administrative Procedure Act. Civil penalties (up to $1,000 per violation) collected by the Board must be remitted to the State Treasurer for disposition under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. The Governor must appoint designated dealer and manufacturer members by January 1, 2027; those appointments are three-year terms beginning January 1, 2027. The act references the Motor Vehicle Registration Act and Motor Vehicle Industry Regulation Act. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
