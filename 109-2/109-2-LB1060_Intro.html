Summary

LB1060 defines "Department" to mean the Department of Health and Human Services. The bill requires that if a licensed child‑placing agency declines to provide or facilitate a placement service for religious reasons, it must promptly provide the requester with either contact information for another agency, a list of other agencies, or a link to a web page on the Department of Health and Human Services' website listing such agencies and contact information. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Health and Human Services focus):

- The term "Department" is defined to mean the Department of Health and Human Services.

- If a child-placing agency declines to provide or facilitate a placement service because it conflicts with its sincerely held religious beliefs, the agency must promptly give the requester either contact information for one or more alternate child-placing agencies or a link to a list on the Department of Health and Human Services' website listing such agencies.

- The state (including state agencies) is barred, "to the fullest extent permitted by law," from taking adverse actions against a child-placing agency for such religiously based refusals.

- An aggrieved agency may assert a violation of section 5 as a defense in administrative or judicial proceedings.

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
