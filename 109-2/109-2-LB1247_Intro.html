Summary

LB1247 assigns the Department of Economic Development to designate eligible postsecondary programs (beginning AY 2024‑25) in collaboration with the Board of Regents and the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges based on periodic workforce reviews. The Coordinating Commission must consult with the Department of Economic Development and the Department of Labor (and the State Department of Education) to identify and periodically review shortage areas and skilled trades. The commission will use Department of Labor data to report the number of scholarship recipients employed in‑state within five fiscal quarters of program completion. Funding: boards must allocate funds appropriated under the Nebraska Career Scholarship Act between campuses; the Coordinating Commission shall establish a separate budget subprogram for community college allocations. Funds may be withheld from campuses that fail to submit required workforce data.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds conditions that the three higher-education governing bodies (Board of Trustees of Nebraska State Colleges, Board of Regents, and the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education) must withhold Nebraska Career Scholarship Act allocations from any campus or community college area that fails to meet MOU or subsequent agreement requirements to submit data to the Nebraska Statewide Workforce and Education Reporting System. No provisions in the added text reference the Department of Economic Development or the Department of Labor, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for those agencies.
