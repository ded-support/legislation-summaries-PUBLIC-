Summary

LB1170 amends State Miscellaneous Claims and related statutes to (1) define "division" as the risk management and state claims division of the Department of Administrative Services and (2) vest the Risk Manager (of that division) with authority to receive and investigate claims and to develop claim forms and procedures. The bill requires the Risk Manager to submit applicable claims to the Legislature for review and potential appropriation. It also preserves administrative payment authority: the Risk Manager may direct agency payment of claims up to $5,000 (with agency head agreement) and the State Claims Board may direct payment up to $50,000 (with agreement and sufficient agency funds). The Risk Manager must report paid claims and judgments electronically to the Legislature.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a county may file multiple claims for a single correctional‑institution incident if each meets the threshold or prosecution is resolved, and bars including prosecution costs for claims where the Risk Manager has already issued a decision. Establishes presumptions that certain deaths/illnesses of public safety officers arise in the line of duty: (1) heart attack/stroke/vascular rupture occurring or manifesting within 24 hours of nonroutine stressful/strenuous duty; (2) exposure‑related cancer when service began at least five years before diagnosis; (3) death from cumulative traumatic events where diagnosis or documented attempts to seek help exist. Each presumption may be rebutted by competent medical evidence. Provisions repeatedly reference the Risk Manager (within the Department of Administrative Services). No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
