Summary

Creates a new annual "hangtag" motor-vehicle permit under the Game Law, effective January 1, 2027, to be administered by the commission (Nebraska Game and Parks Commission). Key agency-related changes and fiscal points:

- The commission must offer annual, temporary, hangtag, disabled-veteran, and active-duty permits and may set fees by rule. Active-duty permit fee remains $5.

- Hangtag fee = cost of one resident annual permit + one duplicate annual permit + a commission convenience fee (convenience fee capped at $15; commission sets exact amount).

- The commission, county offices, and designated private permit agents may collect and retain issuing fees pursuant to §37-406.

- Hangtag permits may be transferred between vehicles registered to the permit holder in Nebraska; other permits remain nontransferable.

- Hangtags are displayed from the rearview mirror or on the dash and may be procured only from commission central/district offices and designated state park areas. Repeals prior sections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new "hangtag" annual permit, effective January 1, 2027, administered by "the commission." Key agency points: hangtag permits are sold at the commission's central and district offices and at designated areas of the Nebraska state park system. The fee equals the combined cost of one resident annual permit, one duplicate annual permit, plus a commission-established convenience fee (capped at $15). Hangtags are valid through December 31 of the issuance year, transferable among the holder's registered vehicles, not replaceable if lost, and must be hung from the rearview mirror or placed on the front dash. No explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations are specified.
