Summary

LB1127 (Game Law) directs the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission to create annual, temporary, hangtag, disabled-veteran, and active-duty military permits and to set related fees and rules. Key agency-related changes:

- Commission: sets permit designs, fees (resident annual ≤ $50; resident temporary ≤ $10; nonresident = 2× resident; active-duty = $5), a hangtag convenience fee cap of $15, and may adopt rules and reduced-rate offerings.

- Nebraska State Park System and commission central/district offices: authorized locations to procure hangtag permits (hangtags are not sold via county offices, vending stations, internet or private agents).

- County government offices and designated private permit agents remain authorized for other permit types and may retain issuing fees under §37-406.

Other provisions: hangtags are vehicle-specific but transferable among vehicles registered to the holder, must hang from the rearview mirror or rest on the dash, and are not replaceable if lost; disabled-veteran permits are perpetual and free. Penalty remains a Class V misdemeanor for violations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new hangtag permit (effective Jan 1, 2027) administered by "the commission" and available at the commission’s central and district offices and at designated Nebraska state park system sites. Fee equals the combined cost of one resident annual permit, one duplicate annual permit, plus a commission-established convenience fee capped at $15. Hangtags are valid through Dec. 31 of the issue year, tied to the vehicle in which displayed but transferable among vehicles registered to the permit holder, and cannot be reproduced or replaced if lost. Display rules require hanging from the rearview mirror or placement on the front dash. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
