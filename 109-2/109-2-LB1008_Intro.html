Summary

LB1008 amends section 77-7305 of the School District Property Tax Relief Act to tie future minimum property tax relief amounts to changes in statewide assessed real property value "as determined by the Department of Revenue." For tax year 2030 the minimum relief becomes the prior year minimum plus the percentage increase in total assessed value (per the Department of Revenue) plus an additional $75 million. For tax year 2031 and each year thereafter, the minimum relief equals the prior year minimum plus the percentage increase in total assessed value as determined by the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text makes the Department of Revenue responsible for calculating the year‑over‑year percentage change in total assessed value of all real property statewide. That percentage is used to increase the statutory minimum relief amount. For tax year 2030 the minimum relief equals the prior year minimum plus the DOR‑determined percentage increase and an additional $75 million. For tax year 2031 the minimum is adjusted similarly (prior minimum plus the DOR‑determined percentage increase). The only explicit funding change is the one‑time/additional $75 million for 2030.
