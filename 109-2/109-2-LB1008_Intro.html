Summary

LB1008 amends section 77-7305 (School District Property Tax Relief Act) to change how annual minimum property tax relief amounts are calculated. The Department of Revenue is assigned responsibility to determine the percentage change in total assessed value of all real property statewide. For tax year 2030 the minimum relief becomes the prior year minimum plus that Department of Revenue–determined percentage increase plus an additional $75 million. For tax year 2031 and each year thereafter the minimum equals the prior year minimum plus the Department of Revenue–determined percentage increase. The bill retains specified minimum dollar amounts for tax years 2024–2029 and preserves that transfers to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund (per §77-4602) are added to the minimum when calculating total relief.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Revenue is assigned to determine the year‑over‑year percentage change in total assessed value of all real property in the state. That percentage will be applied to increase the act’s minimum relief amounts. For tax year 2030 the minimum equals the prior year minimum plus that percentage increase and an additional $75 million. For tax year 2031 the minimum equals the prior year minimum plus the percentage increase (no additional $75 million). No other agency appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
