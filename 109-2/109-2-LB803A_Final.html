Summary

The bill appropriates General Fund sums to the Department of Revenue to implement Legislative Bill 803. For Program 102: $1,110,157 for FY2026‑27 and $986,400 for FY2027‑28, with a limit on permanent and temporary salaries and per diems of $81,300 (FY2026‑27) and $83,800 (FY2027‑28). For Program 112: $177,711 for FY2026‑27 and $17,383 for FY2027‑28; none of the Program 112 funds may be used for state employee permanent or temporary salaries and per diems. The act contains an emergency clause and takes effect when passed and approved.
