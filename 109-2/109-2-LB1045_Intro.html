Summary

Creates the Nebraska Public Housing Preservation Trust, a new public trust that will contract with local housing agencies (agreements required by Jan 1, 2027) to preserve and rehabilitate public housing while retaining public ownership. The trust administers a newly created Public Housing Capital Stabilization Fund (capitalized by private donations and federal grants), whose investable balances are managed by the state investment officer under existing state investment statutes. Trust-issued bonds may be secured by project-based federal vouchers, municipal or county contributions, or Fund balances; bond proceeds are dedicated to capital improvements. The Auditor of Public Accounts will perform annual evaluations as a condition for phased conversions. The trust must report quarterly to the Legislature, may enter emergency contracts bypassing normal procurement for imminent threats, and assets are irrevocably dedicated to public housing purposes. Operative July 1, 2026; emergency clause makes it effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the Nebraska Public Housing Preservation Trust and the Public Housing Capital Stabilization Fund (administered by the Trust). Agencies/departments and oversight named:

- Local housing agencies: may enter agreements with the Trust; resident councils gain statutory powers.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: must give positive annual evaluations as a condition for phased preservation conversions.

- State investment officer: will invest Fund monies pursuant to the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Legislature: receives quarterly electronic reports from the Trust.

Funding/appropriations and budget effects:

- Fund sources: private donations and federal grants.

- Bonds issued by the Trust may be backed by project-based federal vouchers, municipal/county contributions, or monies in the Stabilization Fund; bond proceeds are restricted to capital improvements.

- Trust assets, revenues, and bond proceeds are irrevocably dedicated to public housing and protected from foreclosure or reversion to private use.
