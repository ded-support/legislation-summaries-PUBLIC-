Summary

Creates the Nebraska Public Housing Preservation Trust to preserve and rehabilitate public housing. The trust must contract with local housing agencies by January 1, 2027, and its board owes fiduciary duties to residents. Funding and finance provisions: the trust may issue bonds backed by project-based federal vouchers, municipal or county contributions, or the newly created Public Housing Capital Stabilization Fund (fed grants and private donations). The Stabilization Fund is administered by the trust and invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act; bond proceeds are restricted to capital improvements. Phased conversions require positive evaluations from the Auditor of Public Accounts. The trust reports quarterly to the Legislature and resident councils. Act operative July 1, 2026; emergency clause accelerates effect.
