Summary

LB1179 moves the Nebraska Broadband Office administratively and budgetarily into the Department of Transportation; the office may use federal BEAD funds for broadband location-mapping, and the Infrastructure Review Task Force must utilize DOT’s annual state‑highway needs report. The Director of the Department of Economic Development (or a designee) is added as a member of the Nuclear and Hydrogen Industry Work Group. The Department of Agriculture is designated as a stakeholder to be engaged by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment in developing the state flood mitigation plan. Funding: member per diems and travel for the Nuclear and Hydrogen Work Group are reimbursable from the Nuclear and Hydrogen Development Fund; no other state appropriations or dollar amounts are changed.
