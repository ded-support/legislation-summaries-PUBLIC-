Summary

The bill places the Nebraska Broadband Office administratively and budgetarily within the Department of Transportation (DOT), vesting the Broadband Director with authority over the office’s administrative and budgetary decisions. The Infrastructure Review Task Force must use DOT’s annual state‑highway‑system needs report. DOT, the Department of Economic Development (DED), and the Department of Agriculture are named stakeholders to be engaged in development of the state flood mitigation plan. The DED Director (or designee) is appointed to the Nuclear and Hydrogen Industry Work Group. Funding changes: the Broadband Office will use federal BEAD funds for mapping work, and Work Group member per diem and travel reimbursements are payable from the Nuclear and Hydrogen Development Fund (subject to specified per‑diem limits).
