Summary

LB1122 expresses legislative intent regarding the Department of Health and Human Services’ FY2026-27 appropriation for Medicaid nursing facility rates (Program No. 348) set at $462,480,546 (breakdown between General and Federal funds not specified). The bill directs that this total be used in the Program No. 348 rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor, and includes amounts for rate enhancement and other Medicaid nursing facility purposes. It requires DHHS to file: (1) an electronic report by August 1, 2026, explaining how the FY2026-27 inflation factor was calculated; and (2) an electronic report between December 15–31, 2026, identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations, encumbrances, and retroactive payments. The act takes effect immediately as an emergency measure.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For DHHS: the bill states the Legislature’s intent that Program No. 348 receive a total Medicaid nursing facility rates appropriation of $462,480,546 for FY2026‑27 (General/Federal split not specified) and requires that this total be used in the Program No. 348 rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor. The total is to cover rate enhancements and other Medicaid nursing facility purposes. DHHS must file (1) an August 1, 2026 report to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and Clerk explaining how the FY2026‑27 inflation factor was calculated, and (2) a Dec 15–31, 2026 report identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations, including encumbrances and retroactive payments.
