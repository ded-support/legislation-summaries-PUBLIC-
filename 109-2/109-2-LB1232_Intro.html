Summary

LB1232 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§37-455 and 37-456 to authorize limited mountain lion permits under the Game Law. The Nebraska Game and Parks Commission (referred to in the bill as "the commission") may issue such permits to qualifying landowners, leaseholders, or designated immediate family members, adopt rules for documentation/qualification, and set application procedures and allocations. Resident qualification/acreage thresholds, lease criteria, unit-based limits, preference for applicants who did not receive prior permits, and a cap that resident landowner application fees not exceed one-half the regular fee are specified. The bill also limits issuance in any management unit to no more than 75% of regular mountain lion permits. No changes to state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. The original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds detailed eligibility and administrative rules for limited mountain lion hunting permits. Key provisions: the commission (the bill’s regulating commission) may designate mountain lion management zones and set rules; qualifying applicants must be Nebraska residents who (a) own ≥320 acres of farm/ranch land, (b) lease ≥640 acres or have ≥640 combined leased+owned acres used agriculturally, or (c) be an immediate family member of such an owner/lessee. The commission may prefer applicants who did not receive a permit in prior years. Resident landowner limited permit application fees are capped at one-half the regular permit fee. Applications per property are limited by acreage/minimum-acreage ratio (one qualifier per described property), and limited permits in a management unit may not exceed 75% of that unit’s regular permits. No funding or appropriations changes.
