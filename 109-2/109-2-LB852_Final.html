Summary

LB852 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-2610. Key agencies and fiscal actions:

- Creates the Convention Center Support Fund. The State Treasurer must transfer certified amounts into the fund; the state investment officer may invest fund balances per existing state investment law. The Legislature may direct transfers from the fund to the General Fund.

- Legislative intent: up to 70% of specified state sales tax revenue may be appropriated to approved political subdivisions under the Convention Center Facility Financing Assistance Act (not for operating subsidies); the remaining 30% is transferred to the Civic and Community Center Financing Fund.

- New recapture authority: counties may recapture, on a pro rata basis, funds paid to recipients who fail to maintain principal business/operations in designated high‑poverty areas for three years. Recaptured amounts return to the Convention Center Support Fund or a county development fund; counties may adopt enforcement policies. Recapture is in addition to other remedies.

- Operative date: January 1, 2027; original section repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a 3-year local residency requirement for any entity receiving committee funding under subdivision (3)(b): recipients must keep their principal place of business or primary operations in the high-poverty area for at least three years. If they fail, the county may recapture funds on a pro rata basis for the portion of the three-year period not complied with. Recaptured monies must be returned to the Convention Center Support Fund or to an applicable county‑administered development fund, as the county decides. Counties may adopt resolutions setting enforcement policies (notice, repayment schedules, documentation) and retain all other legal or contractual remedies in addition to recapture.
