Summary

Requires the State Treasurer, after audit and annual certification, to transfer certified amounts into a newly created Convention Center Support Fund; the state investment officer may invest Fund moneys under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Legislature may direct transfers from the Fund to the General Fund.

Authorizes appropriation from the Fund of up to 70% of state sales tax revenue attributable to approved convention center facilities to political subdivisions (with an unspecified aggregate cap); the remaining 30% is to be transferred to the Civic and Community Center Financing Fund. For metropolitan-class cities, 10% of certain appropriations (and all funds under a related subdivision) must be equally distributed to high-poverty areas, with 55% for historical/violence-reduction projects and 45% for small business/entrepreneurship. Counties may recapture funds pro rata if recipients relocate within three years. Annual committee reports to the Legislature required. Operative January 1, 2027.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a 3-year residency/operations requirement for any entity receiving funding from the committee under subdivision (3)(b): recipients must keep their principal place of business or primary operations within the high‑poverty area for at least three years. If they fail to comply, the county where the recipient is located may recapture funds on a pro rata basis for the period out of compliance. Recaptured money must be returned to the Convention Center Support Fund or to a county‑administered development fund (as the county chooses). Counties may adopt resolutions setting enforcement policies, notice/repayment terms, and documentation standards. Recapture is in addition to any other legal or contractual remedies.
