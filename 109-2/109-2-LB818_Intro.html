Summary

LB818 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-310.01 and §28-323 to revise elements and penalties for assault by strangulation/suffocation and domestic assault. Assault by strangulation/suffocation is defined as knowingly impeding breathing by throat/neck pressure or covering mouth/nose; it is a Class IIIA felony, elevated to Class IIA if a dangerous instrument was used, serious bodily injury resulted, or the offender has a prior conviction of the same or an equivalent out-of-state/federal offense. Domestic assault definitions and degree-level elements are revised (third-, second-, and first-degree distinctions tied to intent, recklessness, dangerous instrument use, and serious bodily injury). Penalties are increased in multiple circumstances based on prior convictions, including mandatory minimums for repeat first-degree offenders. No state agencies or departments are referenced and there are no funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text defines aggravated intimate-partner offenses and escalates penalties based on prior convictions. It criminalizes intentionally or recklessly causing serious bodily injury to an intimate partner with a dangerous instrument, and sets punishments by subsection: generally a Class I misdemeanor for first-time violations of subsection (1), elevated to Class IIIA felony after one prior relevant conviction and to Class IIA felony after two or more. Violations of subsection (2) are Class IIA (upgraded to Class II with prior convictions). Violations of subsection (3) are Class ID, rising to Class IB with a mandatory minimum 15-year term for repeat offenders. No government agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
