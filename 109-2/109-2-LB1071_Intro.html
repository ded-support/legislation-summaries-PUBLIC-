Summary

LB1071 directs the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) to monitor appropriations across multiple agencies (DHHS, Corrections, Veterans' Affairs, OCIO/Information Management Services, State colleges, Historical Society, State Patrol, State Building Division, Personnel, Capitol Commission), authorize administrative transfers/reallocations of certified unexpended balances, increase salary limits on certification, schedule large General Fund transfers (Property Tax Credit Cash Fund $422M→$442M; School Property Tax Relief Credit $780M→$808M; Education Future Fund $242M/year), and administer capital construction reappropriations.

Key agency funding changes: OCIO Operations Revolving Fund ~$133.7M (FY25‑26) and ~$134.4M (FY26‑27); DHHS GF increases (Administration ~$188.7M→$213.4M; Behavioral Health ~$51.1M→$54.0M) plus targeted opioid, youth behavioral health, and gambling-service cash appropriations; Dept. of Agriculture totals ~$21.85M→$21.99M (includes $300K GF/year for AgrAbility); Dept. of Labor increases workforce‑development cash aid ($9.88M→$13.88M) and LB293 implementation funds; Dept. of Revenue tax‑credit distributions rise (School credit ~$798M→$808M; Property Tax Credit ~$467.7M→$490M). DOT, DMV, and Agriculture make small transfers to the Tort Claims Fund; DOT will pay State Patrol ~$489,979/year for road‑construction coverage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for FY25-26 through FY28-29; appropriates sums for FY2025-26 and FY2026-27; lapses unexpended General, Cash, and Revolving Fund balances as of June 30, 2025, but reappropriates certified encumbrances and unexpended FY2025-26 balances to FY2026-27. Establishes salary/per diem spending limitations, carve-outs for awards under Neb. law, and grants the Department of Administrative Services’ budget administrator authority to administratively increase those limits for payments to former employees. Specific funding highlights include Community College levy reduction (Cash Fund $5.46M → $5.73M) and major DHHS totals: FY25-26 GF $1,085,315,619 / Cash $740,239,274 / Federal $3,928,839,289; FY26-27 GF $802,423,273 / Cash $727,330,910 / Federal $3,624,210,663, plus multiple Health Care Cash Fund earmarks (state ward education, child welfare, screening programs). Department of Agriculture ($865) and DAS/state building ($5,630) small charges noted. No explicit new line-item appropriations shown for Banking & Finance, Economic Development, Insurance, Labor, Information Management Services, Correctional Services, Motor Vehicles, Revenue, Transportation, Veterans’ Affairs, or the Military Department.
