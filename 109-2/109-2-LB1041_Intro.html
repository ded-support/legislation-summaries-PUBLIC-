Summary

LB1041 requires Nebraska cities, villages, and counties to allow at least one accessory dwelling unit (ADU) on the same lot as a single‑family residence, subject to building codes and size limits (≤1,000 sq ft or 50% of the primary residence, as specified). Local historic ordinances, deed or association restrictions, and Condominium Act limits may still prohibit or limit ADUs. Manufactured/mobile homes used as ADUs must be placed on permanent foundations and assessed as real property. The bill bars cities/counties from imposing requirements on ADU placement, appearance, parking, utility connections, impact fees, occupancy (owner‑occupancy, familial/employment, income/age restrictions), or landlord‑tenant rules more restrictive than those for single‑family homes or the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act. Permits meeting statutory requirements must be approved on the same timeline as single‑family permits; conflicting local ordinances/resolutions are void. No changes to state appropriations or agency funding are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for local governments: cities (including villages) and counties must allow at least one accessory dwelling unit (ADU) on a single‑family lot, subject to building codes, size limits (≤1,000 sq ft or ≤50% of the primary dwelling), and narrow exceptions (historic, deed/HOA/condominium restrictions). Manufactured/mobile ADUs must be placed on permanent foundations and assessed as real property for tax purposes. Cities and counties are prohibited from imposing more restrictive placement, appearance, rental, parking, occupancy, utility, impact‑fee, or street/sidewalk requirements than those for the primary residence. ADU permits must be approved ministerially on the same schedule as single‑family permits; conflicting local ordinances/resolutions are void. No state agencies, appropriations, or budget/funding changes are specified.
