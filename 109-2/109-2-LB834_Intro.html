Summary

LB834 directs specific receipts and remittances involving the Department of Revenue and the Department of Motor Vehicles. Certification exam fees for county assessor applicants are to be paid to and credited to the Department of Revenue’s Property Assessment Division Cash Fund, with the Tax Commissioner setting the fee to cover exam costs. Sales tax on motor vehicles under §60-3,198 is collected at registration by the Department of Motor Vehicles; the DMV may retain its collection fee and deposit it into the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund. County treasurers or the DMV must remit collected taxes to the Tax Commissioner. In counties with population ≥100,000, $1 of the county treasurer’s collection fee for each of the first 5,000 vehicle registrations is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. Other bill provisions reference the Tax Commissioner and property tax officials but do not explicitly name the Department of Revenue or DMV.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary: The added text lets county assessors appoint revocable, written deputies (excluding treasurer, sheriff, clerk, surveyor), requires oath/bond, and allows deputies to perform assessor duties during absence/disability except when actions must be taken jointly with other officers. It gives the Property Tax Administrator rulemaking authority to define a “pressing need” for appointing deputies. It also adds annual reporting/permit requirements for mobile homes (reports to county assessor, forms provided by assessor, permits issued by county treasurer on completed application). No provisions mention the Department of Motor Vehicles, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue or other agencies are included.
