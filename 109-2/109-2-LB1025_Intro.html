Summary

Creates the Social Media Collection of Consumer Data Tax Act, imposing an excise tax (effective Jan 1, 2027) on social media platform businesses based on the number of Nebraska consumers whose data they collect. All tax receipts are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to a newly created Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund.

The Department of Health and Human Services will administer the Fund and use monies for juvenile mental health programming and facilities. The state investment officer will invest fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Tax Commissioner is authorized to prescribe returns, set filing/payment procedures (aligned with sales/use tax cycles), require records, adopt rules, and apply existing tax enforcement, confidentiality, refund, and appeal provisions.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective January 1, 2027, the bill imposes an excise tax on social media platform businesses based on monthly counts of Nebraska consumers; proceeds are remitted to the State Treasurer and credited to a newly created Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund. The Department of Health and Human Services will administer that fund and use it for juvenile mental health programming and facilities. Any fund balances may be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and the Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Tax Commissioner will prescribe returns, filing cycles, recordkeeping, and adopt rules; existing statutory enforcement, penalty, confidentiality, refund, and appeal provisions apply. No other direct appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
