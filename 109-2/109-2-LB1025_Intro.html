Summary

LB1025 (effective Jan. 1, 2027) establishes an excise tax on social media platform businesses and directs all tax receipts to a newly created Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund. Taxes collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to that fund. The Department of Health and Human Services will administer the fund and use it for programming and facilities for juvenile mental health services; funds available for investment will be managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Tax Commissioner prescribes returns, filing cycles (aligned with sales/use taxes), recordkeeping, and may adopt rules; existing statutory provisions for deficiencies, penalties, confidentiality, refunds, collections, and appeals apply.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates an excise tax (effective Jan 1, 2027) on social media platforms for collection of Nebraska consumer data and directs all tax receipts to the Juvenile Mental Health Support Fund. The State Treasurer will credit remitted taxes to that fund. The Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) will administer the fund and must use it for juvenile mental health programming and facilities. The state investment officer will invest fund balances under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The Tax Commissioner is charged with prescribing returns, filing/payment rules (aligned with sales/use tax cycles), recordkeeping, enforcement, and may adopt related regulations; existing deficiency/penalty/refund procedures apply. No separate appropriation language is included.
