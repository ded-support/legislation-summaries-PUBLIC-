Summary

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): The bill requires the DMV to issue and design numerous alternate license plates (including Arbor Day, Husker Red, Czech Heritage, Cornhusker Spirit, Support Our Troops, etc.). Arbor Day plates available Jan 1, 2026; Husker Red plates available Jan 1, 2027. Czech Heritage plate fees are designated to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund. Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit plates carry a $70 fee: 60% is credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 40% to the designated recipient (Highway Trust Fund). Personalized Support Our Troops plates ($70) credit 25% to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 75% to the designated recipient; alphanumeric Support Our Troops plates carry a $5 fee credited to the designated recipient.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): Amends creation/use of the Highway Cash Fund and Roads Operations Cash Fund, expressly permitting these funds to include money credited from section 60-3,163.02 (alternate plate fees). Provides monthly State Treasurer transfer rules from the Highway Trust Fund to the Highway Cash Fund (dependent on bond issuance) and authorizes legislative transfers between Highway Cash and Roads Operations Cash Funds. Specifies DOT-allowable expenditures from those funds and directs investment of idle balances with investment income credited to the Highway Cash Fund.

- Department of Administrative Services: no explicit references.
