Summary

LB1173 renames the "Contractor and Professional Employer Organization Registration Cash Fund" to the "Contractor, Business, and Professional Employer Organization Registration Cash Fund" and designates the Department of Labor as the administering agency. The fund will consist of fees collected by the department (including specified PEO registration fees: initial up to $2,500, annual renewal up to $1,500, limited registration up to $1,000), monies remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the fund, and sums appropriated by the Legislature. Permitted uses are expanded to finance enforcement and administration of the Employment Security Law and numerous labor statutes (Farm Labor Contractors Act; Contractor Registration Act; Employee Classification Act; PEO Registration Act; Non‑English‑Speaking Workers Protection Act; Wage and Hour Act; Nebraska Wage Payment and Collection Act; Nebraska Healthy Families and Workplaces Act; child labor provisions).
