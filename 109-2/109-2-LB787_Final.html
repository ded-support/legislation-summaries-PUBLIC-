Summary

LB787 (2026) amends membership and reporting for the Commission on Military and Veteran Affairs. It expressly names the Director of Veterans’ Affairs (or designee) as a voting member and adds the Department of Labor’s veterans’ program coordinator as a nonvoting, ex officio member. The bill also modifies when the commission must prepare its summary report (at gubernatorial request or on the commission’s own determination). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations affecting the Department of Labor or the Department of Veterans’ Affairs are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The underlined additions revise commission membership/appointment wording (clarifying designees and listing specific USAF wing commanders, Defense POW/MIA director, DoD service representative, future wing/headquarters commanders) and add a clause allowing action "at the request of the Governor or upon the commission's own determination." There is no reference to the Department of Labor or the Department of Veterans' Affairs, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
