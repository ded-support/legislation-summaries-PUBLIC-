Summary

LB1212 amends the Medicine and Surgery Practice Act to authorize the Department (with the Board of Medicine's recommendation) to license internationally trained physicians via time‑limited provisional and transitional licenses. Participating health care entities (e.g., federally qualified health centers, hospitals) must be approved by the Department/Board to provide board‑approved assessment and evaluation programs; the Nebraska Rural Health Advisory Commission designates health‑profession shortage areas. The Board reviews assessment reports and may recommend licensure actions; the Department may discipline, suspend, or revoke provisional/transitional licenses. Appeals follow the Administrative Procedure Act.

The bill also amends the Engineers and Architects Regulation Act, delegating seal application rules and exam/temporary‑permit procedures to the Board of Engineers and Architects, and retains references to the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment for certain permit exclusions and CPI adjustments for public‑works thresholds.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a pathway for "internationally trained physicians" and assigns roles to state entities: the department (which issues and renews provisional and transitional licenses, prescribes forms, approves participating health care entities, and may discipline or suspend those licenses) and the board (which recommends approving entities, reviews assessment reports, and advises the department on issuance, denial, renewal, and discipline). The Nebraska Rural Health Advisory Commission designates "health profession shortage areas" (per §71‑5665). The Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates is cited for foreign medical school recognition. Participating health care entities (FQHCs, hospitals, etc.) must provide assessment/evaluation reports; appeals follow the Administrative Procedure Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
