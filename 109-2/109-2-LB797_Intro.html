Summary

LB797 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§16-321 and 17-568.01 governing cities of the first class, cities of the second class, and villages. It raises the municipal competitive-bidding/approval thresholds so that contracts for enlargements or general improvements costing over $90,000 require prior approval and advertised bids. It also updates municipal electric utility bid-waiver tiers (waivers allowed up to $90,000 and higher graduated thresholds up to $1,000,000 depending on gross annual retail sales). The bill preserves emergency waiver authority and authorizes boards of public works to use engineering staff or hire consultants. Original sections are repealed. No state appropriations or funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language sets dollar ceilings for municipal electric utilities based on gross annual retail sales: (e) up to $500,000 where revenue exceeds $20 million; (f) up to $750,000 where revenue exceeds $30 million; and (g) up to $1,000,000 where revenue exceeds $75 million. No state agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to appropriations or budgetary allocations in the added text.
