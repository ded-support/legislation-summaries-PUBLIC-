Summary

LB1149 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §73-302 to change procedures for state agencies seeking to contract with private entities. Agencies must submit to the Director of Administrative Services: the proposed contract; a long-term actual cost‑savings review; measurable service‑quality goals; an assessment of in‑house alternatives; and any contracts the private entity has with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). It specifies how agencies must calculate projected cost savings for 12‑ and 60‑month periods, detailing direct costs, attributable indirect overhead, transitional costs, and additional state staffing/space costs for oversight. The bill repeals the original section. No new appropriations or funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new subparagraph (e) requiring disclosure or coverage of any contracts that the referenced private entity has entered into with United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
