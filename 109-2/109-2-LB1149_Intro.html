Summary

LB1149 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §73‑302 to expand reporting requirements for any state agency seeking to contract out services under §73‑301. Agencies must submit to the Director of Administrative Services: the proposed contract; a long‑term actual cost‑savings review; measurable service‑quality goals; an assessment of in‑house alternatives; and disclosure of any contracts the proposed private contractor has with U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). The bill also retains detailed twelve‑ and sixty‑month cost projection requirements (direct costs, attributable indirect overhead, transitional costs, and additional state performance costs). The original section is repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed by this bill.
