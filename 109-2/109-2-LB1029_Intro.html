Summary

LB1029 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-906 to redefine terms and require biannual reporting of “reportable funding” from a defined foreign adversarial source by covered two- and four‑year public and private institutions to the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education. Reports must include amount and date, purpose/relationship, identifying details of the foreign source (name and country when not a foreign government), and copies of related agreements. The Coordinating Commission must post reports on its public website. The bill cites foreign adversary determinations per 15 C.F.R. 791.4 (as of Jan 1, 2025) and exempts student tuition/fee payments by natural persons and salary/compensation from reporting. No changes to appropriations, budget allocations, or agency funding are included. The original section is repealed.
