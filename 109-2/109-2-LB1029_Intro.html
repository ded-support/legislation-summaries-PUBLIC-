Summary

LB1029 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-906 to redefine terms and require public and private two- and four-year Nebraska colleges (“covered institutions”) to biannually report “reportable funding” received from foreign adversarial sources to the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education. Reportable funding is defined to include the value of contracts, gifts, grants, endowments, awards, scholarships, donations, and pledges (excluding salary/wages/compensation). Reports must detail amounts, dates, purposes, source identification (including subsidiaries/agents and country information where applicable), and copies of related agreements. Payments from a natural person for a student’s tuition/fees are exempt. The Coordinating Commission must post reports on its public website. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are changed. Foreign adversary is determined per 15 C.F.R. 791.4.
