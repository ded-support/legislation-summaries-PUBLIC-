Summary

LB946 expresses the Legislature’s intent to appropriate $7,046,697 for FY2026-27 to the Department of Health and Human Services for Program No. 348. The amount is specified as $3,104,775 General Funds and $3,941,922 Federal Funds to raise Medicaid assisted-living facility rates paid under Nebraska’s aged and disabled home- and community-based Medicaid 1915(c) waiver to a daily rate of $78.45 for both rural and urban assisted-living waiver days.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Legislature intends to appropriate $7,046,697 to the Department of Health and Human Services for FY2026‑27 ( $3,104,775 General Funds and $3,941,922 Federal Funds ) to raise Medicaid assisted‑living facility rates in Program No. 348. The funds are designated to increase the daily rate for Nebraska’s aged-and-disabled home- and community‑based 1915(c) waiver assisted‑living services to $78.45 for FY2026‑27, applying to both rural and urban assisted‑living waiver days.
