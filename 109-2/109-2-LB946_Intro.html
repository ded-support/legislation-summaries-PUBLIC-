Summary

LB946 expresses legislative intent to appropriate $7,046,697 for FY2026‑27 to the Department of Health and Human Services for Medicaid assisted‑living facility rates (Program No. 348). The funding split is $3,104,775 General Funds and $3,941,922 Federal Funds. The appropriation is designated to increase the Medicaid assisted‑living daily rate to $78.45 for FY2026‑27 for both rural and urban assisted‑living waiver days under Nebraska’s Aged and Disabled Home and Community‑Based Medicaid 1915(c) waiver program.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS will receive a targeted FY2026‑27 appropriation of $7,046,697 ( $3,104,775 General Funds and $3,941,922 Federal Funds) for Program No. 348. The funds must be used to raise Medicaid assisted‑living facility rates paid under Nebraska’s aged and disabled home-and-community-based 1915(c) waiver to a daily rate of $78.45 for FY2026‑27, covering both rural and urban assisted‑living waiver days.
