Summary

LB874 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 28-306 and 28-394 to increase criminal penalties where motor vehicle homicide (including homicide of an unborn child) is proximately caused by violations of specified motor-vehicle statutes (60-6,179.01; 60-6,179.02; 60-6,213; 60-6,214; 60-6,196; 60-6,197.06). Offenses tied to certain sections are reclassified to felony levels (Class IIIA, IIA, or II depending on the statute and prior convictions). Courts may impose driver bans and order suspension or revocation of operator’s licenses (terms vary by offense; felony-level bans can reach up to 15 years). Court orders are to be administered at sentencing, on final appeal, or upon probation revocation. Original sections are repealed. No state agencies are specifically named, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
