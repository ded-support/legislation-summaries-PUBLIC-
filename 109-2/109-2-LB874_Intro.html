Summary

LB874 (Judiciary Committee) increases criminal penalties and driver's-license sanctions for motor vehicle homicide and motor vehicle homicide of an unborn child when the proximate cause is violations of statutory provisions referenced in the bill (including sections 60-6,179.01; 60-6,179.02; 60-6,213; 60-6,214; 60-6,196; and 60-6,197.06). Courts retain authority to order license suspension for up to two years for lower-level convictions and must order lengthy license revocation for more serious felony convictions (up to fifteen years; for unborn-child offenses revocation between 60 days and 15 years, not concurrent with jail). The bill affects court sentencing and city/village ordinance enforcement and repeals the prior versions of the amended sections. No appropriations, funding changes, or state agency budget allocations are included.
