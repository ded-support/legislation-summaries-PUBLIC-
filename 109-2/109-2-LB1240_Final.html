Summary

LB1240 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-1403 (the ABLE/achieving a better life experience program) to provide that, upon the death of an account beneficiary and after the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services has obtained approval from the federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, the state shall not seek recovery from the beneficiary's ABLE account for Medicaid benefits received after the account’s establishment and shall not file a claim under IRC §529A(f). The bill contains no appropriations or changes to DHHS funding.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the phrase “any amount distributed from the account of the designated beneficiary upon the death of the designated beneficiary,” thereby explicitly bringing post‑death distributions from a designated beneficiary’s account within the statute’s scope. The addition does not reference or change Department of Health and Human Services authorities or programs, nor does it include any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS.
