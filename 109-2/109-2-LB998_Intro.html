Summary

LB998 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2716 to expand state income tax treatment for members of the Nebraska National Guard. It allows individuals who are Guard members to exclude 100% of specified Guard compensation from Nebraska taxable income: for tax years 2025–2026 the exclusion applies to pay from 32 U.S.C. duty statuses, employment as a 32 U.S.C. federal dual-status technician, or state active duty; beginning January 1, 2027 the exclusion expressly includes pay from 10 U.S.C. or 32 U.S.C. military duty status. The statute remains administered under the state tax code (Tax Commissioner). No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are contained in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a state individual income tax exclusion, effective for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2027, allowing Nebraska National Guard members to exclude 100% of income (to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income) from: service in a 10 U.S.C. or 32 U.S.C. military duty status, employment as a 32 U.S.C. federal dual‑status technician with the Nebraska National Guard, or service in state active duty. References the Internal Revenue Code and federal statutes (10 U.S.C., 32 U.S.C.). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
