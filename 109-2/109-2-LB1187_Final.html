Summary

LB1187 amends the Livestock Brand Act, principally affecting the Nebraska Brand Committee and related agencies. Key agency provisions:

- Nebraska Brand Committee: membership expanded from five to seven voting members with district-based representation (effective Aug 28, 2026); Secretary of State and Director of Agriculture remain nonvoting, ex officio members. Terms staggered; committee elects chair/vice-chair annually.

- Executive director: appointed by the committee subject to Governor approval; is administrative head, custodian of records, supervises employees, and may hire/fire. Chief investigator reports to the executive director; a brand recorder role is defined.

- Fees and audits: $50 late-notice surcharge; physical inspection fee capped at $1.50/head; travel surcharge up to $30 per location; out-of-area inspections may include DAS mileage rates and up to $30/hour. Registered feedlot fees set per 1,000-head unit equal to 25% of the inspection fee; annual renewal. Creates registered dairy heifer development facility registration with annual fee equal to 25% multiplied by facility capacity as authorized by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment. Brand committee may audit registered feedlots and dairy facilities (generally twice/year, more for cause).

- No explicit appropriations or statewide budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions and regulatory authority to the Nebraska Brand Committee. Key agency mentions and fiscal provisions:

- Nebraska Brand Committee expanded: seven Governor‑appointed, Legislature‑confirmed voting members representing five geographic districts and cattle industry roles; Secretary of State and Director of Agriculture serve as nonvoting ex officio members. Executive director of the Committee provides application forms.

- Department of Water, Energy, and Environment: used to determine facility maximum capacity for fee calculation under the Livestock Waste Management Act.

- Brand inspectors collect inspection fees and any surcharge set under section 54‑1,108; the Committee may also establish a travel surcharge up to $30 per inspection location.

- Registered dairy heifer development facility registration fee = 25% × facility maximum capacity (annual). Registrations may be audited (generally twice/year), revoked for cause, and shipping‑certificate/inspection rules apply.
