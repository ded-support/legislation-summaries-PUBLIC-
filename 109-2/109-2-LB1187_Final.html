Summary

LB1187 revises administration of the Livestock Brand Act, chiefly affecting the Nebraska Brand Committee. The committee’s membership expands to seven voting gubernatorial appointees (five district-based cattle producers, one cattle feeder, one livestock auction operator) with the Secretary of State and the Director of Agriculture as nonvoting, ex officio members. The executive director appointment is subject to Governor approval and is designated brand committee head with hiring, supervisory, probation, and discharge authority. The committee must employ a chief investigator and a brand recorder.

Fee and registration changes: physical inspection fee capped at $1.50/head; $50 late‑notice surcharge; travel surcharge up to $30; out‑of‑area inspections may include DAS mileage rates and up to $30/hour. Registered feedlot fees are 25% of the inspection fee per 1,000‑head increment (annual renewal). Registered dairy heifer development facilities are created; registration fee equals 25% multiplied by facility capacity as authorized by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment. No general fund appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates and defines the Nebraska Brand Committee and related terms; establishes committee composition (seven governor‑appointed, Legislature‑confirmed voting members representing five brand districts, cattle feeders, and an auction market; plus two nonvoting ex officio members—the Secretary of State and the Director of Agriculture). Grants the committee authority to inspect and audit registered feedlots and newly created registered dairy heifer development facilities (application, 30‑day investigation, one‑year registration, records retention, audits up to twice/year—10% of lot files—with procedures for additional audits). Financial provisions: annual registration fee for dairy heifer facilities set as "twenty‑five percent multiplied by" the facility’s maximum capacity as authorized by the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment; brand inspectors collect inspection fees and may charge a travel surcharge up to $30 per inspection location (fees collected at time of inspection). No direct appropriations or agency budget allocations are specified.
