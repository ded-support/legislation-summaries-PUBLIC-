Summary

LB1114 requires two state agencies to provide standard forms: the Department of Economic Development must develop the standard redevelopment-plan form (to be used by redevelopers) specifying items such as existing and proposed uses, years the structure or lot has been within city limits, current assessed value, estimated valuation increase, whether division-of-taxes financing is used, and agreed project costs. The Department of Revenue must develop a standard certification form for county assessors to certify completion, occurrence and amount of any valuation increase, and that the increase resulted from the improvements—enabling use of tax-division receipts to pay related indebtedness. The bill contains no appropriations or funding changes for either agency.
