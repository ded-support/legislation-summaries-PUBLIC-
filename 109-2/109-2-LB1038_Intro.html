Summary

The bill centralizes timing and installment authority with the budget administrator in the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). It directs General Fund transfers: $1,000,000,000 (FY2023–24) and $250,000,000 (FY2024–25) to the Education Future Fund (with an expressed intent for an additional $250,000,000 in FY2025–26 and unspecified future transfers), and a $750,000,000 transfer (FY2024–25) to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund. The Department of Education must certify state aid amounts to the Director of Administrative Services; when the State Board authorizes lump‑sum payments of postponed aid, the Director is to draw warrants from appropriated funds and forward them to districts. No provisions explicitly reference the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue

- Adds an ongoing duty for the Tax Commissioner (beginning after FY2025‑26): within 15 days after each fiscal year end, compare actual General Fund net receipts to certified estimates and certify any excess to the State Treasurer for transfer to the Cash Reserve Fund.

- Terminates the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund (money to transfer to the new Education Future Fund) and limits the Property Tax Credit Act to tax years through 2025 — changes that will affect tax administration and credit processing.
