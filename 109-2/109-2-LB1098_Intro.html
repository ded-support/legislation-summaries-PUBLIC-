Summary

LB1098 designates the Department of Transportation (defined as “Department”) as the state rail transportation authority responsible for preparing, coordinating, maintaining, and administering a state rail plan. The Department must annually study passenger rail service (study completed by Nov. 1) and electronically deliver a findings report to the Clerk of the Legislature by Dec. 31. The Department must prepare an initial state rail plan by June 30, 2027, review and may revise it at least annually, submit each prepared/revised plan to the Governor within 10 days, and upon gubernatorial approval submit it to the U.S. Secretary of Transportation. The bill authorizes the Department to conduct studies, collect public feedback, and adopt rules to implement the Act. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "State Rail Plan Act" and assigns the Department of Transportation as the state's rail transportation authority (Governor or designee is the state plan approval authority). Key DOT duties and deadlines:

- Annual passenger-rail study (completed by Nov. 1) and report to the Clerk of the Legislature by Dec. 31 each year.

- Prepare a federally compliant state rail plan by June 30, 2027; maintain and review at least annually.

- Submit any prepared or revised plan to the Governor within 10 days; Governor must approve/deny within 30 days and notify the department and Clerk; after approval, DOT must send the plan to the U.S. Secretary of Transportation within 10 days.

- Plan content and required stakeholder/public input are specified; DOT may conduct studies, collect feedback, and adopt rules to implement the act.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
