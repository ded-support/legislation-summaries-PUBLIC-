Summary

LB1160 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §30-2323 to clarify priorities for exempt property and assets used to make up deficiencies in an estate. The amendment expressly excepts claims filed by the Department of Health and Human Services pursuant to section 68-919 from the priority otherwise given to exempt property, stating such DHHS claims apply notwithstanding the order of payment in section 30-2487. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Health and Human Services focus):

The added text revises probate, intestacy, trust, and inheritance rules: civil procedure governs probate proceedings unless inconsistent with the Uniform Probate Code; intestate succession and a testator's ability to exclude or limit heirs are clarified; standards for establishing trusts, trust contracts, and certification of trust are specified; anti‑penalty provisions for contesting trusts (unenforceable if probable cause) and application of will‑construction rules to trusts are added; homestead‑ownership information for exemption claims and a detailed list of persons subject to inheritance tax are included. Several dollar thresholds appear inserted for estate/benefit limits.

No provisions change or allocate funding, appropriations, or budget authority to the Department of Health and Human Services.
