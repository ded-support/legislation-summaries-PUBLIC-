Summary

LB1160 amends probate law (see revised 30-2323(2)) to clarify priority of exempt property and related allowances. It provides that rights to exempt property and assets used to make up any deficiency generally have priority over all claims against the estate except (1) costs and expenses of administration and (2) claims filed by the Department of Health and Human Services pursuant to section 68-919, which retain priority notwithstanding the order of payment established in section 30-2487. No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies provisions to probate and trust law: governs proceedings under the Uniform Probate Code by civil procedure rules; confirms intestate succession rules and that wills may limit heirs' intestate shares; establishes how a settlor's intent for trusts is proven (trust instrument or admissible evidence) and when trust terms may be set or amended (trustee action, court order, or nonjudicial settlement); limits how contracts to create or not revoke trusts are proved; voids penalizing trust-contest clauses when probable cause exists; applies will-construction rules to trusts; and includes homestead-ownership information for exemption purposes and enumerates persons subject to inheritance tax. No provisions alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services.
