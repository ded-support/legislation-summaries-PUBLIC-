Summary

The bill amends authorities and fee/assessment procedures involving the Department of Banking and Finance and its director. The director may examine and electronically monitor state-chartered institutions, share examinations with federal agencies, and accept federal or foreign agency examinations (which remain the providing agency’s confidential records). The Director of Banking and Finance must annually estimate required sums (per §8-604) and expected collections (§8-603) to set assessments; assessments may be based on total assets and may also consider fiduciary and off-balance-sheet items, with licensed/registered entities assessed by activity volume. The director may prorate assessments and levy special assessments if needed. The department may set payment deadlines (≥30 days), impose fines after notice/hearing for nonpayment, suspend/revoke charters/licenses for arrears ≥60 days, and permit installment payments. No explicit appropriations, transfers, or budget allocations are included in the provided text.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that the Department of Banking and Finance’s assessment (fee) on each licensed or registered entity — including delayed‑deposit, installment loan and sales, money transmission, and residential mortgage licensees — will be calculated based on the entity’s total volume of licensable activity (e.g., transaction volume, number of makers, loan originations, loan servicing, money transmitted), with the specific measure chosen to match the license type. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
