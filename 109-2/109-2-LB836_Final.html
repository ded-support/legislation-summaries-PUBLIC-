Summary

LB836 amends statutes governing the Department of Banking and Finance. The Director is authorized to examine (including electronically), accept and share examination reports with federal or foreign agencies (accepted reports remain property/confidential of the provider). The Director must annually estimate sums required for department purposes and expected collections and use the difference to set assessments on financial institutions (assessments based on assets and may include fiduciary/off‑balance‑sheet items; proration and special assessments allowed if collections are insufficient). Costs of extra examinations and out‑of‑state travel may be billed to the examined entity. For nonpayment, the Department may impose daily fines after notice/hearing, suspend or revoke charters/licenses after 60 days arrears, and allow installment payments for assessed amounts.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Banking and Finance: the bill adds that assessments on licensed or registered entities (including delayed‑deposit, installment loans/sales, money transmission, and residential mortgage licensees) must be calculated using measures tied to licensable activity — e.g., total transaction volume, number of makers, loan originations or servicing, or money‑transmission volume — appropriate to each license type. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or departmental budget allocations are included in the added text.
