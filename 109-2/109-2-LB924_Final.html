Summary

LB924 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§77-3442, 79-2104, and 79-2111 to (1) expand and clarify authorized uses of learning community levies and (2) add coordinating-council powers. Key points affecting government entities and funding:

- Learning communities may levy (per §77-3442): up to 0.5¢ per $100 of taxable value for leasing/purchasing and remodeling elementary learning centers and for up to 50% of estimated capital costs for approved focus school/program projects; and up to 1.5¢ per $100 for early childhood programs, elementary learning center staff, contracts for center services, and pilot projects (administration capped at 10%).

- Proceeds may reduce school-district bonded indebtedness (transferred to district special building funds) and councils may approve project funding (§79-2111). If facilities cease qualifying use within 10 years, repayment with interest is required (waiver possible).

- Learning community coordinating councils gain explicit authority to partner with public and private entities to increase high school graduation rates and to coordinate with Department of Education staff designated by the Commissioner.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority language concerning the learning community’s administrative staff to lease or purchase facilities, repeats leasing/purchasing facility authority, and adds a duty to partner with public and private entities to support increasing high school graduation rates. No specific government agencies or departments are named and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
