Summary

LB924 amends levy and learning-community statutes. Key agency references and fiscal effects:

- Department of Education: learning community coordinating councils may administer funds, approve focus schools/programs, establish elementary learning centers with educational service units, collect/report data with Commissioner-designated staff, and "partner with public and private entities to support increasing high school graduation rates."

- Tax levy changes (section 77-3442): learning communities may levy (a) up to 0.5¢ per $100 valuation for leasing/purchasing and remodeling elementary learning center facilities and up to 50% of estimated costs for approved focus school/program capital projects, and (b) up to 1.5¢ per $100 for early childhood programs, elementary learning center staff, contracts, and pilots (≤10% of this levy for administrative staff).

- Fund flow (79-2111): levy proceeds may reduce bonded indebtedness for approved projects by up to 50%; those funds transfer to the recipient school district's special building fund. Repayment and interest rules apply if facilities are repurposed within ten years.

- Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (Chief Water Officer) and DOT Division of Aeronautics remain referenced in levy provisions.
