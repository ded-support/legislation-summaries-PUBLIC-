Summary

LB741 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43-4202 to specify that two Department of Health and Human Services officials—the Director of Children and Family Services (Division of Children and Family Services) and the Director of Behavioral Health (Division of Behavioral Health), or their designees—are nonvoting, ex officio members of the Nebraska Children's Commission. As nonvoting members, they may attend meetings and participate in discussions. The bill also retains the Legislature’s intent that commission operations be funded from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund for fiscal years 2019‑20 and 2020‑21.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the chairperson of the Commission on African American Affairs (or their designee) as a member of the specified body. No references to the Department of Health and Human Services, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
