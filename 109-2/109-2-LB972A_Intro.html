Summary

LB972A appropriates funds to implement LB972 as follows:

- Department of Revenue (Program 102): $72,400 General Fund for FY2026‑27 and $69,400 General Fund for FY2027‑28. Limit on permanent/temporary salaries and per diems from these funds: $50,700 (FY2026‑27) and $52,200 (FY2027‑28).

- Military Department (Program 544): $13,500 from the Military Department Cash Fund for FY2026‑27 and $30,000 for FY2027‑28. Each year’s appropriation is designated as Cash Funds for state aid only. No expenditures for permanent/temporary salaries or per diems may be made from these funds.

- Department of Motor Vehicles (Program 90): $520,700 from the License Plate Cash Fund for FY2026‑27 and $528,900 for FY2027‑28. No expenditures for permanent/temporary salaries or per diems may be made from these funds.
