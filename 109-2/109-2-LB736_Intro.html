Summary

LB736 amends section 38-131 to require fingerprint-based FBI criminal background checks for initial licensure of numerous health professions. Applicants must authorize release of FBI national criminal history results to "the department" and pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the check. The bill clarifies that, for FBI interpretation, "the department" means the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added Athletic Trainer Licensure Compact requires each State licensing agency (e.g., a state's Department of Health and Human Services if it serves as the Athletic Trainer Licensing Authority) to: adopt model compact language; license/regulate athletic trainers; require continuing competence; perform FBI-based criminal background checks (including fingerprinting) for Compact‑qualifying licenses; participate in the Compact Commission's Data System and report adverse actions/ investigative information; and enforce Commission rules. The Commission may collect fees, accept grants/donations, levy annual assessments on Member States and fees on licensees, establish a budget, and audit finances. No direct appropriations or specific DHHS budget allocations are specified; however DHHS may incur implementation costs and could be subject to the Commission’s annual assessments.
