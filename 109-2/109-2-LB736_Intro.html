Summary

The bill amends section 38-131 to require applicants for initial professional licenses (including athletic trainers) to submit fingerprints and authorize release of FBI criminal-history results to "the department." For purposes of FBI interpretation, the term "department" is defined as the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. Applicants must pay the actual cost of fingerprinting and the criminal background check, and the department together with the Nebraska State Patrol may adopt rules concerning those costs. The bill contains no direct appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations to the Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions specifically reference the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) or direct DHHS appropriations. The added compact text creates an interstate Athletic Trainer Licensure Compact requiring State licensing authorities (often housed in state health agencies) to: participate in a centralized Data System; report adverse actions and investigative information; perform FBI criminal-background checks (fingerprints/biometrics); and enforce compact rules. The Compact Commission may assess member-states and charge fees to licensees, establish a budget, hire staff, and audit finances. While there are no explicit funding or appropriation changes to DHHS, the compact could impose indirect fiscal duties on state health agencies (IT/data submissions, background-check costs, staffing, and potential assessments or fee administration).
