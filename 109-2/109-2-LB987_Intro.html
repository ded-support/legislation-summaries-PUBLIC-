Summary

The bill references the Department of Insurance in two places. Section 3(1)(h) requires insurers and vision benefit managers to disclose all formal complaints filed with the Department of Insurance against them in the current year and the preceding five years. Section 9(3) makes communications that distinguish or claim superiority among participating eye care providers "readily subject to verification by the Department of Insurance." No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions require insurers and vision benefit managers to disclose extensive information — including all federal/state litigation and all formal complaints to the Department of Insurance from the current year and prior five years — in plain language on a public website and in a separately titled "Required Transparency Information for Patients, Doctors, and Purchasers" document provided with sales/enrollment/contract materials. Insurers/vision benefit managers must also: promptly notify providers of proposed contract changes; maintain phone, mail, and email access (acknowledging email within 1 hour, substantive reply within 72 hours); make current agreements/fee schedules continuously available online; and submit all plan documents on request to any state agency with regulatory/enforcement authority. Communications distinguishing providers must be verifiable by the Department of Insurance. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
