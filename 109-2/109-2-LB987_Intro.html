Summary

The bill requires insurers and vision benefit managers to disclose, as part of mandated transparency information, all formal complaints filed with the Department of Insurance against the insurer or manager in the current year and the preceding five years. It also makes certain provider‑distinguishing communications (e.g., advertised discounts/incentives for noncovered items; dollar/volume/percent usage of purchased materials; brand/source/manufacturer of covered materials) subject to verification by the Department of Insurance. No provisions in the bill change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Insurance.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds transparency and enforcement provisions tied to the Department of Insurance: insurers and vision benefit managers must disclose litigation and all formal complaints to the Department, post a standardized "Required Transparency Information for Patients, Doctors, and Purchasers" on their websites and in plan documents, and produce fee schedules and plan materials on request. State agencies with jurisdiction may demand full plan documents for review. The Department is empowered to verify marketing/claims and to sanction insurers or vision benefit managers (including fines and other remedies) and to provide relief to aggrieved eye care providers. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Insurance are included.
