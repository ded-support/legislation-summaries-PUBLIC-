Summary

LB1003 creates the Renewable Energy Consumer Protection Act and amends the Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act. Agency-related provisions:

- Secretary of State: renewable energy sales companies and agents must register with the Secretary of State and show proof of registration to customers; failure to disclose makes the agreement void.

- State Electric Board: must adopt rules for licensing and bonding of persons who install renewable energy and battery storage systems.

- Municipalities / State of Nebraska: installers must obtain relevant local municipal or State permits before installation.

- Electric utilities (those serving >5,000 meters): must prominently publish on their websites details of available financial incentives, budget spent, application procedures, and timing for securing incentives.

- Advertising/consent: use of a state incentive program's name requires written consent from the administering agency.

Violations are added to the deceptive trade practices statute. Operative January 1, 2027. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (focus on agencies and budgets):

- Requires renewable energy sales companies and agents to register with the Secretary of State and display proof to customers before contracting; failure to disclose voids the agreement.

- Directs the State Electric Board to adopt rules for licensing and bonding installers of renewable energy and battery storage systems.

- Requires installers to hold Nebraska electrical contractor licenses and obtain municipal or state permits.

- Obligates electric utilities serving over 5,000 meters to post on their websites: number of available financial incentives, budget spent-to-date (and last update), how to apply, and where applicants stand in the process.

- References local utilities for interconnection approvals and the state agency administering any incentive program for consent to use program affiliation.

- No new appropriations or budget allocations are created.
