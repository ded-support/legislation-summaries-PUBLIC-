Summary

LB1003 (operative Jan. 1, 2027) creates the Renewable Energy Consumer Protection Act and imposes regulatory requirements involving several government entities. Key agency provisions:

- Secretary of State: renewable energy sales companies must register with the Secretary of State and provide proof to customers before contracting; failure to disclose renders agreements void.

- State Electric Board: directed to adopt rules for licensing and bonding of persons who install renewable energy or battery storage systems.

- Municipalities/State of Nebraska: installations require relevant local municipal or state permits.

- Electric utilities (serving >5,000 meters): must publish on their websites available financial incentives, budget spent-to-date, application procedures, and timing for securing incentives.

Violations are treated as deceptive trade practices under state law. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds consumer-protection requirements and administrative duties involving several government entities. Key agency mentions and duties:

- Secretary of State: renewable energy sales companies/agents must register with the Secretary of State and show proof before contracting; failure to disclose makes agreements void.

- State Electric Board: must adopt rules for licensing and bonding persons who install renewable energy or battery systems.

- Municipalities / State of Nebraska: installers must obtain relevant municipal or state permits; installers must be licensed as Nebraska electrical contractors.

- Electric utilities (those serving >5,000 meters): required to post online program/incentive availability, budget spent, application steps, and timing.

- Federal agencies referenced: Federal Trade Commission standards and Magnuson‑Moss warranty law govern cooling‑off and warranty disclosures.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
