Summary

The bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to establish by January 1, 2027 a program providing behavioral health services for individuals under 21 with complex, multisystem needs. DHHS must ensure the program supports families, prevents institutionalization and out‑of‑home placement, and delivers care management tailored to youth and families. DHHS is directed to provide services including mobile response and stabilization; intensive home‑based treatment; inpatient and outpatient care; psychiatric residential treatment; substance use disorder treatment; psychiatric care; and developmental disability care. Care coordination will occur through DHHS‑determined behavioral health regions. DHHS may procure a single statewide managed care plan, manage administrative coordination/utilization/quality activities, adopt rules, and seek CMS approvals for Medicaid reimbursement. No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS must establish by Jan 1, 2027 a program serving individuals under 21 with complex, multisystem behavioral health needs to support families and avoid institutional or out-of-home placements. Care must be individualized and may include mobile response/stabilization, intensive home-based treatment, inpatient/outpatient care, psychiatric residential treatment, substance use disorder treatment, psychiatric services, and developmental disability care. Care coordination will occur through behavioral health regions; DHHS may procure a single statewide managed care plan to ensure a full continuum (including residential and home- and community-based services). DHHS or the MCO may handle administrative care coordination, utilization management, and quality improvement. DHHS may adopt rules and seek CMS approvals (state plan amendment/waiver) to obtain Medicaid reimbursement. No explicit funding or appropriation changes are included.
