Summary

Creates a new Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) with five divisions (Behavioral Health; Children and Family Services; Disability and Aging; Medicaid and Long‑Term Care; Public Health). The Division of Disability and Aging will oversee Beatrice State Developmental Center and community developmental‑disability and aging services. DHHS must adopt a federal‑poverty‑based care‑management client income schedule (clients <300% FPL may be assessed 0–90% of costs), reimburse care‑management units based on actual casework with standardized rates reviewed at least every three years, and require those appropriations to be separate from Nebraska Community Aging Services Act funds. DHHS may award child‑care grants and use available federal funds to exceed state appropriation, operate the Commodity Supplemental Food program if federal funds exist, and accept federal grants/gifts into an Advisory Council Fund. It will require fingerprint FBI checks for many health professions (Division of Public Health handles submissions). Wholesale drug distributor fees are remitted to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund for licensure or prescription‑monitoring expenses. DHHS must collaborate on an Olmstead strategic plan with Correctional Services, Economic Development, Labor, Transportation, and Veterans' Affairs. State Personnel System exemptions reference Correctional Services, DHHS, Veterans' Affairs, Banking and Finance, and Insurance.
