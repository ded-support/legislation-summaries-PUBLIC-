Summary

The bill reorganizes and strengthens the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): creating five divisions including a Division of Disability and Aging; defining “department”/licensing background‑check authority as DHHS’s Division of Public Health; and requiring DHHS to staff and support a renamed Aging, Alzheimer’s, and Dementia Advisory Council (which also serves as an appeals panel and advises on the state aging plan and community aging services budget). DHHS must develop care‑management eligibility, client cost shares (up to 0–90% for ≤300% of poverty), and reimbursements based on actual casework with standardized rates (adjusted ≥every three years); those reimbursements and DHHS admin costs require separate appropriation from Nebraska Community Aging Services Act funds. DHHS will collaborate with Correctional Services, Economic Development, Labor, Transportation, and Veterans’ Affairs on an Olmstead plan. Child‑care grants may exceed program appropriations using available federal funds. Wholesale drug distributor fees go to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund to support licensure and the prescription drug monitoring program. Personnel exemptions are expanded to specified DHHS, Correctional Services, and Veterans’ Affairs employees and to deputies/examiners of Banking and Finance and Insurance. The state Commodity Supplemental Food program is authorized only while U.S. Department of Agriculture federal CSF funds are available.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

- May award grants beyond the state appropriation by using available federal funds.

- Must consider utilization when setting nursing-facility rates, report any deviation from the budgeted dollar amount to the Legislature, and maintain an average weighted daily rate floor (no lower than Jan 1, 2026, unless Legislature directs or during a gubernatorial emergency).

- Must apply to CMS by 12/31/2026 for a Money Follows the Person program.

- Creates the Aging, Alzheimer's, and Dementia Advisory Council Fund (federal funds, grants, gifts; remitted to State Treasurer and invested by the state investment officer).

- Establishes a youth afterschool eligibility letter for 16–17-year-olds (fee to reimburse state, provisional employment rules, renewal process, rulemaking).

- Allows care‑management clients to make voluntary income‑based contributions.

Department of Insurance

- Health-plan issuers must respond to DHHS claim inquiries within specified timelines and cannot deny DHHS claims solely for submission technicalities.

Department of Administrative Services / State Treasurer

- Fund receipts go to the State Treasurer; investments handled by the state investment officer per existing statutes.

Departments of Agriculture, Banking and Finance, Economic Development, Labor, Correctional Services, Transportation, and Veterans' Affairs

- No changes or funding actions identified.
