Summary

LB913 amends the Alzheimer's Disease and Other Dementia Support Act (sections 71-561 to 71-567) to require the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services to appoint a dementia services coordinator. The coordinator’s duties include acting as a referral point for families, providing information/counseling/education/referral, collecting and monitoring dementia-related data, evaluating needs of individuals and caregivers, recommending interagency coordination strategies, monitoring and assisting with the state plan’s development and implementation, recommending policies/legislation/funding to implement the plan, increasing dementia-specific training and awareness, and organizing community stakeholders. The bill repeals the original section 71-561. No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language requires "the department" to appoint a dementia services coordinator. The coordinator’s duties include serving as a referral hub for families; providing information, counseling, education and referrals; collecting and monitoring state-level data on Alzheimer’s and other dementias; assessing needs of individuals and caregivers; recommending strategies for coordination among agencies; monitoring and assisting implementation of the state plan; recommending policies, legislation, and funding needed to implement that plan; developing dementia-specific training and awareness; and organizing community stakeholders and resources. No direct appropriations or budget allocations are established—only a duty to recommend funding and related policy.
