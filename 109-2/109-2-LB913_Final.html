Summary

LB913 amends section 71-561 of the Alzheimer's Disease and Other Dementia Support Act and requires the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to appoint a dementia services coordinator. The coordinator’s duties include serving as a referral point for families; providing information, counseling, education, and referrals; collecting and monitoring dementia-related data; evaluating needs of individuals and caregivers; coordinating services among state agencies; monitoring and assisting implementation of the state dementia plan; recommending policies, legislation, and funding; developing dementia-specific training; and organizing community stakeholders. The bill repeals the original section 71-561. No specific appropriations or changes to funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires "the department" to appoint a dementia services coordinator. The coordinator must: act as a referral hub linking families to supportive services; provide information, counseling, education and referrals (including safe, secure environments); collect and monitor state data on Alzheimer's/dementia; assess needs of affected individuals and caregivers; recommend interagency coordination strategies; monitor and assist implementation of the state plan; recommend policies, legislation, and funding to implement that plan; develop dementia-specific training and awareness; and organize community stakeholders. The provision references coordination among state agencies but does not itself create any new appropriations or budget allocations.
