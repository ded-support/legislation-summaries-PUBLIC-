Summary

LB887 amends pharmacy and Parkinson’s Disease Registry statutes.

Pharmacy/Board of Pharmacy effects:

- Board-determined passing grade retained; accredited pharmacy programs may set timing (before/after graduation) for the jurisprudence exam.

- A pharmacist may supervise up to four combined pharmacy technicians and pharmacist interns; pharmacist interns must be supervised except during accredited experiential training.

- If supervising four technicians, at least one must be a certified pharmacy technician (section 38-2890).

- Prescription-transfer rules: pharmacies sharing a real-time online database may transfer refills up to the maximum authorized by law and prescriber; recordkeeping and transfer-communication requirements clarified.

Department/Registry effects:

- Removes the department’s duty to receive and record reports under the Parkinson’s Disease Registry Act and repeals section 81-6,103; other Registry provisions referencing mandatory reporting are deleted.

- Department retains rulemaking, contracting authority, and must comply with requirements to obtain funds or grants. No explicit appropriations or new budget allocations provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that the jurisprudence exam may be taken before or after graduation at the timing set by the applicant’s accredited pharmacy program; permits transfer of prescription drugs or devices not listed in section 28-405 between pharmacies for refill dispensing provided transfers don’t exceed authorized refills and the original prescription remains valid; includes a fragmentary reference to “a report” without clear context. No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
