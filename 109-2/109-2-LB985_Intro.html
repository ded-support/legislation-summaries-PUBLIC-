Summary

LB985 amends Nebraska Probate Code sections 30-2628 and 30-2655. Key provisions affecting state courts and court processes:

- Requires guardians to file a complete inventory with the appointing court within 30 days, mail copies to the ward (if ≥14) and all interested persons, and file a certificate of mailing including a form to opt to continue receiving case notifications. Annual updated inventories must be filed when a guardian (not the conservator) controls estate assets, with certificates of mailing to interested persons and the bonding company if a bond is required.

- Affirms the court’s authority to approve out‑of‑state placements and to require professional evaluations for more restrictive placements; guardians must consider the ward’s prior expressed medical intent (without altering attorney‑in‑fact health‑care powers).

- Allows courts to limit conservator powers and to endorse limits on letters; conservators may not relocate a protected person outside Nebraska without court permission.

- Caps service: a person may not accept guardianship or conservatorship if already serving 20 individuals.

No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included.
