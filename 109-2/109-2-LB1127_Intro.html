Summary

LB1127 amends Nebraska Game Law to authorize and regulate new "hangtag" permits and to revise permit display and procurement rules. The Nebraska Game and Parks Commission (the commission) is directed to set permit types and fees: resident annual permits up to $50, nonresident at twice that amount; temporary permits up to $10 (nonresident double); hangtag permits priced as the sum of one resident annual permit, one duplicate annual permit, plus a convenience fee (convenience fee capped at $15); active‑duty military permits $5. Disabled‑veteran permits are issued by the commission without charge based on U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs determinations. Permits may be sold via central and district commission offices and designated state park areas; county offices and commission‑designated private agents may continue to issue permits and retain issuing fees under section 37‑406. The commission may adopt implementing rules.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new hangtag permit, effective Jan 1, 2027. The permit fee equals the cost of one resident annual permit plus one duplicate annual permit plus a commission-established convenience fee (capped at $15). The hangtag is valid through Dec. 31 of the issuance year, is vehicle-specific but may be moved between vehicles registered to the same Nebraska resident, and may not be reproduced or replaced if lost or stolen. Display rules require hanging from the rearview mirror or placement on the front dash if no mirror. Hangtags may be obtained from the commission’s central and district offices and at designated Nebraska state park system locations. No explicit appropriations or budget allocations are added.
