Summary

LB811 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§18-3404 and 18-3405 (Nebraska Municipal Land Bank Act). It clarifies formation options under the Interlocal Cooperation Act: a single municipality may create a land bank by ordinance, multiple municipalities may form one by interlocal agreement, and municipalities may join existing land banks by interlocal agreement. Land banks are confirmed as public corporations and political subdivisions of the State with the goal of returning vacant, abandoned, and tax-delinquent properties to productive use.

Board composition and governance requirements are detailed: voting members are appointed by municipal chief executive officers and confirmed by a two-thirds vote of governing bodies; nonvoting members include municipal planning directors (or designees) and a governing-body appointee; city or village clerks handle hearing filings. Specific expertise categories for voting members, quorum, voting thresholds for major actions, vacancy, removal, and officer-selection procedures are prescribed. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
