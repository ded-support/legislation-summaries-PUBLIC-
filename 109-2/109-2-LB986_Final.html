Summary

LB986 amends sections 49-1446.02 and 49-1446.03 of the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act to explicitly allow campaign committees to expend funds for security services and security systems. The bill adds security services to the list of permitted campaign expenditures (harmonizing related provisions that reference subdivision (10) of section 49-1446.03) and clarifies treatment of office-related expenditures tied to statutory cross-references. The measure repeals the original sections and contains an emergency clause. No state agency or department is newly created or assigned duties in the text, and the bill does not include any changes to appropriations, funding allocations, or budget items.
