Summary

LB901 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-27,132 to direct that, for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023, all proceeds of sales and use taxes imposed under §77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (per §3-101) be credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. The change is part of the Revenue Distribution Fund allocations. The act becomes operative July 1, 2027.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language preserves the existing sales-tax arrangement through July 1, 2027, and then, commencing July 1, 2027, sets the sales tax rate under section 77-2703 at an unspecified "XX percent." There are no explicit provisions in this text changing Department of Transportation appropriations, allocations, or specific DOT funding. Any DOT impact would be indirect—via state revenue changes—but the bill does not specify revenue distribution or budget adjustments.
