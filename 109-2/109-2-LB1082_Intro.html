Summary — LB1082 (2026)

- Amends the Nebraska Telecommunications Regulation Act (Reissue Revised Statutes sections 86-101 and 86-103) and adds two new sections.

- Defines "North American Numbering Plan" by reference to federal regulation 47 C.F.R. 52.5 as it existed on January 1, 2026.

- Imposes a duty on telecommunications companies to protect subscribers/customers from unwanted calls or texts originating from unauthenticated NANP numbers and to prevent fraudulent or misleading caller-identification information.

- Repeals the original versions of sections 86-101 and 86-103.

Agencies/departments: the bill references federal regulation at 47 C.F.R. 52.5 (federal regulatory framework) but does not name any Nebraska agency or department. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
