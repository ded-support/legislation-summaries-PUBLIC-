Summary

LB1113 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §18‑2147 (Community Development Law). Key agencies and roles:

- Redevelopment authority: must notify the county assessor (on forms prescribed by the Property Tax Administrator) of tax‑division provisions on or before July 2 of the effective year; may receive tax‑increment revenues into a special fund to pay bonds, loans, notes or other indebtedness and must notify the county assessor and county treasurer when obligations are retired so taxes revert to taxing bodies.

- County assessor: establishes redevelopment project valuation, provides written notice to the authority and owner.

- Owner/authority: may protest valuations to the county board of equalization within 30 days.

- County board of equalization: decides protests within 30 days; county clerk mails decisions within 7 days.

- Tax Equalization and Review Commission: appellate forum for board decisions.

- City (metropolitan class) and authority: guidance to allocate at least 30% (best efforts) of certain tax‑increment funds to single‑family housing and to adopt a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest‑income, long‑term projects.

No new appropriations or state budget allocations are included.
