Summary

LB910 requires that, upon conviction for a "sexual offense against a child" (an offense requiring sex-offender registration that involved a victim under 18), the court must order the defendant to pay $100 per electronic device seized in connection with the offense. "Electronic device" and "electronic communication device" are defined in the bill. Collected fees are remitted via the State Treasurer to the Nebraska State Patrol Cash Fund. The Nebraska State Patrol is directed to use the funds to defray forensic analysis costs of the devices. The court must waive the fee if it finds the defendant indigent.
