Summary

LB1050 amends the Nebraska Reading Improvement Act, assigning duties primarily to the State Department of Education and to local school boards/districts. The department must approve and publish approved reading assessments and threshold performance levels (including statewide and alternate grade‑3 thresholds by Oct 1, 2026), create a test‑based student portfolio option by Jan 1, 2027, develop a professional learning system for evidence‑based K–3 and early childhood reading instruction, provide technical assistance, coordinate regional coaches with educational service units, adopt rules, and file an annual report (by Sept. 30) with the Clerk of the Legislature. School boards must administer assessments, provide supplemental reading intervention programs and individualized plans, and (beginning 2027‑28) retain third‑grade students with persistent reading deficiencies from advancing to grade four (with exceptions). Intent to appropriate $2 million annually from the Education Future Fund for FY2024‑25 through FY2026‑27 to the Department for regional coaches/training.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- The state Department of Education (referred to as "the department") must by Oct 1, 2026 set and publish threshold performance levels for the statewide grade‑3 reading assessment and for alternate grade‑3 reading assessments.

- By Jan 1, 2027 the department must create and publish a test‑based student portfolio option, including criteria and a threshold level, to demonstrate grade‑3 reading mastery.

- Students not meeting those thresholds are designated as having a persistent reading deficiency and, beginning in 2027–28, school boards must retain such students in grade three (with specified exemptions for assessment‑exempt students, certain special‑education/504/ADA students after two years of intervention and prior retention, and students with two years of intervention and prior cumulative retention). No student may be retained under this rule more than once.

- Schools must notify parents by Feb 1 if a third‑grader remains deficient as of Jan 1 and offer meetings to revise individualized reading plans.

- Required supports include home reading strategies, a transitional instructional setting option, and before/after‑school intervention by teachers/tutors trained in reading intervention.

- Federal statutes referenced: Section 504 (Rehabilitation Act) and Title II (ADA).

- No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
