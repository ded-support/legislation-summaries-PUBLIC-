Summary

This bill amends the Nebraska Reading Improvement Act, assigning responsibilities principally to the State Department of Education (NDE) and local school boards. Key agency duties for NDE: approve and publish approved reading assessments and performance thresholds (including statewide grade‑3 thresholds by Oct 1, 2026), establish a test‑based student portfolio option by Jan 1, 2027, develop/implement a professional learning system for preK–3 teachers, provide technical assistance to school boards, coordinate regional coaches via educational service units, adopt rules, and file an annual report (by Sept. 30) with the Clerk of the Legislature. School boards must adopt reading policies, provide supplemental intervention programs, notify parents, create individualized reading improvement plans, and (beginning 2027–28) retain students with persistent grade‑3 reading deficiencies per the statute. The bill expresses legislative intent to appropriate $2 million annually from the Education Future Fund to NDE for regional coaches/job‑embedded training for FY2024‑25 through FY2026‑27.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text requires the state Department of Education to: (1) by Oct 1, 2026, set and publish threshold performance levels for the grade‑3 statewide and alternate reading assessments; and (2) by Jan 1, 2027, create and publish a test‑based student portfolio option with criteria and a threshold for demonstrating grade‑3 reading mastery. For retention purposes, students with a reading deficiency at the end of grade three are deemed to have a persistent deficiency unless they meet those thresholds; they remain identified until they do. Beginning in 2027–28, school boards must retain persistent‑deficiency students in grade three (with specified exemptions, including certain 504/ADA‑protected students and other limited exceptions). Schools must notify parents by Feb 1 if a child remains deficient as of Jan 1 and offer a meeting. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budgets are included.
