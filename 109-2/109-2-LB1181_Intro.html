Summary

Key provisions referencing the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- County attorneys must forward victim names/addresses to the Board of Parole, DCS, county corrections agencies, DHHS, and Board of Pardons; these agencies must include the victim in the offender's file (not public) and accept victim requests for notification. (81-1850)

- DCS must receive copies of presentence reports upon incarceration and, on request, provide them to the Board of Parole and Board of Pardons. Local/state police and DCS adult facilities must furnish criminal records to probation officers without cost. (29-2261)

- DCS must notify victims of furloughs/releases (including community-based programs), escapes, returns to custody, discharges, early-release calculations, and sentence reductions; DCS forwards victim information to DHHS when commitment petitions are filed. (81-1850; 81-1848.02)

- DHHS must notify victims when petitioned for or upon changes in inpatient mental-health/sex-offender commitments (escapes, discharges, furloughs, program releases) and may release portions of patient records to comply with victim-notification requirements. (81-1850; 83-109)

No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
