Summary

LB819 designates "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns the director responsibility for establishing and administering rural and Middle Income workforce housing grant programs and investment funds. Grants are to be competitively awarded through FY 2036-37. Key financial provisions: Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund and Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund may include legislative appropriations, transfers, grants, and private contributions; interest earned must be applied to the grant programs; the department may solicit additional nonstate funding (including Nebraska Investment Finance Authority). Grant limits: rural—maximum $5 million per nonprofit over two years; middle-income—maximum $10 million per nonprofit over two years; cumulative limits at the department’s discretion. Matching requirements (rural 25%; middle-income 50% pre‑7/19/2024, 25% thereafter). Department must create a Middle Income Fund subaccount for Economic Recovery Act land/affordable-housing activities. Beginning July 1, 2037, funds remaining in both funds held by the department transfer to the General Fund. Unit cost caps will be updated annually using the Producer Price Index from the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. The director may impose up to $5,000 civil penalties for late reports; nonprofits must file annual reports for inclusion in the department's status report.
