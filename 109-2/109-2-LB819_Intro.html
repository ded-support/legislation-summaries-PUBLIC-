Summary

LB819 designates "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and assigns the Director of Economic Development responsibility to establish and administer both the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Grant Program and the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Grant Program. Grants are competitive through FY2026-37 (listed as FY 2036-37) with rural grant maximums (e.g., $5M per grantee over two years) and urban maximums (up to $10M over two years) and specified matching requirements (urban matching: 50% pre‑July 19, 2024; 25% thereafter). The Department administers the Rural and Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Funds (funded by legislative appropriations, transfers, grants, private contributions, etc.), may create a subaccount for Economic Recovery Act land/affordable-housing activities, applies interest to grant programs, and must transfer remaining fund balances to the General Fund beginning July 1, 2037. The bill also uses the U.S. Department of Labor’s Producer Price Index to update construction cost caps.
