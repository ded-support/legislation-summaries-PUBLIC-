Summary

Bill LB726 amends §81‑2104 of the State Electrical Act, updating the edition of the National Electrical Code (NEC) the administering board must use to the 2026 NEC (Publication No. 70‑2026), while retaining specific provisions (sections 210.8(A)(3), 210.8(A)(5), 230.70(A)(1), 230.70(B)(2)) from the 2017 NEC. Each NEC edition in use must be filed with the Secretary of State and the board. The measure preserves the board’s existing authorities under the Act—rulemaking, enforcement, licensing actions (suspension/revocation/refusal to renew), inspections, disconnects/removals of dangerous wiring, electrocution investigations, fee-setting, and continuing‑education enforcement. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed. The original section is repealed.
