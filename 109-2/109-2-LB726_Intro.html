Summary

LB726 amends section 81-2104 of the State Electrical Act, updating the National Electrical Code reference to the 2026 edition as the minimum standard the State Electrical Board must follow, while retaining specific provisions from the 2017 edition for sections 210.8(A)(3), 210.8(A)(5), 230.70(A)(1), and 230.70(B)(2). Each referenced NEC edition must be filed with the Secretary of State and the board as public records. The bill restates the board’s authorities—including adopting rules and wiring standards, hiring staff, setting and collecting fees, enforcing the NEC, revoking or refusing licenses, ordering disconnections/removals, and investigating electrocutions—and notes applicability of political subdivisions’ inspection ordinances. No appropriations or changes to funding are included.
