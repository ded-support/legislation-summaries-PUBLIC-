Summary

LB894 references the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) as follows:

- Warehouses licensed under the federal United States Warehouse Act must submit to the Nebraska Public Service Commission copies of all regulatory filings submitted to or received from the United States Department of Agriculture, including filings relating to securities, insurance, yearly audits, and licenses; the commission may not require other filings.

- Except to enforce the statute, the commission may not take administrative action against a warehouse licensed under the federal United States Warehouse Act without the written consent of the United States Department of Agriculture.

- Annual license fees the commission may set (effective July 1, 2027) for non‑federally licensed warehouses are capped: they may not exceed the commodity fees published in the 2025/2026 "Rates Charged for AMS Services" notice from the Agricultural Marketing Service of the U.S. Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting the Department of Agriculture (the commission that administers grain dealer/warehouse regulation):

- Establishes a Grain Dealer and Grain Warehouse Cash Fund, administered by the commission, composed of specified fees and credits; investment earnings return to the fund and funds cannot be transferred out.

- License fees: grain dealer license fees (capped at $1,500) and warehouse fees to be set by the commission by Dec. 1, 2026 for implementation beginning July 1, 2027; annual adjustments permitted thereafter.

- Revenue rules: fees paid before July 1, 2027 are credited to the General Fund; fees on/after July 1, 2027 are credited to the new Cash Fund. Legislature intends to appropriate all Cash Fund money to the commission for administration; license-fee revenue should not exceed 25% of commission implementation costs.

- Federal USDA-licensed warehouses receive state licenses, submit USDA filings to the commission, and are exempt from separate state annual fees.
