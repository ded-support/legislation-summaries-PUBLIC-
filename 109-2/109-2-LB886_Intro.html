Summary

LB886 assigns the Nebraska Department of Labor the role of certifying nonresidents engaged in temporary agricultural employment in Nebraska (for periods not to exceed 60 days). Such certified nonresidents may be granted an additional 30 days' driving immunity in Nebraska, conditional on reciprocal treatment by their state of residence. The bill includes no appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language narrows the definition of "operator's or driver's license," clarifying it does not include any license, permit, or document issued to persons who are not U.S. citizens and who lack lawful U.S. status, with referenced exceptions in subsection (2).
