Summary

LB975 (Private Attorney Retention Sunshine Act) imposes new transparency requirements on the Nebraska Attorney General’s retention of private counsel. Before retaining private attorneys for contracts exceeding $10,000, the AG must complete an open competitive bidding process (per §73-807) and file the proposed contract with the Legislature’s Appropriations Committee (if in session) or the Executive Board of the Legislative Council (if not). The committee/board must hold a public hearing within 10 days, issue a report, and the AG must file a proposed final contract (with explanation if committee changes are omitted); execution is permitted no earlier than 30 days after filing. Contingent-fee closures require disclosure of hours, expenses, and fee breakdowns; billable rates are capped at $1,000/hour. Act applies to AG contracts executed after its effective date and does not expand AG authority.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Private Attorney Retention Sunshine Act," affecting the Attorney General, the Legislature's Appropriations Committee, and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council. Key provisions:

- Applies to Attorney General contracts for private legal services exceeding $10,000 (fees + expenses); requires an open, competitive bidding process per section 73‑807 before retention.

- The AG must file proposed contracts with the Appropriations Committee (if in session) or the Legislative Council's Executive Board (if not); those bodies must hold a public hearing within 10 days and issue a report with recommended changes.

- The AG must file a proposed final contract, explain any omitted recommendations, and wait at least 30 days before executing.

- For contingent‑fee matters, retained counsel must disclose hours, expenses, aggregate fee, and an hourly‑rate breakdown. Legal fees are capped at $1,000 per hour (excesses reduced to $1,000).
