Summary

LB1162 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75-401 to clarify and expand the Public Service Commission’s (PSC) railroad safety authorities. The PSC may adopt federal railroad safety standards in 49 U.S.C. chs. 201–213 and 49 C.F.R. pts. 200–299 (as of Jan. 1, 2026) for the limited purposes of conducting safety inspections and participating in federal programs. The PSC may perform inspections, surveillance, and related activities under the Federal Railroad Administration’s (FRA) State Safety Participation Program and may report potential federal regulation violations to the FRA for enforcement. The bill repeals the original §75-401. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Authorizes "the commission" to adopt federal railroad safety standards in 49 U.S.C. §§201–213 and 49 C.F.R. parts 200–299 as they existed on Jan. 1, 2026, solely to perform safety inspections and take part in federal railroad safety programs. Grants the commission authority to carry out inspections, surveillance, and related activities under the Federal Railroad Administration's (FRA) State Safety Participation Program and to report suspected federal regulatory violations to the FRA for federal enforcement.
