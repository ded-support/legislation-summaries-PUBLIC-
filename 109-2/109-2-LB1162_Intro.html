Summary

LB1162 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75‑401 to clarify the Public Service Commission’s (PSC) jurisdiction over the service, facilities, and equipment of all railroad carriers and to authorize limited railroad‑safety activities. The PSC may adopt federal railroad safety standards in 49 U.S.C. chapters 201–213 and 49 C.F.R. parts 200–299 (as of Jan 1, 2026) solely to conduct safety inspections and participate in federal programs. The bill expressly permits PSC inspections, surveillance, and activities authorized under the Federal Railroad Administration’s (FRA) State Safety Participation Program and allows reporting potential federal violations to the FRA. The original §75‑401 is repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Allows the commission to adopt federal railroad safety standards (49 U.S.C. chs. 201–213; 49 C.F.R. pts. 200–299) as of Jan 1, 2026, solely to carry out safety inspections and participate in federal railroad safety programs. Authorizes the commission to perform inspections, surveillance, and related activities under the Federal Railroad Administration’s State Safety Participation Program and to report potential federal violations to the FRA for enforcement. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
