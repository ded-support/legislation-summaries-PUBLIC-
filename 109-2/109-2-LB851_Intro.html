Summary

LB851 amends Nebraska income tax law (secs. 77‑2716 and 77‑2734.01). Primary agency: the Tax Commissioner (Nebraska Department of Revenue) — retained rulemaking authority and directed to prescribe the form and manner for electing S‑corporation entity‑level tax filings, to accept retroactive elections, extend claim deadlines to January 31, 2026 for affected filings, and to review proposed deficiency determinations within one year of such filings (enforceable for six years). Substantive change: beginning January 1, 2026, resident shareholders/members must include their full proportionate share of an S‑corporation’s or Nebraska LLC’s federal income in Nebraska taxable income (removing the prior exclusion for income not derived from Nebraska sources). Electing entity‑level tax provisions (entity tax, shareholder refundable credit, estimated tax treatment) remain. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, Nebraska residents who are shareholders of S corporations or members of Nebraska LLCs must include, to the extent included in federal gross income, their proportionate share of the entity’s federal income in Nebraska taxable income. Residents must also include fair compensation for services to the entity; amounts actually paid are presumed fair unless the Tax Commissioner determines they are materially different from fair value or manipulated for tax avoidance. No agencies other than the Nebraska Tax Commissioner are mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
