Summary

LB972A appropriates funds to multiple agencies to implement LB972.

- Department of Revenue (Program 102): $72,400 General Fund for FY2026‑27 and $69,400 General Fund for FY2027‑28. Salary/per diem expenditures from these funds are capped at $50,700 (FY26‑27) and $52,200 (FY27‑28).

- Department of Motor Vehicles (Program 90): $521,520 from the License Plate Cash Fund for FY2026‑27 and $530,540 for FY2027‑28. These appropriated funds may not be used for permanent or temporary employee salaries or per diems.

- Department of Health and Human Services (Program 424): $900,000 (FY2026‑27) and $1,800,000 (FY2027‑28) from the Developmental Disabilities Provider Capital and Equipment Maintenance Cash Fund, all designated as state aid. No salaries/per diems may be paid from these funds.

- Military Department (Program 544): $13,500 (FY2026‑27) and $30,000 (FY2027‑28) from the Military Department Aid Fund, designated as state aid; funds cannot be used for salaries/per diems.
