Summary

LB857 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2714 to update Nebraska’s income-tax comparability language to reference the Internal Revenue Code and other federal income-tax laws, rules, and regulations as amended on January 1, 2024. The amendment clarifies that terms used in Nebraskan tax statutes carry the same meanings as in comparable federal income-tax law, that references to earlier Internal Revenue Code designations are interchangeable where appropriate, and preserves federal income-tax exempt organizations’ exemption under the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967. The bill repeals the original §77-2714. No state agencies, departments, funding changes, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
