Summary

LB765 amends school-bond and joint-entity bond law. Key state/local entities affected: boards of education, Nebraska school districts, educational service units (ESUs), county clerks, election commissioners, and county canvassing boards (Election Act procedures apply). For school-district bonds the bill requires submission to qualified electors, a turnout threshold of at least 50.1% of qualified electors, and a majority favoring issuance; filing deadlines for bond orders at statewide primary/general elections are specified. For joint entities (created on/after Feb 14, 2024) that include a Nebraska school district or ESU, similar per-district elector submission, 50.1% turnout, and majority-approval requirements apply; notice must be given at least 50 days prior. The bill prohibits use of public funds (including building, cash, and discretionary funds) to support bond campaigns, bars paid employee advocacy time, and bans campaign materials on school/ESU property. Original sections are repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds prohibitions affecting school districts and educational service units (and joint entities that include them). Public funds — including building funds, cash funds, and discretionary funds — may not be used to establish, support, promote, underwrite, subsidize, or otherwise advocate for campaigns to influence voters in school- or joint-entity bond elections; privately raised funds (e.g., concessions, booster clubs, PTOs) are excluded. District/ESU employees may not use paid work time on such campaigning but may provide neutral informational details (subject, amount, date). Campaign materials advocating for bonds may not be posted or distributed on school/ESU property. No new appropriations or budget increases are made; this imposes spending restrictions.
