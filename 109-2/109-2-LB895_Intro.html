Summary

LB895 amends the Grain Warehouse Act to limit the commission's annual warehouse license fee: the commission may set the fee but it "shall not exceed the fees established for applicable commodities in the 2025/2026 Rates Charged for AMS Services notice for entities with a Commodity Credit Corporation agreement," as published by the Agricultural Marketing Service of the United States Department of Agriculture (notice as it existed on Jan. 1, 2026). No Department of Agriculture appropriations or budget allocations are changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the commission to create a streamlined licensing route for holders of valid United States Warehouse Act licenses to obtain Grain Warehouse Act licenses, with reduced application and oversight requirements as determined by the commission. License fees set by the commission are capped: they may not exceed the rates for applicable commodities in the USDA Agricultural Marketing Service’s 2025/2026 "Rates Charged for AMS Services" notice for entities with Commodity Credit Corporation agreements (as of Jan 1, 2026). Fees are payable by the deadline the commission sets. No new appropriations or budget allocations to the Department of Agriculture are included.
