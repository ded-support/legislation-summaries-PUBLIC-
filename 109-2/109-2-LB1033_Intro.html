Summary

LB1033 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-1215 to direct the Department of Health and Human Services to: (1) determine LIHEAP eligibility using a household annual income limit of 150% of the federal poverty level; and (2) make crisis assistance payments of up to $800 per program year, with authority to approve amounts over $800 for extenuating circumstances. The bill contains no specific appropriations or new budget allocations for the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The addition directs the Department of Health and Human Services to make crisis assistance payments as needed, capped at $800 per program year, while allowing the department discretion to approve payments above $800 in extenuating circumstances. No specific appropriation, funding source, or budget allocation changes are included in the provision.
