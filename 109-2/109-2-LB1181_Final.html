Summary

LB1181 assigns and clarifies notification and records duties for the Department of Correctional Services (DOC) and Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Key provisions: DOC adult correctional facilities must furnish criminal records to probation officers without cost; presentence reports for incarcerated offenders must be transmitted immediately to DOC, which must provide copies to the Board of Parole and Board of Pardons upon request; facility administrators must notify victims and prosecutors of escapes; DOC must receive and retain victim names in offender files and notify victims of furloughs, community releases, escapes, discharges, sentence-reduction calculations, and related events. DOC must forward victim information to DHHS when commitment petitions are filed. DHHS must notify victims regarding mental-health or sex-offender commitment proceedings, escapes, discharges, furloughs, and community-program releases; limited patient records may be released to comply with these notifications. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No changes affecting the Department of Correctional Services or the Department of Health and Human Services are included, and no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations for either agency appear in the added text.

Summary of additions:

- Defines "plea agreement" and expands/clarifies who qualifies as a "victim" (including intimate-partner crimes, certain sexual offenses, child victims, homicide survivors, theft between intimate partners, and serious-injury motor vehicle victims).

- Requires prosecuting attorneys to consult, or make a good-faith effort to consult, with victims before entering plea agreements.

- Grants victims rights to review publicly available criminal-justice information, submit written statements for bond-modification and sentencing proceedings, and have representatives read impact statements; courts may impose reasonable limits.
