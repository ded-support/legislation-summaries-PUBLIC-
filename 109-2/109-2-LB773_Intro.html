Summary

LB773 locates the Nebraska Prenatal Plus Program within the Department of Health and Human Services and states the program’s purpose: to reduce low birth weight, preterm birth, and adverse birth outcomes while addressing mothers’ lifestyle, behavioral, and nonmedical needs. The bill requires the Department to electronically submit an annual report to the Legislature on or before December 15 each year, beginning December 15, 2024, through December 15, 2034, reporting number of mothers served, services offered, and birth outcomes. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department are contained in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language requires program services administered by the Department of Health and Human Services to be available to mothers through the 60th day of the postpartum period and to provide a minimum of two sessions per mother. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified in the added text.
