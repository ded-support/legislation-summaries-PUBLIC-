Summary

LB1076 amends enforcement and penalty authority under the Nebraska Geologic Storage of Carbon Dioxide Act, affecting the Nebraska Oil and Gas Conservation Commission (the commission), the Attorney General, county attorneys, and the State Treasurer. Key agency actions authorized or clarified:

- The commission may assess civil penalties ($2,500–$10,000 per day), pursue permit revocation/modification, receive public complaints, and compel hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act; it may compromise, mitigate, or remit penalties.

- The Attorney General (or county attorney at the commission’s request) may sue to recover penalties or seek injunctive relief; the commission may itself issue immediate restraining orders against unauthorized, harmful activity.

- Collected civil penalties must be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Carbon Dioxide Storage Facility Administrative Fund. Unpaid penalties become state debts collectible by suit or lien foreclosure.

The bill repeals and replaces sections 57-1601 and 57-1620, R.R.S.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds enforcement provisions giving the commission authority to impose civil penalties of $2,500–$10,000 per day for violations of the Nebraska Geologic Storage of Carbon Dioxide Act, with a 10-day cure period and separate daily penalties for continuing violations. The commission must notify alleged violators, provide hearing rights under the Administrative Procedure Act, and may compromise or remit penalties. The commission must create procedures for public reporting of violations. Collected penalties must be remitted within 30 days to the State Treasurer for credit to the Carbon Dioxide Storage Facility Administrative Fund; unpaid penalties become state debts after 60 days and may be collected by lien foreclosure or suit. The Attorney General or a county attorney may prosecute enforcement or collection actions and may bring suits at the commission’s request; the commission may also seek immediate restraining orders in district court.
