Summary

LB1225 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43‑274 (Nebraska Juvenile Code). Key agency/office provisions:

- County attorneys and city attorneys: may offer pretrial diversion (per §§43‑260.02–43‑260.07), refer juveniles to restorative justice, and file petitions or criminal informations when proceedings transfer to county or district court.

- Restorative justice facilitators: required to perform separate intakes for victims and juveniles, obtain consent, report compliance to the referring county/city attorney, use a sliding fee scale, and not charge victims.

- Juvenile court: must schedule transfer hearings within 15 days and decide within 30 days; transfer orders are final and appealable.

- Court of Appeals: appeals from transfer orders must be filed within 10 days and, as amended, must be decided no later than 90 days after perfection of the appeal.

No changes to appropriations or funding allocations are included.
