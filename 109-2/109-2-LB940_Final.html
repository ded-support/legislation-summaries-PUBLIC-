Summary

LB940 prohibits specified FDA-listed color additives in school meals. It defines "color additive" by reference to 21 U.S.C. 321 and limits "school meal" to meals served under the federal Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act and the federal Child Nutrition Act of 1966. Effective August 1, 2027, public elementary and secondary schools may not serve school-meal foods containing Blue No. 1, Blue No. 2, Green No. 3, Red No. 40, Yellow No. 5, or Yellow No. 6, per the FDA's Regulatory Status of Color Additives as of January 1, 2026. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "color additive" by reference to 21 U.S.C. 321 and defines "school meal" by reference to the federal National School Lunch Act and Child Nutrition Act (as they existed Jan 1, 2026). Beginning Aug. 1, 2027, public elementary and secondary schools may not serve school‑meal foods that contain FDA‑listed color additives per the FDA's Regulatory Status of Color Additives (as of Jan. 1, 2026): Blue No. 1, Blue No. 2, Green No. 3, Red No. 40, Yellow No. 5, or Yellow No. 6. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
