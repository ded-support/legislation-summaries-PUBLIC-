Summary

LB940 prohibits public elementary and secondary schools, beginning August 1, 2027, from offering any food served as part of a federally defined "school meal" (per the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act or the Child Nutrition Act of 1966) that contains specified color additives. The bill adopts the FDA's "Regulatory Status of Color Additives" list as of January 1, 2026, and expressly bans Blue No. 1; Blue No. 2; Green No. 3; Red No. 40; Yellow No. 5; and Yellow No. 6. It references the statutory definition of color additive in 21 U.S.C. 321. No state agencies are assigned new funding or appropriations in the bill, and it contains no budgetary or allocation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions (using federal law as of Jan 1, 2026) and bans certain FDA-listed color additives in school meals. "Color additive" is defined by 21 U.S.C. 321; "school meal" is limited to meals provided under the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act or the Child Nutrition Act of 1966. Effective Aug. 1, 2027, public K–12 schools may not serve meals containing these FDA-listed additives (per the FDA's Regulatory Status of Color Additives as of Jan. 1, 2026): Blue No. 1; Blue No. 2; Green No. 3; Red No. 40; Yellow No. 5; Yellow No. 6. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
