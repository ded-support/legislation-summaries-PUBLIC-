Summary

LB994 assigns multiple duties to the Department of Correctional Services (the department). Key provisions:

- Recalculate parole-eligibility dates for all committed offenders incarcerated on/after July 1, 2011, by April 15, 2027, in coordination with the Board of Parole using phased implementation; administrative recalculation delays cannot alone deny parole eligibility; affected parolees may request reduced parole terms.

- Within 60 days of initial classification, prepare a department‑approved personalized program plan and provide specified programming (education, substance‑abuse, mental‑health, vocational, work programs, etc.).

- Apply sentence reductions (six months per year; plus three days monthly after 12 months of good conduct); reductions affect parole eligibility and discharge dates; facility chief (with director approval) may forfeit/restore reductions.

- Ensure release/reentry plans at 80% of sentence, provide community‑standard mental‑health care, evaluate before release, prepare parolee case plans within 30 days, and collaborate/share records with probation for post‑release supervision plans.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Correctional Services must recalculate parole eligibility dates for all committed offenders incarcerated on or after July 1, 2011, and complete this work by April 15, 2027. Recalculations must be done in coordination with the Board of Parole, using a phased implementation to avoid administrative overload and protect public safety. No one may be denied parole eligibility solely because of administrative delay in recalculation. Individuals on parole who were discharged after July 1, 2011 and before this act’s effective date may ask the Board to reduce their parole term if affected. The bill clarifies that good‑time reductions apply to tentative release and parole eligibility (affecting minimum terms other than mandatory minimums and maximum terms). No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are specified.
