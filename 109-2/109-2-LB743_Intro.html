Summary

LB743 amends section 16-1007 of the Police Officers Retirement Act. It directs the retirement committee and retirement system to offer optional benefit forms that include a single lump‑sum payment or one or more partial lump‑sum payments of a police officer’s retirement value, and allows deferral of the first payment to the first day of any specified month prior to age 70. Annuity contracts purchased by the retirement system may be distributed to the officer, terminating the system’s payment obligations. For officers entitled to statutory minimum pensions, the city must transfer funds into the officer’s employer account if the officer’s retirement value is insufficient to purchase the required benefit. The act becomes operative October 1, 2026.
