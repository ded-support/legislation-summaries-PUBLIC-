Summary

LB1259 (Grid Modernization Act) centers authority in "the board" to regulate large-load electric service. Key agency actions:

- The board must approve or deny large‑load contracts within 60 days, finding compliance with the Act and that incremental costs fall to the large‑load customer and not existing ratepayers; it may adopt rules to expedite review.

- Large-scale generation providers must register with the board, provide financial/operational qualifications, permits, and resource portfolios; the board may suspend or revoke registrations and impose conditions.

- Public power utilities must submit large-scale service evaluations and obtain board approval before construction or providing service under large‑load contracts.

- Interactions with transmission service providers are subject to applicable federal law; FERC authority is preserved.

- Funding: the board shall assess a semiannual, proportionate fee on each large‑scale service requester to pay for independent consultants and costs of board proceedings; fees must be used exclusively for those purposes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key agencies/departments and duties:

- The board (state regulatory board named in the bill): must approve/deny large-load contracts within 60 days, may adopt rules, set cost-allocation methodologies, approve public power utility construction, register and condition/suspend large-scale generation providers, and limit review to specified compliance criteria.

- Public power utilities: must acknowledge and evaluate large-scale service requests on set timelines, obtain board approval before design/construction or providing service under large-load contracts, and may elect (in writing) to purchase up to 10% of a provider's output.

- Large-scale generation providers: must register with the board, meet technical/financial qualifications, post security, and supply permits and proof of capability.

- Transmission service providers and FERC: transmission requests and cost allocation subject to applicable federal law and FERC rules.

Funding/appropriations:

- The board may impose semi‑annual fees on large-load customers submitting requests to pay for independent consultants and other evaluation/proceeding costs; fees are proportionally allocated among pending customers and used exclusively for those costs.
