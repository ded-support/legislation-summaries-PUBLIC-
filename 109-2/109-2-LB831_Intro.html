Summary

LB831 creates an exclusive statutory cause of action for civil claims alleging ethylene oxide exposure. It requires plaintiffs to file with the complaint: (1) a specific breakdown of each element of damages and the factual basis for the damages calculation; and (2) where recovery depends on proof of a particular state of mind, a statement of facts giving rise to a strong inference that the defendant possessed that state of mind. The provisions apply to actions pending on and filed after the bill’s effective date. No state agencies or funding/appropriation changes are referenced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural requirements and substantive limits for "ethylene oxide exposure" lawsuits: complaints must state detailed, itemized damages and the factual basis for each damage calculation; where recovery depends on proving the defendant's particular state of mind, the complaint must allege facts creating a strong inference of that state of mind. Sections 1–5 are declared the exclusive cause of action and set the sole standards for prevailing. These provisions apply to cases pending on the bill’s effective date and to cases filed thereafter. No government agencies or funding/appropriations are mentioned.
