Summary

LB802 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2715.03 to change individual income tax rates and related administration. Key points for government entities:

- Legislature: enacts phased reductions in the higher individual income tax rates (rate three: 5.01% through 2025, 4.55% for 2026, 3.99% from 2027; rate four: staged reductions with 3.98% from 2027).

- Tax Commissioner: required to adjust bracket thresholds for inflation, prescribe updated rate schedules, update and publish schedules when rates change, prepare tax tables (may incorporate personal exemption and standard deduction), and may require use of tax tables for most taxpayers. Also retains a 29.6% rate applied to certain federal taxes in Nebraska computations.

No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included. Original section is repealed.
