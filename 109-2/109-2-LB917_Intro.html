Summary

LB917 (2026) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-105 governing the University of Nebraska. It preserves the Board of Regents’ corporate powers to acquire and dispose of real and personal property but adds an explicit procedural limitation: the Board may not dispose of grounds on which a university building is located without a majority vote of the Board of Regents taken in a public meeting authorizing the disposal. The bill repeals the original section. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the university may dispose of specified property only upon a majority vote of the Board of Regents taken at a public meeting. No other agencies are referenced and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
