Summary

LB1154 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §13-3403 (Property Tax Growth Limitation Act) to revise how a political subdivision’s property tax request authority is calculated. Preliminary authority is set as the prior fiscal year’s property taxes requested and approved on the budget document filed with the auditor (per §13-506(2)), minus exceptions used in the prior year under §13-3404 subdivisions (1), (2), (4), (5), (6), and (7). Additional authority may be added by (a) applying the political subdivision’s growth percentage to prior-year levies after subtracting exceptions used under §13-3404(1) and (2), and (b) applying the positive inflation percentage to that same adjusted prior levy. The bill repeals the original §13-3403. No state agency appropriations or direct funding allocations are created or amended.
