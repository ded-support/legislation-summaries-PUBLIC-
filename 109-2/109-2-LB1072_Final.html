Summary

The bill centralizes transfer authority in the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget administrator, authorizing timed State Treasurer transfers and certifications (including notable moves such as $50 million from the Nebraska Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund and $40 million of permanent school fund earnings to the Education Future Fund and up to $15 million from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to UNMC). DAS must establish subaccounts (Water Sustainability/Recreation) and oversee numerous directed transfers (e.g., $24.5M from the Military Installation Development & Support Fund to the Site & Building Development Fund; $15,017,316 from the Economic Recovery Contingency Fund to the General Fund).

Department of Health and Human Services must track Rural Health Transformation funds, submit reports through 12/31/2032, prohibit supplanting state rural health spending, and transfer $1,898,895 to the General Fund; HHS Cash Fund receives $400,000 annually from the Opioid Recovery Trust Fund.

Department of Economic Development administers multiple infrastructure, housing, workforce and site‑development funds, audits Teleworker reimbursements, and implements job‑training/internship grant rules.

Department of Insurance: new Cash Fund with mandated transfers ($17.2M by 6/30/2026; $16.8M in 2027–2029) to the General Fund as directed by DAS.

Department of Labor: Employment Security funds restructured; special contingent fund earnings credited to the General Fund beginning 10/1/2024; required MOU to exchange workforce/education data.

Department of Motor Vehicles creates an Ignition Interlock Fund for indigent installation/maintenance (fund may transfer to the General Fund).

Department of Revenue establishes a Tobacco Products Administration Cash Fund, manages municipal remittance allocations, and certifies transfers affecting the Cash Reserve and School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund.

Department of Correctional Services creates a Correctional Services Insurance Proceeds Fund (and a $3.5M transfer to the General Fund) and administers inmate welfare accounts.

Department of Veterans' Affairs administers the Military Installation Development & Support Fund (50% public/private match required), may transfer to the Military Department Cash Fund.

Military Department: new Military Department Cash Fund to receive nonfederal National Guard revenue; transfers to the General Fund permitted.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions (by agency):

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): DHHS is designated applicant for CMS Rural Health Transformation Program; must create a unique budget subprogram, limit expenditures to federal-authorized uses, post awards online, require sustainability plans for awardees, prohibit supplanting state rural health spending, and file annual utilization reports to the Legislature through 12/31/2032.

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): budget administrator directed to schedule numerous State Treasurer transfers from many named funds to the General Fund or Education Future Fund; DAS to create a subaccount in the Water Recreation Enhancement Fund for Nebraska Environmental Trust transfers.

- Department of Agriculture: intent to appropriate $26,000 (FY2026-27) from Animal Damage Control Cash Fund; unobligated Panhandle Improvement Project balances to transfer to Animal Damage Control Cash Fund after 7/31/2026.

- Department of Economic Development: intent to appropriate $100,000 (FY2026-27) from Site & Building Development Fund for a grant.

- Department of Correctional Services: creates Correctional Services Insurance Proceeds Fund (insurance claim proceeds for operating/capital costs); fund may be invested; $3.5M transfer to General Fund scheduled 2026–27.

- Military Department: $1,000,000 transfer from Military Installation Development & Support Fund to Military Department Cash Fund (by 6/30/2026); fund may support military installations; transfers to General Fund/State Settlement Cash Fund permitted.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: $1,000,000 transfer from Vehicle Title Registration System Replacement & Maintenance Cash Fund to General Fund (2026–27 window); motor fuel trust fund transfer of $1,730,000 to General Fund (by 6/30/2026).

- Department of Revenue: specifies fund money may be used for Department of Revenue administration/enforcement; procedural certification/transfer of FY2025‑26 excess General Fund receipts to Cash Reserve Fund.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: requires IT software/hardware inventories and a six‑year plan for the Department’s cash fund; fund subject to legislative-directed transfers to the General Fund.

- Department of Insurance: administratively created Insurance Trust fund balance (fund #68930) directed to transfer to the General Fund (by 6/30/2026).

- Department of Labor: no direct substantive funding or appropriation changes identified in the added text.
