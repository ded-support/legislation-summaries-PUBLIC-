Summary

The bill centralizes budget transfer authority with the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) budget administrator, directing numerous State Treasurer transfers and creation of fund subaccounts (Water Sustainability/Water Recreation Enhancement). DAS-certified transfers include $3,500,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to DHHS (by 6/30/2025) and scheduled transfers such as $24,500,000 from the Military Installation Development and Support Fund to the Site and Building Development Fund (FY2025–26) and other multi‑million General Fund transfers.

Department of Health and Human Services must segregate federal Rural Health Transformation Program funds, post awards, file annual reports through 12/31/2032, and may transfer $1,898,895 from its cash fund to the General Fund. Department of Correctional Services creates an Insurance Proceeds Fund (director‑administered) with a $3,500,000 transfer authorized. The Military Department Cash Fund and a $1,000,000 transfer are authorized. Department of Insurance must transfer $17.2 million (by 6/30/2026) and $16.8 million annually thereafter. Department of Revenue creates enforcement and tobacco funds with transfers of $11.5M (FY25‑26) and $12.5M (FY26‑27, FY27‑28). Other agency actions: DED administers Site & Building and other grant funds; DMV creates an Ignition Interlock Fund; Department of Labor and Veterans' Affairs have specified administrative and fund roles.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: Must create a unique budget subprogram for CMS Rural Health Transformation Program funds; post applications/awards online; submit applications and annual utilization reports to the Clerk through 12/31/2032; funds may not supplant state rural health spending and require approved sustainability plans. DHHS Cash Fund transfer of $1,898,895 to the General Fund before June 30, 2026.

- Department of Administrative Services: Budget administrator directs numerous one-time transfers of remaining balances from many named state funds to the General Fund (various deadlines in 2026–2027) and must establish a subaccount in the Water Recreation Enhancement Fund for Nebraska Environmental Trust transfers. Also directs timing of transfers: $150,000 from Cash Reserve to State Self‑Insured Liability Fund; $500,000 Cash Reserve → Electrical Division Fund (and $500,000 return Jan–Jun 2027).

- Department of Correctional Services: Creates Correctional Services Insurance Proceeds Fund (insurance claims for operating/capital costs); $3,500,000 to be transferred from that fund to the General Fund on/after July 1, 2026 (before June 30, 2027).

- Department of Agriculture: Intent to appropriate $26,000 (FY2026‑27) from Animal Damage Control Cash Fund for animal damage control; remaining balances of several agriculture-related funds (e.g., Soil & Plant Analysis Lab, State Apiary Cash Fund) are scheduled for transfer to the General Fund by June 30, 2026.

- Department of Economic Development: Intent to appropriate $100,000 (FY2026‑27) from Site and Building Development Fund for a grant under section 81‑12,147(1)(m).

- Department of Insurance: An administratively created Insurance Trust fund (fund no. 68930) is directed to transfer its remaining balance to the General Fund before June 30, 2026.

- Department of Labor: No substantive changes or specific funding actions identified.

- Department of Motor Vehicles: Vehicle Title Registration System Replacement & Maintenance Cash Fund: $1,000,000 directed to the General Fund on/after July 1, 2026 (before June 30, 2027). Motor fuel trust fund: $1,730,000 to General Fund before June 30, 2026; beginning July 1, 2026 investment earnings credited to General Fund.

- Department of Revenue: Clarifies use of certain revenue/enforcement funds for Tax Commissioner administration/enforcement; requires certification and transfer of any FY2025‑26 excess General Fund net receipts to the Cash Reserve Fund; various fund transfers may affect revenue-administered accounts.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: Language permitting transfers from the Department of Veterans' Affairs Cash Fund to the General Fund at the Legislature's direction.

- Military Department: $1,000,000 transfer from the Military Installation Development and Support Fund to the Military Department Cash Fund before June 30, 2026; Military Department Cash Fund may be used to support Nebraska military installations and is transferable to the General Fund or State Settlement Cash Fund at the Legislature's direction.
