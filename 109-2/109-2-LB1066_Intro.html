Summary

Requires each Nebraska legislator, within 90 days of taking office, to complete a 20-question civics assessment drawn from the civics portion of the U.S. Naturalization Test (USCIS, Department of Homeland Security) or an equivalent test designated by the Secretary of State or the State Department of Education. The Clerk of the Legislature will provide and administer the assessment, certify raw scores to members, publish each member’s raw score on the Legislature’s official website, and include the score in the member’s official public biography; individual question responses remain confidential. Passage is not required and results do not affect office-holding. Members who fail to achieve a passing score are invited, voluntarily, to seminars offered by the Nebraska State Historical Society or the Legislative Research Office. No funding or appropriation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a requirement that each newly sworn member of the Nebraska Legislature complete a 20-question civics assessment (the civics portion of the USCIS U.S. Naturalization Test or an equivalent designated by the Secretary of State or the State Department of Education) within 90 days. The Clerk of the Legislature administers the test, certifies each member's raw score to the member, and must publish raw scores on the Legislature's official website and in members' public biographies; individual question answers remain confidential. Completion is mandatory but passage does not affect officeholding. Members who fail are invited (but not required) to a voluntary seminar offered by the Nebraska State Historical Society or the Legislative Research Office. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
