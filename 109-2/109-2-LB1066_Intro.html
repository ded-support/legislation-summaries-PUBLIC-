Summary

LB1066 requires each newly sworn Nebraska legislator to complete a "civics assessment" within 90 days. The term is defined as the civics portion of the U.S. Naturalization Test administered by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (Department of Homeland Security) or an equivalent exam designated by the Secretary of State or the State Department of Education. The Clerk of the Legislature must administer a 20-question randomly selected assessment, certify each member's raw score to that member, and publish raw scores on the Legislature's official website and in members' official biographies; individual question responses remain confidential. Completion is mandatory but passage is not and does not affect constitutional qualifications or officeholding. Members who do not pass are invited (but not required) to voluntary seminars offered by the Nebraska State Historical Society or the Legislative Research Office. No funding or appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that each legislator complete a 20-question civics assessment within 90 days of taking office. Agencies and roles named:

- United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (Department of Homeland Security): source of the civics portion of the U.S. Naturalization Test; Secretary of State or the State Department of Education may designate an equivalent test.

- Clerk of the Legislature: administers the exam, selects questions, calculates and certifies raw scores to members, keeps individual answers confidential, and publishes raw scores on the Legislature’s website and in members’ official biographies.

- Nebraska State Historical Society and Legislative Research Office: may offer voluntary civic literacy seminars for members who do not achieve a passing score.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
