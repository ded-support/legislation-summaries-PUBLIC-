Summary

This bill amends Laws 2025, LB261, §245, revising appropriations for Agency No. 72 — Department of Economic Development, Program 601 (Community and Rural Development).

Key appropriations:

- FY2025-26: GF $7,294,942; Cash $36,575,991; Federal est. $54,572,337; Program total $98,443,270; salary limit $3,330,071.

- FY2026-27: GF $6,294,942; Cash $21,671,030; Federal est. $54,632,192; Program total $82,598,164; salary limit $3,381,786.

Designated aid and allocations to the Department:

- State aid FY25-26: GF $6,580,762; Cash $33,726,860; Federal $50,530,110. FY26-27: GF $5,580,762; Cash $19,023,140; Federal $50,530,110.

- FY25-26 $250,000 Cash (Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund) for a prefabricated housing study.

- $700,000 GF each fiscal year to development districts.

- $4,880,762 GF each year for mentorship programs; eligibility to be designed by the Department of Economic Development to either improve elementary reading levels or promote professional growth.

- FY25-26 $1,000,000 GF for the grant in §81-1213.04.

- Reappropriation of unexpended Cash Fund balances for specified housing acts.
