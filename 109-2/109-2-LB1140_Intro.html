Summary

Amends Laws 2025, LB261, §54 (Agency No. 13 — State Department of Education, Program No. 351 Vocational Rehabilitation). FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 appropriation lines are shown (General Fund: $6,561,850 → $6,724,525; Cash Fund: $557,883 → $571,719; Federal: $30,807,902 → $31,596,542; Program total: $37,927,635 → $38,892,786; Salary limit: $15,709,139 → $16,203,613). Each year includes $49,289 GF and $8,560,706 federal funds earmarked for state aid. For FY2026-27 the bill adds a $500,000 General Fund appropriation for a multi‑trade preapprenticeship pilot serving young adults in a city of the metropolitan class. It directs the SDE’s Division of Rehabilitation Services and DHHS’s Division of Developmental Disabilities to collaborate on employment for individuals with disabilities and maximize federal matching funds under WIOA and the HCBS Final Rule.
