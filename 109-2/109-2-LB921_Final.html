Summary

LB921 assigns new duties to the Department of Labor (DOL) and to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). DOL/Commissioner of Labor will enforce the Nebraska WARN Act, adopt rules, receive employer notices, consider agreed communication modes in failure-to-appear determinations, and administer a new Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act requiring annual registration and a $1,500 fee per Nebraska physical location (or $1,500 statewide if no Nebraska location). The Act authorizes civil penalties ($500 first, $5,000 subsequent), up to one-year registration revocations, requires a public registration database, and adds this registration to the DOL‑administered Contractor and Professional Employer Organization Registration Cash Fund. DAS’s Risk Management and State Claims Division (the “Division”) and Risk Manager are authorized to receive/investigate/process claims, develop forms/procedures, submit claims to the Legislature, direct payment of approved claims ≤ $5,000, and report paid claims; the State Claims Board may direct payment ≤ $50,000 if funds exist. The State Treasurer must transfer $1,700,000 from the Contractor and PEO Registration Cash Fund to the General Fund by June 15, 2018; funds in that cash fund may be transferred to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor (DOL)/Commissioner

- New Nebraska Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act: DOL is the enforcing agency; employers must give the department written 90‑day notices for covered plant closings/mass layoffs. DOL receives notices (must keep employee names/addresses confidential), may adopt rules, and may assess civil penalties up to $100 per day for violations.

- Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act: Commissioner/DOL must register staffing agencies annually, collect a $1,500 annual fee per physical location (or one $1,500 statewide fee if no Nebraska physical location), maintain a public online registration database, operate a complaint/reporting system, conduct investigations and random audits, subpoena and enforce compliance, assess civil penalties ($500 first, $5,000 subsequent), and revoke registrations (up to one year). Appeal rights and departmental/Attorney General representation are provided.
