Summary

LB921 assigns new duties to the Department of Labor (DOL) and clarifies Department of Administrative Services (DAS) risk/claims functions and fund transfers. DOL must enforce the Nebraska WARN Act (employer notice requirement; civil penalty up to $100/day; rulemaking) and implement a Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act: annual registration ($1,500 per Nebraska location or $1,500 statewide if no Nebraska physical location), certification, public registry, complaint investigations, audits with subpoena/investigatory powers, penalties ($500 first; $5,000 subsequent), and up to one‑year registration revocation. Registration fees are deposited into the Contractor and PEO Registration Cash Fund administered by DOL. DAS’s risk management division and Risk Manager may receive/investigate/process claims, direct payment of approved claims ≤ $5,000 (with agency head agreement), and the State Claims Board may direct payments ≤ $50,000. Risk Manager must electronically report paid claims/judgments to the Legislature. The bill permits transfers from the Contractor/PEO fund to the General Fund and requires a $1,700,000 transfer to the General Fund by June 15, 2018.

Department of Labor

- Designated enforcement authority for the new Nebraska Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act; employers must provide the department written WARN notices (including employee addresses, which the department must keep confidential). - Establishes Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act administered by the Commissioner/Department of Labor: annual registration required ($1,500 per physical location or $1,500 statewide if no Nebraska physical location); department maintains a public online registry (registration and expiration/revocation dates). Commissioner may investigate, audit, subpoena, assess civil penalties ($500 first offense; $5,000 subsequent), and revoke registrations up to one year. Complaint intake and enforcement procedures created.
