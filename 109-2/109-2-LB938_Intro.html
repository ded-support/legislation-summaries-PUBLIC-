Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a First‑Time Home Buyer Savings Account (effective Jan 1, 2027) with contribution limits ($5,000/year individual; $10,000 joint; lifetime caps $25,000/$50,000), qualified‑beneficiary rules, allowable uses, and recapture/penalty rules (recapture if withdrawn within 1 year or used for nonqualified purposes; penalty to be paid to "the department" — 5% if withdrawal ≤10 years after first deposit, 10% if >10 years).

Department of Banking and Finance: may adopt rules to implement the Act but is prohibited from imposing administrative, reporting, or other requirements on financial institutions beyond normal account procedures.

Department of Revenue: administers the tax subtraction (state AGI reduction up to $5,000/$10,000 and excluded earnings) and the recapture/add‑back provisions and collects penalties. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
