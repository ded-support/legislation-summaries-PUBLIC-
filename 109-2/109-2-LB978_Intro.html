Summary

LB978 authorizes the Nebraska Attorney General and county attorneys to bring civil actions against persons who distribute or facilitate prohibited content (obscene materials, child sexual abuse materials, child sexual exploitation devices/images), seeking injunctions, equitable relief, and civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation. Civil penalties recovered are remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, section 5, of the Nebraska Constitution. The bill limits liability for certain entities—Internet utilities (when merely providing access), courts, judges (good‑faith viewing/possession for official purposes), attorneys, and their agents/employees—in specified circumstances. Sovereign immunity is expressly not an affirmative defense for actions under the bill.
