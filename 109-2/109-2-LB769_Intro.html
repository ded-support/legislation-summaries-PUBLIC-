Summary

LB769 directs the Department of Motor Vehicles to issue and design specified alternate license plates, including creation of up to three University of Nebraska State Museum plate designs (in consultation with the University and a nonprofit); applications for those plates begin January 1, 2027. The bill names the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund as the designated recipient for Czech Heritage Plates. It also prescribes fee allocations affecting the DMV Cash Fund: Nebraska Cornhusker Spirit Plates carry a $70 fee with 60% credited to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and 40% to the designated recipient (Highway Trust Fund); personalized Support Our Troops plates carry a $70 fee with 25% credited to the DMV Cash Fund (alphanumeric Support Our Troops fees are $5, credited to the designated recipient).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DMV actions: require creation of up to three University of Nebraska State Museum license plate designs (in consultation with the University and a qualifying nonprofit) that reflect support for Ashfall Fossil Beds, Morrill Hall, and Trailside Museum. DMV will offer the plates beginning January 1, 2027, and alternate plate fees are designated to the University of Nebraska State Museum License Plate Cash Fund.

Funding/appropriations: the bill creates the University of Nebraska State Museum License Plate Cash Fund, administered by the Board of Regents, receiving monies credited under section 60‑3,163.02 (alternate plate fees) plus gifts, grants, bequests, and other sources. Funds are to be invested by the state investment officer under applicable acts and used for research, public education, and outreach at University‑managed museums. No direct DMV appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
