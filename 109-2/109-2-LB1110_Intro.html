Summary

LB1110 primarily amends provisions affecting the Department of Revenue. Key points:

- Creates a Charitable Gaming Division and Charitable Gaming Operations Fund; 40% of specified charitable-gaming taxes available to the division for administration/enforcement and support of the Nebraska Commission on Problem Gambling; the division may charge publication and copying fees. Quarterly transfers of $100,000 to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund are continued through June 30, 2026; remaining funds may be transferred to the General Fund or Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund as directed by the Legislature.

- Authorizes the Tax Commissioner to contract with collection agencies and assign delinquent tax claims; agency fees capped at 50% of amounts collected; remittance rules set.

- Directs various tax receipts (e.g., cash-device tax, tobacco product revenues) to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund and specifies percentage distributions.

- Establishes and specifies uses, transferability, and investment of the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

No provisions explicitly reference the Department of Administrative Services, Department of Labor, or Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Revenue

- Allows collection agencies to add previously assessed collection fees/costs to a delinquent tax balance—up to 50% of the balance—and requires those added fees to be remitted and deposited the same way as the taxes.

- Directs the Department of Revenue to remit all funds received under this section to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

- Authorizes the Tax Commissioner to register delinquent tax claims as judgments in Lancaster County, using the Nebraska Uniform Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Act process.

- Transfers all amounts credited to the Tobacco Products Administration Cash Fund (before credits/refunds) into the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

- Specifies the Enforcement Fund may be used by the Department of Revenue for administration and enforcement of Tax Commissioner functions.
