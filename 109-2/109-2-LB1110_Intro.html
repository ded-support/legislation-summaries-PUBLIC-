Summary

The bill makes multiple amendments affecting the Department of Revenue (DoR) and DoR-controlled funds. It creates a Charitable Gaming Division in DoR, authorizes fees remitted to a Charitable Gaming Operations Fund, allocates 40% of certain charitable gaming taxes for the division’s administration (with specified quarterly $100,000 transfers to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund through June 30, 2026), and allows legislative-directed transfers of remaining funds to the General Fund or Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund. It authorizes the Tax Commissioner/DoR to contract with collection agencies (fees capped at 50%) and requires prompt remittance of collected funds. DoR may charge for publications/listings with receipts credited to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. Mechanical amusement device tax proceeds now allocate 20% to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. Tobacco-product revenues are redirected into DoR funds, with administration costs, credits, and refunds paid from the Enforcement Fund. The Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund is established for enforcement purposes and may receive transfers and be invested.
