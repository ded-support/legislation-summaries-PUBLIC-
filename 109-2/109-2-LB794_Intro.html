Summary

LB794 amends statutes affecting the Department of Agriculture by:

- Specifying department powers and duties: encourage and promote agriculture; promote production/distribution methods; collect and publish agricultural statistics (cooperate with federal gov.); publish/distribute Weeds of the Great Plains and remit proceeds to the State Treasurer; investigate contagious animal diseases; adopt and enforce rules; employ special investigators; perform laboratory testing per section 81-2,293; and enforce the Foreign-owned Real Estate National Security Act.

- Requiring the Department to submit electronically to the Legislature, before January 1, 2027, the Healthy Soils Task Force’s comprehensive action plan, findings/recommendations, agendas, minutes, and submitted documents.

- Addressing funding: receipts under fertilizer/liming laws are credited to the Fertilizers and Soil Conditioners Administrative Fund for department administration (with transfers to the General Fund permitted by the Legislature). The Director may accept gifts/fees credited to the Agricultural Products Marketing Information Cash Fund; reimbursements must be remitted to that fund and may be invested.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Agriculture to electronically submit to the Clerk of the Legislature by January 1, 2027, all materials the department received from the Healthy Soils Task Force (created under Laws 2019, LB243, §2), specifically: the comprehensive action plan filed under former §2‑404 (as of Jan 1, 2021); the findings and recommendations reported under that same former section; and agendas, meeting minutes, and any other documents the task force submitted to the department. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are added or changed.
