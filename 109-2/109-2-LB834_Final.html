Summary

The bill makes multiple DMV and Department of Revenue changes. Department of Motor Vehicles: expands title procedures for manufactured/mobile homes to allow reliance on landlord–tenant disposition law or county assessor records plus owner affidavit; authorizes the DMV or county treasurer to issue titles when submitted evidence is sufficient; permits acceptance of electronic certificate-of-title records after repossession with the Director setting the implementation date; and requires the DMV to collect sales tax on certain vehicle registrations, retain a collection fee (deposited to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund), and forfeit fees if rules are violated. In counties with 100,000+ population, county treasurers must remit $1 of their collection fee for each of the first 5,000 registrations (on/after Jan 1, 2020) to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. Department of Revenue: the Property Tax Administrator will schedule assessor certification exams (quarterly and off-cycle), set fees credited to the Property Assessment Division Cash Fund (fee amount set annually by the Tax Commissioner), and may extend certain county filing deadlines.
