Summary

LB834 directs actions for the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and the Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner):

- DMV title rules: expands acceptable proofs and procedures for issuing certificates of title for manufactured/mobile homes and other vehicles (including assessment records, Disposition of Personal Property Landlord and Tenant Act evidence, other sufficient evidence, and electronic title records after repossession). The DMV Director sets the implementation date for these provisions.

- Tax collection and remittance: DMV may collect sales/use tax on vehicles at registration, remit collections monthly to the Tax Commissioner, and deduct a collection fee deposited into the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund (fee forfeited for rule violations).

- County treasurers in counties ≥100,000 must remit $1 of their collection fee for each of the first 5,000 registrations to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund.

- Certification exam fees for county assessor applicants are credited to the Department of Revenue Property Assessment Division Cash Fund; the Tax Commissioner annually sets fee amounts to cover administration.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions directly affect the Department of Motor Vehicles. For the Department of Revenue (via the Property Tax Administrator), added text:

- Authorizes county assessors to appoint revocable deputies and sets filing, oath, bond, and deputy‑duties rules.

- Grants the Property Tax Administrator rulemaking authority to define when a county has a “pressing need” for a deputy county assessor.

- Adds/clarifies mobile‑home reporting and permit procedures: annual reporting to county assessors and permit issuance by county treasurers (forms to be provided by assessors).

No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency are included in the added text.
