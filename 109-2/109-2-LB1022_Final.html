Summary

LB1022 amends Nebraska teacher certification law. Key agency actions and fiscal provisions:

- State Board of Education: retains rulemaking authority for issuance, renewal, suspension, revocation, and endorsements; no basic skills test required. The bill removes the human‑relations training requirement for certificates and permits to teach, provide special services, or administer.

- Commissioner of Education / Department of Education: issues certificates/permits, operates an online endorsement portal, may set nonrefundable fees (endorsement fee ≤ $55; full‑school certificate/permit fee ≤ $65), and verifies out‑of‑state credentials. Criminal‑history checks remain required.

- Certification Fund: created; fees remitted to State Treasurer for credit to the fund to pay certification costs and reimburse school districts for replacement hires when employees serve on board advisory committees. The fund may transfer money to the Education Future Fund at legislative direction and is investable by the state investment officer.
