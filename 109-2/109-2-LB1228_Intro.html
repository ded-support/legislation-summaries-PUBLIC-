Summary

LB1228 establishes a new state docket fee assessed in civil, dissolution, traffic, and small-claims cases (district/Supreme $116; county/separate juvenile civil $70; dissolution $111; traffic $61; small claims $38). Fees are remitted to the State Treasurer within ten days monthly; $10 of each fee is credited to a newly created JUSTICE Software Cash Fund and the remainder to a newly created Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund. The State Court Administrator administers both funds; the JUSTICE fund is for software purchase/implementation/maintenance, and the Operations fund may supplement state funding for administrative operations of the Supreme Court, Court of Appeals, district courts, separate juvenile courts, county courts, and the Nebraska Probation System. The state investment officer may invest fund balances per state investment statutes. Counties, cities, or villages are not required to pay state docket fees deemed uncollectible; amended collection procedures for uncollectible court costs remain.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a new state docket fee for various court filings (amounts range $38–$116) payable on civil, dissolution, traffic, and small-claims cases filed in the Supreme Court, Court of Appeals/district courts, county courts, and separate juvenile courts. Fees are remitted monthly to the State Treasurer on prescribed forms; $10 per fee is credited to a new JUSTICE Software Cash Fund and the remainder to a new Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund. Both funds are administered by the State Court Administrator, may be invested by the state investment officer under existing investment acts, and fund court administrative operations (Supreme Court, Court of Appeals, district/county/separate juvenile courts) and the Nebraska Probation System; the JUSTICE fund is restricted to software costs. Counties/cities need not pay fees deemed uncollectible.
