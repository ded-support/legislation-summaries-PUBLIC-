Summary

LB1228 imposes a new state docket fee on specified cases (civil district/Supreme $116; civil county/separate juvenile $70; dissolution $111; traffic misdemeanor/infraction $61; small claims $38). Fees are remitted monthly to the State Treasurer on prescribed forms; $10 per fee is credited to a newly created JUSTICE Software Cash Fund and the remainder to a newly created Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund. The State Court Administrator will administer both funds. The Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund may supplement state funding for administrative operations of the Supreme Court, Court of Appeals, district courts, separate juvenile courts, county courts, and the Nebraska Probation System. The JUSTICE Software Cash Fund may be used for software purchase/implementation/maintenance. Funds may be invested by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The bill also amends procedure for deeming certain court costs (including the state docket fee) uncollectible; counties/cities/villages are not required to pay uncollectible state docket fees.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a state docket fee for civil, dissolution, traffic, and small‑claims cases (rates: $116, $70, $111, $61, $38 respectively). Fees are remitted monthly to the State Treasurer (within 10 days after month end). $10 of each fee is credited to a new JUSTICE Software Cash Fund and the remainder to a new Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund. The State Court Administrator administers both funds. Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund may supplement state funding for administrative operations of the Supreme Court, Court of Appeals, district courts, separate juvenile courts, county courts, and the Nebraska Probation System. The JUSTICE fund may defray software purchase/implementation/maintenance. Funds may be invested by the state investment officer under existing state investment laws. Local governments aren’t liable for fees deemed uncollectible.
