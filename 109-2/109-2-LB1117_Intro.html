Summary

References to the Department of Revenue:

- Under amended section 85-2616, a qualifying child must file with the Department of Revenue annual documentation proving that the first responder or eligible disabled person through whom the child claims the waiver (i) is employed as a first responder or is an eligible disabled person and (ii) resides in Nebraska.

- If the first responder died in the line of duty after applying, the qualifying child must file with the Department of Revenue documentation showing that, at the time of death, the first responder was employed as a first responder and resided in Nebraska.

Funding/appropriations (separate provisions):

- Beginning July 1, 2028, subject to available appropriations, the Coordinating Commission shall reimburse institutions for 50% of tuition waivers for dependents of veterans and for waivers under the First Responder Act; reimbursements require commission documentation, and payments will be prorated if appropriations are insufficient.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education, beginning July 1, 2028 and subject to available appropriations, to reimburse the University of Nebraska, each state college, and each community college for 50% of tuition waivers provided under section 80-411. Institutions must submit documentation in a form prescribed by the commission. If state appropriations are insufficient, reimbursements will be prorated across institutions so total payments equal the appropriation; prorated claims may not be resubmitted. There are funding/appropriation implications for the commission and state budget, but no duties, funding changes, or budget allocations are assigned to the Department of Revenue.
