Summary

Creates a new consumption-only class of entertainment-district license within the Nebraska Liquor Control Act. Key government entities and actions:

- Nebraska Liquor Control Commission: receives district designations, processes and approves applications, is notified when local clerks receive applications, and collects a $25 fee for consumption-only licenses.

- Local governing bodies (city or village and their clerks): may designate or revoke entertainment districts and designate consumption-only areas, must file designations with the commission, must approve consumption-only applicants before commission action, collect an additional $300 entertainment-district license fee payable to the local clerk, may impose an occupation tax under section 53-132, and may cancel licenses for cause.

No other appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a consumption-only class of entertainment district license and related procedures. Key government actors: the local governing body (which designates consumption-only areas and must preapprove applicants) and the commission (which prescribes forms, collects a $25 application fee payable to the commission, and must grant licenses when local approval and designation are in place). Licenses are authorized under the Nebraska Liquor Control Act; holders may permit patrons to carry alcoholic beverages sold by other entertainment district licensees into/out of their premises so long as they do not themselves sell/serve alcohol and comply with law (including minors provisions). No other funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are added.
