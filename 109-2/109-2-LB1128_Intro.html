Summary

LB1128 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §53-123.17 to allow local governing bodies (city/village) to designate entertainment districts with designated consumption-only areas and to file that designation with the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission. It creates a new consumption-only class entertainment district license for businesses lacking retail/craft brewery/microdistillery/manufacturer licenses. Consumption-only applications require commission approval and a $25 fee payable to the commission; existing eligible licensees pay a $300 additional entertainment-district fee payable to the local clerk. The commission must notify the clerk when applications are filed and process applications per §53-132. Local governing bodies may revoke designations, cancel licenses for cause, regulate by ordinance, and impose an occupation tax under §53-132. Appeals go to the commission under §53-134. No state appropriations or other budgetary allocations are added.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a new "consumption-only" class of entertainment district license. Local governing bodies must designate consumption-only areas and preapprove applicants. The Nebraska Liquor Control Commission (referred to as "the commission") will accept applications on commission-prescribed forms and issue the license if local approval exists; a $25 application fee is payable to the commission. The license lets a business (which does not sell or serve alcohol and lacks other liquor licenses) permit patrons to carry alcoholic beverages purchased from other entertainment district licensees into and out of its premises, subject to the Nebraska Liquor Control Act (including prohibitions on underage consumption). No other appropriations or budgetary changes are added.
