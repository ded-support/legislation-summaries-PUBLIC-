Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions create a freestanding birth center licensing framework administered by the Licensing Agency (presumably the Department of Health and Human Services if it is the state licensing body). Key DHHS-relevant actions: issuance/renewal/change-of-ownership processes; mandatory CABC accreditation (or in-process) and submission of accreditation materials on request; enforcement authority with 60‑day notice and hearing rights; mandatory patient rights, health record and reporting requirements; and closure notification duties. Fee schedule: $250 for initial, renewal, and change‑of‑ownership licenses; late renewals incur $50/month. Fees are nonrefundable and must accompany applications. No new appropriations, budget allocations, or other funding changes are included.
