Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary for Department of Labor and Department of Health and Human Services (HHS):

- HHS (referred to as "the department") will consult with the licensing office to adopt standards requiring licensed case managers, investigators, and supervisors to act prudently, diligently, with integrity and fairness; no state liability is created for their acts/omissions.

- New standards must reduce investigation duration; provide timely notice to families/foster parents (unless prohibited); require weekly updates; define when investigations remain open; bar placing foster homes on hold absent definitive evidence of violence/malfeasance (allowing respite); and document foster-parent input.

- The office must submit an annual electronic report to the Legislature and publish it on the department’s website covering licensure/disciplinary activity, complaint trends, estimated fiscal impacts of unsubstantiated removals or misconduct, and progress toward efficiency, compliance, and federal Title IV‑E funding eligibility.
