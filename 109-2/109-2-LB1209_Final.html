Summary

LB1209 amends FY2025‑27 appropriations and makes agency-specific allocations and administrative changes:

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS): authority to draw warrants, monitor appropriations, administratively increase salary limits, and effect certified transfers; budget administrator directed to schedule a $45,000,000 transfer from the Nebraska Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund to the General Fund (on/after 7/1/2026).

- Department of Labor: Division of Employment receives $6,500,000 Cash Funds (FY2026‑27) including $4,000,000 Cash for workforce development state aid; Division for Protection of People and Property fund levels amended.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Medical Assistance appropriated (FY2025‑26: $1,085,315,619 GF, $740,239,274 Cash, $3,928,839,289 Federal; FY2026‑27: $843,522,688 GF, $728,739,274 Cash, $3,614,112,414 Federal); $1.5M Cash (Hospital Quality Assurance) and $1,408,364 Cash (assisted‑living) specified; DAS may transfer unexpended balances to Developmental Disability Aid.

- Department of Revenue: $336,404 Cash Funds/year for tobacco MSA audit/enforcement staff (from Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund).

- Department of Economic Development: Industrial Recruitment appropriations (FY2025‑26 total $125,263,933; FY2026‑27 total $99,909,619) including $50,000,000 Cash (Nebraska Transformational Project Fund) each year and $4,000,000 Cash (ImagiNE Revolving Loan Fund) FY2025‑26.

- Sec. 21 lists numerous Cash Funds credited to agencies including Agriculture, Banking and Finance, Insurance, Motor Vehicles, Transportation, Correctional Services, Veterans' Affairs, and the Military Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Key additions affecting agencies:

- Department of Administrative Services: authorizes DAS budget administrator to administratively increase limits on permanent/temporary salary and per diem expenditures to cover actual payments of accumulated vacation/sick leave; Director of Administrative Services authorized to draw warrants; State Treasurer to pay them. General reappropriation/lapsing rules clarified (see below).

- Department of Labor: Division of Employment—Cash Fund appropriation $0 (FY25‑26) and $6,500,000 (FY26‑27); $4,000,000 of FY26‑27 earmarked as state aid for workforce development.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Behavioral Health Aid—Cash Fund $0 (FY25‑26) and $470,000 (FY26‑27) for state aid.

- Other listed agencies (Agriculture; Banking & Finance; Economic Development; Insurance; Correctional Services; Motor Vehicles; Revenue; Transportation; Veterans' Affairs; Military Department): no underlined additions or funding changes shown.

- Fiscal rules: GF/Cash/Revolving unexpended appropriations lapse June 30, 2025 unless provided; certified encumbrances are reappropriated for FY25‑26 and FY26‑27; unexpended balances on June 30, 2026 reappropriated to FY26‑27.
