Summary

LB1209 adjusts FY2025-26–FY2026-27 appropriations and related authorities.

- Department of Administrative Services: authority to draw warrants and monitor program appropriations; budget administrator may increase salary limits administratively; authorized to administratively transfer DHHS Medical Assistance unexpended balances to Developmental Disability Aid as certified; directs the State Treasurer transfer $45,000,000 from the Nebraska Tobacco Settlement Trust Fund to the General Fund in FY2026-27.

- Department of Labor: Division of Employment receives $6,500,000 Cash Funds in FY2026-27 (including $4,000,000 Cash Funds in FY2026-27 for workforce development state aid); Division for Protection of People and Property cash and program totals increased.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Medical Assistance appropriations set (FY25-26 GF $1,085,315,619; FY26-27 GF $843,522,688; sizable Cash and Federal fund amounts); nursing facility rate funding specified ($449,351,518 per year); $1,500,000 Cash (FY25-26) for behavioral health provider reimbursement; $1,408,364 Cash each year for assisted‑living rate increases.

- Department of Revenue: Revenue Administration appropriations (FY25-26 GF $17,399,357; FY26-27 GF $595,791); $336,404 Cash Funds annually for tobacco MSA audit/enforcement staff.

- Department of Economic Development: Industrial Recruitment appropriations (FY25-26 GF $18,060,554; FY26-27 GF $6,195,537) with large Cash Fund/state aid components including $50,000,000 Cash from the Nebraska Transformational Project Fund in both years, $4,000,000 Cash from ImagiNE Revolving Loan Fund (FY25-26), reappropriations, and intent to add $4,500,000 in FY2027-28.

- The act also enumerates Cash Funds for Department of Agriculture, Department of Banking and Finance, Department of Insurance, Department of Motor Vehicles, Department of Transportation, Department of Veterans' Affairs, Military Department, Department of Correctional Services, and others.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines FY2025-26–FY2028-29 appropriation periods and makes appropriations for FY2025-26 and FY2026-27. Key fiscal provisions: June 30, 2025 General Fund, Cash Fund, and Revolving Fund unexpended balances lapse unless expended/encumbered; certified encumbrances on June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026 are reappropriated for FY2025-26 and FY2026-27; unexpended balances on June 30, 2026 are reappropriated to FY2026-27. The Budget Division (Department of Administrative Services) may administratively increase salary/per diem limits to cover leave payouts; the Director of Administrative Services draws warrants and the State Treasurer pays them. Agency-specific additions: Department of Labor — Division of Employment: $6,500,000 Cash Funds in FY2026-27 (including $4,000,000 state aid for workforce development). Department of Health and Human Services — Behavioral Health Aid: $470,000 Cash Funds in FY2026-27 (state aid). No other listed agencies have added appropriations in the excerpt.
