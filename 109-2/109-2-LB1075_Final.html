Summary

The bill requires the Department of Administrative Services to develop, maintain, and publish a statewide database of municipal financial information (using auditor-submitted municipal audit data and potentially revenue sources, expenditures, balance sheets) for every county, city, and village, with implementation on or before January 1, 2027. Section 66 (amending 69‑1317) makes certain State Treasurer records (social security numbers, dates of birth, last-known addresses) confidential and “subject to the same confidentiality as tax return information held by the Department of Revenue,” while allowing the Auditor of Public Accounts unrestricted access. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation for DAS or the Department of Revenue is provided; other bill sections do not affect these agencies.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: Adds a requirement that DAS develop, maintain, and post a public website database of financial information for all counties, cities, and villages, including information provided to the auditor under sections 13-506 and 23-1608, and optionally revenue sources, expenditures, and audited balance sheets. DAS must set an implementation date on or before January 1, 2027. No appropriation, funding source, or budget allocation for this work is specified.

Department of Revenue: No changes, funding, or budgetary provisions affecting the Department of Revenue are included in the added text.
