Summary

The bill directs the Department of Administrative Services to develop, maintain, and publish by January 1, 2027, a web-accessible database of financial information for all counties, cities, and villages, including auditor-provided audit materials and, where available, recent municipal audit revenue, expenditure, and balance-sheet data. It contains no appropriation or specified funding for this DAS requirement. The bill also classifies unclaimed‑property owner records (SSN, DOB, last-known address) as confidential and subjects them to the same protections as tax return information held by the Department of Revenue, while exempting the Auditor of Public Accounts from that restriction. Other shown sections contain no additional duties, reporting requirements, or budget changes for DAS or Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: Requires DAS to create, maintain, and publish an online public database of financial information for all counties, cities, and villages. The database must include data provided to the auditor under sections 13-506 and 23-1608 and may include revenue sources, expenditures, and balance sheets (assets/liabilities) from the most recent municipal audit filed under section 19-2905, if available. DAS must set an implementation date on or before January 1, 2027. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation language is included.

Department of Revenue: No new or amended provisions affecting the Department of Revenue appear in the added text.
