Summary

LB1078 (2026) creates the Fair Online Pricing Act. Enforcement is vested in the Nebraska Attorney General, who may bring actions to recover civil penalties (up to $10,000 per violation), obtain injunctive relief, and recover reasonable attorney fees and investigation expenses. All penalties collected are to be remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution pursuant to Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution. The bill contains no separate appropriations or budget allocations and does not specify new funding for enforcement; duties are declared cumulative with other legal obligations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions create the "Fair Online Pricing Act," banning online price generation based on a device's hardware or hardware state, presence/absence of software, or geolocation-based consumer inferences (with narrow exceptions). Enforcement and penalties: the Nebraska Attorney General may sue in the name of the State to recover civil penalties (up to $10,000 per violation), obtain injunctive relief, and recover reasonable attorney fees and investigation costs. All penalty funds must be remitted to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. No other agencies are assigned duties and no new appropriations or specific budget allocations are specified. Duties are cumulative with existing law.
