Summary

LB735 specifies that, for purposes of national criminal history record checks under the Uniform Credentialing Act, "the department" means the Division of Public Health of the Department of Health and Human Services. The bill requires the Nebraska State Patrol to provide criminal history reports to that department and authorizes the department and the Patrol to adopt rules concerning costs associated with fingerprinting and FBI background checks. No appropriations, budget changes, or other DHHS funding actions are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Respiratory Care Interstate Compact creating a multi‑state Commission to govern interstate respiratory therapist practice. Implications for the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- DHHS (or its Respiratory Therapy Licensing Authority) must appoint a Commissioner, enforce Compact rules, monitor and report adverse actions, and submit a uniform data set to the Commission's centralized Data System.

- DHHS would implement Commission rulemaking, subpoenas, compliance reviews, and disciplinary actions under state law.

- The Commission may levy annual Member‑state assessments and fees on licensees to fund operations; DHHS may be responsible for paying assessments or administering fee collection. Active military members/spouses may receive fee waivers.

- No direct state appropriations or explicit funding amounts are included; potential budget impacts are limited to administrative, data‑reporting, assessment, and legal costs.
