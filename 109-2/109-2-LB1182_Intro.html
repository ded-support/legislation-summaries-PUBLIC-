Summary

LB1182 directs the Legislature to set a $50,000 minimum annual base salary for full‑time certificated public school teachers beginning in the 2027–28 school year, applicable regardless of experience or credentials. Biennial adjustments begin 2029–30, tied to the percentage change in Nebraska's total state General Fund receipts over the prior two years (no reductions; rounded to nearest $100); the State Department of Education must publish the adjusted amount by March 1 each year. The bill expresses legislative intent to convert foundation aid and salary support under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act (TEEOSA) to quarterly block grants to districts starting July 1, 2027, with grant formulas to consider enrollment and the new base salary. The State Board of Education must adopt implementing rules and monitor compliance. The act repeals the original section 79‑1001. No specific appropriations are contained.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Establishes a $50,000 minimum annual base salary for full‑time certificated public school teachers beginning in the 2027–28 school year; districts may pay more or maintain differentiated schedules.

- Indexing: beginning 2029–30 and biennially thereafter, the minimum is adjusted by the percentage change in Nebraska’s total state General Fund receipts over the prior two‑year period (minimum 0% increase; never reduced below prior year); rounded to the nearest $100. The State Department of Education must publish the adjusted minimum annually by March 1.

- Creates a shift to block grant funding for foundation aid and salary support, with quarterly district block grants starting July 1, 2027; grant amounts set by a legislative formula (factors include student enrollment and the new minimum teacher salary).

- The State Board of Education must promulgate rules for implementation, compliance monitoring, and reporting (teacher salaries and student counts).
