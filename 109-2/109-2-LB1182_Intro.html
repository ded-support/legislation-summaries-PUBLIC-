Summary

Creates a statewide minimum base salary of $50,000 for full‑time certificated public school teachers effective 2027‑28. Biennial adjustments begin 2029‑30, tied to the percent change in Nebraska's total state General Fund receipts over the prior two years (floor zero; cannot decrease year‑to‑year); the State Department of Education must publish the adjusted amount by March 1 each year.

Declares legislative intent to convert foundation aid and salary support under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act (TEEOSA) to a block grant system: quarterly district block grants begin July 1, 2027, with a Legislature‑established formula (factors to include enrollment and the statutory minimum salary). The State Board of Education is directed to adopt rules to implement/enforce the sections, including monitoring compliance and reporting teacher salaries and student counts. Includes severability and repeals the original statutory section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Establishes a $50,000 annual minimum base salary for full‑time certificated public school teachers beginning 2027–28; applies to all certificated teachers regardless of experience or education.

- Beginning 2029–30 and biennially thereafter, the minimum is adjusted by the percentage change in Nebraska’s total state General Fund receipts over the prior two years (floor 0%, cannot decrease below prior year), rounded to the nearest $100; the State Department of Education must publish the adjusted amount annually by March 1.

- Legislature intends to convert foundation aid and salary support under the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act to quarterly block grants for each district beginning July 1, 2027; grant amounts set by a legislative formula (factors include enrollment and the minimum base salary).

- The State Board of Education must adopt rules to implement/enforce the law, including compliance monitoring and reporting on teacher salaries and student counts.
