Summary

The bill amends distributions of revenue received from the Department of Revenue under the nameplate capacity tax. County treasurers must distribute amounts as follows: 5% to the community college area; the remainder to local taxing entities that would have received personal property tax revenue from depreciable electric generation property. Counties that adopt the Affordable American Energy best practices receive a 150% multiplier on their distribution share; such county receipts must be split 50/50 between county property tax relief and payments to electric utilities (pro rata by residential customers) to reduce customer bills via efficiency or demand‑side programs. The Department of Revenue is prohibited from retaining any of these collections for General Fund use.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development: directs the department to develop an "Affordable American energy best practices" list (considering other states' siting criteria), publish a preliminary list by Oct 15, 2026 (30-day public comment) and a final list by Dec 15, 2026, using existing staff/resources. Creates the Affordable American Energy Best Practices Cash Fund administered by the department to receive gifts/grants/donations (no General Fund transfers). Fund use limited to educational purposes related to the Act; money may be invested by the state investment officer. The department may adopt rules to implement the Act.

Department of Revenue / tax allocation: modifies nameplate capacity tax revenue distribution to prioritize counties that adopt the best practices by applying a 150% multiplier to their share. County receipts must be split 50% for county property tax relief and 50% to electric utilities to reduce customer bills via efficiency/demand‑side programs (pro rata by residential customers).
