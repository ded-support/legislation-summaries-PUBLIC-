Summary

LB945 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-2106 governing the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund. Key agency/administrative provisions:

- Creates the Fund; interest credited and investments managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- Directs the department to use the Fund (including federal match) to enhance hospital rates under the medical assistance program and prohibits replacing existing state hospital funding.

- Requires Fund use to: reimburse the General Fund the first quarterly payment by June 30 each fiscal year; reimburse the department an administrative fee = 3% of the assessment (capped at $15 million/year); allocate 0.5% of the assessment (capped $2.5 million/year) to the Nebraska Center for Nursing Board for clinical training site expansion; allocate 3.5% of the assessment (capped $17.5 million/year) for nonhospital provider rates, continuous eligibility for children, and the designated health information exchange (§81-6,125).

- Remaining assessment proceeds and federal match must further enhance inpatient/outpatient hospital rates.

- Department may require hospitals that do not file Medicare cost reports to submit revenue/discharge/inpatient-day data.

- Department must prohibit Medicaid managed care organizations from factoring in, delaying, or recouping directed payments; hospitals are barred from passing assessments to patients or non‑Medicaid payors (violation bars directed payments for remainder of rate year).
