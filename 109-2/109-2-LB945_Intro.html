Summary

LB945 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68‑2106 governing the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund. Key agency actions and funding provisions:

- Interest credited to the Fund and investable amounts managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act.

- The department shall use Fund dollars (including federal match) to enhance hospital rates under the medical assistance program and may not replace existing state hospital payments.

- Fund allocations: reimburse the General Fund for the first quarterly payment by June 30 each fiscal year; pay the department an administrative fee = 3% of the assessment (capped at $15 million/year); provide 0.5% of the assessment (capped $2.5 million/year) to the Nebraska Center for Nursing Board for clinical training expansion; and provide 3.5% of the assessment (capped $17.5 million/year) for nonhospital provider rates, continuous eligibility for children, and the designated health information exchange.

- Remaining assessment proceeds and federal match must further enhance hospital inpatient/outpatient rates (in addition to legislative appropriations).

- The department must collect data from hospitals lacking Medicare cost reports and must prohibit Medicaid managed care organizations from factoring in, delaying, recouping, or offsetting directed payments. Hospitals are barred from passing assessment costs to patients/non‑Medicaid payors or using directed payments in rate negotiations; violations suspend directed payments for the remainder of the rate year.
