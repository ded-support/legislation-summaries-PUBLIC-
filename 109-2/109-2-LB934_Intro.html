Summary

LB934 restructures selection of the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission. Key agency changes:

- Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission: shifts from a hybrid body (three Nebraska Liquor Control Commission members serving ex officio and two gubernatorial appointees) to five elected members nominated at the statewide primary and elected at the statewide general election, effective January 4, 2029. One member is elected from each public service commissioner district; members run on the nonpartisan ballot. Staggered elections are set (districts 1,3,5 and districts 2,4 per a 2028/2030 schedule). Vacancies on the commission are filled by gubernatorial appointment.

- Election administration: amendments to the Election Act and ballot layout provisions (Secretary of State/ county election officials) add the commission to nonpartisan ticket ordering and reference election timing.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds election and membership rules for the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission (per section 71-24,109). Key points:

- Commission to have five members, one elected from each of the five public service commissioner districts.

- Districts 1, 3, and 5: members elected at the statewide general election in 2028 and every four years thereafter.

- Districts 2 and 4: members elected at the statewide general election in 2028, again in 2030, and every four years thereafter.

- Members serve until successors are elected and are elected on the nonpartisan ballot.

- Beginning January 4, 2029, members are nominated at the statewide primary and elected at the statewide general election.

- Vacancies are filled by gubernatorial appointment.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
