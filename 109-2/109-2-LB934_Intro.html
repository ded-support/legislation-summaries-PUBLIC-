Summary

LB934 restructures the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission from a body partly composed of the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission and gubernatorial appointees to a five-member, elected commission. Beginning Jan. 4, 2029, one commissioner will be elected from each of the five public service commissioner districts; elections are nonpartisan with initial elections held statewide in 2028 (districts 1,3,5 every four years thereafter; districts 2 and 4 also elected in 2030 to stagger terms). Vacancies are filled by gubernatorial appointment (per amended sec. 32-567 and 71-24,109). The bill updates Election Act ballot placement to list the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission among nonpartisan tickets. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Establishes new election rules and composition for the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission. Beginning January 4, 2029 the Commission will have five members, each elected from one of the five public service commissioner districts (per section 2). Members are nominated at the statewide primary and elected at the statewide general. Districts 1, 3, and 5 seats are elected in 2028 and every four years thereafter; districts 2 and 4 are elected in 2028, 2030, and every four years thereafter. Members serve until successors are elected, are elected on a nonpartisan ballot, and vacancies are filled by gubernatorial appointment. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
