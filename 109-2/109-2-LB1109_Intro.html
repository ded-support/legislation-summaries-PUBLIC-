Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: The bill retains sales-and-use-tax exemptions for nonprofit providers certified by the Department of Health and Human Services to deliver community-based services for persons with developmental disabilities and for organizations certified or contracted by regional behavioral health authorities or the Division of Behavioral Health to provide community-based mental health or substance-use services.

- Department of Revenue: The Department of Revenue is authorized to adopt rules to verify the validity and timeliness of claimed renewable energy tax credits. The statute limits total renewable energy tax credits usable by all taxpayers to $50,000.

- Department of Banking and Finance: The ImagiNE Nebraska Act definition of qualified location clarifies that “financial services” includes services provided by entities licensed by the Department of Banking and Finance.

No direct appropriations or budget allocations are included.
