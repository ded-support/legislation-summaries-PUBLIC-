Summary

LB718 contains no explicit references to the Department of Revenue. The bill amends statutory provisions governing certified public accountants (education, examination, reciprocal practice, permits, and disciplinary provisions) but includes no provisions affecting the Department of Revenue, nor does it alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions revise CPA examination eligibility. Applicants must hold a baccalaureate or higher from a U.S. Department of Education–recognized (or board‑accepted) accredited institution and show required postsecondary credit hours in accounting, auditing, business, and related subjects. Acceptable education/experience pathways: (i) postbaccalaureate degree plus one year experience; (ii) baccalaureate plus 30 additional credits and one year experience; or (iii) baccalaureate plus two years experience. Degrees must include board‑specified accounting subjects. Required experience must be satisfactory to the board, supervised by an active CPA holding a proper permit, and may be gained in public practice, government, industry, or academia. No provisions affect the Department of Revenue, and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
