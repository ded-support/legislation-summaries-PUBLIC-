Summary

LB1046 directs the State Board of Education and local school boards to adopt and administer policies governing high school student name, image, and likeness (NIL) commercial activities. Required elements include: definitions; prohibited promotional uses (e.g., alcohol, tobacco/vaping, controlled substances, gambling, adult entertainment, weapons, illegal or unlawful content); a disclosure process requiring students to notify a designated school official within five business days (with an appeal procedure); a duty for that official to notify any applicable athletic or activities association promptly; procedures for reviewing contracts signed by or for minors; and enforcement limited to nonmonetary sanctions (e.g., temporary ineligibility). The bill does not include any changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires schools to adopt policies governing student commercial name, image, and likeness (NIL) activity that: ban NIL activity during official school hours or school-sponsored events that recruits students or promotes alcohol, tobacco/vaping, controlled substances, gambling/sports betting, adult entertainment, weapons, illegal products, or content violating law/policy; require students to disclose commercial NIL activity to a designated school official prior to or within five business days (with an appeal process); obligate that official to notify any applicable athletic or activities association as soon as administratively possible; establish procedures to review contracts signed for minors; and spell out enforcement and non‑monetary penalties (e.g., temporary ineligibility). Mentions schools, designated school officials, and athletic/activities associations; no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
