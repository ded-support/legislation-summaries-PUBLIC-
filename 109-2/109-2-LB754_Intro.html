Summary

LB754 amends Nebraska law governing explosives permits. It designates the Nebraska State Patrol to issue storage, use, and business purchase permits; defines applicant disqualifications; requires fingerprinting with state and FBI criminal-history checks; and requires storage to meet safety/security standards set by the Patrol and the U.S. Secretary of the Treasury. Permits must specify class/type/quantity/security and may be issued for periods up to three years. Business purchase permits must be for in-state businesses, certify on‑date use (unless a storage permit exists), and require the business to employ at least one person with a valid use permit. Individual applicants must provide a social security number. The State Fire Marshal’s display-fireworks permit application must include proof of a Nebraska State Patrol use permit and a $100 fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the State Fire Marshal Cash Fund.
