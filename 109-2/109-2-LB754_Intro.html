Summary

LB754 updates Nebraska law on explosives permitting and fireworks displays.

- Nebraska State Patrol: retains authority to issue permits to store, use, and for business purchase of explosive materials; sets applicant disqualifications, requires fingerprinting and state/FBI criminal checks (Identification Division), requires storage/use facilities meet Patrol and U.S. Department of the Treasury standards, and specifies permit classes/types and terms (up to three years). Business purchase permits must certify on‑date use or valid storage permit and require at least one employee with a Patrol-issued use permit. Individual applicants must provide social security numbers.

- State Fire Marshal: display‑fireworks permits now must include proof of a Nebraska State Patrol use permit and a $100 application fee remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the State Fire Marshal Cash Fund.

- U.S. Department of the Treasury referenced for federal explosives standards.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that applicants must provide proof of a permit for use of explosive materials issued by the Nebraska State Patrol. No other agencies are mentioned and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
