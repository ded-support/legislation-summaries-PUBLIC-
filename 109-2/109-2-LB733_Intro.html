Summary

LB733 renames the Department of Health and Human Services’ Division of Developmental Disabilities to the Division of Disability and Aging and updates statutory references accordingly. The Director of Disability and Aging is added to the child welfare practice model work group. The Division of Disability and Aging is identified as administering the Beatrice State Developmental Center, publicly funded community developmental disabilities services, and the family support program, which must be administered within Legislature-approved appropriations and reported on annually through December 31, 2027. The bill also clarifies State Personnel System exemptions for positions in the Department of Health and Human Services, the Department of Correctional Services (including facility chief executive officers), the Department of Veterans' Affairs, and deputies/examiners of the Department of Banking and Finance and the Department of Insurance.
