Summary

LB1178 amends provisions of the Nebraska Probate Code (multiple sections) affecting court procedures and guardianship duties. Agency/department items:

- Public Guardian: prohibited from being appointed standby guardian; not required to post bond; authorized to approve mandatory guardian training programs and to set training timing; training completion may be enforced by county court order or Supreme Court rules.

- Nebraska Supreme Court: authority to promulgate rules governing standby guardian activation and training timing; hearings may be closed on request.

- County courts: implement hearings, may appoint attorneys or guardian ad litem (per §§30-4201–30-4210), send visitors, and enforce training/compliance.

- County attorneys/deputies: barred from circumventing ward rights under the Nebraska Mental Health Commitment Act by consenting to psychiatric treatment over a ward’s objection.

- Nebraska veterans homes: guardian officer may serve as guardian/conservator.

The bill permits virtual attendance at guardianship and protection hearings and requires guardians to monitor wards monthly with written visit records and contact with care providers. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a clear right for the ward to attend each court hearing virtually or in person (including the original petition hearing, hearings on change/termination of a guardian, and show-cause hearings). Requires guardians to monitor the ward or protected person at least once monthly, keep a written record of each visit, and maintain periodic contact with all public or private individuals and agencies that provide the ward’s care or related services. No specific government agencies or departments are named and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
