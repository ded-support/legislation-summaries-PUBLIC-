Summary

The bill amends section 72-816 to specify that the Vacant Building and Excess Land Cash Fund is to pay for maintenance of vacant state buildings and for expenses related to disposal of state buildings and land referred to the Department of Administrative Services. The fund is to be administered by the state building division of the Department of Administrative Services, with investment by the state investment officer under existing state investment law. Funds may be transferred from the cash fund to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction. If funds are insufficient, the division shall prioritize implementation to best meet statutory purposes. It also restricts Task Force for Building Renewal appropriations from being used for these purposes except as allowed. The bill repeals the original section.
