Summary

LB716 amends section 72-816 to (re)establish the Vacant Building and Excess Land Cash Fund and explicitly assign administration to the state building division of the Department of Administrative Services. The fund, consisting of proceeds credited under section 72-815, is designated to pay maintenance of vacant state buildings, upkeep of excess state land, and expenses related to disposal of state buildings and land referred to the Department by the committee (secs. 72-811 to 72-818). Funds may be invested by the state investment officer and may be transferred to the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction. If funds are insufficient, the division shall prioritize implementation. Task Force for Building Renewal appropriations are restricted from use for these purposes except as specified.
