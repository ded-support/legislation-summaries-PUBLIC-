Summary

LB1241 requires school boards and governing authorities of public, private, denominational, and parochial schools to obtain and review applicants' employment histories and written authorizations to request records related to child abuse or sexual misconduct. It authorizes immediate termination or rescission of offers for disclosures or findings, imposes up to $500 civil penalties for willful falsehoods or nondisclosure (penalties distributed pursuant to Article VII, §5, Neb. Const.), and grants immunity to employers who provide requested information unless knowingly false. The bill prohibits agreements that suppress or destroy information about investigations of suspected child abuse or sexual misconduct and voids contrary contract provisions. The State Department of Education must run a public awareness campaign and publish guidance on its website. No specific appropriations or funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds mandatory applicant disclosure and employer-check requirements for anyone seeking paid positions with regular student contact, and gives school boards/governing authorities authority to discipline, terminate, or impose civil penalties (up to $500) for willful falsehoods or nondisclosure. Employers contacted must state employment dates and any knowledge of reports, discipline, or license actions; employers providing such information are immune from civil/criminal liability unless knowingly false. Bars contracts, severance agreements, or clauses that suppress, destroy, or require expungement of information about suspected child abuse or sexual misconduct (unless allegations are found false or unsubstantiated); such provisions are void. The State Department of Education must run a public awareness campaign and post guidance on its website. Penalty receipts are distributed per Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. No new funding or appropriations are specified.
