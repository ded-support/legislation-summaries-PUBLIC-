Summary

LB1241 requires school boards and governing authorities of public, private, denominational, and parochial schools to implement enhanced employment-screening practices for positions with regular student contact: applicant disclosures, written authorizations for prior-employer records, and employer checks about prior reports or findings of child abuse or sexual misconduct. Boards may rescind offers or terminate employment for noncompliance and must refuse contracts or agreements that suppress, destroy, or expunge information about suspected or substantiated abuse; contrary contract provisions entered or renewed after the act are void.

The bill authorizes civil penalties (up to $500) for applicants who willfully provide false or incomplete disclosures; collected penalties are distributed pursuant to Article VII, section 5 of the Nebraska Constitution. Employers who provide records in good faith receive immunity from liability. The State Department of Education must run a public-awareness campaign and post guidance on its website. No direct appropriations or other state budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for school boards and governing authorities (public, private, denominational, parochial) to screen applicants for positions with regular student contact, contact listed employers, and document employer responses. Employers who provide information about applicants are granted civil and criminal immunity unless information is knowingly false. Applicants who willfully falsify or omit required disclosures face discipline, reporting to the state agency with authority to revoke licenses/certificates, or a civil penalty up to $500; collected penalties are distributed pursuant to Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. The bill forbids contracts that suppress or destroy information about suspected child abuse or sexual misconduct and voids contrary contract provisions. The State Department of Education must run a public awareness campaign and post guidance on its website. No new appropriations or budgetary allocations are specified.
