Summary

Bill LB1159 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §72-728, directing the Nebraska Hall of Fame Commission to name one living person to the Nebraska Hall of Fame prior to January 1, 2027. The commission retains its existing five-year induction cycle and duties for nominations, finalist review, and public hearings. The bill preserves the commission's obligation to procure a State Capitol plaque listing Nebraskans awarded the Medal of Honor. Required qualifications for the named living inductee: former U.S. House member, former head coach and former athletic director at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, and an attendee of Hastings High School. The original section is repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
