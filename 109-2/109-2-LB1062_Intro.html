Summary

LB1062 amends insurance and pharmacy benefit manager (PBM) statutes, primarily affecting the Nebraska Department of Insurance (the Director of Insurance) and the State Treasurer.

Key agency actions and authorities:

- Director of Insurance: expanded authority to impose retaliatory taxes/fees/obligations on foreign insurers; verify nonresident producer licensure via the NAIC producer database or home-state regulator; cancel nonresident licenses if home-state licensing lapses; suspend/revoke licenses, levy administrative fines (up to $1,000 per violation) and enforce disciplinary proceedings (per Administrative Procedure Act); added cause for discipline for failure to pay state income tax.

- NAIC (and its affiliates/subsidiaries or successor): authorized source for producer licensure verification.

- PBM regulation: Director to license PBMs, adopt rules, set a nonrefundable application fee (≤ $500) and annual renewal fee (≤ $250), require PBMs to notify the Director of material ownership/control changes, and collect late-renewal fines ($100/day) remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions give the director (state licensing official) authority to cancel a nonresident license if the licensee's home‑state license is inactive, require a written demand for hearing within 30 days (hearing held within 30 days under the Administrative Procedure Act), and allow automatic revocation upon notice that the home‑state license is not in good standing. For pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs), failure to comply triggers a $100 per‑day fine while transacting business and suspension if renewal/fee not received by the license anniversary. The director must remit fines to the State Treasurer for distribution under Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. The director may grant a one‑time up to 30‑day extension without fines/suspension for good cause. PBMs must immediately notify the director of material ownership/control or qualification changes.
