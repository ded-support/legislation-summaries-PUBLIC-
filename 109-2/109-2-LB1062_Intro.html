Summary

LB1062 primarily affects actions by the Director of Insurance (and related state insurance regulators) and the State Treasurer:

- Director of Insurance: expanded authority to impose retaliatory measures under reciprocal statutes; verify nonresident producer licensure via the NAIC producer database (or successor) or by contacting the home-state regulator; automatically cancel nonresident licenses if home-state licensure is inactive; issue orders, hold hearings under the Administrative Procedure Act, and levy administrative fines (up to $1,000 per violation) for producer licensing violations.

- Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) regulation (Director): authorizes rules, requires PBM licensure, sets a nonrefundable application fee cap of $500 and an annual renewal fee cap of $250, imposes $100/day fines for late PBM renewals and permits suspension until compliance, and requires PBMs to notify the director of material ownership/control changes.

- State Treasurer: receives PBM fines for constitutionally prescribed distribution.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for the director (insured-regulatory director) to cancel a nonresident license when the licensee's home-state license is inactive (suspension, revocation, lapse, surrender, etc.). The licensee may demand a hearing within 30 days; the hearing must be held within 30 days under the Administrative Procedure Act. If the director has notice a nonresident failed to keep a resident license in good standing, the nonresident license is automatically revoked. For pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs): failure to comply with renewal/related requirements triggers a $100/day fine while transacting business and suspension if renewal/fee not received by the license anniversary. The director remits fines to the State Treasurer for distribution per Article VII, §5 of the Nebraska Constitution. The director may grant one 30‑day extension without fine/suspension; PBMs must immediately notify the director of material ownership/control changes.
