Summary

The bill directs the Department of Administrative Services’ budget division to serve as the basis for the Governor’s budget and authorizes the DAS budget administrator to have the State Treasurer transfer $1,000,000 from the Water Resources Cash Fund to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund after July 19, 2024 and before June 30, 2025. DAS’s budget division will coordinate with the Department of Health and Human Services on goals and benchmarks. The Department of Revenue, in consultation with DHHS, must create a process to verify employer eligibility for the parent/caretaker employee tax credit. The Department of Economic Development and Revenue must provide joint electronic legislative reports (annual by Oct. 31) and hold joint hearings by Dec. 15 of even-numbered years; DED must use identification numbers to protect investor confidentiality and include aggregated confidential data plus audit/review and incentive-recapture explanations.
