Summary

LB853 amends Nebraska's individual and corporate income tax conformity statute (section 77-2716). It (1) adds a required Nebraska add-back of depreciation deductions for qualified production property under IRC §168(n) for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026; and (2) preserves various itemized state adjustments (multiple existing subsections). Agencies/departments referenced: the Nebraska Tax Commissioner (authority to adopt rules/regulations and to adjust dollar thresholds), the Internal Revenue Service, the U.S. Department of Defense and the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (for Forms 1099‑R), and the Nebraska National Guard. No appropriations, new funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Beginning for tax years on or after January 1, 2026, the bill requires taxpayers to add back to their state taxable income any depreciation deduction claimed under Internal Revenue Code section 168(n) for "qualified production property." It applies to individuals (federal adjusted gross income) and to corporations and fiduciaries (federal taxable income). The provision references the federal Internal Revenue Code but does not create or alter funding, appropriations, or budget allocations nor name any state agency.
