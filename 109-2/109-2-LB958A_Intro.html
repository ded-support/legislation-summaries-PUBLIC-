Summary

LB958A appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services to implement Legislative Bill 958 but specifies zero-dollar amounts. It designates $0 from the General Fund for FY2026-27 and $0 for FY2027-28 to DHHS Program 33, with total expenditures for permanent/temporary salaries and per diems limited to $0 for both years. It likewise designates $0 from the General Fund for both FY2026-27 and FY2027-28 to DHHS Program 348, including $0 General Funds for state aid in each year (restricted to state aid use). The bill further prohibits using the Section 2 appropriations for permanent or temporary state-employee salaries or per diems.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For LB 958, the Department of Health and Human Services is given zero-dollar appropriations for both FY2026-27 and FY2027-28. Details:

- Program 33: $0 General Fund each year; expenditures for permanent/temporary salaries and per diems capped at $0.

- Program 348: $0 General Fund each year; included state aid $0 for each year. Funds may not be used for permanent or temporary salaries or per diems for state employees.

In short, the bill directs DHHS to aid implementation of LB 958 but provides no new General Fund dollars or salary/per diem funding for the specified programs.
