Summary

LB958A appropriates funds to the Department of Health and Human Services solely to implement Legislative Bill 958. For both FY2026‑27 and FY2027‑28 the bill designates $0 from the General Fund to DHHS Program 33 and $0 from the General Fund to DHHS Program 348. It specifies total allowable expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems from Program 33 appropriations as $0 for each year. For Program 348, $0 of General Fund is included for state aid for each year and may be used only for that purpose; no state employee salary or per diem expenditures may be made from those funds.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

DHHS: The amendment allocates no General Fund dollars to carry out LB 958. It appropriates $0 for FY2026‑27 and $0 for FY2027‑28 to Program 33 and likewise $0 for both years to Program 348. It also specifies $0 of General Fund state aid for Program 348 for each year. Expenditures for permanent/temporary salaries and per diems from these appropriations are limited to $0, and no state‑employee salaries or per diems may be paid from the funds appropriated in this section. In short: no new funding or budgetary increases for DHHS under LB 958.
