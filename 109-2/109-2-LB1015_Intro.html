Summary

- The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Labor and retains a Hospital definition tied to the Department of Health and Human Services.

- Entities receiving Business Innovation Act assistance must submit quarterly wage reports to the Department of Labor as the Commissioner requires; the department must include this reporting requirement in its qualified action plan.

- Creates the Business Innovation Cash Fund, funded by a portion of state unemployment insurance tax deposits (alongside the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and the Unemployment Trust Fund). The fund finances most Business Innovation Act programs (except the Bioscience Innovation Program) and may be used to pay unemployment benefits if the Commissioner of Labor determines necessity.

- Up to 5% of any appropriation from the fund may be used for administrative expenses.

- Public contracts and surety bonds must ensure payment to the Unemployment Compensation Fund, the Business Innovation Cash Fund, and the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Economic Development: Creates the Business Innovation Cash Fund to carry out the Business Innovation Act (excluding specified sections and the Bioscience Innovation Program). Funded by deposits required under §§48-648 and 48-649–48-649.04; monies invested by the state investment officer. The department (or its contractors) may expend fund money for the Act and use up to 5% of any appropriation for administrative expenses.

- Department of Labor: Entities receiving Business Innovation Act assistance that employ anyone in Nebraska must file quarterly wage reports with the Department of Labor (regardless of Employment Security Law coverage). The Commissioner of Labor may require report forms and may authorize use of fund money to pay unemployment benefits if necessary.

- Department of Health and Human Services: No changes or funding actions in the added text.
