Summary

- Department of Labor: bill defines "department" as the Department of Labor and repeatedly requires entities receiving Business Innovation Act funds (and applicants under the qualified action plan) that employ persons in Nebraska to submit quarterly wage reports to the Department of Labor in forms the Commissioner may require. The Commissioner of Labor may determine that the Business Innovation Cash Fund be used to pay unemployment benefits. The Commissioner administers combined tax collection and reporting rules.

- Department of Health and Human Services: "Hospital" is defined as an institution licensed, certified, or approved by the Department of Health and Human Services.

- Funding/appropriations: creates the Business Innovation Cash Fund, funded by a portion of state unemployment insurance tax collections (with state UI tax directed to the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and the new Business Innovation Cash Fund). Combined taxes, contractor lien/bond and contract-pay provisions are revised to require payments to the Business Innovation Cash Fund.

- Department of Economic Development: not explicitly referenced.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Creates the Business Innovation Cash Fund to carry out the Business Innovation Act; fund receipts per sections 48-648 and 48-649–48-649.04 and investments managed by the state investment officer under the Nebraska Capital Expansion Act and Nebraska State Funds Investment Act. The fund may not be used for the Bioscience Innovation Program or for sections 81-12,155.01 and 81-12,163.01.

- Expenditures: the Department of Economic Development may expend the fund to implement the Business Innovation Act and, if the Commissioner of Labor so determines, to pay unemployment benefits. Up to 5% of any appropriation from the fund may be used by the department or its contractors for administrative expenses.

- Department of Labor: any entity receiving Business Innovation Act assistance that employs Nebraska workers must submit quarterly wage reports in the form/manner required by the Commissioner; selection criteria for awards must require the same.

- Department of Health and Human Services: no changes or funding actions referenced.
