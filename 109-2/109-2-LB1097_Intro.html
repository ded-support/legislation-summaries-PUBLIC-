Summary

Creates the State and Political Subdivisions Sexual Abuse Liability Act, making the State of Nebraska, state agencies, and political subdivisions liable for child sexual abuse and abuse of individuals with developmental disabilities similarly to private actors. Defines "state agency" (departments, agencies, boards, bureaus, commissions, and certain state instrumentalities), "political subdivision" (cities, counties, school districts, public power districts, interlocal entities, etc.), and cross-references employee definitions to sections 81-8,210 and 13-903. The Act waives sovereign immunity for these claims and exempts them from the State Tort Claims Act and Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act (and their limits on damages, jury availability, notice requirements, and statutes of limitations). Amends 25-228 to set differing limitation periods for claims against non-perpetrators (generally 12 years after age 21 for child abuse; special rules for persons with developmental disabilities). No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "State and Political Subdivisions Sexual Abuse Liability Act," making the state, state agencies, and political subdivisions liable like private parties for tort claims arising from child sexual abuse and abuse of individuals with developmental disabilities. Definitions of "state agency," "political subdivision," and "employee" are tied to existing statutes (sections 81-8,210, 13-903, 71-1107). The act waives sovereign immunity for these entities, removes applicability of the State Tort Claims Act and Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act (including their limits, notice rules, jury/damage limits, and statutes of limitation), and bars writs of execution against the state, state agencies, or political subdivisions for claims under this act. It also clarifies timing rules for bringing claims. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
