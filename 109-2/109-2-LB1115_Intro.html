Summary

LB1115 amends Nebraska beer distribution law to exempt microbreweries (per §53-103.21) from the statutory supplier–wholesaler agreement framework for agreements entered, amended, or renewed on or after the act's operative date (Oct. 1, 2026). Existing continuous or open‑ended microbrewery–wholesaler agreements terminate Oct. 1, 2027 unless replaced by a new written agreement. The bill increases the role of the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission: it may extend temporary wholesaler appointments beyond 90 days, must receive notices of temporary service interruptions, and must be notified of supplier test‑marketing plans. It also preserves regulatory actions (e.g., license suspension/revocation) by appropriate agencies and specifies Nebraska courts as proper venue. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an exemption for microbreweries (per §53‑103.21) from statutes in §§53‑201 to 53‑223 for any agreement with a wholesaler entered into, amended, or renewed on or after the act's operative date. Pre‑existing continuous or indefinite agreements in effect before the operative date are deemed terminated October 1, 2027 unless replaced by a new written agreement before that date; such replacement agreements receive the same exemption. Clarifies that “supplier” does not include a microbrewery. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
