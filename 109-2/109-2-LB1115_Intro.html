Summary

LB1115 exempts microbreweries (per §53-103.21) from the statutory distribution-agreement regime in §§53-201–53-223: agreements entered, amended, or renewed on/after the act's operative date are not subject to those sections. Existing continuous or indefinite microbrewery–wholesaler agreements in effect before the operative date are deemed terminated October 1, 2027 unless replaced by a new written agreement. The bill amends the definition of supplier to exclude microbreweries and harmonizes cross-references. The Nebraska Liquor Control Commission retains roles: approving extensions of temporary wholesaler appointments, receiving notices of temporary service interruptions, and receiving supplier notices for test marketing. Legal remedies remain through Nebraska state or federal courts. No funding, appropriations, or budgetary changes are included. Operative date: October 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an exemption to statutes 53-201 through 53-223 for agreements entered into, amended, or renewed on or after the act’s operative date between a microbrewery (per §53-103.21) and a wholesaler. Any continuous or indefinite agreement existing before the operative date is deemed terminated on October 1, 2027 unless the parties execute a new written agreement beforehand; such new agreements are covered by the exemption. The term “supplier” is clarified to exclude microbreweries. No government agencies, departments, appropriations, or funding changes are referenced.
