Summary

LB1053 amends sections 79-263, 79-265, and 79-267 of the Student Discipline Act. It removes the prohibition on suspending students in pre-kindergarten through second grade and preserves principals' authority to impose short-term (up to five school days) suspensions with required notice and parent conference attempts. School districts must adopt a one-year expulsion policy for students who knowingly possess/use/transmit a firearm and may have expulsions modified by the superintendent or school board on an individual basis. Districts must annually provide the State Department of Education an assurance the policy is in effect and a description of expulsions (school, number expelled, weapon types). Grounds for long-term suspension/expulsion/mandatory reassignment are clarified. Original sections are repealed; section 79-265.01 is outright repealed. No funding or appropriations are included.
