Summary

LB954 amends section 77-5701 of the Nebraska Advantage Act to require the Department of Revenue to recalculate a taxpayer’s base-year employees for tier 6 project agreements when the taxpayer sells part of the business operations to an entity outside the taxpayer’s unitary group. The recalculation subtracts the equivalent employees at the sold operations from the base-year employee count on the last Form 312N filed before the sale. The recalculation does not apply if the sold operations cease within 24 months or if the sale’s primary purpose was to close a location. Credits or incentives generated before the sale are not recalculated. The provision applies to agreements entered after December 31, 2016. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Revenue to recalculate a taxpayer’s base‑year employees for tier 6 project agreements when the taxpayer sells part of the business and the sold operations continue under an entity outside the taxpayer’s unitary group. The recalculation subtracts the equivalent employees at the sold operations from the base‑year employee count on the last Form 312N filed before the sale. Exceptions: the rule does not apply if the sold operations cease within 24 months of sale or if the sale’s primary purpose was to close a location. Pre‑sale credits/incentives are not recalculated. Applies to agreements entered after Dec. 31, 2016. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are changed.
