Summary

LB1167 amends Laws 2025, LB261, sec. 246 for Agency No. 72 — Department of Economic Development, Program No. 603 (Industrial Recruitment). Key budget figures: FY2025‑26 — General Fund $15,718,538; Cash Fund $33,019,625; Federal $21,183,754; Program Total $69,921,917; Salary limit $4,603,821. FY2026‑27 — General Fund $15,805,071; Cash Fund $7,525,784; Federal $21,188,298; Program Total $44,519,153; Salary limit $4,733,987. The unexpended Cash Fund balance for the Site and Building Development Act is reappropriated. State aid set‑asides: FY25‑26 includes $11,020,352 GF, $31,839,197 CF, $20,737,571 Federal for state aid; FY26‑27 includes $11,020,352 GF, $6,339,197 CF, $20,737,571 Federal for state aid. The department must use the $11,020,352 GF in FY26‑27 for Business Innovation Act grants (with $3,000,000 designated for microenterprise assistance). The Legislature intends an additional $4,500,000 CF from the Site and Building Development Fund in FY2027‑28.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Economic Development must apply $11,020,352 in General Fund appropriations for FY2026‑27 (state aid) to award grants under the Business Innovation Act. The Legislature expresses intent that $3,000,000 of that $11,020,352 be directed to grants and technical assistance specifically for the microenterprise assistance program.
