Summary

This bill amends Laws 2025, LB261, §246 (Agency No. 72 — Department of Economic Development, Program No. 603 — Industrial Recruitment). Appropriations shown:

- FY2025‑26: General Fund $15,718,538; Cash Funds $33,019,625; Federal Funds est. $21,183,754; Program Total $69,921,917; Salary limit $4,603,821.

- FY2026‑27: General Fund $15,805,071; Cash Funds $7,525,784; Federal Funds est. $21,188,298; Program Total $44,519,153; Salary limit $4,733,987.

Reappropriates unexpended Cash Fund balance for Site and Building Development Act obligations existing June 30, 2025. Specifies state aid set‑asides: for both years $11,020,352 GF and $20,737,571 FF (with Cash Fund $31,839,197 in FY2025‑26 and $6,339,197 in FY2026‑27) for state aid; FY2026‑27 GF must fund Business Innovation Act grants, with $3,000,000 targeted to microenterprise assistance. Intent to appropriate an additional $4,500,000 Cash Funds from the Site and Building Development Fund in FY2027‑28.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Economic Development must use the $11,020,352 General Fund appropriated for FY2026-27 for state aid to award grants under the Business Innovation Act. The Legislature intends that $3,000,000 of that $11,020,352 be allocated specifically for grants and technical assistance through the Business Innovation Act’s microenterprise assistance program.
