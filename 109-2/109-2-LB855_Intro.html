Summary

LB855 directs the State Department of Education to develop and administer a statewide early support system for Nebraska public schools "in consultation with the Department of Health and Human Services," making DHHS's role consultative in design and implementation. The bill text includes no explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions require the State Department of Education, in consultation with the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), to develop and administer a statewide early support system for Nebraska public schools. DHHS’s role is consultative and operationally implied: public health and behavioral health liaisons and community health workers from local public health departments (under DHHS oversight) are explicitly included on school youth support coordination teams, may serve as lead agencies or coordinators, and help with screening, family outreach, trauma‑informed assessments, and referrals to mental/behavioral health and basic‑needs services. No explicit funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DHHS or the program are included in the provided text.
