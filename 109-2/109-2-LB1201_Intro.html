Summary

LB1201 directs the State Treasurer to transfer General Fund moneys to the Education Future Fund as follows: $1,000,000,000 in FY2023-24 and $250,000,000 in FY2024-25. Both transfers are to be made "on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services." The bill also expresses legislative intent for $250,000,000 to be transferred in FY2025-26 and each fiscal year thereafter (no additional Department of Administrative Services duties are specified).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No changes to the Department of Administrative Services. The additions require the State Department of Education (SDE) to develop and run a grant program in 2026‑27 and 2027‑28—subject to available appropriations—to supply classroom libraries and monthly parent/caregiver engagement materials (in cooperation with a Nebraska nonprofit). Grants prioritize lowest-performing, high-poverty, or high reading‑deficiency districts; SDE must provide recommended book lists (English and Spanish) and set classroom tracking/reporting requirements. The text does not appropriate funds to or change responsibilities or budget authority for the Department of Administrative Services.
