Summary

The bill directs State Treasurer transfers from the General Fund to the Education Future Fund to be executed "on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services." Specified transfers: $1.0 billion in FY2023‑24 and $250 million in FY2024‑25. The bill expresses legislative intent for $250 million transfers in FY2025‑26 and each fiscal year thereafter.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No changes or duties are assigned to the Department of Administrative Services. The added provisions require the State Department of Education (SDE), for school years 2026‑27 and 2027‑28 and subject to available appropriations, to administer a grant program (in partnership with a Nebraska nonprofit with ≥10 years’ book‑distribution experience) to supply classroom libraries and monthly parent/caregiver take‑home materials. Priority criteria, required bilingual recommended book lists, detailed classroom tracking (circulation, inventory with ISBNs, enrollment, books taken home), and SDE assistance in selecting/developing engagement materials are specified. Funding is authorized generally (“to provide funding” and “subject to available appropriations”) but no specific appropriation, dollar amounts, or DAS budget allocations are identified.
