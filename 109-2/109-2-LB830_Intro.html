Summary

LB830 — agency-specific summary:

- Military Department: Federal facilities under Military Department control become eligible for renewal and maintenance funding under the Deferred Building Renewal Act, payable from the Building Renewal Allocation Fund. The Committee on Building Maintenance’s monitoring and oversight duties are extended to include such federal facilities.

- Department of Veterans' Affairs: Confirms the Department of Veterans' Affairs structure and that its director (appointed by the Governor) receives a salary fixed by the Governor, payable in equal installments. The department is designated as the state's advocate for veterans.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): Authorizes DHHS to use temporarily surplus space at Lincoln and Norfolk Regional Centers (and other DHHS institutions) for patients/transferees; care, custody, treatment, and charges remain the same and are collected per existing statutes. DHHS is defined as “Department” in the statutory scheme and is required to provide prescribed mental-health medications to eligible indigent outpatients and may adopt rules to implement that duty.

No direct new appropriations are specified beyond authorizing use of the Building Renewal Allocation Fund for Military Department federal facilities.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds eligibility for federal facilities controlled by the Military Department to receive state-funded renewal and maintenance under the Deferred Building Renewal Act, with payments made from the Building Renewal Allocation Fund. No specific appropriation amounts or new budget allocations are set in the text. There are no substantive additions affecting the Department of Health and Human Services or the Department of Veterans' Affairs.
