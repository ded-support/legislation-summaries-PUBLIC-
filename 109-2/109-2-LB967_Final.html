Summary

LB967—agency provisions summary:

- Department of Insurance: creates/uses a Department of Insurance Cash Fund and requires State Treasurer transfers to the General Fund: $14,000,000 by 6/30/2026; $13,200,000 by 6/30/2027; $11,000,000 by 6/30/2028; $11,000,000 by 6/30/2029 (timing per the budget administrator, budget division, Department of Administrative Services). Expands definitions to include pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs) under the Unfair Insurance Trade Practices Act. Establishes PBM licensure, nonrefundable application fee up to $500, annual renewal fee up to $250, late fines ($100/day) and suspension authorities; fines remitted to the State Treasurer. Changes Insurance Producers Licensing rules: director may verify via NAIC database and may impose reciprocal fee/obligation parity when other states charge higher fees.

- Department of Health and Human Services: board membership increased to include two DHHS representatives (one from Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care); DHHS to recommend, draft, promulgate, and enforce rules under the Population Health Information Act and to enter an agreement with the designated health information exchange to share de‑identified Medicaid data.

- Department of Economic Development: authorized to administer the Site and Building Development Fund for loans/grants/subsidies and expanded eligible activities; applicants must provide matching funds (generally 100%) and, for the U.S. Strategic Command facility grant, proof of $20 million in private/other funds and certification to the budget administrator, Department of Administrative Services, before funds are expended.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Administrative Services: No new provisions affecting DAS found in the added text.

Department of Economic Development: Authorizes grants for construction of golf facilities in a metropolitan-class city. Applicants must show programming for veterans/youth, letters of support, and proof of at least 80% of project funding. Grants capped at 20% of total construction costs.

Department of Insurance: Expands licensing enforcement (automatic or director-ordered revocation of nonresident licenses), imposes PBM renewal rules with $100/day fines (remitted to the State Treasurer) and possible suspension, and adds mutual insurance holding company electronic notice rules. Establishes the Nebraska Protection of Seniors from Insurance Exploitation Act: insurers may delay disbursements for investigations, must notify the Director within seven business days, provide training to staff by April 15, 2027 (or one year after hire), grants immunity for good‑faith third parties, and bans forcing card-only claim payments to providers beginning Jan 1, 2027.

Department of Health and Human Services: Adds a Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care representative on the health information body and directs DHHS to draft, promulgate, and enforce Population Health Information Act rules; limits HIE data submission to what the HIT Board requires.
