Summary

- Department of Insurance: creates/maintains the Department of Insurance Cash Fund and directs transfers to the General Fund: $14,000,000 by 6/30/2026; $13,200,000 by 6/30/2027; $11,000,000 by 6/30/2028; $11,000,000 by 6/30/2029 (timing/amounts directed by the budget administrator, DAS). Expands Pharmacy Benefit Manager licensure: nonrefundable application fee (≤ $500), annual renewal fee (≤ $250), renewal fines remitted to State Treasurer; requires immediate notification of material ownership/control changes. Director authority preserved for fee/rulemaking and license enforcement.

- Department of Administrative Services: budget division’s budget administrator directs timing/amounts of the above transfers and receives certification from Director of Economic Development before certain grants.

- Department of Economic Development: Site and Building Development Fund eligible activities expanded (including large-site development grants/partnerships); matching-fund requirements; conditions for specific grants (e.g., $20M private funding proof and DAS certification; golf facility grants capped at 20% and require 80% funding proof).

- Department of Health and Human Services: two board seats on Health Information Technology Board (one from Division of Medicaid and Long-Term Care); the department drafts, promulgates, and enforces Population Health Information Act rules and must execute an agreement with the designated health information exchange to share de-identified Medicaid data.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Insurance: Establishes the Nebraska Protection of Seniors from Insurance Exploitation Act. Director of Insurance defined as the reporting authority; insurers may delay certain life/annuity disbursements pending internal review, must notify the director within seven business days and provide updates. Provides immunity for good‑faith third‑party reporting/assistance. Requires insurer training on identifying exploitation and confidentiality (complete by April 15, 2027, or within one year of hire). Director may cancel nonresident licenses and impose fines on noncompliant pharmacy benefit managers; fines are remitted to the State Treasurer.

- Department of Economic Development: Authorizes grants for construction of golf facilities in a metropolitan city; applicants must provide programming documentation (veterans/youth), letters of support, and proof of at least 80% funding. Grants capped at 20% of total construction costs; Director reviews documentation.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Adds a Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care representative to the Health IT/Population Health bodies and assigns the department rulemaking and enforcement authority under the Population Health Information Act; limits required data submissions and excludes proprietary financial data.

- Department of Administrative Services: No changes identified.
