Summary

LB787 amends membership and reporting provisions for the Commission on Military and Veteran Affairs. It explicitly designates the Director of Veterans’ Affairs (or designee) as a voting member of the commission and lists the Department of Labor’s veterans’ program coordinator as a nonvoting, ex officio member. No provisions in the bill change funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Labor or the Department of Veterans’ Affairs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text clarifies and expands commission membership language—explicitly listing commanders of the USAF 95th Wing and 90th Missile Wing, the Defense POW/MIA Accounting Agency Director, DoD service representatives for Nebraska, commanders of future or other Nebraska-based wings/headquarters, and repeatedly allowing each to serve by “his or her designee.” It also adds that the commission may act “at the request of the Governor or upon the commission’s own determination.” There are no references to the Department of Labor or the Department of Veterans’ Affairs, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
