Summary

The bill references the U.S. Department of Labor in its cost‑of‑living provisions. It specifies the Consumer Price Index — All Urban Consumers (CPI‑U), as published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of the U.S. Department of Labor, as the index for COLA calculations and directs that any substitute index also be published by the U.S. Department of Labor. The bill also cites the Department of Labor’s Medical Price Index as the factual basis for establishing a separate medical COLA. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations affecting the Department of Labor or the retirement system are included.

Summary of added provisions (other agencies/retirement system):

- Clarifies retirement allowance administration for Class V School Employees: monthly payments are effective the first day of the month, paid on the last business day; the first payment includes all accrued amounts; the last payment is paid on the last business day of the month of death or per the chosen payment option.

- Shifts annual adjustment (COLA) effective dates from January 1 to September 1 beginning Sept. 1, 2026, for specified adjustment clauses.

- Creates a medical cost-of-living adjustment payable Sept. 1, 2026 and each Sept. 1 thereafter for annuitants who joined before July 1, 2016 and have received an annuity for at least 10 years.
