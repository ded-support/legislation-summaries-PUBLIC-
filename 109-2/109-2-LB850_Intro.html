Summary

LB850 amends the Local Option Municipal Economic Development Act (sections 18-2705 and 18-2709). It authorizes cities to use locally derived economic development funds for construction or rehabilitation of housing for sale or lease—targeting low- or moderate-income households, workforce housing, or projects tied to an affordable housing action plan (including plans under §19-5505). It explicitly permits payment of city staff salaries/support or contracting with outside entities to implement programs or develop such plans. The bill expands the definition of "qualifying business" to include entities whose principal income is from housing construction or rehabilitation and clarifies other qualifying categories. Political subdivisions and state agencies remain excluded except for specified rural infrastructure activities. No specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided. Population references use the U.S. Bureau of the Census.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that economic development programs may fund construction or rehabilitation of housing for low- or moderate-income persons, as part of a workforce housing plan, or as part of an affordable housing action plan (including plans required under section 19-5505). It also specifies that, for cities of the first class, cities of the second class, and villages, such programs may include grants, loans, or funds. No specific state agencies or departments are named and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
