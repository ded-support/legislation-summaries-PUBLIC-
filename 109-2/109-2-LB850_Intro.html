Summary

LB850 amends the Local Option Municipal Economic Development Act (sections 18-2705 and 18-2709). It explicitly authorizes local economic development programs to fund construction or rehabilitation of housing for sale or lease (including low- and moderate-income, workforce housing, and projects tied to an affordable housing action plan under section 19-5505). It also permits use of local revenues to pay salaries and support for city staff (or to contract with outside entities) to implement economic development programs or develop affordable housing action plans. The bill expands the definition of "qualifying business" to include firms whose principal income derives from construction or rehabilitation of housing. It preserves joint-city program authority and existing provisions for rural and early childhood infrastructure in smaller municipalities. No state funding, appropriations, or specific budget allocations are provided or changed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that economic development programs for cities of the first class, cities of the second class, and villages may include grants, loans, or other funds for construction or rehabilitation of housing to sell or lease: (1) for low- or moderate-income persons, (2) as part of a workforce housing plan, or (3) as part of an affordable housing action plan (including plans required under §19‑5505). No state agencies or departments are newly named, and no specific funding amounts, appropriations, or budget allocations are provided.
