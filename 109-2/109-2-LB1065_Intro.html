Summary

LB1065 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §49‑1701 to formalize post‑amendment publication of the state constitution. Key agencies and duties:

- Revisor of Statutes: compile an updated constitution after each statewide election adopting an amendment, include source notes and notes of sections declared unconstitutional; may compile updates after other statewide elections if notes change; notify Secretary of State and Clerk when compiling.

- Attorney General: promptly notify the Revisor when any section is declared unconstitutional or inoperative.

- Board of State Canvassers: its canvass triggers a ten‑calendar‑day deadline for the Revisor to transmit the update.

- Secretary of State: review and certify the Revisor’s updated copy; maintain every certified edition.

- Clerk of the Legislature: receive certified copy, post it on the Legislature’s website, include it in session laws, and print pamphlets for public distribution. Certified copies are official/prima facie evidence.

The bill repeals the original section and takes effect immediately by emergency clause. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a ten-calendar-day deadline: within ten days after the board of state canvassers completes the canvass of a statewide election that adopts a Nebraska constitutional amendment, the Revisor of Statutes must promptly update the statutory text and transmit the updated copy to the Secretary of State for review and certification under subsection (2). Agencies mentioned: Revisor of Statutes, board of state canvassers, and Secretary of State. No funding or appropriation changes.
