Summary

This bill revises procedures and responsibilities for updating and distributing the Constitution of Nebraska. Agencies and duties:

- Revisor of Statutes: must compile an updated constitution after each statewide election adopting a constitutional amendment (including source notes and notes identifying sections declared unconstitutional). May compile updates to notes after other statewide general elections and must notify the Secretary of State and Clerk of the Legislature if doing so.

- Attorney General: must promptly notify the Revisor when a section is declared unconstitutional or inoperative.

- Board of State Canvassers: completion of the canvass triggers a 10-calendar-day deadline for transmission.

- Secretary of State: reviews the Revisor’s draft, determines accuracy, certifies the copy, and maintains every certified edition.

- Clerk of the Legislature: posts the certified copy online, includes it in session laws, prints pamphlets on request; certified copies are official prima facie evidence.

No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included. The act takes effect immediately under an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a procedural deadline and duties for three state entities: after the board of state canvassers completes its canvass of a statewide election that adopts a constitutional amendment, the Revisor of Statutes must, within ten calendar days, promptly prepare and transmit the updated statutory copy to the Secretary of State for review and certification under subsection (2). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
