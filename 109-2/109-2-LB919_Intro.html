Summary

LB919 permits the use of "other accessible durable medium" (in addition to roll microfilm) for recording instruments, filing documents, and creating preservation duplicates, and defines "accessible" by retrievability. Agencies/departments affected and duties:

- Registers of Deeds: may accept filings via durable accessible media and, when such media are used, need not retain original notices (including bankruptcy and state tax lien notices). Security copies must be maintained off‑premises and meet standards set by the State Records Administrator.

- State Records Administrator / Records board: prescribes standards for durable accessible media and may designate preservation duplicates.

- Secretary of State: continues to receive and process state tax lien notices electronically; fee remittance to the State Treasurer (credit to Secretary of State Cash Fund) and payments to registers of deeds remain unchanged.

- Tax Commissioner and Commissioner of Labor: retain serial‑numbering roles for tax lien notices.

No new appropriations or changes to existing fee structures or budget allocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds repeated wording "or other accessible durable medium" and inserts a definition clarifying that, for this section, a medium is "accessible" if it can be retrieved through intellectual, digital, or physical means within institutional or legal parameters. No government agencies or departments are mentioned and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
