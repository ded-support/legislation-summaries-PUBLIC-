Summary

LB943 establishes statutory definitions for "motor vehicle," "motor vehicle racing facility," "racetrack," and "associated amenities," and bars findings of public or private nuisance against such facilities when (1) the facility predated a subsequent change in surrounding land use or occupancy and (2) the facility would not have been a nuisance before that change. It limits civil nuisance actions to within two years after the offending condition reaches a nuisance-level. The bill preserves court enforcement of prior nuisance orders and allows claims for additional damages or equitable relief if a facility fails to remediate pursuant to such an order. No state agencies, departments, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced or altered.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for “associated amenities,” “motor vehicle,” “motor vehicle racing facility,” and “racetrack.” Establishes that a motor vehicle racing facility is not a public or private nuisance if it predated a change in surrounding land use and would not have been a nuisance before that change. Caps civil nuisance suits at two years from when the offending condition reaches nuisance-level. Preserves actions to enforce prior court orders and claims for additional damages or equitable relief if a facility fails to remediate under such orders.
