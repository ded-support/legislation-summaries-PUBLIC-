Summary

LB719 amends the definition of "Project" in the Nebraska Rural Projects Act (section 81-12,203). It clarifies eligible expenses for development of a new industrial rail‑access business park to include: site acquisition/preparation, transportation infrastructure, utility extensions, and rail spur construction; assistance to an initial tenant engaged in manufacturing, processing, distribution, or transloading; and infrastructure work on property not owned by the applicant that directly benefits the park (rights‑of‑way, easements, adjacent property for street access, turning lanes, rail spurs/drop‑and‑pull yards, and critical utility improvements). It also adds eligible projects within 30 miles of the state's largest artificial water storage reservoir. The original section is repealed and the bill takes effect immediately. No state agencies, departments, or funding/appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds eligibility for infrastructure costs on property not owned by the applicant when those costs directly benefit and are critical to a business park’s development. Covered expenses include acquisition of rights‑of‑way, easements, adjacent properties for street access; construction of turning lanes on adjacent major highways; construction of rail spurs and drop/pull rail yards tied to a main railroad line; and necessary utility upgrades (water, electric, wastewater, storm water, natural gas). No government agencies or departments are named and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
