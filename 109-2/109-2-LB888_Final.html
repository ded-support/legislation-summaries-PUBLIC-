Summary

LB888 amends section 75-156 to define "Consumer Price Index" as the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers "as published by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics." That index is used to compute the "CPI percentage difference" which the Public Service Commission will apply annually (August 1) to adjust maximum civil penalties for violations under the modular/manufactured-housing provisions. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text directs that the Consumer Price Index used to adjust maximum civil penalties is the CPI for All Urban Consumers as published by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Bureau of Labor Statistics. It defines the CPI percentage difference as the year‑over‑year change in October CPI, sets initial maximum penalties ($3,650 per violation; $4,562,282 for related violations within one year), and requires the Public Service Commission to adjust those maxima annually on August 1 by that CPI percentage (rounded to the nearest dollar). No changes to Department of Labor funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
