Summary

- Designates "department" as the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS).

- DHHS shall administer the Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund to enhance hospital rates, must not use the fund to replace existing state hospital payments, and must reimburse the General Fund the amount of the first quarterly payment by June 30 each fiscal year.

- The department may collect hospital revenue/discharge/inpatient-day data and must prohibit Medicaid managed care organizations from adjusting or recouping directed hospital payments.

- The fund may reimburse DHHS an administrative fee equal to 3% of the assessment (capped at $15 million/year); allocate 0.5% to the Nebraska Center for Nursing Board (capped $2.5 million/year); and allocate 3.5% for nonhospital rates/continuous eligibility/designated health information exchange (capped $17.5 million/year).

- DHHS may use state funds and accept grants to implement/operate PDMP technology, shall contract with a vendor to administer the Health Information Technology Board, and the department (and its Division medical and pharmacy directors) are authorized recipients/users of PDMP and HIE data under specified privacy rules.

- No state funds (General, cash, or federal) shall be used for administrative duties of the Health Information Technology Board or for reimbursement of board member expenses.
