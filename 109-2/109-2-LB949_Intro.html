Summary

LB949 (summary of provisions referencing the Department of Health and Human Services)

- The bill defines “department” as the Department of Health and Human Services (71-2454; 81-6,124).

- Hospital Quality Assurance and Access Assessment Fund (68-2106): directs the department to use the fund to enhance hospital rates; requires reimbursement to the General Fund of the first quarterly payment annually; authorizes an administrative fee to the department equal to 3% of the assessment (capped at $15 million/year); allocates 0.5% (capped $2.5 million/year) to the Nebraska Center for Nursing Board and 3.5% (capped $17.5 million/year) for nonhospital provider rates, continuous eligibility for children, or the designated health information exchange.

- Health Information Technology Board: one board member must be a representative of the Department of Health and Human Services; several clinician appointees must be in good standing with the department.

- The department may contract with a vendor to administer the board and PDMP and may use state funds, grants, or gifts to implement technology.
