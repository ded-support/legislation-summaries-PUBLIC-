Summary

LB866 amends funding and administration of opioid-related state funds. Department of Justice must remit opioid-recovery recoveries to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Opioid Recovery Trust Fund; excluded trust receipts go to the State Settlement Trust Fund. The State Treasurer is directed to make annual transfers (on or after July 1 but before July 15) from the Nebraska Opioid Recovery Trust Fund: $1,125,000 to the Training Division Cash Fund (first responder behavioral health/wellness), $400,000 to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund (staff for Overdose Fatality Review Teams), $3,000,000 to the Opioid Prevention and Treatment Cash Fund, and additional amounts as determined by the Legislature to the Opioid Treatment Infrastructure Cash Fund and the newly created Drug Detection and Prevention Cash Fund. The Drug Detection and Prevention Cash Fund is created, administered by the Attorney General, funded only by legislative transfer (no intended General Fund transfers), and its principal may be spent only for investment; investments are managed by the state investment officer under existing investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Drug Detection and Prevention Cash Fund," to be administered by the Attorney General. The fund consists of amounts the Legislature directs to it (including an amount "determined by the Legislature" for section 2 of the act). The Legislature intends not to use General Fund dollars for the fund. The fund's principal may not be spent except for investment as provided in the section. No specific appropriation amounts are set.
