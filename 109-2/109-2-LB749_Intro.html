Summary

LB749 amends section 77-27,139.03 to require the Department of Revenue to use the prior year's Certificate of Taxes Levied (as certified to the Property Tax Administrator under section 77-1613.01) to calculate the bond and nonbond tax levies for municipal state-aid purposes. The bill does not make any appropriations or change funding allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Revenue to base its actions on the prior year's Certificate of Taxes Levied as certified to the Property Tax Administrator under section 77-1613.01. Procedural change only — no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation adjustments are specified.
