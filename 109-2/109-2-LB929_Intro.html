Summary

LB929 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §68-912 to specify responsibilities of the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) regarding the medical assistance program. DHHS may establish premiums, copayments, deductibles, limits on amount/duration/scope of covered goods and services, and recipient requirements (including participation in care coordination and disease‑management programs). In setting coverage and limits DHHS must consider effects on recipients and expenditures, public policy in §68-905, other states’ experience, federal benchmark coverage, and other relevant factors. Coverage and limits in effect before July 1, 2006 remain until lawfully changed; proposed reductions or expansions are subject to review under §68-909. Proposed rules on cost‑sharing or limits are not effective until after the earliest subsequent regular legislative session (text continues). No appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
