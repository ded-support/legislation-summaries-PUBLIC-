Summary

LB879 (Guest Worker Permit Act) references the Department of Revenue in two places. Section 11 requires the Department of Revenue to provide a means for payors to withhold an "income tax equivalent" from compensation paid to permitholders who lack a Social Security number, to set the equivalent amount by rule, and to coordinate with federal agencies to align withholding with federal work‑authorization verification. Section 13 allows the administering department to disclose applicant information to the Department of Revenue to carry out section 11. The bill also directs that fees collected under the act be remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund. The text contains no explicit references to the Department of Labor.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Labor

- Administers Guest Worker and Immediate Family permits: sets eligibility, application, fingerprint and criminal-check requirements, issues/renews within 30 days, designs permit cards, adopts rules, may require $10,000 bonds or country guarantees, and enforces revocations/penalties.

- Collects application and issuance fees: application fee up to $50; initial issuance surcharge $1,000 (entered legally but out of status) or $2,500 (entered illegally); immediate-family application fee up to $20 with same surcharges. Allows interest-free installment payments; default revokes permit.

- Maintains confidentiality but may disclose to Auditor, Department of Revenue, or federal agencies.

- Must remit collected fees to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund.

Department of Revenue

- Must provide a mechanism for withholding an "income tax equivalent" from compensation when permitholders lack SSNs; sets amount by rule to approximate state income tax.

- To the extent feasible, coordinate with federal verification programs and will receive confidential permit data from the Labor department to administer withholding. No explicit new appropriations; fees flow to the General Fund.
