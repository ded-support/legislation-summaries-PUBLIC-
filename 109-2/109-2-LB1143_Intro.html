Summary

LB1143 amends section 68-949 to direct the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to identify the total dollar appropriation for Medicaid nursing facility rates in the state budget and use that amount as the base for annual rate calculations. DHHS may consider client service utilization, needs, and preferred setting when establishing prospective nursing facility rates; if it uses a dollar amount differing from the identified appropriation, DHHS must report the alternate dollar amount and projected resident days to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and the Clerk of the Legislature. DHHS must file an annual August 1 report detailing the inflation-factor calculation and a December 15–31 report identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations (including encumbrances and retroactive payments). DHHS also must submit a Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services application to establish a Money Follows the Person program by December 31, 2026. No explicit new appropriations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two duties for "the department": (1) when setting prospective nursing facility rates the department may consider utilization tied to client needs and preferred setting; if it uses a dollar amount different from the state budget appropriation in those rate calculations, it must report that dollar amount and the projected nursing facility resident days to the Legislature in the subdivision (4)(b) report (and include subdivision (4)(a) information). (2) no later than December 31, 2026, the department must apply to the federal Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services to create a Money Follows the Person program to help qualified individuals move from institutions to community-based long‑term care. No explicit new appropriations or budget allocations are established.
