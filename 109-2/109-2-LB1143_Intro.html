Summary

Amends §68-949 to require the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to: identify the total dollar appropriation for Medicaid nursing facility rates in the state budget and use that amount as the base for rate calculations (including the annual inflation factor); consider client utilization and preferred setting when setting prospective rates; and, if DHHS uses a different dollar amount or projection of resident days, report those figures to the Legislature. DHHS must file an annual August 1 report to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and Clerk explaining the inflation-factor calculation and a December 15–31 report identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations for Medicaid nursing facility payments (including encumbrances and retroactive payments). DHHS also must submit an application to CMS to establish a Money Follows the Person program by December 31, 2026, and develop recommendations on eligibility and defined-benefit modifications. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for the state "department" (state Medicaid/long‑term care agency) to:

- Consider client needs and preferred setting utilization when setting prospective nursing facility rates.

- If the department uses a dollar amount different from the appropriation in its rate calculation, report that dollar amount and the projected number of nursing facility resident days to the Legislature in the required subdivision (4)(b) report.

- By December 31, 2026, submit an application to the federal Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to establish a Money Follows the Person program to help qualified individuals move from institutional to community long‑term care settings.

No new appropriations are specified; the additions impose reporting and an application deadline and increase transparency around any divergence from budgeted appropriations.
