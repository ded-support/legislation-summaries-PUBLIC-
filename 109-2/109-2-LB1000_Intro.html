Summary

LB1000 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §26-118 (Protection Orders Act) to revise criminal penalties for violating protection orders. For domestic-abuse and sexual-assault protection orders (including recognized foreign orders under §§26-123 and 26-124), violations are: first offense — Class I misdemeanor; second — Class IV felony; third — Class IIIA felony; fourth or subsequent — Class IIA felony. Violations of harassment protection orders (and recognized foreign harassment orders) are Class II misdemeanor for a first offense and Class I misdemeanor for subsequent offenses. The bill repeals the original §26-118. No state agencies or departments are named and no funding or appropriations are included.
