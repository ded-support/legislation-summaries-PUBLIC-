Summary

LB745 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79‑730 to restate authority for the Commissioner of Education (Nebraska Department of Education) to issue a diploma of high school equivalency. The bill specifies three issuance conditions: 30‑day Nebraska residency (or final credit‑earning attendance in a Nebraska high school), demonstrated achievement on approved tests and other Commissioner‑determined criteria equivalent to a typical high school graduate, and inability to secure a diploma from the last high school attended. The bill repeals the original section. No agencies beyond the Commissioner/Department of Education are referenced and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
