Summary

LB745 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-730 to clarify the authority and criteria under which the Commissioner of Education may issue a diploma of high school equivalency. The Commissioner may grant such a diploma to a non-graduate who: has been a Nebraska resident for at least 30 days immediately preceding application (or whose final credit-bearing high school attendance was in Nebraska); demonstrates educational attainment via approved tests and other criteria the Commissioner deems pertinent; and is unable to secure a diploma from the high school last attended. The bill repeals the original section. No agencies beyond the Commissioner of Education are named and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
