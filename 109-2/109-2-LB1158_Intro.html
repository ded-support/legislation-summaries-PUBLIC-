Summary — LB1158 (2026)

LB1158 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75-304.04 to require any motor carrier, including transportation network companies, engaged in intrastate transportation for hire of railroad employees to obtain a license from "the commission" before operating. Licenses (initial and annual renewal) require a $250 fee, a completed application identifying the applicant’s principal Nebraska business location, and agreement to comply with §75-307 and commission rules on driver qualifications, equipment, operating standards, and record keeping. Licenses are valid one year, may be suspended or revoked after notice and hearing for noncompliance, and the commission is expressly barred from regulating licensed carriers’ rates. The original section is repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the phrase "including a transportation network company," explicitly expanding the statute/definition to cover transportation network companies (TNCs). No state or federal agency or department is referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
