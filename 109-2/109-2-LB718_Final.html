Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies CPA exam eligibility and qualification pathways. Applicants must hold a baccalaureate or higher from an institution accredited by a U.S. Department of Education–recognized agency (or board‑acceptable equivalent) and show required accounting, auditing, business and related coursework. Three education/experience options are established: (i) postbaccalaureate degree + 1 year experience; (ii) baccalaureate + 30 additional credit hours + 1 year experience; or (iii) baccalaureate + 2 years experience. Degrees must include specified accounting subject areas. Required experience must be acceptable to the board, supervised by an active CPA with a valid permit (or equivalent), and may be gained in public practice, government, industry, or academia. No changes affecting the Department of Revenue or any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
