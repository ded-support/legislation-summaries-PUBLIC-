Summary

LB1262 establishes the Foreign Adversaries Out of Postsecondary Education Act and assigns operational and enforcement roles as follows:

- Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education (CCPE): receives institution disclosures (gifts/contracts ≥ $10,000 or aggregated ≥ $50,000 within 12 months) within one month in a searchable format and maintains a public transparency database; posts Governor designations.

- Governor (with Attorney General): may designate additional foreign adversaries; filings go to the Clerk of the Legislature and CCPE; institutions may petition for reconsideration.

- Attorney General: investigative and enforcement authority (civil actions, subpoena power), must provide whistleblower protections and investigation guidelines.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: annually audits/inspects at least 10% of institutions (including non‑reporters).

- Nebraska State Patrol: to provide an educational module in consultation with Governor and Attorney General.

Funding/budget impacts: prohibited funds must be returned or forfeited to the State Treasurer; forfeitures go to the permanent school fund (Art. VII, §7); collected penalties are remitted to the State Treasurer for constitutional distribution (Art. VII, §5). Violating institutions forfeit sums equal to prohibited funding, face civil penalties (up to $250,000 per violation), and may be barred from state-funded research grants for up to five years.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds reporting, enforcement, and funding controls involving the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education (CCPE), State Treasurer, Auditor of Public Accounts, Attorney General, Governor, Clerk of the Legislature, and Nebraska State Patrol. Key agency duties and budget impacts:

- Postsecondary institutions must report foreign gifts/contracts (≥$10K or $50K/year) to CCPE in a searchable format; CCPE must maintain a public transparency database and post any Governor-designated foreign-adversary notices. Reports must comply with 20 U.S.C. 1011f.

- Funds received in violation must be returned/forfeited to the State Treasurer; forfeitures go into the permanent school fund; other penalties remit to the State Treasurer per constitutional distribution rules.

- Violators forfeit equal value, face civil fines up to $250,000/violation, and may be barred from state-funded research up to five years.

- Auditor must annually audit a random sample (≥10%) of institutions; Attorney General gains investigative/subpoena and enforcement authority; Nebraska State Patrol, with Governor and AG, will provide an educational module.
