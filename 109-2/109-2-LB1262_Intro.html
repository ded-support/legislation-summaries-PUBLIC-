Summary

Bill creates the "Foreign Adversaries Out of Postsecondary Education Act" and assigns implementation, oversight, and enforcement roles to state agencies:

- Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education: requires institutions to report foreign gifts/contracts ≥ $10,000 (or $50,000 aggregated annually), maintain a public searchable transparency database, and receive notices of Governor designations.

- State Treasurer: receives forfeited funds (prohibited gifts/contracts) and collected penalties; forfeitures credited to the permanent school fund per Article VII, §7; penalties distributed per Article VII, §5.

- Attorney General: investigatory and enforcement authority, including subpoenas and civil actions; must provide whistleblower protections and investigation guidelines.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: annual audits of a random sample of ≥10% of postsecondary institutions for compliance.

- Governor (with AG): may designate additional foreign adversaries; Coordinating Commission posts notices; institutions may petition/appeal.

- Nebraska State Patrol: to provide an educational module (in consultation with Governor and AG).

Penalties: forfeiture equal to prohibited value, up to $250,000 civil fines per violation, and up to five years' ineligibility for state-funded research grants; egregious violations may revoke institutional authorization.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates reporting, enforcement, and transparency duties for state agencies:

- Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education (CCPE): receives mandatory digital reports on foreign gifts/contracts ($10,000+ or $50,000 aggregated/year), posts a public transparency database, and must publish Governor designations of "foreign adversary/principal."

- State Treasurer: receives returned/forfeited funds and civil penalties; forfeitures go into the permanent school fund (Art. VII §7); penalties distributed per Art. VII §5.

- Attorney General: investigatory and enforcement authority (including subpoena power), may sue to enforce the law, and must provide whistleblower protections and investigation guidelines.

- Auditor of Public Accounts: must annually audit/inspect a random sample of at least 10% of postsecondary institutions.

- Governor (with AG) designates additional foreign adversaries; notices filed with Clerk of the Legislature.

- Nebraska State Patrol: to provide an educational module.

Funding consequences: forfeiture of prohibited funds, civil fines up to $250,000/violation, and up to five-year ineligibility for state-funded research grants; certain programs must close by Dec. 31, 2026.
