Summary

LB741 amends membership of the Nebraska Children's Commission to specify two Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) nonvoting, ex officio members: the Director of Children and Family Services (Division of Children and Family Services) and the Director of Behavioral Health (Division of Behavioral Health), or their designees. These nonvoting members may attend meetings, participate in discussions, and provide information. The bill retains reimbursement provisions for commission members (see sections 81-1174 to 81-1177) and the stated legislative intent to fund commission operations from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund for FY2019-20 and FY2020-21; it does not create new DHHS appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two membership provisions naming the Commission on African American Affairs: (1) the chairperson (or designee) and (2) one individual selected from a list of three nominees submitted by that Commission. There are no references to the Department of Health and Human Services and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
