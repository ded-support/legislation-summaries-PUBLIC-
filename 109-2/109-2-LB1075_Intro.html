Summary

LB1075 revises Nebraska election and village-governance statutes, reallocating duties among the Secretary of State, election commissioners, county clerks, county canvassing boards and treasurers, county central committees, courts, the State Records Administrator, and the Nebraska State Historical Society. Key administrative changes: Secretary of State must prepare/preserve county vote abstracts, provide abstracts to state party chairs on request, post vote‑count device test certifications, prescribe election‑worker training, and accept party filings 60 days before primaries allowing unaffiliated participation. County officials’ duties are expanded/clarified for precinct creation/size, notices/sample ballots, mail/replacement ballots, signature/ID verification timelines, provisional‑ballot verification (within seven business days), canvass records, and timely abstract delivery (counties may retrieve abstracts by messenger at official expense; negligent officials are liable). Village boards may authorize clerks to issue warrants during majority vacancies, subject to later board review. Fiscal changes: a temporary $100 per‑precinct recount fee (through 12/31/2026) payable to county treasurers and deposited to county general funds (refundable if result is overturned), revised cost‑allocation rules (cost‑per‑precinct, $100 minimum, alternate billing for some public power and school elections), and political subdivisions bear costs of canceled special elections. No statewide appropriations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural and limited fiscal provisions affecting local and state election officials and village governments. Key agencies: village board of trustees and village clerk (clerk may prepare claims/issue warrants only when >50% of trustees’ seats are vacant and only for board‑approved or legally/contractually required expenditures; board must annually authorize this and later review claims). Election agencies: Secretary of State, election commissioners, and county clerks (may accept cancellations of special elections through the fourth Thursday before the election; Secretary must notify commissioners/clerks when a party permits unaffiliated voters in its primary). Fiscal changes: the political subdivision that cancels a special election pays related costs (per §32‑1202); counties must bear overnight delivery costs for vote abstracts; recount fees set at $100 per precinct through Dec. 31, 2026, with candidates liable for any excess actual costs. Also limits who may copy precinct sign‑in registers and criminalizes ballot forgery.
