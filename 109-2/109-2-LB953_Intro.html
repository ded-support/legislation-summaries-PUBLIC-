Summary

The bill assigns the Department of Economic Development (DED) responsibility for use of the Site and Building Development Fund to finance loans, grants, subsidies, credit enhancements, other financial assistance, and department expenses "as appropriated by the Legislature" for administering the fund. DED may provide grants and partner on identification, evaluation, and development of large commercial and industrial sites and must use a designated subaccount to assist Municipal Inland Port authorities. DED will determine required matching funds for recipients (minimum 100% of assistance). The Director of Economic Development must receive specified letters and proof of private funding before approving or certifying awards (including a $20 million availability certification for certain public‑private facilities) and must receive other required letters or documentation before grants or expenditures (including documentation and minimum 8% local funding for certain golf facility grants; such grants capped at 20% of construction costs).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new grant category administered by the Department of Economic Development for political subdivisions and nationally/state accredited golf associations to build golf facilities and related infrastructure within a city of the metropolitan class. Before approval, applicants must supply the Director of Economic Development with: documentation of veterans and youth programming; letters of support from a national and a state accredited golf association; a mayoral support letter; and proof of at least an 8% local funding contribution. Grants are capped at 20% of total construction costs. No specific dollar appropriations are set.
