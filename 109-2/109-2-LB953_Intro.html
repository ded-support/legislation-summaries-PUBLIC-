Summary

The bill directs the Department of Economic Development to use the Site and Building Development Fund to finance loans, grants, subsidies, credit enhancements and other assistance, and to cover department administrative expenses as appropriated by the Legislature. The department shall use the fund subaccount to provide financial assistance to inland port authorities. Recipients must provide matching funds in amounts determined by the Department, at least equal to 100% of assistance. The Director of Economic Development must receive a U.S. Strategic Command letter of support and proof of $20 million in private/other funds (with Director certification to the budget administrator) before expending funds for that project. The Director must also receive specified DoD/contractor or association/mayoral documentation before approving certain grants (including golf-facility requirements and limits).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new grant program administered by the Department of Economic Development for political subdivisions and nationally and state accredited golf associations to build golf facilities and related infrastructure, limited to sites inside a city of the metropolitan class. Prior to approval, applicants must submit to the Director of Economic Development: documentation of facility programming for veterans and for youth; letters of support from a nationally accredited golf association, a state accredited golf association, and the mayor of the host metropolitan-class city; and proof of funding equal to at least 8% of total construction costs. Grants are capped at 20% of total construction costs. No direct appropriations are specified.
