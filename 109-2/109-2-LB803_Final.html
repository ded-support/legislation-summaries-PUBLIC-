Summary

LB803 assigns specific duties to the Department of Revenue (defined as “department”). It requires the Department of Revenue to mail by June 25 a postcard to every person listed in the county assessor’s report directing them to the county website that posts budget‑hearing and joint‑public‑hearing information. The bill also tasks the Department of Revenue with annually administering refundable income tax credits under the Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act. For taxable years beginning on or after Jan. 1, 2027, credits are allocated: $240,000 (split equally among qualifying tribal programs); $150,000 (to a statewide coalition of qualifying nonprofits); $1,044,000 (split equally between the coalition and other nonprofit shelters); and $1,566,000 (to nonprofits — $1,252,800 population‑based; $313,200 square‑mileage‑based). The bill contains no references to the Department of Banking and Finance and shows no direct appropriations or funding transfers for either agency in the provided sections.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Banking and Finance

- Establishes a state-authorized "First‑Time Home Buyer Savings Account" to be offered by financial institutions starting Jan 1, 2027.

- Dept. may adopt rules to implement the program but may not impose administrative, reporting, or other requirements on financial institutions beyond normal account procedures. Financial institutions may charge service fees deductible from accounts.

Department of Revenue (Tax Commissioner)

- Creates Nebraska tax treatment for these accounts effective Jan 1, 2027: deductible contributions up to $5,000 (individual) / $10,000 (joint) plus included interest; recapture rules add amounts back to Nebraska AGI and authorize penalties for nonqualified withdrawals, payable to the department.

- Requires county assessors to report notice recipients and a website address to the Property Tax Administrator; Dept. of Revenue must mail postcards by June 25 with website information.

- Tax Commissioner to prescribe certain homestead‑exemption affirmation forms.

No explicit new appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.
