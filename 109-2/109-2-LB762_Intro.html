Summary

LB762 contains no explicit reference to the Department of Insurance. The bill instead directs "the department" to compile and post, by December 1, 2028 and annually thereafter, a report identifying each reporting insurance policy and the number of coverage denials for each specified pediatric autoimmune treatment. No line-item appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations for the Department of Insurance (or any agency) are included in the provided text. The act includes an emergency clause making it effective upon passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Insurance must compile the section’s required data into a single report and post it on its website by December 1, 2028, and annually on each December 1 thereafter. The report must list each reporting insurance policy and, for each policy, state the number of coverage denials by treatment. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Insurance are specified in the added text.
