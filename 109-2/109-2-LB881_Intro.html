Summary

LB881 regulates immigration‑enforcement agreements by requiring prior approval and public notice. Key agencies and bodies affected:

- Local law enforcement agencies and jails: must notify the appropriate local governing body at least 30 days before an agreement, hold a public hearing, and obtain local governing body approval. Existing agreements entered before July 1, 2026, terminate Dec. 1, 2026 unless notice is given by Oct. 15, 2026 and approved.

- Nebraska State Patrol: must notify and obtain approval from the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice (the commission) under the same timing rules; the commission places notices on its next public agenda and may approve or disapprove.

- Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice: receives notices, agenda items, copies of agreements, and meeting minutes; conducts public hearings and approves or rejects agreements.

Violations render agreements void and are treated as Open Meetings Act violations; residents may sue in district court under §84‑1414. No funding or appropriations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural limits on immigration enforcement agreements for law enforcement/jails and the Nebraska State Patrol. Law enforcement agencies or jails must give their local governing body 30 days' written notice and obtain approval at a public meeting before entering such agreements; agreements in place before July 1, 2026, automatically terminate Dec. 1, 2026 unless notice is given by Oct. 15, 2026 and the local body approves. Agencies/jails must, within 60 days of entry, provide the commission with the agreement, the notice, and public‑meeting minutes. The Nebraska State Patrol must follow analogous steps but notify and obtain approval from the commission; pre‑July 1 agreements have the same termination/notice/approval deadlines; the patrol must file the agreement with the commission within 60 days. Violations render agreements void, constitute Open Meetings Act violations, and permit civil actions. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
