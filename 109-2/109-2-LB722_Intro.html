Summary

LB722 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §71-812 to expand uses of the Behavioral Health Services Fund. The fund is administered by the division (in consultation with the department) and may contain Legislative appropriations or other department-received cash funds; legislative-directed transfers to the General Fund remain permitted. Money transferred under §76-903 must be used for housing-related assistance for very low-income adults with serious mental illness or substance use disorder. If housing obligations are satisfied, the division may redirect up to 20% of that money to regional behavioral health authorities for acquisition or rehabilitation of housing for those individuals. The division will set a distribution formula in consultation with regional behavioral health authorities and the department, contract with regional behavioral health authorities, and regional authorities may subcontract with public, private, or nonprofit entities. Available fund balances are invested by the state investment officer per state investment law.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language treating an "individual" as including a person with a diagnosis indicated by a V‑code, recognizing a disorder, disability, or substance use disorder. It also states that "substance use disorder" has the meaning given in section 71‑430. No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
