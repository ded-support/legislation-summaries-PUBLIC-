Summary

LB1106 requires the State Treasurer to transfer $15,000,000 from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Board of Regents for the University of Nebraska Medical Center for pancreatic cancer research (transfer to occur on or after July 1, 2022, but before June 30, 2023). That transfer is contingent on receipt of matching funds (up to $15,000,000) from private or other sources certified by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services. Upon certification of any matching funds by that DAS budget administrator, the State Treasurer must transfer an equal amount to the Board of Regents.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a recurring obligation requiring the State Treasurer to transfer $300,000 on or before July 15 each fiscal year into the Patient Safety Cash Fund. This creates a new, annual funding stream for that cash fund but does not change appropriations, budgets, or funding for the Department of Administrative Services; DAS is not affected by this provision.
