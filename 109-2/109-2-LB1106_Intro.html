Summary

LB1106 amends section 71-7611 to make transfers from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the University of Nebraska Medical Center for pancreatic cancer research contingent on matched private or other funds. The budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services must certify receipt of matching funds (up to $15 million). Upon that certification, the State Treasurer is required to transfer an equal amount from the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund to the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text requires the State Treasurer to transfer $300,000 annually (on or before July 15 each fiscal year) to the Patient Safety Cash Fund. There is no direct change to the Department of Administrative Services’ responsibilities, funding, or appropriations in this provision. The bill does not specify the transfer’s funding source or alter DAS budget lines.
