Summary

LB805 requires that Nebraska individual and group sickness and accident insurance policies and hospital/medical/surgical expense policies, as well as self-funded employee benefit plans (to the extent not preempted by federal law), cover cranial helmets for infants up to one year of age to treat deformational plagiocephaly or craniosynostosis. Exemptions include short‑term major medical policies of six months or less and specified‑disease or other limited‑benefit policies. The bill references existing statutory authority (section 44‑3,131). No state agencies or departments are specifically named, and no changes to appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires that, notwithstanding section 44-3,131, individual and group sickness and accident insurance policies (and hospital/medical/surgical expense‑incurred policies) — excluding short‑term major medical policies of six months or less and specified disease/limited‑benefit policies — and self‑funded employee benefit plans (to the extent not preempted by federal law) cover cranial helmets for infants up to one year to treat deformational plagiocephaly or craniosynostosis. No specific state agencies, departments, or changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
