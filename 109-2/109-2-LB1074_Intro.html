Summary

LB1074 treats unclaimed‑property owner records (social security number, date of birth, and last‑known address) as confidential and subjects them to the same confidentiality standard as tax return information held by the Department of Revenue. The Auditor of Public Accounts retains unrestricted access to those records. The bill contains no Department of Revenue funding, appropriation, or budget changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a tolling rule: the statute of limitations is tolled when the State Treasurer notifies a holder of an examination under §69-1322 or when the holder elects a voluntary disclosure agreement; tolling ends at exam completion, required report filing, or termination of the agreement. Establishes the Unclaimed Property Liquidation Proceeds Trust Fund, administered by the State Treasurer; all funds received under §69-1316 are deposited there. Money is dedicated to pay claims under §69-1316, may be invested by the state investment officer per Nebraska investment laws, and excess balances revert to the Unclaimed Property Trust Fund. No Department of Revenue appropriations, budget allocations, or funding changes are made.
