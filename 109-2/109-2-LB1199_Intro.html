Summary

The bill directs the State Treasurer to make specified transfers from the State Settlement Cash Fund “on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services.” Those transfers are: $876,998 to the General Fund on or before June 30, 2018; $1,756,639 to the General Fund on or before June 30, 2019; $125,000 to the Legal Education for Public Service and Rural Practice Loan Repayment Assistance Fund on or before April 30, 2018; and $150,000 to that same Loan Repayment Assistance Fund on or before July 9, 2018.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Provides for a one-time transfer of "XXX dollars" from the State Settlement Cash Fund to the Legal Education for Public Service and Rural Practice Loan Repayment Assistance Fund, to be executed by the State Treasurer on the act's effective date or as soon thereafter as administratively possible. No provisions alter Department of Administrative Services' responsibilities, appropriations, or budget allocations; the Treasurer, not DAS, carries out the transfer.
