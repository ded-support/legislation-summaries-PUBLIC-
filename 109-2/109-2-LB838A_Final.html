Appropriates to the Department of Revenue, Program 102, $523,247 General Fund for FY2026‑27 and $136,300 General Fund for FY2027‑28 to aid in carrying out Legislative Bill 838. Department of Revenue (Program 102) receives General Fund appropriations to implement LB 838: $523,247 for FY2026‑27 and $136,300 for FY2027‑28. Of those amounts, expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at $99,500 in FY2026‑27 and $102,500 in FY2027‑28.
