Summary

The bill requires the Department of Economic Development to establish a Small Business Investment Program. The department shall provide grants to microloan delivery organizations, microloan technical assistance organizations, and innovation hubs; serve as the coordinating office for microlending and microenterprise development; identify/coordinate other state and federal funding; and contract with a statewide microenterprise development assistance organization to implement the program. Award criteria, allowable uses (matching funds, revolving loan funds, guaranty funds, up to 20% operating costs, loan-loss reserves), and qualifications (35% nonstate match, $150,000 per microloan cap, minimum 50% of funds for small-business technical assistance) are specified. The department may award up to $3,000,000 per year and, beginning FY2027-28, shall award up to $3,000,000 per year.
