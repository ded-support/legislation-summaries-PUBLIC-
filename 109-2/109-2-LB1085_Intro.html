Summary

This bill amends the Nebraska Liquor Control Act, primarily changing duties and procedures of the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission and interactions with local government.

- Nebraska Liquor Control Commission: may waive the 45-day objection period and issue retail, bottle club, craft brewery, and microdistillery licenses; must hold hearings when local governing bodies recommend denial or protests are filed; may override a local denial for retail licenses if the applicant proves the local body acted arbitrarily and capriciously (not permitted if proposed premises include an area with a craft brewery or microdistillery license). Commission signatures/attestation and electronic delivery of licenses are specified.

- Local officials: city/village/county clerks receive/deliver licenses upon proof of required fees/taxes; occupation taxes limited to twice the state license fee; Class J retail exempt.

- Commission must consider applicant or affiliated-entity delinquent federal, state, or local taxes.

- Enforcement (53-1,100): revised penalties for unlicensed manufacture, distribution, or retail sale, including Class I misdemeanor for first-time unlicensed spirits manufacture or retail sale and Class IV felony for repeat offenses.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two main provisions affecting licensing and review:

- Requires the licensing authority to consider whether an applicant or any affiliated entities are delinquent on federal, state, or local taxes. "Affiliated entities" is defined to include other corporations, partnerships, or LLCs in which the applicant has an ownership interest that hold a liquor license under the Nebraska Liquor Control Act.

- Allows the commission (presumably the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission) to approve a retail license that a city, village, or county recommended denying, but only after a hearing and if the proposed premises do not include a craft brewery or microdistillery area and the applicant proves the local government acted arbitrarily and capriciously.

No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
