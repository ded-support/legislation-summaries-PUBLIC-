Summary

The bill assigns several administrative and data duties to the Department of Administrative Services (DAS): the Director must deduct judges’ retirement contributions, credit them with the State Treasurer, and remit them to the judges’ retirement system; the statutory distribution list for printed statutes is updated to include the Director and DAS’s materiel and personnel divisions (while continuing two copies to the Commissioner of Labor); the State Treasurer’s annual school retirement deposit is to be transferred “on the warrant of the Director of Administrative Services.” The Materiel Division of DAS will conduct competitive sealed bidding for the Public Employees Retirement Board’s actuarial services, and DAS must be consulted on employer data formats and provide access to Nebraska Employees Information System records. The bill grants vesting credit to Department of Labor employees who were active participants in a terminated contributory plan and cites the U.S. Department of Labor’s CPI and Medical Price Index for COLA calculations. No DAS- or Labor-specific appropriations, funding, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary of added provisions affecting Department of Administrative Services (DAS) and Department of Labor:

- Requires members to maintain an approved identification document (per §4-108) and produce it on request to the Public Employees Retirement Board/Nebraska PERS.

- Changes timing for employee retirement contribution rates: the rate calculated July 1, 2025 applies July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026; the rate calculated July 1, 2026 applies July 1, 2026–Aug 31, 2027; beginning 2027, rates calculated July 1 take effect Sept 1 each year.

- Shifts annual adjustment/COLA (including specified medical COLA for long‑term annuitants) from January 1 to September 1 beginning Sept. 1, 2026.

- Clarifies temporary employees (including certain legislative hires) must begin retirement participation within first 30 days in applicable cases.

Impacts: DAS will need to update payroll/withholding, contribution timing, and ID verification procedures; Department of Labor must account for temporary‑employee participation rules and coordination with Nebraska PERS. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
