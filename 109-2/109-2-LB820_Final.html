Summary

The bill assigns these responsibilities and references for the Department of Administrative Services (DAS) and the Department of Labor (DOL):

- DAS must withhold specified judges’ retirement contributions from payroll, credit them to the Judges Retirement Fund, and remit them to the retirement system’s executive director. DAS must also issue a warrant, after board certification, so the State Treasurer can transfer the annual state deposit to the School Retirement Fund effective July 1.

- DAS must provide data to and consult with the Public Employees Retirement Board, grant the Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems’ executive director access to the Nebraska employees information system, and procure actuarial services through its materiel division via competitive sealed bidding with specified contract/renewal limits.

- The materiel and personnel divisions and the Commissioner of Labor are designated recipients of printed statutes. The bill specifies use of CPI measures published by the U.S. Department of Labor (and the Medical Price Index) for COLAs.

- No direct appropriations to DAS or DOL; retirement-board administrative expenses are payable from retirement funds/investment income.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text directly assign duties, funding, appropriations, or budget changes to the Department of Administrative Services or the Department of Labor. The additions instead: require members to maintain and produce an “approved identification document” (as defined in section 4-108) for the Public Employees Retirement Board/Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems; change timing for applying employee contribution rates (shifting annual application to September 1 beginning in 2027); and move cost-of-living and medical COLA adjustments from January 1 to September 1 beginning in 2026. No new appropriations or budget allocations for DAS or the Department of Labor are included.
