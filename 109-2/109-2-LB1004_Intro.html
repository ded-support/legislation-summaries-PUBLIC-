Summary

LB1004 (Introduced by Sen. Clouse; General Affairs Committee) amends provisions of the Nebraska Liquor Control Act by redefining certain terms and changing regulation of nonalcoholic beer. The bill explicitly amends section 53-160.02 to state: “No person shall sell or furnish nonalcoholic beer to any minor.” It repeals the original sections 53-103.02, 53-103.03, 53-103.24, and 53-160.02. No state agencies or departments are newly created or referenced beyond the Act, and the bill contains no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes.
