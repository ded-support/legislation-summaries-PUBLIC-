Summary

LB964 enacts the "Nebraska Public Property Disclosure Act." It requires the governing body of any political subdivision to hold an open, public hearing before any sale or lease of real property owned by the subdivision or before any purchase of real property by the subdivision. Any person may speak at the hearing. If the governing body approves the transaction, it must be completed within two months or a new hearing is required. The governing body must publish notice at least ten days prior in a legal newspaper in or of general circulation, including legal description, address, assessed value, proposed price (and lease term), and the names of proposed parties. No state agencies or budget appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Nebraska Public Property Disclosure Act." It requires the governing body of any political subdivision to:

- hold an open public hearing before any sale, lease, or purchase of real property, with opportunity for public comment;

- complete any approved transaction within two months of that hearing or, if not completed, hold a new hearing before proceeding;

- publish notice at least 10 days beforehand in a legal newspaper circulating in the subdivision, stating legal description, address, assessed value, proposed price or lease terms, and names of the parties.

No specific state agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
