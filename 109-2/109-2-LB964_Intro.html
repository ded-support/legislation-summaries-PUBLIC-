Summary

LB964 (2026) creates the "Nebraska Public Property Disclosure Act," applying to the governing body of any political subdivision. It requires an open, public hearing before any sale, lease, or purchase of real property by the political subdivision, with opportunity for public comment. If the governing body approves the transaction, it must be completed within two months or a new hearing is required. It also mandates publication at least ten days prior in a legal newspaper of record, including legal description, address, assessed value, proposed price or lease terms, and the names of the parties. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
