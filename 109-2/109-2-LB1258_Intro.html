Summary

LB1258 creates a Division of Brand Registration, Brand Inspection, and Livestock Theft Investigation within the Department of Agriculture, including a brand recorder, and makes brand inspection staff department employees or contractors. It establishes the Nebraska Brand Inspection and Theft Prevention Fund to receive fees and pay related expenses and salaries; the State Treasurer and state investment officer will handle receipts and investments. The Department of Agriculture may set fees (caps: recording ≤ $150, research ≤ $50, renewal ≤ $200; inspection travel/inspection ≤ $30/hr), and inspection mileage will use the rate set by the Department of Administrative Services. The bill allows use of undercover plates/drivers’ licenses by DHHS, the Department of Agriculture, and the Insurance Fraud Division, with confidentiality managed by the Department of Motor Vehicles. It also amends State Personnel exemptions for the Departments of Correctional Services, Health and Human Services, Veterans’ Affairs, Banking and Finance, and Insurance. The act becomes operative January 1, 2027.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Agriculture — Establishes a new Division of Brand Registration, Brand Inspection, and Livestock Theft Investigation within the department; creates brand registration and renewal, allows producers to use registered brands, authorizes statewide voluntary brand inspections (as proof of ownership) for a department-established fee, and empowers inspectors to act on requests from law enforcement, courts, lenders, markets, transaction parties, or missing‑owner reports. Provides for livestock theft investigations, statewide uniformity, an industry advisory committee, rulemaking authority, and continuation of pre‑2027 rules and certificates that do not conflict with the bill. Inspection fees are payable as prescribed (by violator or as court directs).
