Summary

LB1258 transfers livestock brand functions into the Department of Agriculture, creating a Division of Brand Registration, Brand Inspection, and Livestock Theft Investigation and a brand recorder; brand inspectors and investigators become Department employees or contractors and statewide voluntary inspections are authorized. The bill establishes the Nebraska Brand Inspection and Theft Prevention Fund (fees remitted to the State Treasurer; funds may be invested by the state investment officer) to cover departmental expenses and salaries. Inspection fees must cover actual costs, include mileage at the rate set by the Department of Administrative Services, and an hourly travel/inspection rate cap of $30; explicit fee caps are set for recording, research, renewals, permits, and filings. The Department of Agriculture, Department of Health and Human Services, and the Insurance Fraud Prevention Division (Dept. of Insurance) may receive undercover plates/licenses; the DMV must keep undercover driver-license records confidential and the DAS Transportation Services Bureau must maintain a confidential list of vehicles. The bill also specifies State Personnel System exemptions affecting Corrections, Health and Human Services, Veterans' Affairs, Banking and Finance, and Insurance.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates within the Department of Agriculture a new Division of Brand Registration, Brand Inspection, and Livestock Theft Investigation to: register/renew brands; permit producers to brand cattle with registered brands; provide statewide voluntary brand inspections (for a department-established fee) on request by law enforcement, courts, lenders, livestock markets/packers, transaction parties, or owners of missing cattle; investigate livestock theft; adopt rules; and convene an industry advisory committee. Inspectors may deem inspected animals estrays, notify the county sheriff, and dispose per law. Existing Livestock Protection Act rules and certificates remain effective through Jan 1, 2027 if not in conflict. No appropriations, funding changes, or other substantive changes affecting the Department of Administrative Services, Banking and Finance, Insurance, Correctional Services, Health and Human Services, Motor Vehicles, Veterans' Affairs, or the Military Department are included.
