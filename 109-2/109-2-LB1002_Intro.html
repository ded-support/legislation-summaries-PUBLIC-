Summary

LB1002 amends multiple provisions of the Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act, primarily affecting the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (the commission), the State Treasurer, and the Clerk of the Legislature. Key agency-related changes:

- Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission: adjusts registration and late‑filing fee schedules across many reporting provisions; enforces collection of those fees; may reduce or waive late fees under specified criteria; approves electronic means of filing reports.

- State Treasurer: registration fees collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission Cash Fund.

- Clerk of the Legislature: retains receipt of lobbyist/principal quarterly and special reports; the bill requires electronic filing of special monthly lobbyist/principal reports when monthly lobbying receipts/expenditures exceed $5,000 during session and makes principals explicitly subject to late‑filing penalties.
