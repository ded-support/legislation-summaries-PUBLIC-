Summary

This bill amends reporting and fee provisions administered by the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission (NADC) and clarifies filing with the Clerk of the Legislature. Key agency-focused changes:

- NADC: registration fee for committees raised to $150; registration fees are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Nebraska Accountability and Disclosure Commission Cash Fund. NADC collects and enforces revised late-filing fees across many report types (statements of organization, campaign statements, changes, independent expenditures, late contributions, earmarked and out‑of‑state reports, lobbyist/principal reports).

- Late-fee structure: many failures now incur $50/day (capped $1,500); formation within 30 days of an election: $200/day (capped $2,000). For certain late contribution/independent‑expenditure/out‑of‑state and lobbyist special-report failures: $200/day for first ten days, then 1% per day up to 10% of the amount required to be reported.

- Filing method: certain lobbyist special reports must be filed electronically; quarterly lobbyist/principal filings involve the Clerk of the Legislature and NADC. NADC may reduce or waive lobbyist/principal late fees under specified criteria.
