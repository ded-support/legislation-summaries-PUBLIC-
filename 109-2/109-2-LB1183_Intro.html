Summary

LB1183 (operative Jan. 1, 2027) revises property valuation rules and related administrative procedures. For school-district taxes levied to pay bonds approved on/after Jan. 1, 2027, agricultural and horticultural land (including land with special valuation) is valued at 25% of actual or special valuation and assigned an acceptable range of 22–25% (previously 37.5% and 34.5–37.5%). The unspecified commission may adjust class/subclass values to the midpoint of acceptable ranges. The bill clarifies certification deadlines and responsibilities for county assessors, the Property Tax Administrator, and the Tax Commissioner in computing and contesting adjusted valuations; appeals go to the Tax Equalization and Review Commission. The State Department of Education receives adjusted valuations. The state board may approve lump‑sum payments of postponed state aid, and the Director of Administrative Services is directed to draw warrants from appropriated funds for such payments.
