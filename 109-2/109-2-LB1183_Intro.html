Summary

LB1183 amends property‑valuation/statutory processes and specifies agency duties and timelines. Key agencies/departments:

- County assessors: must certify school‑district taxable value to the Property Tax Administrator by Aug. 20 (amendments by Aug. 31).

- Property Tax Administrator: compute and certify adjusted valuations by Oct. 10 to the State Department of Education using level‑of‑value determinations; notify districts.

- Tax Commissioner: adopt rules on level‑of‑value standards, hear objections, enter orders by Jan. 1, process clerical and post‑certification correction requests, and direct recertifications.

- The commission (per section 77‑5022): may adjust class/subclass values statewide and sets acceptable valuation ranges (including new 22–25% ranges for certain school‑bond‑related agricultural valuations).

- Tax Equalization and Review Commission: appeal venue for Tax Commissioner orders.

- State Board and Director of Administrative Services: State Board may approve lump‑sum delayed state aid; Director draws warrants from appropriated funds for payment.

Operative date: Jan. 1, 2027. No new appropriation amounts are specified. Original sections are repealed.
