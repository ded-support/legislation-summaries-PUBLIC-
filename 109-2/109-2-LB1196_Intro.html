Summary

LB1196 prohibits Nebraska state or local funds from being allocated or expended—directly or indirectly—for any "low-earning outcome program" as defined by 20 U.S.C. §1087d. Prohibited funding categories include individual student financial aid, base operational/instructional funding, separately appropriated aid or grants, and capital/facilities funding. The Nebraska Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education (CCPE) must annually review federal determinations of such programs and electronically report to the Clerk of the Legislature on those determinations, enforcement of the funding prohibition, and the estimated fiscal impact. The CCPE is authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement the statute. The bill itself contains no new appropriations; it restricts existing state and local funding uses and requires fiscal-impact reporting.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statewide prohibition on allocating or spending any state or local funds—directly or indirectly—on programs the federal government identifies as "low-earning outcome programs" under 20 U.S.C. 1087d, explicitly covering student financial aid, base operational/instructional funding, special grants, and capital/facilities funding. Assigns the Nebraska Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education to annually review federal determinations and electronically report to the Clerk of the Legislature on those determinations, enforcement of the funding prohibition, and the estimated fiscal impact. Gives the Coordinating Commission rulemaking authority to implement the section. No specific appropriation amounts are listed.
