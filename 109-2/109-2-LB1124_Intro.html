Summary

LB1124 reallocates cigarette-tax proceeds and directs specific distributions beginning July 1, 2026. It retains the Tax Commissioner’s role in collection and assigns the State Treasurer responsibility for distributions. Key allocations: an amount equivalent to $0.52 per pack to the General Fund; an amount equivalent to $0.97 per pack plus an additional $1,250,000 annually to the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund (the $0.97 equivalent earmarked for Medicaid); $3,820,000 annually to the Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Cash Fund; prior allocations to the Nebraska Outdoor Recreation Development Cash Fund, the Health and Human Services Cash Fund, and the Building Renewal Allocation Fund remain; any remaining proceeds go to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund. Operative July 1, 2026, with an emergency clause making the act effective on enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds legislative findings that Nebraska spends over $120 million annually on Medicaid costs from smoking-related illness, that such illnesses are preventable, and that Medicaid consumes over 20% of the State General Fund. Directs that a portion of cigarette tax revenue be dedicated to Medicaid—specifically, an amount equivalent to $0.97 of the cigarette tax will be used for Medicaid purposes, effective July 1, 2026. Agencies affected: the Legislature (policy direction) and the State Medicaid program (recipient of the dedicated tax revenue).
