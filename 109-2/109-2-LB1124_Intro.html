Summary

LB1124 raises the cigarette excise tax to $1.64 per standard (20‑cigarette) package and directs collection by stamping agents to the Tax Commissioner. Beginning July 1, 2026, the State Treasurer must allocate tax proceeds as follows: the equivalent of $0.52 per pack to the General Fund; the equivalent of $0.97 per pack plus an additional $1,250,000 annually to the Nebraska Health Care Cash Fund (the $0.97 equivalent expressly designated for Medicaid); existing allocations remain for the Nebraska Outdoor Recreation Development Cash Fund, the Health and Human Services Cash Fund (per sections 81‑637 to 81‑640), the Building Renewal Allocation Fund, and the Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Cash Fund; any remainder goes to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund. The act is operative July 1, 2026 and includes an emergency clause.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text states the Legislature finds Nebraska spends over $120 million annually on smoking-related Medicaid costs, which exceed 20% of General Fund appropriations, and declares intent to direct part of the cigarette tax to Medicaid. It specifies that an amount equivalent to $0.97 of the cigarette tax shall be used for Medicaid, effective July 1, 2026. Agencies mentioned: the Legislature and the State Medicaid program. No other departments are named; the change reallocates cigarette tax revenue to Medicaid but does not list specific appropriation line-item amounts beyond the $0.97-per-tax allocation.
