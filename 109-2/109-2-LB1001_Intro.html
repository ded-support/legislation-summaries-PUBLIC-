Summary

LB1001 places the Charitable Gaming Division within the Department of Revenue and requires the Department to file annual electronic reports to the Governor, Legislature, Auditor, and Attorney General on tax revenue, expenses, and related activities. It allocates 40% of specified charitable gaming taxes to the Charitable Gaming Operations Fund for the division’s administration and to the State Racing and Gaming Commission for administrative support of the Nebraska Commission on Problem Gambling (60% to the General Fund). Through June 30, 2026, $100,000 is transferred quarterly from that fund to the Compulsive Gamblers Assistance Fund. The division may charge and remit publication/record fees to the Charitable Gaming Operations Fund. The Tax Commissioner (Department of Revenue) may employ investigators with limited law-enforcement authority. Authorized gaming operators must withhold and remit setoff amounts from parimutuel and casino winnings to the Department to credit outstanding state tax liabilities.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions in the added text change Department of Revenue responsibilities, appropriations, or budget allocations. The fiscal items added: (1) Track deduction monies are to be given to the breed official registrars for distribution per section 2-1207.01. (2) Each official registrar must annually spend at least 80% of amounts deducted under subsection (2) of section 2-1207 on purse supplements and breeder/stallion awards. (3) Failure to timely register a qualifying Nebraska-bred horse may trigger an administrative fee up to $1,000 payable to the applicable official registrar to cover registrar administrative costs. Other additions address waivers for tracks, keno age/location rules, and consultation on interstate simulcasts, but none create new revenue or appropriation duties for the Department of Revenue.
