Summary

LB1218 assigns the Department of Transportation (DOT) to administer the County Bridge Match Program using Transportation Infrastructure Bank Fund dollars, and requires the Director‑State Engineer to appoint three DOT representatives to the County Bridge Match Working Group. The working group must award at least $4 million to applicants each fiscal year; the program terminates June 30, 2029. The bill specifies that the Highway Cash Fund and Roads Operations Cash Fund are expended by the Department of Transportation for listed highway purposes, including the County Bridge Match Program. It directs the State Treasurer to transfer $5 million from the Roads Operations Cash Fund to the Transportation Infrastructure Bank Fund on or before June 30, 2026, and again on or before June 30, 2027, on dates/amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services.
