Summary

The bill strengthens Department of Revenue (DOR) audit, confidentiality, and enforcement rules: the Tax Commissioner’s enforcement authority and procedures for compiling school‑district income data are clarified; the Auditor of Public Accounts and Legislative Audit Office may inspect tax returns but must notify the Tax Commissioner 30 days in advance, provide an audit plan and list of returns, follow statutory audit procedures, and comply with DOR confidentiality safeguards (secure on‑site storage, restricted access, standardized request records, return of records, and secure storage of identifiable workpapers). Sampling rules for performance audits of tax‑incentive programs and disclosure exceptions/penalties are specified. Department of Labor disclosures needed for Employment Security, Contractor Registration, and Employee Classification are preserved. Department of Motor Vehicles may receive return information for IFTA delinquency enforcement. Department of Insurance may receive tax data for certain tax credit programs. Department of Health and Human Services juvenile‑case dissemination limits are reinforced. Appropriations: the Office of Inspector General of Nebraska Child Welfare may hire staff only within available appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text clarifies that any confidential information provided to the Division of Legislative Oversight/Legislative Audit Office or an Inspector General (including juvenile‑justice materials) remains confidential and must not be disclosed. It permits secure remote electronic access to taxpayer return information that can identify individuals for oversight purposes and requires reports to be submitted in formats the Division/Legislative Audit Office specifies (including timing references such as quarterly). No substantive provisions affect the Department of Administrative Services, Insurance, Labor, Health and Human Services, or Motor Vehicles. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
