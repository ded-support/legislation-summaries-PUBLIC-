Summary

LB1145 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §84-1411 (Open Meetings Act) to require each public body to designate and record in its minutes the method for giving reasonable advance publicized notice to members and the public. It clarifies twice-yearly legal‑newspaper notice for bodies in §84-1409 with narrow exceptions, and requires alternative steps if a newspaper refuses publication (post on the public body’s website if available, request a statewide newspaper repository post, and conspicuously post locally), with written records retained. Agendas must be sufficiently descriptive and not altered later than 24 hours before a meeting (48 hours for certain off‑limits municipal meetings). The bill enumerates state and local entities authorized to use virtual conferencing and sets notice, physical‑site, document‑access, recording, and participation requirements and limits on virtual meetings. Emergency and limited non‑action virtual meeting provisions are specified. The original section is repealed. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds notice requirements for “public bodies”: they must designate a method for providing advance publicized notice and record that method, meeting schedule, and location in their minutes at least twice yearly; notice must be published in a legal newspaper of general circulation within the jurisdiction. Exempts governing bodies of cities of the second class, villages, their advisory committees, and governing bodies of rural or suburban fire protection districts from the specified subdivision. Clarifies that failure to comply with the publication requirement does not render official actions void or voidable. No state agencies or departments are specifically named, and no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
