Summary

LB1047 amends the Nebraska Bingo Act. Key agency action: "the department" administers special-event bingo permits (application on a department form, $15 permit fee), may issue two permits per nonprofit per year (up to 14 days), and requires permits be posted. The bill: (1) expands the definition of bingo to authorize "music bingo" (song titles, artists, genres) and permits mechanical/electronic random-selection devices for music bingo; (2) sets or clarifies $50 prize caps for nonlicensed operators, special-event bingo, and youth exceptions; (3) exempts special-event bingo (when permitted) from Class I/II licensing, licensed-organization record-keeping/reporting requirements, and the act’s gross-receipts tax; (4) limits disposable paper cards at special events to $1 each and specifies permitted uses of gross receipts. No state appropriations or other budget allocations are provided.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that bingo games using song titles, musical artists, or music genres may only use a mechanical, manually operated, or electronic device that ensures those items are randomly selected.
