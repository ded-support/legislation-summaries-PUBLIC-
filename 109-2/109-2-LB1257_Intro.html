Summary

The bill reallocates tax administration, set‑asides, and funding oversight among agencies. DMV will collect sales/use tax on specified vehicle registrations, deduct its collection fee (credited to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund), and remit taxes monthly to the Tax Commissioner; county treasurers collect other vehicle taxes, remit to the Tax Commissioner, and in counties ≥150,000 population remit $1 of their fee for each of the first 5,000 registrations to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. The Tax Commissioner (Department of Revenue) is the central administrator for numerous temporary sales/use tax exemptions (through Jan 1, 2027), enforcement, certificate issuance, and rulemaking; sales tax receipts flow to the Revenue Distribution Fund. DOT receives aircraft sales/use tax proceeds for the Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund and certain vehicle/lease receipts to the Highway Allocation Fund. DED administers Good Life Transformational Projects and provides district data to Revenue for monthly city offsets. DHHS certifies nonprofit community providers for exemption eligibility. DAS directs timing/amounts of a $750 million General Fund transfer (FY2024‑25) to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund (to be wound up July 1, 2027, with balances to the New School Relief Fund). The bill also credits certain boat/ATV sales‑tax proceeds to Game & Parks Capital Maintenance with transfers to EMS Operations, levies a $10 exemption‑certificate fee to the General Fund, and caps renewable energy tax credits at $50,000.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: Major impacts. Presumes services taxable but creates temporary exclusions (through Jan 1, 2027) for enumerated services including "Health services, major group 80." Tax Commissioner must annually determine (by July 1) the increase in sales/use tax revenue caused by these changes and credit that amount to the New School Relief Fund.

- Department of Health and Human Services: Indirect effect — health services (SIC major group 80) are expressly treated as excluded from sales/use tax until Jan 1, 2027.

- State Treasurer / transfers (relevant to budgeting): Beginning FY 2027-28, $838 million annually will be transferred from the General Fund to the New School Relief Fund (on or before Sept 1). The School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund terminates July 1, 2027, and its balance moves to the New School Relief Fund. School levy rates and new levy/backstop authority (up to 3 cents) are adjusted.

- Departments of Administrative Services, Agriculture, Economic Development, Motor Vehicles, and Transportation: No direct changes, funding actions, or responsibilities in the added text.
