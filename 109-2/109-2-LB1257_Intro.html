Summary

The bill assigns specific duties and budget actions across multiple agencies:

- Department of Motor Vehicles: required to collect sales tax on vehicles/semitrailers/trailers registered under §60‑3,198, remit receipts to the Tax Commissioner, may retain a collection fee (depositable to the Motor Carrier Division Cash Fund), and county treasurers remit vehicle/boat taxes to the Department of Revenue; in counties ≥150,000, $1 of the collection fee from each of the first 5,000 registrations is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund. Collection fees may be forfeited for rule violations.

- Department of Revenue / Tax Commissioner: administers sales‑and‑use tax exemptions (including temporary exemptions for DHHS‑certified community behavioral/developmental providers), issues $10 numbered exemption certificates (fee to General Fund), processes refunds/credits, audits C‑BED projects, verifies renewable energy credits, receives retailer/ownership updates for Good Life districts, and certifies revenue variances directing State Treasurer transfers (including to the Cash Reserve Fund, School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund, and New School Relief Fund).

- Department of Health and Human Services: certifies nonprofit providers eligible for temporary sales‑and‑use tax exemptions through Jan 1, 2027.

- Department of Transportation: directs that state sales/use tax on aircraft sales/leases (effective July 1, 2023) be credited to the Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund; Aeronautics’ interest‑free loan repayments are excepted from levy limits.

- Department of Economic Development: designated administrator for the Good Life Transformational Projects Act, responsible for review/approval of districts, project areas, maps, MOUs, and related rulemaking.

- Department of Administrative Services: the budget administrator schedules a $750 million General Fund transfer to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in FY2024‑25.

Key funding actions: $750M FY2024‑25 transfer (DAS direction); aircraft tax proceeds credited to DOT Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund; $10 exemption‑certificate fee to General Fund; $1 remittance per registration in large counties to the Department of Revenue Enforcement Fund; Dept. of Revenue‑directed transfers to School District Property Tax Relief and New School Relief funds (the latter receives $838M annually beginning FY2027‑28).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Revenue: New provisions presume services taxable (with certain temporary exemptions through Jan 1, 2027); the Tax Commissioner must annually (by July 1) calculate the increase in sales/use tax revenue to be credited to the New School Relief Fund. The Property Tax Administrator (in Revenue) will begin crediting specified funds to the New School Relief Fund July 1, 2027; the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund terminates then and its balance transfers to the New School Relief Fund.

- Funding/appropriations: A New School Relief Fund is created (administered by Dept. of Education). The State Treasurer must transfer $838 million from the General Fund to it on or before Sept. 1, 2027, and annually thereafter. Levy rates are set at $0.85/$100 valuation in FY2027‑28 and $0.60/$100 thereafter. If the fund under‑distributes, school boards may approve an additional levy up to $0.03/$100 to make up shortfalls.

- Departments of Administrative Services, Agriculture, Economic Development, Health and Human Services, Motor Vehicles, and Transportation: no direct changes, duties, or appropriations in the added text.
