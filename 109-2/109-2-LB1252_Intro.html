Summary

The bill directs that the 27-cent retail delivery fee "shall be paid by the purchaser or seller, collected by the seller, and remitted to and enforced by the Department of Revenue." It further requires that all fees remitted to the Department of Revenue be forwarded to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. The Department of Revenue is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the section. No appropriations or other funding allocations for the Department are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added provisions impose a $0.27 fee on every retail delivery of tangible personal property (with enumerated exemptions for tax-exempt sales/entities, new businesses' first year, businesses with < $500,000 prior-year retail sales, and wholesale sales). The seller collects the fee (paid by purchaser or seller) and remits it; the Department of Revenue is charged with enforcement and may adopt rules to implement the section. All fees collected are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. The text does not create a direct appropriation or change the Department of Revenue’s budget—it assigns collection/enforcement duties and directs revenue to the specified cash fund.
