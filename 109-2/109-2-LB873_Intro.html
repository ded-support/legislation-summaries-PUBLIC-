Summary

LB873 levies a 10% excise tax on retail kratom sales effective July 1, 2027. It requires each retailer to maintain complete electronic sales records "in the manner prescribed by the Department of Revenue" and to provide those records to the Department on request. Retailers must file monthly returns by the 20th day (on forms prescribed and furnished by the Department) and remit taxes due; the Department may require electronic filing and EFT payments. The Department is directed to collect the excise tax and remit monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.
