Summary

LB1108 amends procurement rules in Neb. statutes governing joint entities (electric-generation joint authorities) and districts (public power districts) by establishing tiered sealed-bid thresholds: $750,000 for entities/districts with gross revenue under $500 million and $1,500,000 for those with gross revenue of $500 million or more. Above those thresholds contracts require advertised sealed bids (advertised once in local general-circulation newspapers). The bill preserves and clarifies exceptions—contracts involving radioactive material, technologically complex or unique equipment, procurements from electric utilities or alliances, maintenance/repairs, original-manufacturer replacement parts, used equipment purchases, and emergency work—subject to engineer certification, two-thirds governing-body approval where required, and specified public notice. Sections 13-824.01, 13-824.02, and 70-637 are amended; original versions are repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds fee/threshold language applying $750,000 for joint entities with gross revenue under $500 million and $1,500,000 for those with gross revenue of $500 million or more. It also authorizes the governing body of a joint entity, upon sudden or unexpected damage or a regulatory body order preventing compliance with sections 13-824.01–13-824.03, to declare an emergency and proceed with necessary work on electric generating facilities without first complying with those sections. No state agencies, appropriations, or budget allocations are named or changed.
