Summary

LB1019 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-1311.03 governing county assessors. It retains annual requirements that county assessors systematically inspect portions of taxable real property (with inspections completed by March 19 generally, and by March 25 in counties with population ≥150,000) and that all parcels be inspected no less frequently than every six years; assessors must adjust values to ensure uniform, proportionate valuations. The bill adds that a county assessor, while performing these official duties, is immune from prosecution for trespass, except the assessor may not enter an enclosed building without the property owner’s or occupant’s express permission. The original section is repealed. No changes to funding or appropriations are included.
