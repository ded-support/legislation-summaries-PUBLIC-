Summary

LB1251 amends Nebraska lien law for homeowners' associations (§52‑2001) and unit owners' associations (§76‑874). Key references to government entities and procedures:

- Recording offices: Liens become effective when a dollar‑amount notice is recorded in the office where mortgages or deeds of trust are recorded. Liens that are not enforced are extinguished after five years, but may be continued for up to five years by filing a continuation statement in that recording office; the continuation must be mailed to the property prior to the original extinguishment date.

- Courts: Judgments in enforcement actions must include costs (including late fees, interest, and attorney fees), but the court shall consider only time, labor and actual costs/fees incurred by the prevailing party.

- Administrative timing: Associations must furnish a recordable statement of unpaid assessments within ten business days of request.

No changes to state appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that a homeowners' association may continue a lien for unpaid assessments up to five years by filing a continuation statement in the office where mortgages or deeds of trust are recorded (i.e., the county recorder/registrar), and that the continuation must be sent to the member/unit before the original extinguishment date. It also limits courts when awarding costs and attorney's fees to only the time, labor and actual costs/fees incurred, and allows recovery of those fees either upon voluntary payment after filing but before judgment, or upon judgment. Agencies mentioned: courts and the mortgage/deed recording office. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
