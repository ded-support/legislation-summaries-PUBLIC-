Summary

LB981 amends the Nebraska Housing Agency Act and municipal authority for cities of the metropolitan class. Key agency-related changes:

- Grants cities of the metropolitan class authority by ordinance to require housing authorities (local housing agencies) to comply with city rental inspection/registration, code enforcement, and rental property inspections.

- Defines terms (including bed bug and pest control professional) and requires housing agencies to establish a formal complaint process with timelines: acknowledge complaints within 5 business days; resolve non‑bed‑bug complaints within 14 days; inspect bed‑bug complaints within 10 days and obtain remediation within 21 days; keep remediation records two years.

- Requires agencies to inspect units for bed bugs before renting, disclose adjacent infestations, and bear investigation/remediation costs.

- Reporting: agencies must report complaint/resolution statistics and staffing to commissioners at each board meeting and submit semiannual reports to the city and the Legislature’s Urban Affairs Committee.

- No appropriations or budget changes are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements for housing authorities/agencies in a "city of the metropolitan class" to comply with local rental inspection/registration ordinances and code enforcement. Defines "pest control professional" under the Pesticide Act. For bed-bug complaints the housing agency must: provide notice before entry; inspect or hire a licensed pest professional within 10 days (including adjacent units); notify affected tenants within 10 days of a confirmed infestation; obtain remediation within 21 days; and keep remediation records (including chemicals used) for two years. The agency bears the costs of investigation and remediation. Housing agencies must biannually report to the city and the Legislature’s Urban Affairs Committee (pest actions, evictions, complaints/resolutions, vacancy rates, board updates). No explicit appropriations or new funding lines.
