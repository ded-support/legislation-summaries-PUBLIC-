Summary

- The Department of Economic Development (DED) will administer the Affordable Housing Trust Fund (AHTF) as part of its housing affordability strategy. The AHTF may include legislative appropriations, authorized transfers, grants, private contributions, loan repayments, and other sources; the Legislature may direct transfers from the AHTF to specified funds (General Fund; Behavioral Health Services Fund; Lead‑Based Paint Hazard Control Cash Fund; Middle Income and Rural Workforce Housing Investment Funds; Site and Building Development Fund).

- DED must: allocate not less than 30% of available AHTF funds to each congressional district annually; announce grant/loan application periods of at least 60 days; require land identification before new-construction applications; and adopt a qualified allocation plan prioritizing lowest‑income and specified zone projects.

- Starting July 1, 2026, DED will disburse 80% of grant awards into an interest‑bearing construction escrow (paid on partial lien releases/invoices) and 20% on completion. Beginning Oct. 1, 2025, recipients must file quarterly use-of-funds schedules; DED may require documentation, recapture unspent funds (including escrow interest), disqualify noncompliant recipients, contract for services, and limit administrative charges from workforce housing funds to actual administration costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary focused on the Department of Economic Development:

- Adds authority for the Nebraska Investment Finance Authority (NIFA) to form, support, invest in, operate, participate in, and own nonprofit entities or consortia to further NIFA’s statutory purposes, and to transfer assets to those entities. Debts/liabilities of such entities are not automatically obligations of NIFA unless NIFA states otherwise.

- Requires grant funds to be held in interest-bearing construction disbursement escrow accounts and disbursed on presentation of partial lien releases and invoices; escrow agents must be licensed title insurers, financial institutions, or third‑party law firms.

- Excludes application of the escrow/holding rule for buildings constructed or repaired with state grant funds that will not be owned or managed by the state.

No direct changes to the Department of Economic Development’s powers, budget, appropriations, or specific funding allocations are included.
