Summary

LB984 makes procedural and substantive changes affecting county clerks and the courts. County clerks are prohibited from issuing marriage licenses to anyone under 18. District and other courts presiding over emancipation petitions must advise minors of specified consequences, apply a clear-and-convincing proof standard, and may enter judgments of emancipation if in the minor’s best interest. A court-ordered emancipation confers most adult legal capacities (contracting, medical consent, property, litigation) while preserving age-specific constitutional/statutory limits; it also suspends existing custody, parenting-time, and support orders. The district court clerk must report any suspended orders to the jurisdiction that issued them. Several statutory sections are repealed. No appropriations, funding changes, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that no marriage license may be issued unless both parties are at least 18 years old. The amendment does not identify or alter responsibilities for any government agency or department, nor does it include any funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
