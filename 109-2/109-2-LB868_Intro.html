Summary

LB868 requires that an application for the inheritance-tax exemption under section 77-2007.03 be filed with the Department of Revenue within twelve months after the decedent’s death. If such an application is timely filed, the bill defers the due date for applicable inheritance taxes until twelve months after a determination that the decedent was not the victim of a homicide. The bill contains no provisions changing funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a rule that if an inheritance-tax exemption application under §77‑2007.03 is filed with the Department of Revenue within 12 months of death, the inheritance tax (§§77‑2001–77‑2006) becomes due 12 months after the determination that the decedent was not a homicide victim. Interest per §45‑104.01 accrues on unpaid tax from its original due date. Personal representatives/trustees who fail to pay within 12 months after that determination must post a payment bond per §77‑2009. Failure to initiate an appropriate proceeding within 12 months incurs a penalty of 5% per month (to a 25% maximum); filing probate or an application under §77‑2018.07 and paying the tentative tax within 12 months stops the penalty. County court may abate the penalty for good cause. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes for the Department of Revenue are included.
