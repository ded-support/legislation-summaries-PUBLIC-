Summary

The bill centralizes workforce program administration in the Department of Labor (DOL): it creates the Nebraska Office of Registered Apprenticeship within DOL as the state registration agency (setting standards, issuing completion credentials, approving/denying programs, and resolving disputes); transfers administration of the student internship program from the Department of Economic Development to DOL (DOL will award internship grants up to $1.5 million per fiscal year from the Job Training Cash Fund and may use up to 5% of any appropriation for administrative costs); expands DOL duties to issue age/schooling employment certificates and maintain records; requires recipients of Business Innovation Act assistance employing Nebraska workers to file quarterly wage reports with DOL. Employment Security Law changes redirect state unemployment tax receipts to the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and the Business Innovation Cash Fund, and allow the Business Innovation Cash Fund to be used to pay unemployment benefits if the Commissioner so directs. The Department of Health and Human Services remains the licensor for the statutory definition of “hospital.” The bill also expresses intent to use $100,000 saved from reduced Angel Investment Tax Credit authority to increase the Department of Revenue’s FY2019‑20 appropriation to cover LB334 implementation costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Labor (DOL): Creates the Nebraska Office of Registered Apprenticeship (placed in "the department") to serve as the state registration agency, set apprenticeship standards, approve/deny registrations, establish credentialing and reciprocity, and resolve disputes. Requires entities receiving Business Innovation Act assistance to submit quarterly wage reports to DOL; the Business Innovation Cash Fund may be used to pay unemployment benefits if the Commissioner of Labor so determines.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): Establishes the Business Innovation Cash Fund to carry out the Business Innovation Act (not for the Bioscience Innovation Program). Allows up to 5% of appropriations for administrative expenses. Expresses legislative intent to appropriate $15 million annually to the department beginning FY2027-28. Requires a minimum annual award beginning FY2027-28 equal to the lesser of $4 million or the total needed to fund qualifying applications.

- Department of Administrative Services, Department of Health and Human Services, Department of Revenue: no substantive additions or funding changes specific to these agencies in the added text.
