Summary

The bill makes multiple changes affecting the Department of Labor (DOL), Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and Department of Revenue (DOR). It creates a Nebraska Office of Registered Apprenticeship within DOL to set standards, approve registrations, provide reciprocity, and resolve disputes; moves statutory administration of internship programs to DOL and creates the Intern Nebraska Cash Fund (DOL may use up to 5% of appropriations for administration); and expands DOL’s enforcement powers (issue minor-employment forms/certificates, subpoena, inspect, investigate, and cite under the Wage Payment and Collection Act and Employee Classification Act). It requires Business Innovation Act applicants who employ Nebraska workers to file quarterly wage reports with DOL. The bill reallocates unemployment funding: abolishes the State Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund, transfers balances to the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund, directs future state UI tax receipts to the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and the Business Innovation Cash Fund, and allows the Business Innovation Cash Fund to pay unemployment benefits if needed. It defines “hospital” by DHHS licensure. DOR receives a one-time $100,000 appropriation for FY2019‑20 to offset LB334 implementation costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Labor (DOL): Adds establishment of a Nebraska Office of Registered Apprenticeship (state registration agency) to set apprenticeship standards, credentialing, approve/deny program registrations, and provide reciprocity. Requires entities receiving Business Innovation Act assistance to submit quarterly wage reports to DOL. Allows the Business Innovation Cash Fund to be used to pay unemployment benefits if the Commissioner of Labor determines necessary.

- Department of Economic Development (DED): Creates the Business Innovation Cash Fund to implement the Business Innovation Act (excluding the Bioscience Innovation Program). The fund accepts specified deposits, may use up to 5% of any appropriation for administrative expenses, and must, beginning FY2027-28, award at least the lesser of $4 million or the total needed to fund qualifying applications. Legislature expresses intent to appropriate $15 million annually to the department beginning FY2027-28 for the Business Innovation Act.

- Department of Administrative Services, Department of Health and Human Services, Department of Revenue: No substantive additions or funding changes.
