Summary

LB867 — agency-specific summary:

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) may award child care grants in excess of the program appropriation using available federal funds (43‑2624).

- Title IV‑D Division (DHHS) must establish a Customer Service Unit for the State Disbursement Unit with statewide toll‑free lines; the unit’s physical location must be in Nebraska (43‑3342.04).

- For professional license fingerprint checks, “department” is defined as the Division of Public Health of DHHS for FBI interpretation (38‑131).

- Multiple provisions confirm DHHS subrogation and automatic assignment rights for medical assistance, expand estate‑recovery procedures (including certification/limits, five‑year recovery window, waiver/compromise authority), and related enforcement processes (68‑716; 68‑916; 68‑919).

- DHHS may operate the state Commodity Supplemental Food program with United States Department of Agriculture donations and federal funds (71‑2226).

- Wholesale drug distributor license fees are credited to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund and may fund licensure activities or prescription drug monitoring program expenses (71‑7450).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Health and Human Services: Adds eligibility language for young‑adult assistance—covers youth who were in out‑of‑home care, discharged to independent living, or had kinship guardianship/adoption assistance agreements effective after age 16—clarifying who qualifies. Also permits payment of expenses for the prescription drug monitoring system created under section 71‑2454.

- Department of Administrative Services and Department of Agriculture: no programmatic changes or agency‑specific appropriations are directly added in the underlined text.

- Funding/appropriations: a new provision allows "the department" to award grants in excess of the stated appropriation by spending available federal funds authorized for such grants, potentially enabling programs to exceed state appropriation limits when federal funds are available.
