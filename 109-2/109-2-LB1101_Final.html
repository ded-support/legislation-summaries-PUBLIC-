Summary

LB1101 amends the Judges Retirement Act and affects the Director of Administrative Services, State Treasurer, the director in charge of the Judges Retirement System, the Judges Retirement Board, the State Court Administrator, and the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee (Legislature), with notices to the Governor and Supreme Court. Key fiscal changes: state General Fund contributions to the Nebraska Retirement Fund for Judges are 5% of total annual member compensation prior to July 1, 2026 and zero percent beginning July 1, 2026; a statutory supplemental appropriation remains triggered if actuarially required contributions exceed statutory contribution rates. Employer payroll deductions are retained and remitted to the retirement director. Contribution rates for judges first serving on/after July 1, 2015 change July 1, 2026 (9% until reaching a 70% benefit cap, then 5%). COLA for those judges is capped at CPI or 2.5% beginning July 1, 2026 (previously 1%). The act takes effect immediately.

Summary of added provisions:

- Judges who first serve on/after July 1, 2015 must begin new monthly contributions to the judicial retirement fund on July 1, 2026: 9% of monthly compensation if their subsection (2) benefits have not reached the “maximum retirement benefit base limit,” or 5% if those benefits have reached the limit.

- Other rate adjustments: a provision changes a previously-specified five percent rate to zero percent beginning July 1, 2026, and another provision establishes a two-and-one-half percent rate effective on or after July 1, 2026.
