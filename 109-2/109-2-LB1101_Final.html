Summary

LB1101 amends the Judges Retirement Act. Key agencies and fiscal changes:

- Director of Administrative Services and the State Treasurer must deduct/credit judge contributions and remit funds to the director in charge of the Judges Retirement System; court clerks remit court-fee revenue to the State Treasurer; the State Court Administrator forwards collection data to the retirement director.

- The Board (Judges Retirement System) and its actuary will continue annual valuations, may combine amortization bases with notice to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee, and must notify the Committee, Governor, and Supreme Court of actuary recommendations. The Committee may propose statutory adjustments to the Legislature.

- State General Fund contributions to the Nebraska Retirement Fund for Judges fall from 5% (prior to July 1, 2026) to 0% beginning July 1, 2026. Supplemental appropriations remain required if actuarial contributions exceed statutory rates.

- Member rates for judges first serving on/after July 1, 2015 change (to 9% until max benefit, then 5% after reaching it, effective July 1, 2026).

- Annual benefit adjustment caps for those members increase from 1% (pre‑7/1/2026) to 2.5% beginning 7/1/2026.

- Act takes effect immediately (emergency).
