Summary

LB878 assigns the Department of Administrative Services responsibility to administer the bill's paid parental leave provisions. The bill contains no explicit funding, appropriation, or budget allocation language directed to the Department of Administrative Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statewide paid parental leave policy for permanent state employees: full‑time employees get 240 hours paid leave (part‑time prorated) for birth or adoption, usable within six months, generally not intermittent, runs concurrent with FMLA, capped at 240 hours per 12 months, requires ~30 days' notice unless impracticable, not charged to other leave, not paid out at termination, preserves return rights and benefits, and forbids interference or retaliation. The Department of Administrative Services is designated to administer the section. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included in the added text.
