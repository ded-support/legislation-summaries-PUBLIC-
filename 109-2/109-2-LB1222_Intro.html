Summary

LB1222 assigns the Department of Insurance to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations implementing the acquired brain injury coverage provisions and to create an expedited appeal process for insurer adverse determinations. The expedited appeals must be resolved within five business days; ongoing rehabilitation must continue during an appeal if the treating clinician determines interruption would risk regression. The Department must monitor compliance and publish an annual report on its website summarizing coverage determinations, denials, and related appeals for acquired brain injury services. The bill contains no specific funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Insurance.
