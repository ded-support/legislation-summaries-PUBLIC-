Summary

LB823 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§81-520.01, 81-520.03, and 81-520.05 to regulate land‑management burning. It (1) defines controlled and prescribed burning and requires open‑burning permits issued/waived by local fire chiefs (who may delegate most authority to department members); (2) requires permit forms to include landowner and responsible person contact information, whether the burn is prescribed or controlled, and approximate acreage, and directs the State Fire Marshal to post a sample form on its website; (3) expands required permit application/plan elements (supervisor, burn objective and type, map/firebreaks, confinement procedures, equipment, vegetation, smoke‑affected roads/habitations, weather conditions, acreage, and any other fire‑chief‑required information); (4) limits permit validity to 30 days and conditions issuance on safety findings. Fiscal: local fire departments may charge up to $10 per permit, remitted to the governing body as general funds for the fire department; fees do not reduce tax requirements and are waived for state or political subdivisions acting in official duties.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds definitions for use in sections 81-520.01–81-520.05: "controlled burning" (small, nonbroadcast burns to remove debris/vegetation with limited spread), "land-management burning" (encompassing controlled and prescribed burning), and "prescribed burning" (planned application of fire to manage grazing/pasture/forests/grassland, control weeds/pests/disease, prevent wildfires, manage watersheds, benefit wildlife, maintain windbreaks, and support research). Also requires indicating whether a burn is prescribed or controlled and the approximate acreage to be burned. No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes in the added text.
