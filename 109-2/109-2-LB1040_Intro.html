Summary

LB1040 provisions referencing the Department of Correctional Services (DCS) and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- Subjects ordered to inpatient treatment are committed to the custody of DHHS; DHHS may be asked to assist or advise mental health boards before entry of treatment orders.

- Victim-notification duties: county attorneys must forward victim names/addresses to DCS and DHHS; DCS must forward victim contact information to DHHS for petitions filed by interested parties when applicable.

- DCS must notify victims of furloughs, releases, escapes, discharges, transfers to community status, release into programs, and provide release-date calculations and related information.

- DHHS must notify victims when a qualifying person becomes the subject of a civil-commitment petition and of escapes, discharge/changes in disposition, furloughs, and program releases for persons under board-ordered inpatient treatment.

- DCS and DHHS must adopt rules to implement these duties; victim contact information is exempt from public disclosure.

- No direct funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for either agency are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Correctional Services (DCS): The bill adds a requirement that when a mental‑health petition is filed by an "interested party," the mental health board must ask the county attorney to notify DCS, and the county attorney must provide that notification. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for DCS are included.

- Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS): The added text does not reference DHHS or change its duties or funding.

- Additional relevant changes (agency‑neutral): defines "interested party," requires a signed declaration under penalty of perjury, criminalizes bad‑faith filings by interested parties (Class IV felony) and requires referral to the county attorney for prosecution if a violation is found. No appropriation language.
