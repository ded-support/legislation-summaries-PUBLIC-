Summary

LB931 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2715.07 to add and modify income tax credits. The bill explicitly authorizes the Department of Revenue to recapture the $5,000 nonrefundable residence purchase credit (for purchases in designated extremely blighted areas) if the claimant sells, transfers, or ceases using the residence as a primary residence within five years of the taxable year the credit was claimed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates a refundable Nebraska income tax credit for taxable years beginning (or deemed to begin) on or after Jan 1, 2026, for individuals who received a federal premium assistance credit under 26 U.S.C. 36B. The state credit equals the difference between the pre-expiration premium assistance amount (36B(b)(3)(A)(iii) as it existed prior to expiration) and the amounts otherwise allowed under 36B(b)(3)(A)(i) and (ii). The Department of Revenue would need to administer the credit, update forms/guidance, and process refunds. The text contains no explicit appropriations, funding, or budget allocations; it would therefore create fiscal exposure through refundable credits and likely increase administrative workload for the Department.
