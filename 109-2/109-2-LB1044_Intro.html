Summary

Section 81-12,163(2)(b) contains a legislative intent that $100,000 saved from a reduction in Angel Investment Tax Credit authority for calendar year 2019 be used to increase the appropriation to the Department of Revenue by $100,000 for fiscal year 2019‑20. The purpose is to offset costs incurred by the Department of Revenue to implement Laws 2019, LB334.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two funding provisions affecting the Department of Revenue: (1) a requirement that the program "shall award at least $4 million per year," and (2) legislative intent to appropriate $15 million to the Department for FY 2026‑27 and each fiscal year thereafter to implement the Business Innovation Act. The $15 million is expressed as intent to appropriate (not an immediate mandatory appropriation); no other budgetary allocations or funding sources are specified.
