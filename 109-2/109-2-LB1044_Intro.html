Summary

The bill states legislative intent that $100,000 saved from a reduction in tax credits under the Angel Investment Tax Credit Act for calendar year 2019 be used to increase the appropriation to the Department of Revenue by $100,000 for fiscal year 2019-20. The purpose is to offset costs the Department of Revenue incurred implementing Laws 2019, LB334. This provision appears in amended section 81-12,163 as an intent language item.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds two funding directives affecting the Department of Revenue: (1) requires the program to award at least $4 million per year; and (2) states the Legislature’s intent to appropriate $15 million to the department beginning in FY 2026‑27 and annually thereafter to implement the Business Innovation Act. The $15 million is stated as legislative intent (not an explicit mandatory appropriation).
