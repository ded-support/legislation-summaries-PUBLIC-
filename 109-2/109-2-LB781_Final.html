Summary

- The bill defines "Department" to mean the Department of Motor Vehicles (sec. 60-501) and identifies the Division of Motor Carrier Services as part of the Department and "Director" as the Director of Motor Vehicles (sec. 75-392).

- The Director of Motor Vehicles must adopt standards for an informal dispute‑settlement procedure substantially complying with 16 C.F.R. part 703 (as of Jan. 1, 2026); participation in certified manufacturer procedures can limit consumer refund/replacement claims (sec. 60-2705).

- The Department and its officers, employees, agents, or contractors who custody motor vehicle records must disclose records (excluding social security numbers) to verified requesters for specified governmental, legal, insurance, and commercial driver‑license purposes (sec. 60-2909.01).

- The Director may participate in and file Nebraska's participation in the Unified Carrier Registration plan and agreement on behalf of the state (sec. 75-393).
