Summary

LB752 expresses legislative intent regarding the Department of Correctional Services for FY2026‑27:

- Directs a net reduction of $5,200,000 General Funds to the Department’s Program 200 (Operations), specifically from the McCook Detention Facility.

- States the Legislature’s intent that $5,000,000 of General Funds be used by the Department in FY2026‑27 for tuition, fees, and other direct costs supporting reentry and reintegration programming for committed offenders.

Both provisions are stated as legislative intent rather than explicit mandatory appropriations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Legislative intent directs a $5,200,000 net reduction in General Funds for FY 2026‑27 to the Department of Correctional Services, Program 200 (Operations), specifically tied to the McCook Detention Facility. Separately, the Legislature intends that the Department use $5,000,000 General Funds in FY 2026‑27 for tuition, fees, and other direct costs for reentry and reintegration programming for committed offenders. Both are expressed as legislative intent; if both are applied as written, the net General Fund effect would be a $200,000 reduction.
