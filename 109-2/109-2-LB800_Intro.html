Summary

LB800 updates Nebraska's state building and energy code references to the 2024 editions and reallocates responsibilities among state and local authorities. Key agency actions:

- Department of Water, Energy, and Environment (DWEE)/Director: adopts the 2024 International Energy Conservation Code as the Nebraska Energy Code; reviews plans/specifications for buildings constructed with state funds (unless a local jurisdiction already reviewed them); must receive verification from the funded agency that constructed buildings comply; may adopt alternative equivalent standards by rule.

- Department of Administrative Services (State Building Administrator): in consultation with DWEE, may specify newer IECC editions, additional efficiency or renewable requirements, and grant waivers based on life‑cycle cost analyses; agencies seeking waivers must provide the analyses.

- Local governments: must adopt or conform to the state building code, notify DWEE within 30 days if deleting specified chapters, and update codes within two years of state updates.

- Section 72‑806: enforcement provisions of Chapter 1 of the 2024 IECC do not apply to buildings under section 72‑804.

No direct appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language incorporating the 2024 editions of model codes—specifically references to Chapter 13 of the 2024 International Building Code and Chapter 11 of the 2024 International Residential Code—and ties their applicability to the act’s effective date. No state or local agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations in the added text.
