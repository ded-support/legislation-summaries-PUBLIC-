Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Department of Health and Human Services to offer at least two separate, cost-competitive options for any department-issued training requirement for provider employees. At least one option must allow a provider to use an in‑house training program if it meets applicable federal safety and content standards. The "Developmental Disabilities Provider Excessive Training and Cost Reduction Act" applies to all provider employee training requirements whether existing or issued, renewed, or enforced on or after the act's effective date. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
