Summary

LB877 amends the Uniform Controlled Substances Act and expressly defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services. The bill retains an exclusion from the definition of "anabolic steroid" for compounds intended for implantation in nonhuman species that "have been approved by the Secretary of Health and Human Services," while clarifying products prescribed, dispensed, or distributed for human use remain covered. It schedules xylazine in Schedule III but exempts specified nonhuman uses tied to federal approvals by the U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services under FD&C Act §512 (including §512(a)(4) and investigational use under §512(j)), and related manufacture/compounding consistent with those approvals. No changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The underlined additions add specific chemical substances to the bill’s controlled-substances list: (1-(1-(1-(4-bromophenyl)ethyl)piperidin-4-yl)-1,3-dihydro-2H-benzo[d]imidazol-2-one), psilocin, and 2,5-dimethoxy-α-methylphenethylamine (2,5-DMA).
