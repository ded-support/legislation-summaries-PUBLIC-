Summary

LB942 amends section 68-901 of the Medical Assistance Act to prescribe reimbursement rules for emergency and inpatient hospital services under the state Medical Assistance program (Nebraska Medicaid). Reimbursement determinations may be made consistent with federal Medicare regulations, and an inpatient admission may be reimbursed even if it does not meet the two-consecutive-midnights standard when the treating physician or qualified practitioner documents clinical judgment based on medical history/comorbidities, symptom severity, current needs, and risk of adverse events. The bill repeals the original section 68-901. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that inpatient admissions may be reimbursed under the medical assistance program—even if they do not meet the two-consecutive-midnights rule—so long as they are consistent with Medicare regulations and based on the clinical judgment documented by the physician or other qualified practitioner. The practitioner's decision must consider medical history and comorbidities, symptom severity, current medical needs, and risk of adverse events. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
