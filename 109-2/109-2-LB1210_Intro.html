Summary

LB1210 amends section 84‑612 to specify multiple Cash Reserve Fund transfers that are contingent on actions by the Department of Administrative Services:

- Transfers from the Cash Reserve Fund to the General Fund require certification by the Director of Administrative Services that the General Fund cash balance is inadequate; such transfers include a certified dollar amount and must be reversed when the Director notifies sufficient funds are available.

- The State Treasurer is directed, per the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services, to make the following transfers:

- $2,425,000 to the Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Revolving Fund (7/1/2024–6/30/2025)

- $4,000,000 to the General Fund (7/1/2024–6/30/2025)

- $29,458,800 to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund (7/1/2024–6/30/2025)

- $3,500,000 to the Health and Human Services Cash Fund (by 6/30/2025)

- $3,250,000 to the State Insurance Fund (as soon as possible after 4/2/2024)

- $25,000,000 to the Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund (by 6/30/2025)

- $10,000,000 to the Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund (7/1/2025–6/30/2026)

- $45,897,000 to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund (by 6/30/2026)

- $100,000 to the Self‑Insured Liability Fund (by 6/30/2025)

- $147,000,000 to the General Fund (7/1/2026–6/30/2027)

- $5,000,000 to the General Fund (7/1/2026–6/30/2027)
