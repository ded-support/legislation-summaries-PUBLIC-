Summary

Amends §84‑612 to (1) require the State Treasurer to transfer Cash Reserve Fund amounts to the General Fund only upon certification (and to reverse transfers upon notification) by the Director of Administrative Services; and (2) direct the State Treasurer, on dates and in amounts as instructed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services, to make specific transfers:

- $2,425,000 to Nebraska Public Safety Communication System Revolving Fund (7/1/24–6/30/25 window);

- $4,000,000 to the General Fund (7/1/24–6/30/25);

- $29,458,800 to Nebraska Capital Construction Fund (7/1/24–6/30/25);

- $3,500,000 to Health and Human Services Cash Fund (7/1/24–6/30/25);

- $3,250,000 to State Insurance Fund (as soon as possible after 4/2/24);

- $25,000,000 to Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund (by 6/30/25);

- $10,000,000 to Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund (7/1/25–6/30/26);

- $45,897,000 to Nebraska Capital Construction Fund (by 6/30/26);

- $100,000 to Self‑Insured Liability Fund (by 6/30/25);

- $147,000,000 and $5,000,000 to the General Fund (7/1/26–6/30/27).
