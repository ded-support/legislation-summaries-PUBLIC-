Summary

LB960 requires Nebraska school boards to include nonvoting student school board members equal in number to elected board members. "School board" and "school district" reference existing definitions in Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-101. Selection rules: if a district has one high school all student members come from it; if the number of high schools equals elected board seats, one student per high school; otherwise the board must adopt rotation procedures to equalize representation over time. Boards establish selection procedures (e.g., student elections, principal nominations, applications/interviews). Student members serve one-year terms (boards may allow reappointment); initial terms must begin no later than the first day of the 2027–28 school year. Duties: attend and participate in public meetings, receive materials, act as student liaisons, and introduce agenda items (sponsorship by an elected member may be required). Prohibitions: no voting, no closed-session participation, no access to confidential student, personnel, or legal information. Each board must adopt related policies by January 1, 2027. No appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Legislature mandates that local school boards (as defined by section 79-101) add nonvoting "student school board members" equal in number to elected members. Selection rules tie membership to the district's high schools (one-per-high-school when numbers match; rotation or procedures otherwise). Students must be 10th–12th graders in good standing, serve one-year terms (boards may allow reappointment), and initial terms must begin no later than the 2027–28 school year; boards must adopt policies by Jan 1, 2027. Student duties: attend public meetings, receive materials, sit with the board, act as liaison, and introduce/advocate agenda items (sponsorship by an elected member may be required). Prohibitions: no voting, no closed-session participation, no access to confidential student/personnel/legal records. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
