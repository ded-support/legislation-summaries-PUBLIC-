Summary

LB960 requires Nebraska school boards to include nonvoting "student school board members"—high school students serving in an advisory capacity—in a number equal to the board’s elected members. "School board" and "school district" reference section 79‑101. Selection rules: if a district has one high school all students come from it; if the number of high schools equals elected members, one from each; otherwise boards must adopt procedures to rotate representation equitably. Students (grades 10–12, in good standing) serve one‑year terms (start no later than the 2027‑28 school year). Duties: attend public meetings, receive materials, sit with the board, liaise with students, and introduce/advocate agenda items (sponsorship by an elected member may be required). Prohibitions: no voting, no closed‑session participation, no access to confidential records. Boards must adopt related policies by January 1, 2027. No funding or appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statutory requirement that school boards (as defined by section 79-101) must include nonvoting student school board members — one student member for each elected board member — drawn from district high schools. School districts/school boards must adopt selection procedures, one-year term rules (with initial terms beginning no later than the first day of the 2027–28 school year), and policies by January 1, 2027. Student members receive meeting materials, may participate in public discussion and introduce agenda items (subject to board rules), but may not vote, join closed sessions, or access confidential records. No state agencies beyond school boards/districts or changes to funding/appropriations are mentioned.
