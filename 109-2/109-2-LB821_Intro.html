Summary

LB821 revises duties and reporting requirements for the Nebraska Investment Council and the Public Employees Retirement Board (Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems).

Key agency changes:

- Nebraska Investment Council: must obtain an independent annual analysis (by March 31) of investment returns for each retirement system (waivable if assets < $1M), present it to the PERS board, and prepare an annual report (by April 10) covering portfolios, strategies, the state investment officer’s duties/limitations, organizational structure, and the analysis; present to the Legislature’s Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee.

- Public Employees Retirement Board (PERS): must provide benefit-liability information to the Council for policy portfolio objectives and prepare an annual report (by April 10) detailing funding policy, administrative costs/fees by fund/plan, member education, director duties/limitations, organizational and internal control structures; present to the same legislative committee.

Funding/budget references:

- Expenses of the retirement systems are to be allocated from investment income unless law specifies otherwise.

- Continued direction to the State Treasurer for transfers (including to the Legislative Council Retirement Study Fund and Class V School Employees funds) and authority for PERS to bill employers for Class V transition costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the Nebraska Investment Council must prepare and present an annual report — no later than March 31 each year — that includes the analysis required by subsection (2). The report must be presented to the Nebraska Retirement Systems Committee of the Legislature at a public hearing. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
