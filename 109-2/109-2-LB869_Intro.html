Summary

LB869 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §69-1311, altering notice procedures the State Treasurer must follow for property presumed abandoned under the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act. The State Treasurer remains responsible for annual publication and for mailing notices within 120 days after receiving the report required by §69-1310 to persons appearing entitled to property of $50 or more. The bill lets published notice either list names and last-known counties or use a display advertisement covering at least 25% of a standard broadsheet that explains how to search for unclaimed property. Mailed-notice content requirements and the $50 publication threshold (subject to the Treasurer’s discretion) remain. The section excluding traveler’s checks and money orders (per §69-1302) is retained. The original section is repealed. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that the notices in subsection (1)(i) or (ii) be published as display ads covering at least 25% of a standard broadsheet and must include a description of how to search for unclaimed property with the State Treasurer. No agencies beyond the State Treasurer are referenced and no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are changed.
