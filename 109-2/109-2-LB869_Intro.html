Summary

LB869 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §69-1311, changing notice requirements administered by the State Treasurer for property presumed abandoned. The State Treasurer must continue to publish an annual "Notice to Owners of Abandoned Property" but may satisfy the published-notice requirement either by (i) listing names alphabetically with counties of last-known address or (ii) running a display ad covering at least 25% of a standard broadsheet that includes instructions on how to search for unclaimed property. The bill preserves the $50 threshold for publication (unless the Treasurer deems otherwise), the 120-day mailing deadline for mailed notices to apparent owners, and the required contents of mailed notices. The original section is repealed. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Adds a requirement that each (1)(i) or (ii) be a display ad covering at least 25% of a standard broadsheet and include a description of how to search for unclaimed property with the State Treasurer. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are referenced.
