Summary

LB747 provisions referencing the Department of Labor:

- Employers must retain child employment certificates for at least 12 months and keep them accessible to attendance officers and the Department of Labor and its assistants and employees; the department (or attendance officers) may demand proof of a child's age and investigate failures to produce it.

- The Department of Labor may approve employment certificates for children residing outside Nebraska, must establish and maintain records and clerical support for certificates, and shall investigate reported complaints about a child's working conditions.

- Age and schooling certificate forms are to be prescribed and furnished by the Department of Labor.

- Information obtained by the department under related statutes or the Employee Classification Act is to be held confidential.

- Fees collected under contractor/PEO registration are remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Contractor and Professional Employer Organization Registration Cash Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for attendance officers and the Department of Labor (and its assistants/employees) to access employer records—including records of a child retained at least 12 months or when a child turns 16—upon request, including requests made outside Nebraska. It also authorizes the commissioner to seek a court order to compel persons to appear before the commissioner or a designated officer and produce documentary evidence or give testimony when subpoenas are ignored; courts may punish failure to obey as contempt. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
