Summary

LB747 assigns several explicit roles and authorities to the Department of Labor and the Commissioner of Labor:

- Employment certificates: employers must keep certificates accessible to attendance officers and the Department of Labor; employers must retain terminated/16‑year‑old certificates for at least 12 months and produce age evidence to attendance officers or the Commissioner on demand. The Department may approve employment certificates for out‑of‑state children, furnish prescribed age/schooling certificate forms, maintain related records and clerical services, receive complaints, and investigate child‑employment conditions.

- Enforcement and investigatory powers: the Commissioner may subpoena records and witnesses, inspect records, gather testimony for Wage Payment and Collection Act enforcement, and issue citations with administrative penalties; unpaid citations bar state or political‑subdivision contracting and citations (unless contested) are public.

- Administrative rules and fees: the Commissioner may set electronic filing/payment criteria and refuse noncompliant submissions. Application/renewal fee (not to exceed $40) is remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Contractor and Professional Employer Organization Registration Cash Fund.

- Confidentiality: department‑obtained information is confidential except as needed in specified enforcement proceedings.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds access and enforcement powers for the Department of Labor: employers retaining a child who reaches age 16 after at least 12 months must make records accessible to attendance officers and to the Department of Labor and its assistants and employees upon request, including requests made outside Nebraska. It also authorizes any court, on the commissioner's application, to enforce subpoenas by ordering persons to appear before the commissioner or produce documents; failure to comply may be punished as contempt. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
