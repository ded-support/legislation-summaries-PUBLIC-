Summary

LB1235 amends the Revenue Distribution Fund statute (77-27,132) to direct all proceeds of sales and use taxes imposed under section 77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (per section 3-101) to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023. This is an explicit tax-revenue allocation to a Department of Transportation fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary focused on the Department of Transportation:

The added text creates and funds a Nebraska Medical Cannabis regulatory scheme (patient/caregiver registry, practitioner directory, licensing, seed‑to‑sale system) but contains no directives, appropriations, transfers, or program changes affecting the Department of Transportation. There are funding provisions for the new Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission Cash Fund (fees, gifts, grants, transfers from Legislature; usable for administration, enforcement, staff, equipment, and electronic systems), a cap on license fees ($25,000), patient/caregiver application fees (up to $30), and a provision crediting dispensary sales/use tax proceeds to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund. No DOT budgetary impacts are specified.
