Summary

LB449 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §39-1365.01 to revise requirements for the department responsible for the state highway system (the Department of Transportation). The department must develop a specific, long-range state highway system plan, annually formulate and assign project priorities, and present the plan and the §39-1365.02 report to the Legislature by December 1 for referral to the appropriate standing committees. The plan must (1) designate portions of the system as expressways and (2) account for highway revenue by each district under §39-1102, requiring that at least 70% of revenue raised in a district be allocated to projects within that district. The bill repeals the original section.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a district-based accounting and allocation rule for highway revenue (referencing section 39-1102). It requires revenues raised by each highway district to be tracked by district and mandates that at least 70% of a district’s revenue be allocated to projects within that same district. No specific agencies or departments are named in the text; operational responsibility would fall to the agency that currently administers highway revenues (e.g., the state transportation/highway authority). No new appropriation amounts are specified—the change is a restriction on how existing district-raised highway revenue must be allocated.
