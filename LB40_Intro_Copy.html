Summary

LB40 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §21-193 (Uniform LLC Act) to require notice of organization, certificate amendments, mergers, conversions, or domestications to be published for three successive weeks in a legal newspaper of general circulation near the LLC’s designated office. Notices must include the information required by §21-117(b); brief resumes of amendments/transactions must be published the same way. Voluntary dissolution notices remain subject to §21-150. Proof of publication must be filed with the Secretary of State; late publication with subsequent filing validates prior and subsequent LLC acts. The bill repeals the original section. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
