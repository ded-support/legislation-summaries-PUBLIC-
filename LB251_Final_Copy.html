Summary

LB251 centralizes and clarifies regulatory authority for the Department of Banking and Finance (DBF) and confirms limited Department of Insurance roles. The bill: defines "Department" and "Director" as DBF for multiple statutes; empowers the Director to adopt rules (including definitions and alignment with 12 U.S.C. §84/Regulation O as of Jan. 1, 2025); grants DBF emergency powers to act against failing or endangered Nebraska‑chartered institutions (conversion/merger, asset/liability transfers, possession) and, on notice from a primary regulator, against federal or out‑of‑state institutions, while preserving hearing and appeal rights. It requires franchise sellers to file a notice with DBF and pay a $100 filing fee (credited to the Securities Act Cash Fund), allows DBF to demand disclosure documents within 10 business days, and to deny/revoke/suspend exemptions with notice/hearing. Section 45‑101.04 exempts certain loan‑rate limits for DBF‑licensed entities and insurers; Section 44‑708 gives the Director of Insurance rulemaking authority over funding agreements by admitted life insurers. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Banking and Finance

- Adds procedural requirements: applicant banks and credit unions must submit an affidavit of publication and proof of payment (in a director‑prescribed form) for required notices.

- Tightens collateral rules for the additional 10% lending limit: livestock (and receipts securing obligations) must maintain a current market value of at least 115% of the secured amount that exceeds specified concentration thresholds (25% of capital/surplus and capital notes/debentures or 15% of unimpaired capital and unimpaired surplus and capital notes/debentures).

- Grants the director immediate (no‑hearing) authority to convert/merge charters, assets, branches, or ownership to facilitate acquisition where a primary regulator deems the out‑of‑state or federally chartered institution impaired or unsafe.

- Clarifies naming use for applicants and fixes certain federal securities definitions to their Jan. 1, 2025 versions.

Department of Insurance

- No added text affects the Department of Insurance; no funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
