Summary

The bill requires financial institutions to file a notice designating a main chartered office, branch, or agent for service of garnishment summons with the Department of Banking and Finance. The department must post and maintain a public list of designated offices/agents on its website, update that list (and show the date records were last updated) within ten business days of a filing, and make modifications/revocations effective upon website update. Notices, modifications, and revocations must be made on forms prescribed by the Department of Banking and Finance. The department, its employees, and agents are granted civil and criminal immunity for acts or omissions arising from these requirements. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds under the Department of Banking and Finance a definitional and service rule for garnishment matters: "corporate entity" is defined to include corporations, LLCs, LLPs, series LLCs, or other entities required to have a Nebraska registered agent, but explicitly excludes financial institutions described in subsection (6). For garnishments or continuing liens against wages payable by a corporate entity, service must be made on the entity's registered agent for service of process in Nebraska. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
