Summary

This bill amends provisions for calculating and accounting school district property tax request authority. The state "department" (responsible for calculating and certifying authority to school boards) must annually: compute each district's property tax request authority using a specified formula that adjusts prior-year requests, non‑property‑tax revenue, investment income from special building funds, and the district's base growth percentage; reduce that amount by current-year non‑property‑tax revenue; and certify the resulting authority to the school board. The department also must calculate and report each district's unused property tax request authority for carryforward, but excludes carryforward of increases attributable to base growth percentage increases under section 79-3405. Original sections 79-3403 and 79-3406 are repealed. No explicit appropriations or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a deduction to a calculation equal to any amount by which a school district’s prior-year property tax request exceeded its property tax request authority under section 79-3405. It explicitly excludes from that comparison any increases in request authority that resulted from increases to the district’s base growth percentage under section 79-3405. No specific state agencies, new appropriations, or funding allocations are created.
