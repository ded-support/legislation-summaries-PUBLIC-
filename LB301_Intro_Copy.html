Summary

- Department of Administrative Services: The bill requires applicants for certain public benefits to attest in a format prescribed by the Department of Administrative Services that they are U.S. citizens or lawfully present; applicants who are eligible aliens and employed as or training to be law enforcement officers must likewise attest in a DAS-prescribed format. State agencies may offer electronic filing substantially similar to the DAS format.

- Department of Labor: If a contributory retirement plan or contract described in prior law (section 48-609 as it existed before 2018) is terminated, Department of Labor employees who were active participants at termination shall receive vesting credit for years of participation.

- Department of Correctional Services and Department of Revenue: Employees of the Department of Correctional Services and employees of the Department of Revenue (under section 77-366) are explicitly excluded from the statutory definition of "law enforcement officer."

No direct appropriations or other funding changes to these agencies are specified.
