Summary

LB611 updates Nebraska's state building and energy code references to the 2021 International Energy Conservation Code (IECC) and incorporates specified 2021 chapters of the International Building and Residential Codes. Key agencies and duties:

- Department of Environment and Energy (DENE): will review plans/specs for state‑funded buildings for IECC compliance, provide reviews to the funding agency, and receive verification that constructed buildings meet or exceed the code; the Director may adopt rules.

- Department of Administrative Services (DAS) — State Building Administrator: in consultation with DENE, may specify a newer IECC edition, add energy/renewable requirements, or approve waivers based on life‑cycle cost analyses; agencies seeking waivers must supply the analyses.

- Counties/cities/villages: must adopt the state code or an equivalent within two years of updates and must notify DENE if they delete certain code chapters.

The bill repeals the prior listed statutory sections. No appropriations or budgetary allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added text references specific parts of the 2021 model codes—namely Chapter 13 of the 2021 International Building Code and Chapter 11 of the 2021 International Residential Code—and indicates those chapters (and related provisions) take effect after this act’s effective date.
