Summary

LB36A appropriates funds to the Nebraska Department of Environment and Energy (Program 513) to implement Legislative Bill 36. It directs (1) $51,585 from the Waste Reduction and Recycling Incentive Fund for FY2025‑26 and (2) $109,036 from the same fund for FY2026‑27. It caps total expenditures from these appropriations for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems at $30,264 for FY2025‑26 and $63,554 for FY2026‑27.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Appropriates funds from the Waste Reduction and Recycling Incentive Fund to the Department of Environment and Energy, Program 513: $51,585 for FY2025‑26 and $109,036 for FY2026‑27 to support implementation of Legislative Bill 36 (109th Legislature, 2025). Limits permanent and temporary salaries and per diems charged to these appropriations to no more than $30,264 in FY2025‑26 and $63,554 in FY2026‑27.
