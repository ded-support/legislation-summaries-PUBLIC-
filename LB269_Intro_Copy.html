Summary

LB269 directs that retailer notices of local sales/use tax changes or terminations may be provided via the Department of Revenue’s website (or other electronic means). It further requires the Tax Commissioner to adopt rules obligating the Department of Revenue to verify: (1) addresses contained in the State’s address‑based boundary database used to assign taxing jurisdictions; and (2) appropriate sales and use tax rates reported on returns when online or remote transactions are included. No appropriations or funding changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires the Tax Commissioner to adopt rules obligating the Department of Revenue to verify addresses in the state's address-based boundary database and to verify that appropriate sales and use tax rates are used on returns that include online or remote transactions. Adds a state-maintained zip code boundary database assigning each 5- and 9-digit ZIP to the correct tax rates/jurisdictions (to be used only as allowed above). For the streamlined sales/use tax agreement, the ZIP database must apply the lowest combined tax rate when multiple rates exist in an area. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are included.
