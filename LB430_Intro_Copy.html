Summary

LB430 amends Student Discipline Act provisions governing short‑ and long‑term suspension and adds an exception to the prohibition on suspending pre‑K–2 students. Changes require principals to investigate and, within 24 (up to 72) hours of a short‑term suspension, send the student and parent/guardian a written statement to the principal/superintendent describing the conduct, reasons for action, prior interventions, available school resources, and an actionable plan to keep the student in school; principals must document efforts to conference with parents. Elementary schools may suspend pre‑K–2 students only if the student brings a deadly weapon (per §28‑109) to school/related vehicle/activity or engages in violent behavior capable of causing physical harm. Long‑term suspension/expulsion/mandatory reassignment notices must also include resources, an actionable reentry plan, hearing rights (including completing missed work without attending alternative programs), record and witness access, and a hearing request form. Original sections are repealed. No appropriations or funding changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds required information schools must include when addressing student misconduct: documentation of prior actions taken to discontinue or alleviate the behavior; resources the school can provide or recommend to assist the student; and an actionable plan for handling future behavior aimed at maximizing strategies to keep the student in school. Also adds language covering bringing prohibited items onto campus and engaging in violent behavior capable of causing physical harm to another student or school employee. No specific government agencies or departments are mentioned and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
