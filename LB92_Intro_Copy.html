Summary

LB92 enacts the Residential Tenant Clean Slate Act. It directs Nebraska trial courts to immediately issue orders sealing (clean slate relief) eviction records when an eviction proceeding is dismissed. Tenants may petition the trial court for sealing when records remain public after dismissal, a writ of restitution or judgment is reversed/vacated or never executed, the eviction was for COVID-19-era nonpayment (3/13/2020–6/30/2021), a named defendant was a minor, or a judgment violated statute; otherwise petitions are allowed three years after writ issuance or final judgment. Petitions require service on former parties, a 30-day objection window, and a hearing if objected to (objector bears burden). Orders are final and appealable. The bill references the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act and Mobile Home Landlord and Tenant Act. No state agencies beyond the trial courts are designated, and no funding or appropriation changes are included.
