Summary

LB117 (Introduced by Holdcroft; Revenue Committee) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §77-2704.13 to expand sales and use tax exemptions. It explicitly exempts sales and purchases of utilities — electricity, natural gas, propane, and sewer — when used for residential purposes (including primary-residence apartments with separately billed utilities and commercial properties primarily used as residences). Existing exemptions for fuels used in irrigation, farming, processing, manufacturing, electricity generation, hospital use, and water for irrigation/manufacturing remain in the statute. The bill becomes operative January 1, 2026, and repeals the original section. No state agencies or departments are named, and no funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition clarifying that sales/purchases of utilities (electricity, natural gas, propane, sewer) for "residential use" are covered. "Residential use" explicitly includes (a) primary-residence apartments where utilities are billed as a separate line-item from rent, and (b) commercial properties whose primary use is one or more primary residences. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
