LB135_Intro LB135_Intro_Copy.docx LB135 harmonizes election procedures for school-related ballot measures and joint entities involving schools. Key agency roles and changes:\n- Requires school district bond, levy, budget-exceeding, lease/lease-purchase, teacherage, and related questions — and joint-entity bonds that include Nebraska school districts or educational service units — be submitted at elections held in compliance with §32‑559(3).\n- Assigns administration duties to election commissioners or county clerks (conducting counting, early-vote distribution, canvass) and requires school boards to certify orders and voter lists to them.\n- Permits election commissioners or county clerks to bill school districts directly for special-election costs under §32‑559; political subdivisions remain responsible for canceled-election costs.\n- Specifies notice periods (e.g., 20–50 days) and majority-approval requirements within each affected school district or ESU.\n- References Secretary of State, Auditor of Public Accounts, Property Tax Administrator, county boards, and other political subdivisions in procedural/reporting roles. Receiving boards deliver ballots to the election commissioner/county clerk; returns go to the school board for canvass. The two appointed counters must be paid at least the minimum wage referenced in section 48-1203. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

LB34_Final LB34_Final_Copy.docx LB34 revises Nebraska statutes governing time and related administrative deadlines. Key agency/office impacts:\n- State-level: Director of Personnel retains rulemaking authority to implement vacation/sick‑leave provisions; heads of employing agencies must provide opportunity to use vacation or pay forfeited leave; Governor may extend annual‑leave deadlines in special cases; Executive Board of the Legislative Council holds analogous discretion for legislative employees.\n- Retirement: Public Employees Retirement Board — cash payment for denied forfeited vacation counts as compensation only for defined‑contribution or cash‑balance plans, not other defined‑benefit plans.\n- Elections: Poll opening/closing and ballot receipt times are aligned to mountain or central time for judges and clerks of election.\n- Federal roles: operability of year‑round daylight saving/standard time tied to actions/orders by U.S. Secretaries of Transportation, Commerce, and Navy and adoption by three adjacent states.\n\nNo funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Adds a new state standard-time provision: Nebraska would set standard time at UTC−5 in the central zone and UTC−6 in the mountain zone. It defines "Coordinated Universal Time" as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and interpreted/modified for the U.S. by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The provision only becomes operative after (1) authorization by U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation (or no longer being in conflict with them) and (2) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round standard time. It also states the section becomes null and void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No agencies are assigned funding, and no appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
