Summary

LB313 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§48-118 and 48-118.04 (Nebraska Workers' Compensation Act), explicitly recognizing the employer's workers' compensation insurer as subrogee and party in third‑party actions. Key changes:

- Clarifies that an employer or the workers' compensation insurer is subrogated to employee/dependent claims and may recover amounts beyond paid compensation.

- Requires third‑party settlements to be agreed in writing by the employee (or representative) and the employer's workers' compensation insurer (or employer if uninsured), or approved by a court as fair and reasonable.

- Specifies court-ordered distribution when parties disagree: deduct recovery expenses, pay one-third of remainder to the employee, reimburse employer/insurer for past and future compensation obligations, with any balance to the employee.

Original sections are repealed. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a formula for distributing recovery proceeds: after reasonable collection costs, one‑third goes to the employee (or their representative); next the employer or workers’ compensation insurer is reimbursed for compensation already paid or owed; any remaining balance goes to the employee (or their representative). No government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to appropriations, budget allocations, or funding.
