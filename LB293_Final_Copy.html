Summary

LB293 amends the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act to (1) impose regulatory duties on the department (with oversight tied to the Commissioner of Labor) for PEO-sponsored health plans: self-funded plans must file quarterly and annual reports with the department, maintain ERISA-style trust accounts, demonstrate actuarially sound reserves, hold stop‑loss coverage naming the PEO and providing 30 days' notice to the PEO and the Commissioner of Labor before termination, and provide participant notice that plans are self-funded; the department may hold compliance hearings, issue 30‑day corrective orders, and revoke a PEO's registration for noncompliance. (2) Clarify that an employee welfare benefit plan (including health benefits) offered by a single PEO is a single employer plan exempt from MEWA registration and that, under the Small Employer Health Insurance Availability Act, the PEO is treated as the employer for insurance acceptance. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions require professional employer organizations (PEOs) offering self‑funded health plans to use a licensed third‑party administrator, hold plan assets in an ERISA §403 trust, meet actuarial reserve standards, notify employees the plan is self‑funded, and file quarterly and annual reports with "the department." Reports must include financial statements, an actuary's rate/reserve certification, confirmation of stop‑loss insurance from an insurer licensed in this state (naming the PEO and requiring 30‑day advance notice to the PEO and the Commissioner of Labor before termination), and a state law compliance certificate. The department may hold compliance hearings, issue corrective orders (to be complied with in 30 days), and revoke PEO registration under the Professional Employer Organization Registration Act for failure to comply. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
