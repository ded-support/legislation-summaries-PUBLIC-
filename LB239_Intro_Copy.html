Summary

LB239 revises DHHS duties for developmental disabilities services. DHHS must administer Medicaid HCBS waivers and set funding amounts using an objective assessment published on the department website and approved by the Governor's developmental disability advisory board or stakeholder group. The assessment may not reduce an individual's budget to the point of removal from a setting, must be person‑centered, include an independent mediated review administered outside the department, and require redeterminations within 30 days. DHHS must provide or contract service coordination (individuals choose the provider) and create an expedited form/process for funding‑priority one requests with a five‑calendar‑day response. The department must maximize federal funding and require eligible residents to apply for Medicaid and other benefits. If funds are insufficient for subsection (4)(d), DHHS must provide comparable day services and annually report by Sept. 15 to legislative committees the estimated need and net additional resources required.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

For the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS):

- Must publish the objective assessment formula on its website and obtain approval from the Governor’s developmental disability advisory board or the required stakeholder group.

- Must ensure the assessment tool cannot reduce an individual’s budget so as to remove them from a setting.

- Must offer residential settings as options for individuals in funding priorities 1, 2, 3, and 5, and make them available for priorities 4 and 6 through the assessment process.

- Must complete redeterminations within 30 days and design the assessment to be person‑centered and consistent with the individual support plan.

- Must create an expedited form/process for requesting funding‑priority‑one consideration and respond within five calendar days.

- The assessment must include an independent mediated review administered outside DHHS; the individual/legal representative selects the review entity.

No explicit changes to appropriations, funding levels, or budget allocations are included.
