Summary

LB302 removes Nebraska's observance of daylight saving time and adds statutory cross‑references to reflect that change, though the DST repeal subsection becomes operative only after Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming enact similar laws. Election statute language is harmonized to specify poll open/close times by mountain (7 a.m.–7 p.m.) and central (8 a.m.–8 p.m.) zones. Personnel provisions clarify that sick‑leave and vacation‑leave accounts are balanced as of 11:59 p.m. central time on December 31. Agencies and officials referenced/affected: Director of Personnel (rulemaking), Governor (authority to extend leave in special cases), Executive Board of the Legislative Council (leave discretion for legislative employees), Public Employees Retirement Board (treatment of cash payments for forfeited leave in defined contribution/cash balance plans), employing agencies/heads (leave administration), and the Legislature/Legislative Council. The bill contains no appropriations or changes to funding.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a new provision making Nebraska’s standard time the time set by the Uniform Time Act of 1966 while declaring the State will not observe daylight saving time and will be exempt from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a) (the federal provision authorizing clock advancement). The subsection becomes operative January 1 after Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming each adopt laws to likewise not observe daylight saving time. The section is void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriations, or budget allocations included.
