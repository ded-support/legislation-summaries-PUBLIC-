Summary

LB302 amends Nebraska time and personnel statutes to eliminate observance of daylight saving time (exempting the state from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a)) effective Jan. 1 after Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming adopt similar laws. Election polling hours remain tied to mountain/central zones. The bill standardizes year‑end balancing of sick and vacation leave accounts to 11:59 p.m. central time on December 31. Administrative provisions: the Director of Personnel must adopt rules; employing agencies must reasonably allow use of leave or pay the cash equivalent when requested and denied; such cash payments count as compensation only for defined contribution or cash‑balance plans administered by the Public Employees Retirement Board. The Governor (or the Executive Board of the Legislative Council for legislative employees) may extend leave in special cases. No funding or appropriation changes.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision that Nebraska’s standard time follows the Uniform Time Act of 1966 for both central and mountain zones but that Nebraska will not observe daylight saving time and is exempt from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a) (the federal provision authorizing clock advancement). The no–daylight‑saving rule becomes operative January 1 after Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming each adopt laws doing the same. It also notes nullification tied to subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becoming operative. Agencies/departments: only the State of Nebraska and the referenced federal statute (15 U.S.C./Uniform Time Act) are implicated. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
