Summary

LB302 amends time-related statutes and state employment leave provisions. Key agency references and actions:

- State Personnel/Director of Personnel: requires adoption of rules to administer amended vacation leave provisions; sick and vacation leave accounts now balance at 11:59 p.m. central time on December 31.

- Public Employees Retirement Board: clarifies that cash payments for denied vacation leave count as compensation for defined contribution or cash‑balance plans but not for other defined‑benefit plans.

- Governor and Executive Board of the Legislative Council: retain discretion to extend annual leave in special cases.

- Election administration: polling hours and ballot-deadline times are specified by time zone (mountain: 7 a.m.–7 p.m.; central: 8 a.m.–8 p.m.).

- Federal law interaction: Nebraska will not observe daylight saving time and seeks exemption from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a) only after Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming enact similar laws; becomes operative Jan 1 following that occurrence.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included. Original sections repealed.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language sets Nebraska's standard time under the federal Uniform Time Act of 1966 but declares the State will not observe daylight saving time and will be exempt from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a) (the federal provision authorizing clock advancement). The exemption becomes operative January 1 after Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming each adopt similar laws. The section is void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No state agencies or departments are named, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes.
