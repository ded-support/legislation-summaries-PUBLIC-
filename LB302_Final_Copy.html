Summary

LB302 amends Nebraska time and related administrative provisions and identifies affected state entities.

Key agency impacts:

- Secretary of State/local election officials: clarifies polling hours by time zone (mountain 7 a.m.–7 p.m.; central 8 a.m.–8 p.m.) and ballot receipt deadlines.

- State agencies/employing agencies and the Governor/Legislature/Legislative Council: sets sick- and vacation-leave accounting deadlines at 11:59 p.m. central time on December 31; requires employing agencies to provide opportunity to use leave or pay cash equivalents when reasonable requests are denied.

- Director of Personnel: directed to adopt rules to administer vacation provisions.

- Public Employees Retirement Board: cash payments for denied vacation leave count as compensation only for defined contribution or cash-balance plans, not other defined-benefit plans.

- Time policy: Nebraska will not observe daylight saving time and will seek exemption from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a) contingent on Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming passing similar laws.

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text designates Nebraska’s standard time per the Uniform Time Act of 1966, but specifies the State will not observe daylight saving time and will be exempt from 15 U.S.C. 260a(a)’s clock-advancement provisions. The provision becomes operative January 1 following when Iowa, Kansas, South Dakota, and Wyoming each enact similar non‑DST laws. The section is voided when subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No state agencies or departments are created or mentioned, and there are no funding, appropriations, or budget allocation changes.
