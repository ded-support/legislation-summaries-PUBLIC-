Summary

LB685 amends section 84-612 to clarify that transfers to and from the Cash Reserve Fund are executed by the State Treasurer on dates and in amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services. It preserves the requirement that the Director of Administrative Services certify when the General Fund cash balance is inadequate (and may notify to reverse transfers). The bill lists numerous specified transfers that are to be carried out under that direction and adds a new transfer: $1,000,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to the School Safety and Security Fund to be made as soon as administratively possible after the act’s effective date.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The addition directs a $1,000,000 transfer from the Cash Reserve Fund into the School Safety and Security Fund to be used for grants (per section 79-3108) for exterior secure master key boxes. A subaccount must be established to hold those funds. The State Treasurer is ordered to make the transfer "as soon as administratively possible" after the bill's effective date, with the timing and amounts to be set by the budget administrator of the Budget Division of the Department of Administrative Services.
