Summary

LB153 requires the Department of Health and Human Services to take multiple federal actions and implement coverage changes under the Medical Assistance Act. Key department obligations:

- Submit a state plan amendment (SPA) to CMS to extend postpartum Medicaid coverage from 60 days to at least six months (may seek 12 months) (by Oct 1, 2023).

- Submit an SPA to CMS to provide at least six months postpartum coverage under the Children's Health Insurance Program for mothers of unborn-child-option children (by Oct 1, 2025); funding is intended from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

- Submit a Medicaid waiver or SPA to designate two medical-respite facilities for homeless adults in the expansion population (by Oct 1, 2025); services funded by the same fund.

- Implement coverage deadlines for continuous glucose monitors, electric personal-use breast pumps, lactation visits, and translation/interpretation services; several of these are to be funded from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund.

The bill amends the fund statute to explicitly permit use for CHIP postpartum coverage and lists fund mechanics (source, first use to offset losses, and investment earning treatment).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services must, by October 1, 2025, seek CMS approval via a CHIP state plan amendment to obtain federal matching funds to provide at least six months of postpartum coverage for mothers whose child is enrolled under the unborn child option. Covered services must match the comprehensive postpartum services provided under Medicaid; the department may pursue a 12‑month option. The Legislature intends to fund these services from the Medicaid Managed Care Excess Profit Fund (section 68‑996). No specific appropriation amounts are specified.
