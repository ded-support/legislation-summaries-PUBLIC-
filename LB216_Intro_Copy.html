Summary

LB216 centralizes court administrative functions under the Nebraska Supreme Court. Key agency effects and fiscal changes:

- Transfers all elected clerks of the district court and their employees to state employment under the Supreme Court effective January 7, 2027; salaries, benefits, and expenses to be paid by the State (funds appropriated to the Supreme Court).

- State Court Administrator receives court records and will negotiate agreements with the Department of Health and Human Services for child-support reporting; federal reimbursements for direct costs will be credited to Agency 5 – Supreme Court.

- Computer hardware/software and related communications for district and separate juvenile courts become state-paid (Supreme Court appropriation) beginning January 7, 2027; county-purchased property remains county-owned.

- Retirement/benefit transfers addressed: county retirement members transition with protections; counties and State share any additional retirement costs proportionately; transferred employees gain State Employees Retirement System eligibility where applicable.

- Counties must reimburse the State or employees for portions of accrued sick/vacation leave when transferred (specified percentages).

- Clerks are prohibited from serving on mental health boards; oaths filed with Secretary of State; various county bond and salary provisions adjusted.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds that beginning January 7, 2027, elected clerks of the district courts and their employees become state employees under the Nebraska Supreme Court. Counties must provide leave accrual data; the State of Nebraska — using funds appropriated to the Supreme Court — will directly pay all salaries, benefits, and expenses of transferred clerks/employees. County-owned furniture/equipment remains with counties; books/files transfer to the State Court Administrator. Retirement rules reference the Retirement System for Nebraska Counties and the State Employees Retirement System, with intent that accrued benefits not be lost and additional retirement costs shared pro rata between county and state. Leave-transfer rules set limits (sick up to 1,440 hrs, vacation up to 280 hrs) and require county reimbursement to the state or employees at specified percentages. Transferred staff may join the Nebraska State Insurance Program immediately. State will also pay certain district court IT and communication costs from Supreme Court appropriations. Federal reimbursements for child-support-related costs credit Agency 5 – Supreme Court via the State Treasurer.
