Summary

LB471 prohibits state agencies and other state entities from restricting the use, purchase, or sale of any motor vehicle (per §39-101) based on the energy source powering any vehicle component (including propulsion or accessory functions). It imposes the same prohibition on counties, cities, and villages. The bill also bars the Department of Environment and Energy from seeking (or causing to be sought) a waiver under the federal Clean Air Act that would allow Nebraska to adopt vehicle emissions standards more stringent than those required by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Prohibits any state agency or other state entity, and any county, city, or village, from restricting the use, purchase, or sale of motor vehicles based on the vehicle’s energy source (including propulsion or other powered functions). Directs the Department of Environment and Energy not to seek (or cause to be sought) any Clean Air Act waiver to adopt vehicle-emission standards more stringent than those required by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
