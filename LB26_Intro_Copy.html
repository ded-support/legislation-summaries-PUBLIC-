Summary

LB26 (Ballard) amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-929.01 to broaden the term "health care professional" for purposes of criminal statutes (§§28-929, 28-929.02, 28-930, 28-931, 28-931.01). The bill (1) retains "emergency care provider" as persons licensed/classified under the Emergency Medical Services Practice Act, (2) explicitly includes physicians and other licensed/certified/registered practitioners who practice at a hospital or health clinic, and (3) adds any other employee of a hospital or health clinic to that definition. It references statutory definitions for health clinic (§71-416) and hospital (§71-419) and repeals the original section. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
