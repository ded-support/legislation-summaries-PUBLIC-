Summary

References to the Department of Agriculture are in section 53-304. The bill directs that the Winery and Grape Producers Promotional Fund be located in the Department of Agriculture for administrative purposes. Revenue sources credited to the fund include winery charges ($20 per 160 gallons), certain shipping-license fees (manufacturer and retail direct-sales shipping license fees), excise taxes, and gifts/grants. The Department, at the direction of and in cooperation with the Nebraska Grape and Winery Board, shall use the fund to develop and maintain research, marketing, promotion, and related programs for grapes, fruits, berries, honey, and other agricultural products for the wine industry, including funding experts in viticulture and enology. Funds may be invested, carried over between fiscal years, and used only for the statute’s specified purposes (subject to transfers under section 2-5604).
