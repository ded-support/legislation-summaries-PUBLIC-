Summary

The bill establishes the Health Care Staffing Agency Registration Act under the Department of Labor. It defines "Commissioner" as the Commissioner of Labor and "Department" as the Department of Labor. Health care staffing agencies must register annually with the department and pay an annual registration fee of $1,500 per Nebraska location (or a $1,500 statewide fee for health care technology platforms without a physical Nebraska location), payable to the department. The commissioner will administer complaints, investigations, random audits, subpoenas, and enforcement (with appeal rights and representation by department counsel or the Attorney General). The department must maintain a public online database of registrants and may adopt rules. The act becomes operative January 1, 2026, and is added to duties in section 81-401.

Summary (Department of Labor focus):

- The bill assigns responsibility to the Nebraska Department of Labor (and the Commissioner of Labor) to administer an annual registration program for health care staffing agencies, including health care technology platforms.

- Agencies must certify they will not enforce pre‑existing noncompete clauses as a condition of registration.

- The department will collect an annual $1,500 registration fee per operating location (or one $1,500 statewide fee for platforms without a Nebraska physical location).

- The department must operate a public online registry (approval and expiration/revocation dates), a complaint reporting system, investigate complaints, conduct random audits (≥3 business days’ notice), and exercise subpoena and enforcement powers; legal representation may be provided by department attorneys or the Attorney General.

- The bill authorizes the department to adopt rules. No other appropriations or explicit budgetary allocations are specified (fee revenue implied).
