Summary

- The bill directs that beginning no later than January 1, 2027, $5,000,000 annually shall be used to obtain federal financial participation to pay a monthly per‑member fee of at least $75 to qualified primary care providers who meet criteria established by the Department of Health and Human Services to serve as primary care medical homes for target populations. Use of these funds is subject to required federal approvals.

- The Department of Health and Human Services is authorized to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations necessary to implement the Medicaid Access and Quality Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Medicaid Access and Quality Act" and tasks the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to implement it, adopt rules, and set provider criteria. The act directs rate enhancements for nonhospital physical‑health providers—emphasizing evaluation & management, labor-and-delivery, and rural services—and specifies intent that Medicaid practitioner fee‑schedule rates and related General Fund appropriations not be reduced below the July 1, 2024 levels. Funding: beginning no later than Jan 1, 2027, $5 million annually will be used to secure federal matching funds to pay a monthly per‑member fee (not less than $75) to qualified primary‑care medical homes; any remaining funds go to the medical assistance program and CHIP. All uses require applicable federal approvals.
