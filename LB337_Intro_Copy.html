Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and directs DMV operational and funding changes. Applicants claiming certain motorcycle/helmet exemptions must submit proof of course completion to DMV; DMV must modify its records system by January 1, 2024 to record the course completion date. The bill authorizes a $50 charge for initial/renewal special‑interest plates, with $25 credited to the DMV Cash Fund and $25 to the Highway Trust Fund; a postage/handling fee for mailing plates/registration is credited to the DMV Cash Fund. If plate manufacturing costs exceed the statutory charge, the excess is credited first to the Highway Trust Fund, then to the DMV Cash Fund. Separately, effective July 1, 2023, sales/use tax proceeds on aircraft are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds all‑terrain vehicles (ATVs) and utility‑type vehicles (UTVs) into the Motor Vehicle Registration Act: DMV must title, register and issue plates for ATVs/UTVs and treat them as "motor vehicles" when registered for highway use under section 60‑6,356. The bill defines UTVs by dimensions (≤74" wide, ≤180" long, four+ non‑highway tires) and retains exclusions (not golf cars/low‑speed vehicles). It allows operation on non‑municipal highways but expressly bars operation on Interstates, state highways, expressways and freeways and permits local ordinances to prohibit use. Operator protection rules (helmets/occupant‑protection or three‑point belts) are clarified. Several registration fee figures appear in the added text ($3, $25, $5) and are inconsistent; no explicit appropriations, budget transfers, or other funding changes for DMV or DOT are specified.
