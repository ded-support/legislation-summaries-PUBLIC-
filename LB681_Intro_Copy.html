Summary

LB681, introduced by the Legislature’s Education Committee, amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §85-931 to revise the statutory definition of "graduate degree programs." The bill lists covered first professional degrees, expands and itemizes master's degree professional and general academic/occupational program examples, and confirms doctoral degrees (e.g., Ph.D., Ed.D., D.A.). No state agencies or departments beyond the Legislature and its Education Committee are referenced, and the bill contains no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations. The original §85-931 is repealed.
