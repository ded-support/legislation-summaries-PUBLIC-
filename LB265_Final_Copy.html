Summary

The bill centralizes fiscal direction with the Department of Administrative Services (DAS): the budget administrator of DAS instructs the State Treasurer to execute multiple timed interfund transfers (notably $440,000,000 from the General Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund; $29,458,800 to the Nebraska Capital Construction Fund; $10,000,000 to the Critical Infrastructure Facilities Cash Fund; transfers into the Governor’s Emergency Cash Fund of $25,000,000 and $10,000,000; $28,000,000 from the JEDI Fund to the Cash Reserve Fund; and smaller transfers including $3,500,000 to the Health & Human Services Cash Fund and $11,320,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to HHS).

The Department of Labor (DOL) will administer a new Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot (grants up to $250,000) and consolidates several workforce-related accounts into a Workforce Development Program Cash Fund (effective July 1, 2025); unemployment tax receipts will flow there, uses are expanded, and the final average combined tax rate is reduced 5% for 2025–2029. The Department of Economic Development retains oversight of the Job Training Cash Fund and authorized transfers.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Department of Economic Development: establishes a Manufacturing Modernization and Workforce Development Pilot Investment Act grant program (applications 10/1/2025–11/1/2026). Grants match private financial support (up to $50,000 per award); projects begun before application are ineligible; grantees who cease to qualify must repay. The administering department may award up to $250,000 for the pilot from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund and adopt rules to implement the program.

- Department of Labor: expands authorized uses of the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund (including the manufacturing pilot, unemployment benefits, studies, admin costs). It codifies the Nebraska Workforce Development Board’s annual grant proposal process (up to 18 proposals; commissioner discretion to approve/modify/reject; up to 3 approvals annually without board sign‑off, with reporting if they exceed 25% of unobligated balance).

- Department of Administrative Services: the budget administrator may direct the State Treasurer to transfer $2,950,000 from the Workforce Development Program Cash Fund to the General Fund on or after 7/1/2025 but before 6/30/2026, and $5,000,000 from the Cash Reserve Fund to the General Fund on or after 7/1/2026 but before 6/30/2027.

- Department of Health and Human Services: no substantive changes or funding actions referenced.
