Summary

LB476 authorizes any person to notify the Department of Agriculture (or the Attorney General) of violations or potential violations of the Foreign‑owned Real Estate National Security Act. The Department of Agriculture is designated to investigate such violations; if the Director of Agriculture has reasonable suspicion a violation occurred, the Director must refer the matter to the Attorney General or to outside counsel retained by the Department for enforcement. The bill permits use of outside counsel retained by the Department. No funding, appropriation, or budget allocation changes are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions affect the Department of Agriculture. The added text clarifies that property interests are exempt from being voided, divested, or otherwise subject to the Foreign‑owned Real Estate National Security Act if they were acquired while the holder was authorized to work in the U.S. (per 8 C.F.R. 274a.12) or acquired while the holder was not a nonresident alien; that protection continues even if the holder later becomes a nonresident alien. The exemption does not apply to restricted entities or their agents/trustees. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Agriculture are included.
