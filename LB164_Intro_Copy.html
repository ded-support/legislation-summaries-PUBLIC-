Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and makes the department the administering agency for the Urban Development Incentive Act. The department must review applications in receipt order, award grants for affordable commercial-space projects (with a per-project maximum of $3.5 million) and administer annual category caps: up to $20M (20% of project costs), $10M (10%), $5M (down payment), $2.5M (job training), $2M (public engagement), $4M (sustainability), and $3M (technical assistance). The department must electronically report annually to the Governor and Legislature (grant totals, project types, jobs created) and may adopt rules and regulations. The Legislature intends to appropriate $46.5 million annually from the General Fund to the department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Defines "Department" as the Department of Economic Development and tasks it with administering the Urban Development Incentive Act: accepting applications on department forms, reviewing them in receipt order, awarding grants (with a $3.5 million per-project cap) across specified categories (up to 20% of project costs; financing assistance up to 10%; down‑payment aid; job training; community engagement; sustainability; technical assistance) and annual category caps: $20M, $10M, $5M, $2.5M, $2M, $4M, and $3M respectively. The department must electronically report annually to the Governor and Legislature (grant totals, project types, jobs created) and may adopt rules to implement the Act. The Legislature intends to appropriate $46,500,000 annually from the General Fund to the department to carry out the program.
