Summary

LB544 amends the Employment Security Law (section 48-601) to disqualify an individual from unemployment benefits for any week in which the individual (1) fails to respond to a job offer within two business days or (2) fails to appear for a scheduled job interview without notifying the prospective employer. The bill requires the department that administers the Employment Security Law to establish an online portal, an email address, and a telephone hotline for employers to report such failures and mandates that the department verify employer-submitted information before denying benefits. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
