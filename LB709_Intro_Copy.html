Summary

LB709 authorizes the Department of Revenue to adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the new Adoption Tax Credit Act. The bill amends section 77-2715.07 to add the Adoption Tax Credit Act to the list of refundable income tax credits. Separately, the statute continues to vest the Department of Revenue with recapture authority for the residence purchase credit (requiring recapture if residence is sold or ceases to be the primary residence within five years). No direct appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are included in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Adoption Tax Credit Act," effective for tax years beginning or deemed to begin on or after Jan. 1, 2026. It allows a Nebraska income tax credit for taxpayers eligible for the federal adoption expenses credit (26 U.S.C. §23). The Nebraska credit is refundable and equals 10% of the federal credit in the same taxable year. The Department of Revenue is authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement the Act. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are specified.
