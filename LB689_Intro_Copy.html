Summary

LB689 amends §§79‑930 and 79‑992 (School Employees Retirement Act and Class V Act). Key agency actions and changes:

- Nebraska Public Employees Retirement Systems (NPERS) director: may determine whether a returning member has satisfied a 180‑day separation requirement based on clear-and-convincing evidence received within 45 days; member may appeal to the retirement board. The board’s appeal decision is final and not judicially reviewable.

- Retirement board/administrator (Class V): analogous authority to issue 180‑day determinations and receive appeals; administrator decisions appealable to the board; board decisions final.

- Substantive benefit rules clarified: work as temporary, substitute, or volunteer generally does not count for contributions or service credit unless the member becomes a regular employee (then contributions and credit begin immediately, including prior temp/substitute work).

- Direct rollover designation for refunds transitions to designation by the retirement board beginning September 1, 2024.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
