Summary

LB102 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §43-513 to set the aid-to-dependent-children (ADC) "standard of need" at $1,132.50 for a single individual plus $393.00 for each additional ADC unit member, effective July 1, 2026. It requires annual adjustments each July 1 beginning July 1, 2026, based on the prior calendar year's Consumer Price Index as determined by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics. The standard of need continues to be used to calculate ADC benefit amounts (capped by §43-512). The bill repeals the original §43-513. No new appropriations or specific funding allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition and a dollar-based standard of need for aid to dependent children (ADC) eligibility and benefit calculation. Effective July 1, 2026, the standard of need is $1,132.50 for a single individual plus $393 for each additional household member, used in the initial income test under the "Budget the Gap" methodology (see §68‑1713(1)(p)) and to compute ADC benefit amounts. No specific government agency or department is named, nor does the text create an explicit appropriation or new funding line—it sets benefit-calculation parameters.
