Summary

LB572 amends the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act to authorize school districts to exceed their general fund budget authority to cover stipends paid to student teachers or interns completing supervised practicums (defined in §79-875). It also amends the School District Property Tax Limitation Act to exempt the portion of a district’s property tax request needed to pay those stipends. The State Board of Education retains authority to approve, deny, or modify budget-exclusion amounts; the bill references the Department (Nebraska Department of Education) in other distance-education provisions. No specific appropriations or dollar amounts are set in the bill.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a permitted expenditure allowing funds described in subsection (a) or (b) to be used to pay stipends to student teachers or interns (as defined in §79‑875) who are completing a supervised teaching practicum in Nebraska public or private schools. The change references subdivision (1)(o) of §79‑1028.01. No specific dollar amounts, new appropriations, or agency budget allocations are specified.
