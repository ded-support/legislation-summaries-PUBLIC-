Summary

Creates the Landlord and Tenant Radon Awareness Act requiring lessors to provide prospective tenants and tenants with radon information and a specified disclosure form. Defines "radon measurement specialist" and "radon mitigation specialist" as individuals licensed under the Radiation Control Act. "Lessor" expressly may include a government agency or subdivision. The State of Nebraska is referenced as strongly recommending radon testing and mitigation. Security deposits may not be withheld to pay for radon testing or mitigation, except where a tenant installed an uncertified mitigation system without the lessor's consent. Applies to leases entered on or after the act's effective date. No funding, appropriations, or new agency creation are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the "Landlord and Tenant Radon Awareness Act," defining terms (dwelling unit, lease, lessor, mitigation, radon, radon hazard defined as ≥4 pCi/L, radon measurement specialist, radon mitigation specialist, tenant). Requires lessors to provide a specified radon-disclosure form to prospective and current tenants (at application and upon request) and to provide radon test results within ten days of receipt. Notes radon measurement/mitigation specialists must be licensed under the Radiation Control Act. The lessor definition explicitly includes "government agency, or subdivision thereof," and the State of Nebraska is cited as recommending testing/mitigation. Prohibits withholding security deposits to pay for radon testing/mitigation except where a tenant improperly installed an unapproved mitigation system; requires itemized statements if withholding occurs. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
