Summary

LB135 standardizes election procedures for school-related ballot measures and joint public/entity bond questions by requiring many votes (school bond issues, levy increases, exceeding budget or levy limits, lease-purchases, teacherage taxes, joint-entity bonds) to be held "in compliance with subsection (3) of section 32-559." It clarifies roles of the Secretary of State, election commissioners and county clerks (ballot preparation, early voting, counting, and canvass), and authorizes election commissioners or county clerks to bill school districts directly for special-election costs. Joint entities and joint public agencies that include Nebraska school districts or ESUs must submit bond questions to district electors and give at least fifty days' notice. Levy-authority allocations must be reported to the Property Tax Administrator; county boards authorize approved excess levies. The Auditor of Public Accounts receives records when residents' meetings approve budget growth. No general fund appropriations are created; election cost allocation and timing/notice requirements are the primary fiscal effects.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural requirements for school-district elections and specifies roles for government officials: school boards must file ballot orders by set deadlines (March 1 for statewide primary; September 1 for statewide general; eighth Friday before a political‑subdivision general). Elections approving bonds or tax levies must comply with subsection (3) of §32‑559. Election commissioners or county clerks will: publish sample ballots; furnish early‑voting ballots at least 21 days before the election; count ballots with two disinterested persons; and coordinate registration books and early‑voting requests across counties. Notices to voters must be mailed first‑class at least 30 days prior; sample ballots published 7–15 days prior. Returns are turned over to the school board for canvass. Compensation: the two disinterested counting persons must be paid at no less than the minimum hourly rate in §48‑1203. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
