Summary

LB135 harmonizes election procedures for school-related ballot measures and joint entities involving schools. Key agency roles and changes:

- Requires school district bond, levy, budget-exceeding, lease/lease-purchase, teacherage, and related questions — and joint-entity bonds that include Nebraska school districts or educational service units — be submitted at elections held in compliance with §32‑559(3).

- Assigns administration duties to election commissioners or county clerks (conducting counting, early-vote distribution, canvass) and requires school boards to certify orders and voter lists to them.

- Permits election commissioners or county clerks to bill school districts directly for special-election costs under §32‑559; political subdivisions remain responsible for canceled-election costs.

- Specifies notice periods (e.g., 20–50 days) and majority-approval requirements within each affected school district or ESU.

- References Secretary of State, Auditor of Public Accounts, Property Tax Administrator, county boards, and other political subdivisions in procedural/reporting roles.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds detailed election procedures for school-district ballot questions and assigns specific roles to government officials: school boards must file/ certify by set deadlines (March 1, Sept 1, or the eighth Friday before a local general election); election commissioners or county clerks conduct elections, give first-class mail notice, publish sample ballots, furnish early-vote ballots 21 days prior, count ballots with two disinterested persons they appoint, and coordinate across counties (certifying registration books and forwarding early-vote requests). Receiving boards deliver ballots to the election commissioner/county clerk; returns go to the school board for canvass. The two appointed counters must be paid at least the minimum wage referenced in section 48-1203. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
