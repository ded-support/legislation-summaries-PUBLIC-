Summary

LB135 amends election procedures across statutes to require numerous school-related bond, levy, budget, and capital vote questions to be submitted in compliance with subsection (3) of §32-559. Affected entities/agencies include: school boards and school districts, educational service units, joint entities and joint public agencies (including those created under the Public Facilities Construction and Finance Act), election commissioners, county clerks, the Secretary of State, county boards, the Property Tax Administrator, and the Auditor of Public Accounts. The bill clarifies election notice and timing (e.g., 50-day notice for certain joint-entity bond votes), allows election commissioners or county clerks to prepare and conduct such elections, and explicitly permits election commissioners/county clerks to bill school districts directly for special-election costs under §32-559. No new appropriations or changes to general funding allocations are made. Several original sections are repealed, including an outright repeal of §10-703.01.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds detailed election procedures for school-district ballot questions (bonds, levies, levy-limit overrides) and assigns roles to: school boards; election commissioners or county clerks; receiving/counting boards; and "two disinterested persons" appointed by the election commissioner/county clerk. Key agency duties: file/certify ballot orders by set deadlines (Mar 1, Sept 1, or eighth Friday prior); give notice by first-class mail and publish a sample ballot; furnish early-voting ballots 21 days prior; count ballots by the election commissioner or county clerk with two appointed persons; certify cross-county registration books and forward early-vote requests when a district spans counties; school board to certify out‑of‑county registered voters five days before the election; canvass returns after counting. Compensation: the two appointed counting persons must be paid no less than the minimum wage set in section 48-1203. References: §§32-559, 32-1203, 48-1203.
