Summary

LB135 harmonizes and centralizes election procedures for school-related bond, levy, and budget questions by requiring many such votes to be held in compliance with section 32-559. It directs school boards to file orders with the election commissioner or county clerk (in the county with the most electors for the district) who will conduct the election and handle ballots/early voting and canvass. Joint entities that include Nebraska school districts or ESUs must submit bond questions to district electors under 32-559 (with at least 50 days' notice). The bill assigns financial responsibilities: political subdivisions bear costs of special elections (including costs of canceled elections), and election commissioners/county clerks may bill school districts directly for special elections held under 32-559. It references the Secretary of State, Auditor of Public Accounts, Property Tax Administrator, county boards, and repeals several original statute sections (including outright repeal of 10-703.01).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedural requirements for school-district elections and assigns duties to election officials. Key agencies/departments referenced: school boards, election commissioners, county clerks, receiving/counting boards, and school districts. New provisions require school bond/tax questions to comply with subsection (3) of §32-559; set filing deadlines for placing questions on ballots (March 1, Sept. 1, or eighth Friday before a local general election); require first-class mail notice 30 days out and a sample ballot published 7–15 days prior. Election commissioners/county clerks must prepare early-vote ballots ≥21 days before the election, count ballots with two disinterested appointees, coordinate registration books and early-vote requests across counties, and deliver returns to the school board for canvass. The two disinterested counters must be paid at least the minimum hourly rate under §48-1203. No other new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.
