Summary

Creates the "Faithful Delegate to Federal Article V Convention Act," establishing how Nebraska's legislative delegates and alternates to a U.S. Article V convention are chosen, instructed, and removed. Key state bodies and roles:

- Legislature: adopts delegate instructions, elects delegates/alternates (based on Committee on Committees' nominations), determines unauthorized votes, fills vacancies.

- Committee on Committees: nominates one delegate and one alternate per legislative caucus.

- Article V Convention Committee: composed of elected delegates/alternates; elects chair and vice-chair.

- Executive Board of the Legislative Council + Lieutenant Governor: serve as the advisory committee when the Legislature is not in session (chair is executive board chair; Lt. Gov. breaks ties).

- Secretary of State: receives delegates' signed oaths; Speaker must notify the committee chair and convention presiding officer of disqualifications.

References existing expense reimbursement statutes (secs. 81-1174 to 81-1177). Delegates receive no compensation; no appropriations or new funding are included. Prohibits accepting gifts from registered lobbyists.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Creates the "Faithful Delegate to Federal Article V Convention Act" and delegates responsibility to state legislative bodies and officers. Key agencies/departments and roles:

- Legislature: adopts instructions, elects delegates/alternates, determines unauthorized votes, fills vacancies.

- Committee on Committees (Legislature): nominates one delegate and one alternate per legislative caucus.

- Executive Board of the Legislative Council + Lieutenant Governor: serve as the advisory committee (chair = executive board chair); Lieutenant Governor breaks ties; advisory committee acts only when Legislature is not in session and requires six votes to find an unauthorized vote.

- Secretary of State: receives executed oaths.

- Speaker of the Legislature: must notify Article V Convention Committee chair and the convention presiding officer of disqualifications.

Also references lobbyist registration (Nebraska Political Accountability and Disclosure Act) and federal offices (Secretary of the U.S. Senate, Clerk of the U.S. House). No new appropriations or funding increases; delegates are unpaid but may be reimbursed under existing statutes (secs. 81-1174 to 81-1177).
