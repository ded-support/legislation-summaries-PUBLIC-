Summary

LB691 requires each public school board and the governing board of any approved or accredited private, denominational, or parochial school that receives state funds to display the Ten Commandments: in every elementary classroom and in each middle‑ or high‑school building. Boards determine the display’s appearance subject to minimum specifications (text, at least 11"×14", specified wording). The State Board of Education is authorized to adopt rules and regulations to implement the law. The bill does not appropriate funds or require schools to expend budgeted dollars for displays; schools may accept contributions or donated displays to cover costs.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Requires each public school district school board—and governing boards of approved/accredited private, denominational, or parochial schools that receive state funds—to display the Ten Commandments: in every elementary classroom and in each middle/high school building. Boards set display style but must include the full text and a poster/framed size of at least 11" x 14". Schools are not required to use their funds to purchase displays; boards may accept donations or contributions to cover costs. The State Board of Education may adopt rules to implement the requirement. No new appropriations or budget allocations are provided.
