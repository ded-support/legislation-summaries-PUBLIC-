Summary

LB461 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §16-1027 governing municipal firefighter retirements. Key institutional provisions:

- Retirement committee: establishes optional annuity benefit forms to be provided under the funding medium for the retirement system.

- Retirement system: may purchase annuity contracts for retirees and may distribute paid-up contracts; payment of lump sums or distribution of contracts terminates system obligations.

- Cities and city councils: may adopt lump-sum distribution options for pre-1997 retirees; if annuity cost comparisons differ by >5%, the retirement committee reviews and the city council, after a hearing, sets the lump-sum amount.

- Funding: if a firefighter’s retirement value is insufficient to provide a statutorily required minimum pension, the city must use funds from the retirement system’s unallocated employer account to make up the shortfall.

The bill repeals the original section.
