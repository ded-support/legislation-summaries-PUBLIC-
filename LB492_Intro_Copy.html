Summary

LB492 explicitly references the Department of Health and Human Services in section 43-247(7), confirming juvenile court jurisdiction over any juvenile voluntarily relinquished pursuant to section 43-106.01 to the Department of Health and Human Services or to a child-placement agency licensed by the Department. The bill makes no express changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Health and Human Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds/clarifies that a "habitually truant" child (age 13–16) accrues 20+ unexcused absences with documented loss of educational attainment. Creates a defense to adjudication if, after subtracting excludable absences, the child has fewer than 20 unexcused days. New exclusions: absences caused by lack of adequate transportation; school failure to coordinate agreed interventions/attendance supports; physical or mental illness excused by a parent/guardian (with or without a doctor’s note); or other excusal by the school or court. Referrals must identify which unexcused absences, if any, were excused by a parent/guardian for physical or mental illness. No Department of Health and Human Services funding, appropriation, or budget allocations are included.
