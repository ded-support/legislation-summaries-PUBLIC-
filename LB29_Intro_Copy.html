Summary

LB29 amends the Administrative Procedure Act to require state agencies to review all existing and pending rules beginning January 1, 2026 and every three years thereafter. Each agency head must designate an oversight individual. Agencies must submit an electronic report to the Clerk of the Legislature by June 30 in review years addressing: public health/safety/welfare necessity; cost–benefit; effectiveness measurement; consideration of less restrictive alternatives; and whether the rule stems from state statute, federal mandate, or court order. The Reference Committee will refer reports to appropriate legislative standing committees, which must report to the Clerk by December 15 with recommended legislative or clarifying actions. Agency rulemaking is suspended during the review except for rules affecting health/safety/welfare, time-sensitive rules, or those subject to statutory deadlines. Section 84-920 is amended to incorporate this new requirement. No funding or appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Beginning Jan 1, 2026, and every three years thereafter, each state agency must: conduct a review of all existing and pending rules; have the agency head designate an individual to oversee the review; and file an electronic detailed report and supporting documentation with the Clerk of the Legislature by June 30 of the review year. Reports must address whether rules are essential to public health/safety/welfare, cost‑benefit, measures of effectiveness, consideration of less‑restrictive alternatives, and whether rules stem from state statute, a federal mandate, or a court decision. The Legislature’s Reference Committee will route reports to appropriate standing committees, which must return committee reports with any legislative recommendations to the Clerk by Dec 15. Rulemaking under the Administrative Procedure Act is suspended during the review, except for rules affecting health/safety, time‑sensitive rules, or those subject to statutory deadlines. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
