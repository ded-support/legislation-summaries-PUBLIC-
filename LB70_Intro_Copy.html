Summary

The bill assigns multiple explicit duties to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). DHHS (or a licensed child‑placement agency) must complete required preplacement or postplacement adoptive home studies, perform central‑registry checks under section 28‑718, and may set or waive the adoptive‑home‑study fee or rate. DHHS must provide the medical‑history form used with adoption reports, be notified if medical histories are not filed, and receive originals of medical histories after decree entry. For children committed to DHHS, prospective adoptive parents must be given the opportunity to read the department's case file; DHHS may redact legally protected material and must file a verification with the court. The bill contains no explicit appropriations or budgetary allocations for DHHS.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds rules allowing a "second adult" to adopt a minor or adult when a sole legal parent consents, requires parent-child relationship and an adoptive home study per section 43-107, and defines "sole legal parent." Creates a six‑month exception: if the sole parent adopted the child less than six months earlier and the second adult was included in that home study, a new study is not required unless the court orders one; otherwise a separate home study is required. Requires spouses to join petitions, bars creating more than two legal parents, and confirms parental support liability. No provisions change Department of Health and Human Services’ funding, appropriations, or budget allocations; adoptive home‑study processes are referenced but no DHHS budget actions are specified.
