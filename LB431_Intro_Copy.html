Summary

LB431 amends the Uniform Controlled Substances Act (sections 28‑401 and 28‑405) to explicitly define "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and to reference the Secretary of Health and Human Services in an exclusion for anabolic steroids. Steroids approved by the Secretary for nonhuman implant use (e.g., cattle) are excluded from the anabolic steroid definition, while prescribing or dispensing those substances for human use remains regulated as anabolic steroids. No appropriations, funding, or budget changes for the Department are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a statutory definition of "kratom" as products or ingredients that: (a) contain any part of the mitragyna speciosa leaf when the plant contains the alkaloids mitragynine or 7‑hydroxymitragynine, or (b) are synthetic materials containing those alkaloids. The bill also inserts "Kratom" into the enumerated list at subsection (46)(f); a subsequent fragmentary insertion "(g) substances their substance" appears unclear/errant. There are no provisions in the quoted text assigning duties, regulatory authority, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations to the Department of Health and Human Services.
