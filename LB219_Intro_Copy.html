Summary

LB219 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §28-105. The provision specifying that "all sentences for maximum terms of imprisonment for one year or more for felonies shall be served in institutions under the jurisdiction of the Department of Correctional Services" remains in the statute. The bill contains no language changing funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Correctional Services.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds language specifying that the statute’s minimum penalties are “none for imprisonment and none for post‑release supervision.” There are no provisions affecting the Department of Correctional Services’ funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
