Summary

The bill assigns multiple administrative, regulatory, and fiscal duties to named agencies. Department of Administrative Services (DAS) must process certification of unpaid obligations, run contract transparency and procurement-preference programs, review landfill disposal‑fee rebate applications, schedule budget transfers (notably $1,000,000 from the Water Resources Cash Fund to the Nitrogen Reduction Incentive Cash Fund), issue warrants, and may direct a $200,000 transfer from the Clean‑burning Motor Fuel Development Fund to the General Fund. Department of Agriculture houses the Riparian Vegetation Management Task Force, gains expanded pesticide rulemaking/enforcement powers (inspections, fines up to $5,000, licensing), participates in species/habitat notice and livestock permitting frameworks, and sits on the Environmental Trust Board. Department of Health and Human Services’ CEO is added to multiple boards/committees, may present evidence at groundwater hearings, and receives specified forfeited sureties and deposits to the HHS Cash Fund for radiation‑site closure (not operating costs). Department of Revenue receives the Litter Fee Collection Fund and expanded ethanol program duties. Transportation, Economic Development, Veterans' Affairs, Military Department, Banking and Finance, Insurance, Labor, and Correctional Services are enumerated stakeholders or receive statutory listings; limited personnel exemptions and session‑law distribution changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective July 1, 2025, the Department of Natural Resources is merged into the Department of Environment and Energy, which is renamed the Department of Water, Energy, and Environment; a Chief Water Officer position is created and employees/positions transfer to the new department with continuity of service and bargaining rights. All appropriations and salary limits enacted for Agency No. 29 (DNR) are voided and reappropriated to Agency No. 84 (Department of Water, Energy, and Environment). Unpaid DNR obligations as of June 30, 2025 may be certified as valid encumbrances to the accounting division of the Department of Administrative Services for payment. No substantive changes, funding, or allocations are made for the Departments of Agriculture, Banking and Finance, Economic Development, Insurance, Labor, Correctional Services, Health and Human Services, Motor Vehicles, Revenue, Transportation, Veterans’ Affairs, or the Military Department.
