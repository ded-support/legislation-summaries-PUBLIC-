Summary

LB248 amends section 71-1910 of the Child Care Licensing Act to explicitly define "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services. The bill repeals the original section and contains no provisions affecting funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds to the bill’s covered settings: programs located on military bases or other federal property, and facilities licensed as family child care providers by a branch of the U.S. Department of Defense or the U.S. Coast Guard. For the Department of Health and Human Services, this expands the scope of locations to which the bill’s requirements or benefits apply. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
