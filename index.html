LB135_IntroLB135_Intro_Copy.docxLB135 standardizes election procedures for school-related ballot measures and joint public/entity bond questions by requiring many votes (school bond issues, levy increases, exceeding budget or levy limits, lease-purchases, teacherage taxes, joint-entity bonds) to be held "in compliance with subsection (3) of section 32-559." Levy-authority allocations must be reported to the Property Tax Administrator; county boards authorize approved excess levies. Elections approving bonds or tax levies must comply with subsection (3) of §32‑559. Election commissioners or county clerks will: publish sample ballots; furnish early‑voting ballots at least 21 days before the election; count ballots with two disinterested persons; and coordinate registration books and early‑voting requests across counties. Compensation: the two disinterested counting persons must be paid at no less than the minimum hourly rate in §48‑1203. No new appropriations or budget allocations are specified.

It conditions year‑round daylight saving time by setting state standard time to UTC‑5 (central) and UTC‑6 (mountain) once authorized by federal law or order and after three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round standard; current daylight‑saving provisions (second Sunday in March to first Sunday in November) become null when that provision becomes operative. It defines Coordinated Universal Time as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and “interpreted or modified for the United States” by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The provision becomes operative on the first Sunday in November after (a) it is authorized or no longer conflicts with U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation and (b) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time.
