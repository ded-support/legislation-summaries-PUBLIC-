Department of Revenue duties: prescribe electronic recordkeeping, require sellers to provide records on request, furnish monthly tax return forms, require filing/remittance by the 20th of the following month (electronic filing/EFT may be mandated), collect the tax and remit receipts monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.
