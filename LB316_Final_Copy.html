Summary

LB316 references the following state agencies:

- Department of Health and Human Services: the bill amends the Uniform Controlled Substances Act to define "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services.

- Department of Revenue: establishes a 10% excise tax on retail sales of cannabidiol products effective January 1, 2026. Retail sellers must maintain electronic sales records as prescribed by the Department of Revenue, file monthly returns by the 20th, remit tax due, and may be required to file and pay electronically. The Department of Revenue will collect the tax and remit proceeds at least monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.

No direct appropriations, budget increases, or other funding changes for the named departments are included. The bill does not explicitly refer to a Department of Agriculture.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

- Defines "cannabidiol product" (CBD from hemp, within THC limits, no chemically synthesized cannabinoids).

- Establishes a 10% excise tax on retail sales of cannabidiol products beginning January 1, 2026; tax is in addition to all other state or local occupation/privilege taxes.

- Department of Revenue (DOR) responsibilities: prescribe electronic sales recordkeeping; require sellers to maintain and provide records on request; require monthly returns and remittances by the 20th of the following month on department forms (may mandate electronic filing and EFT); collect the excise tax and remit receipts at least monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund.

- Department of Agriculture: bill references federally compliant hemp and the Nebraska Hemp Farming Act but does not impose new duties or funding in the added text.

- Department of Health and Human Services: no new duties, funding, or appropriations in the added text.
