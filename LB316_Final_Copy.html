Summary

- Department of Revenue: LB316 imposes a 10% excise tax on retail sales of cannabidiol products effective January 1, 2026. The Department of Revenue is directed to prescribe electronic sales recordkeeping, require monthly returns and remittances (due the 20th day of the following month), may mandate electronic filing and EFT payments, collect the tax, and remit receipts at least monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. Retail sellers must provide sales records to the department on request.

- Department of Health and Human Services: the bill amends the Uniform Controlled Substances Act to define "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services. No departmental appropriations or other funding changes for DHHS or a state Department of Agriculture are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Effective Jan 1, 2026: a 10% excise tax on retail sales of cannabidiol products is added. The tax is supplemental to other state/local taxes. The Department of Revenue is assigned collection and enforcement duties: prescribe electronic recordkeeping, require sellers to file monthly returns by the 20th and remit tax (forms, electronic filing and EFTs may be required), and remit proceeds monthly to the State Treasurer for credit to the Property Tax Credit Cash Fund. The bill adds/clarifies definitions for "cannabidiol product" and references federally compliant hemp and the Nebraska Hemp Farming Act. No specific appropriations, new funding, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue, Department of Agriculture, or Department of Health and Human Services are included in the added text. The Department of Agriculture is referenced only via hemp definitions; DHHS is not addressed.
