Summary

The bill directs the Nebraska Crime Victim Fund to transfer amounts (as determined by the Department of Correctional Services and certified to the Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice) to the Department of Correctional Services Facility Cash Fund and the Supreme Court Automation Cash Fund to pay initial implementation costs of Laws 2010, LB510. After those costs are fully reimbursed, the Crime Victim Fund terminates and remaining receipts are distributed 75% to the Victim’s Compensation Fund and 25% to the Reentry Cash Fund. The Department of Health and Human Services may accept juveniles into temporary care/custody when secure detention is unnecessary, and when a juvenile is committed to DHHS the department must pay costs for the juvenile’s support, study, or treatment not otherwise paid by the parent. No specific appropriations are specified.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds blanket fee exemptions for any person defined in section 1: no court, probation, testing, program, juvenile diversion, filing, or local participation fees or costs may be charged or imposed; counties must pay waived fees “in the same manner as other claims.” For juveniles, diversion programs cannot condition participation on fines/fees, nonpayment cannot block sealing, and courts may inquire about health insurance and order parents to continue paying deductibles.

Impact: Department of Correctional Services — fee prohibitions remove the ability to require or collect probation/program-related fees from covered individuals, potentially reducing agency/local revenue. Department of Health and Human Services — limited operational effect other than courts’ ability to seek/continue private insurance payments (deductibles); no direct programmatic or operational funding changes. No explicit appropriations or new budget allocations are included; potential cost shift to counties.
