Summary

The bill defines "Department" as the Department of Health and Human Services and assigns the department these duties: establish the Foster Child Scholarship Program (HOPE Scholarships) beginning in 2025-26; annually notify educational decisionmakers about the program; adopt and promulgate rules and regulations to implement the Act; and electronically submit annual reports (first due December 1, 2026) to the Governor and Legislature detailing number of recipients, total scholarship amounts, and specified demographic data. The Legislature intends to appropriate $200,000 from the General Fund to the department for each of fiscal years 2025-26 and 2026-27 to carry out the Act.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The Department of Health and Human Services (department) must establish the Foster Child Scholarship Program to award HOPE Scholarships beginning 2025–26 for foster children and their biological siblings to attend qualifying private, nonprofit Nebraska schools. The department may adopt rules, must annually notify educational decisionmakers about the program, and shall not gain governance authority over participating schools. Reporting: by Dec. 1, 2026 and annually thereafter the department must electronically report to the Governor and Legislature the number of recipients, total scholarship dollars awarded, and recipient demographics (income, grade, race/ethnicity, geography). The Legislature intends to appropriate $200,000 from the General Fund to the department for each of FY2025‑26 and FY2026‑27.
