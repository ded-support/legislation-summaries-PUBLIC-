Summary

LB502 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §48-1714 (Farm Labor Contractors Act) to clarify criminal penalties: violations of §48-1711 or §48-1712 are Class II misdemeanors; intentional defacement, use, permitting use, or acting without a farm labor contractor license is a Class II misdemeanor; and a seed corn producer or farm labor contractor who violates §48-1715 is guilty of a Class IV misdemeanor. The bill repeals the original section. No state agency or department is named in the bill text, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
