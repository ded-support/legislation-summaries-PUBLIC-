Summary

LB653 references the Department of Administrative Services in two operational funding provisions. It requires the State Treasurer to transfer $1.0 billion (FY2023‑24) and $250 million (FY2024‑25) from the General Fund to the Education Future Fund on dates and in amounts directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services, with legislative intent for $250 million annually beginning FY2025‑26. For monthly special education reimbursements, the State Treasurer must notify the Director of Administrative Services of available General Fund and Education Future Fund balances, and the Director shall draw warrants against those appropriated funds to effect payments.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Administrative Services focus):

- The added provisions do not reference or change any roles, appropriations, or budget authority for the Department of Administrative Services (DAS). No DAS funding or allocations are affected.

- The bill does create a funding mechanism administered by the State Department of Education: districts may apply (by Oct. 15) to the Education Future Fund for students with IEPs whose expected costs are at least five times the prior year's adjusted average per‑pupil cost. The State Dept. of Education must pay qualifying districts by Nov. 15 an amount equal to the expected cost minus the prior-year adjusted average per pupil; payments are prorated if requests exceed the fund balance. The stated purpose is to fund education/support services so districts can accept more option students. Other non‑fiscal changes address application notices, sibling automatic acceptance, and denial limits for students with IEPs.
