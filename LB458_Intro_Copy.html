Summary

LB458 (amending section 77-1804) requires county treasurers to forward an electronic copy of the list of real property subject to tax sale to the Property Tax Administrator. The Property Tax Administrator must compile a statewide list and publish the compiled list on the Department of Revenue’s website. The bill contains no explicit appropriations, funding increases, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

No provisions change Department of Revenue appropriations or create new funding/allocations for the Department.

Substantive additions affecting tax/tax-deed processes: establishes an administrative $150 charge to the property owner upon issuance of a certificate; directs periodic review (every five years beginning 2030) of an existing $20 fee; clarifies timelines and procedures for tax-deed applications by purchasers and land banks (including a 9‑month application window after specified post‑sale periods and a 2‑year vacancy determination for certain cases); enumerates objective criteria for classifying property as vacant and abandoned and requires an affidavit to the county treasurer. Also adds housing/zoning changes (duplexes, accessory dwelling units) but no Department of Revenue budget or appropriation changes.
