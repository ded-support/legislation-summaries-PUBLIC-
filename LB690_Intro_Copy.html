Summary

The bill designates the "Department" in the Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act as the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) and assigns DMV duties to prescribe the LPE‑learner's permit written exam, collect school and LPE‑learner permit fees/surcharges (by department staff or county treasurers), approve motorcycle safety‑course proof forms, and update DMV systems to record course‑completion dates on driving records. Fiscal provisions: a $50 special‑interest license plate fee is split $25 to the Department of Motor Vehicles Cash Fund and $25 to the Highway Trust Fund; DMV may charge a postage/handling fee for mailing plates, remitted to the DMV Cash Fund; if plate manufacturing costs exceed the statutory charge, the excess is credited first to the Highway Trust Fund (to cover the difference) and any remainder to the DMV Cash Fund. Proceeds of sales/use tax on aircraft sales/leases (on/after July 1, 2023) are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. The act becomes operative January 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary for agencies:

- Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV): Adds ATVs and utility‑type vehicles (UTVs) into the Motor Vehicle Registration Act when registered. Requires certificates of title, issues license plates, and sets registration fees at $10 for both ATVs and UTVs. Incorporates the phrase "manufactured for off‑road use" into title/registration language.

- Department of Transportation (DOT): Clarifies where registered ATVs/UTVs may be operated—permits operation on county and township roads outside municipal limits (per §60‑6,356) but prohibits use on the Interstate System, state highways, expressways, and freeways; adopts vehicle definitions that affect applicability to DOT‑managed roadways.
