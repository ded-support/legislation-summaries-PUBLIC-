Summary

The bill authorizes a person to appeal to the Department of Revenue claiming their single‑family residential property was offered for sale at fair market rate for at least 90 days with no good‑faith offers. If the Department of Revenue sustains the appeal, the person may claim Nebraska income tax deductions for interest, taxes, or maintenance related to that property. The bill contains no provisions altering funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an appeal process to the Department of Revenue: a property owner may appeal to DOR asserting their single‑family residential property was offered for sale at fair market value for at least 90 days with no good‑faith offers. If DOR sustains the appeal, the owner may claim Nebraska income tax deductions for interest, property taxes, and maintenance expenses related to that property. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Revenue are specified.
