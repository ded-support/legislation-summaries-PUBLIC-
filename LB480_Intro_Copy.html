Summary

LB480 amends the Water Recreation Enhancement Act. It broadens legislative findings and authorized projects for Lake McConaughy, the Lewis and Clark State Recreation Area, and Niobrara State Park to include marina construction and other projects that expand water access and recreational opportunities. The bill vests the Nebraska Game and Parks Commission with authority to administer these projects, including entering contracts (construction, management, legal, audit, consulting) and public‑private partnerships, and directs the commission to engage local stakeholders. It encourages political subdivisions holding Federal Energy Regulatory Commission licenses to enter into land use/lease/purchase arrangements (with covenants/easements to ensure FERC compliance). It also bars Game and Parks commissioners and employees (and their immediate family members) from having financial interests in related real‑estate transactions or contracts. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added language authorizes use of projects to increase recreational opportunities and enjoyment of Niobrara State Park and to fund infrastructure improving access to recreational areas and water access.
