Summary

LB152 amends the Property Tax Credit Act (77-4212) to tie future minimum relief amounts to changes in statewide assessed value as determined by the Department of Revenue. For tax year 2029 the minimum relief equals the prior year minimum plus the percentage increase in total assessed value of all real property (as determined by the Department of Revenue) plus an additional $75 million. For tax year 2030 and thereafter the minimum equals the prior year minimum plus the percentage increase in total assessed value as determined by the Department of Revenue.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Summary (Department of Revenue focus):

The added language declares legislative intent to expand the homestead exemption and, effective January 1, 2026, exempts the first $100,000 of actual value of every homestead from property taxation. Existing exemptions in sections 77‑3506, 77‑3507, and 77‑3508 remain available in addition to this new exemption. For the Department of Revenue, this requires implementing the new $100,000 exemption in assessment and exemption processes, issuing guidance, and adjusting administration and revenue forecasting. No explicit changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included in the added text.
