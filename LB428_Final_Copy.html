Summary

LB428 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-532 to impose procedural and parental-rights requirements on school districts and school personnel regarding student surveys. For surveys asking about drug, vape, alcohol, or tobacco use, districts must notify parents/guardians/educational decisionmakers at least 15 days prior via the school's electronic notification system or mail and describe question types, purpose/age-appropriateness, data use, access, privacy protections, and result disclosures. Parents may request a copy, review the survey in person, or exempt their child. Schools may not disclose students' personally identifiable information except as required by law, and surveys requesting sexual information are prohibited for grades K–6. The bill cites consistency with federal statutes/regulations (FERPA, PPRA, COPPA, and the Children’s Internet Protection Act) and repeals the original §79-532. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Adds parental-notice, review, opt-out, and privacy rules for school surveys about drug, vape, alcohol, or tobacco use. School districts must notify parents/guardians/educational decisionmakers at least 15 days before survey administration via the school's electronic system or mail, describe survey content/purpose/privacy/access/disclosure, allow parents to request a copy, review it in person, or exempt their child, and bar disclosure of students' personally identifiable information except as required by law.
