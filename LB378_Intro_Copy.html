Summary

LB378 requires every public or private two- or four-year college or university in Nebraska to file quarterly reports with the Attorney General and electronically to the Legislature detailing "reportable funding" (> $50) received from defined foreign sources. Reports must include amount, purpose, whether ongoing, foreign source identity and address, and copies of related contracts/agreements. Tuition payments by a natural person foreign payer on behalf of an individual are exempt. The Attorney General will prescribe the report format, post reports on the AG website, and enforce the law. Enforcement includes civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation and forfeiture of any reportable funding not disclosed. No appropriations or budget changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a quarterly reporting requirement for Nebraska public and private two‑ and four‑year colleges and universities to disclose to the Attorney General (and electronically to the Legislature) all “reportable funding” from defined foreign sources (broadly: foreign governments, foreign legal entities, non‑U.S. individuals, and their agents) over $50. Reports must include amounts, purpose, donor identity, contracts/MOUs and whether funding is ongoing. The Attorney General must prescribe report format, post reports on the AG website, and enforce the provision, with civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation and forfeiture of unreported funds. Payments by a foreign natural person for an individual’s tuition are exempt. No appropriations or budget allocations for agencies are specified.
