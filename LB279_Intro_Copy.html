Summary

LB279 amends the Motor Vehicle Registration Act to require only one license plate to be displayed on registered motor vehicles and trailers (generally on the rear), clarifies that when two plates are issued only one must be prominently displayed, and retains front-placement for certain apportionable vehicles and truck‑tractors. The bill references the department (responsible for issuing plates and designing/procuring decals), the director (to secure competitive bids under the State Procurement Act when plates change), county treasurers (to furnish decals), the State Treasurer, and the Highway Trust Fund. Existing law’s optional single‑plate authorization for certain passenger cars — including the $50 annual nonrefundable fee plus decal cost remitted to the State Treasurer for credit to the Highway Trust Fund — is preserved. No new appropriations or other budget reallocations are created.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Change clarifies that only one license plate is required (replacing language requiring "at least one"). No government agencies or departments are referenced and there are no funding, appropriation, or budgetary changes in the added text.
