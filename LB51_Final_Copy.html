Summary

LB51 ratifies and adopts the National Crime Prevention and Privacy Compact (34 U.S.C. 40316) to enable authorized interstate exchange of criminal history record information for noncriminal-justice purposes. The bill designates the Superintendent of Law Enforcement and Public Safety to execute the compact for Nebraska and names the superintendent (or a designee) as the state's compact officer responsible for administering the compact. The Nebraska State Patrol is designated the state repository for criminal history record information, charged with carrying out compact duties and authorized to adopt rules, regulations, and procedures for cooperative exchange with federal authorities. The compact remains effective until specifically renounced by statute and does not alter existing Nebraska obligations under state law (including the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act). No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds state approval and ratification of the National Crime Prevention and Privacy Compact (34 U.S.C. 40316). The Superintendent of Law Enforcement and Public Safety must execute the compact and (or the superintendent’s designee) serves as Nebraska’s compact officer to administer it. The Nebraska State Patrol is designated as the state repository for criminal history record information, authorized to take necessary actions, adopt rules and procedures, and coordinate criminal-history exchanges with the federal government for noncriminal-justice purposes. Ratification remains effective until explicitly renounced by statute. The provision preserves existing Nebraska State Patrol duties under state law (including the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act) and does not change public access rules. No funding or appropriation changes are included.
