Summary

LB133 amends statutes governing animal seizure and disposition to reference the Department of Agriculture in two places. It clarifies that a special investigator appointed as a deputy state sheriff may act as a law enforcement officer while operating under the authority of the Director of Agriculture pursuant to the Commercial Dog and Cat Operator Inspection Act. It also requires that any humane society or comparable institution considered as an adoption alternative must be licensed by the Department of Agriculture, have passed the Act’s inspection requirements, and have paid the inspection fee. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
