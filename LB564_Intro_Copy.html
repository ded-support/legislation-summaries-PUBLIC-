Summary

LB564 directs the State Treasurer to transfer $750 million from the General Fund to the School District Property Tax Relief Credit Fund in fiscal year 2024–25 “on such dates and in such amounts as directed by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services.” The bill also states the Legislature’s intent that transfers in fiscal year 2025–26 and each year thereafter equal the prior year’s transfer increased by $150 million.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The amendment changes the timing of a $150,000,000 appropriation for the Department of Administrative Services from a two‑year 2025–26 allocation to a single fiscal year 2025 allocation. Total funding is unchanged at $150 million; the revision only shifts when the department may receive or obligate the funds.
