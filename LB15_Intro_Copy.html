Summary

LB15 states the Legislature's intent to provide additional General Fund appropriations to the Nebraska Supreme Court. Specifically, it calls for an extra $600,000 in FY2025‑26 and an extra $600,000 in FY2026‑27 to the Supreme Court (Agency 5), Court Administration, Program No. 34. These funds are restricted for increased payments for interpreter services for deaf and hard of hearing persons and for persons unable to communicate in English. The bill includes an emergency clause making it effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Legislature intends to appropriate an additional $600,000 General Fund for FY2025‑26 and an additional $600,000 General Fund for FY2026‑27 to the Supreme Court (Agency 5), Court Administration (Program No. 34). The funds are designated to increase payments for interpreter services for deaf and hard of hearing persons and for individuals unable to communicate in English, and must be used solely for that purpose.
