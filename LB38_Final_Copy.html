Summary

This bill restructures administration of the Geologists Regulation Act around the Board of Geologists (seven gubernatorial appointees) and clarifies agency roles and procedures. Key agency actions and changes:

- Board of Geologists: establishes membership, five-year terms, qualifications for professional‑geologist members, authority to adopt rules, a code of practice, issue licenses, enroll geologist‑interns, grant certificates of authorization to organizations, issue temporary permits, set and publish examinations (including Nebraska‑specific exam), maintain a roster, designate emeritus members, hold disciplinary hearings, employ counsel, and notify the Secretary of State of revocations/suspensions/probations. Board may charge fees and set exam fees to recover costs.

- Governor: appoints board members; may remove for cause.

- Secretary of State: prohibited from issuing corporate certificates of authority, registering business names or trade marks that use “geology” unless the board has issued an organizational certificate of authorization or eligibility letter.

- Attorney General: serves as legal advisor; board may also retain counsel.

Fiscal provisions: fee caps established (licensure ≤ $300, geologist‑intern enrollment ≤ $100, certificate of authorization ≤ $300, emeritus ≤ $100) and an additional administrative collection fee up to $100. Board member reimbursement governed by §§81‑1174 to 81‑1177. No direct appropriations or new budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and expands duties, membership, and fee authorities for the state board administering the Geologists Regulation Act. Key government actors: the Nebraska board (which issues licenses, certificates of authorization, temporary permits, and prepares a Nebraska-specific exam); the Governor (appoints board members); college/university presidents (may recommend one faculty member for appointment); and the National Association of State Boards of Geology (board may adopt its exams/guidelines). Statutory cross‑references govern reimbursement and record‑distribution fees (sections 81‑1174 to 81‑1177 and 84‑712 to 84‑712.09). Funding/fee changes: application/renewal and certificate fees are nonrefundable; the board may add up to $100 to cover administrative collection costs; it may charge for roster copies and may waive first reexamination fees. No general appropriations or budget allocations are added.
