Summary

LB227 amends carrier statutes to address Medicaid-related transportation contracts with the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS). Key provisions:

- Regulated motor carriers may receive a designation of authority to provide Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) only for contracts with (i) DHHS, (ii) DHHS-contracted Medicaid managed-care organizations, or (iii) DHHS agents per section 75-303.01.

- In evaluating such designations, the Public Service Commission must consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long-Term Care (or designee) of DHHS’s Division of Medicaid and Long-Term Care.

- Applicants seeking NEMT authority bear the burden to show the service meets public demand; protestants must show existing carriers meet the need or that the applicant would impair existing operations.

- Transportation network companies or participating drivers must obtain specific commission authorization before transporting persons under contract with DHHS or its contractors; the commission must grant authorization unless protestants demonstrate harms as specified.

- No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a requirement that when the commission considers authorization for providers of Medicaid nonemergency medical transportation (NEMT) under a contract with the Department of Health and Human Services, a Medicaid managed‑care organization contracting with the department, or another agent acting for the department per §75‑303.01, the commission must consult with the Director of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care (or designee) of the Division of Medicaid and Long‑Term Care. No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
