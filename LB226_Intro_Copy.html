Summary

LB226 permits courts to set aside convictions or juvenile adjudications for certain "concealed weapon" offenses and requires courts, upon granting relief, to notify and order sealing by relevant agencies. Agencies named: county, district, and separate juvenile courts (venue for motions); Nebraska Commission on Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice; Nebraska State Patrol; local law enforcement agencies; county and city attorneys. The bill expands sealing procedures (including for victims of sex trafficking and pardoned persons) and authorizes motions to seal once convictions are set aside.

It creates a private cause of action under the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act, (1) waives sovereign immunity for the state, its agencies, political subdivisions, officers, and employees, (2) excludes such claims from the State and Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Acts, and (3) sets a four‑year statute of limitations (with a January 1, 2029 backstop for certain governmental-capacity claims). No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds procedures allowing courts to set aside and then order sealing of convictions or juvenile adjudications for certain "concealed weapon" offenses, with filings made in the county, district, or separate juvenile court where convicted. Cites and conditions application of the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act and creates private liability where a state agency, political subdivision, or their officers/employees fail to follow those rules. Consent is given to join the state, any state agency or political subdivision, and their officers/employees as defendants; sovereign immunity is waived for such actions. Claims under this provision are excluded from the State Tort Claims Act and Political Subdivisions Tort Claims Act and are subject to a limitations deadline (generally four years, or by Jan 1, 2029 for certain older accruals). No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
