Summary

LB128 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §75-426, clarifying the Public Service Commission’s authority to investigate railroad accidents and imposing reporting duties on railroad corporations. After any accident in Nebraska that results in death, injury, or property loss, the railroad operator must notify the Public Service Commission within 24 hours and furnish the Commission a copy of the report it files with the Federal Railroad Administration. Those reports are inadmissible in court. A violation is a Class IV misdemeanor. The bill repeals the prior version of §75-426. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds authority for "the commission" to investigate the cause of all railroad accidents in the state that result in loss of life, injury to persons, or property damage.
