Summary

LB353 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §79-873 and prohibits labor organizations representing public school employees or employers of public school employees from imposing, in any membership contract entered or renewed after the bill’s effective date, restrictions on when a public school employee may join or terminate membership. The bill retains authorization procedures for payroll deductions (authorization in writing, employee notice, continued effect until revoked) and preserves certification of deduction amounts by the organization, but clarifies that the new timing restriction controls. The original §79-873 is repealed. Applies to school districts and labor/professional organizations; no changes to funding or appropriations. Emergency clause makes the act effective upon enactment.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a proviso that the ban on labor organizations placing timing restrictions on when public school employees may join or leave (for contracts entered or renewed after the act's effective date) is subject to any exceptions set out in section 1 of the act. No government agencies or departments are referenced, and there are no funding, appropriation, or budget changes indicated.
