Summary

LB648 amends revenue distribution to direct certain sales/use tax proceeds to the Department of Transportation. Specifically, for transactions occurring on or after July 1, 2023, all proceeds of the sales and use taxes imposed under section 77-2703 on the sale or lease of aircraft (per section 3-101) are credited to the Department of Transportation Aeronautics Capital Improvement Fund. The act’s operative date is July 1, 2026.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The added language sets the statewide sales tax rate beginning July 1, 2026 at a placeholder "XX percent," with a special lower rate of 2.75% for transactions inside portions of Good Life Transformational Projects Act districts that lie within a city or village. There are no provisions in this text that change Department of Transportation authority, funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
