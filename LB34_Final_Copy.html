Summary

This bill amends Nebraska time statutes and related state personnel rules. It conditions adoption of a single year‑round standard time (UTC‑5 central, UTC‑6 mountain) on federal authorization or a U.S. Secretary of Transportation order and matching laws in three adjacent states; Coordinated Universal Time is to be interpreted by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. It harmonizes election polling deadlines by time zone. Personnel provisions reference central time for year‑end balancing of sick and vacation accounts, assign rulemaking to the Director of Personnel, and require employing agencies to pay denied, forfeited vacation in cash (counted as compensation only for defined contribution or cash‑balance plans administered by the Public Employees Retirement Board). The Governor and the Executive Board of the Legislative Council retain discretionary authority on special leave. No funding or appropriations changes are made.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state standard-time provision for Nebraska: central zone as UTC−5 and mountain zone as UTC−6 (effectively establishing a single year‑round time standard). It references the General Conference on Weights and Measures as the maintainer of Coordinated Universal Time, with the U.S. Secretary of Commerce interpreting/modifying UTC for the United States in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. Implementation is contingent on authorization by U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation (or no longer conflicting with such), and on three adjacent states adopting a single year‑round time. The bill also voids this section if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are mentioned.
