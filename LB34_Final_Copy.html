Summary

LB34 revises Nebraska time and related state-administration statutes. It conditions year‑round daylight saving time by setting state standard time to UTC‑5 (central) and UTC‑6 (mountain) once authorized by federal law or order and after three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round standard; current daylight‑saving provisions (second Sunday in March to first Sunday in November) become null when that provision becomes operative. The bill amends election statute 32‑908 (poll hours and ballot deadlines tied to mountain/central zones). It standardizes sick‑ and vacation‑leave accounting to 11:59 p.m. central time on December 31, clarifies employing agency responsibilities, gives the Governor (or Legislative Council Executive Board for legislative employees) discretion to extend leave, tasks the Director of Personnel with rulemaking, and addresses treatment by the Public Employees Retirement Board. No new funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state standard-time rule: Nebraska’s central time zone would be UTC−5 and its mountain zone UTC−6. It defines Coordinated Universal Time as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and “interpreted or modified for the United States” by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The provision becomes operative on the first Sunday in November after (a) it is authorized or no longer conflicts with U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation and (b) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time. It also states the section becomes null and void when subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No agencies are assigned funding or appropriation changes.
