Summary

LB34 amends Nebraska time statutes to authorize year‑round daylight saving time by setting state standard time to UTC‑5 (central zone) and UTC‑6 (mountain zone), conditioned on authorization under federal law or order and adoption of a single year‑round standard by three adjacent states. It repeals the current limited DST period once that condition is met. Election law polling and ballot‑receipt deadlines remain tied to the mountain/central zones but are harmonized with the time changes. Administrative adjustments set sick‑ and vacation‑leave accounting deadlines at 11:59 p.m. central time on December 31. State agencies referenced: Director of Personnel (rulemaking), Public Employees Retirement Board (treatment of cash payments to employees denied vacation leave for certain retirement plans), Governor and the Legislative Council Executive Board (discretion to extend leave), and employing agencies (vacation administration). No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added provisions set Nebraska’s standard time as UTC−5 in the central time zone and UTC−6 in the mountain time zone. "Coordinated Universal Time" is defined as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and as interpreted/modified for the United States by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The subsection becomes operative only after it is authorized or no longer conflicts with U.S. law or any order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation and after three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time law. The provision is void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
