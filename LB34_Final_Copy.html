Summary — LB34 (time/harmonization)

- Establishes conditions for year‑round time: if authorized under federal law or orders and three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round standard, Nebraska’s standard time would be UTC−5 in the central zone and UTC−6 in the mountain zone; current daylight saving provisions become null when that subsection is operative.

- Election administration: clarifies poll hours and ballot receipt deadlines by time zone (mountain 7 a.m.–7 p.m.; central 8 a.m.–8 p.m.).

- State personnel administration: harmonizes reference to central time (11:59 p.m. Dec. 31) for balancing sick and vacation leave accounts; Director of Personnel to adopt rules; employing agencies must, in specified denied‑leave hardship cases, pay cash equivalents within 30 days. Such payments count as compensation for defined contribution/cash‑balance plans administered by the Public Employees Retirement Board but not for other defined benefit plans.

- Agencies mentioned: Director of Personnel, employing agencies, Governor, Legislature/Legislative Council/Executive Board, Public Employees Retirement Board. No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Added text defines Nebraska’s standard time and names federal officials involved in U.S. time interpretation. Key points:

- Establishes state standard time: central time zone = UTC‑5; mountain time zone = UTC‑6.

- Defines “Coordinated Universal Time” as the scale maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and interpreted/modified for the U.S. by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy.

- Makes the subsection operative on the first Sunday in November after (a) the change is authorized by U.S. law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation (or no longer conflicts with them) and (b) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time.

- Specifies the section becomes void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative.
