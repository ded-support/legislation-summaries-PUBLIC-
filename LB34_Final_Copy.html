Summary

LB34 authorizes Nebraska to observe year‑round daylight saving time when federal law or a U.S. Secretary of Transportation order allows and three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round standard. It amends state time statutes and polling-hour references for the central and mountain time zones.

Agencies/departments referenced and effects:

- Governor: retains discretion to extend vacation leave in special/meritorious cases.

- Legislature / Legislative Council / Executive Board of the Legislative Council: Executive Board may extend vacation leave for legislative employees; legislative employment service credit rules referenced.

- Director of Personnel: directed to adopt rules to administer vacation leave provisions.

- Public Employees Retirement Board: treatment of cash payment for denied vacation leave specified for defined contribution/cash‑balance plans.

- State agencies / employing agencies / department heads: responsible to provide opportunity to use accrued vacation leave and to pay denied, forfeited leave when appropriate.

- State Personnel System: certain exemptions noted (section 81‑1316).

No changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a state standard-time provision: Nebraska’s central zone = UTC−5 and mountain zone = UTC−6. Defines Coordinated Universal Time as maintained by the General Conference on Weights and Measures and “interpreted or modified for the United States” by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce in coordination with the U.S. Secretary of the Navy. The provision only becomes operative after (1) authorization or nonconflict with federal law or an order of the U.S. Secretary of Transportation and (2) three adjacent states adopt a single year‑round time. It also becomes null and void if subsection (2) of §49‑1301 becomes operative. No state funding, appropriations, or budget changes are referenced.
