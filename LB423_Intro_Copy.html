Summary

LB423 places the Job Training Cash Fund under the direction of the Department of Economic Development, requiring the department to establish subaccounts for Cash Reserve Fund transfers and for training grants for small employers, with prior-year investment earnings allocated to that subaccount. The department must give preference to training activities and grants that occur in enterprise zones or federal opportunity zones and to specified rural or high-poverty-area businesses. The bill maintains the Intern Nebraska Cash Fund and authorizes the Department of Economic Development, or its contractors, to use up to 5% of any appropriation for administrative services. It directs the State Treasurer to transfer any unobligated Intern Nebraska Cash Fund balances to the General Fund on or before June 30, 2026, and allows transfers between the Job Training Cash Fund and the General Fund at the Legislature’s direction.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill directs the State Treasurer to transfer any unobligated balances in the Intern Nebraska Cash Fund to the General Fund on or before June 30, 2026, in amounts and on dates specified by the budget administrator of the budget division of the Department of Administrative Services. For the Department of Economic Development, this reduces or eliminates remaining cash‑fund resources held for the Intern Nebraska program, potentially decreasing available program funding; no new appropriations or budget allocations to the Department are specified in the added text.
