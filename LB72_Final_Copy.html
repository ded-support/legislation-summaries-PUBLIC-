Summary

LB72 amends Neb. Rev. Stat. §§28-405 and 28-416; the provision affecting the Department of Correctional Services requires that any person convicted under §28-416 who is sentenced to the Department must attend appropriate drug‑abuse treatment and counseling. The bill contains no other references to the Department of Correctional Services and includes no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for that department.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds and clarifies controlled-substance definitions: excludes FDA‑approved crystalline polymorph psilocybin pharmaceutical products; expressly adds xylazine and a long list of its isomers/metabolites to the controlled-substance list while carving out uses tied to FDA‑approved or investigational animal drug authorizations and permissible veterinary/pharmaceutical compounding; and adds zuranolone by chemical name. No language allocates funding, appropriations, or budget changes. For the Department of Correctional Services, there are no direct funding or appropriation impacts in the added text (only statutory redefinitions that could affect enforcement/charges, not agency budget.
