Summary

Key provisions referencing the Department of Insurance (the "department"):

- An insurance corporation may waive the statutory requirement that at least one board member be a Nebraska resident for a five-year period upon filing an affidavit with the department affirming specified criteria (including that the corporation’s ultimate controlling entity “as defined by the department” has not changed in ten years).

- If the corporation ceases to meet those criteria during the five-year period, it must notify the Department of Insurance specifying which criteria are no longer met and appoint a Nebraska-resident director within six months.

No funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a five‑year waiver process to the statute requiring at least one board member of a domiciled insurer be a Nebraska resident. The insurer may file an affidavit with the Department of Insurance, signed by the board president/chair and notarized, certifying it: has principal offices in Nebraska; is publicly traded (or wholly owned by a publicly traded company); has been domiciled in Nebraska ≥25 years; employs >500 Nebraska‑based, Nebraska‑taxed workers; and whose ultimate controlling entity (as defined by the Department) has not changed in ten years. The affidavit may be filed when criteria are met and renewed after five years. If criteria lapse during the term, the insurer must notify the Department, identify which criteria failed, and appoint a Nebraska resident director within six months. No funding, appropriation, or budget changes are included.
