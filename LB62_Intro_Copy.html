Summary

LB62 appropriates $19,000,000 from the General Fund for FY2025-26 to the Department of Administrative Services, Program 245. DAS is directed to provide these funds to the Office of the Chief Information Officer to support interoperable communications (portable and mobile radios, programming, installation) and related training via a grant program to connect volunteer departments to the Statewide Radio System. The CIO shall not charge subscription, programming, or training fees to volunteer departments receiving a grant. The Legislature intends all grant funds to be distributed by June 30, 2028.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill appropriates $19,000,000 General Fund for FY2025-26 to the Department of Administrative Services (Program 245). DAS must provide these funds to the Office of the Chief Information Officer to create interoperable communications for volunteer departments and state agencies by funding portable/mobile radios, programming, installation, and interoperability training via a grant program. Grant eligibility is based on each volunteer department's size and radio needs. After a grant, the OCIO may not charge the volunteer department subscription, programming, or training fees. The Legislature intends all grant funds to be distributed by June 30, 2028. "Volunteer department" is defined per section 35-901.
