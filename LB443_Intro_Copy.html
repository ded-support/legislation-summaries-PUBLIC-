Summary

LB443 amends the Nebraska Criminal Code to create the offense of unlawful squatting and assigns specific duties to law enforcement. If an officer has probable cause (e.g., personal property indicating abandonment or a nonpermanent structure), the officer must issue a warning citation. The citation directs the recipient to appear within three business days before a designee of the officer’s law enforcement agency and produce documentation authorizing residency (deed, title, rental agreement, proof of payments; photos/electronic copies accepted). Failure to appear or produce authentic documentation permits arrest; conviction is a Class I misdemeanor. No state agencies, departments, or budget appropriations are named or altered, and no funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds an offense of unlawful squatting: knowingly entering and residing on another’s land without consent with intent to claim or appropriate it. Law enforcement officers with probable cause (e.g., signs of abandonment or nonpermanent structures) must issue a warning citation that requires the recipient, within three business days, to appear before a designee of the issuing law enforcement agency to present documentation authorizing residency (deed, title, rental agreement, proof of payments; photos/electronic copies acceptable). Failure to appear or presenting inauthentic/nonauthorizing documentation permits arrest; conviction is a Class I misdemeanor. The only government entity referenced is the issuing law enforcement agency (and its designee). No funding, appropriations, or budget changes are included.
