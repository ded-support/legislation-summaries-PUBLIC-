Summary

The bill requires the Auditor of Public Accounts to provide the Department of Revenue, by February 1 each year, a list of bond and nonbond tax request amounts from the most recent municipal budgets; the Department will use that information to calculate bond and nonbond tax levies for aid purposes. The bill also removes cities of the first class from eligibility for state aid from the Municipal Equalization Fund and retains provisions for prorated distributions if the Fund is insufficient and crediting any excess to the General Fund.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the phrase "or if such municipality is a city of the first class," broadening applicability or qualification to include municipalities designated as cities of the first class.
