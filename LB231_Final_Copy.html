Summary

LB231 adopts the Uniform Special Deposits Act. The bill’s only explicit reference to the Department of Banking and Finance appears in the definition of “financial institution,” which includes banks, savings banks, building and loan associations, savings and loan associations, credit unions, trust companies, and digital asset depositories “chartered by the United States, the Department of Banking and Finance, or a foreign state agency.” No sections of the bill propose changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations for the Department of Banking and Finance.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

The bill enacts the Uniform Special Deposits Act and explicitly defines "financial institution" to include entities chartered by the Department of Banking and Finance (alongside U.S. and foreign chartering authorities). That means state‑chartered banks, savings associations, trust companies, credit unions, and certain digital asset depositories under the Department's charter are covered by the Act's rules on special deposits, beneficiary rights, obligations, limitations on liability, termination, and related procedures.
