Summary

Bill LB188 states legislative intent regarding appropriations to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) for Medicaid nursing facility rates (Program No. 348). It specifies total amounts: $462,480,546 for FY2025‑26 and $476,717,026 for FY2026‑27 (General/Federal splits not specified). Those amounts are to be used in the Medicaid nursing facility rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor, and include rate enhancements and other Medicaid nursing facility purposes. DHHS must electronically report to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and Clerk by August 1, 2025 on how the FY2025‑26 inflation factor was calculated, and between December 15–31, 2025 on any remaining unobligated prior appropriations (including encumbrances and retroactive payments). The act takes effect immediately upon passage.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Department of Health and Human Services — Program No. 348 (Medicaid nursing facility rates)

- Legislature specifies total appropriations for Medicaid nursing facility rates as $462,480,546 for FY2025‑26 and $476,717,026 for FY2026‑27 (General/Federal splits not specified).

- These amounts must be included in the Program 348 rate calculation, including the annual inflation factor, and cover rate enhancements and other Medicaid nursing facility purposes.

- Reporting requirements: DHHS must electronically report to the Legislative Fiscal Analyst and Clerk of the Legislature by Aug 1, 2025, on how the FY2025‑26 inflation factor was calculated; and again between Dec 15–31, 2025, identifying any remaining unobligated prior appropriations for Medicaid nursing facility payments, including encumbrances and retroactive payments.
