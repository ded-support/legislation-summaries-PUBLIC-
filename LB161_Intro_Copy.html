Summary

LB161 amends section 79-1003 of the Tax Equity and Educational Opportunities Support Act, updating statutory definitions used to calculate state aid. Key agencies/departments referenced: the State Department of Education (defined as "Department"), the State Board of Education, the School Employees Retirement System of the State of Nebraska, the Class V School Employees Retirement Act retirement system, and the Clerk of the Legislature (for lobbyist reporting). The substantive change revises the "formula students" computation to explicitly include one hundred percent of qualified early childhood education fall membership and average daily membership in aid calculations. No direct appropriations or dollar funding changes are contained; the amendment affects aid and budget allocation mechanics by altering the student-count basis used in state aid and general fund operating expenditure calculations. The bill repeals the original section.
