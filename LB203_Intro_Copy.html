Summary

- The bill requires county, district, or city-county health departments to be approved by the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) to qualify as local full‑time public health services and to be operated in conformity with DHHS rules, regulations, and policies.

- Agreements creating city‑county health departments must be approved by DHHS.

- Health directors are directed to distribute free vaccines, drugs, serums, and other public‑health preparations obtained from DHHS.

- The bill allows local departments to utilize local, state, federal, and other funds but contains no explicit changes to DHHS funding, appropriations, or state budget allocations.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a definition of "community-wide directed health measure" as a public‑health action by a local health department covering the entire jurisdiction when there are no known epidemiological links. Requires a local health director to obtain written approval from a majority of the publicly elected county board members (or, for city–county health departments, a majority of the publicly elected city council members) before issuing such measures, and to submit the measures to the city‑county board of health for ratification at its next regular meeting. No references to the Department of Health and Human Services or to any funding, appropriations, or budget allocations; no fiscal changes included.
