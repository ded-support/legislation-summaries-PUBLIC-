Summary

LB119 requires the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges and the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska to execute a memorandum of understanding to jointly administer the Rural Health Opportunity Program, including a joint application/interview process and provisional admission into eligible University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) health programs. Participating students meeting Nebraska high‑school/equivalency and rural‑residency criteria (as determined by the two boards) receive a 100% waiver of tuition and fees for up to four years at a state college to complete prerequisite coursework for transfer to UNMC. The Legislature states its intent to appropriate sufficient funds each fiscal year to implement the section (no specific appropriation amount specified).

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds the Rural Health Opportunity Program and requires the Board of Trustees of the Nebraska State Colleges and the Board of Regents of the University of Nebraska to execute a memorandum of understanding to jointly administer it (shared application/interview process) and provisionally admit students into programs at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. Eligibility: Nebraska high‑school (or equivalent) graduates who have lived in a rural Nebraska area as determined by those Boards. Participating students receive a 100% tuition-and-fee waiver for up to four years at a state college to complete prerequisite health coursework for transfer to UNMC. The Legislature intends to appropriate sufficient annual funds to implement the section (no specific amounts set).
