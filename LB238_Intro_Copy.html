Summary

LB238 amends the Foster Care Review Act. It clarifies that the executive director of the office (responsible for foster care review) selects local foster care review board members (boards of 4–10) and must send member lists to the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) and the Office of Probation Administration. It bars employees of the office, DHHS, residential child‑caring or child‑placing agencies, and courts from board appointment. Local foster care review boards are expressly exempted from the Nebraska Open Meetings Act. The bill preserves board duties to conduct foster care file audit reviews at least every six months, submit findings and recommendations to the court within 30 days, and request court review hearings when appropriate. It repeals original sections 43‑1304 and 43‑1308 and outright repeals section 43‑1306. No funding or appropriation changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds a provision stating that "local boards" are not considered public bodies for purposes of the Open Meetings Act. No specific government agencies or departments are named, and there are no changes to funding, appropriations, or budget allocations.
