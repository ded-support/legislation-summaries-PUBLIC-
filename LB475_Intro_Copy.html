Summary

LB475 (Riepe) amends Nebraska’s controlled‑substance law by classifying tianeptine as a controlled substance, revising Schedules III–V to list numerous drugs, and cross‑referencing federal standards. The bill cites the Drug Enforcement Administration (U.S. Department of Justice) exempted‑products/anabolic‑steroid list (as of Jan. 31, 2022) and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and 21 U.S.C. §355 (FD&C Act §505) for FDA‑approved product exclusions (e.g., dronabinol). It repeals Neb. Rev. Stat. §28‑405 (R.S. Cumulative Supp., 2024) and was referred to the Judiciary Committee. No state funding, appropriations, or budget allocations are included.
