Summary

The bill expresses legislative intent to appropriate $10,000,000 in FY2025‑26 and $10,000,000 in FY2026‑27 (Federal Funds) to the Department of Health and Human Services, Program No. 347 (Public Assistance). The appropriation is to include funds from the federal Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) program for grants to nonprofit organizations that (1) provide food assistance, (2) distribute food in ten or more Nebraska counties, and (3) qualify for the Emergency Food Assistance Program administered by the United States Department of Agriculture. The act takes effect immediately upon passage due to an emergency clause.

DHHS — Program No. 347 (Public Assistance) receives an intent appropriation of $10,000,000 in Federal Funds for FY2025‑26 and $10,000,000 for FY2026‑27.
