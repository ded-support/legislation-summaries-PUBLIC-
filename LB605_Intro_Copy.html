Summary

LB605 requires each school board to adopt a policy governing student participation as a transgender student in school-sponsored extracurricular activities and in activities governed by any athletics or activities association of which the school is a member. Under the policy school administrators or athletic directors must submit a written application from the student (and parent/guardian) and supporting documentation to the school board; the board must determine eligibility—generally based on biological sex on the student's birth certificate unless approved as a transgender participant—and, where applicable, submit required documentation to and cooperate with the athletics/activities association for its eligibility determination. The bill disclaims encouraging medical treatment and does not alter existing cross-gender participation exceptions. No appropriations or funding changes are included.

Underlined (Added) Text Summary

Adds requirements that local school boards (based on information provided by the school administrator or athletic director) determine and document transgender students' eligibility for extracurriculars and, when necessary, submit and cooperate with any athletics or activities association to which the school belongs. Permitted shared documentation includes current school registration, a written statement from the student and parent/guardian, and corroborating statements from others (parents, teachers, friends). Establishes that eligibility for male/female-separated teams defaults to the biological sex on the student's birth certificate unless the student is approved under the board's transgender or cross‑gender policy. Clarifies the policy does not encourage medical transition and does not alter cross‑gender exceptions when no female team exists. No agencies beyond school boards, school administrators/athletic directors, and athletics/activities associations are added, and no funding or appropriations are included.
